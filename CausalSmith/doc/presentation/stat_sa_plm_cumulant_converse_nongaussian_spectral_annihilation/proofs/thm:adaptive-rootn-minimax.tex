\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}]
\begin{enumerate}
\item Define the primitive search radius
\[
R_{\mathrm{fix}}
=
A_{r+1}\left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^{2}}{\delta}\right)^{1/(r-1)}+1,
\]
where \(A_{r+1}\) is the numerical factor in \cref{obj:def:contour-bank-handle}.  Let \(K_{\mathrm{emp}}>0\) be the constant supplied by \cref{obj:lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2} for
\((C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi},R_{\mathrm{fix}})\).  For \(y\in\mathbb R\), put
\[
u_{\mathrm{ceil}}(y)=\left\lceil \max\{1,y+1\}+1\right\rceil ,
\]
and define
\[
\begin{gathered}
U_{\psi}^{\circ}=u_{\mathrm{ceil}}(\psi_{\eta}),\qquad
U_R^{\circ}=u_{\mathrm{ceil}}(R_{\mathrm{fix}}),\\
N_{\mathrm{cert}}^{\circ}=4(U_{\psi}^{\circ})^2(U_R^{\circ}+2)^3,\qquad
m_{\star}^{\circ}=N_{\mathrm{cert}}^{\circ}+3,\\
e_{\star}^{\circ}=8U_R^{\circ}(U_{\psi}^{\circ})^2(U_R^{\circ}+1)^2,\qquad
d_{\star,\mathrm{den}}^{\circ}=3(2U_R^{\circ}+1),\\
u_{\star}^{\circ}=2e_{\star}^{\circ}
+N_{\mathrm{cert}}^{\circ}\bigl(m_{\star}^{\circ}+d_{\star,\mathrm{den}}^{\circ}\bigr),
\qquad
a_{\star}^{\circ}=2^{-u_{\star}^{\circ}}.
\end{gathered}
\]
Thus \(a_{\star}^{\circ}>0\).  Set
\[
C_G^{\circ}
=
\left[
64\{C_q^4+4C_{\theta}^4\psi_{\eta}^4+4\psi_{\xi}^4\}
+2\exp\{16R_{\mathrm{fix}}C_g+16R_{\mathrm{fix}}^2\psi_{\eta}^2\}
\right]^{1/2},
\]
\[
L_{\mathrm{fix}}
=
R_{\mathrm{fix}}
\max\left\{
\left(\frac{23a_{\star}^{\circ}}{64}\right)^{-1},
C_G^{\circ}
\left(\frac{31a_{\star}^{\circ}}{64}\frac{23a_{\star}^{\circ}}{64}\right)^{-1}
\right\},
\]
and
\[
C_{\mathrm{fix}}
=
12L_{\mathrm{fix}}^2K_{\mathrm{emp}}
+\frac{1536C_{\theta}^2K_{\mathrm{emp}}}{(a_{\star}^{\circ})^2}
+2.
\]
Since \(R_{\mathrm{fix}}>0\), \(K_{\mathrm{emp}}>0\), and \(a_{\star}^{\circ}>0\), one has \(C_{\mathrm{fix}}>0\).  The two fixed-code converse arguments below give positive constants \(\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{NG}}\) and \(\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{JMS}}\), depending only on the same primitive tuple: \(\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{NG}}\) comes from the fixed-code non-Gaussian converse, and \(\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{JMS}}\) comes from the separate fixed-code JMS ACE converse.  We take
\[
c=\min\{\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{NG}},\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{JMS}}\},
\qquad
C=C_{\mathrm{fix}}.
\]
Then \(c>0\) and \(C>0\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.adaptive_rootn_minimax

\item Fix the covariate measurable space, \(p_0\), the fixed records, \(p\), and the base model as in the statement.  The transported-record hypothesis gives
\[
p.C_{\theta}=C_{\theta},\quad p.C_g=C_g,\quad p.C_q=C_q,\quad p.\psi_{\eta}=\psi_{\eta},\quad p.\psi_{\xi}=\psi_{\xi},
\quad \operatorname{searchRadius}(p)=R_{\mathrm{fix}}.
\]
Let \(\mathfrak p_{\star,p}\) and \(\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,p}\) denote the transported bank and range records.  If \(a_{\star,p}\) is the lower-modulus certificate of the actual contour bank, then the width-one certified refinement containing \(\psi_{\eta}\) and \(R_{\mathrm{fix}}\) gives
\[
u_{\star,p}\le u_{\star}^{\circ},
\qquad
a_{\star}^{\circ}=2^{-u_{\star}^{\circ}}\le 2^{-u_{\star,p}}=a_{\star,p}.
\]
Consequently the actual selector constant
\[
C_{\mathrm{sel}}(p,\mathfrak p_{\star,p})
=
12L_p^2K_{\mathrm{emp}}
+\frac{1536p.C_{\theta}^2K_{\mathrm{emp}}}{a_{\star,p}^2}
+2,
\]
with
\[
L_p
=
\operatorname{searchRadius}(p)
\max\left\{
\left(\frac{23a_{\star,p}}{64}\right)^{-1},
C_G(p)
\left(\frac{31a_{\star,p}}{64}\frac{23a_{\star,p}}{64}\right)^{-1}
\right\},
\]
satisfies
\[
C_{\mathrm{sel}}(p,\mathfrak p_{\star,p})\le C.
\]
The monotonicity is exactly the monotonicity of the bank exponent, the map \(u\mapsto 2^{-u}\), and positive reciprocals. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.adaptiveSelectorRiskConstant_le_uniform

\item The statistic is measurable.  Indeed, \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) is obtained by running the finite rational contour program of \cref{obj:def:adaptive-contour-estimator} and then clipping its raw real output:
\[
\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}(O_1,\ldots,O_n)
=
\min\left\{
\max\left\{Y_{\mathrm{raw}}(O_1,\ldots,O_n),-C_{\theta}\right\},
C_{\theta}
\right\}.
\]
On each finite branch trace, the queried observation endpoints are floor-dyadic functions of the sample coordinates, and the remaining operations are rational arithmetic and finite comparisons.  The terminal traces are countable and exhaustive, so \(Y_{\mathrm{raw}}\) is Borel measurable; clipping by constants preserves measurability. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.thetaHatSpec_measurable

\item The represented execution clause follows from the canonical compilation contract.  For every sample,
\[
\operatorname{Rep}_{\mathrm{compiled}}
\bigl(\operatorname{canonicalInput}_{p,\mathfrak p_{\star,p},\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,p}}\bigr)
=
\operatorname{Ord}_{p,\mathfrak p_{\star,p},\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,p}},
\]
the represented trace equals the ordinary trace, and the represented output record certifies
\[
\min\{\max\{Y_{\mathrm{raw}},-C_{\theta}\},C_{\theta}\}.
\]
The last identity is the elementary three-case equality
\[
\frac{|y+C_{\theta}|-|y-C_{\theta}|}{2}
=
\min\{\max\{y,-C_{\theta}\},C_{\theta}\},
\]
used with the positive range record for \(C_{\theta}\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.representedExecution_of_fullCanonicalBuildAndCompilation

\item The class relations are those of \cref{obj:lem:jms-ace-class-relations}: for every model \(m\),
\[
m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n},
\]
and, if \(s=r\),
\[
m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}.
\]
The proof uses monotonicity of \(L^p(P_X)\)-norms on a probability space to pass from the published \(L^r(P_X)\) treatment-code condition to the \(L^1(P_X)\) treatment-code condition, and when \(s=r\) the comparison subclass imposes the same code-radius requirement. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.jms_ace_class_relations

\item We record the pointwise risk bound used in both fixed-code classes.  Let \(m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), assume \(n\ge2\), and assume
\[
\varepsilon_{1,n}\le
\{4R_1\exp(2C_gR_1)\}^{-1},
\]
where \(R_1=\operatorname{searchRadius}(p)\).  For \(a\in\{0,1\}\) and every sample define
\[
d_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|,
\qquad
e_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|.
\]
Set
\[
\mathcal A=\{d_0\le a_{\star,p}/8,
              \ d_1\le a_{\star,p}/8\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal A\), the canonical selector packet supplies exactly the hypotheses of \cref{obj:lem:selected-contour-perturbation-bound}.  The packet is obtained as follows.  First, \cref{obj:lem:finite-contour-bank} gives a bank contour \(C_{j_0}\) for the treatment-innovation transform with positive zero count and boundary modulus at least \(a_{\star,p}\).  By \cref{obj:lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}, the smallness condition gives \(|H_n(z)|\ge3/4\) on the search disk and preserves the zero count from the treatment-innovation transform to \(F_n=M H_n\); hence \(C_{j_0}\) has positive \(F_n\)-zero count, \(H_n\) is zero-free on and inside the contour, and \(|F_n(z)|\ge3a_{\star,p}/4\) on its boundary.

The pilot-fold inequality \(d_0\le a_{\star,p}/8\), together with the certified modulus width \(a_{\star,p}/64\) and the certified winding enclosure in \cref{obj:def:adaptive-contour-estimator}, makes this population contour admissible: its pilot lower endpoint is at least \(39a_{\star,p}/64\), and its decoded pilot integer is the positive population zero count.  The selected contour maximizes the pilot lower endpoint among admissible contours, so for the selected contour \(C_j\) and decoded integer \(N\),
\[
\mu_p:=\frac{31a_{\star,p}}{64}\le |F_n(z)|
\qquad (z\in C_j),
\]
while the evaluation-fold inequality \(d_1\le a_{\star,p}/8\) gives the evaluation modulus margin required by the selector and
\[
\nu_p:=\frac{23a_{\star,p}}{64}>0,
\qquad
 d_1\le \mu_p-\nu_p.
\]
The same certified node specifications give
\[
|\widehat F_{1,n}(z)-F_n(z)|\le d_1,
\qquad
|\widehat G_{1,n}(z)-G_n(z)|\le e_1
\qquad (z\in C_j),
\]
and \cref{obj:lem:population-numerator-envelope} gives \(|G_n(z)|\le C_G(p)\) on \(|z|\le R_1\).  Continuity and the positive denominator margins give the two contour-integrability hypotheses for the empirical and population ratios.  Finally, \cref{obj:thm:exact-contour-identification} applies to the selected contour, because \(F_n\) is zero-free on \(C_j\), \(H_n\) is zero-free on and inside \(C_j\), and the decoded integer \(N\) is the positive population zero count; therefore the selected population contour ratio has real part \(\theta_0(m)\).

Applying \cref{obj:lem:selected-contour-perturbation-bound} with
\[
\mu_p=\frac{31a_{\star,p}}{64},
\qquad
\nu_p=\frac{23a_{\star,p}}{64}
\]
gives, on \(\mathcal A\),
\[
|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(m)|
\le
L_p(d_1+e_1)+\frac1n.
\]
Hence, on \(\mathcal A\),
\[
(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(m))^2
\le
4L_p^2(d_1^2+e_1^2)+\frac{2}{n^2}.
\]
The folds have cardinality at least \(n/3\), and \cref{obj:lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2} gives
\[
E(d_a^2+e_a^2)\le \frac{3K_{\mathrm{emp}}}{n}
\qquad(a=0,1).
\]
Markov's inequality therefore yields
\[
P(\mathcal A^c)
\le
\frac{384K_{\mathrm{emp}}}{a_{\star,p}^2n}.
\]
Since the estimator and the target both lie in \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\),
\[
(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(m))^2\le 4C_{\theta}^2
\]
on \(\mathcal A^c\).  Integrating the two displays gives
\[
E_m\!\left[
(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(m))^2
\right]
\le
\frac{C_{\mathrm{sel}}(p,\mathfrak p_{\star,p})}{n}
\le
\frac{C}{n}.
\] % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.thetaHatSpec_mseRisk_le_explicit

\item The quantile bound is the Markov consequence of the preceding mean-square bound.  For
\[
W(O_1,\ldots,O_n)
=
|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}(O_1,\ldots,O_n)-\theta_0(m)|,
\qquad
t=\sqrt{\frac{C}{\gamma n}},
\]
the risk bound gives
\[
P(W>t)\le \frac{C/n}{t^2}=\gamma.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
P(W\le t)\ge 1-\gamma,
\]
and the defining property of the generalized lower quantile in \cref{obj:def:generalized-quantile} gives
\[
Q_{P,1-\gamma}(W)\le \sqrt{\frac{C}{\gamma n}}.
\]
 % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.thetaHatSpec_generalizedQuantile_le_of_mseRisk_le

\item It remains to identify the two lower constants.  Put
\[
b_{\theta}=\min\{C_{\theta},1\},
\qquad
V_{\mathrm{KL}}=\max\left\{1,\frac{16\psi_{\eta}^2}{\psi_{\xi}^2}\right\},
\qquad
\tau_{\mathrm{lo}}=\frac{\psi_{\xi}}4.
\]
For a nonempty fixed-code non-Gaussian class, choose \(m^{\circ}\) in that class.  For \(n\ge2\), define
\[
\theta_-=-\frac{b_{\theta}}{2\sqrt n},
\qquad
\theta_+=\frac{b_{\theta}}{2\sqrt n}.
\]
The affine Gaussian perturbations \(m_-\) and \(m_+\) keep the law of \((X,T)\), \(g_0,q_0\), and the supplied clipped codes from \(m^{\circ}\), and use the outcome equation
\[
Y=q_0(X)+\theta_{\pm}\eta+\xi_{\tau},
\qquad
\xi_{\tau}\sim N(0,\tau_{\mathrm{lo}}^2),
\]
with \(\xi_{\tau}\) independent of \((X,T)\).  Since \(|\theta_{\pm}|\le b_{\theta}\le C_{\theta}\), and since \cref{obj:lem:gaussian-exponential-square-quarter-scale} gives the required outcome-innovation Luxemburg envelope at scale \(\psi_{\xi}\), both \(m_-\) and \(m_+\) remain in the same fixed-code non-Gaussian class.  Their target separation is
\[
|\theta_+-\theta_-|=\frac{b_{\theta}}{\sqrt n}.
\]
The Gaussian likelihood ratio calculation gives
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(m_-^{\otimes n}\,\middle\|\,m_+^{\otimes n}\right)
\le
n\,\frac{(\theta_+-\theta_-)^2\,2\psi_{\eta}^2}{2\tau_{\mathrm{lo}}^2}
=
\frac{16\psi_{\eta}^2}{\psi_{\xi}^2}b_{\theta}^2
\le
V_{\mathrm{KL}}b_{\theta}^2.
\]
Le Cam's two-point MSE inequality therefore supplies a constant \(\lambda_{\mathrm{LC}}>0\), depending only on \(V_{\mathrm{KL}}b_{\theta}^2\), such that every measurable estimator \(\widetilde\theta\) satisfies
\[
\max_{\upsilon\in\{-,+\}}
E_{m_{\upsilon}}\!\left[
(\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(m_{\upsilon}))^2
\right]
\ge
\lambda_{\mathrm{LC}}\frac{b_{\theta}^2}{n}.
\]
Thus the fixed-code non-Gaussian minimax risk is at least
\[
\frac{\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{NG}}}{n},
\qquad
\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{NG}}=\lambda_{\mathrm{LC}}b_{\theta}^2>0.
\]
This is the fixed-code non-Gaussian converse. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.fixed_code_non_gaussian_minimax_lower

For a nonempty fixed-code JMS ACE class, the separate fixed-code ACE converse applies the same two-point argument starting from a law in that JMS ACE class.  The affine perturbations preserve JMS ACE membership, the target values \(\theta_-\) and \(\theta_+\), the law of \((X,T)\), the nuisance functions \(g_0,q_0\), and both clipped supplied codes.  The same product relative-entropy calculation and Le Cam inequality give a constant \(\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{JMS}}>0\), depending only on the primitive tuple, such that
\[
\frac{\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{JMS}}}{n}
\le
\inf_{\widetilde\theta}
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}(p;\mathrm{base})}
E_P\!\left[(\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P))^2\right].
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.fixed_code_ace_minimax_lower

\item For the fixed-code non-Gaussian class, assume the hypotheses in the theorem.  The lower bound from the preceding step and \(c\le\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{NG}}\) give
\[
\frac{c}{n}\le \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}.
\]
The statistic \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) is measurable by Step 3, so it is an admissible rule in the minimax infimum, and therefore
\[
\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}
\le
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}(p;\mathrm{base})}
E_P\!\left[
(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2
\right].
\]
For every \(P\) in this fixed-code class, the current clipped treatment code agrees with the base clipped treatment code.  Since the canonical inputs in \cref{obj:def:adaptive-contour-estimator} use the current clipped treatment code, the statistic assembled from the base treatment-code sequence agrees pointwise with the statistic assembled from the class member's treatment-code sequence.  Applying Step 6 to the latter statistic and then using this pointwise equality gives
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}(p;\mathrm{base})}
E_P\!\left[
(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2
\right]
\le
\frac{C}{n}.
\]
The same pointwise equality reduces the absolute-error statistic in the generalized-quantile display to the statistic covered by Step 7, giving the displayed generalized-quantile bound over the same supremum. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.adaptive_rootn_minimax

\item For the fixed-code JMS ACE class, assume its nonemptiness, \(n\ge2\), and the same smallness condition on \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\).  The ACE lower bound from Step 8 and \(c\le\mathfrak c_{\mathrm{JMS}}\) give
\[
\frac{c}{n}
\le
\inf_{\widetilde\theta}
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}(p;\mathrm{base})}
E_P\!\left[
(\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P))^2
\right].
\]
The estimator is measurable, hence admissible, so the infimum is bounded above by its worst-case risk.  By Step 5, every JMS ACE law lies in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  The fixed-code restriction gives the same current clipped treatment code as the base model, so the pointwise congruence from \cref{obj:def:adaptive-contour-estimator} identifies the statistic built from the base treatment-code sequence with the statistic built from the class member's treatment-code sequence.  Step 6 therefore yields
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}(p;\mathrm{base})}
E_P\!\left[
(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2
\right]
\le
\frac{C}{n}.
\]
The same pointwise equality and Step 7 give the asserted generalized-quantile bound on this class as well. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.adaptive_rootn_minimax
\end{enumerate}
\end{proof}
