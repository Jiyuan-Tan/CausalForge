\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:common-experiment-dichotomy}]
\begin{enumerate}
\item
Apply \cref{obj:thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax} with the fixed constants
\[
r,\delta,C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi}.
\]
It gives constants \(c,C>0\), depending only on these displayed constants, with the fixed-code non-Gaussian lower and upper bounds stated there. Also apply \cref{obj:prop:bounded-outcome-gaussian-degeneracy} on the chosen measurable covariate space. It gives, for every parameter record \(p\), writing \(n\) for the sample size of \(p\), and every \(m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\),
\[
\theta_0(m)=0,
\]
and it gives the two zero Gaussian minimax conclusions for every nonempty clipped-code intersection at that same sample size.
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.common_experiment_dichotomy

\item
Fix \(p_0\), the fixed primitive records, the sequence \((p_j)\), the supplied measurable deterministic codes \((\widehat g,\widehat q)\), and the displayed eventual non-Gaussian event. Write \(n_j\) for the sample size of \(p_j\), and put
\[
\mathfrak p_{\star,j}:=\text{the transport of }\mathfrak p_{\star}\text{ from }p_0\text{ to }p_j,
\qquad
\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,j}:=\text{the transport of }\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star}\text{ from }p_0\text{ to }p_j.
\]
For every sufficiently large \(j\), the displayed event in the theorem supplies a base model
\(m_j^{\mathrm{anc}}\) such that
\[
m_j^{\mathrm{anc}}\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n_j}(p_j),
\qquad
\bar g_{m_j^{\mathrm{anc}},n_j}=\bar g_{n_j},
\qquad
\bar q_{m_j^{\mathrm{anc}},n_j}=\bar q_{n_j},
\]
together with
\[
n_j\ge2,
\qquad
\varepsilon_{1,n_j}\le
\Bigl(4R_1(p_j)\exp\{2C_gR_1(p_j)\}\Bigr)^{-1}.
\]
Let \(g_j^{\mathrm{anc}}\) and \(q_j^{\mathrm{anc}}\) be the supplied code sequences carried by
\(m_j^{\mathrm{anc}}\). The two clipped-code equalities above imply equality of the two fixed-code law sets,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\{m:\ m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n_j}(p_j),\ \bar g_{m,n_j}=\bar g_{n_j},\ \bar q_{m,n_j}=\bar q_{n_j}\}  \\
&\qquad =
\{m:\ m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n_j}(p_j),\ \bar g_{m,n_j}=\bar g_{m_j^{\mathrm{anc}},n_j},\ \bar q_{m,n_j}=\bar q_{m_j^{\mathrm{anc}},n_j}\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The statistic also agrees pointwise when the supplied treatment code is replaced by
\(g_j^{\mathrm{anc}}\):
\[
\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n_j}
(p_j,\mathfrak p_{\star,j},\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,j};\widehat g)
=
\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n_j}
(p_j,\mathfrak p_{\star,j},\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,j};g_j^{\mathrm{anc}}),
\]
because \cref{obj:def:adaptive-contour-estimator} forms the represented observation record with the current clipped values \(\bar g_{n_j}(X_i)\). Applying \cref{obj:thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax} to \(p_j\) with base model
\(m_j^{\mathrm{anc}}\), and using the i.i.d. product sampling law of that base model, yields the minimax bounds
\[
\frac{c}{n_j}
\le
\mathfrak R_{n_j}^{\mathrm{NG}}(p_j;\widehat g,\widehat q)
\le
\frac{C}{n_j},
\]
and, after the preceding law-set and statistic equalities, the estimator upper bound
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n_j}(p_j;\bar g_{n_j},\bar q_{n_j})}
\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\bigl(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n_j}-\theta_0(P)\bigr)^2
\right]
\le
\frac{C}{n_j}.
\]
The measurability of
\(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n_j}\) follows from the same pointwise equality and the measurability conclusion of
\cref{obj:thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}. The transported primitive bank record reuses the same certified names for
\(k,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0\), and \(R_1\). By \cref{obj:def:contour-bank-handle}, the translated-dyadic contour-bank data are a deterministic function of those names and their error-one refinements. Thus, writing
\(B\) for the bank generated from \(p_0\) and \(\mathfrak p_{\star}\), the bank generated from
\(p_j\) and \(\mathfrak p_{\star,j}\) is also \(B\).

For the compiled statement, fix a compiled bounded spectral adapter satisfying the full canonical build-and-compilation specification for
\((p_j,\mathfrak p_{\star,j},\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,j},\widehat g)\). The represented-data soundness implication for compiled bounded spectral adapters states that this full canonical build-and-compilation specification entails the represented-execution contract for the same parameter record, primitive bank record, range record, and treatment-code sequence. Its proof rewrites the pilot-boundary, winding-quadrature, and evaluation-quadrature entry points to the reference finite programs, identifies the compiled represented program with the ordinary finite-rational program on canonical represented inputs, and then applies the clipping certificate for \(\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,j}\). Therefore the compiled represented-data execution realizes
\[
(p_j,\mathfrak p_{\star,j},\mathfrak c_{\theta,\star,j},\widehat g)
\]
with the same ordinary finite-rational result, trace, and certified clipped output required by the represented-execution clause of the theorem.
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.common_experiment_dichotomy

\item
For the Gaussian part, fix a parameter record \(p\), write \(n\) for its sample size, and fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\). The first conclusion of \cref{obj:prop:bounded-outcome-gaussian-degeneracy} gives
\[
\theta_0(P)=0.
\]
Now fix measurable deterministic codes \((\widehat g,\widehat q)\) whose clipped-code Gaussian intersection at this sample size is nonempty. The second conclusion of \cref{obj:prop:bounded-outcome-gaussian-degeneracy}, applied to this same code pair, gives
\[
\mathfrak R_{n}^{\mathrm G}(p;\widehat g,\widehat q)=0,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}(p;\widehat g,\widehat q)=0.
\]
These are precisely the two Gaussian assertions.
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SaPlmCumulantConverse.common_experiment_dichotomy
\end{enumerate}
\end{proof}
