\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:population-numerator-envelope}]
Put \(R=R_1\), \(Z_n=T-\bar g_n(X)\), and
\[
A=
64\{C_q^4+4C_\theta^4\psi_\eta^4+4\psi_\xi^4\}
+2\exp\{16RC_g+16R^2\psi_\eta^2\}.
\]
The radius is nonnegative, since \(R=R_0+1\) and \(R_0\ge0\) by its displayed construction in \cref{obj:def:contour-bank-handle}. % lean: outcomeResidualTransform_norm_le_populationNumeratorEnvelope

First,
\[
E_P|Y|^4
\le
64\{C_q^4+4C_\theta^4\psi_\eta^4+4\psi_\xi^4\}.
\]
Indeed, \(Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi\); the range bounds give
\(|q_0(X)|\le C_q\) and \(|\theta_0|\le C_\theta\), while the Luxemburg bounds give
\(E_P|\eta|^4\le4\psi_\eta^4\) and, after integrating the conditional bound,
\(E_P|\xi|^4\le4\psi_\xi^4\). Applying the elementary fourth-power bound to the
three summands gives the display. % lean: outcome_fourth_moment_le

Next,
\[
E_P\exp\{8R|Z_n|\}
\le
2\exp\{16RC_g+16R^2\psi_\eta^2\}.
\]
This is the residual exponential envelope applied with radius \(2R\). In detail,
\(Z_n=\eta+D_n\), where \(D_n=g_0(X)-\bar g_n(X)\) satisfies
\(|D_n|\le2C_g\) almost surely. Hence
\(8R|Z_n|\le 8R|\eta|+16RC_g\), and Young's inequality at this radius gives
\[
8R|\eta|\le \eta^2/\psi_\eta^2+16R^2\psi_\eta^2.
\]
The Luxemburg bound \(E_P\exp(\eta^2/\psi_\eta^2)\le2\) then yields the displayed
exponential bound. % lean: learnedResidual_exp_abs_sq_lintegral_le

Therefore
\[
E_P\left[\{|Y|\exp(2R|Z_n|)\}^2\right]
\le
A.
\]
The pointwise inequality used here is
\[
\{|Y|\exp(2R|Z_n|)\}^2
\le
|Y|^4+\exp(8R|Z_n|),
\]
and the preceding two displays bound the two expectations. % lean: outcome_exp_abs_sq_lintegral_le

Let
\[
E(o)=|Y(o)|\exp\{2R|Z_n(o)|\}.
\]
Then \(E\in L^2(P)\) and \(\|E\|_2\le\sqrt A\). For \(|z|\le R\),
\[
\left|Y\exp(zZ_n)\right|
=
|Y|\exp\{\operatorname{Re}(zZ_n)\}
\le
|Y|\exp\{2R|Z_n|\}
=E,
\]
because \(\operatorname{Re}(zZ_n)\le |z|\,|Z_n|\le2R|Z_n|\). Hence
\[
|G_n(z)|
=
\left|E_P\{Y\exp(zZ_n)\}\right|
\le
E_P E
\le
\|E\|_2
\le
\sqrt A.
\]
Since \(\sqrt A=C_G(p)\), the claimed bound follows. % lean: outcomeResidualTransform_norm_le_populationNumeratorEnvelope
\end{proof}
