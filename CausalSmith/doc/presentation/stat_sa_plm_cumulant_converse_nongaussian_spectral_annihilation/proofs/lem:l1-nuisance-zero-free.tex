\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}]
Let
\[
D_n(X)=g_0(X)-\bar g_n(X),
\qquad
H_n(z)=E_P[e^{zD_n(X)}].
\]
Since \(|g_0(X)|\le C_g\) almost surely and \(|\bar g_n(X)|\le C_g\) by
clipping,
\[
|D_n(X)|\le |g_0(X)|+|\bar g_n(X)|\le2C_g
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
% lean: l1_nuisance_zero_free

The treatment-code condition in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) gives
\[
E_P|D_n(X)|
=
\int |g_0(x)-\bar g_n(x)|\,dP_X(x)
\le
\varepsilon_{1,n}.
\]
% lean: l1_nuisance_zero_free

For \(|z|\le R_1\), the exponential remainder bound gives, with
\(w=zD_n(X)\),
\[
|e^{zD_n(X)}-1|
\le
|z|\,|D_n(X)|\,e^{|z|\,|D_n(X)|}.
\]
Together with \(|D_n(X)|\le2C_g\), this yields
\[
|H_n(z)-1|
\le
E_P|e^{zD_n(X)}-1|
\le
R_1 e^{2C_gR_1}E_P|D_n(X)|
\le
R_1 e^{2C_gR_1}\varepsilon_{1,n}.
\]
Taking the supremum over \(|z|\le R_1\) gives the displayed uniform bound. % lean: l1_nuisance_zero_free

Assume
\[
\varepsilon_{1,n}\le (4R_1e^{2C_gR_1})^{-1}.
\]
Then the preceding display gives \(|H_n(z)-1|\le1/4\) throughout the disk, and
therefore
\[
|H_n(z)|\ge 1-|H_n(z)-1|\ge3/4
\qquad(|z|\le R_1).
\]
% lean: l1_nuisance_zero_free

It remains to record why the multiplicity comparison is legitimate at each
point of the disk.  The almost-sure bound \(|D_n(X)|\le2C_g\) implies that
\(E_P\exp\{tD_n(X)\}<\infty\) for every real \(t\), so the complex MGF
\(H_n\) is analytic in a neighborhood of every \(|z|\le R_1\).  The treatment-tail
condition in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), under the Luxemburg convention of
\cref{obj:def:model-parameters}, gives the same local exponential-moment
condition for \(\eta\): for every real \(t\), completing the square bounds
\(\exp\{t\eta\}\) by a constant multiple of
\(\exp\{\eta^2/\psi_\eta^2\}\), which is integrable.  Hence the treatment-noise
MGF \(M(z)=E_P[e^{z\eta}]\) is analytic in a neighborhood of every point of the
disk as well. % lean: l1_nuisance_zero_free

By \cref{obj:lem:observable-factorization},
\[
F_n(z)=M(z)H_n(z).
\]
Fix \(|z|\le R_1\).  The preceding lower bound gives \(H_n(z)\ne0\), so the
analytic order of \(H_n\) at \(z\) is zero.  Applying additivity of analytic
order to the displayed factorization gives
\[
\operatorname{ord}_z(F_n)
=
\operatorname{ord}_z(M)+\operatorname{ord}_z(H_n)
=
\operatorname{ord}_z(M).
\]
Thus \(F_n\) and \(M\) have exactly the same zeros in \(\{|z|\le R_1\}\), counted
with multiplicity. % lean: residualMGF_eq_treatmentMGF_mul_nuisanceMGF analyticOrderAt_mul l1_nuisance_zero_free
\end{proof}
