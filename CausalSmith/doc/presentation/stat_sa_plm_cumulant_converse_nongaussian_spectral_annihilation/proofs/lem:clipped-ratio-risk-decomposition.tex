\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:clipped-ratio-risk-decomposition}]
Write
\[
\bar W=\mathbb P_n W,\qquad
\bar R=\mathbb P_n R,\qquad
\Delta_W=\mathbb P_n(W-\mu_W).
\]
Since \(n\ge1\),
\[
\Delta_W=\bar W-\mu_W.
\]
% lean: clippedRatioFromScores_sq_le

Consider first the branch \(\bar W\ge A/4\). Then \(\bar W>0\), and the
unclipped ratio obeys
\[
\frac{\theta\bar W+\bar R}{\bar W}-\theta=\frac{\bar R}{\bar W}.
\]
The projection onto \([-C_\theta,C_\theta]\) fixes \(\theta\), because
\(|\theta|\le C_\theta\), and is \(1\)-Lipschitz. Hence
\[
|\widehat\theta-\theta|
\le
\left|\frac{\bar R}{\bar W}\right|
\le
\frac{4|\bar R|}{A}.
\]
Squaring and adding the nonnegative term involving \(\Delta_W\) gives
\[
(\widehat\theta-\theta)^2
\le
\frac{16}{A^2}\bar R^2
\le
\frac{16}{A^2}
\left\{\bar R^2+C_\theta^2\Delta_W^2\right\}.
\]
% lean: clippedRatioFromScores_sq_le

It remains to handle the branch \(\bar W<A/4\), where \(\widehat\theta=0\).
Since \(\mu_W\ge A/2\),
\[
\mu_W-\bar W\ge A/4,
\qquad\text{so}\qquad
A^2\le16(\bar W-\mu_W)^2=16\Delta_W^2.
\]
Also \(|\theta|\le C_\theta\), hence \(\theta^2\le C_\theta^2\). Therefore
\[
(\widehat\theta-\theta)^2
=
\theta^2
\le
C_\theta^2
\le
\frac{16}{A^2}C_\theta^2\Delta_W^2
\le
\frac{16}{A^2}\left\{\bar R^2+C_\theta^2\Delta_W^2\right\}.
\]
The two branches give the claimed pathwise bound. % lean: clippedRatioFromScores_sq_le
\end{proof}
