\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:finite-contour-bank}]
Let \(B\) be the bank produced from \((p,\mathfrak p_\star)\).  The construction in \cref{obj:def:contour-bank-handle} gives
\[
N_{\mathrm{cert}}=4U_\psi^2(U_R+2)^3,\qquad
m_\star=N_{\mathrm{cert}}+3,\qquad
J=2^{m_\star-1}+1,
\]
\[
d_\star=2^{-m_\star},\qquad h_\star=d_\star/3,
\]
\[
e_\star=8U_RU_\psi^2(U_R+1)^2,\qquad
d_\star^{\mathrm{den}}=3(2U_R+1),
\]
and
\[
u_\star=2e_\star+N_{\mathrm{cert}}(m_\star+d_\star^{\mathrm{den}}),
\qquad
a_\star=2^{-u_\star}.
\]
Thus \(a_\star>0\) and the displayed bookkeeping identities hold. % lean: finite_contour_bank

The radii have the explicit form
\[
\rho_j=R_0+\frac14+j\,2^{-m_\star},
\qquad 0\le j\le 2^{m_\star-1}.
\]
Hence \(j\mapsto\rho_j\) is strictly increasing.  Since
\[
\frac14+j2^{-m_\star}\le \frac14+2^{m_\star-1}2^{-m_\star}=\frac34<1,
\]
and \(R_1=R_0+1\), each bank radius satisfies
\[
R_0<\rho_j<R_1.
\]
% lean: finite_contour_bank

It remains to prove the treatment-noise certificate.  The error-one certified refinements and the ceiling construction give
\[
\psi_\eta\le U_\psi,\qquad R_1\le U_R.
\]
Let
\[
M(z)=E_Pe^{z\eta},\qquad R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1,
\qquad R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_1+2.
\]
The conditional-mean identity for \(g_0\) centers \(\eta\).  The treatment-tail condition in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), together with \cref{obj:lem:luxemburg-mgf-envelope}, gives for every \(t\in\mathbb R\)
\[
E_P e^{t\eta}\le \exp(4\psi_\eta^2t^2).
\]
The same real exponential integrability gives the entire complex moment-generating function, normalization at the origin follows from the probability law, and the complex modulus estimate yields
\[
M(0)=1,\qquad M\text{ is entire},\qquad
|M(z)|\le \exp(4\psi_\eta^2|z|^2)
\quad (z\in\mathbb C).
\]
% lean: treatmentMGF_entire_normalized_bound

Let
\[
N_0=N_{\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}}(M)
\]
be the number of zeros of \(M\) in the open disk of radius \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), counted with multiplicity.  Jensen's zero-counting bound applied on \(R_{\mathrm{out}}\) gives
\[
N_0\log\!\left(\frac{R_{\mathrm{out}}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}}\right)
\le 4\psi_\eta^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2.
\]
% lean: jensen_zeroMultiplicityCount_bound
Since \(R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_{\mathrm{fac}}+1\),
\[
\frac1{R_{\mathrm{out}}}
\le
\log\!\left(\frac{R_{\mathrm{out}}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}}\right).
\]
Therefore
\[
N_0
\le 4\psi_\eta^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^3
\le 4U_\psi^2(U_R+2)^3
=N_{\mathrm{cert}}.
\]
% lean: finite_contour_bank

List the \(N_0\) zeros in \(\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\), with multiplicity, as
\[
a_1,\ldots,a_{N_0}.
\]
The finite Blaschke factorization on the disk of radius \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) gives a holomorphic function \(g\), zero-free in \(\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\), such that
\[
M(z)=Q(z)g(z),\qquad |z|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}},
\]
where
\[
Q(z)=
\prod_{\nu=1}^{N_0}
\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
     {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}.
\]
% lean: exists_complete_pointwise_blaschke_factorization

The grid spacing is \(d_\star\), and the mesh identities give \(d_\star>0\), \(h_\star=d_\star/3\), and \(2h_\star<d_\star\).  Thus one listed zero modulus \(|a_\nu|\) can be within distance \(<h_\star\) of at most one grid radius: two distinct grid radii are separated by at least \(d_\star\), while two distances \(<h_\star\) would put them less than \(2h_\star\) apart.  Also
\[
N_0\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}<2^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}+2}+1
=2^{m_\star-1}+1=J.
\]
The pigeonhole argument for this separated dyadic grid gives an index \(j\) such that
\[
h_\star\le \bigl||a_\nu|-\rho_j\bigr|
\qquad (1\le \nu\le N_0).
\]
% lean: exists_translated_grid_radius_separated

By \cref{obj:lem:zero-localization}, \(M\) has a zero \(z_0\) with \(|z_0|\le R_0\).  Since \(R_0<\rho_j\), this zero lies strictly inside \(C_j=\{|z|=\rho_j\}\).  If \(M\) had a zero \(z\) on \(C_j\), then \(z\in\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\), and the factorization \(M=Qg\) together with the zero-freeness of \(g\) would force \(z=a_\nu\) for some \(\nu\).  This would give
\[
\bigl||a_\nu|-\rho_j\bigr|=0<h_\star,
\]
contradicting the separation of the chosen grid point.  Hence the contour has positive zero count:
\[
N_{C_j}(M)\ge1.
\]
% lean: zeroMultiplicityCount_pos_of_exists_zero

It remains to lower-bound \(|M|\) on this circle.  Put
\[
A=4\psi_\eta^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2.
\]
On \(|w|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), the Blaschke product has \(|Q(w)|=1\), so the boundary envelope for \(M\) gives \(|g(w)|\le e^A\).  Also \(M(0)=1\) and \(|Q(0)|\le1\), hence \(|g(0)|\ge1\).  Applying Harnack's inequality to the positive harmonic function \(A-\log|g|\) gives, for every \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
\exp\!\left\{-\left(\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}-R_1}-1\right)A\right\}|Q(z)|
\le |M(z)|.
\]
Since \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1\),
\[
\left(\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}-R_1}-1\right)A
=2R_1\cdot 4\psi_\eta^2(R_1+1)^2
=8\psi_\eta^2R_1(R_1+1)^2.
\]
Thus, for every \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
\exp\{-8\psi_\eta^2R_1(R_1+1)^2\}\,|Q(z)|
\le |M(z)|.
\]
% lean: norm_lower_of_blaschke_factorization
Since
\[
8\psi_\eta^2R_1(R_1+1)^2\le
8U_RU_\psi^2(U_R+1)^2=e_\star,
\]
the elementary dyadic-exponential comparison \(2^{-2E}\le e^{-E}\) for natural \(E\), applied to \(E=e_\star\), gives
\[
2^{-2e_\star}
\le \exp\{-8\psi_\eta^2R_1(R_1+1)^2\}.
\]
% lean: dyadic_two_mul_le_exp_neg

Now take \(z\in C_j\).  For each zero \(a_\nu\),
\[
|z-a_\nu|
\ge \bigl||z|-|a_\nu|\bigr|
=\bigl|\rho_j-|a_\nu|\bigr|
\ge h_\star.
\]
Also \(|z|\le R_1\), and therefore
\[
R_{\mathrm{fac}}+|z|\le 2R_1+1\le 2U_R+1.
\]
For each Blaschke factor,
\[
\left|
\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
     {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}
\right|
\ge
\frac{|z-a_\nu|}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+|z|}
\ge
\frac{h_\star}{2U_R+1}
=
\frac{d_\star}{d_\star^{\mathrm{den}}}.
\]
% lean: norm_blaschkeFactor_ge
Multiplying over the at most \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) factors and using
\((d_\star^{\mathrm{den}})^{-1}\ge2^{-d_\star^{\mathrm{den}}}\) gives
\[
|Q(z)|
\ge
2^{-N_{\mathrm{cert}}(m_\star+d_\star^{\mathrm{den}})}.
\]
% lean: dyadic_product_certificate

Combining the last two lower bounds,
\[
|M(z)|
\ge
2^{-2e_\star}\,
2^{-N_{\mathrm{cert}}(m_\star+d_\star^{\mathrm{den}})}
=
2^{-u_\star}
=a_\star.
\]
Thus for this bank index \(j\),
\[
a_\star\le |M(z)|\qquad (z\in C_j),
\]
and the asserted uniform treatment-noise certificate follows. % lean: finite_contour_bank
\end{proof}
