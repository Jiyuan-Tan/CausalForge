\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:selected-contour-perturbation-bound}]
On the selector event, choose the selected bank index \(j\) and decoded integer
\(N\ge1\). Write \(C_j\) for the selected circle and let
\[
\widehat T_j
=
\Re\left[
\{N\,2\pi i\}^{-1}
\oint_{C_j}
\frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}\,dz
\right],
\qquad
T_j
=
\Re\left[
\{N\,2\pi i\}^{-1}
\oint_{C_j}
\frac{G(z)}{F(z)}\,dz
\right].
\]
The selected-contour identification hypothesis gives \(T_j=\vartheta\). % lean: hidentify

For each \(z\in C_j\), the selector assumptions give
\[
\mu\le |F(z)|,\qquad
|G(z)|\le C_G,\qquad
|\widehat F_{1,n}(z)-F(z)|\le d,\qquad
|\widehat G_{1,n}(z)-G(z)|\le e .
\]
Since \(d\le\mu-\nu\), the reverse triangle inequality gives
\[
|\widehat F_{1,n}(z)|
\ge
|F(z)|-|\widehat F_{1,n}(z)-F(z)|
\ge
\mu-d
\ge
\nu .
\]
% lean: empirical_denominator_margin_of_population_margin

Thus the pointwise ratio perturbation bound is
\[
\left|
\frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}
-
\frac{G(z)}{F(z)}
\right|
\le
\frac{e}{\nu}+\frac{C_Gd}{\mu\nu}.
\]
Indeed,
\[
\frac{\widehat G_{1,n}}{\widehat F_{1,n}}-\frac{G}{F}
=
\frac{\widehat G_{1,n}-G}{\widehat F_{1,n}}
+
\frac{G(F-\widehat F_{1,n})}{\widehat F_{1,n}F},
\]
and the two terms are bounded by \(e/\nu\) and \(C_Gd/(\mu\nu)\), respectively. % lean: norm_div_sub_div_le

The two selected-circle integrability assumptions allow integration of this
pointwise bound around \(C_j\), so
\[
\left|
\oint_{C_j}
\frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}\,dz
-
\oint_{C_j}
\frac{G(z)}{F(z)}\,dz
\right|
\le
2\pi\rho_j
\left(\frac{e}{\nu}+\frac{C_Gd}{\mu\nu}\right).
\]
After multiplication by \(\{N2\pi i\}^{-1}\) and taking real parts,
\[
|\widehat T_j-\vartheta|
\le
\frac{\rho_j}{N}
\left(\frac{e}{\nu}+\frac{C_Gd}{\mu\nu}\right).
\]
% lean: circleIntegral_ratio_difference_bound

The certified evaluation rectangle used by the statistic contains
\(\widehat T_j\), and its real-coordinate width is at most
\(2/\max\{n,1\}\). If \(y\) is the rational midpoint used before clipping, then
\[
|y-\widehat T_j|\le \frac{1}{\max\{n,1\}}.
\]
% lean: representedNodeSpecifications midpoint_error_le_half_width

Because \(|\vartheta|\le C_\theta\), clipping to \([-C_\theta,C_\theta]\) is
contractive relative to \(\vartheta\). Hence
\[
|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\vartheta|
\le
|y-\vartheta|
\le
|y-\widehat T_j|+|\widehat T_j-\vartheta|.
\]
% lean: thetaHatSpec_eq_clip_finite_rational

Finally, \(\rho_j<R_1\) by the bank geometry in \cref{obj:def:contour-bank-handle}
and \(N\ge1\). Since \(d,e,C_G,\mu,\nu\) have the stated signs,
\[
\frac{\rho_j}{N}
\left(\frac{e}{\nu}+\frac{C_Gd}{\mu\nu}\right)
\le
R_1\max\{\nu^{-1},\,C_G(\mu\nu)^{-1}\}(d+e).
\]
Combining the last three displays proves the asserted inequality. % lean: thetaHatSpec_good_event_exact_contour_perturbation
\end{proof}
