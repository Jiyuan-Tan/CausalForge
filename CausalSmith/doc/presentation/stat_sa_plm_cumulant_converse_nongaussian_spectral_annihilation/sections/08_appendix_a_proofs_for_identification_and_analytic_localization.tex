\section{Appendix A: proofs for identification and analytic localization}

This appendix collects the analytic statements that support the identification argument in \cref{obj:thm:known-zero-instrument,obj:lem:observable-factorization,obj:thm:exact-contour-identification} and the fixed-bank localization used in \cref{obj:thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}. The common theme is that zeros of moment-generating functions can be controlled by classical complex-analytic arguments and then transferred to residualized-treatment transforms. The zero and completeness perspective is standard in identification arguments based on transforms \citep{KaganLinnikRao1973,Mattner1992}, and the finite-bank construction uses the same analytic geometry that underlies modern zero-localization bounds for moment-generating functions \citep{MichelenSahasrabudhe2019,EremenkoFryntov2021,DinhGhoshTranTran2021}.

For \cref{obj:thm:known-zero-instrument}, the analytic order of a zero of \(M\) determines which polynomial-exponential weight annihilates shifted treatment innovations. Independence of the treatment innovation from \(X\), as imposed in \cref{obj:ass:independent-treatment-noise}, then turns the shifted identity into a conditional moment for the code residual \(Z_n\). The denominator calculation is the corresponding differentiated transform identity, and the partially linear conditional-mean restriction in \cref{obj:ass:outcome-mean-independence} supplies the numerator relation.

The factorization in \cref{obj:lem:observable-factorization} isolates the effect of treatment-code contamination. Since \(Z_n=\eta+D_n(X)\), independence separates the residual transform into the treatment-innovation transform and the nuisance transform. The outcome-weighted transform has the derivative term carrying \(\theta_0\) plus the covariate-only contamination transform from \cref{obj:def:outcome-contamination}. This decomposition is the algebraic input for \cref{obj:thm:exact-contour-identification}: on a contour where the residual transform is separated from zero and the nuisance transform has no zero on the closed disk, the logarithmic-derivative contribution counts the enclosed zeros and the analytic contamination contribution integrates to zero.

The remaining analytic step is uniform localization. \Cref{obj:lem:zero-localization} gives a disk, determined by the fixed cumulant order, sub-Gaussian scale, and separation constant, that contains at least one zero of the treatment-innovation transform. The finite-bank statement below turns that disk-level information into a deterministic list of circles, with explicit dyadic bookkeeping and a positive boundary-modulus certificate. Its role is to provide the law-blind contour geometry used later by the empirical statistic.

\begin{lemmav}{lem:finite-contour-bank}[Finite contour bank]
Let \(p\) be a primitive parameter record with \(k=r+1\), \(r\ge2\), \(\gamma\in(1/2,1)\), \(s\ge r\), positive constants \(C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi},\delta,\sigma\), and nonnegative nonincreasing code-radius sequences \(\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2\). Let \(\mathfrak p_{\star}\) be a certified-bank input record for \(p\) in the sense of \cref{obj:def:contour-bank-handle}, so that
\[
R_0=A_k\left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta}\right)^{(k-2)^{-1}},
\qquad
R_1=R_0+1 .
\]
For any sample size \(n\ge1\), any model \(m\) with observed-data law \(P\), and any membership certificate \(m\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) as defined in \cref{obj:def:non-gaussian-class}, form the bank data \(B\) of \cref{obj:def:contour-bank-handle} for \(p\) and \(\mathfrak p_{\star}\). Then \(a_{\star}>0\), and the components of \(B\) satisfy
\[
N_{\mathrm{cert}}=4U_{\psi}^{2}(U_R+2)^3,\qquad
m_{\star}=N_{\mathrm{cert}}+3,\qquad
J=2^{m_{\star}-1}+1,
\]
\[
d_{\star}=\left(\frac12\right)^{m_{\star}},\qquad
h_{\star}=\frac{d_{\star}}{3},\qquad
e_{\star}=8U_RU_{\psi}^{2}(U_R+1)^2,
\]
\[
d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}=3(2U_R+1),\qquad
u_{\star}=2e_{\star}+N_{\mathrm{cert}}\bigl(m_{\star}+d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}\bigr),\qquad
a_{\star,\mathbb Q}=\left(\frac12\right)^{u_{\star}}.
\]
The bank radii are strictly increasing and lie in the search annulus:
\[
R_0<\rho_j<R_1
\quad\text{for every }j\in\{1,\ldots,J\}.
\]
Writing \(C_j=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=\rho_j\}\) and \(M(z)=E_P[e^{z\eta}]\) for the treatment-innovation moment-generating function, there exists an index \(j\in\{1,\ldots,J\}\) such that
\[
N_{C_j}(M)\ge1
\quad\text{and}\quad
a_{\star}\le |M(z)|\ \text{for every }z\in C_j .
\]
\end{lemmav}

\Cref{obj:lem:finite-contour-bank} is the population geometric certificate used by the empirical selector in \cref{obj:def:adaptive-contour-estimator}. The proof below records the dyadic bookkeeping and the zero-localization argument behind that certificate.
