\section{Appendix: Proofs, Auxiliary Arguments, and Verification Note}
\label{sec:deferred-proofs}

The appendix gives the mathematical arguments in dependency order.  It begins with the compact causal-range calculation, then proves the fixed-geometry frontier and its unit-weight specialization, then gives the oracle lower and upper bounds, and finally treats the regular and uniform finite-cell constructions.  Throughout, confidence sets take values in the compact parameter space \(\Theta\), and expected length is the Lebesgue length criterion from \cref{obj:def:frontier-risk}.

The fixed-geometry lower bounds use the centered law and distance notation in \cref{obj:synth_5,obj:synth_3,obj:synth_4}.  The following anchored statement records the separation calculation for the least-favorable laws generated by \cref{obj:def:least-favorable-witness}.

\begin{definitionv}{def:geometry-handle}[Geometry handle \(Q_{n,h}^{\mathfrak g}\)]
Fix an admissible deterministic array \(\mathfrak g=(P_S^X,w,e)_{n\ge1}\). For \(t_0>0\), set
\[
\mu_n=\left(\frac{t_0\kappa_n}{n}\right)^{1/2},
\qquad
p_n(x)=\frac{\mu_n w(x)}{\kappa_n}.
\]
For \(0<\alpha<1\), let \(\rho=(1-\alpha)/8\). As the complier outcome mean varies over \(h\in\mathbb R\) with
\[
|h|\le \min\{1/4,\rho t_0^{-1/2}\},
\]
using the chi-square divergence and total variation distance defined in \cref{obj:synth_3,obj:synth_4}, the corresponding least-favorable laws satisfy
\[
1+\chi^2(Q_{n,h}^{\mathfrak g}\Vert Q_{n,0}^{\mathfrak g})
\le \exp(8t_0h^2),
\qquad
\|Q_{n,h}^{\mathfrak g}-Q_{n,0}^{\mathfrak g}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\le 2\rho .
\]
\end{definitionv}

The proof of \cref{obj:prop:compact-causal-range} expands the inverse-propensity score in \cref{obj:synth_1} conditional on \(X\).  Conditional instrument randomization and the source observation law turn the source score moments into the conditional assignment contrasts \(\Delta_Y(x)\) and \(\Delta_D(x)\).  Exclusion and monotonicity express those contrasts through complier potential outcomes, while \cref{obj:ass:outcome-transport,obj:ass:receipt-transport,obj:ass:transport-domination} move the reduced form and first stage to the target covariate law.  The bounded outcome support in \cref{obj:ass:full-data-support} gives the compact range \([-1,1]\), so the transported Wald ratio is interpreted as the target complier-conditional contrast in \cref{obj:prop:compact-causal-range}.

The lower-bound arguments for \cref{obj:thm:oracle-converse,obj:thm:fixed-geometry-frontier} use the least-favorable family in \cref{obj:def:least-favorable-witness}.  That construction sets the complier probability proportional to the transport weight, which calibrates the transported first stage to the selected effective-strength threshold.  Varying the complier outcome tilt changes the target complier contrast by a controlled amount, while \cref{obj:def:geometry-handle} bounds the chi-square and total-variation separations between the tilted law and the centered law.  Le Cam's testing argument then converts closeness of the observed-data laws into a lower bound on the expected length of any honest confidence set.  The single-population specialization in \cref{obj:prop:no-shift-reduction} follows by setting \(w=1\), which gives \(\kappa_n=1\) and the effective-strength index \(n\mu_n^2\).

For the oracle score-inversion result, \cref{obj:def:inversion-handle} is an Anderson--Rubin style inversion of the transported residual moment, in the tradition of \citet{AndersonRubin1949} and \citet{Fieller1954}.  At the true value \(\theta_T\), the centered score has variance controlled by the oracle weight second moment and the overlap constant.  The radius \(L_\alpha\sqrt{\widehat\kappa_n/n}\) combines that variance control with a uniform probability bound, yielding the honesty assertion in \cref{obj:thm:oracle-score-inversion-attainment}.  The expected-length calculation uses the same score inequality together with the effective-strength lower restriction \(t_n\ge t_0\), producing the order \(\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}\).

The finite-cell arguments show that sample-learned weights preserve the oracle rate.  For \cref{obj:thm:finite-cell-unknown-weight-attainment}, the target empirical frequencies estimate target cell probabilities, the source cellwise averages estimate reduced-form and first-stage contrasts, and the U-statistic \(\widehat\kappa_{U,n}\) estimates the collision scale associated with target-to-source weight dispersion.  The sub-root cell-growth condition ensures that the additional sampling variation from cell learning remains on the same scale as the oracle score radius.  The regular-cell extension in \cref{obj:thm:regular-cell-unknown-weight-attainment} repeats this control with known source-cell masses \(q_{x,n}\) and known cell-varying propensity, using the regularity constants \(c_-\) and \(c_+\) to keep the cellwise variance terms uniformly bounded.
