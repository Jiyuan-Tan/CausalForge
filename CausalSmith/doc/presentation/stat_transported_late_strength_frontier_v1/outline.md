# Title
**Honest expected length for transported complier effects with weak first stages**

**Contribution statement.** The paper characterizes honest minimax expected length for bounded transported complier-ratio confidence sets under fixed instrument overlap, conditional outcome-contrast transport, target-average receipt-contrast transport, domination, and Kish weight dispersion, and constructs oracle and finite-cell score-inversion procedures attaining the rate \(\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}\).

# Sections
## section: Abstract
The abstract states the two-sample transported complier-ratio problem, introduces \(t_n\), the effective identification strength, in plain words at first use, and summarizes the matched honest minimax expected-length rate \(\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}\). It names the oracle source score inversion and the finite-cell target-weight-learning constructions, with the compact causal range supplied by bounded outcomes and monotone binary treatment receipt.

## section: Introduction
The introduction motivates transported complier effects in encouragement designs where the source sample contains outcomes, receipt, and encouragement while the target sample contributes covariates. It previews the identifying ratio, the compact parameter range, the honest expected-length criterion, and the effective-strength comparison under fixed overlap, transport domination, weight-envelope, second-moment, and sub-root cell-scale conditions; it closes with a single factual pointer to the appendix verification note.

## section: Oracle minimax rates
This section presents the oracle lower bound, the oracle source score-inversion rule, and the matched minimax expected-length rate in \(t_0\). It interprets \(t_n=n\mu_n^2/\kappa_n\) as the scalar combining transported first-stage strength with weight dispersion, gives the no-shift specialization \(w(X)=1\), defines the fixed-geometry value, and explains how the score radius \(L_\alpha\sqrt{\widehat\kappa_n/n}\) attains the same order over admissible geometries.
