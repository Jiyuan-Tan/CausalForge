# Title
**Transported Complier Effects with Weak First Stages**

**Contribution statement.** The paper characterizes minimax expected-length rates for honest confidence sets for a transported complier ratio under bounded outcomes, fixed instrument overlap, conditional outcome-contrast transport, target-average receipt-contrast transport, transport domination, and Kish weight dispersion, and constructs oracle and finite-cell score-inversion procedures attaining the same order.

## section: Abstract
The abstract states the transported complier-ratio problem, the effective-strength index \(t_n=n\mu_n^2/\kappa_n\), the matched minimax expected-length rate \(\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}\), and the oracle and finite-cell score-inversion constructions. It distinguishes conditional transport of the outcome contrast from target-average transport of the receipt contrast.

## section: Introduction
The introduction motivates transported complier effects in two-sample encouragement designs, connects weak first stages to ratio-inference expected length, and previews the main rate characterization and feasible finite-cell procedures. It states the fixed-overlap, domination, envelope, second-moment, and sub-root cell-scale gates for the effective-strength comparison and points readers to the appendix verification note for the checked theorem scope.

## section: Oracle Frontier and Score Inversion
This section presents the oracle lower bound, the oracle score-inversion rule, and the resulting matched rate in \(t_0\).
