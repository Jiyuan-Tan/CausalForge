\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:sharp-rho-star}]
Throughout \cref{obj:ass:two-block-homophily} gives \(m\ge2\), hence \(q=2(m-1)>0\), \(a+b>0\), and \(2b>0\); together with \(r\ge0\), this makes the reduced linear coefficients nonnegative,
\[
c_x=q\Bigl((a+b)+\frac{r}{a+b}\Bigr)\ge0,
\qquad
c_y=2b+\frac{r}{2b}\ge0,
\qquad
c_z=2m\ge0 .
\]
Write \(M=2m\) and record the change of variables used below:
\[
t=(qx,\,y,\,z_{\mathrm{sp}}),
\qquad
\alpha^\sharp=\Bigl(\frac{c_x}{q},c_y,c_z\Bigr),
\qquad
\beta^\sharp=\Bigl(\frac1q,1,1\Bigr).
\]
Since \(q>0\), this map carries \(T_m\) to the simplex \(\Delta_M\) and the inverse formula \(t\mapsto(t_x/q,t_y,t_z)\) carries \(\Delta_M\) back to \(T_m\).  The mass equation \(qx+y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}=2m\) becomes \(t_x+t_y+t_z=M\), the parity constraint becomes \(t_y+t_z\ge d_m\), and the objectives match:
\[
\sum_i\alpha^\sharp_i t_i=\frac{c_x}{q}\,qx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}
=c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}},
\qquad
\sum_i\beta^\sharp_i t_i^2=\frac{(qx)^2}{q}+y^2+z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2
=qx^2+y^2+z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2,
\]
so that
\[
\Phi_{\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa}(t)=\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}}).
\]
We also use \(0\le d_m\le M\), since \(d_m\in\{0,2/m\}\) and \(m\ge2\).
% lean: reducedTriangle_to_simplex % lean: simplex_to_reducedTriangle % lean: wsObj_eq_reducedObjective

\textbf{Equality and range.}
By definition, \(\rho_\star(m,a,b,r,\kappa)\) is the reduced implementable-minus-relaxed value,
\[
\rho_\star(m,a,b,r,\kappa)
=
\inf_{\substack{(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\\ y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}\ge d_m}}\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})
-
\inf_{(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m}\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}}).
\]
The reduced gap identity in \cref{obj:lem:rounding-gap-reduction} identifies \(\Delta_m^{\pm}(r,\kappa)\) with this same difference.  Hence
\[
\rho_\star(m,a,b,r,\kappa)=\Delta_m^{\pm}(r,\kappa).
\]
% lean: rounding_gap_reduction
For the range, every point of \(T_m\) has nonnegative coordinates, and \(\kappa\ge0\); with the coefficient inequalities above,
\[
\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})
=c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}+\kappa\sqrt{qx^2+y^2+z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2}\ge0 .
\]
The truncated feasible set is nonempty because \((1,1,1)\in T_m\) and \(1+1\ge d_m\).  Since the truncated feasible set is contained in \(T_m\), the constrained reduced infimum is at least the unconstrained one, and therefore
\[
\rho_\star(m,a,b,r,\kappa)\ge0 .
\]
Together with the equality just proved, this gives \(\Delta_m^{\pm}(r,\kappa)\ge0\). % lean: roundingLossCertificate_nonneg % lean: implementabilityGap_nonneg

\textbf{Zero loss.}
The reduced minima used in the zero-loss argument are attained by the simplex construction above.  For the relaxed problem, if \(\kappa>0\), the positive-robustness clause of \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-active-set} supplies an active-set minimizer of \(\Phi_{\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa}\) on \(\Delta_M\); if \(\kappa=0\), the zero-robustness clause identifies the exposed \(\alpha^\sharp\)-minimizing face, and placing the mass \(M\) on a coordinate where \(\alpha^\sharp_i\) is minimal gives a relaxed minimizer.  Transporting this point back gives \(\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}\in T_m\) such that
\[
\phi_{r,\kappa}(\xi^{\mathrm{rel}})
\le\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})
\quad\text{for all }(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m .
\]
For the truncated problem, apply the truncation dichotomy in \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-truncation} to the image of a relaxed minimizer.  If that image lies in \(K_{d_m}=\{t\in\Delta_M:t_y+t_z\ge d_m\}\), it remains minimizing on \(K_{d_m}\); otherwise the selector point on the segment \(t_y+t_z=d_m\) minimizes on \(K_{d_m}\).  Transporting the selected point back gives \(\xi^{\mathrm{impl}}\in T_m\) with \(d_m\le \xi^{\mathrm{impl}}_y+\xi^{\mathrm{impl}}_z\) and
\[
\phi_{r,\kappa}(\xi^{\mathrm{impl}})
\le\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})
\quad\text{whenever }(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\text{ and }d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}} .
\]
Thus the two reduced values are the objective values at \(\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}\) and \(\xi^{\mathrm{impl}}\), and
\[
\rho_\star
=\phi_{r,\kappa}(\xi^{\mathrm{impl}})-\phi_{r,\kappa}(\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}).
\]
% lean: exists_relaxed_reduced_minimizer % lean: exists_implementable_reduced_minimizer % lean: relaxedReducedValue_eq_of_min % lean: implementableReducedValue_eq_of_min
If \(\rho_\star=0\), the two displayed objective values are equal.  Since \(\xi^{\mathrm{impl}}\) is feasible for the relaxed problem and attains the relaxed value, it is a relaxed minimizer satisfying \(d_m\le \xi^{\mathrm{impl}}_y+\xi^{\mathrm{impl}}_z\).  Conversely, if some \((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\) minimizes \(\phi_{r,\kappa}\) over \(T_m\) and satisfies \(d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}\), then it is feasible for the truncated problem, so
\[
\phi_{r,\kappa}(\xi^{\mathrm{rel}})
\le\phi_{r,\kappa}(\xi^{\mathrm{impl}})
\le\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})
=\phi_{r,\kappa}(\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}),
\]
forcing equality throughout and \(\rho_\star=0\).  This proves
\[
\rho_\star=0
\iff
\text{some relaxed minimizer of }\phi_{r,\kappa}\text{ on }T_m\text{ satisfies }d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}} .
\]
% lean: sharp_roundingLoss_zero_iff_argmin_meets_slice % lean: zero_gap_iff_argmin_meets_slice

\textbf{Strict robustness.}
Assume \(\kappa>0\).  Under the same change of variables, the positive-robustness clause of \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-active-set} gives a strictly unique minimizer of \(\Phi_{\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa}\) on \(\Delta_M\).  Transporting it back gives exactly one minimizer of \(\phi_{r,\kappa}\) on \(T_m\). % lean: sharp_reduced_active_set_and_unique
With uniqueness in hand, the assertion that some relaxed minimizer satisfies \(d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}\) is equivalent to the assertion that every relaxed minimizer satisfies it.  The zero-loss criterion therefore becomes
\[
\rho_\star=0
\iff
\text{every minimizer }(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\text{ of }\phi_{r,\kappa}\text{ on }T_m\text{ has }d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}} .
\]
% lean: sharp_roundingLoss_zero_iff_unique_argmin_subset_slice

\textbf{Linear case.}
Assume \(\kappa=0\), and fix \((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\).  Passing to \(t=(qx,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\), the reduced objective is the linear weighted-simplex objective \(\sum_i\alpha^\sharp_i t_i\).  The zero-robustness clause of \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-active-set} says that \(t\) minimizes it over \(\Delta_M\) exactly when
\[
t_i\ne0\ \Longrightarrow\ \alpha^\sharp_i\le\alpha^\sharp_j\ \text{ for every }j .
\]
Since \(q>0\), \(t_x\ne0\) is equivalent to \(x\ne0\), while \(t_y=y\) and \(t_z=z_{\mathrm{sp}}\); and \(\alpha^\sharp=(c_x/q,c_y,c_z)\).  Reading the three coordinates in turn, this condition is exactly
\[
x\ne0\Rightarrow\frac{c_x}{q}\le c_y\ \text{and}\ \frac{c_x}{q}\le c_z,
\qquad
y\ne0\Rightarrow c_y\le\frac{c_x}{q}\ \text{and}\ c_y\le c_z,
\qquad
z_{\mathrm{sp}}\ne0\Rightarrow c_z\le\frac{c_x}{q}\ \text{and}\ c_z\le c_y,
\]
as asserted. % lean: sharp_kappa_zero_reduced_min_iff

\textbf{Active-set formula.}
Assume \(\kappa>0\).  The active-set construction from \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-active-set} supplies an admissible support \(S\subseteq\{x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}}\}\) and multiplier \(\lambda\) for \(\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa\).  Write the associated simplex point as \(t^{S,\lambda}\).  It is nonnegative and satisfies \(t^{S,\lambda}_x+t^{S,\lambda}_y+t^{S,\lambda}_z=M=2m\); therefore its preimage has nonnegative coordinates and satisfies
\[
q\Bigl(\frac{t_x^{S,\lambda}}{q}\Bigr)+t_y^{S,\lambda}+t_z^{S,\lambda}=2m,
\qquad
\Bigl(\frac{t_x^{S,\lambda}}{q},t_y^{S,\lambda},t_z^{S,\lambda}\Bigr)\in T_m .
\]
The weighted-simplex minimizer property transfers back through the objective identity, so this reduced point minimizes \(\phi_{r,\kappa}\) over \(T_m\).  The value formula in \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-active-set} gives \(\Phi_{\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa}(t^{S,\lambda})=M\lambda\), and hence the relaxed reduced value is
\[
\inf_{(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m}\phi_{r,\kappa}=2m\,\lambda .
\]
% lean: sharp_reduced_active_set_and_unique

\textbf{Truncation correction.}
Suppose no relaxed minimizer in \(T_m\) satisfies \(d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}\).  Let \(\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}\) be a relaxed minimizer supplied by the simplex construction, and let \(t^{\mathrm{rel}}=(q\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}_x,\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}_y,\xi^{\mathrm{rel}}_z)\).  Then \(t^{\mathrm{rel}}\in\Delta_M\), it minimizes \(\Phi_{\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa}\) over \(\Delta_M\), and the hypothesis gives \(t^{\mathrm{rel}}_y+t^{\mathrm{rel}}_z<d_m\).  In the case \(\kappa=0\), the tie convention in \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-truncation} is vacuous under this hypothesis, because the exposed relaxed face is disjoint from \(K_{d_m}\).  Since \(0\le d_m\le M\), \cref{obj:lem:weighted-simplex-truncation} puts a minimizer of \(\Phi_{\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa}\) over
\[
K_{d_m}=\{t\in\Delta_M:t_y+t_z\ge d_m\}
\]
on the boundary segment
\[
H_{d_m}=\{(2m-d_m,s,d_m-s):0\le s\le d_m\}
\]
at
\[
s_\star=\operatorname{truncSelector}(2m,d_m,\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa).
\]
Define the reduced implementable value by
\[
\operatorname{val}_{\mathrm{impl}}(m,a,b,r,\kappa)
:=
\inf_{\substack{(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\\ d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}}}
\phi_{r,\kappa}(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}}).
\]
The change of variables carries \(K_{d_m}\) onto the parity-truncated slice of \(T_m\) and preserves objective values.  Therefore
\[
\operatorname{val}_{\mathrm{impl}}(m,a,b,r,\kappa)
=
\Phi_{\alpha^\sharp,\beta^\sharp,\kappa}
(2m-d_m,s_\star,d_m-s_\star).
\]
% lean: sharp_truncation_value_of_no_reduced_argmin_in_slice

\textbf{Even block size.}
If \(m\) is even, then \(d_m=0\).  A relaxed minimizer exists by the simplex construction, and every point of \(T_m\) has \(y,z_{\mathrm{sp}}\ge0\), hence \(0=d_m\le y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}\).  The zero-loss criterion gives
\[
\rho_\star(m,a,b,r,\kappa)=0 .
\]
% lean: sharp_roundingLoss_zero_of_even
\end{proof}
