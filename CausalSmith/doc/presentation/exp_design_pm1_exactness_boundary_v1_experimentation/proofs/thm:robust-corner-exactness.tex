\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:robust-corner-exactness}]
\begin{enumerate}
\item We first prove the finite-weight equivalence.  Write
\[
q=2(m-1),\qquad
c_x=q\left((a+b)+\frac r{a+b}\right),\qquad
c_y=2b+\frac r{2b},\qquad
c_z=2m .
\]
Suppose that some \(\kappa\ge 0\) makes \(I_n\) a relaxed minimizer over
\(\mathcal E_m^{\mathrm{blk}}\).  For an arbitrary
\((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\), set
\[
u=1-x,\qquad v=\frac{z_{\mathrm{sp}}-y}{2m}.
\]
The simplex equation \(qx+y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}=2m\) gives \(1+(m-1)u=\tfrac12(y+z_{\mathrm{sp}})\), so these formulas recover
\[
1-u=x,\qquad
1+(m-1)u-mv=y,\qquad
1+(m-1)u+mv=z_{\mathrm{sp}} .
\]
% lean: reduced_coord_inverse
Using \cref{obj:lem:block-spectral-coordinates}, this inverse change of variables sends
\((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\) to a feasible block matrix \(X(u,v)\), and the matrix
objective equals the reduced objective at \((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\).  Since
\(I_n=X(0,0)\) corresponds to \((1,1,1)\), so that
\[
F_{r,\kappa}(I_n)=\phi_{r,\kappa}(1,1,1),
\]
% lean: identity_objective_eq_reduced_center
the assumed matrix minimality says that
\((1,1,1)\) minimizes
\[
c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}
+\kappa\sqrt{q x^2+y^2+z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2}
\]
on \(T_m\).  The certificate-necessity part of
\cref{obj:lem:frobenius-center-certificate} therefore gives
\[
\frac{c_x}{q}=c_y,\qquad c_y=c_z .
\]
% lean: center_coeffs_of_identity_relaxed_min
The first equality is
\[
a+b+\frac r{a+b}=2b+\frac r{2b},
\]
and by the identity
\[
\Bigl(a+b+\frac r{a+b}\Bigr)-\Bigl(2b+\frac r{2b}\Bigr)
=(a-b)\left(1-\frac{r}{2b(a+b)}\right)
\]
this is equivalent to
\[
(a-b)\left(1-\frac{r}{2b(a+b)}\right)=0.
\]
By two-block homophily, \(a>b>0\), so \(a-b\ne0\) and the second factor vanishes:
\[
r=2b(a+b).
\]
% lean: robust_coeff_r_eq_of_center
Substituting this into \(c_y=c_z\) gives
\[
2b+\frac{2b(a+b)}{2b}=2m,
\]
that is \(a+3b=2m\).  Thus the affine-balanced locus holds. % lean: robust_locus_of_center_coeffs
Conversely, if
\[
a+3b=2m,\qquad r=2b(a+b),
\]
then substituting \(r=2b(a+b)\) into the three coefficients gives
\[
\frac{c_x}{q}=(a+b)+\frac{2b(a+b)}{a+b}=a+3b,
\qquad
c_y=2b+\frac{2b(a+b)}{2b}=a+3b,
\qquad
c_z=2m=a+3b,
\]
so \(c_x/q=c_y=c_z\). % lean: robust_center_coeffs_of_locus
Taking \(\kappa=1\), the strict
optimality part of \cref{obj:lem:frobenius-center-certificate}, transported through
\cref{obj:lem:block-spectral-coordinates}, gives \(I_n\in\mathcal E_m^{\mathrm{blk}}\)
and strict optimality against every \(X\ne I_n\).  For \(X=I_n\) the desired inequality
is equality, while for \(X\ne I_n\) it follows from strict optimality.  Hence a finite
nonnegative robustness weight exists exactly on the stated locus. % lean: robust_corner_exactness

\item On the affine-balanced locus, let \(\kappa>0\).  As above,
\[
\frac{c_x}{q}=c_y=c_z .
\]
The point \(I_n=X(0,0)\) has reduced coordinates \((1,1,1)\) by
\cref{obj:lem:block-spectral-coordinates}.  The Frobenius-center certificate, \cref{obj:lem:frobenius-center-certificate}, gives strict reduced optimality of
\((1,1,1)\) on \(T_m\).  Translating back by
\cref{obj:lem:block-spectral-coordinates}, \(I_n\in\mathcal E_m^{\mathrm{blk}}\), and
for any \(X=X(u,v)\in\mathcal E_m^{\mathrm{blk}}\) with \(X\ne I_n\), the associated
reduced coordinates are not \((1,1,1)\): if they were, then \(x=1\) gives \(u=0\), and then \(z_{\mathrm{sp}}=1\) reads \(1+mv=1\), so \(v=0\) since \(m>0\), i.e.\ \(X=I_n\).  Therefore
\[
F_{r,\kappa}(I_n)<F_{r,\kappa}(X).
\] % lean: identity_strict_relaxed_min_of_locus % lean: identity_mem_blockElliptope

\item The iid law assigns mass \(2^{-n}\) to every sign vector, so it is invariant under
sign flip, under within-block permutations, and under the block swap, whence
\[
P_{\mathrm{iid}}\in\mathcal P_m^{\mathrm{sym}}.
\] % lean: iidDesign_mem_blockExchangeable
Its second moments are diagonal: the diagonal entries are \(\mathbb E[Z_i^2]=1\), and for \(i\ne j\)
the involution \(z\mapsto z'\) that flips only the \(i\)-th sign is a bijection of the
assignment space preserving the uniform law and sending \(Z_iZ_j\) to \(-Z_iZ_j\), so
\[
\mathbb E[Z_iZ_j]=-\mathbb E[Z_iZ_j]=0
\qquad (i\ne j).
\]
Thus all off-diagonal moments vanish and
\[
X(P_{\mathrm{iid}})=I_n .
\] % lean: iidDesign_secondMoment

\item Now assume the affine-balanced locus fails.  If for some \(\kappa>0\) the matrix
\(I_n\) were a relaxed minimizer, then the same center-coefficient necessity argument
from the first step would give
\[
\frac{c_x}{q}=c_y,\qquad c_y=c_z,
\]
and the same algebra would force
\[
a+3b=2m,\qquad r=2b(a+b),
\]
contradicting the assumption.  Hence \(I_n\) is not a relaxed minimizer at any positive
finite robustness weight off the locus. % lean: robust_corner_exactness

\item Finally, let \((X_\kappa)_{\kappa\in\mathbb R}\) be any family of relaxed
minimizers for positive \(\kappa\).  Since \(r\ge0\), \(a>b>0\) and \(m\ge2\), the reduced linear
coefficients satisfy \(c_x,c_y,c_z\ge0\); and every point of \(T_m\) has nonnegative coordinates, so the linear part \(c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}\) is nonnegative on \(T_m\).  Put
\[
L=c_x+c_y+c_z .
\]
The reduced coordinates of \(I_n\) are \((1,1,1)\), whose weighted squared norm is \(q\cdot1+1+1=q+2=2m\); hence, by \cref{obj:lem:block-spectral-coordinates},
\[
F_{r,\kappa}(I_n)=L+\kappa\sqrt{2m}
\qquad\text{for every }\kappa .
\]
Comparing each minimizer with the feasible point \(I_n\) gives
\[
F_{r,\kappa}(X_\kappa)\le F_{r,\kappa}(I_n)=L+\kappa\sqrt{2m}.
\]
Writing \(X_\kappa=X(u_\kappa,v_\kappa)\) and setting
\[
N_{\mathrm{w}}=2m(m-1)>0,
\qquad
N_{\mathrm{a}}=2m^2>0,
\]
the reduced squared norm decomposes as
\[
q(1-u_\kappa)^2+
\bigl(1+(m-1)u_\kappa-mv_\kappa\bigr)^2+
\bigl(1+(m-1)u_\kappa+mv_\kappa\bigr)^2
=
2m+N_{\mathrm{w}}u_\kappa^2+N_{\mathrm{a}}v_\kappa^2 .
\]
% lean: reduced_norm_sq_block_coords
Suppose some within-community off-diagonal entry stayed at distance at least \(\varepsilon>0\) from its target for arbitrarily large \(\kappa\); that entry equals \(u_\kappa\), so \(|u_\kappa|\ge\varepsilon\) and the displayed squared norm is at least \(2m+N_{\mathrm{w}}\varepsilon^2\).  Setting
\[
\delta=\sqrt{2m+N_{\mathrm{w}}\varepsilon^2}-\sqrt{2m}>0,
\]
the robustness term at \(X_\kappa\) is therefore at least \(\kappa(\sqrt{2m}+\delta)\), and, the linear part being nonnegative,
\[
\kappa\bigl(\sqrt{2m}+\delta\bigr)\le F_{r,\kappa}(X_\kappa)
\le L+\kappa\sqrt{2m},
\]
which forces \(\kappa\delta\le L\); this fails for every \(\kappa>L/\delta\), a contradiction.  Hence \(u_\kappa\to0\).  The same argument with \(N_{\mathrm{a}}\) in place of \(N_{\mathrm{w}}\), applied to an across-community entry (which equals \(v_\kappa\)), shows \(v_\kappa\to0\).  Diagonal entries of \(X_\kappa\) are identically \(1\), matching \(I_n\) exactly, while off-diagonal entries
are either \(u_\kappa\) or \(v_\kappa\) and the corresponding entries of \(I_n\) are \(0\). Hence every entry of \(X_\kappa\) converges to
the corresponding entry of \(I_n\) as \(\kappa\to\infty\). % lean: robust_minimizers_tendsto_identity_entries
\end{enumerate}
\end{proof}
