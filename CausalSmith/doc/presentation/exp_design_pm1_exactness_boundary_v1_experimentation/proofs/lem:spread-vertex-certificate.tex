\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:spread-vertex-certificate}]
Put \(q=2(m-1)\), \(M=2m\) and \(\sigma=\sqrt q\).  Since \(q>0\) we have \(\sigma>0\), \(\sigma^2=q\), and \(M=q+2>0\).

\emph{Step 1 (feasibility of the spread vertex).}  The point \((M/q,0,0)\) has nonnegative coordinates and satisfies \(q(M/q)+0+0=M=2m\), so \((M/q,0,0)\in T_m\). % lean: spread_vertex_certificate

\emph{Step 2 (the value at the spread vertex).}  Since \(M/q\ge0\),
\[
\sqrt{q(M/q)^2+0^2+0^2}=\sqrt{\bigl(\sigma\,(M/q)\bigr)^2}=\sigma\,\frac{M}{q},
\]
hence
\[
\phi\Bigl(\frac Mq,0,0\Bigr)=c_x\frac Mq+\kappa\sigma\frac Mq .
\]
% lean: spread_vertex_certificate

\emph{Step 3 (a lower bound for the norm term).}  Let \((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\).  From \(\sigma^2=q\) and \(y^2,z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2\ge0\),
\[
(\sigma x)^2=qx^2\le qx^2+y^2+z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2,
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
\sigma x\le\sqrt{qx^2+y^2+z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2},
\]
and multiplying by \(\kappa\ge0\) gives \(\kappa\sigma x\le\kappa\sqrt{qx^2+y^2+z_{\mathrm{sp}}^2}\).  Therefore
\[
c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}+\kappa\sigma x\le\phi(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}}).
\]
% lean: spread_vertex_certificate

\emph{Step 4 (the coefficient separation in cleared form).}  Multiplying the hypotheses \(c_y>c_x/q+\kappa/\sigma\) and \(c_z>c_x/q+\kappa/\sigma\) by \(\sigma>0\) gives
\[
\Bigl(c_y-\frac{c_x}{q}\Bigr)\sigma-\kappa>0,
\qquad
\Bigl(c_z-\frac{c_x}{q}\Bigr)\sigma-\kappa>0 .
\]
% lean: spread_vertex_certificate

\emph{Step 5 (some off-\(x\) mass is present).}  Assume in addition \((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\ne(M/q,0,0)\).  If \(y=z_{\mathrm{sp}}=0\), the simplex equation \(qx+y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}=M\) would force \(x=M/q\), i.e.\ the point would be the spread vertex; hence \(y>0\) or \(z_{\mathrm{sp}}>0\).  Since \(y,z_{\mathrm{sp}}\ge0\) and both coefficients of Step 4 are strictly positive,
\[
0<\Bigl(\bigl(c_y-\tfrac{c_x}{q}\bigr)\sigma-\kappa\Bigr)y
+\Bigl(\bigl(c_z-\tfrac{c_x}{q}\bigr)\sigma-\kappa\Bigr)z_{\mathrm{sp}} .
\]
% lean: spread_vertex_certificate

\emph{Step 6 (the difference identity).}  Write \(M/q=x+(y+z_{\mathrm{sp}})/q\), which is the simplex equation divided by \(q\).  Then
\[
\bigl(c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}+\kappa\sigma x\bigr)
-\Bigl(c_x\frac Mq+\kappa\sigma\frac Mq\Bigr)
=c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}-\frac{c_x}{q}(y+z_{\mathrm{sp}})-\frac{\kappa\sigma}{q}(y+z_{\mathrm{sp}}),
\]
and multiplying by \(\sigma\) and using \(\sigma^2=q\) turns the last term into \(\kappa(y+z_{\mathrm{sp}})\), giving
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Bigl(c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}+\kappa\sigma x
-c_x\frac Mq-\kappa\sigma\frac Mq\Bigr)\sigma\\
&\qquad=\Bigl(\bigl(c_y-\tfrac{c_x}{q}\bigr)\sigma-\kappa\Bigr)y
+\Bigl(\bigl(c_z-\tfrac{c_x}{q}\bigr)\sigma-\kappa\Bigr)z_{\mathrm{sp}} .
\end{aligned}
\]
% lean: spreadObjectiveDiff_mul_sqrt
By Step 5 the right-hand side is strictly positive, and \(\sigma>0\), so the bracket on the left is strictly positive:
\[
c_x\frac Mq+\kappa\sigma\frac Mq<c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}+\kappa\sigma x .
\]

\emph{Step 7 (conclusion).}  Chaining Steps 2, 6 and 3,
\[
\phi\Bigl(\frac Mq,0,0\Bigr)
=c_x\frac Mq+\kappa\sigma\frac Mq
<c_xx+c_yy+c_zz_{\mathrm{sp}}+\kappa\sigma x
\le\phi(x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})
\]
for every \((x,y,z_{\mathrm{sp}})\in T_m\) other than \((2m/q,0,0)\), which is the asserted strict inequality. % lean: spread_vertex_certificate
\end{proof}
