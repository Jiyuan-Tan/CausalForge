# Title
**Uniform Expected Risk for Distance-Based Boundary Regression Designs**

**Contribution statement.** The paper establishes logarithmic uniform-risk lower bounds for distance-compressed boundary regression rules and a matched outer-expected minimax rate for signed-distance local-polynomial estimation on the displayed Euclidean boundary-discontinuity law class.

## section: Discussion and Extensions
This section will state unambiguously that the unsigned results are lower bounds only and establish no matching unsigned upper bound here, while the signed-distance result is matched only conditional on \(\mathsf{AI}_{p,\nu,L}\). The common \(a_n\) scale is therefore an agreement of lower scales, not evidence that signed distance has no rate advantage. It will discuss the exact Euclidean, uniform-kernel, and envelope scope and reserve genuine future directions for a clearly labelled open-questions paragraph.
