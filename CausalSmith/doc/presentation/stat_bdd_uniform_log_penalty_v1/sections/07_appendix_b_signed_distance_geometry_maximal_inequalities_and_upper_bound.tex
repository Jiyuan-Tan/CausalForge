\section{Appendix B: Signed-Distance Geometry, Maximal Inequalities, and Upper Bound}

This appendix collects the empirical-process ingredients used by the signed-distance upper bound in \cref{obj:prop:cty-a1-a2-winsorized-expected-outer-upper}. The objects are those of the known-geometry design in \cref{obj:def:cty-known-geometry, obj:def:cty-known-geometry-signed-distance-data,obj:def:cty-a1-a2-class,obj:def:cty-stabilized-local-polynomial-estimator}: local neighborhoods are Euclidean balls generated by the uniform weight \(K_\square\), the outcome moment envelope is handled through winsorization, and the stochastic term is measured by an outer-expected supremum over arms and interface points. The three statements below organize these roles before the estimator bound is assembled in \cref{obj:prop:cty-a1-a2-winsorized-expected-outer-upper}.

The first ingredient is geometric. Because the local windows are closed Euclidean balls, the relevant index class has finite VC complexity, giving the covering control used by the maximal inequality \citep{Vapnik1971,Pollard1984,VanderVaart1996}.

\begin{lemmav}{lem:euclidean-balls-vc}[Planar Balls VC Bound]
For every finite set \(S\) of score points in \(\mathbb R^2\), if \(|S|\ge 4\), then \(S\) is not shattered by the class of closed Euclidean balls
\[
\{B_2(x,r): x\in\mathbb R^2,\ r\ge 0\}.
\]
Equivalently, no such \(S\) has the property that every subset of \(S\) can be written as \(S\cap B_2(x,r)\) for some center \(x\in\mathbb R^2\) and radius \(r\ge 0\).
\end{lemmav}

The bound in \cref{obj:lem:euclidean-balls-vc} is used with the ball notation introduced in \cref{obj:synth_43}. In the signed-distance design, the event selected by the uniform kernel is a side-restricted Euclidean neighborhood of the query point. Finite VC complexity therefore converts the continuum of possible interface centers into an empirical-process class with logarithmic entropy, matching the \(\log(h^{-1})\) factors in the risk calculation.

The second ingredient controls the deterministic effect of replacing the outcome by its winsorized version. The \(2+\nu\) moment envelope in \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-class} implies a uniform tail contribution bound at every arm and covariate value.

\begin{lemmav}{lem:cty-winsorization-bias}[Winsorization Bias Bound]
Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\) as in \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-class}, and let \(B\geq 1\).  Define
\[
\psi_B(y)=\operatorname{sign}(y)\min\{|y|,B\}.
\]
Then, for each \(t\in\{0,1\}\) and each \(x\in\mathcal X_P\), under the selected conditional law of \(Y(t)\) given \(X=x\),
\[
\left|
\int y\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x}(y)
-
\int \psi_B(y)\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x}(y)
\right|
\leq L B^{-3}.
\]
\end{lemmav}

\Cref{obj:lem:cty-winsorization-bias} links the envelope condition to the estimator in \cref{obj:def:cty-stabilized-local-polynomial-estimator}. With \(B(h)=h^{-1/3}\), the displayed \(B^{-3}\) term is of order \(h\), so winsorization contributes on the same scale as the first-order local-polynomial bias assumed in \cref{obj:prop:cty-a1-a2-winsorized-expected-outer-upper}. The calculation preserves uniformity over the law class because the conditional moment bound is part of \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-class}.

The final auxiliary statement supplies the expected maximal bound for the centered winsorized local-polynomial score. It combines the VC geometry of \cref{obj:lem:euclidean-balls-vc} with the bounded envelope created by \cref{obj:lem:cty-winsorization-bias}, using the outer-expectation convention already built into \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-outer-risk}.

\begin{lemmav}{lem:cty-winsorized-score-maximal-bound}[Winsorized Score Maximal Bound]
Fix a nonnegative integer \(p\) and a real number \(L\).  There is a constant
\(C=C(p,L)>0\) such that the following holds for every real \(\nu\).

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Law class.)} The law \(P\) belongs to the A1/A2 class
\(\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\) of \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-class}.
\item \textbf{(Sample size.)} The sample size satisfies \(n\geq 1\).
\item \textbf{(Bandwidth and clipping.)} The bandwidth and winsorization level satisfy
\(0<h\leq L^{-1}\) and \(B\geq 1\).
\end{itemize}

Let \(P^n\) denote the \(n\)-fold i.i.d. law of the potential-outcome observations.
For each \(t\in\{0,1\}\) and \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), let the winsorized centered local-polynomial
score be the vector whose \(j\)th coordinate is
\[
\operatorname{score}^{B}_{t,x,h,j}
  - \mathbb E_{P^n}\!\left[\operatorname{score}^{B}_{t,x,h,j}\right],
\]
where the empirical score uses the uniform kernel, the polynomial basis \(r_p\), bandwidth \(h\),
and the clipped outcome transform \(\psi_B\), centered around the corresponding population score.
Then
\[
\mathbb E_{P^n}^{*}\!\left[
\sup_{t\in\{0,1\},\,x\in\mathcal B_P}
\left\|
\operatorname{score}^{B}_{t,x,h}
-\mathbb E_{P^n}\!\left[\operatorname{score}^{B}_{t,x,h}\right]
\right\|
\right]
\leq
C\left\{
\sqrt{\frac{\log(B/h)}{n h^2}}
+
\frac{B\log(B/h)}{n h^2}
\right\}.
\]
\end{lemmav}

The two terms in \cref{obj:lem:cty-winsorized-score-maximal-bound} have the usual variance and envelope forms for bounded empirical processes indexed by VC-type sets \citep{Pollard1984,VanderVaart1996}. The scaling \(h^{-2}\) reflects the two-dimensional local window around an interface point, while the supremum over \(t\in\{0,1\}\) and \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) matches the signed-distance risk criterion in \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-outer-risk}. Together with the Gram lower bound in \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-class}, the statement controls the stochastic perturbation of the intercepts in the stabilized estimator.

These ingredients enter the upper-bound calculation as follows. The population part is separated into the first-order local-polynomial approximation term and the winsorization term from \cref{obj:lem:cty-winsorization-bias}. The sample part is decomposed into empirical Gram fluctuation and centered score fluctuation, with the score controlled by \cref{obj:lem:cty-winsorized-score-maximal-bound} and the Gram term handled by the expected maximal condition stated in \cref{obj:prop:cty-a1-a2-winsorized-expected-outer-upper}. The stabilization rule in \cref{obj:def:cty-stabilized-local-polynomial-estimator} converts these uniform matrix and score bounds into a uniform intercept bound for each arm, and clipping keeps the treatment-effect estimate on the envelope scale.

At the bandwidth \(h_n=a_n\) from \cref{obj:def:frontier-rates} and winsorization level \(B_n=a_n^{-1/3}\), the first-order bias, winsorization bias, and stochastic terms in \cref{obj:prop:cty-a1-a2-winsorized-expected-outer-upper} are all bounded by a constant multiple of \(a_n\) for sufficiently large \(n\). Thus the auxiliary results in this appendix support the expected outer-risk upper bound for the explicit signed-distance local-polynomial estimator, completing the upper side of the matched-rate comparison stated in \cref{obj:thm:cty-a1-a2-winsorized-matched-frontier}.
