## escalate — out of causalsmith scope (bank/causalsmith)
- [major·citation·citation_research] (Related Literature) The novelty boundary relative to Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu (2026) remains under-adjudicated. The manuscript says CTY develop distance-based identification and estimation tools, while the related-work context indicates CTY already supply identification, bias, stochastic-process, inference, and minimax lower-bound ingredients. The paper still does not crisply separate the new lower-bound architecture, decision-class enlargement, fixed-geometry hypercube, expected-risk formulation, and estimator stabilization from conditional repackaging or application of CTY inputs.

- [minor·prose·rewrite] (abstract) The unsigned result is technically accurate but its econometric interpretation remains too compressed. - [minor·structure·rewrite] (global) The result titles are still difficult to distinguish.
