\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:cty-winsorization-bias}]
Fix \(t\in\{0,1\}\) and \(x\in\mathcal X_P\), and work under the selected
conditional law \(P_{Y(t)\mid X=x}\) of \(Y(t)\) given \(X=x\).  The class
conditions in \cref{obj:def:cty-a1-a2-class} give \(\nu\ge2\), \(L\ge4\), and the
conditional moment envelope
\[
\int |y|^{2+\nu}\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x}(y)\le L .
\]
% lean: A1A2Class
In particular \(|y|^{2+\nu}\) is integrable, hence so is \(y\) because
\(1\le 2+\nu\) and \(P_{Y(t)\mid X=x}\) is a probability law; and \(\psi_B\) is
measurable with \(|\psi_B(y)|\le B\), so \(\psi_B(y)\) is integrable as well.
% lean: hpow_int
% lean: hy
% lean: hwin

\emph{Two pointwise facts about the winsorization remainder.}  First, for every
real \(y\),
\[
|y-\psi_B(y)|\le |y| ,
\]
because \(\psi_B(y)=\operatorname{sign}(y)\min\{|y|,B\}\) has the same sign as
\(y\) and modulus \(\min\{|y|,B\}\le|y|\), so the remainder \(y-\psi_B(y)\) has
modulus \(|y|-\min\{|y|,B\}\le|y|\).
% lean: abs_sub_winsorize_le_abs
Second,
\[
\psi_B(y)=y
\qquad\text{whenever } |y|\le B ,
\]
since then \(\min\{|y|,B\}=|y|\), and \(\operatorname{sign}(y)|y|=y\) in each of
the three defining cases \(y<0\), \(y=0\), and \(0<y\).
% lean: winsorize

\emph{The pointwise majorant.}  For every real \(y\),
\[
|y-\psi_B(y)|\le |y|^{2+\nu}B^{-3}.
\]
If \(|y|\le B\), the left-hand side vanishes by the preceding identity and the
right-hand side is nonnegative.  If \(B<|y|\), then \(B\ge1\) gives \(|y|\ge1\),
so \(B^3\le|y|^3\) and \(4\le 2+\nu\) yield
\[
|y|\le |y|^{4}B^{-3}\le |y|^{2+\nu}B^{-3},
\]
the first inequality because \(|y|B^3\le|y|\cdot|y|^3=|y|^4\) and the second
because \(|y|\ge1\) makes the exponent monotone; combining with
\(|y-\psi_B(y)|\le|y|\) gives the displayed majorant.
% lean: winsorize_tail_le_moment

\emph{Integration.}  Using integrability to subtract inside the integral and
applying the pointwise majorant,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left|\int y\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x}-\int \psi_B(y)\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x}\right|
&=\left|\int \{y-\psi_B(y)\}\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x}\right| \\
&\le \int |y|^{2+\nu}B^{-3}\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x} \\
&= B^{-3}\int |y|^{2+\nu}\,dP_{Y(t)\mid X=x} \\
&\le L B^{-3},
\end{aligned}
\]
the last step by the conditional moment envelope together with \(B^{-3}\ge0\).
% lean: norm_integral_le_of_norm_le
This is the claimed bias bound.
% lean: cty_winsorization_bias
\end{proof}
