\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:euclidean-balls-vc}]
Suppose, for contradiction, that a finite set \(S\subset\mathbb R^2\) with \(|S|\ge4\) is
shattered by closed Euclidean balls, that is, for every subset \(T\subseteq S\) there are a
center \(c\in\mathbb R^2\) and a radius \(r\ge0\) with
\[
T=S\cap B_2(c,r)=\{z\in S:\|z-c\|_2\leq r\} .
\]
Since \(|S|\ge4\) we may fix four pairwise distinct points \(z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4\in S\).
% lean: Finset.exists_subset_card_eq
Writing \(z_i=(z_{i,1},z_{i,2})\), put
\[
\Phi(z_i)=\bigl(1,z_{i,1},z_{i,2},\|z_i\|_2^2\bigr)\in\mathbb R^4 ,
\qquad i\in\{1,2,3,4\} .
\]

\emph{An algebraic identity.}  Let \(\ell=(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3,\ell_4)\in\mathbb R^4\) be
arbitrary coefficients and let \(w\in\mathbb R^4\) be the corresponding combination,
\[
w=\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\Phi(z_i) .
\]
For a center \(c=(c_1,c_2)\in\mathbb R^2\) and a radius \(r\ge0\), expanding the squared
distance gives, for each \(i\),
\[
\|z_i-c\|_2^2-r^2
=
\|z_i\|_2^2-2c_1z_{i,1}-2c_2z_{i,2}+(\|c\|_2^2-r^2)\cdot 1 ,
\]
which is one and the same linear combination of the four entries of \(\Phi(z_i)\), with
coefficients not depending on \(i\).  Multiplying by \(\ell_i\) and summing therefore
replaces those entries by the entries of \(w\):
\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\{\|z_i-c\|_2^2-r^2\}
=
w_4-2c_1w_2-2c_2w_3+(\|c\|_2^2-r^2)w_1 .
\]
% lean: hweighted

\emph{A sign contradiction.}  We next record the contradiction used in both cases below.
Suppose the coefficients \(\ell\) are not all zero, that their combination \(w\) satisfies
\(w_1=0\), and that
\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\{\|z_i-c\|_2^2-r^2\}\ge 0
\qquad\text{for every }c\in\mathbb R^2\text{ and every }r\ge0 .
\]
Reading off the first entry of \(w\) and using \(\Phi(z_i)_1=1\) for every \(i\),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i=w_1=0 ,
\]
% lean: hlsum
so, some \(\ell_i\) being nonzero, at least one coefficient is strictly negative; fix an
index \(i_-\) with \(\ell_{i_-}<0\).
% lean: hineg
Apply the shattering hypothesis to the subset
\[
T=\{z_i:1\le i\le 4,\ \ell_i>0\}\subseteq S ,
\]
obtaining a center \(c\) and a radius \(r\ge0\) with \(T=S\cap B_2(c,r)\).  Because
\(z_1,\ldots,z_4\) are distinct, \(z_i\in T\) holds exactly when \(\ell_i>0\).  Hence every
term of the weighted sum is nonpositive,
\[
\ell_i\{\|z_i-c\|_2^2-r^2\}\le0 ,\qquad i\in\{1,2,3,4\} :
\]
if \(\ell_i>0\) then \(z_i\in T\subseteq B_2(c,r)\), so \(\|z_i-c\|_2\le r\), the bracket
is nonpositive, and the product is nonpositive; if \(\ell_i\le0\) then \(z_i\notin T\), so
\(\|z_i-c\|_2>r\), the bracket is strictly positive, and again the product is nonpositive.
% lean: hterm
At \(i_-\) the second alternative occurs with a strictly negative coefficient, so
\[
\ell_{i_-}\{\|z_{i_-}-c\|_2^2-r^2\}<0 ,
\]
% lean: hstrict
and summing the four terms gives
\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\{\|z_i-c\|_2^2-r^2\}<0 ,
\]
which contradicts the assumed nonnegativity at this \((c,r)\).
% lean: htotal
% lean: hsign_contra

\emph{Case 1: \(\Phi(z_1),\ldots,\Phi(z_4)\) are linearly independent.}  Four linearly
independent vectors in \(\mathbb R^4\) form a basis, so the fourth standard basis vector has
an expansion
\[
e_4=(0,0,0,1)=\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\Phi(z_i)
\]
with coefficients \(\ell\) that are not all zero, since \(e_4\neq0\).  Here \(w=e_4\), so
\(w_1=w_2=w_3=0\) and \(w_4=1\), and the algebraic identity gives, for every
\(c\in\mathbb R^2\) and every \(r\ge0\),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\{\|z_i-c\|_2^2-r^2\}=1\ge0 .
\]
The sign contradiction applies.
% lean: basisOfLinearIndependentOfCardEqFinrank

\emph{Case 2: \(\Phi(z_1),\ldots,\Phi(z_4)\) are linearly dependent.}  Choose coefficients
\(\ell\), not all zero, with
\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\Phi(z_i)=0 .
\]
Here \(w=0\), so in particular \(w_1=0\), and the algebraic identity gives, for every
\(c\in\mathbb R^2\) and every \(r\ge0\),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^4\ell_i\{\|z_i-c\|_2^2-r^2\}=0\ge0 .
\]
The sign contradiction applies again.
% lean: Fintype.not_linearIndependent_iff

Both cases are impossible, so no finite \(S\) with \(|S|\ge4\) is shattered by closed
Euclidean balls.
% lean: euclidean_balls_vc
\end{proof}
