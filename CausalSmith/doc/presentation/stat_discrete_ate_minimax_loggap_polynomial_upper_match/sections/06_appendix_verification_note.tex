\section{Appendix: verification note}

This appendix records the verification boundary for the finite-alphabet product-law formulation used throughout the paper. The formal development represents the observed-data experiment in two compatible ways: a finite single-observation law for \(O=(X,A,Y)\), as used in \cref{obj:ass:iid-sampling,obj:def:experiment-class}, and an ambient infinite iid sample sequence from which finite sample segments can be extracted. The following statement gives the bridge between those two views.

\begin{lemmav}{lem:ambient-sample-marginal}[Ambient sample marginal law]
Let \(P\) be a probability law on a measurable single-observation space, and let \(n\in\mathbb N\). Write \(P^{\otimes\infty}\) for the infinite product law on sequences \((O_i)_{i\ge 0}\), and write \(P^{\otimes n}\) for the finite product law on \(n\)-tuples. Then the coordinate restriction map
\[
(O_i)_{i\ge 0}\longmapsto (O_i)_{i\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}}
\]
pushes \(P^{\otimes\infty}\) forward to \(P^{\otimes n}\):
\[
(P^{\otimes\infty})\circ\bigl((O_i)_{i\ge 0}\mapsto (O_i)_{i\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}}\bigr)^{-1}
=
P^{\otimes n}.
\]
In particular, for the observed-data laws \(P\) used in \cref{obj:ass:iid-sampling}, the initial sample segment has the \(n\)-fold product law \(P^{\otimes n}\) appearing in \cref{obj:def:experiment-class}.
\end{lemmav}

\Cref{obj:lem:ambient-sample-marginal} justifies using an infinite iid product space as an ambient construction while retaining the finite-sample experiment class in \cref{obj:def:experiment-class} as the law governing \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\). This connection is useful for statements that are naturally organized around coordinate projections, while the statistical risk in \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk} remains the finite-\(n\) mean-squared risk under \(P^{\otimes n}\).

The verification covers the finite-alphabet iid observational model, the potential-outcome overlay, the overlap-restricted four-cell cone, the cell functional, the ATE functional, the minimax risk, the deterministic split counts, and the hybrid estimator defined in \cref{obj:ass:iid-sampling,obj:ass:overlap,obj:def:overlap-cone,obj:def:cell-functional,obj:def:ate-functional,obj:def:minimax-risk,obj:def:sample-splits,obj:def:hybrid-estimator-handle}. The auxiliary proof layer verifies the estimator, approximation, calibration, residual, missing-arm, factorial-moment, and aggregation statements used by the main rate theorems. The theorem statements and proofs are machine-checked in Lean 4; theorem-local verification footnotes identify the published mathematical conclusions invoked as external dependencies.
