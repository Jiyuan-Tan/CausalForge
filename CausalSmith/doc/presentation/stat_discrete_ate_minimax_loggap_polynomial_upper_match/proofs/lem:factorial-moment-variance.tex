\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:factorial-moment-variance}]
All expectations below are taken under the iid sequence law generated by \(P\). Write \(M=M(n)\), \(B=B(n)\), \(m_1=|I_1|\), and \(q=q_k\). The coefficients in the sparse expansion are the coefficients of the polynomial continuation \(G_M\):
\[
g_{M,j}=(-1)^j\frac{2^{2j+3}}{M(M+j+2)}\binom{M+j+2}{2j+4},
\qquad
G_M(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{M-2} g_{M,j}x^j .
\]
For \(a\in\{0,1\}\), \(c\in\{0,1\}^2\), and \(0\le t\le j\le M-2\), let \(r^{a,c,j,t}\) be the sparse multi-index determined by
\[
u^{r^{a,c,j,t}}=u_c\,u_{a1}\,u_{a0}^{\,t}\,u_{a1}^{\,j-t}.
\]
The sparse arm mean is
\[
\bar A_{a,k}
=
\sum_{j=0}^{M-2}\sum_{t=0}^{j}\sum_{c\in\{0,1\}^2}
B^{-1}g_{M,j}B^{-j}\binom jt\,q^{r^{a,c,j,t}},
\]
and the sparse polynomial mean is
\[
\bar P_k=\bar A_{1,k}-\bar A_{0,k}.
\]
For a nonnegative four-cell vector \(v\), set
\[
\mathcal E_a(v)
=
\sum_{j=0}^{M-2}\sum_{t=0}^{j}\sum_{c\in\{0,1\}^2}
\left|B^{-1}g_{M,j}B^{-j}\binom jt\right|\,v^{r^{a,c,j,t}}.
\]
The triangle inequality gives \(|\bar A_{a,k}|\le \mathcal E_a(q)\). We first record the envelope bound used for both \(q\) and its shifted version. If \(v\ge0\), \(\sum_c v_c\le B\), \(M>0\), and \(B>0\), then summing first over \(c\) and then using the binomial theorem gives
\[
\mathcal E_a(v)
= B^{-1}\left(\sum_c v_c\right)v_{a1}
\sum_{j=0}^{M-2}|g_{M,j}|
\left(\frac{v_{a0}+v_{a1}}{B}\right)^j .
\]
The absolute-coefficient series satisfies, for every \(z\ge0\),
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{M-2}|g_{M,j}|z^j
\le 6^M\max\{1,z^{M-2}\}.
\]
Indeed, the displayed coefficient formula gives \(|g_{M,j}|=(-1)^jg_{M,j}\), hence \(\sum_j |g_{M,j}|=G_M(-1)\). The shifted-Chebyshev identity
\[
T_M(1-2x)=1-2M^2x+2M^2x^2G_M(x)
\]
at \(x=-1\) gives \(T_M(3)=1+2M^2+2M^2G_M(-1)\), and since \(G_M(-1)\ge0\) and \(M>0\), this bounds \(\sum_j|g_{M,j}|\) by \(T_M(3)\). The Chebyshev recurrence at \(3\), together with nonnegativity of the preceding values, gives \(T_M(3)\le6^M\). For \(0\le z\le1\), monotonicity of the nonnegative terms gives the bound from the value at \(1\); for \(z\ge1\), the inequality \(z^j\le z^{M-2}\) for \(j\le M-2\) gives the displayed maximum factor.

In the envelope application, \(z=(v_{a0}+v_{a1})/B\) lies in \([0,1]\). Since \(0\le v_{a1}\le \sum_c v_c\le B\),
\[
\mathcal E_a(v)
\le B^{-1}\left(\sum_c v_c\right)^2 6^M
\le B6^M .
\]
Applying this with \(v=q\), using \(q\ge0\) and \(\sum_c q_c=p_k\le B\), gives
\[
|\bar P_k|
\le |\bar A_{1,k}|+|\bar A_{0,k}|
\le 2B6^M,
\qquad
\bar P_k^2\le4(B6^M)^2. % lean: sparsePolynomialMean_abs_le
\]

The sample arm contribution has the parallel factorial-moment expansion
\[
\widehat A_{a,k}
=
\sum_{j=0}^{M-2}\sum_{t=0}^{j}\sum_{c\in\{0,1\}^2}
B^{-1}g_{M,j}B^{-j}\binom jt\,
\frac{\prod_{b,y}(N^{(1)}_{bky})_{r^{a,c,j,t}_{by}}}{(m_1)_{|r^{a,c,j,t}|}},
\qquad
\widehat P_k=\widehat A_{1,k}-\widehat A_{0,k}.
\]
For a multi-index \(r\), write
\[
F_r=\frac{\prod_{b,y}(N^{(1)}_{bky})_{r_{by}}}{(m_1)_{|r|}}.
\]
For two displayed terms with multi-indices \(r\) and \(s\), the range restrictions give \(|r|=j+2\le M\) and \(|s|=j'+2\le M\). Suppose first that \(|r|\le |s|\). Expanding the product of factorial counts by the cellwise overlap vector \(\eta\) gives
\[
\mathbb E[F_rF_s]
=
\sum_\eta C_{r,s}(\eta)
\frac{(m_1)_{|r|+|s|-|\eta|}}{(m_1)_{|r|}(m_1)_{|s|}}
\prod_{b,y}q_{by}^{\,r_{by}+s_{by}-\eta_{by}},
\]
where the sum is over all four-cell vectors \(\eta=(\eta_{by})\) with integer coordinates satisfying \(0\le \eta_{by}\le\min\{r_{by},s_{by}\}\), where \(|\eta|=\sum_{b,y}\eta_{by}\), and where
\[
C_{r,s}(\eta)=\prod_{b,y}\binom{r_{by}}{\eta_{by}}\binom{s_{by}}{\eta_{by}}\eta_{by}! .
\]
The falling-factorial ratio in this display obeys
\[
\frac{(m_1)_{|r|+|s|-|\eta|}}{(m_1)_{|r|}(m_1)_{|s|}}
\le \frac{\mathrm e}{m_1^{|\eta|}} .
\]
If \(|s|=0\), then \(|r|=|\eta|=0\) and the bound is immediate. Otherwise, \(4|s|^2\le m_1\) gives \(0<|s|<m_1\). The elementary bounds
\[
(m_1)_{|r|+|s|-|\eta|}\le m_1^{|r|+|s|-|\eta|},
\qquad
(m_1-|s|)^{|r|}\le (m_1)_{|r|},
\qquad
(m_1-|s|)^{|s|}\le (m_1)_{|s|}
\]
give
\[
\frac{(m_1)_{|r|+|s|-|\eta|}}{(m_1)_{|r|}(m_1)_{|s|}}
\le
\frac{m_1^{|r|+|s|-|\eta|}}{(m_1-|s|)^{|r|+|s|}}
=
\frac{\{m_1/(m_1-|s|)\}^{|r|+|s|}}{m_1^{|\eta|}}.
\]
Finally,
\[
(|r|+|s|)\log\!\left(\frac{m_1}{m_1-|s|}\right)
\le
\frac{(|r|+|s|)|s|}{m_1-|s|}
\le
\frac{2|s|^2}{m_1-|s|}
\le 1,
\]
where the first inequality uses \(\log x\le x-1\), the second uses \(|r|\le |s|\), and the last follows from \(4|s|^2\le m_1\) together with \(|s|\le2|s|^2\). Exponentiating gives the displayed ratio bound.

The coefficient bound \(\binom{r_{by}}{\eta_{by}}\binom{s_{by}}{\eta_{by}}\eta_{by}!\le \binom{s_{by}}{\eta_{by}}|s|^{\eta_{by}}\), applied cell by cell and summed with the binomial theorem, yields
\[
\mathbb E[F_rF_s]
\le
\mathrm e\,q^r\left(q+\frac{|s|}{m_1}\mathbf 1\right)^s.
\]
When \(|s|\le |r|\), the same argument with \(r\) and \(s\) interchanged applies. Since \(q\le q+M\mathbf 1/m_1\) coordinatewise and \(|r|,|s|\le M\), monotonicity of nonnegative monomials gives, in either case,
\[
\mathbb E[F_rF_s]
\le
\mathrm e\,
\left(q+\frac{M}{m_1}\mathbf 1\right)^r
\left(q+\frac{M}{m_1}\mathbf 1\right)^s,
\]
using the size condition \(4M^2\le m_1\). The unweighted factorial monomial product has nonnegative expectation, and the product of the two displayed coefficients is bounded above by the product of their absolute values; hence
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathbb E\!\left[
\left(B^{-1}g_{M,j}B^{-j}\binom jt\,
F_{r^{a,c,j,t}}\right)
\left(B^{-1}g_{M,j'}B^{-j'}\binom {j'}{t'}\,
F_{r^{a,c',j',t'}}\right)
\right]
\\
&\quad\le
\mathrm e\,
\left|B^{-1}g_{M,j}B^{-j}\binom jt\right|
\left(q+\frac{M}{m_1}\mathbf 1\right)^{r^{a,c,j,t}}
\left|B^{-1}g_{M,j'}B^{-j'}\binom {j'}{t'}\right|
\left(q+\frac{M}{m_1}\mathbf 1\right)^{r^{a,c',j',t'}} .
\end{aligned} % lean: integral_sparse_terms_mul_shift_le
\]
Expanding \(\widehat A_{a,k}^2\) as the finite double sum, applying this estimate term by term, and reassembling the absolute-coefficient sums gives
\[
\mathbb E[\widehat A_{a,k}^2]
\le
\mathrm e\,\mathcal E_a\!\left(q+\frac{M}{m_1}\mathbf 1\right)^2.
\]
The shifted vector is nonnegative and has total mass
\[
\sum_{b,y}\left(q_{by}+\frac{M}{m_1}\right)
=p_k+\frac{4M}{m_1}
\le B,
\]
by the shifted light-cell mass condition. Applying the envelope bound to the shifted vector gives
\[
\mathbb E[\widehat A_{a,k}^2]
\le \mathrm e\,(B6^M)^2
\qquad(a=0,1). % lean: integral_sparseArm_sq_rate_le
\]
Using \((u-v)^2\le2u^2+2v^2\) with \(u=\widehat A_{1,k}\) and \(v=\widehat A_{0,k}\),
\[
\mathbb E[\widehat P_k^2]
\le 2\mathbb E[\widehat A_{1,k}^2]+2\mathbb E[\widehat A_{0,k}^2]
\le4\mathrm e\,(B6^M)^2.
\]

Finally, pointwise,
\[
(\widehat P_k-\bar P_k)^2
\le 2\widehat P_k^2+2\bar P_k^2,
\]
because \((\widehat P_k+\bar P_k)^2\ge0\). Integrating and substituting the two bounds above gives
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[(\widehat P_k-\bar P_k)^2\right]
\le
2\cdot4\mathrm e\,(B6^M)^2+2\cdot4(B6^M)^2
=
8(\mathrm e+1)(B6^M)^2,
\]
as required. % lean: integral_factorialPolynomial_centered_sq_le
\end{proof}
