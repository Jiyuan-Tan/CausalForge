\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:crossfit-localized-process-reduction}]
The localized VC envelope, in the quantitative form assumed, gives constants \(C_0>0\) and \(p\ge0\) such that every policy-compatible increment class \(h\) with envelope \(B\) and second moment bounded by \(B^2P_X(D_\pi)\) satisfies, for each sample size \(m>0\) and each radius \(r\ge0\),
\[
E_P Z_m(r)
=
E_P\!\left[\sup_{\pi\in\Pi:\,R_P(\pi)\le r}\left|\frac1m\sum_{i=1}^m h(\pi,O_i)-\int h(\pi,O)\,dP(O)\right|\right]
\le
C_0 Bm^{-1/2}r^{\alpha/(2+2\alpha)}(\log m)^p .
\]
The fixed-fold assumption of \cref{obj:ass:fixed-crossfit-fold-count} supplies \(K\ge1\).  Set \(C=C_0K\), so \(C>0\) and \(C_0\le C\). % lean: VCLocalizedEnvelope % lean: localized_vc_process_bound % lean: crossfit_localized_process_reduction

Fix \(n>0\), \(B\ge0\), \(r\ge0\), and a fold-indexed family \(g=(g_k)_{k=1}^K\) satisfying the stated compatibility, measurability, integrability, envelope, and second-moment hypotheses.

\emph{Step 1: the foldwise \(n\)-observation bound.}  For each fixed fold label \(k\), the single increment class \(g_k\) is itself policy-compatible with envelope \(B\) and second moment bounded by \(B^2P_X(D_\pi)\), so the displayed envelope applies to it at the full sample size \(m=n\):
\[
E_P\!\left[\sup_{\pi\in\Pi:\,R_P(\pi)\le r}
\left|\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n g_k(\pi,O_i)-\int g_k(\pi,O)\,dP(O)\right|\right]
\le
C_0 B n^{-1/2}r^{\alpha/(2+2\alpha)}(\log n)^p
\le
C B n^{-1/2}r^{\alpha/(2+2\alpha)}(\log n)^p .
\]
This is the first assertion of the lemma; it is not the fold-size term used below in the pooled decomposition. % lean: Henv % lean: hC0_le_C

\emph{Step 2: decompose the pooled process by fold.}  Write \(m_k=|I_k|\) and \(w_k=m_k/n\), and, for a nonempty fold \(k\), let
\[
G_k(\pi)=\frac1{m_k}\sum_{i\in I_k}\left\{g_k(\pi,O_i)-\int g_k(\pi,O)\,dP(O)\right\},
\qquad
Z_k(r)=\sup_{\pi\in\Pi:\,R_P(\pi)\le r}\left|G_k(\pi)\right| .
\]
Grouping the pooled sum in the definition of \(Z_{\mathrm{cf}}(r)\) by evaluation fold gives the exact identity
\[
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\left\{g_{k(i)}(\pi,O_i)-\int g_{k(i)}(\pi,O)\,dP(O)\right\}
=
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\,G_k(\pi),
\qquad
w_k\ge0 .
\]
% lean: pooledCrossfitProcess_eq_sum_fold
Hence, for every \(\pi\in\Pi\) with \(R_P(\pi)\le r\), the triangle inequality gives
\[
\left|\sum_{k=1}^K w_kG_k(\pi)\right|
\le
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k|G_k(\pi)|
\le
\sum_{k=1}^K w_kZ_k(r).
\]
Each \(Z_k(r)\) is finite because the envelope hypothesis gives \(|G_k(\pi)|\le2B\) uniformly in \(\pi\), and the right-hand side does not depend on \(\pi\); taking the supremum over the localized policy set yields the pointwise-in-sample inequality
\[
Z_{\mathrm{cf}}(r)\le \sum_{k=1}^K w_k Z_k(r).
\]
% lean: abs_foldCenteredProcess_le % lean: pooledLocalizedSup_pointwise_le_sum_fold

\emph{Step 3: integrate.}  The stated integrability hypotheses make both sides integrable, so the inequality may be integrated and the finite sum exchanged with the integral:
\[
E_P Z_{\mathrm{cf}}(r)\le\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\,E_P Z_k(r).
\]
The coordinate projection from the full product sample onto the coordinates indexed by \(I_k\) is measure preserving onto the product law of \(m_k\) i.i.d.\ observations from \(P\), and \(Z_k(r)\) depends on the sample only through those coordinates; therefore \(E_PZ_k(r)\) is exactly the ordinary localized-supremum expectation for \(m_k\) i.i.d.\ observations from \(P\). % lean: measurePreserving_foldProjection % lean: integral_foldLocalizedSup_eq_expected

If \(m_k=0\), then \(w_k=0\) and the fold contributes zero.  If \(m_k>0\), applying the localized VC envelope at sample size \(m=m_k\) to \(g_k\) gives
\[
w_k E_PZ_k(r)
\le
w_k\,C_0 B m_k^{-1/2}r^{\alpha/(2+2\alpha)}(\log m_k)^p .
\]

\emph{Step 4: compare each weighted fold rate with the target \(n\)-rate.}  The inclusion \(I_k\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}\) gives \(m_k\le n\); therefore
\[
w_k m_k^{-1/2}
=
\frac{m_k}{n}\cdot m_k^{-1/2}
=
\frac{\sqrt{m_k}}{n}
\le
\frac{\sqrt n}{n}
=
n^{-1/2},
\qquad
(\log m_k)^p\le(\log n)^p,
\]
the latter because \(1\le m_k\le n\) and \(p\ge0\). % lean: fold_weight_mul_inv_sqrt_le % lean: log_nat_rpow_le
Thus each fold contributes at most
\[
C_0 B n^{-1/2}r^{\alpha/(2+2\alpha)}(\log n)^p,
\]
and summing over the fixed \(K\) folds yields
\[
E_P Z_{\mathrm{cf}}(r)
\le
C_0K B n^{-1/2}r^{\alpha/(2+2\alpha)}(\log n)^p
=
C B n^{-1/2}r^{\alpha/(2+2\alpha)}(\log n)^p .
\]
% lean: crossfit_localized_process_reduction
\end{proof}
