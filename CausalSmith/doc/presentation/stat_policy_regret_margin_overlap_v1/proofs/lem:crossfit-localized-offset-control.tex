\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:crossfit-localized-offset-control}]
Write \(m_k=|I_k|\) for the size of fold \(k\), \(w_k=m_k/n\) for its weight, and
\[
A_\alpha=\frac{1+\alpha}{2+\alpha}.
\]
The pooled cross-fit centered process is
\[
G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)
=
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\left\{g_{k(i),\pi}(O_i)-\int g_{k(i),\pi}(O)\,dP(O)\right\}.
\]
For every fold, use the totalized foldwise centered process
\[
G_k(\pi)=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle
\frac1{m_k}\sum_{i\in I_k}\left\{g_{k,\pi}(O_i)-\int g_{k,\pi}(O)\,dP(O)\right\}, & m_k>0,\\[1.2ex]
0, & m_k=0.
\end{cases}
\]
With this convention, empty folds are included in fold sums and have zero weight. Grouping the pooled sum by fold gives the exact decomposition
\[
G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)=\sum_{k=1}^K w_kG_k(\pi),
\qquad
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k=1,
\qquad
w_k\ge0,
\]
the weight identity because \(I_1,\ldots,I_K\) partitions \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\). % lean: pooledCrossfitProcess_eq_sum_fold % lean: sum_foldWeights_eq_one

Apply \cref{obj:ass:vc-localized-offset-envelope} in its uniform class-level form: it supplies constants \(C_0>0\) and \(p\ge0\), not depending on the law, and we set
\[
C=C_0K,
\]
which is positive because \cref{obj:ass:fixed-crossfit-fold-count} gives \(K\ge1\). % lean: crossfit_localized_offset_control
Also, \cref{obj:ass:margin} gives \(\alpha\ge0\), hence
\[
A_\alpha=\frac{1+\alpha}{2+\alpha}\le1 .
\]

\emph{Step 1: a pointwise foldwise bound on the offset supremum.}  Fix a sample and a policy \(\pi\in\Pi\), and write \(R=R_P(\pi)\).  By the triangle inequality and \(\sum_kw_k=1\),
\[
2\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)\right|-\frac R4
\le
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\left\{2|G_k(\pi)|-\frac R4\right\}
\le
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\left\{2|G_k(\pi)|-\frac R4\right\}_+ ,
\]
the second step because \(w_k\ge0\) and \(z\le\{z\}_+\).  The right-hand side is a nonnegative sum, so it also dominates \(0\), and therefore dominates the positive part on the left:
\[
\left\{2\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)\right|-\frac{R_P(\pi)}4\right\}_+
\le
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\left\{2|G_k(\pi)|-\frac{R_P(\pi)}4\right\}_+ .
\]
The regret is bounded below by a fixed constant on all of \(\Pi\): the setup identity \(\tau_P=\mu_1-\mu_0\) and \cref{obj:ass:bounded-outcome} give \(|\tau_P(x)|\le2\) for every \(x\), every \(\pi\in\Pi\) is measurable by \cref{obj:ass:policy-class}, and the oracle \(\pi^\star_P=\mathbf 1\{\tau_P\ge0\}\) is measurable because \(\tau_P\) is; hence
\[
\left|V_P(\pi)\right|
=
\left|\int \mathbf 1\{\pi(x)=1\}\tau_P(x)\,dP_X(x)\right|
\le 2
\qquad\text{for }\pi\in\Pi\text{ and for }\pi=\pi^\star_P,
\]
so \(R_P(\pi)=V_P(\pi^\star_P)-V_P(\pi)\ge-2-2=-4\). % lean: abs_lawWelfare_le_two % lean: lawRegret_lower_bound
Each foldwise offset term is therefore bounded above uniformly over \(\pi\in\Pi\): the envelope hypothesis gives \(|G_k(\pi)|\le2B\), with the totalized definition giving the same bound when \(m_k=0\), and \(R_P(\pi)\ge-4\). Thus each foldwise supremum
\[
\Omega_k=\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}\left\{2|G_k(\pi)|-\frac{R_P(\pi)}4\right\}_+
\]
is finite, and taking the supremum over \(\pi\in\Pi\) in the previous display yields the pointwise-in-sample bound
\[
\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}\left\{2\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)\right|-\frac{R_P(\pi)}4\right\}_+
\le
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\,\Omega_k .
\]
% lean: max_weighted_abs_sub_le_sum % lean: abs_foldCenteredProcess_le % lean: lawRegret_lower_bound % lean: pooledOffsetSup_pointwise_le_sum_fold

\emph{Step 2: integrate and reduce each nonempty fold to an i.i.d.\ sample of its own size.}  The stated regularity hypotheses give integrability of the pooled offset supremum and of the foldwise offset suprema on their fold product spaces.  The coordinate projection onto fold \(k\) is measure preserving, so the corresponding full-sample foldwise supremum is integrable.  Integrating the last display over the product law and exchanging the finite sum with the integral gives
\[
\mathbb E_P\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}\left\{2\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)\right|-\frac{R_P(\pi)}4\right\}_+
\le
\sum_{k=1}^K w_k\,\mathbb E_P\Omega_k .
\]
If \(m_k=0\), then \(w_k=0\) and that fold contributes nothing.  If \(m_k>0\), the same measure-preserving projection identifies \(\mathbb E_P\Omega_k\) with the offset-supremum expectation for an i.i.d.\ sample of size \(m_k\).  For such a fold, \(g_{k,\pi}\) is policy-compatible with envelope \(|g_{k,\pi}(O)|\le B\) and second moment
\[
\int g_{k,\pi}(O)^2\,dP(O)\le B^2P_X(D_\pi),
\]
so \cref{obj:ass:vc-localized-offset-envelope} applies at sample size \(m_k\) and gives
\[
\mathbb E_P\Omega_k
\le
C_0\left(\frac{B^2}{m_k}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log m_k)^p .
\]
% lean: measurePreserving_foldProjection % lean: integral_foldOffsetSup_eq_expected

\emph{Step 3: compare each weighted fold rate with the target \(n\)-rate.}  Put \(x=m_k/n\in(0,1]\).  Then \(B^2/m_k=(B^2/n)/x\), so
\[
w_k\left(\frac{B^2}{m_k}\right)^{A_\alpha}
=
x\cdot\frac{(B^2/n)^{A_\alpha}}{x^{A_\alpha}}
=
\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}x^{1-A_\alpha}
\le
\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha},
\]
because \(0<x\le1\) and \(1-A_\alpha\ge0\).  Moreover \(m_k\le n\) and \(p\ge0\) give
\[
(\log m_k)^p\le(\log n)^p .
\]
% lean: fold_weight_mul_offset_rpow_le % lean: log_nat_rpow_le
Combining the last three displays, every fold contributes at most
\[
w_k\,\mathbb E_P\Omega_k
\le
C_0\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p .
\]

\emph{Conclusion.}  Summing over the \(K\) folds,
\[
\mathbb E_P\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}
\left\{2\lvert G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)\rvert-\frac{R_P(\pi)}{4}\right\}_{+}
\le
C_0K\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p
=
C\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{(1+\alpha)/(2+\alpha)}(\log n)^p ,
\]
which is the asserted bound. % lean: crossfit_localized_offset_control
\end{proof}
