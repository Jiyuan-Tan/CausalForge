\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:crude-clipped-score-envelope}]
Fix \(q\in(0,1/2]\) and one foldwise nuisance triple, and write
\[
e_q=e_q\!\left(X;\widehat\eta^{(-k)}\right)
=\min\left\{1-q,\max\{q,\widehat e^{(-k)}(X)\}\right\}.
\]
Since \(q\le1/2\le 1-q\), the outer minimum and inner maximum give the two-sided clip
\[
q\le e_q\le 1-q,
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
q\le 1-e_q,
\]
so both denominators are at least \(q>0\) and
\[
\frac1{e_q}\le\frac1q,
\qquad
\frac1{1-e_q}\le\frac1q .
\]
The treatment indicators satisfy \(|A|\le1\) and \(|1-A|\le1\).  By \cref{obj:ass:bounded-outcome}, \(|Y|\le1\) almost surely, and by the assumed foldwise range of the cross-fitted outcome regressions, \(|\widehat\mu_0^{(-k)}(X)|\le1\) and \(|\widehat\mu_1^{(-k)}(X)|\le1\).  Therefore, by the triangle inequality,
\[
\left|\widehat\mu_1^{(-k)}(X)-\widehat\mu_0^{(-k)}(X)\right|\le2,\qquad
\left|Y-\widehat\mu_a^{(-k)}(X)\right|\le2,\quad a=0,1 .
\]
% lean: crude_clipped_score_envelope
Multiplying the indicator, inverse-denominator, and residual bounds gives, for the two inverse-propensity terms,
\[
\left|\frac{A}{e_q}\left\{Y-\widehat\mu_1^{(-k)}(X)\right\}\right|\le 1\cdot\frac1q\cdot 2=\frac{2}{q},
\qquad
\left|\frac{1-A}{1-e_q}\left\{Y-\widehat\mu_0^{(-k)}(X)\right\}\right|\le \frac{2}{q}.
\]
Applying the triangle inequality to the three summands of \cref{obj:def:clipped-aipw-score},
\[
\left|\Gamma_q\!\left(O;\widehat\eta^{(-k)}\right)\right|
\le
\left|\widehat\mu_1^{(-k)}(X)-\widehat\mu_0^{(-k)}(X)\right|
+\left|\frac{A}{e_q}\left\{Y-\widehat\mu_1^{(-k)}(X)\right\}\right|
+\left|\frac{1-A}{1-e_q}\left\{Y-\widehat\mu_0^{(-k)}(X)\right\}\right|
\le 2+\frac{2}{q}+\frac{2}{q}.
\]
Since \(q\le1/2<1\), we have \(2\le 2/q\), so the constant summand is absorbed and
\[
\left|\Gamma_q\!\left(O;\widehat\eta^{(-k)}\right)\right|\le\frac{6}{q}
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
As \(q>0\) gives \(6/q\le 36/q\), the choice \(C=36\) delivers the asserted absolute envelope
\[
\left|\Gamma_q\!\left(O;\widehat\eta^{(-k)}\right)\right|\le\frac{C}{q}
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
% lean: crude_clipped_score_envelope
Squaring the sharper bound \(|\Gamma_q|\le 6/q\), whose right-hand side is nonnegative, yields
\[
\Gamma_q\!\left(O;\widehat\eta^{(-k)}\right)^2\le \left(\frac{6}{q}\right)^2=\frac{36}{q^2}=\frac{C}{q^2}
\quad\text{almost surely},
\]
so the same constant \(C=36\) serves both conclusions. % lean: crude_clipped_score_envelope
\end{proof}
