\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:welfare-identity}]
Fix a well-formed observed law \(P\) satisfying \cref{obj:ass:bounded-outcome}, and fix \(\pi\in\Pi\).  By \cref{obj:ass:policy-class}, \(\pi\) is measurable.  Well-formedness gives that \(P_X\) is a probability measure, that \(\tau_P\) is measurable, and that
\(\tau_P(x)=\mu_1(x)-\mu_0(x)\) for every \(x\).  The regression part of \cref{obj:ass:bounded-outcome} gives
\(\mu_0(x),\mu_1(x)\in[-1,1]\) for every \(x\), and therefore
\[
|\tau_P(x)|
=
|\mu_1(x)-\mu_0(x)|
\le |\mu_1(x)|+|\mu_0(x)|
\le 2 .
\]
% lean: regret_eq_disagreement_integral

The set \(\{x:0\le\tau_P(x)\}\) is measurable because \(\tau_P\) is measurable, and \(\{x:\pi(x)=1\}\) is measurable because \(\pi\) is measurable.  Hence the functions
\[
x\mapsto
\begin{cases}
\tau_P(x),&0\le \tau_P(x),\\
0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\qquad
x\mapsto
\begin{cases}
\tau_P(x),&\pi(x)=1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\]
are measurable.  Since \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(|\tau_P|\le2\), both functions are integrable with respect to \(P_X\), so the difference of the two welfare integrals may be written as one \(P_X\)-integral.
% lean: regret_eq_disagreement_integral

Writing \cref{obj:def:welfare-regret} in its covariate-marginal form,
\[
V_P(\rho)=\int_{\mathcal X}\mathbf 1\{\rho(x)=1\}\tau_P(x)\,dP_X(x),
\]
with \(\pi^\star_P(x)=\mathbf 1\{\tau_P(x)\ge0\}\) and \(R_P(\pi)=V_P(\pi^\star_P)-V_P(\pi)\), the preceding integrability gives
\[
R_P(\pi)
=
\int_{\mathcal X}
\left[
\mathbf 1\{0\le\tau_P(x)\}\tau_P(x)
-
\mathbf 1\{\pi(x)=1\}\tau_P(x)
\right] \,dP_X(x).
\]
% lean: regret_eq_disagreement_integral

Fix \(x\).  If \(0\le\tau_P(x)\), then \(\pi^\star_P(x)=1\).  When \(\pi(x)=1\), the two terms in the bracket are both \(\tau_P(x)\), so the bracket and the disagreement indicator are both zero.  When \(\pi(x)\ne1\), the bracket is \(\tau_P(x)=|\tau_P(x)|\), and the disagreement indicator is one.  If \(\tau_P(x)<0\), then \(\pi^\star_P(x)=0\).  When \(\pi(x)=1\), the bracket is \(-\tau_P(x)=|\tau_P(x)|\), and the disagreement indicator is one.  When \(\pi(x)\ne1\), the two terms in the bracket are both zero, and the disagreement indicator is zero.  Therefore, for every \(x\),
\[
\mathbf 1\{0\le\tau_P(x)\}\tau_P(x)
-
\mathbf 1\{\pi(x)=1\}\tau_P(x)
=
|\tau_P(x)|\,\mathbf 1\{\pi(x)\ne\pi^\star_P(x)\}.
\]
% lean: regret_eq_disagreement_integral

Substituting this pointwise identity into the preceding integral yields
\[
R_P(\pi)
=
\int_{\mathcal X}
|\tau_P(x)|\,\mathbf 1\{\pi(x)\ne\pi^\star_P(x)\}\,dP_X(x).
\]
This is the \(|\tau_P|\)-weighted disagreement representation of regret with respect to the covariate marginal.
% lean: regret_eq_disagreement_integral
\end{proof}
