\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:crude-localized-master-bound}]
\emph{Constants.}  \Cref{obj:lem:crossfit-localized-offset-control} supplies constants \(C^0_{\mathrm{off}}>0\) and \(p\ge0\); put
\[
C_{\mathrm{off}}=\max\{C^0_{\mathrm{off}},1\}\ \ (\ge1),
\qquad
C_d=4\left\{1+\sqrt{\max\{C_o,1\}}\right\},
\]
where \(C_d\) is the constant appearing in the \(\gamma>0\) branch of \cref{obj:lem:localized-clipped-drift-bound}.  Let \(q_0>0\) be the eventual fixed clip level of the assumed clipping schedule when \(\gamma=0\), and put \(q_0=1\) when \(\gamma>0\), so that the constants below are defined in both branches; set \(C_q=\max\{1,2/q_0\}\), which for \(\gamma=0\) is the constant appearing in the \(\gamma=0\) branch of \cref{obj:lem:localized-clipped-drift-bound}.  Set
\[
K_{\mathrm{pos}}=(36^2)^{A_\alpha},
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{zero}}=\left(\left(\frac{36}{q_0}\right)^2\right)^{A_\alpha},
\qquad
C=100+20\left(C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{pos}}+C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{zero}}+C_d+C_d^2+C_q\right),
\]
so that \(C>0\).  The logarithmic power in the conclusion is this same \(p\). % lean: crude_localized_master_bound

\emph{Large-\(n\) restriction.}  Work on the eventual index set on which \(n\ge1\), \(\log n\ge1\), \(0<q_n\le1/2\), \(r_{\mu,n}\ge0\), \(r_{e,n}\ge0\), the foldwise nuisance errors lie in \(L^2(P_X)\), and, when \(\gamma=0\), \(q_n=q_0\).  Fix such an \(n\) and a law \(P\) satisfying the stated law-class, oracle, sampling, nuisance-rate, and bounded-nuisance conditions.  The measurability, integrability, and boundedness-above properties of the selected regret and of the offset suprema follow from the bounded cross-fitted nuisances, the measurability of the foldwise nuisance estimators, the positive clip, and the countable pointwise-dense skeleton of \cref{obj:ass:policy-class}, which reduces each supremum over \(\Pi\) to a supremum over the countable \(\Pi_0\); these integrability side conditions are therefore established rather than assumed.  The nuisance measurability and the \(L^2(P_X)\) memberships of the foldwise nuisance errors are not established here: they are the hypotheses collected in the statement's measurability and \(L^2\) regularity condition, and the \(L^2\) memberships enter only through the Cauchy--Schwarz step of the drift bound invoked below. % lean: bochner_discharge

\emph{Objects.}  Write \(q=q_n\) and set the envelope
\[
B=\frac{36}{q},
\]
so \(B\ge72\ge1\) because \(q\le1/2\).  For each fold \(k\), let
\[
g_{k,\pi}(O)=\left\{\mathbf 1\{\pi(X)=1\}-\mathbf 1\{\pi^\star_P(X)=1\}\right\}\Gamma_q\!\left(O;\widehat\eta^{(-k)}\right),
\qquad
\Delta_k(\pi)=\int_{\mathcal X}\left\{\mathbf 1\{\pi(x)=1\}-\mathbf 1\{\pi^\star_P(x)=1\}\right\}b_q^{(k)}(x)\,dP_X(x),
\]
where \(b_q^{(k)}\) is the clipped-score drift of \cref{obj:lem:clip-bias} formed from the fold-\(k\) nuisance triple \(\widehat\eta^{(-k)}\).  Let \(w_k=|I_k|/n\), so \(w_k\ge0\) and \(\sum_kw_k=1\), and let
\[
G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\left\{g_{k(i),\pi}(O_i)-\int g_{k(i),\pi}(O)\,dP(O)\right\}
\]
be the pooled cross-fit centered process built from these increments.

\emph{Step 1: the offset bound at envelope \(B\).}  By \cref{obj:lem:crude-clipped-score-envelope}, \(|\Gamma_q(O;\widehat\eta^{(-k)})|\le 36/q=B\) almost surely, so truncating the score at level \(B\) changes nothing almost surely, and the pooled and foldwise offset suprema built from \(g\) and from its \(B\)-truncation coincide almost surely and therefore have equal expectations. % lean: pooledOffsetSup_trunc_eq_original_ae_36 % lean: expectedPooledOffsetSup_trunc_eq_original_ae_36 % lean: foldOffsetSubSup_trunc_eq_original_ae_36
The truncated increments are policy-compatible (each depends on \(\pi\) only through \(\pi(X)\)), measurable, bounded by \(B\), and, since they vanish off \(D_\pi\),
\[
\int g_{k,\pi}(O)^2\,dP(O)\le B^2P_X(D_\pi),
\qquad \pi\in\Pi .
\]
% lean: clippedPolicyIncrementTrunc_compatible % lean: clippedPolicyIncrementTrunc_bound % lean: clippedPolicyIncrementTrunc_second_moment
Hence \cref{obj:lem:crossfit-localized-offset-control} applies at sample size \(n\) with this envelope and, transporting back to the untruncated increments, gives
\[
\mathbb E_P\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}\left\{2|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)|-\frac{R_P(\pi)}4\right\}_+
\le
C_{\mathrm{off}}\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p .
\]
Moreover, since \(0\le A_\alpha\le1\), \(1/n\le1\), \(B\ge1\), \(\log n\ge1\), and \(C_{\mathrm{off}}\ge1\),
\[
\frac1n\le\left(\frac1n\right)^{A_\alpha}\le\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}
\le C_{\mathrm{off}}\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p .
\]
% lean: crude_self_large

\emph{Step 2 (\(\gamma>0\)): the samplewise selection inequality.}  Assume \(\gamma>0\), \(0<u_n\le u_0\), and \(q_n\le c_ou_n^\gamma\); write \(u=u_n\) and \(R=R_P(\widehat\pi_n)\).  For every \(\pi\in\Pi\), applying \cref{obj:lem:clip-bias} foldwise with the bounded measurable test function \(\varphi(x)=\mathbf 1\{\pi(x)=1\}-\mathbf 1\{\pi^\star_P(x)=1\}\), and using the welfare identity of \cref{obj:thm:welfare-identity} in the form \(\int\varphi\,\tau_P\,dP_X=-R_P(\pi)\), gives the exact empirical-welfare identity
\[
\widehat V_{n,q}(\pi)-\widehat V_{n,q}(\pi^\star_P)
=
G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\pi)-R_P(\pi)+\sum_{k=1}^Kw_k\Delta_k(\pi).
\]
By \cref{obj:ass:optimal-in-class} the oracle \(\pi^\star_P\) lies in \(\Pi\), so \cref{obj:lem:feasible-erm-basic-inequality} applies with comparator \(\pi^b=\pi^\star_P\) and gives \(\widehat V_{n,q}(\pi^\star_P)-\widehat V_{n,q}(\widehat\pi_n)\le1/n\).  Evaluating the identity at \(\pi=\widehat\pi_n\) and bounding each term by its absolute value yields the samplewise selection bound
\[
R
\le
\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\widehat\pi_n)\right|
+\left|\sum_{k=1}^Kw_k\Delta_k(\widehat\pi_n)\right|
+\frac1n .
\]
% lean: feasible_erm_welfare_bridge

The weighted drift sum is controlled foldwise.  Each fold's nuisance triple satisfies the same \(L^2(P_X)\) error bounds \(r_{\mu,n},r_{e,n}\), so \cref{obj:lem:localized-clipped-drift-bound} applies to each \(\Delta_k(\widehat\pi_n)\) with the localization window \(u\) and the admissible clip \(q\le c_ou^\gamma\); since \(w_k\ge0\) and \(\sum_kw_k=1\), averaging preserves the bound:
\[
\left|\sum_{k=1}^Kw_k\Delta_k(\widehat\pi_n)\right|
\le
\sum_{k=1}^Kw_k\left|\Delta_k(\widehat\pi_n)\right|
\le
C_d\left\{
\frac{r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}}q
+r_{\mu,n}u^{\alpha/2}q^{1/(2\gamma)}
+r_{\mu,n}\left(\frac{R}u\right)^{1/2}
\right\}.
\]
% lean: weighted_drift_sum_bound_pos

The last summand still involves \(R\), and is absorbed by Young's inequality \(2xy\le x^2+y^2\) applied with \(x=\sqrt R\) and \(y=C_dr_{\mu,n}/\sqrt u\):
\[
C_d r_{\mu,n}\left(\frac{R}u\right)^{1/2}
\le \frac{R}2+\frac{C_d^2r_{\mu,n}^2}{2u}.
\]
% lean: young_sqrt_absorb
Substituting the last two displays into the selection bound and moving \(R/2\) to the left,
\[
\frac R2
\le
\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\widehat\pi_n)\right|
+C_d\left\{\frac{r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}}q+r_{\mu,n}u^{\alpha/2}q^{1/(2\gamma)}\right\}
+\frac{C_d^2r_{\mu,n}^2}{2u}
+\frac1n,
\]
that is,
\[
R_P(\widehat\pi_n)\le
2\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\widehat\pi_n)\right|+\delta,
\qquad
\delta
=
2\left[
C_d\left\{\frac{r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}}q
+r_{\mu,n}u^{\alpha/2}q^{1/(2\gamma)}\right\}
+\frac{C_d^2r_{\mu,n}^2}{2u}
+\frac1n
\right]\ \ (\ge0).
\]

\emph{Step 3 (\(\gamma>0\)): self-bounding and algebra.}  The samplewise inequality of Step 2 is exactly the input of \cref{obj:lem:localized-vc-self-bound}, applied with envelope \(B\ge1\), offset bound \(C_{\mathrm{off}}(B^2/n)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p\) from Step 1, and slack \(\delta\).  It gives
\[
\mathbb E_P R_P(\widehat\pi_n)
\le
\frac83\left\{C_{\mathrm{off}}\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p+\delta\right\}.
\]
Since \(B=36/q\), the envelope factor is exactly the clipped empirical-process term:
\[
\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}
=
\left(\frac{36^2}{nq^2}\right)^{A_\alpha}
=
K_{\mathrm{pos}}\,(nq^2)^{-A_\alpha}.
\]
% lean: crude_trunc_offset_rpow_eq
Now abbreviate the five nonnegative target terms
\[
T_1=n^{-r_\star(\alpha,\gamma)},\quad
T_2=(nq^2)^{-A_\alpha},\quad
T_3=\frac{r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}}q,\quad
T_4=r_{\mu,n}u^{\alpha/2}q^{1/(2\gamma)},\quad
T_5=\frac{r_{\mu,n}^2}{u},
\]
and write \(L=(\log n)^p\ge1\).  Then \(r_{\mu,n}^2/(2u)=T_5/2\), and the last display of Step 1 reads \(1/n\le C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{pos}}T_2L\).  Expanding \(\delta\), the previous bound becomes
\[
\mathbb E_PR_P(\widehat\pi_n)
\le
\frac83\left\{C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{pos}}T_2L
+2\left(C_d(T_3+T_4)+C_d^2\frac{T_5}{2}+\frac1n\right)\right\}
\le
8\,C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{pos}}T_2L+\frac{16}3C_dT_3+\frac{16}3C_dT_4+\frac83C_d^2T_5 .
\]
Each coefficient \(8C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{pos}}\), \(\tfrac{16}3C_d\), \(\tfrac83C_d^2\) is at most \(C\) by the definition of \(C\); using \(L\ge1\) to insert the factor \(L\) into the last three terms, and adding the nonnegative term \(CT_1L\), gives
\[
\mathbb E_P R_P(\widehat\pi_n)
\le
C\left(T_1+T_2+T_3+T_4+T_5\right)L
=
C\left\{
n^{-r_\star(\alpha,\gamma)}
+(nq_n^2)^{-A_\alpha}
+\frac{r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}}{q_n}
+r_{\mu,n}u_n^{\alpha/2}q_n^{1/(2\gamma)}
+\frac{r_{\mu,n}^2}{u_n}
\right\}(\log n)^p .
\]
This proves the positive-\(\gamma\) branch. % lean: crude_master_pos_algebra

\emph{Step 4 (\(\gamma=0\)).}  Now assume \(\gamma=0\) and \(q_n\le\underline p/2\); on the eventual set considered, \(q=q_n=q_0\) is the fixed clip, so \(B=36/q_0\) does not depend on \(n\).  The empirical-welfare identity and \cref{obj:lem:feasible-erm-basic-inequality} give the same samplewise selection bound as in Step 2, and the strict-overlap branch of \cref{obj:lem:localized-clipped-drift-bound}, averaged over the fold weights exactly as before, gives
\[
\left|\sum_{k=1}^Kw_k\Delta_k(\widehat\pi_n)\right|
\le C_q\,r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}.
\]
% lean: weighted_drift_sum_bound_zero
Hence, using \(|G_{\mathrm{cf}}|\le2|G_{\mathrm{cf}}|\),
\[
R_P(\widehat\pi_n)\le 2\left|G_{\mathrm{cf}}(\widehat\pi_n)\right|+\delta,
\qquad
\delta=2\left(C_q r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}+\frac1n\right),
\]
and \cref{obj:lem:localized-vc-self-bound} gives
\[
\mathbb E_PR_P(\widehat\pi_n)
\le
\frac83\left\{C_{\mathrm{off}}\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p+\delta\right\}.
\]
With the fixed clip, the envelope factor is a constant times the parametric-envelope rate,
\[
\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}
=
\left(\left(\frac{36}{q_0}\right)^2\right)^{A_\alpha}n^{-A_\alpha}
=
K_{\mathrm{zero}}\,n^{-A_\alpha}.
\]
% lean: crude_trunc_offset_fixed_rpow_eq
Writing \(Z_1=n^{-A_\alpha}\ge0\) and \(Z_2=r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}\ge0\), Step 1 gives \(1/n\le C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{zero}}Z_1L\), so
\[
\mathbb E_PR_P(\widehat\pi_n)
\le
\frac83\left\{C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{zero}}Z_1L+2\left(C_qZ_2+\frac1n\right)\right\}
\le
8\,C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{zero}}Z_1L+\frac{16}3C_qZ_2 .
\]
Since \(8C_{\mathrm{off}}K_{\mathrm{zero}}\le C\) and \(\tfrac{16}3C_q\le C\), and \(L\ge1\),
\[
\mathbb E_P R_P(\widehat\pi_n)
\le
C\left\{
n^{-A_\alpha}+r_{\mu,n}r_{e,n}
\right\}(\log n)^p ,
\]
which is the strict-overlap branch. % lean: crude_master_zero_algebra % lean: crude_localized_master_bound
\end{proof}
