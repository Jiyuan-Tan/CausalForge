\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:localized-vc-self-bound}]
Write
\[
\rho=C\rho_n=C\left(\frac{B^2}{n}\right)^{A_\alpha}(\log n)^p
\]
for the offset scale supplied to the lemma, so that the hypothesis on the centered \(\Pi\)-indexed process \(z_\pi\) reads
\[
\mathbb E_P\,\Omega_{\mathrm{off}}\le\rho,
\qquad
\Omega_{\mathrm{off}}=
\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}
\left\{2|z_\pi|-\frac{R_P(\pi)}4\right\}_{+},
\]
and the large-\(n\) hypothesis reads \(1/n\le\rho\).  In particular \(\rho>0\).

\emph{Step 1: a samplewise bound.}  Fix a realization of the sample.  Since \(\widetilde\pi\in\Pi\), the value of the offset functional at \(\pi=\widetilde\pi\) is one of the terms in the supremum defining \(\Omega_{\mathrm{off}}\), so
\[
\left\{2|z_{\widetilde\pi}|-\frac{R_P(\widetilde\pi)}4\right\}_{+}\le\Omega_{\mathrm{off}} .
\]
Put \(R=R_P(\widetilde\pi)\), \(z=z_{\widetilde\pi}\), and \(\Delta=2|z|-R/4\), so that \(2|z|=\Delta+R/4\).  Substituting this into the assumed selection inequality \(R\le2|z|+\delta_n\) gives
\[
R\le \Delta+\frac R4+\delta_n,
\qquad\text{that is,}\qquad
\frac34R\le \Delta+\delta_n .
\]
If \(\Delta\ge0\), then \(\{\Delta\}_+=\Delta\) and multiplying by \(4/3\) gives \(R\le \tfrac43\Delta+\tfrac43\delta_n=\tfrac43\{\Delta\}_++\tfrac43\delta_n\).  If \(\Delta<0\), then \(\{\Delta\}_+=0\) and \(\tfrac34R\le \Delta+\delta_n\le\delta_n\), so \(R\le\tfrac43\delta_n=\tfrac43\{\Delta\}_++\tfrac43\delta_n\).  In both cases, using the first display of this step,
\[
R_P(\widetilde\pi)
\le
\frac43\,\{\Delta\}_+
+\frac43\,\delta_n
\le
\frac43\,\Omega_{\mathrm{off}}+\frac43\,\delta_n .
\]
% lean: localized_vc_self_bound

\emph{Step 2: integrate.}  Both sides are integrable by the stated regularity, so integrating the samplewise bound and using \(\mathbb E_P\Omega_{\mathrm{off}}\le\rho\) gives
\[
\mathbb E_P R_P(\widetilde\pi)
\le
\frac43\rho+\frac43\delta_n .
\]
Since \(\rho+\delta_n\ge0\), we may enlarge the factor \(4/3\) to \(8/3\), obtaining
\[
\mathbb E_P R_P(\widetilde\pi)
\le
\frac83(\rho+\delta_n)
=
\frac83\left(C\rho_n+\delta_n\right),
\]
which is the asserted bound. % lean: localized_vc_self_bound

\emph{Step 3: absorbing the slack.}  If in addition \(\delta_n\le1/n\), then the assumed large-\(n\) comparison \(1/n\le C\rho_n=\rho\) gives \(\delta_n\le\rho\), and the bound of Step 2 becomes
\[
\mathbb E_P R_P(\widetilde\pi)
\le
\frac43\rho+\frac43\delta_n
\le
\frac43\rho+\frac43\rho
=
\frac83\rho
=
\frac83\,C\,\rho_n ,
\]
so the \(\delta_n\) term is absorbed into the localized rate. % lean: localized_vc_self_bound
\end{proof}
