\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:certified-beta-frontier-bracket}]
\emph{Step 1 (the lower floor).} The assumed positivity \(\alpha>0\), \(\beta>0\), \(s>0\), the
regime constants \(M>0\), \(c_0>0\), \(t_0\in(0,1)\), \(\varepsilon_0\in(0,1/2)\) with
\([t_0-\varepsilon_0,t_0+\varepsilon_0]\subset(0,1)\), and the strict-slack baseline for
\((d,\beta,s,M,c_0,\varepsilon_0,t_0)\) are precisely the hypotheses of
\cref{obj:thm:sharp-pointwise-lower-bound}. That theorem therefore supplies a constant \(c>0\) such
that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
c\,n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le
R_n\!\left(\mathcal P_{\alpha,\beta,s}(M,c_0,\varepsilon_0,t_0),t_0\right).
\]
Fix this \(c\); it is the constant asserted in the statement, and no further constant is introduced
below. % lean: sharp_pointwise_lower_bound

\emph{Step 2 (the two regime identities).} The remaining two clauses concern only the benchmark
sequence \(\rho_n\) of \cref{obj:def:published-hoif-rate}; they are asserted for every \(n\ge1\),
not merely for large \(n\), and they are conditional implications, so no case split on the position
of \(d\) relative to \(4s\) is needed: each implication is proved on its own hypothesis. Fix
\(n\ge1\).

If \(d\le 4s\), then \(\alpha>0\), \(s>0\), \(d\le 4s\) and \(n\ge1\) are exactly the hypotheses of
\cref{obj:lem:rho-oracle-regime-algebra}, which gives
\[
\rho_n=n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)} .
\]
% lean: rho_oracle_regime_algebra

If instead \(4s<d\), then \(\alpha>0\), \(s>0\), \(4s<d\) and \(n\ge1\) are exactly the hypotheses of
\cref{obj:lem:rho-deficient-regime-algebra}, which gives both
\[
\rho_n=n^{-2/(1+d/(4s)+1/\alpha)}
\]
and
\[
\frac{2}{1+d/(4s)+1/\alpha}
<
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1}.
\]
% lean: rho_deficient_regime_algebra

\emph{Step 3 (assembly).} The constant \(c>0\) of Step 1, the eventual lower bound of Step 1, and
the two implications of Step 2, valid for every \(n\ge1\), are together exactly the three clauses of
the stated bracket.
% lean: certified_beta_frontier_bracket
\end{proof}
