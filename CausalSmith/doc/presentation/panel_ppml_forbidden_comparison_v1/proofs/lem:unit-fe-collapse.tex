\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:unit-fe-collapse}]
Throughout, a collapsed parameter \(\theta\in\mathbb R^{|\mathcal C|+T}\) is written through the levels it induces,
\[
c_g(\theta)=
\begin{cases}
\theta_{\text{int}}, & g=\infty,\\
\theta_{\text{int}}+\theta_{\text{coh},g}, & g\neq\infty,
\end{cases}
\qquad
w_t(\theta)=
\begin{cases}
0, & t=1,\\
\theta_{\text{time},t}, & t\neq1,
\end{cases}
\qquad
\beta(\theta)=\theta_{\text{trt}},
\]
so that for every supported cell \((g,t)\), \(g\in\mathcal C\),
\[
r_{gt}'\theta=c_g(\theta)+w_t(\theta)+\beta(\theta)D_{gt}.
\]
A unit parameter \(\theta_N\in\mathbb R^{N+T}\) is written the same way,
\[
\alpha_i(\theta_N)=
\begin{cases}
\theta_{N,\text{int}}, & i=1,\\
\theta_{N,\text{int}}+\theta_{N,\text{unit},i}, & i\neq1,
\end{cases}
\qquad
w_t(\theta_N)=
\begin{cases}
0, & t=1,\\
\theta_{N,\text{time},t}, & t\neq1,
\end{cases}
\qquad
\beta(\theta_N)=\theta_{N,\text{trt}},
\]
so that
\[
v_{itN}'\theta_N=\alpha_i(\theta_N)+w_t(\theta_N)+\beta(\theta_N)D_{it},
\qquad D_{it}=D_{G_it}.
\]
Both identities are the statement that the intercept, dummy, and treatment coordinates of the regressor pick out exactly these terms.
% lean: unitIndex_eq_levels, collapsedIndex_eq_levels

Write the finite-array collapsed primitives as
\[
q_{gtN}=\frac{n_{gN}}{NT},
\qquad
m_{gtN}(\delta)=\bar b_{gN}\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{gt}\delta_{gt}),
\]
\[
L_{Nc}(\theta;\delta)
=
\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T q_{gtN}\{m_{gtN}(\delta)\,r_{gt}'\theta-\exp(r_{gt}'\theta)\},
\]
and write the unit-time observed mean entering the criterion \(L_N(\theta_N;\delta)\) of \cref{obj:def:unit-fe-population-projection} as
\[
m_{itN}(\delta)=b_{iN}\exp(\gamma_{t0}+D_{it}\delta_{G_it}),
\]
which is the form generated by the untreated mean of \cref{obj:ass:unit-untreated-exponential-mean} together with the proportional effects of \cref{obj:ass:proportional-effects}.

\textit{Step 0: standing positivity.} The horizon assumption \(T\ge4\) gives \(T>0\), and \(\infty\in\mathcal C\) makes \(\mathcal C\) nonempty, so at least one supported cell exists. Fix \(N\) with \(|\mathcal C|\le N\). \Cref{obj:ass:cohort-share-limit} then supplies \(N>0\) and \(n_{gN}>0\) for every \(g\in\mathcal C\); every unit label lies in \(\mathcal C\) by the array-support hypothesis. Consequently, for every \(g\in\mathcal C\) and every \(t\),
\[
q_{gtN}=\frac{n_{gN}}{NT}>0,
\qquad
\bar b_{gN}=n_{gN}^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_{gN}}b_{iN}>0,
\qquad
m_{gtN}(\delta)>0,
\qquad
m_{itN}(\delta)>0 ,
\]
the last two because they are products of positive baselines and exponentials.
% lean: unit_fe_collapse, withinCohortBaseline_pos, finiteObservedCohortMean_pos

\textit{Step 1: the collapsed designs are injective, and \(L_{Nc}\) has a unique maximizer.} If \(r_{gt}'\theta_1=r_{gt}'\theta_2\) at every supported cell, then \(a=\theta_1-\theta_2\) has \(r_{gt}'a=0\) at every supported cell, so \(\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_t q_{gt}(r_{gt}'a)^2=0\), and \cref{obj:ass:collapsed-design-rank} forces \(a=0\). Thus \(\theta\mapsto(r_{gt}'\theta)_{(g,t)}\) is injective.
% lean: collapsedDesignMap_injective
With positive masses \(q_{gtN}\), positive means \(m_{gtN}(\delta)\), and this injective design, the finite collapsed criterion is a finite positive-mass, positive-mean Poisson criterion, so it has exactly one global maximizer \(\theta_N^c\): each cell contribution \(\eta\mapsto m\eta-\exp(\eta)\) is strictly concave and satisfies \(m\eta-\exp(\eta)\le (m+1)^2/2-\min(m,1)|\eta|\), which by injectivity makes \(L_{Nc}(\cdot;\delta)\) continuous, coercive, and strictly concave.
% lean: finiteCollapsedCriterion_exists_unique_max, Causalean.Stat.finitePoissonObjective_exists_unique_max
Because \(\theta_N^c\) maximizes and the criterion is differentiable, every collapsed directional score vanishes: for every \(a\in\mathbb R^{|\mathcal C|+T}\),
\[
\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T
q_{gtN}\,(r_{gt}'a)\{m_{gtN}(\delta)-\exp(r_{gt}'\theta_N^c)\}=0 .
\]
% lean: Causalean.Stat.finitePoissonObjective_score

\textit{Step 2: lifting the collapsed maximizer to unit fixed effects.} Define the unit parameter \(\theta_N^{\text{lift}}\) by
\[
\alpha_i(\theta_N^{\text{lift}})
=
\log\frac{b_{iN}}{\bar b_{G_iN}}+c_{G_i}(\theta_N^c),
\qquad
w_t(\theta_N^{\text{lift}})=w_t(\theta_N^c),
\qquad
\beta(\theta_N^{\text{lift}})=\beta(\theta_N^c).
\]
(Any prescribed levels are realized by a unique choice of intercept, dummy, and treatment coordinates.) By the two level identities,
\[
v_{itN}'\theta_N^{\text{lift}}
=
\log\frac{b_{iN}}{\bar b_{G_iN}}+r_{G_it}'\theta_N^c .
\]
% lean: liftCollapsedParameter, unitIndex_liftCollapsedParameter
Exponentiating and using \(m_{itN}(\delta)=(b_{iN}/\bar b_{G_iN})\,m_{G_it,N}(\delta)\), which holds because \(m_{itN}(\delta)=b_{iN}\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{G_it}\delta_{G_it})\) and \(m_{G_it,N}(\delta)=\bar b_{G_iN}\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{G_it}\delta_{G_it})\), each unit residual is the corresponding cell residual scaled by the baseline ratio:
\[
m_{itN}(\delta)-\exp(v_{itN}'\theta_N^{\text{lift}})
=
\frac{b_{iN}}{\bar b_{G_iN}}
\left\{m_{G_it,N}(\delta)-\exp(r_{G_it}'\theta_N^c)\right\}.
\]
% lean: unitResidual_liftCollapsedParameter

\textit{Step 3: unit scores at the lift are collapsed scores.} For every \(g\in\mathcal C\), the definition of \(\bar b_{gN}\) gives
\[
\sum_{i\in I_{gN}}\frac{b_{iN}}{\bar b_{gN}}=n_{gN}.
\]
% lean: sum_baselineRatio_within_cohort
Given a unit direction \(d\in\mathbb R^{N+T}\), define the collapsed direction \(d^{c}\in\mathbb R^{|\mathcal C|+T}\) by its levels
\[
c_g(d^{c})=n_{gN}^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_{gN}}\frac{b_{iN}}{\bar b_{gN}}\,\alpha_i(d),
\qquad
w_t(d^{c})=w_t(d),
\qquad
\beta(d^{c})=\beta(d).
\]
% lean: aggregateUnitDirection, collapsedIndex_aggregateUnitDirection
Then
\[
(NT)^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N\sum_{t=1}^T
(v_{itN}'d)\left\{m_{itN}(\delta)-\exp(v_{itN}'\theta_N^{\text{lift}})\right\}
=
\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T
q_{gtN}\,(r_{gt}'d^{c})\left\{m_{gtN}(\delta)-\exp(r_{gt}'\theta_N^c)\right\}.
\]
Indeed, substituting the residual identity of Step 2 and grouping units by cohort (legitimate because every \(G_i\) lies in \(\mathcal C\)), the inner sum over \(i\in I_{gN}\) at fixed \((g,t)\) is
\[
(NT)^{-1}\left\{m_{gtN}(\delta)-\exp(r_{gt}'\theta_N^c)\right\}
\sum_{i\in I_{gN}}\frac{b_{iN}}{\bar b_{gN}}
\left\{\alpha_i(d)+w_t(d)+\beta(d)D_{gt}\right\},
\]
and the displayed baseline-ratio identity turns that bracketed sum into \(n_{gN}\{c_g(d^{c})+w_t(d^{c})+\beta(d^{c})D_{gt}\}=n_{gN}\,r_{gt}'d^{c}\), while \(n_{gN}/(NT)=q_{gtN}\).
% lean: unitScore_eq_collapsedScore, sum_baselineRatio_fiberwise, sum_units_by_supported_cohort
Applying the vanishing collapsed score of Step 1 to \(a=d^{c}\), we conclude that every unit directional score at \(\theta_N^{\text{lift}}\) vanishes:
\[
(NT)^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N\sum_{t=1}^T
(v_{itN}'d)\left\{m_{itN}(\delta)-\exp(v_{itN}'\theta_N^{\text{lift}})\right\}=0
\qquad\text{for every }d .
\]

\textit{Step 4: the lift maximizes the unit criterion.} For a Poisson criterion with nonnegative weights, vanishing of all directional scores is sufficient for global maximality:
\[
L_N(\theta_N;\delta)\le L_N(\theta_N^{\text{lift}};\delta)
\qquad\text{for every }\theta_N\in\mathbb R^{N+T},
\]
because the tangent-line bound \(\exp(\eta')\ge\exp(\eta)\{1+(\eta'-\eta)\}\) makes each cell increment at most its linearization, and the sum of the linearizations is the score in the direction \(\theta_N-\theta_N^{\text{lift}}\), which is zero by Step 3.
% lean: Causalean.Stat.finitePoissonObjective_isMax_of_score

\textit{Step 5: the unit design is injective.} Suppose \(v_{itN}'d=0\) for every unit \(i\) and every period \(t\). For each \(g\in\mathcal C\) choose a representative unit \(j_g\in I_{gN}\); this is possible because \(n_{gN}>0\). Define a collapsed parameter \(a\) by \(c_g(a)=\alpha_{j_g}(d)\), \(w_t(a)=w_t(d)\), \(\beta(a)=\beta(d)\). Then for every supported cell,
\[
r_{gt}'a
=
\alpha_{j_g}(d)+w_t(d)+\beta(d)D_{gt}
=
v_{j_gtN}'d
=
0,
\]
using \(G_{j_g}=g\). Hence \(\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_t q_{gt}(r_{gt}'a)^2=0\), so \cref{obj:ass:collapsed-design-rank} gives \(a=0\); in particular \(\beta(d)=0\) and \(w_t(d)=0\) for every \(t\). Evaluating \(0=v_{i1N}'d=\alpha_i(d)+w_1(d)+\beta(d)D_{i1}\) in the first period and substituting \(w_1(d)=0\) and \(\beta(d)=0\) gives \(\alpha_i(d)=0\) for every unit \(i\). All levels of \(d\) vanish, so \(d=0\), and the unit design \(\theta_N\mapsto(v_{itN}'\theta_N)_{i,t}\) is injective.
% lean: unitDesignMap_injective

\textit{Step 6: unique unit maximizer and equality of treatment coordinates.} The unit criterion \(L_N(\cdot;\delta)\) is a finite Poisson criterion with the constant positive weights \((NT)^{-1}\), positive means \(m_{itN}(\delta)\), and, by Step 5, an injective design; exactly as in Step 1 it therefore has a unique global maximizer. By Step 4 that maximizer is \(\theta_N^{\text{lift}}\). Hence \(L_N(\cdot;\delta)\) and \(L_{Nc}(\cdot;\delta)\) each have a unique global maximizer, and
\[
\beta_N^\star(\delta)=\beta(\theta_N^{\text{lift}})=\beta(\theta_N^c),
\]
i.e. \(\beta_N^\star(\delta)\) is the treatment coordinate of the unique maximizer of \(L_{Nc}(\theta;\delta)\).
% lean: finite_unit_and_collapsed_unique_beta, Causalean.Stat.uniqueGlobalMax_maximizerOrZero

\textit{Step 7: convergence to the limiting collapsed projection.} Fix a supported cell \((g,t)\). By \cref{obj:ass:cohort-share-limit},
\[
q_{gtN}=\frac{n_{gN}/N}{T}\longrightarrow \frac{\pi_g}{T}=q_{gt},
\]
and by \cref{obj:ass:within-cohort-baseline-limit}, since \(\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{gt}\delta_{gt})\) does not depend on \(N\),
\[
m_{gtN}(\delta)=\bar b_{gN}\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{gt}\delta_{gt})
\longrightarrow
\bar b_g\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{gt}\delta_{gt})=m_{gt}(\delta).
\]
The limiting masses \(q_{gt}\) and means \(m_{gt}(\delta)\) are strictly positive, the design is injective by Step 1, the points \(\theta_N^c\) are global maximizers of \(L_{Nc}(\cdot;\delta)\) for all \(N\ge|\mathcal C|\), and by \cref{obj:lem:pseudo-true-ppml-projection} the limiting criterion \(L(\cdot;\delta)\) has the unique global maximizer \(\theta^\star(\delta)\). For finitely many cells, convergence of the masses and means makes \(L_{Nc}(\cdot;\delta)\to L(\cdot;\delta)\) uniformly on compacta and traps the maximizers \(\theta_N^c\) eventually in one compact set (the common coercivity bound of Step 1 holds with constants converging to those of the limit), so
\[
\theta_N^c\longrightarrow\theta^\star(\delta).
\]
% lean: selectedFiniteCollapsed_tendsto, Causalean.Stat.finitePoissonObjective_argmax_tendsto
Taking the treatment coordinate, which is a continuous function of the parameter, gives \(\beta(\theta_N^c)\to\beta^\star(\delta)\); since \(\beta_N^\star(\delta)=\beta(\theta_N^c)\) for every \(N\ge|\mathcal C|\) by Step 6,
\[
\beta_N^\star(\delta)\longrightarrow\beta^\star(\delta).
\]

\textit{Step 8: invariance to within-cohort baseline rearrangement.} Fix \(N\ge|\mathcal C|\) and a positive baseline array \(b'_{iN}\) with \(\bar b'_{gN}=\bar b_{gN}\) for every \(g\in\mathcal C\). The finite collapsed observed mean depends on the baselines only through the within-cohort average,
\[
m_{gtN}(\delta;b')=\bar b'_{gN}\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{gt}\delta_{gt})
=\bar b_{gN}\exp(\gamma_{t0})\exp(D_{gt}\delta_{gt})=m_{gtN}(\delta;b),
\]
and the masses \(q_{gtN}\) do not involve the baselines at all. Hence \(L_{Nc}(\cdot;\delta;b')=L_{Nc}(\cdot;\delta;b)\) as functions of \(\theta\), so their unique maximizers coincide. Applying Step 6 once with \(b'\) and once with \(b\),
\[
\beta_N^\star(\delta;b')=\beta(\theta_N^c)=\beta_N^\star(\delta;b).
\]
% lean: unit_fe_collapse
\end{proof}
