\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:sharp-ppml-forbidden-sign}]
Write \(W_{gt}=\widetilde W_{gt}(\delta)\), \(\mu^\star_{gt}=\mu^\star_{gt}(\delta)\), and
\[
\mathcal E=\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T q_{gt}\mu^\star_{gt}W_{gt}^2 .
\]
A collapsed parameter is written \(\theta=(a,\beta)\) with \(r_{gt}'\theta=\sum_jX_{gt,j}a_j+D_{gt}\beta\), and \(\rho^\star(\delta)\) is the weighted nuisance coefficient of \cref{obj:def:weighted-fwl-residual}, so that
\[
W_{gt}=D_{gt}-\sum_jX_{gt,j}\rho^\star_j(\delta).
\]

\textit{Step 1: \(\mathcal E>0\).} Each summand is nonnegative, since \(q_{gt}>0\), \(\mu^\star_{gt}=\exp\{r_{gt}'\theta^\star(\delta)\}>0\), and \(W_{gt}^2\ge0\). If \(\mathcal E=0\), every summand vanishes, and positivity of \(q_{gt}\mu^\star_{gt}\) forces \(W_{gt}=0\) at every supported cell. Consider the collapsed parameter
\[
a=(-\rho^\star(\delta),1),
\]
whose treatment coordinate is one, so \(a\neq0\). Its collapsed index is exactly the residual,
\[
r_{gt}'a=D_{gt}-\sum_jX_{gt,j}\rho^\star_j(\delta)=W_{gt}=0
\qquad\text{at every supported cell,}
\]
whence \(\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T q_{gt}(r_{gt}'a)^2=0\), contradicting \cref{obj:ass:collapsed-design-rank}. Thus \(\mathcal E>0\).
% lean: fwlEnergy_pos_of_collapsedDesignRank, weightedFWLResidual_eq_rhoStar

\textit{Step 2: the perturbed criterion.} For \(x\in\mathbb R\) let \(\delta^x\) be \(\delta\) with the coordinate \(\delta_{ks}\) replaced by \(x\), and write \(x_0=\delta_{ks}\). Because \(D_{ks}=1\) and \(m_{gt}(\delta)=B_{gt}\exp(D_{gt}\delta_{gt})\), the perturbation moves exactly one collapsed mean:
\[
m_{ks}(\delta^x)=B_{ks}\exp(x),
\qquad
m_{gt}(\delta^x)=m_{gt}(\delta)\quad\text{for }(g,t)\neq(k,s),
\]
Setting
\[
p_{gt}=
\begin{cases}
B_{ks}\exp(\delta_{ks}), & (g,t)=(k,s),\\
0, & (g,t)\neq(k,s),
\end{cases}
\qquad\text{we have}\qquad
\frac{d}{dx}\,m_{gt}(\delta^x)\Big|_{x=x_0}=p_{gt}.
\]
% lean: Causalean.Stat.expCellUpdatedMean, betaStar_update_hasDerivAt

\textit{Step 3: the score system and its solution branch.} For a direction \(d\) put
\[
S_d(x,\theta)
=
\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T q_{gt}(r_{gt}'d)\left\{m_{gt}(\delta^x)-\exp(r_{gt}'\theta)\right\}.
\]
By \cref{obj:lem:pseudo-true-ppml-projection} applied at the effect array \(\delta^x\), the criterion \(L(\cdot;\delta^x)\) has a unique global maximizer, and \(S_d(x,\theta)=0\) for every \(d\) both holds at that maximizer and, conversely, characterizes it: vanishing of every directional score is sufficient for global maximality of a Poisson criterion with nonnegative weights, by the tangent-line bound \(\exp(\eta')\ge\exp(\eta)\{1+(\eta'-\eta)\}\).
% lean: Causalean.Stat.finitePoissonObjective_score, Causalean.Stat.finitePoissonObjective_isMax_of_score
The system is smooth in \((x,\theta)\), it is solved at \((x_0,\theta^\star(\delta))\), and its derivative in \(\theta\) at that point is the bilinear form
\[
d,e\ \mapsto\ -\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T q_{gt}\,\mu^\star_{gt}\,(r_{gt}'d)(r_{gt}'e),
\]
which is negative definite — \(e\neq0\) gives some supported cell with \(r_{gt}'e\neq0\) by the injectivity of \(\theta\mapsto(r_{gt}'\theta)_{(g,t)}\) established from \cref{obj:ass:collapsed-design-rank} in \cref{obj:lem:pseudo-true-ppml-projection} — and hence invertible. The implicit function theorem therefore supplies a continuously differentiable \(x\mapsto\theta(x)\) defined near \(x_0\), with \(\theta(x_0)=\theta^\star(\delta)\) and \(S_d(x,\theta(x))=0\) for every \(d\); by the characterization above and uniqueness of the maximizer, \(\theta(x)=\theta^\star(\delta^x)\) for \(x\) near \(x_0\). In particular \(x\mapsto\beta^\star(\delta^x)\) is differentiable at \(x_0\), with derivative the treatment coordinate \(v_\beta\) of \(v=\theta'(x_0)\).
% lean: Causalean.Stat.finitePoissonObjective_expCell_argmax_snd_hasDerivAt
Differentiating \(S_d(x,\theta(x))=0\) at \(x=x_0\) and using Step 2 gives the linearized score: for every direction \(d\),
\[
\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T
q_{gt}(r_{gt}'d)
\left\{
p_{gt}-\mu^\star_{gt}\,(r_{gt}'v)
\right\}=0 .
\]

\textit{Step 4: solving the linearized score by weighted FWL.} Put
\[
K=\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T q_{gt}\mu^\star_{gt}>0,
\qquad
\psi_{gt}=\frac{q_{gt}\mu^\star_{gt}}{K},
\qquad
\langle f,h\rangle=\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T\psi_{gt}f_{gt}h_{gt},
\]
and define the mean-normalized one-cell source and the nuisance part of \(v=(v_a,v_\beta)\) by
\[
u_{gt}=\frac{p_{gt}}{\mu^\star_{gt}},
\qquad
\alpha_{gt}=\sum_jX_{gt,j}v_{a,j},
\]
so that \(r_{gt}'v=\alpha_{gt}+v_\beta D_{gt}\) and \(\alpha\) lies in the span of the nuisance columns \(X_{\cdot,j}\). Because \(\mu^\star_{gt}>0\) and \(K\psi_{gt}=q_{gt}\mu^\star_{gt}\), each summand of the linearized score factors as
\[
q_{gt}(r_{gt}'d)\left\{p_{gt}-\mu^\star_{gt}\,(r_{gt}'v)\right\}
=
K\,\psi_{gt}\,(r_{gt}'d)\left\{u_{gt}-v_\beta D_{gt}-\alpha_{gt}\right\},
\]
so dividing the whole linearized score by \(K>0\) rewrites it as
\[
\langle u-v_\beta D-\alpha,\ (r_{gt}'d)_{g,t}\rangle=0
\qquad\text{for every direction }d .
\]
Taking \(d\) to be the pure treatment direction gives \(\langle u-v_\beta D-\alpha,\ D\rangle=0\); taking \(d=(\rho,0)\) for arbitrary \(\rho\) gives \(\langle u-v_\beta D-\alpha,\ h\rangle=0\) for every \(h\) in the nuisance span.
% lean: linearizedScore_snd_eq_weightedFWL
By \cref{obj:def:weighted-fwl-residual}, \(D-W\) is the \(\psi\)-weighted projection of \(D\) on the nuisance span, so \(W\) is \(\psi\)-orthogonal to that span and \(\langle W,D\rangle=\langle W,W\rangle\). Pairing \(W\) with \(u-v_\beta D-\alpha\) and using both normal equations (the second one at \(h=D-W\) and at \(h=\alpha\)),
\[
0=\langle W,\ u-v_\beta D-\alpha\rangle=\langle W,u\rangle-v_\beta\langle W,W\rangle .
\]
% lean: Causalean.Panel.Weighted.WeightedSupport.scalar_fwl_of_normalEqs
Now \(\langle W,W\rangle=\mathcal E/K>0\) by Step 1, and, since \(p\) is supported on the single cell \((k,s)\),
\[
\langle W,u\rangle
=
\frac{1}{K}\sum_{g\in\mathcal C}\sum_{t=1}^T q_{gt}\mu^\star_{gt}W_{gt}\frac{p_{gt}}{\mu^\star_{gt}}
=
\frac{q_{ks}B_{ks}\exp(\delta_{ks})W_{ks}}{K}.
\]
Dividing,
\[
v_\beta=\frac{\langle W,u\rangle}{\langle W,W\rangle}
=\frac{q_{ks}B_{ks}\exp(\delta_{ks})W_{ks}}{\mathcal E}.
\]

\textit{Step 5: the derivative formula.} Combining Steps 3 and 4,
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\,\beta^\star\!\bigl(\delta\text{ with }\delta_{ks}\text{ replaced by }x\bigr)\Big|_{x=\delta_{ks}}
=
v_\beta
=
\frac{q_{ks}B_{ks}\exp(\delta_{ks})\widetilde W_{ks}(\delta)}{\mathcal E(\delta)} ,
\]
which is the asserted differentiability at \(x=\delta_{ks}\) together with the displayed derivative.
% lean: betaStar_update_hasDerivAt, sharp_ppml_forbidden_sign

\textit{Step 6: the sign equivalences.} The derivative is \(cW_{ks}\) with
\[
c=\frac{q_{ks}B_{ks}\exp(\delta_{ks})}{\mathcal E}>0,
\]
because \(q_{ks}>0\), \(B_{ks}=\bar b_k\exp(\gamma_{s0})>0\), \(\exp(\delta_{ks})>0\), and \(\mathcal E>0\) by Step 1. Multiplication by a positive scalar preserves and reflects each of the three order relations, so
\[
cW_{ks}<0\iff W_{ks}<0,
\qquad
cW_{ks}=0\iff W_{ks}=0,
\qquad
0<cW_{ks}\iff 0<W_{ks},
\]
which, with \(W_{ks}=\widetilde W_{ks}(\delta)\), are exactly the three asserted equivalences.
% lean: sharp_ppml_forbidden_sign
\end{proof}
