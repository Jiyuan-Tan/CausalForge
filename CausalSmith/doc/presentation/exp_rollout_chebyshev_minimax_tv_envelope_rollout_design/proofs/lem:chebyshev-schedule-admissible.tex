\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:chebyshev-schedule-admissible}]
Let \(k\ge1\) and \(0<q\le1\), so that \(k>0\) and the nodes
\[
p^{\mathrm{Ch}}_j(k,q)=\frac q2\left(1-\cos\frac{\pi j}{k}\right),
\qquad j=0,\ldots,k,
\]
are well defined. By \cref{obj:def:budgeted-schedule-class} it suffices to check four things: that every coordinate lies in \([0,1]\), that the schedule starts at \(0\), that it is strictly increasing in \(j\), and that it ends at \(q\).

\emph{Range.}
The inequalities \(-1\le \cos(\pi j/k)\le1\) give \(0\le 1-\cos(\pi j/k)\le2\), and multiplying by \(q/2>0\) yields
\[
0
\le
p^{\mathrm{Ch}}_j(k,q)
=
\frac q2\left(1-\cos\frac{\pi j}{k}\right)
\le
\frac q2\cdot 2
=
q
\le
1 .
\]
Hence \(p^{\mathrm{Ch}}_j(k,q)\in[0,1]\) for every \(j=0,\ldots,k\). % lean: chebyshev_schedule_admissible

\emph{Endpoints.}
At \(j=0\), \(\cos 0=1\), so
\[
p^{\mathrm{Ch}}_0(k,q)=\frac q2(1-1)=0 ;
\]
at \(j=k\), \(\pi k/k=\pi\) and \(\cos\pi=-1\), so
\[
p^{\mathrm{Ch}}_k(k,q)=\frac q2\bigl(1-(-1)\bigr)=q .
\] % lean: chebyshev_schedule_admissible

\emph{Strict increase.}
If \(0\le a<b\le k\), then, since \(k>0\),
\[
0\le \frac{\pi a}{k}<\frac{\pi b}{k}\le \pi .
\]
Cosine is strictly decreasing on \([0,\pi]\), hence
\[
\cos\frac{\pi b}{k}<\cos\frac{\pi a}{k},
\]
so that
\[
1-\cos\frac{\pi a}{k}
<
1-\cos\frac{\pi b}{k},
\]
and multiplication by the positive number \(q/2\) gives
\[
p^{\mathrm{Ch}}_a(k,q)<p^{\mathrm{Ch}}_b(k,q).
\] % lean: chebyshev_schedule_admissible
The coordinates are therefore in \([0,1]\), start at \(0\), are strictly increasing, and end at \(q\), so \(p^{\mathrm{Ch}}(k,q)\in S_{k,q}\). % lean: chebyshev_schedule_admissible
\end{proof}
