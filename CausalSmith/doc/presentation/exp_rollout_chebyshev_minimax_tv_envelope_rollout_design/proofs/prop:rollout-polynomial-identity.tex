\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:prop:rollout-polynomial-identity}]
The static consistency hypothesis of \cref{obj:ass:static-rollout-consistency} is part of the rollout environment and fixes the interpretation of \(\bar Y_j\), but the endpoint calculation uses only the polynomial mean assumption of \cref{obj:ass:beta-order-polynomial}.

Both endpoints \(u=1\) and \(u=0\) belong to \([0,1]\), so the degree-\(\beta\) expansion applies at each. Evaluating at \(u=1\) gives
\[
m_P(1)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{\beta}a_{P,\ell}\,1^\ell=\sum_{\ell=0}^{\beta}a_{P,\ell},
\]
because \(1^\ell=1\) for every \(\ell\). % lean: h1
Evaluating the same expansion at \(u=0\) gives
\[
m_P(0)=\sum_{\ell=0}^{\beta}a_{P,\ell}\,0^\ell=a_{P,0},
\]
since \(0^\ell=0\) for every \(\ell\ge1\), while the \(\ell=0\) term is \(a_{P,0}\cdot 0^0=a_{P,0}\). % lean: h0
Finally, the finite sum over \(\{0,\ldots,\beta\}\) splits into its constant term and the positive-degree terms:
\[
\sum_{\ell=0}^{\beta}a_{P,\ell}
=
a_{P,0}+\sum_{\ell=1}^{\beta}a_{P,\ell}.
\]
This finite-sum identity is obtained by induction on \(\beta\), adjoining the top endpoint \(\ell=\beta+1\) to both sides at each step. % lean: hsplit
Substituting the two endpoint evaluations into this split and cancelling the constant term \(a_{P,0}\) yields
\[
m_P(1)-m_P(0)
=
\left(a_{P,0}+\sum_{\ell=1}^{\beta}a_{P,\ell}\right)-a_{P,0}
=
\sum_{\ell=1}^{\beta}a_{P,\ell}.
\] % lean: rollout_polynomial_identity
\end{proof}
