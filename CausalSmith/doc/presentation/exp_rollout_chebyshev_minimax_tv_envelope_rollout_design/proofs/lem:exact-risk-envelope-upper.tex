\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:exact-risk-envelope-upper}]
Write \(s=\sigma_0^2/n\); since \(\sigma_0^2\ge0\) and \(n\ge0\) we have \(s\ge0\). Recall that \(\Gamma_P(p)\) is the design covariance matrix of \((\bar Y_0,\ldots,\bar Y_k)\), so that for every weight vector \(w\)
\[
w^\prime\Gamma_P(p)w
=
\operatorname{Var}_\pi\!\left(\sum_{j=0}^k w_j\bar Y_j\right).
\]

\emph{An order-theoretic comparison of infima.}
Both assertions are instances of the following elementary fact. Let \(F,G\subseteq\mathbb R\), let \(c\ge0\), suppose \(F\) is nonempty and bounded below and \(G\) is nonempty, and suppose that
\[
\text{for every }g\in G\text{ there is }f\in F\text{ with } f\le c\,g .
\]
Then
\[
\inf F\le c\,\inf G .
\]
Indeed, \(c\ge0\) gives \(c\inf G=\inf\{c\,g:g\in G\}\); and for each \(g\in G\) the witness \(f\) satisfies \(\inf F\le f\le c\,g\), so \(\inf F\) is a lower bound for \(\{c\,g:g\in G\}\) and hence at most its infimum. % lean: sInf_le_mul_sInf_of_forall_exists_le

\emph{Step 1: the fixed-schedule bound, for every admissible schedule.}
Fix any \(p'\in S_{k,q}\) and apply the fact with \(c=s\) and
\[
F=\left\{
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_\beta}
\operatorname{Var}_\pi\!\left(\sum_{j=0}^k w_j\bar Y_j\right):
w\in W_\beta(p')
\right\},
\qquad
G=\left\{
\left(\sum_{j=0}^k |w_j|\right)^2:
w\in W_\beta(p')
\right\}.
\]
Since \(\beta\ge1\), \(k\ge\beta\) and \(p'\in S_{k,q}\), \cref{obj:lem:unbiased-weight-set-nonempty} supplies some \(w\in W_\beta(p')\), so both \(F\) and \(G\) are nonempty. % lean: unbiased_weight_set_nonempty
Moreover \(F\) is bounded below by \(0\): design variances are nonnegative, so each supremum in \(F\) is a supremum of nonnegative numbers. % lean: fixedRisk_nonneg
For the link condition, take \(g=\left(\sum_j|w_j|\right)^2\in G\) and let \(f\in F\) be the element indexed by the same \(w\). For every law \(P\in\mathcal P_\beta\) the round means \(\bar Y_0,\ldots,\bar Y_k\) satisfy the round-mean variance envelope of \cref{obj:ass:round-mean-variance-envelope} with scale \(\sigma_0^2/n\), so \cref{obj:lem:variance-envelope-sharpness} gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_\pi\!\left(\sum_{j=0}^k w_j\bar Y_j\right)
\le
s\left(\sum_{j=0}^k |w_j|\right)^2 .
\]
The right-hand side does not depend on \(P\) and is nonnegative, so taking the supremum over \(P\in\mathcal P_\beta\) preserves the bound and yields \(f\le s\,g\). % lean: variance_envelope_sharpness
The fact therefore applies, and since \(\inf G=A_\beta(p')\) by \cref{obj:def:amplification-criterion},
\[
\inf_{w\in W_\beta(p')}
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_\beta}
w^\prime\Gamma_P(p')w
\le
s\,
\inf_{w\in W_\beta(p')}
\left(\sum_{j=0}^k |w_j|\right)^2
=
\frac{\sigma_0^2}{n}A_\beta(p').
\]
Applying this to the given schedule \(p\) proves the first assertion,
\[
\inf_{w\in W_\beta(p)}
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_\beta}
w^\prime\Gamma_P(p)w
\le
\frac{\sigma_0^2}{n}A_\beta(p).
\]
% lean: exact_risk_envelope_upper

\emph{Step 2: passing to the nested risk.}
Apply the same fact a second time, now with \(c=s\) and
\[
F=\left\{
\inf_{w\in W_\beta(p')}
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_\beta}
w^\prime\Gamma_P(p')w:
p'\in S_{k,q}
\right\},
\qquad
G=\left\{A_\beta(p'):p'\in S_{k,q}\right\},
\]
so that \(\inf F=R_{\mathrm{exact}}(\beta,k,q)\) by \cref{obj:def:exact-nested-risk} and \(\inf G=M_{\beta,k,q}\) by \cref{obj:def:amplification-criterion}. Both sets are nonempty because \(p\in S_{k,q}\), and \(F\) is bounded below by \(0\) because each of its elements is an infimum of the nonnegative quantities of Step 1. The link condition is exactly the fixed-schedule bound of Step 1: for \(g=A_\beta(p')\) take the element of \(F\) indexed by the same \(p'\). Hence
\[
R_{\mathrm{exact}}(\beta,k,q)
\le
\frac{\sigma_0^2}{n}
\inf_{p'\in S_{k,q}} A_\beta(p')
=
\frac{\sigma_0^2}{n}M_{\beta,k,q}.
\]
% lean: sInf_le_mul_sInf_of_forall_exists_le
\end{proof}
