\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:unbiased-weight-set-nonempty}]
Because \(p\in S_{k,q}\), the entries \(p_0<p_1<\cdots<p_k\) are strictly increasing in \cref{obj:def:budgeted-schedule-class}, hence the \(k+1\) nodes are pairwise distinct. Let \(L_j\) be the Lagrange basis polynomial for the full collection of nodes,
\[
L_j(t)=\prod_{\substack{0\le i\le k\\ i\ne j}}\frac{t-p_i}{p_j-p_i},
\qquad j=0,\ldots,k,
\]
and define the endpoint weights
\[
w_j=L_j(1)-L_j(0),
\qquad j=0,\ldots,k.
\] % lean: lagrange_endpoint_weights_unbiased
Fix \(0\le \ell\le\beta\) and let \(e_\ell(t)=t^\ell\), a polynomial of degree \(\ell\le\beta\le k\), hence of degree strictly less than the number \(k+1\) of nodes. Lagrange interpolation on the \(k+1\) distinct nodes therefore reproduces it exactly,
\[
e_\ell(t)=\sum_{j=0}^k e_\ell(p_j)\,L_j(t)
=\sum_{j=0}^k p_j^\ell\,L_j(t).
\]
Evaluating at \(t=1\) and at \(t=0\) gives
\[
1^\ell=\sum_{j=0}^k p_j^\ell\,L_j(1),
\qquad
0^\ell=\sum_{j=0}^k p_j^\ell\,L_j(0),
\]
and subtracting the second from the first yields
\[
\sum_{j=0}^k w_j p_j^\ell
=
1^\ell-0^\ell.
\] % lean: lagrange_endpoint_weights_unbiased
For \(\ell=0\) this is \(1-1=0\), while for every \(1\le\ell\le\beta\) it is \(1-0=1\). These are exactly the moment equations of \cref{obj:def:unbiased-weight-set} defining \(W_\beta(p)\). Thus the vector \(w=(w_0,\ldots,w_k)\) belongs to \(W_\beta(p)\), and the set is nonempty. % lean: unbiased_weight_set_nonempty
\end{proof}
