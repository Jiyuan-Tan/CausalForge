\section{Main bracket and matched regimes}

The lower bounds in \cref{obj:lem:scaled-binary-exact-lower-transfer-all-d,obj:thm:radius-channel-converse-all-d} and the selector upper bound in \cref{obj:thm:frontier-upper-all-d} combine into the paper's main finite-sample statement. The result is indexed by the same sample size, alphabet size, outcome scale, overlap constant, and heterogeneity radius as the model class in \cref{obj:def:model-class}; the constants depend only on the fixed overlap parameter. The two displayed benchmarks separate the exact-homogeneity collision baseline from the radius-sensitive rare-cell component on the lower side, and the best available selector branch on the upper side.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:two-sided-minimax-bracket-all-d}[All-alphabet minimax bracket]
For every overlap parameter \(\epsilon\) with \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are real constants \(c_{\epsilon}\) and \(C_{\epsilon}\) such that
\[
0<c_{\epsilon}\le 1,\qquad 1\le C_{\epsilon},\qquad c_{\epsilon}\le C_{\epsilon}.
\]
For every \(n,d\in\mathbb N\), \(M\in\mathbb R\), and \(\sigma\in\mathbb R\), suppose that
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Sample and alphabet sizes.)} \(n\ge 1\) and \(d\ge 1\).
\item \textbf{(Outcome envelope.)} \(M\ge 1\).
\item \textbf{(Radius range.)} \(0\le \sigma\le 2\).
\end{itemize}
Let \(\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) be the minimax mean-squared risk in \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk} over the real-outcome class \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) with fixed overlap \(\epsilon\), conditional exchangeability, consistency, conditional mean normalization, conditional second central moment envelope \(M\), and approximate-homogeneity radius \(\sigma M\). Define
\[
u_{n,d}=\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)},
\qquad
h_{n,d,\sigma}=\sigma^2+\frac{d}{n^2},
\]
and write
\[
\ell_{n,d,\sigma}
=
\frac{1}{n}
+
\min\left\{1,\frac{d}{n^2}\right\}
+
\sigma^2\min\left\{1,u_{n,d}\right\},
\qquad
r_{n,d,\sigma}
=
\frac{1}{n}
+
\min\left\{1,u_{n,d},h_{n,d,\sigma}\right\}.
\]
Then
\[
c_{\epsilon}M^2\ell_{n,d,\sigma}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
\le
C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\]
\end{theoremv}

\Cref{obj:thm:two-sided-minimax-bracket-all-d} gives a same-class minimax bracket for the scalar average treatment effect over arbitrary cell masses and real outcomes under the moment envelope in \cref{obj:def:model-class}. The upper benchmark is constructive through the known-radius selector in \cref{obj:def:total-estimator}. The lower benchmark adds two sources of difficulty: the capped collision term already present at exact homogeneity and the radius-channel rare-cell term transported in \cref{obj:thm:radius-channel-converse-all-d}. Thus the radius governs whether borrowing across cells, crossed-cell contrasts, or clipping to the outcome scale supplies the sharpest available upper benchmark.

The endpoint reductions turn the bracket into familiar rates at the two ends of the radius scale. At \(\sigma=0\), the collision remainder \(d/n^2\) is the relevant sparse-cell term. At radius two, \cref{obj:lem:scale-sanity} identifies the radius-indexed class with the unrestricted-radius class, and the polynomial remainder gives the unrestricted all-alphabet benchmark associated with large-alphabet functional estimation \citep{JiaoVenkatHanWeissman2015,WuYang2016Entropy}.

\begin{propositionv}{prop:endpoint-reductions-all-d}[Endpoint rate reductions]
There exist constants \(c,C\in(0,\infty)\), with \(c\le C\), such that the following hold.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Exact homogeneity.)} For every pair of integers \(n,d\ge 1\),
\[
c\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}
\le
n^{-1}+\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)},\frac d{n^2}\right\}
\le
C\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\},
\]
and
\[
c\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}
\le
n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)
\le
C\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}.
\]
\item \textbf{(Unrestricted radius.)} For every pair of integers \(n,d\ge 1\),
\[
c\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}
\le
n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
\le
C\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\},
\]
and
\[
c\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}
\le
n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)
+4\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
\le
C\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}.
\]
\item \textbf{(Class equality at radius two.)} For every alphabet size \(d\), every \(\epsilon\in\mathbb R\), and every \(M\in\mathbb R\), the laws represented by \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\) in \cref{obj:def:unrestricted-class} are exactly the laws represented by \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,2}\) in \cref{obj:def:model-class}.
\item \textbf{(Endpoint identity.)} For every pair of integers \(n,d\ge 1\), the radius-two upper frontier expression is exactly
\[
n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right).
\]
\end{itemize}
\end{propositionv}

\Cref{obj:prop:endpoint-reductions-all-d} calibrates the two endpoints of the regime summary. Exact homogeneity gives the rate \(n^{-1}+\min(1,d/n^2)\), matching the collision lower transfer from \citet{ZengBalakrishnanHanKennedy2024}. The unrestricted endpoint gives \(n^{-1}+\min\{1,d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\}\), the polynomial large-alphabet scale. The class equality clause also fixes the interpretation of the radius normalization: radius two represents the full unrestricted-radius law class introduced in \cref{obj:def:unrestricted-class}.

The regime theorem supplies the order comparisons away from the endpoints. It covers every fixed positive radius, the saturation region, small alphabets, and the two parametric-dominance elbows. It also records the \leanref{P-10}{residual shrinking-radius wedge} generated by the proved benchmarks.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:fixed-interior-tightness-and-shrinking-radius-gap-all-d}[Fixed-radius wedge characterization]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  For \(n,d\in\mathbb N\), put \(L_n=\log(en)\),
\[
b_{n,d}=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2},
\qquad
u_{n,d}=\frac{d^2}{n^2L_n^2},
\qquad
h_{n,d,\sigma}=\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2},
\qquad
r_{n,d,\sigma}=\frac1n+\min\{1,\min(u_{n,d},h_{n,d,\sigma})\},
\]
and define
\[
q_{n,d,\sigma}=\frac1n+\min\left\{u_{n,d},\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right\},
\qquad
\ell_{n,d,\sigma}=b_{n,d}+\sigma^2u_{n,d}.
\]
Let \(\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) be the minimax mean-squared risk in \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk}.  Then all of the following hold.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Triangular benchmarks.)} For every \(n,d\in\mathbb N\) and every \(0\le\sigma\le2\), if
\[
L_n^2<d<nL_n,
\]
then
\[
\frac12\{b_{n,d}+\min(u_{n,d},\sigma^2)\}
\le q_{n,d,\sigma}
\le b_{n,d}+\min(u_{n,d},\sigma^2),
\qquad
\ell_{n,d,\sigma}=b_{n,d}+\sigma^2u_{n,d}.
\]
\item \textbf{(Fixed interior radii.)} For every \(\underline\sigma\in(0,2]\), there exist constants \(c,C\) with \(0<c\le C\) such that, for every \(n,d\in\mathbb N\), every \(M\ge1\), and every \(\sigma\) satisfying \(\underline\sigma\le\sigma\le2\),
\[
cM^2r_{n,d,\sigma}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
\le
CM^2r_{n,d,\sigma},
\]
whenever \(n\ge1\) and \(d\ge1\).
\item \textbf{(Matching elbows.)} For every \(K\ge0\), there exist constants \(c,C\) with \(0<c\le C\) such that, for every \(n,d\in\mathbb N\), every \(M\ge1\), and every \(0\le\sigma\le2\),
\[
cM^2r_{n,d,\sigma}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
\le
CM^2r_{n,d,\sigma},
\]
whenever \(n\ge1\), \(d\ge1\), and at least one of
\[
1\le u_{n,d},
\qquad
u_{n,d}\le Kb_{n,d},
\qquad
\sigma^2\le Kb_{n,d}
\]
holds.
\item \textbf{(Exact algebraic elbow.)} For every \(n,d\in\mathbb N\) with \(n\ge1\),
\[
u_{n,d}\le b_{n,d}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
d^2\le nL_n^2+dL_n^2.
\]
\item \textbf{(Sufficient elbows.)} For every \(n,d\in\mathbb N\) with \(n\ge1\),
\[
d\le\sqrt n\,L_n \quad\Longrightarrow\quad u_{n,d}\le b_{n,d},
\]
and
\[
d\le L_n^2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad u_{n,d}\le b_{n,d}.
\]
\item \textbf{(Residual wedge implication.)} For any sequences \(d_n\in\mathbb N\) and \(\sigma_n\in\mathbb R\), if eventually \(d_n\ge1\) and \(0\le\sigma_n\le2\), and if the frontier-to-capped-converse benchmark ratio
\[
\frac{r_{n,d_n,\sigma_n}}
{n^{-1}+\min\{1,d_n/n^2\}+\sigma_n^2\min\{1,u_{n,d_n}\}}
\to\infty,
\]
then the sequence lies in the residual wedge:
\[
\frac{b_{n,d_n}}{u_{n,d_n}}\to0,
\qquad
u_{n,d_n}\to0,
\qquad
\frac{b_{n,d_n}}{\sigma_n^2}\to0,
\qquad
\sigma_n^2\to0.
\]
\item \textbf{(Nonempty wedge.)} The sequence \(d_n=n\) and \(\sigma_n=L_n^{-1/2}\) lies in the residual wedge.
\item \textbf{(Diagonal divergence.)} Along \(d_n=n\) and \(\sigma_n=L_n^{-1/2}\),
\[
\frac{q_{n,n,L_n^{-1/2}}}{\ell_{n,n,L_n^{-1/2}}}
\asymp L_n
\]
eventually, and
\[
\frac{q_{n,n,L_n^{-1/2}}}{\ell_{n,n,L_n^{-1/2}}}\to\infty .
\]
\end{itemize}
\end{theoremv}

\Cref{obj:thm:fixed-interior-tightness-and-shrinking-radius-gap-all-d} gives the rate consequences of the bracket. For every radius bounded away from zero, the minimax risk is equivalent to the selector benchmark \(M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}\). The same equivalence holds in the saturated region \(1\le u_{n,d}\), in regimes where the polynomial term is dominated by the exact-homogeneity baseline, and in regimes where the squared radius is dominated by that baseline. The algebraic and sufficient elbow clauses identify concrete alphabet ranges, including \(d\le \sqrt n\log(en)\) and \(d\le \log^2(en)\), where the baseline already controls the polynomial remainder.

The final row of \cref{tab:matched-regimes} is a localization statement about the displayed benchmarks. In the intermediate alphabet range \(L_n^2<d<nL_n\), the selector benchmark is comparable to \(b_{n,d}+\min(u_{n,d},\sigma^2)\), while the lower benchmark has the product form \(b_{n,d}+\sigma^2u_{n,d}\). These expressions agree in the fixed-radius and parametric-dominance regimes described in the theorem. The residual shrinking-radius wedge consists of sequences where the baseline is small relative to both the polynomial scale and the squared radius, while both the polynomial scale and squared radius vanish. The diagonal sequence \(d_n=n\), \(\sigma_n=L_n^{-1/2}\) shows that this localized separation region is nonempty and that the ratio between the displayed upper and lower benchmarks can diverge at logarithmic order.

Taken together, \cref{obj:thm:two-sided-minimax-bracket-all-d,obj:prop:endpoint-reductions-all-d,obj:thm:fixed-interior-tightness-and-shrinking-radius-gap-all-d} provide the all-alphabet minimax summary. The paper establishes a constructive upper benchmark and a same-class lower benchmark for the radius-indexed real-outcome model, matches them at the two endpoints and throughout the fixed-positive-radius and elbow regimes, and isolates the shrinking-radius intermediate region generated by the proved finite-sample inequalities.
