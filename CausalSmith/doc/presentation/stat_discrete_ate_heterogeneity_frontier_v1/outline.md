# Title
**A minimax bracket for average treatment effect estimation with discrete adjustment and bounded heterogeneity**

**Contribution statement.** The paper establishes an all-alphabet, radius-indexed minimax bracket for average treatment effect estimation with a finite discrete confounder, fixed overlap, arbitrary cell masses, real outcomes with bounded conditional means and conditional second central moments, and a known maximal cell-effect deviation radius.
