\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:prop:zeng-class-inclusion-and-lower-transfer}]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  Choose the exact-transfer constant from \cref{obj:lem:scaled-binary-exact-lower-transfer-all-d}, the radius-transfer constant and source cap from \cref{obj:thm:radius-channel-converse-all-d}, and the polynomial handle and upper constant from \cref{obj:lem:continuous-ratio-polynomial-upper-all-d}.  Shrink the exact constant, if necessary, so that it also fits the explicit binary collision witness used on the finite exact-homogeneity range.  All four constants are positive, and \(c_{\mathrm{ex}}\le1\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.zeng_class_inclusion_and_lower_transfer

For each \(d\ge1\) and \(M\ge1\), take \(\Phi_M\) to be the affine binary-to-real map \(B(a)\mapsto M\{B(a)-1/2\}\).  The embedding preserves cell masses and propensities, pushes each arm-cell binary outcome law through the affine map, and sends binary treatment effects to \(M\) times their binary contrasts.  Therefore every binary law satisfying the overlap condition embeds into the unrestricted real-outcome class, and every exactly homogeneous binary law embeds into the zero-radius real-outcome class:
\[
\Phi_M(P)\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,0}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.affineBinaryRealLaw_model_zero

The affine image is a strict subclass of the real-outcome model because real laws may have conditional outcome distributions beyond two affine-transformed binary support points while still satisfying the same mean, moment, overlap, and radius restrictions.  The checked witness law gives, for every \(0\le\sigma\le2\), a member \(Q\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) outside the image of \(\Phi_M\):
\[
\Phi_M(P)\ne Q
\]
for every binary law \(P\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.affineBinaryRealLaw_ne_testModelLaw

On the exact-homogeneity range \(d\le c_{\mathrm{ex}}n^2\), apply the binary exact-homogeneity hard family to an arbitrary clipped real-outcome estimator after composing that estimator with the affine observation map.  The target under \(\Phi_M\) is \(M\psi(P)\), so the binary squared-risk lower bound scales by \(M^2\).  This proves the estimator-wise exact-subclass lower transfer. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.binaryExactMinimaxRisk_hard_family_of_lt

The radius lower term and its transfer certificate are exactly the all-d radius-channel statement, with constants chosen above:
\[
c_{\mathrm{rad}}M^2\sigma^2\min\{1,u_{n,d}\}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.radius_channel_converse_all_d

Finally, the polynomial handle is the one selected by the all-alphabet polynomial upper bound.  Its admissibility and risk inequality hold uniformly over every \(Q\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), and its active-range implication for \(K_n\) is the handle property recorded in the first step. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d
\end{proof}
