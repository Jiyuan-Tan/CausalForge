\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:prop:zeng-class-inclusion-and-lower-transfer}]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  Write \(\bar c_{\mathrm{ex}}\) for the constant supplied by \cref{obj:lem:scaled-binary-exact-lower-transfer-all-d}, take \(c_{\mathrm{rad}}\) and \(b_{\mathrm{rad}}\) from \cref{obj:thm:radius-channel-converse-all-d}, and take \(C_{\mathrm{up}}\) and the polynomial handle from \cref{obj:lem:continuous-ratio-polynomial-upper-all-d}.  Let
\[
u_{
\epsilon}:=\min\{1/2,8(1/2-\epsilon)^2\}
\]
and set
\[
c_{\mathrm{ex}}
:=
\min\left\{
\bar c_{\mathrm{ex}},\,
\frac14\min\left\{\frac1{100},\frac{u_{\epsilon}^2}{64}\right\},\,
1
\right\}.
\]
Then \(c_{\mathrm{ex}}>0\), \(c_{\mathrm{ex}}\le \bar c_{\mathrm{ex}}\), \(c_{\mathrm{ex}}\le1\), and
\[
c_{\mathrm{ex}}\le
\frac14\min\left\{\frac1{100},\frac{u_{\epsilon}^2}{64}\right\}.
\]
The selected polynomial handle retains its cutoff and active-range multiplier from \cref{obj:lem:continuous-ratio-polynomial-upper-all-d}. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.zeng_class_inclusion_and_lower_transfer

For each \(d\ge1\) and \(M\ge1\), define \(\Phi_M\) to be the affine binary-to-real pushforward of \cref{obj:synth_4}, namely \(B(a)\mapsto M\{B(a)-1/2\}\).  The embedding identities preserve cell masses and propensities, push each positive arm-cell binary outcome law through this affine map, and make each arm-cell mean equal to \(M\) times the corresponding binary mean minus \(M/2\).  Hence every binary law satisfying the overlap condition gives a member of \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\) as in \cref{obj:def:unrestricted-class}, and every uniform-mass exactly homogeneous binary law gives a member of the zero-radius class,
\[
\Phi_M(P)\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,0}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.affineBinaryRealLaw_model_zero

Fix a cell \(k\) in the nonempty alphabet and fix \(0\le\sigma\le2\).  Let \(Q\) be the one-cell test law that puts unit cell mass on \(k\), has propensity \(1/2\), has control arm-cell outcome law \(\delta_0\), and has treated arm-cell outcome law given by the two-point mean-zero law on \(\{-M/2,M/2\}\).  With local mean parameter zero, this test law belongs to \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\).  For any binary law \(P\), the control arm-cell outcome law of \(\Phi_M(P)\) is supported on the two affine binary values \(\{-M/2,M/2\}\), which assign zero mass to \(\{0\}\) since \(M\ge1\).  The corresponding control arm-cell law of \(Q\) assigns mass one to \(\{0\}\).  Therefore
\[
\Phi_M(P)\ne Q
\]
for every binary law \(P\), giving the strict-image assertion at radius \(\sigma\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.affineBinaryRealLaw_ne_testModelLaw

Now fix \(n\ge1\) and \(0\le\sigma\le2\), and assume \(d\le c_{\mathrm{ex}}n^2\).  Since \(c_{\mathrm{ex}}\le1\), this gives \(d\le n^2\).  For the binary source experiment in \cref{obj:synth_3}, a two-point subexperiment supplies the parametric term, as follows.  Put \(\delta:=(2/5)/\sqrt n\), so that \(\delta\ge0\), \(\delta^2=4/(25n)\), and \(1/2+\delta\le1\).  Let \(P_0,P_1\) be the two uniform-mass exactly homogeneous binary laws with constant propensity \(1/2\), control-arm response mean \(1/2\) under both, and treated-arm response mean \(1/2\) under \(P_0\) and \(1/2+\delta\) under \(P_1\); constant propensity \(1/2\) meets the overlap constraint because \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), and both laws are exactly homogeneous because the cell contrast is the same in every cell.  Their targets differ by \(|\psi(P_0)-\psi(P_1)|=\delta\), and the product-law calibration
\[
n\,\frac{(1/2)\delta^2}{(1/2)(1-1/2)}
=
\frac{8}{25}
\le\log 2
\]
gives \(\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})\le1/2\).  The two-point testing bound, with centres \(1/2\) and \(1/2+\delta\), threshold \(\delta/2\), and total-variation constant \(1/2\), therefore yields
\[
\frac1{100n}
\le
\inf_{\widehat\psi}\sup_{P\in\mathcal H_{n,d}^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)}
E_{P^{\otimes n}}\!\left[\{\widehat\psi-\psi(P)\}^2\right],
\]
and \cref{obj:lem:binary-exact-collision-lower} gives
\[
\frac{u_{\epsilon}^2}{64}\frac d{n^2}
\le
\inf_{\widehat\psi}\sup_{P\in\mathcal H_{n,d}^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)}
E_{P^{\otimes n}}\!\left[\{\widehat\psi-\psi(P)\}^2\right].
\]
Together with the definition of \(c_{\mathrm{ex}}\), these two inequalities place
\[
c_{\mathrm{ex}}\left(\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}\right)
\]
strictly below the fixed-sample binary exact-homogeneity minimax risk.  Thus every measurable binary estimator has a source law \(P\in\mathcal H_{n,d}^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) whose binary squared risk is at least this level.  Applying this statement to the binary estimator obtained by composing an arbitrary clipped real-outcome estimator with the affine observation map, and using \(\tau(\Phi_M(P))=M\psi(P)\) on the exact-homogeneous source class, multiplies the lower bound by \(M^2\).  Hence every such real-outcome estimator has some \(P\in\mathcal H_{n,d}^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) satisfying
\[
c_{\mathrm{ex}}M^2\left(\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}\right)
\le
\operatorname{MSE}_{\Phi_M(P)}(\widehat\tau).
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.binaryExactMinimaxRisk_hard_family_of_lt

The exact-range minimax-risk bound follows from \cref{obj:lem:scaled-binary-exact-lower-transfer-all-d}.  Since \(c_{\mathrm{ex}}\le\bar c_{\mathrm{ex}}\) and \(d\le n^2\), the term \(\min\{1,d/n^2\}\) in that all-alphabet transfer equals \(d/n^2\) on the present range, giving
\[
c_{\mathrm{ex}}M^2\left(\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}\right)
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.scaled_binary_exact_lower_transfer_all_d

For the radius term, \cref{obj:thm:radius-channel-converse-all-d} supplies
\[
c_{\mathrm{rad}}M^2
\left\{
\frac1n+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)
+\sigma^2\min\{1,u_{n,d}\}
\right\}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma},
\]
together with the transfer certificate with constants \(c_{\mathrm{rad}}\) and \(b_{\mathrm{rad}}\).  The base summand \(1/n+\min\{1,d/n^2\}\) is nonnegative, so the same inequality yields
\[
c_{\mathrm{rad}}M^2\sigma^2\min\{1,u_{n,d}\}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.radius_channel_converse_all_d

Finally, \cref{obj:lem:continuous-ratio-polynomial-upper-all-d} supplies the selected polynomial handle.  The associated estimator \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\) is measurable, takes values in \([-M,M]\), satisfies the handle's active-range implication for \(K_n\), and obeys uniformly for every \(Q\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\)
\[
\operatorname{MSE}_{Q}(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}})
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}M^2\left(\frac1n+\min\{1,u_{n,d}\}\right).
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d
\end{proof}
