\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:binary-exact-collision-lower}]
Set
\[
u_\epsilon:=\min\{1/2,8(1/2-\epsilon)^2\},
\qquad
\Delta:=u_\epsilon\frac{\sqrt d}{n},
\qquad
\rho:=\sqrt{\Delta/8}.
\]
The hypotheses imply \(0<u_\epsilon\), \(0\le\Delta\le1/2\), and \(\rho\) is small enough that the propensities and Bernoulli means constructed below stay inside the allowed overlap and probability ranges. % lean: binaryExactMinimaxRisk_collision_lower

For each sign vector \(\xi\in\{\pm1\}^d\), define a uniform-mass binary law \(P_\xi\) with
\[
\pi_k^{(\xi)}=\frac12+\rho\xi_k,
\qquad
\mu_{1k}^{(\xi)}=\frac12-\rho\xi_k+\frac{\Delta}{4},
\qquad
\mu_{0k}^{(\xi)}=\frac12-\rho\xi_k-\frac{\Delta}{4}.
\]
Let \(P_0\) be the corresponding centered law with \(\Delta=\rho=0\).  Each \(P_\xi\) is exactly homogeneous with \(\psi(P_\xi)=\Delta/2\), while \(\psi(P_0)=0\). % lean: exactSignExactLaw_ate

The single-observation likelihood-overlap calculation reduces the finite-mixture chi-square distance between \(P_0^{\otimes n}\) and the uniform mixture over the \(P_\xi^{\otimes n}\)'s to the standard Rademacher-sign expression with signal parameter
\[
\Gamma=\Delta-\frac{\Delta^2}{2}+\frac{\Delta^3}{8}.
\]
The preceding bounds give \(0\le\Gamma\le\Delta\) and the Ingster condition
\[
2n^2(\Gamma/2)^2\le d\log2 .
\]
% lean: binaryExactMinimaxRisk_collision_lower

The finite-sign mixture bound therefore gives chi-square distance at most one, hence total variation at most \(1/2\), between the null product law and the mixture over alternatives. % lean: exact_tvDist_le_half

Le Cam's two-point testing inequality applied to the null target \(0\) and the alternative target \(\Delta/2\) gives squared risk at least \(\Delta^2/64\) for some law in the binary exactly homogeneous class. % lean: binaryExactMinimaxRisk_collision_aux

Since \(\Delta^2=u_\epsilon^2d/n^2\), the asserted lower bound follows. % lean: binaryExactMinimaxRisk_collision_lower
\end{proof}
