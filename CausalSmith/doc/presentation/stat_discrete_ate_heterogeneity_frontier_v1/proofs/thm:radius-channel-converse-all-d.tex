\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:radius-channel-converse-all-d}]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  The proof combines two transported binary source problems.  The radial source uses the one-arm lower bound with a capped alphabet of order \(n\log(en)\); the exact source uses the exactly homogeneous binary lower bound with a capped alphabet of order \(n^2\).  The cap constants and sample cutoffs are chosen as overlap-dependent constants before \(n,d,M,\sigma\) are fixed.  The radial cap is calibrated so that the source range, the large-sample lower bound, and the transport scale all hold on the active range. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.exists_capped_radial_transport_package

The exact-homogeneity package supplies an exact cap \(d_{\mathrm{ex}}\), a source risk level \(L_{\mathrm{ex}}\), and a normalization that dominates
\[
M^2\left\{n^{-1}+\min(1,d/n^2)\right\}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.exists_capped_exact_transport_package

Choose the final lower-bound constant \(c_\epsilon\) as a sufficiently small overlap-dependent minimum of the exact, radial, and finite-sample constants, and take \(b_\epsilon^{\mathrm{rad}}\) from the radial package.  This gives \(0<c_\epsilon\le1\) and \(b_\epsilon^{\mathrm{rad}}>0\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.radius_channel_converse_all_d

For fixed \(n,d,M,\sigma\) in the theorem's range, embed the radial source by padding its capped alphabet into \(\{1,\ldots,d\}\), applying the hypothesis-independent Bernoulli channel with success probability
\[
\frac12+\frac{\sigma}{2}\left(B(a)-\frac12\right),
\]
and scaling \(B'(a)\) to \(Y(a)=M\{B'(a)-1/2\}\).  The exact source uses the deterministic affine map \(Y(a)=M\{B(a)-1/2\}\).  The checked membership declarations show that these embeddings land in \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) and that the exact embedding has target slope \(M\) on the binary exact-homogeneity source. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.radialPaddedAffineLaw_model

The radial source-risk statement is transported through the Markov kernel induced by the Bernoulli channel.  Data processing preserves the testing lower bound, and the target identity multiplies source separation by \(M\sigma/2\), yielding the estimator-wise lower term
\[
c_\epsilon M^2\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right).
\]
The same certificate includes the cap, source family, padding map, embedding, independent full-data coupling, channel, and product data-processing assertion. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.radial_risk_of_kernel_transport

The exact source-risk statement is transported by the affine map, giving the estimator-wise contribution
\[
c_\epsilon M^2\left\{n^{-1}+\min(1,d/n^2)\right\}.
\]
This is the exact half of the common transfer certificate. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.exactRiskTransfer_of_affine_transport

Taking the larger of the two source risks for an arbitrary clipped estimator and then passing to the minimax infimum gives
\[
c_\epsilon M^2
\left\{
\frac1n+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)
+\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
\right\}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\]
The radial certificate asserted in the theorem is the package fixed above. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.converseRate_lower_of_exact_transfer_and_handle
\end{proof}
