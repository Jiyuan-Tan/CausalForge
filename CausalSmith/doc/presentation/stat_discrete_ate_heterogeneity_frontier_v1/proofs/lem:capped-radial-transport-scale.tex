\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:capped-radial-transport-scale}]
Let
\[
L:=\log(en),
\qquad
x:=\frac b2\,nL,
\qquad
d_{\mathrm{rad}}
=
\min\left\{d,\max\{1,\lfloor x\rfloor\}\right\}.
\]
Since \(n\ge3\), both \(\log n\) and \(L\) are positive, \(L\ge\log n\), and the denominator \(nL\) is positive. The range assumption gives \(1\le x\), hence \(\lfloor x\rfloor>0\) and \(\max\{1,\lfloor x\rfloor\}=\lfloor x\rfloor\). % lean: cappedRadial_sourceRate_dominates

First,
\[
\frac b4\min\left\{1,\frac d{nL}\right\}
\le
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}}{nL}.
\]
If \(d\le\lfloor x\rfloor\), then \(d_{\mathrm{rad}}=d\). The fraction \(d/(nL)\) is nonnegative, so \(\min\{1,d/(nL)\}\) is nonnegative and at most \(d/(nL)\). Together with \(b/4\le1\), this gives
\[
\frac b4\min\left\{1,\frac d{nL}\right\}
\le
\min\left\{1,\frac d{nL}\right\}
\le
\frac d{nL}
=
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}}{nL}.
\]
If \(d>\lfloor x\rfloor\), then \(d_{\mathrm{rad}}=\lfloor x\rfloor\). Since \(x\ge1\), \(\lfloor x\rfloor\ge x/2\), and positivity of \(nL\) gives
\[
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}}{nL}
\ge
\frac{x/2}{nL}
=
\frac b4.
\]
Also \(\min\{1,d/(nL)\}\le1\) and \(b/4\ge0\), so the desired inequality follows in this case as well. % lean: cappedRadial_sourceRate_dominates

Both sides of the preceding inequality are nonnegative, hence squaring gives
\[
\left(
\frac b4\min\left\{1,\frac d{nL}\right\}
\right)^2
\le
\left(\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}}{nL}\right)^2.
\]
It remains to rewrite the squared minimum. Since \(d/(nL)\ge0\),
\[
\left(\min\left\{1,\frac d{nL}\right\}\right)^2
=
\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2}\right\}.
\]
Indeed, if \(d/(nL)\le1\), then the left minimum is \(d/(nL)\), and nonnegativity gives \((d/(nL))^2\le1\). If \(1\le d/(nL)\), then the left minimum is \(1\), and nonnegativity gives \(1\le(d/(nL))^2\). Using also \((d/(nL))^2=d^2/(n^2L^2)\), the squared inequality becomes
\[
\frac{b^2}{16}
\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2}\right\}
\le
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}^2}{n^2L^2}.
\]
Since \(L\ge\log n>0\),
\[
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}^2}{n^2L^2}
\le
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}^2}{n^2\log^2 n}.
\]
Adding the nonnegative term \(1/n\) on the right therefore yields
\[
\frac{b^2}{16}
\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}
\le
\frac1n+
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}^2}{n^2\log^2 n}.
\]
% lean: cappedRadial_sourceRate_dominates

Multiplying this inequality by the nonnegative factor
\[
\left(\frac{M\sigma}{2}\right)^2\frac a2
\]
and simplifying constants yields
\[
\frac{ab^2}{128}M^2\sigma^2
\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}
\le
\left(\frac{M\sigma}{2}\right)^2
\frac a2
\left\{
\frac1n+
\frac{d_{\mathrm{rad}}^2}{n^2\log^2 n}
\right\}.
\]
% lean: cappedRadial_transportScale
\end{proof}
