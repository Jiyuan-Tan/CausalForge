\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:scaled-binary-exact-lower-transfer-all-d}]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  Let \(a_\epsilon,b_\epsilon>0\) and
\(N_\epsilon\in\mathbb N\) be the constants supplied by
\cref{obj:lem:zeng-binary-exact-homogeneity-lower}.  Let
\(c_{\mathrm{par}}>0\) be the constant in \cref{obj:lem:parametric-lower}.
Set
\[
\bar b_\epsilon:=\min\{b_\epsilon,1/2\},
\qquad
K_\epsilon:=1+N_\epsilon+\frac1{\bar b_\epsilon},
\qquad
c_\epsilon:=\min\left\{\frac{a_\epsilon \bar b_\epsilon}{4},
\frac{c_{\mathrm{par}}}{K_\epsilon}\right\}.
\]
Then \(\bar b_\epsilon>0\), \(K_\epsilon>0\), and \(c_\epsilon>0\). % lean: scaled_binary_exact_lower_transfer_all_d

Fix \(n,d\ge1\), \(M\ge1\), and \(0\le\sigma\le2\).  Put
\[
x:=\bar b_\epsilon n^2 .
\]
First suppose that
\[
N_\epsilon\le n,
\qquad
1\le x .
\]
Define the capped source alphabet size
\[
d_0:=\min\{d,\lfloor x\rfloor\}.
\]
Then \(d_0\ge1\), \(d_0\le d\), and
\[
d_0\le b_\epsilon n^2 .
\] % lean: scaled_binary_exact_lower_transfer_all_d
The exact binary source bound gives
\[
a_\epsilon\left(\frac1n+\frac{d_0}{n^2}\right)
\le
\mathsf R^{\mathrm{bin}}_{n,d_0,\epsilon},
\]
where \(\mathsf R^{\mathrm{bin}}_{n,d_0,\epsilon}\) denotes the minimax risk over the uniform-mass exactly homogeneous binary source experiment. % lean: zengBinaryExactHomogeneityLower
Since \(a_\epsilon>0\) and \(n,d_0\ge1\), the source lower-risk level
\[
L_{\mathrm{ex}}:=\frac{a_\epsilon}{2}
\left(\frac1n+\frac{d_0}{n^2}\right)
\]
is strictly below \(\mathsf R^{\mathrm{bin}}_{n,d_0,\epsilon}\).  Hence, for every measurable binary estimator there is a source law \(P\) such that
\[
\frac{a_\epsilon}{2}
\left(\frac1n+\frac{d_0}{n^2}\right)
\le
E_P\left[
\left\{\widehat\psi-\psi(P)\right\}^2
\right].
\] % lean: binaryExactMinimaxRisk_hard_family_of_lt

Embed this source law into the \(d\)-cell real-outcome experiment by zero-mass padding and by the affine outcome map
\[
Y(a)=M\{B(a)-1/2\},\qquad a\in\{0,1\}.
\]
The padding keeps the source cells inside the first \(d_0\) target cells and assigns zero mass to cells \(d_0+1,\ldots,d\).  The affine map gives conditional means in \([-M/2,M/2]\), conditional second central moments bounded by \(M^2\), and exact homogeneity; with the assumed bounds on \(\epsilon\), \(M\), and \(\sigma\), the embedded law belongs to
\[
\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\] % lean: affineBinaryPaddedLaw_model
Write \(\Phi_M(P)\) for this embedded real law.  Its target satisfies
\[
\tau(\Phi_M(P))=M\psi(P),
\]
and the observed-data law is the pushforward of the binary observed-data law under the padded affine sample map.  Thus every \(T\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\) from \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk} induces a measurable binary estimator by applying \(M^{-1}T\) after that sample map, and
\[
M^2\frac{a_\epsilon}{2}
\left(\frac1n+\frac{d_0}{n^2}\right)
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\] % lean: minimaxRisk_ge_of_padded_exact_hard_family

It remains in this branch to compare the capped source rate with the desired all-\(d\) rate.  Since \(1\le x\),
\[
\frac{x}{2}\le \lfloor x\rfloor .
\] % lean: half_le_natFloor_of_one_le
If \(d\le\lfloor x\rfloor\), then \(d_0=d\) and \(d/n^2\le \bar b_\epsilon\le1\).  If \(d>\lfloor x\rfloor\), then
\(d_0=\lfloor x\rfloor\) and \(d_0/n^2\ge \bar b_\epsilon/2\).  In both cases,
\[
\frac{\bar b_\epsilon}{2}
\left\{\frac1n+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}
\le
\frac1n+\frac{d_0}{n^2}.
\] % lean: scaled_binary_exact_lower_transfer_all_d
Since \(c_\epsilon\le a_\epsilon \bar b_\epsilon/4\),
\[
c_\epsilon M^2
\left\{\frac1n+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}
\le
M^2\frac{a_\epsilon}{2}
\left(\frac1n+\frac{d_0}{n^2}\right)
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\] % lean: scaled_binary_exact_lower_transfer_all_d

On the complementary branch, either \(n<N_\epsilon\) or \(\bar b_\epsilon n^2<1\).  In the first case, \(N_\epsilon/n\ge1\); in the second, \(\bar b_\epsilon n\le1\), so \((1/\bar b_\epsilon)/n\ge1\).  Thus, in either case,
\[
\frac1n+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)
\le
\frac{K_\epsilon}{n}.
\] % lean: scaled_binary_exact_lower_transfer_all_d
Using \(c_\epsilon\le c_{\mathrm{par}}/K_\epsilon\) and
\cref{obj:lem:parametric-lower},
\[
c_\epsilon M^2
\left\{\frac1n+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}
\le
\frac{c_{\mathrm{par}}M^2}{n}
\le
\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}.
\] % lean: parametric_lower

Combining the two cases proves the stated bound for all \(n,d\ge1\), \(M\ge1\), and \(0\le\sigma\le2\). % lean: scaled_binary_exact_lower_transfer_all_d
\end{proof}
