\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:robust-upper-construction-resolution}]
Fix an overlap parameter \(\epsilon\) with \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).

\begin{enumerate}
\item First form the all-alphabet package.  By
\cref{obj:lem:continuous-ratio-polynomial-upper-all-d}, there are
\(C_{\mathrm{poly},\epsilon}>0\) and a polynomial handle
\[
\mathfrak h_\epsilon=(N_{\mathrm{cal},\epsilon},\rho_{\mathrm{cal},\epsilon}),
\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{cal},\epsilon}>0,
\]
such that, for all integers \(n,d\ge1\),
\[
N_{\mathrm{cal},\epsilon}\le n,\qquad
d\le \rho_{\mathrm{cal},\epsilon}n\log(en)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
2\le K_n,
\]
and, for every \(M\ge1\), \(0\le\sigma\le2\), and
\(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\),
\[
E_{Q_P^{(n)}}\!\left[
\left(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P)\right)^2
\right]
\le
C_{\mathrm{poly},\epsilon}M^2
\left\{
\frac1n+
\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
\right\}.
\]
The same cited construction gives measurability of \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\) and
\[
T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}(s)\in[-M,M]
\qquad\text{for every }s\in\mathcal O_d^n .
\]

By \cref{obj:lem:continuous-occupancy-collision-upper-all-d}, there is
\(C_{\mathrm{col},\epsilon}>0\) such that, for every \(n,d\ge1\),
\(M\ge1\), \(0\le\sigma\le2\), and
\(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\),
\[
E_{Q_P^{(n)}}\!\left[
\left(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P)\right)^2
\right]
\le
C_{\mathrm{col},\epsilon}M^2
\left(
\frac1n+\sigma^2+\frac{d}{n^2}
\right).
\]
For the admissibility of the collision estimator, write its unclipped inner
statistic as the total sample function
\[
\Gamma_{\mathrm{col}}(s)
:=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle
\frac{1}{R_n(s)}
\sum_{k=1}^d
N_k(s)I_k(s)
\left[
\mathbf 1\{N_{1k}(s)>0\}\frac{S_{1k}(s)}{N_{1k}(s)\vee 1}
-
\mathbf 1\{N_{0k}(s)>0\}\frac{S_{0k}(s)}{N_{0k}(s)\vee 1}
\right],
& R_n(s)>0,\\[1.2em]
0,& R_n(s)=0.
\end{cases}
\]
Here \(m\vee1\) denotes \(\max\{m,1\}\).  The displayed arm factors are total
functions of the sample and agree with the arm-cell means of
\cref{obj:def:collision-handle} on positive arm-cell counts; multiplication by
\(I_k\) selects exactly the cells where both arm-cell counts are positive.  Hence
\cref{obj:def:collision-handle} gives
\[
T_n^{\mathrm{col}}(s)=\max\{-M,\min(M,\Gamma_{\mathrm{col}}(s))\}.
\]
The counts, indicators, outcome totals, finite sums, totalized ratios, and the
fallback at \(R_n=0\) are measurable functions of the sample, so
\(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\) is measurable.  Since \(M\ge1\), clipping gives
\[
T_n^{\mathrm{col}}(s)\in[-M,M]
\qquad\text{for every }s\in\mathcal O_d^n .
\]

Set
\[
C_\epsilon:=\max\{C_{\mathrm{poly},\epsilon},
                 C_{\mathrm{col},\epsilon}\}.
\]
Then \(C_\epsilon>0\).  The two displayed rate factors are nonnegative for
\(n,d\ge1\) and \(0\le\sigma\le2\), and \(M^2\ge0\); hence replacing
\(C_{\mathrm{poly},\epsilon}\) and \(C_{\mathrm{col},\epsilon}\) by
\(C_\epsilon\) preserves the two upper bounds. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.robust_upper_construction_resolution_all_d

\item Define
\[
c_\epsilon:=1.
\]
Then \(c_\epsilon>0\).  For arbitrary \(n,d\ge1\), \(M\ge1\), and
\(0\le\sigma\le2\), the all-alphabet package from the first step gives the
measurability and range assertions for both estimators.  If
\[
d\le c_\epsilon\frac{n^2}{\log(en)},
\]
then for every \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) the same all-alphabet
risk bounds apply under this range condition:
\[
E_{Q_P^{(n)}}\!\left[
\left(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P)\right)^2
\right]
\le
C_\epsilon M^2
\left\{
\frac1n+
\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
\right\},
\]
and
\[
E_{Q_P^{(n)}}\!\left[
\left(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P)\right)^2
\right]
\le
C_\epsilon M^2
\left(
\frac1n+\sigma^2+\frac{d}{n^2}
\right).
\]
Together with the active-range property of \(\mathfrak h_\epsilon\), these are
the asserted constants and calibrated handle. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.DiscreteAteHeterogeneityFrontier.robust_upper_construction_resolution
\end{enumerate}
\end{proof}
