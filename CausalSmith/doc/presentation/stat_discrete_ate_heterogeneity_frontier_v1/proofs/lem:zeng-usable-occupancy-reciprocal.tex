\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:zeng-usable-occupancy-reciprocal}]
Choose an overlap-dependent constant \(c_\epsilon>0\) on the active range \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), and set \(b_\epsilon=c_\epsilon/8\) and \(B_\epsilon\ge\max\{64/c_\epsilon,2\}\), with harmless positive fallback values outside the active range.  For any \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), the active branch applies by the model-class hypotheses. % lean: zengUsableOccupancyReciprocal

The endpoint cases \(d=0\) and \(n=0\) are resolved by the totalized definitions of the counts and reciprocal term, together with the probability-law mass identity. % lean: zengUsableOccupancyReciprocal

Assume \(n,d>0\) and write
\[
\kappa_{n,d}:=c_\epsilon\frac{(n/2)^2}{\max\{n/2,d\}},
\qquad
s_{n,d}:=\frac{n^2}{n\vee d}.
\]
Then \((c_\epsilon/4)s_{n,d}\le\kappa_{n,d}\). % lean: zengUsableOccupancyReciprocal

Under the half-intensity marked-Poisson experiment for the cell-arm marks, fixed overlap gives a lower bound on each positive arm intensity as an \(\epsilon\) fraction of its cell intensity.  The checked Poisson Laplace calculation gives
\[
E\exp\{-R_{n/2}^{\mathrm P}\}\le \exp\{-\kappa_{n,d}\},
\]
and the same calculation bounds the combined zero and reciprocal penalty by
\[
\frac{2}{\kappa_{n,d}}+\exp(-\kappa_{n,d}/2).
\]
% lean: markedPoisson_zero_penalty_bounds

Usable occupancy is monotone in the prefix length of an i.i.d. stream, so half-intensity de-Poissonization transfers the Poisson zero and reciprocal bounds to the fixed \(n\)-sample experiment, losing only the displayed factor of two. % lean: augmented_half_depoissonization

Using \(\kappa_{n,d}\ge(c_\epsilon/4)s_{n,d}\) and \(b_\epsilon=c_\epsilon/8\), the zero probability satisfies
\[
Q_P^{(n)}(R_n=0)\le 2\exp\{-b_\epsilon s_{n,d}\}.
\]
% lean: zengUsableOccupancyReciprocal

The reciprocal term is bounded by the same exponential contribution plus
\[
\frac{64}{c_\epsilon}\frac{n\vee d}{n^2},
\]
which is absorbed by the choice of \(B_\epsilon\).  This gives the asserted reciprocal inequality. % lean: zengUsableOccupancyReciprocal
\end{proof}
