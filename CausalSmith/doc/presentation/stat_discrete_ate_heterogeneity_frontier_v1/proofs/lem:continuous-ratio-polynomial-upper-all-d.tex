\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:continuous-ratio-polynomial-upper-all-d}]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  The fixed-branch theorem supplies a constant \(C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fix}}\) controlling the heavy-set plug-in and light-set polynomial error for every deterministic pilot-induced branch. % lean: polynomial_fixedBranch_uniform_bound

Choose a cutoff \(N_\epsilon\) large enough that the logarithmic degree satisfies \(2\le K_n\) and the logarithmic lower bounds used by the calibration hold for all \(n\ge N_\epsilon\). % lean: polynomialDegree_eventually_two
% lean: logEN_eventually_ge_240

With \(\rho_\epsilon=1\), the pair \((N_\epsilon,\rho_\epsilon)\) is a valid polynomial handle: in the active range \(N_\epsilon\le n\) and \(d\le\rho_\epsilon n\log(en)\), the handle certifies \(2\le K_n\). % lean: continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d

For every \(n,d,M\), the estimator defined by this handle is a measurable total statistic and is clipped into \([-M,M]\). % lean: polyEstimator_total_and_clipped

The operation bound follows from the aggregated implementation of \cref{obj:def:polynomial-handle}: after the pilot pass and the estimation-block aggregation, the light-cell program loops over cells, arms, and polynomial orders. This gives a post-aggregation arithmetic count bounded by \(C_{\mathrm{op}}(d+1)(K_n+1)^2\). % lean: polynomialComplexityBound

On the active range, condition on the pilot block.  The good pilot event places the empirical heavy set inside the deterministic heavy band and the light cells below the quarter-scale \(B/4\); the chosen degree and shift calibration meet the fixed-branch theorem's size hypotheses. % lean: polynomialPilotUpperBand_le_lightScale_quarter
% lean: polynomial_fixedBranch_size_condition
% lean: polynomial_shift_condition

Applying the fixed-branch risk theorem conditionally, and then integrating over the pilot block, gives the active-branch risk bound up to the calibrated polynomial rate. % lean: polynomial_fixedBranch_uniform_bound

The deterministic branch expression produced by that calculation is bounded by
\[
C_\epsilon M^2\left\{n^{-1}+\min(1,u_{n,d})\right\}
\]
after substituting \(K_n\asymp\log(en)\) and \(B\asymp \log(en)/n\). % lean: polynomial_uniformBranch_expression_le_rate

The complement of the good pilot event is exponentially small at the selected threshold, and clipping into \([-M,M]\) absorbs its contribution into the same benchmark. % lean: polynomialPilotGood_compl_probability_calibrated_le
% lean: polynomial_bad_event_absorption

Combining the good-event risk with the clipped bad-event bound gives the active-range mean-squared error bound for \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\). % lean: polynomial_clipped_finiteSelector_risk
% lean: mse_polyEstimator_le_of_clipped_stream
% lean: continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d

Outside the active range, the polynomial handle returns the zero estimator.  Since \(|\tau(P)|\le M\) under the model class and the benchmark is capped by \(1\), this fallback obeys the same all-alphabet bound after increasing the constant. % lean: polyEstimator_uncalibrated_rate
% lean: continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d
\end{proof}
