\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:continuous-ratio-polynomial-upper-all-d}]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  \Cref{obj:lem:polynomial-fixed-branch-risk} supplies a constant \(C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fix}}>0\) for every deterministic heavy set \(H\) satisfying the heavy-band and light-scale eligibility inequalities. % lean: polynomial_fixedBranch_uniform_bound

Choose \(N_\epsilon\) large enough that, for all \(n\ge N_\epsilon\),
\[
8\le n,
\qquad
2\le K_n,
\qquad
240\le L:=\log(en).
\]
% lean: polynomialDegree_eventually_two
% lean: logEN_eventually_ge_240

Set \(\rho_\epsilon=1\).  The pair \((N_\epsilon,\rho_\epsilon)\) is a polynomial handle in the sense of \cref{obj:def:polynomial-handle}: whenever \(0<n\), \(0<d\), \(N_\epsilon\le n\), and \(d\le \rho_\epsilon nL\), the handle certifies \(2\le K_n\). % lean: continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d

For every \(n,d\ge1\) and \(M\ge1\), the estimator determined by this handle is measurable and takes values in \([-M,M]\). % lean: polyEstimator_total_and_clipped

The post-aggregation complexity certificate uses the aggregate table associated with \cref{obj:def:polynomial-handle}: pilot cell counts, estimation-block arm counts, and estimation-block arm outcome sums.  Consider arithmetic programs over this table in which constants and table reads have zero structural cost; arithmetic and comparison nodes have unit cost; a cell sum contributes \(d\) accumulator operations plus the costs of its cell bodies; an \(r\)-term polynomial-order sum contributes \(r\) accumulator operations plus the costs of its term bodies; a falling-factorial primitive of order \(q\) has cost \(q\); and a cell-subtraction primitive has cost one.  For every \(n,d\) and \(M\), there is such a post-aggregation program \(A_{n,d,M}\) computing the handle-indexed estimator on every sample and satisfying
\[
\operatorname{op}(A_{n,d,M})
\le
128(d+1)(K_n+1)^2.
\]
On the active branch, this program evaluates the heavy contribution, the Chebyshev light sums, the cell sum, and the clipping operation from the aggregate table; on the inactive branch, it is the constant-zero program.  Thus the complexity item holds with \(C_{\mathrm{op}}=128\). % lean: polynomialComplexityBound

Now work on the active range \(N_\epsilon\le n\) and \(d\le nL\), and fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) as in \cref{obj:def:model-class}.  With \(m_1=n-\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) and \(B=4096L/m_1\), condition on the pilot block.  The good pilot event is the simultaneous eligibility event
\[
\left\{
\begin{array}{ll}
128L/|I_0|\le p_k, & k\in\widehat{\mathcal H},\\
p_k\le 512L/|I_0|, & k\in\widehat{\mathcal L}.
\end{array}
\right.
\]
On this event, the realized set \(H=\widehat{\mathcal H}\) is eligible with lower band \(128L/|I_0|\) and upper band \(512L/|I_0|\).  Since \(n\ge2\), the calibration gives \(512L/|I_0|\le B/4\), so the light-scale hypothesis of \cref{obj:lem:polynomial-fixed-branch-risk} holds for every \(k\notin H\).  The same active-range calibration gives
\[
4(K_n+2)^2\le m_1,
\qquad
\frac{4(K_n+2)}{m_1}\le \frac{3B}{4}.
\]
% lean: polynomialPilotUpperBand_le_lightScale_quarter
% lean: polynomial_fixedBranch_size_condition
% lean: polynomial_shift_condition

Applying \cref{obj:lem:polynomial-fixed-branch-risk} conditionally on the pilot block, then using the uniform cardinality, total-mass, and missing-envelope bounds built into the fixed-branch simplification, gives for every eligible deterministic branch
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\Delta_H^2\mid I_0\right]
\le R_{n,d}^{\mathrm{br}},
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
R_{n,d}^{\mathrm{br}}
={}&8\left\{
\frac{8}{m_1\epsilon}+\frac{6}{m_1}
+4\left(
\frac{d}{\{((m_1-2)/2)\epsilon\}^2(128L/|I_0|)}
\right)^2
\right\}\\
&+2\left\{
\frac{C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fix}}}{m_1}
+C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fix}}6^{2K_n}
\left(dB^2+\frac{d^2K_n^2B^2}{m_1}\right)
+\left(\frac{dB}{\epsilon K_n^2}\right)^2
\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
% lean: polynomial_fixedBranch_uniform_bound

The rate algebra uses the concrete hypotheses \(8\le n\), \(2\le K_n\), and \(240\le L\) to bound this normalized deterministic-branch expression by
\[
R_{n,d}^{\mathrm{br}}
\le
D_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+u_{n,d}\right\},
\qquad
u_{n,d}:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},
\]
with
\[
D_\epsilon
:=
\frac{128}{\epsilon}+96+\frac{2}{\epsilon^4}
+4C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fix}}
+6C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fix}}8192^2
+2\left(\frac{32768}{\epsilon\alpha_0^2}\right)^2.
\]
% lean: polynomial_uniformBranch_expression_le_rate

At the calibrated pilot bands, the bad-selector probability satisfies
\[
\mathbb P\{\text{the displayed pilot eligibility event fails}\}
\le 2d\exp(-32L).
\]
The finite-selector inequality for the clipped normalized statistic therefore gives a normalized stream-risk bound with penalty \(4\cdot 2d\exp(-32L)=8d\exp(-32L)\).  Since the active range has \(d\le nL\) and the cutoff gives \(240\le L\),
\[
8d\exp(-32L)\le \frac1n.
\]
Thus the clipped normalized active-branch risk is bounded by
\[
(D_\epsilon+1)\left\{\frac1n+u_{n,d}\right\}.
\]
% lean: polynomialPilotGood_compl_probability_calibrated_le
% lean: polynomial_bad_event_absorption

Rescaling the clipped normalized bound by \(M^2\) gives
\[
\operatorname{MSE}_{P}(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}})
\le
M^2(D_\epsilon+1)\left\{\frac1n+u_{n,d}\right\}.
\]
On the active range with \(\rho_\epsilon=1\), \(d\le nL\) implies \(u_{n,d}\le1\), so this is the stated active-range bound with \(\min\{1,u_{n,d}\}=u_{n,d}\). % lean: polynomial_clipped_finiteSelector_risk
% lean: mse_polyEstimator_le_of_clipped_stream
% lean: continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d

It remains to cover the inactive branch of \cref{obj:def:polynomial-handle}.  There the estimator is the zero fallback and \cref{obj:def:model-class} gives \(|\tau(P)|\le M\), hence its mean-squared error is at most \(M^2\).  The fallback rate certificate splits the inactive condition into the two cases used by the handle: if \(n<N_\epsilon\), the finite cutoff makes a constant multiple of \(1/n\) at least one; if \(d>\rho_\epsilon nL\), then \(\min\{1,u_{n,d}\}\) is bounded below by \(\min\{1,\rho_\epsilon^2\}\).  A constant \(C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fb}}>0\) therefore satisfies
\[
M^2
\le
C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fb}}M^2
\left\{\frac1n+\min\{1,u_{n,d}\}\right\}
\]
throughout the inactive branch.  Taking the final risk constant to dominate both \(C_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{fb}}\) and \(D_\epsilon+1\) proves the all-alphabet risk bound. % lean: polyEstimator_uncalibrated_rate
% lean: continuous_ratio_polynomial_upper_all_d
\end{proof}
