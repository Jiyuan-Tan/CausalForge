\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:distinct-cell-factorial-cross-moment}]
Let \(P_O\) be the one-observation marginal of \(Q_P^{(m)}\), and for an observed record \(O=(X,A,Y)\) write \(V=Y/M\).  Define the left coordinate factors
\[
L_q(O):=
\begin{cases}
V\mathbf 1\{X=k,A=a\}, & q=0,\\
\mathbf 1\{X=k\}, & q=1,\\
\mathbf 1\{X=k,A=a\}, & 2\le q\le j+1,
\end{cases}
\]
and the right coordinate factors
\[
R_s(O):=
\begin{cases}
V\mathbf 1\{X=\ell,A=b\}, & s=0,\\
\mathbf 1\{X=\ell\}, & s=1,\\
\mathbf 1\{X=\ell,A=b\}, & 2\le s\le r+1.
\end{cases}
\]
Put
\[
\mu_{k,a,j}:=
\int \prod_{q=0}^{j+1}L_q(O_q)\,
  dP_O^{\otimes(j+2)}((O_q)_q),
\qquad
\mu_{\ell,b,r}:=
\int \prod_{s=0}^{r+1}R_s(O_s)\,
  dP_O^{\otimes(r+2)}((O_s)_s).
\]
For a partial matching \(N\) of size \(h\) between \(\{0,\ldots,j+1\}\) and \(\{0,\ldots,r+1\}\), let \(T_N\) be the merged coordinate set and let \(\iota_L,\iota_R\) send left and right coordinates into \(T_N\), with \(\iota_L(q)=\iota_R(s)\) exactly for matched pairs.  Define
\[
\mathcal M_{j,r}(h):=\{N: |N|=h\},
\qquad
\eta_N:=
\frac{(m)_{(j+2)+(r+2)-h}}{(m)_{j+2}(m)_{r+2}},
\]
and
\[
\Gamma_N:=
\int
\left\{\prod_{q=0}^{j+1}L_q(O_{\iota_L(q)})\right\}
\left\{\prod_{s=0}^{r+1}R_s(O_{\iota_R(s)})\right\}
\,dP_O^{\otimes T_N}((O_t)_{t\in T_N}).
\]
The ordered-factorial covariance expansion gives
\[
\operatorname{Cov}_{Q_P^{(m)}}(U_{k,a,j},U_{\ell,b,r})
=
(\eta_{\varnothing}-1)\mu_{k,a,j}\mu_{\ell,b,r}
+
\sum_{h=1}^{\min(j+2,r+2)}
\sum_{N\in\mathcal M_{j,r}(h)}
\eta_N\Gamma_N .
\]
% lean: centeredCrossMoment_allBlockOrderedMarkedFactorial

Fix \(N\in\mathcal M_{j,r}(h)\) with \(h>0\).  Choose a matched pair \((q,s)\).  At the merged coordinate \(\iota_L(q)=\iota_R(s)\), the factor \(L_q\) contains \(\mathbf 1\{X=k\}\) and the factor \(R_s\) contains \(\mathbf 1\{X=\ell\}\).  Since \(k\ne\ell\), their product is zero pointwise:
\[
\mathbf 1\{X=k\}\mathbf 1\{X=\ell\}=0.
\]
Thus the merged kernel is identically zero and
\[
\Gamma_N=0
\qquad (h>0).
\]
All positive-size matching terms in the expansion therefore vanish. % lean: mergedProductMoment_markedFactorialCoordinate_eq_zero_of_cell_ne

It remains to bound the empty-matching term.  The admissible-order and block-size assumptions give
\[
|\eta_{\varnothing}-1|
\le
\frac{2(K+2)^2}{m}.
\]
For the product means, each one-coordinate mean is bounded by the corresponding cell mass:
\[
\left|\int L_q(O)\,dP_O(O)\right|\le p_k,
\qquad
\left|\int R_s(O)\,dP_O(O)\right|\le p_{\ell}.
\]
For the outcome-marked coordinate, if the relevant cell mass is zero then the arm-cell event is null and the marked integral is zero.  If the relevant cell \(x\) has positive mass, \cref{obj:ass:mean-normalization} and \(M\ge1\) from \cref{obj:def:model-class} give, for the corresponding arm \(c\),
\[
\left|\int y/M\,dP_{Y\mid A=c,X=x}(y)\right|\le \frac12,
\]
so multiplying by the arm-cell probability bounds the marked coordinate by one half of the cell mass.  The selector-only coordinates integrate to \(P_O(X=x)\) or \(P_O(X=x,A=c)\), both bounded by the cell mass.  Since the product-law means factor coordinatewise,
\[
|\mu_{k,a,j}|
\le p_k^{j+2},
\qquad
|\mu_{\ell,b,r}|
\le p_{\ell}^{r+2}.
\]
% lean: marked_emptyMatchingNormalization_sub_one_le
% lean: orderedProductMean_markedFactorialCoordinate_abs_le

Combining the vanished positive-size terms with the preceding bounds yields
\[
\left|\operatorname{Cov}_{Q_P^{(m)}}(U_{k,a,j},U_{\ell,b,r})\right|
\le
\frac{2(K+2)^2}{m}\,
p_k^{j+2}p_{\ell}^{r+2},
\]
which is the claimed bound. % lean: centeredCrossMoment_allBlockOrderedMarkedFactorial_cell_ne_abs_le
\end{proof}
