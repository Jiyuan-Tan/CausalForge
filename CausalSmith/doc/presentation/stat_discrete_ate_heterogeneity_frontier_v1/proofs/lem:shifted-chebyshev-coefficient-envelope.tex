\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:shifted-chebyshev-coefficient-envelope}]
Define the absolute coefficient envelope
\[
\mathcal G_K(x)
:=
\sum_{j=0}^{K-2}|g_{Kj}|x^j .
\]
Let \(T_K\) be the Chebyshev polynomial of the first kind. The shifted expansion gives
\[
T_K(1-2x)
=
1-2K^2x
+
2K^2x^2
\sum_{j=0}^{K-2} g_{Kj}x^j .
\] % lean: chebyshev_gPolynomial_identity
This follows by separating the linear term in the standard shifted-Chebyshev expansion
\[
T_K(1-2x)
=
1+
\sum_{\ell=1}^{K}
(-1)^\ell
\frac{K}{K+\ell}
\binom{K+\ell}{2\ell}
4^\ell x^\ell
\]
and reindexing the terms \(\ell=j+2\), whose coefficients are \(2K^2g_{Kj}\). % lean: shifted_chebyshev_expansion

Since the sign of \(g_{Kj}\) is \((-1)^j\), one has
\[
|g_{Kj}|=(-1)^jg_{Kj},
\qquad
\mathcal G_K(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{K-2}g_{Kj}(-z)^j .
\] % lean: sign_gCoefficient
Putting \(x=-1\) in the shifted identity therefore yields
\[
T_K(3)=1+2K^2+2K^2\mathcal G_K(1).
\] % lean: gpos_eq
Because \(\mathcal G_K(1)\ge0\) and \(K\ge1\),
\[
\mathcal G_K(1)\le T_K(3).
\] % lean: gpos_nonneg
The Chebyshev recurrence \(T_{n+2}(3)=6T_{n+1}(3)-T_n(3)\), with the initial cases
\(T_0(3)=1\) and \(T_1(3)=3\), gives
\[
T_K(3)\le 6^K .
\] % lean: chebyshev_three_le
If \(0\le x\le1\), then termwise monotonicity gives
\[
\mathcal G_K(x)
=
\sum_{j=0}^{K-2}|g_{Kj}|x^j
\le
\sum_{j=0}^{K-2}|g_{Kj}|
=
\mathcal G_K(1)
\le 6^K .
\] % lean: shiftedCoefficientEnvelope_le
% lean: shiftedCoefficientEnvelope_le_six_pow

Now set
\[
x:=\frac pB .
\]
The assumptions \(B>0\), \(p\ge0\), and \(p\le B\) imply
\[
0\le x\le1 .
\] % lean: shiftedCoefficient_weighted_sum_le_of_le
For each \(j\), using \(B>0\),
\[
\left|\frac{g_{Kj}}{B^{j+1}}\right|p^{j+2}
=
\frac{p^2}{B}\,|g_{Kj}|
\left(\frac pB\right)^j .
\]
Summing this identity over \(j=0,\ldots,K-2\) gives
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{K-2}
\left|\frac{g_{Kj}}{B^{j+1}}\right|p^{j+2}
=
\frac{p^2}{B}\,
\mathcal G_K\!\left(\frac pB\right).
\]
The envelope bound at \(x=p/B\) and the inequality \(p^2\le B^2\) give
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{K-2}
\left|\frac{g_{Kj}}{B^{j+1}}\right|p^{j+2}
\le
\frac{p^2}{B}6^K
\le
B6^K .
\]
\end{proof}
