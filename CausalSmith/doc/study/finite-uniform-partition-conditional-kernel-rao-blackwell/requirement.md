# Substrate requirement: finite-uniform-partition-conditional-kernel-rao-blackwell

## Goal

Build a reusable finite conditional-kernel and Rao--Blackwell reduction for experiments generated by a uniformly selected fixed-size partition or allocation.

## Provides (API contract)

- A finite conditional Markov kernel for a latent finite state given a coarsened finite count/statistic under a uniform fixed-size partition law.
- A finite-sum disintegration identity for the joint law, statistic marginal, and conditional kernel, including zero-marginal fibers.
- A real-valued conditional-mean estimator and a squared-loss Rao--Blackwell theorem showing risk weakly decreases statewise or under any finite prior.
- A bridge compatible with `Causalean.Stat.Minimax.FiniteKernelBayes`, finite labeled states, and real loss without `ENNReal` conversion.

## Statement / milestones

For finite latent, allocation, observation, and statistic types, a uniform law on a nonempty finite fixed-size allocation set, a finite observation kernel, and a statistic map, define the statistic mass and the guarded conditional kernel of the latent/observation data on each statistic fiber. Prove nonnegativity and normalization on positive-mass fibers, and prove exact finite-sum disintegration for every test function.

Given a real estimator on the full observation, define its conditional mean given the statistic, using a guarded value on zero-mass fibers. For any real target depending on the latent state, prove the conditional Jensen inequality and hence that the conditional-mean estimator has no larger squared-error risk. Supply finite-prior and minimax-compatible corollaries.

## Standard reference

This is the finite-space disintegration formula and the classical Rao--Blackwell theorem for squared loss. The proof is finite Bayes conditioning followed by Jensen's inequality; zero-probability fibers are handled by a guarded kernel.

## Intended reuse

The immediate consumer uniformly partitions labeled units into fixed-size allocation cells, coarsens the observed binary schedule to a scalar count, and requires estimator-wise domination through that count. The substrate must remain generic enough for other finite randomized experiments and compose with `Causalean.Stat.Minimax.FiniteKernelBayes`.

## May assume / must derive

May assume all carrier types are finite, the admissible allocation set is nonempty, the allocation law is uniform on it, the observation kernel is nonnegative and normalized, and the target/estimator are real-valued. Must derive the conditional kernel, positive-fiber normalization, zero-fiber safety, exact disintegration, conditional-mean measurability in the finite setting, conditional Jensen, squared-loss contraction, and finite-prior/minimax-compatible forms.

## Non-goals (optional)

- Do not formalize two-arm response types, effect triples, binomial coefficients, schedule counts, or the paper's minimax constants.
- Do not import any `CausalSmith/*_Research` module.
- Do not move the experiment-specific scalar-kernel identity into the substrate.
- No `sorry`, `admit`, `native_decide`, or custom axioms may remain.
