\textbf{Proof.}
Assume \(K=0\) and \(S_\eta=\varnothing\) for every \(\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  There is then only the indicator-free observational branch.  By \(\texttt{def:twin-graph}\), for each compatible \(\eta\), the graph \(\operatorname{Tw}(\eta,0)\) is the DAG \(\mathcal G_\eta[V]\): there is no basal system copy, shared-noise vertex, intervention-indicator vertex, or conditioned selection vertex.  By \(\texttt{def:mag-projection}\), marginalizing and conditioning the empty sets leaves
\[
\mathcal M_\eta^{(0)}=\mathcal G_\eta[V].
\]

We next identify exactly which DAGs occur.  Membership in \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\), as specified by \(\texttt{def:dai-compatible-class}\), implies that every \(\mathcal G_\eta[V]\) is a finite causally sufficient DAG Markov equivalent to \(\mathcal M^{(0)}\).  Conversely, let \(G\) be any DAG on \(V\) in that same Markov-equivalence class.  Taking \(\mathcal G_\eta=G\), \(S_\eta=\varnothing\), and \(I_\eta^{(0)}=\varnothing\) satisfies every clause of \(\texttt{def:dai-compatible-class}\): all selection clauses are vacuous, the observational-target clause holds by construction, there is no nonobservational modularity or equivalence clause because \(\mathcal K_+=\varnothing\), and observational equivalence holds by the choice of \(G\).  Hence the projected graphs indexed by \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) are exactly the ordinary observational DAG Markov-equivalence class.

By \(\texttt{def:canonical-si-pag}\), an endpoint of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}^{(0)}(\mathbf M)\) is directed precisely when its direction is common to every DAG in this class, and it is circular precisely when both directions occur.  By \(\texttt{lem:cpdag-endpoint-invariance}\), this endpoint-invariance representative is the ordinary CPDAG and agrees endpointwise with complete PC/Meek orientation.  Finally, \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) gives
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)
=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M).
\]
Because \(K=0\), the family on the right has only component zero, so this equality is exactly
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)
=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}^{(0)}(\mathbf M),
\]
and the common graph is the stated CPDAG.  This argument compares only the two outputs.  It invokes no runtime equivalence: the enumerative bound of \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) remains distinct from the polynomial implementation of PC/Meek.  No positivity, overlap, support, rank, inverse, division, boundary-limit, or sampling condition is used. \(\square\)
