For every compatible oracle family \(\mathbf M\), normalized-certificate enumeration in \(\texttt{def:complete-cdis}\) terminates and satisfies
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M).
\]
For every endpoint left circular, the retained tail and arrowhead certificates reconstruct two members of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) that agree with every fixed output mark and realize the two opposite marks at that endpoint.  The enumeration visits at most
\[
3^{\binom D2}2^{2^D+DK}
\]
certificates and admits the crude finite runtime bound
\[
O\!\left(3^{\binom D2}2^{2^D+DK}
\left[D^2 2^D+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]\right).
\]
This is an exact, generally doubly exponential population algorithm, not a polynomial local-rule completeness result.
