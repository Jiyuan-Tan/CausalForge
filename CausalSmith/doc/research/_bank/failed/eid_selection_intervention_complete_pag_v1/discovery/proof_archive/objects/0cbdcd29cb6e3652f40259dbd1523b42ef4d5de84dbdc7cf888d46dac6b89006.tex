This note uses those formulas to prove an input-conditioned two-section quotient: for fixed \((E,A)\), the observational skeleton forces \(Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)=S_0(\mathbf M)\setminus B(E,A)\), eliminating independent enumeration of \(Q\).  Zhang2008CompleteFCI gives complete orientation for one ordinary MAG class, while JaberKocaogluShanmugamBareinboim2020 treats unknown soft targets without selection; neither supplies the common-basal witnesses required here.  LuoEtAl2026PostTreatmentSelection studies post-treatment selection and F-PAG semantics, not Dai et al.'s basal-selection SI-PAG.
