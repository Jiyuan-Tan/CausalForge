For every compatible encoded oracle family \(\mathbf M\), the enumerative algorithm satisfies \(\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M)\), is population sound and complete, and returns actual paired compatible certificates for every residual circle.  It terminates after checking at most \(6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\) normalized Dai two-section certificates, with crude runtime
\[
O\!\left(6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]\right).
\]
The global search is singly exponential in \(D^2+DK\).  The observational output is on \(V\), each nonobservational certificate component is on \(V\cup\{\zeta_k\}\) for \(k\in\mathcal K_+\), and no polynomial claim is made for this global search or for the conditional-independence and invariance queries needed to obtain \(\mathbf M\).  No completeness assertion is made for a fixed polynomial local CDIS calculus.
