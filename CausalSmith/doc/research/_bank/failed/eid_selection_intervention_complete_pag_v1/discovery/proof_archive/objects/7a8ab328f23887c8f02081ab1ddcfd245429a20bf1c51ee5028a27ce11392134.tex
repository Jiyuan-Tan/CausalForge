Let \(S_0=\operatorname{Adj}(\mathcal M^{(0)})\).  For a candidate basal edge set \(E\) and ancestor-closed selected set \(A\), let \(B(E,A)\) consist of the unordered pairs joined by a basal edge or sharing an observed collider child in \(A\).  The sibling-owned normalization and realizability results prove that it suffices to reject unless \(B(E,A)\subseteq S_0\), set the unique residual \(Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)=S_0\setminus B(E,A)\), and reject unless \(Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)\subseteq\binom A2\).  Enumeration over only \(E,A,(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}\) then visits at most \(3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\) candidates.  The results \(\texttt{thm:finite-common-realizability}\), \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\), and \(\texttt{thm:complete-population-cdis}\) establish, on every compatible oracle family, that the accepted candidates are exactly the common realizations, that endpointwise feasible-mark invariance equals \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M)\), and that every residual circle has two accepted common-basal certificates realizing its opposing marks.  With per-candidate work \(O(D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}})\), the total global-search bound is \[O\!\left(3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]\right).\] Independently, for any separately supplied fixed finite monotone rule list encoded by \(\texttt{def:finite-rule-encoding}\), \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves termination after at most \(N_{\mathrm{end}}\) strict endpoint updates and one terminal scan, with mark-propagation time \[O\!\left((K+1)(D+1)^3+(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)\right).\] Thus exact population SI-PAG computation and paired witnesses are available by a singly exponential search in \(D^2+DK\), including \(K=0\), while closure is polynomial for any fixed finite rule list.  This proves neither the existence nor completeness of the requested fixed local calculus and makes no polynomial conditional-independence-discovery claim.
