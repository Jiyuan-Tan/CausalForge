\textbf{Proof.}
For a normalized certificate \(\mathcal R=(E,H,(T_k))\), construct \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\) on \(V\cup\{s_h:h\in H\}\) by using the edges of \(E\), adding \(i\to s_h\) exactly for \(i\in h\), and taking \(I_{\eta_{\mathcal R}}^{(0)}=\varnothing\) and \(I_{\eta_{\mathcal R}}^{(k)}=T_k\).  Choose mutually independent exogenous inputs, arbitrary nondegenerate structural mechanisms on \(V\), binary childless mechanisms for the \(s_h\), and modular soft replacements exactly on \(T_k\).  This realizes every structural condition in \(\texttt{def:dai-compatible-class}\); no division, positivity, overlap, rank, or boundary assumption is required for this purely graphical construction.  By \(\texttt{lem:dai-certificate-mag-characterization}\), its regime projections are exactly \(\mathbf N(\mathcal R)\).

First suppose \(\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  By \(\texttt{lem:selection-normalization}\), replace it without changing any projected MAG by a normalized candidate \(\bar\eta\), and let \(\mathcal R_{\bar\eta}\) be its certificate.  Markov-equivalent MAGs have the same adjacencies and unshielded colliders.  Thus each component of \(\mathbf N(\mathcal R_{\bar\eta})\) has the adjacency and collider pattern of the corresponding supplied component, so the checker accepts.

Conversely, suppose the checker accepts \(\mathcal R\).  By \(\operatorname{ass{:}compatible\text{-}input}\), choose an anchor \(\eta_0\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  For every \(k\), compatibility of \(\eta_0\) implies that \(\mathcal M_{eta_0}^{(k)}\) and \(\mathcal M^{(k)}\) have the same adjacencies and unshielded colliders.  Acceptance gives the same statement for \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\) and \(\mathcal M^{(k)}\).  Hence \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\) and \(\mathcal M_{eta_0}^{(k)}\) have identical adjacency and unshielded-collider patterns in every regime.  Both families arise from Dai twin constructions, so the equivalence clause of \(\texttt{lem:dai-certificate-mag-characterization}\) gives
\[
\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\equiv_m\mathcal M_{eta_0}^{(k)}
\equiv_m\mathcal M^{(k)}
\qquad(k\in\mathcal K).
\]
Therefore \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  The two constructions establish the asserted correspondence.  The displayed existence equivalence follows immediately. \(\square\)
