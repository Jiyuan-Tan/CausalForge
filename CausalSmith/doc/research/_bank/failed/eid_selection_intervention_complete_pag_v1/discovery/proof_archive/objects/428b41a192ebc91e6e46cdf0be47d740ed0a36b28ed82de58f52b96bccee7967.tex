Selective enrollment precedes intervention in the target setting, so the indicator-free observational MAG on \(V\) and the nonobservational MAGs on \(V\cup\{\zeta_k\}\) must be projections of one basal DAG rather than independently completed equivalence classes.  The paper defines the canonical SI-PAG by endpoint-mark invariance over Dai et al.'s full causally sufficient pre-treatment-selection class and computes it exactly by enumerating normalized common-realization certificates.  The procedure returns a global tail witness and a global arrowhead witness for every circle and has the explicit, generally doubly exponential runtime \(O\!\left(3^{\binom D2}2^{2^D+DK}[D^2 2^D+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}]\right)\).  Constructing a complete fixed finite local orientation calculus remains open; no polynomial claim is made for global enumeration or for discovering the oracle regime MAGs.
