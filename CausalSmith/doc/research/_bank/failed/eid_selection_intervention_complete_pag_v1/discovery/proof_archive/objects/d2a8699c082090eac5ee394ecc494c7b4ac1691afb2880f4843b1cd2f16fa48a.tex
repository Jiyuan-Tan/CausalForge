\textbf{Proof.}
The matrix family in \(\texttt{def:oracle-input-encoding}\) is exactly the input consumed by \(\texttt{def:complete-cdis}\).  Applying \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) gives
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)
=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M),
\]
the candidate-count bound
\[
3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK},
\]
and the runtime bound
\[
O\!\left(
3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]
\right).
\]
The endpointwise feasible-mark identity in that theorem proves population soundness because every fixed output mark occurs in every compatible member.  It proves population completeness because every invariant mark is fixed, whereas every residual circle is accompanied by accepted tail and arrowhead certificates.  By \(\texttt{thm:finite-common-realizability}\), those certificates reconstruct actual members of the full Dai compatibility class and are globally coherent across all regimes.

The domain statement follows from the two branches of \(\texttt{def:dai-certificate-checker}\): \(\mathcal N^{(0)}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})\) is on \(V\) and contains no intervention indicator, while \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})\) is on \(V\cup\{\zeta_k\}\) only for \(k\in\mathcal K_+\).  Moreover,
\[
\log\!\left(3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\right)=O(D^2+DK),
\]
so the displayed complexity is a singly exponential upper bound, not a polynomial or tight bound.  The procedure starts from supplied oracle MAG matrices; its correctness and runtime do not include their discovery by conditional-independence or invariance queries.  No step invokes a complete fixed local calculus, which remains the separate open-ended question.  The cases \(K=0\) and empty selection summary are covered, and no numerical overlap, rank, inverse, or division assumption is used. \(\square\)
