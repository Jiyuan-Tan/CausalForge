For every finite encoded monotone \(\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}\), the stated implementation terminates after at most \(N_{\mathrm{end}}\) strict endpoint updates and has total oracle-input runtime \(T_{\mathrm{cl}}(D,K)\).  If \(\texttt{thm:mark-completeness}\) is established, then the definition gives the equation chain \[\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\operatorname{Cl}_{\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{init}}(\mathbf M))=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M).\]The output domains follow directly from \(\texttt{def:complete-cdis}\): the observational component is on \(V\), and component \(k\in\mathcal K_+\) is on \(V\cup\{\zeta_k\}\).  All costs in the runtime proof begin with the already supplied oracle matrices, so they make no claim about discovering those matrices by conditional-independence or invariance queries.
