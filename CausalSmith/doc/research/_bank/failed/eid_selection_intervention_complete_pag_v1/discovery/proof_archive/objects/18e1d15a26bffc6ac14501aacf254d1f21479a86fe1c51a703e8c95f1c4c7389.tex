For every \(\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\), let \(E=\mathcal G_\eta[V]\), let
\[
A=\operatorname{an}_E\!\left(\bigcup_{s\in S_\eta}\operatorname{pa}_{\mathcal G_\eta}(s)\right),
\]
and let \(Q_\eta\) contain \(\{i,j\}\) exactly when some \(s\in S_\eta\) has \(\{i,j\}\subseteq\operatorname{pa}_{\mathcal G_\eta}(s)\).  Then
\[
B(E,A)\cup Q_\eta=S_0(\mathbf M),
\qquad
Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)=S_0(\mathbf M)\setminus B(E,A)\subseteq Q_\eta.
\]
For \(k\in\mathcal K_+\), put \(T_k=I_\eta^{(k)}\) and \(F_k=\operatorname{de}_E(T_k)\).  The supplied indicator-neighborhood satisfies
\[
Z_k(\mathbf M)=T_k\cup(F_k\cap A),
\qquad
T_k\setminus A=O_k(\mathbf M,A),
\qquad
F_k\cap A=R_k(\mathbf M,A),
\]
the set \(R_k(\mathbf M,A)\) is descendant-closed in \(E[A]\), and the derived target residual obeys
\[
\operatorname{de}_E\!\left(T^*_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\right)=F_k.
\]
Consequently \(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M}(E,A)\) is an input-conditioned normalized target-residual certificate.  Replacing the selection nodes by the pair-node and isolated-singleton reconstruction and replacing every nonobservational target by \(T^*_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\) produces \(\bar\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) with exactly the same projected MAG in every regime.  Conversely, every normalized target-residual certificate is the exact \((A,Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A))\) selection summary with the uniquely derived target family of this reconstruction, which uses at most \(\binom D2+D\) selection nodes.  When \(K=0\), there are no target-residual objects or target replacements.
