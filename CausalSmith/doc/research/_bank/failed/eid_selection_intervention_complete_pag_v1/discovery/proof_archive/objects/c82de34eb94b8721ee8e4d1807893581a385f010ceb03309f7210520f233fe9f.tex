\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a normalized Dai two-section certificate \(\mathcal R=(E,A,Q,(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+})\).  By \(\texttt{def:normalized-dai-certificate}\), \(E\) is acyclic, \(\operatorname{an}_E(A)=A\), and \(Q\subseteq\binom A2\).  Form a selection structure with one childless node having parents \(\{i,j\}\) for every \(\{i,j\}\in Q\), and one childless node having singleton parent \(\{i\}\) for every \(Q\)-isolated \(i\in A\).  The union of these parent sets is exactly \(A\), so its ancestor closure is
\[
\operatorname{an}_E(A)=A,
\]
and two distinct observed vertices share a selection child exactly when their pair belongs to \(Q\).  Thus this construction has precisely the summary \((E,A,Q,(T_k))\).

Apply \(\texttt{lem:dai-two-section-substrate}\).  Its observational adjacency cases are, respectively, an edge of \(E\), an observed common child in \(A\), and a pair in \(Q\); these are exactly the three clauses defining \(\mathcal N^{(0)}(\mathcal R)\).  Its observational endpoint criterion is exactly membership in \(A\) or ancestry of the opposite endpoint.  With \(F_k=\operatorname{de}_E(T_k)\), its intervention-induced path condition, indicator adjacency set \(T_k\cup(F_k\cap A)\), and four endpoint cases coincide term by term with \(\texttt{def:dai-certificate-checker}\).  Hence the checker construction is precisely the regime-MAG family supplied by Dai et al.'s formulas.  The final equivalence assertion follows directly from the Theorem 2 clause of the cited substrate.  This is a finite graph identity; no support, overlap, rank, inverse, or division condition is used. \(\square\)
