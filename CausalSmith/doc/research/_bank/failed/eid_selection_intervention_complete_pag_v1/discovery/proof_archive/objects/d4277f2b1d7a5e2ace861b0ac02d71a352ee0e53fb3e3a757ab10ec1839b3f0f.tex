For every compatible oracle family \(\mathbf M\), input-conditioned residual-certificate enumeration in \(\texttt{def:complete-cdis}\) terminates and satisfies
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M).
\]
For every endpoint left circular, the retained tail and arrowhead certificates reconstruct two members of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) that agree with every fixed output mark and realize the two opposite marks at that endpoint.  Because \(Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)\) is uniquely computed rather than enumerated, the algorithm visits at most
\[
3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\]
candidates and admits the crude finite runtime bound
\[
O\!\left(3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]\right).
\]
This is an exact population algorithm with a singly exponential upper bound in \(D^2+DK\), not a polynomial global search, not a tight complexity claim, and not a polynomial local-rule completeness result.
