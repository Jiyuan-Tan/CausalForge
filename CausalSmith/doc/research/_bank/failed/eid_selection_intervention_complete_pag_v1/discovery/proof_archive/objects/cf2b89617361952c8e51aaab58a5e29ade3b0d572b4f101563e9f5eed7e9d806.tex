\textbf{Proof.}
Fix an input-conditioned normalized certificate
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathbf M}
=\bigl(E,A,Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A),(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}\bigr).
\]
Its canonical reconstruction retains the basal DAG \(E\), adds one binary childless selection node with parent set \(\{i,j\}\) for every residual pair, adds one binary childless singleton-parent node for every residual-graph-isolated vertex of \(A\), and sets
\[
I^{(0)}=\varnothing,
\qquad
I^{(k)}=T_k\quad(k\in\mathcal K_+).
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:selection-normalization}\), this reconstruction has exact summary \((A,Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A))\), is finite, and satisfies the structural clauses of the full published class.  Choosing every reconstructed Bernoulli selection kernel strictly between zero and one on every parent configuration gives positive probability to the finite joint selected stratum.  By \(\texttt{lem:dai-two-section-checker-characterization}\), its projected regime family is exactly
\[
\mathbf N(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})
=\bigl(\mathcal N^{(0)}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M}),\ldots,
\mathcal N^{(K)}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})\bigr).
\]

Suppose first that the canonical reconstruction belongs to \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Then \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})\equiv_m\mathcal M^{(k)}\) for every \(k\in\mathcal K\).  Markov-equivalent MAGs have identical adjacencies and unshielded collider triples, so every checker comparison succeeds:
\[
\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M},\mathbf M)=1.
\]

Conversely, suppose the checker accepts.  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:compatible-input}\) supplies an anchor \(\eta_0\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Thus \(\mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}\equiv_m\mathcal M^{(k)}\), so these two graphs have identical adjacency and unshielded-collider patterns.  Acceptance supplies the same two patterns for \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})\) and \(\mathcal M^{(k)}\).  Both \(\mathbf N(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})\) and the family generated by \(\eta_0\) arise from a single basal DAG, childless pre-treatment selection, and one ordered target family.  The cross-regime equivalence criterion in \(\texttt{lem:dai-two-section-checker-characterization}\) therefore gives
\[
\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M})
\equiv_m\mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}
\equiv_m\mathcal M^{(k)}
\qquad(k\in\mathcal K).
\]
The other membership clauses hold by construction, so the canonical reconstruction lies in \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).

For exhaustiveness, take any \(\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  The forward direction of \(\texttt{lem:selection-normalization}\) constructs its residual certificate \(\mathcal R_\eta^*\) and a canonical reconstruction with exactly the same fully oriented projected MAG in every regime.  The implication already proved gives
\[
\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R_\eta^*,\mathbf M)=1.
\]
Conversely, every passing residual certificate reconstructs a compatible member by the reverse implication.  Hence compatible models and passing residual certificates realize exactly the same regime-MAG families.  In particular,
\[
\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists E,A,(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}\;
\operatorname{Check}_{D}\!\left(
(E,A,Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A),(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}),\mathbf M
\right)=1.
\]
When \(K=0\), the ordered nonobservational target family is empty and the same argument applies.  No overlap, rank, inverse, or division condition is used. \(\square\)
