The proved contribution is population mark completeness for Dai et al.'s full causally sufficient pre-treatment-selection compatibility class through normalized-certificate enumeration.  Regime zero is indicator-free on \(V\), while only regimes in \(\mathcal K_+\) retain \(\zeta_k\).  The algorithm is finite and computable but generally doubly exponential; no polynomial claim is made for the global search, for conditional-independence or invariance discovery of the oracle MAGs, or for a fixed local CDIS calculus.  Latent common causes among vertices in \(V\), post-treatment selection, finite-sample guarantees, and causal-effect identification are outside scope.  In the \(K=0\), no-selection boundary, the output agrees extensionally with the ordinary CPDAG without claiming that the enumerative implementation becomes PC.
