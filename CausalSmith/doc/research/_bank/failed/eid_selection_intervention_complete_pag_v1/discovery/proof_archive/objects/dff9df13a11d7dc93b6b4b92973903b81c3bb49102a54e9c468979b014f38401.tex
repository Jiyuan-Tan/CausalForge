For any separately supplied finite monotone rule list, \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves termination after at most \(N_{\mathrm{end}}=D(D-1)+KD(D+1)\) strict endpoint updates and the mark-propagation bound
\[
+O\!\left((K+1)(D+1)^3+(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)\right).
\]
Independently, \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) computes the exact canonical SI-PAG on the full compatibility class and returns two globally compatible opposing certificates for each canonical circle. It checks at most
\[
+6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\]
normalized certificates, with total running-time bound
\[
+O\!\left(6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]\right).
\]
Thus exact population mark completeness and paired witnesses are computable by a singly exponential global search, while any separately supplied finite local rule system has polynomial mark-closure time. What is not proved is the existence of one uniform finite sound-and-complete local calculus joining these two facts. This partial result makes no polynomial conditional-independence-discovery claim.
