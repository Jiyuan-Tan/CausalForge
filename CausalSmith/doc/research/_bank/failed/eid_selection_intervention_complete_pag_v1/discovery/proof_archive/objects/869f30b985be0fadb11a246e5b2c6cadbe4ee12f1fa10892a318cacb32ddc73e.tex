\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a normalized Dai two-section certificate
\[
\mathcal R=(E,A,Q,(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}).
\]
Its canonical reconstruction \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\) uses the observed DAG \(E\), one binary childless selection node with parents \(\{i,j\}\) for each \(\{i,j\}\in Q\), one binary childless selection node with parent \(\{i\}\) for each \(Q\)-isolated \(i\in A\), and no empty-parent selection node.  Set
\[
I_{\eta_{\mathcal R}}^{(0)}=\varnothing,
\qquad
I_{\eta_{\mathcal R}}^{(k)}=T_k
\quad(k\in\mathcal K_+).
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:selection-normalization}\), this reconstruction has exact selection summary \((A,Q)\), is finite, and satisfies the structural clauses of the published class.  Choose parent-dependent Bernoulli kernels taking values strictly between zero and one for the finitely many reconstructed selection nodes.  Their joint selected stratum then has positive probability, so the conditioning operation is well-defined without an additional positivity assumption.  The class and checker remain graphical; no overlap, rank, inverse, or division condition is used.  By \(\texttt{lem:dai-two-section-checker-characterization}\), the projected regime family is exactly
\[
\mathbf N(\mathcal R)
=\bigl(\mathcal N^{(0)}(\mathcal R),\ldots,\mathcal N^{(K)}(\mathcal R)\bigr).
\]

Suppose first that \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Then \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\equiv_m\mathcal M^{(k)}\) for every \(k\in\mathcal K\).  Markov-equivalent MAGs have the same adjacencies and unshielded collider triples, so every comparison in \(\operatorname{Check}_{D}\) succeeds and
\[
\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)=1.
\]

Conversely, suppose \(\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)=1\).  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:compatible-input}\) supplies an anchor \(\eta_0\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Compatibility of \(\eta_0\) implies \(\mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}\equiv_m\mathcal M^{(k)}\), hence those two MAGs have the same adjacencies and unshielded colliders.  Checker acceptance gives the same two patterns for \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\) and \(\mathcal M^{(k)}\).  Therefore \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\) and \(\mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}\) have identical adjacency and unshielded-collider patterns for every \(k\in\mathcal K\).  Both families arise from one basal DAG, childless pre-treatment selection, and one ordered target family.  The equivalence criterion in \(\texttt{lem:dai-two-section-checker-characterization}\) now yields
\[
\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\equiv_m\mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}
\equiv_m\mathcal M^{(k)}
\qquad(k\in\mathcal K).
\]
All other membership clauses were verified by the canonical reconstruction, so \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).

It remains to prove exhaustiveness.  Given any \(\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\), \(\texttt{lem:selection-normalization}\) gives its certificate \(\mathcal R_\eta=(E,A,Q,(T_k))\) and a canonical reconstruction having exactly the same projected MAGs.  The forward implication just proved gives
\[
\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R_\eta,\mathbf M)=1.
\]
Conversely, the checker equivalence shows that every passing certificate reconstructs a member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Thus compatible candidates and passing normalized certificates realize exactly the same regime-MAG families.  In particular,
\[
\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\mathcal R\;\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)=1.
\]
The boundary case \(K=0\) is covered by the same construction, with an empty target family. \(\square\)
