\textbf{Proof.}
The matrix family in \(\texttt{def:oracle-input-encoding}\) is exactly the oracle input consumed by \(\texttt{def:complete-cdis}\).  Applying \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) gives
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)
=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M),
\]
the certificate-count bound
\[
6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK},
\]
and the runtime bound
\[
O\!\left(
6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]
\right).
\]
The endpointwise feasible-mark identity in that theorem proves population soundness because every fixed output mark occurs in every compatible member.  It proves population completeness because every invariant mark is fixed, whereas every residual circle is accompanied by accepted tail and arrowhead certificates.  By \(\texttt{thm:finite-common-realizability}\), the retained certificates reconstruct actual members of the full Dai compatibility class and hence are globally coherent across all regimes.

The domain statement follows from the two branches of \(\texttt{def:dai-certificate-checker}\): \(\mathcal N^{(0)}(\mathcal R)\) is on \(V\) and contains no intervention indicator, while \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\) is on \(V\cup\{\zeta_k\}\) only for \(k\in\mathcal K_+\).  Since
\[
\log\!\left(6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\right)=O(D^2+DK),
\]
the global enumeration is singly exponential in \(D^2+DK\), but it is not polynomial.  The procedure starts from supplied oracle MAG matrices, so its correctness and runtime do not include their discovery by conditional-independence or invariance queries.  Finally, no step invokes a complete fixed local calculus; that construction remains the separate open question \(\texttt{oeq:si-extension-calculus}\).  The boundary cases \(K=0\) and empty selection summary are covered, and no numerical support, overlap, rank, or division assumption is used. \(\square\)
