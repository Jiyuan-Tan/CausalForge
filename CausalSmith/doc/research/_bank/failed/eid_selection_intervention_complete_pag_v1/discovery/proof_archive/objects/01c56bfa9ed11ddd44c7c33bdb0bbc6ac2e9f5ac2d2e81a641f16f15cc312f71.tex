\textbf{Proof.}
When \(K=0\), there is only the indicator-free observational branch.  Under the stated condition \(S_\eta=\varnothing\), \(\operatorname{Tw}(\eta,0)\) is simply the DAG \(\mathcal G_\eta[V]\), with no marginalized basal copy, shared-noise vertex, indicator, or conditioned selection vertex.  Its MAG projection is therefore that DAG itself.  Conversely, every DAG on \(V\) in the same observational DAG Markov-equivalence class defines a candidate by taking empty selection and the empty observational target.  Thus the projected graphs indexed by \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) are exactly the ordinary observational DAG Markov-equivalence class.

By \(\texttt{def:canonical-si-pag}\), its observational endpoint is fixed precisely when every DAG in this class has that direction.  The classical CPDAG representation in \(\texttt{lem:cpdag-endpoint-invariance}\) says that this endpointwise invariant graph is the CPDAG and is the output of complete PC/Meek orientation.  Finally, \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) gives \(\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M)\).  Since there is only component zero, the displayed equality and extensional reduction follow.  The proof uses no runtime comparison between the two implementations. \(\square\)
