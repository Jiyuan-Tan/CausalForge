For every separately supplied finite monotone rule list represented by \(\texttt{def:finite-rule-encoding}\), \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves termination after at most \(N_{\mathrm{end}}=D(D-1)+KD(D+1)\) strict endpoint updates and one terminal no-update scan, with oracle-input mark-propagation time
\[
O\!\left((K+1)(D+1)^3+(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)\right).
\]
Independently, \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) proves that normalized Dai two-section certificate enumeration equals the canonical SI-PAG on the full compatibility class and returns two globally compatible opposing certificates for every canonical circle. It visits at most
\[
6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\]
certificates and has total running time
\[
O\!\left(6^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]\right).
\]
Hence exact population mark completeness, including the boundary case \(K=0\), and paired common-realization witnesses are computable by a singly exponential global search, while mark closure is polynomial for any separately supplied fixed finite rule system. No polynomial conditional-independence-discovery claim is made. The unresolved step is precisely the existence and completeness proof of one fixed local within- and cross-regime calculus joining these two established facts.
