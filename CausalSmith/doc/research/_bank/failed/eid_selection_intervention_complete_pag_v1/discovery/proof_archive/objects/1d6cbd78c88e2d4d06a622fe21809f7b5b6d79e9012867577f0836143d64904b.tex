The note gives an exact finite population CDIS map on Dai et al.'s full class by enumerating only basal DAG states on the supplied observational skeleton and ancestor-closed selection summaries, deriving rather than enumerating co-selection and target residuals, and taking endpointwise feasible-mark invariance.  It proves the candidate bound \(3^{m_0(\mathbf M)}2^D\), the runtime \(O(3^{m_0(\mathbf M)}2^D[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}])\), and paired common-basal witnesses, while leaving fixed-local-calculus completeness as a separate open question.
