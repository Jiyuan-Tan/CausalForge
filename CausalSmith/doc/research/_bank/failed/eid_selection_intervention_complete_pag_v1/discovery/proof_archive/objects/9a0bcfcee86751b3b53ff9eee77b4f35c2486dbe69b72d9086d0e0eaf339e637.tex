\textbf{Established partial result.}

Assume \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing\), as required by \(\texttt{ass:compatible-input}\).  Write \(S_0(\mathbf M)=E(\mathcal M^{(0)})\).  For a candidate acyclic basal edge set \(E\) and an ancestor-closed set \(A\subseteq V\), put
\[
B(E,A)=\left\{\{i,j\}\in\binom V2:
i\to j\in E\ \text{or}\ j\to i\in E\ \text{or}\
\exists c\in A:\ i\to c\leftarrow j\ \text{in }E
\right\}.
\]
Whenever \(B(E,A)\subseteq S_0(\mathbf M)\), the observational input forces the unique residual co-selection two-section
\[
Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)=S_0(\mathbf M)\setminus B(E,A).
\]
Thus a candidate is rejected unless
\[
B(E,A)\subseteq S_0(\mathbf M),
\qquad
Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)\subseteq\binom A2.
\]

The nonobservational targets are forced as well.  For each \(k\in\mathcal K_+\), define directly from the supplied oracle MAG
\[
Z_k=\{j\in V:\zeta_k\text{ is adjacent to }j\text{ in }\mathcal M^{(k)}\},
\qquad
R_k=Z_k\cap A,
\qquad
O_k=Z_k\setminus A.
\]
Reject the candidate unless \(R_k\) is descendant-closed in the induced DAG \(E[A]\).  Let
\[
\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)
=\{r\in R_k:\text{no strict }E\text{-ancestor of }r\text{ belongs to }R_k\},
\]
and define the input-determined canonical target and affected set by
\[
T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)=O_k\cup\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k),
\qquad
F_k^{*}=\operatorname{de}_E\!\left(T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\right).
\]

To verify exact preservation, take any passing certificate with target \(T_k\) and let \(F_k=\operatorname{de}_E(T_k)\).  The target-indicator characterization in \(\texttt{lem:dai-two-section-checker-characterization}\) gives
\[
Z_k=T_k\cup(F_k\cap A).
\]
Because \(T_k\subseteq F_k\), intersection with \(A\) and subtraction of \(A\) yield, respectively,
\[
R_k=F_k\cap A,
\qquad
O_k=T_k\setminus A.
\]
If a vertex outside \(A\) had an \(E\)-directed path to a vertex of \(A\), it would be an ancestor of a member of \(A\), contradicting ancestor closure.  Hence outside-\(A\) targets do not reach \(A\), and \(R_k\) is descendant-closed in \(E[A]\).

Since \(E[A]\) is finite and acyclic, backward traversal from any vertex of the nonempty set \(R_k\) ends at a member of \(\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\).  Therefore
\[
\operatorname{de}_{E[A]}\!\left(\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\right)=R_k.
\]
Moreover, every minimal member of \(R_k\) is itself in \(T_k\cap A\): it is a descendant of some target in \(T_k\cap A\), and any strict such target ancestor would be a strict ancestor lying in \(R_k\).  Conversely, every member of \(T_k\cap A\) belongs to \(R_k\) and hence descends from a minimal member.  It follows that
\[
\operatorname{de}_E(T_k\cap A)
=\operatorname{de}_E\!\left(\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\right).
\]
Together with \(O_k=T_k\setminus A\), this proves
\[
F_k^{*}
=\operatorname{de}_E\!\left(O_k\cup\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\right)
=\operatorname{de}_E(T_k)
=F_k.
\]
Also,
\[
T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\cup(F_k^{*}\cap A)
=O_k\cup\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\cup R_k
=O_k\cup R_k
=Z_k.
\]
Thus the replacement preserves the affected set, every indicator adjacency, every intervention-induced inducing path and endpoint, and hence every regime MAG.  The reconstruction direction of \(\texttt{thm:finite-common-realizability}\) consequently supplies a full-class common-basal model with the canonical targets \(T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\).

It is therefore enough to enumerate only \(E\) and \(A\).  There are at most
\[
3^{\binom D2}2^D
\]
candidates.  By \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\), their accepted canonical reconstructions realize exactly the endpointwise feasible marks of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Hence
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M),
\]
and every output circle has two accepted common-basal certificates agreeing with all fixed output marks and realizing, respectively, a tail and an arrowhead at that endpoint.  The global population running-time bound in \(\texttt{thm:complete-population-cdis}\) becomes
\[
O\!\left(
3^{\binom D2}2^D
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]
\right).
\]
This bound is non-tight and singly exponential in \(D^2\); its dependence on \(K\) is polynomial.  When \(K=0\), there are no sets \(Z_k,R_k,O_k\), no target reconstruction, and only the indicator-free observational branch is checked.

Separately, \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves that any supplied finite monotone rule list satisfying \(\texttt{def:finite-rule-encoding}\) makes at most
\[
N_{\mathrm{end}}=D(D-1)+KD(D+1)
\]
strict endpoint updates and one terminal scan, with total mark-propagation time
\[
O\!\left(
(K+1)(D+1)^3+
(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)
\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)
\right).
\]
No overlap, positivity, rank, inverse, or division condition is used.  The input is the supplied oracle regime-MAG family, so no polynomial claim is made for conditional-independence or invariance discovery.  These conclusions establish exact global population SI-PAG computation and opposing common-basal witnesses, but they do not construct or prove completeness of the requested fixed local calculus.
