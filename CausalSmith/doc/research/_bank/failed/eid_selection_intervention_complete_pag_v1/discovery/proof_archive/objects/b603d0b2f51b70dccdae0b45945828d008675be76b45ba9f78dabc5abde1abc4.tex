A normalized Dai two-section certificate is a tuple
\[
\mathcal R=(E,A,Q,(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}),
\]
where \(E\) is a directed acyclic graph on \(V\), \(A\subseteq V\) is ancestor-closed in \(E\), meaning \(\operatorname{an}_E(A)=A\), \(Q\subseteq\binom{A}{2}\) is a simple undirected graph, and \(T_k\subseteq V\) for every \(k\in\mathcal K_+\); set \(T_0=\varnothing\).  The set \(A\) represents the observed ancestors of the conditioned selection nodes, \(Q\) records the unordered pairs that share at least one conditioned selection child, and \(T_k\) is the unknown soft-intervention target in regime \(k\).
