\textbf{Established partial result.}

Assume \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing\), as required by \(\texttt{ass:compatible-input}\).  The already established target-residual certificate results give a finite global construction, even though they do not give the requested fixed local rule calculus.

Write \(S_0(\mathbf M)=E(\mathcal M^{(0)})\).  For a candidate acyclic basal edge set \(E\) and an ancestor-closed set \(A\subseteq V\), define
\[
B(E,A)=\left\{\{i,j\}\in\binom V2:
i\to j\in E\ \text{or}\ j\to i\in E\ \text{or}\
\exists c\in A:\ i\to c\leftarrow j\ \text{in }E
\right\}.
\]
Whenever \(B(E,A)\subseteq S_0(\mathbf M)\), the observational input forces the unique residual co-selection two-section
\[
Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)=S_0(\mathbf M)\setminus B(E,A).
\]
The candidate is admissible only if
\[
B(E,A)\subseteq S_0(\mathbf M),
\qquad
Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)\subseteq\binom A2.
\]

For each \(k\in\mathcal K_+\), define from the supplied oracle MAG
\[
Z_k=\{j\in V:\zeta_k\text{ is adjacent to }j\text{ in }\mathcal M^{(k)}\},
\qquad
R_k=Z_k\cap A,
\qquad
O_k=Z_k\setminus A.
\]
Reject the candidate unless \(R_k\) is descendant-closed in the induced DAG \(E[A]\).  Put
\[
\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)
=\{r\in R_k:\text{ no strict }E\text{-ancestor of }r\text{ belongs to }R_k\}
\]
and
\[
T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)=O_k\cup\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k),
\qquad
F_k^{*}=\operatorname{de}_E\!\left(T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\right).
\]

To see why this target residual is exact, take any passing certificate with target \(T_k\) and affected set \(F_k=\operatorname{de}_E(T_k)\).  By \(\texttt{lem:dai-two-section-checker-characterization}\),
\[
Z_k=T_k\cup(F_k\cap A).
\]
Since \(T_k\subseteq F_k\), intersection with \(A\) and subtraction of \(A\) give
\[
R_k=F_k\cap A,
\qquad
O_k=T_k\setminus A.
\]
Ancestor closure of \(A\) implies that no vertex outside \(A\) has an \(E\)-directed path to a vertex of \(A\).  Hence outside-\(A\) targets cannot reach \(A\), and \(R_k\) is descendant-closed in \(E[A]\).  Because \(E[A]\) is finite and acyclic, backward traversal from any member of \(R_k\) terminates at a member of \(\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\); therefore
\[
\operatorname{de}_{E[A]}\!\left(\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\right)=R_k.
\]
Every minimal member of \(R_k\) lies in \(T_k\cap A\): it descends from a target in \(T_k\cap A\), and a strict target ancestor would be a strict ancestor in \(R_k\), contrary to minimality.  Conversely, every member of \(T_k\cap A\) belongs to \(R_k\) and hence descends from a minimal member.  Thus
\[
\operatorname{de}_E(T_k\cap A)
=\operatorname{de}_E\!\left(\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\right).
\]
Together with \(O_k=T_k\setminus A\), this yields
\[
F_k^{*}
=\operatorname{de}_E\!\left(O_k\cup\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\right)
=\operatorname{de}_E(T_k)
=F_k,
\]
and
\[
T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\cup(F_k^{*}\cap A)
=O_k\cup\operatorname{Min}_E(R_k)\cup R_k
=O_k\cup R_k
=Z_k.
\]
Consequently the normalized replacement preserves the affected set and every indicator adjacency.  The reconstruction direction of \(\texttt{thm:finite-common-realizability}\) then supplies a common-basal compatible model with the canonical targets \(T^{*}_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\).

It is therefore sufficient to enumerate only \(E\) and \(A\), giving at most
\[
3^{\binom D2}2^D
\]
candidates.  By \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\), the accepted canonical reconstructions realize exactly the endpointwise feasible marks of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Hence
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M),
\]
and every output circle has two accepted common-basal certificates agreeing with every fixed output mark and realizing respectively a tail and an arrowhead at that endpoint.  The global population runtime from \(\texttt{thm:complete-population-cdis}\) is
\[
O\!\left(
3^{\binom D2}2^D
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]
\right).
\]
This bound is non-tight and singly exponential in \(D^2\), while its dependence on \(K\) is polynomial.  When \(K=0\), there are no sets \(Z_k,R_k,O_k\), no target reconstruction, and only the indicator-free observational branch is checked.

Separately, \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves that any supplied finite monotone rule list satisfying \(\texttt{def:finite-rule-encoding}\) makes at most
\[
N_{\mathrm{end}}=D(D-1)+KD(D+1)
\]
strict endpoint updates and one terminal scan, with total mark-propagation time
\[
O\!\left(
(K+1)(D+1)^3+
(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)
\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)
\right).
\]
No overlap, positivity, rank, inverse, division, or boundary assumption beyond finiteness and compatibility is used.  The input is the supplied oracle regime-MAG family, so this result makes no polynomial claim for conditional-independence or invariance discovery.

These conclusions establish exact global population SI-PAG computation, target residualization, opposing common-basal witnesses for canonical circles, and polynomial termination conditional on a supplied finite rule list.  They do not construct a fixed \(D,K\)-independent local calculus or prove that the closure of such a calculus equals the canonical SI-PAG.
