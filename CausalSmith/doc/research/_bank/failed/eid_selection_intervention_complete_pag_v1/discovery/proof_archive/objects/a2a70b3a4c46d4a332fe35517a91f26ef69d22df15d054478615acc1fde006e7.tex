Selective enrollment precedes intervention in the model studied here, so the indicator-free observational MAG on \(V\) and the indicator-bearing nonobservational MAGs on \(V\cup\{\zeta_k\}\) must be projections of one common basal DAG and one ordered unknown-target family.  The canonical SI-PAG is endpoint-mark invariance over Dai et al.'s full causally sufficient compatibility class.  A complete population algorithm enumerates only \((E,A,(T_k))\), derives the unique residual selection two-section \(Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)=S_0(\mathbf M)\setminus B(E,A)\) from the supplied observational skeleton, and returns the exact SI-PAG plus globally compatible opposing certificates for every circle.  It checks at most \(3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\) candidates, a singly exponential but neither polynomial nor tight upper bound.  A finite polynomial-time local orientation calculus remains open, as does discovery of the oracle MAG inputs.
