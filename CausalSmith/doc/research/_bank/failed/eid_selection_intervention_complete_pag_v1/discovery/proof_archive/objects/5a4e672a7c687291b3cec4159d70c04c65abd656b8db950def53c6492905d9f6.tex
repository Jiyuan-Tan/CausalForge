The first clause is already forced by the definitions.  When \(K=0\) and \(S_\eta=\varnothing\) for every compatible candidate, \(\operatorname{Tw}(\eta,0)\) is just the DAG \(\mathcal G_\eta[V]\), and its MAG projection retains all vertices with neither marginalization nor selection conditioning, hence equals that DAG.  Nonemptiness supplies one compatible DAG, and \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) is exactly its ordinary DAG Markov equivalence class.  The invariant endpoint representative of this class is the ordinary CPDAG, so \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}^{(0)}(\mathbf M)\) equals that CPDAG.  Conditional on \(\texttt{thm:complete-population-cdis}\), the equation \(\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M)\) then supplies the algorithmic reduction as well.
