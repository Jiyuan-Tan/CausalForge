Pre-treatment selection couples the indicator-free observational MAG on \(V\) and all indicator-bearing nonobservational MAGs on \(V\cup\{\zeta_k\}\) through one basal DAG.  The note defines the canonical SI-PAG by endpoint invariance over Dai et al.'s full causally sufficient compatibility class and computes it exactly by target-residual certificate enumeration: with \(m_0(\mathbf M)=|E(\mathcal M^{(0)})|\), only observed-skeleton pairs receive an absent/either-orientation state, giving at most \(3^{m_0(\mathbf M)}2^D\) candidates and actual opposing common-basal witnesses for every circle.  The dense envelope is \(3^{\binom D2}2^D\), while \(m_0(\mathbf M)=O(D)\) yields \(\exp(O(D))\); both are non-tight, nonpolynomial bounds with polynomial dependence on \(K\).  Construction of a complete fixed finite local SI orientation calculus remains open, and no polynomial claim is made for discovering the supplied oracle MAGs by conditional-independence or invariance queries.
