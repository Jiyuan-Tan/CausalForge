Assume \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing\).  An input-conditioned normalized Dai two-section certificate \(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M}=(E,A,Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A),(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+})\) passes \(\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M},\mathbf M)\) if and only if its canonical reconstruction is a member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Every member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) has an equivalent passing residual certificate of this form, and every passing residual certificate reconstructs a compatible member.  Consequently,
\[
\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists E,A,(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}\;
\operatorname{Check}_{D}\!\left(
(E,A,Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A),(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}),\mathbf M
\right)=1.
\]
