For every compatible oracle family \(\mathbf M\), target-residual certificate enumeration in \(\texttt{def:complete-cdis}\) terminates and satisfies
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M).
\]
For every endpoint left circular, the retained tail and arrowhead certificates reconstruct two members of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) that agree with every fixed output mark and realize the two opposite marks at that endpoint.  Both \(Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)\) and every \(T^*_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\) are uniquely computed from \((\mathbf M,E,A)\), so the algorithm visits at most
\[
3^{\binom D2}2^D
\]
pairs \((E,A)\) and admits the crude finite runtime bound
\[
O\!\left(3^{\binom D2}2^D
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]\right).
\]
This is an exact population algorithm with a non-tight singly exponential upper bound in \(D^2\) and only polynomial dependence on \(K\) outside the search factor.  It is not a polynomial global search and not a polynomial local-rule completeness result.  For \(K=0\), no indicator-neighborhood or target-residual object is formed.
