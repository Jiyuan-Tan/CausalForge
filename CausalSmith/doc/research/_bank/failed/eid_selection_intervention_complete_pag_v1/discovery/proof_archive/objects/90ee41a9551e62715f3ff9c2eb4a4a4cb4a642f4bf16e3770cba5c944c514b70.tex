\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a normalized certificate
\[
\mathcal R=(E,H,(T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}).
\]
Its canonical reconstruction \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\) is obtained as follows.  On the observed vertices use the directed acyclic graph \(E\).  For each \(h\in H\), add a distinct binary vertex \(s_h\), add precisely the arrows \(i\to s_h\) with \(i\in h\), and give \(s_h\) no children.  Set
\[
I_{\eta_{\mathcal R}}^{(0)}=\varnothing,
\qquad
I_{\eta_{\mathcal R}}^{(k)}=T_k
\quad(k\in\mathcal K_+).
\]
Choose mutually independent exogenous inputs for the observed-variable mechanisms.  For each selection node take an independent \(U_h\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]\) and use the binary structural kernel
\[
s_h=\mathbf 1\{U_h\le 1/2\}.
\]
The structural equation may take \((X_i)_{i\in h}\) as formal parent arguments; no causal-minimality condition is imposed by \(\texttt{def:dai-compatible-class}\), so this choice preserves the declared graphical parent set \(h\).  Conditional on the observed variables,
\[
\Pr\!\left((s_h)_{h\in H}=\mathbf 1\mid X_V\right)=2^{-|H|}>0,
\]
including the boundary case \(H=\varnothing\), where the probability is one.  Thus conditioning on the selected stratum is well-defined.  Finally, in regime \(k\), retain the basal mechanism and exogenous input outside \(T_k\), and choose an arbitrary soft replacement on every vertex in \(T_k\).

Because \(E\) is acyclic and \(H\) is finite with \(|H|\le 2^D\), this construction is a finite DAG.  The independent observed-variable exogenous inputs give causal sufficiency on \(V\); the new selection nodes are binary, childless, and have only observed parents; the positive selected stratum, basal timing, empty observational target, and modular soft replacements verify the remaining structural clauses of \(\texttt{def:dai-compatible-class}\).  By \(\texttt{lem:dai-certificate-mag-characterization}\), the projected regime family of \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\) is exactly
\[
\mathbf N(\mathcal R)
=\bigl(\mathcal N^{(0)}(\mathcal R),\ldots,\mathcal N^{(K)}(\mathcal R)\bigr).
\]

We first prove the checker equivalence.  If \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\), then every \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\) is Markov equivalent to \(\mathcal M^{(k)}\).  Markov-equivalent MAGs have the same adjacencies and the same unshielded collider triples.  Therefore every component comparison in \(\operatorname{Check}_{D}\) succeeds, and
\[
\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)=1.
\]

Conversely, suppose that \(\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)=1\).  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:compatible-input}\) supplies an anchor \(\eta_0\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  For every \(k\in\mathcal K\), compatibility of \(\eta_0\) implies that \(\mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}\) and \(\mathcal M^{(k)}\) have the same adjacencies and unshielded collider triples.  Checker acceptance gives the same conclusion for \(\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)\) and \(\mathcal M^{(k)}\).  Hence
\[
\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}
\]
have identical adjacency and unshielded-collider patterns in every regime.  Both families arise from the causally sufficient, childless-selection, pre-treatment twin construction.  The cross-regime equivalence clause of \(\texttt{lem:dai-certificate-mag-characterization}\) therefore yields
\[
\mathcal N^{(k)}(\mathcal R)
\equiv_m \mathcal M_{\eta_0}^{(k)}
\equiv_m \mathcal M^{(k)}
\qquad(k\in\mathcal K).
\]
All non-equivalence clauses of the class were already verified in the explicit reconstruction.  Thus \(\eta_{\mathcal R}\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).

It remains to relate arbitrary compatible candidates to normalized certificates.  If \(\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\), then \(\texttt{lem:selection-normalization}\) produces \(\bar\eta\in\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) with the same projected MAGs and a normalized certificate \(\mathcal R_{\bar\eta}\).  The forward implication just proved gives
\[
\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R_{\bar\eta},\mathbf M)=1.
\]
Conversely, every passing certificate has just been shown to reconstruct a member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Consequently, nonemptiness of the class produces a passing certificate, while a passing certificate produces a member of the class.  Hence
\[
\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\mathcal R\;\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)=1.
\]
No division, rank, overlap, or boundary condition is used; the only positivity needed for the constructed conditioning event was verified directly above. \(\square\)
