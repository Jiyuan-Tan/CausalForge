Assume \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing\).  A normalized certificate \(\mathcal R\) passes \(\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)\) if and only if its canonical reconstruction is a member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Every member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) has an equivalent passing normalized certificate, and every passing certificate reconstructs a compatible member.  Consequently,
\[
\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\mathcal R\;\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R,\mathbf M)=1.
\]
