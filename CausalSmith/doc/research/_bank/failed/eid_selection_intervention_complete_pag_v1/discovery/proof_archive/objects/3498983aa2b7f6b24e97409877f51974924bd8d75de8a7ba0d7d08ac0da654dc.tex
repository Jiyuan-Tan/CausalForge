For any separately supplied fixed finite monotone rule list, \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves termination after at most \(N_{\mathrm{end}}=D(D-1)+KD(D+1)\) strict endpoint updates and polynomial mark-propagation time \(O\!\left((K+1)(D+1)^3+(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)\right)\). Independently, \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) computes the exact canonical SI-PAG over the same full Dai class and returns two globally compatible opposing certificates for every circle. Hence sound finite closure has a proved polynomial conditional runtime once a rule list is given, and exact population mark completeness is computable by finite, generally doubly exponential certificate enumeration; only their conjunction in one fixed complete local calculus remains open. No polynomial conditional-independence discovery claim is made.
