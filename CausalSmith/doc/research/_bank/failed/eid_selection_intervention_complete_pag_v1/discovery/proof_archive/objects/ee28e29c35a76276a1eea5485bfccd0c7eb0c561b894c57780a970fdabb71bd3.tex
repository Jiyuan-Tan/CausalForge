The exact algorithm is a global search with upper bound \(3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}\) and polynomial work per candidate; the bound is neither polynomial nor claimed tight.  Completeness of a fixed finite within- and cross-regime orientation calculus remains the explicit open question.  Independent regime-wise MAG completions are never accepted as witnesses, and latent common causes, post-treatment filtering, and finite-sample oracle-MAG discovery are outside the model.
