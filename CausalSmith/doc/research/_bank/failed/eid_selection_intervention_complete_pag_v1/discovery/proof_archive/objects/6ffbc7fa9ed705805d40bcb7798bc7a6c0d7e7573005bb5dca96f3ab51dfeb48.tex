\textbf{Established partial result.}
Assume \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing\), as required by \(\texttt{ass:compatible-input}\), and put \(S_0(\mathbf M)=E(\mathcal M^{(0)})\).  For a candidate basal directed edge set \(E\) and an ancestor-closed set \(A\subseteq V\), define
\[
B(E,A)=\left\{\{i,j\}\in\binom V2:
i\to j\in E\ \text{or}\ j\to i\in E\ \text{or}\
\exists c\in A:\ i\to c\leftarrow j\ \text{in }E
\right\}
\]
and, whenever \(B(E,A)\subseteq S_0(\mathbf M)\), define the unique residual two-section
\[
Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)=S_0(\mathbf M)\setminus B(E,A).
\]
The input-conditioned normalization in \(\texttt{lem:selection-normalization}\) and the exact realizability equivalence in \(\texttt{thm:finite-common-realizability}\) show that it is sufficient to reject a candidate unless
\[
B(E,A)\subseteq S_0(\mathbf M),
\qquad
Q_{\mathbf M}(E,A)\subseteq\binom A2,
\]
and thereafter to enumerate only \(E\), \(A\), and \((T_k)_{k\in\mathcal K_+}\).  There are at most
\[
3^{\binom D2}2^D2^{DK}=3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\]
such candidates.  By \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\), the accepted candidates realize exactly the endpoint-mark feasible sets of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Consequently,
\[
\operatorname{CDIS}^{\mathrm{cmp}}(\mathbf M)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{SI}}(\mathbf M),
\]
and each output circle comes with two accepted certificates whose canonical reconstructions are members of the same common-basal class, agree with every fixed output mark, and realize respectively a tail and an arrowhead at that endpoint.  Constructing \(B(E,A)\) costs \(O(D^3)\), which is absorbed by the per-candidate regime-graph work.  Hence \(\texttt{thm:complete-population-cdis}\) gives the finite global-search bound
\[
O\!\left(
3^{\binom D2}2^{D+DK}
\left[D^2+(K+1)(D+1)^3+N_{\mathrm{end}}\right]
\right).
\]

Separately, \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves that any already supplied finite monotone rule list having the encoding in \(\texttt{def:finite-rule-encoding}\) performs at most
\[
N_{\mathrm{end}}=D(D-1)+KD(D+1)
\]
strict endpoint updates and one terminal scan, with total mark-propagation time
\[
O\!\left(
(K+1)(D+1)^3+
(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)
\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)
\right).
\]
This proves exact population SI-PAG computation and opposing common-basal witnesses by a singly exponential search, and polynomial closure conditional on a fixed finite encoded rule list.  The boundary \(K=0\) is included; regime zero remains indicator-free; no overlap, positivity, rank, inverse, or division condition is used.  The input is the supplied oracle regime-MAG family, so no polynomial claim is made for conditional-independence or invariance discovery.  These conclusions do not construct, or prove completeness of, the requested fixed local calculus.
