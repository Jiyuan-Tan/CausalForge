Assume \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing\).  A residual-admissible pair \((E,A)\) passes
\[
\operatorname{Check}_{D}(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M}(E,A),\mathbf M)=1
\]
if and only if its canonical selection reconstruction with the derived targets \(T^*_{k,\mathbf M}(E,A)\) is a member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\).  Every member of \(\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\) has an equivalent passing target-residual certificate of this form, and every passing pair reconstructs a compatible member.  Consequently,
\[
\mathfrak C_D(\mathbf M)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists E,A\;
\operatorname{Check}_{D}\!\left(\mathcal R_{\mathbf M}(E,A),\mathbf M\right)=1.
\]
For \(K=0\), the certificate and equivalence contain no nonobservational target coordinate.
