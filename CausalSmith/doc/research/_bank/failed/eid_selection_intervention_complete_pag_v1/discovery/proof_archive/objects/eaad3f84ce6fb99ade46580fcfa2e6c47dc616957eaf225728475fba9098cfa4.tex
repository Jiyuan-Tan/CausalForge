For any separately supplied finite monotone rule list, \(\texttt{lem:oracle-closure-runtime}\) proves termination after at most \(N_{\mathrm{end}}=D(D-1)+KD(D+1)\) strict endpoint updates and polynomial mark-propagation time \[O\!\left((K+1)(D+1)^3+(N_{\mathrm{end}}+1)\sum_{r\in\mathsf R_{\mathrm{SI}}}T_r(D,K)\right).\] Independently, \(\texttt{thm:enumerative-si-pag-completeness}\) computes the exact canonical SI-PAG on the same full compatibility class and returns two globally compatible opposing certificates for every canonical circle, in finite, generally doubly exponential time.  Thus sound finite closure has a proved conditional polynomial runtime, and exact population mark completeness with paired witnesses is computable nonlocally; only the uniform finite local-calculus theorem joining these two results remains unproved.
