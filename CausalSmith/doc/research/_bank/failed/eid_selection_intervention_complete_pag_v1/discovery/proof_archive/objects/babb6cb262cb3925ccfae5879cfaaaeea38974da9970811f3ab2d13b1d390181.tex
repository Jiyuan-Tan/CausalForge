Let \(E\) be the basal DAG on \(V\), let \(U\) be the union of the observed parent sets of the childless conditioned selection nodes, set \(A=\operatorname{an}_E(U)\), let \(\{i,j\}\in Q\) exactly when \(i\) and \(j\) share a conditioned selection child, and let \(F_k=\operatorname{de}_E(T_k)\).  In the causally sufficient pre-treatment twin construction, Dai et al.'s MAG formulas depend on selection only through \(A\) and \(Q\): Lemmas 3--6 give the observational adjacencies and endpoints, intervention-induced path adjacencies, indicator adjacencies, and interventional endpoints as functions of \((E,A,Q,(T_k))\).  Their Theorem 2 states that two such basal-DAG and ordered-target families are Markov equivalent across all regimes if and only if their regime MAGs have the same adjacencies and unshielded collider triples in every regime.
