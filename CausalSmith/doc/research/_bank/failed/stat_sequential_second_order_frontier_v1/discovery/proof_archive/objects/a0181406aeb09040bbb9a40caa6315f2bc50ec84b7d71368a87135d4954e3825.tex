For each fixed known pair \((s,r)\), separately tuned local-polynomial mean learners have integrated risk \(n^{-2s/(2s+d)}\), and residual or pairwise-difference variance learners admit the envelope \(n^{-2r/(2r+d)}\vee n^{-2s/(2s+d)}\).  The algebraic nuisance maximum is therefore log-free at fixed smoothness.  What remains open is one common predictable procedure attaining these fixed-class bounds simultaneously, together with the allocation-sensitive causal converse.
