The mean and weight bounds imply \(|\theta(P)|\leq KWM\), so every conditional expectation below is square-integrable. For any observed-path estimator \(T\), conditional orthogonality gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
E[(T-\theta(P))^2\mid H_n]
&=(T-\overline\theta_n)^2
+E[(\overline\theta_n-\theta(P))^2\mid H_n]\\
&=(T-\overline\theta_n)^2
+\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n).
\end{aligned}
\tag{59}
\]
After expectation, the first term is nonnegative and vanishes at the posterior mean, which has an \(H_n\)-measurable version on the canonical observed-path space. This proves (57).

For (58), the variance range and positivity make the pointwise objective finite and continuous on the nonempty compact simplex \(\{u:u_a\geq\varepsilon,\sum_au_a=1\}\). A lexicographically selected minimizer is measurable, and pointwise minimization commutes with integration because allocations in the oracle definition may depend measurably on \(x\). Substitution of \(m_a(x)=z_{P,a}(x)^2\) into the oracle-benchmark definition therefore gives
\[
\inf_e E_P\sum_a\frac{m_a(X_t)}{e_a(X_t)}
=E_P\min_{u_a\geq\varepsilon,\,\sum_au_a=1}
\sum_a\frac{z_{P,a}(X_t)^2}{u_a}
=E_P\Phi\{z_P(X_t)\}.
\tag{60}
\]
The first, allocation-invariant term in the benchmark is \(\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X_t)\}\), proving (58). Combining (57)--(58) proves the final reduction.
