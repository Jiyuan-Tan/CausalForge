Fix \(D\in\mathfrak D_{n,\varepsilon}\) and any prior \(\Pi_n\) on \(\mathcal P(s,r)\). Under the induced joint prior-predictive law, let \(\overline\theta_n=E[\theta(P)\mid H_n]\). Then
\[
\inf_{T\in\mathfrak T_n}
E_{P\sim\Pi_n}E_{\mathbb P_n^{D,P}}[(T-\theta(P))^2]
=E\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\},
\tag{57}
\]
and the infimum is attained by \(T=\overline\theta_n\). Moreover, with
\[
h_P(x)=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\omega_a(x)\mu_a(x),
\qquad
z_{P,a}(x)=|\omega_a(x)|\sigma_a(x),
\qquad
\Phi(z)=\min_{\substack{u_a\geq\varepsilon\\\sum_a u_a=1}}
\sum_a\frac{z_a^2}{u_a},
\]
one has
\[
V^*(P)=\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X_t)\}+E_P\Phi\{z_P(X_t)\}.
\tag{58}
\]
Thus any centered Bayes converse is exactly a lower bound on the posterior variance in (57) after subtracting the prior mean of the two terms in (58) divided by \(n\).
