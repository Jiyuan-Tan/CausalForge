Under the latent-i.i.d., bounded-density, Gaussian-outcome, Hölder-smoothness, boundedness, and uniform-positivity conditions, there exist a law-independent non-anticipating design \(D_n^{\circ}\in\mathfrak D_{n,\varepsilon}\) and an explicit predictable augmented inverse-probability estimator \(T_n^{\circ}\in\mathfrak T_n\) such that, for a constant \(C<\infty\) depending only on the fixed class constants,
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P(s,r)}
\left\{
E_{\mathbb P_n^{D_n^{\circ},P}}[(T_n^{\circ}-\theta(P))^2]
-\frac{V^*(P)}{n}
\right\}
\leq C\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right).
\]
The construction uses geometric epochs, predictable clipped local-polynomial regression, predictable clipped residual-square variance regression, the positivity-constrained generalized Neyman allocation, and recorded assignment probabilities; no union bound over epochs is used.
