Let \((D_n^{\circ},T_n^{\circ})\) be the single data-driven pair constructed in \(\mathrm{lem{:}predictable\text{-}aipw\text{-}holder\text{-}upper}\).  By the definition of \(\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)\), evaluating its two infima at this pair gives
\[
\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\leq C\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right).
\tag{19}
\]
Under the active-weight-region and strict-variance-range conditions, \(\mathrm{lem{:}variance\text{-}risk\text{-}absorption}\) gives constants \(c_0,C_0>0\) such that
\[
\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee
\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)
\geq c_0\left(n^{-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-2\beta_d}\right)
\tag{20}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Multiplying (20) by \(C/(c_0n)\) and comparing with (19) proves the displayed inequality.  Both cited ingredients are expected-risk statements with constants independent of \(n\); consequently the comparison introduces no logarithm.  Because only one admissible pair was inserted into the defining infima, this is an achievability theorem for the unrestricted decision problem and supplies no converse for arbitrary \(T\).
