Under the active-weight-region condition, there exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) and \(n_0\), depending only on the fixed class constants, such that for every \(n\ge n_0\),
\[
\frac{c}{n}\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)
\leq \mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\leq \frac{C}{n}\left\{\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee
\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)\right\}.
\]
The lower inequality is asserted over every \(T\in\mathfrak T_n\), including biased estimators, and neither displayed inequality contains a logarithmic factor.  No lower bound proportional to \(\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)/n\) is asserted without an additional allocation-sensitivity condition on the fixed weights.
