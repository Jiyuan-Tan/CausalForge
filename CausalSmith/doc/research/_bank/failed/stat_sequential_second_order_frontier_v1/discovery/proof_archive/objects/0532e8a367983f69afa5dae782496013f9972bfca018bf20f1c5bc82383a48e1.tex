For each fixed known pair \((s,r)\), the repaired predictable construction proves
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P(s,r)}
\left\{E_P[(T_n^{\circ}-\theta(P))^2]-\frac{V^*(P)}n\right\}
\leq C\left(n^{-1-2s/(2s+d)}\vee n^{-1-2r/(2r+d)}\right)
\]
without a logarithm.  Its proof establishes quadratic allocation regret and the same-scale predictable AIPW remainder for fixed bandwidths.  What remains unproved is the expected adaptive-risk oracle inequality for one learner chosen without \(s\) or \(r\), first with polylogarithmic cost and then with constant-factor cost.
