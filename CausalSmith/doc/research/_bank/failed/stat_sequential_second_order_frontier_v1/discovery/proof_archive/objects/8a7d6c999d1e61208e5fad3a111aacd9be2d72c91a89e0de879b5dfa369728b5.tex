Under the joint prior-predictive law, put
\(\overline\theta_n=E[\theta(P)\mid H_n]\).  Since
\(|\theta(P)|\leq KWM\), for every \(T\in\mathfrak T_n\), conditional orthogonality gives
\[
E[(T-\theta(P))^2]
=E[(T-\overline\theta_n)^2]
+E\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\}.
\]
Consequently
\[
\inf_{T\in\mathfrak T_n}E[(T-\theta(P))^2]
=E\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\},
\]
with the infimum attained by the posterior mean.  Moreover, writing
\[
h_P(x)=\sum_a\omega_a(x)\mu_a(x),
\qquad
z_{P,a}(x)=|\omega_a(x)|\sigma_a(x),
\qquad
\Phi(z)=\min_{u_a\geq\varepsilon,\,\sum_a u_a=1}
\sum_a\frac{z_a^2}{u_a},
\]
the measurable pointwise water-filling minimizer in the oracle definition gives
\[
V^*(P)=\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X)\}+E_P\Phi\{z_P(X)\}.
\]
Hence the unresolved assertion is exactly the centered posterior-variance inequality
\[
E\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\}
-\frac1nE_{P\sim\Pi_n}
\left[\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X)\}+E_P\Phi\{z_P(X)\}\right]
\geq c n^{-1-2\alpha_d}.
\]
This reduction isolates the missing Bayes calculation and proves why a lower bound on total risk alone is insufficient.
