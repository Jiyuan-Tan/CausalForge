For the sequential experiment whose decision class contains every law-independent positive non-anticipating design and every measurable, possibly biased estimator, we construct one predictable augmented inverse-probability pair with oracle-subtracted mean-squared error at most
\[
C\left(n^{-1-2s/(2s+d)}\vee n^{-1-2r/(2r+d)}\right)
\]
and no logarithmic factor.  Under the active-weight-region condition and the disclosed strict variance range, the standalone weighted mean/variance nuisance maximum has exactly the corresponding two Hölder scales.  A matching positive converse after subtracting the law-dependent \(V^*(P)/n\) remains open; the note therefore establishes a constructive upper law, not an exact minimax frontier.
