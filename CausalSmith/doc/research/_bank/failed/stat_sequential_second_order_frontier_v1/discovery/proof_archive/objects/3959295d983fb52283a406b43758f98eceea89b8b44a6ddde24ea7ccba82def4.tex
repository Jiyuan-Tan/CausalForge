Applied posterior orthogonal projection and measurable pointwise oracle minimization, then examined active-arm fixed-density, fixed-variance Hölder bump priors through Le Cam, Assouad, and Gaussian conjugate calculations.  These routes control total Bayes risk but not its signed difference from the law-dependent oracle benchmark.  The conjugate calculation above rules out the naive independent critical-bump construction.  A two-level construction with a diffuse coarse target coordinate, critical fine nuisance coordinates having zero target functional, and simultaneous prior variation of the covariate law was considered, but its sequential prior-predictive posterior variance and both vertex-dependent benchmark contributions could not be bounded with the required positive constant uniformly over every fixed \(D\).  No design--estimator pair was obtained that disproves the requested prior inequality either.
