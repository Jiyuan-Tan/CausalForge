The chosen weights satisfy the bounded-weight and active-region conditions.  Put
\[
p=1-(K-1)\varepsilon
\]
and use the static law-independent design assigning arm \(a_0\) with probability \(p\) and every other arm with probability \(\varepsilon\).  Since the oracle objective has only the term \(w^2\sigma_{a_0}^2(x)/e_{a_0}(x)\), it is minimized by maximizing \(e_{a_0}(x)\).  Hence this fixed design is the oracle design for every \(P\in\mathcal P(s,r)\); it requires no variance learning.

Split the observations into two folds \(I_1,I_2\) of sizes differing by at most one.  In fold \(j\), fit a clipped boundary-corrected local polynomial of degree \(\lfloor s\rfloor\) to the \(a_0\)-arm outcomes and put \(\widehat g_j=w\widehat\mu_j\).  Because assignment is independent with fixed probability \(p>0\), the number of training outcomes is binomial of order \(n\).  The cube-wise Taylor, Gram-matrix, and variance calculation gives, uniformly over \(P\),
\[
E_P\|\widehat g_j-g\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2\leq Cn^{-2\alpha_d},
\qquad g=w\mu_{a_0}.
\]
Define the two-fold cross-fitted estimator
\[
T=\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^2\sum_{i\in I_j}
\left[
\widehat g_{3-j}(X_i)
+\frac{\mathbf 1\{A_i=a_0\}}p\{wY_i-\widehat g_{3-j}(X_i)\}
\right].
\]
It is a Borel map of the observed path and hence belongs to \(\mathfrak T_n\).  Let
\[
\phi_i=g(X_i)-\theta(P)
+\frac{\mathbf 1\{A_i=a_0\}}p\{wY_i-g(X_i)\},
\qquad
\delta_j=\widehat g_j-g.
\]
Then
\[
T-\theta(P)=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\phi_i+R,
\qquad
R=\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^2\sum_{i\in I_j}
\delta_{3-j}(X_i)\left(1-\frac{\mathbf 1\{A_i=a_0\}}p\right).
\]
The oracle calculation in the identification proposition gives
\[
E_P\left(\frac1n\sum_i\phi_i\right)^2=\frac{V^*(P)}n.
\]
Conditional on the opposite fold, each summand in \(R\) is centered.  Moreover its product with the same-observation oracle score has conditional expectation zero: the factor multiplying \(g(X)-\theta(P)\) is centered in \(A\), and the residual \(Y-\mu(X)\) is centered given \((X,A=a_0)\).  Products with oracle scores in the training fold vanish by conditioning on that fold and then averaging the evaluation fold.  Thus
\[
E_P\left[\left(\frac1n\sum_i\phi_i\right)R\right]=0.
\]
Finally, using \((u+v)^2\leq2u^2+2v^2\) for the two fold contributions and conditioning on each training fold,
\[
E_PR^2
\leq\frac{C}{n}\sum_{j=1}^2E_P\|\delta_j\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2
\leq Cn^{-1-2\alpha_d}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P(s,r)}
\left\{E_P[(T-\theta(P))^2]-\frac{V^*(P)}n\right\}
\leq Cn^{-1-2\alpha_d},
\]
which upper-bounds \(\mathsf R_n\).

For the variance-risk lower bound, set all means to zero, take \(p_X\) uniform, choose \(v_0\in(v_{\min},v_{\max})\), and use disjoint \(r\)-Hölder variance bumps of width \(h\) and height \(h^r\) inside \(B_0\).  For adjacent bump signs the \(n\)-sample Gaussian-scale Kullback--Leibler divergence is at most \(Cnh^{2r+d}\), while the squared \(m_{a_0}\)-separation is at least \(cw^4h^{2r+d}\).  Taking \(h\asymp n^{-1/(2r+d)}\) and applying the bit-testing inequality over \(h^{-d}\) bumps gives
\[
\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)\geq ch^{2r}=cn^{-2\beta_d}.
\]
Since \(x\mapsto x/(2x+d)\) is strictly increasing, \(r<s\) implies \(\beta_d<\alpha_d\).  Hence \(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}=o(n^{-1-2\beta_d})\), proving both contradictions.
