Suppose \(d=1\), every contrast weight is a constant with magnitude at least \(w_{\min}\), and the variance range has nonempty interior.  Then
\[
\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,1;\omega)\asymp n^{-2s/(2s+1)},
\]
and there are fixed constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) such that
\[
c n^{-2r/(2r+1)}
\leq\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,1;\omega)
\leq C n^{-2\gamma_1},
\qquad
\gamma_1=\min\left\{\frac{4sr}{4sr+2s+r},\frac{r}{2r+1}\right\}.
\]
Nevertheless the maximum is exact:
\[
\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,1;\omega)\vee\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,1;\omega)
\asymp n^{-2s/(2s+1)}\vee n^{-2r/(2r+1)}.
\]
