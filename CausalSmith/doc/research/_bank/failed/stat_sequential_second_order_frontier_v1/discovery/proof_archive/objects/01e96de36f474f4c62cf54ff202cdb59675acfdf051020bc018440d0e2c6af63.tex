The explicit geometric-epoch predictable AIPW construction in \(\mathrm{lem{:}predictable\text{-}aipw\text{-}holder\text{-}upper}\) proves the log-free upper rate
\[
\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\leq C\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right).
\]
The standalone Hölder lemma proves \(\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}\asymp n^{-2\alpha_d}\) and, under \(v_{\min}<v_{\max}\), the two-nuisance maximum has order \(n^{-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-2\beta_d}\).  The missing step is solely the universal lower transport
\(\mathsf R_n\geq c\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}/n\) for arbitrary biased estimators.  A coupled density--mean Rademacher hypercube with cell width \(h\asymp n^{-1/(2s+d)}\) has second-order target fluctuation \(h^{4s+d}=n^{-1-2s/(2s+d)}\), but the Bayes-risk calculation has not yet been shown to retain this term after subtracting the law-dependent \(V^*(P)/n\) along every adaptive trajectory law.
