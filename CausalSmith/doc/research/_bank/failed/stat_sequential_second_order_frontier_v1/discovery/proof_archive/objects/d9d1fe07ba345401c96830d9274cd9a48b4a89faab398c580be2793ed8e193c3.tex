\(\mathsf H_n\) starts from fold-specific history-measurable propensities and pooled cross-fitted augmented inverse-probability scores. It replaces residual variance regression by pairwise-difference, two-bandwidth, local-polynomial \(U\)-statistics for \(m_a\), updates the positivity-constrained generalized Neyman allocation only from the opposite fold, and analyzes integrated expected risk directly. Its converse component transports a Brown--Low--Duchi--Ruan constrained-risk pair or mixture through the adaptive trajectory likelihood while retaining the model-dependent subtraction \(V^*(P)/n\). For smoothness adaptation, the handle starts from predictable Lepski selection over dyadic mean and variance bandwidth grids, performed on training folds before the opposite-fold design update.
