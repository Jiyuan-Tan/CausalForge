The predictable construction in \(\mathrm{lem{:}predictable\text{-}aipw\text{-}holder\text{-}upper}\) supplies a single pair \((D_n^{\circ},T_n^{\circ})\in\mathfrak D_{n,\varepsilon}\times\mathfrak T_n\) for which
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P(s,r)}
\left\{
E_{\mathbb P_n^{D_n^{\circ},P}}[(T_n^{\circ}-\theta(P))^2]
-\frac{V^*(P)}n
\right\}
\leq C\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right).
\tag{21}
\]
The definition of \(\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)\) takes an infimum over a class containing this pair.  Substitution of the pair into that infimum proves the displayed upper bound.  The identities \(\alpha_d=s/(2s+d)\) and \(\beta_d=r/(2r+d)\) are the declared exponent definitions.  The proof of the cited lemma sums expected epoch risks geometrically and never takes a simultaneous high-probability maximum, so (21) has no logarithmic factor.  No step reverses the defining infimum or lower-bounds the centered loss, which is why the conclusion is exclusively an upper theorem.
