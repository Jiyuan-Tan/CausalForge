Under the active-weight-region condition, there exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) and \(n_0\) such that for every \(n\ge n_0\), \[c\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right)\le\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)\le C\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right),\qquad \alpha_d=\frac{s}{2s+d},\quad\beta_d=\frac{r}{2r+d}.\] The infimum is over the law-independent kernels \(D\in\mathfrak D_{n,\varepsilon}\) and all \(T\in\mathfrak T_n\), and both inequalities are free of logarithmic factors. This strictly tightens Li and Simchi-Levi's Section 4.4 Theorem 4.6, equations (16)--(18), and Theorem 4.7, equation (19): the upper bound replaces \(\widetilde O\) by a constant-factor rate, and the lower bound replaces oracle-subtracted variance for an unbiased estimator pair by oracle-subtracted MSE over all measurable estimators.
