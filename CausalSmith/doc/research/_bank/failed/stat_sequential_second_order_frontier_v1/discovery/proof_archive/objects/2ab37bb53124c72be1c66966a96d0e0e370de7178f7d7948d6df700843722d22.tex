For every fixed \(s,r>0\), the causal functional \(\theta(P)\) admits the constructive log-free upper bound
\[
\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\lesssim n^{-1-2s/(2s+d)}\vee n^{-1-2r/(2r+d)}.
\]
Under the active-weight-region condition and \(v_{\min}<v_{\max}\), the standalone nuisance maximum satisfies
\[
\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)
\asymp n^{-2s/(2s+d)}\vee n^{-2r/(2r+d)},
\]
and hence the causal upper bound is at most a constant times this maximum divided by \(n\).  No positive unrestricted oracle-subtracted lower bound is established, so the exact minimax order is not claimed.
