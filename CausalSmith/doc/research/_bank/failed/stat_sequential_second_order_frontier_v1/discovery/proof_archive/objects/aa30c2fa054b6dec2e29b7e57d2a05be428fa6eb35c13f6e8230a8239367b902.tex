Under the active-weight-region condition, there exist constants \(C<\infty\) and \(n_0\), depending only on the fixed class constants, such that for every \(n\geq n_0\),
\[
\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\leq C\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right),
\qquad
\alpha_d=\frac{s}{2s+d},\quad
\beta_d=\frac{r}{2r+d}.
\]
The infimum remains over all law-independent kernels \(D\in\mathfrak D_{n,\varepsilon}\) and all estimators \(T\in\mathfrak T_n\), including biased estimators.  The displayed upper bound is constructive and contains no logarithmic factor.  No positive lower bound for the oracle-subtracted unrestricted mean-squared-error criterion, and hence no matching minimax-rate assertion in any smoothness regime, is made here.
