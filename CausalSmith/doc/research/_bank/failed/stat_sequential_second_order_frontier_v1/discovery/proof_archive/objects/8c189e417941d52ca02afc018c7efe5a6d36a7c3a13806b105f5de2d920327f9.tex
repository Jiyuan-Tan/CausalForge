The proved posterior-projection identity in \(\mathrm{lem{:}unrestricted\text{-}bayes\text{-}risk\text{-}decomposition}\) reduces the problem exactly, but the frozen primitives do not determine the sign of the centered posterior variance.  Total-risk separation, Le Cam, Assouad, and constrained-risk inequalities do not retain the vertex-dependent terms \(\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X_t)\}/n\) and \(E_P\Phi\{z_P(X_t)\}/n\).  Moreover, the usual one-level critical product prior has the wrong behavior already in the balanced Gaussian cell analogue.  With \(J N=n\), observations \(Y_{ji}\mid\eta_j\sim\mathcal N(\eta_j,1)\), target \(\vartheta=J^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^J\eta_j\), and independent priors \(\eta_j\sim\mathcal N(0,\tau^2)\), conjugacy gives
\[
R_{\mathrm B}=\frac{1}{J(N+\tau^{-2})},
\qquad
R_{\mathrm B}-\frac1n
=-\frac{\tau^{-2}}{JN(N+\tau^{-2})}<0.
\]
At the Hölder-critical scaling \(J\asymp n^{d/(2s+d)}\) and \(\tau^2\asymp J^{-2s/d}\), one has \(N\tau^2\asymp1\), so this deficit is of order \(n^{-1}\), not a positive remainder of order \(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\).  Making this product prior diffuse forces \(N\tau^2\to\infty\), which moves \(J\) below the critical resolution and loses the desired exponent.  Fixing \(P_X\) also gives the Bayes rule information that the oracle benchmark, computed in the full model, still charges through \(\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X_t)\}/n\).  Thus a valid proof requires a new correlated coarse--fine prior and a vertexwise calculation under arbitrary sequential assignment; no such calculation follows from the current nodes.
