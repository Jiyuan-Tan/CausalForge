The frozen range condition permits the boundary case \(v_{\max}=v_{\min}\).  In that case every admissible conditional variance is the known constant \(v_{\min}\), so \(m_a=\omega_a^2v_{\min}\) is known and \(\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}=0\).  A positive lower bound of order \(n^{-2\beta_d}\) is therefore impossible.  Requiring a nonempty variance interval is the minimal natural restriction that permits the variance Assouad submodel; it does not assume any estimation conclusion.
