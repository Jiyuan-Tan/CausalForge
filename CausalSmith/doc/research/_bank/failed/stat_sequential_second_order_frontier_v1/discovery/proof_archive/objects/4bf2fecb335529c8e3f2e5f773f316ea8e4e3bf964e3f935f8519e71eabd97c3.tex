There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) and \(n_0\), depending only on the fixed class constants, such that for every \(n\ge n_0\), \[\frac{c}{n}\left\{\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)\right\}\le \mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)\le\frac{C}{n}\left\{\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)\right\}.\] The lower inequality holds after the infimum over every \(T\in\mathfrak T_n\), including biased and superefficient estimators, and neither inequality contains a logarithmic factor.
