Let \((D_n^{\circ},T_n^{\circ})\) be the single data-driven pair furnished by \(\mathrm{lem{:}predictable\text{-}aipw\text{-}holder\text{-}upper}\). By \(\mathrm{def{:}excess\text{-}risk}\), evaluating the two infima defining \(\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)\) at this admissible pair gives
\[
\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\leq C_1\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right).
\tag{62}
\]
The active-weight-region assumption and \(v_{\min}<v_{\max}\) are precisely the side conditions under which \(\mathrm{lem{:}variance\text{-}risk\text{-}absorption}\) gives, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee
\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)
\geq c_0\left(n^{-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-2\beta_d}\right)
\tag{63}
\]
with \(c_0>0\). Combining (62) and (63) yields
\[
\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\leq \frac{C_1}{c_0n}
\left\{\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee
\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)\right\}.
\]
Increasing the constant and the threshold \(n_0\) proves the theorem. The witness is the same law-independent non-anticipating design and predictable augmented inverse-probability estimator as in the cited lemma. Both ingredients are expected-risk inequalities with constants independent of \(n\), so their comparison introduces no logarithmic factor. Finally, this argument only inserts one admissible pair into the defining infima. It neither restricts the remaining decision class nor reverses an infimum, and therefore asserts no lower bound for arbitrary, possibly biased, estimators.
