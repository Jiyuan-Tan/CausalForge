The proof retains conditional Gaussianity, fixed positive overlap, fixed known smoothness orders, and an \(n\)-independent Hölder class.  Gaussianity is used for a uniform fourth-moment bound for raw squared residuals; no sub-Gaussian extension is proved.  The exact standalone nuisance maximum uses the strict variance range.  A leading constant, vanishing-overlap theory, smoothness adaptation, the unrestricted centered converse, and a variance-driven converse under merely bounded weights remain outside the proved scope.
