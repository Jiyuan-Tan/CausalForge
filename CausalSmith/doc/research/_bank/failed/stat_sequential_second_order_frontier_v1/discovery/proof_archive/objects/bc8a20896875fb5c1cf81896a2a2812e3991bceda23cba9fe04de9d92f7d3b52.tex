The cited random-design variance paper proves the displayed \(n^{-2\gamma_1}\) upper bound, but its rough-mean lower construction allows design densities with gaps.  Its own discussion leaves the corresponding slower-than-parametric lower bound open for more regular designs, including uniform design.  The frozen class here is strictly more regular because \(p_X\geq c_X\) on all of \([0,1]\).  Thus the exact equality \(\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}\asymp n^{-2\gamma_1}\) is unsupported.  The known-mean variance submodel gives the stated lower bound, the published estimator gives the upper bound, and variance-risk absorption makes the maximum exact.  The strict variance-range condition is also necessary because the permitted equality \(v_{\max}=v_{\min}\) makes \(\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}=0\).
