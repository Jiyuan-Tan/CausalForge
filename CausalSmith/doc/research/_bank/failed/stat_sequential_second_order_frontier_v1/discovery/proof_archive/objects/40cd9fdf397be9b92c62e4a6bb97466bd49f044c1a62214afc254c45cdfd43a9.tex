Under the active-weight-region condition, there exist fixed constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) such that \[c n^{-2\alpha_d}\le\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\le C n^{-2\alpha_d},\] \[c n^{-2\beta_d}\le\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)\le C\left(n^{-2\beta_d}\vee n^{-2\alpha_d}\right).\] Consequently, \(\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,d;\omega)\vee\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,d;\omega)\asymp n^{-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-2\beta_d}\), even when the exact standalone variance-function rate has an additional unknown-mean regime.
