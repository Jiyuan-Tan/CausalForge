For every prior \(\Pi_n\) and every fixed design \(D\), conditional orthogonality shows that the posterior mean \(\overline\theta_n=E[\theta(P)\mid H_n]\) attains the unrestricted Bayes infimum and that
\[
\inf_{T\in\mathfrak T_n}
E_{P\sim\Pi_n}E_{\mathbb P_n^{D,P}}[(T-\theta(P))^2]
=E\!\left\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\right\}.
\]
Writing
\[
h_P(x)=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\omega_a(x)\mu_a(x),
\qquad
z_{P,a}(x)=|\omega_a(x)|\sigma_a(x),
\]
pointwise minimization over the compact positivity-constrained simplex gives
\[
V^*(P)=\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X_t)\}
+E_P\!\left[
\min_{\substack{u_a\geq\varepsilon\\\sum_a u_a=1}}
\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\frac{z_{P,a}(X_t)^2}{u_a}
\right].
\]
Consequently the desired positive converse is equivalent, without any unbiasedness restriction, to proving
\[
E\!\left\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\right\}
-\frac1n E_{P\sim\Pi_n}\!\left[
\operatorname{Var}_P\{h_P(X_t)\}
+E_P\!\left[
\min_{\substack{u_a\geq\varepsilon\\\sum_a u_a=1}}
\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\frac{z_{P,a}(X_t)^2}{u_a}
\right]
\right]
\geq c n^{-1-2\alpha_d}.
\]
The displayed centered posterior-variance inequality, including its sign at the second-order scale, is the sole unresolved step.
