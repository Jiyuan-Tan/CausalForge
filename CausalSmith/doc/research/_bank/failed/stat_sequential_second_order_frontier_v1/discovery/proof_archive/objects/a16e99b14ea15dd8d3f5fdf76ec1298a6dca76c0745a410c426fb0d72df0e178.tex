For every prior \(\Pi_n\) and fixed design \(D\), the unrestricted Bayes risk has been reduced exactly to
\[
E\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\}.
\]
The full centered quantity is therefore
\[
E\{\operatorname{Var}(\theta(P)\mid H_n)\}
-\frac1nE_{P\sim\Pi_n}
\left[
\operatorname{Var}_P\!\left\{\sum_a\omega_a(X)\mu_a(X)\right\}
+E_P\min_{\substack{u_a\geq\varepsilon\\\sum_a u_a=1}}
\sum_a\frac{\omega_a(X)^2\sigma_a^2(X)}{u_a}
\right].
\]
The posterior mean attains the estimator infimum. What remains is to construct a prior, uniformly for each fixed law-independent design, for which the displayed difference is bounded below by \(c n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\), or to give an explicit obstruction.
