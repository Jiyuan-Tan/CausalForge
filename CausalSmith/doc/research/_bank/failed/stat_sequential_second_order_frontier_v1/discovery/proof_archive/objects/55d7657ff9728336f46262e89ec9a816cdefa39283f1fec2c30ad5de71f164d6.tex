For every fixed known pair \((s,r)\), the predictable geometric-epoch construction in \(\mathrm{lem{:}predictable\text{-}aipw\text{-}holder\text{-}upper}\) attains
\[
n^{-1-2s/(2s+d)}\vee n^{-1-2r/(2r+d)}
\]
without a logarithm, and the standalone nuisance maximum has the corresponding two-scale order under \(v_{\min}<v_{\max}\).  This does not furnish one learner chosen without \(s\) or \(r\), and it does not prove that adaptive selection penalties disappear.
