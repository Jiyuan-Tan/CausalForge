Li and Simchi-Levi, \cite{LiSimchiLevi2026SequentialEfficiency}, Definition 2.1, supply the law-independent non-anticipating design convention.  Their Section 4.4 gives a logarithmically slack second-order upper result, whereas its converse quantifies over an unbiased estimator pair and uses excess variance; this does not settle the present oracle-subtracted mean-squared-error problem over arbitrary measurable estimators.  Li and Owen, \cite{LiOwen2024BatchDML}, and Kato, Ishihara, Honda, and Narita, \cite{KatoIshiharaHondaNarita2020AdaptiveATE}, motivate predictable cross-fitting and augmented inverse-probability martingales at first order.  The present upper proof supplies the fixed-Hölder expected second-order calculation, including active-set-stable allocation regret and log-free epoch summation.  Shen, Gao, Witten, and Han, \cite{ShenGaoWittenHan2020Variance}, and Wang, Brown, Cai, and Levine, \cite{WangBrownCaiLevine2008MeanVariance}, motivate the unknown-mean variance component; the note uses only the standalone claims actually established in the frozen core.  Brown and Low, \cite{BrownLow1996ConstrainedRisk}, and Duchi and Ruan, \cite{DuchiRuan2021ConstrainedRisk}, control total risk between laws but do not by themselves preserve a law-dependent semiparametric subtraction.  Cai and Wang, \cite{CaiWang2008AdaptiveVariance}, inform the separate smoothness-adaptation question, for which neither the predictable adaptive oracle inequality nor removal of selection penalties is claimed here.
