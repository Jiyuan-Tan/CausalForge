Suppose \(d=1\) and every contrast weight is a constant with magnitude at least \(w_{\min}\). Then \(\mathsf Q_n^{\mu}(s,1;\omega)\asymp n^{-2s/(2s+1)}\), \(\mathsf Q_n^{\sigma}(s,r,1;\omega)\asymp n^{-2\gamma_1}\), and the nuisance-reduction theorem yields \[\mathsf R_n(s,r,1;\omega)\asymp n^{-1}\left(n^{-2s/(2s+1)}\vee n^{-2\gamma_1}\right),\qquad \gamma_1=\min\left\{\frac{4sr}{4sr+2s+r},\frac{r}{2r+1}\right\}.\]
