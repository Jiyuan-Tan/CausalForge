Suppose there is a single known Borel-measurable allocation \(e:[0,1]^d\to\Delta_K\), independent of \(P\), \(s\), and \(r\), such that \(e_a(x)\geq\varepsilon\) for every \(a\in\mathcal A\) and \(x\in[0,1]^d\), and such that, simultaneously for every \(s,r>0\), every \(P\in\mathcal P(s,r)\), and \(P_X\)-almost every \(x\),
\[
\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\frac{m_a(x)}{e_a(x)}
=\min_{\substack{u_a\geq\varepsilon\\ \sum_{a\in\mathcal A}u_a=1}}
\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\frac{m_a(x)}{u_a}.
\tag{43}
\]
Then there is one law-independent algorithm, depending on this same known allocation \(e\) but not on \(s\) or \(r\), with the following property. For every fixed \(s,r>0\), there are constants \(C_s<\infty\) and \(n_0(s)<\infty\) such that, for all \(n\geq n_0(s)\), it returns \(D\in\mathfrak D_{n,\varepsilon}\) and \(T\in\mathfrak T_n\) satisfying
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P(s,r)}
\left\{
E_{\mathbb P_n^{D,P}}[(T-\theta(P))^2]-\frac{V^*(P)}n
\right\}
\leq C_s n^{-1-2s/(2s+d)}.
\tag{44}
\]
Consequently \(\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)\leq C_s n^{-1-2s/(2s+d)}\). This is a mean-only upper result and asserts no centered converse.
