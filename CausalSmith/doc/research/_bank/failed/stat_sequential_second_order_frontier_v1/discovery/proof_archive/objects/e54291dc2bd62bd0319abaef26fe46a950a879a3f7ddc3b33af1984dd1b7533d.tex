The proved contribution is a constructive log-free upper bound for the unrestricted sequential minimax excess-MSE problem, together with the already-established standalone nuisance maximum under the active-region and strict-range conditions and its one-dimensional refinements.  There is no claimed positive lower bound for the unrestricted centered criterion, no equality claim when \(s\leq r\), and no exact all-regime minimax frontier.  The centered Bayes-risk converse, allocation-sensitive lower theory when \(r<s\), and simultaneous adaptation to unknown \(s,r\) are three separate open problems.
