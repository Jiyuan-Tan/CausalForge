At a fixed covariate value \(x\), singleton support makes every design-relevant variance coordinate zero except
\[
m_{a(x)}(x)=\omega_{a(x)}(x)^2\sigma_{a(x)}^2(x)>0.
\]
Thus the pointwise objective is \(m_{a(x)}(x)/u_{a(x)}\), which is strictly decreasing in \(u_{a(x)}\). Under \(u_b\geq\varepsilon\) and \(\sum_bu_b=1\), the largest feasible value is \(u_{a(x)}=1-(K-1)\varepsilon\), attained only by assigning \(\varepsilon\) to every other arm. The optimizer therefore does not depend on the unknown variance. The active-index map is Borel because the finite collection of known weights is Borel measurable and the support is a singleton; hence the displayed allocation is a known measurable rule. It satisfies the hypothesis of \(\mathrm{thm{:}law\text{-}invariant\text{-}oracle\text{-}allocation\text{-}upper}\), which gives the asserted smoothness-agnostic mean-only upper rate.
