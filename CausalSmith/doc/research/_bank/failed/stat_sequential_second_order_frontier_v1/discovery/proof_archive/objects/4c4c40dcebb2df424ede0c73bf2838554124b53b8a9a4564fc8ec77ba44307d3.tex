Under the active-weight-region condition, there exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) and \(n_0\) such that for every \(n\geq n_0\),
\[
c n^{-1-2\alpha_d}
\leq\mathsf R_n(s,r,d;\omega)
\leq C\left(n^{-1-2\alpha_d}\vee n^{-1-2\beta_d}\right),
\qquad
\alpha_d=\frac{s}{2s+d},\quad
\beta_d=\frac{r}{2r+d}.
\]
The infimum remains over the law-independent kernels \(D\in\mathfrak D_{n,\varepsilon}\) and every \(T\in\mathfrak T_n\), and neither inequality contains a logarithmic factor.  Equality of the two exponents is asserted when \(\alpha_d\leq\beta_d\); when \(\beta_d<\alpha_d\), a variance-driven converse requires an additional allocation-sensitivity condition and is not claimed here.
