Verdict: no source establishes an all-\((s,r)\), constant-factor, log-free sequential construction for the honest mean-lower/two-scale-upper bracket.

Key sources:

- [Shen–Gao–Witten–Han, arXiv:1902.10822](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10822), §6.5, Propositions on adaptive constant-variance estimation: Lepski achieves a polylogarithmic rate, and the extra logarithm is proved unavoidable for that standalone integrated-risk problem.
- [Cai–Wang, arXiv:0810.4780](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.4780), §3.2, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1: simultaneous adaptation to mean and variance regularity via squared-difference wavelet thresholding, but only within a logarithmic factor under global MISE.
- [Li–Owen, arXiv:2309.15297](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15297), CSBAE definition and feasible-CLT theorem: lawful fold-specific, previous-batch-measurable propensities and cross-fitting, but only first-order asymptotic equivalence under assumed nuisance rates.
- [Kato et al., arXiv:2002.05308](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05308), adaptive A2IPW algorithm and regret theorem: a predictable plug-in allocation template, but its bound is linear in allocation/regression errors and does not prove the sharp second-order smoothness frontier.
- [Neopane–Ramdas–Singh, arXiv:2411.14341](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14341): logarithmic Neyman regret only in the noncontextual second-moment setting.
- [Li–Simchi-Levi, arXiv:2606.31190v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31190), §4.4, Theorems 4.6–4.7 and Appendix F, Lemma F.6: closest sequential second-order comparison remains logarithmically slack, with the converse restricted to unbiased estimator pairs.

The precise barrier is a missing synthesis theorem: adaptive global nuisance control, predictable conditional-variance allocation, and expected cross-fit remainder control are known only separately. Shen’s lower bound makes log-free standalone nuisance adaptation genuinely problematic, but it does not prove log-free causal oracle-excess impossible because that penalty might be absorbed by the mean scale. Global nonconstant-variance impossibility remains open.

Recommended OEQ directive:

> Fix geometric epochs and permanent folds. From each fold’s prior epochs, use an \(L_2\)-adaptive mean learner and a squared-pairwise-difference Goldenshluger–Lepski/wavelet variance learner; assign the next epoch using the positivity-clipped generalized Neyman rule; evaluate a fold-complement AIPW estimator with recorded propensities. First prove a polylogarithmic two-scale upper bound. Ask whether selection penalties can be removed or absorbed to recover the constant-factor bracket, separating \(s\le r\) from \(r<s\). Require expected adaptive-risk oracle inequalities under predictable inverse-propensity sampling, quadratic allocation sensitivity under \(v_{\min}>0\), and a same-scale expected cross-fit remainder bound. Do not claim log-free attainment until all three are proved.
