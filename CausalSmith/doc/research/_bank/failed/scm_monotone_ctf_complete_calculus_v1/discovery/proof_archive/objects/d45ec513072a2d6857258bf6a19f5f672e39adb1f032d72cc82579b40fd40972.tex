The proved oracle theorem gives
\[
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MID})\subseteq\operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MCTFID})
 =\{(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q):\operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\}.
\]
For \(M\ne\varnothing\), a successful \(\mathsf{MID}\) expression is returned literally; for \(M=\varnothing\), soundness plus conditional \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) completeness gives only success-set containment.  Neither the frozen soundness statement nor the finite oracle specifies a failed \(\mathsf{MID}\) instance, so they do not decide strictness.
