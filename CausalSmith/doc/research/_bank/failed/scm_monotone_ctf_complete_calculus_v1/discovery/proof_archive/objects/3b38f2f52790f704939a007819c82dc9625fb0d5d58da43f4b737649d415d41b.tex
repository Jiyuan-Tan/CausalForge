The proved finite decider gives a terminating global oracle.  On a negative decision it extracts two bounded canonical algebraic countermodels.  On a positive decision the relation
\[
 \Gamma_Q=\{(p,t):\exists a,\lambda,\ p=\phi_a(\lambda),\ d_a(\lambda)>0,\ t d_a(\lambda)=h_a(\lambda)\}
\]
is semialgebraic and has singleton \(t\)-fibers on its attainable \(p\)-domain; cylindrical algebraic decomposition therefore gives a finite piecewise-algebraic functional once a Thom-root representation is added to \(\mathcal R(p)\).  Any already selected isotone local table can be realized by the threshold disjunctive normal form
\[
 f_v(z,c)=\bigvee_{\eta\in\min U_{v,c}}\mathbf 1\{z\geq\eta\}
\]
for each unrestricted-parent cell \(c\).  This realizes a global witness after it has been found, but does not compile the prescribed local failure trace into that witness.
