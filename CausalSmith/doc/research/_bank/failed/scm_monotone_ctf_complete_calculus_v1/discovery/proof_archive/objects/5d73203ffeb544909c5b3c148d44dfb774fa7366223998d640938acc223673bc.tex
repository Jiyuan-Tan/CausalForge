\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(G\) be the unconfounded DAG \(X\to Y\leftarrow Z\), let both arrows have positive sign, and let \(\mathcal Z=\{\varnothing\}\) be the observational menu, equivalently the singleton menu containing the empty hard intervention.  Write \(Y_{xz}\) for the response of \(Y\) under \(\operatorname{do}(X=x,Z=z)\), and set
\[
 A_Q=\{Y_{11}=1\},
 \qquad
 B_Q=\{Y_{10}=1,Y_{01}=1\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The positive-denominator domain is nonempty: take independent private exogenous variables, \(X=U_X\), \(Z=U_Z\), and \(Y=X\lor Z\).  This is a binary recursive semi-Markovian model obeying both positive signs, and it has \(P(B_Q)=1\).

Fix any \(\mathfrak m\in\mathfrak M(G,M)\).  For every exogenous assignment \(u\), monotonicity of \(Z\to Y\) with \(X=1\) fixed and monotonicity of \(X\to Y\) with \(Z=1\) fixed give
\[
 Y_{10}(u)\le Y_{11}(u),
 \qquad
 Y_{01}(u)\le Y_{11}(u).
\tag{2}
\]
Since the responses are binary, (2) implies \(B_Q\subseteq A_Q\).  For every model with \(P_{\mathfrak m}(B_Q)>0\),
\[
 \theta_Q(\mathfrak m)
 =\frac{P_{\mathfrak m}(A_Q\cap B_Q)}{P_{\mathfrak m}(B_Q)}
 =1.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus the query is constant on all positive-denominator models.  In particular it is constant on every common observational-menu fiber, so Definition \(\mathrm{def:identification\text{-}criterion}\) gives \(\operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\).  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}oracle\text{-}complete\text{-}mctfid}\) then gives a successful \(F\in\mathcal R(p)\) satisfying \(F(P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m))=1\) for every compatible positive-denominator model.

We next follow the published \(\mathsf{MID}\) control flow.  Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:mid\text{-}binary\text{-}simplification\text{-}flow}\) states that Algorithm 1 first constructs the joint ctf-factor for its queried and conditioning literals and invokes the reduction before forming the conditional ratio.  For (1), the three literals concern copies of the same primitive vertex \(Y\), use complete assignments of its parent set \(\{X,Z\}\), and share the same exogenous response table.  The factor passed to the reduction step is therefore
\[
 P(Y_{11}=1,Y_{10}=1,Y_{01}=1).
\tag{4}
\]
No parent-child inconsistency is introduced because every literal already fixes the complete parent assignment of \(Y\), and no two literals impose contradictory values at the same assignment.

In the Boolean order on the two positively signed parent coordinates,
\[
 (1,0)\le(1,1),
 \qquad
 (0,1)\le(1,1).
\tag{5}
\]
The binary simplification rule in Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:mid\text{-}binary\text{-}simplification\text{-}flow}\) deletes the upper one-valued literal from an ordered equal-one pair.  Applying it to either comparison in (5) deletes \(Y_{11}=1\) from (4), leaving
\[
 P(Y_{10}=1,Y_{01}=1).
\tag{6}
\]
Neither literal in (6) can be deleted by that rule, because
\[
 (1,0)\not\le(0,1),
 \qquad
 (0,1)\not\le(1,0).
\tag{7}
\]
Thus repeated simplification terminates with two responses of the same variable whose monotone-parent assignments are not totally ordered.  Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:mid\text{-}incomparable\text{-}failure}\) makes the reduction subroutine return \(\mathsf{FAIL}\), and Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:mid\text{-}binary\text{-}simplification\text{-}flow}\) makes Algorithm 1 propagate that result before the numerator-denominator ratio is formed.  Consequently,
\[
 \mathsf{MID}(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q)=\mathsf{FAIL},
 \qquad
 \mathsf{MCTFID}(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q)\ne\mathsf{FAIL}.
\tag{8}
\]
The weak inclusion follows from Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}oracle\text{-}complete\text{-}mctfid}\), and (8) makes it strict.

As a finite exact stress test, the six isotone Boolean tables, with entries ordered as \((Y_{00},Y_{01},Y_{10},Y_{11})\), are
\[
 0000,\quad 0001,\quad 0011,\quad 0101,\quad 0111,\quad 1111.
\]
The event \(B_Q\) selects precisely the last two tables, and both have \(Y_{11}=1\), confirming (3).  If the two positive-sign restrictions are removed, the table \(0110\), realized by \(Y=X\mathbin{\oplus}Z\), has \(B_Q=1\) and \(A_Q=0\); signed monotonicity is therefore load-bearing.  The witness proves only a strict control-flow success-set separation on this common three-binary-variable subclass.  It is not a district-local completeness or gluing theorem. \(\square\)
