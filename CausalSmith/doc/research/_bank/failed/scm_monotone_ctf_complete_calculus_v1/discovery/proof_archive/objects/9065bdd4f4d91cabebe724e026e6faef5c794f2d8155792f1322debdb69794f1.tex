\textbf{Proof.}
\paragraph{Finite parameterization.}
The model domain in the statement is exactly Definition \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\).  By Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:signed\text{-}canonical\text{-}reduction}\), every model relevant to menu equivalence may be replaced without changing any hard-intervention menu cell or any finite counterfactual law by one satisfying \(|\Omega_u|\le b_u\).  Pad smaller supports by zero-mass states.  For
\[
 U(v)=\{u\in U_G:v\in\operatorname{ch}(u)\},
\]
there are \(\prod_{u\in U(v)}b_u\) exogenous-parent configurations at vertex \(v\), and at most \(T_v\) admissible signed-isotone complete tables at each configuration.  Hence the number of joint structural profiles is at most
\[
 \prod_{v\in V}T_v^{\prod_{u\in U(v)}b_u}=A_{G,M}.
\tag{1}
\]
For a fixed profile, eliminating one coordinate from every exogenous simplex leaves at most
\[
 N_{G,M}=\sum_{u\in U_G}(b_u-1)
\tag{2}
\]
free coordinates.  An ordered pair therefore has at most \(2N_{G,M}\) free coordinates.  A model sum ranges over at most \(R_{G,M}=\prod_ub_u\) joint latent states, and a pair over at most \(2R_{G,M}\).

For each specified hard intervention \(z_k=\operatorname{do}(X_{W_k}=w_k)\), Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}decision}\) constructs the integer-coefficient menu polynomial \(\phi_{a,k,x}\) by replacing the equations in \(W_k\) by constants and evaluating the remaining acyclic system.  It likewise constructs \(h_a\) and \(d_a\).  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}decision}\) proves that the ordered-profile-pair system in the corrected Definition \(\mathrm{def:mctfid\text{-}handle}\) is satisfiable exactly when
\[
 \operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=0.
\tag{3}
\]
Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:effective\text{-}real\text{-}closed\text{-}field\text{-}decision}\) makes the decision and exact algebraic sample-point extraction finite and effective.

\paragraph{Failure and its global decoder.}
If (3) holds, sample-point extraction supplies two signed-isotone profiles and exact algebraic product-simplex masses.  Their table constraints imply signed local monotonicity; their simplex products imply the required mutually independent exogenous coordinates.  The sampled equalities and inequalities give
\[
 P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m_0)=p^\star=P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m_1),
 \qquad
 d_{a_i}(\lambda^i)>0\quad(i=0,1),
\]
and
\[
 h_{a_0}(\lambda^0)d_{a_1}(\lambda^1)
 \ne h_{a_1}(\lambda^1)d_{a_0}(\lambda^0).
\]
Division by the two strictly positive denominators gives
\(
 \theta_Q(\mathfrak m_0)\ne\theta_Q(\mathfrak m_1)
\).
All exogenous coordinates affecting a district \(D\) lie in the block \(\{u:C(u)=D\}\); these blocks are disjoint and independent.  The two models are therefore district-factorized.  Definition \(\mathrm{def:fail\text{-}compiler\text{-}handle}\) deletes only zero-mass padding states and checks every displayed equality, inequality, table sign, and simplex constraint, so its output retains \(|\Omega_u|\le b_u\) and all asserted witness properties.  This decoder is global: the proof does not reinterpret its profile-pair certificate as a local graphical trace.

\paragraph{Compilation on the identified branch.}
If the pair system is unsatisfiable, form
\[
 \Gamma_Q
 =\bigcup_a\left\{(p,t):\exists\lambda\,
 \bigl[\Lambda_a(\lambda)\wedge p=\phi_a(\lambda)
 \wedge d_a(\lambda)>0
 \wedge t d_a(\lambda)=h_a(\lambda)\bigr]\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Every nonempty fiber of (4) is a singleton.  Otherwise two distinct ordinates \(t_0\ne t_1\) would give two feasible canonical models with equal menu cells, positive denominators, and
\[
 h_{a_0}(\lambda^0)d_{a_1}(\lambda^1)
 \ne h_{a_1}(\lambda^1)d_{a_0}(\lambda^0),
\]
contradicting unsatisfiability.  Lemmas \(\mathrm{lem:effective\text{-}real\text{-}closed\text{-}field\text{-}decision}\) and \(\mathrm{lem:effective\text{-}cad\text{-}thom\text{-}sections}\) give a finite sign-invariant cylindrical decomposition of the projected menu space and a uniquely encoded algebraic root section on each cell in the projection.  Using its sign description as a guard, its root section as the branch value, and zero off the projection constructs a globally total finite \(F\in\mathcal R(p)\).

For any \(\mathfrak m\in\mathfrak M(G,M)\) with \(P_{\mathfrak m}(B_Q)>0\), canonical reduction supplies a finite model preserving its menu law, numerator, and denominator.  Thus
\[
 \bigl(P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m),\theta_Q(\mathfrak m)\bigr)\in\Gamma_Q,
\]
and singleton-fiber compilation yields
\[
 F(P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m))=\theta_Q(\mathfrak m).
\tag{5}
\]
Equation (5) distinguishes the causal target from the observed-menu functional: it is proved on compatible positive-denominator laws and is not a definition or an off-model equality.

\paragraph{Frontend certificates.}
If \(\mathsf{MID}\) succeeds, Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:mid\text{-}soundness}\) makes the query constant on every compatible positive-denominator menu fiber, so Definition \(\mathrm{def:identification\text{-}criterion}\) gives \(\operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\).  The oracle may therefore skip the search for a separating pair, but it still compiles \(F\) from (4); it never treats the published expression as a literal member of the custom grammar.  This proves
\[
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MID})
 \subseteq\operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MCTFID})
\tag{6}
\]
on the common finite-binary domain.

When \(M=\varnothing\), the signed restrictions are vacuous and the local response tables are all Boolean response functions.  Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:conditional\text{-}ctfid\text{-}completeness}\), specialized to this fixed binary signature, identifies \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) success with functional identification on the unrestricted recursive semi-Markovian class.  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:signed\text{-}canonical\text{-}reduction}\), also specialized to \(M=\varnothing\), is the required countermodel-transfer step: any separating model pair on that class has a counterfactually equivalent finite canonical pair preserving the entire menu and query.  Hence restriction of the oracle's search to canonical pairs neither loses nor creates a separating pair.  The \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) and oracle success sets therefore coincide on the common empty-sign domain.  If \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) returns \(H\), its soundness and (5) imply only
\[
 H(P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m))
 =F(P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m))
 =\theta_Q(\mathfrak m)
\]
on compatible positive-denominator laws.  No equality of syntax or off-model extensions is used.

Finally, (1)--(2) give the advertised profile, pair, coordinate, and latent-configuration bounds.  The two cited real-algebraic lemmas terminate every decision, sample-point, and section-compilation call.  Equations (3)--(5) prove the if-and-only-if oracle dichotomy.  This proof deliberately stops at the global semantic substrate: it establishes neither a terminating district-local ctf-calculus nor a signed-isotone gluing theorem for terminal local failures. \(\square\)
