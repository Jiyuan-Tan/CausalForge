The proved oracle theorem and M-ID soundness establish
\[
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MID})
 \subseteq
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MCTFID})
 =\{(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q):
 \operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\}.
\]
For \(M\ne\varnothing\), every successful M-ID expression is returned literally.  For \(M=\varnothing\), M-ID soundness and conditional CTFID completeness imply only success-set containment.  These facts are the strongest comparison derivable from the declared nodes: neither a checked M-ID failure on an identified instance nor an M-ID completeness theorem is available.
