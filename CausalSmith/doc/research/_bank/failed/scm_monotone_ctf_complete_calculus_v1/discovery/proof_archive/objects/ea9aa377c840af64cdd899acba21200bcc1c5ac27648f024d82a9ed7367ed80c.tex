The established oracle theorem and \(\mathsf{MID}\) soundness prove
\[
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MID})
 \subseteq\operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MCTFID})
 =\{(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q):
 \operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\}.
\]
For \(M\ne\varnothing\), a successful \(\mathsf{MID}\) expression is returned literally.  For \(M=\varnothing\), soundness plus conditional \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) completeness proves success-set containment, but the returned expression is the \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) expression.  No frozen premise supplies either a concrete failed \(\mathsf{MID}\) instance that is globally identified or a completeness invariant for all \(\mathsf{MID}\) failures.
