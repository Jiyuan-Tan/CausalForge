The proved substrate reduces the residual question to one precise composition theorem.  By Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:order\text{-}normal\text{-}factorization}\), for each of \(E=A_Q\cap B_Q\) and \(E=B_Q\),
\[
 \mathbf 1_E
 =\sum_{t:e_E(t)=1}
   \prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}
   \mathbf 1\{(R_v)_{v\in D}=t_D\},
\]
uniquely in the exact response-table atom basis; every deleted order-inconsistent cell is null in every \(\mathfrak m\in\mathfrak M(G,M)\).  Thus the query numerator and denominator already have finite district-factorized order-normal representations.

By Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}oracle\text{-}complete\text{-}mctfid}\), the global problem has the exact dichotomy
\[
 \operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \mathsf{MCTFID}(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q)=F\in\mathcal R(p),
\]
and on this branch
\[
 F(P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m))=\theta_Q(\mathfrak m)
\]
for every compatible positive-denominator model.  On the complementary branch its exact certificate decodes to finite signed-isotone models \(\mathfrak m_0,\mathfrak m_1\) satisfying
\[
 P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m_0)
 =P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m_1),
 \qquad
 P_{\mathfrak m_i}(B_Q)>0\quad(i=0,1),
 \qquad
 \theta_Q(\mathfrak m_0)\ne\theta_Q(\mathfrak m_1).
\]
Consequently, existence of finite identifying functionals and finite signed-isotone countermodels is settled.  What is not proved is the strictly stronger graphical compression: a finite district-local rule calculus complete for the global criterion, together with a composable local-to-global signed-isotone countermodel lemma.  The two established endpoint theorems do not imply that lemma.
