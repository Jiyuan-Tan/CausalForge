Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:order\text{-}normal\text{-}factorization}\) gives a unique finite exact response-table atom expansion with one factor per district.  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}oracle\text{-}complete\text{-}mctfid}\) gives a terminating global semialgebraic decision and exact finite signed-isotone countermodels on failure.  Therefore finite order-normalization and global countermodel existence are proved.  What remains unproved is a uniform district-local ctf-calculus failure rule and a composable binary signed-isotone threshold gadget that converts every such local failure into the certified countermodels.
