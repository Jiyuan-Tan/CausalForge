Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:order\text{-}normal\text{-}factorization}\) supplies a unique finite exact response-table atom expansion with one factor per district, while Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}oracle\text{-}complete\text{-}mctfid}\) supplies a terminating global semialgebraic decision and exact finite signed-isotone countermodels on failure.  Thus both endpoints of the proposed compression are available.  What remains unproved is a uniform district-local ctf-calculus failure rule and a composable binary signed-isotone gadget turning every such local failure into the required pair of countermodels.
