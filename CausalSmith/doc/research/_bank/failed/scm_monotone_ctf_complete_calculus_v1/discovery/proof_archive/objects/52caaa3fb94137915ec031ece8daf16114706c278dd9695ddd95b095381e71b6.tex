There is an effective finite procedure \(\mathsf{MCTFID}\), with failure decoder \(\mathsf C_{\mathsf F}\), having the following properties for every \((G,M,\mathcal Z,Q)\).  Put
\[
 N_{G,M}=\sum_{u\in U_G}(b_u-1),
 \qquad
 A_{G,M}=\prod_{v\in V}T_v^{\prod_{u\in U_G:\,v\in\operatorname{ch}(u)}b_u}.
\]
The procedure enumerates at most \(A_{G,M}\) canonical signed-isotone structural profiles, tests at most \(A_{G,M}^2\) ordered profile pairs, uses at most \(2N_{G,M}\) free mass coordinates and \(2R_{G,M}\) joint latent configurations per pair, and makes finitely many exact quantifier-elimination, sample-point, and cylindrical-decomposition calls on the resulting rational polynomial systems.  It returns a globally totalized \(F\in\mathcal R(p)\) if and only if \(\operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\), and then
\[
 F(P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m))=\theta_Q(\mathfrak m)
\]
for every \(\mathfrak m\in\mathfrak M(G,M)\) with \(P_{\mathfrak m}(B_Q)>0\).  Otherwise it returns \((\mathsf{FAIL},\tau_{\mathsf F})\), and \(\mathsf C_{\mathsf F}(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q,\tau_{\mathsf F})=(\mathfrak m_0,\mathfrak m_1,p^{\star})\) gives two finite district-factorized signed-isotone SCMs satisfying \(|\Omega_u|\le b_u\), positive query denominators,
\[
 P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m_0)=p^{\star}=P_{\mathcal Z}(\mathfrak m_1),
 \qquad
 \theta_Q(\mathfrak m_0)\ne\theta_Q(\mathfrak m_1).
\]
When \(M=\varnothing\), the success branch is definitionally the unchanged lift of \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) and has exactly the \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) success set.  When \(M\ne\varnothing\), every successful \(\mathsf{MID}\) output is returned unchanged.  If \(M=\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{MID}\) succeeds, only success-set containment is asserted: soundness of \(\mathsf{MID}\) and completeness of conditional \(\mathsf{CTFID}\) imply that \(\mathsf{MCTFID}\) succeeds, but its expression need not be the literal \(\mathsf{MID}\) expression.  Consequently,
\[
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MID})
 \subseteq\operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MCTFID})
 =\{(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q):\operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\}.
\]
No claim is made here that the first inclusion is strict or that the finite oracle has a district-compositional graphical failure trace.
