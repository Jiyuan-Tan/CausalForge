\paragraph{Response tables.}
Fix \(\mathfrak m\in\mathfrak M(G,M)\).  For a latent assignment \(u\), define the complete local response table
\[
 R_v(u)(x_{\operatorname{pa}_G(v)})
 =f_v(x_{\operatorname{pa}_G(v)},u_{\operatorname{pa}_U(v)}).
\]
For a signed parent \(w\in S_v\), put \(z_w=x_w\) for sign \(+1\) and \(z_w=1-x_w\) for sign \(-1\).  Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:signed\text{-}local\text{-}monotonicity}\) says, coordinate by coordinate, that the resulting table is nondecreasing in every \(z_w\).  Hence, for each fixed assignment of the \(r_v\) unrestricted parents, the set of normalized signed-parent assignments on which the output is one is an up-set of \(\{0,1\}^{m_v}\).  There are \(D_{m_v}\) choices for that up-set and \(2^{r_v}\) unrestricted-parent assignments.  Thus the number of possible complete tables at \(v\) is at most
\[
 T_v=D_{m_v}^{,2^{r_v}}.
\]
Write \(\mathcal T_v\) for this finite table set.

\paragraph{Finite support without changing the latent graph.}
For a district \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), let
\[
 U_D=\{u\in U_G:C(u)=D\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal T_D=\prod_{v\in D}\mathcal T_v,
 \qquad
 q_D=|\mathcal T_D|\leq\prod_{v\in D}T_v.
\]
Every exogenous parent of a vertex in \(D\) belongs to \(U_D\), so the district response-table vector has the form
\[
 R_D=(R_v:v\in D)=H_D(U_D)in\mathcal T_D.
\]
The sets \(U_D\) are disjoint across districts, and their coordinates are independent by \(\mathrm{ass:semi\text{-}markovian}\).

Choose one \(u_0\in U_D\), temporarily keeping the laws of the other sources fixed.  For \(a\in\Omega_{u_0}\) and \(t\in\mathcal T_D\), set
\[
 q_a(t)
 =P\{H_D(a,U_D\setminus\{u_0\})=t\}.
\]
Then \(q_a\) is a point of the simplex \(\Delta_{q_D-1}\), and the current law of \(R_D\) is the barycenter
\[
 \bar q=\int q_a\,d\mu_{u_0}(a).
\]
We use the following elementary finite-barycenter fact.  If a probability measure has values in a subset \(S\subset\mathbb R^d\), its barycenter belongs to \(\operatorname{conv}(S)\): approximation by simple functions first puts it in \(\overline{\operatorname{conv}}(S)\); if it lay on a missing boundary face, a supporting affine functional would be constant almost surely, and induction on the affine dimension places the barycenter in the convex hull of that face.  Once the barycenter is in \(\operatorname{conv}(S)\), any representation using more than \(d+1\) points can be shortened: affine dependence gives coefficients \(c_j\), not all zero, with \(\sum_jc_j=0\) and \(\sum_jc_js_j=0\); replacing weights \(\lambda_j\) by \(\lambda_j-\varepsilon c_j\), with \(\varepsilon\) chosen so that all new weights are nonnegative and at least one becomes zero, preserves the barycenter.  Repetition proves the \(d+1\) bound.

Here the affine dimension is at most \(q_D-1\).  Consequently there are \(a_1,\ldots,a_\ell\in\Omega_{u_0}\) and \(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_\ell\geq0\), with
\[
 \ell\leq q_D,
 \qquad
 \sum_{j=1}^{\ell}\lambda_j=1,
 \qquad
 \bar q=\sum_{j=1}^{\ell}\lambda_jq_{a_j}.
\]
Replacing \(\mu_{u_0}\) by \(\sum_j\lambda_j\delta_{a_j}\) therefore leaves the entire law of \(R_D\) unchanged.  It also preserves independence because only one factor of the product exogenous law was replaced.  Repeat this operation for every \(u\in U_D\) and then for every district.  Since the number of exogenous indices is finite, the iteration terminates, and for every \(u\in U_G\),
\[
 |\Omega_u|
 \leq q_{C(u)}
 \leq\prod_{v\in C(u)}T_v
 =b_u.
\]

The construction leaves the selected values of every structural table unchanged, so signed local monotonicity is preserved.  It preserves the law of \(R_D\) for each district; independence across districts therefore preserves the joint law of \((R_v:v\in V)\).  By acyclicity, every finite vector of potential responses is obtained by recursively evaluating a deterministic function of this complete response-table vector.  Its law is consequently unchanged.  Thus the resulting finite model \(\widetilde{\mathfrak m}\) is counterfactually equivalent to \(\mathfrak m\), and in particular preserves every finite menu law and every finite counterfactual law.  Its joint latent support has size at most
\[
 \prod_{u\in U_G}|\Omega_u|
 \leq\prod_{u\in U_G}b_u
 =R_{G,M}.
\]

\paragraph{Strict reduction.}
Because \(S_v\) and \(\operatorname{pa}_G(v)\setminus S_v\) partition the observed parents,
\[
 |\operatorname{pa}_G(v)|=m_v+r_v.
\]
If \(m_v>0\), not every Boolean function on \(m_v\) coordinates is isotone; for example \(z\mapsto1-z_1\) is not.  Hence
\[
 D_{m_v}<2^{2^{m_v}}.
\]
Raising both sides to the positive integer power \(2^{r_v}\) gives
\[
 T_v=D_{m_v}^{2^{r_v}}
 <\left(2^{2^{m_v}}\right)^{2^{r_v}}
 =2^{2^{m_v+r_v}}
 =2^{2^{|\operatorname{pa}_G(v)|}},
\]
which is the asserted strict comparison.  This completes the proof.
