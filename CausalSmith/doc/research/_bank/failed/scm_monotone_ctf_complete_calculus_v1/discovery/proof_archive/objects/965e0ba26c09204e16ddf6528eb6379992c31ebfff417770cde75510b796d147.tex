Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}oracle\text{-}complete\text{-}mctfid}\) and Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:mid\text{-}soundness}\) prove
\[
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MID})
 \subseteq
 \operatorname{Succ}(\mathsf{MCTFID})
 =\{(G,M,\mathcal Z,Q):
 \operatorname{GID}_{G,M,\mathcal Z}(Q)=1\}.
\]
Consequently, strictness is equivalent to the existence of an identified instance on which \(\mathsf{MID}\) returns \(\mathsf{FAIL}\), while equality is equivalent to the absence of every such instance. For \(M\ne\varnothing\), every successful \(\mathsf{MID}\) expression is returned literally by \(\mathsf{MCTFID}\); for \(M=\varnothing\), the frozen theorem asserts success-set containment but not literal expression equality. This is the strongest comparison proved from the declared nodes.
