The established upper and lower bounds give, with \(L:=\log(en)\), \(b:=n^{-1}+d/n^2\), and \(u:=d^2/(n^2L^2)\),
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\}
 \le \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{\frac1n+\min\left(1,u,\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}.
\]
These benchmarks are order-equivalent at \(\sigma=0\), at \(\sigma=2\), at every radius bounded away from zero, under saturation \(u\ge1\), whenever \(u\lesssim b\), and whenever \(\sigma^2\lesssim b\).  Their ratio can diverge only when \(b\ll u\ll1\) and \(b\ll\sigma^2\ll1\), and it does diverge for the benchmark sequence \(d_n=n\), \(\sigma_n=\log(en)^{-1/2}\).  The existing hypothesis-independent Bernoulli channel yields only separation of order \(M\sigma\min\{1,d/[n\log(en)]\}\), whose square is the proved product term.
