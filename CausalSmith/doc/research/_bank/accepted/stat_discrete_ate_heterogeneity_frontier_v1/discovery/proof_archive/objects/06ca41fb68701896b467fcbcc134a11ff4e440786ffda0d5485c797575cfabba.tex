Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), and write \(S_P:=\{k\in\{1,\ldots,d\}:p_k>0\}\). For \(k\in S_P\), Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:overlap}\) gives
\[
P(A=1,X=k)=p_k\pi_k\ge p_k\epsilon>0,\qquad P(A=0,X=k)=p_k(1-\pi_k)\ge p_k\epsilon>0.
\]
Hence both observed conditional means in cell \(k\) are well-defined and are functionals of \(P_O\). Assumptions \(\mathrm{ass:mean\text{-}normalization}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:fourth\text{-}central\text{-}moment}\), which enter through \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\), ensure the needed integrability: conditional Lyapunov's inequality gives
\[
E_P[|Y-\mu_{ak}|\mid A=a,X=k]\le\{E_P[(Y-\mu_{ak})^4\mid A=a,X=k]\}^{1/4}\le M,
\]
and therefore \(E_P[|Y|\mid A=a,X=k]\le 3M/2\). By consistency, for every \(a\in\{0,1\}\) and \(k\in S_P\),
\[
E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=k]=E_P[Y(a)\mid A=a,X=k].
\]
By conditional exchangeability, the right-hand side equals \(E_P[Y(a)\mid X=k]\). Consequently, the law of total expectation over the support of \(X\) yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
E_P[Y(1)-Y(0)]
&=\sum_{k\in S_P}p_k\{E_P[Y(1)\mid X=k]-E_P[Y(0)\mid X=k]\}\\
&=\sum_{k\in S_P}p_k\{E_P[Y\mid A=1,X=k]-E_P[Y\mid A=0,X=k]\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
For \(k\notin S_P\), choose arbitrary finite versions of the two conditional means; their contributions are zero because \(p_k=0\). Thus the last display can equivalently be written as a sum from \(1\) to \(d\), independently of those null-cell versions. By \(\mathrm{def:ate\text{-}functional}\), its left-hand side is \(\tau(P)\). Finally, \(p_k=P_O(X=k)\) and, on every positive-mass cell, both conditional means are determined by \(P_O\). Hence the displayed expression is a well-defined observed-law functional and identifies the causal average treatment effect.
