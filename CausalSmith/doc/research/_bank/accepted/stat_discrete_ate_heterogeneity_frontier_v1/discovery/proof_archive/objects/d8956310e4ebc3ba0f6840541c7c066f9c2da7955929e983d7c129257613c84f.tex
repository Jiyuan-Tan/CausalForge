\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), and write
\(S_P:=\{k\in\{1,\ldots,d\}:p_k>0\}\).  Membership in
\(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), through
\(\mathrm{ass:overlap}\), gives, for every \(k\in S_P\),
\[
 P(A=1,X=k)=p_k\pi_k\ge p_k\epsilon>0,
 \qquad
 P(A=0,X=k)=p_k(1-\pi_k)\ge p_k\epsilon>0.
\]
Thus both arm-specific observed conditional laws are defined in every
supported cell.  Moreover, by \(\mathrm{ass:second\text{-}central\text{-}moment}\)
and \(\mathrm{ass:mean\text{-}normalization}\), for each
\(a\in\{0,1\}\) and \(k\in S_P\),
\[
 E_P[Y^2\mid A=a,X=k]
 =E_P[(Y-\mu_{ak})^2\mid A=a,X=k]+\mu_{ak}^2
 \le M^2+\frac{M^2}{4}=\frac{5M^2}{4}.
\]
The equality uses the defining identity
\(E_P[Y-\mu_{ak}\mid A=a,X=k]=0\).  Hence the displayed conditional
means are finite.  Conditional exchangeability also transfers these
integrable arm-specific conditional laws to the corresponding potential
outcomes given \(X=k\).

For \(a\in\{0,1\}\) and \(k\in S_P\),
\(\mathrm{ass:conditional\text{-}exchangeability}\) and the just-verified
arm positivity imply
\[
 E_P[Y(a)\mid X=k]
 =E_P[Y(a)\mid A=a,X=k].
\]
By \(\mathrm{ass:consistency}\), \(Y(a)=Y\) almost surely on
\(\{A=a\}\), and therefore
\[
 E_P[Y(a)\mid A=a,X=k]
 =E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=k].
\]
Applying the law of total expectation over the finite support of \(X\)
now yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_P[Y(1)-Y(0)]
 &=\sum_{k\in S_P}p_k
   \bigl\{E_P[Y(1)\mid X=k]-E_P[Y(0)\mid X=k]\bigr\}\\
 &=\sum_{k\in S_P}p_k
   \bigl\{E_P[Y\mid A=1,X=k]-E_P[Y\mid A=0,X=k]\bigr\}\\
 &=\sum_{k=1}^{d}p_k
   \bigl\{E_P[Y\mid A=1,X=k]-E_P[Y\mid A=0,X=k]\bigr\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
In the last line, terms with \(p_k=0\) are assigned value zero; equivalently,
the functional is the preceding sum over \(S_P\), so it never requires a
conditional law on a null cell.  By \(\mathrm{def:ate\text{-}functional}\),
the left-hand side is \(\tau(P)\).  Finally, \(p_k\) and, on every supported
cell, each conditional mean in the last display are functionals of the
observed margin \(P_O\).  Consequently the displayed map is computable from
\(P_O\) and identifies the causal average treatment effect. \(\square\)
