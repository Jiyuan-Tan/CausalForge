Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  Let \(c_{U,\epsilon}>0\) be the dimension-range constant in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\), and let \(c_{L,\epsilon}>0\) be the corresponding constant in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\).  Put
\[
 c_{0,\epsilon}:=\min\{c_{U,\epsilon},c_{L,\epsilon},1\}.
\]
For every \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{0,\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\) gives
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{U,\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\tag{1}
\]
The witness in (1) is the single data-driven total selector from Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\), so (1) is a minimax upper bound for Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\), not a law-dependent oracle bound.  Its divisions have the bounded fallbacks specified in the two component constructions, and clipping keeps its range inside \([-M,M]\).

On the same radius and dimension range, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) gives
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c'_{L,\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
The \(n^{-1}\) boundary component is also supplied for every \(n,d\ge1\) by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:parametric\text{-}lower}\).  The other terms in (2) are lower bounds obtained by restricting the supremum in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\) to subclasses of \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\); hence the lower and upper sides use the same model class.

Choose
\[
 c_{\epsilon}:=\min\{c_{0,\epsilon},c'_{L,\epsilon},C_{U,\epsilon}\},
 \qquad
 C_{\epsilon}:=\max\{C_{U,\epsilon},c_{\epsilon}\}.
\]
After decreasing the common dimension-range constant if necessary, \(0<c_{\epsilon}\le C_{\epsilon}<\infty\), and (1)--(2) give the displayed bracket.

Definition~\(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\) imposes fixed overlap only on positive-mass cells, the conditional-mean envelope, the conditional fourth-central-moment bound, and the radius constraint.  Thus zero-mass cells are immaterial, no rank or density condition is used, and all conditioning and division steps are covered by the stated overlap and fallback conditions.  Equations (1)--(2) establish only the bracket; no comparison of its two benchmarks at shrinking interior radii was used or asserted.
