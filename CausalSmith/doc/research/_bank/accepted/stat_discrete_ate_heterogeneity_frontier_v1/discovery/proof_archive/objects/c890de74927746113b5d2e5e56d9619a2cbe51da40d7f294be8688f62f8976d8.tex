Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\) constructs the first estimator and proves its displayed bound uniformly over the model; the proof also establishes totality, all denominator fallbacks, and the fixed-sample falling-factorial correction.  Define \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\) by the formula in \(\mathrm{def:collision\text{-}handle}\).  That formula is exactly the estimator called \(T_n^{\mathrm{occ}}\) in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}occupancy\text{-}collision\text{-}upper}\), so that lemma proves the second display.  Both estimators are clipped into \([-M,M]\), hence belong to \(\mathcal T_{n,M}\).  Taking the smaller of the two dimension-range constants and the larger of the two risk constants proves the theorem, including the boundary cases \(\sigma=0\), \(\sigma=2\), empty empirical arms, and \(R_n=0\).
