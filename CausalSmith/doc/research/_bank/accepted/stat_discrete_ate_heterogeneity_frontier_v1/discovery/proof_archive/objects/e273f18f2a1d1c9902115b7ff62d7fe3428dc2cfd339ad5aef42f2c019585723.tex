The all-alphabet selector theorem establishes the algebraic comparison \(\min(1,u,h)\le h\), where \(u=d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\) and \(h=\sigma^2+d/n^2\). If \(u=o(h)\) and \(d=o(n\log(en))\), then \(u=o(1)\), so the selected remainder is eventually \(u=o(h)\). The published binary collision guarantee is retained explicitly as the comparison premise and is not used by any headline or headline-support theorem.
