For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), the explicit linear-mark Chebyshev factorial estimator \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\) and the occupancy-weighted estimator \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\) are total elements of \(\mathcal T_{n,M}\).  A constant \(C_{\epsilon}<\infty\) satisfies, for every pair of integers \(n,d\ge1\) and every \(0\le\sigma\le2\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left(n^{-1}+\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right).
\]
Both maps have explicit zero fallbacks and clipping, and neither bound contains an additional logarithmic factor.
