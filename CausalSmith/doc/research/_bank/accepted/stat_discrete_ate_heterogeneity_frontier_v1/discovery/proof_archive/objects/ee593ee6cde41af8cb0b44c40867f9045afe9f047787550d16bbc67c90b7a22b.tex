Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\) constructs the explicit linear-mark Chebyshev factorial estimator \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\) as a measurable sample map, supplies its zero fallbacks and clipping, and proves uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) that
\[
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon M^2\left\{n^{-1}+
 \min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Its clipping shows directly that \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\).

Next define \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\) by Definition~\(\mathrm{def:collision\text{-}handle}\).  This is exactly the total map denoted by \(T_n^{\mathrm{occ}}\) in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}occupancy\text{-}collision\text{-}upper}\): empirical arm means are evaluated only when both arm counts are positive, the outer ratio is evaluated only when \(R_n>0\), the remaining cases use the specified zero value, and the result is clipped into \([-M,M]\).  Hence \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\), and that lemma gives
\[
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon M^2\left(n^{-1}+\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right).
\tag{2}
\]
Take \(c_\epsilon\) to be the smaller of the positive dimension constants in the two lemmas and enlarge \(C_\epsilon\) to dominate both constants.  Then (1)--(2) hold simultaneously for every \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_\epsilon n^2/\log(en)\).  The arguments include \(\sigma=0\), \(\sigma=2\), empty empirical treatment arms, and \(R_n=0\).  Since the rates in (1)--(2) are exactly the displayed rates, neither construction incurs an additional logarithmic factor.
