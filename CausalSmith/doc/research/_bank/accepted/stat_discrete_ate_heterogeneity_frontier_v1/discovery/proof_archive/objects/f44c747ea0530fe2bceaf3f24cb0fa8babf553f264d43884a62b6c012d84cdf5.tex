The shrinking-radius question has an affirmative estimator-side answer.  The explicit centered mixed-arm factorial estimator \(T_n^{\mathrm{ctr}}\), using an independent clipped occupancy pilot and the signed kernel in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:centered\text{-}product\text{-}estimator}\), satisfies throughout \(u\le\kappa_{\epsilon}\)
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{ctr}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2u\}.
\]
Combined with the occupancy branch for \(u>\kappa_{\epsilon}\) and the radius-channel converse, this yields the uniform sharp minimax rate \(M^2\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\}\).  In particular, the earlier residual wedge is resolved without a stronger fuzzy experiment.
