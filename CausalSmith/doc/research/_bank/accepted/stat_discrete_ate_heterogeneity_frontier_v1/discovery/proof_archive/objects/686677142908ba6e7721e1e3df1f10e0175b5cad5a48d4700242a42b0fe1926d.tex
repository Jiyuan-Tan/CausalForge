Put
\[
 L:=\log(en),\qquad b:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2},\qquad
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},\qquad \rho:=\sqrt u=\frac d{nL}.
\]
Theorems~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\) and
\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\), together with the
localization in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:fixed\text{-}interior\text{-}tightness\text{-}and\text{-}shrinking\text{-}radius\text{-}gap}\), prove throughout the residual wedge that
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2u\}
 \le \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\min(u,\sigma^2)\}.
\tag{1}
\]

There is a rigorous obstruction to every arm-dependent but cell- and
hypothesis-independent outcome channel applied to the binary one-arm source
family.  Write its conditional output means as
\[
 \nu_{ab}:=\int y\,K_a(\mathrm dy\mid b),\qquad
 c:=\nu_{10}-\nu_{00},\qquad \gamma:=\nu_{11}-\nu_{10}.
\]
If the treated source success probability in cell \(k\) is \(\eta_k\),
linearity gives
\[
 \tau_k=c+\gamma\eta_k,
 \qquad
 \tau=c+\gamma\sum_kp_k\eta_k.
\tag{2}
\]
Apply the same channel to the supported two-cell law with equal masses and
\((\eta_1,\eta_2)=(0,1)\).  The realization-wise radius restriction gives
\[
 \frac{|\gamma|}{2}
 =\max_{k\in\{1,2\}}|\tau_k-\tau|
 \le\sigma M,
 \qquad |\gamma|\le2\sigma M.
\tag{3}
\]
Consequently a source target gap of order \(\rho\) produces at most order
\(M\sigma\rho\) separation.  Data processing preserves the source
total-variation certificate, so the obstruction is separation rather than
distinguishability.  Since
\[
 \frac{\min(\sigma,\rho)}{\sigma\rho}
 =\frac1{\max(\sigma,\rho)}\longrightarrow\infty
\tag{4}
\]
in the residual wedge, this fixed-channel architecture cannot attain the
requested order \(M\min(\sigma,\rho)\).

A centered mixed-arm factorial lift does achieve the desired deterministic
approximation bias, but fails stochastically on the frozen weak-moment class.
Take a block of size \(m\), set \(d=m\), \(M=1\), \(p_k=1/m\),
\(\pi_k=1/2\), and give both arms zero conditional means and independent
conditional Rademacher outcomes.  This law satisfies consistency,
conditional exchangeability, overlap, mean normalization, the conditional
second-central-moment bound, and exact homogeneity.  Give the construction
its oracle pilot.  For the proposed mixed-arm monomials, the constant
Chebyshev coefficient is
\[
 g_{K0}=\frac{K^2-1}{3}.
\tag{5}
\]
On a cell containing exactly one treated and one control observation, every
higher-degree monomial vanishes, whereas the two degree-zero terms coincide.
Conditional independence of the Rademacher marks and the exact multinomial
occupancy calculation therefore give, for the complete light statistic
\(Z\),
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(Z)
 \ge c_1\frac{g_{K0}^2}{B^2m^3}
 \asymp\frac{\log^2(em)}m
\tag{6}
\]
under the reciprocal-approximation calibration
\(K\asymp\log(em)\) and \(B\asymp\log(em)/m\).  The product-rate covariance
claim would require order \(m^{-1}+d/m^2\asymp m^{-1}\), contradicting
\((6)\).  An oracle pilot and independent sample splitting do not remove
this singleton-pair diagonal.

The population approximation calculation remains valid.  With
\(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\),
\(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\),
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t),
 \qquad
 c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\},
\]
and
\[
 S_K(t):=\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\},
\]
exact algebra gives
\[
 t+S_K(t)-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right).
\tag{7}
\]
The reciprocal approximation and overlap imply
\[
 |t+S_K(t)-\vartheta|^2
 \le C_{\epsilon}u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus the product-scaled deterministic bias is genuine, but this stochastic
lift cannot realize it.  A stronger converse must use a radius-coupled,
hypothesis-dependent construction with joint moment matching and
fixed-sample normalization, or a sharper estimator must suppress the
singleton-pair noise amplification.  Neither construction has been derived,
and the route-specific obstructions above do not prove nonexistence.
