For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \(C_{\epsilon}<\infty\), \(\kappa_{\epsilon}\in(0,1)\), \(c_{\epsilon}>0\), and \(N_{\epsilon}<\infty\) such that the explicit estimator \(T_n^{\mathrm{ctr}}\) of Definition~\(\mathrm{def:centered\text{-}product\text{-}estimator}\) belongs to \(\mathcal T_{n,M}\) and, uniformly for \(0\le\sigma\le2\), \(n\ge N_{\epsilon}\), \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), and
\[
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\le\kappa_{\epsilon},
\]
satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{ctr}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}+\sigma^2u
 \right\}.
\]
The construction is clipped and total and requires no outcome tail condition beyond Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass:second\text{-}central\text{-}moment}\).
