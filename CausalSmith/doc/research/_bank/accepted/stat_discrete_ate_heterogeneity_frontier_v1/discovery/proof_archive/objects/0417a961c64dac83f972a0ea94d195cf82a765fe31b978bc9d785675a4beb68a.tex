Put
\[
 L:=\log(en),\qquad b:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2},\qquad
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},\qquad m:=\min\{u,\sigma^2\}.
\]
In the residual wedge, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:fixed\text{-}interior\text{-}tightness\text{-}and\text{-}shrinking\text{-}radius\text{-}gap}\) proves that the selector benchmark is within fixed factors of \(b+m\), whereas Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) gives \(b+\sigma^2u\).

There is an exact lower-side radius parametrization.  For a pair of total mass \(\omega>0\) and share \(r\in[0,1]\), set
\[
 p_+:=\omega r,\quad p_-:=\omega(1-r),\qquad
 \vartheta_+:=\theta+\sigma(1-r),\quad
 \vartheta_-:=\theta-\sigma r.
\]
Then the pair average is \(\theta\) and both deviations are bounded by \(\sigma\).  When \(\sigma>0\), the translation \(\theta'=\theta+\Delta\), \(r'=r+\Delta/\sigma\) leaves both cell effects unchanged whenever \(r'\in[0,1]\), while changing the pair-average effect by \(\Delta\).  Thus the desired minimum-type separation is algebraically compatible with realization-wise radius control.  With deterministic \(\omega\), however, first-intensity matching forces \(E_0r=E_1r\), excluding a nonzero mean translation.  The remaining lower route is a correlated moment problem in \((\omega,r)\), plus normalization concentration and fixed-sample de-Poissonization.  Moreover, equal-mass reflected propensities have equal collision factors \(\pi(1-\pi)\), so a nonzero common-effect shift is already revealed by the degree-two collision moment.

For the upper route, normalize \(V:=Y/M\), \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\), and \(z_{ak}:=E[V\mathbf1\{X=k,A=a\}]\).  For an independent pilot \(t\in[-1,1]\), define
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}z_{1k}-s_{1k}z_{0k}-ts_{0k}s_{1k}
 =s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t).
\]
For a reciprocal polynomial \(r_K\), put
\[
 e_{ak}:=1-s_{ak}r_K(s_{ak}),\qquad
 c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\},
\]
and
\[
 S_K(t):=\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}.
\]
Direct algebra gives
\[
 p_k-c_k=s_{1k}e_{0k}+s_{0k}e_{1k}
\]
and, using \(\sum_kp_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)=0\),
\[
 t+S_K(t)-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right).
\]
If \(|e_{ak}|\le B/(K^2s_{ak})\) on light cells, overlap and approximate homogeneity imply
\[
 |t+S_K(t)-\vartheta|
 \le C_\epsilon\{\sigma+|t-\vartheta|\}\frac{dB}{K^2}.
\]
With \(B\asymp L/n\) and \(K\asymp L\), the squared deterministic error is at most \(C_\epsilon u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}\).  Therefore, a total clipped factorial lift \(\widehat S_K(t)\) satisfying
\[
 E[(\widehat S_K(t)-S_K(t))^2\mid t]
 \le C_\epsilon\left\{b+u[\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2]\right\}
\]
would, with the independent collision pilot bound \(E[(t-\vartheta)^2]\le C_\epsilon(b+\sigma^2)\), attain risk \(C_\epsilon M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\).  The unresolved step is precisely this centered mixed-arm factorial variance inequality: the existing linear-mark covariance lemma does not contain its kernel or establish its exact-homogeneity degeneracy.
