Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy study the same discrete observational structure for binary outcomes.  Their equation (12) and Theorem 3 give the approximate-homogeneity collision upper branch, Theorem 2 gives the unrestricted lower branch, and Theorem 4 with Section 4.2 gives the exact-homogeneity lower bound and identifies sharp radius dependence as open.  Their 401(k) analysis is the downstream motivating consumer, not an inference claim made here.  Jiao, Venkat, Han, and Weissman provide the heavy-light best-polynomial and factorial-moment architecture, while Wu and Yang connect polynomial approximation with moment-matching priors.  The present result extends this architecture to signed continuous marks under a fourth-moment envelope and localizes the remaining radius question to \(b\ll u,\sigma^2\ll1\).
