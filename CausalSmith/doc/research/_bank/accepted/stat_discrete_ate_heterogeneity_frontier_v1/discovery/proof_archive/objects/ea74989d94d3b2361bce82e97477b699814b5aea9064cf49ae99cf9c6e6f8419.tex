Identification and the robust component estimators are established on the real-outcome fourth-moment class, and the lower bounds transfer from scaled binary subclasses of that same class.  The minimax rate is matched at \(\sigma=0\), \(\sigma=2\), every radius bounded away from zero, saturation, \(d^2\le n\log^2(en)+d\log^2(en)\), and every admissible alphabet with \(\sigma^2\lesssim n^{-1}+d/n^2\).  The sole acknowledged rate problem is the shrinking-radius wedge \(n^{-1}+d/n^2\ll d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\ll1\) and \(n^{-1}+d/n^2\ll\sigma^2\ll1\).  Sampling inference and adaptation are outside scope.
