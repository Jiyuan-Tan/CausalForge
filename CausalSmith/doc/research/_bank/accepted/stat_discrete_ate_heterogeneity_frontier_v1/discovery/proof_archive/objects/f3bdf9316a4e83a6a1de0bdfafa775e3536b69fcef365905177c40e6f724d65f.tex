Put
\[
 L:=\log(en),\qquad b:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2},\qquad
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},\qquad s:=\sigma^2.
\tag{1}
\]
If \(u\le\kappa_{\epsilon}\), corrected Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\) selects \(T_n^{\mathrm{ctr}}\).  Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:centered\text{-}product\text{-}upper}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2(b+su)
 =C_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+s\min(1,u)\}.
\tag{2}
\]
For the finitely many sample sizes below that theorem's calibration cutoff, the centered construction uses its zero fallback and has loss at most \(M^2\); since \(b\ge1/n\), increasing \(C_{\epsilon}\) absorbs them.

If \(u>\kappa_{\epsilon}\), the corrected selector uses \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\).  Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:robust\text{-}upper\text{-}construction\text{-}resolution}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E[(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2(b+s).
\tag{3}
\]
Because \(\min(1,u)\ge\min(1,\kappa_{\epsilon})=\kappa_{\epsilon}\),
\[
 b+s\le\kappa_{\epsilon}^{-1}\{b+s\min(1,u)\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations \((2)\)--\((4)\), after enlarging the constant and taking the smaller dimension constant from the component results, prove the displayed bound.  Both branches are clipped total sample maps, and their definitions cover empty empirical arms and zero pilot or occupancy denominators.

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:zeng\text{-}binary\text{-}collision\text{-}upper}\) records a radius remainder of order \(s\) and an occupancy remainder \(d/n^2\).  The new radius remainder is \(s\min(1,u)\le s\), with strict order reduction whenever \(u=o(1)\) and the radius term is nonnegligible.  This is a comparison only: equations \((2)\)--\((4)\) hold on the frozen real-outcome class and use no binary-outcome restriction.
