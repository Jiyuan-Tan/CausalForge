For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), the total known-radius selector \(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}\) in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\) satisfies, for a constant \(C_{\epsilon}<\infty\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}
\]
for every pair of integers \(n,d\ge1\) and every \(0\le\sigma\le2\).  Relative to the collision guarantee in Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy, Theorem 3 on page 11, the heterogeneity-dependent remainder is never larger in order and is strictly smaller when \(d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]=o(\sigma^2+d/n^2)\) and \(d=o(n\log(en))\).
