Shrink the common dimension constant so that Theorems~\(\mathrm{thm:sharp\text{-}frontier\text{-}upper}\) and \(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) both apply.  By corrected Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\), \(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\).  It is therefore an admissible witness in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\), and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:sharp\text{-}frontier\text{-}upper}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 &\le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]\\
 &\le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) gives the reverse inequality with \(c_{\epsilon}>0\) and exactly the same expression:
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Definition~\(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\) is the class used in both \((1)\) and \((2)\).  In particular, the upper proof uses the conditional-second-moment centered-mark calculation, while the lower proof restricts to a scaled binary subclass; restricting to a subclass proves a lower bound for the broader class and does not substitute the model.  Combining \((1)\) and \((2)\) proves the matched minimax theorem, including \(\sigma=0\), \(\sigma=2\), zero-mass cells, and all displayed division boundaries.
