The upper inequality is Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\).  The theorem resolving \(\mathrm{oeq:sigma\text{-}indexed\text{-}converse}\) proves the lower inequality on exactly the same class and over the same dimension and radius range.  Taking the smaller of their range constants and adjusting the two risk constants proves the displayed bracket.  No equality of the two interior expressions is asserted: the upper nonparametric remainder is
\[
 \min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)},\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right\},
\]
whereas the proved radius-dependent lower remainder is
\[
 \frac d{n^2}+\sigma^2\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\]
