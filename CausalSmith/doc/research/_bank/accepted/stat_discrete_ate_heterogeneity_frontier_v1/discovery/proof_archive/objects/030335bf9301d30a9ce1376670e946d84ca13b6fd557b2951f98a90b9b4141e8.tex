The established upper and lower benchmarks are order-equivalent for every fixed \(\sigma\) bounded away from zero, for \(d\le\log^2(en)\), and when \(d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\ge1\).  Along \(d=n\) and \(\sigma=\log(en)^{-1/2}\), their ratio is of order \(\log(en)\).  The existing hypothesis-independent channel achieves only separation \(M\sigma\min\{1,d/[n\log(en)]\}\), so it cannot supply the minimum-type separation posed by the open question.
