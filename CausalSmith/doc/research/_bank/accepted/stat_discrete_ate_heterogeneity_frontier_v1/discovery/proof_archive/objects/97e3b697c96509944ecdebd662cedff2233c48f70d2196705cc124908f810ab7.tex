Put
\[
 L:=\log(en),\qquad
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},\qquad
 h:=\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2},
 \qquad \gamma:=\min\{1,u,h\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:robust\text{-}upper\text{-}construction\text{-}resolution}\) supplies explicit total maps \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}},T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\), including all positive-denominator indicators, zero fallbacks, and final clipping, and constants depending only on \(\epsilon\) such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon M^2\left\{\frac1n+\min(1,u)\right\}
\tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon M^2\left(\frac1n+h\right).
\tag{3}
\]
After taking the smaller of the two admissible dimension constants and the larger of the two risk constants, (2)--(3) hold simultaneously on the range in the theorem.

We now inspect the three exhaustive branches of Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\).  If \(h\le\min\{1,u\}\), then \(\gamma=h\), the selector equals \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\), and (3) is the required bound.  If \(u<\min\{1,h\}\), then \(\gamma=u<1\), the selector equals \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\), and (2) is the required bound.  In the remaining branch, \(\gamma=1\).  Indeed, if \(h\) were a minimum, the first branch would apply; if \(u\) were a minimum, then either \(u<h\), when the second branch would apply, or \(u=h\), when the first branch would apply.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scale\text{-}sanity}\) gives \(|\tau(P)|\le M\), so the fallback value zero satisfies
\[
 (0-\tau(P))^2\le M^2\le M^2\left(\frac1n+\gamma\right).
\tag{4}
\]
Combining the three branches and using \(r_{n,d,\sigma}=n^{-1}+\gamma\) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^\star-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\tag{5}
\]
This also verifies measurability and every boundary case: the component maps are total, the branch choice depends only on the fixed values \((n,d,\sigma)\), and the displayed weak and strict inequalities specify all ties.

For the comparison, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:zeng\text{-}binary\text{-}collision\text{-}upper}\) gives the algebraic collision remainder of order \(\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}^2+d/n^2\), in addition to the parametric term and an exponentially small term.  Our nonparametric remainder is
\[
 \gamma=\min\left\{1,u,\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right\}
 \le \sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus it is never larger in order.  If \(u=o(\sigma^2+d/n^2)\), then \(\gamma\le u=o(\sigma^2+d/n^2)\), so it is strictly smaller.  Under the additional condition \(d=o(nL)\), the exponential term in the cited collision guarantee is negligible: if \(d<n\), its exponent is of order \(n\), while if \(d\ge n\), then \(n^2/d\gg n/L\to\infty\).  This proves the stated comparator claim without extending it from binary outcomes to the present real-outcome class.
