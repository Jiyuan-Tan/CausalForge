Write
\[
 L:=\log(en),\qquad b:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2},\qquad
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},\qquad \rho:=\sqrt u=\frac d{nL}.
\]
The already established all-alphabet results imply, in the residual wedge where all relevant caps are inactive,
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2u\}
 \le \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\min(u,\sigma^2)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The following two calculations strictly strengthen the diagnosis of the gap.

First, consider a binary one-arm source experiment with \(B(0)=0\),
\(B(1)\mid X=k\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\eta_k)\), and
\(\psi:=\sum_kp_k\eta_k\).  Let \(K_a(\mathrm dy\mid b)\) be an arm-dependent but cell- and hypothesis-independent outcome channel, and suppose its output has the integrable conditional means required for membership in the target model.  Put
\[
 \nu_{ab}:=\int y\,K_a(\mathrm dy\mid b),\qquad
 c:=\nu_{10}-\nu_{00},\qquad \gamma:=\nu_{11}-\nu_{10}.
\]
Linearity of integration gives, cell by cell,
\[
 \mu_{0k}=\nu_{00},\qquad
 \mu_{1k}=\nu_{10}+\gamma\eta_k,qquad
 \tau_k=c+\gamma\eta_k,qquad
 \tau=c+\gamma\psi.
\tag{2}
\]
If this fixed channel maps the entire binary one-arm source class into
\(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), apply it to the two-cell source law
\(p_1=p_2=1/2\), \(\eta_1=0\), and \(\eta_2=1\).  Equation~\((2)\) and the realization-wise radius restriction yield
\[
 \frac{|\gamma|}{2}
 =\max_{k\in\{1,2\}}|\tau_k-\tau|
 \le \sigma M,
 \qquad\text{hence}\qquad |\gamma|\le2\sigma M.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus every source target gap \(\Delta_{\psi}\) is sent to a gap at most
\(2\sigma M\Delta_{\psi}\).  The source family available through the frozen one-arm lower lemma has
\(\Delta_{\psi}\asymp\rho\) in the nonsaturated range.  Data processing preserves its bounded-mixture-total-variation certificate, but equations~\((2)\)--\((3)\) give only output separation of order \(M\sigma\rho\).  In the residual wedge, \(\sigma\to0\) and \(\rho\to0\), while
\[
 \frac{\min(\sigma,\rho)}{\sigma\rho}
 =\frac1{\max(\sigma,\rho)}\longrightarrow\infty.
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently the established hypothesis-independent channel architecture cannot deliver the requested separation.  This is a route-specific obstruction, not an impossibility result for a hypothesis-dependent or jointly moment-matched paired construction.

Second, the natural centered mixed-arm factorial lift does achieve the desired deterministic approximation bias, but its proposed product-rate covariance bound is false on the frozen class.  To see this, take an estimation block of size \(m\), set \(d=m\), \(M=1\),
\[
 p_k=\frac1m,qquad \pi_k=\frac12,qquad \mu_{0k}=\mu_{1k}=0,
\]
and construct potential outcomes that, conditionally on \(X\), are independent Rademacher variables independent of \(A\), with \(Y=Y(A)\).  This law satisfies consistency, conditional exchangeability, overlap, mean normalization, the conditional-second-moment envelope with equality, and exact homogeneity.  Give the centered construction its oracle pilot \(t=\tau=0\).

For \(c\in\{0,1\}\) and \(j\ge0\), the proposed mixed-arm monomial is
\[
 \widehat H_{k,c,j}(0)
 :=\frac1{(m)_{j+2}}
 \sum_{i_0,\ldots,i_{j+1}\ {\rm distinct}}
 \mathbf1\{X_{i_0}=k,A_{i_0}=0\}
 \mathbf1\{X_{i_1}=k,A_{i_1}=1\}
 (Y_{i_1}-Y_{i_0})
 \prod_{q=2}^{j+1}\mathbf1\{X_{i_q}=k,A_{i_q}=c\}.
\tag{5}
\]
The light-cell lift sums \((5)\) against the Chebyshev coefficients
\(g_{Kj}/B^{j+1}\) over \(k\), \(c\), and \(0\le j\le K-2\).  The constant coefficient is exactly
\[
 g_{K0}=\frac{K^2-1}{3}.
\tag{6}
\]
Let \(J_k:=\mathbf1\{N_{0k}=N_{1k}=1\}\) on the estimation block.  On \(J_k=1\), every term with \(j\ge1\) in \((5)\) vanishes because it requires at least three distinct observations from cell \(k\).  The two \(c\)-terms at \(j=0\) coincide.  Conditional on all \((X_i,A_i)\), the Rademacher marks are independent, so the full light statistic \(Z\) satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(Z\mid X,A)
 \ge
 2\left\{\frac{2g_{K0}}{B(m)_2}\right\}^{\!2}
 \sum_{k=1}^m J_k.
\tag{7}
\]
The multinomial occupancy law gives the exact expectation
\[
 E\!\left[\sum_{k=1}^mJ_k\right]
 =m\frac{(m)_2}{4m^2}
 \left(1-\frac1m\right)^{m-2}
 \ge c m
\tag{8}
\]
for a numerical \(c>0\) and all sufficiently large \(m\).  Taking expectations in \((7)\), using \((6)\)--\((8)\), and applying the necessary reciprocal-approximation calibration
\(K\asymp\log(em)\), \(B\asymp\log(em)/m\), yields
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(Z)
 \ge c\frac{g_{K0}^2}{B^2m^3}
 \asymp \frac{\log^2(em)}m.
\tag{9}
\]
At \(d=m\) and \(\sigma=0\), the covariance inequality needed for the proposed product-rate proof would instead be of order
\(m^{-1}+d/m^2\asymp m^{-1}\).  Equation~\((9)\) contradicts it by a factor of order \(\log^2(em)\).  Fixing the pilot at the oracle value shows that pilot error is irrelevant; independent sample splitting retains the same estimation-block singleton-pair diagonal.  Dropping the \(j=0\) term is not a repair of this construction because \((6)\) is the constant coefficient responsible for its reciprocal approximation.

For completeness, the valid population part of that attempted upper route is the identity
\[
 t+S_K(t)-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right),
\tag{10}
\]
where
\[
 \vartheta:=\frac{\tau(P)}M,qquad
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t),qquad
 S_K(t):=\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\},
\]
and
\[
 c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}.
\]
The reciprocal approximation and overlap imply from \((10)\) that
\[
 |t+S_K(t)-\vartheta|^2
 \le C_{\epsilon}u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}.
\tag{11}
\]
Thus the product-scaled deterministic bias is genuine, but the stochastic lift required to turn \((11)\) into an estimator is not.

Equations~\((1)\)--\((11)\) leave the mathematical alternatives sharply delimited.  A stronger converse must use a radius-coupled, hypothesis-dependent construction with joint moment matching of pair mass, within-pair share, propensity, and outcome marks, plus exact normalization and fixed-sample control.  A sharper upper bound must use an estimator whose rare-cell noise is not amplified by the singleton-pair coefficient in \((6)\).  Neither object is supplied by the frozen core, and the calculations above do not prove that neither object exists.
