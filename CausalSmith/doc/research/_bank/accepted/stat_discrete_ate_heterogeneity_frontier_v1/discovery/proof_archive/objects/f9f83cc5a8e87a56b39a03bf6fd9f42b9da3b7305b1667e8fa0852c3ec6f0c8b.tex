With \(L:=\log(en)\), \(b:=n^{-1}+d/n^2\), and \(u:=d^2/(n^2L^2)\),
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\}
 \le\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\}.
\]
Both inequalities range over the identical arbitrary-cell-mass real-outcome model with conditional means in \([-M/2,M/2]\) and conditional second central moments bounded by \(M^2\).  Thus the product expression is the minimax mean-squared-error rate at every declared radius, including shrinking radii; no inference or adaptation claim is included.
