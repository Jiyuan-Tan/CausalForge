For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is \(c_{\epsilon}>0\) such that, uniformly over \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), the same model admits the radius-indexed lower bound
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}+\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
The radius-dependent family is obtained by passing the fixed-sample binary one-arm hard experiment through the hypothesis-independent channel
\[
 B\longmapsto B'\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}\!\left(\frac12+\frac\sigma2\left(B-\frac12\right)\right),
 \qquad Y=M(B'-1/2).
\]
This construction has realization-wise radius at most \(\sigma M\) and preserves total-variation control by data processing.  It yields the natural product term \(\sigma^2\min\{1,d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\}\); it does not establish the stronger interior term \(\min\{\sigma^2,d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\}\).
