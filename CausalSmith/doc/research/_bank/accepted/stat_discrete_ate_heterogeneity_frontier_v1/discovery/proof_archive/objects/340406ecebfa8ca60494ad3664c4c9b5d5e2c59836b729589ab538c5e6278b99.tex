Let \(\lambda:=\sigma/2\).  Start from a fixed-sample binary one-arm hard family with at most \(d_{\mathrm{rad}}:=\min\{d,\max(1,\lfloor b_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{rad}}n\log(en)\rfloor)\}\) positive-mass cells, assign zero mass to unused cells, and pass each binary potential response through the hypothesis-independent kernel
\[
 B(a)\longmapsto B'(a)\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}\!\left\{\frac12+\lambda\left(B(a)-\frac12\right)\right\},
 \qquad Y(a):=M\{B'(a)-1/2\}.
\]
For the exact-homogeneity component, scale a uniform-mass binary hard family on at most \(d_{\mathrm{ex}}:=\min\{d,\max(1,\lfloor b_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{ex}}n^2\rfloor)\}\) positive-mass cells by \(B(a)\mapsto M\{B(a)-1/2\}\), again assigning zero mass to unused cells.  Here \(b_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{rad}},b_{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{ex}}>0\) are fixed constants depending only on \(\epsilon\).  This construction handle names only the two embedded families and the channel; class membership, target separation, data processing, finite-sample cutoffs, and the resulting capped all-alphabet risk bounds are proved separately.
