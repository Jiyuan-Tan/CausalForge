Put
\[
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)},\qquad h:=\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}.
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:robust\text{-}upper\text{-}construction\text{-}answer}\) gives
\[
 \sup_PE[(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon M^2\{n^{-1}+\min(1,u)\},
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
 \sup_PE[(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon M^2(n^{-1}+h),
\tag{2}
\]
where both suprema are over \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\).  If \(h\le\min\{1,u\}\), Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\) selects \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\), and (2) has remainder \(\min\{1,u,h\}=h\).  If \(u<\min\{1,h\}\), it selects \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\); then \(u<1\), and (1) has remainder \(\min\{1,u,h\}=u\).  If neither inequality holds, the minimum equals one: otherwise either \(h\le u\) or \(u<h\) would trigger one of the first two cases.  The selector then uses zero, and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scale\text{-}sanity}\) gives
\[
 (0-\tau(P))^2\le M^2\le M^2\{n^{-1}+\min(1,u,h)\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Equations (1)--(3) prove the uniform risk display, including all tie and boundary cases.

For the comparison clause, \(\min\{1,u,h\}\le h\), so the new remainder is never larger than the collision remainder.  If \(d=o(n\log(en))\), then \(u=o(1)\); if also \(u=o(h)\), eventually \(u<\min\{1,h\}\), and the selected remainder is \(u=o(h)\).  This is the stated strict order improvement.  The argument uses known \(M\) and \(\sigma\) only and makes no inference or adaptation claim.
