Let \(\widetilde T_n\) denote the expression inside the clipping operation.  By Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scale\text{-}sanity}\), \(\tau(P)\in[-M,M]\); projection onto this interval therefore cannot increase squared loss, so it suffices to control \(\widetilde T_n\).

Let \(\mathcal G=\sigma((X_i,A_i)_{i=1}^n)\).  Conditional independence of the outcomes and the definitions of the cell means give
\[
 E[\widetilde T_n\mid\mathcal G]
 =\frac{\mathbf 1\{R_n>0\}}{R_n}\sum_{k=1}^dN_kI_k\tau_k.
\tag{1}
\]
On \(\{R_n>0\}\), subtracting \(\tau(P)\) from (1) produces a convex combination of the \(\delta_k\)'s, whose absolute value is at most \(\sigma M\).  On \(\{R_n=0\}\), the absolute error of the conditional mean is \(|\tau(P)|\le M\).  Hence
\[
 E\!\left[\{E[\widetilde T_n\mid\mathcal G]-\tau(P)\}^2\right]
 \le M^2\{\sigma^2+Q_P^{(n)}(R_n=0)\}.
\tag{2}
\]

Write \(v_{ak}=\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid A=a,X=k)\).  Lyapunov's inequality and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass:fourth\text{-}central\text{-}moment}\) imply \(v_{ak}\le M^2\).  Thus
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(\widetilde T_n\mid\mathcal G)
 \le \frac{M^2\mathbf 1\{R_n>0\}}{R_n^2}
 \sum_{k=1}^dN_k^2I_k\left(\frac1{N_{1k}}+\frac1{N_{0k}}\right).
\tag{3}
\]

It remains to average (3) over treatment assignment.  Conditional on the category counts, \(N_{1k}\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(N_k,\pi_k)\), independently across \(k\).  If \(V\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(m,\pi)\), \(m\ge2\), and \(J=\mathbf 1\{0<V<m\}\), then
\[
 E\left[\frac1V\,\middle|\,J=1\right]
 \le \frac{2}{(m+1)\pi\{1-\pi^m-(1-\pi)^m\}}
 \le \frac{C_{\epsilon}}m.
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, \(1/v\le2/(v+1)\) for \(v\ge1\),
\[
 E[(V+1)^{-1}]=\frac{1-(1-\pi)^{m+1}}{(m+1)\pi},
\]
and \(1-\pi^m-(1-\pi)^m\ge2\pi(1-\pi)\ge2\epsilon(1-\epsilon)\).  The same bound applies to \(1/(m-V)\).  Conditional on the category counts and the indicators \((I_k)_k\), the quantity \(R_n\) is fixed.  Applying (4) termwise in (3) therefore yields
\[
 E[\operatorname{Var}(\widetilde T_n\mid\mathcal G)\mid (N_k,I_k)_k]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\frac{\mathbf 1\{R_n>0\}}{R_n}.
\tag{5}
\]

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:zeng\text{-}usable\text{-}occupancy\text{-}reciprocal}\), (2), (5), and the conditional bias--variance identity now give
\[
 E[(\widetilde T_n-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{sigma^2+\frac{n\vee d}{n^2}
 +\exp\left(-b_{\epsilon}\frac{n^2}{n\vee d}\right)\right\}.
\tag{6}
\]
If \(d\le n\), the exponential in (6) is at most \(C_{\epsilon}/n\).  If \(n<d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), decrease \(c_{\epsilon}\), if necessary, so that the same exponential is at most \(C_{\epsilon}d/n^2\).  Finally \((n\vee d)/n^2\le n^{-1}+d/n^2\).  These observations turn (6) into the asserted bound.  All divisions occur only on \(I_k=1\) or \(R_n>0\); the displayed fallback covers their boundaries.
