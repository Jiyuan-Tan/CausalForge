Let
\[
 B_{n,d,\sigma}:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) gives, on the stated real-outcome class and uniformly over the stated radius and dimension range,
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c_\epsilon M^2 B_{n,d,\sigma}.
\tag{2}
\]
Its proof combines the radius channel with the exact-homogeneity transfer and the parametric lower bound; Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:parametric\text{-}lower}\) also covers every finite-sample boundary absorbed into the constant.

For the other direction, Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\) takes the infimum over all total estimators in \(\mathcal T_{n,M}\).  The selector \(\widehat\tau_n^\star\) is one such estimator by Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\) and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\).  Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 &\le
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^\star-\tau(P))^2]\\
 &\le C_\epsilon M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Taking the smaller dimension constant and enlarging the risk constants makes (2)--(3) simultaneous.  Both suprema range over the single class \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), whose support, overlap, conditional-mean, conditional-second-moment, and radius conditions are fixed by Definition~\(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\); no subclass substitution occurs in (3), while subclass restriction inside the proof of (2) is a valid minimax lower-bound device.  Equations (2)--(3) prove the bracket.  They do not imply uniform order equivalence because the ratio of their numerical benchmarks can diverge at shrinking interior radii, as localized separately in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:fixed\text{-}interior\text{-}tightness\text{-}and\text{-}shrinking\text{-}radius\text{-}gap}\).
