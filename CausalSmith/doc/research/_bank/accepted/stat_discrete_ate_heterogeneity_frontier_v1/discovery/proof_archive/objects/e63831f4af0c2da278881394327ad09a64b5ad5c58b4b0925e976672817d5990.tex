Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy give the unrestricted lower bound, the approximate-homogeneity collision upper bound, and an exact-homogeneity lower bound, but leave sharp dependence on the maximal heterogeneity radius open.  The present construction and radius channel settle all fixed-radius and parametric-dominance regimes; only the joint shrinking-radius wedge \(b\ll u,\sigma^2\ll1\) remains unresolved.
