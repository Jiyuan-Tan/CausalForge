The two-sided bracket is matched at both required endpoints.  At \(\sigma=0\),
\[
 r_{n,d,0}=n^{-1}+\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)},\frac d{n^2}\right\}
 \asymp n^{-1}+\frac d{n^2}
\]
uniformly for \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\).  At \(\sigma=2\),
\(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,2}=\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\) and
\[
 r_{n,d,2}=n^{-1}+\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\]
The lower expression in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:matched\text{-}minimax\text{-}frontier}\) is of the same order as these two rates at \(\sigma=0\) and \(\sigma=2\), respectively.
