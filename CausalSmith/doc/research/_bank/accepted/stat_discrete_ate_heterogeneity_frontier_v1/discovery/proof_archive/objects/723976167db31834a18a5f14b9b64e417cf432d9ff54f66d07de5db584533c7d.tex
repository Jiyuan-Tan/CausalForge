Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), \(0\le\sigma\le2\), and integers \(n,d\ge1\).  Decrease the constants supplied by Theorems~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\) and \(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\), if necessary, so that
\[
 1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}\frac{n^2}{\log(en)}
\tag{1}
\]
implies the range conditions of both theorems.  Enlarge the common upper constant and decrease the common lower constant once more, if necessary, so that \(0<c_{\epsilon}\le C_{\epsilon}<\infty\).

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\) supplies the component estimators in \(\mathcal T_{n,M}\), and Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\) makes their selected map \(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}\) a total element of \(\mathcal T_{n,M}\).  It is therefore an admissible witness in the infimum defining the minimax risk.  Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\) and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 &=\inf_{T\in\mathcal T_{n,M}}
   \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
   E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T-\tau(P))^2]\\
 &\le
   \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
   E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]\\
 &\le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
This is a data-driven minimax upper bound: the estimator is a fixed measurable function of the observed sample and the known inputs \(n,d,M,\sigma\), and its clipping and division-boundary fallbacks are part of the declared constructions.

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) yields on the same range
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Its parametric component is valid uniformly, including all boundary values, by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:parametric\text{-}lower}\).

Both (2) and (3) quantify over the identical class \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\).  By Definition~\(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\), this is the arbitrary-cell-mass real-outcome class with fixed overlap, conditional means in \([-M/2,M/2]\), and conditional second central moments at most \(M^2\); conditional quantities are required only on positive-mass cells.  Thus no class substitution, bounded-outcome restriction, positivity strengthening, or additional rank condition enters either side.  Combining (2) and (3) proves the displayed bracket.

Finally, the two inequalities do not assert uniform order equivalence.  With \(u=d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\), the proved lower radius term is \(\sigma^2\min(1,u)\), whereas the selected upper remainder is \(\min(1,u,\sigma^2+d/n^2)\).  The theorem therefore preserves the possible shrinking-radius mismatch.  Its final comparison paragraph records provenance and scope; it does not enlarge the mathematical claim in (2)--(3).
