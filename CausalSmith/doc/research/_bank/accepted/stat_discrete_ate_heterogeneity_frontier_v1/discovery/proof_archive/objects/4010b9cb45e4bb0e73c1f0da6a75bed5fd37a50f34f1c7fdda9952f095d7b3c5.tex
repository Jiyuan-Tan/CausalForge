The established two-sided bracket is
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{n^{-1}+d/n^2+\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}
 \le \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\]
It is rate-matched at \(\sigma=0\), at \(\sigma=2\), for every fixed \(\sigma\ge s>0\) with constants depending on \(s\), uniformly when \(d\le\log^2(en)\), and uniformly when \(d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\ge1\).  Along \(d=n\) and \(\sigma=\log(en)^{-1/2}\), the two established benchmarks differ by order \(\log(en)\).
