The two-sided minimax bracket in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:two\text{-}sided\text{-}minimax\text{-}bracket}\) is order-matched at the two endpoint radii.  At \(\sigma=0\),
\[
 r_{n,d,0}=n^{-1}+\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)},\frac d{n^2}\right\}
 \asymp n^{-1}+\frac d{n^2}
\]
uniformly for \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\).  At \(\sigma=2\),
\(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,2}=\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\) and
\[
 r_{n,d,2}=n^{-1}+\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\]
The lower benchmark in that theorem is of the same order as these rates at \(\sigma=0\) and \(\sigma=2\), respectively.  This proposition makes no claim of uniform matching for shrinking interior radii.
