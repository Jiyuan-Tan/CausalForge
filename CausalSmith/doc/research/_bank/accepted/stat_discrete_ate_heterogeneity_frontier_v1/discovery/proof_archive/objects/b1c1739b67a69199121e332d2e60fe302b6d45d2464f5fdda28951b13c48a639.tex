Put \(L:=\log(en)\), \(b:=n^{-1}+d/n^2\), \(u:=d^2/(n^2L^2)\), and \(s:=\sigma^2\).  Uniformly on the range of Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:sharp\text{-}two\text{-}sided\text{-}minimax}\),
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \asymp_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+s\min(1,u)\}.
\]
Consequently: exact homogeneity has rate \(M^2b\); unrestricted heterogeneity \((\sigma=2)\) has rate \(M^2\{b+\min(1,u)\}\); every fixed positive radius has rate \(M^2\{b+\min(1,u)\}\); saturation \(u\ge1\) has rate \(M^2(b+s)\); and either \(u\lesssim b\) or \(s\min(1,u)\lesssim b\) yields the parametric-dominance rate \(M^2b\).  In the formerly residual wedge \(b\ll u\ll1\) and \(b\ll s\ll1\), the sharp rate is \(M^2(b+su)\).  For \(d_n=n\) and \(\sigma_n=\log(en)^{-1/2}\), it is of order \(M^2\log(en)^{-3}\), improving the former selector benchmark by a factor of order \(\log(en)\).  No shrinking-radius regime remains unresolved at the level of minimax mean-squared-error order.
