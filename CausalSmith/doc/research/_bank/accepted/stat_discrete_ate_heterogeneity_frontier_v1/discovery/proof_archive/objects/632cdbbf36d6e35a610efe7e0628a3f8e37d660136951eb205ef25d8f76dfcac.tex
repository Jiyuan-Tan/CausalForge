In the binary-outcome model of Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy, let \(\psi:=\sum_{k=1}^dp_k(\mu_{1k}-\mu_{0k})\), let \(\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}:=\max_{1\le k\le d}|\mu_{1k}-\mu_{0k}-\psi|\), and let \(\widehat\psi_{\mathrm{col}}\) be their occupancy-weighted estimator in equation (13).  Under overlap \(\epsilon\), their Theorem 3 states
\[
 |E(\widehat\psi_{\mathrm{col}}-\psi)|
 \le \sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}+2\exp\left\{-C(\epsilon)\frac{n^2}{n\vee d}\right\},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(\widehat\psi_{\mathrm{col}})
 \lesssim_{\epsilon}\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}^2}{n}+\frac d{n^2}+\frac1n.
\]
