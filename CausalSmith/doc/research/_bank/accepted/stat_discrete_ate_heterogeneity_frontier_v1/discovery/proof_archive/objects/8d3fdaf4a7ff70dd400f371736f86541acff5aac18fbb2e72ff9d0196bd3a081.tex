Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\) constructs the explicit clipped linear-mark Chebyshev factorial estimator and proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,
 \frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The final clipping and the explicit zero fallback outside the calibrated range make this a measurable map into \([-M,M]\), hence \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\).

Definition~\(\mathrm{def:collision\text{-}handle}\) is exactly the total map denoted by \(T_n^{\mathrm{occ}}\) in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}occupancy\text{-}collision\text{-}upper}\).  That lemma gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left(n^{-1}+\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right).
\tag{2}
\]
Every empirical ratio in the definition is multiplied by its positive-denominator indicator, the inner value is zero on \(R_n=0\), and the result is clipped to \([-M,M]\).  Therefore \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\).

Taking the smaller dimension constant and the larger risk constant from the two lemmas makes (1)--(2) simultaneous over \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_\epsilon n^2/\log(en)\).  Both component proofs use only the stated conditional second-central-moment envelope: conditional variance controls the occupancy estimator, and the one-mark covariance calculation controls the polynomial estimator.  The endpoints \(\sigma=0,2\), empty empirical arms, and \(R_n=0\) are included, and neither display contains an additional logarithmic factor.
