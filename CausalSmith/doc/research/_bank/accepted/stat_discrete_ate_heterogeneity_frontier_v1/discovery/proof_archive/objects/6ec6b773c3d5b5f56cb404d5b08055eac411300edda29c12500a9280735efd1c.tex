Normalize \(V:=Y/M\), \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\), and \(z_{ak}:=E_P[V\mathbf 1\{X=k,A=a\}]\).  For an independent pilot \(t\in[-1,1]\), define
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}z_{1k}-s_{1k}z_{0k}-t s_{0k}s_{1k}
 =s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t).
\]
Given a reciprocal polynomial \(r_K\), let
\[
 e_{ak}:=1-s_{ak}r_K(s_{ak}),\qquad
 c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}.
\]
Direct expansion proves the deterministic identity
\[
 t+\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right).
\]
With \(B\asymp L/n\) and \(K\asymp L\), the reciprocal-approximation envelope bounds its squared deterministic error by
\[
 C_{\epsilon}u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}.
\]
Consequently, a centered mixed-arm factorial lift having conditional variance
\[
 C_{\epsilon}\{b+u[\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2]\}
\]
would, when paired with an independent collision pilot, attain \(C_{\epsilon}M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\).  What remains unproved is exactly that covariance bound and its fixed-sample totalization.  On the converse side, a pair with total mass \(\omega\) and share \(r\) can be parametrized by \(p_+=\omega r\), \(p_-=\omega(1-r)\), \(\vartheta_+=\theta+\sigma(1-r)\), and \(\vartheta_-=\theta-\sigma r\).  Translating \(\theta\) by \(\Delta\) and \(r\) by \(\Delta/\sigma\) preserves both cell effects, but deterministic \(\omega\) leaves a degree-two collision moment that reveals the common-effect shift.  Thus a successful fuzzy construction requires correlated moment matching in \((\omega,r)\), normalization control, and fixed-sample de-Poissonization.
