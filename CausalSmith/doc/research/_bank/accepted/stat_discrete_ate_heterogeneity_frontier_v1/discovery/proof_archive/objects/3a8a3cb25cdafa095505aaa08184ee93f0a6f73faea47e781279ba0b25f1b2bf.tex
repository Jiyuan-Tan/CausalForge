Two same-class endpoint transfers are established.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scaled\text{-}binary\text{-}exact\text{-}lower\text{-}transfer}\) gives \(c_{\epsilon}M^2(n^{-1}+d/n^2)\) for every radius, and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scaled\text{-}binary\text{-}unrestricted\text{-}lower\text{-}transfer}\) gives
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}
\]
at \(\sigma=2\).  The parametric lower bound is proved directly for every radius.  The missing result is a single interior-radius fuzzy pair with squared separation of order \(M^2\min\{d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)],\sigma^2\}\).
