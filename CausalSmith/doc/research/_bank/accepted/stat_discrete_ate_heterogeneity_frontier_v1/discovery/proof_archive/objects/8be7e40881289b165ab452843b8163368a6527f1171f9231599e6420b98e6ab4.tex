Let \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\), and \(z_{ak}:=E_P[(Y/M)\mathbf 1\{X=k,A=a\}]\).  For an independent pilot \(t\in[-1,1]\), define
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}z_{1k}-s_{1k}z_{0k}-t s_{0k}s_{1k}
 =s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t).
\]
For a reciprocal polynomial \(r_K\), set \(c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}\).  Direct expansion yields
\[
 t+\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right).
\]
With reciprocal-approximation threshold \(B\asymp\log(en)/n\) and degree \(K\asymp\log(en)\), this identity reduces the squared deterministic error to order \(u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}\).  Thus a centered mixed-arm factorial lift with conditional variance of order \(b+u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}\), combined with an independent collision pilot, would attain risk of order \(M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\).  On the converse side, a paired cell of mass \(\omega\) and share \(r\) admits the radius-preserving parametrization
\[
 p_+=\omega r,\quad p_-=\omega(1-r),\quad
 \vartheta_+=\theta+\sigma(1-r),\quad
 \vartheta_-=\theta-\sigma r.
\]
Translating \(\theta\) by \(\Delta\) and \(r\) by \(\Delta/\sigma\) preserves both cell effects, but deterministic \(\omega\) leaves a degree-two collision moment that reveals the common-effect shift.  Hence the unresolved alternatives are a proof of the mixed-arm covariance and totalization bound, or a correlated \((\omega,r)\) moment-matching construction with normalization and fixed-sample de-Poissonization.
