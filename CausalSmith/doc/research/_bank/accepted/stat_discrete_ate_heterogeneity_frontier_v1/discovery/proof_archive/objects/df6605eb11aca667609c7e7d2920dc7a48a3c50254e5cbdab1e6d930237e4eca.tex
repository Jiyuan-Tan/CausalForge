The established same-class bracket is
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\right\}
 \le \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{\frac1n+\min\left(1,u,\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\},
\]
where \(b=n^{-1}+d/n^2\) and \(u=d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\).  The bounds match at \(\sigma=0\), \(\sigma=2\), every radius bounded away from zero, whenever \(u\ge1\), whenever \(u\lesssim b\), and whenever \(\sigma^2\lesssim b\).  Their ratio can diverge only when \(b\ll u\ll1\) and \(b\ll\sigma^2\ll1\), and it does diverge for the displayed benchmarks at \(d=n\) and \(\sigma=\log(en)^{-1/2}\).
