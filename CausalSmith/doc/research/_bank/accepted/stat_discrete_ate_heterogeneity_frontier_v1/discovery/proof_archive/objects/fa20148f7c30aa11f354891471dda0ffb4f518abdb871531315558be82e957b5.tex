Let
\[
 B_{n,d,\sigma}:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\), with the parametric component supplied by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:parametric\text{-}lower}\), gives a constant \(c_{\epsilon}>0\) such that
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c_{\epsilon}M^2 B_{n,d,\sigma}
\tag{2}
\]
uniformly on the stated radius and dimension ranges.  Its construction belongs to the same class \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) appearing in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\), so (2) involves no change of model.

By Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\), the explicit total selector \(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\) satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\tag{3}
\]
Taking \(T=\widehat\tau_n^{\star}\) in the infimum defining \(\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) makes the minimax risk no larger than the left side of (3).  Hence
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\tag{4}
\]
Taking the smaller dimension constant and enlarging the upper constant if necessary makes (2) and (4) simultaneous.  Both use the same arbitrary-cell-mass, real-outcome class and its product sampling law.  The proof compares neither benchmark to the other, so it makes no uniform order-equivalence claim at shrinking interior radii.
