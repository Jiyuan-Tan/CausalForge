Let \(\mathcal D_d(\epsilon)\) denote the binary class of Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy with arbitrary cell masses \(p_k\), fixed overlap \(\epsilon\le\pi_k\le1-\epsilon\), and binary potential responses \(B(a)\in\{0,1\}\) having conditional means \(\eta_{ak}\in[0,1]\).  Let \(\mathcal D_d(\epsilon,\sigma)\) be its subclass satisfying
\[
 \max_{k:p_k>0}\left|\eta_{1k}-\eta_{0k}-\sum_{\ell=1}^dp_{\ell}(\eta_{1\ell}-\eta_{0\ell})\right|\le\sigma,
\]
and let \(\mathcal H_d(\epsilon)\subset\mathcal D_d(\epsilon,0)\) be the uniform-mass exact-homogeneity subclass.  The affine observation map
\[
 B(a)\longmapsto Y(a):=M\{B(a)-1/2\},\qquad Y=Y(A),
\]
embeds \(\mathcal D_d(\epsilon,\sigma)\) into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), and it embeds \(\mathcal H_d(\epsilon)\) into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,0}\).  These images are proper subclasses of the note's real-outcome class.  Consequently the \(\mathcal H_d(\epsilon)\) lower bound transfers as \(M^2\{n^{-1}+d/n^2\}\), while the binary one-arm hard family, after the hypothesis-independent channel in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\), transfers as
\[
 M^2\sigma^2\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}
\]
up to constants depending only on \(\epsilon\).  Thus the lower bounds are subclass transfers, whereas the explicit polynomial upper estimator is proved uniformly on the strictly broader fourth-moment real-outcome class.
