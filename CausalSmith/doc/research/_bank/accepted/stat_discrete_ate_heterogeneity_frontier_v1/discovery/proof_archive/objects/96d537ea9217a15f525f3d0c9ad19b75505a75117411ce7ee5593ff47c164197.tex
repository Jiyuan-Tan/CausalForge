Put
\[
 L:=\log(en),\qquad b:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2},\qquad
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},\qquad \rho:=\sqrt u=\frac d{nL}.
\]
The established upper and lower bounds imply, throughout the residual wedge where the caps are inactive,
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2u\}
 \le \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\min(u,\sigma^2)\}.
\tag{1}
\]

There is a rigorous obstruction to every cell- and hypothesis-independent outcome channel applied to the binary one-arm source family.  Write the channel means as
\[
 \nu_{ab}:=\int y\,K_a(\mathrm dy\mid b),\qquad
 c:=\nu_{10}-\nu_{00},\qquad \gamma:=\nu_{11}-\nu_{10}.
\]
If the source treated success probability in cell \(k\) is \(\eta_k\), linearity gives
\[
 \tau_k=c+\gamma\eta_k,qquad
 \tau=c+\gamma\sum_kp_k\eta_k.
\tag{2}
\]
Applying the same channel to the supported two-cell law with equal masses and \((\eta_1,\eta_2)=(0,1)\), the radius restriction gives \(|\gamma|\le2\sigma M\).  Hence a source target gap of order \(\rho\) can produce at most order \(M\sigma\rho\) separation.  Since
\[
 \frac{\min(\sigma,\rho)}{\sigma\rho}
 =\frac1{\max(\sigma,\rho)}\longrightarrow\infty
\]
in the residual wedge, this entire fixed-channel architecture cannot attain the requested order \(M\min(\sigma,\rho)\).  Data processing retains the source total-variation certificate, so the obstruction is separation rather than distinguishability.

A centered mixed-arm factorial lift does achieve the desired deterministic approximation bias but fails stochastically on the frozen weak-moment class.  In the exact-homogeneity law with block size \(m\), \(d=m\), \(M=1\), \(p_k=1/m\), \(\pi_k=1/2\), zero arm means, and independent conditional Rademacher outcomes, give the construction its oracle pilot.  For the proposed mixed-arm monomials, the constant Chebyshev coefficient is
\[
 g_{K0}=\frac{K^2-1}{3}.
\]
On a cell containing exactly one treated and one control observation, every higher-degree monomial vanishes while the two degree-zero terms coincide.  Conditional independence of the Rademacher marks and the exact multinomial occupancy calculation therefore yield
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(Z)
 \ge c_1\frac{g_{K0}^2}{B^2m^3}
 \asymp \frac{\log^2(em)}m
\tag{3}
\]
under the reciprocal-approximation calibration \(K\asymp\log(em)\) and \(B\asymp\log(em)/m\).  The product-rate covariance claim would require order \(m^{-1}+d/m^2\asymp m^{-1}\), contradicting (3).  Oracle centering and independent sample splitting do not remove this singleton-pair diagonal.

The population approximation calculation itself remains valid.  With \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\),
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t),
 \qquad
 c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\},
\]
and \(S_K(t):=\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}\), exact algebra gives
\[
 t+S_K(t)-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right).
\]
The reciprocal approximation and overlap consequently imply
\[
 |t+S_K(t)-\vartheta|^2
 \le C_{\epsilon}u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Thus the product-scaled deterministic bias is genuine, but the tested stochastic lift cannot realize it.  A stronger converse must use a radius-coupled, hypothesis-dependent construction with joint moment matching and fixed-sample normalization, or a sharper estimator must suppress the singleton-pair noise amplification.  Neither construction has been derived, and these route-specific obstructions do not prove nonexistence.
