Let \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\), and \(z_{ak}:=E_P[(Y/M)\mathbf 1\{X=k,A=a\}]\).  For an independent pilot \(t\in[-1,1]\), define
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}z_{1k}-s_{1k}z_{0k}-t s_{0k}s_{1k}
 =s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t).
\]
For a reciprocal polynomial \(r_K\), put
\[
 c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}.
\]
Direct expansion gives the deterministic identity
\[
 t+\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right).
\]
With reciprocal-approximation threshold \(B\asymp\log(en)/n\) and degree \(K\asymp\log(en)\), the squared deterministic error is bounded by
\[
 C_\epsilon u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}.
\]
Consequently, a centered mixed-arm factorial lift satisfying conditional variance
\[
 C_\epsilon\{b+u[\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2]\}
\]
would, when paired with an independent collision pilot, attain \(C_\epsilon M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\).  On the converse side, a pair of total mass \(\omega\) and share \(r\) has the radius-preserving parametrization
\[
 p_+=\omega r,\quad p_-=\omega(1-r),\quad
 \vartheta_+=\theta+\sigma(1-r),\quad
 \vartheta_-=\theta-\sigma r.
\]
Translating \(\theta\) by \(\Delta\) and \(r\) by \(\Delta/\sigma\) preserves both cell effects, but deterministic \(\omega\) leaves a degree-two collision moment revealing the common-effect shift.  Thus the residual tasks are precisely the mixed-arm covariance plus totalization bound, or a correlated \((\omega,r)\) moment-matching construction with normalization and fixed-sample de-Poissonization.
