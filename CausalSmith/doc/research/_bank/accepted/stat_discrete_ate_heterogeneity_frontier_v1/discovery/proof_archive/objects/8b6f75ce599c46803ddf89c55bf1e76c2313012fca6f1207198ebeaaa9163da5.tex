For the causal ATE under fixed overlap, arbitrary discrete cell masses, known scale \(M\), and conditional fourth moments bounded by \(M^4\), an explicit signed-mark polynomial estimator and a collision estimator yield the upper benchmark \(M^2\{n^{-1}+\min(1,u,\sigma^2+d/n^2)\}\), while one same-class binary-subclass construction yields \(M^2\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\}\), where \(L=\log(en)\), \(b=n^{-1}+d/n^2\), and \(u=d^2/(n^2L^2)\).  The bounds match at both endpoints, every fixed positive radius, saturation, and whenever \(u\lesssim b\) or \(\sigma^2\lesssim b\).  The only potentially divergent shrinking-radius wedge is \(b\ll u,\sigma^2\ll1\), equivalently up to boundaries \(\sqrt n\,L\ll d\ll nL\) and \(\sigma^2\gg b\).
