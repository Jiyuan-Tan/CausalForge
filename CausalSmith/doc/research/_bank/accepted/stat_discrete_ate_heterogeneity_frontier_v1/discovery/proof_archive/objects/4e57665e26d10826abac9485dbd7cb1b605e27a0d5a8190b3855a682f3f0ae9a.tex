Put \(L:=\log(en)\), \(b:=n^{-1}+d/n^2\), \(u:=d^2/(n^2L^2)\), and \(m:=\min\{u,\sigma^2\}\).  The established selector benchmark is within fixed factors of \(b+m\), whereas the established channel converse gives \(b+\sigma^2u\).

On the lower side, for a cell pair of total mass \(\omega>0\) and share \(r\in[0,1]\), the parametrization
\[
p_+=\omega r,\qquad p_-=\omega(1-r),\qquad
\vartheta_+=\theta+\sigma(1-r),\qquad
\vartheta_-=\theta-\sigma r
\]
has pair average \(\theta\) and realization-wise deviations bounded by \(\sigma\).  The translation \(\theta'=\theta+\Delta\), \(r'=r+\Delta/\sigma\) leaves both cell effects unchanged whenever \(r'\in[0,1]\), while shifting the pair average by \(\Delta\).  With deterministic \(\omega\), however, matching first observable intensities forces equal means of \(r\), and equal-mass reflected propensities expose a common-effect shift in the degree-two collision moment.  Thus the unresolved lower construction is a correlated moment problem in \((\omega,r)\), together with normalization concentration and fixed-sample de-Poissonization.

On the upper side, normalize \(V:=Y/M\), \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\), and \(z_{ak}:=E[V\mathbf1\{X=k,A=a\}]\).  For an independent pilot \(t\in[-1,1]\), define
\[
H_k(t):=s_{0k}z_{1k}-s_{1k}z_{0k}-ts_{0k}s_{1k}
=s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t).
\]
For a reciprocal polynomial \(r_K\), put
\[
e_{ak}:=1-s_{ak}r_K(s_{ak}),\qquad
c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}.
\]
Then the deterministic identity
\[
t+\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}-\vartheta
=\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
+(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right)
\]
and the usual reciprocal approximation envelope yield squared deterministic error at most
\[
C_\epsilon u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}
\]
when \(B\asymp L/n\) and \(K\asymp L\).  A centered mixed-arm factorial lift with conditional variance bounded by
\[
C_\epsilon\{b+u[\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2]\}
\]
would therefore attain \(C_\epsilon M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\) when coupled to the independent collision pilot.  The present linear-mark covariance lemma does not cover that mixed-arm kernel or prove its exact-homogeneity degeneracy.
