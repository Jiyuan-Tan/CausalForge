Define \(N_{ak}\), \(N_k\), \(I_k\), and \(R_n\) as in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:zeng\text{-}usable\text{-}occupancy\text{-}reciprocal}\), put \(S_{ak}:=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf 1\{X_i=k,A_i=a\}Y_i\), and set \(\overline Y_{ak}:=S_{ak}/N_{ak}\) when \(N_{ak}>0\).  The explicit total estimator
\[
 T_n^{\mathrm{occ}}:=\operatorname{clip}_{[-M,M]}\!\left(
 \frac{\mathbf 1\{R_n>0\}}{R_n}
 \sum_{k=1}^dN_kI_k(\overline Y_{1k}-\overline Y_{0k})
 \right),
\]
with inner value zero when \(R_n=0\), satisfies, for constants \(C_{\epsilon},c_{\epsilon}>0\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{occ}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left(\frac1n+\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right)
\]
whenever \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\).
