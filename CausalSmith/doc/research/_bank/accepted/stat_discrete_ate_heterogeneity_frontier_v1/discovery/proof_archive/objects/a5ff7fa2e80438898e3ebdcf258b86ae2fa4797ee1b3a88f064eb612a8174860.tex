It remains open whether, in \(b\ll u\ll1\) and \(b\ll\sigma^2\ll1\), one can either prove a same-class lower separation of order \(M\min\{\sigma,d/[n\log(en)]\}\) under bounded sample-mixture total variation, or construct an explicit total estimator with risk \(o\!\left(M^2\{b+\min(u,\sigma^2)\}\right)\), ideally of order \(M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\).
