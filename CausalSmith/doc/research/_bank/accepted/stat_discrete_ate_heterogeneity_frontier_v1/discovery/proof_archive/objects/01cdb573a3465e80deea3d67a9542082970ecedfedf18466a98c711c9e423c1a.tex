Split the observations into two independent blocks \(I_0\) and \(I_1\).  The block \(I_0\) is used only to classify cells as heavy or light by empirical occupancy.  On light cells, use fixed shifted-Chebyshev coefficients of degree proportional to \(\log(en)\), evaluated on \(I_1\) by ordered distinct-index falling-factorial statistics carrying exactly one normalized outcome mark.  On heavy cells, use indicator-totalized arm sample-mean differences on \(I_1\), weighted by the \(I_1\) empirical cell frequency.  Sum the light and heavy contributions, multiply by \(M\), and clip to \([-M,M]\).  Set the estimator to zero outside the calibrated range.  The constants, degree, occupancy threshold, positive-denominator indicators, and fallbacks are those in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\).
