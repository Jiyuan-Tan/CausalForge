Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\) constructs the explicit clipped linear-mark Chebyshev factorial estimator and proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{n^{-1}+\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Its clipping and zero fallback make it an element of \(\mathcal T_{n,M}\).  Definition~\(\mathrm{def:collision\text{-}handle}\) is exactly the total map denoted by \(T_n^{\mathrm{occ}}\) in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}occupancy\text{-}collision\text{-}upper}\); that lemma gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left(n^{-1}+\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right).
\tag{2}
\]
The denominator indicators, zero values, and clipping in the definition make \(T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\).  Taking the smaller dimension constant and the larger risk constant from the two lemmas makes \((1)\)--\((2)\) simultaneous over the stated range.  Both proofs use only the conditional second-central-moment envelope: conditional variance for the occupancy estimator and the one-mark/two-mark covariance calculation for the polynomial estimator.  The endpoints \(\sigma=0,2\), empty empirical arms, and \(R_n=0\) are included, and neither display has an additional logarithmic factor.
