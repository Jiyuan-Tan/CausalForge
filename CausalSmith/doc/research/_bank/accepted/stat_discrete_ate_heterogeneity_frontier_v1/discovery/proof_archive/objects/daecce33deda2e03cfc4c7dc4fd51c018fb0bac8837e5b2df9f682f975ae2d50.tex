With \(L:=\log(en)\), \(b:=n^{-1}+d/n^2\), and \(u:=d^2/(n^2L^2)\),
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\}
 \le\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\{n^{-1}+\min(1,u,\sigma^2+d/n^2)\}.
\]
The benchmarks are order-equivalent at fixed positive radii and whenever \(u\ge1\), \(u\lesssim b\), or \(\sigma^2\lesssim b\); only \(b\ll u,\sigma^2\ll1\) remains potentially divergent.
