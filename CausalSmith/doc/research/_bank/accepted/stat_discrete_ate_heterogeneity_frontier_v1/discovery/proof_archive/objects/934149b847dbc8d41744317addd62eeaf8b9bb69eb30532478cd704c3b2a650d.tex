For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \(C_{\epsilon},c_{\epsilon}>0\) such that, uniformly for \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), the explicit total estimator \(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}\) in the corrected Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\) satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma},
\]
where
\[
 r_{n,d,\sigma}:=\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\]
Relative to the collision guarantee of Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy, the radius contribution is multiplied by \(\min\{1,d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\}\); the bound is on the same arbitrary-cell-mass real-outcome class under the conditional-second-central-moment envelope.
