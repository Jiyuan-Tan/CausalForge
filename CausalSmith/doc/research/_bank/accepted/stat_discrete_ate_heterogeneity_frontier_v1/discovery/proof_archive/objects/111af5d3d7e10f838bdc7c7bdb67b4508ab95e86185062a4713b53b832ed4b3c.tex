For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \(0<c_{\epsilon}\le C_{\epsilon}<\infty\) such that, uniformly over \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), \[c_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}\le\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.\] The lower bound is over the same continuous-outcome, arbitrary-cell-mass class as the upper bound and simultaneously yields the exact-homogeneity collision branch, the intermediate \(\sigma^2\) branch, and the unrestricted effective-sample-enlargement branch.
