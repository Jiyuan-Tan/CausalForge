Let \(u_{n,d}:=d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\), and let \(\kappa_{\epsilon}\in(0,1)\) be the fixed calibration constant in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:centered\text{-}product\text{-}upper}\).  For \(T_n^{\mathrm{ctr}},T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\), define the total known-radius estimator
\[
 \widehat\tau_n^{\star}:=
 \begin{cases}
 T_n^{\mathrm{ctr}},&u_{n,d}\le\kappa_{\epsilon},\\
 T_n^{\mathrm{col}},&u_{n,d}>\kappa_{\epsilon}.
 \end{cases}
\]
The branch is determined by the fixed known inputs, while all empirical divisions, unavailable factorials, and clipping operations are totalized in the two component definitions.
