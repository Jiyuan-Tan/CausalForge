Take a source law in \(\mathcal D_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\), with binary potential responses \(B(0),B(1)\).  If only the observed conditional Bernoulli laws are specified, augment them by drawing \(B(0),B(1)\) conditional on \(X\), independently of \(A\), with the prescribed arm means.  This augmentation preserves the observed source experiment and makes consistency and conditional exchangeability explicit.  Define
\[
 Y(a):=M\{B(a)-1/2\},\qquad Y:=Y(A).
\tag{1}
\]
Consistency follows from (1), conditional exchangeability follows from the source construction, and the propensity and cell-mass coordinates are unchanged, so fixed overlap is preserved.

If \(\eta_{ak}:=E[B\mid A=a,X=k]\), then
\[
 \mu_{ak}=M(\eta_{ak}-1/2)\in[-M/2,M/2].
\tag{2}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 |Y-\mu_{ak}|=M|B-\eta_{ak}|\le M,
\]
so
\[
 E[(Y-\mu_{ak})^4\mid A=a,X=k]\le M^4.
\tag{3}
\]
Equations (1)--(3) verify every member-property of \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\), including the support and moment boundaries.  Cell effects and the ATE are scaled by \(M\):
\[
 \tau_k=M(\eta_{1k}-\eta_{0k}),
 \qquad
 \tau(P)=M\sum_{k=1}^dp_k(\eta_{1k}-\eta_{0k}).
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently exact homogeneity in \(\mathcal H_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) gives \(\delta_k=0\) on every positive-mass cell.  The uniform masses remain \(p_k=1/d\), and the image lies in \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,0}\), hence in every radius class.

The containment is strict.  Put all mass on \(X=1\), draw \(A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) independently of two independent variables \(Z_0,Z_1\), each uniform on \(\{-M/4,0,M/4\}\), and set \(Y(a)=Z_a\), \(Y=Y(A)\).  The arm means and every cell effect are zero, so the mean and every radius condition hold.  Also
\[
 E[Z_a^4]=M^4/384\le M^4.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus this law is in \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) for every \(\sigma\), but its three-point conditional outcome law is not supported on the two points \(\{-M/2,M/2\}\); it is not in the affine binary image.

On the exact-homogeneity image, \(B=Y/M+1/2\) is invertible and squared ATE error is multiplied by \(M^2\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scaled\text{-}binary\text{-}exact\text{-}lower\text{-}transfer}\) therefore transfers the \(M^2(n^{-1}+d/n^2)\) lower bound from the uniform-mass subclass.  For the unrestricted one-arm family, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) applies a hypothesis-independent Markov kernel, so data processing preserves the source total-variation control and scales its separation to yield
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\sigma^2
 \min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\tag{6}
\]
These are lower transfers by restriction to binary subclasses.  By contrast, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\) proves its signed-mark estimator uniformly over all laws in the fourth-moment real-outcome class, including the three-point witness above.  This distinguishes the proved broader-class construction from a claim that merely allowing real outcomes is itself novel.
