Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  Let \(\mathcal H_{n,d}^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) be the fixed-sample binary-outcome experiment with \(p_k=1/d\), \(\epsilon\le\pi_k\le1-\epsilon\), conditional response means in \([0,1]\), and a common cell treatment effect, and write \(\psi(P):=d^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^d(\mu_{1k}-\mu_{0k})\).  There are constants \(a_{\epsilon},b_{\epsilon}>0\) and \(N_{\epsilon}<\infty\) such that, for \(n\ge N_{\epsilon}\) and \(d\le b_{\epsilon}n^2\),
\[
 \inf_{\widehat\psi}\sup_{P^{\otimes n}\in\mathcal H_{n,d}^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)}
 E_P[(\widehat\psi-\psi(P))^2]
 \ge a_{\epsilon}\left(\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}\right).
\]
