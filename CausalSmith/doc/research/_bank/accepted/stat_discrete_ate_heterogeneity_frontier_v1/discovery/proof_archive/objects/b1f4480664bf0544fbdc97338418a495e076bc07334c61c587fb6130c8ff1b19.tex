Put
\[
 L:=\log(en),\qquad
 u:=\frac{d^2}{n^2L^2},\qquad
 h:=\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2},
 \qquad m:=\min\{1,u,h\}.
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper\text{-}all\text{-}d}\) shows that, after the common parametric term \(n^{-1}\) is separated, the selector frontier has heterogeneity-dependent remainder \(m\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:zeng\text{-}binary\text{-}collision\text{-}upper}\) gives the published binary collision variance remainder \(\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}^2+d/n^2\), in addition to the same parametric term \(n^{-1}\), and its bias bound contributes a term of order \(\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}^2\) plus the squared adverse-event exponential.  Thus, under the comparison's normalization \(\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}=\sigma\), the nonparametric, heterogeneity-dependent algebraic remainder in that guarantee is \(h\).  By the elementary identity defining a minimum,
\[
 m=\min\{1,u,h\}\le h.
\]
This proves the asserted weak comparison for every \(n,d\ge1\) and \(0\le\sigma\le2\); it uses the cited binary result only to name the comparator benchmark, not to establish the selector bound.

For the strict comparison, consider any sequence along which
\[
 u=o(h)
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 d=o(nL).
\]
The second relation gives
\[
 u=\left(\frac d{nL}\right)^2=o(1).
\]
Since \(d\ge1\), one has \(h\ge d/n^2>0\).  Consequently,
\[
 0\le \frac{m}{h}
 \le \frac{u}{h}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Hence the selector's heterogeneity-dependent remainder is strictly smaller in order than the published collision remainder.

For completeness, the adverse-event term in the cited bias bound does not alter this order statement under the second displayed condition.  Indeed, if \(d\le n\), then \(n^2/(n\vee d)=n\); if \(d>n\), then \(d=o(nL)\) implies
\[
 \frac{n^2}{n\vee d}=\frac{n^2}{d}=\omega\!\left(\frac nL\right).
\]
In both cases, for every fixed \(c>0\),
\[
 \exp\!\left\{-c\frac{n^2}{n\vee d}\right\}=o(n^{-2})=o(h),
\]
because \(h\ge n^{-2}\).  Therefore the same strict comparison holds whether that exponentially small boundary contribution is displayed separately or absorbed into the published collision guarantee.  This completes the proof.
