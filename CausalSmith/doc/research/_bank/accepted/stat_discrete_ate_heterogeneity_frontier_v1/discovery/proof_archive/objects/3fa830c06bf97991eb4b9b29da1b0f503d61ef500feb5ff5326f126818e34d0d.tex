Take a source law in \(\mathcal D_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\), augmenting it if necessary by drawing \(B(0),B(1)\) conditionally on \(X\), independently of \(A\), with the prescribed arm means.  This preserves the observed experiment and makes consistency and conditional exchangeability explicit.  Define
\[
 Y(a):=M\{B(a)-1/2\},\qquad Y:=Y(A).
\tag{1}
\]
Overlap and the cell masses are unchanged.  If \(\eta_{ak}:=E[B\mid A=a,X=k]\), then
\[
 \mu_{ak}=M(\eta_{ak}-1/2)\in[-M/2,M/2].
\tag{2}
\]
Moreover \(|Y-\mu_{ak}|=M|B-\eta_{ak}|\le M\), so
\[
 E[(Y-\mu_{ak})^2\mid A=a,X=k]\le M^2.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus every source law maps into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\).  Cell effects and the ATE are scaled by \(M\):
\[
 \tau_k=M(\eta_{1k}-\eta_{0k}),\qquad
 \tau(P)=M\sum_{k=1}^dp_k(\eta_{1k}-\eta_{0k}).
\tag{4}
\]
Exact homogeneity therefore maps \(\mathcal H_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,0}\), hence into every radius class.

The inclusion is strict.  Put all mass on \(X=1\), take \(A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) independent of independent variables \(Z_0,Z_1\), each uniform on \(\{-M/4,0,M/4\}\), and set \(Y(a)=Z_a\), \(Y=Y(A)\).  Its arm means and effects are zero and
\[
 E[Z_a^2]=M^2/24\le M^2.
\tag{5}
\]
It belongs to every radius class but its three-point conditional law is not in the affine two-point binary image.

On the exactly homogeneous image, \(B=Y/M+1/2\) is invertible and squared ATE error is multiplied by \(M^2\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scaled\text{-}binary\text{-}exact\text{-}lower\text{-}transfer}\) transfers \(M^2(n^{-1}+d/n^2)\).  For the one-arm family, the hypothesis-independent channel of Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) preserves total-variation control by data processing and gives
\[
 c_\epsilon M^2\sigma^2\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\tag{6}
\]
These are lower transfers by subclass restriction.  In contrast, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\) proves the explicit signed-mark estimator uniformly over the larger conditional-second-moment class, including the three-point witness.  The comparison attributes novelty to the fixed-sample signed-mark construction and covariance proof, not merely to allowing real outcomes.
