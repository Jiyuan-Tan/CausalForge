Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  In the fixed-sample binary-outcome model with arbitrary cell masses, overlap \(\epsilon\le\pi_k\le1-\epsilon\), arbitrary treated response means in \([0,1]\), and control response identically zero, constants \(a_{\epsilon},b_{\epsilon}>0\) and \(N_{\epsilon}<\infty\) satisfy, for \(n\ge N_{\epsilon}\) and \(d\le b_{\epsilon}n\log n\),
\[
 \inf_{\widehat\psi}\sup_P E_P[(\widehat\psi-\sum_{k=1}^dp_k\mu_{1k})^2]
 \ge a_{\epsilon}\left\{\frac1n+\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2 n}\right\}.
\]
