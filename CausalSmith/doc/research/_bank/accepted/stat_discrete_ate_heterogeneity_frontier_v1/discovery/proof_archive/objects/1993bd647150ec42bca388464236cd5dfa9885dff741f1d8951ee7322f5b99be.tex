Put \(L:=\log(en)\), \(b:=n^{-1}+d/n^2\), and \(u:=d^2/(n^2L^2)\).  Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:fixed\text{-}interior\text{-}tightness\text{-}and\text{-}shrinking\text{-}radius\text{-}gap}\) proves that the selector benchmark is equivalent to \(b+\min(u,\sigma^2)\), whereas the current converse is \(b+\sigma^2u\), and that no other regime can produce divergence.

For a paired lower construction with total mass \(\omega\) and share \(r\), the parametrization
\[
 p_+=\omega r,\quad p_-=\omega(1-r),\quad
 \vartheta_+=\theta+\sigma(1-r),\quad
 \vartheta_-=\theta-\sigma r
\]
has pair average \(\theta\) and realization-wise radius at most \(\sigma\).  The translation \(\theta'=\theta+\Delta\), \(r'=r+\Delta/\sigma\) leaves both cell effects unchanged whenever \(r'\in[0,1]\), but deterministic \(\omega\) makes first-moment matching force \(E_0r=E_1r\).  Equal-mass reflected pairing also fails at the collision moment: with deviations \(\pm e\) and \(v_\pm:=\pi_\pm(1-\pi_\pm)\), the pair contribution is
\[
 (v_++v_-)\theta+(v_+-v_-)e,
\]
so reflected propensities reveal every shift of \(\theta\).  A successful lower proof therefore needs a correlated moment problem in \((\omega,r,e,\pi_+,\pi_-)\), normalization control, and fixed-sample de-Poissonization.

For the upper route, with \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\), and \(z_{ak}:=E[(Y/M)\mathbf1\{X=k,A=a\}]\), define
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}z_{1k}-s_{1k}z_{0k}-ts_{0k}s_{1k}
 =s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t).
\]
For a reciprocal polynomial \(r_K\), put
\[
 c_k:=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\},\qquad
 S_K(t):=\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}.
\]
If \(e_{ak}:=1-s_{ak}r_K(s_{ak})\), then
\[
 p_k-c_k=s_{1k}e_{0k}+s_{0k}e_{1k}
\]
and the exact centering identity is
\[
 t+S_K(t)-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right).
\]
The Chebyshev error \(|e_{ak}|\le B/(K^2s_{ak})\), overlap, and approximate homogeneity imply, for \(B\asymp L/n\) and \(K\asymp L\),
\[
 |t+S_K(t)-\vartheta|^2
 \le C_\epsilon u\{\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\}.
\]
Thus a centered mixed-arm factorial lift satisfying
\[
 E[(\widehat S_K(t)-S_K(t))^2\mid t]
 \le C_\epsilon\{b+u[\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2]\}
\]
would combine with an independent collision pilot to attain \(M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\).  The existing one-mark covariance lemma does not prove this mixed-arm kernel's exact-homogeneity degeneracy, so this variance inequality remains the precise unresolved upper-side step.
