Fix distinct \(i,j\).  Independence under \(Q_P^{(n)}\) gives, for each positive-mass cell \(k\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E[D_{ij}\mathbf 1\{X_i=k\}]
 &=p_k^2\{\pi_k(1-\pi_k)+(1-\pi_k)\pi_k\}\\
 &=2p_k^2\pi_k(1-\pi_k)=w_k.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
Summing (1) over the disjoint cells proves \(E[D_{ij}]=W_P\).  For the numerator, on \(\{X_i=X_j=k,A_i=1,A_j=0\}\) the conditional mean of \((A_i-A_j)(Y_i-Y_j)\) is \(\mu_{1k}-\mu_{0k}=\tau_k\).  On \(\{X_i=X_j=k,A_i=0,A_j=1\}\), both factors change sign, so the same conditional mean \(\tau_k\) results.  Hence
\[
 E[N_{ij}\mathbf 1\{X_i=k\}]
 =2p_k^2\pi_k(1-\pi_k)\tau_k=w_k\tau_k,
\tag{2}
\]
and summing proves \(E[N_{ij}]=\sum_{k=1}^d w_k\tau_k\).

At least one \(p_k\) is positive.  Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass:overlap}\) therefore yields
\[
 W_P=2\sum_{k=1}^d p_k^2\pi_k(1-\pi_k)
 \ge 2\epsilon(1-\epsilon)\sum_{k=1}^d p_k^2>0,
\tag{3}
\]
so division is legitimate.  Equations (1)--(3) and the definition of \(\tau_{\mathrm{col}}(P)\) give the claimed ratio.  Finally,
\[
 \tau_{\mathrm{col}}(P)-\tau(P)
 =\frac{1}{W_P}\sum_{k=1}^d w_k\delta_k,
\]
and the weights \(w_k/W_P\) are nonnegative and sum to one.  Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass:approximate\text{-}homogeneity}\) consequently gives
\[
 |\tau_{\mathrm{col}}(P)-\tau(P)|
 \le \max_{k:p_k>0}|\delta_k|
 \le \sigma M.
\]
