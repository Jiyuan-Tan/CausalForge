For \(d\le b_{\epsilon}n\log n\), apply the transformation \(Y=M(B-1/2)\) to the binary one-arm subclass in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:zeng\text{-}binary\text{-}one\text{-}arm\text{-}lower}\).  The same augmentation argument as in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scaled\text{-}binary\text{-}exact\text{-}lower\text{-}transfer}\) supplies consistent, conditionally exchangeable potential outcomes.  The mean and fourth-moment conditions follow from \(Y\in\{-M/2,M/2\}\), and the scale-sanity bound places every resulting law in the radius-two class.  The control response \(B(0)=0\) gives
\[
 \tau(P)=M\sum_{k=1}^dp_k\mu_{1k}.
\tag{1}
\]
Invertibility of the scaling and the cited lower bound therefore yield
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,2}
 \ge c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{\frac1n+\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\},
\tag{2}
\]
where replacing \(\log n\) by \(\log(en)\) changes only constants for \(n\ge N_{\epsilon}\).

For \(d>b_{\epsilon}n\log n\), take \(d_0=\lfloor b_{\epsilon}n\log n\rfloor\), after decreasing \(b_{\epsilon}\) and increasing \(N_{\epsilon}\) so that \(d_0\ge1\).  Embed every \(d_0\)-cell law into the \(d\)-cell model by assigning zero mass to the added cells.  The minimax risk is therefore no smaller than the \(d_0\)-cell risk, and (2) at \(d_0\) is bounded below by a positive constant times \(M^2\).  This dominates the truncated term \(M^2\min\{1,d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\}\).  Combining the two ranges proves the display.
