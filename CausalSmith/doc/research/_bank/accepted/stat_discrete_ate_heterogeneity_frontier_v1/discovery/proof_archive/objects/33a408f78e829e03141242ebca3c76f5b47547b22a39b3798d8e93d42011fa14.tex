There are constants \(C_{\epsilon},c_{\epsilon}>0\) and explicit estimators \(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}},T_n^{\mathrm{col}}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\) obtained from \(\mathrm{oeq:robust\text{-}upper\text{-}construction}\) such that, uniformly for \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), the total selector in \(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\) obeys \[\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]\le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.\] Relative to the collision guarantee in Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy, Theorem 3 on page 11, the heterogeneity-dependent remainder is never larger in order and is strictly smaller when \(d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]=o(\sigma^2+d/n^2)\) and \(d=o(n\log(en))\).
