Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  Let \(c_{U,\epsilon}>0\) be the dimension-range constant in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\), and let \(c_{L,\epsilon}>0\) be the corresponding constant in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\).  Put
\[
 c_{0,\epsilon}:=\min\{c_{U,\epsilon},c_{L,\epsilon},1\}.
\]
For every \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{0,\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\), Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper}\) gives
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{U,\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\tag{1}
\]
The estimator in that theorem is the single total selector from Definition~\(\mathrm{def:total\text{-}estimator}\); hence (1) is a minimax upper bound for the risk in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\), not a law-dependent oracle bound.

On the same radius and dimension range, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) gives
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c_{L,\epsilon}'M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Its \(n^{-1}\) component is valid at every boundary value by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:parametric\text{-}lower}\).  The remaining components are obtained there from subclasses of the very same model class, so the supremum in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\) transfers those lower bounds to all of \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\).

Choose the theorem's displayed constants as
\[
 c_{\epsilon}:=\min\{c_{0,\epsilon},c_{L,\epsilon}',C_{U,\epsilon}\}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 C_{\epsilon}:=\max\{C_{U,\epsilon},c_{\epsilon}\}.
\]
Then \(0<c_{\epsilon}\le C_{\epsilon}<\infty\), decreasing the dimension range if necessary, and (1)--(2) are exactly the two displayed inequalities.

Both arguments quantify over the model in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\), whose observed outcome has support in \(\mathbb R\), whose positive cell masses are otherwise unrestricted, and whose sampling law is the iid product in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:sample\text{-}experiment}\).  Overlap, the conditional-mean envelope, the conditional fourth-moment bound, and the radius condition are therefore identical on the two sides.  Zero-mass cells are immaterial because all cellwise conditions are imposed only when \(p_k>0\).  The proof establishes only the stated bracket: its two nonparametric interior expressions are not asserted to be equivalent, so interior tightness is not claimed.
