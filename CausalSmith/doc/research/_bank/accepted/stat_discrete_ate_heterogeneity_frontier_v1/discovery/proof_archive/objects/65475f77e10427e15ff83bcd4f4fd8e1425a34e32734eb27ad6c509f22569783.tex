Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:frontier\text{-}upper\text{-}all\text{-}d}\) supplies one fixed, data-driven, total estimator \(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}\in\mathcal T_{n,M}\) for every known \(n,d,M,\sigma\).  It is therefore an admissible witness in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\), and
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 &\le \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(\widehat\tau_n^{\star}-\tau(P))^2]\\
 &\le C_{\epsilon}M^2r_{n,d,\sigma}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
This is a genuine minimax upper bound, not an oracle family: the estimator is the explicit selector of the two sample maps, including clipping and every zero fallback.

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse\text{-}all\text{-}d}\) gives on the identical class
\[
 \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \ge c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{\frac1n+\min\left(1,\frac d{n^2}\right)
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Both inequalities hold for every \(n,d\ge1\) and every \(0\le\sigma\le2\).  Definition~\(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\) confirms that the common supremum ranges over arbitrary unknown cell masses, fixed overlap, conditional means in \([-M/2,M/2]\), real outcomes with conditional second central moments at most \(M^2\), and the realization-wise radius constraint on positive-mass cells.  No bounded-outcome substitution, rank assumption, or strengthened positivity condition enters either side.

The lower radius remainder in (2) is \(\sigma^2\min(1,u)\), while the upper remainder is \(\min(1,u,\sigma^2+d/n^2)\).  Hence (1)--(2) prove an honest bracket rather than uniform matching at every shrinking radius.  The cited accepted-bank result supplies provenance for the unrestricted binary endpoint; the all-alphabet radius bracket, explicit weak-moment selector, capped embeddings, and wedge localization are the present additions, and real outcomes alone are not asserted to be novel.
