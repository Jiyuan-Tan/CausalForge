The corrected frontier has the two required endpoint reductions. At \(\sigma=0\), \(r_{n,d,0}=n^{-1}+\min\{1,d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)],d/n^2\}\asymp n^{-1}+d/n^2\) uniformly for \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\). At \(\sigma=2\), \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,2}=\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\) and \(r_{n,d,2}=n^{-1}+\min\{1,d^2/[n^2\log^2(en)]\}\). Thus the matched frontier reduces respectively to the exact-homogeneity rate and the unrestricted effective-sample-enlargement rate.
