Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  There are constants \(C_{\epsilon}<\infty\), \(\rho_{\epsilon}>0\), and \(N_{\epsilon}<\infty\) such that the following holds.  Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\), use a block of \(m\ge N_{\epsilon}\) observations, put \(L_m:=\log(em)\), \(K:=\lfloor\alpha L_m\rfloor\), and \(B:=\beta_{\epsilon}L_m/m\), with \(\alpha\) and \(\beta_{\epsilon}\) as in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:centered\text{-}product\text{-}estimator}\).  Write \(V:=Y/M\), \(\vartheta:=\tau(P)/M\), \(\vartheta_k:=\tau_k/M\), \(s_{ak}:=P(X=k,A=a)\), and \(z_{ak}:=E_P[V\mathbf 1\{X=k,A=a\}]\).  For deterministic \(S\subseteq\{k:p_k\le B/4\}\), fixed \(t\in[-1,1]\), and the signed mixed-arm statistics \(\widehat P_k(t)\) in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:centered\text{-}product\text{-}estimator}\), if \(d\le\rho_{\epsilon}mL_m\), then
\[
 E[\widehat P_k(t)]
 =H_k(t)\{r_{K,B}(s_{0k})+r_{K,B}(s_{1k})\},
 \qquad
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t),
\]
where \(r_{K,B}(s):=B^{-1}G_K(s/B)\), and
\[
 \operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_{k\in S}\widehat P_k(t)\right)
 \le C_{\epsilon}\left\{
 \frac1m+\frac d{m^2}
 +\frac{d^2}{m^2L_m^2}
 \bigl[\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2\bigr]
 \right\}.
\]
The inequality uses only the conditional-second-central-moment envelope; it does not require bounded outcomes.
