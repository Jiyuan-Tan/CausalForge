The proved same-class bracket is
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{b+\sigma^2\min(1,u)\right\}
 \le \mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{\frac1n+\min\left(1,u,\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right)\right\}.
\]
The two sides are order-equivalent at \(\sigma=0\), at \(\sigma=2\), for every radius bounded away from zero, whenever \(u\ge1\), whenever \(u\lesssim b\), and whenever \(\sigma^2\lesssim b\).  Their ratio can diverge only when \(b\ll u\ll1\) and \(b\ll\sigma^2\ll1\); the sequence \(d_n=n\), \(\sigma_n=\log(en)^{-1/2}\) makes the ratio grow as \(\log(en)\).  No valid argument presently selects either benchmark as the exact minimax frontier within that wedge.
