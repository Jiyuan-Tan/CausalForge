It remains open to prove, in the wedge \(b\ll u\ll1\) and \(b\ll\sigma^2\ll1\), either a same-class lower experiment with ATE separation of order \(M\min\{\sigma,d/[n\log(en)]\}\) and sample-mixture total variation bounded away from one, or an explicit total estimator with risk strictly below \(M^2\{b+\min(u,\sigma^2)\}\), ideally of order \(M^2(b+\sigma^2u)\).
