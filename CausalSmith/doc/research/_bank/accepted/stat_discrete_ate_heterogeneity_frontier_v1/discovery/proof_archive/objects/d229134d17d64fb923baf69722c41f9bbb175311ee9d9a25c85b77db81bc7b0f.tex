Let \(\mathcal D_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) denote the binary-outcome class with arbitrary cell masses, fixed overlap \(\epsilon\), consistency, conditional exchangeability, and arm means in \([0,1]\), and let \(\mathcal H_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) be its uniform-mass, exactly homogeneous subclass.  The affine map
\[
 \Phi_M:\quad B(a)\longmapsto Y(a):=M\{B(a)-1/2\},\qquad Y=Y(A),
\]
embeds \(\mathcal D_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\), and embeds \(\mathcal H_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,0}\subseteq\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) for every \(0\le\sigma\le2\).  The image is a strict subclass of the note's real-outcome class.  Restriction to the image of \(\mathcal H_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) transfers the lower term \(M^2(n^{-1}+d/n^2)\), while applying the hypothesis-independent channel in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) to the binary one-arm hard family transfers the radius term
\[
 M^2\sigma^2\min\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}.
\]
Thus the lower bounds arise by subclass restriction, whereas the explicit signed-mark upper estimator is proved on the broader fourth-moment real-outcome class.
