For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \(0<c_{\epsilon}\le C_{\epsilon}<\infty\) such that, uniformly over \(0\le\sigma\le2\) and \(1\le d\le c_{\epsilon}n^2/\log(en)\),
\[
 c_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}
 \le\mathsf R_{n,d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{
 \frac1n+\frac d{n^2}
 +\sigma^2\min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
Both sides range over the identical arbitrary-cell-mass real-outcome class with fixed overlap, conditional means in \([-M/2,M/2]\), and conditional second central moments at most \(M^2\).  Thus the product expression is the minimax mean-squared-error rate, including shrinking radii; no limit law, inference statement, or adaptation claim is made.
