Let \(m:=\min(u,\sigma^2)\).  In the residual wedge, direct minimum algebra proves
\[
 \frac12\{b+m\}\le
 \frac1n+\min\left\{u,\sigma^2+\frac d{n^2}\right\}
 \le b+m,
\]
whereas the established converse benchmark is exactly \(b+\sigma^2u\).  Hence the OEQ is now confined to sequences with \(b\ll u\ll1,\ b\ll\sigma^2\ll1\); it is already settled whenever \(u\lesssim b\), \(\sigma^2\lesssim b\), \(u\gtrsim1\), or either \(u\) or \(\sigma^2\) is bounded below.

For the upper route, normalize \(V=Y/M\), put \(\vartheta=\tau(P)/M\), \(s_{ak}=P(X=k,A=a)\), \(z_{ak}=E_P[V\mathbf 1\{X=k,A=a\}]\), and \(\vartheta_k=\tau_k/M\).  The score
\[
 H_k(t):=s_{0k}z_{1k}-s_{1k}z_{0k}-t s_{0k}s_{1k}
 =s_{0k}s_{1k}(\vartheta_k-t)
\]
combined with a reciprocal polynomial \(r_K\) satisfies the exact population identity
\[
 t+\sum_kH_k(t)\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}-\vartheta
 =\sum_k(\vartheta_k-\vartheta)(c_k-p_k)
 +(t-\vartheta)\left(1-\sum_kc_k\right),
\]
where \(c_k=s_{0k}s_{1k}\{r_K(s_{0k})+r_K(s_{1k})\}\).  If \(|1-s_{ak}r_K(s_{ak})|\le B/(K^2s_{ak})\), overlap gives population bias at most \(C_{\epsilon}\{\sigma+|t-\vartheta|\}dB/K^2\).  With \(B\asymp L/n\) and \(K\asymp L\), this has the desired product-radius order.  The unresolved stochastic step is a cancellation-aware factorial variance bound of order
\[
 C_{\epsilon}\left\{\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}+
 (\sigma^2+(t-\vartheta)^2)u\right\}.
\]
For the lower route, the paired formula above proves that separation \(\min\{\sigma,d/(nL)\}\) is algebraically compatible with realization-wise radius control; only the correlated observed-mixture moment problem remains.
