Take a source law in \(\mathcal D_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\).  If only its observed conditional Bernoulli distributions are specified, augment it by drawing \(B(0),B(1)\) conditionally on \(X\), independently of \(A\), with the prescribed arm means.  This leaves the observed experiment unchanged and makes consistency and conditional exchangeability explicit.  Define
\[
 Y(a):=M\{B(a)-1/2\},\qquad Y:=Y(A).
\tag{1}
\]
The cell masses and propensities are unchanged, so overlap is preserved.  If
\(
 \eta_{ak}:=E[B\mid A=a,X=k]
\), then
\[
 \mu_{ak}=M(\eta_{ak}-1/2)\in[-M/2,M/2].
\tag{2}
\]
Moreover, \(|Y-\mu_{ak}|=M|B-\eta_{ak}|\le M\), and hence
\[
 E[(Y-\mu_{ak})^2\mid A=a,X=k]\le M^2.
\tag{3}
\]
Equations~\((1)\)--\((3)\), together with consistency, conditional exchangeability, and overlap, prove that every source law maps into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M}^{\mathrm{unr}}\).

The cell effects and the ATE are scaled by \(M\):
\[
 \tau_k=M(\eta_{1k}-\eta_{0k}),
 \qquad
 \tau(P)=M\sum_{k=1}^dp_k(\eta_{1k}-\eta_{0k}).
\tag{4}
\]
Therefore exact homogeneity of a uniform-mass source law is preserved.  Thus \(\Phi_M\) maps \(\mathcal H_d^{\mathrm{bin}}(\epsilon)\) into \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,0}\), which is contained in \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\) for each \(0\le\sigma\le2\).

The formal inclusion is strict.  Put all mass on \(X=1\), let \(A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\), and, independently of \(A\), let \(Z_0,Z_1\) be independent and uniform on \(\{-M/4,0,M/4\}\).  Set \(Y(a)=Z_a\) and \(Y=Y(A)\).  The arm means and all treatment effects vanish, while
\[
 E[Z_a^2]=\frac{M^2}{24}\le M^2.
\tag{5}
\]
This law satisfies consistency, conditional exchangeability, overlap, mean normalization, the second-central-moment bound, and approximate homogeneity at every radius.  It is not in the affine binary image because each conditional outcome law has three support points, whereas every image law is supported on \(\{-M/2,M/2\}\).  Hence the image is a strict subclass of the note's formal real-outcome class.

On the exactly homogeneous image, the observed transformation \(B=Y/M+1/2\) is invertible and squared ATE error is multiplied by \(M^2\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:scaled\text{-}binary\text{-}exact\text{-}lower\text{-}transfer}\) therefore transfers the lower term
\[
 M^2\left(\frac1n+\frac d{n^2}\right).
\tag{6}
\]
For the one-arm family, the hypothesis-independent channel in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:radius\text{-}channel\text{-}converse}\) preserves total-variation control by data processing and transfers
\[
 M^2\sigma^2\min\left\{1,
 \frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right\}
\tag{7}
\]
up to constants depending only on \(\epsilon\).  Both lower bounds are therefore obtained by restricting the note's supremum to scaled binary subclasses.

By contrast, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:continuous\text{-}ratio\text{-}polynomial\text{-}upper}\) proves its signed-mark estimator uniformly on \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}\).  Every law in that class satisfies
\[
 E[Y^2\mid A=a,X=k]
 =\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid A=a,X=k)+\mu_{ak}^2
 \le\frac{5M^2}{4}.
\tag{8}
\]
The anchor's formal model is binary, but the anchor also states that its rates remain valid under a uniform weak-second-moment envelope of this kind.  Accordingly, the three-point example witnesses strict inclusion only at the level of the formal classes; it is not a novelty claim based on allowing real outcomes.  The theorem-level increment is instead the explicit fixed-sample signed one-mark Chebyshev/factorial construction, its covariance proof without an extra logarithmic loss, and its downstream combination with the collision estimator in the known-radius selector and converse comparison.
