\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon,M,\sigma}}
 E_{Q_P^{(n)}}[(T_n^{\mathrm{poly}}-\tau(P))^2]
 \le C_{\epsilon}M^2\left\{\frac1n+
 \min\left(1,\frac{d^2}{n^2\log^2(en)}\right)\right\}
\]
for every pair of integers \(n,d\ge1\) and every \(0\le\sigma\le2\).  In its calibrated branch it uses \(O(dK^2)\) arithmetic operations after cell aggregation, and every branch has bounded fallbacks on every division boundary.
