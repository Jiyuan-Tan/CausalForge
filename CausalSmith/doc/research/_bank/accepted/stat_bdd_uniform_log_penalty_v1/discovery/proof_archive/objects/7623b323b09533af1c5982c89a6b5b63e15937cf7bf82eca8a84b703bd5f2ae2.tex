This is a conditional transfer theorem: the joint normalized-coarea remainder
and two-sided radial bounds are hypotheses on \(\mathcal C\), not consequences
of the ambient class.  The lower radial bound makes every displayed division
valid and gives \(\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\supseteq(0,r_*]\).  The repaired radial
coarea lemma identifies
\(g_{t,P,x}(s)=N_{t,P,x}(s)/Q_{t,P,x}(s)\) as the observed exact-distance
regression almost everywhere.  The assumed remainder implies its armwise limit
is \(\mu_{t,P}(x)\), so the separate observed-arm limits identify
\(\tau_P(x)\).

Fix \(0<h\leq r_*\), write \(v(u)=v_m(u)\), and define
\[
 G_{t,P,x}(h)=\frac1{h^2}\int_0^hK_\triangle(s/h)
 v(s/h)v(s/h)^\top Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds,
\]
\[
 r_{t,P,x}(h)=\frac1{h^2}\int_0^hK_\triangle(s/h)
 v(s/h)g_{t,P,x}(s)Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds.
\]
The observable Radon--Nikodym identities show that these are the expectations
of the declared empirical Gram matrix and outcome vector.  With
\[
 G_m=\int_0^1uK_\triangle(u)v(u)v(u)^\top\,du,
\]
the radial bounds give
\[
 \underline cG_m\preceq G_{t,P,x}(h)\preceq\overline cG_m.
\]
For nonzero \(a\in\mathbb R^m\), the polynomial \(a^\top v(u)\) cannot vanish
on \((0,1)\), and hence
\[
 a^\top G_ma=\int_0^1uK_\triangle(u)\{a^\top v(u)\}^2\,du>0.
\]
Therefore \(G_m\) is positive definite and
\[
 \lVert G_{t,P,x}(h)^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq\{\underline c\lambda_{\min}(G_m)\}^{-1}.
\]

Let \(B\) bound the coefficient functions and set
\[
 \beta_{t,P,x}(h)=
 (\mu_{t,P}(x),b_{t,1,P}(x)h,\ldots,
 b_{t,m-1,P}(x)h^{m-1})^\top.
\]
The H\"older ball and coefficient bound imply
\(\lVert\beta_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert\leq C\) for \(h\leq1\).  Polynomial
reproduction gives
\[
 r_{t,P,x}(h)=G_{t,P,x}(h)\beta_{t,P,x}(h)
 +\zeta_{t,P,x}(h),
\]
where the assumed remainder and radial upper bound imply
\[
 \lVert\zeta_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert
 \leq\frac A{h^2}\int_0^hK_\triangle(s/h)
 \lVert v(s/h)\rVert s^mQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds
 \leq Ch^m.
\]
Thus the population intercept has bias at most \(Ch^m\).

The radial upper bound also gives
\[
 P\{T=t,\ R_x\leq h\}=\int_0^hQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds
 \leq\frac{\overline c}{2}h^2.
\]
Every normalized Gram coordinate is bounded by \(h^{-2}\) and has second
moment at most \(Ch^{-2}\).  Conditional sub-Gaussianity, consistency, and the
bounded regression envelope imply, for every \(k\geq2\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P\left|h^{-2}\mathbf1\{T=t,R_x\leq h\}
 K_\triangle(R_x/h)v_\ell(R_x/h)Y\right|^k
 \leq k!C^kh^{2-2k}.
\]
Exponential Markov inequality therefore yields the Bernstein bound
\[
 P\{|(\mathbb P_n-P)Z_{t,x,h}|>u\}
 \leq2\exp\!\left\{-\frac{cnu^2}{h^{-2}+h^{-2}u}\right\}
\]
for every centered coordinate.

Cover \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\subseteq[-L,L]^2\) by an \(n^{-4}\)-net of
cardinality at most \(C_Ln^8\).  The embedded boundary has planar Lebesgue
measure zero, so no sample score lies on it almost surely.  Hence the strict sign
in the estimator agrees simultaneously with the observed arm along the
boundary.  Kernel-polynomial products are Lipschitz on the unit ball, giving
empirical oscillations at most
\[
 C_mn^{-4}h^{-3},
 \qquad C_mn^{-4}h^{-3}\mathbb P_n|Y|,
\]
and analogous population bounds.  A union bound over arms, coordinates, and
net points gives, with probability at least \(1-Cn^{-A_0}\),
\[
 \sup_{t,x}\lVert\widehat G^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)-G_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 +\sup_{t,x}\lVert\widehat r^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)-r_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert
 \leq C\left\{\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+
 \frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
For large \(n\) the right side is at most
\(\underline c\lambda_{\min}(G_m)/2\).  The Rayleigh-quotient inequality
\(\lambda_{\min}(\widehat G)\geq
\lambda_{\min}(G)-\lVert\widehat G-G\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\) activates the
cutoff and bounds the empirical inverse by
\(2/\{\underline c\lambda_{\min}(G_m)\}\).  Since
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat r^{(m)}-\widehat G^{(m)}\beta
 &=(\widehat r^{(m)}-r)+\zeta+(G-\widehat G^{(m)})\beta,
\end{aligned}
\]
multiplication by the inverse and extraction of the intercept yield
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h^m+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+
 \frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
On the exceptional event, the established admissibility lemma bounds an active
intercept by \(Ch^{-2}\mathbb P_n|Y|\), while a failed-cutoff intercept is zero.
Cauchy--Schwarz and sufficiently large \(A_0\) make its expected contribution
negligible.  At
\[
 h_n\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2m+2)},
\]
the bias and square-root stochastic terms both have order
\((\log n/n)^{m/(2m+2)}\), and the linear Bernstein term is smaller.  The
admissibility lemma places this single estimator in
\(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\), proving both upper bounds.

For the conditional converse, use a uniform design on
\([-1/2,1/2]^2\) with straight interface and
\(M\asymp h_n^{-1}\) disjoint trimmed radius-\(h_n\) cells.  Keep the control
regression fixed and in alternative \(j\) add
\[
 \Delta\phi((z-x_j)/h_n),\qquad \Delta=\eta h_n^m,
\]
to the treated regression, where
\(\phi\in C_c^\infty(B_2(0,1))\) and \(\phi(0)=1\).  If \(m\geq q\), then
for \(k\leq q\),
\[
 \lVert D^k\{\Delta\phi((\cdot-x_j)/h_n)\}\rVert_\infty
 \leq C\eta h_n^{m-k}\leq C\eta.
\]
Thus small \(\eta\) keeps the alternatives in the common H\"older ball.
Their derivatives through order \(m\) are bounded at the scale required by
Taylor's formula, and half-circle integration verifies the assumed order-
\(m\) normalized-coarea remainder; also \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)=\pi s\) at small
trimmed radii.  The theorem explicitly requires \(\mathcal C\) to contain
these laws.  When \(m<q\), the order-\(q\) derivative has size
\(h_n^{m-q}\), so this construction supplies no converse.

The Bernoulli relative-entropy bound and data processing through the common
Gaussian channel give
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P_j^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq Cn\Delta^2h_n^2=C\eta^2\log n.
\]
Since \(\log M=(1/(2m+2)+o(1))\log n\), choose \(\eta\) so this is at most
\(\alpha\log M\) for a fixed \(\alpha<1/4\).  The log-sum inequality bounds
the mutual information for a uniform alternative index \(J\) by
\(\alpha\log M\).  If \(E\) is any decoding-error event, conditioning first
on \(\mathbf1_E\) gives
\[
 H(J\mid O_{1:n})\leq\log2+P(E)\log M.
\]
Since \(H(J)=\log M\), the identity
\(I(J;O_{1:n})=H(J)-H(J\mid O_{1:n})\) bounds the decoding error away from
zero.
Uniform error below \(\Delta/3\) identifies the bumped center.  Every
full-sample estimator, hence every coordinatewise-distance estimator, therefore
has risk at least \(c\Delta=ch_n^m\) on the contained family.  This proves
exactness only under containment and \(m\geq q\).

Independently, if a class contains the repaired angular-imbalance hypercube,
Poissonization into disjoint cells, the coordinatewise-overlap direct-product
lemma, midpoint decoding, and marked de-Poissonization give the lower bound
\(ca_n\).  For \(m=1\) this matches the upper order; for \(m\geq2\),
\(m/(2m+2)>1/4\).  These are separate conditional certificates, not a
same-class universal converse.

Finally take the square \([-1,1]^2\), interface \(z_2=0\), a number
\(0<\alpha<1\), and a continuous radial cutoff \(\chi\) equal to one near
zero and zero for radius at least \(r_c<1\).  With the value at zero set by
continuity, define
\[
 f(z)=\frac14\left\{1+\chi(\lVert z\rVert_2)
 \lVert z\rVert_2^\alpha\frac{z_1}{\lVert z\rVert_2}\right\}.
\]
The bracket is bounded above and away from zero, and its perturbation is odd in
\(z_1\), so \(f\) is a normalized bounded positive continuous density.  At
the origin and sufficiently small treated-half-circle radii,
\[
 Q(s)=\frac s4\int_0^\pi(1+s^\alpha\cos\theta)\,d\theta
 =\frac{\pi s}{4}.
\]
For \(\mu\equiv c\), the observed ratio equals \(c\).  For
\(\mu(z)=z_1\),
\[
 \frac{N(s)}{Q(s)}
 =\frac{(s^2/4)\int_0^\pi\cos\theta(1+s^\alpha\cos\theta)\,d\theta}
 {\pi s/4}=\frac{s^{1+\alpha}}2.
\]
If an order-two remainder existed, some \(b\) would satisfy
\(s^{1+\alpha}/2-bs=O(s^2)\).  Division by \(s\) and passage to zero force
\(b=0\), after which division by \(s^2\) gives
\(s^{\alpha-1}/2\to\infty\), a contradiction.  The normalized-coarea
remainder is therefore joint in density, regression, and geometry; this witness
does not assert a universal weakest-threshold impossibility.
