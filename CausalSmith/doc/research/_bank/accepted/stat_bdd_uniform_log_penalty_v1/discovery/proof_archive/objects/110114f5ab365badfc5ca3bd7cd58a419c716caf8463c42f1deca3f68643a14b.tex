Let \(\mathcal I_q=\{\alpha=(\alpha_1,\alpha_2)\in\mathbb N_0^2:|\alpha|\leq q-1\}\), let \(p_q=|\mathcal I_q|\), and let \(r_q(u)=(u^\alpha:\alpha\in\mathcal I_q)^\top\), ordered with the constant monomial first.  Put \(e_1=(1,0,\ldots,0)^\top\) and
\[
 K_2(u)=(1-\lVert u\rVert_2)_+,
 \qquad H_t=\{u\in\mathbb R^2:(2t-1)u_2>0\}.
\]
Define the deterministic half-ball reference matrix and cutoff by
\[
 G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}
 =L^{-1}\int_{H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)}
 K_2(u)r_q(u)r_q(u)^\top\,du,
 \qquad
 \lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}
 =\lambda_{\min}(G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}).
\]
For \(x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), \(h>0\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), put \(U_{x,i}=(X_i-x)/h\) and define
\[
 \widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)
 =\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n
 \mathbf 1\{T_i=t\}K_2(U_{x,i})r_q(U_{x,i})r_q(U_{x,i})^\top,
\]
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)
 =\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n
 \mathbf 1\{T_i=t\}K_2(U_{x,i})r_q(U_{x,i})Y_i.
\]
The stabilized arm intercept is
\[
 \widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{t,h}(x)
 =\begin{cases}
 e_1^\top\{\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\}^{-1}
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h),
 &\lambda_{\min}\{\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\}
 \geq\lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}/2,\\
 0,&\lambda_{\min}\{\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\}
 <\lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}/2,
 \end{cases}
\]
and the fitted-intercept difference is
\[
 \widehat\tau^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)
 =\widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{1,h}(x)
 -\widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{0,h}(x).
\]
Finite sums, the eigenvalue cutoff, matrix inversion on the positive-definite branch, and the zero failure branch make this a jointly Borel map of the full sample and known assignment geometry.
