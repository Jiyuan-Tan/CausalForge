All constants may depend on the frozen class parameters, \(q\), and \(\delta\),
but not on \(n\) or \(P\).  The class is nonempty: take the uniform score law
on \([-1,1]^2\), the prescribed half-planes and chart, constant regressions in
\((1/4,3/4)\), and independent Gaussian-noised Bernoulli potential outcomes.
The actual observed arms contain quarter disks at the endpoints, and the moment
generating calculation used in the packing verifies the tail envelope.

We first analyze the exposed full-location estimator.  A nonzero polynomial of
total degree at most \(q-1\) cannot vanish on the open half-ball
\(H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)\).  Hence, for every nonzero coefficient vector \(v\),
\[
 v^\top G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}v
 =L^{-1}\int_{H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)}K_2(u)
 \{v^\top r_q(u)\}^2\,du>0.
\]
Compactness of the unit sphere in this fixed-dimensional coefficient space
proves \(\lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}>0\), so the cutoff in the construction
is valid.  Finite sums of Borel functions are Borel, the minimum eigenvalue is
continuous on symmetric matrices, and inversion is continuous on the positive
definite cone.  The cutoff and zero branch therefore make the exposed rule
jointly measurable in the full sample and known geometry, hence a member of
\(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{loc}}\).  For trimmed \(x\) and sufficiently small
\(h\), rescaling the square
shows that each arm-specific population Gram matrix dominates
\(G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}\).  Conditional sub-Gaussian concentration on a
polynomial planar net, extended by the Lipschitz kernel, gives uniformly
\[
 \sup_{t,x}\left(
 \lVert\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)-G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert
 +\lVert\widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)-r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert
 \right)
 =O_P\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}
 +\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right).
\]
Taylor's formula through total degree \(q-1\) gives intercept bias \(Ch^q\).
The Rayleigh-quotient perturbation bound activates the cutoff on the good event,
and the inverse is uniformly bounded there.  On every active branch, including
the exceptional event, the cutoff and bounded monomial vector imply
\(|\widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{t,h}(x)|
\leq Ch^{-2}\mathbb P_n|Y|\); on a failed branch it is zero.
The sub-Gaussian second moment and a polynomially small exceptional-event
probability therefore make its expected contribution negligible by
Cauchy--Schwarz.  Thus the explicitly defined estimator satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+
 \frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
Taking \(h\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2q+2)}\) proves the location upper bound
\(Cs_{n,q}\) with the named data-driven witness.

For its converse, choose \(M\asymp h^{-1}\) trimmed centers with disjoint
radius-\(h\) disks under the uniform design.  Keep the control regression fixed
and add to the treated regression in alternative \(j\)
\[
 \Delta\phi((z-x_j)/h),\qquad \Delta=\eta h^q,
\]
where \(\phi\in C_c^\infty(B_2(0,1))\) and \(\phi(0)=1\).  For \(k\leq q\),
\[
 \lVert D^k\{\Delta\phi((\cdot-x_j)/h)\}\rVert_\infty
 \leq C\eta h^{q-k}\leq C\eta,
\]
so small \(\eta\) keeps every alternative in the H\"older ball.  With the
same Gaussian-noised Bernoulli channel,
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P_j^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq Cn\Delta^2h^2=C\eta^2nh^{2q+2}.
\]
At the displayed bandwidth this is a small multiple of \(\log M\).  The
log-sum bound gives mutual information at most a fixed fraction of \(\log M\).
For a uniform center index \(J\), the elementary entropy bound
\(H(J\mid O_{1:n})\leq\log2+P(E)\log M\) therefore keeps the
center-decoding error away from zero; sup-norm accuracy below \(\Delta/3\)
would reveal the unique center.
Therefore
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \geq c\Delta=cs_{n,q}.
\]

Suppose \(L>4\).  The repaired actual-arm stability and upper-mass lemmas,
applied to the fixed local-constant distance estimator on a straight-segment
mesh, give
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}
 +\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}\leq Ca_n
\]
at \(h\asymp a_n\).  The canonical-square angular-density family exists
because \(L>4\) leaves nonzero density slack around \(1/4\).  Its direct-product
lower-bound argument gives the reverse inequality \(ca_n\).

Suppose \(L=4\).  The support has area four and the retained density floor is
\(1/4\), so
\[
 1=\int_{[-1,1]^2}f_P(z)\,dz
 \geq\int_{[-1,1]^2}\frac14\,dz=1.
\]
Thus \(f_P=1/4\) almost everywhere, and continuity makes the equality
pointwise.  This is normalization degeneracy, not an additional smoothness
premise.  For every trimmed boundary point and sufficiently small \(s\), the
observed arms are the two half-circles and
\[
 Q_{t,P,x}(s)=\frac\pi4s.
\]
Taylor expansion of \(\mu_{t,P}\) through degree \(q-1\), followed by
half-circle integration, supplies uniformly bounded coefficients satisfying
\[
 \left|\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}-\mu_{t,P}(x)
 -\sum_{k=1}^{q-1}b_{t,k,P}(x)s^k\right|\leq Cs^q.
\]
Instantiate the stabilized distance local polynomial with \(m=q\) and radial
lower constant \(\pi/4\).  Positive definiteness of its one-sided polynomial
Gram matrix, the established admissibility lemma, and the same concentration
and reproduction calculation yield
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+
 \frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}\leq Cs_{n,q}.
\]
Because the coordinatewise-distance class is a restriction of the full-location
class, the full-location lower bound applies to it and gives the reverse order.
Finally
\[
 \frac{q}{2q+2}>\frac14\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q>1,
\]
which proves the strict-slack elbow and both coincidence cases.
