For \(t\in\{0,1\}\), define the observed assignment arm
\[
 \mathcal E_{t,P}
 =\left\{z\in\mathcal X_P:
 \mathbf 1\{z\in\mathcal A_{1,P}\}=t\right\}.
\]
The assignment-partition convention makes these two sets a measurable partition of \(\mathcal X_P\), and deterministic assignment gives
\[
 \mathbf 1\{T=t\}=\mathbf 1\{X\in\mathcal E_{t,P}\}
 \quad P\text{-almost surely}.
\]
