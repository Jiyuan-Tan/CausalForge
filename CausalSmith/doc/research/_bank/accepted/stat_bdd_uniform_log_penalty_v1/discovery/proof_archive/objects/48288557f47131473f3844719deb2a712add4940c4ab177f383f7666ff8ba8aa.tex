Take \(0<h\leq2r_0\), fix a displayed law \(P\), arm \(t\), and eligible
boundary point \(x\), and put \(R_x=\lVert X-x\rVert_2\).  Since
\(K_\triangle\) is symmetric and
\(K_\triangle(u)=\max\{1-|u|,0\}\),
\[
 K_\triangle(D_{P,x}/h)=K_\triangle(R_x/h)\geq\frac12
 \quad\text{on }\{R_x\leq h/2\}.
\]
The observed-arm definition and deterministic assignment give
\(\mathbf1\{T=t\}=\mathbf1\{X\in\mathcal E_{t,P}\}\) almost surely.
The repaired thickness premise therefore yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Psi^{(0)}_{t,P,x}(h)
 &=\frac1{h^2}\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 K_\triangle(D_{P,x}/h)\mathbf1\{T=t\}\right]\\
 &\geq\frac1{2h^2}P\{T=t,\ R_x\leq h/2\}\\
 &\geq\frac1{2h^2}c_-\left(\frac h2\right)^2
 =\frac{c_-}{8}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every division is by \(h^2>0\), and thickness applies because
\(h/2\leq r_0\).  The bound is uniform over the primitive class, both arms,
and the entire boundary.  It also holds over the smooth class on the trimmed
boundary because \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\subseteq\mathcal B_P\).  Thus one
may take \(\kappa_0=c_-/8\).  Interface membership never enters the argument:
the lower mass is imposed directly on the observed treatment event.
