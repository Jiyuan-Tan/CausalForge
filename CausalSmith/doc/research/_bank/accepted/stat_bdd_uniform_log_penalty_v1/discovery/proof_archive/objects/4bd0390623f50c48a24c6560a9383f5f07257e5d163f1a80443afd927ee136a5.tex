For every \(q\geq1\), there exist constants \(0<c_{\mathrm{rect}}\leq C_{\mathrm{rect}}<\infty\), depending only on \(q,L,c_{-},r_0\), such that \[c_{\mathrm{rect}}\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{rect}})}{a_n}\leq\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{rect}})}{a_n}\leq C_{\mathrm{rect}}.\] The upper inequality is attained by the fixed degree-zero triangular-kernel estimator \(\widehat{\tau}^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}\) with \(h\asymp a_n\). The lower inequality is supplied by the separated compressed-experiment family in \(\mathrm{lem:angular\text{-}density\text{-}packing}\). This theorem concerns the explicitly defined primitive two-sided-thickness class and asserts no inclusion in, or same-class tightening of, the nonparametric class in Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem 6.
