The lower inequality is the converse theorem at \(\nu=2\).  The upper proposition gives \(R^{12,\pm}_n(p,2,L)\leq Cb_{n,2}\), and
\[
 b_{n,2}=\left({\log n\over n}\right)^{1/4}
 +\left({\log n\over n^{3/4}}\right)^{1/3}
 \asymp n^{-1/4}(\log n)^{1/3}.
\]
This proves the stated bracket without claiming that either logarithmic power is the exact endpoint frontier.
