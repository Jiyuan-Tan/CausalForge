Put \(q\geq1\), \(\Delta=a_n\), and \(w=A\Delta^{1/q}\), where \(A\) is a sufficiently large fixed constant.  On the unit square use the horizontal assignment interface and choose \(M\asymp w^{-1}\) centers \(x_j=(s_j,0)\) with spacing \(4w\).  Let \(\phi\) be a smooth radial cutoff supported on \([0,1]\), equal to one near zero, and let \(\eta\) be smooth with \(\eta=0\) on \([0,1]\) and \(\eta=1\) on \([2,\infty)\).  For constants \(a>0\) small and \(C>0\) large, set
\[
 b(r)={2\Delta\over ar}\phi(r/w)\eta(r/(C\Delta)),\quad b(0)=0,
\]
and, in polar coordinates \((r_j,\alpha_j)\) about \(x_j\), define
\[
 f_\omega(z)=1+\sum_j\omega_j b(r_j)\cos\alpha_j,
 \quad \mu_{0,\omega}(z)=0,
 \quad \mu_{1,\omega}(z)=az_1-\Delta\sum_j\omega_j\phi(r_j/w).
\]
All summands vanish outside their disjoint disks.  The angular integral of every density perturbation is zero.  Since it also vanishes for \(r\leq C\Delta\), the density is continuous at each center; choosing \(C\) large makes \(3/4\leq f_\omega\leq5/4\).  Each regression derivative of order \(k\leq q\) is bounded by a constant times \(\Delta w^{-k}=A^{-k}\Delta^{1-k/q}\), and disjointness prevents accumulation.  Gaussian errors of variance one give the common variance and tail envelopes.  Quarter-disk geometry gives a uniform two-sided local-mass constant.  Thus, after fixed harmless rescaling when needed, the laws lie in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cap\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\).  For \(L>4\), the same construction with baseline density \(1/4\) on \([-1,1]^2\) has strict density slack and lies in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\).

At \(x_j\), flipping \(\omega_j\) changes \(\tau_\omega(x_j)\) by exactly \(\Delta\).  On the treated semicircle, the identities \(\int_0^\pi\cos\alpha\,d\alpha=0\) and \(\int_0^\pi\cos^2\alpha\,d\alpha=\pi/2\) show that the signed-radius marginal is unchanged and that the conditional outcome-mean change is
\[
 -\Delta\phi(r/w)\{1-\eta(r/(C\Delta))\}.
\]
It vanishes for \(r\geq2C\Delta\); the control radial law is unchanged.  Since the outcome variance is bounded below, the one-observation conditional Kullback--Leibler divergence in a cell is at most
\[
 C\int_0^{2C\Delta}\Delta^2{r\over w^2}\,dr
 \leq C{\Delta^4\over w^2}.
\]
The cell probability is of order \(w^2\), so the product divergence for \(n\) observations is at most \(Cn\Delta^4\).  Now \(n\Delta^4=\log n\), whereas \(\log M=(1/q)\log(1/\Delta)+O(1)\asymp\log n\).  Multiplying \(\Delta\) by a sufficiently small fixed constant makes the divergence at most \(\alpha\log M\) with \(\alpha<1/8\).  Finally \(M\asymp w^{-1}\asymp a_n^{-1/q}\), which proves every asserted packing property.
