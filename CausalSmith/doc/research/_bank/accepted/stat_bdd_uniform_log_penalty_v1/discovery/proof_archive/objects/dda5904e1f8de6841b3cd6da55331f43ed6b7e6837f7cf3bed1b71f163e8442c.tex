Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\).  On the nonempty canonical class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\),
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp s_{n,q}.
\]
If \(L>4\), then
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp a_n,
\]
and the distance lower bound is direct from the canonical-square version of the narrowed \(\mathrm{lem:angular\text{-}density\text{-}packing}\).  If \(L=4\), then the density envelope and the fixed square support force \(f_P\equiv1/4\), and
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp s_{n,q}.
\]
Consequently the distance and location exponents are strictly separated for \(q>1\) when \(L>4\), while they coincide when \(q=1\) and also throughout the no-density-slack boundary case \(L=4\).
