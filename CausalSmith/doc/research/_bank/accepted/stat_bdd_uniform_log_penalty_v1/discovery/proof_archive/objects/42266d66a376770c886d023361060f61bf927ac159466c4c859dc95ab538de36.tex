For each \(x\in\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)\), the observed input is \(V_{n,P}(x)=((Y_i,\lVert X_i-x\rVert_2):1\leq i\leq n)\). \[
 R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}
 =\inf_{\widehat\mu_n\in\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{NP}}}
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)}

 \right].
\]
