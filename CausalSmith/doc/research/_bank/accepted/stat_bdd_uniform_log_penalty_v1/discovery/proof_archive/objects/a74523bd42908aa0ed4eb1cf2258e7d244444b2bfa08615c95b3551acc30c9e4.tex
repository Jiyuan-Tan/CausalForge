The results identify minimax rates up to constants, not exact asymptotic constants.  The rectifiable logarithmic frontier is proved only for the project-defined primitive two-sided-thickness class; the logarithmic frontier on the exact class of Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem 6, remains unresolved.  The smooth saturation result is endpoint-trimmed and permits bounded continuous densities without a shared modulus.  The order-\(m\) coarea theorem gives achievability for every fixed integer \(m\geq1\) under the stated joint radial remainder and mass conditions.  Its explicit contained-bump exactness certificate applies for every \(m\geq q\); for \(m<q\), only the upper bound is established and tightness remains unresolved.  A joint multi-center distance release is a different statistical experiment.
