For each \(x\in\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)\), the observed input is \(V_{n,P}(x)=((Y_i,\lVert X_i-x\rVert_2):1\leq i\leq n)\).  The inherited coordinatewise decision class is \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\), whose members use one common law-independent measurable map \(T_n\) and satisfy \(\widehat\mu_n(x)=T_n(V_{n,P}(x))\) at every boundary point.  The explicit minimax functional is
\[
 R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}
 =\inf_{\widehat\mu_n\in\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{NP}}}
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)}
 |\widehat\mu_n(x)-\mu_P(x)|
 \right].
\]
The theorem \(\mathrm{thm:cty\text{-}same\text{-}class\text{-}log\text{-}converse}\) proves the full-class lower bound \(R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\gtrsim(\log n/n)^{1/4}\).  The published uniform upper theorem imposes its additional Assumption~2 geometric condition, so it supplies an upper bound only on that upper-theory subclass and does not establish a two-sided frontier over all of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\)
