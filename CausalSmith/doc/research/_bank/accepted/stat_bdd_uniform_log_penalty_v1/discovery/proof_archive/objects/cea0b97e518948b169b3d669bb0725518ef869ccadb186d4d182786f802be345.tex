Fix \(p\geq0\), \(\nu\geq2\), and \(L\geq L_0(p)\), and put \(q=p+1\geq1\).  Let
\[
 \Delta_n=\gamma a_n,
 \qquad w_n=A\Delta_n^{1/q},
\]
where \(\gamma>0\) will be chosen small, and apply \(\mathrm{lem:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}rectangle\text{-}angular\text{-}hypercube\text{-}all\text{-}orders}\).  It gives \(M_n\asymp w_n^{-1}\to\infty\), common cell probability \(\rho_n=\pi w_n^2/36\), target separation \(\Delta_n\), and adjacent conditional cell divergence at most \(C_0\Delta_n^4/w_n^2\).

Put independent uniform bits on the vertices.  For the exactly \(n\) observations, let \(J_i\) record the cell containing \(X_i\), with label zero outside all cells.  By equal cell masses and locality, the label vector is bit-independent.  Conditional on it, the cell blocks are independent, block \(j\) depends only on bit \(j\), and block zero is bit-independent.  Marginally \(N_j=\sum_i\mathbf1\{J_i=j\}\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,\rho_n)\).  The multiplicative Chernoff bound and a union bound give
\[
 P\!\left(\max_jN_j>2n\rho_n\right)
 \leq M_n\exp(-n\rho_n/3).
\]
Now
\[
 n\rho_n\asymp n\Delta_n^{2/q}
 =n^{1-1/(2q)}(\log n)^{1/(2q)},
 \qquad \log M_n=O(\log n).
\]
Their ratio diverges for every \(q\geq1\); at the endpoint \(q=1\) it is of order \(n^{1/2}(\log n)^{-1/2}\).  Hence the simultaneous good-count event \(G_n=\{\max_jN_j\leq2n\rho_n\}\) has probability tending to one.

Fix labels in \(G_n\).  For decoder \(j\), reveal the complete off-cell raw blocks and retain from its own block only the ordered outcome--signed-distance observations.  These data reconstruct the compressed sample at \(x_j\), so the fixed Borel section and its midpoint decoder are measurable with respect to \((S_j,Z_{-j},Z_0)\).  Conditional tensorization gives, in both orientations,
\[
 \operatorname{KL}\{\mathcal L(S_j\mid\Omega_j=0,J_{1:n}),
 \mathcal L(S_j\mid\Omega_j=1,J_{1:n})\}
 \leq N_jC_0{\Delta_n^4\over w_n^2}
 \leq C_1n\Delta_n^4.
\]
Since \(n\Delta_n^4=\gamma^4\log n\) and
\[
 \log M_n={1\over4q}\log n+O(\log\log n),
\]
one may choose \(\gamma\) so small that this divergence is at most \(\kappa\log M_n\) for a fixed \(\kappa<1\).  The coordinatewise overlap direct-product lemma, conditional on the labels in \(G_n\), yields
\[
 P\{\text{some coordinate decoder errs}\mid J_{1:n}\}
 \geq {1\over2}\left[1-\exp\{-M_n^{1-\kappa}/2\}\right].
\]
Averaging over labels and the uniform vertex prior gives probability \(1/2-o(1)\) of at least one error.

An erroneous nearest-target decoder implies the measurable finite-coordinate loss is at least \(\Delta_n/2\).  Therefore
\[
 2^{-M_n}\sum_\omega
 \mathbb E_{P_\omega}\max_{j\leq M_n}
 |\widehat\tau_{n,P_\omega}(x_j)-\tau_{P_\omega}(x_j)|
 \geq {\Delta_n\over2}\{1/2-o(1)\}.
\]
The finite maximum is pointwise bounded by the possibly nonmeasurable full boundary supremum.  By monotonicity of outer expectation, the latter has outer expectation at least the expectation of the former.  Every vertex belongs to the displayed class by the hypercube lemma.  Taking the supremum over \(P\), then the infimum over the arbitrary point-indexed rule, dividing by \(a_n\), and taking the lower limit proves the result with a positive constant proportional to \(\gamma\).  No joint regularity in the boundary index has entered the argument.
