The decision restriction is coordinatewise: \(\widehat\tau_n(x)\) may use only \(U_{n,P}(x)\), and may not triangulate score locations from distances released at other boundary points.  The RN densities \(Q_{t,P,x}\) and \(N_{t,P,x}\) are fixed measurable versions and agree with circle integrals only almost everywhere; every identification and remainder claim is therefore phrased through essential support and separate armwise essential limits.  The order-\(m\) theorem is conditional on its radial hypotheses, and its converse additionally requires an explicitly contained bump family with \(m\geq q\).  The angular hard family supplies a separate first-order lower-bound certificate.  No implication from density regularity alone to the joint remainder, and no universal weakest threshold, is claimed.
