There is no density-only weakest regularity threshold for the faster distance frontier.  The operational upper condition is a joint coarea remainder of the density, regression, and boundary geometry.  Precisely, fix an integer \(m\) with \(1\leq m\leq\min\{q,\iota\}\), and let \(\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\) be any subclass for which there are constants \(A<\infty\), \(r_*>0\), and \(0<\underline c\leq\overline c<\infty\) such that, for every \(P\in\mathcal C\), \(x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(0<s\leq r_*\),
\[
 \underline c s\leq Q_{t,P,x}(s)\leq\overline c s,
 \qquad
 \left|\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}-\mu_{t,P}(x)-\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}b_{t,k,P}(x)s^k\right|\leq As^m,
\]
where the coefficient functions are uniformly bounded over \(P,t,x,k\).  Let \(\widehat\tau^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}\) be the stabilized coordinatewise degree-\((m-1)\) estimator in \(\mathrm{def:stabilized\text{-}distance\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial}\), using the lower radial constant \(\underline c\).  With
\[
 h_n\asymp\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(2m+2)},
\]
this estimator belongs to \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\) and satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal C}\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 \left|\widehat\tau^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h_n}(x)-\tau_P(x)\right|
 \right]
 \leq C\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{m/(2m+2)}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal C)
 \leq C\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{m/(2m+2)}.
\]
If \(m=q\) and \(\mathcal C\) contains, for every sufficiently large \(n\), the uniform-design straight-interface \(q\)-H\"older bump submodel of width \(h_n\) and height proportional to \(h_n^q\) constructed in the proof, then the reverse inequality holds and the displayed rate is exact.  No matching converse is asserted here when \(m<q\).  Independently, every law class containing the shrinking angular-imbalance family generated by \(\mathfrak H_{\mathrm{ang}}\) has distance lower frontier \(ca_n\).  Thus the order-one upper rate is the first-order saturation rate when that hard family is contained, while every \(m\geq2\) yields a faster geometry-sensitive upper rate; the matching contained-bump certificate supplied here applies exactly at \(m=q\).  A condition on \(f_P\) alone is neither necessary nor sufficient to determine the order: the normalized product integrals \(N/Q\), rather than the density in isolation, are the operational objects.
