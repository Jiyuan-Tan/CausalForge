Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem~6, gives an \(n^{-1/4}\) lower rate on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\) and conjectures in its following discussion that the uniform factor \(\log^{1/4}n\) is unavoidable.  The missing step is a same-class construction producing many hard boundary coordinates.  Their Assumption~2 upper theory is not an achievability theorem on this class: Assumption~2(i) uses an assignment boundary inside the score support, while Theorem~6(ii) sets the minimax curve equal to the support boundary.
