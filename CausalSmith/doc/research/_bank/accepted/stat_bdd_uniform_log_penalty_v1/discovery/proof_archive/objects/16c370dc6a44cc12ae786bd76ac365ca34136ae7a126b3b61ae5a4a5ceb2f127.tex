Let \(\Omega_1,\ldots,\Omega_M\) be independent uniform binary variables.  Conditionally on them, let \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_M\) be independent, with the law of \(Z_j\) depending only on \(\Omega_j\), and let \(Z_0\) be ancillary.  Suppose decoder \(j\) is measurable with respect to \((S_j,Z_{-j},Z_0)\), where \(S_j\) is a measurable compression of \(Z_j\), and write \(Q_{j,b}=\mathcal L(S_j\mid\Omega_j=b)\) and \(\rho_j=1-\lVert Q_{j,0}-Q_{j,1}\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}\).  Then every such decentralized decoder obeys
\[
\Pr\{\widehat\Omega_j=\Omega_j\text{ for every }j\}
\leq\frac12\left\{1+\prod_{j=1}^M(1-\rho_j)\right\}.
\]
In particular, if \(\operatorname{KL}(Q_{j,0},Q_{j,1})\leq\kappa\log M\) for all \(j\) and a fixed \(\kappa<1\), then the probability of at least one decoding error is at least \(1/2-o(1)\).
