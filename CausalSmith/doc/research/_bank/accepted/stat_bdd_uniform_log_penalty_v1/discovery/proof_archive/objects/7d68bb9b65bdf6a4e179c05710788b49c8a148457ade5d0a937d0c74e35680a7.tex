Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\).  The local-constant proof for \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\) applies on \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\) with the following simplification.  The stability lemma gives a uniform denominator lower bound, the upper-mass lemma gives kernel-window probability at most \(\pi Lh^2\), and \(q\geq1\) gives first-order bias at most \(Lh\).  The trimmed boundary is compact and contained in \([-L,L]^2\), so an ambient \(n^{-3}\)-net has at most \(C_Ln^6\) points.  Bernstein concentration and a union bound give probability at least \(1-Cn^{-12}\) that all net denominators are bounded below and all centered numerators are at most
\[
 C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}.
\]

An injective \(C^{\iota+2}\) chart is a \(C^1\) image of a compact interval, hence its image has two-dimensional Lebesgue measure zero.  Absolute continuity therefore implies that almost surely no sample score lies on \(\mathcal B_P\).  Along the boundary the strict arm indicators are consequently constant, while the reverse triangle inequality and Lipschitz continuity of \(K_\triangle\) give Gram oscillation \(C n^{-3}h^{-3}\) and outcome-numerator oscillation
\[
 Cn^{-3}h^{-3}\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n|Y_i|\right).
\]
At \(h\asymp a_n\) these terms are negligible.  Thus, on the good event,
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}.
\]
On its complement, the convex-combination and zero-branch structure of the estimator, together with \(\mathbb E_P\max_i|Y_i|^2\leq C\log n\), makes the expected contribution negligible by Cauchy--Schwarz.  Balancing at \(h\asymp a_n\) proves
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})\leq C_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}a_n.
\]
No quantitative derivative, speed, reach, or length constant is used; the constants may therefore be chosen to depend only on the parameters listed in the theorem.

For the lower bound, use the unit-square straight-interface family in the angular-density packing.  Its affine chart is injective and \(C^\infty\), its centers lie in \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), and the packing proof verifies compact support, bounded continuous density, the H\"older regression envelope, common variance and tail bounds, and two-sided thickness.  Hence every vertex lies in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\).

Poissonize at mean \(2n\), attach continuous marks, and split into the disjoint bump cells and their complement.  The local compressed relative entropy is at most \(2\alpha\log M_n\), where \(2\alpha<1\), while adjacent target values differ by at least \(c_1a_n\).  The coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma gives probability at least \(1/2-o(1)\) of some bit error.  Midpoint thresholding would recover all bits whenever the trimmed-boundary sup loss were below \(c_1a_n/2\).  Marked subsampling back to exactly \(n\) observations loses only \(O(e^{-cn})\), because the target is uniformly bounded.  Therefore
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})\geq c_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}a_n.
\]
The upper and lower bounds give the displayed liminf and limsup.  The lower densities are continuous law by law but have no common modulus, so smooth assignment geometry alone does not improve the coordinatewise-distance exponent.
