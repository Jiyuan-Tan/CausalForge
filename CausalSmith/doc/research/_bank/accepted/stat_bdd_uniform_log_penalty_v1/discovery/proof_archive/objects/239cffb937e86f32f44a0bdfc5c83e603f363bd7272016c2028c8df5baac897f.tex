Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem~6, gives an \(n^{-1/4}\) lower rate on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\), while its discussion conjectures that the logarithmic uniform penalty is unavoidable.  The missing mathematical step is a same-class construction that produces a growing number of boundary alternatives while remaining hard for the inherited coordinatewise distance release.  Their uniform upper theorem has an additional Assumption~2 geometric condition and hence does not furnish a full-class upper bound under rectifiability alone.
