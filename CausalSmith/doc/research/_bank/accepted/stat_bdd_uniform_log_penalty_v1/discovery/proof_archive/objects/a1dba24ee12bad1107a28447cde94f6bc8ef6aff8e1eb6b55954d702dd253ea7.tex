Fix \(p\geq0\), \(\nu\geq2\), and \(L\geq L_0(p)\).  By \(\mathrm{thm:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}point\text{-}indexed\text{-}log\text{-}converse\text{-}all\text{-}orders}\), there is a constant \(c_0=c_0(p,\nu,L)>0\) such that
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}{R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)\over a_n}\geq c_0.
\]
The converse quantifies over the full law-independent known-geometry point-indexed class with Borel fixed sections and uses the same outer-expectation risk and the same class \(\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\).

The winsorized, Gram-stabilized estimator is an explicit member of \(\mathcal T_n^{12,\pm}\).  Therefore the infimum in \(\mathrm{def:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}outer\text{-}risk}\), evaluated at this single law-independent rule, and \(\mathrm{prop:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}winsorized\text{-}expected\text{-}outer\text{-}upper}\) give a finite constant \(C_0=C_0(p,\nu,L)\) such that, for every sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)
 &\leq \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)}
 \mathbb E_P^*\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{12,\mathrm{LP}}_{n,a_n}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \right]\\
 &\leq C_0a_n.
 \end{split}
\]
Since \(a_n>0\), division by \(a_n\) and passage to the upper limit yield
\[
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}{R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)\over a_n}\leq C_0<\infty.
\]
For every real sequence, its lower limit does not exceed its upper limit.  Thus, with
\[
 c={1\over2}\min\{c_0,C_0\}>0,
 \qquad C=C_0,
\]
the lower and upper displays combine to give
\[
 0<c\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}{R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)\over a_n}
 \leq\limsup_{n\to\infty}{R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)\over a_n}
 \leq C<\infty.
\]
Finally, \(a_n=(\log n/n)^{1/4}\) by \(\mathrm{def:frontier\text{-}rates}\), and \(q=p+1\geq1\).  This proves the stated matched minimax frontier on the same displayed class: the proposition supplies the explicit attaining upper-bound witness, while the cited converse supplies the lower bound against every permitted point-indexed Borel section.
