Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\).  On the nonempty canonical class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\),
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp s_{n,q}.
\]
The location upper bound is attained by the named stabilized bivariate degree-\((q-1)\) estimator \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}\) from \(\mathrm{def:stabilized\text{-}location\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial}\), with
\[
 h\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2q+2)}.
\]
If \(L>4\), then
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp a_n,
\]
and the distance lower bound is direct from the canonical-square version of \(\mathrm{lem:angular\text{-}density\text{-}packing}\).  If \(L=4\), normalization, the density floor, and the fixed square support force \(f_P\equiv1/4\), and
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp s_{n,q}.
\]
Consequently, for \(q>1\), distance compression has the slower exponent in the strict-slack case \(L>4\); the exponents coincide when \(q=1\), and the no-slack normalization case \(L=4\) coincides for every \(q\).
