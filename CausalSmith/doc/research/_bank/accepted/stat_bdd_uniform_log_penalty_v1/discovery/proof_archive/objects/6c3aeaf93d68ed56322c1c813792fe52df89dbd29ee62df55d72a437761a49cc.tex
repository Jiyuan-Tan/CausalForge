The rate results are equivalences up to constants, not exact asymptotic constants.  The primitive and smooth frontiers concern a project-defined actual-arm two-sided-thickness class with bounded density from above; their hard family is a contained continuous-density subclass.  The straight comparison retains a density floor and continuity, with \(L>4\) providing strict density slack and \(L=4\) forcing uniform density by normalization.  Faster order-\(m\) rates hold only for subclasses satisfying the displayed almost-everywhere normalized-coarea conditions, and matching lower bounds require contained bump submodels and \(m\geq q\).  The logarithmic frontier on the exact class of Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem~6, remains unresolved.
