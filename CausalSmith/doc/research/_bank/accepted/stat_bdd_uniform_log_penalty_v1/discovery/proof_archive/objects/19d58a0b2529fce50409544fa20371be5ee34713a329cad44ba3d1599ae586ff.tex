The regression \(\mu_P\) lies in \(\mathcal H^q(\mathcal X_P,L)\), and the continuous conditional variance satisfies \(L^{-1}\leq\sigma_P^2(x)\leq L\).  At each boundary point the estimator observes only \(V_{n,P}(x)\).  In the strengthened class it may apply an arbitrary point-specific section \(T_{n,x}\), provided every fixed section is Borel measurable and the family is selected independently of \(P\); no joint regularity in \(x\) is required.  The inherited CTY class restricts all sections to one common map.
