We first prove achievability.  Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\), an arm
\(t\), a boundary point \(x\), and \(0<h\leq r_0\).  Put
\(R_x=\lVert X-x\rVert_2\) and
\[
 W_{t,x,h}=h^{-2}\mathbf1\{T=t\}K_\triangle(R_x/h).
\]
The repaired stability lemma gives
\(d_{t,P,x}(h):=\mathbb E_PW_{t,x,h}\geq\kappa_0\).  The observed-arm
upper-mass lemma, \(0\leq K_\triangle\leq1\), consistency, the H\"older-ball
supremum bound, and conditional sub-Gaussianity imply
\[
 \mathbb E_PW_{t,x,h}^2\leq\pi Lh^{-2},
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P\{W_{t,x,h}Y\}^2\leq Ch^{-2}.
\]
For every integer \(k\geq2\), conditioning on \(X\) also gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P|W_{t,x,h}\{Y-\mu_{t,P}(X)\}|^k
 \leq k!C^kh^{2-2k}.
\]
Expanding the exponential series and applying exponential Markov inequality
therefore yields, for any centered numerator or denominator summand
\(Z_{t,x,h}\),
\[
 P\!\left(\left|(\mathbb P_n-P)Z_{t,x,h}\right|>u\right)
 \leq2\exp\!\left\{-\frac{cnu^2}{h^{-2}+h^{-2}u}\right\}.
\]

Cover \(\mathcal B_P\subseteq[-L,L]^2\) by an ambient
\(\epsilon_n\)-net with \(\epsilon_n=n^{-4}\); its cardinality is at most
\(C_Ln^8\), without geometric regularity of the interface.  For a fixed
observation the arm indicator \(\mathbf1\{T_i=t\}\) is independent of the
index \(x\), while
\(x\mapsto K_\triangle(\lVert X_i-x\rVert_2/h)\) is
\(h^{-1}\)-Lipschitz.  Thus normalized denominator and numerator oscillations
are at most
\[
 C\epsilon_nh^{-3},
 \qquad C\epsilon_nh^{-3}\mathbb P_n|Y|,
\]
with the analogous population bounds.  Score absolute continuity makes sample
scores distinct almost surely.  The strict-sign formula in the declared
estimator differs from the observed-arm formula only at \(x=X_i\), so uniformly
over \(x\) it changes the normalized denominator by at most
\((nh^2)^{-1}\) and the numerator by at most
\((nh^2)^{-1}\max_i|Y_i|\).  The tail and regression envelopes give
\[
 \sup_P\mathbb E_P\max_{i\leq n}|Y_i|^2\leq C\log n,
 \qquad
 \sup_P\mathbb E_P(\mathbb P_n|Y|)^2\leq C.
\]
A union bound over the net and the oscillation estimates shows that, for every
fixed \(A_0>0\), with probability at least \(1-Cn^{-A_0}\),
\[
 \sup_{t,x}|\mathbb P_nW_{t,x,h}-d_{t,P,x}(h)|
 +\sup_{t,x}|\mathbb P_n(W_{t,x,h}Y)-\mathbb E_P(W_{t,x,h}Y)|
 \leq C\left\{\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+
 \frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}
\]
when \(h\asymp a_n\).

For large \(n\), the right side is below \(\kappa_0/2\).  Every empirical
denominator is then positive and bounded below.  By consistency, the population
ratio is a kernel-weighted average of \(\mu_{t,P}(z)\) on the observed arm and
within distance \(h\) of \(x\); hence
\[
 \left|\frac{\mathbb E_P(W_{t,x,h}Y)}{d_{t,P,x}(h)}
 -\mu_{t,P}(x)\right|\leq Lh.
\]
The ratio identity
\[
 \frac{\widehat r}{\widehat d}-\frac rd
 =\frac{\widehat r-r}{\widehat d}
 +\frac rd\frac{d-\widehat d}{\widehat d}
\]
then gives on the good event
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+
 \frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
The canonical observed-distance lemma separately identifies the armwise
essential limits with the causal regression values.

On the exceptional event each arm estimate is zero or a convex combination of
outcomes, so the loss is at most \(2\max_i|Y_i|+2L\).  Cauchy--Schwarz and a
sufficiently large \(A_0\) make its expected contribution negligible.  With
\(h\asymp a_n\), every term is at most \(Ca_n\).  The estimator is a single
measurable member of \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\), proving
\[
 R_n^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}})\leq C_{\mathrm{rect}}a_n.
\]

For the converse, use the observed-arm hypercube from the angular-density
packing.  Poissonize at mean \(2n\), attach independent continuous marks, and
partition the process into its disjoint bump cells and complement.  Conditional
on the bits these restrictions are independent, the complement is bit
independent, and cell \(j\) depends only on bit \(j\).  Midpoint thresholding
\(\widehat\tau_n(x_j)\) gives decoder \(j\); its local cell is observed only
through \(U_{n,P}(x_j)\), with the other cells and complement ancillary for its
bit.  Poisson tensorization and the packing slack give divergence at most
\(2\alpha\log M_n\), with \(2\alpha<1\).  The coordinatewise-overlap
direct-product lemma yields
\[
 P\{\text{at least one bit is decoded incorrectly}\}\geq\frac12-o(1).
\]
Loss below \(c_1a_n/2\) would decode every bit, so the Poissonized expected
loss is at least \(ca_n\).  Marked subsampling produces exactly \(n\)
independent observations whenever the Poisson count is at least \(n\), whose
failure probability is exponentially small.  Thus the fixed-sample risk also
obeys
\[
 R_n^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}})\geq c_{\mathrm{rect}}a_n.
\]
Division by \(a_n\) and passage to \(\liminf\) and \(\limsup\) prove the
claim.  The family lies in this project-defined class; no inclusion in the class
of Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem~6, is used.
