Fix \(n\) large enough for the packing lemma and abbreviate \(M=M_n\) and \(\Delta=c_1a_n\).  Let \(\widehat\mu_n\in\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\) be an arbitrary fixed-\(n\) rule.  We first construct from it a marked-Poisson rule, and only then invoke the product lower bound.

Generate a Poisson point process of mean \(2n\) from a packing law \(P_\omega\), and attach independent marks with a continuous distribution to its points.  If its count \(N\) is at least \(n\), retain the \(n\) points with the smallest marks and apply \(\widehat\mu_n\) to those retained observations; if \(N<n\), return the identically zero boundary function.  Call the resulting random-size rule \(\widehat\mu_n^{\mathrm{Pois}}\).  Selection by marks is independent of the observations.  Conditional on \(N=k\geq n\), the retained observations therefore have law \(P_\omega^{\otimes n}\), and the construction is independent of \(P_\omega\).  At each boundary point \(x\), its output uses the retained marked pairs \((Y_i,\lVert X_i-x\rVert_2)\), so it is a coordinatewise rule in the marked compressed experiment.

Partition the Poisson process into the disjoint half-disc cells supporting the bumps and their complement.  Conditional on the independent uniform bits \(\Omega_1,\ldots,\Omega_M\), Poisson restrictions to these sets are independent; cell \(j\) depends only on \(\Omega_j\), and the complement is bit independent.  For center \(x_j\), threshold \(\widehat\mu_n^{\mathrm{Pois}}(x_j)\) at the midpoint of the two possible values of \(\mu_{P_\omega}(x_j)\).  This gives a decoder of \(\Omega_j\).  In cell \(j\), the decoder uses only the marked compression \((Y_i,\lVert X_i-x_j\rVert_2)\).  Giving it the marked uncompressed restrictions of all other cells and of the complement can only make decoding easier.  Thus the enlarged experiment has the conditional product form of \(\mathrm{lem:coordinatewise\text{-}overlap\text{-}direct\text{-}product}\).  The marks are bit independent and contribute zero relative entropy.

Poisson tensorization multiplies the one-observation relative entropy by \(2n\).  The slack fixed in the packing proof gives, for every \(j\),
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(Q_{j,0},Q_{j,1})
 \leq 2\alpha\log M,
 \qquad 2\alpha<1.
\]
The direct-product lemma consequently yields
\[
 \Pr\{\text{at least one coordinate is decoded incorrectly}\}
 \geq\frac12-o(1).
\]
Because the regression value at \(x_j\) depends only on \(\Omega_j\), uniform loss smaller than \(\Delta/2\) makes every midpoint decoder correct.  Hence the marked-Poisson maximal risk of the induced rule over the finite family is at least
\[
 \frac{\Delta}{2}\left(\frac12-o(1)\right)\geq c a_n.
\]

We now compare that risk with the risk of the original fixed-\(n\) rule.  Since the retained sample has law \(P_\omega^{\otimes n}\) on \(\{N\geq n\}\), and every packing regression is bounded by one, the zero default has loss at most one and
\[
 \sup_\omega \mathbb E_{P_\omega}
 \left[\left\|\widehat\mu_n^{\mathrm{Pois}}-\mu_{P_\omega}\right\|_{\infty,\operatorname{bd}(S)}\right]
 \leq
 \sup_\omega \mathbb E_{P_\omega}
 \left[\left\|\widehat\mu_n-\mu_{P_\omega}\right\|_{\infty,\operatorname{bd}(S)}\right]
 +\Pr\{\operatorname{Pois}(2n)<n\}.
\]
The Poisson Chernoff bound gives \(\Pr\{\operatorname{Pois}(2n)<n\}\leq e^{-c'n}=o(a_n)\).  Combining the last two displays proves, for every fixed-\(n\) rule,
\[
 \sup_\omega \mathbb E_{P_\omega}
 \left[\left\|\widehat\mu_n-\mu_{P_\omega}\right\|_{\infty,\operatorname{bd}(S)}\right]
 \geq c a_n
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\), after reducing \(c\) if necessary.  The packing proof verified conditions \(\mathrm{(i)}\)--\(\mathrm{(iv)}\) of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\) for every member and every sufficiently large \(n\).  Thus the supremum over the full CTY class is no smaller.  Taking the infimum over the inherited law-independent decision class gives \(R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\geq ca_n\).  Division by \(a_n\) and passage to the lower limit prove the displayed inequality and its equality with the expanded definition of \(R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\).
