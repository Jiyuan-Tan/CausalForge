The headline contribution is the lower bound \(R_n^{\mathrm{PI}}\gtrsim(\log n/n)^{1/4}\) on the exact \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\) law class for arbitrary law-independent point-indexed coordinatewise distance rules with Borel fixed sections and no regularity in \(x\).  Because the inherited common-map class is a strict subset, the CTY lower-bound conjecture follows as a corollary.
