Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\). On the nonempty canonical class \(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{straight}}\),
\[
R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp a_n,\qquad R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{straight}})\asymp s_{n,q}.
\]
The distance lower bound is direct from \(\mathrm{lem:angular\text{-}density\text{-}packing}\), not inherited from the larger smooth class, and remains valid for its affine-regression hard family. The location lower and upper bounds are the ordinary two-dimensional H\"older sup-norm bounds on the same trimmed straight interface. Therefore the exponents are strictly separated when \(q>1\) and coincide when \(q=1\).
