Let \(0<h\leq2r_0\).  Since \(K_\triangle(u)\geq1/2\) whenever \(|u|\leq1/2\), deterministic assignment and the definition of signed distance imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Psi^{(0)}_{t,P,x}(h)
 &\geq {1\over2h^2}P\{T=t,\ |D_{P,x}|\leq h/2\}\\
 &= {1\over2h^2}P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,h/2)\}\\
 &\geq {1\over2h^2}c_- (h/2)^2={c_-\over8}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The last line is the two-sided-thickness assumption, applicable because \(h/2\leq r_0\).  Thus one may take \(\kappa_0=c_-/8\).  The calculation holds for every boundary point of the rectifiable class and hence also for every point of the trimmed boundary in the smooth class.
