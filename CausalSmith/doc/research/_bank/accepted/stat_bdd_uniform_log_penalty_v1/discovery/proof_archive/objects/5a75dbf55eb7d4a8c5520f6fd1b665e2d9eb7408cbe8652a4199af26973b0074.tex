The rectifiable and smooth theorems establish the exact first-order logarithmic frontier on the paper's own primitive classes, the straight theorem identifies the \(L>4\) versus \(L=4\) information elbow, and the coarea-transition theorem gives an explicit order-\(m\) estimator and rate.  Its converse uses only a genuinely contained \(q\)-H\"older bump family at \(m=q\); it makes no exactness claim for \(m<q\).
