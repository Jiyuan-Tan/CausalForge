The note constructs a hypercube of laws on the square \([-1/2,1/2]^2\), verifies all four defining conditions of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\), exposes cellwise bit locality and common cell mass, and proves adjacent compressed Kullback--Leibler divergence at most \(\alpha\log M_n\).  A marked-Poisson cell decomposition and the coordinatewise overlap direct-product lemma lower-bound a measurable finite packing maximum.  De-Poissonization is performed for that finite maximum, which is then bounded by the full boundary supremum using outer expectation.  This proves \(R_n^{\mathrm{PI}}\gtrsim(\log n/n)^{1/4}\) for the sectionwise-Borel point-indexed class; the inherited common-map theorem follows by inclusion.
