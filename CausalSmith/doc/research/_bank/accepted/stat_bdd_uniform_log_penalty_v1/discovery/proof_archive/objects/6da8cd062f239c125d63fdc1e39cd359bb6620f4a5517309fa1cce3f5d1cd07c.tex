Whenever both separate observed-arm essential limits exist, define the observed-distance functional by \(\tau_P^{\mathrm{obs,dist}}(x)=\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{1,P}(x)-\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{0,P}(x)\).  The lemma \(\mathrm{lem:canonical\text{-}exact\text{-}distance\text{-}regression}\) proves \(\tau_P^{\mathrm{obs,dist}}(x)=\tau_P(x)\) on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\); on a subclass consumed by \(\mathrm{thm:angular\text{-}remainder\text{-}transition}\), its assumed almost-everywhere radial lower bound and normalized-coarea remainder separately imply \(\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)=\mu_{t,P}(x)\) for each arm and hence the same causal equality.
