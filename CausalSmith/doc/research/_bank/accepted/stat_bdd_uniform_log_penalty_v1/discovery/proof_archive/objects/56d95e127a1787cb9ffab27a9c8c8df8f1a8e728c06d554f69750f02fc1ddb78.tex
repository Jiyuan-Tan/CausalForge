For every integer \(p\geq1\) and every \(\nu\geq2\), there is a finite \(L_0=L_0(p)\geq48\) such that, for every \(L\geq L_0\) and all sufficiently small \(\Delta>0\), the class \(\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\) contains a hypercube \(\{P_\omega:\omega\in\{0,1\}^M\}\) with the common known geometry
\[
 \mathcal X=[-3,3]^2,
 \quad \mathcal A_1=[-1,1]\times[0,2],
 \quad \mathcal A_0=\mathcal X\setminus\mathcal A_1,
 \quad \mathcal B=\partial\mathcal A_1,
\]
Euclidean distance, and \(K_\square\).  Writing \(q=p+1\), the family has \(M\geq c\Delta^{-1/q}\) disjoint disks \(S_j=B_2(x_j,w)\), with \(x_j\) on the middle of the bottom edge, \(w=A\Delta^{1/q}\), and the following properties:

\(\mathrm{(a)}\) every member satisfies every clause of \(\mathrm{def:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}class}\), including density normalization and envelopes, the raw conditional moment bound, the corner geometry, local kernel mass, Euclidean-ball VC condition, radial Hausdorff positivity, and the uniform Gram eigenvalue bound;

\(\mathrm{(b)}\) the cell label probabilities are bit-independent and equal,
\[
 P_\omega(X\in S_j)=\rho={\pi w^2\over36};
\]
the law restricted to \(S_j\) depends only on \(\omega_j\), and the law outside \(\bigcup_jS_j\) is bit-independent;

\(\mathrm{(c)}\) if \(\omega^{(j)}\) differs only in coordinate \(j\), then
\[
 |\tau_{P_\omega}(x_j)-\tau_{P_{\omega^{(j)}}}(x_j)|=\Delta;
\]

\(\mathrm{(d)}\) conditional on \(X\in S_j\), the adjacent one-observation signed-distance laws \(Q_{j,0}\) and \(Q_{j,1}\) of \((Y,D^{\pm}_{P,x_j})\) have the same signed-radius marginal, agree outside \(0<D^{\pm}_{P,x_j}<2C\Delta\), and obey, in both orientations,
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(Q_{j,0},Q_{j,1})\leq C_0{\Delta^4\over w^2}.
\]
All constants are independent of \(M\), \(\omega\), \(\Delta\), and \(n\).
