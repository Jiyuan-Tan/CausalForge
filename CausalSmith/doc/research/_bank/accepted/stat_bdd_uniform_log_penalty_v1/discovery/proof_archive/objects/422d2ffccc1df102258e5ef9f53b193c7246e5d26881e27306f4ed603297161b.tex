\[
 \mathcal X=[-3,3]^2,\quad \mathcal A_1=[-1,1]\times[0,2],\quad \mathcal A_0=\mathcal X\setminus\mathcal A_1,\quad \mathcal B=\partial\mathcal A_1,
\]
Euclidean metric, and uniform kernel.  It has \(M\geq c\Delta^{-1/(p+1)}\) disjoint sites \(x_j\) on the bottom edge, target separation \(|\tau_{P_\omega}(x_j)-\tau_{P_{\omega^{(j)}}}(x_j)|=\Delta\), bit-local equal-probability cells, and adjacent signed-distance product divergence at most \(Cn\Delta^4\).
