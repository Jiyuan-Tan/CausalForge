For every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{coarea}}(q,\iota,L)\),
\(x\in\mathcal B_P\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), polar coarea gives, for
every \(r>0\),
\[
 P\{T=t,\ X\in B_2(x,r)\}
 =P\{X\in\mathcal E_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}
 =\int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds.
\]
Absolute continuity gives \(P(X=x)=0\), so the sign of \(D_{P,x}\)
recovers the treatment arm almost surely.  For Lebesgue-almost every
\(s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mid D_{P,x}=(2t-1)s]
 =g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)
 =\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}.
\]
If both \(\mathcal S_{0,P,x}\) and \(\mathcal S_{1,P,x}\) accumulate
at zero and the two separate armwise essential limits exist with
\(\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)=\mu_{t,P}(x)\), then
\[
 \tau_P(x)=\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{1,P}(x)
 -\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{0,P}(x).
\]
