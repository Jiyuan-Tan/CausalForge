For every integer \(p\geq1\), every \(\nu\geq2\), and every \(L\geq4\), the explicit stabilized estimator in \(\mathrm{def:cty\text{-}stabilized\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial\text{-}estimator}\), with bandwidth \(h_n=b_{n,\nu}\), satisfies for all sufficiently large \(n\)
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)}
 \mathbb E_P^*\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{12,\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h_n}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \right]
 \leq Cb_{n,\nu}.
\]
This expected outer-risk statement is a derived corollary of CTY's first-order bias theorem and the expected maximal-inequality displays in its supplement; it is not asserted to be the verbatim statement of CTY Theorem~4(ii).
