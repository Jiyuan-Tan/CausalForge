Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:smooth\text{-}embedding}\), \(\gamma_P\) is an injective \(C^1\) map with image \(\mathcal B_P\).  The one-dimensional area formula for an injective \(C^1\) parameterization follows directly by applying the definition of curve length to partitions and passing to the Riemann integral; it gives
\[
 \mathcal H^1(\mathcal B_P)=\int_0^1\lVert\gamma_P'(u)\rVert_2\,du.
\]
The lower-speed assumption gives
\[
 \int_0^1\lVert\gamma_P'(u)\rVert_2\,du\geq\int_0^1L^{-1}\,du=L^{-1}.
\]
The definition of the \(C^{\iota+2}\)-norm dominates the uniform norm of the first derivative.  Hence \(\mathrm{ass:chart\text{-}derivative\text{-}envelope}\) gives
\[
 \int_0^1\lVert\gamma_P'(u)\rVert_2\,du
 \leq\sup_{u\in[0,1]}\lVert\gamma_P'(u)\rVert_2\leq L.
\]
These displays prove both asserted bounds.
