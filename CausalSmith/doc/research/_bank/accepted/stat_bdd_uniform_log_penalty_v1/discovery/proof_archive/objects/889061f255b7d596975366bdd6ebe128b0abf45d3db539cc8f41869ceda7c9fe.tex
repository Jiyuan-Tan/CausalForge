Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\).  The smooth-embedding assumption makes \(\gamma_P\) an injective \(C^1\) map, while the speed assumption gives \(\lVert\gamma_P'(u)\rVert_2>0\) for every \(u\).  Thus the standard change-of-parameter formula for the length of a regular injective curve gives
\[
\mathcal H^1(\mathcal B_P)=\int_0^1\lVert\gamma_P'(u)\rVert_2\,du.
\]
The lower speed bound yields
\[
\mathcal H^1(\mathcal B_P)\geq\int_0^1L^{-1}\,du=L^{-1}.
\]
The chart-derivative envelope includes \(\sup_u\lVert\gamma_P'(u)\rVert_2\leq L\), and hence
\[
\mathcal H^1(\mathcal B_P)
\leq\int_0^1L\,du=L.
\]
All boundary and endpoint conditions are covered by the parameter interval \([0,1]\), so no additional endpoint term occurs.
