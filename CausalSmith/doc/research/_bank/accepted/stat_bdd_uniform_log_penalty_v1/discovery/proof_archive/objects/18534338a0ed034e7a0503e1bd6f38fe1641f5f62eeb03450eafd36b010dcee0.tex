For \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), define the partial
observed exact-distance regression only on its positive-radial-density domain by
\[
 g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}:\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\to\mathbb R,
 \qquad
 g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)=\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}.
\]
No value is assigned at \(s=0\) or at a positive radius outside
\(\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\).
