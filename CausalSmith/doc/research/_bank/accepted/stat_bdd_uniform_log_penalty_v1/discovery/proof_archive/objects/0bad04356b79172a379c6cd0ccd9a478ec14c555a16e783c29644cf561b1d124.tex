Let \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) and \(t\in\{0,1\}\).  When there is a unique
\(a\in\mathbb R\) such that, for every \(\varepsilon>0\), there is an
\(r>0\) for which
\[
 \left|g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)-a\right|\leq\varepsilon
\]
for \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds\)-almost every
\(s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\cap(0,r)\), define
\[
 \ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)=a
 =\mathop{\operatorname{ess\,lim}}_{\substack{s\downarrow0\\
 s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}}}^{Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds}
 g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s).
\]
