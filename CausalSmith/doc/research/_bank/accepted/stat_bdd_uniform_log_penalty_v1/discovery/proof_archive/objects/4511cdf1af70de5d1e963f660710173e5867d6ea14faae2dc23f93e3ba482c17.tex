The result is a full-\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\) converse, not a full-class two-sided frontier.  Rectifiability in the displayed class does not supply the additional uniform Gram/geometric condition used by the published upper theory, so that upper result must remain confined to its Assumption~2 subclass.  The proof itself begins with an arbitrary rule in the inherited common-map class, embeds it into a marked mean-\(2n\) Poisson experiment to obtain independent cell restrictions, applies the direct-product bound, and then de-Poissonizes.
