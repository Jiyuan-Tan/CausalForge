For every \(P\in\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{sm}}\), \(x\in\mathcal{B}_P\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), coarea gives, for \(0<r\leq r_0\), \[P\{X\in\mathcal{A}_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}=\int_0^r Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds\geq c_{-}r^2.\] Hence the set \(\{s>0:Q_{t,P,x}(s)>0\}\) accumulates at zero, without requiring positivity at every radius. For Lebesgue-almost every \(s>0\) with \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)>0\), \(g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)=N_{t,P,x}(s)/Q_{t,P,x}(s)\) is a version of \(\mathbb{E}_P[Y\mid D_{P,x}=(2t-1)s]\). The canonical values on zero-denominator radii satisfy \[|g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)-\mu_{t,P}(x)|\leq Ls,\qquad s\geq0,\] and therefore \[\tau_P(x)=\lim_{s\downarrow0}\bigl(g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{1,P,x}(s)-g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{0,P,x}(s)\bigr).\]
