The upper proof is the rectifiable upper argument restricted to \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\).  The stability lemma supplies the same denominator lower bound, the density lemma supplies the same upper local mass, and the H\"older assumption with \(q\geq1\) supplies the same \(Lh\) bias.  The mesh may be taken over the fixed square \([-L,L]^2\), so no unproved entropy premise is needed; alternatively, the length lemma gives a one-dimensional cover of the trimmed curve.  Thus
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}}\mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\},
\]
and \(h\asymp a_n\) proves the upper bound.

For the lower bound, use the unit-square-support version of the narrowed angular packing.  Its interface is a straight \(C^\infty\) embedded segment of positive reach, its chart speed and all derivatives satisfy the frozen envelopes, and its centers were chosen in \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\).  Hence every vertex belongs to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\) for every \(\iota\geq1\).  The exact-recovery reduction, factorized Poisson argument, and de-Poissonization in the proof of \(\mathrm{thm:rectifiable\text{-}exact\text{-}frontier}\) use only these centers and their coordinatewise compressed laws, so they give
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})\geq c a_n.
\]
Together with the upper bound this proves the proposed statement.  The construction uses \(q\)-H\"older bumps rather than affine regressions; the affine clause in the frozen statement is not used and is not true for this many-coordinate exact-recovery construction.
