Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem 6, gives an \(n^{-1/4}\) pointwise-style lower bound over its stated nonparametric class, while the following discussion predicts a logarithmic penalty for uniform estimation.  Their Theorem 3(ii) is a fixed-law smooth-bias result and does not provide a class-uniform normalized-coarea remainder under continuous score densities without a common modulus.  The remaining transition gap here is converse tightness for the order-\(m\) upper rate when \(m<q\), not the achievability rate itself.
