Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\).  The observed-arm local-constant argument from the primitive theorem applies on \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\).  The stability lemma gives a uniform denominator lower bound, the observed-arm upper-mass lemma bounds a kernel window by \(\pi Lh^2\), and \(q\geq1\) gives bias at most \(Lh\).  An ambient \(n^{-4}\)-net of \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\subseteq[-L,L]^2\) has polynomial cardinality.  The same conditional moment-series calculation, exponential Markov bound, and union bound give an event of probability at least \(1-Cn^{-A_0}\) on which all centered numerators and denominators are bounded by
\[
 C\left\{\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]

An injective \(C^1\) image of a compact interval is Lipschitz and therefore has planar Lebesgue measure zero.  Score absolute continuity consequently implies that almost surely no sample score lies on \(\mathcal B_P\).  Along the boundary, the strict sign in the estimator equals the observed treatment arm simultaneously for all sample indices.  Reverse triangle inequality and kernel Lipschitz continuity bound the net oscillations by
\[
 Cn^{-4}h^{-3},
 \qquad Cn^{-4}h^{-3}\mathbb P_n|Y|.
\]
At \(h\asymp a_n\) these terms are negligible.  The ratio algebra gives on the good event
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
On its complement, the convex-combination or zero-branch bound and \(\mathbb E_P\max_i|Y_i|^2\leq C\log n\) make the expected contribution negligible.  Balancing at \(h\asymp a_n\) proves
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})
 \leq C_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}a_n.
\]
Only actual observed-arm thickness is used; there is no density floor or continuity premise and no common chart length, speed, reach, or derivative bound.

For the lower bound use the straight-interface family from the angular packing.  Its observed arms are the two half-squares, its centers lie in the trimmed boundary, and the construction verifies all smooth-class member properties, including actual-arm thickness.  Poissonization, independent cell restrictions, the coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma, midpoint decoding, and the marked de-Poissonization argument from the preceding proof yield
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})
 \geq c_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}a_n.
\]
Dividing by \(a_n\) proves the displayed limits.  Smooth assignment geometry alone therefore does not improve the coordinatewise-distance exponent when bounded score densities have no shared modulus.
