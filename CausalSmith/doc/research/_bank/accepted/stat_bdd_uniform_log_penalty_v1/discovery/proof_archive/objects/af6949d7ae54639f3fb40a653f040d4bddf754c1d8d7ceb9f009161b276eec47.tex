Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(r>0\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:density\text{-}envelope}\), \(f_P(z)\leq L\) on \(\mathcal X_P\).  Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}
 &=\int_{\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)}f_P(z)\,dz\\
 &\leq L\,\operatorname{Leb}_2\!\left(\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)\right)\\
 &\leq L\,\operatorname{Leb}_2(B_2(x,r))
 =\pi Lr^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
No boundary regularity is used.  The support and positivity conditions needed for the integral are already part of the two class definitions.
