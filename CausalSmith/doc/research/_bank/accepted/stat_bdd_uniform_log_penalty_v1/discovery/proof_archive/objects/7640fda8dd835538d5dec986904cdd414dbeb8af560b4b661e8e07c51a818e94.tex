For a member law \(P\), the known design object is
\[
 G_P=(\mathcal X_P,\mathcal A_{0,P},\mathcal A_{1,P},\mathcal B_P,d_P,K_\square),
\]
where \(\mathcal A_{0,P},\mathcal A_{1,P}\) partition \(\mathcal X_P\), \(\mathcal B_P\subset\operatorname{int}(\mathcal X_P)\) is their common interface, and \(d_P\) is the known design metric.
