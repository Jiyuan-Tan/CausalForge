Fix an integer \(m\geq1\), a cutoff constant \(c>0\), a bandwidth \(h>0\), and \(x\in\mathcal B_P\).  Put \(v_m(u)=(1,u,\ldots,u^{m-1})^\top\), let \(e_1=(1,0,\ldots,0)^\top\), and set \(S_{P,x,i}=|D_{P,x,i}|\).  At the probability-zero value \(D_{P,x,i}=0\), assign the observation to neither arm.  Define
\[
 \widehat G^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)
 =\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n
 \mathbf 1\{(2t-1)D_{P,x,i}>0\}
 K_\triangle(S_{P,x,i}/h)
 v_m(S_{P,x,i}/h)v_m(S_{P,x,i}/h)^\top
\]
and
\[
 \widehat r^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)
 =\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n
 \mathbf 1\{(2t-1)D_{P,x,i}>0\}
 K_\triangle(S_{P,x,i}/h)
 v_m(S_{P,x,i}/h)Y_i.
\]
Let
\[
 G_m=\int_0^1uK_\triangle(u)v_m(u)v_m(u)^\top\,du,
 \qquad \lambda_m(c)=c\lambda_{\min}(G_m),
\]
and define the stabilized arm intercept and arm difference by
\[
 \widehat\mu^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{t,h,c}(x)
 =\begin{cases}
 e_1^\top\{\widehat G^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)\}^{-1}
 \widehat r^{(m)}_{t,x}(h),
 &\lambda_{\min}\{\widehat G^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)\}\geq\lambda_m(c)/2,\\
 0,&\lambda_{\min}\{\widehat G^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)\}<\lambda_m(c)/2,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 \widehat\tau^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)
 =\widehat\mu^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{1,h,c}(x)
 -\widehat\mu^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{0,h,c}(x).
\]
The cutoff input \(c\) is suppressed from the notation for \(\widehat\tau^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}\).  The formula is defined for every \(h>0\); in an order-\(m\) coarea class it is used with \(c=\underline c\) and \(0<h\leq r_*\).  The strict sign convention, eigenvalue cutoff, inverse branch, and zero branch make this a pointwise Borel formula of \(U_{n,P}(x)\).
