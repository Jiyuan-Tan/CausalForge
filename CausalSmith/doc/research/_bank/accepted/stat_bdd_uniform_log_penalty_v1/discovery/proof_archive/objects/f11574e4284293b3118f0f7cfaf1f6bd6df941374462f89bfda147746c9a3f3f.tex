If \(P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\), then for each \(t\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[|Y(t)-\mu_{t,P}(X)|^{2+\nu}\mid X\right]
 \leq 2^{2+\nu}L
\]
almost surely.  Thus every centered residual moment invoked by the local-polynomial expansion follows from, and is not substituted for, CTY's raw conditional-moment envelope.
