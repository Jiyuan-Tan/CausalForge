Fix \(p\geq0\), \(\nu\geq2\), \(L\geq4\), and \(P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\).  Put
\[
 h=h_n=a_n=\left({\log n\over n}\right)^{1/4},
 \qquad B=B_n=a_n^{-1/3}.
\]
For each arm \(t\) and boundary point \(x\), let \(\beta^*_{t,P,x}(h)\) be the coefficient solving the original, unwinsorized population normal equations.  Define the nonnegative Gram deviation
\[
 D_h=\sup_{t\in\{0,1\},\,x\in\mathcal B_P}
 \left\|\widehat\Psi_{t,x}(h)-\Psi_{t,P,x}(h)\right\|
\]
and the pointwise event
\[
 \mathcal G_h=\{D_h\leq(2L)^{-1}\}.
\]
No measurability of this supremum is needed because all expectations below are outer expectations.

On \(\mathcal G_h\), clause \(\mathrm{(viii)}\) of \(\mathrm{def:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}class}\) and Weyl's eigenvalue inequality give, simultaneously in \(t\) and \(x\),
\[
 \lambda_{\min}\{\widehat\Psi_{t,x}(h)\}
 \geq \lambda_{\min}\{\Psi_{t,P,x}(h)\}-D_h
 \geq L^{-1}-(2L)^{-1}=(2L)^{-1}.
\]
Thus the stabilization branch does not activate, \(\|\widehat\Psi_{t,x}(h)^{-1}\|\leq2L\), and the empirical normal equations yield the algebraic identity
\[
 \widehat\beta^B_{t,x}(h)-\beta^*_{t,P,x}(h)
 =\widehat\Psi_{t,x}(h)^{-1}\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h).
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \sup_{t,x}\left\|\widehat\beta^B_{t,x}(h)-\beta^*_{t,P,x}(h)\right\|
 \leq2L\sup_{t,x}\left\|\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h)\right\|
 \quad\hbox{on }\mathcal G_h.
\]

Because \(\beta^*_{t,P,x}(h)\) solves the unwinsorized population equations, subtraction of those equations from the definition of \(\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h)\) gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h)
 ={1\over h^2}\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \mathbf1\{D^{\pm}_{P,x}\in I_t\}K_\square(D^{\pm}_{P,x}/h)
 r_p(D^{\pm}_{P,x}/h)\{\psi_B(Y)-Y\}
 \right].
\]
On the observed arm, clause \(\mathrm{(i)}\) of the class definition gives \(Y=Y(t)\).  On the support of \(K_\square(D^{\pm}_{P,x}/h)\), every coordinate of \(r_p(D^{\pm}_{P,x}/h)\) is bounded by one.  The conditional bound in \(\mathrm{lem:cty\text{-}winsorization\text{-}bias}\), the density bound \(f_P\leq L\), and containment of the kernel support in a radius-\(h\) disk therefore imply
\[
 \begin{split}
 \sup_{t,x}\left\|\mathbb E_P\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h)\right\|
 &\leq {C\over h^2}B^{-3}
 \sup_x P\{\|X-x\|_2\leq h\}\\
 &\leq {C\over h^2}B^{-3}(\pi Lh^2)
 \leq CB^{-3}.
 \end{split}
\]
The triangle inequality for outer expectation and \(\mathrm{lem:cty\text{-}winsorized\text{-}score\text{-}maximal\text{-}bound}\) now give
\[
 \mathbb E_P^*\!\left[\sup_{t,x}\|\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h)\|\right]
 \leq C\left\{
 B^{-3}+\sqrt{{\log(1/h)\over nh^2}}
 +{B\log(1/h)\over nh^2}
 \right\}.
\]

By \(\mathrm{lem:cty\text{-}uniform\text{-}first\text{-}order\text{-}bias}\),
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 \left|e_1^\top\{\beta^*_{1,P,x}(h)-\beta^*_{0,P,x}(h)\}-\tau_P(x)\right|
 \leq Ch.
\]
Clause \(\mathrm{(iii)}\) of the class definition implies \(|\mu_{t,P}(x)|\leq L\), so \(|\tau_P(x)|\leq2L\).  Projection onto \([-2L,2L]\) is one-Lipschitz and fixes \(\tau_P(x)\).  Hence, on \(\mathcal G_h\),
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{12,\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sup_{t,x}\|\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h)\|\right\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal G_h^c\), clipping both the estimator and the target to \([-2L,2L]\) bounds the loss by \(4L\).  These two cases give the pointwise inequality
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{12,\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq Ch+C\sup_{t,x}\|\widehat O^B_{t,x}(h)\|
 +4L\mathbf1\{D_h>(2L)^{-1}\}.
\]
Since \(\mathbf1\{D_h>(2L)^{-1}\}\leq2LD_h\) pointwise, monotonicity and positive homogeneity of outer expectation, followed by \(\mathrm{lem:cty\text{-}expected\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial\text{-}maximal\text{-}bounds}\), show that
\[
 \mathbb E_P^*\mathbf1\{D_h>(2L)^{-1}\}
 \leq2L\mathbb E_P^*D_h
 \leq C\sqrt{{\log(1/h)\over nh^2}}.
\]
Combining the last three displays yields, uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P^*\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{12,\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \right]
 \leq C\left\{
 h+B^{-3}+\sqrt{{\log(1/h)\over nh^2}}
 +{B\log(1/h)\over nh^2}
 \right\}.
\]

It remains to evaluate the four terms.  For all sufficiently large \(n\), \(h\leq L^{-1}\), \(B\geq1\), and
\[
 {nh^2\over\log(1/h)}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
so both cited maximal bounds apply.  Moreover,
\[
 h=a_n,\qquad B^{-3}=a_n,
 \qquad \sqrt{{\log(1/h)\over nh^2}}=O(a_n),
\]
because \(\log(1/h)=\tfrac14\{\log n-\log\log n\}\), while
\[
 {B\log(1/h)\over nh^2}
 =O\!\left(n^{-5/12}(\log n)^{5/12}\right)
 =o(a_n).
\]
The constant is independent of \(P\) and \(n\).  Taking the supremum over the class proves the asserted \(Ca_n\) outer-expectation bound.  The construction definition makes this explicit rule a law-independent member of \(\mathcal T_n^{12,\pm}\); no regularity of an arbitrary point-indexed rule is used.
