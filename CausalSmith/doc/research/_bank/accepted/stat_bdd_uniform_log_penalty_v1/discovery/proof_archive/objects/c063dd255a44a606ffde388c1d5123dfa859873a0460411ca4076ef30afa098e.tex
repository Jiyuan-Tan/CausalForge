Take \(0<h\leq2r_0\).  Symmetry and the explicit triangular kernel give
\[
 K_\triangle(D_{P,x}/h)=K_\triangle(|D_{P,x}|/h)\geq\frac12
 \quad\text{on }\{\lVert X-x\rVert_2\leq h/2\}.
\]
Under deterministic assignment, \(T=t\) almost surely on \(\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\}\), subject only to the fixed interface convention.  Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Psi^{(0)}_{t,P,x}(h)
 &=\frac1{h^2}\mathbb E_P\!\left[K_\triangle(D_{P,x}/h)\mathbf1\{T=t\}\right]\\
 &\geq\frac1{2h^2}P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,h/2)\}\\
 &\geq\frac1{2h^2}c_-\left(\frac h2\right)^2
 =\frac{c_-}{8}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every division is by \(h^2>0\), and the use of thickness is valid because \(h/2\leq r_0\).  The bound is uniform over \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\), both arms, and \(x\in\mathcal B_P\).  It also holds over \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\) and \(x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), since \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\subseteq\mathcal B_P\).  Thus one may take \(\kappa_0=c_-/8\).  The argument uses neither density continuity nor a density lower envelope.
