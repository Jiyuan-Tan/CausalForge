For every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{coarea}}(q,\iota,L)\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), the fixed observable radial Radon--Nikodym versions satisfy, for every \(r>0\),
\[
 P\{T=t,\ 0<\lVert X-x\rVert_2\leq r\}
 =\int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds,
\]
and agree with the corresponding observed-arm circle integrals for Lebesgue-almost every \(s>0\).  Absolute continuity gives \(P(X=x)=0\), so the sign of \(D_{P,x}\) recovers the treatment arm almost surely.  For Lebesgue-almost every \(s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mid D_{P,x}=(2t-1)s]
 =g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)
 =\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}.
\]
If zero lies in the essential support of each arm-specific radial measure and the two separate essential limits satisfy \(\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)=\mu_{t,P}(x)\), then
\[
 \ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{1,P}(x)-\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{0,P}(x)
 =\tau_P(x).
\]
