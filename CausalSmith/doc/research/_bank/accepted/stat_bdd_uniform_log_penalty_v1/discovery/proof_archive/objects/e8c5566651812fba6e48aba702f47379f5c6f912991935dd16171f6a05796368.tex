Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(r>0\).  By the density envelope,
\[
\begin{aligned}
P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}
&=\int_{\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)}f_P(z)\,dz\\
&\leq L\,\operatorname{Leb}_2(B_2(x,r))
=\pi Lr^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
The argument does not require the ball to be contained in the support, since intersecting with \(\mathcal X_P\) only decreases Lebesgue measure.  This proves the assertion uniformly over both classes.
