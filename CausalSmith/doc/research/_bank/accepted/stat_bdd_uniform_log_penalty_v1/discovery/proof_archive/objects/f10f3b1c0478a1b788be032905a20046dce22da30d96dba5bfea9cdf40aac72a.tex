For \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\), the smooth-embedding assumption makes \(\gamma_P\) an injective \(C^1\) parameterization of \(\mathcal B_P\).  The one-dimensional area formula for an injective \(C^1\) curve therefore gives
\[
 \mathcal H^1(\mathcal B_P)=\int_0^1\|\gamma_P'(u)\|_2\,du.
\]
The chart-speed assumption bounds the integrand below by \(L^{-1}\), whereas the \(C^{\iota+2}\)-norm envelope bounds it above by \(L\).  Integration over an interval of length one yields
\[
 L^{-1}\leq\mathcal H^1(\mathcal B_P)\leq L.
\]
