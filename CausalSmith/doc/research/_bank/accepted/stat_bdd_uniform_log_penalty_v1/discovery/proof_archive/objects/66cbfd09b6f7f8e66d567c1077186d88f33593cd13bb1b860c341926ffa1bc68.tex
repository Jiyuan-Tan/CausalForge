Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu give the distance-based identification background, a fixed-law smooth-bias analysis, and an \(n^{-1/4}\) converse over their own nonparametric class, while their discussion predicts a logarithmic uniform penalty.  Those results do not determine the class-uniform frontier under bounded continuous densities without a common angular modulus, nor the order-\(m\) rate under an explicit normalized-coarea remainder.  The remaining internal converse gap is now precisely \(m<q\), not all \(m\neq q\).
