The upper bound uses the same explicit local-constant estimator, now over \(x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\).  For either arm, the stability lemma gives
\(\mathbb E_PA_{t,x}\geq\kappa_0nh^2\), the upper-mass lemma gives \(\mathbb E_PA_{t,x}\leq\pi Lnh^2\), and \(q\geq1\) gives bias at most \(Lh\).  A maximal \(n^{-3}\)-net has polynomial cardinality because the trimmed boundary lies in \([-L,L]^2\); the smooth-boundary length lemma alternatively gives a one-dimensional cover.  The bounded-summand exponential inequality and the conditional sub-Gaussian calculation used for the rectifiable class, followed by the one-Lipschitz kernel extension, yield an event of complement probability at most \(n^{-4}\) on which both empirical denominators are between fixed multiples of \(nh^2\), and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}.
\]
On the exceptional event, a nonempty-denominator local average is bounded by \(\max_i|Y_i|\), whose squared expectation is \(O(\log n)\); the zero-denominator convention handles the remaining boundary case.  Thus the exceptional contribution is negligible.  Taking \(h\asymp a_n\) proves the upper limsup with a single distance-only estimator.

For the lower bound, use the unit-square version of the angular-density packing.  Its interface is a straight \(C^\infty\) embedded segment, its chart has speed one and vanishing higher derivatives, and the segment has infinite reach.  Its centers lie in \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), and the packing proof verifies the density, variance, tail, H\"older, and two-sided-mass envelopes uniformly.  Consequently every vertex belongs to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\) for every \(\iota\geq1\).

Poissonizing at mean \(2n\) makes the marked observations in the disjoint disks conditionally independent standard-Borel point processes, with a bit-independent outside process.  At center \(x_j\), the own-disk signed-radius process is the compression \(S_j\), while all other processes may be revealed.  The packing entropy bound gives
\(\operatorname{KL}(Q_{j,0},Q_{j,1})\leq2\alpha\log M\) with \(2\alpha<1\).  The coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma then makes the probability of at least one bit error at least \(1/2-o(1)\).  Adjacent target values are separated by \(c_1a_n\), so midpoint thresholding turns a sup-norm error smaller than half this amount into exact recovery.  Uniformly subsampling \(n\) points when the Poisson count of mean \(2n\) exceeds \(n\), whose complement has exponentially small probability, proves
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}
 \frac{R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})}{a_n}>0.
\]
Together with the upper bound this proves the theorem.  The hard regressions are disjoint \(q\)-H\"older bump perturbations of affine baselines; smooth boundary geometry by itself does not remove their angular-density confounding.
