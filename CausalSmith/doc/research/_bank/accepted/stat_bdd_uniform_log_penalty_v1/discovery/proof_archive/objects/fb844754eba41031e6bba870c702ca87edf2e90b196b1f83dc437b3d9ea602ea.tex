The result is a full-\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\) converse, not a full-class two-sided frontier.  Rectifiability in the displayed class does not supply the additional uniform Gram or geometric condition used by the published upper theory, so that upper result remains confined to its Assumption~2 subclass.  The proof begins with an arbitrary rule in the inherited common-map class, embeds it into a marked mean-\(2n\) Poisson experiment to obtain independent cell restrictions, applies the finite-\(M\) direct-product bound, and then de-Poissonizes.
