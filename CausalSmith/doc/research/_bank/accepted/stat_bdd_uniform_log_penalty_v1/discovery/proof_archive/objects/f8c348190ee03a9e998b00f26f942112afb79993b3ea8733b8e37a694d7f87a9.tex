Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(r>0\).  By the density envelope and monotonicity of Lebesgue integration,
\[
\begin{aligned}
P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}
&=\int_{\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)}f_P(z)\,dz\\
&\leq L\,\operatorname{Leb}_2\{B_2(x,r)\}
=\pi Lr^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
The calculation is valid for every \(r>0\); no boundary regularity, positivity, or small-radius condition is needed for this upper bound.
