For every integer \(m\geq1\), cutoff constant \(c>0\), bandwidth \(h>0\), law \(P\), and boundary point \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), the rule in \(\mathrm{def:stabilized\text{-}distance\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial}\) is a measurable function of \(U_{n,P}(x)\), and therefore its arm difference is an element of \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\).  Moreover, on the active-cutoff branch,
\[
 \left|\widehat\mu^{(m),\mathrm{LP}}_{t,h,c}(x)\right|
 \leq
 \frac{2\sqrt m}{c\lambda_{\min}(G_m)}h^{-2}
 \left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n|Y_i|\right),
\]
whereas on the failed-cutoff branch the arm intercept is exactly zero.
