For every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\),
\(x\in\mathcal B_P\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), observed-arm coarea gives,
for \(0<r\leq r_0\),
\[
 P\{T=t,\ X\in B_2(x,r)\}
 =\int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds\geq c_-r^2.
\]
Hence \(\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\) accumulates at zero.  For
Lebesgue-almost every \(s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\),
\(g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)=N_{t,P,x}(s)/Q_{t,P,x}(s)\)
is a version of
\(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid D_{P,x}=(2t-1)s]\), and, for every
\(s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\),
\[
 \left|g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)-\mu_{t,P}(x)\right|\leq Ls.
\]
Consequently the separate armwise essential limit exists and satisfies
\[
 \ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)=\mu_{t,P}(x),
 \qquad
 \tau_P(x)=\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{1,P}(x)
 -\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{0,P}(x).
\]
