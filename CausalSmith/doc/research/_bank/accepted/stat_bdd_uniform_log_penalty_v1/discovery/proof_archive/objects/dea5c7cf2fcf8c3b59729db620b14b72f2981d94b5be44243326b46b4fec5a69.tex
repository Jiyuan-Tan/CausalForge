There are constants \(c_0,c_1>0\) and \(0<\alpha<1/8\), depending only on the law-class parameters and \(\delta\), such that for all sufficiently large \(n\) one can construct at least \(c_0a_n^{-1}\) separated centers on the canonical trimmed straight boundary and a corresponding finite family of laws in \(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{rect}}\cap\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{straight}}\) with the following properties. Every boundary is straight, every assignment is the two-half-plane partition, every \(\mu_{t,P}\) in the hard family is affine, every \(f_P\) is continuous and remains between the same fixed density bounds, and the variance, tail, and arm-specific local-mass constants are common. The target curves are separated by at least \(c_1a_n\) in sup norm, while each relevant compressed product law obeys a Kullback--Leibler bound at most \(\alpha\log(c_0a_n^{-1})\) against the packing center. The density perturbations use \(\mathfrak{H}_{\mathrm{ang}}\), vary angular imbalance at radial scale \(a_n\), and preserve the radial mass envelope.
