Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(r>0\).  Membership in either class gives absolute continuity of the score law and the upper density envelope.  Since \(X\) is supported on \(\mathcal X_P\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}
 &=\int_{\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)}f_P(z)\,dz\\
 &\leq L\,\operatorname{Leb}_2\{B_2(x,r)\}
 =\pi Lr^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
The almost-everywhere upper bound is sufficient for the integral inequality.  No lower density bound, density continuity, boundary regularity, or thickness condition is used.
