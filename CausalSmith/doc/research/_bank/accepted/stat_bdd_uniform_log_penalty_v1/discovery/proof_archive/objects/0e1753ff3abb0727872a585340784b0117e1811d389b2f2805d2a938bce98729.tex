Fix \(P,x,t,r\) as in the statement.  By the density envelope and because \(\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)\) is Lebesgue measurable,
\[
 P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}
 =\int_{\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)}f_P(z)\,dz
 \leq L\,\operatorname{Leb}_2(B_2(x,r))=\pi Lr^2.
\]
This argument is uniform over both displayed model classes and uses no regularity of the boundary.
