Suppose zero belongs to the essential support of the observed radial measure \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds\), meaning
\[
 \int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds>0
 \quad\text{for every }r>0.
\]
Define \(\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)\) to be the essential limit of the partial observed regression at zero, relative to that radial measure,
\[
 \ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)
 =\mathop{\operatorname{ess\,lim}}_{s\downarrow0,\ s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}}^{Q_{t,P,x}(s)ds}
 g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s),
\]
whenever there is a unique \(a\in\mathbb R\) such that, for every \(\varepsilon>0\), some \(r>0\) satisfies
\[
 \int_{(0,r)\cap\mathcal S_{t,P,x}}
 \mathbf 1\{|g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)-a|>\varepsilon\}
 Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds=0.
\]
The two treatment arms have separate essential limits; no common radius sequence is part of the definition.
