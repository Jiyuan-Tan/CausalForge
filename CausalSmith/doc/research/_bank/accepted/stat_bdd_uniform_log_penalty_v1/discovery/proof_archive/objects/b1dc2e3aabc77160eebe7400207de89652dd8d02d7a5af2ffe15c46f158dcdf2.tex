For every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{coarea}}(q,\iota,L)\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), polar coarea gives, for every \(r>0\),
\[
 P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}=\int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds.
\]
Absolute continuity gives \(P(X=x)=0\), so the sign of \(D_{P,x}\) recovers the treatment arm almost surely.  For Lebesgue-almost every \(s>0\) with \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)>0\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mid D_{P,x}=(2t-1)s]=\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}.
\]
If, on some \((0,r_*]\), both arm denominators are positive and each ratio converges to \(\mu_{t,P}(x)\) as \(s\downarrow0\), then
\[
 \tau_P(x)=\lim_{s\downarrow0}\left\{\frac{N_{1,P,x}(s)}{Q_{1,P,x}(s)}-\frac{N_{0,P,x}(s)}{Q_{0,P,x}(s)}\right\}.
\]
