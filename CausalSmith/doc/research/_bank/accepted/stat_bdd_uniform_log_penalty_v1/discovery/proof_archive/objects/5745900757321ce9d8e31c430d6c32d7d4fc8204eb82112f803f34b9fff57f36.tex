All constants may depend on the frozen class parameters, \(q\), and \(\delta\), but not on \(n\) or \(P\).  The enlarged smooth class does not enlarge \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\), because its definition explicitly retains density continuity and the original two-sided density envelope.  The straight class is nonempty: take \(X\) uniform on \([-1,1]^2\), the prescribed half-planes and chart, constant regressions of magnitude at most \(1/4\), and conditionally independent two-point potential outcomes in \(\{-1,1\}\) with those means.  Each endpoint arm contains a quarter disk and hence has mass \(\pi r^2/16\geq c_-r^2\); the bounded outcomes satisfy the conditional sub-Gaussian envelope.

For the full-location upper bound, let \(r_q(u)\) be the vector of bivariate monomials of total degree at most \(q-1\), and let \(K_2(u)=(1-\lVert u\rVert_2)_+\).  Define, for each arm and trimmed boundary point,
\[
 \widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)=\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n
 \mathbf1\{T_i=t\}K_2((X_i-x)/h)
 r_q((X_i-x)/h)r_q((X_i-x)/h)^\top,
\]
and define \(\widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\) by replacing the final copy of \(r_q\) by \(r_qY_i\).  Use the intercept of \((\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}})^{-1}\widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}\) when the empirical minimum eigenvalue exceeds half the population lower bound, and zero otherwise.  This is a jointly measurable function of the full sample and known geometry.

For sufficiently small \(h\), the rescaled support in each arm contains a fixed open half-disk.  Hence, for every coefficient vector \(v\),
\[
 v^\top\mathbb E_P\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)v
 \geq L^{-1}\int_{H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)}K_2(u)
 \{v^\top r_q(u)\}^2\,du.
\]
A nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on that open set.  Compactness of the unit sphere in the fixed-dimensional coefficient space supplies a uniform positive minimum eigenvalue.  The conditional sub-Gaussian moment expansion on a polynomial planar grid, extended by kernel Lipschitz continuity, gives stochastic error \(C\sqrt{\log n/(nh^2)}\).  Taylor's formula through total degree \(q-1\) gives bias \(Ch^q\).  The cutoff controls the inverse, and the exceptional event is negligible by the same maximal-outcome and Cauchy--Schwarz argument used for the local-constant estimator.  Therefore
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}.
\]
Balancing at \(h\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2q+2)}\) gives \(Cs_{n,q}\).

For the matching location lower bound, choose \(M\asymp h^{-1}\) trimmed centers with disjoint radius-\(h\) disks.  Keep the uniform density and control regression fixed, and perturb the treated regression in alternative \(j\) by
\[
 \Delta\phi((z-x_j)/h),
 \qquad \Delta=\eta h^q,
\]
where \(\phi\in C_c^\infty(B_2(0,1))\), \(\phi(0)=1\), and \(\eta>0\) is small.  For \(k\leq q\),
\[
 \lVert D^k\{\Delta\phi((\cdot-x_j)/h)\}\rVert_\infty
 \leq C\eta h^{q-k}\leq C\eta,
\]
so all alternatives lie in the H\"older ball.  Use bounded two-point outcomes with the prescribed conditional means.  For probabilities bounded away from zero and one, \(\operatorname{kl}(p,p')\leq C(p-p')^2\), and therefore
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P_j^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq Cn\Delta^2h^2=C\eta^2nh^{2q+2}.
\]
At the displayed bandwidth this is a small multiple of \(\log M\).  If \(J\) is uniform on the \(M\) alternatives, the log-sum inequality gives \(I(J;O_{1:n})\leq\alpha\log M\).  For the decoding error event \(E\), listing the error indicator and then the at most \(M\) possible indices gives
\[
 H(J\mid O_{1:n})\leq\log2+P(E)\log M.
\]
Since \(H(J)=\log M\), the error probability is bounded away from zero.  Sup-norm accuracy below \(\Delta/3\) identifies the unique bumped center, proving
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \geq c\Delta=cs_{n,q}.
\]

Suppose \(L>4\).  The strict signed-distance local-constant estimator, stability, upper mass, and a straight-segment mesh yield
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}\leq Ca_n
\]
at \(h\asymp a_n\).  The canonical-square angular-density family lies in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\).  Its Poissonized disjoint-cell factorization, the coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma, midpoint decoding, and marked subsampling give the reverse bound \(ca_n\).

Suppose \(L=4\).  The support has area four and the explicitly retained lower envelope gives \(f_P\geq1/4\), so
\[
 1=\int_{[-1,1]^2}f_P(z)\,dz
 \geq\int_{[-1,1]^2}\frac14\,dz=1.
\]
Thus \(f_P=1/4\) almost everywhere, and retained continuity makes the equality pointwise.  Every straight-class law belongs to the enlarged \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{coarea}}(q,\iota,L)\), whose density requirement is only absolute continuity, so radial coarea identifies the signed-radius regression.  At every trimmed boundary point and all sufficiently small \(s\), each arm contributes a half-circle and
\[
 Q_{t,P,x}(s)=\frac\pi4s.
\]
Taylor expansion of \(\mu_{t,P}\) through degree \(q-1\), followed by half-circle integration, yields uniformly bounded coefficients with
\[
 \left|\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}-\mu_{t,P}(x)
 -\sum_{k=1}^{q-1}b_{t,k,P}(x)s^k\right|\leq Cs^q.
\]
The one-sided radial Gram matrix is a positive multiple of
\[
 G_q=\int_0^1uK_\triangle(u)v_q(u)v_q(u)^\top\,du,
\]
which is positive definite because a nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on \((0,1)\).  Instantiate the stabilized distance local polynomial with \(m=q\) and lower radial constant \(\pi/4\).  Its admissibility lemma and the same Gram, stochastic-error, and polynomial-reproduction calculation give
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}
 \leq Cs_{n,q}.
\]
Every coordinatewise-distance rule is also a full-location rule after computing its signed distances from the known geometry.  Hence the full-location lower bound applies to this smaller decision class and gives the reverse bound \(cs_{n,q}\).  Finally,
\[
 \frac{q}{2q+2}>\frac14\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q>1,
\]
which proves the stated elbow, including coincidence at \(q=1\) and throughout the no-density-slack case.
