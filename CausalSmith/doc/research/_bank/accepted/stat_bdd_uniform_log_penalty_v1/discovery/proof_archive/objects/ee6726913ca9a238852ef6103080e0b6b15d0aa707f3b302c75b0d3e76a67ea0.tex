For coordinatewise distance releases, the primitive actual-arm two-sided-thickness class has the sharp uniform frontier \((\log n/n)^{1/4}\), and the same rate persists on the endpoint-trimmed smooth-boundary class when bounded score densities have no common angular modulus.  On the identical straight design, the explicitly defined full-location local-polynomial estimator attains \((\log n/n)^{q/(2q+2)}\).  Distance compression remains at the first-order rate when \(L>4\) permits angular density slack, whereas \(L=4\) is a no-slack normalization case forcing the uniform density and restoring the full-location rate.  Faster order-\(m\) distance rates are proved conditionally under an almost-everywhere joint normalized-coarea remainder, with matching converses only for contained bump submodels and \(m\geq q\).  None of these results resolves the same-class conjecture following Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem~6.
