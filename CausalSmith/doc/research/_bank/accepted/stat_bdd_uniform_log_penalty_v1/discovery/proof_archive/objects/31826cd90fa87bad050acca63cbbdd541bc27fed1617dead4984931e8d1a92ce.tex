\[
 \mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{PI}}
 =\left\{\widehat\mu_n^{\mathrm{PI}}:\begin{array}{l}
 \text{there is a jointly Borel measurable map }\mathsf T_n:\mathbb R^2\times
 (\mathbb R\times[0,\infty))^n\to\mathbb R
 \text{ selected independently of }P,\\
 T_{n,x}(v):=\mathsf T_n(x,v),\qquad
 \widehat\mu_{n,P}^{\mathrm{PI}}(x)=T_{n,x}(V_{n,P}(x))
 \text{ for every }P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)
 \text{ and }x\in\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)
 \end{array}\right\}.
\]
For fixed \(P\), the boundary loss is the supremum of the jointly Borel sample--point loss over the compact set \(\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)\); it is upper semianalytic and hence measurable in the universal completion.  All expectations below use that completion.  The point-indexed maps may vary with \(n\) and \(x\), but the jointly measurable family must be fixed independently of \(P\).
