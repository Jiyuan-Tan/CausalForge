For every integer \(p\geq1\), every \(\nu\geq2\), and every \(L\geq L_0(p)\), there is \(c=c(p,\nu,L)>0\) such that
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\left({n\over\log n}\right)^{1/4}R^{12,\pm}_n(p,\nu,L)\geq c.
\]
The conclusion holds for arbitrary law-independent known-geometry point-indexed families with Borel fixed sections, under outer expectation and without joint regularity in the boundary index.
