The contribution is a full-\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\) converse, not a two-sided frontier.  The requested Assumption~2 transfer is formally unavailable: for every set \(A\), \(\operatorname{bd}(A)\cap\operatorname{int}(A)=\varnothing\), while CTY Assumption~2(i) requires an interior assignment boundary and Theorem~6(ii) defines its minimax curve as the support boundary.  Constructing an upper bound under rectifiability alone would therefore be a new theorem on the full class, not a consequence of checking the packing family.  The lower-bound proof uses a marked mean-\(2n\) Poisson experiment for independent cell restrictions and then de-Poissonizes with an exponentially small count event.
