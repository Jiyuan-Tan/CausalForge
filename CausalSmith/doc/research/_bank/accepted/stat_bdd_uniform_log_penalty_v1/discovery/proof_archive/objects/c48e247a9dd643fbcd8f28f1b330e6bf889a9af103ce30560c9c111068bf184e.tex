All constants may depend on the frozen class parameters, \(q\), and \(\delta\), but not on \(n\) or \(P\).  The class is nonempty: take the uniform score law on \([-1,1]^2\), the prescribed half-planes and chart, constant regressions in \((1/4,3/4)\), and independent Gaussian-noised Bernoulli potential outcomes.  The actual observed arms contain quarter disks at endpoints, and the moment-generating calculation in the packing verifies the tail envelope.

We first analyze the named full-location estimator.  A nonzero polynomial of total degree at most \(q-1\) cannot vanish on the open half-ball \(H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)\).  Hence, for every nonzero coefficient vector \(v\),
\[
 v^\top G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}v
 =L^{-1}\int_{H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)}K_2(u)
 \{v^\top r_q(u)\}^2\,du>0.
\]
Compactness of the unit sphere in this fixed-dimensional space proves \(\lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}>0\), so the cutoff is valid.  Finite sums of Borel functions are Borel, the minimum eigenvalue is continuous on symmetric matrices, and inversion is continuous on the positive-definite cone.  The cutoff and zero branch therefore make \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}\) jointly measurable in the full sample and known geometry, hence a member of \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{loc}}\).

For a trimmed \(x\) and sufficiently small \(h\), rescaling the square and using \(f_P\geq L^{-1}\) shows that each arm-specific population Gram matrix dominates \(G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}\).  Conditional sub-Gaussian concentration on a polynomial planar net, extended by the Lipschitz kernel, gives uniformly
\[
 \sup_{t,x}\left(
 \lVert\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)-G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert
 +\lVert\widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)-r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,P,x}(h)\rVert
 \right)
 =O_P\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right).
\]
Taylor's formula through total degree \(q-1\) gives intercept bias \(Ch^q\).  The Rayleigh-quotient perturbation bound activates the cutoff on the good event and uniformly bounds the inverse.  On an active branch the cutoff and bounded monomial vector imply \(|\widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{t,h}(x)|\leq Ch^{-2}\mathbb P_n|Y|\); on a failed branch it is zero.  The sub-Gaussian second moment and a polynomially small exceptional-event probability make the expected exceptional contribution negligible by Cauchy--Schwarz.  Therefore
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
Taking \(h\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2q+2)}\) proves the location upper bound \(Cs_{n,q}\) with the named data-driven witness.

For the location converse choose \(M\asymp h^{-1}\) trimmed centers with disjoint radius-\(h\) disks under the uniform design.  Keep the control regression fixed and add to the treated regression in alternative \(j\)
\[
 \Delta\phi((z-x_j)/h),
 \qquad \Delta=\eta h^q,
\]
where \(\phi\in C_c^\infty(B_2(0,1))\) and \(\phi(0)=1\).  For \(k\leq q\),
\[
 \lVert D^k\{\Delta\phi((\cdot-x_j)/h)\}\rVert_\infty
 \leq C\eta h^{q-k}\leq C\eta,
\]
so small \(\eta\) keeps every alternative in the H\"older ball.  With the Gaussian-noised Bernoulli channel,
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P_j^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq Cn\Delta^2h^2=C\eta^2nh^{2q+2}.
\]
At the displayed bandwidth this is a small multiple of \(\log M\).  The log-sum inequality bounds mutual information by a fixed fraction of \(\log M\), while the elementary entropy bound \(H(J\mid O_{1:n})\leq\log2+P(E)\log M\) keeps center-decoding error away from zero.  Uniform accuracy below \(\Delta/3\) would identify the unique center.  Therefore
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \geq c\Delta=cs_{n,q}.
\]

Suppose \(L>4\).  Actual-arm stability and upper mass, applied to the fixed local-constant distance estimator, give
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}
 \leq Ca_n
\]
at \(h\asymp a_n\).  The canonical-square angular-density family exists because \(L>4\) leaves nonzero density slack around \(1/4\), and its direct-product argument gives the reverse bound \(ca_n\).  This is the strict-slack comparison.

Suppose \(L=4\).  The support has area four and the retained floor is \(1/4\), so
\[
 1=\int_{[-1,1]^2}f_P(z)\,dz
 \geq\int_{[-1,1]^2}\frac14\,dz=1.
\]
Thus \(f_P=1/4\) almost everywhere, and continuity makes equality pointwise.  This is the no-slack normalization case, not an added smoothness premise.  For every trimmed boundary point and Lebesgue-almost every sufficiently small \(s\),
\[
 Q_{t,P,x}(s)=\frac\pi4s.
\]
Taylor expansion of \(\mu_{t,P}\) through degree \(q-1\), followed by half-circle integration, supplies uniformly bounded coefficients such that
\[
 \left|g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)-\mu_{t,P}(x)
 -\sum_{k=1}^{q-1}b_{t,k,P}(x)s^k\right|\leq Cs^q
\]
for Lebesgue-almost every sufficiently small \(s\).  Instantiate the stabilized distance local polynomial with \(m=q\) and lower radial constant \(\pi/4\).  Positive definiteness of its polynomial Gram matrix, the established admissibility lemma, integration of the preceding almost-everywhere bounds, and the same concentration and reproduction calculation give
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}
 \leq Cs_{n,q}.
\]
Because \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\) is a restriction of \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{loc}}\), the location lower bound applies and gives the reverse order.  Finally
\[
 \frac{q}{2q+2}>\frac14\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q>1,
\]
which proves the asserted strict-slack separation and the two coincidence cases without treating \(L=4\) as a smoothness phenomenon.
