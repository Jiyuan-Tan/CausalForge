All constants below depend only on \(q,L,c_-,r_0\).  For an arm \(t\) and boundary point \(x\), write
\[
 W_{i,t,x}(h)=\mathbf1\{(2t-1)D_{P,x,i}>0\}
 K_\triangle(|D_{P,x,i}|/h),
 \qquad
 G_{t,x}(h)=\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^nW_{i,t,x}(h).
\]
The strict sign, absolute value, kernel, finite sums, positive-denominator ratio, and fixed zero branch in the declared local-constant rule are Borel functions of \(U_{n,P}(x)\); hence its arm difference belongs to \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\).  For each fixed \(x\), absolute continuity gives \(P(X=x)=0\), so these weights agree almost surely with the arm weights in \(\Psi^{(0)}_{t,P,x}(h)\).  The stability lemma gives
\[
 \inf_{P,t,x}\mathbb E_PG_{t,x}(h)\geq\kappa_0
\]
for \(h\leq2r_0\), and the upper-mass lemma bounds the probability of a nonzero summand by \(\pi Lh^2\).

Conditional sub-Gaussianity, \(0\leq K_\triangle\leq1\), and that support-probability bound imply, uniformly in \(P,t,x\), Bernstein moment estimates
\[
 \mathbb E_P|W_{i,t,x}(h)\{Y_i-\mu_{t,P}(X_i)\}|^k
 \leq k!C^kh^2,
 \qquad k\geq2,
\]
and the analogous estimates for centered Gram summands.  Exponential Markov optimization yields, for \(0<u\leq1\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &P\!\left(|G_{t,x}(h)-\mathbb E_PG_{t,x}(h)|>u\right)\\
 &\quad+P\!\left(\left|\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^nW_{i,t,x}(h)
 \{Y_i-\mu_{t,P}(X_i)\}\right|>u\right)
 \leq Ce^{-cnh^2u^2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Take a maximal \(\varepsilon_n=n^{-3}\) separated subset of \(\mathcal B_P\).  The interface is relatively closed in the compact support and hence compact.  Because it is contained in \([-L,L]^2\), ambient disk packing bounds the net size by \(C_L\varepsilon_n^{-2}\leq C_Ln^6\), without rectifiability or a length bound.  A union bound over the net and both arms, with \(u=C\sqrt{\log n/(nh^2)}\), gives an event \(E_{n,h}\) such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}}P(E_{n,h}^c)\leq Cn^{-12},
\]
on which every net denominator is at least \(3\kappa_0/4\) and every net centered numerator is at most \(u\).

For a boundary point \(x\) and a net point \(x'\) with \(\lVert x-x'\rVert_2\leq\varepsilon_n\), the strict arm indicators agree for every observation except possibly observations with \(X_i=x\) or \(X_i=x'\).  Absolute continuity makes sample scores pairwise distinct almost surely, so there are at most two exceptional summands.  For all other summands the reverse triangle inequality and the one-Lipschitz property of \(K_\triangle\) give
\[
 |G_{t,x}(h)-G_{t,x'}(h)|
 \leq C\varepsilon_nh^{-3}+\frac2{nh^2}.
\]
The corresponding scaled outcome numerators differ by at most
\[
 C\varepsilon_nh^{-3}\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n|Y_i|\right)
 +\frac{2\max_{i\leq n}|Y_i|}{nh^2}.
\]
The H\"older envelope bounds the regression means, and conditional sub-Gaussianity gives
\[
 \sup_P\mathbb E_PY^2<\infty,
 \qquad
 \sup_P\mathbb E_P\max_{i\leq n}|Y_i|\leq C\sqrt{\log n}.
\]
At \(h\asymp a_n\), all mesh and exceptional-summand terms are \(o(a_n)\).  Hence the net bounds extend to the whole interface and every denominator is at least \(\kappa_0/2\).

On the kernel support, \(q\geq1\) implies
\(|\mu_{t,P}(X_i)-\mu_{t,P}(x)|\leq Lh\).  Dividing the weighted outcome decomposition only on the positive-denominator branch gives
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}|\widehat\mu_{t,h}(x)-\mu_{t,P}(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}
\]
on \(E_{n,h}\).  Off that event, a nonzero arm estimate is a convex combination of observed outcomes, the other branch is zero, and \(\sup_x|\tau_P(x)|\leq2L\).  Since \(\mathbb E_P\max_i|Y_i|^2\leq C\log n\), Cauchy--Schwarz makes the exceptional expected loss negligible.  With \(h\asymp a_n\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}}\mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq Ca_n.
\]
This is one data-driven witness in the declared decision class, so it proves the minimax upper bound.

For the converse, use the hypercube and centers from the angular-density packing.  Poissonize at mean \(2n\) and attach independent continuous ordering marks.  Conditional on independent uniform bits, split the marked Poisson process into the disjoint bump cells \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_M\) and their complement \(Z_0\).  Cell \(j\) depends only on bit \(j\), the complement is bit-independent, and the cells are conditionally independent.  Let \(S_j\) retain from cell \(j\) its outcomes, signed radii about \(x_j\), and marks.  A distance decoder at \(x_j\), even after receiving \(Z_{-j}\) and \(Z_0\), is measurable with respect to \((S_j,Z_{-j},Z_0)\).  Poisson relative entropy equals the intensity times the one-observation relative entropy, and the local calculation in the packing proof gives
\[
 \operatorname{KL}\{\mathcal L(S_j\mid\Omega_j=0),
 \mathcal L(S_j\mid\Omega_j=1)\}
 \leq2\alpha\log M.
\]
Because \(2\alpha<1\), the coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma implies probability at least \(1/2-o(1)\) of at least one bit error.  Midpoint thresholding of the estimated target at each center would recover every bit whenever the sup-norm loss were below \(c_1a_n/2\).  Thus every distance rule in the mean-\(2n\) Poisson experiment has Bayes expected loss at least \(ca_n\).

Given any fixed-\(n\) rule, apply it in that Poisson experiment to the \(n\) observations with smallest marks when the count is at least \(n\), and output zero otherwise.  Conditional on the former event the selected observations are iid from \(P\); exponential Markov optimization gives \(P\{\operatorname{Poi}(2n)<n\}\leq e^{-cn}\).  Since \(\sup_x|\tau_P(x)|\leq2L\),
\[
 ca_n\leq\sup_P\mathbb E_P^{\operatorname{Poi}(2n)}[\text{sup-loss}]
 \leq\sup_P\mathbb E_P^n[\text{sup-loss}]+2Le^{-cn}.
\]
Taking the infimum proves \(R_n^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}})\geq c'a_n\).  Combining the two sides yields the asserted finite positive liminf and limsup.  The lower family is contained in the project-defined class; no inclusion in the distinct class of Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu is used.
