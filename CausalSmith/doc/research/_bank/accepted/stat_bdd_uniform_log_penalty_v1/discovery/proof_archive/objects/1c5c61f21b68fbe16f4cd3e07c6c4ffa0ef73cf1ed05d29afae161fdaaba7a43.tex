Fix \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and write
\(R_x=\lVert X-x\rVert_2\).  On the Borel subsets \(A\) of
\((0,\infty)\), define the observable outcome-weighted signed measure
\[
 \zeta_{t,P,x}(A)=
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[Y\mathbf 1\{T=t,\ R_x\in A\}\right].
\]
Whenever the score law is absolutely continuous and \(Y\) is integrable,
define \(N_{t,P,x}\) to be the measurable Radon--Nikodym density
\(d\zeta_{t,P,x}/ds\), choosing the polar-coarea representative
\[
 N_{t,P,x}(s)=
 \int_{\partial B_2(x,s)\cap\mathcal E_{t,P}}
 \mu_{t,P}(z)f_P(z)\,d\mathcal H^1(z),\qquad s>0.
\]
