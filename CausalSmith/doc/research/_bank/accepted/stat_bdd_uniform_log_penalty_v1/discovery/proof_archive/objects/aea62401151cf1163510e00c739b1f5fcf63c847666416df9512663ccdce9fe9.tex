Fix \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and suppose \(\mathbb E_P|Y|<\infty\).  On the Borel subsets \(A\) of \((0,\infty)\), define the finite signed observable radial measure
\[
 \zeta_{t,P,x}(A)
 =\mathbb E_P\!\left[Y\mathbf 1\{T=t,\ \lVert X-x\rVert_2\in A\}\right].
\]
Consistency, deterministic assignment, and score absolute continuity imply \(\zeta_{t,P,x}\ll ds\).  Define \(N_{t,P,x}:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb R\) to be one fixed finite Borel-measurable Radon--Nikodym version
\[
 N_{t,P,x}(s)=\frac{d\zeta_{t,P,x}}{ds}(s).
\]
For Lebesgue-almost every radial index \(s>0\), this fixed version agrees with the coarea identity
\[
 N_{t,P,x}(s)
 =\int_{\partial B_2(x,s)\cap\mathcal E_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P}
 \mu_{t,P}(z)f_P(z)\,d\mathcal H^1(z).
\]
No equality with the displayed circle integral is stipulated at exceptional radii.
