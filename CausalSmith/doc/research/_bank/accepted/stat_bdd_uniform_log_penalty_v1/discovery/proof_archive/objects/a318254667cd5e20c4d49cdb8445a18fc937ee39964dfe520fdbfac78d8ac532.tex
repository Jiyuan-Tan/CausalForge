Let \(r_q(u)\) be the vector of all bivariate monomials in
\(u=(u_1,u_2)\) of total degree at most \(q-1\), ordered with the constant
monomial first, let \(e_1\) select that coordinate, and put
\(K_2(u)=(1-\lVert u\rVert_2)_+\).  For the two half-planes
\(H_0=\{u:u_2<0\}\) and \(H_1=\{u:u_2\geq0\}\), define
\[
 G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}
 =L^{-1}\int_{H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)}
 K_2(u)r_q(u)r_q(u)^\top\,du,
 \qquad
 \lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}
 =\lambda_{\min}(G^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L})>0.
\]
For \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\),
\(x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(h>0\), set
\[
 \widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)
 =\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf1\{T_i=t\}
 K_2\!\left(\frac{X_i-x}{h}\right)
 r_q\!\left(\frac{X_i-x}{h}\right)
 r_q\!\left(\frac{X_i-x}{h}\right)^\top
\]
and
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)
 =\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf1\{T_i=t\}
 K_2\!\left(\frac{X_i-x}{h}\right)
 r_q\!\left(\frac{X_i-x}{h}\right)Y_i.
\]
Define
\[
 \widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{t,h}(x)
 =\begin{cases}
 e_1^\top\{\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\}^{-1}
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h),
 &\lambda_{\min}\{\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\}
 \geq\lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}/2,\\
 0,&\lambda_{\min}\{\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\}
 <\lambda^{\mathrm{hb}}_{t,q,L}/2,
 \end{cases}
\]
and expose the fitted-intercept difference
\[
 \widehat\tau^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}(x)
 =\widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{1,h}(x)
 -\widehat\mu^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{0,h}(x).
\]
The eigenvalue cutoff, inverse branch, and zero branch make this a jointly
measurable function of the full sample and the known straight assignment geometry.
