For every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
 P\{T=t,\ \lVert X-x\rVert_2\leq r\}
 =\int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds
 \geq c_-r^2,
 \qquad 0<r\leq r_0.
\]
Thus zero belongs to the essential support of each observed arm-specific radial measure.  For Lebesgue-almost every \(s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\),
\[
 g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)
 =\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}
 =\mathbb E_P[Y\mid D_{P,x}=(2t-1)s].
\]
Moreover,
\[
 |g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)-\mu_{t,P}(x)|\leq Ls
\]
for \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds\)-almost every sufficiently small \(s\).  Consequently the separate essential arm limit exists and satisfies
\[
 \ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P}(x)=\mu_{t,P}(x),
 \qquad
 \ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{1,P}(x)-\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{0,P}(x)=\tau_P(x).
\]
No value at \(s=0\) or at a zero-denominator radius, and no radius sequence common to the two arms, is used.
