Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\).  The local-constant upper-bound proof applies on \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\).  The stability lemma gives a uniform denominator lower bound, the upper-mass lemma gives kernel-window probability at most \(\pi Lh^2\), and \(q\geq1\) gives bias at most \(Lh\).  The trimmed boundary is compact and contained in \([-L,L]^2\), so an ambient \(n^{-4}\)-net has polynomial cardinality uniformly over the class.  The conditional moment-series calculation, exponential Markov inequality, and a union bound give probability at least \(1-Cn^{-A_0}\) that all net denominators are bounded below and all centered numerators and denominators are at most
\[
 C\left\{\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]

An injective \(C^{\iota+2}\) chart is a \(C^1\) image of a compact interval and therefore has planar Lebesgue measure zero.  Score absolute continuity implies that almost surely no sample score lies on \(\mathcal B_P\).  Along the boundary the strict arm indicators are consequently constant, while the reverse triangle inequality and Lipschitz continuity of \(K_\triangle\) give normalized denominator oscillation \(Cn^{-4}h^{-3}\) and outcome-numerator oscillation
\[
 Cn^{-4}h^{-3}\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n|Y_i|\right).
\]
At \(h\asymp a_n\) these terms are negligible.  Thus, on the good event,
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
On its complement, the convex-combination and zero-branch structure, together with \(\mathbb E_P\max_i|Y_i|^2\leq C\log n\), makes the expected contribution negligible by Cauchy--Schwarz.  Balancing at \(h\asymp a_n\) proves
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})
 \leq C_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}a_n.
\]
The proof uses no density floor or continuity, no common chart length, speed, reach, or derivative bound, and no conditional-variance envelope.

For the lower bound, use the straight-interface family in the angular-density packing.  Its affine chart is injective and \(C^\infty\), its centers lie in \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), and the packing proof verifies compact support, an absolutely continuous density obeying the upper envelope, the H\"older regression envelope, common tails, and two-sided thickness.  In fact its densities satisfy the stronger continuity and two-sided envelope.  Thus every vertex lies in the enlarged \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\).  Poissonization at mean \(2n\), independent restrictions to the disjoint bump cells, the coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma, midpoint decoding, and marked subsampling give
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})
 \geq c_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}a_n.
\]
Dividing the two bounds by \(a_n\) proves the displayed liminf and limsup.  Thus smooth assignment geometry alone does not improve the coordinatewise-distance exponent even after the ambient score-density class is enlarged to bounded densities without continuity or a floor.
