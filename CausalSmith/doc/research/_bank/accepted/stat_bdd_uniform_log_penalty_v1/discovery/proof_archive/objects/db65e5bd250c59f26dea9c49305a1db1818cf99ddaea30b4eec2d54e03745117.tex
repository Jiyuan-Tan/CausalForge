Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\cup\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(r>0\).  Membership in either revised class supplies a Lebesgue density by the score-absolute-continuity assumption and the almost-everywhere ceiling \(f_P\leq L\) by the density-upper-envelope assumption.  Since \(X\) is supported on \(\mathcal X_P\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,r)\}
 &=\int_{\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\cap B_2(x,r)}f_P(z)\,dz\\
 &\leq L\,\operatorname{Leb}_2\{B_2(x,r)\}
 =\pi Lr^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
The inequality is unchanged by modifications of the density on a Lebesgue-null set.  It holds for every \(r>0\) and uses neither density continuity, a density floor, boundary regularity, nor the lower-thickness condition.
