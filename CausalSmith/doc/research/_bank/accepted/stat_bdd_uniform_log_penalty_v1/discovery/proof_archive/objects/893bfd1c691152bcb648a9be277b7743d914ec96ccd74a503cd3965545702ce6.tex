Nonparametric regression with a bivariate random-design covariate \(X\), compact support \(\mathcal X_P\subseteq[-L,L]^2\), and rectifiable support boundary; the target is the single observed-outcome regression \(\mu_P\) restricted to that boundary.  The lower-bound experiment uses an independently and identically distributed hypercube family and a proof-only marked-Poisson embedding, followed by de-Poissonization back to the fixed sample size \(n\).
