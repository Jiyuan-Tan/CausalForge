For every integer \(p\geq1\) and every \(\nu>2\), there is a finite \(L_0=L_0(p)\) such that, for every \(L\geq L_0\), there are \(0<c\leq C<\infty\) with
\[
 c\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}{R^{12,\pm}_n(p,\nu,L)\over a_n}
 \leq\limsup_{n\to\infty}{R^{12,\pm}_n(p,\nu,L)\over a_n}\leq C.
\]
Thus the outer-expected minimax frontier over the same exact uniformized CTY Assumptions~1--2 class is \((\log n/n)^{1/4}\).  The upper bound is attained by the explicit stabilized signed-distance local-polynomial estimator, while the lower bound holds over the larger class of all law-independent known-geometry point-indexed Borel sections.
