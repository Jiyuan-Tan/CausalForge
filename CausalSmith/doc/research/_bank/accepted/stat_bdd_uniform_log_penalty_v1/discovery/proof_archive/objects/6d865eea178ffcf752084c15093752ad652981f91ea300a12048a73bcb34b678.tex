Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\). For every \(q\geq1\) and \(\iota\geq1\), there exist constants \(0<c_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}\leq C_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}<\infty\), depending only on \(q,\iota,L,c_{-},r_0,\rho_0,\delta\), such that \[c_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{sm}})}{a_n}\leq\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{sm}})}{a_n}\leq C_{\mathrm{sm},\delta}.\] The upper inequality is attained by \(\widehat{\tau}^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}\) with \(h\asymp a_n\) and uses only the first-order continuity implied by \(q\geq1\), arm-specific local mass, and the fixed triangular kernel. The lower inequality remains valid on the canonical straight-boundary hard family in \(\mathrm{lem:angular\text{-}density\text{-}packing}\), whose regressions are affine and whose score densities are bounded and continuous with no common modulus.
