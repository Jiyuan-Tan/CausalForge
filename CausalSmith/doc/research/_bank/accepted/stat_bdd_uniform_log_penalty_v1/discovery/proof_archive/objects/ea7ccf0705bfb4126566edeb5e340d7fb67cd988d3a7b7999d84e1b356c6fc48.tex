Set \(\Delta_n=\gamma(log n/n)^{1/4}\) in the hard-family lemma, with \(\gamma>0\) fixed and sufficiently small.  Then \(M_n\asymp\Delta_n^{-1/(p+1)}\to\infty\), while every adjacent compressed-product divergence is at most
\[
 Cn\Delta_n^4=C\gamma^4\log n\leq\kappa\log M_n
\]
for some fixed \(\kappa<1\).  For an arbitrary rule in \(\mathcal T^{12,\pm}_n\), decode \(\omega_j\) by choosing the target value nearest to \(\widehat\tau_{n,P_\omega}(x_j)\).  The hard-family cell labels have a bit-independent multinomial law; conditional on those labels the cells are independent and cell \(j\) depends only on \(\omega_j\).  The observation supplied to decoder \(j\) is a measurable compression of its cell together with bit-independent or other-coordinate cells.  The coordinatewise overlap direct-product lemma therefore yields
\[
 {1\over2^{M_n}}\sum_\omega P_\omega\{\text{some decoding error}\}
 \geq\tfrac12-o(1).
\]
If a decoding error occurs, the finite packing maximum of estimation errors is at least \(\Delta_n/2\).  This finite maximum is measurable because every fixed-point section is Borel, even though the full boundary supremum need not be.  Pointwise it is bounded by that supremum, and outer expectation is monotone.  Hence
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{12}}\mathbb E_P^*\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}|\widehat\tau_{n,P}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \geq {\Delta_n\over2}(\tfrac12-o(1)).
\]
Taking the infimum over all rules and the lower limit proves the theorem.
