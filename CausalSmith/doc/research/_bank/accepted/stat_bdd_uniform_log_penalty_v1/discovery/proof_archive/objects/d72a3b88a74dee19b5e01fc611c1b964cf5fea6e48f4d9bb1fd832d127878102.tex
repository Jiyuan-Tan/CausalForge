For the degree-\(p\) signed-distance local-polynomial score and Gram processes with the fixed compactly supported kernel, the expected uniform deviations over \(P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\), both arms, and \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) satisfy
\[
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{t,x}\|\widehat\Psi_{t,x}(h)-\Psi_{t,P,x}(h)\|
 \leq C\sqrt{\log(1/h)\over nh^2},
\]
and
\[
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{t,x}\|\widehat O_{t,x}(h)\|
 \leq C\left\{\sqrt{\log(1/h)\over nh^2}+{\log(1/h)\over n^{(1+\nu)/(2+\nu)}h^2}\right\},
\]
whenever \(h\downarrow0\) and \(n^{(1+\nu)/(2+\nu)}h^2/\log(1/h)\to\infty\); \(C\) depends only on the uniform class constants and \(p\).
