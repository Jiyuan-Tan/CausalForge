Fix \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\).  On the nonempty canonical class
\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\),
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \asymp s_{n,q}.
\]
The location upper bound is attained by the exposed estimator
\(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{loc},\mathrm{LP}}_{n,h}\) with
\(h\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2q+2)}\).  If \(L>4\), then
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \asymp a_n,
\]
and the distance lower bound is direct from the canonical-square version of
\(\mathrm{lem:angular\text{-}density\text{-}packing}\).  If \(L=4\),
then the density envelope and the fixed square support force
\(f_P\equiv1/4\), and
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \asymp s_{n,q}.
\]
Consequently the distance and location exponents are strictly separated for
\(q>1\) when \(L>4\), while they coincide when \(q=1\) and also throughout
the no-density-slack normalization case \(L=4\).
