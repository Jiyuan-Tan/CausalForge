Fix \(p\geq1\), \(\nu\geq2\), and \(L\geq L_0(p)\), and write \(q=p+1\).  Choose
\[
 \Delta_n=\gamma a_n=\gamma\left({\log n\over n}\right)^{1/4},
 \qquad w_n=A\Delta_n^{1/q},
\]
where \(\gamma>0\) will be fixed sufficiently small.  Apply \(\mathrm{lem:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}rectangle\text{-}angular\text{-}hypercube}\).  It gives \(M=M_n\asymp w_n^{-1}\to\infty\), common cell probability \(\rho_n=\pi w_n^2/36\), and adjacent conditional cell divergence at most \(C_0\Delta_n^4/w_n^2\).

Put independent uniform bits \(\Omega_1,\ldots,\Omega_M\) on the vertices.  For the exactly \(n\) independent observations, let \(J_i\in\{0,1,\ldots,M\}\) record whether \(X_i\) lies outside all disks or in disk \(S_j\).  The entire label vector has a distribution independent of the bits, by the common cell-mass and locality properties.  Conditional on the labels, let \(N_j=\sum_i\mathbf 1\{J_i=j\}\).  The observations in the labeled blocks are independent; block \(j\) depends only on \(\Omega_j\), and block zero is bit-independent.

We verify the simultaneous good-count step before applying any testing inequality.  Marginally, \(N_j\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,\rho_n)\).  The multiplicative Chernoff inequality at factor two gives
\[
 \Pr\{N_j>2n\rho_n\}\leq(e/4)^{n\rho_n}\leq\exp(-n\rho_n/3).
\]
Consequently, for
\[
 G_n=\left\{\max_{1\leq j\leq M_n}N_j\leq2n\rho_n\right\},
\]
the union bound yields
\[
 \Pr(G_n^c)\leq M_n\exp(-n\rho_n/3).
\]
Now
\[
 n\rho_n\asymp n\Delta_n^{2/q}
 =n^{1-1/(2q)}(\log n)^{1/(2q)},
 \qquad \log M_n=O(\log n).
\]
Since \(q\geq2\), \(n\rho_n/\log M_n\to\infty\), and hence \(\Pr(G_n)\to1\).

Fix a realization of the labels in \(G_n\).  For decoder \(j\), let \(S_j\) be its own cell's ordered outcome--signed-distance block, and reveal the complete raw blocks \(Z_{-j}\) and \(Z_0\) outside cell \(j\).  These revealed blocks reconstruct every off-cell signed distance to \(x_j\), while \(S_j\) supplies the in-cell part.  Therefore the fixed Borel section \(T_{n,G,x_j}\), and its midpoint bit decoder, are measurable with respect to \((S_j,Z_{-j},Z_0)\).  This is a coordinatewise statement and uses no joint regularity in \(x\).

Conditional tensorization and the hard-family KL calculation give, in both orientations,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{KL}\{\mathcal L(S_j\mid\Omega_j=0,J_{1:n}),
                      \mathcal L(S_j\mid\Omega_j=1,J_{1:n})\}
 &\leq N_jC_0{\Delta_n^4\over w_n^2}\\
 &\leq2n\rho_nC_0{\Delta_n^4\over w_n^2}
 =C_1n\Delta_n^4.
\end{aligned}
\]
Because
\[
 C_1n\Delta_n^4=C_1\gamma^4\log n,
 \qquad
 \log M_n={1\over4q}\log n+O(\log\log n),
\]
we may choose \(\gamma\) so small that, for one fixed \(\kappa<1\), the last divergence is at most \(\kappa\log M_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\).

Apply \(\mathrm{lem:coordinatewise\text{-}overlap\text{-}direct\text{-}product}\) conditionally on every label realization in \(G_n\).  It yields
\[
 \Pr\{\text{some coordinate decoder errs}\mid J_{1:n}\}
 \geq {1\over2}\left[1-\exp\{-M_n^{1-\kappa}/2\}\right].
\]
Averaging over the labels and using \(\Pr(G_n)\to1\) and \(M_n\to\infty\) gives
\[
 2^{-M_n}\sum_\omega P_\omega\{\text{some decoder errs}\}\geq {1\over2}-o(1).
\]

At \(x_j\), decode by selecting the nearer of the two target values.  If this decoder errs, then
\[
 \max_{1\leq j\leq M_n}|\widehat\tau_{n,P_\omega}(x_j)-\tau_{P_\omega}(x_j)|\geq{\Delta_n\over2}.
\]
The finite maximum is measurable because every fixed-point section is Borel.  It is pointwise no larger than the possibly nonmeasurable full boundary supremum.  Monotonicity in the definition of outer expectation therefore gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)}
 \mathbb E_P^*\!\left[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}|\widehat\tau_{n,P}(x)-\tau_P(x)|\right]
 \geq {\Delta_n\over2}\left({1\over2}-o(1)\right).
\]
The rule was arbitrary.  Taking its infimum, dividing by \(a_n\), and taking the lower limit proves the theorem with a positive constant proportional to \(\gamma\).
