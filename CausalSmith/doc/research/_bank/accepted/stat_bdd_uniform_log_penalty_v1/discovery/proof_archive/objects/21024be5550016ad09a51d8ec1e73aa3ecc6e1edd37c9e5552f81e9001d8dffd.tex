For \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), define the partial observed exact-distance regression only on \(\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\) by
\[
 g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)
 =\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)},
 \qquad s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}.
\]
No value is defined at \(s=0\) or at a radius for which \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)=0\).
