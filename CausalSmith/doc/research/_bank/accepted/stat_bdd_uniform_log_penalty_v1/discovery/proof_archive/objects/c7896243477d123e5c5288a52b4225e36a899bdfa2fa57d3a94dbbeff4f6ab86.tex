We first prove achievability.  Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\), an arm \(t\), a boundary point \(x\), and \(0<h\leq r_0\).  Put \(R_x=\lVert X-x\rVert_2\) and
\[
 W_{t,x,h}=h^{-2}\mathbf1\{T=t\}K_\triangle(R_x/h).
\]
The stability lemma gives \(d_{t,P,x}(h)=\mathbb E_PW_{t,x,h}\geq\kappa_0\).  The observed-arm upper-mass lemma, \(0\leq K_\triangle\leq1\), consistency, the H\"older-ball supremum bound, and conditional sub-Gaussianity imply
\[
 \mathbb E_PW_{t,x,h}^2\leq\pi Lh^{-2},
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P[\{W_{t,x,h}Y\}^2]\leq Ch^{-2}.
\]
Conditioning on \(X\), for every integer \(k\geq2\), also gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P|W_{t,x,h}\{Y-\mu_{t,P}(X)\}|^k
 \leq k!C^kh^{2-2k}.
\]
Expanding the exponential series and applying exponential Markov inequality yields, for a centered numerator or denominator summand \(Z_{t,x,h}\),
\[
 P\!\left(\left|(\mathbb P_n-P)Z_{t,x,h}\right|>u\right)
 \leq2\exp\!\left\{-\frac{cnu^2}{h^{-2}+h^{-2}u}\right\}.
\]

Cover \(\mathcal B_P\subseteq[-L,L]^2\) by an ambient \(\epsilon_n\)-net with \(\epsilon_n=n^{-4}\); its cardinality is at most \(C_Ln^8\), without any geometric regularity of the interface.  For a fixed observation, \(\mathbf1\{T_i=t\}\) is independent of \(x\), while \(x\mapsto K_\triangle(\lVert X_i-x\rVert_2/h)\) is \(h^{-1}\)-Lipschitz.  Normalized denominator and numerator oscillations are therefore at most
\[
 C\epsilon_nh^{-3},
 \qquad C\epsilon_nh^{-3}\mathbb P_n|Y|,
\]
with analogous population bounds.  The strict-sign formula in the declared estimator differs from the observed-arm formula only at \(x=X_i\).  Absolute continuity makes sample scores distinct almost surely, so uniformly over \(x\) this changes a normalized denominator by at most \((nh^2)^{-1}\) and a numerator by at most \((nh^2)^{-1}\max_i|Y_i|\).  The tail and regression envelopes give
\[
 \sup_P\mathbb E_P\max_{i\leq n}|Y_i|^2\leq C\log n,
 \qquad
 \sup_P\mathbb E_P(\mathbb P_n|Y|)^2\leq C.
\]
A union bound over the net and the oscillation estimates shows that, for every fixed \(A_0>0\), with probability at least \(1-Cn^{-A_0}\),
\[
 \sup_{t,x}|\mathbb P_nW_{t,x,h}-d_{t,P,x}(h)|
 +\sup_{t,x}|\mathbb P_n(W_{t,x,h}Y)-\mathbb E_P(W_{t,x,h}Y)|
 \leq C\left\{\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}
\]
when \(h\asymp a_n\).

For large \(n\), the right side is below \(\kappa_0/2\).  All empirical denominators are positive and bounded below.  By consistency, the population ratio is a kernel-weighted average of \(\mu_{t,P}(z)\) over the observed arm within distance \(h\), so its bias from \(\mu_{t,P}(x)\) is at most \(Lh\).  The ratio identity
\[
 \frac{\widehat r}{\widehat d}-\frac rd
 =\frac{\widehat r-r}{\widehat d}
 +\frac rd\frac{d-\widehat d}{\widehat d}
\]
then gives
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}+\frac{\log n}{nh^2}\right\}.
\]
The canonical observed-distance lemma separately identifies the two essential arm limits with the causal regression values.

On the exceptional event, each arm estimate is zero or a convex combination of outcomes, so the loss is at most \(2\max_i|Y_i|+2L\).  Cauchy--Schwarz and sufficiently large \(A_0\) make its expected contribution negligible.  With \(h\asymp a_n\), all three displayed terms are at most \(Ca_n\).  The estimator is a single measurable member of \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\), proving the upper bound.

For the converse, use the actual-arm hypercube from the angular-density packing.  Poissonize at mean \(2n\), attach independent continuous marks, and partition the process into the disjoint bump cells and their complement.  Conditional on the bits, the restrictions are independent; the complement is bit independent; and cell \(j\) depends only on bit \(j\).  Midpoint thresholding \(\widehat\tau_n(x_j)\) gives decoder \(j\), whose local cell is observed only through \(U_{n,P}(x_j)\), with other cells and the complement ancillary.  Poisson tensorization and the packing slack give divergence at most \(2\alpha\log M_n\), where \(2\alpha<1\).  The coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma yields
\[
 P\{\text{at least one bit is decoded incorrectly}\}\geq\frac12-o(1).
\]
Loss below \(c_1a_n/2\) would decode every bit, so the Poissonized expected loss is at least \(ca_n\).  If a fixed-\(n\) rule had smaller risk, applying it to the \(n\) observations with the smallest independent marks whenever the Poisson count is at least \(n\), and returning zero otherwise, would contradict this Poisson lower bound; the failure probability \(P\{\operatorname{Pois}(2n)<n\}\) is exponentially small and the zero-branch target loss is bounded by \(2L\).  Thus
\[
 R_n^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}})\geq c_{\mathrm{rect}}a_n.
\]
Division by \(a_n\) and passage to \(\liminf\) and \(\limsup\) prove the theorem.  The hard family lies in this project-defined class; no inclusion in the class of Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu, Theorem~6, is used.
