The upper bound uses the same explicit local-constant estimator as the rectifiable theorem, restricted to \(x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\).  The stability lemma gives a population denominator at least \(\kappa_0h^2\), the upper-mass lemma gives an upper bound \(\pi Lh^2\), and the H\"older assumption with \(q\geq1\) gives bias at most \(Lh\).  A maximal \(n^{-3}\)-net of the trimmed boundary has polynomial cardinality because it lies in \([-L,L]^2\); equivalently, the smooth-boundary length lemma supplies a one-dimensional cover.  Bounded-weight Bernstein concentration, conditional sub-Gaussian concentration, and the one-Lipschitz kernel extension therefore give, uniformly over the smooth class,
\[
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}}
 |\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{LC}}_{n,h}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}.
\]
The divisions are valid on the event where both empirical denominators exceed \(\kappa_0nh^2/2\); its complement has probability at most \(n^{-4}\), and conditional sub-Gaussianity makes its expected contribution negligible.  Taking \(h\asymp a_n\) proves the upper limsup.

For the lower bound, use the unit-square version of \(\mathrm{lem:angular\text{-}density\text{-}packing}\).  Its interface is a straight \(C^\infty\) embedded segment, its chart has speed one and vanishing higher derivatives, and its image is convex and hence has infinite reach.  Its centers lie in \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), and the packing proof verifies the common density, variance, tail, H\"older, and two-sided local-mass envelopes.  Thus every vertex belongs to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\) for every \(\iota\geq1\).

Poissonizing the disjoint disks at mean \(2n\) gives conditionally independent standard-Borel local point processes and a bit-independent outside process.  Decoder \(j\)'s own-center signed-radius process is its compression \(S_j\), while all other processes may be revealed.  The packing's Kullback--Leibler slack gives \(\operatorname{KL}(Q_{j,0},Q_{j,1})\leq\kappa\log M\) with \(\kappa<1\).  The coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma implies probability at least \(1/2-o(1)\) of at least one bit error.  Since adjacent target values are separated by \(c_1a_n\), midpoint thresholding converts sup-norm error below half this amount into exact recovery.  Uniform subsampling on the event that the Poisson count exceeds \(n\), whose complement is exponentially unlikely, gives
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}})}{a_n}>0.
\]
Together with the upper bound this proves the theorem.  The hard regressions are disjoint \(q\)-H\"older bump perturbations of affine baselines, and only adjacent vertices at the attached center have equal arm-specific radial mass densities; no global pairwise radial-equality claim is used.
