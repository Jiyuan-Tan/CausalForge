All coordinates in the construction are Borel functions of the pairs \((Y_i,D_{P,x,i})\): absolute value and the strict sign indicators are Borel, \(K_\triangle\) and \(v_m\) are continuous, finite sums and products are Borel, the minimum eigenvalue is continuous on the symmetric matrices, and matrix inversion is continuous on the invertible matrices.  The active branch is contained in the set on which the minimum eigenvalue is at least \(c\lambda_{\min}(G_m)/2>0\); the other branch is assigned the fixed value zero.  Thus the piecewise formula is Borel.  It uses only \(U_{n,P}(x)\), including the declared convention when \(D_{P,x,i}=0\), so the definition of \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\) gives admissibility.

On the active branch, the spectral cutoff gives
\[
 \left\|\{\widehat G^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)\}^{-1}\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq\frac{2}{c\lambda_{\min}(G_m)}.
\]
Because \(0\leq K_\triangle\leq1\) and \(\|v_m(u)\|_2\leq\sqrt m\) on \(0\leq u\leq1\),
\[
 \left\|\widehat r^{(m)}_{t,x}(h)\right\|_2
 \leq\frac{\sqrt m}{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n|Y_i|.
\]
Multiplication by \(e_1^\top\), whose Euclidean norm is one, proves the displayed bound.  The failed-cutoff assertion is the zero convention in the construction.
