For integers \(p\geq1\), real \(\nu\geq2\), and \(L\geq4\), let \(\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)\) be the set of laws \(P\) satisfying all of the following conditions.  The observations \((Y_i(0),Y_i(1),X_i)_{i=1}^n\) are independently and identically distributed for every \(n\), treatment is \(T=\mathbf1\{X\in\mathcal A_{1,P}\}\), and \(Y=TY(1)+(1-T)Y(0)\).  The support \(\mathcal X_P\) is a compact rectangle contained in \([-L,L]^2\); \(X\) has a continuous Lebesgue density with \(L^{-1}\leq f_P\leq L\) on that support.  For each \(t\), \(\mu_{t,P}\) has a \(C^{p+1}\) extension to an open neighborhood of the support and
\[
 \max_{|\alpha|\leq p}\|\partial^\alpha\mu_{t,P}\|_\infty+
 \max_{|\alpha|=p}\sup_{x\neq z}{|\partial^\alpha\mu_{t,P}(x)-\partial^\alpha\mu_{t,P}(z)|\over\|x-z\|_2}\leq L.
\]
The conditional variance is continuous and lies in \([L^{-1},L]\), and
\[
 \mathbb E_P[|Y(t)-\mu_{t,P}(X)|^{2+\nu}\mid X]\leq L
\]
almost surely.  The known interface \(\mathcal B_P\subset\operatorname{int}(\mathcal X_P)\) is rectifiable with \(L^{-1}\leq\mathcal H^1(\mathcal B_P)\leq L\); the known metric obeys
\[
 L^{-1}\|x-z\|_2\leq d_P(x,z)\leq L\|x-z\|_2.
\]
Put \(r_p(u)=(1,u,\ldots,u^p)^\top\), \(I_1=[0,\infty)\), \(I_0=(-\infty,0)\), and
\[
 \Psi_{t,P,x}(h)=\mathbb E_P\!left[{1\over h^2}\mathbf1\{D^{\pm}_{P,x}\in I_t\}K_\square(D^{\pm}_{P,x}/h)r_p(D^{\pm}_{P,x}/h)r_p(D^{\pm}_{P,x}/h)^\top\right].
\]
For every \(0<h\leq L^{-1}\), every \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), and both arms,
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(\Psi_{t,P,x}(h))\geq L^{-1},\qquad
 {1\over h^2}\int_{\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P}K_\square((2t-1)d_P(z,x)/h)\,dz\geq L^{-1}.
\]
Finally, for every \(0<s\leq L^{-1}\), the one-dimensional Hausdorff integral of \(f_P\) over \(\{z\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P:d_P(z,x)=s\}\) is finite and strictly positive.  These are the uniform-envelope versions of Cattaneo--Titiunik--Yu Assumptions~1--2 and Theorem~2's envelope conditions, with the uniform kernel fixed and no common smoothness modulus imposed on \(f_P\).
