The logarithm is an information-loss phenomenon for simultaneous boundary recovery from coordinatewise outcome--distance samples: many disjoint angular perturbations remain individually hard after radial compression, so at least one boundary coordinate fails with probability bounded away from zero.  The common map \(T_n\) is inherited from the quantifiers and estimator formula printed in CTY Theorem~6, not imposed as an extra restriction here.  The square witness does not turn the published upper theorem into a matching full-class result, because a nonempty support boundary cannot be a subset of its support's interior.
