Take any \(\widehat\mu_n\in\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\), generated by the common law-independent Borel map \(T_n\) from \(\mathrm{def:cty\text{-}distance\text{-}decision\text{-}class}\).  Define
\[
 \mathsf T_n(x,v)=T_n(v),
 \qquad (x,v)\in\mathbb R^2\times(\mathbb R\times[0,\infty))^n.
\]
This map is jointly Borel, is selected independently of \(P\), and its section at every \(x\) reproduces the original estimator.  Hence
\[
 \mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\subseteq\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{PI}}.
\]
The definitions of the two risks use the same law class and loss.  Taking an infimum over the smaller decision class therefore gives
\[
 R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\geq R_n^{\mathrm{PI}}.
\]
By \(\mathrm{thm:point\text{-}indexed\text{-}distance\text{-}log\text{-}converse}\),
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}}{a_n}
 \geq
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{PI}}}{a_n}
 \geq c_{\mathrm{PI}}>0.
\]
Using \(a_n=(\log n/n)^{1/4}\) and the displayed definition of \(R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\) proves the stated equality and lower bound with \(c_{\mathrm{CTY}}=c_{\mathrm{PI}}\).  Thus the Cattaneo--Titiunik--Yu common-map conclusion is an immediate corollary of the stronger point-indexed result.
