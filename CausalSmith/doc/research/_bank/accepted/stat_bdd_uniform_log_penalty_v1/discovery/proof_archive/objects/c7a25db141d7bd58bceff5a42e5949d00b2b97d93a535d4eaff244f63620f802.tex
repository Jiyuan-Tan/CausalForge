The converse \(\mathrm{thm:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}point\text{-}indexed\text{-}log\text{-}converse}\) gives the positive lower limit.  The explicit stabilized estimator is one member of \(\mathcal T_n^{12,\pm}\), so the minimax risk is no larger than its worst-law outer risk.  The upper proposition therefore gives
\[
 R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)\leq Cb_{n,\nu}.
\]
When \(\nu>2\),
\[
 {1+\nu\over3(2+\nu)}>{1\over4},
\]
and hence
\[
 \left\{{\log n\over n^{(1+\nu)/(2+\nu)}}\right\}^{1/3}=o(a_n).
\]
It follows that \(b_{n,\nu}/a_n\to1\).  Dividing the upper inequality by \(a_n\), taking the upper limit, and combining it with the converse proves the matched display.
