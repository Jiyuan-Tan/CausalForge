All constants may depend on the frozen class parameters, \(q\), and \(\delta\), but not on \(n\) or \(P\).  The class is nonempty: take \(X\) uniform on \([-1,1]^2\), the prescribed half-planes and chart, constant regressions in \((1/4,3/4)\), and Gaussian-noised Bernoulli potential outcomes.  The density is \(1/4\), each endpoint arm contains a quarter disk and hence has mass \(\pi r^2/16\geq c_-r^2\), and Hoeffding's bound times the Gaussian moment-generating identity verifies the tail envelope.  This example also has conditional variances in \([1,5/4]\), although no variance-envelope premise is needed for membership.

For the full-location upper bound, let \(r_q(u)\) be the vector of bivariate monomials of total degree at most \(q-1\), and let \(K_2(u)=(1-\lVert u\rVert_2)_+\).  Define, for each arm and trimmed boundary point,
\[
 \widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)=\frac1{nh^2}\sum_{i=1}^n
 \mathbf1\{T_i=t\}K_2((X_i-x)/h)
 r_q((X_i-x)/h)r_q((X_i-x)/h)^\top
\]
and define \(\widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)\) by replacing the final copy of \(r_q\) by \(r_qY_i\).  Use the intercept of \((\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}})^{-1}\widehat r^{\mathrm{loc}}\) when the empirical minimum eigenvalue exceeds half the population lower bound, and zero otherwise.  This is an explicit jointly measurable function of the full sample and known geometry.

For sufficiently small \(h\), the rescaled support in each arm contains a fixed open half-disk.  Hence, for every coefficient vector \(v\),
\[
 v^\top\mathbb E_P\widehat G^{\mathrm{loc}}_{t,x}(h)v
 \geq L^{-1}\int_{H_t\cap B_2(0,1/2)}K_2(u)
 \{v^\top r_q(u)\}^2\,du.
\]
A nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on that open set.  Compactness of the unit sphere in the fixed-dimensional coefficient space therefore supplies a uniform positive minimum eigenvalue.  Conditional sub-Gaussian Bernstein bounds on a polynomial-size grid, extended by kernel Lipschitz continuity, give stochastic error
\[
 C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}.
\]
Taylor's formula through total degree \(q-1\) gives bias \(Ch^q\).  The cutoff controls the inverse, and its exceptional event is negligible by the conditional sub-Gaussian second-moment bound and Cauchy--Schwarz.  Thus
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}.
\]
Balancing at \(h\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2q+2)}\) gives the upper bound \(Cs_{n,q}\).

For the matching full-location lower bound, choose \(M\asymp h^{-1}\) trimmed centers with disjoint radius-\(h\) disks.  Keep the uniform density and control regression fixed, and perturb the treated regression in alternative \(j\) by
\[
 \Delta\phi((z-x_j)/h),
 \qquad \Delta=\eta h^q,
\]
where \(\phi\in C_c^\infty(B_2(0,1))\), \(\phi(0)=1\), and \(\eta>0\) is fixed and small.  For every \(k\leq q\),
\[
 \lVert D^k\{\Delta\phi((\cdot-x_j)/h)\}\rVert_\infty
 \leq C\eta h^{q-k}\leq C\eta,
\]
so all alternatives lie in the H\"older ball.  Gaussian-noised Bernoulli outcomes retain the common tail bounds and directly have bounded positive conditional variances.  Relative entropy from the null satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P_j^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq Cn\Delta^2h^2=C\eta^2nh^{2q+2}.
\]
At the displayed bandwidth this is a small multiple of \(\log M\) after decreasing \(\eta\).  If \(J\) is uniform on the alternatives, the log-sum inequality gives \(I(J;O_{1:n})\leq\alpha\log M\).  For any decoder with error event \(E\),
\[
 H(J\mid O_{1:n})\leq\log2+P(E)\log M,
\]
so \(P(E)\geq1-\alpha-o(1)\).  Sup-norm accuracy below \(\Delta/3\) identifies the unique bumped center, proving
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \geq c\Delta=cs_{n,q}.
\]

Suppose \(L>4\).  The strict signed-distance local-constant estimator, the stability and upper-mass lemmas, and the straight-segment mesh yield
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}\leq Ca_n
\]
at \(h\asymp a_n\).  The canonical-square angular-density family lies in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}}\).  Its Poissonized disjoint-cell factorization, the coordinatewise-overlap direct-product lemma, midpoint decoding, and marked subsampling give the reverse bound \(ca_n\).

Suppose \(L=4\).  The support has area four and \(f_P\geq1/4\), so
\[
 1=\int_{[-1,1]^2}f_P(z)\,dz
 \geq\int_{[-1,1]^2}\frac14\,dz=1.
\]
Thus \(f_P=1/4\) almost everywhere, and continuity makes this equality pointwise.  The member properties of the straight class imply membership in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{coarea}}(q,\iota,L)\), so radial coarea identifies the signed-radius regression.  At every trimmed boundary point, for all sufficiently small \(s\), each arm contributes a half-circle inside the square and
\[
 Q_{t,P,x}(s)=\frac\pi4s.
\]
Taylor expansion of \(\mu_{t,P}\) through degree \(q-1\), followed by half-circle integration, yields uniformly bounded coefficients such that
\[
 \left|\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}-\mu_{t,P}(x)
 -\sum_{k=1}^{q-1}b_{t,k,P}(x)s^k\right|\leq Cs^q.
\]
The one-sided radial Gram matrix is a positive multiple of
\[
 G_q=\int_0^1uK_\triangle(u)v_q(u)v_q(u)^\top\,du,
\]
which is positive definite because a nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on \((0,1)\).  Instantiate the stabilized distance local polynomial with \(m=q\) and lower radial constant \(\pi/4\).  Its admissibility lemma makes it a member of \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{dist}}\).  Gram and radial-error concentration on a polynomial mesh, polynomial reproduction, and the displayed remainder give
\[
 R_{n,\delta}^{\mathrm{dist}}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{straight}})
 \leq C\left\{h^q+\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{nh^2}}\right\}
 \leq Cs_{n,q}.
\]
The preceding Fano family yields the lower bound \(cs_{n,q}\) even against full-location rules and hence, a fortiori, against coordinatewise-distance rules.  This proves the \(L=4\) equivalence.  Finally,
\[
 \frac{q}{2q+2}>\frac14\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q>1,
\]
which proves the stated elbow, including coincidence at \(q=1\) and throughout the no-density-slack case.
