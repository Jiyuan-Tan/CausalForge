Cattaneo, Titiunik, and Yu give distance-based identification, fixed-law smooth-bias results, and an \(n^{-1/4}\) converse on their own nonparametric class, while their discussion predicts a logarithmic uniform penalty.  Their results do not establish the logarithmic frontier on the distinct primitive actual-arm-thickness class used here, nor do they provide a class-uniform higher-order normalized-coarea remainder without a shared density modulus.
