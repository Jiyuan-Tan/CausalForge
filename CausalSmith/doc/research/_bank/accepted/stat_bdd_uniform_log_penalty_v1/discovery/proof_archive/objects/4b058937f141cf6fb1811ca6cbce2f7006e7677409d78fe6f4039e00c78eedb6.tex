Put \(r=2+\nu\geq4\).  Conditional Jensen gives
\[
 |\mu_{t,P}(X)|^r
 =|\mathbb E_P[Y(t)\mid X]|^r
 \leq\mathbb E_P[|Y(t)|^r\mid X].
\]
The elementary convexity inequality \(|a-b|^r\leq2^{r-1}(|a|^r+|b|^r)\) therefore yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_P[|Y(t)-\mu_{t,P}(X)|^r\mid X]
 &\leq2^{r-1}\{\mathbb E_P[|Y(t)|^r\mid X]+|\mu_{t,P}(X)|^r\}\\
 &\leq2^r\mathbb E_P[|Y(t)|^r\mid X]
 \leq2^{2+\nu}L,
\end{aligned}
\]
where the last inequality is clause \(\mathrm{(v)}\) of \(\mathrm{def:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}class}\).  This proves the claim.
