For \(x\in\mathcal B_P\) and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), define the positive-Radon--Nikodym radius domain
\[
 \mathcal S_{t,P,x}=\{s\in(0,\infty):Q_{t,P,x}(s)>0\}.
\]
It is defined only up to a Lebesgue-null modification inherited from the fixed version of \(Q_{t,P,x}\), and all essential-limit statements are invariant to such modifications.
