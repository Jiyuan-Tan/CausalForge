The lower inequality is the finite-\(n\) consequence of \(\mathrm{thm:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}point\text{-}indexed\text{-}log\text{-}converse}\), after decreasing its positive constant.  At \(\nu=2\),
\[
 b_{n,2}=\left({\log n\over n}\right)^{1/4}
 +\left({\log n\over n^{3/4}}\right)^{1/3}
 =a_n+n^{-1/4}(\log n)^{1/3}.
\]
The second summand dominates the first for all large \(n\).  Applying \(\mathrm{prop:cty\text{-}a1\text{-}a2\text{-}expected\text{-}outer\text{-}upper}\) to the explicit minimax witness gives the stated upper inequality.  No equality of the two logarithmic exponents follows from these assumptions.
