For each \(x\in\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)\), the observed input is \(V_{n,P}(x)=((Y_i,\lVert X_i-x\rVert_2):1\leq i\leq n)\).  The enlarged point-indexed class \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{PI}}\) consists of all law-independent families \(\{T_{n,x}:x\in\mathbb R^2\}\) for which every fixed-\(x\) section is Borel measurable and \(\widehat\mu_{n,P}^{\mathrm{PI}}(x)=T_{n,x}(V_{n,P}(x))\).  No joint measurability or other regularity in \(x\) is imposed.  Its explicit minimax functional is
\[
 R_n^{\mathrm{PI}}
 =\inf_{\widehat\mu_n^{\mathrm{PI}}\in\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{PI}}}
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)}
 \mathbb E_P^*\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\operatorname{bd}(\mathcal X_P)}
 \left|\widehat\mu_{n,P}^{\mathrm{PI}}(x)-\mu_P(x)\right|
 \right],
\]
where \(\mathbb E_P^*\) is outer expectation.  The inherited class \(\mathcal T_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\) is the strict subclass obtained by requiring one common Borel map \(T_n\).  For a common map, the sample-point loss is jointly Borel in the sample and \(x\); its supremum over the compact boundary is universally measurable, and its ordinary completed expectation equals its outer expectation.  Consequently \(R_n^{\mathrm{NP}}\geq R_n^{\mathrm{PI}}\).  The point-indexed theorem proves \(R_n^{\mathrm{PI}}\gtrsim(\log n/n)^{1/4}\), and class inclusion gives the CTY common-map converse.  The published uniform upper theorem imposes its additional Assumption~2 geometric condition, so no full-\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NP}}(L,q)\) upper bound or matched two-sided frontier is claimed.
