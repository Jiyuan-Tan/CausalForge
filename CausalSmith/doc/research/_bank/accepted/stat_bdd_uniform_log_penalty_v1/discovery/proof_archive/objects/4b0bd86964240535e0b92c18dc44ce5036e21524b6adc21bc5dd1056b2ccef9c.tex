For every integer \(p\geq1\) and every \(L\geq L_0(p)\), there are constants \(0<c\leq C<\infty\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 c\left({\log n\over n}\right)^{1/4}
 \leq R^{12,\pm}_n(p,2,L)
 \leq C n^{-1/4}(\log n)^{1/3}.
\]
The assumptions used here do not close the residual logarithmic gap at the fourth-moment endpoint.
