The all-order point-indexed converse gives
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}{R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)\over a_n}\geq c>0
\]
for \(p\geq0\), \(\nu\geq2\), and \(L\geq L_0(p)\).  The explicit winsorized estimator is a member of \(\mathcal T_n^{12,\pm}\).  Hence the definition of minimax risk and the winsorized expected outer-risk proposition give, for all large \(n\),
\[
 R_n^{12,\pm}(p,\nu,L)
 \leq \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L)}
 \mathbb E_P^*\!\left[
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B_P}
 |\widehat\tau^{12,\mathrm{LP}}_{n,a_n}(x)-\tau_P(x)|
 \right]
 \leq Ca_n.
\]
Divide by \(a_n>0\) and take the upper limit.  Combining the two displays proves the claimed chain of inequalities.  Both directions quantify over the same displayed class and the same outer-expectation risk.
