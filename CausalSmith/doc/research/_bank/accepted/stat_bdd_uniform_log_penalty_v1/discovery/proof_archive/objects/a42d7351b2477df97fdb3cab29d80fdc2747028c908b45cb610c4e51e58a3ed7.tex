The paper proves the exact \((\log n/n)^{1/4}\) frontier on its project-defined rectifiable and smooth classes, characterizes the straight-design \(L>4\) versus \(L=4\) information elbow, and gives a stabilized degree-\((m-1)\) distance local-polynomial estimator attaining \((\log n/n)^{m/(2m+2)}\) for every fixed \(m\geq1\).  Its contained-bump construction matches that upper rate for every \(m\geq q\): derivatives through order \(q\) are bounded by constants times \(h^{m-k}\).  For \(m<q\), the same construction is invalid because its \(q\)-th derivative diverges, and no matching converse is claimed.
