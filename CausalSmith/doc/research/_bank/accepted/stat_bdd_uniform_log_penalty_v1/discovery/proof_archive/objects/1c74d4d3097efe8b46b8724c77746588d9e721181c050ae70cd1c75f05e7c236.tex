The decision restriction is coordinatewise: the estimate at \(x\) may use only \(U_{n,P}(x)\), not a joint collection of distances at multiple boundary points.  Exact-radius conditional expectations are determined only almost everywhere; two-sided thickness guarantees positive-denominator radii accumulate at zero but does not make every radius positive.  The angular packing uses disjoint \(q\)-H\"older bump perturbations of affine baselines together with continuous shrinking-scale density imbalances and verifies common support, density, tail, variance, reach, assignment, and arm-mass constants.  The higher-order contained-bump converse uses width \(h_n\) and height \(h_n^m\).  It is valid for every \(m\geq q\), since its derivative of order \(k\leq q\) is bounded by a constant times \(h_n^{m-k}\); for \(m<q\), its \(q\)-th derivative is of order \(h_n^{m-q}\) and the construction is not contained in the frozen H\"older class.  In the uniform proof, kernel-polynomial maps are extended by zero only for \(u>1\) and are used on \([0,\infty)\), since \(u=|D|/h\geq0\); no false zero extension across \(u=0\) is used.  The exceptional-event contribution is controlled on both cutoff branches by the explicit admissibility envelope and a polynomially small concentration failure probability.
