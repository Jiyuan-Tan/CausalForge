The converse theorem gives the lower inequality.  The upper proposition gives risk at most \(Cb_{n,\nu}\).  If \(\nu>2\), then
\[
 {1+\nu\over3(2+\nu)}>{1\over4},
\]
so \((\log n/n^{(1+\nu)/(2+\nu)})^{1/3}=o(a_n)\).  Hence \(b_{n,\nu}\asymp a_n\), and the explicit estimator is a single law-independent witness for the minimax upper bound.  Combining the bounds proves the result.
