The theorem concerns the inherited coordinatewise class with one common map \(T_n\); the map cannot vary with \(x\) except through its input \(V_{n,P}(x)\), and it cannot combine released distances across centers to reconstruct locations.  The proof begins with an arbitrary fixed-\(n\) common-map rule, embeds it into a marked mean-\(2n\) Poisson experiment to obtain independent cell restrictions, applies the direct-product bound, and only then de-Poissonizes.  The packing explicitly verifies independent sampling, density normalization and bounds, compact square support with rectifiable boundary, the \(q\)-H\"older regression envelope, and the continuous conditional-variance envelope.
