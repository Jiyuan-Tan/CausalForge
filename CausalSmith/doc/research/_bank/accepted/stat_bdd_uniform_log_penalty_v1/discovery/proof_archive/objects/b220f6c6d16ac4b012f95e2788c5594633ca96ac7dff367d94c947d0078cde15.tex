Let \(\mathcal T^{12,\pm}_n\) be the class of rules for which there is a law-independent collection \(\{T_{n,G,x}:G\text{ is an admissible known geometry},\ x\in\mathbb R^2\}\) such that every fixed \((G,x)\) section is a Borel map from \((\mathbb R^2)^n\) to \(\mathbb R\) and
\[
 \widehat\tau_{n,P}(x)=T_{n,G_P,x}(U^{\pm}_{n,P}(x)),
 \qquad P\in\mathcal P_{12}(p,\nu,L),\quad x\in\mathcal B_P.
\]
No joint measurability or continuity in \(x\) is required.
