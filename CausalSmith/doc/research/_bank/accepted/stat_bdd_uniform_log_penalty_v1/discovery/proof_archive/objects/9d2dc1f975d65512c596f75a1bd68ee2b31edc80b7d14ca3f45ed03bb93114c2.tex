Take \(0<h\leq2r_0\).  Symmetry of the triangular kernel and its explicit formula give
\[
 K_\triangle(D_{P,x}/h)=K_\triangle(|D_{P,x}|/h)\geq\frac12
 \quad\text{whenever }\lVert X-x\rVert_2\leq h/2.
\]
Under deterministic assignment, on \(\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\}\) one has \(T=t\) almost surely, up to the fixed interface convention.  Consequently the definition of \(\Psi^{(0)}_{t,P,x}(h)\) and the two-sided thickness assumption at radius \(h/2\leq r_0\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Psi^{(0)}_{t,P,x}(h)
 &=\frac1{h^2}\mathbb E_P\!\left[K_\triangle(D_{P,x}/h)\mathbf1\{T=t\}\right]\\
 &\geq\frac1{2h^2}P\{X\in\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap B_2(x,h/2)\}\\
 &\geq\frac1{2h^2}c_-\left(\frac h2\right)^2
 =\frac{c_-}{8}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This bound holds uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{rect}}\), both arms, and every \(x\in\mathcal B_P\).  It also holds uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{sm}}\) and all \(x\in\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\), because \(\mathcal B_{P,\delta}\subseteq\mathcal B_P\).  Thus the assertion follows with \(\kappa_0=c_-/8\) and ``sufficiently small'' meaning \(0<h\leq2r_0\).  Every division is by the positive number \(h^2\).
