The rate equivalences are up to constants, not exact asymptotic constants.  The rectifiable frontier applies to the paper's enlarged primitive two-sided-thickness class and does not require a separate boundary-length envelope; it does not settle the logarithmic frontier on the original Cattaneo--Titiunik--Yu class.  The smooth theorem is endpoint-trimmed and assumes no common density modulus.  The straight information comparison distinguishes strict density slack \(L>4\) from the forced-uniform case \(L=4\).  The order-\(m\) coarea theorem proves a complete, explicit achievability rate for every \(1\leq m\leq\min\{q,\iota\}\), but proves a matching contained-bump converse only for \(m=q\).  Whether the displayed upper exponent is minimax sharp for \(m<q\) is left open; no invalid \(h^m\)-height bump is treated as a member of the \(q\)-H\"older class.
