Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{coarea}}(q,\iota,L)\), \(x\in\mathcal B_P\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and write \(R_x=\lVert X-x\rVert_2\).  By the definitions of the observable radial measure and its fixed Radon--Nikodym version,
\[
 P\{T=t,\ 0<R_x\leq r\}
 =\nu_{t,P,x}((0,r])
 =\int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds
\]
for every \(r>0\).  The polar coarea identities in the definitions of \(Q_{t,P,x}\) and \(N_{t,P,x}\) give agreement with the respective observed-arm circle integrals outside a Lebesgue-null set of radii.  These are almost-everywhere statements about the fixed versions, not pointwise identities at exceptional radii.

Score absolute continuity gives \(P(X=x)=0\).  Hence \(R_x>0\) almost surely and
\[
 \operatorname{sgn}(D_{P,x})=2T-1
 \quad P\text{-almost surely}.
\]
For every bounded Borel function \(a\), the two Radon--Nikodym definitions yield
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf1\{T=t\}a(R_x)]
 =\int_0^\infty a(s)N_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P[\mathbf1\{T=t\}a(R_x)]
 =\int_0^\infty a(s)Q_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds.
\]
The H\"older and conditional sub-Gaussian member properties imply \(\mathbb E_P|Y|<\infty\).  The Radon--Nikodym characterization of conditional expectation, together with sign recovery, therefore gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mid D_{P,x}=(2t-1)s]
 =\frac{N_{t,P,x}(s)}{Q_{t,P,x}(s)}
 =g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)
\]
for Lebesgue-almost every \(s\in\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\).  The quotient is used only where \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)>0\).

Now suppose zero is in the essential support in both arms and the two separate essential limits equal their respective boundary regressions.  Applying the definition of \(\tau_P\) gives
\[
 \ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{1,P}(x)-\ell^{\mathrm{dist}}_{0,P}(x)
 =\mu_{1,P}(x)-\mu_{0,P}(x)=\tau_P(x).
\]
Finally, under the radial hypotheses displayed in the transition theorem,
\[
 \int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds\geq\frac{\underline c}{2}r^2>0,
 \qquad 0<r\leq r_*,
\]
and \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)>0\) for Lebesgue-almost every \(s\in(0,r_*]\).  Thus zero is in essential support and \(\mathcal S_{t,P,x}\) agrees with \((0,r_*]\) modulo a null set.  If \(B\) uniformly bounds the displayed coefficients, the normalized-coarea remainder gives
\[
 |g^{\mathrm{dist}}_{t,P,x}(s)-\mu_{t,P}(x)|
 \leq B\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}s^k+As^m\longrightarrow0
\]
outside a null set as \(s\downarrow0\).  This proves each separate essential limit and hence all stated identification conclusions.
