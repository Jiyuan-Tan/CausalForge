Fix \(P,x,t\) as in the statement and put \(A=\mathcal A_{t,P}\cap\mathcal X_P\).  Since \(X\) has Lebesgue density \(f_P\), polar coordinates centered at \(x\), followed by Tonelli's theorem for the nonnegative integrand, give
\[
\begin{aligned}
P\{X\in A\cap B_2(x,r)\}
&=\int_{A\cap B_2(x,r)}f_P(z)\,dz\\
&=\int_0^r\int_{\partial B_2(x,s)\cap A}f_P(z)\,d\mathcal H^1(z)\,ds
=\int_0^rQ_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds.
\end{aligned}
\]
The same absolute continuity gives \(P(X=x)=0\).  On \(X\ne x\), the definition \(D_{P,x}=(2T-1)\lVert X-x\rVert_2\) has positive sign exactly when \(T=1\) and negative sign exactly when \(T=0\).  Thus no whole-interface nullness or quantitative geometry is needed for arm recovery at this fixed center.

For a bounded Borel test function \(a\) of the positive radius, consistency and deterministic assignment give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_P\!\left[Y\mathbf1\{T=t\}a(\lVert X-x\rVert_2)\right]
&=\int_A a(\lVert z-x\rVert_2)\mu_{t,P}(z)f_P(z)\,dz\\
&=\int_0^\infty a(s)N_{t,P,x}(s)\,ds,
\end{aligned}
\]
where the last equality is the same polar-coordinate calculation, applied to positive and negative parts.  The corresponding identity without \(Y\) has density \(Q_{t,P,x}(s)\).  The Radon--Nikodym characterization of conditional expectation therefore yields \(N_{t,P,x}(s)/Q_{t,P,x}(s)\) for almost every radius with positive denominator.  If the stated positivity and two limits hold, subtraction gives
\[
\lim_{s\downarrow0}\left\{\frac{N_{1,P,x}(s)}{Q_{1,P,x}(s)}-\frac{N_{0,P,x}(s)}{Q_{0,P,x}(s)}\right\}
=\mu_{1,P}(x)-\mu_{0,P}(x)=\tau_P(x),
\]
where the last equality is the causal target definition.  This proves identification of the target by the radial functional under the displayed limiting condition; it does not define the causal target to be that functional.
