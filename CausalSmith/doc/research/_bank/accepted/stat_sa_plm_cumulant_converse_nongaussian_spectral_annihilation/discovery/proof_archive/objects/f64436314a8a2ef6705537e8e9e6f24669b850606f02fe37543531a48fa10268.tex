The spectral class imposes only the direct \(L^1(P_X)\) bound \(\lVert\bar g_n-g_0\rVert_1\le\varepsilon_{1,n}\), and the spectral statistic uses \(\bar g_n\) but not \(\bar q_n\) or \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\).  Its additional contour-bank input is deterministic after an existential once-for-all choice; it is neither learned from the sample nor claimed to be effectively obtainable from the class constants.
