Conditional on the once-for-all deterministic bank fixed in \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\), form \(\widehat F_{a,n}\) and \(\widehat G_{a,n}\) on \(I_a\).  Declare \(j\) pilot-admissible when \(\inf_{z\in C_j}|\widehat F_{0,n}(z)|\ge a_{\star}/2\) and \(\widehat N_{0j,n}\ge1\).  Let \(\widehat j_n\) be the smallest index among the admissible maximizers of \(\inf_{z\in C_j}|\widehat F_{0,n}(z)|\).  Return zero if there is no admissible index or if \(\inf_{z\in C_{\widehat j_n}}|\widehat F_{1,n}(z)|<a_{\star}/4\); otherwise set
\[
 \widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}
 =\Pi_{[-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]}
 \operatorname{Re}\!\left[
 \frac{1}{\widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}2\pi i}
 \oint_{C_{\widehat j_n}}
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}\,dz
 \right].
\]
All circles use fixed parameterizations, and all ties and failures use the displayed deterministic rules.  Thus, after the bank has been fixed, this is a specified sample map.  The definition does not assert that the bank itself, its radii, or \(a_{\star}\) can be computed or effectively enumerated from the class constants.
