By \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\), select one once-for-all deterministic witness bank \((J,\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_J,a_{\star})\), and fix it throughout.  All constants below are allowed to depend on this selected finite tuple; because the lemma constructs the tuple from only \(k=r+1,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1\), after fixation this is dependence only on the primitive class constants asserted in the theorem.  Write \(m_a=|I_a|\).  For \(n\ge2\), both halves are nonempty and \(m_a\ge n/3\).

Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  The selected bank supplies an index \(j_{\star}=j_{\star}(P)\) such that
\[
 N_{C_{j_{\star}}}(M)\ge1,
 \qquad \inf_{z\in C_{j_{\star}}}|M(z)|\ge a_{\star}.
\]
Since \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\) gives \(|H_n(z)|\ge3/4\) on \(|z|\le R_1\), makes \(H_n\) zero-free there, and shows that \(F_n=MH_n\) and \(M\) have the same zeros with multiplicity.  Hence
\[
 N_{C_{j_{\star}}}(F_n)\ge1,
 \qquad \inf_{z\in C_{j_{\star}}}|F_n(z)|\ge3a_{\star}/4. \tag{1}
\]

Put
\[
 d_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|,
 \qquad
 e_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|,
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_a=\{d_a\le a_{\star}/8\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\), (1) implies \(\inf_{C_{j_{\star}}}|\widehat F_{0,n}|\ge5a_{\star}/8\).  Rouch\'e's theorem gives
\[
 \widehat N_{0j_{\star},n}=N_{C_{j_{\star}}}(F_n)\ge1,
\]
so \(j_{\star}\) is pilot-admissible.  The finite maximizing rule therefore selects \(\widehat j_n\) with
\[
 \inf_{C_{\widehat j_n}}|\widehat F_{0,n}|\ge5a_{\star}/8,
 \qquad
 \inf_{C_{\widehat j_n}}|F_n|\ge a_{\star}/2. \tag{2}
\]
A second application of Rouch\'e's theorem gives
\[
 \widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}=N_{C_{\widehat j_n}}(F_n)\ge1. \tag{3}
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\cap\mathcal A_1\), (2) also gives \(\inf_{C_{\widehat j_n}}|\widehat F_{1,n}|\ge3a_{\star}/8>a_{\star}/4\), so the evaluation fallback is not used.

Conditional on the selected bank, the selector and statistic are measurable.  Under every fixed circle parameterization, the empirical transforms and their derivatives are jointly measurable in the sample and continuous in the angle.  A boundary infimum equals the infimum over a fixed countable dense set of angles.  On the measurable zero-free event, the winding integral is the integral of a jointly measurable continuous integrand.  Finiteness of the bank and the deterministic tie and failure rules complete the measurability argument.  This establishes measurability after fixation and supplies no procedure for constructing the selected bank.

There is a class-uniform \(C_G<\infty\) such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|G_n(z)|\le C_G. \tag{4}
\]
Indeed, \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), \(Z_n=\eta+D_n\), and Cauchy--Schwarz give
\[
 |G_n(z)|\le e^{2C_gR_1}\|Y\|_{L^2(P)}
 \{E_P[e^{2R_1|\eta|}]\}^{1/2}.
\]
The bounds on \(q_0,\theta_0,\eta,\xi\) uniformly bound both factors.

On \(\mathcal A_0\cap\mathcal A_1\), let \(C=C_{\widehat j_n}\) and \(N=N_{C_{\widehat j_n}}(F_n)\).  Equations (2)--(3), zero-freeness of \(H_n\), and \(\text{thm:exact-contour-identification}\) give
\[
 \theta_0=\frac{1}{N2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{G_n(z)}{F_n(z)}\,dz.
\]
The circle length is at most \(2\pi R_1\), \(N\ge1\), and the denominator bounds in (2) imply, by subtracting the two ratios,
\[
 \left|\frac{1}{N2\pi i}\oint_C
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}}{\widehat F_{1,n}}\,dz-\theta_0\right|
 \le L_{\star}(d_1+e_1), \tag{5}
\]
where \(L_{\star}<\infty\) depends only on the primitive constants after the selected bank is fixed.  Taking real parts and projecting onto \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\) cannot increase distance from the real number \(\theta_0\).

By \(\text{lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2}\) and \(m_a\ge n/3\),
\[
 E_P[d_a^2+e_a^2]\le K_1/n. \tag{6}
\]
Markov's inequality yields
\[
 P(\mathcal A_0^c\cup\mathcal A_1^c)\le K_2/n. \tag{7}
\]
On the exceptional event, clipping bounds squared error by \(4C_{\theta}^2\).  Combining (5)--(7) proves uniformly in \(P\) that
\[
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0)^2]
 \le2L_{\star}^2E_P[d_1^2+e_1^2]
 +4C_{\theta}^2P(\mathcal A_0^c\cup\mathcal A_1^c)
 \le C/n. \tag{8}
\]
The statistic associated with the selected bank is one measurable decision rule, so (8) also gives \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le C/n\).

For the converse, apply \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\).  Let \(c_0,C_0\) be its constants and choose
\[
 0<h\le\min\{c_0,1,(8C_0)^{-1/2}\},
 \qquad \vartheta_{\pm}=\pm h/(2\sqrt n).
\]
Both laws belong to the submodel, their target separation is \(h/\sqrt n\), and
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,\vartheta_-}^{\otimes n}\|P_{n,\vartheta_+}^{\otimes n})\le1/8.
\]
Pinsker's inequality gives total variation at most \(1/4\).  For an arbitrary estimator \(\widetilde\theta\), test with \(A=\{\widetilde\theta\ge0\}\).  On \(A\) under the negative law, or on \(A^c\) under the positive law, squared error is at least \(h^2/(4n)\).  Therefore
\[
 \max_{v\in\{-,+\}}E_{P_{n,\vartheta_v}}[(\widetilde\theta-\vartheta_v)^2]
 \ge \frac{h^2}{8n}\{1-\|P_-^{\otimes n}-P_+^{\otimes n}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\}
 \ge\frac{3h^2}{32n}.
\]
Taking the class supremum and estimator infimum proves the lower bound.

The selected-bank statistic uses only \(Y\), \(T-\bar g_n(X)\), and the fixed bank, not an outcome code or the law of \(\eta\).  Finally, (8) and Markov's inequality give
\[
 P\{|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0|>\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}\}\le\gamma,
\]
which is the asserted generalized-quantile bound.  The class disclaimer follows from the separate definitions of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) and \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).
