Fix \(n\) and choose one law \(P_\star\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  Retain from \(P_\star\) the joint law of \((X,T)\), hence the same \(g_0\), \(\eta=T-g_0(X)\), covariate law, and treatment-code error.  Also retain the bounded function \(q_0\).  The clipped codes \(\bar g_n\) and \(\bar q_n\) are deterministic experiment inputs and therefore remain unchanged.

Let \(c_{\mathrm N}=\lVert N(0,1)\rVert_{\psi_2}<\infty\), set
\[
 \tau=\frac{\psi_\xi}{2c_{\mathrm N}}>0,
 \qquad
 c_0=\min\{C_\theta,1\}>0,
\]
and let \(\xi_\star\sim N(0,\tau^2)\) be independent of \((X,T)\).  For \(|\vartheta|\le c_0\), define
\[
 Y_\vartheta=q_0(X)+\vartheta\eta+\xi_\star
\]
and denote the resulting law by \(P_{n,\vartheta}\).  Independence and centering of \(\eta\) give
\[
 E_{P_{n,\vartheta}}[Y_\vartheta\mid X]
 =q_0(X)+\vartheta E[\eta\mid X]
 =q_0(X),
\]
so the displayed \(q_0\) is the outcome regression for every submodel member.  The residual outcome innovation is exactly \(\xi_\star\), and
\[
 E[\xi_\star\mid X,T]=0,
 \qquad
 \lVert\xi_\star\rVert_{\psi_2\mid X}
 =c_{\mathrm N}\tau\le\psi_\xi.
\]
All treatment-side conditions, including independence, sub-Gaussianity, cumulant separation, the range of \(g_0\), and the direct \(L^1(P_X)\) code restriction, are inherited from \(P_\star\).  The range of \(q_0\) is also inherited, and \(|\vartheta|\le C_\theta\).  Hence every \(P_{n,\vartheta}\) belongs to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  This verifies membership without imposing any outcome-code restriction.

For two parameter values, the conditional outcome laws given \((X,T)\) are Gaussian with common variance \(\tau^2\) and conditional means differing by \((\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0)\eta\).  Since their \((X,T)\) marginals agree,
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,\vartheta_0}\Vert P_{n,\vartheta_1})
 =\frac{(\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0)^2}{2\tau^2}E_{P_\star}[\eta^2].
\]
The treatment sub-Gaussian condition implies \(E_{P_\star}[\eta^2]\le c_2\psi_\eta^2\) for a universal \(c_2\).  Independence of the \(n\) observations and additivity of relative entropy therefore give, when \(|\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0|=h/\sqrt n\),
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,\vartheta_0}^{\otimes n}\Vert
                  P_{n,\vartheta_1}^{\otimes n})
 \le \frac{c_2\psi_\eta^2}{2\tau^2}h^2
 =:C_0h^2.
\]
Both \(c_0\) and \(C_0\) depend only on the fixed class constants, as required.
