The definition has two levels.  The executable level accepts a represented sample
\[
 \mathfrak s=
 \bigl((\mathfrak t_i,\mathfrak y_i,\mathfrak g_i)_{i=1}^n,
       \mathfrak p,C_{\theta}\bigr),
\]
where \(\mathfrak t_i\), \(\mathfrak y_i\), and \(\mathfrak g_i\) are the supplied canonical certified-real names of \(T_i\), \(Y_i\), and \(\bar g_n(X_i)\), and \(\mathfrak p\) is the certified primitive-name record of \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\).  The mathematical interpretation sends this record to the real sample given by the value fields.  The executable algorithm itself never reads those value fields and never manufactures a certified name from an exact real.

Invoke \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\) on \(\mathfrak p\).  Form \(\mathfrak z_i=\mathfrak t_i-\mathfrak g_i\) by certified subtraction.  At every certified circle node \(\mathfrak c_{jq}\), use complex rectangle multiplication and the nested complex-exponential extension from \(\text{def:certified-contour-arithmetic-substrate}\) to construct, by finite sums, rectangles for
\[
 \widehat F_{a,n}^{(p)}(z)
 =|I_a|^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_a}Z_{n,i}^{p}e^{zZ_{n,i}},
 \quad p\in\{0,1,2\},
\]
and
\[
 \widehat G_{a,n}^{(p)}(z)
 =|I_a|^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_a}Y_iZ_{n,i}^{p}e^{zZ_{n,i}},
 \quad p\in\{0,1\}.
\]
All node precisions are the values of the compositional effective moduli at the requested rational error.

For explicit mesh fuel, refine every input name to width one and take rational upper magnitude bounds \(U_{Z,i}\), \(U_{Y,i}\), and \(U_{\rho,j}\).  The inequalities \(e^x\le3^{\lceil x\rceil}\) for \(x\ge0\) and \(\pi<4\) turn the displayed empirical derivative sums into rational upper bounds for
\[
 2\pi\rho_j\sup_{C_j}|\widehat F_{a,n}'|,
 \quad
 \sup|W_{0j}'|,
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \sup|V_j'|,
\]
where
\[
 W_{0j}(t)=2\pi i\rho_je^{2\pi it}
 \frac{\widehat F'_{0,n}(\rho_je^{2\pi it})}
      {\widehat F_{0,n}(\rho_je^{2\pi it})}
\]
and
\[
 V_j(t)=\frac{2\pi i\rho_je^{2\pi it}}{N_j}
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(\rho_je^{2\pi it})}
      {\widehat F_{1,n}(\rho_je^{2\pi it})}.
\]
Given a rational tolerance \(e>0\) and such a bound \(L\), the mesh size is \(\lceil L/e\rceil+1\); node precisions are chosen by the effective modulus at an error budget divided by the finite number of arithmetic operations.  Thus no refinement loop is open-ended.

On the pilot fold, compute for every \(j\) a sound interval of width at most \(a_{\star}/64\) for \(\inf_{C_j}|\widehat F_{0,n}|\).  If its lower endpoint \(m_j\) is positive, guarded complex division and certified quadrature produce a rectangle of coordinate widths below \(1/4\) for \((2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_{C_j}\widehat F'_{0,n}/\widehat F_{0,n}\).  Record the unique nonnegative integer \(N_j\) in that rectangle if the imaginary interval contains zero; otherwise reject the circle.  A circle is admissible exactly when \(m_j\ge a_{\star}/2\) and \(N_j\ge1\).  Select an admissible circle with greatest rational lower endpoint, resolving ties by the smallest index; return zero if none exists.

On the evaluation fold, compute the analogous modulus interval on the selected circle and return zero if its lower endpoint is below \(a_{\star}/4\).  Otherwise guarded division and certified quadrature give a complex rectangle of coordinate widths at most \(1/n\) for
\[
 \frac{1}{N_j2\pi i}\oint_{C_j}
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}\,dz.
\]
Return the real part of its rational midpoint projected onto \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\); this is \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\).

An execution trace is the finite list of name-modulus calls, their returned precisions, mesh sizes, rational rectangle operations, guarded divisions, endpoint comparisons, fallback decisions, and the tie-break.  Every item is computed by primitive recursion or a displayed integer formula, so the trace has explicit finite fuel and contains no exact-real evaluation, rewrapping, classical choice, or proof-selected search.  With the canonical floor-dyadic representation of observations, equal represented real samples have identical traces and outputs.  The algorithm uses \(\bar g_n\), no supplied outcome code, and no information about the law of \(\eta\).
