Specialize to \(k=4\).  For \(0<a\le1\), let
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 t_a=\frac{\pi}{2a},
 \qquad
 A_a=a\exp\!\left\{-\frac{(1-a^2)\pi^2}{8a^2}\right\},
\]
where \(G_{\circ}\sim N(0,1)\), \(S\) is symmetric Rademacher, and \(G_{\circ}\perp S\).  Consider any law satisfying \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}P\), \(\eta=\eta_a\perp X\), \(E_P[\xi\mid X,T]=0\) almost surely, \(\lvert\theta_0(P)\rvert\le C_{\theta}\), \(\lVert g_0\rVert_{\infty}\le C_g\), \(\lVert q_0\rVert_{\infty}\le C_q\), \(\lVert\xi\rVert_{\psi_2\mid X}\le\psi_{\xi}\) almost surely, and the direct condition \(\lVert\bar g_n-g_0\rVert_{L^1(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n}\).  Then
\[
 M_a(z)=\exp\!\left(\frac{1-a^2}{2}z^2\right)\cosh(az),
 \qquad
 \kappa_4(\eta_a)=-2a^4,
\]
\(t_a\) is the first positive characteristic zero, and \(E[\sin\{t_a(\eta_a+d)\}]=0\) for every real \(d\).  If \(t_a\varepsilon_{1,n}\le1/2\), then
\[
 E_P[Z_n\sin(t_aZ_n)]
 =A_aE_P[\cos(t_aD_n)]
 \ge A_a/2.
\]
Define
\[
 \widehat\theta_{a,n}
 =
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle
 \Pi_{[-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]}
 \frac{\mathbb P_n[Y\sin(t_aZ_n)]}
      {\mathbb P_n[Z_n\sin(t_aZ_n)]},
 &\displaystyle \mathbb P_n[Z_n\sin(t_aZ_n)]\ge A_a/4,\\[3mm]
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
There is \(C<\infty\), depending only on \(C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\xi}\), such that uniformly over these laws,
\[
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{1,\frac1n a^{-2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{(1-a^2)\pi^2}{4a^2}\right)\right\}.
\]
Equivalently, for \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), the condition is \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\), and the risk is at most
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}.
\]
