Let \(B=c_{\psi}\psi_{\eta}^2\), where \(c_{\psi}\) is the universal constant in the sub-Gaussian moment-generating-function bound, and put \(T=R_1+1\) and \(U=T+1\).  Every treatment-noise transform represented in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) has at most
\[
 N_{\star}=\left\lceil\frac{BU^2}{\log(U/T)}\right\rceil
\]
zeros, counted with multiplicity, in \(\{|z|<T\}\).
