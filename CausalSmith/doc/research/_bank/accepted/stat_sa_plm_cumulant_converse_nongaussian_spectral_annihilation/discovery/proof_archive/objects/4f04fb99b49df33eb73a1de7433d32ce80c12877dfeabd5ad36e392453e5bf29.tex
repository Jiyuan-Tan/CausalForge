The three class relations are exactly \(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\).  In particular, the first inclusion is maximal in the direction needed here: every published ACE law is a member of the broad spectral class, whereas no reverse inclusion is used or claimed.

Assume \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\).  If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), the cited result \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) applies directly on the full class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and gives exactly
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}. \tag{1}
\]

Suppose instead that \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).  For \(t\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\), enlarge only the outcome-code radius from zero to \(t\).  Every law in the zero-radius class remains in the radius-\(t\) class.  Moreover,
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and hence the eligibility predicate, is unchanged.  Apply the positive-radius cited theorem with radius \(t\), then restrict its supremum to the zero-radius class.  The resulting right-hand side differs from the claimed boundary value only by
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt.
\]
The published statistic is the same order-\(r\) ACE statistic for every radius.  Letting \(t\downarrow0\) proves (1) at the boundary.

Now also assume \(n\ge2\), \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\neq\varnothing\).  By \(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\), this class is contained in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), so the latter is nonempty.  Restricting the generalized-quantile inequality in the proposed form of \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) to the subset gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}. \tag{2}
\]

Finally put
\[
 S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
     +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}.
\]
All multiplicative quantities in \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) other than \(S_n\) and \(n^{-1/2}\) are fixed positive class constants.  Along any eligible sequence for which \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), (1) is therefore of order \(S_n\), while (2) is of order \(n^{-1/2}\).  Their ratio tends to zero.  This proves a strict comparison of the two displayed upper guarantees on the full published ACE class and, because no risk lower bound for the ACE statistic was used, proves the final caveat.
