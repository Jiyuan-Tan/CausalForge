Fix \(n\) and \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).  The support restriction in \(\text{def:gaussian-class}\) gives
\[
 |Y|\le C_q
 \quad P\text{-almost surely}. \tag{1}
\]
Because \(Y\) is bounded, all conditional expectations below exist.  Conditional Jensen's inequality gives
\[
 |q_0(X)|=|E_P[Y\mid X]|
 \le E_P[|Y|\mid X]\le C_q, \tag{2}
\]
and, on writing \(m(X,T)=E_P[Y\mid X,T]\),
\[
 |m(X,T)|\le E_P[|Y|\mid X,T]\le C_q
 \quad P\text{-almost surely}. \tag{3}
\]
By the residual definitions,
\[
 Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0(P)\eta+\xi,
 \qquad
 \eta=T-g_0(X).
\]
Both \(q_0(X)\) and \(\eta\) are \(\sigma(X,T)\)-measurable.  The restriction \(E_P[\xi\mid X,T]=0\) therefore implies
\[
 m(X,T)=q_0(X)+\theta_0(P)\eta
 \quad P\text{-almost surely}. \tag{4}
\]

Suppose for contradiction that \(\theta_0(P)\ne0\).  Under the Gaussian-treatment restriction,
\[
 \theta_0(P)\eta
 \sim N(0,\theta_0(P)^2\sigma^2).
\]
The boundary condition \(\sigma>0\) makes this Gaussian nondegenerate.  Thus
\[
 A=\{\theta_0(P)\eta>2C_q\}
\]
has strictly positive probability.  On the intersection of \(A\) with the probability-one event on which (2)--(4) hold,
\[
 m(X,T)=q_0(X)+\theta_0(P)\eta
 \ge-C_q+\theta_0(P)\eta>C_q,
\]
contradicting (3).  Hence \(\theta_0(P)=0\).  Since \(n\) and \(P\) were arbitrary, this holds throughout \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).

Now suppose that the class is nonempty.  The statistic \(\widetilde\theta\equiv0\) is admissible in \(\text{def:minimax-risks}\).  For every class member,
\[
 (\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P))^2=0
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P)|)=0.
\]
Both minimax criteria are nonnegative.  Taking the class supremum and then the infimum over all statistics proves
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}
 =\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}
 =0.
\]
Thus the zero statistic is minimax for both losses.  No overlap, positivity, rank, division, or inverse condition is used.  The finiteness of \(C_q\) supplies the support bound, \(\sigma>0\) supplies nondegenerate Gaussian support, and nonemptiness is needed only for the minimax display.
