Let \(\mathscr M\) be the locally uniform closure of the centered sub-Gaussian moment generating functions satisfying \(\lvert\kappa_k\rvert\ge\delta\).  A once-for-all contour bank is a tuple consisting of a finite \(J\ge1\), radii \((\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_J)\in(R_0,R_1)^J\), and \(a_{\star}>0\) such that, with \(C_j=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=\rho_j\}\), every \(M\in\mathscr M\) has some \(j\) for which \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\) and \(\inf_{z\in C_j}|M(z)|\ge a_{\star}\).  After \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\) proves that such tuples exist, fix one arbitrary tuple and denote it by \((J,\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_J,a_{\star})\).  The choice is deterministic and depends only on the class constants \((k,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1)\); no effective enumeration, computable radii, computable modulus, or algorithm for finding the tuple is asserted.
