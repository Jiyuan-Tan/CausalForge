The three class relations are exactly \(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\).  In particular, for every \(n\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
 \subseteq \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}.
 \tag{1}
\]
For \(s\ge r\), the same lemma gives
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}},
 \tag{2}
\]
and when \(s=r\), equality holds under the common clipped-code convention.  Relation (1) is the only inclusion involving the broad spectral class used below; no reverse inclusion is asserted.

Assume \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\).  If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) applies directly on the full published class and yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \bigl(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|\bigr)
 \le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma},
 \tag{3}
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}
 &=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Bigl[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\qquadqquad
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}
      +C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]

It remains to justify the boundary \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).  Fix \(t\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\) and enlarge only the outcome-code uncertainty radius at the displayed index from zero to \(t\).  Every law satisfying the zero-radius restriction also satisfies the radius-\(t\) restriction.  Moreover,
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
 \tag{5}
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and consequently the eligibility predicate \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), is unchanged.  Apply \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) with positive outcome radius \(t\), and then restrict the supremum to the zero-radius class.  The resulting right-hand side exceeds the value in (4) at \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\) only by
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt.
 \tag{6}
\]
The order-\(r\) ACE statistic is unchanged by this enlargement of the uncertainty radius.  Letting \(t\downarrow0\) in (6) proves (3) also when \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).

Now also assume that \(n\ge2\), \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\neq\varnothing\).  By (1), the broad class is then nonempty.  Restricting the generalized-quantile conclusion of \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) through (1) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \bigl(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|\bigr)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}.
 \tag{7}
\]

Finally define
\[
 S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
     +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1},
 \qquad
 K_0=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1},
\]
and
\[
 K_1=64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}
      +C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}\gamma^{-1/2}.
\]
All quantities in \(K_0\) and \(K_1\) are fixed positive class constants, and (4) becomes
\[
 B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}=K_0(S_n+K_1n^{-1/2}).
 \tag{8}
\]
Along any eligible sequence satisfying \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), equations (7) and (8) imply
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}}{B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}}
 \le
 \frac{\sqrt{C/\gamma}\,n^{-1/2}}{K_0S_n}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Hence the spectral upper guarantee is strictly smaller in order than the displayed ACE upper guarantee on the full published ACE class.  The argument compares only the two upper bounds (3) and (7); it uses no risk lower bound for \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}\), proving the final caveat.
