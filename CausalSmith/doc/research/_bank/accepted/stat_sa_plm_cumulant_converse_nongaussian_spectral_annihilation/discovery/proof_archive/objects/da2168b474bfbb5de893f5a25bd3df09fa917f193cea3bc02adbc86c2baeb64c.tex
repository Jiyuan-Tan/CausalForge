The paper proves exact annihilator and observable contour identities, the numerical zero radius, an explicit finite translated-dyadic bank with a positive dyadic modulus, empirical-transform control, a total Borel statistic, and matching \(c/n\) and \(C/n\) MSE bounds on the broad spectral class and full published ACE class. It also specifies a paper-independent bounded-build complex interval arithmetic obligation. Only after that implementation is added and compiled does the represented-data layer yield a certified-real output corresponding to the Borel statistic on the same fixed primitive records.
