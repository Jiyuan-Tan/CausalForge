The proof of \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) established the uniform bound
\[
 |M(z)|\le e^{4\psi_{\eta}^2|z|^2},\qquad M(0)=1. \tag{1}
\]
We first count zeros, including multiplicity, without introducing another formal lemma.  Choose radii \(u_q\uparrow R_{\mathrm{out}}\) with \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}<u_q<R_{\mathrm{out}}\) and with no zero on \(|z|=u_q\); this is possible because the zero radii of a nonzero entire function are countable and locally finite.  Jensen's formula and (1) give
\[
 \sum_{|a|<u_q}\log\frac{u_q}{|a|}
 =\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log|M(u_qe^{it})|\,dt
 \le4\psi_{\eta}^2u_q^2. \tag{2}
\]
Every zero with \(|a|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) contributes at least \(\log(u_q/R_{\mathrm{fac}})\).  Letting the radii increase to \(R_{\mathrm{out}}\) in (2) proves
\[
 N_M(R_{\mathrm{fac}})
 \le
 \frac{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2}
      {\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})}
 \le N_{\star}. \tag{3}
\]
This upward limiting argument includes zeros on \(|z|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}\).

The least \(m_{\star}\) exists because the translated-dyadic grids have diverging cardinality.  Distinct bank radii are separated by at least \(d_{\star}\).  Since \(2h_{\star}<d_{\star}\), the modulus of any one zero is within \(h_{\star}\) of at most one bank radius.  There are more bank radii than the at most \(N_{\star}\) zeros counted in (3).  Hence some \(\rho_j\) satisfies
\[
 \bigl||a|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge h_{\star}
 \quad\text{for every zero }a\text{ with }|a|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}}. \tag{4}
\]
Thus \(M\) is zero-free on \(C_j\).  Moreover \(\rho_j>R_0\), so the zero in \(|z|\le R_0\) supplied by \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) lies strictly inside \(C_j\), proving \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\).

List only the zeros strictly inside the factorization disk as \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\), with multiplicity, and define
\[
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^N
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}.
\]
Each reflected pole lies strictly outside the closed radius-\(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) disk.  After removable cancellation at the interior zeros, \(f=M/Q\) is holomorphic on a neighborhood of that closed disk and zero-free on its open disk.  It may vanish on the boundary; this causes no problem.  Each factor has unit modulus on the boundary, so
\[
 |f(z)|=|M(z)|\le e^{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2},
 \qquad |z|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}.
\]
The maximum-modulus principle gives the same upper bound inside.  Hence
\[
 u(z)=4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\log|f(z)|
\]
is nonnegative and harmonic on the open disk.  Since \(M(0)=1\) and \(|Q(0)|\le1\), one has \(u(0)\le4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\).  Harnack's inequality therefore gives, for \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
 |f(z)|\ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}. \tag{5}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), (4) gives \(|z-a_\nu|\ge h_{\star}\), while
\[
 |R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z|
 \le R_{\mathrm{fac}}(R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1).
\]
Thus, because the base below lies in \((0,1)\) and \(N\le N_{\star}\),
\[
 |Q(z)|\ge
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^N
 \ge
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}}. \tag{6}
\]
Multiplying (5) and (6) gives \(\inf_{C_j}|M|\ge a_{\star}\).  Every displayed operation defining the translated-dyadic bank is finite and depends only on the primitive class constants, proving computability and uniformity in \(n\).
