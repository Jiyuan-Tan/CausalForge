The paper proves exact annihilator and observable contour identities, the numerical zero radius, a terminating certified translated-dyadic bank construction with a positive dyadic modulus, empirical-transform control, a total certified statistic, and matching \(c/n\) and \(C/n\) MSE bounds. It proves \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), retains the exact Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 5.4 ACE upper bound, and compares that bound with the spectral generalized-quantile guarantee on the full published ACE class. It treats the bounded-outcome Gaussian calculation solely as a separate source-assumption diagnostic.
