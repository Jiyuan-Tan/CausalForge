The paper proves exact annihilator and observable contour identities, the numerical zero radius, a terminating certified translated-dyadic bank construction with a positive dyadic modulus, empirical-transform control, a total certified statistic, and matching \(c/n\) and \(C/n\) MSE bounds on the broad spectral class. By preserving both published \(L^r(P_X)\) code restrictions in the outcome-tilt submodel, it also proves matching class-minimax MSE bounds on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\). It retains Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 5.4 only as an estimator-specific generalized-quantile upper comparator and treats the bounded-outcome Gaussian calculation solely as a separate source-assumption diagnostic.
