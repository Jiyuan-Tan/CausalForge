The three class relations are exactly
\(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\).  In particular,
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n},                             \tag{1}
\]
while for \(s\ge r\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}},            \tag{2}
\]
with equality at \(s=r\) under the common clipped-code convention.  No
reverse inclusion in (1) is used or claimed.

Assume \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and
\(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\).  If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\),
\(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) applies directly on the full class and
gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}.                                  \tag{3}
\]

Suppose instead that \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).  For
\(t\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\), replace the whole nonincreasing outcome-radius
sequence by \(\varepsilon'_{2,m}=\max\{\varepsilon_{2,m},t\}\).  This is
again nonincreasing, its value at index \(n\) is \(t\), and every law in the
original zero-radius class belongs to the enlarged radius-\(t\) class at
that index.  Moreover,
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and hence eligibility, is unchanged.  Apply the cited
positive-radius theorem, restrict its supremum to the original class, and
let \(t\downarrow0\).  The only disappearing term is
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt,
\]
so (3) also holds at the boundary \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).

Now also assume \(n\ge2\),
\(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and
\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\ne\varnothing\).  Inclusion
(1) makes the broad class nonempty.  Restricting the generalized-quantile
conclusion of \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) through (1) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}.                                         \tag{4}
\]

Finally, put
\[
 S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
     +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}.
\]
Every multiplicative quantity in \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) other than
\(S_n\) and \(n^{-1/2}\) is a fixed positive class constant.  Along an
eligible sequence with \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), the ratio of the bound in (4)
to \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) tends to zero.  This is a strict comparison
of two upper guarantees on the full published ACE class.  Since no risk
lower bound for \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}\) was invoked, it is not
a lower-bound statement about that estimator.
