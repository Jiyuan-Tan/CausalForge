Let \(c_{\psi}<\infty\) be the universal constant for which the sub-Gaussian envelope implies \(|M(z)|\le \exp(B|z|^2)\), where \(B=c_{\psi}\psi_{\eta}^2\).  Put
\[
 T=R_1+1,\qquad U=T+1,\qquad
 N_{\star}=\left\lceil\frac{BU^2}{\log(U/T)}\right\rceil,
 \qquad \Lambda=\frac{T+R_1}{T-R_1}.
\]
For each integer \(m\ge1\), let
\[
 \mathcal Q_m=\{q2^{-m}:q\in\mathbb Z,\ R_0+1/4<q2^{-m}<R_0+3/4\}.
\]
Take the least \(m\) for which \(|\mathcal Q_m|>N_{\star}\), enumerate \(\mathcal Q_m\) increasingly as \(\rho_1<\cdots<\rho_J\), and set
\[
 d=2^{-m},\qquad h=d/3,\qquad
 a_{\star}=a_{\star}^{\mathrm{eff}}
 =\exp\{-(\Lambda-1)BT^2\}
 \left(\frac{h}{T+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}}.
\]
The bank consists of the known circles \(C_j=\{z:|z|=\rho_j\}\).  All quantities are obtained by finite integer arithmetic, elementary functions, ceilings, and comparisons from \((k,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1)\), with \(R_0\) fixed by \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\).  Thus the bank, its radii, and its strictly positive conditioning modulus are effective functions of the displayed primitive constants; they do not depend on \(n\), the unknown law, or the sample.
