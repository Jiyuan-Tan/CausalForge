Choose any \(P_{n,\star}\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), and retain from it the joint law of \((X,T)\), the functions \(g_0,q_0\), and the treatment noise \(\eta=T-g_0(X)\).  Choose \(\tau>0\), depending only on \(\psi_{\xi}\), so small that a centered \(N(0,\tau^2)\) variable has \(\psi_2\)-norm at most \(\psi_{\xi}\).  Let \(\xi_{\star}\sim N(0,\tau^2)\) be independent of \((X,T)\), and put
\[
 c_0=\min\{C_{\theta}/2,1\}>0,
 \qquad
 Y_{\vartheta}=q_0(X)+\vartheta\eta+\xi_{\star},
 \qquad |\vartheta|\le c_0.
\]
Denote the resulting law by \(P_{n,\vartheta}\).  Since \(\eta\perp X\) and \(E_P\eta=0\),
\[
 E_{P_{n,\vartheta}}[Y_{\vartheta}\mid X]=q_0(X).
\]
The residual outcome innovation under parameter \(\theta_0(P_{n,\vartheta})=\vartheta\) is exactly \(\xi_{\star}\), and
\[
 E[\xi_{\star}\mid X,T]=0,
 \qquad
 \|\xi_{\star}\|_{\psi_2\mid X}\le\psi_{\xi}.
\]
All treatment-side restrictions, the cumulant separation, and the treatment-code radius are unchanged.  The outcome regression \(q_0\), and therefore its range and its distance from the fixed code \(\bar q_n\), is also unchanged.  Finally \(|\vartheta|\le C_{\theta}\).  Hence every \(P_{n,\vartheta}\) belongs to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), with the asserted common objects.

Conditional on \((X,T)\), the two outcome laws at \(\vartheta_0\) and \(\vartheta_1\) are normal with common variance \(\tau^2\) and means differing by \((\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0)\eta\).  Thus
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,\vartheta_0}\|P_{n,\vartheta_1})
 =\frac{(\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0)^2}{2\tau^2}E_P[\eta^2].
\]
The treatment sub-Gaussian bound implies \(E_P[\eta^2]\le C\psi_{\eta}^2\).  Additivity of relative entropy for independent products therefore gives, when \(|\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0|=h/\sqrt n\),
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,\vartheta_0}^{\otimes n}\|P_{n,\vartheta_1}^{\otimes n})
 =\frac{h^2}{2\tau^2}E_P[\eta^2]
 \le C_0h^2,
\]
where \(C_0\) depends only on \(\psi_{\eta}\) and \(\psi_{\xi}\).
