Use the notation of \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\).  The least \(m_{\star}\) exists because \(|\mathcal Q_m|\to\infty\), and it is found by finite search.  Distinct grid radii are separated by at least \(d_{\star}=2^{-m_{\star}}\).  By \(\text{lem:jensen-zero-count}\), a represented transform \(M\) has at most \(N_{\star}\) zeros in \(|z|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), with multiplicity.  Since \(2h_{\star}=2d_{\star}/3<d_{\star}\), the modulus of one zero lies within distance \(h_{\star}\) of at most one grid radius.  As \(J>N_{\star}\), some \(\rho_j\) satisfies
\[
 \bigl||a|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge h_{\star}
 \quad\text{for every zero }a\text{ with }|a|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}}. \tag{1}
\]
Thus \(M\) is zero-free on \(C_j\).  Since \(\rho_j>R_0\), the zero in \(|z|\le R_0\) supplied by \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) lies strictly inside, so \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\).

List the zeros in the closed radius-\(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) disk as \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\), with multiplicity, where \(N\le N_{\star}\), and set
\[
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^{N}
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}.
\]
For \(|a_\nu|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), the reflected pole \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2/\overline{a_\nu}\) lies outside the closed factorization disk.  If \(|a_\nu|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), numerator and denominator vanish at the same boundary point and their quotient is a unimodular constant after cancellation.  Hence boundary zeros are harmless: cancellation of every zero extends \(f=M/Q\) holomorphically and without zeros to a neighborhood of the closed disk.  Each extended factor has unit modulus on its boundary.  From the fixed MGF bound,
\[
 |f(z)|=|M(z)|\le e^{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2},
 \qquad |z|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}.
\]
The maximum-modulus principle applies directly on the closed factorization disk and gives the same upper bound inside.

The function \(u=4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\log|f|\) is nonnegative and harmonic.  Since \(M(0)=1\) and \(|Q(0)|\le1\), \(u(0)\le4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\).  Harnack's inequality gives, for \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
 |f(z)|\ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}. \tag{2}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), (1) gives \(|z-a_\nu|\ge h_{\star}\), while
\(|R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}}(R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1)\).  Therefore
\[
 |Q(z)|\ge
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^N
 \ge
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}}. \tag{3}
\]
Multiplying (2) and (3) gives exactly the explicit \(a_{\star}\) in the definition.  All searches and constants depend only on the fixed class constants, proving effectiveness and uniformity in \(n\).
