Let \(m=|I_a|\).  Write \(U_i(z)=e^{zZ_{n,i}}\).  The power series and the triangle inequality give
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R_1}|(\mathbb P_{I_a}-P)U(z)|
 \le\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{R_1^j}{j!}
 |(\mathbb P_{I_a}-P)Z_n^j|.
\]
Minkowski's inequality in \(L^2\), independence, and the variance bound by the second moment imply
\[
 \left\|\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|\right\|_2
 \le m^{-1/2}\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{R_1^j}{j!}|Z_n^j\|_2
 \le m^{-1/2}\{E_Pe^{2R_1|Z_n|}\}^{1/2}. \tag{1}
\]
The same calculation with \(Y_iZ_{n,i}^j\) gives
\[
 \left\|\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|\right\|_2
 \le m^{-1/2}\{E_P[Y^2e^{2R_1|Z_n|}]\}^{1/2}. \tag{2}
\]
Clipping and the range restriction give \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), while \(Z_n=\eta+D_n\).  The Luxemburg treatment bound uniformly controls every exponential moment of \(|\eta|\).  Also
\(Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi\); the bounds on \(q_0,\theta_0\), the treatment tail, and the conditional Luxemburg bound for \(\xi\), followed by Cauchy--Schwarz, uniformly control the expectation in (2).  Squaring (1)--(2) and adding proves the display with a finite \(K\) depending only on the listed constants.
