Fix \(n\) and \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).  The support restriction gives
\[
 |Y|\le C_q\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Conditional Jensen's inequality and \(q_0(X)=E_P[Y\mid X]\) imply
\[
 |E_P[Y\mid X,T]|\le C_q,
 \qquad
 |q_0(X)|\le C_q
 \quad\text{almost surely}. \tag{1}
\]
The residual identity is
\[
 Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi.
\]
Because \(\eta=T-g_0(X)\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(X,T)\), the restriction
\(E_P[\xi\mid X,T]=0\) yields
\[
 E_P[Y\mid X,T]=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta
 \quad\text{almost surely}. \tag{2}
\]
Every conditioning step is licensed by boundedness of \(Y\) and the displayed conditional-mean assumption.

Suppose that \(\theta_0\ne0\).  Since \(\eta\sim N(0,\sigma^2)\) with
\(\sigma>0\), the random variable \(\theta_0\eta\) is a nondegenerate centered Gaussian and has unbounded positive support.  Hence
\[
 P(\theta_0\eta>2C_q)>0. \tag{3}
\]
On the event in (3), (1) gives
\[
 q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta
 \ge-C_q+\theta_0\eta>C_q,
\]
contradicting (1) and (2).  Therefore \(\theta_0(P)=0\).

Assume now that \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\ne\varnothing\).  For the statistic \(\widetilde\theta\equiv0\), both its squared error and its absolute error are identically zero under every class member.  Consequently,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 E_P[(\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P))^2]=0
\]
and, from the definition of the generalized quantile,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P)|)=0.
\]
Both criteria are nonnegative.  Taking the infimum over statistics in
\(\text{def:minimax-risks}\) proves
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}
 =\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}
 =0.
\]
Thus the zero statistic is minimax for both losses.
