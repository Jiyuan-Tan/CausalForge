The analytic factorization, contour identity, numerical zero localization, certified translated-dyadic bank, positive dyadic modulus, nuisance zero-free bound, empirical-transform \(L^2\) control, total computability and measurability, failure-event bound, and Le Cam submodel are proved for fixed separation.  The bank constants deteriorate as \(\delta\downarrow0\), and the present proof gives no useful shrinking-separation rate.  The sharp joint phase diagram, adaptive switching, uniform inference, and matching local lower bounds remain open.
