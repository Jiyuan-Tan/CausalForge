Form the translated-dyadic bank in \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\), and form \(\widehat F_{a,n}\) and \(\widehat G_{a,n}\) on the deterministic split \(I_a\).  All contour computations below use the fixed parameterization \(z=\rho_je^{it}\), \(0\le t\le2\pi\), outward-rounded interval arithmetic, and deterministic uniform angular meshes.

For each fold and circle, bound the angular Lipschitz constant of \(|\widehat F_{a,n}(\rho_je^{it})|\) by the observable quantity
\[
 L^F_{aj}=\rho_j\sup_{z\in C_j}|\widehat F'_{a,n}(z)|
 \le \rho_j|I_a|^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_a}|Z_{n,i}|e^{\rho_j|Z_{n,i}|}.
\]
Use a finite mesh with angular spacing small enough that its Lipschitz error is at most \(a_{\star}/128\), evaluate every node with outward error at most \(a_{\star}/128\), and thereby obtain a certified interval for \(\inf_{C_j}|\widehat F_{a,n}|\) of width at most \(a_{\star}/64\).

Whenever the certified lower modulus \(m>0\), compute the winding integral using the integrand
\[
 W(t)=\rho_je^{it}\frac{\widehat F'_{0,n}(\rho_je^{it})}
                              {\widehat F_{0,n}(\rho_je^{it})}.
\]
The observable derivative sums for \(\widehat F'_{0,n}\) and \(\widehat F''_{0,n}\), together with \(m\), give the angular Lipschitz bound
\[
 |W'(t)|\le
 \rho_j\frac{\sup_{C_j}|\widehat F'_{0,n}|}{m}
 +\rho_j^2\left{
 \frac{\sup_{C_j}|\widehat F''_{0,n}|}{m}
 +\frac{\sup_{C_j}|\widehat F'_{0,n}|^2}{m^2}
 \right}.
\]
Refine the mesh until the sum of the deterministic quadrature error and all outward node-evaluation errors is strictly less than \(1/4\).  Set \(\widehat N_{0j,n}\) to the unique nonnegative integer certified by that enclosure; if there is none, declare the circle inadmissible.

Declare \(j\) pilot-admissible when its certified lower modulus is at least \(a_{\star}/2\) and \(\widehat N_{0j,n}\ge1\).  Let \(\widehat j_n\) be the smallest index among the admissible maximizers of the certified lower modulus.  Return zero if no index is admissible or if the evaluation-fold certified lower modulus on \(C_{\widehat j_n}\) is below \(a_{\star}/4\).

Otherwise compute the normalized contour ratio using
\[
 V(t)=\rho_{\widehat j_n}e^{it}
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(\rho_{\widehat j_n}e^{it})}
      {\widehat F_{1,n}(\rho_{\widehat j_n}e^{it})}.
\]
The observable derivative sums for \(\widehat F'_{1,n}\), \(\widehat G_{1,n}\), and \(\widehat G'_{1,n}\), together with the certified positive denominator, give
\[
 |V'(t)|\le
 \rho_{\widehat j_n}\frac{\sup|\widehat G_{1,n}|}{m}
 +\rho_{\widehat j_n}^2\left{
 \frac{\sup|\widehat G'_{1,n}|}{m}
 +\frac{\sup|\widehat G_{1,n}|\sup|\widehat F'_{1,n}|}{m^2}
 \right}.
\]
Refine one interval enclosure for the normalized contour ratio until its radius is at most \(1/n\).  Its rational midpoint is the sole numerical output; there is no second rounding.  Return the projection onto \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\) of its real part.  Positive certified denominators make every displayed Lipschitz bound finite; the prescribed positive target widths make every refinement terminate.  Finite sums, interval refinements, deterministic tie-breaking, and fallback are Borel measurable, so this defines a total measurable statistic \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) computable from the sample and displayed class constants.  It uses \(\bar g_n\), uses no supplied outcome code, and does not know the law of \(\eta\).
