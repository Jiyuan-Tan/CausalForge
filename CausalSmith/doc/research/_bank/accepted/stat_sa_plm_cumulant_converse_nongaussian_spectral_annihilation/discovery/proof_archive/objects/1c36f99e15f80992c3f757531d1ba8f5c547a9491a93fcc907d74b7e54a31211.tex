The finite bank, its cardinality, its radii, and \(a_{\star}\) are obtained by compactness, choice of zero-avoiding radii, and finite-subcover extraction.  The proof supplies no effective enumeration, numerical conditioning certificate, complexity bound, or operational selector that starts only from the class constants.  Accordingly, the established \(\Theta(n^{-1})\) statement is an existence-level minimax theorem conditional on fixing one such bank, although the contour identity and the sample map given a bank are explicit.  The shrinking-separation phase diagram and uniformly valid local-to-Gaussian inference remain open.
