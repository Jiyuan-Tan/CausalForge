Let \(\operatorname{Name}_{+}(x)\) denote a positive certified-real record from \(\text{def:certified-contour-arithmetic-substrate}\).  The actual input is
\[
 \mathfrak p=
 \bigl(k,\mathfrak d,\mathfrak p,\mathfrak r_0,\mathfrak r_1\bigr),
\]
where \(k\) is exact, \(\mathfrak d:\operatorname{Name}_{+}(\delta)\), \(\mathfrak p:\operatorname{Name}_{+}(\psi_{\eta})\), \(\mathfrak r_0:\operatorname{Name}_{+}(R_0)\), and \(\mathfrak r_1:\operatorname{Name}_{+}(R_1)\), together with the value contracts
\[
 k=r+1,\qquad
 R_0=(2^{k+4}k!)^{1/(k-2)}
       (\psi_{\eta}^2/\delta)^{1/(k-2)},
 \qquad R_1=R_0+1.
\]
Each positive name carries its own rational positivity witness.  The executable call reads only approximation, modulus, and positivity fields, never an exact-real value field.

Refine \(\mathfrak p\) and \(\mathfrak r_1\) once to rational width at most one.  If their returned upper endpoints are \(u_{\psi}\) and \(u_R\), put
\[
 U_{\psi}=\left\lceil\max\{1,u_{\psi}\}\right\rceil,
 \qquad
 U_R=\left\lceil\max\{1,u_R\}\right\rceil,
 \qquad
 N_{\mathrm{cert}}=4U_{\psi}^2(U_R+2)^3.
\]
Set
\[
 m_{\star}=N_{\mathrm{cert}}+3,
 \quad d_{\star}=2^{-m_{\star}},
 \quad h_{\star}=d_{\star}/3,
 \quad J=2^{m_{\star}-1}+1,
\]
and, for \(q=0,\ldots,2^{m_{\star}-1}\), form compositionally the certified radius name
\[
 \mathfrak\rho_q
 =\mathfrak r_0+\operatorname{Name}(1/4+q2^{-m_{\star}}).
\]
Its mathematical value is \(\rho_q=R_0+1/4+q2^{-m_{\star}}\), and \(C_q=\{z:|z|=\rho_q\}\).

For a completely rational positive lower certificate, define the integers
\[
 e_{\star}=8U_RU_{\psi}^2(U_R+1)^2,
 \qquad d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}=3(2U_R+1),
\]
\[
 u_{\star}=2e_{\star}
 +N_{\mathrm{cert}}(m_{\star}+d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}),
 \qquad a_{\star}=2^{-u_{\star}}>0.
\]
Every datum is obtained by a fixed finite sequence of calls to the concrete certified arithmetic, rational endpoint operations, and primitive recursion on displayed integers.  There is no unbounded search, reconstruction of a name from an exact real, \(\operatorname{Classical.choose}\), or proof-selected \(\operatorname{Nat.find}\).  The returned bank and \(a_{\star}\) depend only on the supplied primitive-name records; neither the law nor transform of \(\eta\) is an input.  Its universal guarantee is \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\).
