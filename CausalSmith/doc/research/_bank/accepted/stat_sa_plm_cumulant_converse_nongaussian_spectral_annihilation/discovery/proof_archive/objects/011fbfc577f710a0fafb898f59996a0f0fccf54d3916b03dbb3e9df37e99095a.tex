For \(n\ge2\), suppose \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\neq\varnothing\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\). There are \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on \(r,\delta,C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi}\), such that \(c/n\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]\le C/n\), uniformly over every deterministic nested \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\). The estimator uses \(\bar g_n\), does not use \(\bar q_n\), and does not know the law of \(\eta\). Its MSE bound implies \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)\le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}\). On \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\cap\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), for every sequence of indices satisfying \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), this removes the two nuisance terms in Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis bound (22); whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2}\), the spectral generalized-quantile guarantee is strictly smaller in order than \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\).
