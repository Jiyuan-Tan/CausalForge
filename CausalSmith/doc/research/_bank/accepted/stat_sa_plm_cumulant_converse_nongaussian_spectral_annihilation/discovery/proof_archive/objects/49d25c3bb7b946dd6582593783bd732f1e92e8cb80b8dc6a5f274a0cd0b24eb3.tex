Use the notation and the effective grid of \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\).  The set \(\mathcal Q_m\) contains asymptotically \(2^{m-1}\) points, so a least \(m\) with \(J=|\mathcal Q_m|>N_{\star}\) exists and is found by finite search.  Distinct grid radii are separated by at least \(d=2^{-m}\).  By \(\text{lem:jensen-zero-count}\), a represented transform \(M\) has at most \(N_{\star}\) zeros in \(|z|<T\), with multiplicity.  Since \(2h=2d/3<d\), the modulus of any one zero can lie within distance \(h\) of at most one grid radius.  The pigeonhole principle therefore supplies a grid radius \(\rho_j\) such that
\[
 \bigl||a|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge h
 \quad\text{for every zero }a\text{ of }M\text{ with }|a|<T. \tag{1}
\]
In particular, \(M\) is zero-free on \(C_j\).  Moreover \(\rho_j>R_0+1/4>R_0\), so the zero in \(|z|\le R_0\) supplied by \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) lies strictly inside \(C_j\).  Hence
\[
 N_{C_j}(M)\ge1. \tag{2}
\]

List the zeros of \(M\) in \(|z|<T\), with multiplicity, as \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\), where \(N\le N_{\star}\), and form the radius-\(T\) finite Blaschke product
\[
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^{N}
 \frac{T(z-a_{\nu})}{T^2-\overline{a_{\nu}}z}.
\]
The quotient \(f=M/Q\) extends holomorphically and without zeros throughout \(|z|<T\).  Since each Blaschke factor has modulus one on \(|z|=T\), the maximum principle, applied on exhausting smaller disks and then by a limit, gives
\[
 |f(z)|\le e^{BT^2},\qquad |z|<T. \tag{3}
\]
Thus \(u(z)=BT^2-\log|f(z)|\) is nonnegative and harmonic.  Because \(M(0)=1\) and \(|Q(0)|=\prod_{\nu}|a_{\nu}|/T\le1\), one has \(u(0)\le BT^2\).  Harnack's inequality on the radius-\(T\) disk gives, for \(|z|\le R_1<T\),
\[
 u(z)\le\frac{T+R_1}{T-R_1}u(0)
 \le\Lambda BT^2,
\]
and hence
\[
 |f(z)|\ge \exp\{-(\Lambda-1)BT^2\}. \tag{4}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), (1) implies \(|z-a_{\nu}|\ge h\), while
\[
 |T^2-\overline{a_{\nu}}z|
 \le T(T+|z|)\le T(T+R_1).
\]
Consequently,
\[
 |Q(z)|
 \ge\left(\frac{h}{T+R_1}\right)^N
 \ge\left(\frac{h}{T+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}}. \tag{5}
\]
Multiplying (4) and (5) proves
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_j}|M(z)|
 \ge \exp\{-(\Lambda-1)BT^2\}
 \left(\frac{h}{T+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}}
 =a_{\star}>0. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (2) and (6) prove the bank property.  Every ingredient is an explicit finite operation on the fixed class constants, so the same effective bank works uniformly in \(n\).
