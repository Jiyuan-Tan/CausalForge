Three rigorous benchmarks are available, but none determines the full-class minimax phase diagram.  First, the exact specialization of Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 5.4 gives the displayed local ACE generalized-quantile bound with separation \(\delta_n\), subject to its exact positivity and eligibility conditions.  Second, on the explicit Gaussian--Rademacher path with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), \(\text{lem:gaussian-rademacher-l1-benchmark}\) proves under
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi}
\]
that
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}.
\]
This uses only \(\|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^1(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n}\), not an \(L^s(P_X)\) bound.  Third, zero localization at separation \(\delta_n\) gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k\left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)},
\]
and nuisance zero-freeness on \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\) follows when
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
At each fixed positive \(\delta_n\), compactness then supplies a finite non-effective contour bank and a positive boundary modulus, yielding only an existential bound \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)/n\).  No proved estimate controls that constant, the bank size, or the modulus as \(\delta_n\downarrow0\).  Thus the adaptive selector, sharp phase diagram, uniform studentized limit law and Wald coverage, and matching local lower bounds remain unresolved.
