The strongest closed result is the already-established theorem
\(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\), supplemented by the
zero-localization and nuisance-factor calculations.  It gives the following
three benchmarks, none of which is a sharp full-class phase diagram.

First, on the class \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) obtained from the published
ACE class by replacing the fixed cumulant separation by
\(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\geq\delta_n\), the exact local eligibility
condition gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(
   \left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|
 \right)
 \leq U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}},
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
 U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}
 &=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{24mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
The required eligibility condition is
\[
 r\leq
 \min\!\left\{
   \frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}
   -\frac{1}{a_{1,n}}
       \log\!\left(\frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}\right),
   \frac{\widetilde b_{2,n}}
        {a_2\log(a_2\widetilde b_{2,n})}
 \right\},
\]
with all logarithms and denominators defined and
\[
 \widetilde b_{2,n}
 =200\min\{1,C_{\theta}\}\delta_n
 \max\!\left\{
   \varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n},
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})
 \right\}^{-1}.
\]
Together with a separately valid ordinary-DML bound
\(U_{\mathrm{DML},n}\), this proves only the oracle comparison
\(\min\{U_{\mathrm{DML},n},U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}\}\).

Second, on the explicit local path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=\lvert\kappa_4(\eta_a)\rvert=2a^4,
\]
the computable sine estimator from
\(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) satisfies, whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \leq\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the pathwise uniform bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[
   (\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2
 \right]
 \leq
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(
    \frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is an upper bound for one known path, not a cumulant-only full-class
rate or a converse.

Third, the zero-localization lemma gives the deterministic radius
\[
 R_{0,n}
 \leq A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
Writing \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), the nuisance-transform calculation preserves
exact contour identification under the explicit sufficient condition
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \leq
 \{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
This last statement is an identification-and-stability benchmark only.  It
does not lower-bound the population boundary modulus, control the empirical
contour variance, construct an adaptive selector, yield a limit law or Wald
coverage, or provide a matching minimax lower bound.
