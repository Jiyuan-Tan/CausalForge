The two branches are separate law classes sharing only the displayed deterministic experiment inputs.  Along every indicated sequence of non-Gaussian indices, \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) supplies constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
 \frac cn\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le\frac Cn.
\]
The only supplied-code restriction in the broad spectral class is
\[
 \lVert\bar g_n-g_0\rVert_{L^1(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0.
\]
It therefore permits a fixed positive treatment-code radius and neither constrains nor uses \(\widehat q_n\), \(\bar q_n\), or \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\).  This proves \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\asymp n^{-1}\).

Separately, for every sufficiently large index satisfying the Gaussian hypotheses, \(\text{thm:gaussian-quantile-to-mse}\) gives
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}\ge\gamma L_{\mathrm G,n,\gamma}^2.
\]
Substitution of the definition of \(L_{\mathrm G,n,\gamma}\) yields
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}
 \ge\gamma c_\gamma^2
 \left[
  \sigma^{-2}\varepsilon_{1,n}\varepsilon_{2,n}
  \left\{\frac{\log(1/\varepsilon_{1,n})}{\log n}\right\}^3
  +\sigma^{-1}n^{-1/2}
 \right]^2.
\]
This invocation leaves unchanged the bounded-outcome Gaussian class, its known variance, its positivity and logarithmic conditions, and the common deterministic inputs.  No equality or inclusion between the Gaussian and non-Gaussian classes is used.
