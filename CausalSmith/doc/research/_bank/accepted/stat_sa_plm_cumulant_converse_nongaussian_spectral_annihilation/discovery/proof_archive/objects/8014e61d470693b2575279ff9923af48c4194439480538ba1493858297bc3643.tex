Because \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r\), the power-mean inequality gives
\[
 \|f\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\|f\|_{L^s(P_X)},
\]
with the essential-supremum interpretation when \(s=\infty\).  Thus each of the two \(L^s(P_X)\) code restrictions in \(\text{def:ace-comparison-subclass}\) implies the corresponding \(L^r(P_X)\) restriction in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\).  Every other member condition of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) is inherited from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  Hence
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.
\]
The definitions impose the two extra code restrictions only on the comparison subclass, so this argument gives neither inclusion of the broad spectral class nor equality of the broad and published classes.

Assume \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\).  If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), apply \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and restrict its supremum to the displayed subclass.  This is exactly the asserted bound \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\).

If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\), fix \(t\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\).  Every law satisfying the zero outcome-code radius also satisfies the radius-\(t\) restriction, and
\[
 \max\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\}
 =
 \max\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\}.
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and hence the eligibility predicate, is unchanged.  The positive-radius result applies for every such \(t\), and its right-hand side differs from the asserted boundary value only by
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt.
\]
Letting \(t\downarrow0\) proves the monotone boundary extension.

If also \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\), restricted to the subclass, gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le \sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}=O(n^{-1/2}).
\]
Writing \(S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\), the ACE upper bound is a fixed positive constant times \(S_n+O(n^{-1/2})\).  If \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), the ratio of the spectral upper guarantee to \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) tends to zero.  This compares only the two proved upper guarantees and supplies no lower bound for ACE.
