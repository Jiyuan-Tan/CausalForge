The strongest proved partial answer consists of three upper or identification benchmarks, none of which is a full-class converse.  First, the sibling-owned theorem \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) applies Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem~5.4 pointwise in \(n\), with the fixed separation constant replaced by \(\delta_n\).  Under the theorem's exact local version of condition~(21), including its stated positivity and domain conditions, it gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)\\
 &\quad\le
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{38mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
Putting this bound beside a separately valid DML bound yields only an oracle upper envelope; it neither constructs a data-driven selector nor proves a minimax rate.

Second, on the explicit path with \(k=4\),
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=\left|\kappa_4(\eta_a)\right|=2a^4,
\]
where \(G_{\circ}\sim N(0,1)\), \(S\) is symmetric Rademacher, and \(G_{\circ}\perp S\), the characteristic function has a zero at \(t_a=\pi/(2a)\).  Exact sine annihilation and the relevance bound
\[
 E_P[Z_n\sin(t_aZ_n)]
 \ge \frac a2
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{(1-a^2)\pi^2}{8a^2}\right\}
\]
hold whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le \frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi}.
\]
The computable clipped ratio in that theorem consequently satisfies
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[
   (\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2
 \right]
 \le
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(
    \frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is pathwise and uses the complete Gaussian--Rademacher transform; it is not a uniform statement over the cumulant-only class.

Third, \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) gives
\[
 R_{0,n}
 \le
 A_k\left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
Together with \(\text{lem:nuisance-zero-free}\), this preserves exact contour identification whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le
 \left[
 4(R_{0,n}+1)e^{2C_g(R_{0,n}+1)}
 \right]^{-1}.
\]
This is an identification condition, not a uniform risk bound, because the fixed-separation compactness argument does not quantify the contour boundary modulus as \(\delta_n\downarrow0\).  Thus the partial answer supplies no cumulant-only matching lower bound, no full-class elbow, no adaptive selector, and no uniform coverage theorem.
