At each fixed \(\delta>0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives a canonical computable certified statistic with uniform \(C/n\) mean-squared error whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\).  Along \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives the exact local ACE generalized-quantile upper benchmark proportional to \(\delta_n^{-1}\) under its displayed eligibility condition.  On the Gaussian--Rademacher path with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), the same theorem gives the computable mean-squared-error upper bound
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}
\]
under \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  These are exactly the fixed-separation, local-ACE, and path-specific spectral upper benchmarks.  They do not construct a data-driven selector, a uniform coverage result, a sharp full-class phase diagram, or a matching local lower bound.
