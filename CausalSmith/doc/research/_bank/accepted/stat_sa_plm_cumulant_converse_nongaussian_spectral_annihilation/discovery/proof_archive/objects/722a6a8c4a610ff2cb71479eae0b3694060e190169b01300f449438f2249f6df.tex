Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).  By
\(\text{def:ace-comparison-subclass}\), \(P\) satisfies every member property of
\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) and also
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n},
 \qquad
 \|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{2,n}.
\]
Since \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r+1>r\), Lyapunov's inequality, with its usual \(L^{\infty}\) interpretation, gives
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}
 \le\|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n},
\]
and the identical argument gives
\[
 \|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{2,n}.
\]
All remaining member properties in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) are already properties of the broad non-Gaussian class.  Hence
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.
\]
This is only a one-way inclusion for the explicitly restricted comparison subclass; neither definition supplies either of the broader relations disclaimed in the statement.

Suppose next that \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and
\(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\).  Applying
\(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) on
\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), and then restricting the supremum to the just-proved subclass, yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)\\
 &\quad\le C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Big[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{31mm}
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Big],
\end{aligned}
\]
which is exactly \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\).

It remains to justify the boundary value \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).  Fix
\(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n}]\), and temporarily enlarge only the outcome-code radius at index \(n\) from zero to \(u\).  Every law satisfying the zero-radius restriction also satisfies this enlarged restriction.  Moreover,
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},u,
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and hence the exact eligibility predicate, is unchanged.  The positive-radius result applied at radius \(u\) therefore gives the same left-hand side bounded by
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Big[\varepsilon_{1,n}^ru+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Big].
\]
The left-hand side does not depend on \(u\).  Letting \(u\downarrow0\) proves the displayed boundary formula.  Positivity of \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\) is exactly what permits this monotone extension without changing the maximum in the eligibility condition.

Finally suppose \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\).  The comparison subclass is contained in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  If it is nonempty, then the broad class is nonempty, so
\(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{\frac{C}{\gamma n}};
\]
if it is empty, the uniform assertion is vacuous.  Along any asymptotic sequence on which
\[
 A_n:=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
      +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 \gg n^{-1/2},
\]
the fixed positive prefactors in \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) give
\[
 B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\asymp A_n,
 \qquad
 n^{-1/2}=o(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}).
\]
Hence the spectral \(O(n^{-1/2})\) generalized-quantile guarantee is strictly smaller in order.  Only two upper guarantees have been compared; no risk lower bound for the ACE statistic follows.
