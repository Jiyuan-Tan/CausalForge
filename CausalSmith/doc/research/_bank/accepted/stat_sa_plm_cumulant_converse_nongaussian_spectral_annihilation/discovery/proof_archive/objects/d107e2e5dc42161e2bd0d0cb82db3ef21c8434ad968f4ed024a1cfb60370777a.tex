Three rigorous benchmarks are available.

First, the local specialization of the published order-\(r\) ACE theorem in
\(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives, on the class
\(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) and under its displayed positivity and exact
eligibility conditions,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)\\
 &\quad\le
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{38mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
This is an oracle upper benchmark, not a selector or a minimax rate.

Second, on the explicit fourth-cumulant path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=\lvert\kappa_4(\eta_a)\rvert=2a^4,
\]
the computable clipped sine-ratio estimator based on the known zero
\(t_a=\pi/(2a)\) satisfies, whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \leq\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the pathwise bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[
   (\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2
 \right]
 \leq
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(
    \frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is construction-specific and supplies no converse for a class defined
only by a fourth-cumulant lower bound.

Third, applying \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) at separation \(\delta_n\)
gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\leq A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
With \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), the nuisance-transform calculation preserves
exact contour identification whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \leq\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
For each fixed \(n\), treating the positive number \(\delta_n\) as a fixed
class constant, the compactness argument in
\(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\) also supplies some finite bank and some
positive modulus \(a_{\star,n}\).  Consequently the fixed-separation proof
gives an existential bound of the form
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[
  (\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2
 \right]
 \leq \frac{C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)}{n}
\]
under a non-effective threshold
\(\varepsilon_{1,n}\leq\varepsilon_{0}(\delta_n)\).  Neither
\(a_{\star,n}\), \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)\), nor the size and radii of
the bank have a proved quantitative dependence on \(\delta_n\).  Thus this
last display is pointwise in \(n\) and is not an asymptotic rate statement.
The established arguments control zero location and nuisance stability, but
not uniform contour conditioning, the error of a growing-library selector,
a studentized limit law or Wald coverage, or a matching local minimax lower
bound.
