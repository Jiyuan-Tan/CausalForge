Fix \(n\) and \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).  By the bounded-observed-outcome restriction and conditional Jensen's inequality,
\[
 \left|E_P[Y\mid X,T]\right|
 \le E_P[|Y|\mid X,T]
 \le C_q
 \quad\text{almost surely}. \tag{1}
\]
The same argument conditional only on \(X\) gives
\[
 |q_0(X)|=|E_P[Y\mid X]|
 \le C_q
 \quad\text{almost surely}. \tag{2}
\]
By the definition
\(
 \xi=Y-q_0(X)-\theta_0\eta
\)
and the class's conditional-mean restriction,
\[
 0=E_P[\xi\mid X,T]
  =E_P[Y\mid X,T]-q_0(X)-\theta_0\eta.
\]
Therefore
\[
 E_P[Y\mid X,T]=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta
 \quad\text{almost surely}. \tag{3}
\]
Suppose for contradiction that \(\theta_0\ne0\).  The Gaussian restriction is \(\eta\sim N(0,\sigma^2)\), with \(\sigma>0\), so
\[
 P\!\left(|\eta|>\frac{2C_q}{|\theta_0|}\right)>0. \tag{4}
\]
On the event in \((4)\), equation \((2)\) and the reverse triangle inequality imply
\[
 |q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta|
 \ge |\theta_0|\,|\eta|-|q_0(X)|
 >C_q.
\]
This contradicts \((1)\) and \((3)\).  Hence \(\theta_0(P)=0\) for every member of the class.

Now take the single measurable statistic \(\widetilde\theta\equiv0\).  For every class member,
\[
 E_P[(\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P))^2]=0
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P)|)=0.
\]
Both minimax losses are nonnegative by definition, while this common statistic makes their suprema zero.  Thus
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}=0,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}=0.
\]
The lower bound formerly attributed to Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis is strictly positive because its displayed expression contains the positive term \(c_\gamma\sigma^{-1}n^{-1/2}\).  It therefore cannot hold on the present zero-risk class.  The attested source theorem concerns a different source class, so citation cannot repair this class mismatch.
