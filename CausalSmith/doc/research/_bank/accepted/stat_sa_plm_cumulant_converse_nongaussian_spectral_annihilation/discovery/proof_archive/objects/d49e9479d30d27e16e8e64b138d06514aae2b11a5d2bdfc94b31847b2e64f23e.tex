This node records an unresolved research agenda and is not an existence claim.  For triangular treatment-noise laws with \(|\kappa_k(\eta_n)|\ge\delta_n\downarrow0\) under the same supplied code sequences, the agenda asks whether one can construct a data-driven selector among ordinary DML, finite-order ACE, and global-contour procedures that attains a sharp minimax mean-squared-error rate as a function of \((n,\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n},\delta_n)\), yields uniformly valid inference across regimes, and admits a matching local minimax lower bound.  The present paper specifies no unique sharp-rate functional or uniform-inference criterion and asserts no such selector, confidence procedure, or matching local converse.
