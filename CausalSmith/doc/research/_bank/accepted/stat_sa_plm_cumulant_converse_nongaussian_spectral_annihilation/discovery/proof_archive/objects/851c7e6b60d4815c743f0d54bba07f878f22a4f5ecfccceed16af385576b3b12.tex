Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) and write \(\psi=\psi_{\eta}\).  We prove in turn the zero-count budget, the existence of a separated grid circle, the uniform boundary modulus, and effectiveness.

By \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\), \(M\) has a zero in \(\{|z|\le R_0\}\), and its proof also gives
\[
 |M(z)|\le \exp(4\psi^2|z|^2),\qquad z\in\mathbb C. \tag{1}
\]
Put \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1\) and \(R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_1+2\).  Jensen's identity, obtained by factoring the finitely many zeros in a disk and applying the mean-value identity to the remaining zero-free factor, implies that the number \(N\), with multiplicity, of zeros satisfying \(|a|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) obeys
\[
 N\log\frac{R_{\mathrm{out}}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}}
 \le4\psi^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2. \tag{2}
\]
Indeed, first apply the identity on zero-free radii increasing to \(R_{\mathrm{out}}\); each zero inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) contributes at least \(\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})\), and (1) bounds the boundary average.

For \(x>0\), integration of \((1+t)^{-1}\) on \([0,x]\) gives
\[
 \log(1+x)\ge \frac{x}{1+x}. \tag{3}
\]
Since \(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}}=1+1/(R_1+1)\), (2)--(3) yield
\[
 N\le4\psi^2(R_1+2)^3. \tag{4}
\]
The certified intervals used in \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\) contain \(\psi\) and \(R_1\), so \(\psi\le U_{\psi}\) and \(R_1\le U_R\).  Hence (4) gives \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\).

There are \(J=2^{m_{\star}-1}+1>N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) grid radii, all in \((R_0,R_1)\), with spacing \(d_{\star}\).  Since \(2h_{\star}<d_{\star}\), a fixed zero modulus can lie within distance \(h_{\star}\) of at most one grid radius.  Every zero that could violate this separation has modulus below \(R_1+h_{\star}<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), so there are at most \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) such zeros.  The pigeonhole principle therefore supplies a grid radius \(\rho_j\) such that
\[
 \bigl||a|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge h_{\star} \tag{5}
\]
for every zero \(a\) in the disk of radius \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\).  Thus \(M\) is zero-free on \(C_j\).  Since \(\rho_j>R_0\), the localized zero is strictly inside \(C_j\), whence \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\).

Let \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\) be the zeros in \(|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), repeated by multiplicity, and form
\[
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^N
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}.
\]
After removable singularities are filled, \(f=M/Q\) is holomorphic and zero-free in that disk, while every factor of \(Q\) has modulus one on its boundary.  Applying Cauchy's formula to \(f^m\), taking the \(m\)-th root, and letting \(m\to\infty\) in (1) gives
\[
 |f(z)|\le e^A,qquad A=4\psi^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2. \tag{6}
\]
Moreover, \(|Q(0)|\le1=M(0)\), so \(|f(0)|\ge1\).  The function \(u=A-\log|f|\) is nonnegative and harmonic, with \(u(0)\le A\).  The Poisson kernel on a circle of radius increasing to \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) is bounded, for \(|z|\le R_1\), by
\[
 \Lambda_{\star}
 =\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}-R_1}.
\]
The mean-value identity thus gives \(u(z)\le\Lambda_{\star}A\), and hence
\[
 |f(z)|\ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}. \tag{7}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), (5) gives, factor by factor,
\[
 \left|
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}
 \right|
 \ge\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}. \tag{8}
\]
Because this base lies in \((0,1)\) and \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\), (7)--(8) imply
\[
 \inf_{C_j}|M|
 \ge e^{-8R_1\psi^2(R_1+1)^2}
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{2R_1+1}\right)^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}}. \tag{9}
\]
The integer bounds satisfy
\[
 8R_1\psi^2(R_1+1)^2\le e_{\star},
 \qquad
 \frac{h_{\star}}{2R_1+1}
 \ge\frac{2^{-m_{\star}}}{3(2U_R+1)}.
\]
Since \(\exp(1)<3\), since \(3^{-e_{\star}}\ge2^{-2e_{\star}}\), and since \(2^{d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}}\ge d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}=3(2U_R+1)\), (9) is at least
\[
 2^{-2e_{\star}}
 \left\{2^{-(m_{\star}+d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}})}\right\}^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}}
 =2^{-u_{\star}}=a_{\star}. \tag{10}
\]
This proves the asserted modulus.
