The straight-line homotopy from \(F_n\) to \(\widehat F_{0,n}\) remains nonzero on \(C_{j_{\star}}\), so winding number is invariant.  Thus the certified pilot count is positive and \(j_{\star}\) is admissible.

 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}

The estimator definition uses finite sums, deterministic interval refinements with positive stopping margins, deterministic tie-breaking, projection, and fallback.
