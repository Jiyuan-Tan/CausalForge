The strengthened theorem now gives matched \(\Theta(n^{-1})\) minimax MSE on both the broad spectral class and the full published ACE class at every fixed positive separation. This does not close the shrinking-separation problem: its constants may deteriorate as \(\delta\downarrow0\), the local ACE result is only a generalized-quantile upper bound under its explicit eligibility condition, and the Gaussian--Rademacher calculation is an upper bound on one path rather than a converse for the cumulant-only triangular class. No declared node supplies a uniformly consistent regime test, post-selection coverage control, or a least-favorable triangular family for a local lower bound.
