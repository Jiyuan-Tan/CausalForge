Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).  By \(\text{def:ace-comparison-subclass}\), \(P\) satisfies every member property of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) and also
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n},
 \qquad
 \|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{2,n}.
\]
Since \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r+1>r\), Lyapunov's inequality, with its usual \(L^{\infty}\) interpretation, gives
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}
 \le\|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n},
\]
and the identical argument gives \(\|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{2,n}\).  All remaining member properties in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) are already properties of the broad non-Gaussian class.  Hence
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.
\]
This is only a one-way inclusion for the explicitly restricted comparison subclass.

Suppose that \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\).  Applying \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), and then restricting the supremum to the subclass, yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)\\
 &\quad\le C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Big[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{31mm}+64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Big]
 =B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}.
\end{aligned}
\]

For the boundary value \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\), fix \(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n}]\) and enlarge only the outcome-code radius at index \(n\) from zero to \(u\).  The zero-radius class is contained in the enlarged class, and
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},u,(\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =\max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,(\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and hence eligibility, is unchanged.  Apply the positive-radius bound and let \(u\downarrow0\).  This proves the boundary formula; positivity of \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\) is exactly what preserves the maximum.

Finally suppose \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\).  The comparison subclass is contained in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  If it is nonempty, \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{\frac{C}{\gamma n}};
\]
if it is empty, the assertion is vacuous.  Along any sequence for which
\[
 A_n:=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2},
\]
the positive prefactors imply \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\asymp A_n\), and hence \(n^{-1/2}=o(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma})\).  The spectral generalized-quantile upper guarantee is therefore strictly smaller in order.  This compares only upper guarantees and supplies no lower bound for ACE.
