Assume the class is nonempty and fix one member \(P^{\circ}\).  Preserve its joint law of \((X,T)\), hence also the laws and functions \(g_0\), \(q_0\), \(\eta\), and the deterministic codes.  Choose \(\tau>0\), depending only on \(\psi_{\xi}\), so small that a centered \(N(0,\tau^2)\) variable has Luxemburg \(\psi_2\)-norm at most \(\psi_{\xi}\), and let \(\xi_{\star}\sim N(0,\tau^2)\) be independent of \((X,T)\).  Put
\[
 Y_{\vartheta}=q_0(X)+\vartheta\eta+\xi_{\star},
 \qquad |\vartheta|\le c_0:=\min\{C_{\theta},1\}.
\]
Because \(E_P(\eta\mid X)=0\), \(E_P(Y_{\vartheta}\mid X)=q_0(X)\).  Its partially linear coefficient is \(\vartheta\), its outcome innovation is \(\xi_{\star}\), and
\(E_P(\xi_{\star}\mid X,T)=0\).  Every treatment-side condition, the bound on \(q_0\), the code restrictions, and all cumulant and tail conditions for \(\eta\) are unchanged.  Thus every resulting law \(P_{n,\vartheta}\) belongs to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), and all objects asserted to be common really are common.

Conditional on \((X,T)\), two path laws differ only in the means of normals with common variance \(\tau^2\).  Hence
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,\vartheta_0}\Vert P_{n,\vartheta_1})
 =\frac{(\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0)^2}{2\tau^2}E_P[\eta^2].
\]
Jensen's inequality and the exact Luxemburg condition give
\(E_P\eta^2\le\psi_{\eta}^2\log2\).  Additivity of relative entropy under independent products therefore yields, when \(|\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0|=h/\sqrt n\),
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,\vartheta_0}^{\otimes n}\Vert
 P_{n,\vartheta_1}^{\otimes n})
 \le C_0h^2,
 \qquad C_0=\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2\log2}{2\tau^2}.
\]
The constants depend only on fixed class constants.
