At every fixed \(\delta>0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives a computable certified statistic with uniform mean-squared error at most \(C/n\) whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\) supplies the matching \(c/n\) converse.  Along \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives the exact local ACE generalized-quantile upper benchmark proportional to \(\delta_n^{-1}\) under its displayed eligibility condition.  On the Gaussian--Rademacher path, writing \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), the same theorem gives the computable mean-squared-error upper bound
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}
\]
whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  These results close the fixed-separation problem and give two honest local upper benchmarks, but not the requested full shrinking-separation answer.
