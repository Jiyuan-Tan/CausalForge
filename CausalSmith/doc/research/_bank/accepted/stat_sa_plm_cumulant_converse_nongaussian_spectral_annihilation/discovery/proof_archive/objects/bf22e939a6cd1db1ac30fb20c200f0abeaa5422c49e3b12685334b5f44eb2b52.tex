The stated Orlicz convention implies the tail inequality \(P(|\eta|>u)\le2e^{-u^2/\psi_{\eta}^2}\).  Integration by parts therefore gives \(E|\eta|^p\le2\psi_{\eta}^p\Gamma(p/2+1)\) for every \(p>0\).  Because \(E\eta=0\), expansion from order two, grouping consecutive even and odd terms, and the elementary bounds \(\Gamma(q+1)=q!\) and \((2q)!\ge q!\,q!\) yield
\[
 E[e^{t\eta}]
 \le1+\sum_{p=2}^{\infty}\frac{|t|^pE|\eta|^p}{p!}
 \le \exp(4\psi_{\eta}^2t^2).
\]
Thus, for complex \(z\),
\[
 |M(z)|\le E[e^{\operatorname{Re}(z)\eta}]
 \le e^{4\psi_{\eta}^2|z|^2},
 \qquad M(0)=1. \tag{1}
\]
Let \(u_q=R_{\mathrm{out}}-1/q\), restricted to sufficiently large \(q\) that \(u_q>R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), and perturb it downward if necessary so that \(M\) has no zero on \(|z|=u_q\).  Then \(u_q\uparrow R_{\mathrm{out}}\).  Jensen's formula and (1) give
\[
 \sum_{|a|<u_q}\log\frac{u_q}{|a|}
 =\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log|M(u_qe^{it})|\,dt
 \le4\psi_{\eta}^2u_q^2. \tag{2}
\]
Every zero with \(|a|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) contributes at least \(\log(u_q/R_{\mathrm{fac}})\).  Passing through these radii increasing to the outer radius from below in (2) gives
\[
 N_M(R_{\mathrm{fac}})\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})
 \le4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2.
\]
The logarithm is positive because \(R_{\mathrm{out}}>R_{\mathrm{fac}}>0\), and taking the ceiling proves the claim.  Zeros on \(|z|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) are included by the non-strict inequality and cause no boundary exception.
