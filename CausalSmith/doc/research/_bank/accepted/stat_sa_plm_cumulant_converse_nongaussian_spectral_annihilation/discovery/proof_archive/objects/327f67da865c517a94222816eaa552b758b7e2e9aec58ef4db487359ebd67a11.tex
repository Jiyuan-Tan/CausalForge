We prove the representation claim, the uniform upper bound, the two minimax converses, and the quantile and comparator conclusions.

\emph{1. Mathematical and represented-data layers.}  Unfold \(\text{def:certified-contour-arithmetic-substrate}\).  The real interval formulas are sound because the extrema of a bilinear function on a rectangle occur at its corners.  Substitution into
\[
 (x+iy)(u+iv)=(xu-yv)+i(xv+yu)
\]
proves complex multiplication containment.  The square lower and upper formulas enclose each coordinate square, rational square-root bisection preserves its bracketing invariant, and a positive lower modulus certificate makes \(u^2+v^2\) strictly positive.  The displayed conjugate formula and outward division therefore prove guarded complex-division containment.

Machin's identity and the alternating remainder prove the certified \(\pi\) contract with the displayed finite cutoff.  On a bounded input interval, the Lagrange remainder bounds and the explicit cutoff in the definition prove the real exponential, sine, and cosine contracts.  Hence
\[
 e^{x+iy}=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)
\]
proves the complex-exponential rectangle contract.  Finite induction proves the sum and product contracts.  On every mesh cell, a function with Lipschitz constant \(L\) differs from its node value by at most \(L/N_{\mathrm{mesh}}\); integration and taking finite endpoint minima prove the quadrature and boundary-infimum contracts.  Every cutoff is an explicit integer formula and every operation is finite.

Consequently, if a concrete module implementing these algorithms and contracts is added and compiled, the represented-data procedure in \(\text{def:adaptive-contour-estimator}\) is a total transducer.  Its output is a certified-real record because the final clipping formula
\[
 \frac{|y+C_{\theta}|-|y-C_{\theta}|}{2}
\]
is a composition of implemented certified operations and equals \(\Pi_{[-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]}(y)\) by the three cases \(y<-C_{\theta}\), \(|y|\le C_{\theta}\), and \(y>C_{\theta}\).  No undecidable comparison is used.  Identical canonical input records make the same finite modulus calls and rational comparisons, hence have identical traces and output records.  This is the claimed conditional represented-data conclusion, and it makes no assertion about arbitrary alternative names.

Independently of compilation, the canonical floor-dyadic interpretation defines the ordinary-sample statistic mathematically.  For a fixed terminal trace, every queried endpoint is a floor of a scaled sample coordinate, and is therefore Borel measurable.  Rational arithmetic and rational endpoint comparisons preserve Borel measurability.  The set of finite traces is countable, so partitioning the sample space by terminal trace proves that \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) is a total Borel statistic.  Inspection of its record shows that it uses \(\bar g_n\), no supplied outcome code, and no treatment-noise law.

\emph{2. Uniform upper bound.}  Fix \(n\ge2\), \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\).  Let \(j_{\star}\) be supplied by \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\).  By \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\),
\[
 N_{C_{j_{\star}}}(F_n)\ge1,
 \qquad
 \inf_{C_{j_{\star}}}|F_n|\ge\frac{3a_{\star}}4. \tag{1}
\]
For \(a\in\{0,1\}\), put
\[
 d_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|,
 \quad
 e_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|,
 \quad
 \mathcal A_a=\{d_a\le a_{\star}/8\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\), soundness and the width \(a_{\star}/64\) of the certified modulus interval give the candidate \(j_{\star}\) a lower endpoint at least
\[
 \left(\frac34-\frac18-\frac1{64}\right)a_{\star}
 =\frac{39a_{\star}}{64}. \tag{2}
\]
The straight-line homotopy between \(F_n\) and \(\widehat F_{0,n}\) is nonzero on this circle, so their winding numbers agree and are positive.  Thus \(j_{\star}\) is admissible.

Let \(C=C_{\widehat j_n}\).  Maximality of the selected rational lower endpoint, (2), and subtraction of \(d_0\) give
\[
 \inf_C|F_n|\ge\frac{31a_{\star}}{64}. \tag{3}
\]
The same homotopy and the sound winding enclosure give
\[
 \widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}=N_C(F_n)=:N\ge1. \tag{4}
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\cap\mathcal A_1\), the evaluation empirical modulus is at least \(23a_{\star}/64\); after the enclosure width is subtracted, its lower endpoint exceeds \(a_{\star}/4\).  Hence the evaluation branch does not fall back.

There is a constant \(C_G<\infty\), depending only on \(C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi},R_1\), such that
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R_1}|G_n(z)|\le C_G. \tag{5}
\]
Indeed, \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), \(Z_n=\eta+D_n\), and \(Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi\).  The exact Luxemburg inequalities and Young's inequality give
\[
 E_Pe^{t|\eta|}\le\sqrt2e^{t^2\psi_{\eta}^2/2},
\]
and the same convention bounds the second moments of \(\eta\) and \(\xi\).  Cauchy--Schwarz then bounds \(E_P[|Y|e^{R_1|Z_n|}]\), proving (5) without independence between \(\xi\) and \(\eta\).

Subtract the empirical and population ratios on \(C\).  From (3)--(5), \(\operatorname{len}(C)\le2\pi R_1\), and \(N\ge1\),
\[
 \left|
 \frac1{N2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{\widehat G_{1,n}}{\widehat F_{1,n}}\,dz
 -\frac1{N2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{G_n}{F_n}\,dz
 \right|
 \le L_{\mathrm{rat}}(d_1+e_1), \tag{6}
\]
where, with \(\mu=31a_{\star}/64\) and \(\nu=23a_{\star}/64\), one may take
\[
 L_{\mathrm{rat}}=R_1\max\{\nu^{-1},C_G(\mu\nu)^{-1}\}. \tag{7}
\]
The hypotheses of \(\text{thm:exact-contour-identification}\) hold: (3) makes \(F_n\) zero-free on \(C\), \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\) makes \(H_n\) zero-free on and inside \(C\), and (4) supplies a positive count.  Hence the population contour in (6) equals the causal target \(\theta_0(P)\).  The final rational midpoint adds at most \(1/n\), and real part followed by projection onto \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\) cannot increase distance from \(\theta_0(P)\).

Both fold sizes are at least \(n/3\).  Therefore \(\text{lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2}\) and Markov's inequality give, for its constant \(K\),
\[
 E_P(d_a^2+e_a^2)\le\frac{3K}{n},
 \qquad
 P(\mathcal A_0^c\cup\mathcal A_1^c)
 \le\frac{384K}{a_{\star}^2n}. \tag{8}
\]
Projection bounds squared error by \(4C_{\theta}^2\) on the exceptional event.  On the good event, (6), \((d_1+e_1)^2\le2(d_1^2+e_1^2)\), and the numerical error give
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le\frac{C_{\mathrm{up}}}{n}, \tag{9}
\]
with, for example,
\[
 C_{\mathrm{up}}
 =12L_{\mathrm{rat}}^2K
 +\frac{1536C_{\theta}^2K}{a_{\star}^2}+2. \tag{10}
\]
The bank, \(a_{\star}\), \(C_G\), \(K\), and \(L_{\mathrm{rat}}\) depend only on \(r,\delta,C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi}\).  A width-one refinement of any valid supplied name has an upper endpoint at most its represented value plus one, so these bounds are also uniform over supplied names and traces.  Thus \(C_{\mathrm{up}}\) is fixed outside the quantifiers over \(P,n\), names, and traces, and is independent of \(\gamma\).  Since the Borel statistic is one data-driven rule, (9) proves the minimax upper bound.

\emph{3. Converses and quantiles.}  Apply the first branch of \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\).  Choose fixed \(h>0\), depending only on class constants, such that \(\vartheta_{\pm}=\pm h/(2\sqrt n)\) lie on its path and the product relative entropy is at most \(1/8\).  Pinsker's inequality gives total variation at most \(1/4\).  Testing by the sign of an arbitrary estimator yields
\[
 \max_{v\in\{-,+\}}
 E_{P_{n,\vartheta_v}}[(\widetilde\theta-\vartheta_v)^2]
 \ge\frac{h^2}{8n}(1-1/4)
 =\frac{3h^2}{32n}. \tag{11}
\]
Thus \(c_{\mathrm{NG}}/n\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\).

By \(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}. \tag{12}
\]
Restriction of (9) proves the spectral upper bound on the full published ACE class.  The second branch of \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\) gives on this same class
\[
 \inf_{\widetilde\theta}
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 E_P[(\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \ge\frac{c_{\mathrm{ACE}}}{n}. \tag{13}
\]
The Borel statistic is admissible in this infimum.  Taking
\[
 c=\min\{c_{\mathrm{NG}},c_{\mathrm{ACE}}\}>0,
 \qquad C=C_{\mathrm{up}}
\]
proves both MSE brackets.

Finally, (9) and Markov's inequality give
\[
 P\left(
 |\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|
 >\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}
 \right)\le\gamma,
\]
which is the stated generalized-quantile bound; (12) gives its ACE-class restriction.  The class-relation lemma also proves the \(L^s(P_X)\) subclass statement and equality at \(s=r\), but no reverse inclusion for the broad spectral class.  The constant \(C_{\gamma}\) belongs only to the cited ACE theorem: for fixed \(\gamma\) it is chosen outside all \(P,n\) quantifiers, is not an algorithm input, and does not enter (9)--(10).  The lower bound (13) is over all estimators, not the ACE estimator.  Every constant may deteriorate as fixed \(\delta\) decreases; no shrinking-separation rate, adaptive switch, uniform inference result, or local converse has been proved.
