Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) and \(z\in\mathbb C\).  Since \(D_n\) is measurable with respect to \(X\), the independence \(\eta\perp X\) gives
\[
 F_n(z)
 =E_P[e^{z(\eta+D_n)}]
 =E_P[e^{z\eta}]E_P[e^{zD_n}]
 =M(z)H_n(z).
\]
All transforms in this display are entire: \(D_n\) is bounded by the clipping and range restrictions, while the sub-Gaussian bound on \(\eta\) supplies an integrable exponential envelope on every compact subset of \(\mathbb C\).  The same envelope permits differentiation under the expectation, so
\[
 F_n'(z)=E_P[Z_ne^{zZ_n}].
\]
Using the identity \(Y=\theta_0Z_n+b_n(X)+\xi\), we obtain
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 G_n(z)
 &=\theta_0E_P[Z_ne^{zZ_n}]
   +E_P[b_n(X)e^{zZ_n}]
   +E_P[\xi e^{zZ_n}]\\
 &=\theta_0F_n'(z)
   +E_P[b_n(X)e^{zD_n}e^{z\eta}].
 \end{aligned}
\]
Indeed, \(e^{zZ_n}\) is measurable with respect to \((X,T)\), and therefore the outcome mean-independence restriction gives
\[
 E_P[\xi e^{zZ_n}]
 =E_P\!\left[e^{zZ_n}E_P[\xi\mid X,T]\right]=0.
\]
Finally, \(b_n(X)e^{zD_n}\) is measurable with respect to \(X\), so a second use of \(\eta\perp X\) gives
\[
 E_P[b_n(X)e^{zD_n}e^{z\eta}]
 =E_P[e^{z\eta}]E_P[b_n(X)e^{zD_n}]
 =M(z)B_n(z).
\]
Combining the displays proves both identities for every \(z\in\mathbb C\).
