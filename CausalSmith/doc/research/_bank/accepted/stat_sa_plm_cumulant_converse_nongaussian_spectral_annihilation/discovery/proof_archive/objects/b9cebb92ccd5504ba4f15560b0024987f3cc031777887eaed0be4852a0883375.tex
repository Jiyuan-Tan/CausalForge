The class relations are exactly \(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\).  In particular,
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}, \tag{1}
\]
while for \(s\ge r\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}, \tag{2}
\]
and equality holds at \(s=r\) under the common clipped-code convention.  No reverse inclusion in (1) is supplied.

Fix \(\gamma\), and fix the single constant \(C_{\gamma}\) in the cited theorem before quantifying over \(n\) and \(P\).  If \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 (|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}. \tag{3}
\]
Here \(C_{\gamma}\) is neither sample-dependent nor an input to the spectral statistic.

For the boundary \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\), choose \(0<t<\varepsilon_{1,n}\) and enlarge only the outcome-code radius to \(t\).  The zero-radius class is contained in the enlarged class, and
\[
 \max\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\}
 =
 \max\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\}. \tag{4}
\]
Thus eligibility is unchanged.  Apply (3) at radius \(t\), restrict its supremum to the zero-radius class, and let \(t\downarrow0\).  Only the nonnegative term proportional to \(\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt\) disappears, proving the stated ACE bound for every \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\ge0\).

If also \(n\ge2\), \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and the published class is nonempty, restrict the generalized-quantile conclusion of the proposed form of \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) through (1).  This gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 (|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}. \tag{5}
\]
The constant \(C\) is fixed independently of \(\gamma\).

Put
\[
 S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}.
\]
All other factors in \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) are fixed and positive.  If \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), then
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}}{B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}}
 \longrightarrow0. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (3), (5), and (6) compare two upper guarantees on the full published ACE class.  No step lower-bounds the ACE estimator, proving the final qualification.
