Three rigorous benchmarks remain available, but they do not determine the full-class phase diagram.

First, let \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) be the class obtained from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) by replacing \(|\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)|\ge\delta\) with \(|\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)|\ge\delta_n\), without changing any other restriction, and define
\[
 \widetilde b_{2,n}
 =200\min\{1,C_{\theta}\}\delta_n
 \max\!\left\{
   \varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n},
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})
 \right\}^{-1}.
\]
When \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), all displayed logarithms and denominators are defined, and
\[
 r\le
 \min\!\left\{
   \frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}
   -\frac{1}{a_{1,n}}\log\!\left(\frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}\right),
   \frac{\widetilde b_{2,n}}{a_2\log(a_2\widetilde b_{2,n})}
 \right\},
\]
the established local ACE benchmark is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|\right)\\
 &\quad\le
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{36mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
Together with any separately valid ordinary-DML upper bound, this gives only an oracle envelope, not a data-driven selector or a minimax rate.

Second, on the explicit fourth-cumulant path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=|\kappa_4(\eta_a)|=2a^4,
\]
the clipped sine-ratio estimator using the known zero \(t_a=\pi/(2a)\) satisfies, under the displayed path assumptions and
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the computable bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is a pathwise upper bound, not a rate for the class defined only by a fourth-cumulant lower bound.

Third, zero localization with \(\delta_n\) gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
With \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), the nuisance-transform argument preserves exact contour identification whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1},
\]
because then \(|H_n(z)|\ge3/4\) on the relevant disk. This last condition supplies no quantitative treatment-transform boundary modulus, empirical contour-variance rate, selector-error bound, limit law, coverage theorem, or matching lower bound.
