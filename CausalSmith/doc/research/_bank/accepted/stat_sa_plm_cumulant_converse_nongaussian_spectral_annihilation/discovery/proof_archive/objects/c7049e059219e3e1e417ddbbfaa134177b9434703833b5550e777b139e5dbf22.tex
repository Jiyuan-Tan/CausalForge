Three rigorous benchmarks are available.  First, on the local ACE class \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\), under the positivity conditions and exact local eligibility condition stated in \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}\!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)\\
 &\quad\le C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
This is an oracle upper benchmark, not a data-driven selector or a minimax rate.

Second, on the explicit path \(\eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS\), with \(\Delta_a=|\kappa_4(\eta_a)|=2a^4\), the computable known-zero sine ratio satisfies, whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the pathwise bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{1,
 \frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is construction-specific and is not a converse for the cumulant-only class.

Third, \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) at separation \(\delta_n\) gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k\left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
With \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), exact contour identification remains stable whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
For each fixed \(n\), compactness at the positive value \(\delta_n\) supplies a finite bank and positive modulus, hence only a pointwise existential bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le\frac{C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)}{n}
\]
under a non-effective threshold \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0(\delta_n)\).  No quantitative dependence of the bank size, radii, boundary modulus, or \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)\) on \(\delta_n\) is established, so this pointwise display is not an asymptotic rate.  The sharp full-class phase diagram, a data-driven weak-identification selector, uniform studentized inference with Wald coverage, and matching local minimax lower bounds remain open.
