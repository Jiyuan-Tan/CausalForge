Fix \(n\) and \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\). By the bounded-outcome restriction,
\[
 |Y|\le C_q\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Conditional Jensen's inequality and the definition \(q_0(X)=E_P[Y\mid X]\) therefore give
\[
 \left|E_P[Y\mid X,T]\right|\le C_q,
 \qquad
 |q_0(X)|\le C_q
 \quad\text{almost surely}. \tag{1}
\]
The residual identity is
\[
 Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi.
\]
Because \(\eta=T-g_0(X)\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(X,T)\), and because the class imposes \(E_P[\xi\mid X,T]=0\), conditioning the preceding identity yields
\[
 E_P[Y\mid X,T]=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta
 \quad\text{almost surely}. \tag{2}
\]
Suppose for contradiction that \(\theta_0\ne0\). The class also imposes \(\eta\sim N(0,\sigma^2)\), where \(\sigma>0\). Hence \(\theta_0\eta\) is a nondegenerate centered Gaussian random variable and
\[
 P(\theta_0\eta>2C_q)>0. \tag{3}
\]
On the event in \((3)\), the second inequality in \((1)\) gives
\[
 q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta
 \ge -C_q+\theta_0\eta
 >C_q,
\]
contradicting the first inequality in \((1)\) together with \((2)\). Thus \(\theta_0(P)=0\).

Assume now that \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) is nonempty. For the statistic \(\widetilde\theta\equiv0\), the squared error and the absolute error are identically zero under every member of the class. Therefore
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 E_P[(\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P))^2]=0,
\]
and, by the displayed definition of the generalized quantile,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widetilde\theta-\theta_0(P)|)=0.
\]
Both loss criteria are nonnegative. Taking the infimum over all statistics in \(\text{def:minimax-risks}\) proves \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}=\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}=0\).
