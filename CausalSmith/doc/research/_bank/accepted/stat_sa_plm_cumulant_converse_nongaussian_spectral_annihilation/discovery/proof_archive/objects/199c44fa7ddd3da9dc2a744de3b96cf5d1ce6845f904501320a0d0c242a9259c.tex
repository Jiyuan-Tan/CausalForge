The explicit deterministic bank in \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\) and constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on \(r,\delta,C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi}\), have the following property.  The total certified statistic \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) in \(\text{def:adaptive-contour-estimator}\) is a measurable and computable sample map and, for every \(n\ge2\) such that \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\neq\varnothing\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\),
\[
 \frac cn\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}
 \le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le\frac Cn.
\]
The statistic uses \(\bar g_n\), uses no supplied outcome code, and does not know the law of \(\eta\).  Its MSE bound implies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}.
\]
No inclusion of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) is asserted; the finite-order ACE comparison is confined to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).
