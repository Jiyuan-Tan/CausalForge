For \(n\ge2\), suppose \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\neq\varnothing\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\). There are \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on \(r,\delta,C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi}\), such that
\[
 \frac{c}{n}
 \le \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}
 \le \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le \frac{C}{n}.
\]
The class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) imposes only the displayed \(L^1(P_X)\) treatment-code restriction and imposes no supplied outcome-code restriction. The estimator uses \(\bar g_n\), uses no supplied outcome code, and does not know the law of \(\eta\). Its MSE bound implies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le \sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}.
\]
No inclusion of the broad class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) is asserted; the published ACE comparison is confined to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\) in \(\text{prop:jms-ace-alignment}\).
