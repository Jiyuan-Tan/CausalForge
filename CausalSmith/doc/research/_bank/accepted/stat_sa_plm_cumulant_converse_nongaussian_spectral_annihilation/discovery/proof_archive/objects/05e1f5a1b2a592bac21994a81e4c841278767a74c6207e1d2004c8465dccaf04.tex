The strongest closed result is \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\): under its exact eligibility condition the local ACE generalized-quantile upper bound is explicit, and for \(k=4\) the Gaussian--Rademacher path has a computable sine estimator with risk at most \(C\min\{1,n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\exp[(\pi^2\sqrt2/4)\Delta_a^{-1/2}]\}\) when \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\). At each fixed positive separation, one selected compactness bank gives an existential \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)/n\) bound, but no uniform estimate of \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)\) is proved.
