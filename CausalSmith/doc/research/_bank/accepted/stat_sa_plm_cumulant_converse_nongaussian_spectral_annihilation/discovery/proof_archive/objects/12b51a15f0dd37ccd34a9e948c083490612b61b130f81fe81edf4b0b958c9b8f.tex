The three class relations are exactly \(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\). In particular, every published ACE law belongs to the broad spectral class, whereas no reverse inclusion is used or claimed.

Assume \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\). If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) applies directly on the full class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and gives exactly
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Suppose instead that \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\). For \(t\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\), enlarge only the outcome-code radius from zero to \(t\). Every law in the zero-radius class remains in the radius-\(t\) class. Moreover,
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and hence the eligibility predicate, is unchanged. Apply the positive-radius cited theorem with radius \(t\), restrict its supremum to the zero-radius class, and let \(t\downarrow0\). The right-hand side decreases to the claimed boundary value because the only added term is
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt.
\]
The published order-\(r\) ACE statistic is unchanged with the radius. This proves (1) for every \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\ge0\).

Now also assume \(n\ge2\), \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\neq\varnothing\). The strengthened \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives both
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}
 \tag{2}
\]
and a matched \(\Theta(n^{-1})\) minimax MSE bracket for this class. The latter is a lower bound on the infimum over all estimators, not a lower bound on \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}\), so it does not alter the upper-versus-upper nature of (1)--(2).

Finally put
\[
 S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
     +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}.
\]
All multiplicative quantities in \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) other than \(S_n\) and \(n^{-1/2}\) are fixed positive class constants. Along any eligible sequence with \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), (1) is of order \(S_n\), whereas (2) is of order \(n^{-1/2}\). The ratio of the spectral upper guarantee to the ACE upper guarantee tends to zero. This proves the stated strict order comparison on the full published ACE class, without asserting a lower bound for the ACE estimator.
