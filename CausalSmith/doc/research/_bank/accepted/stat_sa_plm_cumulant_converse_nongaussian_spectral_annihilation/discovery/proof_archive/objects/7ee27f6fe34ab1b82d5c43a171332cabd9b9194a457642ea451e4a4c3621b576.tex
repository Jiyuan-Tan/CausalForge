The construction in \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\) computes, once for all, a finite bank and a positive rational \(a_{\star}\) from certified-real names of the primitive constants.  Put \(m_a=|I_a|\).  For \(n\ge2\), \(m_a\ge n/3\).  Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), and choose an index \(j_{\star}\) guaranteed by that lemma.  By \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\),
\[
 N_{C_{j_{\star}}}(F_n)\ge1,
 \qquad
 \inf_{C_{j_{\star}}}|F_n|ge3a_{\star}/4. \tag{1}
\]
Define
\[
 d_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|,
 \quad
 e_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|,
 \quad
 \mathcal A_a=\{d_a\le a_{\star}/8\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\), (1) gives pilot empirical modulus at least \(5a_{\star}/8\).  The certified lower endpoint differs from the true empirical infimum by at most \(a_{\star}/64\), so \(j_{\star}\) is pilot-admissible.  The homotopy
\(F_n+t(\widehat F_{0,n}-F_n)\), \(0\le t\le1\), remains nonzero on \(C_{j_{\star}}\); hence winding number is invariant and the certified pilot count is positive.

The selected index maximizes the certified lower modulus among admissible indices.  Consequently, still on \(\mathcal A_0\), its empirical boundary modulus is bounded below by the certified lower modulus of \(j_{\star}\), and therefore its population modulus is bounded below by a fixed positive multiple of \(a_{\star}\).  In particular the same homotopy argument applies on the selected circle \(C=C_{\widehat j_n}\), giving
\[
 \widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}=N_C(F_n)=:N\ge1,
 \qquad
 \inf_C|F_n|\ge3a_{\star}/8. \tag{2}
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\cap\mathcal A_1\), the evaluation empirical modulus, after subtracting both the \(a_{\star}/8\) stochastic error and the \(a_{\star}/64\) enclosure width, remains above \(a_{\star}/4\).  Hence the definition does not invoke its fallback.

Uniformly over the class,
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R_1}|G_n(z)|\le C_G<\infty, \tag{3}
\]
where (3) follows from \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), the equation \(Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi\), Cauchy--Schwarz, and the two sub-Gaussian bounds.  Using (2), the quotient identity, and \(\text{thm:exact-contour-identification}\), elementary subtraction of two ratios gives
\[
 \left|
 \frac{1}{N2\pi i}\oint_C
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}\,dz
 -\theta_0
 \right|
 \le L_{\star}(d_1+e_1), \tag{4}
\]
where \(L_{\star}<\infty\) depends only on the fixed primitive constants.  The certified quadrature midpoint adds at most \(1/n\).  Taking its real part and projecting onto \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\) cannot increase its distance from \(\theta_0\).

By \(\text{lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2}\), uniformly in \(P\),
\[
 E_P(d_a^2+e_a^2)\le K_1/n,
 \qquad
 P(\mathcal A_0^c\cup\mathcal A_1^c)\le K_2/n. \tag{5}
\]
On the exceptional event, projection bounds squared error by \(4C_{\theta}^2\).  Equations (4)--(5), including the \(n^{-2}\) deterministic numerical error, imply
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C/n. \tag{6}
\]
The definition uses only finite sums, deterministic interval refinements with positive stopping margins, deterministic tie-breaking, projection, and fallback.  Each operation is Borel measurable and terminates; thus the statistic is a total measurable computable sample map from the observations and certified primitive-constant names.  It uses \(\bar g_n\), but neither a supplied outcome code nor the law of \(\eta\).  Hence it is an admissible minimax rule and \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le C/n\).

For the converse, invoke \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\).  Choose fixed \(h>0\) sufficiently small that \(\vartheta_{\pm}=\pm h/(2\sqrt n)\) belong to the path and the relative entropy between the corresponding product laws is at most \(1/8\).  Pinsker's inequality gives total variation at most \(1/4\).  Testing by the sign of an arbitrary estimator yields
\[
 \max_{v\in\{-,+\}}E_{P_{n,\vartheta_v}}
 [ (\widetilde\theta-\vartheta_v)^2]
 \ge\frac{h^2}{8n}
 \{1-\|P_-^{\otimes n}-P_+^{\otimes n}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\}
 \ge\frac{3h^2}{32n}.
\]
Taking the supremum over the class and then the infimum over estimators proves \(c/n\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\).  Finally, (6) and Markov's inequality give the displayed generalized-quantile bound.  The separate class definitions give the ACE disclaimer.  Every constant here is fixed only for fixed \(\delta>0\); the proof supplies no useful shrinking-\(\delta_n\) rate.
