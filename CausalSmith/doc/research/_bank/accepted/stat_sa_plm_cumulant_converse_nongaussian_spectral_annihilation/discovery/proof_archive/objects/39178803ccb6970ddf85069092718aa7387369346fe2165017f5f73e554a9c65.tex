The established \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives three rigorous pieces.  First, on the local ACE class obtained by replacing \(\delta\) with \(\delta_n\), the exact eligibility condition yields the generalized-quantile upper bound
\[
 U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}
 =C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
  \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
  +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
  +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
   \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\]
Second, on the explicit fourth-cumulant path
\(
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}G_{\circ}+aS
\)
with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), a computable known-zero sine estimator satisfies, when
\(
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le \Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)
\),
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
Third, substituting \(\delta_n\) into \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)},
\]
and the nuisance factor remains zero-free on the corresponding disk if
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\{4(R_{0,n}+1)e^{2C_g(R_{0,n}+1)}\}^{-1}.
\]
These are respectively a finite-order upper benchmark, a path-specific spectral upper bound, and an identification-stability condition.  They do not determine a full-class minimax rate, construct an adaptive selector with a limit law, or provide matching lower bounds.
