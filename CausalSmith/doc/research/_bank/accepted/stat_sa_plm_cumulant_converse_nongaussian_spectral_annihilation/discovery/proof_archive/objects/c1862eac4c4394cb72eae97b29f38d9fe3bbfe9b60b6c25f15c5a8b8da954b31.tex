\emph{Uniform upper bound.}  Put \(m_a=|I_a|\).  For \(n\ge2\), \(m_a\ge n/3\).  Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), and choose \(j_{\star}\) from \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\).  By \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\),
\[
 N_{C_{j_{\star}}}(F_n)\ge1,
 \qquad
 \inf_{C_{j_{\star}}}|F_n|
 \ge\frac{3a_{\star}}4. \tag{1}
\]
Define
\[
 d_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|,
 \quad
 e_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|,
 \quad
 \mathcal A_a=\{d_a\le a_{\star}/8\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\), the certified pilot lower endpoint for \(j_{\star}\) is at least
\[
 \left(\frac34-\frac18-\frac1{64}\right)a_{\star}
 =\frac{39}{64}a_{\star}. \tag{2}
\]
The straight-line homotopy from \(F_n\) to \(\widehat F_{0,n}\) is nonzero on \(C_{j_{\star}}\), so the empirical winding count equals the positive population count and \(j_{\star}\) is admissible.

Let \(C=C_{\widehat j_n}\).  Maximality of the selected certified lower endpoint and (2) imply, after subtracting \(d_0\),
\[
 \inf_C|F_n|
 \ge\left(\frac{39}{64}-\frac18\right)a_{\star}
 =\frac{31}{64}a_{\star}. \tag{3}
\]
The same homotopy argument on the selected circle gives
\[
 \widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}=N_C(F_n)=:N\ge1. \tag{4}
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\cap\mathcal A_1\), (3) gives an evaluation empirical modulus of at least \(23a_{\star}/64\); subtracting the enclosure width gives the certified lower endpoint
\[
 \frac{22}{64}a_{\star}>\frac14a_{\star}. \tag{5}
\]
Thus no fallback occurs on this event.

There is a finite class-uniform constant \(C_G\) such that
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R_1}|G_n(z)|\le C_G. \tag{6}
\]
Indeed, \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), \(Z_n=\eta+D_n\), and \(Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi\).  Cauchy--Schwarz, the range restrictions, and the two Luxemburg bounds uniformly control \(E_P[|Y|e^{R_1|Z_n|}]\), without requiring independence of \(\xi\) and \(\eta\).  On \(\mathcal A_0\cap\mathcal A_1\), subtracting the two ratios, using (3), (5), and (6), and using \(\operatorname{len}(C)\le2\pi R_1\) gives
\[
 \left|
 \frac{1}{N2\pi i}\oint_C
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}\,dz
 -
 \frac{1}{N2\pi i}\oint_C
 \frac{G_n(z)}{F_n(z)}\,dz
 \right|
 \le L_{\star}(d_1+e_1), \tag{7}
\]
where, with \(\mu=31a_{\star}/64\) and \(\nu=23a_{\star}/64\), one may take
\[
 L_{\star}=R_1\max\{\nu^{-1},C_G(\mu\nu)^{-1}\}.
\]
The hypotheses of \(\text{thm:exact-contour-identification}\) hold: (3) makes \(F_n\) zero-free on \(C\), \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\) makes \(H_n\) zero-free on and inside \(C\), and (4) gives the positive zero count.  The population contour functional in (7) is therefore the causal target \(\theta_0\).  The canonical numerical midpoint adds complex error at most \(1/n\), and taking its real part and projecting onto \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\) cannot increase distance from \(\theta_0\).

By \(\text{lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2}\), uniformly in \(P\),
\[
 E_P(d_a^2+e_a^2)\le\frac{3K}{n},
 \qquad
 P(\mathcal A_0^c\cup\mathcal A_1^c)
 \le\frac{384K}{a_{\star}^2n}. \tag{8}
\]
On the exceptional event, projection bounds squared error by \(4C_{\theta}^2\).  On the good event, (7) and the numerical error contribute at most \(12L_{\star}^2K/n+2/n^2\).  Hence
\[
 C=12L_{\star}^2K+\frac{1536C_{\theta}^2K}{a_{\star}^2}+2
\]
is valid and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le\frac Cn. \tag{9}
\]
The bank, \(a_{\star}\), \(C_G\), \(K\), and \(L_{\star}\) depend only on the displayed fixed class constants.  The canonical statistic is therefore one admissible data-driven minimax rule, proving \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le C/n\).

\emph{Converse and quantile consequence.}  Invoke \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\).  Choose fixed \(h>0\), depending only on the class constants, small enough that \(\vartheta_{\pm}=\pm h/(2\sqrt n)\) belong to the path for every \(n\ge2\) and the product relative entropy is at most \(1/8\).  Pinsker's inequality gives total variation at most \(1/4\).  Testing by the sign of an arbitrary estimator yields
\[
 \max_{v\in\{-,+\}}E_{P_{n,\vartheta_v}}
 [ (\widetilde\theta-\vartheta_v)^2]
 \ge\frac{h^2}{8n}
 \{1-\|P_-^{\otimes n}-P_+^{\otimes n}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\}
 \ge\frac{3h^2}{32n}.
\]
Taking the supremum over the class and the infimum over estimators proves \(c/n\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\), with \(c=3h^2/32\).  Finally, (9) and Markov's inequality give
\[
 P\!\left(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|>
 \sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}\right)\le\gamma,
\]
which is exactly the displayed generalized-quantile bound.  The separate broad and comparison-class definitions prove the ACE scope disclaimer.
