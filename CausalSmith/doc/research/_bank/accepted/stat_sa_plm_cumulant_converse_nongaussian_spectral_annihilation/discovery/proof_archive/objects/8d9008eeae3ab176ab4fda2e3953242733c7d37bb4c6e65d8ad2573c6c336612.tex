The strongest closed benchmark is \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\).  Under its exact local eligibility condition, the order-\(r\) ACE generalized-quantile upper bound equals the published fixed-separation expression with \(\delta^{-1}\) replaced by \(\delta_n^{-1}\).  On the explicit path \(\eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}G_\circ+aS\), with \(\Delta_a=2a^4=|\kappa_4(\eta_a)|\), its computable sine estimator obeys
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{1,
  \frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}
\]
whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  More generally, \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k(\psi_\eta^2/\delta_n)^{1/(k-2)},
\]
and the nuisance-factor calculation preserves exact contour identification whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\{4(R_{0,n}+1)e^{2C_g(R_{0,n}+1)}\}^{-1}.
\]
The latter is an identification and stability benchmark, not a uniform risk rate or a matching minimax phase diagram.
