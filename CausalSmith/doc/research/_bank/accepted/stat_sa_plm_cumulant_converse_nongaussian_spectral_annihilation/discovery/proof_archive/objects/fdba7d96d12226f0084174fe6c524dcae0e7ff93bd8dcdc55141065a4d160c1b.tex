\cite{Robinson1988} supplies the partially linear conditional-mean model, and \cite{ChernozhukovEtAl2018DML} supplies the familiar orthogonal-score and sample-splitting background.  \cite{MackeySyrgkanisZadik2018} and \cite[Theorem 5.4]{JinMackeySyrgkanis2025} motivate the contrast with finite-order non-Gaussian orthogonality and ACE.  The narrow classical antecedent is location-family completeness: \cite{Mattner1992} concerns transform zeros, translated moments, and completeness, but does not establish the learned-residual contour identity, its uniform compactness bank, or the causal minimax theorem here.  \cite{HuShiu2022} informs observable-zero testing, \cite{ChackoViceira2003} is a complex-moment GMM precedent, and \cite{ReizingerEtAl2025} provides adjacent non-Gaussian treatment-effect motivation.  None of these references is invoked as an effective construction of the once-for-all bank.  The Gaussian lower-bound comparison from \cite[Theorem 3.2]{JinMackeySyrgkanis2025} is kept on its separately defined class rather than presented as a common-class inclusion theorem.
