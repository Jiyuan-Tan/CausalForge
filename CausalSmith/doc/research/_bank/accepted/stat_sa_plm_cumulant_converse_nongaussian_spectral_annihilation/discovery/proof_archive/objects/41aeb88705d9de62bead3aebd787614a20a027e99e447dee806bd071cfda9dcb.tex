The certified-real convention is the following.  The integer \(k\) is given exactly, and each of the positive reals \(\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1\) is supplied by its canonical nested dyadic enclosure name.  No transform, distribution, oracle, or sample-dependent object is an input.  Put
\[
 R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1,
 \qquad R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_{\mathrm{fac}}+1,
 \qquad
 Q_{\star}=
 \frac{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2}
      {\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})}.
\]
Refine outward intervals until the positive denominator in \(Q_{\star}\) has a positive rational lower endpoint and \(Q_{\star}\) has a finite rational upper endpoint \(U_{\star}\), and set \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}=\lceil U_{\star}\rceil\).  For \(m\ge3\), define
\[
 \mathcal Q_m=
 \{R_0+1/4+q2^{-m}:q\in\mathbb Z,\ 0\le q2^{-m}\le1/2\}.
\]
Let \(m_{\star}\) be the least \(m\ge3\) for which \(|\mathcal Q_m|>N_{\mathrm{cert}}\), enumerate \(\mathcal Q_{m_{\star}}\) increasingly as \(\rho_1<\cdots<\rho_J\), and set
\[
 d_{\star}=2^{-m_{\star}},\qquad h_{\star}=d_{\star}/3,
 \qquad
 \Lambda_{\star}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}-R_1},
\]
\[
 a_{\mathrm{an}}=
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}}.
\]
Refine an outward interval for \(a_{\mathrm{an}}\) until its rational lower endpoint \(L_{\star}\) is positive.  Let \(u_{\star}\ge1\) be the least integer with \(2^{-u_{\star}}\le L_{\star}\), and set \(a_{\star}=2^{-u_{\star}}\).  Set \(C_j=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=\rho_j\}\).  This is the once-for-all translated-dyadic bank.  Its construction inputs are exactly the certified primitive constants \((k,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1)\); the unknown law of \(\eta\) is not an input.  The universal guarantee for treatment-noise transforms is stated and proved separately in \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\).
