Three rigorous benchmarks survive, but they do not determine the requested full-class minimax phase diagram.

First, on the class \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) obtained from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) by replacing \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta\) with \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta_n\), the exact local eligibility condition in \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)
 \le U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}},
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
 U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}
 &=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{24mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
The eligibility condition is
\[
 r\le
 \min\!\left\{
   \frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}
   -\frac{1}{a_{1,n}}\log\!\left(\frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}\right),
   \frac{\widetilde b_{2,n}}{a_2\log(a_2\widetilde b_{2,n})}
 \right\},
\]
with every displayed logarithm and denominator defined and
\[
 \widetilde b_{2,n}
 =200\min\{1,C_{\theta}\}\delta_n
 \max\!\left\{
   \varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n},
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})
 \right\}^{-1}.
\]
Together with a separately valid ordinary-DML bound \(U_{\mathrm{DML},n}\), this yields only the oracle envelope \(\min\{U_{\mathrm{DML},n},U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}\}\), not a data-driven selector or a minimax converse.

Second, for the explicit fourth-cumulant path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=\lvert\kappa_4(\eta_a)\rvert=2a^4,
\]
the computable sine estimator in \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) satisfies, when
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the pathwise uniform bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2\right]
 \le
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is an upper bound for one known local path, not a cumulant-only full-class rate.

Third, substituting \(\delta_n\) in \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
With \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), the proof of \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\) preserves the exact contour identity under the sufficient stability condition
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
This last display controls nuisance-factor zeros only.  It supplies neither a quantitative lower bound for the treatment-noise boundary modulus nor the empirical contour variance, selector error, limit law, Wald coverage, or a matching minimax lower bound.
