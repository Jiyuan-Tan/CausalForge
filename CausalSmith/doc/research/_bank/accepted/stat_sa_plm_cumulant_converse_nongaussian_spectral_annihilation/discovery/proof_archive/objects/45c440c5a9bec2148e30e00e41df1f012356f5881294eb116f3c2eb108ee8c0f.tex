The construction in \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\) fixes, once for all, a finite bank and a positive rational \(a_{\star}\) from the primitive constants.  Put \(m_a=|I_a|\); for \(n\ge2\), \(m_a\ge n/3\).  Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), and choose its guaranteed index \(j_{\star}\).  By \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\),
\[
 N_{C_{j_{\star}}}(F_n)\ge1,
 \qquad \inf_{C_{j_{\star}}}|F_n|\ge3a_{\star}/4. \tag{1}
\]
Define
\[
 d_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|,
 \quad e_a=\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|,
 \quad \mathcal A_a=\{d_a\le a_{\star}/8\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\), (1) gives pilot modulus at least \(5a_{\star}/8\).  Homotopy along \(F_n+t(\widehat F_{0,n}-F_n)\) stays nonzero on the circle, so its winding number is constant and the certified pilot count equals the positive population count.  Accounting for the certified modulus enclosure width \(a_{\star}/64\), the deterministic maximizing rule selects \(\widehat j_n\) with
\[
 \inf_{C_{\widehat j_n}}|F_n|>a_{\star}/2,
 \qquad \widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}=N_{C_{\widehat j_n}}(F_n)ge1. \tag{2}
\]
On \(\mathcal A_0\cap\mathcal A_1\), the evaluation empirical modulus exceeds \(3a_{\star}/8\), and its certified lower endpoint exceeds \(a_{\star}/4\); thus no fallback occurs.

Uniformly on the class, \(\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|G_n(z)|\le C_G<\infty\), by \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), the equation \(Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi\), Cauchy--Schwarz, and the two sub-Gaussian bounds.  With \(C=C_{\widehat j_n}\) and \(N=N_C(F_n)\), (2) and \(\text{thm:exact-contour-identification}\) therefore imply
\[
 \left|\frac{1}{N2\pi i}\oint_C
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}}{\widehat F_{1,n}}\,dz-\theta_0\right|
 \le L_{\star}(d_1+e_1), \tag{3}
\]
where \(L_{\star}\) depends only on the fixed primitive constants.  The certified quadrature midpoint adds at most \(1/n\), and taking real parts and projecting cannot increase distance from \(\theta_0\).

By \(\text{lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2}\),
\[
 E_P(d_a^2+e_a^2)\le K_1/n,
 \qquad P(\mathcal A_0^c\cup\mathcal A_1^c)\le K_2/n. \tag{4}
\]
On the exceptional event clipping bounds squared error by \(4C_{\theta}^2\).  Equations (3)--(4), including the \(n^{-2}\) numerical term, yield
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]\le C/n. \tag{5}
\]
All operations in the definition are finite sums, deterministic interval refinements with positive stopping margins, deterministic ties, projection, and fallback; hence the statistic is a total measurable sample map.  It uses \(\bar g_n\), but neither an outcome code nor the law of \(\eta\).  In particular it is an admissible minimax decision rule, so \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le C/n\).

For the converse, apply \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\) and choose a fixed \(h>0\) small enough that \(\vartheta_{\pm}=\pm h/(2\sqrt n)\) belong to the path and its product-law relative entropy is at most \(1/8\).  The elementary inequality \(\|P-Q\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\le\sqrt{D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P\Vert Q)/2}\) makes total variation at most \(1/4\).  Testing by the sign of an arbitrary estimator gives
\[
 \max_{v\in\{-,+\}}E_{P_{n,\vartheta_v}}
 [(widetilde\theta-\vartheta_v)^2]
 \ge\frac{h^2}{8n}(1-\|P_-^{\otimes n}-P_+^{\otimes n}\|_{\mathrm{TV}})
 \ge\frac{3h^2}{32n}.
\]
Taking the supremum and infimum proves \(c/n\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\).  Finally (5) and Markov's inequality imply the displayed generalized-quantile bound.  The ACE disclaimer follows directly from the distinct broad and comparison-subclass definitions.
