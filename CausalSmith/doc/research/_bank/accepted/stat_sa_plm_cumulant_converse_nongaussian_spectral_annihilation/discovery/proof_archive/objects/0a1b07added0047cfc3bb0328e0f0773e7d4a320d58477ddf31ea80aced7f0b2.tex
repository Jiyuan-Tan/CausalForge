The following are separate facts on separately defined, incomparable classes.  For the common deterministic code inputs and nested radii, the effective statistic in \(\text{def:adaptive-contour-estimator}\) satisfies, along every sequence with \(n\ge2\), \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\neq\varnothing\), and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\),
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\asymp n^{-1}.
\]
This includes fixed nonzero \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\).  The statistic and class use \(\bar g_n\) but impose no restriction on and make no use of \(\widehat q_n,\bar q_n,\varepsilon_{2,n}\).  Separately, every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) satisfies \(\theta_0(P)=0\), and whenever this intersection is nonempty,
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}=\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}=0.
\]
The latter is solely a diagnostic of the simultaneous bounded-\(Y\), exact-PLM, nondegenerate Gaussian assumptions around Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 3.2.  The two facts are not a Gaussian-versus-non-Gaussian minimax dichotomy or a local-to-Gaussian frontier, and no class inclusion or equality is asserted.
