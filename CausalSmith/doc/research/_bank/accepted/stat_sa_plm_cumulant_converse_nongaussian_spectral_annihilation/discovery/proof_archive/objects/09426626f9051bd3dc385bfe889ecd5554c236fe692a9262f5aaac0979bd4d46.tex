The strongest established result is \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\). Under the exact local ACE eligibility condition, its first branch gives the generalized-quantile upper bound \[ U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}} = C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}\Bigl[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}+64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r\{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}(\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Bigr]. \] This is an oracle upper benchmark, not a data-driven regime selector. For \(k=4\), the explicit path \(\eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}G_{\circ}+aS\), with \(\Delta_a=|\kappa_4(\eta_a)|=2a^4\), admits the computable sine estimator from \(\text{lem:gaussian-rademacher-l1-benchmark}\). Whenever \[ \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi}, \] it satisfies \[ \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2] \le C\min\left\{1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}\exp\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}. \] Finally, at every fixed \(\delta_n>0\), \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\), \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\), and the nuisance zero-free calculation give an existential contour upper bound \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)/n\) provided \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}\), where \(R_{1,n}=1+A_k(\psi_{\eta}^2/\delta_n)^{1/(k-2)}\). No uniform estimate for \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)\) is proved. Thus the fixed-separation endpoint is closed, while the sharp local-to-Gaussian interpolation and uniform inference remain open.
