Three rigorous benchmarks survive, but they do not determine the requested full-class minimax phase diagram.

First, let \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) be the class obtained from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) by replacing \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta\) with \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta_n\), without changing any other restriction.  Define
\[
 \widetilde b_{2,n}
 =200\min\{1,C_{\theta}\}\delta_n
 \max\!\left\{
   \varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n},
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})
 \right\}^{-1}.
\]
When \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), all displayed logarithms and denominators are defined, and
\[
 r\le
 \min\!\left\{
   \frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}
   -\frac{1}{a_{1,n}}\log\!\left(\frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}\right),
   \frac{\widetilde b_{2,n}}{a_2\log(a_2\widetilde b_{2,n})}
 \right\},
\]
the already established theorem \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)\\
 &\quad\le
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{42mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr]
 =:U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Together with any separately valid ordinary-DML bound \(U_{\mathrm{DML},n}\), this yields only the oracle envelope
\[
 \min\{U_{\mathrm{DML},n},U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}\};
\]
it does not construct a data-driven selector and is not a minimax converse.

Second, on the explicit fourth-cumulant path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=\lvert\kappa_4(\eta_a)\rvert=2a^4,
\]
where \(G_{\circ}\sim N(0,1)\), \(S\) is symmetric Rademacher, and \(G_{\circ}\perp S\), the same established theorem constructs the clipped sine-ratio estimator based on the known characteristic zero \(t_a=\pi/(2a)\).  Under the displayed path assumptions, including independent and identically distributed sampling, \(\eta_a\perp X\), \(E_P[\xi\mid X,T]=0\), the stated range and conditional sub-Gaussian bounds, and
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
it proves
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2\right]
 \le
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is a computable upper bound on one known local path; it is not a rate for the full class specified only through a fourth-cumulant lower bound.

Third, substituting \(\delta_n\) into \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) yields the search-radius bound
\[
 R_{0,n}le A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
With \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), the argument of \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\) preserves the exact contour identity whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
Indeed, this condition implies \(\sup_{|z|\le R_{1,n}}|H_n(z)-1|\le1/4\), hence \(|H_n(z)|\ge3/4\) on the disk and the nuisance factor creates no zeros there.  This calculation supplies the required positivity and boundary condition for \(H_n\), but it supplies no quantitative lower bound for the treatment-noise boundary modulus, no empirical contour-variance rate, no selector-error bound, no limit law or coverage theorem, and no matching minimax lower bound.
