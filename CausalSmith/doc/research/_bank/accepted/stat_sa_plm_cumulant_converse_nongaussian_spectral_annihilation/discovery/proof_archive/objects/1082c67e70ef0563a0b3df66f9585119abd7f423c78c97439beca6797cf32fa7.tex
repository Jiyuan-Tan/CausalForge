The following are separate facts on separately defined, incomparable classes. Fix the same deterministic code pair \((\widehat g_n,\widehat q_n)\), its clipped pair \((\bar g_n,\bar q_n)\), and the same nested radii \((\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n})\) as external inputs. Under the certified-real input convention, compute and fix the once-for-all translated-dyadic bank and positive dyadic certificate supplied by \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\). Along every sequence of indices \(n\to\infty\) for which \(n\ge2\), \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\neq\varnothing\), and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\),
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\asymp n^{-1}.
\]
This includes fixed nonzero \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\). The witnessing total certified statistic \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\), as well as membership in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), imposes no restriction on and makes no use of \(\widehat q_n\), \(\bar q_n\), or \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\). Separately, every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) in the simultaneous bounded-observed-outcome Gaussian intersection of \(\text{def:gaussian-class}\) satisfies \(\theta_0(P)=0\). Whenever that intersection is nonempty,
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}
 =\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}
 =0.
\]
Thus \(\text{prop:bounded-outcome-gaussian-degeneracy}\) is solely a diagnostic of the simultaneous exact-PLM, bounded-\(Y\), and nondegenerate conditional-Gaussian assumptions stated around Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 3.2; its positive lower-bound claim is not used. These conclusions neither form a Gaussian-versus-non-Gaussian minimax dichotomy nor determine a local-to-Gaussian frontier, and no equality or inclusion between the two classes is asserted.
