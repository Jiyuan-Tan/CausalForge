For each library depth \(m\), let \(\mathscr C_m\) be a finite deterministic library of rational circles, increasing with \(m\).  On prespecified independent folds, form the empirical transforms \(\widehat F_{a,n}\), \(\widehat G_{a,n}\), and their derivatives.  For a circle \(C=\{\rho e^{it}:0\le t\le2\pi\}\) on which the empirical denominator is certified positive, the candidate record may contain only quantities definitionally computed from the actual empirical contour integrands
\[
 W_{a,C}(t)=\rho e^{it}
 \frac{\widehat F'_{a,n}(\rho e^{it})}
      {\widehat F_{a,n}(\rho e^{it})},
 \qquad
 V_{a,C}(t)=\rho e^{it}
 \frac{\widehat G_{a,n}(\rho e^{it})}
      {\widehat F_{a,n}(\rho e^{it})}.
\]
Specifically, it may record the boundary modulus \(\inf_{z\in C}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)|\), the winding enclosure for \((2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}W_{a,C}(t)\,dt\), the contour-moment enclosure for \((2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}V_{a,C}(t)\,dt\), and the angular variance
\[
 \widehat v_{a,C}
 =\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}
 \left|V_{a,C}(t)-
 \frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}V_{a,C}(u)\,du\right|^2dt.
\]
These records constitute candidate data only.
