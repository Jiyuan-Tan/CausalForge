In the notation of \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) and
\(\text{def:jms-eligibility}\), Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 5.4
states that, whenever \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and
\(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\),
\[
\begin{split}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|\right)\\
 &\quad\le C_\gamma r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Bigl[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_\theta\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 +64(C_g+\psi_\eta)^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_\eta)+\psi_\xi+C_\theta\psi_\eta\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Bigr].
\end{split}
\]
