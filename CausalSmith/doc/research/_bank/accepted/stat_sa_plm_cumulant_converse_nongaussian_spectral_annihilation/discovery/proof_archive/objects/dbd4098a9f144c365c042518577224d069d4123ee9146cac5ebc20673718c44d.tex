Write \(\psi=\psi_{\eta}\).  The exact Luxemburg premise gives
\[
 E_P\exp(\eta^2/\psi^2)\le2,
 \qquad E_P\eta=0.
\]
Consequently Markov's inequality gives
\[
 P(|\eta|>u)\le2e^{-u^2/\psi^2},\qquad u\ge0.
\]
We next derive a numerical Laplace bound.  Let \(\eta'\) be an independent copy of \(\eta\).  If \(|t|\psi\le1\), Jensen's inequality conditional on \(\eta\), symmetry of \(\eta-\eta'\), the inequality \(\cosh u\le e^{u^2/2}\), and \((\eta-\eta')^2\le2\eta^2+2(\eta')^2\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 E_Pe^{t\eta}
 &\le E_Pe^{t(\eta-\eta')}
 =E_P\cosh\{t(\eta-\eta')\}\\
 &\le E_P\exp\{t^2(\eta-\eta')^2/2\}
 \le\{E_Pe^{t^2\eta^2}\}^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
By Hölder's inequality and the Luxemburg premise,
\[
 E_Pe^{t^2\eta^2}
 \le\{E_Pe^{\eta^2/\psi^2}\}^{t^2\psi^2}
 \le2^{t^2\psi^2}.
\]
Thus the last display is at most
\(\exp(2\log(2)\psi^2t^2)\le\exp(4\psi^2t^2)\).
If \(|t|\psi>1\), Young's inequality gives
\[
 t\eta\le\frac{\eta^2}{2\psi^2}+\frac{t^2\psi^2}{2},
\]
and Cauchy--Schwarz with the Luxemburg premise yields
\[
 E_Pe^{t\eta}
 \le\sqrt2\,e^{t^2\psi^2/2}
 \le e^{4\psi^2t^2}.
\]
Consequently, for every \(z\in\mathbb C\),
\[
 |M(z)|
 \le E_Pe^{\operatorname{Re}(z)\eta}
 \le \exp(4\psi^2|z|^2). \tag{1}
\]

Suppose, toward a contradiction, that \(M\) has no zero in the closed disk of radius \(R_0\).  Since \(M(0)=1\), an analytic logarithm \(f=\log M\) exists on a neighborhood of that disk and can be normalized by \(f(0)=0\).  Centering gives \(f'(0)=0\).  For \(0<R<R_0\), equation \((1)\) implies
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R}\operatorname{Re}f(z)
 \le4\psi^2R^2.
\]
Apply the Carath\'eodory coefficient estimate on the radius-\(R\) disk to the positive-real-part function \(4\psi^2R^2-f\).  Because its value at zero is \(4\psi^2R^2\),
\[
 |f^{(k)}(0)|
 \le 2k!R^{-k}\,4\psi^2R^2
 =8k!\psi^2R^{2-k}. \tag{2}
\]
Letting \(R\uparrow R_0\), using \(f^{(k)}(0)=\kappa_k(\eta)\), and invoking \(\text{ass:cumulant-separation}\), we obtain
\[
 \delta\le8k!\psi^2R_0^{2-k}.
\]
But the displayed definition of \(R_0\), with
\(A_k=(2^{k+4}k!)^{1/(k-2)}\), gives
\[
 8k!\psi^2R_0^{2-k}
 =\frac{8k!}{2^{k+4}k!}\,\delta
 =2^{-k-1}\delta<\delta,
\]
a contradiction.  Thus \(M\) has a zero in \(\{|z|\le R_0\}\).  The stated radius equality in particular implies the asserted inequality with the finite constant \(A_k\), which depends only on \(k\).
