The strongest proved partial answer consists of two upper benchmarks.  The theorem \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) first specializes Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 5.4 pointwise in \(n\), under its exact condition (21), to give the generalized-quantile bound
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!6^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 +4(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\]
This may be placed beside an ordinary-DML bound only as an oracle upper envelope.  Second, on the explicit \(k=4\) Gaussian--Rademacher path \(\eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}G_{\circ}+aS\), the same theorem proves exact sine annihilation at \(t_a=\pi/(2a)\), relevance at least
\[
 \frac a2\exp\!\left\{-\frac{(1-a^2)\pi^2}{8a^2}\right\},
\]
and the computable clipped-ratio risk bound
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,\frac1n a^{-2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{(1-a^2)\pi^2}{4a^2}\right)\right\}.
\]
With \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), this is the construction-specific bound
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}
\]
under \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  In addition, the already established zero-localization and nuisance-zero-free results retain exact contour identification at radius \(R_{0,n}\le A_k(\psi_{\eta}^2/\delta_n)^{1/(k-2)}\) when \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\{4(R_{0,n}+1)e^{2C_g(R_{0,n}+1)}\}^{-1}\).  None of these upper or identification statements establishes the full cumulant-only minimax frontier, a matching adversarial-nuisance lower bound, an optimally switching estimator, or uniform coverage.
