The strongest closed result is \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\), which has two parts.  First, on the class \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) obtained from the published ACE class by replacing \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta\) with \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta_n\), the exact local eligibility condition gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)
 \le U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}},
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
 U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}
 &=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{24mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
Together with any separately valid DML upper bound, this yields only the oracle envelope \(\min\{U_{\mathrm{DML},n},U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}\}\), not a data-driven selector or a minimax rate.

Second, for the explicit path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=\lvert\kappa_4(\eta_a)\rvert=2a^4,
\]
the computable sine estimator in \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) satisfies, whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the uniform pathwise bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[
   (\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2
 \right]
 \le
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(
    \frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \right)
 \right\}.
\]
More generally, \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) implies
\[
 R_{0,n}
 \le A_k(\psi_{\eta}^2/\delta_n)^{1/(k-2)},
\]
and the nuisance-factor argument preserves exact contour identification whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le
 \{4(R_{0,n}+1)e^{2C_g(R_{0,n}+1)}\}^{-1}.
\]
The last two displays provide an identification-and-stability benchmark, not a uniform full-class risk rate.  None of these results supplies a cumulant-only matching converse, an observable adaptive selector with a proved limit law and Wald coverage, or the requested sharp four-parameter minimax phase diagram.
