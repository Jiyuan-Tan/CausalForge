At each fixed \(\delta>0\), the certified translated-dyadic statistic has uniform \(C/n\) MSE under \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\).  Along \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives the exact local ACE quantile upper benchmark proportional to \(\delta_n^{-1}\) under its displayed eligibility condition.  On the Gaussian--Rademacher path with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), it also gives
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}
\]
under \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  These are valid explicit upper benchmarks, but neither is a sharp full-class phase diagram.
