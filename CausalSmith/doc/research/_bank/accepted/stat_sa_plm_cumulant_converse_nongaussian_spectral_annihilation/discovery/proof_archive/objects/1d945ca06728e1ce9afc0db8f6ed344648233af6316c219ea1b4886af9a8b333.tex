For \(s\ge r+1\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}},
\]
because both \(L^s(P_X)\) supplied-code restrictions imply their \(L^r(P_X)\) counterparts. Neither \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) nor equality of the broad spectral and published ACE classes is asserted. If \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\), then, for every \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\ge0\), with \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\) obtained by monotone boundary extension,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)\\
 &\quad\le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\\
 &\quad=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Big[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Big].
\end{aligned}
\]
If also \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), the spectral generalized-quantile guarantee holds on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\); whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2}\), that spectral upper guarantee is strictly smaller in order than \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\). This is a comparison of upper guarantees, not a lower bound for ACE.
