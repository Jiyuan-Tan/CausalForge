Three rigorous benchmarks survive.  First, on the local ACE class \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) obtained by replacing \(|\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)|\ge\delta\) with \(|\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)|\ge\delta_n\), the exact local eligibility condition in \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}
 &=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{20mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
Together with any separately valid ordinary-DML bound, this is only an oracle upper envelope, not an adaptive estimator or minimax converse.

Second, on the explicit path \(\eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}G_{\circ}+aS\), where \(\Delta_a=|\kappa_4(\eta_a)|=2a^4\), the known-zero clipped sine estimator satisfies, when \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\),
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
This is a construction-specific upper bound, not a rate for the cumulant-only class.

Third, zero localization gives
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
With \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), the nuisance factor remains zero-free and exact contour identification remains valid whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
This last condition is an identification-stability benchmark only; it gives no quantitative boundary modulus, risk rate, selector guarantee, limit law, coverage theorem, or matching lower bound.
