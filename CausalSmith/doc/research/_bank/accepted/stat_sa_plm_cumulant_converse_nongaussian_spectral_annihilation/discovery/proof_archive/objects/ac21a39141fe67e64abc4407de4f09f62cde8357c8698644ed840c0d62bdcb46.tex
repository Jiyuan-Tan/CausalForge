Fix a treatment-noise transform \(M\) represented in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  By \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\), it has a zero in \(\{|z|\le R_0\}\), so every circle in the constructed bank encloses at least one zero.  The transform bound established in that lemma gives
\[
 \log\max_{|z|=R_{\mathrm{out}}}|M(z)|
 \le4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2.
\]
Jensen's formula, using \(M(0)=1\), shows that the number of zeros strictly inside \(\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\), counted with multiplicity, is at most
\[
 Q_{\star}
 =\frac{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2}
        {\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})}
 \le N_{\mathrm{cert}}. \tag{1}
\]
Only zeros strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) are counted and factored below.

The finite certified translated-dyadic grid \(\mathcal Q_{m_{\star}}\) lies in \((R_0,R_1)\), has more than \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) points, and has spacing \(d_{\star}\).  The modulus of a fixed zero can lie within \(h_{\star}=d_{\star}/3\) of at most one grid point.  Every zero capable of violating this separation at a grid radius lies strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), because every grid radius is below \(R_1\) and \(R_1+h_{\star}<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\).  Thus (1) and the pigeonhole principle give a grid radius \(\rho_j\) satisfying
\[
 \bigl||a|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge h_{\star}
\]
for every zero \(a\) strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\).  This circle encloses the zero already localized in \(\{|z|\le R_0\}\); hence \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\).

Let \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\), repeated according to multiplicity, be precisely the zeros of \(M\) strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), where \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\), and form the corresponding disk Blaschke product
\[
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^N
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}.
\]
Set \(f=M/Q\), with removable singularities filled in.  Then \(f\) is holomorphic and zero-free on the open disk \(\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\).  It may vanish on the boundary because zeros on \(|z|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) were deliberately not factored.  On that boundary \(|Q|=1\), so the boundary transform bound and the maximum-modulus principle, applied first on smaller concentric disks and then by passage to the limit, give
\[
 |f(z)|\le \exp(4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2),
 \qquad |z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}. \tag{2}
\]
Moreover \(|Q(0)|\le1\) and \(M(0)=1\), so \(|f(0)|\ge1\).

Put \(A=4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\).  Since \(f\) is zero-free on the open disk, \(A-\log|f|\) is a nonnegative harmonic function there by (2), and its value at zero is at most \(A\).  The open-disk Harnack inequality therefore gives, for \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
 A-\log|f(z)|
 \le \Lambda_{\star}A,
 \qquad
 |f(z)|\ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}. \tag{3}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), radial separation and \(|z|\le R_1\) imply for each disk factor
\[
 \left|
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}
 \right|
 \ge \frac{|z-a_\nu|}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+|z|}
 \ge \frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}.
\]
The base on the right lies in \((0,1)\), and \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\).  Combining this product bound with (3) yields
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_j}|M(z)|
 \ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}}
 =a_{\mathrm{an}}
 \ge a_{\star}>0. \tag{4}
\]

Finally, certified-real names permit terminating outward interval refinement to obtain the integer \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\ge Q_{\star}\).  The search for \(m_{\star}\), enumeration of the finite certified translated-dyadic grid, and every subsequent arithmetic operation are finite.  Because \(a_{\mathrm{an}}>0\), interval refinement also terminates with a positive dyadic rational \(a_{\star}\le a_{\mathrm{an}}\).  These operations use only the displayed primitive constants, never a value of the unknown transform or the law of \(\eta\).  Equations (1)--(4) are uniform in \(n\), so the same once-for-all bank and certificate work for every represented transform.
