Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).  By \(\text{def:ace-comparison-subclass}\), the two supplied-code errors have \(L^s(P_X)\) norms at most \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\) and \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\).  Since \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r+1>r\), Lyapunov's inequality gives the corresponding \(L^r(P_X)\) bounds.  Every other condition in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) is inherited from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  Thus
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.
\]
No relation involving the unrestricted broad spectral class follows.

If \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), restricting the supremum in \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) to this subclass gives exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)\\
 &\quad\le C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Big[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{28mm}+64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}(\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Big]
 =B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}.
\end{aligned}
\]
For \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\), temporarily enlarge only that radius to \(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n}]\).  The zero-radius class is contained in the enlarged class, while the maximum defining \(b_{2,n}\) and hence eligibility are unchanged.  Apply the positive-radius result and let \(u\downarrow0\); this proves the boundary formula.

If also \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\), restricted to the subclass, gives its \(O(n^{-1/2})\) generalized-quantile guarantee (vacuously if the subclass is empty).  When
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2},
\]
the fixed positive prefactors make \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) of the order of the left-hand nuisance expression, so \(n^{-1/2}=o(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma})\).  This compares only upper guarantees and proves no lower bound for ACE.
