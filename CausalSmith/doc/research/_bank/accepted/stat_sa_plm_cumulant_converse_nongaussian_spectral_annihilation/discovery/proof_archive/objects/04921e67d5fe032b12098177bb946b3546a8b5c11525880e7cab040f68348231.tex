The deterministic radii \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\) and \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\) are nonincreasing. The broad spectral class and estimator require only sufficiently small direct \(L^1(P_X)\) treatment-code error and impose no supplied outcome-code restriction. The full published ACE class has stronger \(L^r(P_X)\) treatment- and outcome-code restrictions and is therefore contained in the broad spectral class. The auxiliary class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\) adds both \(L^s(P_X)\) restrictions; it is a subclass for \(s\ge r\) and equals the published ACE class at \(s=r\).
