Because \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r\), Lyapunov's inequality gives \(\|f\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\|f\|_{L^s(P_X)}\), with the usual essential-supremum interpretation when \(s=\infty\). Thus each of the two \(L^s(P_X)\) code restrictions in \(\text{def:ace-comparison-subclass}\) implies the corresponding \(L^r(P_X)\) restriction in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\). Every other member condition of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) is already inherited from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\). Hence
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.
\]
This argument uses only \(s\ge r\); no stronger relation between the exponents is needed. The definitions impose the two extra code restrictions only on the comparison subclass, so they imply neither inclusion of the broad spectral class nor equality of the broad and published classes.

Assume next that \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\). If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), apply \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and then restrict the supremum to its displayed subclass. This gives exactly the asserted bound \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\).

Suppose instead that \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\). For every \(t\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\), a law satisfying the zero outcome-code radius also satisfies the radius-\(t\) restriction. Moreover,
\[
 \max\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\}
 =
 \max\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\}.
\]
Consequently the eligibility quantity \(b_{2,n}\), and hence \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), is unchanged when the zero radius is replaced by such a \(t\). The positive-radius result therefore applies for every such \(t\). Its right-hand side differs from the asserted boundary value only by the term \(C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt\). Letting \(t\downarrow0\) proves the stated monotone boundary extension.

If additionally \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), then \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\), restricted to the subclass, gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le \sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}=O(n^{-1/2}).
\]
Writing \(S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\), the ACE bound is a fixed positive constant times \(S_n+O(n^{-1/2})\). If \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), the ratio of the spectral upper guarantee to \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) tends to zero. This compares only the two proved upper guarantees and supplies no lower bound for the ACE estimator.
