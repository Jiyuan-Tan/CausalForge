The two branches share only the deterministic external inputs specified in the statement; their law classes remain distinct.

Along the stated sequence of non-Gaussian experiments, \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) supplies constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
 \frac cn\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le\frac Cn.
\]
Therefore \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\asymp n^{-1}\).  The proof and definition of the witnessing spectral estimator use \(\bar g_n\) only through \(Z_n=T-\bar g_n(X)\).  Neither membership in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) nor the estimator uses \(\widehat q_n\), \(\bar q_n\), or \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\).  In particular, the conclusion allows any fixed nonzero \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\).

Separately, under the eventual Gaussian-branch hypotheses, \(\text{thm:gaussian-quantile-to-mse}\) gives
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}\ge\gamma L_{\mathrm G,n,\gamma}^{,2}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Substituting the definition
\[
 L_{\mathrm G,n,\gamma}
 =c_{\gamma}\left[
 \sigma^{-2}\varepsilon_{1,n}\varepsilon_{2,n}
 \left\{\frac{\log(1/\varepsilon_{1,n})}{\log n}\right\}^{3}
 +\sigma^{-1}n^{-1/2}
 \right]
\]
gives exactly
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}\ge\gamma c_{\gamma}^{2}
 \left[
 \sigma^{-2}\varepsilon_{1,n}\varepsilon_{2,n}
 \left\{\frac{\log(1/\varepsilon_{1,n})}{\log n}\right\}^{3}
 +\sigma^{-1}n^{-1/2}
 \right]^2.
\]
The first conclusion is over the broad cumulant-separated spectral class, whereas the second is over the bounded-outcome, known-variance Gaussian class.  Combining the conclusions does not require, and does not establish, equality or inclusion between those classes.
