The three class relations follow from \(\text{lem:jms-ace-class-relations}\).  Explicitly, for every \(n\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
 \subseteq \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}.
 \tag{1}
\]
For \(s\ge r\), the same lemma gives
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}},
 \tag{2}
\]
and, when \(s=r\), equality under the common clipped-code convention.  Relation (1) is the only inclusion involving the broad spectral class used below; no reverse inclusion is asserted.

Assume \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\).  If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) applies directly on the full published class and yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \bigl(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|\bigr)
 \le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma},
 \tag{3}
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}
 &=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Bigl[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\qquad\qquad
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}
      +C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]

It remains to justify the boundary \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).  Fix \(t\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\) and replace only the outcome-code radius at the displayed index by \(t\).  Every law satisfying the zero-radius restriction also satisfies the radius-\(t\) restriction.  Moreover,
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},t,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
 \tag{5}
\]
Thus \(b_{2,n}\), and consequently the eligibility predicate \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), is unchanged.  Apply \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) with positive outcome radius \(t\) and restrict the supremum to the zero-radius class.  The resulting right-hand side exceeds the value in (4) at \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\) only by
\[
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}\varepsilon_{1,n}^rt.
 \tag{6}
\]
The order-\(r\) ACE statistic itself is unchanged by this enlargement of the uncertainty radius.  Letting \(t\downarrow0\) in (6) proves (3) also when \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).

Now assume additionally that \(n\ge2\), \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\neq\varnothing\).  By (1), the broad class is then nonempty.  The generalized-quantile conclusion of \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\), restricted through (1), gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \bigl(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|\bigr)
 \le \sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}.
 \tag{7}
\]

Finally, define
\[
 S_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
     +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}.
\]
Writing
\[
 K_0=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}>0
\]
and
\[
 K_1=64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}
      +C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}\gamma^{-1/2}>0,
\]
formula (4) becomes
\[
 B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}=K_0(S_n+K_1n^{-1/2}).
 \tag{8}
\]
Along any eligible sequence with \(S_n\gg n^{-1/2}\), equations (7)--(8) imply
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}}{B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}}
 \le
 \frac{\sqrt{C/\gamma}\,n^{-1/2}}{K_0S_n}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Hence the spectral upper guarantee is strictly smaller in order than the displayed ACE upper guarantee on the full published ACE class.  The argument compares only the two upper bounds (3) and (7); it supplies no lower bound for the risk of \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}\).
