Under the project convention \(\|\eta\|_{\psi_2}=\inf\{u>0:E[\exp(\eta^2/u^2)]\le2\}\), every centered \(\eta\) with \(\|\eta\|_{\psi_2}\le\psi_{\eta}\) satisfies \(E[e^{t\eta}]\le\exp(4\psi_{\eta}^2t^2)\) for \(t\in\mathbb R\).  Put \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1\) and \(R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_{\mathrm{fac}}+1\).  Every treatment-noise transform represented in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) has at most
\[
 N_{\star}=\left\lceil\frac{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2}{\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})}\right\rceil
\]
zeros, counted with multiplicity, in \(\{|z|\le R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\).
