The construction has a mathematical layer and a represented-data layer.

At the mathematical layer, for an ordinary real sample set
\[
 \mathcal I_m(x)=
 \left[2^{-m}\lfloor2^m x\rfloor,
       2^{-m}\{\lfloor2^m x\rfloor+1\}\right],
 \qquad m\ge1.
\]
Interpret every \(T_i\), \(Y_i\), and \(\bar g_n(X_i)\), and every positive primitive constant, through its stipulated canonical floor-dyadic record, run the finite rational procedure below, and take the value represented by its output record.  This defines \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) as a total map on ordinary real samples.  The canonical floor digits are part of this mathematical interpretation; they are not claimed to be Type-2 computable from an arbitrary real name at a dyadic boundary.

At the represented-data layer, the actual input is
\[
 \mathfrak s=
 \bigl((\mathfrak t_i,\mathfrak y_i,\mathfrak g_i)_{i=1}^n,
       \mathfrak p,\mathfrak c_{\theta}\bigr),
\]
where \(\mathfrak t_i\), \(\mathfrak y_i\), and \(\mathfrak g_i\) are supplied canonical digital records for \(T_i\), \(Y_i\), and \(\bar g_n(X_i)\); \(\mathfrak p\) is the actual positive primitive-name record in \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\); and \(\mathfrak c_{\theta}:\operatorname{Name}_{+}(C_{\theta})\) is supplied.  A transducer exists at this layer only relative to a concrete implementation that has been added and compiled with all contracts of \(\text{def:certified-contour-arithmetic-substrate}\).  It reads no semantic exact-real field.

Invoke \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\) on \(\mathfrak p\), and form \(\mathfrak z_i=\mathfrak t_i-\mathfrak g_i\) by certified subtraction.  At every certified circle node, use only the substrate's complex rectangle operations and nested complex-exponential extension to construct, by finite sums, enclosures of
\[
 \widehat F_{a,n}^{(p)}(z)
 =|I_a|^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_a}Z_{n,i}^{p}e^{zZ_{n,i}},
 \quad p\in\{0,1,2\},
\]
and
\[
 \widehat G_{a,n}^{(p)}(z)
 =|I_a|^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_a}Y_iZ_{n,i}^{p}e^{zZ_{n,i}},
 \quad p\in\{0,1\}.
\]
Every node precision is obtained by applying the compositional modulus to a rational error budget.

For explicit mesh fuel, refine each input record at error one and take the resulting rational upper magnitude bounds \(U_{Z,i}\), \(U_{Y,i}\), and \(U_{\rho,j}\).  Use \(e^x\le3^{\lceil x\rceil}\) for \(x\ge0\) and \(\pi<4\) to turn the finite empirical derivative sums into rational bounds for all functions below.  With \(z_j(t)=\rho_je^{2\pi it}\), put
\[
 W_{0j}(t)=
 \rho_je^{2\pi it}
 \frac{\widehat F'_{0,n}(z_j(t))}
      {\widehat F_{0,n}(z_j(t))},
\]
and, after a positive integer \(N_j\) has been certified,
\[
 V_j(t)=
 \frac{\rho_je^{2\pi it}}{N_j}
 \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z_j(t))}
      {\widehat F_{1,n}(z_j(t))}.
\]
If a rational lower modulus certificate is \(m>0\), use
\[
 |W'_{0j}(t)|\le
 2\pi\left[
 \rho_j\frac{\sup|\widehat F'_{0,n}|}{m}
 +\rho_j^2\left\{
 \frac{\sup|\widehat F''_{0,n}|}{m}
 +\frac{\sup|\widehat F'_{0,n}|^2}{m^2}
 \right\}\right],
\]
and
\[
 |V'_j(t)|\le
 \frac{2\pi}{N_j}\left[
 \rho_j\frac{\sup|\widehat G_{1,n}|}{m}
 +\rho_j^2\left\{
 \frac{\sup|\widehat G'_{1,n}|}{m}
 +\frac{\sup|\widehat G_{1,n}|\sup|\widehat F'_{1,n}|}{m^2}
 \right\}\right].
\]
For a rational tolerance \(e>0\) and any resulting rational bound \(L\), take the mesh size \(\lceil L/e\rceil+1\), and allocate node error by the finite number of operations.  Thus every precision and mesh has displayed finite fuel.

On the pilot fold, compute for every \(j\) a sound interval of width at most \(a_{\star}/64\) enclosing \(\inf_{C_j}|\widehat F_{0,n}|\).  If its lower endpoint \(m_j\) is positive, guarded division and finite quadrature enclose
\[
 \int_0^1W_{0j}(t)\,dt
 =\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{C_j}
   \frac{\widehat F'_{0,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{0,n}(z)}\,dz
\]
in a complex rectangle whose coordinate widths are strictly below \(1/4\).  Record \(N_j\) exactly when the real interval contains a unique nonnegative integer and the imaginary interval contains zero; otherwise reject the circle.  A circle is admissible exactly when \(m_j\ge a_{\star}/2\) and \(N_j\ge1\).  Select an admissible circle having the greatest rational lower endpoint, with smallest-index tie-break, and return the certified zero name if none exists.

On the evaluation fold, compute the analogous modulus enclosure and return the certified zero name if its lower endpoint is below \(a_{\star}/4\).  Otherwise guarded division and finite quadrature give a rectangle of coordinate radius at most \(1/n\) enclosing
\[
 \int_0^1V_j(t)\,dt
 =\frac{1}{N_j2\pi i}\oint_{C_j}
   \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}\,dz.
\]
Let \(y\in\mathbb Q\) be the rational midpoint of its real interval, represented by its rational point name.  Projection uses no exact comparison: certified addition, subtraction, multiplication by \(1/2\), and the interval absolute-value operation construct a certified name whose value is
\[
 \operatorname{clip}_{C_{\theta}}(y)
 =\frac{|y+C_{\theta}|-|y-C_{\theta}|}{2}
 =\Pi_{[-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]}(y).
\]
This certified-real name is the transducer output.

An execution trace is the finite list of input-modulus calls, returned precisions, Taylor and square-root cutoffs, mesh sizes, rational rectangle operations, guarded divisions, rational endpoint comparisons, fallback decisions, and the tie-break.  Identical supplied canonical digital records have identical traces and output records.  No extensionality across arbitrary alternative names is asserted.  The procedure uses \(\bar g_n\), uses no supplied outcome code, and has no access to the law of \(\eta\).  The represented-data transducer and its compiled computability conclusion remain conditional on completing the bounded-build obligation in \(\text{def:certified-contour-arithmetic-substrate}\); the ordinary-sample Borel statistic and its statistical risk are mathematical objects independently of that build status.
