For every \(n\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}.
\]
For \(s\ge r\),
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}},
\]
and, under the common clipped-code convention, \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) when \(s=r\). No inclusion \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) is asserted. If \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\), then, for every \(\varepsilon_{2,n}\ge0\), with \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\) obtained by monotone boundary extension,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)\\
 &\quad\le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\\
 &\quad=C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Big[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Big].
\end{aligned}
\]
If also \(n\ge2\), \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\neq\varnothing\), then
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)},
\]
where \(C\) is the fixed-separation constant in \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\). Whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2}\), the spectral upper guarantee is strictly smaller in order than \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) on the full published ACE class. This is a comparison of upper guarantees, not a lower bound for ACE.
