By \(\text{lem:observable-factorization}\), on \(C_j\),
\[
 \frac{G_n(z)}{F_n(z)}
 =\theta_0\frac{F_n'(z)}{F_n(z)}+\frac{B_n(z)}{H_n(z)}.
\]
The hypotheses make the first quotient continuous on the contour.  Since \(B_n\) and \(H_n\) are entire and \(H_n\) has no zero on or inside \(C_j\), the quotient \(B_n/H_n\) is holomorphic inside and on the contour; its contour integral is zero.  The logarithmic-derivative identity, obtained by locally choosing logarithms between successive zeros and summing their changes of argument, gives
\[
 \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{C_j}\frac{F_n'(z)}{F_n(z)}\,dz=N_{C_j}(F_n).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_{C_j}\frac{G_n(z)}{F_n(z)}\,dz
 =\theta_0N_{C_j}(F_n).
\]
The assumed positive integer \(N_{C_j}(F_n)\) may be divided out.  The definition in \(\text{def:contour-functional}\) then gives the two asserted equalities.  Thus the causal coefficient is distinguished from, and identified by, the observable contour functional under the stated zero-free and positive-zero-count conditions.
