Fix \(n\) and \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  All norms in the first part of the proof are taken with respect to the probability measure \(P_X\).  The code errors are measurable and bounded: \(g_0\) and \(\bar g_n\) take values in \([-C_g,C_g]\), while \(q_0\) and \(\bar q_n\) take values in \([-C_q,C_q]\).  Thus all displayed norms are finite, including at \(s=\infty\).

Suppose first that \(r\le s<\infty\).  For any measurable \(f\), Jensen's inequality applied to the concave map \(u\mapsto u^{r/s}\) gives
\[
 E_P[|f(X)|^r]
 =E_P\!\left[(|f(X)|^s)^{r/s}\right]
 \le \left(E_P[|f(X)|^s]\right)^{r/s}.
\]
Taking \(r\)-th roots yields
\[
 \|f\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le \|f\|_{L^s(P_X)}.                 \tag{1}
\]
If \(s=\infty\), then \(|f(X)|\le\|f\|_{L^\infty(P_X)}\) almost surely, and integration followed by an \(r\)-th root proves equation (1) again.  The assumed inequality \(s\ge r+1\) therefore suffices.

Apply equation (1) to \(f=\bar g_n-g_0\) and \(f=\bar q_n-q_0\).  The two radius conditions in \(\text{def:non-gaussian-class}\) imply
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n},
 \qquad
 \|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{2,n}.       \tag{2}
\]
These are exactly the two code restrictions in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\).  Every remaining member condition in that definition---mean exclusion, the target and nuisance range restrictions, the two sub-Gaussian restrictions, independence of \(\eta\) and \(X\), and the order-\(r+1\) cumulant separation---is also a member condition in \(\text{def:non-gaussian-class}\).  Hence equation (2) and the two class definitions prove the one-way inclusion
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}
 \subseteq \mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.  \tag{3}
\]

The norm implication alone does not prove equality.  Indeed, on any probability space containing an event \(A\) with \(0<P_X(A)<1\), for \(c\ne0\) the bounded function \(f=c\mathbf 1_A\) obeys
\[
 \frac{\|f\|_{L^s(P_X)}}{\|f\|_{L^r(P_X)}}
 =P_X(A)^{1/s-1/r}>1
\]
when \(s<\infty\), whereas for \(s=\infty\) the ratio is \(P_X(A)^{-1/r}>1\).  Thus no reverse implication with the same radius follows from norm monotonicity.

It remains to establish the comparator bound.  Assume \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).  By \(\text{def:jms-eligibility}\), every logarithm and denominator in the eligibility display is defined.  Since \(a_{1,n}\) contains \(\log\{6(C_g+\psi_\eta)\varepsilon_{1,n}^{-1}\}\), this antecedent excludes \(\varepsilon_{1,n}=0\).  Define
\[
 V_n=(C_g+\psi_\eta)^r
 \left\{r^2(C_g+\psi_\eta)+\psi_\xi+C_\theta\psi_\eta\right\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}.
\]
If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), the revalidated cited leaf \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|\right)\\
 &\quad\le C_\gamma r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \left[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_\theta\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}+64V_n\right]
 =B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}.                            \tag{4}
 \end{aligned}
\]

If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\), fix \(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n})\) and, at the current index, enlarge only the outcome-code ball from radius zero to radius \(u\).  The original class is contained in the enlarged class, while the maximum defining \(b_{2,n}\) is unchanged because \(u<\varepsilon_{1,n}\).  Thus the same eligibility condition holds.  Applying the cited positive-radius inequality to the enlarged class gives the right-hand side
\[
 C_\gamma r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \left[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r u+C_\theta\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
 +64V_n\right].
\]
Letting \(u\downarrow0\) proves equation (4) at the zero-radius boundary.  Finally, restricting the supremum in equation (4) by the inclusion in equation (3) proves the same actionable bound throughout \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  This establishes the norm alignment, the strict one-way class relation, and the exact comparator consequence claimed.
