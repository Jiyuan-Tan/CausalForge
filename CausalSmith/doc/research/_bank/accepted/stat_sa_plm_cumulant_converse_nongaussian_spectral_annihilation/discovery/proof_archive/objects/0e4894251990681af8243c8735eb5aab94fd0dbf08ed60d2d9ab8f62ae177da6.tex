The strongest closed result is \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\).  Under its exact local eligibility condition, the order-\(r\) ACE generalized-quantile upper bound is the explicit quantity \(U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}\), which scales as \(\delta_n^{-1}\) times the displayed nuisance and sampling terms.  For \(k=4\), the Gaussian--Rademacher path admits the computable sine estimator with
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[
   \{\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P)\}^{2}
 \right]
 \le C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}
\]
whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  At each fixed positive separation, \(\text{thm:common-experiment-dichotomy}\) gives an existence-level fixed-bank \(n^{-1}\) MSE rate, but the frozen results provide no uniform quantitative bound on the selected bank's modulus or on the associated risk constant as \(\delta_n\downarrow0\).
