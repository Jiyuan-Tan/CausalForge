Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).  Since \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r+1>r\), Lyapunov's inequality gives both \(L^r(P_X)\) code bounds from the defining \(L^s(P_X)\) bounds.  Every other condition of \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) is inherited from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), proving \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).

When \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and both radii are positive, \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\), restricted to this subclass, gives exactly \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\), including the factors \(r!16^r\) and \(64\).  If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0<\varepsilon_{1,n}\), enlarge only that radius to \(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n}]\).  The maximum defining \(b_{2,n}\), hence eligibility, is unchanged.  Apply the positive-radius result and let \(u\downarrow0\).

If also \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) restricts its \(O(n^{-1/2})\) generalized-quantile guarantee to the comparison subclass.  Whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_\theta\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2}\), the exact ACE upper bound is of the larger order.  This compares upper guarantees only and proves no lower bound for ACE.
