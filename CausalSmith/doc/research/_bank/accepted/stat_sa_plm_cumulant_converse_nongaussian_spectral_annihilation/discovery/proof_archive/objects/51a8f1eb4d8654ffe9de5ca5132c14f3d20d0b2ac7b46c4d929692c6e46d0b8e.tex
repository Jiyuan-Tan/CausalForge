Use the project convention \(\|W\|_{\psi_2}=\inf\{u>0:E[\exp(W^2/u^2)]\le2\}\), and put \(c_{\mathrm{mgf}}=4\), \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1\), and \(R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_{\mathrm{fac}}+1\).  Define
\[
 N_{\star}=\left\lceil\frac{c_{\mathrm{mgf}}\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2}{\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})}\right\rceil.
\]
For \(m\ge3\), let
\[
 \mathcal Q_m=\{R_0+1/4+q2^{-m}:q\in\mathbb Z,\ 0\le q2^{-m}\le1/2\}.
\]
Choose the least \(m_{\star}\) for which \(|\mathcal Q_{m_{\star}}|>N_{\star}\), enumerate this finite set increasingly as \(\rho_1<\cdots<\rho_J\), and put \(d_{\star}=2^{-m_{\star}}\), \(h_{\star}=d_{\star}/3\),
\[
 \Lambda_{\star}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}-R_1},
 \qquad
 a_{\star}=\exp\{-c_{\mathrm{mgf}}(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}}.
\]
The resulting rational-radius circles \(C_j=\{z:|z|=\rho_j\}\) form the once-for-all effective bank.  Every quantity is obtained by explicit arithmetic, logarithm, exponential, ceiling, and finite search from \((k,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1)\); \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\) proves its uniform conditioning property.
