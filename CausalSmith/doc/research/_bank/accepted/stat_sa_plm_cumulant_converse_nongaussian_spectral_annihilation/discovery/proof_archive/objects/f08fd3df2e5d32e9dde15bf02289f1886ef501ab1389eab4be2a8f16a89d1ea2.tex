At each fixed \(\delta>0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives a computable certified statistic with uniform \(C/n\) mean-squared error whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and its ACE-preserving two-point branch now proves matched \(\Theta(n^{-1})\) minimax MSE on the full published ACE class as well. Along \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives the exact local ACE generalized-quantile upper benchmark proportional to \(\delta_n^{-1}\) under its displayed eligibility condition. On the Gaussian--Rademacher path with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), it also gives the computable mean-squared-error upper bound
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}
\]
under \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\). These results close the fixed-separation problem and provide two local upper benchmarks, but they do not determine the sharp full-class shrinking-separation phase diagram or construct the requested selector, coverage theorem, or local converse.
