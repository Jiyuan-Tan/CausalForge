Fix a represented transform \(M\).  The sub-Gaussian moment bound used in \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\) gives
\[
 |M(z)|\le E[e^{\operatorname{Re}(z)\eta}]
 \le e^{B|z|^2},\qquad z\in\mathbb C,
\]
and \(M(0)=1\).  Let \((a_{\nu})\) be its zeros in \(|z|<U\), repeated according to multiplicity.  Jensen's formula on the radius-\(U\) disk yields
\[
 \sum_{|a_{\nu}|<U}\log\frac{U}{|a_{\nu}|}
 =\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log|M(Ue^{it})|\,dt
 \le BU^2.
\]
If a zero lies on \(|z|=U\), apply Jensen's formula at radii decreasing to \(U\); it contributes zero in the limit.  Every zero satisfying \(|a_{\nu}|<T\) contributes more than \(\log(U/T)>0\).  Consequently, if \(N_M(T)\) is the number of such zeros, then
\[
 N_M(T)\log(U/T)\le BU^2,
 \qquad N_M(T)\le N_{\star}.
\]
The strict positivity of \(\log(U/T)\) follows from \(U=T+1>T>0\).
