Assume \(0<\varepsilon_{1,n}<1\) for all sufficiently large \(n\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\varepsilon_{2,n}=o\{(\log n)^{-1/2}\}\). Then, for all sufficiently large \(n\), Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 3.2 and equation (6), applied exactly to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), give \(\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}\ge L_{\mathrm G,n,\gamma}\). Under \(Q_{P,1-\gamma}(W)=\inf\{t:P(W\le t)\ge1-\gamma\}\), the implication \(Q_{P,1-\gamma}(W)\ge L\Rightarrow P(W\ge L)\ge\gamma\Rightarrow E_P[W^2]\ge\gamma L^2\) yields \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}\ge\gamma L_{\mathrm G,n,\gamma}^2\) for all sufficiently large \(n\).
