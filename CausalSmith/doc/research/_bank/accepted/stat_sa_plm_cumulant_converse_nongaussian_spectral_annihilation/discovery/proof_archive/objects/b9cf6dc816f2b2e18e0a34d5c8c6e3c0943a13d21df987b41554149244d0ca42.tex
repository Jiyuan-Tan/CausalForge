At each fixed \(\delta>0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives a computable certified statistic with uniform \(C/n\) mean-squared error whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and its hard submodel gives the matching \(c/n\) lower bound.  Along \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives the explicit local ACE generalized-quantile upper benchmark proportional to \(\delta_n^{-1}\) under its displayed eligibility condition.  On the Gaussian--Rademacher path, with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), the same theorem gives the computable bound
\[
 C\min\left\{1,
 n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}
\]
provided \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  These results close fixed separation and provide two honest local upper benchmarks, but do not determine the full shrinking-separation phase diagram.
