For every \(n\) and every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), the simultaneous bounded-observed-outcome, Gaussian-treatment-noise, and partially linear conditional-mean restrictions imply \(\theta_0(P)=0\). Consequently the fixed zero estimator is exact throughout this class and
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}=0,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}=0.
\]
In particular, the positive generalized-quantile lower bound in Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 3.2 cannot be imported as a result about this simultaneous-hypothesis class; the eventual nuisance-radius conditions in the former statement do not alter the degeneracy.
