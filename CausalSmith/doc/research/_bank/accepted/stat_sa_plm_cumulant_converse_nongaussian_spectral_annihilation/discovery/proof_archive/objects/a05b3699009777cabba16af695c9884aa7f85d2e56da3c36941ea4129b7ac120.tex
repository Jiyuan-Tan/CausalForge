At every fixed \(\delta>0\),
\(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives a total Borel statistic with
uniform mean-squared error at most \(C/n\) under
\(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), and its hard submodel gives the
matching lower bound \(c/n\).  Along \(\delta_n\downarrow0\),
\(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) gives the explicit local
ACE generalized-quantile upper benchmark proportional to
\(\delta_n^{-1}\) under its displayed eligibility condition.  On the
Gaussian--Rademacher path, with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), it also gives the
computable mean-squared-error bound
\[
 C\min\!\left\{1,
 n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}
\]
provided
\(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\).  These results close
fixed separation and supply two honest shrinking-separation upper
benchmarks, but not the full-class phase diagram, an adaptive selector,
uniform coverage, or a matching local lower bound.
