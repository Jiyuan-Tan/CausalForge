Fix a treatment-noise transform \(M\) represented in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  By \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\), it has a zero in \(\{|z|\le R_0\}\), so every bank circle encloses at least one zero.  The transform bound proved there gives
\[
 \log\max_{|z|=R_{\mathrm{out}}}|M(z)|
 \le4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2.
\]
Jensen's formula, with \(M(0)=1\), shows that the number of zeros strictly inside \(\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\), counted with multiplicity, is at most
\[
 Q_{\star}
 =\frac{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2}
        {\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})}
 \le N_{\mathrm{cert}}. \tag{1}
\]
Only zeros strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) are counted and factored below.

The grid \(\mathcal Q_{m_{\star}}\) is finite, lies in \((R_0,R_1)\), has more than \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) points, and has spacing \(d_{\star}\).  The modulus of a fixed zero can lie within \(h_{\star}=d_{\star}/3\) of at most one grid point.  Every zero capable of violating this separation at a grid radius lies strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), because every grid radius is below \(R_1\) and \(R_1+h_{\star}<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\).  Hence (1) and the pigeonhole principle give a grid radius \(\rho_j\) such that
\[
 \bigl||a|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge h_{\star}
\]
for every zero \(a\) strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\).  This circle encloses the zero localized in \(\{|z|\le R_0\}\), so \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\).

Let \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\), repeated according to multiplicity, be precisely the zeros of \(M\) strictly inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), where \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\), and form the disk Blaschke product
\[
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^N
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}.
\]
Set \(f=M/Q\), filling removable singularities.  Then \(f\) is holomorphic and zero-free on \(\{|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\}\).  On \(|z|=R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), one has \(|Q(z)|=1\).  The transform bound and the maximum-modulus principle give
\[
 |f(z)|\le \exp(4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2),
 \qquad |z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}. \tag{2}
\]
Also \(|Q(0)|\le1\) and \(M(0)=1\), so \(|f(0)|\ge1\).

Put \(A=4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\).  Since \(f\) is zero-free, \(A-\log|f|\) is a nonnegative harmonic function on the open disk, and its value at zero is at most \(A\).  Harnack's inequality therefore gives, for \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
 A-\log|f(z)|\le\Lambda_{\star}A,
 \qquad
 |f(z)|\ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}. \tag{3}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), radial separation implies for every disk factor
\[
 \left|
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}
 \right|
 \ge \frac{|z-a_\nu|}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+|z|}
 \ge \frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}.
\]
The last base lies in \((0,1)\), and \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\).  Multiplying this bound and using (3) yields
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_j}|M(z)|
 \ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}}
 =a_{\mathrm{an}}
 \ge a_{\star}>0. \tag{4}
\]

It remains to audit effectiveness.  The canonical certified names of \((k,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1)\) give outward rational intervals for every displayed arithmetic expression.  Because \(\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})>0\), refinement eventually supplies a positive rational lower endpoint for this denominator and a finite rational upper bound \(U_{\star}\) for \(Q_{\star}\); thus \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}=\lceil U_{\star}\rceil\) is obtained after finitely many operations.  The search for \(m_{\star}\) and the enumeration of the resulting finite grid are finite.  Since \(a_{\mathrm{an}}>0\), refinement eventually produces a strictly positive rational lower endpoint \(L_{\star}\); the least \(u_{\star}\ge1\) with \(2^{-u_{\star}}\le L_{\star}\) is found by a finite integer search and gives the prescribed \(0<a_{\star}\le a_{\mathrm{an}}\).  No step queries \(M\), \(\eta\), or their law.  Equations (1)--(4) apply universally after the bank is computed and are uniform in \(n\), proving the claim.
