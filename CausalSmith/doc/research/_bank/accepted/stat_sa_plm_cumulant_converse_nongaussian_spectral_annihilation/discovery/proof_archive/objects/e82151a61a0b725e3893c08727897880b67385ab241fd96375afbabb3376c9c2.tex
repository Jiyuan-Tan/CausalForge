Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).  By \(\text{def:ace-comparison-subclass}\), \(P\) satisfies every member property of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) and also
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n},
 \qquad
 \|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{2,n}.
\]
Since \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r+1>r\), Lyapunov's inequality, with its usual \(L^{\infty}\) interpretation, gives
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}
 \le\|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n},
\]
and identically \(\|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{2,n}\).  All other member properties in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) already hold in the broad non-Gaussian class.  Hence
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.
\]
This proves only the stated one-way inclusion for the explicitly restricted subclass.

Suppose \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\).  Apply \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) and restrict its supremum to the subclass.  This gives exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)\\
 &\quad\le C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta^{-1}
 \Big[\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{31mm}
 +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
 \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}\Big]
 =B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}.
\end{aligned}
\]

For \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\), fix \(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n}]\) and enlarge only the outcome-code radius at index \(n\) from zero to \(u\).  Every zero-radius law remains in the enlarged class, while
\[
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},u,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}
 =
 \max\!\left\{\varepsilon_{1,n},0,
 (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})\right\}.
\]
Thus eligibility is unchanged.  Apply the positive-radius result and let \(u\downarrow0\); the left side is independent of \(u\), while its bound decreases to the displayed boundary formula.  Positivity of \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\) is precisely what licenses this extension.

Finally, if \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), then the comparison subclass is contained in the broad spectral class.  If nonempty, \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{\frac{C}{\gamma n}};
\]
if empty, the assertion is vacuous.  When
\[
 A_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
 +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2},
\]
the fixed positive prefactors give \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\asymp A_n\), while the spectral guarantee is \(O(n^{-1/2})=o(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma})\).  This compares upper guarantees only and proves no lower bound for ACE.
