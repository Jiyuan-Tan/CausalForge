Fix once and for all one deterministic finite bank whose existence is established by \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\), and use that same bank for every \(n\), law, and sample.  This is an existence-level choice depending only on the fixed class constants; no effective construction of its radii or modulus is used.

\emph{Uniform upper bound.}
Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  By \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\), some \(j_0\in\{1,\ldots,J\}\) satisfies
\[
 N_{C_{j_0}}(M)\ge1,
 \qquad
 \inf_{z\in C_{j_0}}|M(z)|\ge a_{\star}.
\]
The condition \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\) and \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\) imply that \(|H_n(z)|\ge3/4\) on \(\{|z|\le R_1\}\), that \(H_n\) is zero-free there, and that \(F_n=M H_n\) has the same zeros as \(M\), with multiplicity.  Hence
\[
 N_{C_{j_0}}(F_n)\ge1,
 \qquad
 \inf_{z\in C_{j_0}}|F_n(z)|\ge\frac{3a_{\star}}4. \tag{1}
\]

For \(a\in\{0,1\}\), define the proof intermediates
\[
 \Delta_{F,a}
 =\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat F_{a,n}(z)-F_n(z)|,
 \qquad
 \Delta_{G,a}
 =\sup_{|z|\le R_1}|\widehat G_{a,n}(z)-G_n(z)|,
\]
and the event
\[
 \mathcal E_n
 =\{\Delta_{F,0}\le a_{\star}/8,\ 
     \Delta_{F,1}\le a_{\star}/8\}.
\]
For \(n\ge2\), both half-sample sizes are at least \(n/3\).  Two applications of \(\text{lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2}\), followed by Markov's inequality at the fixed threshold \(a_{\star}/8\), give a constant \(K_0<\infty\), uniform over the class, such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}P(\mathcal E_n^c)
 \le\frac{K_0}{n}. \tag{2}
\]

On \(\mathcal E_n\), equation \((1)\) implies
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_{j_0}}|\widehat F_{0,n}(z)|
 \ge\frac{5a_{\star}}8.
\]
The straight-line boundary homotopy from \(F_n\) to \(\widehat F_{0,n}\) is zero-free on \(C_{j_0}\), so its argument-principle count is constant.  Thus \(j_0\) is pilot-admissible.  The maximizing rule in \(\text{def:adaptive-contour-estimator}\) therefore selects an index \(\widehat j_n\) satisfying
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_{\widehat j_n}}|\widehat F_{0,n}(z)|
 \ge\frac{5a_{\star}}8. \tag{3}
\]
The pilot part of \(\mathcal E_n\) and \((3)\) give
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_{\widehat j_n}}|F_n(z)|
 \ge\frac{a_{\star}}2. \tag{4}
\]
The same zero-free homotopy gives
\[
 \widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}
 =N_{C_{\widehat j_n}}(F_n)\ge1. \tag{5}
\]
Finally, \((4)\) and the evaluation-half part of \(\mathcal E_n\) imply
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_{\widehat j_n}}|\widehat F_{1,n}(z)|
 \ge\frac{3a_{\star}}8>\frac{a_{\star}}4, \tag{6}
\]
so the evaluation fallback is not used.

Conditional on the once-for-all fixed bank, the selector and estimator are measurable.  The bank is finite; for each fixed circle, continuity identifies a boundary infimum with the infimum over a fixed countable dense parameter set; the empirical transforms and their derivatives are sample-measurable at every parameter value; and every contour integral uses a fixed circle parameterization.  The prescribed smallest-index tie rule and fixed failure values preserve measurability.  This establishes measurability of the statistic conditional on the fixed bank and makes no claim that the bank itself can be computed from the class constants.

On the selected contour, \((4)\) gives boundary zero-freeness of \(F_n\), \(\text{lem:l1-nuisance-zero-free}\) gives zero-freeness of \(H_n\) on the enclosed disk, and \((5)\) gives a positive zero count.  Thus \(\text{thm:exact-contour-identification}\) applies.  Put
\[
 N=N_{C_{\widehat j_n}}(F_n)
  =\widehat N_{0\widehat j_n,n}.
\]
The class restrictions yield a finite uniform constant \(K_G\) such that
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R_1}|G_n(z)|
 \le E_P[|Y|e^{R_1|Z_n|}]
 \le K_G. \tag{7}
\]
Indeed, \(Y=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta+\xi\), \(Z_n=\eta+D_n\), and \(|D_n|\le2C_g\).  Cauchy--Schwarz, the range restrictions, the sub-Gaussian exponential moments of \(\eta\), and the unconditional moment consequence of the conditional sub-Gaussian bound on \(\xi\) bound the last expectation by a constant depending only on \(C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi},R_1\).

For every \(z\in C_{\widehat j_n}\), equations \((4)\), \((6)\), and \((7)\), together with \(u^{-1}-v^{-1}=(v-u)/(uv)\), imply
\[
 \left|
  \frac{\widehat G_{1,n}(z)}{\widehat F_{1,n}(z)}
  -\frac{G_n(z)}{F_n(z)}
 \right|
 \le
 \frac{8}{3a_{\star}}\Delta_{G,1}
 +\frac{16K_G}{3a_{\star}^2}\Delta_{F,1}. \tag{8}
\]
Because \(N\ge1\), the length of every bank circle is at most \(2\pi R_1\), and taking the real part and projecting onto \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\) cannot increase distance from the real number \(\theta_0(P)\).  The exact contour identity and \((8)\) therefore give
\[
 |\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|\mathbf 1_{\mathcal E_n}
 \le R_1\left\{
   \frac{8}{3a_{\star}}\Delta_{G,1}
   +\frac{16K_G}{3a_{\star}^2}\Delta_{F,1}
 \right\}. \tag{9}
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:empirical-transform-uniform-l2}\) to \((9)\) yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P\!\left[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2
              \mathbf 1_{\mathcal E_n}\right]
 \le\frac{K_1}{n}. \tag{10}
\]
On \(\mathcal E_n^c\), both the estimator and the target lie in \([-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]\), so their squared distance is at most \(4C_{\theta}^2\).  Combining \((2)\) and \((10)\) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le\frac Cn. \tag{11}
\]
The fixed bank depends only on \(r,\delta,\psi_{\eta}\) through its existence proof, so all constants in \((11)\) have the dependence asserted in the theorem.  Once that bank is fixed, \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) is one common measurable statistic for the entire class.  It uses \(\bar g_n\) through \(Z_n\), does not use \(\bar q_n\), and its rule does not depend on the particular law of \(\eta\).  The definition of minimax risk therefore gives
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}
 \le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]. \tag{12}
\]

\emph{Matching lower bound.}
Invoke \(\text{lem:non-gaussian-hard-submodel}\).  Choose a fixed \(h>0\), depending only on its constants, small enough that
\[
 \vartheta_0=-\frac{h}{2\sqrt n},
 \qquad
 \vartheta_1=\frac{h}{2\sqrt n}
\]
belong to the submodel for every \(n\ge2\).  Let \(P_0=P_{n,\vartheta_0}^{\otimes n}\), \(P_1=P_{n,\vartheta_1}^{\otimes n}\), and \(d=\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0=h/\sqrt n\).  The lemma gives
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_0\Vert P_1)\le C_0h^2. \tag{13}
\]
For a common dominating measure, let \(A=\int\sqrt{p_0p_1}\) be the Hellinger affinity.  Jensen's inequality gives
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_0\Vert P_1)
 =-2E_{P_0}\!\left[\log\sqrt{p_1/p_0}\right]
 \ge-2\log A,
\]
so \((13)\) implies \(A\ge a_0:=e^{-C_0h^2/2}>0\).  Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(P_0,P_1)
 =\frac12\int|\sqrt{p_0}-\sqrt{p_1}|
                  (\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{p_1})
 \le\sqrt{1-A^2}. \tag{14}
\]
Turn any estimator \(\widetilde\theta\) into the test selecting the nearer of \(\vartheta_0\) and \(\vartheta_1\).  On a testing error, the squared estimation error is at least \(d^2/4\), while the sum of the two testing-error probabilities is at least \(1-\operatorname{TV}(P_0,P_1)\).  Since \(1-\sqrt{1-u}\ge u/2\) for \(u\in[0,1]\), equation \((14)\) yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \max_{v\in\{0,1\}}
 E_{P_v}[(\widetilde\theta-\vartheta_v)^2]
 &\ge\frac{d^2}{8}\{1-\operatorname{TV}(P_0,P_1)\}\\
 &\ge\frac{d^2a_0^2}{16}
 =\frac{h^2a_0^2}{16n}.
 \end{aligned} \tag{15}
\]
Both laws belong to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\).  Taking the infimum over estimators in \((15)\) proves \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\ge c/n\), with \(c=h^2a_0^2/16>0\).

\emph{Generalized-quantile consequence.}
Set \(W=|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|\).  Equation \((11)\) and Markov's inequality at \(t=\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}\) give \(P(W>t)\le\gamma\), hence \(P(W\le t)\ge1-\gamma\).  By the definition of \(Q_{P,1-\gamma}\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(W)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}.
\]
