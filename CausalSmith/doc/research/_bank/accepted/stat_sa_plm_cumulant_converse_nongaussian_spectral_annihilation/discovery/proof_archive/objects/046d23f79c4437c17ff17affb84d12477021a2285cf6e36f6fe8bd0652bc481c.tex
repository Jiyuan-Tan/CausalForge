Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).  This law inherits every defining condition of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\), and it additionally obeys the two displayed \(L^s(P_X)\) code restrictions.  Because \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r+1>r\), monotonicity of probability-space norms gives
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}
 \le\|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n}
\]
and
\[
 \|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}
 \le\|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^s(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{2,n}.
\]
All other conditions in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) are inherited from the broad class, so
\[
 \mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}.
\]
This argument uses the two additional restrictions in the comparison subclass.  The definition of the broad class itself imposes neither the \(L^s(P_X)\) treatment restriction nor any outcome-code restriction, so no reverse or broad-class inclusion is being claimed.

Suppose next that \(\mathsf E_n^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) holds and \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\).  By \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\), the published estimator satisfies its displayed bound over all of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).  Restricting the supremum to the subset just proved gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma},
\]
and expanding \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) by its definition gives exactly the formula in the proposition.

It remains to justify the boundary \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\).  Since \(\varepsilon_{1,n}>0\), replace the zero outcome radius temporarily by any \(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n}]\).  The maximum defining \(b_{2,n}\) is unchanged for all sufficiently small \(u\), so the same eligibility condition holds.  Every law satisfying the zero-radius restriction also satisfies the radius-\(u\) restriction.  Applying the positive-radius bound and sending \(u\downarrow0\) makes its right-hand side decrease to the displayed value with \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0\), which proves the monotone boundary extension.

If also \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), then \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\), restricted from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) to its comparison subclass, yields a spectral generalized-quantile guarantee of order \(n^{-1/2}\).  Put
\[
 A_n=\varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
     +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}.
\]
All constants in \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) are fixed and positive.  Hence \(A_n\gg n^{-1/2}\) implies that the ratio of the spectral upper guarantee to \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\) tends to zero.  This compares two valid upper guarantees; no lower bound for the ACE estimator follows.
