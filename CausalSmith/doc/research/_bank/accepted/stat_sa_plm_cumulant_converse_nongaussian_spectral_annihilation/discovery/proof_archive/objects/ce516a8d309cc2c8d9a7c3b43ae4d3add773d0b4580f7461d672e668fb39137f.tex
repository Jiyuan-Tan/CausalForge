Three rigorous benchmarks are available, but none is a full-class minimax phase diagram.

First, let \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) be the local ACE class obtained by replacing the fixed cumulant separation in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) by \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta_n\), leaving all other restrictions unchanged, and put
\[
 \widetilde b_{2,n}
 =200\min\{1,C_{\theta}\}\delta_n
 \max\!\left\{
   \varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n},
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})
 \right\}^{-1}.
\]
When \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), all displayed logarithms and denominators are defined, and
\[
 r\le
 \min\!\left\{
   \frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}
   -\frac{1}{a_{1,n}}\log\!\left(\frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}\right),
   \frac{\widetilde b_{2,n}}{a_2\log(a_2\widetilde b_{2,n})}
 \right\},
\]
the already established local benchmark gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)\\
 &\quad\le
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{35mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
This is an oracle upper benchmark under its exact eligibility condition; it is neither an MSE converse nor a sample-measurable switching rule.

Second, on the explicit path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=\lvert\kappa_4(\eta_a)\rvert=2a^4,
\]
the computable known-zero sine ratio satisfies, whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le
 \frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the pathwise risk bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,
  \frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
The supremum here is only over the displayed Gaussian--Rademacher path laws, so this calculation is not a converse, nor is it a uniform upper bound for the class specified solely by fourth-cumulant separation.

Third, the zero-localization lemma at separation \(\delta_n\) yields
\[
 R_{0,n}\le
 A_k\left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
With \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\), the nuisance factor is zero-free on the relevant disk whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le
 \{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}.
\]
For every fixed \(n\), compactness at the positive separation \(\delta_n\) then supplies a finite contour bank and a positive boundary modulus. Consequently, after fixing that non-effective bank, the fixed-separation argument gives only the pointwise existential statement
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le \frac{C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)}{n}
\]
under a non-effective threshold \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0(\delta_n)\). No proved bound controls \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)\), the bank size, or its boundary modulus as \(\delta_n\downarrow0\); therefore this last display is not an asymptotic rate. The sharp phase diagram, adaptive selector, uniform studentized limit law and Wald coverage, and matching local lower bounds remain unresolved.
