For \(n\ge2\), suppose \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\neq\varnothing\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\). There are \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on \(r,\delta,C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\eta},\psi_{\xi}\), such that
\[
 \frac cn\le\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le\frac Cn.
\]
More precisely, there exists a once-for-all deterministic witness bank satisfying \(\text{lem:finite-contour-bank}\), and, conditional on fixing any such witness bank, the statistic \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) in \(\text{def:adaptive-contour-estimator}\) is a measurable sample map satisfying
\[
 \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}
 \le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 E_P[(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le\frac Cn.
\]
This is an existence-level fixed-bank minimax result: no algorithm for obtaining the bank, its radii, or \(a_{\star}\) from the observations and displayed class constants is asserted. After the bank is fixed, the statistic uses \(\bar g_n\), uses no supplied outcome code, and does not know the law of \(\eta\). Its MSE bound implies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}-\theta_0(P)|)
 \le\sqrt{C/(\gamma n)}.
\]
No inclusion of the broad class \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) is asserted; the finite-order ACE comparison is confined to the separately defined subclass \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).
