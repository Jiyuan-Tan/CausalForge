Complex zeros of the treatment-noise moment generating function yield exact shift-annihilating instruments. With \(Z_n=T-\bar g_n(X)\), the identities \(F_n=M H_n\) and \(G_n=\theta_0F_n'+M B_n\) give an observable contour formula, and a once-for-all non-effective contour bank plus a measurable split-sample selector yields uniform minimax MSE \(\Theta(n^{-1})\) on the fixed-separated non-Gaussian class whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), including fixed nonzero treatment-code error and without an outcome learner. The formerly advertised Gaussian lower-bound arm is invalid for the displayed simultaneous class: bounded \(Y\), conditional mean independence, and nondegenerate Gaussian treatment noise force \(\theta_0=0\), so its minimax MSE and generalized-quantile risks are zero. The paper therefore leads with the proved non-Gaussian exact-annihilation result, retains the ACE comparison only on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\), and leaves the local-to-Gaussian frontier open.
