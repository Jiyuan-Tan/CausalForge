Use the exact Luxemburg convention in \(\text{ass:eta-subgaussian}\), and put
\[
 R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1,
 \qquad R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_{\mathrm{fac}}+1,
 \qquad
 N_{\star}=\left\lceil
 \frac{4\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2}
      {\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})}
 \right\rceil.
\]
For \(m\ge3\), define the translated-dyadic grid
\[
 \mathcal Q_m=
 \{R_0+1/4+q2^{-m}:q\in\mathbb Z,\ 0\le q2^{-m}\le1/2\}.
\]
Let \(m_{\star}\) be the least \(m\ge3\) for which \(|\mathcal Q_m|>N_{\star}\), enumerate \(\mathcal Q_{m_{\star}}\) increasingly as \(\rho_1<\cdots<\rho_J\), and set
\[
 d_{\star}=2^{-m_{\star}},\qquad h_{\star}=d_{\star}/3,
 \qquad
 \Lambda_{\star}=\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}-R_1},
\]
\[
 a_{\star}=
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi_{\eta}^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}}.
\]
The circles \(C_j=\{z:|z|=\rho_j\}\) form the once-for-all translated-dyadic contour bank.  The bank, its cardinality, and its positive modulus are explicit functions of \((k,\delta,\psi_{\eta},R_0,R_1)\) through the displayed arithmetic, logarithm, exponential, ceiling, and finite search.  The construction does not use the unknown law of \(\eta\).
