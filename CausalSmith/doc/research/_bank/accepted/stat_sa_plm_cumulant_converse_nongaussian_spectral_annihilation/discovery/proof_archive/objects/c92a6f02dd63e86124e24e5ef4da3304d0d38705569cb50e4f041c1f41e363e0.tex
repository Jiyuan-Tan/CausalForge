Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{mathrm G,n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).  Outcome mean independence and the residual equation imply
\[
 E_P[Y\mid X,T]=q_0(X)+\theta_0\eta.
\]
Because \(|Y|\le C_q\) almost surely, its conditional mean has absolute value at most \(C_q\) almost surely.  Conditional on almost every \(X=x\), independence and the Gaussian assumption make \(\eta\sim N(0,\sigma^2)\) with \(\sigma>0\).  If \(\theta_0\ne0\), the affine Gaussian variable \(q_0(x)+\theta_0\eta\) has unbounded support, contradicting the preceding almost-sure bound.  Hence \(\theta_0(P)=0\).  If the class is nonempty, the identically zero statistic has zero squared loss and zero generalized-quantile loss for every member.  Nonnegativity of both losses and \(\text{def:minimax-risks}\) give \(\mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm G}=\mathfrak M_{n,1-\gamma}^{\mathrm G}=0\).
