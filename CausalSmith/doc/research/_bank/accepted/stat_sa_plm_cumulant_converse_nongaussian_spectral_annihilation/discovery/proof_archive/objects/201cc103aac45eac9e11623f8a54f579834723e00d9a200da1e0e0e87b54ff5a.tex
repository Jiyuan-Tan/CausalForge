Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) and write \(\psi=\psi_{\eta}\).  We prove the zero-count budget, the separated grid circle, the boundary modulus, and the finite-name computation.

By \(\text{lem:zero-localization}\), \(M\) has a zero in \(\{|z|\le R_0\}\), and its proof gives
\[
 |M(z)|\le \exp(4\psi^2|z|^2),\qquad z\in\mathbb C. \tag{1}
\]
Put \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_1+1\) and \(R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_1+2\).  Jensen's identity implies that the number \(N\), with multiplicity, of zeros satisfying \(|a|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) obeys
\[
 N\log\frac{R_{\mathrm{out}}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}}
 \le4\psi^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2. \tag{2}
\]
Indeed, apply Jensen's identity on zero-free radii increasing to \(R_{\mathrm{out}}\).  Each zero inside \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) contributes at least \(\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})\), while \(M(0)=1\) and (1) bound the boundary average.

For \(x>0\), integration of \((1+t)^{-1}\) on \([0,x]\) gives
\[
 \log(1+x)\ge \frac{x}{1+x}. \tag{3}
\]
Since \(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}}=1+1/(R_1+1)\), (2)--(3) yield
\[
 N\le4\psi^2(R_1+2)^3. \tag{4}
\]
The width-one supplied intervals in \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\) contain \(\psi\) and \(R_1\), so \(\psi\le U_{\psi}\) and \(R_1\le U_R\).  Hence (4) gives \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\).

There are \(J=2^{m_{\star}-1}+1>N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) grid radii, all in \((R_0,R_1)\), with spacing \(d_{\star}\).  Since \(2h_{\star}<d_{\star}\), a fixed zero modulus lies within distance \(h_{\star}\) of at most one grid radius.  Every zero that could violate this separation has modulus below \(R_1+h_{\star}<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), so at most \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) grid radii are excluded.  The pigeonhole principle supplies a radius \(\rho_j\) such that
\[
 \bigl||a|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge h_{\star} \tag{5}
\]
for every zero \(a\) in \(|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\).  Thus \(M\) is zero-free on \(C_j\).  Since \(\rho_j>R_0\), the localized zero lies strictly inside \(C_j\), so \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\).

Let \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\) be the zeros in \(|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), repeated by multiplicity, and form
\[
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^N
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}.
\]
After filling removable singularities, \(f=M/Q\) is holomorphic and zero-free in that disk, while every factor of \(Q\) has modulus one on its boundary.  Apply Cauchy's formula to \(f^m\), take the \(m\)-th root, and let \(m\to\infty\) in (1).  This gives
\[
 |f(z)|\le e^A,qquad A=4\psi^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2. \tag{6}
\]
Moreover \(|Q(0)|\le1=M(0)\), so \(|f(0)|\ge1\).  The function \(u=A-\log|f|\) is nonnegative and harmonic, with \(u(0)\le A\).  The Poisson kernel on a circle increasing to radius \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) is bounded, for \(|z|\le R_1\), by
\[
 \Lambda_{\star}
 =\frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}-R_1}.
\]
The mean-value identity therefore gives \(u(z)\le\Lambda_{\star}A\), hence
\[
 |f(z)|\ge
 \exp\{-4(\Lambda_{\star}-1)\psi^2R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2\}. \tag{7}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), (5) gives, factor by factor,
\[
 \left|
 \frac{R_{\mathrm{fac}}(z-a_\nu)}
      {R_{\mathrm{fac}}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}
 \right|
 \ge\frac{h_{\star}}{R_{\mathrm{fac}}+R_1}. \tag{8}
\]
Because this base lies in \((0,1)\) and \(N\le N_{\mathrm{cert}}\), (7)--(8) imply
\[
 \inf_{C_j}|M|
 \ge e^{-8R_1\psi^2(R_1+1)^2}
 \left(\frac{h_{\star}}{2R_1+1}\right)^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}}. \tag{9}
\]
The displayed integer bounds in the definition satisfy
\[
 8R_1\psi^2(R_1+1)^2\le e_{\star},
 \qquad
 \frac{h_{\star}}{2R_1+1}
 \ge\frac{2^{-m_{\star}}}{3(2U_R+1)}.
\]
Since \(e<3\), \(3^{-e_{\star}}\ge2^{-2e_{\star}}\), and \(2^{d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}}\ge d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}}=3(2U_R+1)\), the right-hand side of (9) is at least
\[
 2^{-2e_{\star}}
 \left\{2^{-(m_{\star}+d_{\star}^{\mathrm{den}})}\right\}^{N_{\mathrm{cert}}}
 =2^{-u_{\star}}=a_{\star}. \tag{10}
\]

It remains to audit effectiveness.  The only refinements are the two calls of supplied moduli at error one.  All later quantities are rational endpoint operations, certified addition with a rational point record, or primitive-recursive integer powers and finite enumerations.  Thus the trace is finite and makes no exact-real query and no call to the unknown transform or law.  An interval of width at most one containing \(\psi\) has upper endpoint at most \(\psi+1\), and similarly for \(R_1\); therefore the bank size and inverse-dyadic certificate are bounded by functions of the represented values uniformly over valid supplied names.  Identical supplied records give identical outputs, and the record is fixed across \(n\).  This proves the claimed once-for-all bank and modulus certificate.
