The frozen primitives provide only the qualitative compactness constant \(a_{\star}>0\) at each fixed separation.  They give no quantitative lower bound on \(a_{\star}(\delta_n)\), no variance bound for the growing-radius contour bank, and no contiguous Gaussian-versus-non-Gaussian alternatives.  Those three quantities are exactly what is needed to optimize among the contour, ACE, and DML regimes and to prove matching lower bounds and uniform coverage.
