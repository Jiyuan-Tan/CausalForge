For \(s\ge r+1\), every \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) satisfies the \(L^r(P_X)\) code controls needed to test membership in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\), because \(\|f\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\|f\|_{L^s(P_X)}\).
