Three rigorous benchmarks are available, but they do not determine the requested full-class minimax phase diagram.  First, applying the Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis finite-order theorem pointwise with separation \(\delta_n\) gives, under its exact local eligibility and positivity conditions,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)|\right)\\
 &\quad\le C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Big[
  \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
  +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}
  +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
  \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
  (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Big].
 \end{aligned}
\]
Together with any separately valid DML bound, this yields only an oracle upper envelope, not a data-driven selector or a minimax rate.

Second, on the explicit path
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
 \qquad
 \Delta_a=|\kappa_4(\eta_a)|=2a^4,
\]
the exact sine-annihilation estimator obeys, whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
the pathwise bound
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
The load-bearing moment bounds in that calculation are, with the corrected inequalities,
\[
 E_P|D_n|
 \le\|D_n\|_{L^s(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n},
 \qquad
 E_P[Z_n^2]\le1+4C_g^2,
\]
and
\[
 E_P[(\mathbb P_nR)^2]
 =\frac{\operatorname{Var}_P(R)}{n}
 \le\frac{K_R}{n}.
\]
This bound uses the full Gaussian--Rademacher transform and is not uniform over the class specified only by a fourth-cumulant lower bound.

Third, zero localization gives the proof-intermediate radius
\[
 R_{0,n}\le A_k
 \left(\frac{\psi_{\eta}^2}{\delta_n}\right)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
The nuisance-factor argument then preserves exact contour identification whenever
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}
 \le
 \left[4(R_{0,n}+1)e^{2C_g(R_{0,n}+1)}\right]^{-1}.
\]
This last condition is an identification benchmark, not a uniform risk rate: fixed-separation compactness supplies no quantitative lower bound on the contour boundary modulus as \(\delta_n\downarrow0\).  The present primitives therefore provide no cumulant-only matching lower bound, full-class elbow, adaptive selector, or uniform coverage theorem.
