At every fixed \(\delta>0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) gives a total ordinary-sample Borel statistic satisfying
\[
 \frac{c}{n}\le \mathfrak R_n^{\mathrm{NG}}\le \frac{C}{n}
\]
whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\); the executable represented-data conclusion is conditional on implementing and compiling \(\text{def:certified-contour-arithmetic-substrate}\). \[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2\right]
 \le C\min\!\left\{1,
 n^{-1}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}
\]
provided \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\Delta_a^{1/4}/(2^{1/4}\pi)\). These results do not define or attain a sharp full-class shrinking-separation rate, construct an adaptive selector or confidence procedure, or furnish a matching local lower bound.
