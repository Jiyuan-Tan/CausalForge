Every treatment-noise transform represented in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) has some \(j\) such that \(N_{C_j}(M)\ge1\) and
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_j}|M(z)|\ge a_{\star}.
\]
