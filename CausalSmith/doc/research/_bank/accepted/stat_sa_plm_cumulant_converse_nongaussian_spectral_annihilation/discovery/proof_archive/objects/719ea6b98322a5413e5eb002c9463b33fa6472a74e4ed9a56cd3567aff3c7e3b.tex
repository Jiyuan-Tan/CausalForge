Finite-order orthogonal and ACE methods provide nuisance-dependent upper guarantees, while classical location-family work recognizes transform zeros; neither supplies the present learned-residual contour estimator with an explicit certified translated-dyadic bank and matched fixed-separation minimax mean-squared error.  The published ACE class embeds in the broad direct-\(L^1(P_X)\) spectral class, which licenses a full-class spectral upper guarantee and a class-minimax converse but not a lower bound for the ACE estimator.  The simultaneous bounded-outcome Gaussian assumptions around Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 3.2 instead force the target to vanish and therefore serve only as a source-assumption diagnostic.
