Membership in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}\) gives directly
\[
 E_P|D_n|
 =\lVert g_0-\bar g_n\rVert_{L^1(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n}.
\]
The treatment regression and its clipped supplied code both take values in \([-C_g,C_g]\).  Therefore
\[
 |D_n|=|g_0(X)-\bar g_n(X)|\le2C_g
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
For every \(w\in\mathbb C\),
\[
 e^w-1=w\int_0^1e^{tw}\,dt,
 \qquad
 |e^w-1|\le|w|e^{|w|}.
\]
Consequently, uniformly over \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |H_n(z)-1|
 &\le E_P|e^{zD_n}-1|\\
 &\le |z|E_P[|D_n|e^{|z||D_n|}]\\
 &\le R_1e^{2C_gR_1}\varepsilon_{1,n}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
If \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0=\{4R_1e^{2C_gR_1}\}^{-1}\), this upper bound is at most \(1/4\), and hence
\[
 |H_n(z)|\ge1-|H_n(z)-1|\ge\frac34,
 \qquad |z|\le R_1.
\]
Thus \(H_n\) is zero-free throughout the disk.  By \(\text{lem:observable-factorization}\), \(F_n=MH_n\).  Multiplication by a nonvanishing analytic function neither creates a zero nor changes a zero's multiplicity, which proves the final assertion.
