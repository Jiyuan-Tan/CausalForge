The strongest closed results are constructive at every fixed positive separation.  The effective bank has
\[
 N_{\star}=\left\lceil\frac{c_{\psi}\psi_{\eta}^2U^2}{\log(U/T)}\right\rceil,\qquad
 a_{\star}^{\mathrm{eff}}
 =\exp\{-(\Lambda-1)c_{\psi}\psi_{\eta}^2T^2\}
 \left(\frac{h}{T+R_1}\right)^{N_{\star}},
\]
with \(R_0\) determined by fixed cumulant separation, \(R_1=R_0+1\), \(T=R_1+1\), and \(U=T+1\).  The certified statistic has \(C/n\) MSE whenever \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\).  Under shrinking separation, substitution of the corresponding \(R_{0,n}\) gives a computable but rapidly deteriorating upper bound; it is not matched by a local converse and does not establish honest adaptive inference.  Independently, \(\text{thm:local-to-gaussian-partial-benchmarks}\) supplies the exact local ACE generalized-quantile upper benchmark and, for \(k=4\), the Gaussian--Rademacher-path sine-estimator bound.  No proved result identifies the sharp full-class phase diagram or validates a data-driven switch among these regimes.
