Let \(c_{\psi}<\infty\) be the universal constant in the moment-generating-function consequence of the \(\psi_2\) bound:
\[
 E_P[e^{t\eta}]\le \exp(c_{\psi}\psi_{\eta}^{2}t^{2}),
 \qquad t\in\mathbb R.
\]
This follows directly by expanding the exponential and using the sub-Gaussian moment bounds \(\|\eta\|_{L^p}\le C\psi_{\eta}\sqrt p\).  Hence, for complex \(z\),
\[
 |M(z)|\le E_P[e^{\operatorname{Re}(z)\eta}]
 \le \exp(c_{\psi}\psi_{\eta}^{2}|z|^{2}).
\]

Fix \(R>0\) and suppose, toward a contradiction, that \(M\) has no zero in \(\{|z|\le R\}\).  Since the disk is simply connected and \(M(0)=1\), there is an analytic logarithm \(K=\log M\) on a neighborhood of the disk, normalized by \(K(0)=0\).  Put \(A=c_{\psi}\psi_{\eta}^{2}R^{2}\).  The preceding display implies
\[
 \operatorname{Re}K(z)=\log|M(z)|\le A,
 \qquad |z|\le R.
\]
For completeness, the needed analytic bound follows without an additional probabilistic assumption.  The function
\[
 h(z)=\frac{K(z)}{2A-K(z)}
\]
satisfies \(|h(z)|\le1\), because
\[
 |2A-K(z)|^{2}-|K(z)|^{2}
 =4A\{A-\operatorname{Re}K(z)\}\ge0,
\]
and \(h(0)=0\).  Applying the Schwarz inequality after rescaling the radius-
\(R\) disk gives \(|h(z)|\le |z|/R\).  Thus, for \(|z|\le R/2\),
\[
 |K(z)|\le \frac{2A(|z|/R)}{1-|z|/R}\le2A.
\]
The Cauchy coefficient formula on \(|z|=R/2\) now yields
\[
 |\kappa_k(\eta)|=|K^{(k)}(0)|
 \le k!\frac{2A}{(R/2)^k}
 =2^{k+1}c_{\psi}k!\,\psi_{\eta}^{2}R^{2-k}.
\]
Choose
\[
 A_k=(2^{k+2}c_{\psi}k!)^{1/(k-2)}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 R_0=A_k(\psi_{\eta}^{2}/\delta)^{1/(k-2)}.
\]
If \(M\) were zero-free on the radius-\(R_0\) disk, the preceding inequality would give \(|\kappa_k(\eta)|\le\delta/2\), contradicting cumulant separation.  Therefore \(M\) has a zero with \(|z|\le R_0\).  This choice has the asserted dependence and, in particular, \(R_0\le A_k(\psi_{\eta}^{2}/\delta)^{1/(k-2)}\), with equality for the displayed choice.
