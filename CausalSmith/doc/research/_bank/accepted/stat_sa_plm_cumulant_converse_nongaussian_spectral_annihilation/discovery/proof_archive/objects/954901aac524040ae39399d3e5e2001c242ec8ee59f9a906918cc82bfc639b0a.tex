We first derive the exact moment-generating-function constant required below.  The Luxemburg convention and monotone convergence from scales larger than \(\psi_{\eta}\) imply
\[
 E_P\exp(\eta^2/\psi_{\eta}^2)\le2.
\]
Consequently, tail integration gives, for every \(p>0\),
\[
 E_P|\eta|^p
 \le2\psi_{\eta}^p\Gamma(p/2+1).
\]
For the integer moments \(p\ge1\) used below, \(\Gamma(p/2+1)\le p!\), and hence
\[
 E_P|\eta|^p\le2\psi_{\eta}^p p!.
\]
Put \(s=|t|\psi_{\eta}\).  If \(s\le1/2\), centering removes the linear term and therefore
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 E_Pe^{t\eta}
 &\le1+\sum_{p\ge2}\frac{|t|^pE_P|\eta|^p}{p!}
 \le1+2\sum_{p\ge2}s^p\\
 &=1+\frac{2s^2}{1-s}
 \le1+4s^2
 \le e^{4s^2}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
If \(s\ge1/2\), Young's inequality and Cauchy--Schwarz give
\[
 E_Pe^{t\eta}
 \le e^{s^2/2}E_Pe^{\eta^2/(2\psi_{\eta}^2)}
 \le\sqrt2e^{s^2/2}
 \le e^{4s^2},
\]
where the last inequality follows from \((\log2)/2+s^2/2\le4s^2\) for \(s\ge1/2\).  Hence, for every \(z\in\mathbb C\),
\[
 |M(z)|\le E_Pe^{\operatorname{Re}(z)\eta}
 \le e^{4\psi_{\eta}^2|z|^2}. \tag{1}
\]

Suppose for contradiction that \(M\) is zero-free on the closed radius-\(R\) disk.  Since \(M(0)=1\), there is an analytic logarithm \(K=\log M\) there with \(K(0)=0\).  By (1),
\[
 \operatorname{Re}K(z)\le A:=4\psi_{\eta}^2R^2,
 \qquad |z|\le R.
\]
The function \(h(z)=K(z)/(2A-K(z))\) maps the disk into the closed unit disk, because
\[
 |2A-K(z)|^2-|K(z)|^2=4A\{A-\operatorname{Re}K(z)\}\ge0,
\]
and \(h(0)=0\).  Schwarz's lemma after rescaling gives \(|h(z)|\le|z|/R\), whence \(|K(z)|\le2A\) for \(|z|\le R/2\).  Cauchy's estimate now yields
\[
 |\kappa_k(\eta)|=|K^{(k)}(0)|
 \le \frac{k!\,2A}{(R/2)^k}
 =2^{k+3}k!\psi_{\eta}^2R^{2-k}. \tag{2}
\]
At \(R=R_0\), the right-hand side of (2) is \(\delta/2\), contradicting \(|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta\).  Thus a zero lies in the stated closed disk.  The divisions use \(k\ge3\), \(\delta>0\), and \(\psi_{\eta}>0\); no overlap, rank, or positivity condition is involved.
