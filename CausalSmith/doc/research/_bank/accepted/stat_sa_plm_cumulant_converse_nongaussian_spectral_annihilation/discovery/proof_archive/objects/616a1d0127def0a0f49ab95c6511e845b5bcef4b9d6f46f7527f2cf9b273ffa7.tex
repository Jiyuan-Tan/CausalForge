The exact local specialization of Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 5.4 gives the displayed ACE generalized-quantile upper bound with separation \(\delta_n\), subject to its unchanged positivity and eligibility conditions.  On the explicit Gaussian--Rademacher path with \(\Delta_a=2a^4\), \(\text{lem:gaussian-rademacher-l1-benchmark}\) proves under
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi}
\]
that
\[
 \sup_P E_P[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2]
 \le C\min\!\left\{1,\frac1n\Delta_a^{-1/2}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)\right\}.
\]
This path result needs only direct \(L^1(P_X)\) treatment-code control.  Finally, zero localization at separation \(\delta_n\) gives \(R_{0,n}\le A_k(\psi_{\eta}^2/\delta_n)^{1/(k-2)}\), and the nuisance transform is zero-free on \(R_{1,n}=R_{0,n}+1\) when \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\{4R_{1,n}e^{2C_gR_{1,n}}\}^{-1}\).  For each fixed positive \(\delta_n\), compactness then yields a finite non-effective contour bank and a positive boundary modulus, hence only an existential bound \(C_{\mathrm{spec}}(\delta_n)/n\).  No proved estimate controls that constant, the bank size, or the boundary modulus as \(\delta_n\downarrow0\).
