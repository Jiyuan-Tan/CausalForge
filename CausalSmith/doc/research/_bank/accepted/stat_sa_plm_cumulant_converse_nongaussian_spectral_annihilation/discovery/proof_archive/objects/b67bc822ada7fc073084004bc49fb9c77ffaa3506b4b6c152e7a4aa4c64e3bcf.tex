The causal target remains \(\theta_0(P)\); the contour expression is its observed-law identifying functional under the stated factorization and zero-free conditions, and \(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) is a distinct sample statistic.  The rate theorem is an existence result in statistical decision theory: one deterministic bank works for the whole fixed-separation class, and conditional on fixing it the sample rule is measurable and achieves matched parametric MSE.  This does not imply that a downstream analyst can recover that bank from the numerical class constants.
