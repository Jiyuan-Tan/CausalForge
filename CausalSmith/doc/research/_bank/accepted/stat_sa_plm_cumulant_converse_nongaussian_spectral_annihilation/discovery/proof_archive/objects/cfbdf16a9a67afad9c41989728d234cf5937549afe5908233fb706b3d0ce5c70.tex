The concrete paper-independent substrate is the object \(\mathsf{CCIA}\) exported by \(\mathsf{Causalean.Mathlib.Analysis.CertifiedContourIntervalArithmetic}\), extended by the following executable operations.  A certified real name is a record
\[
 \mathfrak x=(x,(I_m)_{m\ge0},\mu_x)
\]
with rational closed intervals \(I_{m+1}\subseteq I_m\), \(x\in I_m\), and a computable modulus \(\mu_x:\mathbb Q_{>0}\to\mathbb N\) satisfying \(\operatorname{width}(I_{\mu_x(e)})\le e\).  A positive certified name additionally carries a rational witness \(p_x>0\) with \(p_x\le x\).  Its executable fields are \(I_m\), \(\mu_x\), and \(p_x\); algorithms below do not inspect the exact-real value field \(x\).

A complex rational rectangle is a pair of rational intervals.  The substrate implements, by the usual endpoint formulas, outward addition, subtraction, multiplication
\[
 (A+iB)(C+iD)=(AC-BD)+i(AD+BC),
\]
and division
\[
 \frac{A+iB}{C+iD}
 =\frac{(A+iB)(C-iD)}{C^2+D^2}
\]
when a positive rational lower bound for \(|C+iD|\) has been certified.  It implements rational lower and upper modulus bounds by outward square, square-root bisection, and monotonicity.  Each operation returns both a nested enclosure and an effective width modulus obtained compositionally from the operand moduli and rational magnitude bounds.

The substrate contains a fixed certified name of \(\pi\).  Rational range reduction and the alternating Taylor series with its first-omitted-term bound give nested certified enclosures of \(\sin u\) and \(\cos u\), with an explicit precision function.  Consequently, from a certified positive radius name \(\mathfrak r\), integers \(N\ge1\) and \(0\le q\le N\), it computes certified rectangles for
\[
 r\exp(2\pi iq/N)
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 2\pi i r\exp(2\pi iq/N).
\]
For a complex rectangle \(A+iB\), the complex-exponential extension is computed as
\[
 \exp(A+iB)=\exp(A)\{\cos(B)+i\sin(B)\},
\]
using the rational real-exponential Taylor extension and the preceding trigonometric extensions.  Range reduction and explicit Taylor remainders provide a nested effectively refining extension on every rational rectangle.

Finally, finite complex-rectangle sums and products are primitive recursive, and a uniform-mesh trapezoidal enclosure with a supplied rational Lipschitz bound \(L\) uses mesh error \(L/N\).  Finite infimum and supremum enclosures use the same error allowance.  All divisions are guarded by a positive rational modulus certificate; all stopping tests compare rational endpoints; and every precision and mesh size is obtained by applying a displayed operand modulus or by a formula such as \(N=\lceil L/e\rceil+1\).  Thus every call has an explicit finite fuel bound.  These clauses specify the concrete implementation used by \(\text{def:contour-bank-handle}\) and \(\text{def:adaptive-contour-estimator}\); \(\mathsf{CCIA}\) is not an interface variable, theorem-local oracle, or additional statistical input.
