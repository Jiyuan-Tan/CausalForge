Because both \(g_0\) and \(\bar g_n\) take values in \([-C_g,C_g]\),
\[
 |D_n|=|g_0(X)-\bar g_n(X)|\le2C_g
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Since \(P_X\) is a probability law and \(s\ge r+1\ge1\), Lyapunov's inequality and the treatment-code-radius assumption give
\[
 E_P|D_n|=\|g_0-\bar g_n\|_{L^1(P_X)}
 \le\|g_0-\bar g_n\|_{L^s(P_X)}
 \le\varepsilon_{1,n}.
\]
For arbitrary complex \(w\), the identity
\[
 e^w-1=w\int_0^1e^{tw}\,dt
\]
implies \(|e^w-1|\le |w|e^{|w|}\).  Therefore, uniformly over \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |H_n(z)-1|
 &\le E_P|e^{zD_n}-1|\\
 &\le |z|E_P[|D_n|e^{|z||D_n|}]\\
 &\le R_1e^{2C_gR_1}\varepsilon_{1,n}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
If \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0=\{4R_1e^{2C_gR_1}\}^{-1}\), then this bound is at most \(1/4\), whence
\[
 |H_n(z)|\ge1-|H_n(z)-1|\ge3/4
 \qquad (|z|\le R_1).
\]
Thus \(H_n\) has no zero in the disk.  By observable factorization, \(F_n=M H_n\); multiplication by a nonvanishing analytic function neither creates a zero nor changes its multiplicity.  Hence \(F_n\) and \(M\) have exactly the same zeros there, counted with multiplicity.
