The following two conclusions are valid, but they do not determine the full-class minimax phase diagram.

First, for each \(n\), let \(\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)\) be the class obtained from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\) in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) by replacing the fixed restriction \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta\) by \(\lvert\kappa_{r+1}(\eta)\rvert\ge\delta_n\), without changing any other restriction.  Define
\[
 \widetilde b_{2,n}
 =200\min\{1,C_{\theta}\}\delta_n
 \max\!\left\{
   \varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n},
   (\gamma n)^{-1/2}(\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta})
 \right\}^{-1}.
\]
Suppose \(\varepsilon_{1,n},\varepsilon_{2,n}>0\), all logarithms and denominators in the following display are defined, and the exact local eligibility condition
\[
 r\le
 \min\!\left\{
   \frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}
   -\frac{1}{a_{1,n}}\log\!\left(\frac{b_{1,n}}{a_{1,n}}\right),
   \frac{\widetilde b_{2,n}}{a_2\log(a_2\widetilde b_{2,n})}
 \right\}
\]
holds.  Then the order-\(r\) ACE estimator obeys the local oracle benchmark
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal C_n(\delta_n)}
 Q_{P,1-\gamma}
 \!\left(\left|\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{ACE},r,n}-\theta_0(P)\right|\right)\\
 &\quad\le
 C_{\gamma}r!16^r\delta_n^{-1}
 \Bigl[
   \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}
   +C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\\
 &\hspace{42mm}
   +64(C_g+\psi_{\eta})^r
    \{r^2(C_g+\psi_{\eta})+\psi_{\xi}+C_{\theta}\psi_{\eta}\}
    (\gamma n)^{-1/2}
 \Bigr].
\end{aligned}
\]
Denote the right-hand side by \(U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}\).  If \(U_{\mathrm{DML},n}\) denotes a separately valid ordinary-DML upper bound under its own hypotheses, then \(\min\{U_{\mathrm{DML},n},U_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{loc}}\}\) is only an oracle upper envelope; this comparison neither asserts a minimax rate nor constructs a data-driven selector.

Second, specialize to \(k=4\), hence \(r=3\).  For \(0<a\le1\), let
\[
 \eta_a=\sqrt{1-a^2}\,G_{\circ}+aS,
\]
where \(G_{\circ}\sim N(0,1)\), \(S\) is symmetric Rademacher, and \(G_{\circ}\perp S\).  Its moment generating function, fourth cumulant, and first positive characteristic zero are
\[
 M_a(z)=\exp\!\left(\frac{1-a^2}{2}z^2\right)\cosh(az),
 \qquad
 \kappa_4(\eta_a)=-2a^4,
 \qquad
 t_a=\frac{\pi}{2a}.
\]
For every real \(d\),
\[
 E\!\left[\sin\{t_a(\eta_a+d)\}\right]=0.
\]
Consider any law satisfying \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}P\), \(\eta=\eta_a\perp X\), \(E_P[\xi\mid X,T]=0\) almost surely, \(\lvert\theta_0(P)\rvert\le C_{\theta}\), \(\lVert g_0\rVert_{\infty}\le C_g\), \(\lVert q_0\rVert_{\infty}\le C_q\), \(\lVert\xi\rVert_{\psi_2\mid X}\le\psi_{\xi}\) almost surely, and \(\lVert\bar g_n-g_0\rVert_{L^1(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n}\).  Put
\[
 A_a=a\exp\!\left\{-\frac{(1-a^2)\pi^2}{8a^2}\right\}.
\]
If \(t_a\varepsilon_{1,n}\le1/2\), then
\[
 E_P\!\left[Z_n\sin(t_aZ_n)\right]
 =A_aE_P[\cos(t_aD_n)]
 \ge\frac{A_a}{2}.
\]
Writing \(\mathbb P_n\) for the empirical average, define the computable clipped ratio
\[
 \widehat\theta_{a,n}
 =
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle
 \Pi_{[-C_{\theta},C_{\theta}]}
 \frac{\mathbb P_n[Y\sin(t_aZ_n)]}
      {\mathbb P_n[Z_n\sin(t_aZ_n)]},
 &\displaystyle
 \mathbb P_n[Z_n\sin(t_aZ_n)]\ge A_a/4,\\[3mm]
 0,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
There is a finite constant \(C\), depending only on \(C_{\theta},C_g,C_q,\psi_{\xi}\), such that
\[
 \sup_P E_P\!\left[(\widehat\theta_{a,n}-\theta_0(P))^2\right]
 \le
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}a^{-2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{(1-a^2)\pi^2}{4a^2}\right)
 \right\},
\]
where the supremum is over the just-displayed path laws.  Equivalently, with \(\Delta_a=2a^4=\lvert\kappa_4(\eta_a)\rvert\), the condition \(t_a\varepsilon_{1,n}\le1/2\) is
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}\le\frac{\Delta_a^{1/4}}{2^{1/4}\pi},
\]
and the preceding risk is at most
\[
 C\min\!\left\{
  1,\frac{1}{n}\Delta_a^{-1/2}
  \exp\!\left(\frac{\pi^2\sqrt2}{4}\Delta_a^{-1/2}\right)
 \right\}.
\]
This last display is a construction-specific upper bound with \(\varepsilon_{1,n}=O(\Delta_a^{1/4})\); it is not asserted to be sharp for the class specified only by a fourth-cumulant lower bound.
