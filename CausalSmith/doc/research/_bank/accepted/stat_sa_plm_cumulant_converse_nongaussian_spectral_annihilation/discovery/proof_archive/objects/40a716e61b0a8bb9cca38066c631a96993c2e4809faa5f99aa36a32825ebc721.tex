Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\).  Since \(P_X\) is a probability measure and \(s\ge r+1>r\), Lyapunov's inequality gives
\[
 \|\bar g_n-g_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{1,n},
 \qquad
 \|\bar q_n-q_0\|_{L^r(P_X)}\le\varepsilon_{2,n}.
\]
All other properties in \(\text{def:jms-ace-class}\) are inherited from the broad class, proving \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\subseteq\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ACE},n}^{\mathrm{JMS}}\).  For positive radii, restriction of \(\text{lem:jms-ace-quantile-upper}\) gives exactly the displayed \(B_{\mathrm{ACE},n,\gamma}\).  If \(\varepsilon_{2,n}=0<\varepsilon_{1,n}\), enlarge only the outcome radius to \(u\in(0,\varepsilon_{1,n}]\).  The maximum defining \(b_{2,n}\), and hence eligibility, is unchanged; letting \(u\downarrow0\) gives the boundary formula.

When \(n\ge2\) and \(\varepsilon_{1,n}\le\varepsilon_0\), \(\text{thm:adaptive-rootn-minimax}\) restricts its \(O(n^{-1/2})\) generalized-quantile bound to the comparison subclass.  If
\[
 \varepsilon_{1,n}^r\varepsilon_{2,n}+C_{\theta}\varepsilon_{1,n}^{r+1}\gg n^{-1/2},
\]
the fixed positive prefactors show that the exact ACE upper guarantee is of strictly larger order.  This compares upper guarantees only and proves no lower bound for ACE.
