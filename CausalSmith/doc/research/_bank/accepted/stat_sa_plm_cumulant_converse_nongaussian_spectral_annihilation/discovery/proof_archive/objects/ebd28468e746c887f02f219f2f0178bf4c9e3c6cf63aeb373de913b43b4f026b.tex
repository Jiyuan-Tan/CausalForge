The project proves exact annihilation and the observable contour formula, localizes a transform zero uniformly, derives a finite conditioned bank by compact-open compactness and Rouch\'e stability, and proves matched \(n^{-1}\) minimax MSE using a split-sample selector conditional on one fixed bank.  It explicitly separates the usable observable identity and measurable conditional statistic from the non-effective existence of the bank; it makes no claim of computable radii, a computable conditioning modulus, or an operational bank-construction algorithm.
