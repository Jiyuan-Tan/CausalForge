The paper proves the exact annihilator and observable contour identities, a numerical zero-localization radius under the exact Luxemburg convention, an explicit certified translated-dyadic bank with positive dyadic modulus, empirical-transform control, total computability and measurability of the split-sample statistic, and matching \(c/n\) and \(C/n\) MSE bounds.  It retains the exact Jin--Mackey--Syrgkanis Theorem 5.4 ACE upper comparison only on \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{NG},n}^{\mathrm{ACE}}\), and treats the bounded-outcome Gaussian calculation solely as a separate source-assumption diagnostic.
