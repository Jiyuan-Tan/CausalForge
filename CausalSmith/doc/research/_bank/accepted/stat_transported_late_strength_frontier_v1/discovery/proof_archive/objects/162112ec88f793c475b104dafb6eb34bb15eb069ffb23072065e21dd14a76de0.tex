For \(\vartheta\in\Theta\), define
\[
 \psi_{i,\vartheta}=w(X_i)H_i(Y_i-\vartheta D_i).
\]
Conditional randomization, consistency, and the definition of \(H_i\) give
\[
 \mathbb E_P[\psi_{i,\vartheta}]=\mu_{Y,n}-\vartheta\mu_n.
\]
By prop:compact-causal-range this mean is zero at \(\vartheta=\theta_T\).  Moreover, \(|H_i|\le\varepsilon^{-1}\), \(|Y_i-\vartheta D_i|\le2\), and \(\mathbb E_S[w(X)^2]=\kappa_n\), whence
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\psi_{i,\theta_T})\le\frac{4\kappa_n}{\varepsilon^2}.
\]
The weight envelope implies \(w^4\le4k_n^2w^2\).  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\widehat\kappa_n)
 \le\frac{\mathbb E_P[w(X)^4]}{n}
 \le\frac{4k_n^2\kappa_n}{n}.
\]
Since \(\kappa_n\ge1\) by Cauchy--Schwarz, \(k_n=o(n^{1/2})\), and \(\mathbb E_P[\widehat\kappa_n]=\kappa_n\), Chebyshev's inequality yields, uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal P_n\),
\[
 P(\widehat\kappa_n<\kappa_n/2)
 \le\frac{16k_n^2}{n\kappa_n}=o(1).
\]
On the complementary event, failure to include \(\theta_T\) implies
\[
 \left|\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\psi_{i,\theta_T}\right|
 >L_\alpha\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_n}{2n}}.
\]
A second application of Chebyshev's inequality gives
\[
 P\left(\left|\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\psi_{i,\theta_T}\right|
 >L_\alpha\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_n}{2n}}\right)
 \le\frac{8}{L_\alpha^2\varepsilon^2}=\alpha.
\]
Thus
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n}
 P(\theta_T\in C_n)\ge1-\alpha,
\]
so \((C_n)\in\mathfrak C^{\mathrm{or}}\).

For the length bound, put \(r_n=L_\alpha\sqrt{\widehat\kappa_n/n}\).  The acceptance region is the intersection of \(\Theta\) with the inverse image of \([-r_n,r_n]\) under the affine map \(\vartheta\mapsto\widehat A_n-\vartheta\widehat B_n\).  Consequently,
\[
 \lambda(C_n)\le
 \begin{cases}
 \min\{2,2r_n/|\widehat B_n|\},&\widehat B_n\ne0,\\
 2,&\widehat B_n=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
The denominator summand \(w(X_i)H_iD_i\) has mean \(\mu_n\) and variance at most \(\kappa_n/\varepsilon^2\).  Hence
\[
 P(|\widehat B_n-\mu_n|>\mu_n/2)
 \le\frac{4\kappa_n}{\varepsilon^2n\mu_n^2}
 =\frac{4}{\varepsilon^2t_n}.
\]
On the complementary event \(|\widehat B_n|\ge\mu_n/2\), while Jensen's inequality gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P[r_n]
 \le L_\alpha\sqrt{\frac{\mathbb E_P[\widehat\kappa_n]}n}
 =L_\alpha\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_n}{n}}.
\]
It follows that
\[
 \mathbb E_P[\lambda(C_n)]
 \le\frac{4L_\alpha}{\sqrt{t_n}}+
 \frac{8}{\varepsilon^2t_n}.
\]
For \(t_0\ge1\), taking the supremum over \(t_n\ge t_0\) bounds the right-hand side by \((4L_\alpha+8\varepsilon^{-2})t_0^{-1/2}\).  For \(0<t_0<1\), use the deterministic bound \(\lambda(C_n)\le2\).  Taking the required limit superior proves
\[
 R((C_n),t_0)\le C_0\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}.
\]
Combining this bound with thm:oracle-converse proves the asserted global minimax order.  The calculation used the geometry only through \(\kappa_n\) and the uniform envelope required to estimate it, which proves the stated achievability-side invariance.
