Let \(c_0=3(1-\alpha)^2/16\) and \(t_c=1\).  The fixed-geometry theorem, thm:fixed-geometry-frontier, gives the lower bound \(c_0\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}\) for the conditional frontier associated with every admissible deterministic geometry.  Every globally oracle-honest sequence is honest on each fixed-geometry slice, and its global risk is at least its risk on that slice.  Hence, for any fixed admissible geometry \(\mathfrak g\),
\[
 V^\star(t_0)\ge V_{\mathfrak g}^\star(t_0)\ge c_0\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}.
\]
For the finite-cell value, choose the admissible geometry with \(P_S^X\) uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,k_n\}\), \(w=1\), and \(e=1/2\).  Its fixed-geometry slice constructed in thm:fixed-geometry-frontier is contained in \(\mathcal N_n\).  By prop:no-shift-reduction, \(\kappa_n=1\) and the effective-strength index on this slice is \(t_n=n\mu_n^2\).  Thus the same inclusion-of-procedure-classes and inclusion-of-risk-sets argument yields
\[
 V_{\mathcal N}^\star(t_0)\ge c_0\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}.
\]
These conclusions hold for every \(t_0>0\).  When \(0<t_0\le t_c=1\), the minimum equals one, so \(V^\star(t_0)\ge c_0\).  The fixed-geometry construction holds \(P_S^X\), \(w\), \(e\), and \(\kappa_n\) fixed and sets \(\mu_n=\sqrt{t_0\kappa_n/n}\); consequently the constant is independent of how \(t_0=n\mu_n^2/\kappa_n\) is split between first-stage strength and weight dispersion.
