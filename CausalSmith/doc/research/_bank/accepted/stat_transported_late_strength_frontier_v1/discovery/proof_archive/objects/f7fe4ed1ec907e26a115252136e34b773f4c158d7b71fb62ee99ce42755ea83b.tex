Fix \(P\in\mathcal N_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\), write \(p_x=P_T(X=x)\), and note that
\[
 \kappa_n=\sum_{x=1}^{k_n}\frac{p_x^2}{q_{x,n}}.
\]
For \(\vartheta\in\Theta\), set
\[
 g_{i,\vartheta}=H_i(Y_i-\vartheta D_i),
 \qquad a_\vartheta(x)=\mathbb E_S[g_{i,\vartheta}\mid X_i=x].
\]
Conditional randomization gives
\[
 a_\vartheta(x)=\Delta_Y(x)-\vartheta\Delta_D(x),
 \qquad
 \sum_xp_xa_{\theta_T}(x)=\mu_{Y,n}-\theta_T\mu_n=0.
\]
Also \(|g_{i,\vartheta}|\le2/\varepsilon\) and \(|a_\vartheta(x)|\le2\).  The estimated transported moment is the cross-average
\[
 \widehat M_{Y,n}-\vartheta\widehat M_{D,n}
 =\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n
 \frac{\widehat p_{X_i,n}}{q_{X_i,n}}g_{i,\vartheta}.
\]
Conditional on the target sample, its source-sample mean is \(\sum_x\widehat p_{x,n}a_\vartheta(x)\), and its conditional variance is at most
\[
 \frac{4}{\varepsilon^2n}
 \sum_{x=1}^{k_n}\frac{\widehat p_{x,n}^2}{q_{x,n}}.
\]
At \(\vartheta=\theta_T\), independence of the samples and the zero target mean give
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_P\left(\sum_x\widehat p_{x,n}a_{\theta_T}(x)\right)
 \le\frac4{N_n}.
\]
The multinomial second-moment identity yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_P\left[\sum_x\frac{\widehat p_{x,n}^2}{q_{x,n}}\right]
 &=\kappa_n+\frac1{N_n}\sum_x\frac{p_x-p_x^2}{q_{x,n}}\\
 &\le\kappa_n+\frac{k_n}{c_-N_n}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Because \(\kappa_n\ge1\), \(N_n/n\to c\), and \(k_n/N_n\to0\), the last display is at most \(2\kappa_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), uniformly over the class.  The law of total variance therefore gives
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\widehat M_{Y,n}-\theta_T\widehat M_{D,n})
 \le B_{\varepsilon,c}\frac{\kappa_n}{n}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  The same bound holds for \(\widehat M_{D,n}\), whose mean is \(\mu_n\).  The displayed value \(B_{\varepsilon,c}=8(\varepsilon^{-2}+c^{-1})\) follows from taking \(N_n\ge cn/2\).

We next control the random scale.  Its collision component is unbiased because
\[
 \mathbb E_P[\widehat\kappa_{U,n}]
 =\sum_x\frac{p_x^2}{q_{x,n}}=\kappa_n.
\]
The symmetric collision kernel is bounded by \(k_n/c_-\).  In the variance expansion of the order-two U-statistic, terms indexed by disjoint pairs are independent; there are order \(N_n^2\) individual variance terms and order \(N_n^3\) overlapping pair covariances.  Bounding each by \(k_n^2/c_-^2\) gives the explicit elementary inequality
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\widehat\kappa_{U,n})
 \le\frac{32k_n^2}{c_-^2N_n}.
\]
If \(\kappa_n\le2\), then \(\widehat K_n\ge1\ge\kappa_n/2\) deterministically.  If \(\kappa_n>2\), Chebyshev's inequality gives
\[
 P(\widehat K_n<\kappa_n/2)
 \le\frac{128k_n^2}{c_-^2N_n\kappa_n^2}
 \le\frac{128k_n^2}{c_-^2N_n}=o(1),
\]
uniformly, since \(k_n=o(n^{1/2})\) and \(N_n/n\to c\).

On \(\{\widehat K_n\ge\kappa_n/2\}\), exclusion of \(\theta_T\) implies that its centered moment exceeds \(L\sqrt{\kappa_n/(2n)}\).  Hence Chebyshev's inequality and the variance bound yield
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal N_n^{\mathrm{reg}}}P(\theta_T\notin C_n^{\mathrm{reg}})
 \le o(1)+\frac{2B_{\varepsilon,c}}{L^2}
 \le\alpha+o(1).
\]
This proves \((C_n^{\mathrm{reg}})\in\mathfrak C^{\mathrm{reg}}\).

For length, let \(r_n=L\sqrt{\widehat K_n/n}\).  Affine inversion gives length at most \(4r_n/\mu_n\) on \(\{|\widehat M_{D,n}-\mu_n|\le\mu_n/2\}\), and at most two otherwise.  Since
\[
 P(|\widehat M_{D,n}-\mu_n|>\mu_n/2)
 \le\frac{4B_{\varepsilon,c}}{t_n},
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P[\widehat K_n]=1+\kappa_n\le2\kappa_n,
\]
Jensen's inequality gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P[\lambda(C_n^{\mathrm{reg}})]
 \le\frac{4\sqrt2L}{\sqrt{t_n}}+
 \frac{8B_{\varepsilon,c}}{t_n}.
\]
Taking the supremum over \(t_n\ge t_0\) when \(t_0\ge1\), and using \(\lambda(C_n^{\mathrm{reg}})\le2\) when \(t_0<1\), proves the upper bound.

For the converse, take the regular geometry with \(q_{x,n}=1/k_n\), \(w=1\), and \(e=1/2\).  It belongs to \(\mathcal N_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\) because \(c_-\le1\le c_+\).  Every regular-cell-honest sequence is conditionally honest on this fixed geometry, while its regular-class risk dominates its slice risk.  The lower half of thm:fixed-geometry-frontier therefore gives
\[
 \inf_{(C_n)\in\mathfrak C^{\mathrm{reg}}}
 \limsup_n\sup_{P\in\mathcal N_n^{\mathrm{reg}}:\,t_n\ge t_0}
 \mathbb E_P[\lambda(C_n)]
 \ge\frac{3(1-\alpha)^2}{16}\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}.
\]
This completes the matched-order result.
