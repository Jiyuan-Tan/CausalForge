Fix \(t_0>0\).  We first construct a continuum, rather than merely two points, on the finite-cell skeleton in the frozen witness.  For all sufficiently large \(n\), continuity of
\[
 a\longmapsto \frac{n a^2(3/2-a)^2}{k_n\{(1-a)^2+a^2/(k_n-1)\}},\qquad 0<a<1/2,
\]
and its limits at the two endpoints give a solution \(a=a_n\) for which the display equals \(t_0\).  Moreover,
\[
 t_0\ge \frac{n a_n^2}{k_n},
\]
because \((3/2-a_n)^2\ge1\) and \((1-a_n)^2+a_n^2/(k_n-1)\le1\).  Hence \(a_n^2\le t_0k_n/n\to0\).

Let the source covariate law be uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,k_n\}\), and put
\[
 p_{T,n}(k_n)=1-a_n,\qquad p_{T,n}(j)=\frac{a_n}{k_n-1}\quad(j<k_n).
\]
The resulting density ratio is
\[
 w_n(k_n)=k_n(1-a_n),\qquad w_n(j)=\frac{k_na_n}{k_n-1}\quad(j<k_n),
\]
so that
\[
 \mathbb E_S[w_n]=1,\qquad
 \kappa_n=k_n\left\{(1-a_n)^2+\frac{a_n^2}{k_n-1}\right\}\le k_n,\qquad 0\le w_n\le2k_n.
\]
Set \(e=1/2\).  Conditional on a covariate value \(x\), introduce the compliance types \(C,A,N\), with receipt potentials \((D(0),D(1))=(0,1),(1,1),(0,0)\), respectively.  Give type \(C\) probability \(p_x\), where \(p_{k_n}=a_n\) and \(p_j=1/2\) for \(j<k_n\), and split the remaining probability equally between \(A\) and \(N\).  Use the same conditional full-data law in the source and target populations, and give both populations positive probability.  Let \(Y(0)=0\).  For every unit other than a complier in cell \(k_n\), let \(Y(1)\) be Bernoulli with success probability \(1/2\).  For a complier in cell \(k_n\), let \(Y(1)\) be Bernoulli with success probability \(1/2+h\).  Denote the resulting joint observed-data law, including the oracle inputs \((w,e)\), by \(Q_{n,h}\).

For
\[
 \rho=\frac{1-\alpha}{4\sqrt2},\qquad
 H(t_0)=\min\left\{\frac14,\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{t_0}}\right\},
\]
consider every \(h\in[-H(t_0),H(t_0)]\).  The construction has bounded outcomes, randomized encouragement, exclusion, monotonicity, conditional outcome-contrast transport, integrated first-stage transport, domination, and positive target complier probability.  Thus it satisfies all member conditions of \(\mathcal N_n\), and hence of \(\mathcal P_n\).  Its transported first stage is
\[
 \mu_n=(1-a_n)a_n+a_n\frac12=a_n(3/2-a_n)\longrightarrow0,
\]
and the calibration of \(a_n\) gives exactly
\[
 \frac{n\mu_n^2}{\kappa_n}=t_0.
\]
All these quantities, as well as \(w\) and \(e\), are common to the entire \(h\)-family.  The transported complier effect is
\[
 \theta_{n,h}=\frac{\mu_n/2+(1-a_n)a_n h}{\mu_n}
 =\frac12+q_nh,\qquad q_n=\frac{1-a_n}{3/2-a_n}.
\]
Since \(1/2\le q_n\le2/3\) and \(|h|\le1/4\), every \(\theta_{n,h}\) belongs to the forced range \(\Theta=[-1,1]\).

We next bound the distance between the experiments directly.  The observed source law can depend on \(h\) only on the event
\[
 E_n=\{X=k_n,Z=1,D=1\},\qquad Q_{n,0}(E_n)=\frac{1+a_n}{4k_n}.
\]
Conditional on \(E_n\), the complier fraction is \(2a_n/(1+a_n)\), so the conditional distribution of \(Y\) under \(Q_{n,h}\) is Bernoulli with success probability
\[
 \frac12+\frac{2a_nh}{1+a_n}.
\]
For a Bernoulli law with success probability \(1/2+x\) relative to Bernoulli \((1/2)\), direct summation gives chi-square divergence \(4x^2\).  Therefore the one-source-observation divergence is exactly
\[
 \chi^2(Q_{n,h}^{S}\Vert Q_{n,0}^{S})
 =\frac{4a_n^2h^2}{k_n(1+a_n)}.
\]
The target sample and the oracle inputs have the same law for every \(h\).  Factorization of the likelihood ratio for the \(n\) independent source observations gives
\[
 1+\chi^2(Q_{n,h}\Vert Q_{n,0})
 =\left(1+\frac{4a_n^2h^2}{k_n(1+a_n)}\right)^n
 \le \exp\left(\frac{4na_n^2h^2}{k_n}\right)
 \le \exp(4t_0h^2)
 \le \exp(4\rho^2).
\]
The penultimate inequality again uses
\[
 \frac{na_n^2}{k_n}
 =t_0\frac{(1-a_n)^2+a_n^2/(k_n-1)}{(3/2-a_n)^2}
 \le t_0.
\]
If \(L_{n,h}=dQ_{n,h}/dQ_{n,0}\), the definition of total variation and Cauchy--Schwarz yield
\[
 \lVert Q_{n,h}-Q_{n,0}\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}
 =\frac12\mathbb E_{Q_{n,0}}|L_{n,h}-1|
 \le\frac12\sqrt{\chi^2(Q_{n,h}\Vert Q_{n,0})}.
\]
Because \(4\rho^2\le1/8\) and \(e^x-1\le2x\) for \(0\le x\le1\), it follows that, uniformly over \(|h|\le H(t_0)\),
\[
 \lVert Q_{n,h}-Q_{n,0}\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}
 \le\frac12\sqrt{e^{4\rho^2}-1}
 \le\sqrt2\rho=\frac{1-\alpha}{4}.
\]

Now take an arbitrary \((C_n)\in\mathfrak C^{\mathrm{or}}\).  Oracle honesty supplies a deterministic \(\gamma_n\downarrow0\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n}P(\theta_T\in C_n)\ge1-\alpha-\gamma_n.
\]
Let
\[
 I_n=\left[\frac12-q_nH(t_0),\frac12+q_nH(t_0)\right].
\]
For each \(u\in I_n\), set \(h(u)=(u-1/2)/q_n\).  Tonelli's identity and the defining inequality for total variation give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_{Q_{n,0}}[\lambda(C_n)]
 &=\int_{\Theta}Q_{n,0}(u\in C_n)\,du\\
 &\ge\int_{I_n}\left\{Q_{n,h(u)}(u\in C_n)-
 \lVert Q_{n,h(u)}-Q_{n,0}\rVert_{\mathrm{TV}}\right\}\,du\\
 &\ge 2q_nH(t_0)\left\{\frac{3(1-\alpha)}4-\gamma_n\right\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Since \(2q_n\ge1\),
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\mathbb E_{Q_{n,0}}[\lambda(C_n)]
 \ge\frac{3(1-\alpha)}4H(t_0).
\]
Our choice \(\rho<1/4\) implies
\[
 H(t_0)\ge \rho\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}.
\]
The law \(Q_{n,0}\) is in \(\mathcal N_n\), has \(t_n=t_0\), and was constructed for an arbitrary oracle-honest sequence.  Taking the relevant supremum, then the limsup, and finally the infimum over such sequences proves both claimed lower bounds with
\[
 c_0=\frac{3(1-\alpha)\rho}{4}
 =\frac{3(1-\alpha)^2}{16\sqrt2}.
\]
These constants never use a particular factorization of \(t_0=n\mu_n^2/\kappa_n\).  Finally, take \(t_c=1\).  Then \(\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}=1\) for \(0<t_0\le t_c\), proving the last assertion.
