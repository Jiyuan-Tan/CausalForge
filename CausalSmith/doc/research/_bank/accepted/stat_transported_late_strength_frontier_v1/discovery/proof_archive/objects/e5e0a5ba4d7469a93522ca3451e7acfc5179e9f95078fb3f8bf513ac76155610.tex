Apply thm:regular-cell-unknown-weight-attainment with \(q_{x,n}=1/k_n\), \(e(x)=1/2\), and
\[
 L=L_{\alpha,c}=\left\{\frac{2B_c}{\alpha}\right\}^{1/2},
 \qquad B_c=32(1+c^{-1}).
\]
Then the regular-cell score is \(H_i=2(2Z_i-1)\), and its source cell estimator becomes
\[
 \frac{1}{nq_{x,n}}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf 1\{X_i=x\}H_iG_i
 =\frac{k_n}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf 1\{X_i=x\}H_iG_i.
\]
Likewise, the regular-cell collision estimator becomes
\[
 \frac{1}{N_n(N_n-1)}\sum_{j\ne\ell}
 \frac{\mathbf 1\{X_j^T=X_\ell^T\}}{q_{X_j^T,n}}
 =\frac{k_n}{N_n(N_n-1)}\sum_{j\ne\ell}
 \mathbf 1\{X_j^T=X_\ell^T\}.
\]
The variance constant in the regular-cell theorem is
\[
 B_{\varepsilon,c}=8(\varepsilon^{-2}+c^{-1})
 =32+8c^{-1}\le32+32c^{-1}=B_c
\]
when \(\varepsilon=1/2\).  Therefore \(L_{\alpha,c}\ge\{2B_{\varepsilon,c}/\alpha\}^{1/2}\), so the exact confidence set displayed in the present theorem is honest.  Its regular-cell length bound is at most
\[
 \max\{2,4\sqrt2L_{\alpha,c}+8B_{\varepsilon,c}\}
 \min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}
 \le C_0\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}.
\]
Because the specialized construction depends only on the two observed samples, it belongs to \(\mathfrak C^{\mathrm{cell}}\).  Finally, the uniform geometry \(q_{x,n}=1/k_n\), \(w=1\), and \(e=1/2\) used in the lower-bound part of thm:fixed-geometry-frontier is a submodel of \(\mathcal N_n\), and its observable target law is fixed over the continuum of alternatives.  Thus the Tonelli change-of-measure lower bound applies to every member of \(\mathfrak C^{\mathrm{cell}}\), giving \(c_0\min\{1,t_0^{-1/2}\}\).  This proves every assertion, including the absence of an additional minimax-order cost from estimating the target cell probabilities.
