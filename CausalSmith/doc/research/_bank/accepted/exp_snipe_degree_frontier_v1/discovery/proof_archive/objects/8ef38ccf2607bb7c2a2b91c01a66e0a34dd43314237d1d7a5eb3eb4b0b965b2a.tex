Fix \(B>0\) and \(1\le d\le n\). First consider the graph. Every active vertex belongs to exactly one complete directed block \(V_b\) of size \(d\). Because all within-block arrows, including self-loops, are present, each active vertex has in-degree and out-degree exactly \(d\). Every inactive vertex is isolated and therefore has both degrees zero. Thus \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\) satisfies \({\rm ass:bounded-degree}\), and hence \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\).

Now fix \(\sigma\in\{-1,1\}\) and a schedule in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\). For every block draw in that support, \(|U_b|\le s=B/2\). If \(i\in V_b\) is active, \({\rm def:block-family}\) gives
\[
c_{i,T}=U_b\mathbf 1\{T=\varnothing\}+\sigma\delta h_{d,T}\quad\text{for }|T|\le k_\star,
\]
and all its remaining coefficients are zero. Since \(k_\star\le\bar\beta_d\le\beta\), this schedule satisfies \({\rm ass:low-order}\). The same condition is immediate for inactive units, whose coefficients all vanish.

By \({\rm lem:block-energy-representer}\), \(H_{\beta,p}=\sup_{a\ge1}\sum_T|h_{a,T}|\) is finite. It is also positive because \(h_d(\mathbf 1_d)-h_d(\mathbf 0_d)=1\). The definition of \(\delta\) therefore implies
\[
\delta\le \frac{B}{2H_{\beta,p}}.
\]
Consequently, for each active \(i\in V_b\), the triangle inequality yields
\[
\sum_{T\subseteq N_i}|c_{i,T}|
\le |U_b|+\delta\sum_{|T|\le k_\star}|h_{d,T}|
\le \frac B2+\delta H_{\beta,p}
\le B.
\]
For an inactive unit the same sum is zero. Hence every prior-supported schedule satisfies \({\rm ass:bounded-coefficient-mass}\) as well as \({\rm ass:low-order}\), so it belongs to \(\mathcal C_{n,d,\beta}(B)\). Combining this fact with \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\) and applying \({\rm def:model-class}\) proves that each graph--schedule pair in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)\).
