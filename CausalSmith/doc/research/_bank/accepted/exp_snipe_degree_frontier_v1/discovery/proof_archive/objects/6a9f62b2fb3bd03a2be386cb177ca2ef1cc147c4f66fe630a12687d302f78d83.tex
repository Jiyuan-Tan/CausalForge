Fix an index \(t\), and abbreviate \(n=n_t\), \(d=d_t\), \(m=m_t=n/d\), \(A=A_d\), \(g=g_d\), and \(\mathcal P=\mathcal P_d\). For \(S\subseteq V_b\), write \(Z_S=\prod_{j\in S}Z_j\). By \({\rm def:local-linear-class}\), each admissible weight \(w_i:=w_{i,t}\) satisfies
\[
\mathbb E_Z[w_i(Z_b)f(Z_b)]=L_d(f),\qquad f\in\mathcal P.
\]
By \({\rm lem:block-energy-representer}\), the same equality holds with \(g\) in place of \(w_i\), and \(\|g\|_2^2=A\). Consequently
\[
w_i=g+u_i,\qquad u_i\perp\mathcal P.
\tag{1}
\]
All norms below are in \(L^2(P_Z)\).

We first construct an attaining estimator. Set \(w_i=g\) for every unit, and denote the resulting estimator by \(\widehat\tau_g\). For \(S\subseteq V_b\) with \(|S|\le\bar\beta_d\), define
\[
X_S:=g(Z_b)Z_S-\mathbf1(S\ne\varnothing).
\]
If \(S=\varnothing\), then \(\|X_S\|_2^2=A\). If \(S\ne\varnothing\), the representer identity, \(Z_S^2=Z_S\), and \(0\le Z_S\le1\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|X_S\|_2^2
&=\mathbb E_Z[g(Z_b)^2Z_S]-2\mathbb E_Z[g(Z_b)Z_S]+1\\
&=\mathbb E_Z[g(Z_b)^2Z_S]-1
\le \mathbb E_Z[g(Z_b)^2]=A.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
For an admissible unit-level polynomial \(f_i=\sum_Sc_{i,S}Z_S\), its centered estimation error therefore obeys, by the triangle inequality and the coefficient-mass condition in \({\rm def:local-linear-class}\),
\[
\|g f_i-L_d(f_i)\|_2
\le\sum_S|c_{i,S}|\,\|X_S\|_2
\le B\sqrt A.
\tag{3}
\]
Under \({\rm ass:bernoulli-design}\), assignments of disjoint blocks are independent. Each block error is centered by the moment restrictions in \({\rm def:local-linear-class}\), so block variances add. Applying the triangle inequality within each block to (3) yields
\[
\sup_{c\in\mathcal M_t(G_t)}
\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_g-\tau_n)^2]
\le \frac1{n^2}\sum_{b=1}^m(dB\sqrt A)^2
=\frac{B^2A}{m}.
\tag{4}
\]

For the matching converse, take the admissible constant schedule \(f_i\equiv B\) for every unit. Its coefficient mass is \(B\), and \(L_d(f_i)=0\). For arbitrary admissible weights, (1), \(g\in\mathcal P\), and \(u_i\perp\mathcal P\) imply in each block
\[
\left\|\sum_{i\in V_b}w_i\right\|_2^2
=\left\|dg+\sum_{i\in V_b}u_i\right\|_2^2
=d^2A+\left\|\sum_{i\in V_b}u_i\right\|_2^2
\ge d^2A.
\tag{5}
\]
Independence across blocks then gives
\[
\sup_{c\in\mathcal M_t(G_t)}
\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_w-\tau_n)^2]
\ge\frac{B^2}{n^2}\sum_{b=1}^m d^2A
=\frac{B^2A}{m}.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (4) and (6), and restoring subscripts, proves for every \(t\)
\[
R_t^{\mathrm{loc,lin}}=\frac{B^2A_{d_t}}{m_t}
=\frac{B^2d_tA_{d_t}}{n_t},
\]
and hence the displayed finite ratio is exactly one.

We next establish the exact finite extremal formula. For a fixed block \(V_b\), define its unscaled centered error by
\[
E_b(c,w):=\sum_{i\in V_b}\{w_i(Z_b)f_i(Z_b)-L_d(f_i)\}.
\]
As a function of the block coefficient vectors \((c_{i,S})_{i\in V_b}\), the map \(\|E_b(c,w)\|_2^2\) is convex. Each unit's feasible coefficient vector lies in a finite-dimensional \(\ell_1\)-ball of radius \(B\). That ball is the convex hull of the vectors \(\varepsilon_iB e_{S_i}\), where \(\varepsilon_i\in\{-1,1\}\) and \(|S_i|\le\bar\beta_d\). The product of the unit-level balls is the convex hull of the corresponding product points. Convexity therefore shows that the maximum is attained at one of those product points, where
\[
E_b(c,w)=B\sum_{i\in V_b}\varepsilon_i
\{w_i(Z_b)Z_{S_i}-\mathbf1(S_i\ne\varnothing)\}.
\tag{7}
\]
The block errors are centered and independent under \({\rm ass:bernoulli-design}\), and the coefficient vectors can be chosen independently across blocks. Thus the supremum of the sum of block variances equals the sum of their suprema. Equation (7) gives exactly
\[
\sup_{c\in\mathcal M_t(G_t)}
\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_{w,t}-\tau_{n_t})^2]
=\frac{B^2}{n_t^2}\sum_{b=1}^{m_t}\Psi_{b,t}(w).
\tag{8}
\]
In the maximization defining \(\Psi_{b,t}\), choose \(S_i=\varnothing\) and \(\varepsilon_i=1\) for every \(i\in V_b\). Equation (5) then yields
\[
\Psi_{b,t}(w)\ge d_t^2A_{d_t}.
\tag{9}
\]
This proves both finite assertions.

Call a sequence asymptotically minimax when its worst-case risk divided by \(R_t^{\mathrm{loc,lin}}\) tends to one. Equations (8), (9), and the exact minimax identity imply, without any condition that \(d_t\) diverge,
\[
\frac{\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_{w,t}-\tau_{n_t})^2]}
{R_t^{\mathrm{loc,lin}}}
=1+\frac{1}{m_td_t^2A_{d_t}}
\sum_{b=1}^{m_t}\{\Psi_{b,t}(w)-d_t^2A_{d_t}\}.
\tag{10}
\]
Every summand in the final numerator is nonnegative by (9). Hence the risk ratio tends to one if and only if the normalized excess in the theorem tends to zero. This proves the asserted characterization for every admissible sequence.

We now prove the stated sufficient condition, still without assuming \(d_t\to\infty\). Suppress \(t\) again and put
\[
a_b^2:=\sum_{i\in V_b}\|u_i\|_2^2,
\qquad U:=\sum_{b=1}^m a_b^2.
\]
Every admissible polynomial satisfies \(|f_i(Z_b)|\le B\). Therefore
\[
\left\|\sum_{i\in V_b}u_i f_i\right\|_2
\le B\sum_{i\in V_b}\|u_i\|_2
\le B\sqrt d\,a_b.
\tag{11}
\]
By (3), the part of the block error involving \(g\) has norm at most \(dB\sqrt A\). Combining this fact with (11), summing squared block bounds, and using \(\sum_ba_b\le\sqrt{mU}\), we obtain
\[
1\le
\frac{\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_w-\tau_n)^2]}{B^2A/m}
\le1+2\sqrt{\frac{U}{mdA}}+\frac{U}{mdA}
=\left(1+\sqrt{\frac{U}{nA}}\right)^2.
\tag{12}
\]
By (1),
\[
U=\sum_{i\in V_n}\mathbb E_Z[(w_i(Z_{b(i)})-g(Z_{b(i)}))^2].
\]
Thus the normalized \(L^2\) condition in the theorem implies that the right side of (12) tends to one. The exact lower bound in (12) then proves asymptotic minimaxity for every admissible sequence.

It remains to prove non-necessity under the additional hypothesis \(d_t\to\infty\). Since \(\beta\) is fixed, for every sufficiently large \(t\),
\[
\dim(\mathcal P_{d_t})=\sum_{r=0}^{\beta}\binom{d_t}{r}<2^{d_t}=\dim L^2(P_Z).
\tag{13}
\]
The last equality follows because \(p\in(0,1)\) gives full support on the block assignment space. Hence \(\mathcal P_{d_t}^{\perp}\) contains a nonzero function. Moreover, \(A_{d_t}>0\) because \(\Delta_1(p)=1\). Choose \(v_{d_t}\in\mathcal P_{d_t}^{\perp}\) such that
\[
\|v_{d_t}\|_2^2=d_tA_{d_t}.
\tag{14}
\]
For every block, set
\[
u_1=v_{d_t},\qquad u_2=-v_{d_t},\qquad u_i=0\quad(i\ge3),
\qquad w_i=g_{d_t}+u_i.
\tag{15}
\]
Because \(d_t\to\infty\), there are at least two units per block for all sufficiently large \(t\). Orthogonality in (14) verifies every moment restriction in \({\rm def:local-linear-class}\). For arbitrary admissible unit polynomials, the additional block error contributed by (15) is \(v_{d_t}(f_1-f_2)\), whose norm is at most \(2B\sqrt{d_tA_{d_t}}\). The canonical \(g_{d_t}\)-part has norm at most \(d_tB\sqrt{A_{d_t}}\) by (3). Consequently
\[
1\le
\frac{\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_w-\tau_{n_t})^2]}
{B^2A_{d_t}/m_t}
\le\left(1+\frac{2}{\sqrt{d_t}}\right)^2\longrightarrow1.
\tag{16}
\]
These weights are asymptotically minimax. On the other hand, (14) and (15) give for every sufficiently large \(t\)
\[
\frac{1}{n_tA_{d_t}}\sum_{i\in V_{n_t}}
\|w_i-g_{d_t}\|_2^2
=\frac{m_t(2d_tA_{d_t})}{m_td_tA_{d_t}}=2.
\tag{17}
\]
A relabeling of coordinates within a block preserves the product Bernoulli law, the symmetric function \(g_{d_t}\), and the sum of squared distances in (17). It therefore cannot make the sufficient condition hold. This proves the non-necessity and the distance-exactly-two witness under precisely the stated divergence hypothesis.
