By \({\rm def:two-class-minimax-risks}\), \(R_{n,\ell_1}^\star(d,\beta,p,B)=R_n^\star(d,\beta,p,B)\). The simultaneous \(A_d\)-scale sandwich in \({\rm thm:bounded-outcome-degree-frontier}\), restricted to the coefficient-mass class, gives
\[
c_{\beta,p}B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{dA_d}{n}\right\}
\le R_n^\star(d,\beta,p,B)
\le \sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)}
\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{up}}-\tau_n)^2]
\le C_{\beta,p}B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{dA_d}{n}\right\}.
\]
The same theorem records \(dA_d\asymp_{\beta,p}d\binom d{k_\star}\); absorbing the two comparison constants into \(c_{\beta,p}\) and \(C_{\beta,p}\) yields the displayed binomial form in the present statement. Its unclipped bound
\[
\sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)}
\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{SNIPE}}-\tau_n)^2]
\le B^2\frac{dA_d}{n}
\]
and the preceding lower bound show rate attainment by unclipped SNIPE whenever \(d\binom d{k_\star}/n\lesssim_{\beta,p}1\). Finally, \({\rm thm:bounded-outcome-degree-frontier}\) gives the asserted formula for \(A_i\), the single-out-degree covariance charge, the exact block-mixture separation \(2\rho\delta\), the squared Hellinger bound \(4\pi^2m\delta^2/(B^2A_d)\), and the identities \(1/2\le\rho\le1\) and \(A_d/m=(dA_d/n)/\rho\). These are precisely the remaining clauses of the theorem.
