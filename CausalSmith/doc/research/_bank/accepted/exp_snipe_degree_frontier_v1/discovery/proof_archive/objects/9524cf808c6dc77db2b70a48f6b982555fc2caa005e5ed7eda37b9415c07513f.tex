Fix \(t\) and suppress its subscript, writing \(n=md\), \(A=A_d\), \(g=g_d\), and \(\mathcal P=\mathcal P_d\). For every admissible weight \(w_i\), the moment restrictions in \({\rm def:local-linear-class}\) imply
\[
\mathbb E_Z[w_i(Z)f(Z)]=L_d(f),\qquad f\in\mathcal P.
\]
The representer identity in \({\rm lem:block-energy-representer}\) gives the same equality with \(g\) in place of \(w_i\). Consequently
\[
w_i=g+u_i,\qquad u_i\perp\mathcal P,
\]
and \(\|g\|_2^2=A\).

First take \(w_i=g\) for every unit. For \(S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,d\}\), \(|S|\le\bar\beta_d\), set
\[
X_S:=g(Z)Z_S-\mathbf1(S\ne\varnothing).
\]
If \(S=\varnothing\), then \(\|X_S\|_2^2=A\). If \(S\ne\varnothing\), the representer identity and \(Z_S^2=Z_S\) give
\[
\|X_S\|_2^2=\mathbb E_Z[g(Z)^2Z_S]-1\le\mathbb E_Z[g(Z)^2]=A.
\]
Every admissible unit-level polynomial is \(f_i=\sum_Sc_{i,S}Z_S\), with \(\sum_S|c_{i,S}|\le B\). Hence
\[
\|gf_i-L_d(f_i)\|_2
\le\sum_S|c_{i,S}|\|X_S\|_2\le B\sqrt A.
\]
Errors from distinct blocks are independent and centered. The triangle inequality within each block therefore gives
\[
\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_g-\tau_n)^2]
\le\frac{1}{n^2}\sum_{b=1}^{m}(dB\sqrt A)^2
=\frac{B^2A}{m}.
\]
For the reverse inequality, use the admissible fixed schedule \(f_i\equiv B\) for every unit. Its total-treatment effect is zero. In block \(b\), orthogonality gives
\[
\left\|\sum_{i\in V_b}w_i\right\|_2^2
=\left\|dg+\sum_{i\in V_b}u_i\right\|_2^2
=d^2A+\left\|\sum_{i\in V_b}u_i\right\|_2^2
\ge d^2A.
\]
Independence across blocks yields, for every admissible estimator,
\[
\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_w-\tau_n)^2]
\ge\frac{B^2}{n^2}\sum_{b=1}^{m}d^2A
=\frac{B^2A}{m}.
\]
This proves the exact minimax identity.

For the finite characterization, fix a block. Its squared centered-error norm is a convex quadratic function of the raw coefficient vectors \((c_{i,S})_S\). Each feasible vector lies in a finite-dimensional \(\ell_1\)-ball of radius \(B\), whose extreme points are \(\varepsilon_iBe_{S_i}\), where \(\varepsilon_i\in\{-1,1\}\). A convex function on the product of these polytopes is bounded above by its largest value at a product of extreme points. Blocks are independent, their variances add, and their coefficient vectors may be chosen separately. Therefore
\[
\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_w-\tau_n)^2]
=\frac{B^2}{n^2}\sum_{b=1}^{m}\Psi_b(w).
\]
Choosing \(S_i=\varnothing\) and \(\varepsilon_i=1\) for all \(i\in V_b\) gives \(\Psi_b(w)\ge d^2A\) by the orthogonal decomposition above. Dividing the exact risk formula by \(B^2A/m\) proves the necessary-and-sufficient asymptotic characterization.

Now let \(a_b^2:=\sum_{i\in V_b}\|u_i\|_2^2\) and \(U:=\sum_ba_b^2\). Since \(|f_i(Z)|\le B\),
\[
\left\|\sum_{i\in V_b}u_if_i\right\|_2
\le B\sum_{i\in V_b}\|u_i\|_2
\le B\sqrt d\,a_b.
\]
Combining this with \(\|\sum_i(gf_i-L_d(f_i))\|_2\le dB\sqrt A\), summing over blocks, and applying Cauchy--Schwarz to \(\sum_ba_b\), gives
\[
1\le
\frac{\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_w-\tau_n)^2]}{B^2A/m}
\le1+2\sqrt{\frac{U}{mdA}}+\frac{U}{mdA}
=\left(1+\sqrt{\frac{U}{nA}}\right)^2.
\]
Thus the proposed average \(L^2\) convergence is sufficient.

It is not necessary. For all sufficiently large \(d\),
\[
\dim(\mathcal P_d)=\sum_{r=0}^{\beta}\binom dr<2^d=\dim L^2(P_Z),
\]
because \(\beta\) is fixed and \(p\in(0,1)\) gives full support. Choose \(v_d\in\mathcal P_d^\perp\) with \(\|v_d\|_2^2=dA_d\). In every block take
\[
u_1=v_d,\qquad u_2=-v_d,\qquad u_i=0\quad(i\ge3),
\]
and set \(w_i=g_d+u_i\). These weights obey all unbiasedness restrictions. For arbitrary admissible \(f_i\), the residual block error is \(v_d(f_1-f_2)\), whose norm is at most \(2B\sqrt{dA_d}\). Hence
\[
1\le
\frac{\sup_c\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_w-\tau_n)^2]}{B^2A_d/m}
\le\left(1+\frac{2}{\sqrt d}\right)^2\longrightarrow1.
\]
The sequence is asymptotically minimax, whereas
\[
\frac{1}{nA_d}\sum_i\|w_i-g_d\|_2^2
=\frac{m(2dA_d)}{mdA_d}=2.
\]
Within-block relabeling only moves the two nonzero residuals and cannot change this norm. This proves failure of necessity and completes the characterization.
