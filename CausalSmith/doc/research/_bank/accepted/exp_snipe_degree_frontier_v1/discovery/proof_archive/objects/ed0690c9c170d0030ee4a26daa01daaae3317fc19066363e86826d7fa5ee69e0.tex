Use the corrected construction in \({\rm def:block-family}\), and abbreviate its fixed tilt factor by
\[
\kappa_{\beta,p}:=\min\{(2H_{\beta,p})^{-1},(4\pi)^{-1}\}.
\]
Then \(\delta=\kappa_{\beta,p}B\min\{1,\sqrt{A_d/m}\}\) and \(\kappa_{\beta,p}H_{\beta,p}\le1/2\). Every active vertex of \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\) has exactly \(d\) incoming and \(d\) outgoing arrows, including its self-loop, while every inactive vertex has both degrees zero. Hence \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\).

By the definition of \(k_\star\), \(\Delta_r(p)=0\) for \(k_\star<r\le\bar\beta_d\). Expanding a centered monomial \(\prod_{j\in S}(z_j-p)\) creates only raw monomials indexed by subsets of \(S\). It follows that \(g_d\), and therefore \(h_d=g_d/A_d\), has raw degree at most \(k_\star\le\beta\). Thus every schedule in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\) satisfies \({\rm ass:low-order}\).

For an active unit \(i\in V_b\), the triangle inequality, \(|U_b|\le s=B/2\), and \({\rm lem:block-energy-representer}\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{T\subseteq V_b}|c_{i,T}|
&\le |U_b|+\delta\sum_{T\subseteq V_b}|h_{d,T}|\\
&\le \frac B2+\kappa_{\beta,p}BH_{\beta,p}\le B.
\end{aligned}
\]
All coefficients of inactive units vanish. Hence every prior-realized schedule satisfies \({\rm ass:bounded-coefficient-mass}\), and so it belongs to \(\mathcal C_{n,d,\beta}(B)\). Combining graph and schedule membership in \({\rm def:model-class}\) proves \(\operatorname{supp}(\Pi_\sigma)\subseteq\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)\).
