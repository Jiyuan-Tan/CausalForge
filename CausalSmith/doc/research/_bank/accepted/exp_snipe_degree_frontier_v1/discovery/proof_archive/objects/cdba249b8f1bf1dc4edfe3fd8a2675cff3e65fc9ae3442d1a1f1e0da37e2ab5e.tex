Fix \(B>0\), \(1\le d\le n\), and \(\sigma\in\{-1,1\}\). Since \(m=\lfloor n/d\rfloor\ge1\), the construction in \({\rm def:block-family}\) partitions the \(q=md\) active units into disjoint blocks of size \(d\). Every active unit has exactly the \(d\) units of its block, including itself, as both its in-neighbors and its out-neighbors. Every inactive unit is isolated and hence has in-degree and out-degree zero. Thus the maximum in-degree and the maximum out-degree are both at most \(d\), as required by \({\rm ass:bounded-degree}\). By \({\rm def:graph-class}\), \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\).

Now fix any schedule \(c\) in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\). The support of \(f_s\) is \([-s,s]\), where \(s=B/2\), so every realized block baseline satisfies \(|U_b|\le B/2\). For an inactive unit, all coefficients vanish, and hence both the low-order and coefficient-mass restrictions hold. For an active unit \(i\in V_b\), every possibly nonzero coefficient is indexed by a set \(T\subseteq V_b=N_i\) with \(|T|\le k_\star\). Because \(k_\star\le\bar\beta_d=\min\{\beta,d\}\le\beta\), the schedule satisfies \({\rm ass:low-order}\).

It remains to verify the coefficient-mass bound. The conclusion \(H_{\beta,p}<\infty\) of \({\rm lem:block-energy-representer}\), together with the definition of \(\delta\), gives
\[
\delta H_{\beta,p}
=BH_{\beta,p}\min\!\left\{\frac{1}{2H_{\beta,p}},\frac{1}{4\pi}\right\}
\min\!\left\{1,\sqrt{\frac{A_d}{m}}\right\}
\le \frac B2.
\]
Using the triangle inequality, the raw-monomial expansion of \(h_d\), and \(|\sigma|=1\), we therefore obtain for every active \(i\in V_b\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{S\subseteq N_i}|c_{i,S}|
&=\left|U_b+\sigma\delta h_{d,\varnothing}\right|
  +\delta\sum_{\substack{T\subseteq V_b\\1\le |T|\le k_\star}}|h_{d,T}|\\
&\le |U_b|+\delta\sum_{T\subseteq V_b}|h_{d,T}|\\
&\le \frac B2+\delta H_{\beta,p}\le B.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \({\rm ass:bounded-coefficient-mass}\) holds for every unit. By \({\rm def:coefficient-class}\), \(c\in\mathcal C_{n,d,\beta}(B)\). Combining this with \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\) and applying \({\rm def:model-class}\) yields \((G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}},c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)\). Since \(c\) was arbitrary in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\), the asserted support inclusion follows.
