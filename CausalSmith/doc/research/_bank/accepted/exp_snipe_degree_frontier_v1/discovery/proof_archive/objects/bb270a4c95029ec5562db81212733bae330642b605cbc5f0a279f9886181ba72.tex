By \({\rm def:two-class-minimax-risks}\),
\[
R_{n,\ell_1}^{\star}(d,\beta,p,B)=R_n^\star(d,\beta,p,B).
\]
Apply \({\rm thm:bounded-outcome-degree-frontier}\) and retain the coefficient-mass part of its simultaneous two-class conclusion. There exist constants \(0<c_0\le C_0<\infty\), depending only on \(\beta\) and \(p\), such that
\[
c_0B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{dA_d}{n}\right\}
\le R_n^\star(d,\beta,p,B)
\le \sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)}
\mathbb E_Z\!\left[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{up}}-\tau_n)^2\right]
\le C_0B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{dA_d}{n}\right\}.
\]
The same theorem establishes, uniformly for \(0\le d\le n\),
\[
dA_d\asymp_{\beta,p}d\binom d{k_\star}.
\]
Thus there are constants \(0<a\le b<\infty\), depending only on \(\beta\) and \(p\), for which
\[
a\,d\binom d{k_\star}\le dA_d\le b\,d\binom d{k_\star}.
\]
This comparison also covers \(d=0\), because both sides then vanish. For every \(x\ge0\),
\[
\min\{1,ax\}\ge \min\{1,a\}\min\{1,x\},
\qquad
\min\{1,bx\}\le \max\{1,b\}\min\{1,x\}.
\]
Substituting \(x=d\binom d{k_\star}/n\) into the preceding risk sandwich and absorbing \(a\), \(b\), \(c_0\), and \(C_0\) into constants \(c_{\beta,p}\) and \(C_{\beta,p}\) proves the displayed binomial-scale inequalities.

For the unclipped estimator, \({\rm thm:bounded-outcome-degree-frontier}\) gives the design-unbiasedness identity and the uniform bound
\[
\sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)}
\mathbb E_Z\!\left[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{SNIPE}}-\tau_n)^2\right]
\le B^2\frac{dA_d}{n}
\le bB^2\frac{d\binom d{k_\star}}{n}.
\]
If \(d\binom d{k_\star}/n\lesssim_{\beta,p}1\), then this last quantity is, up to a constant depending only on \(\beta\) and \(p\), bounded by the minimax lower branch already proved. Hence unclipped SNIPE attains the same rate in the nonsaturated regime.

Finally, the proof and conclusion of \({\rm thm:bounded-outcome-degree-frontier}\) give the exact local energy
\[
A_i=\sum_{r=1}^{\min\{\beta,|N_i|\}}\binom{|N_i|}{r}
\frac{\Delta_r(p)^2}{[p(1-p)]^r}\le A_d
\]
and bound covariance by charging the out-degree constraint only once. For \(B>0\) and \(d\ge1\), that theorem applies the block family in \({\rm def:block-family}\) and establishes TTE separation \(2\rho\delta\) together with
\[
H^2(P_+,P_-)\le \frac{4\pi^2m\delta^2}{B^2A_d}.
\]
It also proves \(1/2\le\rho\le1\) and
\[
\frac{A_d}{m}=\frac{dA_d/n}{\rho}.
\]
These identities convert the two-prior testing bound to the lower branch above without requiring \(d\mid n\), and they prove every remaining assertion.
