Fix \(B>0\) and \(1\le d\le n\). In the graph \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\), every active unit lies in exactly one complete directed block \(V_b\) of size \(d\). Because all within-block arrows, including self-loops, are present and there are no arrows between blocks, every active unit has in-degree and out-degree exactly \(d\). Every inactive unit is isolated and therefore has both degrees zero. Thus the two degree bounds in \({\rm ass:bounded-degree}\) hold, and \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\) by \({\rm def:model-class}\) and the graph-class definition it incorporates.

Now fix \(\sigma\in\{-1,1\}\) and a schedule in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\). For each active block \(V_b\), its realized baseline satisfies \(|U_b|\le s=B/2\). By \({\rm lem:block-energy-representer}\), \(H_{\beta,p}=\sup_{d'\ge1}\sum_T|h_{d',T}|<\infty\). The choice of \(\delta\) in \({\rm def:block-family}\) therefore gives
\[
0<\delta\le \frac{B}{2H_{\beta,p}}.
\]
For an active unit \(i\in V_b\), the triangle inequality and the coefficient formula in \({\rm def:block-family}\) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{S\subseteq N_i}|c_{i,S}|
&=\left|U_b+\sigma\delta h_{d,\varnothing}\right|
  +\delta\sum_{\substack{T\subseteq V_b\\1\le |T|\le k_\star}}|h_{d,T}|\\
&\le |U_b|+\delta\sum_{T\subseteq V_b}|h_{d,T}|\\
&\le \frac B2+\delta H_{\beta,p}\le B.
\end{aligned}
\]
For an inactive unit all coefficients vanish, so the same bound holds trivially. Hence \({\rm ass:bounded-coefficient-mass}\) holds for every unit.

The only possibly nonzero nonconstant coefficients in an active block have order at most \(k_\star\). By the exposed-order construction used in \({\rm def:block-family}\), \(k_\star\le\bar\beta_d=\min\{\beta,d\}\le\beta\). The constant coefficient has order zero, and all coefficients of inactive units vanish. Consequently \(c_{i,S}=0\) whenever \(|S|>\beta\), which is \({\rm ass:low-order}\). The coefficient-class definition now implies that every schedule in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\) belongs to \(\mathcal C_{n,d,\beta}(B)\). Combining this fact with \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\) and \({\rm def:model-class}\) gives
\[
\operatorname{supp}(\Pi_\sigma)\subseteq\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B),
\]
as claimed.
