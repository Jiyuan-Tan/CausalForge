By the definition of the Bernoulli contrast, for every integer \(r\ge1\),
\[
\Delta_r(1/2)=(1-1/2)^r-(-1/2)^r
=2^{-r}\{1-(-1)^r\}
=\begin{cases}
2^{1-r},&r\text{ odd},\\
0,&r\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
\]
For odd \(r\), this identity and \(p(1-p)=1/4\) imply
\[
\frac{\Delta_r(1/2)^2}{[p(1-p)]^r}
=\frac{2^{2-2r}}{4^{-r}}=4,
\]
whereas for even \(r\) the same summand is zero. Substitution into \({\rm def:block-score-energy}\) therefore gives
\[
A_d
=4\sum_{\substack{1\le r\le\min\{\beta,d\}\\r\text{ odd}}}\binom dr.
\]
Because \(d\ge1\) and \(\beta\ge1\), the admissible orders include \(r=1\). Hence the nonzero contrasts are exactly the odd admissible orders, and \({\rm def:exposed-order}\) shows that \(k_\star\) is the largest odd integer not exceeding \(\min\{\beta,d\}\).

Now set \(\beta=1\). Only the term \(r=1\) occurs in the preceding sum, so
\[
A_d=4\binom d1=4d.
\]
By \({\rm def:two-class-minimax-risks}\),
\[
R_{n,\ell_1}^{\star}(d,1,1/2,B)=R_n^\star(d,1,1/2,B).
\]
Applying the simultaneous two-class sandwich in \({\rm thm:bounded-outcome-degree-frontier}\) and substituting \(A_d=4d\) yields
\[
R_n^\star(d,1,1/2,B)
=R_{n,\ell_1}^{\star}(d,1,1/2,B)
\asymp R_{n,\infty}^{\star}(d,1,1/2,B)
\asymp B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{4d^2}{n}\right\}.
\]
For every \(x\ge0\),
\[
\min\{1,x\}\le\min\{1,4x\}\le4\min\{1,x\}.
\]
Taking \(x=d^2/n\) absorbs the factor four into the two-sided constants and proves the displayed minimax frontier, including the boundary case \(B=0\).

Finally, suppose \(d\mid n\). The exact complete-block clause of \({\rm thm:bounded-outcome-degree-frontier}\) states that the worst-case mean squared error of canonical unclipped SNIPE over each of the coefficient-mass and uniformly bounded-outcome model classes equals \(B^2dA_d/n\). Since \(A_d=4d\), this common exact value is
\[
\frac{B^2dA_d}{n}=\frac{4B^2d^2}{n},
\]
as required.
