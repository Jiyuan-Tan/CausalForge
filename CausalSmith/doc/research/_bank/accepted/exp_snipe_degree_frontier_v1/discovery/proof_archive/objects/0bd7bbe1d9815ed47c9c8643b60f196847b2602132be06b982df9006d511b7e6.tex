Fix \(B>0\) and \(1\le d\le n\). First consider the graph. Every active vertex belongs to exactly one complete directed block \(V_b\) of size \(d\). Because all within-block arrows, including self-loops, are present, each active vertex has in-degree and out-degree exactly \(d\). Every inactive vertex is isolated and therefore has both degrees zero. Thus \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\) satisfies \({\rm ass:bounded-degree}\), and the definition of \(\mathcal G_{n,d}\) gives
\[
G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}.
\]

Now fix \(\sigma\in\{-1,1\}\) and a schedule in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\). For every block draw in that support, \(|U_b|\le s=B/2\). If \(i\in V_b\) is active, \({\rm def:block-family}\) gives
\[
c_{i,T}=U_b\mathbf 1\{T=\varnothing\}+\sigma\delta h_{d,T}
\quad\text{for }|T|\le k_\star,
\]
and all remaining coefficients are zero. Since \(k_\star\le\bar\beta_d\le\beta\), every nonzero coefficient has order at most \(\beta\). The same condition is immediate for inactive units, whose coefficients all vanish. Hence the schedule satisfies \({\rm ass:low-order}\).

By \({\rm lem:block-energy-representer}\),
\[
H_{\beta,p}=\sup_{a\ge1}\sum_T|h_{a,T}|<\infty.
\]
Moreover, \(H_{\beta,p}>0\), because that lemma also gives \(h_d(\mathbf 1_d)-h_d(\mathbf 0_d)=1\). From the definition of \(\delta\),
\[
\delta
=B\min\!\left\{\frac{1}{2H_{\beta,p}},\frac{1}{4\pi}\right\}
       \min\!\left\{1,\sqrt{\frac{A_d}{m}}\right\}
\le \frac{B}{2H_{\beta,p}}.
\]
Consequently, for every active \(i\in V_b\), the triangle inequality yields
\[
\sum_{T\subseteq N_i}|c_{i,T}|
\le |U_b|+\delta\sum_{|T|\le k_\star}|h_{d,T}|
\le \frac B2+\delta H_{\beta,p}
\le B.
\]
For an inactive unit the coefficient sum is zero. Thus the schedule also satisfies \({\rm ass:bounded-coefficient-mass}\). By the definition of \(\mathcal C_{n,d,\beta}(B)\), every schedule in the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\) therefore belongs to \(\mathcal C_{n,d,\beta}(B)\).

Finally, combine \(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}}\in\mathcal G_{n,d}\) with the preceding schedule inclusion. The product-class definition \({\rm def:model-class}\) then gives
\[
\bigl(G_{n,d}^{\mathrm{blk}},c\bigr)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)
\]
for every prior-supported schedule \(c\). Equivalently, with the block graph fixed as in \({\rm def:block-family}\), the support of \(\Pi_\sigma\) is contained in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)\), as claimed.
