At \(p=1/2\), direct substitution gives
\[
\Delta_r(1/2)=2^{-r}-(-2^{-1})^r
=\begin{cases}
2^{1-r},&r\text{ odd},\\
0,&r\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
\]
Since \(\Delta_1(1/2)=1\), \({\rm def:exposed-order}\) says that \(k_\star\) is precisely the largest odd integer not exceeding \(\min\{\beta,d\}\). When \(\beta=1\), this gives \(k_\star=1\) and \(\binom d{k_\star}=d\). Substitution in \({\rm thm:degree-frontier}\) yields
\[
R_n^\star(d,1,1/2,B)\asymp B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n}\right\},
\]
with constants independent of \(n,d,B\).
