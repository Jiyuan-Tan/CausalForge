The parity formula, the description of \(k_\star\), and the exact fair-coin energy identity are proved in \({\rm thm:fair-coin-energy-frontier}\). In particular, when \(\beta=1\), that theorem gives \(A_d=4d\) and

R_{n,\ell_1}^\star(d,1,1/2,B)\asymp B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n}\right\}.

Because \({\rm def:two-class-minimax-risks}\) defines \(R_{n,\ell_1}^\star(d,\beta,p,B)=R_n^\star(d,\beta,p,B)\), this is the claimed specialization of \({\rm thm:degree-frontier}\).
