### T-block: t1 — Fix \(\beta\ge1\), \(p\in(0,1)\), \(B\ge0\), integers \(n…
**Statement.** Fix \(\beta\ge1\), \(p\in(0,1)\), \(B\ge0\), integers \(n\ge1\), and \(0\le d\le n\). There are \(0<c_{\beta,p}\le C_{\beta,p}<\infty\) such that \[c_{\beta,p}B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{d\binom{d}{k_\star}}{n}\right\}\le R_n^\star(d,\beta,p,B)\le\sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)}\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{up}}-\tau_n)^2]\le C_{\beta,p}B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{d\binom{d}{k_\star}}{n}\right\}.\] In the nonsaturated regime \(d\binom{d}{k_\star}/n\lesssim_{\beta,p}1\), unclipped SNIPE attains the same rate. The upper bound uses \(A_i=\sum_{r=1}^{\min\{\beta,|N_i|\}}\binom{|N_i|}{r}\Delta_r(p)^2/[p(1-p)]^r\le A_d\) and the single-out-degree overlap count. For \(B>0\) and \(d\ge1\), the block mixtures have TTE separation \(2\rho\delta\) and squared Hellinger distance at most \(4\pi^2m\delta^2/(B^2A_d)\); since \(1/2\le\rho\le1\) and \(A_d/m=(dA_d/n)/\rho\), this yields the stated lower branch without a divisibility condition.

### T-block: t2 — Fix \(\beta\ge1\), \(p\in(0,1)\), and \(B>0\)
**Statement.** Fix \(\beta\ge1\), \(p\in(0,1)\), and \(B>0\). For every index \(t\), let \(n_t\) and \(d_t\) be positive integers such that \(d_t\mid n_t\), write \(m_t:=n_t/d_t\), and let \(G_t\) be the fixed directed graph that is the disjoint union of \(m_t\) complete directed blocks \(V_b\) of size \(d_t\), including all self-loops. Then, for every \(t\), \[ R_t^{\mathrm{loc,lin}}=\frac{B^2A_{d_t}}{m_t}=\frac{B^2d_tA_{d_t}}{n_t}. \] In particular, \(R_t^{\mathrm{loc,lin}}/(B^2d_tA_{d_t}/n_t)=1\) for every \(t\). More precisely, put \(Z_S:=\prod_{j\in S}Z_j\) and \(Z_b:=(Z_j)_{j\in V_b}\), and for every block \(V_b\) define \[ \Psi_{b,t}(w):=\max_{\substack{(\varepsilon_i,S_i)_{i\in V_b}\\\varepsilon_i\in\{-1,1\},\ S_i\subseteq V_b,\ |S_i|\le\bar\beta_{d_t}}} \mathbb E_Z\!\left[\left(\sum_{i\in V_b}\varepsilon_i\{w_{i,t}(Z_b)Z_{S_i}-\mathbf1(S_i\ne\varnothing)\}\right)^2\right]. \] Then, for every \(t\), \[ \sup_{c\in\mathcal M_t(G_t)}\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_{w,t}-\tau_{n_t})^2] =\frac{B^2}{n_t^2}\sum_{b=1}^{m_t}\Psi_{b,t}(w), \qquad \Psi_{b,t}(w)\ge d_t^2A_{d_t}. \] A sequence in \(\mathcal E_t^{\mathrm{loc,lin}}\) is asymptotically minimax if and only if \[ \frac{1}{m_td_t^2A_{d_t}}\sum_{b=1}^{m_t}\{\Psi_{b,t}(w)-d_t^2A_{d_t}\}\longrightarrow0. \] The condition \[ \frac{1}{n_tA_{d_t}}\sum_{i\in V_{n_t}}\mathbb E_Z[(w_{i,t}(Z_{b(i)})-g_{d_t}(Z_{b(i)}))^2]\longrightarrow0 \] is sufficient for asymptotic minimaxity. Suppose in addition that \(d_t\to\infty\). This sufficient condition is not necessary, even up to relabeling coordinates within blocks. There are asymptotically minimax weights for which the last display equals \(2\) for every sufficiently large \(t\).

### T-block: t3 — Fix \(\beta\ge1\), \(p\in(0,1)\), \(B\ge0\), integers \(n…
**Statement.** Fix \(\beta\ge1\), \(p\in(0,1)\), \(B\ge0\), integers \(n\ge1\), and \(0\le d\le n\). The coefficient-mass model is contained in the uniformly bounded-outcome model, and the inclusion is strict whenever \(B>0\) and \(d\ge1\). There are \(0<c_{\beta,p}\le C_{\beta,p}<\infty\) such that \[ c_{\beta,p}B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{dA_d}{n}\right\} \le R_{n,\ell_1}^\star(d,\beta,p,B)=R_n^\star(d,\beta,p,B) \le R_{n,\infty}^\star(d,\beta,p,B) \le \sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}^{\infty}(B)} \mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_{n,\infty}^{\mathrm{up}}-\tau_n)^2] \le C_{\beta,p}B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{dA_d}{n}\right\}. \] Moreover, \[ \sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)} \mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{up}}-\tau_n)^2] \le C_{\beta,p}B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{dA_d}{n}\right\}, \] and on either model class unclipped SNIPE is design-unbiased and has worst-case mean squared error at most \(B^2dA_d/n\). For every \(0\le d\le n\), \(dA_d\asymp_{\beta,p}d\binom d{k_\star}\), so both minimax risks are also \(\asymp_{\beta,p}B^2\min\{1,d\binom d{k_\star}/n\}\), and unclipped SNIPE attains the nonsaturated branch. If \(1\le d\le n\) and \(d\mid n\), put \(m=n/d\) and take the disjoint union of \(m\) complete directed \(d\)-blocks, including loops. Then canonical unclipped SNIPE has the exact global worst-case risk on both classes, \[ \sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}(B)}\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{SNIPE}}-\tau_n)^2] =\sup_{(G_n,c)\in\mathcal M_{n,d,\beta}^{\infty}(B)}\mathbb E_Z[(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{SNIPE}}-\tau_n)^2] =\frac{B^2A_d}{m}=\frac{B^2dA_d}{n}, \] so the constant one in the global unclipped variance bound is sharp. The upper-bound calculation uses \[ A_i=\sum_{r=1}^{\min\{\beta,|N_i|\}}\binom{|N_i|}{r}\frac{\Delta_r(p)^2}{[p(1-p)]^r}\le A_d \] and charges out-degree only once. For \(B>0\) and \(d\ge1\), the block mixtures in \({\rm def:block-family}\) lie in the smaller class, have TTE separation \(2\rho\delta\), and have squared Hellinger distance at most \(4\pi^2m\delta^2/(B^2A_d)\); furthermore \(1/2\le\rho\le1\) and \(A_d/m=(dA_d/n)/\rho\).

### T-block: t4 — Fix \(n\ge1\), \(1\le d\le n\), \(\beta\ge1\), \(B\ge0\),…
**Statement.** Fix \(n\ge1\), \(1\le d\le n\), \(\beta\ge1\), \(B\ge0\), and \(p=1/2\). Then \[ \Delta_r(1/2)=\begin{cases}2^{1-r},&r\text{ odd},\\0,&r\text{ even},\end{cases} \qquad A_d=4\sum_{\substack{1\le r\le\min\{\beta,d\}\\r\text{ odd}}}\binom dr. \] Consequently \(k_\star\) is the largest odd integer no greater than \(\min\{\beta,d\}\). For \(\beta=1\), \(A_d=4d\) and \[ R_n^\star(d,1,1/2,B)=R_{n,\ell_1}^\star(d,1,1/2,B) \asymp R_{n,\infty}^\star(d,1,1/2,B) \asymp B^2\min\!\left\{1,\frac{d^2}{n}\right\}. \] If additionally \(d\mid n\), canonical unclipped SNIPE has exact worst-case mean squared error \(4B^2d^2/n\) on both model classes.
