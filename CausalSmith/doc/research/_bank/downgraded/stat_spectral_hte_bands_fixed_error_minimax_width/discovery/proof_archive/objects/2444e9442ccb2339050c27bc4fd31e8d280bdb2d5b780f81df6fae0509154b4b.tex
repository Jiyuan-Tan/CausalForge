\textbf{Proof.}
Choose \((p_k,a_k,\sigma_k)\in\mathfrak S_k\). Since \(p_k\in(1/4,3/4)\),
\[
 \eta=a_k(B-p_k)\in\{-a_kp_k,a_k(1-p_k)\}
\]
is centered, bounded, independent of \((X,V)\), and nonzero almost surely. Compatibility gives \(\|\eta\|_{\psi_2}\le\psi_\eta\), and direct differentiation of its log moment-generating function gives
\[
 \kappa_k(\eta)=a_k^k\left.\partial_t^k\log(1-p_k+p_ke^t)\right|_{t=0},
 \qquad |\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta. \tag{1}
\]
This works for odd and even \(k\), because the selected asymmetric Bernoulli parameter avoids the finite zero set of the displayed cumulant polynomial.

Here \(V\) is uniform, so its density is one and compatibility states \(c_V\le1\le C_V\). The choices \(g_0=q_0=\bar g_n=0\) obey the range restrictions. The assumed H\"older and range restrictions on \(\tau_0\) supply the remaining curve conditions.

Conditional on \((X,V,T)\), \(T=\eta\) is fixed and \(\epsilon\) remains centered Gaussian. Thus
\[
 E_P(\xi\mid X,V,T)=0,
 \quad
 \|\xi\|_{\psi_2\mid X,V,T}
 =|\eta|\sigma_k\|N(0,1)\|_{\psi_2}
 \le\psi_\xi, \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 E_P(\xi^2\mid X,V,T)=\eta^2\sigma_k^2
 \ge a_k^2\sigma_k^2\min\{p_k,1-p_k\}^2
 \ge\underline\sigma_\xi^2. \tag{3}
\]
All support, tail, separation, and nondegeneracy conditions of the primitive law class have now been checked. Since \(0\in\mathcal G\), \(g_0=\bar g_n=0\), and the zero function belongs to \(\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\),
\[
 d_P(\bar g_n,g_0)
 \le \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{v\in[0,1]}
 E_P[|g_0(X,V)-\bar g_n(X,V)-0|\mid V=v]
 =0\le\varepsilon_0. \tag{4}
\]
Therefore \(\bar g_n\in\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\), and the law--code construction belongs to \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\).

Finally, \(T=\eta\ne0\) almost surely and
\[
 \frac{Y}{T}=\tau_0(V)+\epsilon. \tag{5}
\]
The map \((V,T,Y)\mapsto(V,T,Y/T)\) is almost surely invertible, \(T\) has a law independent of \(\tau_0\), and \((V,Y/T)\) is the uniform-design Gaussian regression experiment. Thus the observed experiment is ordinary random-design Gaussian nonparametric regression for \(\tau_0\), together with ancillary \(T\). \(\square\)
