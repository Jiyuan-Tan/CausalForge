\textbf{Proof.}
The calculation in (1)--(3) of \(\text{lem:noise-only-contour-bank}\), using only centering and the exact Luxemburg premise, gives
\[
 |M(w)|\le\exp(4\psi_\eta^2|w|^2),
 \qquad w\in\mathbb C. \tag{1}
\]
Every point \(z\) on every bank circle satisfies \(|z|\le R_0\).  The coefficient formula for the power series of \(w\mapsto M(z+w)\) on \(|w|=1\), together with (1), yields
\[
 |M'(z)|\le
 \sup_{|w-z|=1}|M(w)|
 \le\exp\{4\psi_\eta^2(R_0+1)^2\}=A_M. \tag{2}
\]
Every bank circle has length at most \(2\pi R_0\), and the definition
\(\Lambda_0=2\pi R_0\{1+2A_M+A_M/\rho_M\}\) dominates \(2\pi R_0A_M\).  Taking the maximum over the finite bank proves the claim. \(\square\)
