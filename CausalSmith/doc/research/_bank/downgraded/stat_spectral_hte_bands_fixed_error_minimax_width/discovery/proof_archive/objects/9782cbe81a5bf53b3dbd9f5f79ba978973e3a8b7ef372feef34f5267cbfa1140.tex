\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
Fix a pair and choose a witness \(d\in\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\) from the corrected external-input ball.  Put
\[
 D_n=d(V)+R_n,\qquad
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{u\in[0,1]}E_P(|R_n|\mid V=u)
 \le\varepsilon_0. \tag{1}
\]
Because \(g_0\) and \(\bar g_n\) are both bounded by \(C_g\),
\(|D_n|\le2C_g\); because \(|d|\le2C_g\),
\[
 |D_n-d(v)|\le4C_g. \tag{2}
\]
For \(|z|\le R_0+1\), the fundamental theorem of calculus gives
\[
 |e^{z\{D_n-d(v)\}}-1|
 \le A_D|D_n-d(v)|,\qquad
 A_D=(R_0+1)e^{4C_g(R_0+1)}. \tag{3}
\]

Let \(w=w_{v,h}=h^{-1}K((V-v)/h)\) and
\(\mu_{v,h}=E_Pw\).  If
\[
 0<h<\operatorname{dist}(\mathcal I,\{0,1\}), \tag{4}
\]
then kernel normalization, nonnegativity, and the design-density bounds give
\[
 c_V\le\mu_{v,h}\le C_V. \tag{5}
\]
Conditioning (3) on \(V=u\), using (1), the Hölder property of \(d\), and the support of \(K\), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \left|e^{-zd(v)}E_P[we^{zD_n}]-\mu_{v,h}\right|
 &\le A_DE_P[w|D_n-d(v)|]\\
 &\le A_D\{L_Dh^{\gamma_D}+\varepsilon_0\}\mu_{v,h}. \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]
This is the required localized factorization bound with an explicit uniform constant.

The corrected threshold satisfies \(A_D\varepsilon_0\le1/8\).
For all sufficiently small \(h\), also
\(A_DL_Dh^{\gamma_D}\le1/8\).  Thus the left side of (6) is at most
\(\mu_{v,h}/4\).  The function
\[
 z\longmapsto e^{-zd(v)}E_P[we^{zD_n}]
\]
therefore lies uniformly within \(\mu_{v,h}/4\) of the positive constant
\(\mu_{v,h}\) throughout \(|z|\le R_0+1\).  The straight-line homotopy is zero-free, so this function, and hence
\(A_{v,h}(z)=E_P[we^{zD_n}]\), is zero-free and has winding zero on every bank circle.  Moreover,
\[
 |A_{v,h}(z)|\ge3\mu_{v,h}/4\ge3c_V/4. \tag{7}
\]

Finally, \(D_n\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V)\), whereas
\(\eta\perp(X,V)\).  Therefore
\[
 F_{v,h}(z)=E_P[we^{z(\eta+D_n)}]
 =M(z)A_{v,h}(z). \tag{8}
\]
On the contour certified by the noise-only bank,
\(|M|\ge\rho_M\); equations (7)--(8) give the asserted
\(3\rho_Mc_V/4\) modulus.  Notice that no step factors or lower-bounds the unlocalized contaminated transform.  Setting \(d=0\) recovers every code allowed by the former direct conditional-\(L^1\) ball. \(\square\)
