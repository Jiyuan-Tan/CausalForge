Put
\[
 R_0=(2^{k+4}k!)^{1/(k-2)}
       (\psi_\eta^2/\delta)^{1/(k-2)},
 \qquad R_1=R_0+1.
\]
Let \(U_\psi=\lceil\max\{1,\psi_\eta\}\rceil\), \(U_R=\lceil\max\{1,R_1\}\rceil\),
\[
 N_{\rm cert}=4U_\psi^2(U_R+2)^3,
 \quad m_\circ=N_{\rm cert}+3,
 \quad d_\circ=2^{-m_\circ},
 \quad J_\circ=2^{m_\circ-1},
\]
and, for \(j=0,\ldots,J_\circ\), let
\[
 C_j=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=\rho_j\},
 \qquad \rho_j=R_0+\tfrac14+j2^{-m_\circ}.
\]
Thus \(R_0<\rho_j<R_1\). Define
\[
 e_\circ=8U_RU_\psi^2(U_R+1)^2,
 \quad d_\circ^{\rm den}=3(2U_R+1),
 \quad u_\circ=2e_\circ+N_{\rm cert}(m_\circ+d_\circ^{\rm den}),
 \quad \rho_M=2^{-u_\circ}.
\]
The noise-only bank is \(\mathfrak C_0=\{C_0,\ldots,C_{J_\circ}\}\). Finally put
\[
 A_M=\exp\{4\psi_\eta^2(R_0+2)^2\},
 \qquad
 \Lambda_0=2\pi(R_0+1)
 \{1+2A_M+A_M/\rho_M\}.
\]
Every displayed operation is a finite law-blind operation on \((k,\delta,\psi_\eta)\); neither a supplied code nor its error radius enters the construction.
