\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
Write \(F_v(z)=F_{v,h_n}(z)\).  Since \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), exact treatment sub-Gaussianity gives uniformly bounded moments of every fixed order for
\[
 (1+|Z_n|)e^{R_0|Z_n|}. \tag{1}
\]
The bank is finite, its circles are smooth, and translations of the bounded Lipschitz kernel have polynomial covering grids.  Truncate at \(|\eta|\le A\sqrt{\log n}\), apply the iid moment inequality
\[
 E|(P_{n_1}-P)f|^q
 \le C_q\{(Pf^2/n_1)^{q/2}+P|f|^q/n_1^{q-1}\}, \tag{2}
\]
on polynomial grids in \(v\) and contour arclength, and control oscillations by the Lipschitz constants.  Since \(n_1h_n\) diverges polynomially, for every fixed \(D>0\),
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}P(\mathcal U_n^c)=O(n^{-D}), \tag{3}
\]
where
\[
 \mathcal U_n=\left\{\max_j\sup_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_j}
 |\widehat F_{1j,v}(z)-F_v(z)|\le\rho_F/4\right\}. \tag{4}
\]
The same calculation controls the reference-localized \(z\)-derivative.

On the noise-certified contour, robustness gives \(\inf_{v,z}|F_v(z)|\ge3\rho_F/2\).  Hence on \(\mathcal U_n\) it passes the empirical threshold \(5\rho_F/4\).  Moreover
\[
 F_{v_{\mathrm{ref}}}(z)=M(z)A_{v_{\mathrm{ref}},h_n}(z),
\]
and robustness proves that the second factor is zero-free with winding zero.  Thus the population reference-localized winding equals \(m_\star\ne0\).  The straight-line boundary homotopy between \(F_{v_{\mathrm{ref}}}\) and \(\widehat F_{1j,v_{\mathrm{ref}}}\) preserves it.  At least one index therefore passes.

Conversely, if \(j=\widehat j_n\) on \(\mathcal U_n\), its empirical threshold and (4) give \(\inf_{v,z\in C_j}|F_v(z)|\ge\rho_F\).  The same homotopy, followed by the factorization and zero winding of \(A_{v_{\mathrm{ref}},h_n}\), gives
\[
 \widehat m_j=\operatorname{wind}(F_{v_{\mathrm{ref}}},C_j)
 =\operatorname{wind}(M,C_j)=m_j\ne0. \tag{5}
\]
Together with (3), this proves the claim. \(\square\)
