\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a pair in \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\), a certified contour \(C\in\mathfrak C_0\), and sufficiently small \(h\).  Let \(w=w_{v,h}\), and define the entire functions
\[
 A(z)=E_P[we^{zD_n}],\quad
 A_\tau(z)=E_P[w\tau_0(V)e^{zD_n}],\quad
 Q(z)=E_P[wq_0(X,V)e^{zD_n}]. \tag{1}
\]
The boundedness of \(q_0\), \(\tau_0\), and \(D_n\) makes these functions entire.  The robustness lemma gives \(|A(z)|\ge c_V/2\) on the whole disk containing \(C\), not merely on its boundary.

Independence of \(\eta\) from \((X,V)\) gives
\[
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)A(z). \tag{2}
\]
Moreover \(w e^{zZ_n}\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V,T)\), so the conditional mean restriction implies
\[
 E_P[w\xi e^{zZ_n}]
 =E_P\{we^{zZ_n}E_P(\xi\mid X,V,T)\}=0. \tag{3}
\]
Using \(Y=q_0(X,V)+\tau_0(V)\eta+\xi\), (3), and independence once more yields
\[
 G_{v,h}(z)=M(z)Q(z)+M'(z)A_\tau(z). \tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) imply on \(C\)
\[
 \frac{G_{v,h}}{F_{v,h}}
 =\frac{Q}{A}+\frac{M'}{M}\frac{A_\tau}{A}. \tag{5}
\]
Since \(A\) is zero-free on and inside \(C\), \(Q/A\) is holomorphic there and its contour integral is zero.  If
\[
 m(C)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{M'}M\,dz\ne0,
\]
then subtracting \(\tau_0(v)\) from the contour functional gives the exact identity
\[
 \Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)
 =\operatorname{Re}\left\{
 \frac{1}{2\pi i m(C)}\oint_C\frac{M'(z)}{M(z)}
 \left(\frac{A_\tau(z)}{A(z)}-\tau_0(v)\right)dz
 \right\}. \tag{6}
\]
This proves both analytic annihilation of the baseline term and exact recovery whenever \(\tau_0\) is constant.

For the varying curve, the support of \(K\) and the H\"older restriction give
\[
 |A_\tau(z)-\tau_0(v)A(z)|
 \le L_\tau h^\beta e^{2C_gR_0}E_Pw
 \le C_Ve^{2C_gR_0}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{7}
\]
Because \(|A|\ge c_V/2\), \(|m(C)|\ge1\), and the bank envelope controls \(\operatorname{len}(C)\sup_C|M'/M|\), (6)--(7) imply
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le 2(C_V/c_V)e^{2C_gR_0}\Lambda_0L_\tau h^\beta
 =B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h^\beta.
\]
The argument is uniform over every certified selected contour.  It used no modulus of continuity for \(q_0\), \(g_0\), or \(\bar g_n\). \(\square\)
