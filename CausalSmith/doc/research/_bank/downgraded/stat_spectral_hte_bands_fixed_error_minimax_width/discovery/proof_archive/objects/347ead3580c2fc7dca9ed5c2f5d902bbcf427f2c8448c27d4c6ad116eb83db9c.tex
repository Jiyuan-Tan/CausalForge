\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a pair in \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\), a selected contour \(C\) on the good pilot event, and
\[
 0<h<\operatorname{dist}(\mathcal I,\{0,1\}).
\]
Let \(w=w_{v,h}\), and define
\[
 A(z)=E_P[we^{zD_n}],\qquad
 A_\tau(z)=E_P[w\tau_0(V)e^{zD_n}],\qquad
 Q(z)=E_P[wq_0(X,V)e^{zD_n}]. \tag{1}
\]
Boundedness of \(q_0\), \(\tau_0\), and \(D_n\) makes these functions entire.  The direct conditional perturbation inequality gives \(|A(z)|\ge c_V/2\) throughout \(|z|\le R_0\), so \(A\) is zero-free on and inside every bank contour.

Independence of \(\eta\) from \((X,V)\) gives
\[
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)A(z). \tag{2}
\]
Furthermore, \(we^{zZ_n}\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V,T)\); hence the conditional mean restriction yields
\[
 E_P[w\xi e^{zZ_n}]
 =E_P\{we^{zZ_n}E_P(\xi\mid X,V,T)\}=0. \tag{3}
\]
Using \(Y=q_0(X,V)+\tau_0(V)\eta+\xi\), (3), and independence again gives
\[
 G_{v,h}(z)=M(z)Q(z)+M'(z)A_\tau(z). \tag{4}
\]
Thus, on \(C\),
\[
 \frac{G_{v,h}}{F_{v,h}}
 =\frac{Q}{A}+\frac{M'}{M}\frac{A_\tau}{A}. \tag{5}
\]
The selected population transform modulus and (8) in the selector proof imply
\[
 \inf_{z\in C}|M(z)|\ge
 \frac{\rho_M/2}{5/4}=\frac{2\rho_M}{5}. \tag{6}
\]
Consequently \(M\) is boundary-zero-free, the selected winding \(m(C)\) in (9) is nonzero, and the choice (6)--(7) in the noise-bank proof gives the \(\Lambda_0\) envelope on this possibly data-selected contour.

Since \(A\) is zero-free on the whole interior, \(Q/A\) is holomorphic there and its contour integral vanishes.  Subtracting \(\tau_0(v)\) from the localized functional gives the exact identity
\[
 \Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)
 =\operatorname{Re}\left\{
 \frac{1}{2\pi i m(C)}\oint_C\frac{M'(z)}{M(z)}
 \left(\frac{A_\tau(z)}{A(z)}-\tau_0(v)\right)dz
 \right\}. \tag{7}
\]
This proves analytic annihilation of the baseline term.  It also proves exact recovery when the treatment slope is constant, with no restriction on \(D_n\) beyond the stated code certificate.

For a varying curve, support of \(K\), the H\"older condition, and the design upper bound give
\[
 |A_\tau(z)-\tau_0(v)A(z)|
 \le L_\tau h^\beta e^{2C_gR_0}E_Pw
 \le C_Ve^{2C_gR_0}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{8}
\]
Because \(|A|\ge c_V/2\), \(|m(C)|\ge1\), and \(\Lambda_0\) controls \(\operatorname{len}(C)\sup_C|M'/M|\), equations (7)--(8) imply
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le2(C_V/c_V)e^{2C_gR_0}\Lambda_0L_\tau h^\beta
 =B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{9}
\]
The proof used only boundedness, not smoothness, of \(q_0\), and no smoothness at all of \(g_0\) or \(\bar g_n\). \(\square\)
