\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
 \Phi(d)=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{v\in[0,1]}
 E_P[|g'(X,V)-g(X,V)-d(V)|\mid V=v].
\]
For \(d,d'\in\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\), the reverse triangle inequality gives
\[
 |\Phi(d)-\Phi(d')|\le\|d-d'\|_\infty. \tag{1}
\]
The class \(\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\) is uniformly bounded and has the common modulus \(L_D|u-v|^{\gamma_D}\).  Given a minimizing sequence, diagonal extraction on the dyadic rationals yields a subsequence converging on that countable dense set.  The common modulus upgrades it to a uniformly Cauchy subsequence on \([0,1]\).  Its uniform limit remains bounded by \(2C_g\) and retains the H\"older bound, hence belongs to \(\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\).  Continuity (1) shows that the limit attains the infimum.

If \(g\in\Gamma_{\mathrm{dir}}(P,\varepsilon_0)\), choose \(d\equiv0\).  This gives \(d_P(g,g_0)\le d_P^{\mathrm{dir}}(g,g_0)\), proving both inclusion and the inequality between approximation radii.  If \(a_{\mathcal G}(P)\le\varepsilon_{\mathrm{app}}<\varepsilon_0\), the definition of the outer infimum supplies some \(g\in\mathcal G\) with \(d_P(g,g_0)<\varepsilon_0\), so \(g\in\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\).

For strictness under the displayed compatibility, take \(g_0=0\), \(g_a=-a\), and \(d(v)=a\).  The shift is in \(\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\), and \(g_0-g_a-d(V)=0\), so \(g_a\in\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\).  Directly, \(d_P^{\mathrm{dir}}(g_a,g_0)=a>\varepsilon_0\).  Thus \(g_a\notin\Gamma_{\mathrm{dir}}(P,\varepsilon_0)\). \(\square\)
