Let
\[
 \mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}
 =\left\{d:[0,1]\to[-2C_g,2C_g]:
 \sup_{u\ne v}\frac{|d(u)-d(v)|}{|u-v|^{\gamma_D}}\le L_D\right\}.
\]
Define
\[
 \Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)
 =\left\{g\in\mathcal G:
 \inf_{d\in\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}}
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{v\in[0,1]}
 E_P[|g_0(X,V)-g(X,V)-d(V)|\mid V=v]
 \le\varepsilon_0\right\},
\]
where \(\varepsilon_0\in(0,\varepsilon_\star]\) is fixed after \(\mathfrak C_0\) and its maximal radius are derived and before the inference sample.
