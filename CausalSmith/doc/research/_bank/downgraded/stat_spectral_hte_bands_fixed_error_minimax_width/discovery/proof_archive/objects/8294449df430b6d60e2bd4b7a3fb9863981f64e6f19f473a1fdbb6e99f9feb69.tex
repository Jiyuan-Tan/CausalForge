\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(\mathcal A\in\mathfrak H_\alpha\) and the \(M_n=m_n+1\) exact-code hypotheses. Put
\[
 d_n=\min_{j\ne\ell}\|\tau_j-\tau_\ell\|_\infty\ge c_dr_n,
 \qquad \Delta_n(\mathcal A)=\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\{U_n(v)-L_n(v)\}. \tag{1}
\]
Let \(\mathcal C_{j,n}\) be whole-curve coverage under \(P_j^n\). Because the exact-code pairs belong to the enlarged class, honesty gives
\[
 \kappa_n=\min_jP_j^n(\mathcal C_{j,n})\ge1-\alpha-o(1). \tag{2}
\]
Evaluate at translated maximizers of the bumps. The resulting finite vectors are pairwise separated by \(d_n\) in some coordinate. Decode by the unique candidate vector contained in all reported coordinate intervals, with a fixed default otherwise. On \(\mathcal C_{j,n}\cap\{\Delta_n(\mathcal A)<d_n\}\), decoding is correct, so Markov's inequality gives
\[
 \frac1{M_n}\sum_jP_j^n(\widehat J=j)
 \ge\kappa_n-\frac1{M_nd_n}\sum_jE_j\Delta_n(\mathcal A). \tag{3}
\]
For uniform \(J\) and \(\overline P=M_n^{-1}\sum_jP_j^n\),
\[
 I(J;O_1^n)=\frac1{M_n}\sum_j\operatorname{KL}(P_j^n,\overline P)
 \le\frac1{M_n}\sum_j\operatorname{KL}(P_j^n,P_0^n)
 \le c_0\log m_n. \tag{4}
\]
The entropy inequality \(H(J\mid O_1^n)\le\log2+p_e\log M_n\) yields
\[
 \frac1{M_n}\sum_jP_j^n(\widehat J=j)
 \le\frac{c_0\log m_n+\log2}{\log M_n}. \tag{5}
\]
Choose \(c_0<(1-\alpha)/2\). Combining (2)--(5) gives
\[
 \max_jE_j\Delta_n(\mathcal A)
 \ge d_n\{1-\alpha-c_0-o(1)\}\ge cr_n. \tag{6}
\]
The supremum over \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) dominates this maximum. The honest-spectral-band theorem supplies a band with risk at most \(Cr_n\) over the same class. No contour, symmetry, or fixed-width restriction entered the decoder, so the result holds for every measurable honest band. \(\square\)
