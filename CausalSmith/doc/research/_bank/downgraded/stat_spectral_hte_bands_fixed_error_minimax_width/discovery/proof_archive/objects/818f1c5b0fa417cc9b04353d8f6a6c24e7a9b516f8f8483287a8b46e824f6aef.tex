\textbf{Proof.}
Choose \((p_k,a_k,\sigma_k)\in\mathfrak S_k\). Then
\[
 \eta=a_k(B-p_k)\in\{-a_kp_k,a_k(1-p_k)\}
\]
is centered, bounded, independent of \((X,V)\), and nonzero almost surely. Compatibility and direct differentiation give
\[
 \|\eta\|_{\psi_2}\le\psi_\eta,\qquad
 \kappa_k(\eta)=a_k^k\left.\partial_t^k\log(1-p_k+p_ke^t)\right|_{t=0},
 \qquad |\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta. \tag{1}
\]
The asymmetric \(p_k\) avoids the finite zero set, so this works for odd and even \(k\).

The modifier is uniform and compatibility gives \(c_V\le1\le C_V\). The choices \(g_0=q_0=\bar g_n=0\) obey the range restrictions. Since \(0\in\mathcal G\), both the law approximation and code membership hold with \(d=0\) and remainder \(D_n-d(V)=0\). Thus the exact-code family remains in the enlarged pair class. The stated H\"older and range restrictions verify the curve conditions.

Conditional on \((X,V,T)\), \(T=\eta\) is fixed and \(\epsilon\) is centered Gaussian. Therefore
\[
 E(\xi\mid X,V,T)=0,
 \quad \|\xi\|_{\psi_2\mid X,V,T}
 \le a_k\sigma_k\max\{p_k,1-p_k\}\|N(0,1)\|_{\psi_2}\le\psi_\xi, \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 E(\xi^2\mid X,V,T)
 \ge a_k^2\sigma_k^2\min\{p_k,1-p_k\}^2
 \ge\underline\sigma_\xi^2. \tag{3}
\]
Finally \(T\ne0\) almost surely and \(Y/T=\tau_0(V)+\epsilon\). The experiment is uniform-design Gaussian nonparametric regression plus ancillary \(T\). \(\square\)
