\textbf{Proof.}
The exact Luxemburg premise and centering imply
\[
 |M(w)|\le\exp(4\psi_\eta^2|w|^2),\qquad w\in\mathbb C. \tag{1}
\]
Every \(z\) on a bank circle has \(|z|\le R_0\).  Cauchy's derivative formula on the unit circle about \(z\), followed by (1), gives
\[
 |M'(z)|\le\exp\{4\psi_\eta^2(R_0+1)^2\}=A_M. \tag{2}
\]
Every bank circle has length at most \(2\pi R_0\), and the definition of \(\Lambda_0\) dominates \(2\pi R_0A_M\).  The result follows. \(\square\)
