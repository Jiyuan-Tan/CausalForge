\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(R_1=R_0+1\), and use the quantities in the corrected construction. The Luxemburg sub-Gaussian inequality and centering give
\[
 M(0)=1,\qquad M'(0)=0,\qquad
 |M(z)|\le \exp(4\psi_\eta^2|z|^2),\quad z\in\mathbb C. \tag{1}
\]
The same envelope justifies differentiation under the expectation, so \(M\) is entire.

Suppose that \(M\) has no zero in \(\{|z|\le R\}\). The analytic logarithm \(G=\log M\), normalized by \(G(0)=0\), exists on that disk. Applying Borel--Carath\'eodory to (1) on nested disks and then Cauchy's derivative bound yields, for every \(k\ge3\),
\[
 |G^{(k)}(0)|\le 2^{k+3}k!\,\psi_\eta^2R^{2-k}. \tag{2}
\]
Indeed, on the outer disk \(\operatorname{Re}G=\log|M|\le4\psi_\eta^2R^2\); Borel--Carath\'eodory bounds \(G\) on the half disk and Cauchy's formula bounds its \(k\)th coefficient. Taking
\[
 R=R_0=(2^{k+4}k!)^{1/(k-2)}
       (\psi_\eta^2/\delta)^{1/(k-2)}
\]
makes the right side of (2) equal to \(\delta/2\), contradicting \(|G^{(k)}(0)|=|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta\). Hence \(M\) has a zero \(z_0\) with \(|z_0|\le R_0\). This is the numerical zero-localization step also used in \(\text{stat\_sa\_plm\_cumulant\_converse/lem:finite-contour-bank}\).

Put
\[
 R_{\rm fac}=R_1+1,\qquad R_{\rm out}=R_1+2.
\]
Jensen's formula and (1) show that the number \(N_0\) of zeros in \(\{|z|<R_{\rm fac}\}\), counted with multiplicity, satisfies
\[
 N_0\log(R_{\rm out}/R_{\rm fac})
 \le4\psi_\eta^2R_{\rm out}^2.
\]
Since \(R_{\rm out}=R_{\rm fac}+1\) and \(R_{\rm out}^{-1}\le\log(R_{\rm out}/R_{\rm fac})\),
\[
 N_0\le4\psi_\eta^2R_{\rm out}^3
 \le4U_\psi^2(U_R+2)^3=N_{\rm cert}. \tag{3}
\]
List these zeros as \(a_1,\ldots,a_{N_0}\), with multiplicity. The finite Blaschke factorization on the disk of radius \(R_{\rm fac}\) gives
\[
 M(z)=Q(z)g(z),\qquad
 Q(z)=\prod_{\nu=1}^{N_0}
 \frac{R_{\rm fac}(z-a_\nu)}
 {R_{\rm fac}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}, \tag{4}
\]
where \(g\) is holomorphic and zero-free there.

The grid radii have spacing \(d_\circ=2^{-m_\circ}\). A number \(|a_\nu|\) can be within \(d_\circ/3\) of at most one grid radius. By (3), while the grid has \(2^{m_\circ-1}+1>N_{\rm cert}\) points, there is a \(j\) such that
\[
 \bigl||a_\nu|-\rho_j\bigr|\ge d_\circ/3
 \quad(\nu=1,\ldots,N_0). \tag{5}
\]
Because \(R_0<\rho_j<R_1\), the zero \(z_0\) lies strictly inside \(C_j\), while (5) puts no zero on \(C_j\). The argument principle therefore gives
\[
 m_j=(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_{C_j}M'/M\,dz\in\mathbb N,qquad m_j\ge1. \tag{6}
\]

On \(|z|=R_{\rm fac}\), \(|Q(z)|=1\), whereas (1) bounds \(|M(z)|\). Also \(M(0)=1\) and \(|Q(0)|\le1\), so \(|g(0)|\ge1\). Harnack's inequality applied to \(4\psi_\eta^2R_{\rm fac}^2-\log|g|\) gives, for \(|z|\le R_1\),
\[
 |M(z)|\ge
 \exp\{-8\psi_\eta^2R_1(R_1+1)^2\}|Q(z)|. \tag{7}
\]
For \(z\in C_j\), (5) and (4) imply for every factor
\[
 \left|
 \frac{R_{\rm fac}(z-a_\nu)}
 {R_{\rm fac}^2-\overline{a_\nu}z}
 \right|
 \ge\frac{d_\circ}{3(2U_R+1)}
 \ge2^{-(m_\circ+d_\circ^{\rm den})}. \tag{8}
\]
The exponential in (7) is at least \(2^{-2e_\circ}\). Multiplying (8) over at most \(N_{\rm cert}\) zeros yields
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_j}|M(z)|
 \ge2^{-2e_\circ-N_{\rm cert}(m_\circ+d_\circ^{\rm den})}
 =2^{-u_\circ}=\rho_M. \tag{9}
\]

Finally, Cauchy's derivative inequality applied on the unit enlargement of the search disk and (1) gives
\[
 \sup_{|z|\le R_1}\{|M(z)|,|M'(z)|\}\le A_M.
\]
Every bank circle has length at most \(2\pi R_1\); combining this with (9) and the definition of \(\Lambda_0\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{len}(C_j)\sup_{z\in C_j}
 \{1+|M(z)|+|M'(z)|+|M'(z)/M(z)|\}\le\Lambda_0.
\]
All constants and every circle were constructed from \((k,\delta,\psi_\eta)\) alone. \(\square\)
