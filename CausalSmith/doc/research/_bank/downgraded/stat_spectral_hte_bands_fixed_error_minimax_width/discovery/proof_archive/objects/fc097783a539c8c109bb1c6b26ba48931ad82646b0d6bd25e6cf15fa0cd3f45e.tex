\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) and \(\mathcal E_{2n}\) be the events from the Bahadur--multiplier lemma. On their intersection, the uniform linearization, Gaussian approximation, multiplier approximation, anti-concentration, and ratio-consistent studentization imply
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P\left\{ |\widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta_{v,h_n}|
 \le\frac{\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I\right\}\ge1-\alpha. \tag{1}
\]
The estimator, center, and score use the same nonzero winding on this event. Its complement has probability \(o(r_n)=o(1)\) uniformly.

The localized-bias lemma gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta_{v,h_n}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{2}
\]
The triangle inequality combines (1)--(2) to give simultaneous coverage by the success-event band. On either failure event the procedure returns \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\), which contains the target curve by the slope-range assumption. This proves uniform asymptotic coverage.

On both success events,
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\operatorname{length}\{\mathcal B_n(v)\}
 \le\frac{2\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_v\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{3}
\]
On failure its width is \(2C_\tau\). The expected critical-value bound and the \(o(r_n)\) fallback probability imply
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 W_n(\mathcal B,P,\bar g_n)
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta+o(r_n). \tag{4}
\]
For \(h_n=c_h(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\) and \(n_2/n\to1/2\),
\[
 \sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}\asymp r_n,
 \qquad h_n^\beta=c_h^\beta r_n. \tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) prove the width bound. The construction uses only the observed sample and the fixed supplied code, contains no estimate of \(q_0\), and permits every fixed \(\varepsilon_0\in(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{app}},\varepsilon_\star]\). \(\square\)
