For sufficiently small fixed \(\gamma>0\), every law in \(\text{def:many-bump-family}\) belongs to \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{law}}\), and every pair with \(\bar g_n=0\) belongs to \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\). The \(m+1\) laws have pairwise sup-norm separation at least \(c r_n\), and each active-bump law has Kullback--Leibler divergence from the zero-bump law at most \(c_0\log m\) for a sufficiently small \(c_0>0\).
