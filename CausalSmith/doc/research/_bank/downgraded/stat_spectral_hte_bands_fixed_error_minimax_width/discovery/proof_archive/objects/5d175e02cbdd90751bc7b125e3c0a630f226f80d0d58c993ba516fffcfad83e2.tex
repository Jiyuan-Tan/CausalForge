\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \((P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\).  Since both \(g_0\) and every member of \(\mathcal G\), hence \(\bar g_n\), take values in \([-C_g,C_g]\),
\[
 |D_n|\le2C_g. \tag{1}
\]
For \(|z|\le R_0\), the identity
\[
 e^{zD_n}-1=zD_n\int_0^1e^{tzD_n}\,dt
\]
and (1) imply
\[
 |e^{zD_n}-1|\le A_D|D_n|,
 \qquad A_D:=R_0e^{2C_gR_0}. \tag{2}
\]
Code admissibility is precisely
\[
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{u\in[0,1]}E_P(|D_n|\mid V=u)\le\varepsilon_0. \tag{3}
\]
Taking expectations in (2), using (3), and using \(A_D\varepsilon_0\le1/4\), which follows from \(\varepsilon_0\le\varepsilon_\star\), gives
\[
 |E_Pe^{zD_n}-1|\le\frac14,
 \qquad |E_Pe^{zD_n}|\ge\frac34\ge\frac12. \tag{4}
\]

Put \(w_{v,h}=h^{-1}K((V-v)/h)\), and require
\[
 0<h<\operatorname{dist}(\mathcal I,\{0,1\}). \tag{5}
\]
Nonnegativity and normalization of \(K\), followed by the density bounds, yield
\[
 c_V\le E_Pw_{v,h}\le C_V. \tag{6}
\]
Conditioning (2) on \(V\) and applying (3) shows
\[
 \left|E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]-E_Pw_{v,h}\right|
 \le A_D\varepsilon_0E_Pw_{v,h}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 |E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]|
 \ge\frac34E_Pw_{v,h}\ge\frac{3c_V}{4}\ge\frac{c_V}{2}, \tag{7}
\]
uniformly in \(v\in\mathcal I\) and \(|z|\le R_0\).

Finally, \(D_n\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V)\), whereas \(\eta\perp(X,V)\).  Thus
\[
 E_Pe^{zZ_n}=M(z)E_Pe^{zD_n},
 \qquad
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]. \tag{8}
\]
On a noise-certified contour, \(|M(z)|\ge\rho_M\).  Equations (4), (7), and (8) give the stronger inequalities
\[
 |E_Pe^{zZ_n}|\ge\frac{3\rho_M}{4},
 \qquad
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_\star}|F_{v,h}(z)|
 \ge\frac{3\rho_Mc_V}{4}, \tag{9}
\]
which imply all displayed one-half bounds in the statement.  Every division and localization is protected by (4), (5), and (7). \(\square\)
