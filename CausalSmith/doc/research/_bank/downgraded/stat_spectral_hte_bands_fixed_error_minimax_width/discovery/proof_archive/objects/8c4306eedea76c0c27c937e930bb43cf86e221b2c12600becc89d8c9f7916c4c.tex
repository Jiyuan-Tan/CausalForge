\textbf{Proof.}
For the fixed compatible exact-code triple, put
\[
 \Delta_k=|\kappa_k(a_k(B-p_k))|. \tag{1}
\]
Compatibility and the exact-code calculation give \(\Delta_k\ge\delta>0\).  Since \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), there is \(n_0\) such that \(\delta_n\le\Delta_k\) for every \(n\ge n_0\).  Hence every exact-code many-bump law used in the fixed-separation converse belongs, from that index onward, to \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\): every unchanged law and code restriction holds, while the new cumulant inequality follows from (1).

Fix \(\mathcal A\in\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\).  Honesty over the enlarged class implies honesty on this embedded many-bump family.  Repeating the explicit decoder and entropy calculation in \(\text{thm:minimax-all-band-width}\), with the same separation \(c_dr_n\) and divergence bound \(c_0\log m_n\), gives
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)\ge c. \tag{2}
\]

For achievability, the unchanged slope-range condition gives
\(\tau_0(v)\in[-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) for every \(v\).  The deterministic measurable band
\[
 \mathcal A_n(v)=[-C_\tau,C_\tau] \tag{3}
\]
therefore has coverage one over \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\), including all boundary cases, and
\[
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)=2C_\tau. \tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) establish the stated honest bracket.  At the fixed endpoint \(\delta_n\equiv\delta\), the upper theorem gives \(O(r_n)\) and the all-band theorem gives \(\Omega(r_n)\), so the minimax order closes.  When \(\delta_n\to0\), the frozen primitives give no uniform control of the resulting contour radius or boundary modulus, and the present argument consequently yields no sharper dependence on \(\delta_n\). \(\square\)
