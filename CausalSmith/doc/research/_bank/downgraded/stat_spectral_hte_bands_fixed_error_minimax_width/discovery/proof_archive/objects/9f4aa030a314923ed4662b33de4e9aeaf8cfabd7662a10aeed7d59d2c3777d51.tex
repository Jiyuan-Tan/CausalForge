\textbf{Proof.}
For a fixed pair write
\[
 H(z)=E_Pe^{zZ_n},\qquad F_v(z)=F_{v,h_n}(z). \tag{1}
\]
Because \(|D_n|\le2C_g\) and \(\eta\) is uniformly sub-Gaussian, every fixed moment of
\[
 (1+|Z_n|)e^{R_0|Z_n|} \tag{2}
\]
is bounded uniformly over the pair class. The bank is finite. For its contours and the one-dimensional translation class \(v\mapsto h_n^{-1}K((V-v)/h_n)\), truncation at \(|\eta|\le A\sqrt{\log n}\), polynomial grids, and the Lipschitz property of \(K\) give, for every fixed \(D>0\),
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P(\mathcal U_n^c)=O(n^{-D}), \tag{3}
\]
where \(\mathcal U_n\) is the event
\[
 \max_j\sup_{z\in C_j}|\widehat H_{1j}(z)-H(z)|\le\rho_M/8,
 \qquad
 \max_j\sup_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_j}
 |\widehat F_{1j,v}(z)-F_v(z)|\le\rho_Mc_V/8. \tag{4}
\]
Indeed, the centered \(q\)-th moments of the unlocalized averages are \(O(n_1^{-q/2}+n_1^{1-q})\), while those of the localized averages are \(O((n_1h_n)^{-q/2}+(n_1h_n)^{1-q})\), up to logarithmic truncation factors. Since \(n_1h_n\) diverges at a polynomial rate, choosing \(q\) and then the polynomial grid orders proves (3); the grid oscillations are bounded by the displayed Lipschitz constants. The same argument applies to the complex derivatives in \(z\).

On the certified contour \(C_\star\), the stronger bounds in the robustness proof are
\[
 \inf_{C_\star}|H|\ge3\rho_M/4,\qquad
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_\star}|F_v(z)|
 \ge3\rho_Mc_V/4. \tag{5}
\]
Thus on \(\mathcal U_n\) this contour passes both empirical thresholds.

Moreover, (4) in the robustness proof holds throughout the disk bounded by every bank circle. The analytic function \(A(z)=E_Pe^{zD_n}\) is therefore within \(1/4\) of one throughout those disks and is zero-free there. Since \(H=MA\),
\[
 \operatorname{wind}(H,C_\star)
 =\operatorname{wind}(M,C_\star)=m_\star\ne0. \tag{6}
\]
The boundary distance in (4) makes the straight-line homotopy between \(H\) and \(\widehat H_{1\star}\) zero-free on \(C_\star\). Hence its empirical winding is also \(m_\star\), so at least one bank index passes and \(\widehat j_n\ne\varnothing\).

Conversely, if \(j=\widehat j_n\) is selected on \(\mathcal U_n\), its empirical thresholds and (4) imply
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_j}|H(z)|\ge\rho_M/2,\qquad
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_j}|F_v(z)|
 \ge\rho_Mc_V/2. \tag{7}
\]
The same boundary homotopy gives
\[
 \widehat m_j=\operatorname{wind}(H,C_j)\ne0. \tag{8}
\]
Equations (3), (7), and (8) prove the uniform conclusion. \(\square\)
