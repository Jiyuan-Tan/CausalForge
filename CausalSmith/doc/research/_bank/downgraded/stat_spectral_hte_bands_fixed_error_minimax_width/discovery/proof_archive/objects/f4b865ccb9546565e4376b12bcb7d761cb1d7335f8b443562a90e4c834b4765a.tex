\textbf{Proof.}
Because \(\delta_n\downarrow0\) and the fixed-separation constant \(\delta\) is positive, there is \(N\) such that \(\delta_n\le\delta\) for every \(n\ge N\).  Therefore every pair in the fixed class \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) also belongs to \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\) for \(n\ge N\): all conditions other than cumulant separation are identical, and
\[
 |\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta\ge\delta_n. \tag{1}
\]
If \(\mathcal A\in\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\), honesty over the larger class and the inclusion just proved imply
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P\{\tau_0(v)\in\mathcal A_n(v;\bar g_n)\text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge1-\alpha. \tag{2}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal A\in\mathfrak H_\alpha\).  Applying \(\text{thm:minimax-all-band-width}\) and then enlarging the supremum gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)
 &\ge
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)\\
 &\ge c. \tag{3}
 \end{aligned}
\]
This proves the lower side without any contour-stability claim at separation \(\delta_n\).

For the upper side, define \(\mathcal A_n(v)=[-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) for every sample, code, and \(v\in\mathcal I\).  The slope-range condition gives simultaneous coverage probability one under every member of \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\), while
\[
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)=2C_\tau. \tag{4}
\]
Finally, when \(\delta_n\equiv\delta\), the enlarged class is the fixed class.  The lower bound (3) and the \(Cr_n\) upper bound supplied by \(\text{thm:honest-spectral-band}\) then match.  When \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), neither (3) nor (4) supplies a quantitative contour modulus as a function of \(\delta_n\); consequently these proved bounds do not determine a sharper phase transition or an adaptive shrinking-width construction. \(\square\)
