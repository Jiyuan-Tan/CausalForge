On the selector-success and inference-denominator-success events, conditionally on the pilot and uniformly over \((P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) and \(v\in\mathcal I\),
\[
 \widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta^{\widehat j_n}_{v,h_n}
 =n_2^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_2}\psi^{\widehat j_n}_{v,h_n}(O_i)
 +o_P\!\left((n_2h_n)^{-1/2}/\sqrt{\log n}\right).
\]
Conditionally on the pilot, the studentized absolute supremum of the leading process admits a uniform Gaussian approximation, its conditional Gaussian multiplier law consistently approximates that absolute supremum, and \(\widehat s_n\) is uniformly ratio-consistent and bounded above and away from zero.  In addition, the two fallback probabilities are \(o(r_n)\) uniformly and
\[
 E_P\!\left[\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v)\,; \text{both success events}\right]\le C\sqrt{\log n}.
