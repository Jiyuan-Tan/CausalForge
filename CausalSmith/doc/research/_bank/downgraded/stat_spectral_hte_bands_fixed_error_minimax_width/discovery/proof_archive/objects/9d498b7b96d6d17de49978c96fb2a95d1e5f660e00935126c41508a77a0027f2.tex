\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \((P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\).  The range restrictions on \(g_0\) and \(\bar g_n\) give \(|D_n|\le2C_g\).  For \(|z|\le R_0\), the identity
\[
 e^{zD_n}-1=zD_n\int_0^1e^{tzD_n}\,dt
\]
therefore implies
\[
 |e^{zD_n}-1|
 \le R_0e^{2C_gR_0}|D_n|. \tag{1}
\]
Put \(A_0=R_0e^{2C_gR_0}\).  Admissibility of the code says
\[
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{u\in[0,1]}E_P(|D_n|\mid V=u)\le\varepsilon_0. \tag{2}
\]
Taking expectations in (1), using (2), and using \(A_0\varepsilon_0\le1/4\), which follows from \(\varepsilon_0\le\varepsilon_\star\), gives
\[
 |E_Pe^{zD_n}-1|\le A_0\varepsilon_0\le\frac14,
 \qquad |E_Pe^{zD_n}|\ge\frac34\ge\frac12. \tag{3}
\]

For \(w_{v,h}=h^{-1}K((V-v)/h)\), choose \(h\) small enough that \([v-h,v+h]\subset[0,1]\) for all \(v\in\mathcal I\).  Nonnegativity and normalization of \(K\), together with the density bounds, yield
\[
 c_V\le E_Pw_{v,h}\le C_V. \tag{4}
\]
Conditioning (1) on \(V\) and applying (2) gives
\[
 \left|E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]-E_Pw_{v,h}\right|
 \le A_0E_P(w_{v,h}|D_n|)
 \le A_0\varepsilon_0E_Pw_{v,h}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \left|E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]\right|
 \ge\frac34E_Pw_{v,h}\ge\frac{3c_V}{4}\ge\frac{c_V}{2}, \tag{5}
\]
uniformly in \(v\) and throughout the disk \(|z|\le R_0\).

Finally, \(D_n\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V)\) and \(\eta\perp(X,V)\).  Hence, for every complex \(z\),
\[
 E_Pe^{zZ_n}=M(z)E_Pe^{zD_n},
 \qquad
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]. \tag{6}
\]
On a good contour, \(|M(z)|\ge\rho_M\).  Combining (3), (5), and (6) proves respectively
\[
 |E_Pe^{zZ_n}|\ge\rho_M/2,
 \qquad
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,\ z\in C_\star}|F_{v,h}(z)|\ge\rho_Mc_V/2.
\]
All inequalities were derived from the conditional direct-error bound and the displayed ordering of \(\varepsilon_0\), as asserted. \(\square\)
