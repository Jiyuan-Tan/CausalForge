\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
By \(\text{lem:modifier-shift-attainment-and-inclusion}\), membership in \(\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\) supplies an attained \(d\in\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\) such that, with \(R_n=D_n-d(V)\),
\[
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{u\in[0,1]}E_P(|R_n|\mid V=u)\le\varepsilon_0. \tag{1}
\]
The range restrictions give \(|D_n|\le2C_g\) and \(|d|\le2C_g\), hence \(|D_n-d(v)|\le4C_g\).  For \(|z|\le R_0\),
\[
 |e^{z\{D_n-d(v)\}}-1|
 \le R_0e^{4C_gR_0}|D_n-d(v)|=A_D|D_n-d(v)|. \tag{2}
\]
If \(h<\operatorname{dist}(\mathcal I,\{0,1\})\), kernel normalization, nonnegativity, and the density bounds imply
\[
 c_V\le\mu_{v,h}=E_Pw_{v,h}\le C_V. \tag{3}
\]
On the support of \(w_{v,h}\), \(|d(V)-d(v)|\le L_Dh^{\gamma_D}\).  Conditioning (2) on \(V\), then using (1), gives
\[
 \left|e^{-zd(v)}E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]-\mu_{v,h}\right|
 \le A_D\{L_Dh^{\gamma_D}+\varepsilon_0\}\mu_{v,h}. \tag{4}
\]
The corrected threshold is \(\varepsilon_\star=(8A_D)^{-1}\).  For all sufficiently small \(h\), \(A_DL_Dh^{\gamma_D}\le1/8\), so (4) is at most \(\mu_{v,h}/4\).

The straight-line homotopy from \(e^{-zd(v)}A_{v,h}(z)\) to \(\mu_{v,h}\) is zero-free throughout the bank disk.  Multiplication by \(e^{zd(v)}\) neither creates zeros nor winding.  Thus \(A_{v,h}\) is zero-free with winding zero, and
\[
 |A_{v,h}(z)|\ge e^{-2C_gR_0}\frac34\mu_{v,h}
 \ge\frac34c_Ve^{-2C_gR_0}. \tag{5}
\]
Independence of \(\eta\) from \((X,V)\) gives
\[
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)A_{v,h}(z). \tag{6}
\]
Combining (5) with the certified noise modulus proves the last display in the proposed statement and its equality to \(3\rho_F/2\).  No unlocalized transform was used. \(\square\)
