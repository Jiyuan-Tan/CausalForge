For every \((P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\), let \(d\in\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\) attain the infimum in the modifier-shift certificate for \(\bar g_n\), put \(D_n=g_0(X,V)-\bar g_n(X,V)\), \(w_{v,h}=h^{-1}K((V-v)/h)\), and \(\mu_{v,h}=E_Pw_{v,h}\). With \(A_D=R_0e^{4C_gR_0}\), for every \(|z|\le R_0\), \(v\in\mathcal I\), and sufficiently small \(h>0\),
\[
 \left|e^{-zd(v)}E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]-\mu_{v,h}\right|
 \le A_D\{L_Dh^{\gamma_D}+\varepsilon_0\}\mu_{v,h}

\]

 |A_{v,h}(z)|\ge\frac34c_Ve^{-2C_gR_0}.
\]
On the noise-certified contour \(C_\star\),
\[
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_\star}|F_{v,h}(z)|
 \ge\frac34\rho_Mc_Ve^{-2C_gR_0}=\frac32\rho_F.
\]
