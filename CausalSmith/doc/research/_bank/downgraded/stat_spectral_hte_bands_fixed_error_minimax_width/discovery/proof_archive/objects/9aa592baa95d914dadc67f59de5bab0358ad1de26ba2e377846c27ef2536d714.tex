\textbf{Proof.}
Because \(\delta_n\downarrow0\) and the fixed core constant \(\delta\) is positive, there is \(n_0\) such that \(\delta_n\le\delta\) for every \(n\ge n_0\).  Hence every law satisfying the fixed separation condition \(|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta\) also satisfies the weak condition \(|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta_n\), while all other law and code conditions are identical.  Therefore
\[
 \mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\subseteq\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n,
 \qquad n\ge n_0. \tag{1}
\]
If \(\mathcal A\in\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\), taking the infimum of its coverage probability over the smaller class in (1) can only increase that infimum.  Thus \(\mathcal A\in\mathfrak H_\alpha\).  Applying \(\text{thm:minimax-all-band-width}\), and then enlarging the supremum using (1), yields
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)
 \ge
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)
 \ge c. \tag{2}
\]

Every weak-class law retains \(\|\tau_0\|_\infty\le C_\tau\).  Consequently the deterministic band \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) covers the entire target curve with probability one under every weak-class pair, and
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)=2C_\tau. \tag{3}
\]
Equations (2)--(3) prove the bracket.  In a regime with a fixed positive lower separation, the two fixed-separation theorems give the matching lower and upper orders \(r_n\).  The final sentence of the statement is a scope qualification: neither (1)--(3) nor the fixed-separation contour constants quantify their deterioration as \(\delta_n\) vanishes. \(\square\)
