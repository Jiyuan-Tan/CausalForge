Form the natural triangular weak-separation enlargement \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\) by retaining every member condition and every external-code restriction in \(\text{def:spectral-law-class}\) and \(\text{def:experiment-pair-class}\), except that \(|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta\) is replaced at sample size \(n\) by \(|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta_n\), where \(\delta_n\downarrow0\).  Let \(\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\) be the measurable band sequences satisfying the displayed uniform asymptotic honesty condition with \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\) in place of \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\).  Then there is \(c>0\) such that every \(\mathcal A\in\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\) satisfies
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)\ge c.
\]
The deterministic band \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) is honest over this enlargement and has expected sup-width \(2C_\tau\) for every \(n\).  Hence the conclusion derivable from the frozen primitive conditions in the vanishing-separation regime is the honest bracket
\[
 c\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}
 \quad\text{as an all-band asymptotic lower order},
 \qquad
 2C_\tau\quad\text{as a uniform achievable upper bound}.
\]
At the fixed endpoint \(\delta_n\equiv\delta\), the two established fixed-separation theorems close the bracket at order \(r_n\).  For \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), the sharp dependence on \(\delta_n\), the threshold for shrinking width, and adaptation across the weak and Gaussian regimes remain unresolved by these bounds.
