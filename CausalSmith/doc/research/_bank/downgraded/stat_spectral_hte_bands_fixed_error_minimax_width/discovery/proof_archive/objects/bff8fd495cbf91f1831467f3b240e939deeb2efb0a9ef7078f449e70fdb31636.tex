\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\psi=\psi_\eta\). The exact Luxemburg convention and centering give
\[
 E_P\exp(\eta^2/\psi^2)\le2,
 \qquad E_P\eta=0.
\]
Symmetrization for \(|t|\psi\le1\), using an independent copy \(\eta'\), \(\cosh u\le e^{u^2/2}\), and Lyapunov's inequality, and Young's inequality for \(|t|\psi>1\), yield
\[
 E_Pe^{t\eta}\le e^{4\psi^2t^2},\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
\]
Consequently
\[
 |M(z)|\le\exp(4\psi^2|z|^2),\qquad z\in\mathbb C. \tag{1}
\]
The same compact-disk envelope justifies differentiation under the expectation, so \(M\) is entire.

Suppose \(M\) were zero-free on the closed disk of radius \(R_{\mathrm z}\). Its analytic logarithm \(f=\log M\), normalized by \(f(0)=0\), satisfies \(f'(0)=0\) and \(\sup_{|z|\le R}\operatorname{Re}f(z)\le4\psi^2R^2\) for \(R<R_{\mathrm z}\). The Carath\'eodory coefficient estimate applied to \(4\psi^2R^2-f\) gives
\[
 |f^{(k)}(0)|\le8k!\psi^2R^{2-k}. \tag{2}
\]
Letting \(R\uparrow R_{\mathrm z}\) contradicts \(|f^{(k)}(0)|=|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta\), because the displayed definition of \(R_{\mathrm z}\) makes the right side of (2) at most \(2^{-k-1}\delta<\delta\). Thus \(M\) has a zero in \(|z|\le R_{\mathrm z}\). This is the noise-level transfer from \(\text{stat\_sa\_plm\_cumulant\_converse/lem:finite-contour-bank}\), re-derived here under the same Luxemburg convention.

Put \(R_{\mathrm{fac}}=R_++1\) and \(R_{\mathrm{out}}=R_++2\). Jensen's formula and (1) show that the number \(N\) of zeros in \(|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\), with multiplicity, satisfies
\[
 N\log(R_{\mathrm{out}}/R_{\mathrm{fac}})
 \le4\psi^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^2.
\]
Since \(\log(1+x)\ge x/(1+x)\),
\[
 N\le4\psi^2R_{\mathrm{out}}^3
 \le4U_\psi^2(U_R+2)^3=N_{\mathrm{cert}}. \tag{3}
\]
The grid has more than \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) radii and spacing \(2^{-m_\circ}\). A zero modulus can lie within \(2^{-m_\circ}/3\) of at most one grid radius. Hence some \(C_\star\) is separated from every zero in \(|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) by that radial distance. It strictly contains the zero localized above and has no boundary zero, so the argument principle gives \(m_\star\in\mathbb N\setminus\{0\}\).

Factor the zeros in \(|z|<R_{\mathrm{fac}}\) as the disk Blaschke product \(Q\) and write \(M=Qg\), with \(g\) zero-free. The maximum principle, \(M(0)=1\), and Harnack's inequality give on \(|z|\le R_+\)
\[
 |g(z)|\ge2^{-2e_\circ}. \tag{4}
\]
On \(C_\star\), each Blaschke factor is at least
\[
 2^{-(m_\circ+d_\circ^{\mathrm{den}})}.
\]
There are at most \(N_{\mathrm{cert}}\) factors, so
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_\star}|M(z)|
 \ge2^{-2e_\circ-N_{\mathrm{cert}}(m_\circ+d_\circ^{\mathrm{den}})}
 =\rho_M. \tag{5}
\]
Finally, (1) and Cauchy's estimate on unit circles give \(\sup_{|z|\le R_0}|M'(z)|\le A_M\). The definition of \(\Lambda_0\), (5), and \(\operatorname{len}(C_\star)\le2\pi R_0\) give the displayed contour envelope. Every construction and bound depends only on \((k,\delta,\psi_\eta)\), and \(R_{\mathrm z}\ne R_0\) keeps zero localization distinct from the maximal bank radius. \(\square\)
