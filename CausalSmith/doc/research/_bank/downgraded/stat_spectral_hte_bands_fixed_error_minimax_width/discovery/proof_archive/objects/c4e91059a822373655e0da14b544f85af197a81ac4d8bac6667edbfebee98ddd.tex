\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
Fix a pair and abbreviate
\[
 F_v(z)=F_{v,h_n}(z),\qquad
 \widehat F_{1j,v}(z)=n_1^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_1}
 h_n^{-1}K((V_i-v)/h_n)e^{zZ_{n,i}}. \tag{1}
\]
Because \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), treatment sub-Gaussianity implies uniformly bounded moments of every fixed order for
\[
 (1+|\eta|+2C_g)e^{(R_0+1)(|\eta|+2C_g)}. \tag{2}
\]
The kernel is bounded and Lipschitz, the bank is finite, and its circles are piecewise smooth. Truncate at \(\max_{i\in I_1}|\eta_i|\le A\sqrt{\log n}\), cover \(\mathcal I\) and each contour by polynomial grids, apply the iid moment inequality
\[
 E|(P_{n_1}-P)f|^q
 \le C_q\left\{(Pf^2/n_1)^{q/2}
 +P|f|^q/n_1^{q-1}\right\}, \tag{3}
\]
and control grid oscillations by the Lipschitz constants. Since \(n_1h_n\) diverges polynomially up to logarithms, \(q\) can be taken arbitrarily large. Hence, for every fixed \(D>0\),
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P(\mathcal U_n^c)=O(n^{-D}), \tag{4}
\]
where
\[
 \mathcal U_n=
 \left\{\max_j\sup_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_j}
 |\widehat F_{1j,v}(z)-F_v(z)|
 \le\rho_Mc_V/8\right\}. \tag{5}
\]
The same grid argument controls the \(z\)-derivative at \(v_0\), although winding equality below only needs the boundary homotopy.

For the noise-certified circle \(C_\star\), the robustness lemma gives
\[
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_\star}|F_v(z)|
 \ge3\rho_Mc_V/4. \tag{6}
\]
On \(\mathcal U_n\), its empirical modulus is at least \(5\rho_Mc_V/8\). Moreover \(F_{v_0}=MA_{v_0,h_n}\); the second factor is zero-free with winding zero on the whole search disk. Thus
\[
 \operatorname{wind}(F_{v_0},C_\star)
 =\operatorname{wind}(M,C_\star)=m_\star\ne0. \tag{7}
\]
The straight-line homotopy between \(\widehat F_{1j,v_0}\) and \(F_{v_0}\) is boundary-zero-free on \(C_\star\), so the empirical winding also equals \(m_\star\). Consequently at least one index passes and \(\widehat j_n\ne\varnothing\).

Conversely, if \(j=\widehat j_n\) is selected on \(\mathcal U_n\), the test threshold and (5) imply
\[
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_j}|F_v(z)|
 \ge\rho_Mc_V/2. \tag{8}
\]
The same homotopy gives \(\widehat m_j=\operatorname{wind}(F_{v_0},C_j)\). The localized factorization and zero-free shift factor then yield
\[
 \widehat m_j=\operatorname{wind}(M,C_j)=m_j\ne0. \tag{9}
\]
Equations (4), (8), and (9) prove the claim, including its stronger polynomial failure bound. \(\square\)
