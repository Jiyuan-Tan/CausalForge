\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\Delta_k=|\kappa_k(a_k(B-p_k))|\). By hard-subclass compatibility and the exact-code reduction, \(\Delta_k\ge\delta>0\). Since \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), there is \(n_0\) such that \(\delta_n\le\Delta_k\) for every \(n\ge n_0\). Hence every exact-code many-bump law used in the fixed-separation converse belongs, for all \(n\ge n_0\), to the triangular class \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\): all unchanged member and external-code conditions hold with shift witness \(d=0\) and remainder zero, while the weakened cumulant inequality holds by \(\Delta_k\ge\delta_n\).

Fix \(\mathcal A\in\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\). Uniform honesty over \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\) implies honesty on this many-bump subclass. Repeating equations (1)--(6) of \(\text{thm:minimax-all-band-width}\), with the same separation \(c_dr_n\) and information bound \(c_0\log m_n\), yields
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)\ge c. \tag{1}
\]
This proves the lower side without any assumption on the rate at which \(\delta_n\) vanishes.

For the upper side, the slope-range condition gives \(\tau_0(v)\in[-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) for every \(v\). Thus the deterministic band \(\mathcal A_n(v)=[-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) has coverage one uniformly over \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\) and
\[
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)=2C_\tau. \tag{2}
\]
Equations (1)--(2) give the displayed bracket. If \(\delta_n\equiv\delta\), the fixed-separation upper theorem supplies width \(O(r_n)\), while the same lower bound supplies \(\Omega(r_n)\); hence the bracket closes at that endpoint. Nothing in (1)--(2) controls the deterioration of the contour radius or boundary modulus as \(\delta_n\to0\), so no sharper \(\delta_n\)-dependent conclusion follows from these arguments. \(\square\)
