\textbf{Proof.}
On \(\mathcal E_{1n}\cap\mathcal E_{2n}\), \(\text{lem:uniform-bahadur-multiplier}\) gives a uniform Bahadur expansion whose normalized remainder is \(o_P(1/\sqrt{\log n})\), a ratio-consistent standard error, and empirical and conditional multiplier absolute-supremum laws coupled to the same unit-variance Gaussian absolute supremum. The coupling failure probabilities vanish uniformly, and the coupling radii multiplied by the Gaussian expected supremum plus one converge to zero. The anti-concentration conclusion in the corrected cited interface therefore transfers the conditional multiplier quantile to the studentized leading empirical process. Consequently,
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P\left\{
 |\widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta_{v,h_n}|
 \le\frac{\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I
 \right\}\ge1-\alpha. \tag{1}
\]
The center, score, standard error, and multiplier process in (1) use the same pilot-selected contour and the same nonzero treatment-noise winding. By \(\text{lem:localized-contour-bias}\),
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta_{v,h_n}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{2}
\]
The triangle inequality combines (1) and (2) to give simultaneous coverage on the two success events. On either actual failure event, \(\text{def:spectral-band}\) returns \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\), which contains the entire target curve by the slope-range restriction. The two failure probabilities are \(o(r_n)=o(1)\), uniformly, so the displayed uniform asymptotic honesty conclusion follows.

On success, the supremum width is at most
\[
 \frac{2\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{3}
\]
On failure it is exactly \(2C_\tau\). The critical-value moment bound and the uniform \(o(r_n)\) fallback probability therefore imply
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 W_n(\mathcal B,P,\bar g_n)
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta+o(r_n). \tag{4}
\]
For \(h_n=c_h(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\) and \(n_2\asymp n\), both leading terms in (4) have order
\[
 r_n=\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}. \tag{5}
\]
The estimator and band are the total measurable sample maps in \(\text{def:spectral-band}\). They use the fixed admissible external treatment code, never estimate \(q_0\), and remain valid for each fixed \(\varepsilon_0\in(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{app}},\varepsilon_\star]\). Equations (1)--(5) prove the theorem. \(\square\)
