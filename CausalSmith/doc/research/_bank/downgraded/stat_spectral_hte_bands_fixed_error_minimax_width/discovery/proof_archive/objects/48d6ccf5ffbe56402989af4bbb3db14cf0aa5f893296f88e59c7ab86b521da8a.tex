Let
\[
 \mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}
 =\left\{d:[0,1]\to[-2C_g,2C_g]:d\text{ is measurable and }
 \sup_{u\ne v}\frac{|d(u)-d(v)|}{|u-v|^{\gamma_D}}\le L_D\right\}.
\]
Define the modifier-shift external-input ball by
\[
 \Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)
 =\left\{g\in\mathcal G:\ \exists d\in\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\text{ such that }
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{v\in[0,1]}
 E_P[|g_0(X,V)-g(X,V)-d(V)|\mid V=v]
 \le\varepsilon_0\right\}.
\]
Here \(\varepsilon_0\in(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{app}},\varepsilon_\star]\) is fixed after the law-blind bank and before the inference sample.  The former direct conditional-\(L^1\) ball is included by taking \(d=0\); the enlarged ball also permits a persistent bounded smooth modifier-only displacement.
