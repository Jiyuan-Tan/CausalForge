\textbf{Proof.}
Condition on the pilot. By the selector argument, outside an event of probability smaller than any prescribed polynomial order, \(j=\widehat j_n\) is fixed and
\[
 \inf_{v,z\in C_j}|F_{v,h_n}(z)|\ge\rho_Mc_V/2,
 \qquad \widehat m_j=m_j\ne0. \tag{1}
\]
The second equality follows from the boundary homotopy between the empirical contaminated transform and \(H=M E_Pe^{zD_n}\), whose second factor is zero-free.

The range conditions and the structural identity imply
\[
 |Y|\le C_q+C_\tau|\eta|+|\xi|,
 \qquad |Z_n|\le|\eta|+2C_g. \tag{2}
\]
Hence treatment sub-Gaussianity and conditional outcome sub-Gaussianity give, for every fixed \(s<\infty\),
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 E_P[(1+|Y|+|Z_n|)^s e^{sR_0|Z_n|}]<\infty. \tag{3}
\]
The finite contour bank and translations of the bounded Lipschitz kernel have logarithmic covering numbers. The truncation, grid, and moment calculation used in the selector proof therefore gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_j}
 \{ |\widehat F_{2j,v}(z)-F_{v,h_n}(z)|
   +|\widehat G_{2j,v}(z)-G_{v,h_n}(z)|\}
 =O_P\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}\right) \tag{4}
\]
uniformly over the pair class and all possible pilot choices. Every fixed-modulus failure in (4) has probability \(O(n^{-D})\) for arbitrarily large fixed \(D\).

Write \(\Delta_F=\widehat F-F\) and \(\Delta_G=\widehat G-G\). On inference-denominator success, the exact ratio expansion is
\[
 \frac{G+\Delta_G}{F+\Delta_F}-\frac GF
 =\frac{F\Delta_G-G\Delta_F}{F^2}+R,
 \qquad |R|\le C(|\Delta_F\Delta_G|+|\Delta_F|^2). \tag{5}
\]
After contour integration and normalization by the common winding in (1), the linear term equals
\[
 n_2^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_2}\psi^j_{v,h_n}(O_i). \tag{6}
\]
It is centered because it is the derivative at the population law. Equations (4)--(5) bound the uniform remainder by
\[
 O_P\!\left(\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}\right)
 =o_P\!\left((n_2h_n)^{-1/2}/\sqrt{\log n}\right), \tag{7}
\]
because \(n_2h_n/(\log n)^3\to\infty\). This proves the asserted Bahadur representation.

We next derive variance nondegeneracy. The coefficient of \(Y\), hence of \(\xi\), in the score is
\[
 b_{v,j}(O)=\operatorname{Re}\left\{
 \frac{w_{v,h_n}}{2\pi i m_j}
 \oint_{C_j}\frac{e^{zZ_n}}{F_{v,h_n}(z)}\,dz\right\}. \tag{8}
\]
It vanishes unless \(|V-v|\le h_n\). Independence and the factorizations in the bias proof give
\[
 E_P[\eta b_{v,j}(O)]
 =\operatorname{Re}\left\{\frac{1}{2\pi i m_j}
 \oint_{C_j}\frac{M'(z)}{M(z)}\,dz\right\}=1. \tag{9}
\]
Since \(\eta\perp V\), \(E_P\eta^2\le C\psi_\eta^2\), and \(P(|V-v|\le h_n)\le2C_Vh_n\), Cauchy--Schwarz in (9) yields
\[
 E_Pb_{v,j}^2\ge c/h_n. \tag{10}
\]
The rest of the score is measurable with respect to \((X,V,T)\), whereas \(E_P(\xi\mid X,V,T)=0\). Conditional variance nondegeneracy, (3), the safe denominator, and \(E_Pw_{v,h_n}^2\le C/h_n\) consequently give
\[
 0<c\le h_n\operatorname{Var}_P(\psi^j_{v,h_n})
 \le h_nE_P[(\psi^j_{v,h_n})^2]\le C. \tag{11}
\]

Apply \(\text{lem:cck-empirical-multiplier-coupling}\) to the symmetric class of plus and minus the centered functions
\[
 \sqrt{h_n}\psi^j_{v,h_n}/s_j(v),
 \qquad s_j(v)^2=h_n\operatorname{Var}_P(\psi^j_{v,h_n}). \tag{12}
\]
After truncation its envelope is \(h_n^{-1/2}n^{o(1)}\), its variance is bounded by one, and its entropy logarithm is \(O(\log n)\). Because \(n_2h_n\) diverges polynomially, the cited empirical and multiplier coupling errors vanish uniformly. The cited anti-concentration bound and the normalized \(o_P(1/\sqrt{\log n})\) remainder in (7) transfer conditional multiplier quantiles to the studentized absolute supremum.

The same moment calculation applied to squared scores, using (11), gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\widehat s_n(v)/s_j(v)-1|=o_P(1),
 \qquad 0<c\le\inf_vs_j(v)\le\sup_vs_j(v)\le C. \tag{13}
\]
Equation (4) and the ratio identity show that replacing population scores by plug-in scores changes their normalized empirical \(L^2\) distance by \(o_P(1/\sqrt{\log n})\). Thus the same coupling applies to the implemented multiplier process.

Let \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) collect the pilot concentration and winding events, and let \(\mathcal E_{2n}\) collect inference-denominator success, (4), (11), (13), and the plug-in comparison event. Taking the fixed moment orders large enough gives
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P(\mathcal E_{1n}^c\cup\mathcal E_{2n}^c)=o(r_n). \tag{14}
\]
The two actual fallback events are contained in this complement. Finally, the Gaussian maximal inequality for the class in (12), followed by Gaussian concentration at fixed \(\alpha\), gives
\[
 E_P\left[\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v);
 \text{both actual success events}\right]\le C\sqrt{\log n}. \tag{15}
\]
Equations (1), (7), and (11)--(15) prove every assertion. \(\square\)
