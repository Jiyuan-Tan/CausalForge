\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \((P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\). Membership of the external code gives
\[
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{u\in[0,1]}
 E_P(|D_n|\mid V=u)\le\varepsilon_0,\qquad |D_n|\le2C_g. \tag{1}
\]
For \(|z|\le R_0\), the fundamental theorem of calculus along the line segment from \(0\) to \(zD_n\) gives
\[
 |e^{zD_n}-1|
 \le |z|\,|D_n|e^{|z|\,|D_n|}
 \le R_0e^{2C_gR_0}|D_n|. \tag{2}
\]
Write \(A_0=R_0e^{2C_gR_0}\). By the definition of \(\varepsilon_\star\) and the radius ordering,
\[
 A_0\varepsilon_0\le A_0\varepsilon_\star
 =\frac14\min\{1,c_V/C_V\}\le\frac14. \tag{3}
\]
Taking expectations in (2), using (1)--(3), yields
\[
 |E_Pe^{zD_n}-1|\le A_0\varepsilon_0\le1/4,
 \qquad |E_Pe^{zD_n}|\ge3/4. \tag{4}
\]

Let \(w_{v,h}=h^{-1}K((V-v)/h)\). Because \(\mathcal I\) is an interior compact interval, for all \(h<\operatorname{dist}(\mathcal I,\{0,1\})\), kernel normalization, nonnegativity, and the density bounds imply
\[
 c_V\le\mu_{v,h}:=E_Pw_{v,h}\le C_V
 \quad\text{uniformly in }v\in\mathcal I. \tag{5}
\]
Conditioning on \(V\) in (1) and using (2)--(3),
\[
 \left|E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]-\mu_{v,h}\right|
 \le A_0E_P(w_{v,h}|D_n|)
 \le A_0\varepsilon_0\mu_{v,h}
 \le\mu_{v,h}/4. \tag{6}
\]
Thus
\[
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,\,|z|\le R_0}
 |E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]|
 \ge3c_V/4. \tag{7}
\]

The variable \(D_n\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V)\), while \(\eta\perp(X,V)\). Hence
\[
 E_Pe^{zZ_n}=M(z)E_Pe^{zD_n},
 \qquad
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]. \tag{8}
\]
On the contour supplied by the noise-only bank, (4), (7), and \(\inf_{C_\star}|M|\ge\rho_M\) give
\[
 \inf_{z\in C_\star}|E_Pe^{zZ_n}|
 \ge3\rho_M/4\ge\rho_M/2,
 \quad
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_\star}|F_{v,h}(z)|
 \ge3\rho_Mc_V/4\ge\rho_Mc_V/2. \tag{9}
\]
Equations (2), (4), (7), and (9) prove all asserted inequalities. \(\square\)
