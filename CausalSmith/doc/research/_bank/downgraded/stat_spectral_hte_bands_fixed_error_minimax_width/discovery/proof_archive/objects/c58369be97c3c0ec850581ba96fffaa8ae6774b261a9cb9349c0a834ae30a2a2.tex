On reference-localized selector success and inference-denominator success,
\[
 \widehat\tau_n(v)=\operatorname{Re}\left\{
 (2\pi i\widehat m_{\widehat j_n})^{-1}
 \oint_{C_{\widehat j_n}}
 \frac{\widehat G_{2\widehat j_n,v}(z)}
 {\widehat F_{2\widehat j_n,v}(z)}\,dz\right\},
\]
where \(\widehat j_n\) and \(\widehat m_{\widehat j_n}\) are selected from the pilot reference-localized transform at \(v_{\mathrm{ref}}\). The half-width is \(\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)/\sqrt{n_2h_n}+B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta\), with \(h_n\asymp(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\); on either selector or inference-denominator failure the total procedure returns \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\). Its expected sup-width is \(\asymp(\log n/n)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}\).
