For each \(n\), let \(\widetilde{\mathcal P}_n\) be the class obtained from \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{law}}\) by retaining every member property in \(\text{def:spectral-law-class}\) except that \(\text{ass:cumulant-separation}\) is replaced by \(|\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta_n\), and let \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n=\{(P,\bar g_n):P\in\widetilde{\mathcal P}_n,\ \bar g_n\in\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\}\).  Let \(\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\) denote the measurable band sequences satisfying the analogue of \(\text{ass:asymptotic-band-honesty}\) over \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\).  Then there is a constant \(c>0\) such that every \(\mathcal A\in\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\) satisfies
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)\ge c.
\]
The deterministic full-range band \(\mathcal A_n(v)=[-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) belongs to \(\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\) and has worst-case expected sup-width exactly \(2C_\tau\).  Thus, under merely \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), the rigorously justified weak-separation frontier is bracketed between the fixed-separation H\"older rate \(r_n=(\log n/n)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}\) and a constant.  When separation is bounded below by a fixed positive constant, \(\text{thm:honest-spectral-band}\) and \(\text{thm:minimax-all-band-width}\) close the bracket at order \(r_n\).  No sharper joint dependence on \(\delta_n\), impossibility threshold for shrinking width, or adaptive fixed-to-Gaussian procedure follows from the frozen fixed-separation results.
