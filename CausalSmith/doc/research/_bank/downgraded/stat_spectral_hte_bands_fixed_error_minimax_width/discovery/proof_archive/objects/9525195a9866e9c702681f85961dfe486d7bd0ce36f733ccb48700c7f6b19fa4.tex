\textbf{Proof.}
If \(g\in\Gamma_{\mathrm{dir}}(P,\varepsilon_0)\), choose the modifier-shift witness \(d\equiv0\). It belongs to \(\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\), and the residual condition in the corrected external-input ball becomes exactly \(d_P(g,g_0)\le\varepsilon_0\). Hence \(g\in\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\). Taking the infimum over \(g\) proves \(a_{\mathcal G}(P)\le a_{\mathrm{dir}}(P)\).

For strictness under the displayed compatible ordering, take \(g_0=0\), \(g_a(x,v)=-a\), and the constant witness \(d(v)=a\). Because \(a\le C_g\), this witness takes values in \([-2C_g,2C_g]\); its H\"older seminorm is zero, so it lies in \(\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\). The shifted residual is identically zero, proving \(g_a\in\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\). On the other hand,
\[
 d_P(g_a,g_0)=a>\varepsilon_0,
\]
so \(g_a\notin\Gamma_{\mathrm{dir}}(P,\varepsilon_0)\). Thus strict enlargement is available under the explicit candidate-class and fixed-constant compatibility just stated, while only inclusion is claimed without it. \(\square\)
