For a pointwise-measurable VC-type class with bounded envelope and a uniform variance bound, the supremum of the centered iid empirical process and the conditional supremum of its centered Gaussian multiplier process admit the covariance-matched Gaussian couplings stated in Appendix A, Theorems 5.1 and 5.2, of Chernozhukov--Chetverikov--Kato; their Corollary 2.1 supplies anti-concentration for the absolute Gaussian supremum.  In any triangular sequence for which the displayed coupling errors vanish, the empirical and conditional multiplier absolute-supremum laws are therefore asymptotically equivalent, uniformly over the underlying laws satisfying those bounds.
