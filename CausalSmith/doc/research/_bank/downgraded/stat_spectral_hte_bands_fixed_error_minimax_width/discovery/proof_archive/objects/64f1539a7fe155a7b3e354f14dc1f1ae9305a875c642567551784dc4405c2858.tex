For every \((P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\), every \(z\) with \(|z|\le R_0\), and \(D_n=g_0(X,V)-\bar g_n(X,V)\), \(|e^{zD_n}-1|\le R_0e^{2C_gR_0}|D_n|\). These bounds are outputs of the essential-supremum conditional-\(L^1\) restriction and \(\varepsilon_0\le\varepsilon_\star\), not assumptions.
