\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) and \(\mathcal E_{2n}\) be the events from the revised Bahadur--multiplier lemma.  On their intersection, conditional Gaussian approximation, multiplier approximation, anti-concentration, ratio-consistent studentization, and the uniform remainder imply
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{Q\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P_Q\left\{|\widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta_{v,h_n}|
 \le\frac{\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)}
 {\sqrt{n_2h_n}}\ \forall v\in\mathcal I\right\}\ge1-\alpha. \tag{1}
\]
The estimator, center, and score use the same winding because
\(\widehat m_{\widehat j_n}=m_{\widehat j_n}\ne0\) on
\(\mathcal E_{1n}\).  The complement has uniformly \(o(r_n)=o(1)\) probability.

The revised bias lemma gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta_{v,h_n}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{2}
\]
The triangle inequality proves simultaneous coverage by the success-event band.  On either failure event the procedure returns
\([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\), which contains the target curve by the slope-range restriction.  This proves the displayed uniform asymptotic coverage.

On success,
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\operatorname{length}\{\mathcal B_n(v)\}
 \le\frac{2\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_v\widehat s_n(v)}
 {\sqrt{n_2h_n}}+2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{3}
\]
On failure the width is \(2C_\tau\).  The expected critical-value bound and the
\(o(r_n)\) fallback probabilities therefore imply
\[
 \sup_QW_n(\mathcal B,P,\bar g_n)
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta+o(r_n). \tag{4}
\]
For \(h_n=c_h(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\) and \(n_2/n\to1/2\),
\[
 \sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}\asymp r_n,\qquad
 h_n^\beta=c_h^\beta r_n. \tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) establish the expected-width upper bound.
