\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(\varepsilon_0\in(0,\varepsilon_\star]\). All probability statements below are uniform over \((P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\). On \(\mathcal E_{1n}\cap\mathcal E_{2n}\), \(\text{lem:uniform-bahadur-multiplier}\) gives a uniform Bahadur expansion whose studentized remainder is \(o_P(1/\sqrt{\log n})\), a uniformly ratio-consistent standard error, and empirical and conditional multiplier absolute-supremum laws coupled to the same unit-variance Gaussian absolute supremum. The empirical and conditional multiplier coupling failure probabilities vanish uniformly, and each coupling radius multiplied by the Gaussian expected supremum plus one converges to zero. The anti-concentration conclusion in \(\text{lem:cck-empirical-multiplier-coupling}\) therefore transfers the conditional multiplier quantile to the studentized leading empirical process. Consequently,
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P\left\{
 |\widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta_{v,h_n}|
 \le\frac{\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I
 \right\}\ge1-\alpha. \tag{1}
\]
The center, score, standard error, and multiplier process in (1) use the same pilot-selected contour and the same nonzero treatment-noise winding. By \(\text{lem:localized-contour-bias}\),
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta_{v,h_n}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{2}
\]
The triangle inequality combines (1) and (2) to give simultaneous coverage on the two success events. On either actual failure event, \(\text{def:spectral-band}\) returns \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\), which contains the entire target curve by the slope-range restriction. The two failure probabilities are \(o(r_n)=o(1)\), uniformly, so
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P\{\tau_0(v)\in\mathcal B_n(v)\text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge1-\alpha. \tag{3}
\]

On success, the supremum width is at most
\[
 \frac{2\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v)}
 {\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{4}
\]
On failure it is exactly \(2C_\tau\). The critical-value moment bound and the uniform \(o(r_n)\) fallback probability from \(\text{lem:uniform-bahadur-multiplier}\) therefore imply, for every sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 W_n(\mathcal B,P,\bar g_n)
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta+o(r_n). \tag{5}
\]
For \(h_n=c_h(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\) and \(n_2\asymp n\), direct exponent arithmetic gives
\[
 \sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 \asymp
 \left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)},
 \qquad
 h_n^\beta
 \asymp
 \left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}
 =r_n. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (5)--(6) prove the asserted expected-width upper bound after enlarging \(C\).

Finally, the estimator and band are the total measurable sample maps in \(\text{def:spectral-band}\). They use the fixed admissible external treatment code and never estimate \(q_0\). The revised radius condition \(0<\varepsilon_0\le\varepsilon_\star\) enters only through the revalidated contour-robustness and selector chain; no law-level approximation radius is used. Thus the argument applies to every fixed strictly positive \(\varepsilon_0\) in the stated interval. Equations (3), (5), and (6) prove the theorem. \(\square\)
