\textbf{Proof.}
By \(\text{lem:modifier-shift-attainment-and-inclusion}\), admissibility of \(\bar g_n\) supplies an attained \(d\in\mathcal D_{\gamma_D,L_D}\) such that, with \(R_n=D_n-d(V)\),
\[
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{u\in[0,1]}E_P(|R_n|\mid V=u)\le\varepsilon_0. \tag{1}
\]
The range restrictions imply \(|D_n|\le2C_g\), while the displacement class gives \(|d|\le2C_g\). Therefore, for \(|z|\le R_0\),
\[
 \left|e^{z\{D_n-d(v)\}}-1\right|
 \le R_0e^{4C_gR_0}|D_n-d(v)|=A_D|D_n-d(v)|. \tag{2}
\]
If \(h<\operatorname{dist}(\mathcal I,\{0,1\})\), kernel support, normalization, nonnegativity, and the density bounds give
\[
 c_V\le\mu_{v,h}=E_Pw_{v,h}\le C_V. \tag{3}
\]
On the support of \(w_{v,h}\), the displacement condition gives \(|d(V)-d(v)|\le L_Dh^{\gamma_D}\). Since \(D_n-d(v)=R_n+d(V)-d(v)\), conditioning (2) on \(V\), applying (1), and integrating against the nonnegative weight yield
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \left|e^{-zd(v)}E_P[w_{v,h}e^{zD_n}]-\mu_{v,h}\right|
 &\le A_DE_P[w_{v,h}|D_n-d(v)|]\\
 &\le A_D\{L_Dh^{\gamma_D}+\varepsilon_0\}\mu_{v,h}.
 \end{aligned} \tag{4}
\]
The revised error-radius assumption gives \(0<\varepsilon_0\le\varepsilon_\star=(8A_D)^{-1}\). For sufficiently small \(h\), also \(A_DL_Dh^{\gamma_D}\le1/8\). Thus the last member of (4) is at most \(\mu_{v,h}/4\).

For each \(z\) in the closed bank disk, the straight segment from \(e^{-zd(v)}A_{v,h}(z)\) to the positive number \(\mu_{v,h}\) stays at distance at least \(3\mu_{v,h}/4\) from zero. This gives a zero-free homotopy to a constant. Multiplication by the zero-free entire function \(e^{zd(v)}\) creates neither zeros nor winding. Consequently \(A_{v,h}\) is zero-free on \(|z|\le R_0\), has winding zero on every bank circle, and, by (3)--(4),
\[
 |A_{v,h}(z)|
 \ge e^{-2C_gR_0}\frac34\mu_{v,h}
 \ge\frac34c_Ve^{-2C_gR_0}. \tag{5}
\]
Finally, \(\eta\perp(X,V)\) and \(Z_n=\eta+D_n\) imply the exact factorization
\[
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)A_{v,h}(z). \tag{6}
\]
On the contour supplied by \(\text{lem:noise-only-contour-bank}\), (5)--(6) give
\[
 \inf_{v\in\mathcal I,z\in C_\star}|F_{v,h}(z)|
 \ge\frac34\rho_Mc_Ve^{-2C_gR_0}
 =\frac32\rho_F. \tag{7}
\]
Every step is localized in \(v\); no assertion about an unlocalized contaminated transform was used. \(\square\)
