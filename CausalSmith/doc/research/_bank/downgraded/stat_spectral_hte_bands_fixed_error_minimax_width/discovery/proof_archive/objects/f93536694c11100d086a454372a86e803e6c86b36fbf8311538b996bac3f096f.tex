\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\varphi\) be the fixed compactly supported \(\beta\)-H\"older bump. Since \(0<v_-<v_+<1\), for small \(h\) one may place \(m\asymp h^{-1}\) disjoint radius-\(h\) supports in \(\mathcal I\). Put
\[
 \tau_j(v)=\gamma h^\beta\varphi((v-v_j)/h),\quad j=1,\ldots,m,
 \qquad \tau_0(v)=0. \tag{1}
\]
Scaling gives \([\tau_j]_\beta\le\gamma[\varphi]_\beta\) and \(\|\tau_j\|_\infty=\gamma h^\beta\). Choose \(\gamma\) small for the H\"older and range bounds. The exact-code proposition checks all other conditions, including modifier-shift membership with \(d=0\) and remainder zero.

Disjoint supports give \(\|\tau_j-\tau_\ell\|_\infty\ge\gamma h^\beta\) for \(j\ne\ell\), which is at least \(cr_n\). Under the exact-code reduction,
\[
 Y/T=\tau_j(V)+\epsilon,\qquad V\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1],
 \qquad \epsilon\sim N(0,\sigma_k^2).
\]
Thus
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P_j^n,P_0^n)
 =\frac{n}{2\sigma_k^2}\int_0^1\tau_j(v)^2\,dv
 =\frac{\gamma^2\|\varphi\|_2^2}{2\sigma_k^2}nh^{2\beta+1}. \tag{2}
\]
Here \(nh^{2\beta+1}\asymp\log n\) and \(\log m\asymp\log n\). Decreasing \(\gamma\) makes (2) at most \(c_0\log m\) for any prescribed small \(c_0>0\). \(\square\)
