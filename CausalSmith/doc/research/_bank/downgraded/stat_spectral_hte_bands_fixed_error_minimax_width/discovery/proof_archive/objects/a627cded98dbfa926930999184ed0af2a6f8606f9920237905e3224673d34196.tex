\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(\mathcal A\in\mathfrak H_\alpha\) and the \(M_n=m_n+1\) exact-code hypotheses of \(\text{lem:many-bump-subexperiment}\).  Put
\[
 d_n=\min_{j\ne\ell}\|\tau_j-\tau_\ell\|_\infty\ge c_dr_n,
 \qquad
 \Delta_n=\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\{U_n(v)-L_n(v)\}. \tag{1}
\]
Let \(\mathcal C_{j,n}\) be the event that the band covers the entire curve \(\tau_j\).  Since every testing pair lies in \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\), uniform honesty gives
\[
 \kappa_n:=\min_{0\le j<M_n}P_j^n(\mathcal C_{j,n})
 \ge1-\alpha-o(1). \tag{2}
\]

Choose one maximizer from each bump support and evaluate all candidate curves and reported intervals at these finitely many points.  Decode by the unique candidate vector lying in every reported coordinate interval, using a fixed default if there is no unique candidate.  Pairwise separation in (1) implies that, on
\(\mathcal C_{j,n}\cap\{\Delta_n<d_n\}\), candidate \(j\) is the unique admissible vector.  Hence Markov's inequality gives
\[
 \frac1{M_n}\sum_{j=0}^{M_n-1}P_j^n(\widehat J=j)
 \ge\kappa_n-\frac1{M_nd_n}\sum_{j=0}^{M_n-1}E_j\Delta_n. \tag{3}
\]

Let \(J\) be uniform on the hypotheses and \(\overline P=M_n^{-1}\sum_jP_j^n\).  The identity
\[
 \frac1{M_n}\sum_j\operatorname{KL}(P_j^n,P_0^n)
 =\frac1{M_n}\sum_j\operatorname{KL}(P_j^n,\overline P)
 +\operatorname{KL}(\overline P,P_0^n)
\]
shows, using the bump divergence bound, that
\[
 I(J;O_1^n)
 =\frac1{M_n}\sum_j\operatorname{KL}(P_j^n,\overline P)
 \le c_0\log m_n. \tag{4}
\]
If \(p_e=P(\widehat J\ne J)\), conditioning on the error indicator gives
\[
 H(J\mid O_1^n)\le\log2+p_e\log M_n.
\]
Since \(I(J;O_1^n)=\log M_n-H(J\mid O_1^n)\), every decoder satisfies
\[
 \frac1{M_n}\sum_jP_j^n(\widehat J=j)
 \le\frac{c_0\log m_n+\log2}{\log M_n}. \tag{5}
\]
Choose the bump amplitude so that \(c_0<(1-\alpha)/2\).  Combining (2)--(5) yields
\[
 \max_jE_j\Delta_n
 \ge d_n\{1-\alpha-c_0-o(1)\}
 \ge cr_n. \tag{6}
\]
The supremum over \(\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}\) dominates this maximum, proving the asserted liminf lower bound.  Theorem \(\text{thm:honest-spectral-band}\) supplies one measurable honest band whose supremum expected width is at most \(Cr_n\) over the same pair class.  No property of the generic band beyond measurability and honesty entered (1)--(6), so the converse covers nonspectral, asymmetric, and variable-width bands. \(\square\)
