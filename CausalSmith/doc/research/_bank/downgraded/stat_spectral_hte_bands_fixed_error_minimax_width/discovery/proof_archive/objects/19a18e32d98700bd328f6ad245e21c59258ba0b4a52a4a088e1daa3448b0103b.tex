\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) and \(\mathcal E_{2n}\) be the events supplied by \(\text{lem:uniform-bahadur-multiplier}\).  On their intersection, conditional on the pilot, the uniform Gaussian approximation, the conditional multiplier approximation, Gaussian anti-concentration, uniform ratio consistency of \(\widehat s_n\), and the stated Bahadur remainder imply
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{Q\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P_Q\left\{
 |\widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta_{v,h_n}|
 \le \frac{\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I
 \right\}\ge 1-\alpha. \tag{1}
\]
Here the probability lost on \(\mathcal E_{1n}^c\cup\mathcal E_{2n}^c\) is \(o(r_n)=o(1)\), uniformly in \(Q\), and the winding number in the center agrees with the population winding on \(\mathcal E_{1n}\), exactly as required by that lemma.

On the same event, \(\text{lem:localized-contour-bias}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta_{v,h_n}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{2}
\]
The triangle inequality applied to (1)--(2) is precisely simultaneous containment in the implemented band on the two actual success events.  On either actual failure event, \(\text{def:spectral-band}\) returns \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\), which contains \(\tau_0(v)\) for every \(v\) by the slope-range property of every law in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{law}}\).  Therefore
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{Q\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P_Q\{\tau_0(v)\in\mathcal B_n(v)\text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge 1-\alpha. \tag{3}
\]

It remains to bound expected width.  On the two success events,
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\operatorname{length}\{\mathcal B_n(v)\}
 \le
 \frac{2\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta, \tag{4}
\]
whereas on a fallback event this supremum equals \(2C_\tau\).  The critical-value moment bound and the uniform \(o(r_n)\) bound for each actual fallback probability in \(\text{lem:uniform-bahadur-multiplier}\) hence imply, uniformly in \(Q\),
\[
 W_n(\mathcal B,P,\bar g_n)
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta+o(r_n). \tag{5}
\]
Because \(n_2/n\to1/2\) and \(h_n=c_h(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\), direct algebra gives
\[
 \sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}\asymp
 \left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}=r_n,
 \qquad
 h_n^\beta=c_h^\beta r_n. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (5)--(6) establish the asserted \(Cr_n\) inequality for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Finally, the displayed construction uses only the fixed supplied code and empirical contour transforms, contains no estimate of \(q_0\), permits every fixed \(\varepsilon_0\in(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{app}},\varepsilon_\star]\), and nowhere treats the external code as learned from the inference sample. \(\square\)
