Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\) be independent and identically distributed, and let \(\mathcal G\) be a pointwise-measurable \(\operatorname{VC}(b,a,v)\)-type class: \(|g|\le b\), \(a\ge e\), \(v\ge1\), and
\[
 \sup_Q N\{\mathcal G,L^2(Q),b\epsilon\}\le(a/\epsilon)^v,
 \qquad 0<\epsilon\le1.
\]
Suppose \(\sup_{g\in\mathcal G}E[g(X_1)^2]\le\sigma^2\le b^2\), let \(B\) be the tight centered Gaussian process with the covariance of \(g(X_1)-E[g(X_1)]\), and put
\[
 K_n=Av\{\log n\vee\log(ab/\sigma)\}\ge1.
\]
If \(W_n=\sup_{g\in\mathcal G}|n^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^n\{g(X_i)-E[g(X_i)]\}|\), then for each \(\gamma\in(0,1)\) there is a coupling \(W^0\stackrel d=\sup_{g\in\mathcal G}|B(g)|\) such that
\[
 P\left\{|W_n-W^0|>
 \frac{bK_n}{(\gamma n)^{1/2}}+
 \frac{(b\sigma)^{1/2}K_n^{3/4}}{\gamma^{1/2}n^{1/4}}+
 \frac{b^{1/3}\sigma^{2/3}K_n^{2/3}}{\gamma^{1/3}n^{1/6}}
 \right\}
 \le A'\left(\gamma+\frac{\log n}{n}\right).
\]
For independent standard Gaussian multipliers, let \(\widetilde W_n(x_1^n)\) be the conditional supremum of the centered multiplier process. If, in addition,
\[
 b^2K_n\le n\sigma^2,
\]
and
\[
 \psi_n=\left(\frac{\sigma^2K_n}{n}\right)^{1/2}
 +\left(\frac{b^2\sigma^2K_n^3}{n}\right)^{1/4},
 \qquad
 \gamma_n(\delta)=\delta^{-1}
 \left(\frac{b^2\sigma^2K_n^3}{n}\right)^{1/4}+\frac1n,
\]
then for every \(\delta>0\) there is a measurable set \(S_{n,0}\) with \(P(X_1^n\in S_{n,0})\ge1-3/n\) such that, for every \(x_1^n\in S_{n,0}\), a coupling to the same law \(W^0\) satisfies
\[
 P\{|\widetilde W_n(x_1^n)-W^0|>\psi_n+\delta\}
 \le A''\gamma_n(\delta).
\]
Moreover, if \(X=(X_t)_{t\in T}\) is a centered separable Gaussian process with \(E[X_t^2]=1\) for every \(t\) and \(\sup_t|X_t|<\infty\) almost surely, then
\[
 \sup_{x\in\mathbb R}P\left(\left|\sup_t|X_t|-x\right|\le\epsilon\right)
 \le4\epsilon\left\{E\sup_t|X_t|+1\right\}.
\]
Consequently, in a triangular application, distributional equivalence requires both that the empirical and conditional-multiplier coupling failure probabilities vanish uniformly and that the common coupling radius \(\epsilon_n\) satisfy \(\epsilon_n\{E\sup_t|B_n(t)|+1\}\to0\). In particular, when \(E\sup_t|B_n(t)|=O(\sqrt{\log n})\), the quantitative condition \(\epsilon_n\sqrt{\log n}\to0\) is sufficient; convergence of the radii without these probability and anti-concentration conditions is not sufficient.
