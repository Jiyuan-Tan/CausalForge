\textbf{Proof.}
Choose \((p_k,a_k,\sigma_k)\in\mathfrak S_k\).  Since \(p_k\in(1/4,3/4)\),
\[
 \eta=a_k(B-p_k)\in\{-a_kp_k,a_k(1-p_k)\}
\]
is centered, bounded, independent of \((X,V)\), and nonzero almost surely.  Compatibility gives \(\|\eta\|_{\psi_2}\le\psi_\eta\), and direct differentiation of its log moment-generating function gives
\[
 \kappa_k(\eta)=a_k^k\left.\partial_t^k\log(1-p_k+p_ke^t)\right|_{t=0},
 \qquad |\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta. \tag{1}
\]
This works for odd and even \(k\), because the selected asymmetric Bernoulli parameter avoids the finite zero set of the displayed cumulant polynomial.

Here \(V\) is uniform, so its density is one and compatibility states \(c_V\le1\le C_V\).  Since \(E_P\eta=0\) and \(\eta\perp(X,V)\), the declared treatment regression is indeed \(g_0=E_P[T\mid X,V]=0\).  The choices \(g_0=q_0=\bar g_n=0\) obey the range restrictions.  The primitive law conditions in \(\text{def:spectral-law-class}\) therefore hold once the assumed H\"older and range restrictions on \(\tau_0\) are imposed.  Moreover, \(0\in\mathcal G\), and the choice \(d=0\) gives
\[
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{v\in[0,1]}
 E_P[|g_0(X,V)-\bar g_n(X,V)-d(V)|\mid V=v]=0\le\varepsilon_0. \tag{2}
\]
Thus the actually supplied code lies in \(\Gamma(P,\varepsilon_0)\), establishing pair membership without any law-level approximation premise.

Conditional on \((X,V,T)\), \(T=\eta\) is fixed and \(\epsilon\) remains centered Gaussian.  Thus
\[
 E_P(\xi\mid X,V,T)=0,
 \quad
 \|\xi\|_{\psi_2\mid X,V,T}
 =|\eta|\sigma_k\|N(0,1)\|_{\psi_2}
 \le\psi_\xi, \tag{3}
\]
and
\[
 E_P(\xi^2\mid X,V,T)=\eta^2\sigma_k^2
 \ge a_k^2\sigma_k^2\min\{p_k,1-p_k\}^2
 \ge\underline\sigma_\xi^2. \tag{4}
\]
All support, tail, separation, and nondegeneracy conditions of the primitive law class have now been checked.  Finally, \(T=\eta\ne0\) almost surely and
\[
 \frac{Y}{T}=\tau_0(V)+\epsilon. \tag{5}
\]
Thus the observed experiment is ordinary uniform-design Gaussian nonparametric regression for \(\tau_0\), together with ancillary \(T\). \(\square\)
