\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) and \(\mathcal E_{2n}\) be the events supplied by \(\text{lem:uniform-bahadur-multiplier}\).  On their intersection, the conditional Gaussian approximation, conditional multiplier approximation, Gaussian anti-concentration, ratio-consistent studentization, and the uniform Bahadur remainder imply
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{Q\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P_Q\left\{
 |\widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta_{v,h_n}|
 \le \frac{\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I\right\}\ge 1-\alpha. \tag{1}
\]
Here the normalization in the estimator, population center, and score is the same because \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) includes \(\widehat m_{\widehat j_n}=m_{\widehat j_n}\ne0\).  The complement of \(\mathcal E_{1n}\cap\mathcal E_{2n}\) has probability \(o(r_n)=o(1)\), uniformly over the pair class, so it does not alter (1).

On the same event, \(\text{lem:localized-contour-bias}\) gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta_{v,h_n}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{2}
\]
The triangle inequality applied to (1)--(2) is precisely simultaneous containment in the band defined on the two actual success events.  On either actual failure event, \(\text{def:spectral-band}\) returns \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\), which contains \(\tau_0(v)\) for every \(v\) by the slope-range condition incorporated in the law class.  Consequently
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{Q\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P_Q\{\tau_0(v)\in\mathcal B_n(v)\text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge 1-\alpha. \tag{3}
\]

It remains to bound expected width.  On the two success events,
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\operatorname{length}\{\mathcal B_n(v)\}
 \le
 \frac{2\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta, \tag{4}
\]
whereas on a fallback event this supremum equals \(2C_\tau\).  The expected-critical-value bound and the \(o(r_n)\) bounds for both actual fallback probabilities from \(\text{lem:uniform-bahadur-multiplier}\) therefore yield, uniformly in \(Q=(P,\bar g_n)\),
\[
 W_n(\mathcal B,P,\bar g_n)
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta+o(r_n). \tag{5}
\]
Because \(n_2/n\to1/2\) and \(h_n=c_h(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\), direct algebra gives
\[
 \sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}\asymp
 \left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}=r_n,
 \qquad
 h_n^\beta=c_h^\beta r_n. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (5)--(6) establish the asserted \(Cr_n\) bound for all sufficiently large \(n\).
