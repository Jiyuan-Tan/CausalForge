\textbf{Proof.}
Choose \((p_k,a_k,\sigma_k)\in\mathfrak S_k\), whose existence is the hard-subclass compatibility assumption. Then
\[
 \eta=a_k(B-p_k)\in\{-a_kp_k,a_k(1-p_k)\} \tag{1}
\]
is centered, bounded, independent of \((X,V)\), and nonzero almost surely. Compatibility gives \(\|\eta\|_{\psi_2}\le\psi_\eta\), and direct differentiation gives
\[
 \kappa_k(\eta)=a_k^k
 \left.\partial_t^k\log(1-p_k+p_ke^t)\right|_{t=0},
 \qquad |\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta. \tag{2}
\]
The selected asymmetric \(p_k\) avoids the zero set of the displayed cumulant polynomial, so this applies to odd and even \(k\).

The modifier is uniform, and compatibility gives \(c_V\le1\le C_V\). The choices \(g_0=q_0=\bar g_n=0\) satisfy their range restrictions. Since \(0\in\mathcal G\), both the law-level approximation error and the supplied-code error are zero. The stated H\"older and range restrictions on \(\tau_0\) verify the remaining curve conditions.

Conditional on \((X,V,T)\), \(T=\eta\) is fixed and \(\epsilon\) remains centered Gaussian. Therefore
\[
 E(\xi\mid X,V,T)=0,\quad
 \|\xi\|_{\psi_2\mid X,V,T}
 \le a_k\sigma_k\max\{p_k,1-p_k\}\|N(0,1)\|_{\psi_2}
 \le\psi_\xi, \tag{3}
\]
and
\[
 E(\xi^2\mid X,V,T)=\eta^2\sigma_k^2
 \ge a_k^2\sigma_k^2\min\{p_k,1-p_k\}^2
 \ge\underline\sigma_\xi^2. \tag{4}
\]
Thus the constructed law and exact code belong to the pair class. Finally, \(T=\eta\ne0\) almost surely and
\[
 Y/T=\tau_0(V)+\epsilon. \tag{5}
\]
The remaining treatment variable has a parameter-free law independent of \((V,\epsilon)\), so it is ancillary to the ordinary random-design Gaussian regression experiment in (5). \(\square\)
