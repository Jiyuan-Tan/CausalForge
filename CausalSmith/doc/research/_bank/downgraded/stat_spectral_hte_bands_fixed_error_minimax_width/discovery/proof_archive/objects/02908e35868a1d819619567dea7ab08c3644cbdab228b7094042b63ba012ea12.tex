\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a pair, a selected good contour \(C=C_j\), \(v\in\mathcal I\), and a sufficiently small \(h\). Put \(w=w_{v,h}\) and define
\[
 A(z)=E_P[we^{zD_n}],\quad
 A_\tau(z)=E_P[w\tau_0(V)e^{zD_n}],\quad
 Q(z)=E_P[wq_0(X,V)e^{zD_n}]. \tag{1}
\]
These functions are entire. Equation (6) of the robustness proof shows throughout the disk bounded by \(C\) that \(|A(z)-\mu_{v,h}|\le\mu_{v,h}/4\). Thus \(A\) is zero-free there, and the robustness lemma gives denominator safety.

Independence of \(\eta\) from \((X,V)\) yields
\[
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)A(z). \tag{2}
\]
Because \(we^{zZ_n}\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V,T)\), the conditional mean restriction gives
\[
 E_P[w\xi e^{zZ_n}]=0. \tag{3}
\]
Using \(Y=q_0(X,V)+\tau_0(V)\eta+\xi\), equations (2)--(3), and independence again,
\[
 G_{v,h}(z)=M(z)Q(z)+M'(z)A_\tau(z),\qquad
 \frac{G_{v,h}}{F_{v,h}}
 =\frac QA+\frac{M'}M\frac{A_\tau}{A}. \tag{4}
\]
The first ratio is analytic inside \(C\), so its contour integral is zero. The selector's winding homotopy and the zero-free factor \(E_Pe^{zD_n}\) imply
\[
 m_j=(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_C M'(z)/M(z)\,dz\ne0. \tag{5}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \Theta^j_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)
 =\operatorname{Re}\left\{\frac{1}{2\pi i m_j}
 \oint_C\frac{M'}M
 \left(\frac{A_\tau}{A}-\tau_0(v)\right)dz\right\}. \tag{6}
\]
This proves analytic annihilation of the arbitrary bounded baseline regression and exact recovery when \(\tau_0\) is constant.

For a varying slope, kernel support, H\"older smoothness, the density upper bound, and \(|D_n|\le2C_g\) give
\[
 |A_\tau(z)-\tau_0(v)A(z)|
 \le C_Ve^{2C_gR_0}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{7}
\]
By the robustness proof, \(|A(z)|\ge3c_V/4\). Selection and \(|E_Pe^{zD_n}|\le5/4\) imply \(\inf_C|M|\ge2\rho_M/5\). Therefore (12) in the noise-only bank proof gives
\[
 \operatorname{len}(C)\sup_C|M'/M|\le\Lambda_0. \tag{8}
\]
Since \(|m_j|\ge1\), equations (6)--(8) yield
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta^j_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le 2(C_V/c_V)e^{2C_gR_0}\Lambda_0L_\tau h^\beta
 =B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{9}
\]
No smoothness of \(q_0\), \(g_0\), or \(\bar g_n\) was used. \(\square\)
