\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
Fix a pair, a selected circle \(C\), and a sufficiently small \(h\).  Put \(w=w_{v,h}\) and
\[
 A(z)=E_P[we^{zD_n}],\quad
 A_\tau(z)=E_P[w\tau_0(V)e^{zD_n}],\quad
 Q(z)=E_P[wq_0(X,V)e^{zD_n}]. \tag{1}
\]
These functions are entire.  Robustness makes \(A\) zero-free in the bank disk, and independence gives \(F_{v,h}=MA\).  Since \(we^{zZ_n}\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V,T)\), the conditional mean restriction gives \(E_P[w\xi e^{zZ_n}]=0\).  Hence
\[
 G_{v,h}=MQ+M'A_\tau,qquad
 \frac{G_{v,h}}{F_{v,h}}=\frac QA+\frac{M'}M\frac{A_\tau}{A}. \tag{2}
\]
The first ratio is analytic inside \(C\) and integrates to zero.  The selected winding is \(m(C)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_C M'/M\,dz\ne0\), so
\[
 \Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)
 =\operatorname{Re}\left\{\frac1{2\pi i m(C)}\oint_C\frac{M'}M
 \left(\frac{A_\tau}{A}-\tau_0(v)\right)dz\right\}. \tag{3}
\]
This proves cancellation of the arbitrary bounded baseline and exact recovery for a constant slope.

Kernel support and H\"older smoothness give
\[
 |A_\tau(z)-\tau_0(v)A(z)|
 \le C_Ve^{2C_gR_0}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{4}
\]
Robustness gives \(|A(z)|\ge(c_V/2)e^{-2C_gR_0}\).  It also gives \(|A(z)|\le2C_Ve^{2C_gR_0}\).  Since the selected population denominator is at least \(\rho_F\),
\[
 \inf_{z\in C}|M(z)|\ge\frac{\rho_F}{2C_Ve^{2C_gR_0}}. \tag{5}
\]
By \(\text{lem:all-bank-transform-derivative-envelope}\), \(\operatorname{len}(C)\sup_C|M'|\le\Lambda_0\).  Combining (3)--(5), using \(|m(C)|\ge1\), and dropping \(1/(2\pi)\le1\), yields
\[
 \sup_v|\Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le\frac{4C_V^2}{c_V\rho_F}e^{6C_gR_0}\Lambda_0L_\tau h^\beta.
\]
This is exactly the proposed \(B_{\mathrm{bias}}\). \(\square\)
