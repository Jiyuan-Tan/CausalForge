\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
Condition on the pilot.  On the event supplied by the selector proof, \(j=\widehat j_n\) is fixed,
\[
 \inf_{v,z\in C_j}|F_{v,h_n}(z)|\ge\rho_Mc_V/2,
 \qquad \widehat m_j=m_j\ne0. \tag{1}
\]
The equality now follows from the localized winding at \(v_0\), not from any global contaminated transform.

The range restrictions and the structural equation imply
\[
 |Y|\le C_q+C_\tau|\eta|+|\xi|,\qquad |D_n|\le2C_g. \tag{2}
\]
Treatment and conditional outcome sub-Gaussianity therefore give, for each fixed \(s<\infty\),
\[
 \sup_QE_Q[(1+|Y|+|Z_n|)^s
 e^{s(R_0+1)|Z_n|}]<\infty. \tag{3}
\]
Translations of the fixed bounded Lipschitz kernel form a one-dimensional VC-type class, and the bank is finite.  Truncation at
\(|\eta|+|\xi|\le A\sqrt{\log n}\), polynomial grids in \(v\) and contour arclength, the iid moment inequality used in the selector proof, and oscillation control yield
\[
 \sup_{v,z\in C_j}
 \{|\widehat F_{2j,v}(z)-F_{v,h_n}(z)|
 +|\widehat G_{2j,v}(z)-G_{v,h_n}(z)|\}
 =O_P\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}\right) \tag{4}
\]
uniformly over the pair class and the finitely many pilot choices.  By taking fixed moments arbitrarily large, all fixed-modulus denominator and truncation failures have probability \(O(n^{-D})\) for arbitrary \(D\).

Let \(\Delta_F=\widehat F-F\) and \(\Delta_G=\widehat G-G\).  On inference-denominator success,
\[
 \frac{G+\Delta_G}{F+\Delta_F}-\frac GF
 =\frac{F\Delta_G-G\Delta_F}{F^2}+R,\qquad
 |R|\le C\{|\Delta_F\Delta_G|+|\Delta_F|^2\}. \tag{5}
\]
After contour integration and normalization by the common nonzero winding in (1), the linear term is exactly
\[
 n_2^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_2}\psi^j_{v,h_n}(O_i). \tag{6}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) bound the uniform remainder by
\[
 O_P\!\left(\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}\right)
 =o_P\!\left(\frac{(n_2h_n)^{-1/2}}{\sqrt{\log n}}\right), \tag{7}
\]
because \(n_2h_n/(\log n)^3\to\infty\).

We verify studentization from the primitive variance assumption.  Let the coefficient of \(Y\), hence of \(\xi\), in the score be
\[
 b_{v,j}(O)=\operatorname{Re}\left\{
 \frac{w_{v,h_n}}{2\pi i m_j}
 \oint_{C_j}\frac{e^{zZ_n}}{F_{v,h_n}(z)}\,dz\right\}. \tag{8}
\]
The coefficient vanishes unless \(|V-v|\le h_n\).  Differentiating the contour functional along the constant-slope path
\(Y\mapsto Y+t\eta\), and using the exact constant-slope recovery proved above, gives
\[
 E_Q[\eta b_{v,j}(O)]=1. \tag{9}
\]
Since \(\eta\perp V\), \(E\eta^2\le C\psi_\eta^2\), and
\(P(|V-v|\le h_n)\le2C_Vh_n\), Cauchy--Schwarz in (9) yields
\[
 E_Qb_{v,j}^2\ge c/h_n. \tag{10}
\]
The part of the score not multiplied by \(\xi\) is measurable with respect to
\((X,V,T)\), whereas \(E(\xi\mid X,V,T)=0\).  Conditional variance nondegeneracy and (10), followed by the upper bound from (3) and the safe denominator, give
\[
 0<c\le h_n\operatorname{Var}_Q(\psi^j_{v,h_n})
 \le h_nE_Q[(\psi^j_{v,h_n})^2]\le C. \tag{11}
\]

Apply \(\text{lem:cck-empirical-multiplier-coupling}\) to the symmetric class of plus and minus the centered studentized functions
\(\sqrt{h_n}\psi^j_{v,h_n}/s_j(v)\).  After truncation its envelope is
\(h_n^{-1/2}n^{o(1)}\), its variance is bounded by one, and its entropy logarithm is \(O(\log n)\).  Since \(n_2h_n\) diverges polynomially, the empirical and conditional multiplier coupling errors vanish uniformly.  The cited anti-concentration conclusion and the normalized \(o(1/\sqrt{\log n})\) remainder in (7) transfer multiplier quantiles to the studentized absolute supremum.

Applying the same grid moment argument to squared scores, using (11), gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\left|\widehat s_n(v)/s_j(v)-1\right|=o_P(1),
 \qquad 0<c\le\inf_v s_j(v)\le\sup_v s_j(v)\le C. \tag{12}
\]
Equation (4) and the ratio identity show that replacing population scores by plug-in scores changes their normalized empirical \(L^2\) distance by
\(o_P(1/\sqrt{\log n})\), so the same coupling applies to the implemented process.

Let \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) be the selector event with the polynomial concentration bounds, and let \(\mathcal E_{2n}\) intersect inference-denominator success with (4), (11), (12), and the plug-in comparison event.  Choosing the fixed moment orders sufficiently large gives
\[
 \sup_QP_Q(\mathcal E_{1n}^c\cup\mathcal E_{2n}^c)=o(r_n), \tag{13}
\]
and hence each actual fallback probability is \(o(r_n)\).
Finally, the Gaussian maximal inequality for the same logarithmic-entropy class gives conditional mean at most \(C\sqrt{\log n}\) for the multiplier supremum.  Gaussian concentration adds only a fixed constant to its conditional \((1-\alpha)\)-quantile.  Integrating (3), (11)--(13), including the truncation complement, yields
\[
 E_P\left[\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v);
 \text{both actual success events}\right]\le C\sqrt{\log n}. \tag{14}
\]
Equations (1), (7), and (11)--(14) prove every assertion. \(\square\)
