\textbf{Proof.}
On \(\mathcal E_{1n}\cap\mathcal E_{2n}\), the Bahadur representation, the two covariance-matched Gaussian comparisons, anti-concentration, and ratio-consistent studentization imply
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 P\left\{
 |\widehat\tau_n(v)-\Theta_{v,h_n}|
 \le\frac{\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\widehat s_n(v)}{\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 \text{ for every }v\in\mathcal I
 \right\}\ge1-\alpha. \tag{1}
\]
The center, score, and estimator in (1) all use the same pilot-selected contour and the same nonzero treatment-noise winding.  The bias lemma gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta_{v,h_n}-\tau_0(v)|
 \le B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{2}
\]
The triangle inequality combines (1)--(2) to give simultaneous coverage on both success events.  On either failure event the total procedure returns \([-C_\tau,C_\tau]\), which covers the entire target curve by the slope-range restriction.  This proves uniform asymptotic honesty.

On success, the supremum width is bounded by
\[
 \frac{2\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_{v\in\mathcal I}\widehat s_n(v)}
 {\sqrt{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta. \tag{3}
\]
On failure it equals \(2C_\tau\).  The critical-value moment bound and the \(o(r_n)\) fallback probability therefore give
\[
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{spec},n}}
 W_n(\mathcal B,P,\bar g_n)
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}
 +2B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h_n^\beta+o(r_n). \tag{4}
\]
For \(h_n=c_h(\log n/n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\) and \(n_2\asymp n\), both displayed leading terms have order
\[
 r_n=\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{\beta/(2\beta+1)}. \tag{5}
\]
The estimator is the explicit sample map in \(\text{def:spectral-band}\): it uses the fixed external treatment code, and neither its center nor its radius estimates \(q_0\).  The admissible ordering permits fixed \(\varepsilon_0>0\).  Equations (1)--(5) prove the theorem. \(\square\)
