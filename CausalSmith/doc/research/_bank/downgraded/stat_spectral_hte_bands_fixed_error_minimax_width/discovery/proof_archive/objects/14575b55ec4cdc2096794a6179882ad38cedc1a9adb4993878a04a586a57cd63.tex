\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
Fix a pair, a selected good circle \(C\), and a bandwidth satisfying the support and smallness restrictions of the robustness lemma. Write \(w=w_{v,h}\) and
\[
 A(z)=E_P[we^{zD_n}],\quad
 A_\tau(z)=E_P[w\tau_0(V)e^{zD_n}],\quad
 Q(z)=E_P[wq_0(X,V)e^{zD_n}]. \tag{1}
\]
Boundedness makes these functions entire. The modifier-shift factorization proves that \(A\) is zero-free on the whole search disk. Independence of \(\eta\) from \((X,V)\) gives
\[
 F_{v,h}(z)=M(z)A(z). \tag{2}
\]
Since \(we^{zZ_n}\) is measurable with respect to \((X,V,T)\), the conditional-mean restriction gives
\[
 E_P[w\xi e^{zZ_n}]=0. \tag{3}
\]
Using \(Y=q_0(X,V)+\tau_0(V)\eta+\xi\), (3), and independence once more,
\[
 G_{v,h}(z)=M(z)Q(z)+M'(z)A_\tau(z), \tag{4}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \frac{G_{v,h}}{F_{v,h}}
 =\frac QA+\frac{M'}M\frac{A_\tau}{A}. \tag{5}
\]

Because \(A\) is zero-free inside \(C\), \(Q/A\) is holomorphic there and its contour integral is zero. Also the winding used by the selected functional equals
\[
 m(C)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_C M'/M\,dz\ne0. \tag{6}
\]
Subtracting \(\tau_0(v)\) from the contour functional gives the exact identity
\[
 \Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)
 =\operatorname{Re}\left\{\frac1{2\pi i m(C)}
 \oint_C\frac{M'}M
 \left(\frac{A_\tau}{A}-\tau_0(v)\right)dz\right\}. \tag{7}
\]
This proves analytic cancellation of the arbitrary bounded baseline regression. If \(\tau_0\) is constant, the bracket in (7) is identically zero, proving exact recovery despite the persistent modifier shift.

For a varying slope, kernel support and H\"older smoothness give
\[
 |A_\tau(z)-\tau_0(v)A(z)|
 \le C_Ve^{2C_g(R_0+1)}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{8}
\]
The robustness lemma gives \(|A|\ge c_V/2\). The selected population denominator bound and
\[
 |A(z)|\le e^{2C_g(R_0+1)}E_Pw
 \le C_Ve^{2C_g(R_0+1)}
\]
imply, on \(C\),
\[
 |M(z)|\ge
 \frac{\rho_Mc_V}{2C_Ve^{2C_g(R_0+1)}}. \tag{9}
\]
The explicit bank construction gives \(\operatorname{len}(C)\sup_C|M'|\le\Lambda_0\). Since \(|m(C)|\ge1\), combining (7)--(9), and dropping the harmless factor \(1/(2\pi)\le1\), yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sup_{v\in\mathcal I}|\Theta^C_{v,h}-\tau_0(v)|
 &\le
 4(C_V/c_V)^2e^{4C_g(R_0+1)}
 \frac{\Lambda_0}{\rho_M}L_\tau h^\beta\\
 &=B_{\mathrm{bias}}L_\tau h^\beta. \tag{10}
\end{aligned}
\]
No smoothness of \(q_0\), \(g_0\), or \(\bar g_n\) was used. The only added regularity is the explicitly declared H\"older property of the modifier-only displacement witness; its remainder remains merely conditional \(L^1\). \(\square\)
