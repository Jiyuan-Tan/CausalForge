\textbf{Proof of the proposed statement.}
Condition on the pilot and work on the selector event of \(\text{lem:selector-consistency}\). Then \(j=\widehat j_n\) is fixed,
\[
 \inf_{v,z\in C_j}|F_{v,h_n}(z)|\ge\rho_F,
 \qquad \widehat m_j=m_j\ne0. \tag{1}
\]
The range and structural restrictions imply
\[
 |Y|\le C_q+C_\tau|\eta|+|\xi|,\qquad |Z_n|\le|\eta|+2C_g. \tag{2}
\]
Thus, for every fixed \(s<\infty\),
\[
 \sup_QE_Q[(1+|Y|+|Z_n|)^se^{sR_0|Z_n|}]<\infty. \tag{3}
\]
Truncation at \(|\eta|+|\xi|\le A\sqrt{\log n}\), polynomial grids in \(v\) and contour arclength, and the iid moment inequality used in the selector proof give
\[
 \sup_{v,z}\{|\widehat F_{2j,v}-F_{v,h_n}|+|\widehat G_{2j,v}-G_{v,h_n}|\}
 =O_P\left(\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}}\right), \tag{4}
\]
with every fixed-modulus failure having probability \(O(n^{-D})\) for arbitrarily large fixed \(D\).

For \(\Delta_F=\widehat F-F\) and \(\Delta_G=\widehat G-G\),
\[
 \frac{G+\Delta_G}{F+\Delta_F}-\frac GF
 =\frac{F\Delta_G-G\Delta_F}{F^2}+R,
 \qquad |R|\le C(|\Delta_F\Delta_G|+|\Delta_F|^2). \tag{5}
\]
Contour integration of the linear term is exactly \(n_2^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_2}\psi^j_{v,h_n}(O_i)\). Equations (4)--(5) make the remainder
\[
 O_P\left(\frac{\log n}{n_2h_n}\right)
 =o_P\left((n_2h_n)^{-1/2}/\sqrt{\log n}\right), \tag{6}
\]
because \(n_2h_n/(\log n)^3\to\infty\).

Let \(b_{v,j}(O)\) be the coefficient of \(Y\), hence of \(\xi\), in the score. It vanishes off \(|V-v|\le h_n\), and direct factorization gives
\[
 E_Q[\eta b_{v,j}(O)]
 =\operatorname{Re}\left\{\frac1{2\pi i m_j}\oint_{C_j}\frac{M'}M\,dz\right\}=1. \tag{7}
\]
Since \(\eta\perp V\), \(E\eta^2\le C\psi_\eta^2\), and \(P(|V-v|\le h_n)\le2C_Vh_n\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(E_Qb_{v,j}^2\ge c/h_n\). Conditional variance nondegeneracy and conditional mean zero then yield
\[
 0<c\le h_n\operatorname{Var}_Q(\psi^j_{v,h_n})
 \le h_nE_Q[(\psi^j_{v,h_n})^2]\le C. \tag{8}
\]

Apply \(\text{lem:cck-empirical-multiplier-coupling}\) to plus and minus the centered functions \(\sqrt{h_n}\psi^j_{v,h_n}/s_j(v)\). After truncation their variance is bounded by one, entropy logarithm is \(O(\log n)\), and envelope is \(b_n=h_n^{-1/2}n^{o(1)}\). In particular
\[
 b_n^2\log^7n/n_2\le n^{o(1)}\log^7n/(n_2h_n)\to0, \tag{9}
\]
so the cited empirical and multiplier coupling errors vanish uniformly. Its anti-concentration conclusion and (6) transfer conditional quantiles.

The same grid calculation applied to squared scores, using (8), gives uniform variance-ratio consistency. The ratio identity and (4) give normalized empirical \(L^2\) plug-in-score error \(o_P(1/\sqrt{\log n})\), so the implemented multiplier process has the same limit. Taking moment orders in (3)--(4) larger than the exponent of \(r_n^{-1}\), define \(\mathcal E_{1n}\) and \(\mathcal E_{2n}\) so that
\[
 \sup_QP_Q(\mathcal E_{1n}^c\cup\mathcal E_{2n}^c)=o(r_n). \tag{10}
\]
Both fallback events lie in this complement. Finally, the Gaussian maximal inequality and fixed-\(\alpha\) Gaussian concentration give a conditional critical value at most \(C\sqrt{\log n}\) on the score-comparison event. Uniform high moments from (3) control its complement and the empirical standard deviations, yielding
\[
 E_P[\widehat c_{1-\alpha}\sup_v\widehat s_n(v);\text{ both success events}]
 \le C\sqrt{\log n}. \tag{11}
\]
Equations (1), (6), and (8)--(11) prove all assertions. \(\square\)
