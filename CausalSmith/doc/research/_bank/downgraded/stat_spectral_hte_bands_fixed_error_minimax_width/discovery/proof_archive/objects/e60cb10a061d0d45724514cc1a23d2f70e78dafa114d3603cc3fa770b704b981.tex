\textbf{Proof.}
Choose \((p_k,a_k,\sigma_k)\in\mathfrak S_k\).  Then
\[
 \eta=a_k(B-p_k)\in\{-a_kp_k,a_k(1-p_k)\}
\]
is centered, bounded, independent of \((X,V)\), and nonzero almost surely.
Compatibility gives \(\|\eta\|_{\psi_2}\le\psi_\eta\), and direct differentiation gives
\[
 \kappa_k(\eta)=a_k^k
 \left.\partial_t^k\log(1-p_k+p_ke^t)\right|_{t=0},
 \qquad |\kappa_k(\eta)|\ge\delta. \tag{1}
\]
The asymmetric \(p_k\) lies outside the finite zero set of the cumulant polynomial, so (1) holds for odd as well as even \(k\).

The modifier \(V\) is uniform and compatibility gives
\(c_V\le1\le C_V\).  The choices
\(g_0=q_0=\bar g_n=0\) satisfy the range restrictions.  Since
\(0\in\mathcal G\), the enlarged law approximation and supplied-code conditions hold with the modifier-shift witness \(d=0\) and remainder zero.  Thus the exact-code family remains a subclass of the enlarged pair class.  The assumed Hölder and uniform range restrictions on \(\tau_0\) provide the remaining curve conditions.

Conditional on \((X,V,T)\), \(T=\eta\) is fixed and \(\epsilon\) remains centered Gaussian.  Therefore
\[
 E(\xi\mid X,V,T)=0,\qquad
 \|\xi\|_{\psi_2\mid X,V,T}
 \le a_k\sigma_k\max\{p_k,1-p_k\}\|N(0,1)\|_{\psi_2}
 \le\psi_\xi, \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 E(\xi^2\mid X,V,T)
 =\eta^2\sigma_k^2
 \ge a_k^2\sigma_k^2\min\{p_k,1-p_k\}^2
 \ge\underline\sigma_\xi^2. \tag{3}
\]
All law-class conditions have been checked.  Finally \(T=\eta\ne0\) almost surely and
\[
 Y/T=\tau_0(V)+\epsilon. \tag{4}
\]
Thus the observed experiment factors into uniform-design Gaussian nonparametric regression for \(\tau_0\) and an ancillary \(T\), proving the proposition. \(\square\)
