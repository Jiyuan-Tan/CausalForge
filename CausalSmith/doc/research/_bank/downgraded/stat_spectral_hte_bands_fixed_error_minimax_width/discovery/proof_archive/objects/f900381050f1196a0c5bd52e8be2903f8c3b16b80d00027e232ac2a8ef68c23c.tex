The closed theorems fix \(\beta\in(0,1]\), a known Hölder radius, an interior modifier interval, fixed positive cumulant separation, and fixed modifier-shift regularity \((\gamma_D,L_D)\).  The law class is procedure-independent.  Honesty is uniform over primitive laws and every external code fixed before inference whose discrepancy from \(g_0\) decomposes into a bounded \(\gamma_D\)-Hölder modifier-only shift and a uniformly conditionally \(L^1\)-small remainder.  The shift may remain nonzero and large relative to \(\varepsilon_0\); the procedure neither knows it nor estimates \(q_0\).  No auxiliary-sample learning guarantee is claimed.  The accepted scalar bank class and this modifier-indexed band class are not globally comparable.  Unknown-smoothness adaptation, boundary bands, multivariate modifiers, and sharp dependence on vanishing cumulant separation remain outside the closed result; weak separation is a separate open frontier.
