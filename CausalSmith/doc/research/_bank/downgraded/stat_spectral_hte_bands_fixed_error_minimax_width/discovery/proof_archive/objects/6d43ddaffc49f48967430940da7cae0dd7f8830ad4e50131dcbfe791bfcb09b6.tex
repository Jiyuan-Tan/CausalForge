For every bank contour \(C_j\) that is zero-free for the relevant transform, define \(m_j=(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_{C_j}M'(z)/M(z)\,dz\).  For \(v\in\mathcal I\) and \(h>0\), define
\[
 \Theta^{j}_{v,h}=\operatorname{Re}\left\{\frac{1}{2\pi i m_j}\oint_{C_j}\frac{G_{v,h}(z)}{F_{v,h}(z)}\,dz\right\}
\]
and
\[
 \psi^{j}_{v,h}(O)=\operatorname{Re}\left\{\frac{1}{2\pi i m_j}\oint_{C_j}h^{-1}K((V-v)/h)e^{zZ_n}\frac{YF_{v,h}(z)-G_{v,h}(z)}{F_{v,h}(z)^2}\,dz\right\}.
\]
After the independent pilot selects \(\widehat j_n\), use the shorthand \(\Theta_{v,h}=\Theta^{\widehat j_n}_{v,h}\) and \(\psi_{v,h}=\psi^{\widehat j_n}_{v,h}\) conditionally on the pilot.  Population objects are therefore evaluated on the same contour as the empirical estimator.
