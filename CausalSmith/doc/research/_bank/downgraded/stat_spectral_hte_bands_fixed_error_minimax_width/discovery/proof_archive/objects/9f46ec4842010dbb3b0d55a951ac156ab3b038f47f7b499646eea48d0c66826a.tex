\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
 \Delta_k=|\kappa_k(a_k(B-p_k))|. \tag{1}
\]
Hard-subclass compatibility and the exact-code calculation give \(\Delta_k\ge\delta>0\). Since \(\delta_n\downarrow0\), there is \(n_0\) such that \(\delta_n\le\Delta_k\) for every \(n\ge n_0\). Hence every exact-code many-bump law used in the fixed-separation converse belongs, from that index onward, to \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\): all unchanged restrictions hold, the external code is exact, and the weakened cumulant inequality follows from (1).

Fix \(\mathcal A\in\widetilde{\mathfrak H}_\alpha\). Uniform honesty over \(\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n\) implies honesty on this many-bump subclass. Repeating the decoder and information calculation from the minimax-width proof, with the same separation \(c_dr_n\) and divergence bound \(c_0\log m_n\), gives
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}r_n^{-1}
 \sup_{(P,\bar g_n)\in\widetilde{\mathcal Q}_n}
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)\ge c. \tag{2}
\]

For the achievable side, the slope-range condition gives \(\tau_0(v)\in[-C_\tau,C_\tau]\) for every \(v\). Therefore the deterministic band
\[
 \mathcal A_n(v)=[-C_\tau,C_\tau] \tag{3}
\]
has coverage one over the enlarged class and
\[
 W_n(\mathcal A,P,\bar g_n)=2C_\tau. \tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) prove the bracket. At the fixed endpoint \(\delta_n\equiv\delta\), the honest-spectral-band upper bound is \(O(r_n)\) and the all-band lower bound is \(\Omega(r_n)\), so the order closes. When \(\delta_n\to0\), these arguments do not bound deterioration of the contour radius or boundary modulus and yield no sharper dependence on \(\delta_n\). \(\square\)
