\begin{proof}
Fix \(B>0\), \(\gamma\in(0,1]\), \(\beta\in(0,2]\), and \(\eta=1/4\). Apply \(\texttt{lem:parameterized-blind-path-witness}\) to the constant two-atom configuration in \(\texttt{def:two-atom-constant-configuration}\). That lemma verifies membership in \(\mathcal M_n\), exact learned images, the three zero defects for candidate \(2\), collapse of every trained path element to zero, and the identities
\[
 \|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2
 =\varepsilon_{\mathrm{tr},2}^2
 =\gamma B^2.
\]
It also gives
\[
 R_n:=\inf_{k\in\mathcal K,\lambda\in\Lambda_n}
 \left\{\lambda^{\bar\beta}+\frac{s_{k,n}}{\lambda}\right\}
 \longrightarrow0
\]
for every fixed finite numerical choice of the constant in \(\texttt{def:grid-and-scale}\). Since \(\gamma B^2>0\), for every fixed finite outer constant \(C\) there is \(n_0\) such that \(CR_n<\gamma B^2\) whenever \(n\ge n_0\). On every sample from such a configuration,
\[
 \min\{4B^2,CR_n\}<\gamma B^2
 =\|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2.
\]
Thus the proposed inequality fails on the whole sample space, not merely on an event of probability exceeding \(\eta\).

By \(\texttt{prop:path-restricted-selection-frontier}\), every random, data-dependent, or oracle-dependent signed linear or affine combination of the already-trained path is also the zero function. Hence its squared error is \(\gamma B^2\). Varying \(\gamma\) over \((0,1]\) produces every value in \((0,B^2]\), and \(\gamma=1\) attains \(B^2\) on the declared two-atom law.

It remains to verify maximality of this collapsed-zero frontier. By \(\texttt{lem:operator-contraction}\), \(\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le1\), so \(A=T^*T\) is positive self-adjoint and satisfies \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le1\). Because \(\beta/2\in(0,1]\), \(\texttt{lem:positive-contraction-fractional-power}\), \(\texttt{ass:source-representation}\), and \(\texttt{ass:source-radius}\) yield
\[
 \|h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 =\|A^{\beta/2}w\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 \le\|w\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 \le B.
\]
Any collapsed-zero path therefore has squared strong error \(\|h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2\le B^2\), proving endpoint maximality.

Finally, \(\texttt{prop:universal-clipping-constant-sharp}\) gives \(\|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2\le4B^2\) for every realization in the full class and proves that no smaller uniform coefficient replaces \(4\). This clipping fact is logically separate from the failed oracle rate.
\end{proof}
