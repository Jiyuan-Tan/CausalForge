\begin{proof}
The exact distance representation and its zero-defect characterization are proved in \(\texttt{lem:training-coverage-distance-identity}\). We prove the remaining conclusions separately.

Fix \(B>0\), \(\gamma\in(0,1)\), \(\beta\in(0,2]\), and any finite positive value of \(C\). By \(\texttt{lem:parameterized-blind-path-witness}\), there is a balanced-split sequence in \(\mathcal M_n\) such that every fitted path is zero,
\[
 \|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2=\gamma B^2>0,
\]
and the infimum in the proposed right-hand side tends to zero. Consequently, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 C\inf_{k,\lambda}
 \left\{\lambda^{\bar\beta}+\frac{s_{k,n}}{\lambda}\right\}
 <\gamma B^2.
\]
The event asserted by the proposed oracle inequality is then empty, so its probability is zero rather than at least \(1-\eta=3/4\). Since \(C\) was arbitrary, no finite universal constant establishes that inequality.

For any \(\rho\in(0,B^2)\), setting \(\gamma=\rho/B^2\) gives strong error and training-coverage defect exactly \(\rho\). Letting \(\gamma\uparrow1\) gives supremum \(B^2\); attaining \(B^2\) would require \(\gamma=1\), which collapses the first support point and lies outside the genuine two-point family. By \(\texttt{prop:path-restricted-selection-frontier}\), arbitrary measurable or oracle selection among these paths and every convex aggregation of them remain zero because their convex hull is \(\{0\}\).

For clipping, \(\texttt{prop:universal-clipping-constant-sharp}\) proves both directions. Its upper-bound argument obtains \(\|\widehat h\|_{L_2(P_X)}\le B\) from the coefficient and basis envelopes. It obtains \(\|h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}\le B\) from \(\texttt{lem:operator-contraction}\), the source representation, the source radius, and the positive-contraction fractional-power lemma. The triangle inequality gives \(4B^2\). The explicit missed-support construction in that proposition violates every smaller coefficient on an event of positive probability, establishing supremum-sharpness.

All counterexamples select or aggregate only already-trained functions. They therefore establish an architecture-level obstruction, not an impossibility for retraining, richer critics, noncollapsed cross-candidate aggregation, or NPIV and DeepIV estimation generally.
\end{proof}
