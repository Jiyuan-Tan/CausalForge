By \(\texttt{def:two-coordinate-configuration}\), \(X=Z=(U,V)\), where \(U\), \(V\), and \(\xi\) are mutually independent Rademacher random variables. Hence, for every \(h\in L_X\),
\[
 (Th)(U,V)=E_P[h(X)\mid Z]=E_P[h(U,V)\mid U,V]=h(U,V).
\]
Thus \(T=I\), after the natural identification of \(L_2(P_X)\) with \(L_2(P_Z)\). The same construction directly sets
\[
 w=h_0=a_0U+b_0V.
\]
These assignments obey the stated envelope and source conditions. Indeed, \(|a_0|,|b_0|\le 1/4\), \(B=2\), and therefore
\[
 |Y|=|a_0U+b_0V+\xi|\le \tfrac14+\tfrac14+1<2=B
\]
almost surely. Independence and centering give
\[
 \|w\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2=E_P[(a_0U+b_0V)^2]=a_0^2+b_0^2\le \tfrac18<B^2.
\]
Moreover, \(T^*T=I\), so \((T^*T)^{\beta/2}w=w=h_0\), consistently with \(\texttt{ass:source-representation}\) and \(\texttt{ass:source-radius}\).

Suppose that every learned image equals its ideal image. From \(\texttt{def:learned-images}\),
\[
 \alpha_{k,n}=\max_{1\le j\le d_{H,k}}\|\widehat g_{kj}-g_{kj}\|_{L_2(P_Z)}=0,
 \qquad k\in\{1,2\}.
\]
In particular, \(\alpha_{1,n}=\alpha_{2,n}=0\). Since \(T=I\), the ideal images of the common target basis are
\[
 g_{k1}=T\phi_{k1}=U,
 \qquad
 g_{k2}=T\phi_{k2}=V.
\]
Candidate \(2\) uses \(J_2=\{1\}\), so ideal-image equality and \(B=2\) imply
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal F}_2=\{sU:|s|\le2\}.
\]
For any \(f=sU\) in this class,
\[
 E_P[Vf(Z)]=sE_P[VU]=sE_P[V]E_P[U]=0,
\]
which proves that candidate \(2\)'s critic is orthogonal to the \(V\) coordinate. At the blind fit \(a_0U\), the residual is
\[
 Y-a_0U=b_0V+\xi.
\]
Thus, using mutual independence, the zero means of the three Rademacher variables, and \(E_P[U^2]=1\), we obtain for \(|s|\le2\)
\[
\begin{aligned}
 2E_P[(Y-a_0U)sU]-E_P[(sU)^2]
 &=2s\bigl(b_0E_P[VU]+E_P[\xi U]\bigr)-s^2E_P[U^2]\\
 &=-s^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
The right-hand side is nonpositive for every feasible \(s\) and equals zero at \(s=0\). Consequently,
\[
 \sup_{f\in\widehat{\mathcal F}_2}
 \left\{2E_P[(Y-a_0U)f(Z)]-E_P[f(Z)^2]\right\}=0.
\]

Candidate \(1\) uses \(J_1=\{1,2\}\), and hence under ideal-image equality
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal F}_1
 =\{sU+tV:|s|+|t|\le2\}.
\]
Taking \((s,t)=(0,1)\) shows that \(V\in\widehat{\mathcal F}_1\). By \(\texttt{def:common-audit}\), the absolute convex hull defining \(\widehat{\mathcal F}^{\mathrm A}\) contains each generating critic class, so
\[
 V\in\widehat{\mathcal F}_1\subseteq\widehat{\mathcal F}^{\mathrm A}.
\]
Furthermore, \(|b_0|\le1/4\) makes \(f=b_0V\) a feasible member of \(\widehat{\mathcal F}_1\), hence also of \(\widehat{\mathcal F}^{\mathrm A}\). Evaluating the common-audit population criterion at this witness, and using \(E_P[\xi V]=0\) and \(E_P[V^2]=1\), yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 2E_P[(Y-a_0U)b_0V]-E_P[(b_0V)^2]
 &=2E_P[(b_0V+\xi)b_0V]-b_0^2E_P[V^2]\\
 &=2b_0^2-b_0^2\\
 &=b_0^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Taking the supremum over \(\widehat{\mathcal F}^{\mathrm A}\) therefore gives
\[
 \sup_{f\in\widehat{\mathcal F}^{\mathrm A}}
 \left\{2E_P[(Y-a_0U)f(Z)]-E_P[f(Z)^2]\right\}\ge b_0^2,
\]
which proves every assertion.
