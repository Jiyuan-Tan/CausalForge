\begin{proof}
Fix an arbitrary finite value of \(C\). Apply \(\texttt{lem:parameterized-blind-path-witness}\) with \(B=2\), \(\gamma=1/128\), and \(\eta=1/4\). It yields a sequence of configurations in \(\mathcal M_n\), along \(n=3m\) with balanced deterministic splits, satisfying the two source assumptions and having exact learned images. For the benchmark index \(k_0=2\), the lemma gives
\[
 \varepsilon_{H,2}=\varepsilon_{A,2}=\alpha_{2,n}=0
\]
and, for every realization of all three sample splits,
\[
 \widehat h_{k,\lambda}=0
 \quad\text{for every }(k,\lambda)\in\mathcal K\times\Lambda_n.
\]
Because the selector is restricted to this trained path, \(\widehat h=0\) for every sample. The same lemma therefore yields
\[
 \|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2
 =\gamma B^2
 =\frac1{128}\,4
 =\frac1{32}
\]
almost surely.

On this sequence, the lemma also states that, for every fixed finite value of \(C\), the proposed oracle functional satisfies
\[
 R_n:=C\inf_{k\in\mathcal K,\lambda\in\Lambda_n}
 \left\{\lambda^{\bar\beta}+\frac{s_{k,n}}{\lambda}\right\}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Choose \(m\) sufficiently large that \(R_{3m}<1/32\). Since
\[
 \min\{4B^2,R_{3m}\}\le R_{3m}<\frac1{32}
 =\|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2
\]
for every sample, the proposed oracle event is empty and has probability zero. In particular, its probability is not at least \(1-\eta=3/4\). Because \(C\) was arbitrary, no universal finite constant can validate the proposed inequality uniformly over the declared configuration class.

It remains to identify what survives this counterexample. By \(\texttt{prop:universal-clipping-constant-sharp}\), under \(\mathcal M_n\) and the same two source assumptions, every realization satisfies
\[
 \|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2\le4B^2.
\]
That proposition also states that for every \(c<4\) there is a configuration in the same class and an event of positive sampling probability on which the squared error exceeds \(cB^2\). Hence the coefficient \(4\) is supremum-sharp. The clipping conclusion is therefore valid, but the vanishing oracle term is impossible without additional control of the training critic's coverage.
\end{proof}
