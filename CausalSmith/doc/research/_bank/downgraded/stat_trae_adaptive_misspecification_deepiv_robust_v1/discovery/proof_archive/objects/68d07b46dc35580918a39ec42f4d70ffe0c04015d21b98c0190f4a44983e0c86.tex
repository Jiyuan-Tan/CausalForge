Because \(G_k\) is finite-dimensional, it is a closed subspace of \(L_2(P_Z)\). Since \(|Y|\le B\), conditional Jensen's inequality gives \(r\in L_2(P_Z)\), so the Hilbert projection \(\Pi_{G_k}r\) exists. For every \(g\in G_k\) and \(t\in\mathbb R\), minimality of the projection gives
\[
 \|r-\Pi_{G_k}r\|_{L_2(P_Z)}^2
 \le \|r-(\Pi_{G_k}r+t g)\|_{L_2(P_Z)}^2.
\]
Expanding the right-hand side and using both signs of \(t\) yields
\[
 \langle r-\Pi_{G_k}r,g\rangle_{L_2(P_Z)}=0.
\]
Thus \(r-\Pi_{G_k}r\perp G_k\).

Let \(q^\dagger=r-Th^\dagger\). Under \(\texttt{ass:pseudotrue-attainment}\), \(h^\dagger\) minimizes \(\|Th-r\|_{L_2(P_Z)}^2\) over the linear space \(L_X\). For arbitrary \(v\in L_X\), the real quadratic
\[
 t\longmapsto \|T(h^\dagger+t v)-r\|_{L_2(P_Z)}^2
\]
has its minimum at \(t=0\). Its linear coefficient therefore vanishes, giving
\[
 \langle q^\dagger,Tv\rangle_{L_2(P_Z)}=0.
\]
For every \(h\in L_X\),
\[
 q_h=q^\dagger-T(h-h^\dagger),
 \qquad
 \|q_h\|_{L_2(P_Z)}^2
 =\|q^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_Z)}^2
  +\|T(h-h^\dagger)\|_{L_2(P_Z)}^2.
\]
Compactness of \(\mathcal H_k\) makes the infimum defining \(\varepsilon_{H,k}\) attainable at some \(h_k^\circ\in\mathcal H_k\). Conditional Jensen's inequality then gives
\[
 \|q_{h_k^\circ}-q_{h^\dagger}\|_{L_2(P_Z)}
 =\|T(h_k^\circ-h^\dagger)\|_{L_2(P_Z)}
 \le\|h_k^\circ-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 =\varepsilon_{H,k}.
\]
This is the claimed residual decomposition and target-approximation charge.

Finally, consider any ideal critic element \(f=\sum_{j\in J_k}u_jg_{kj}\) with \(\sum_{j\in J_k}|u_j|\le B\), and pair it with \(\widehat f=\sum_{j\in J_k}u_j\widehat g_{kj}\). By the triangle inequality and the definition of \(\alpha_{k,n}\),
\[
 \|\widehat f-f\|_{L_2(P_Z)}
 \le\sum_{j\in J_k}|u_j|\,\|\widehat g_{kj}-g_{kj}\|_{L_2(P_Z)}
 \le B\alpha_{k,n}.
\]
Thus replacement of the ideal images by their learned versions costs at most \(B\alpha_{k,n}\), as asserted.
