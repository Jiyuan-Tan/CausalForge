If the selector chooses candidate \(k\), then \(\widehat h\in\mathcal H_k\). Write \(\widehat h=\sum_j\widehat\theta_j\phi_{kj}\). The basis and coefficient bounds give
\[
 \|\widehat h\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 \le\|\widehat h\|_{L_\infty(P_X)}
 \le\sum_j|\widehat\theta_j|\,\|\phi_{kj}\|_{L_\infty(P_X)}
 \le B.
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:operator-contraction}\), \(\|T\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le1\), hence the positive self-adjoint operator \(A=T^*T\) satisfies \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le1\). Since \(\beta/2\in(0,1]\), \(\texttt{lem:positive-contraction-fractional-power}\), the source representation, and the source radius yield
\[
 \|h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 =\|A^{\beta/2}w\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 \le\|w\|_{L_2(P_X)}
 \le B.
\]
The triangle inequality now gives
\[
 \|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2
 \le(\|\widehat h\|_{L_2(P_X)}+\|h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)})^2
 \le4B^2.
\]

To prove sharpness, fix \(c<4\), choose \(\gamma\in(0,1)\) with \(4\gamma>c\), and again let \(e_j(x)=\mathbf 1\{x=j\}\) under
\[
 P(X=Z=2)=\gamma,
 \qquad
 P(X=Z=1)=1-\gamma,
 \qquad
 Y=B e_2(X).
\]
Then \(T=I\), \(h^\dagger=B e_2\), and the source conditions hold with \(w=h^\dagger\). Use one candidate with basis \((e_1,e_2)\), target class
\[
 \mathcal H_1=\{a e_2:-B\le a\le B\},
\]
blind critic coordinate \(J_1=\{1\}\), exact learned images, and \(\mathcal A_0=\{0\}\). Choose a countable dense enumeration whose first element is \(-B e_2\) and which also contains zero. These choices satisfy every condition defining \(\mathcal M_n\).

Let \(E\) be the event that no training observation has \(X=2\). Independence and the positive split size give
\[
 P(E)=(1-\gamma)^{n_{\mathrm{tr}}}>0.
\]
For \(h=a e_2\) and every blind critic \(f=u e_1\), the moment product is identically zero. On \(E\), the empirical regularizer also vanishes because \(P_{\mathrm{tr}}e_2=0\). Hence
\[
 A_{1,\lambda,\ell}=0
\]
for every enumerated target and every \(\lambda\in\Lambda_n\). The least-index rule selects \(-B e_2\) for every tuning value. Since there is only one candidate and all its tuning paths coincide on \(E\), validation also returns \(\widehat h=-B e_2\). Therefore, on \(E\),
\[
 \|\widehat h-h^\dagger\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2
 =\|-2B e_2\|_{L_2(P_X)}^2
 =4\gamma B^2
 >cB^2.
\]
Thus no coefficient smaller than four is a universal deterministic bound. As \(\gamma\uparrow1\), the ratio approaches four, while for every \(\gamma<1\) the missed-support event remains of strictly positive probability. This is the asserted supremum-sharpness.
