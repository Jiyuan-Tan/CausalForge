Let \(\mathscr C=\sigma(W_i:i\in I_{\mathrm{cr}})\). We work conditionally on \(\mathscr C\), so every learned image \(\widehat g_{kj}\) is fixed.

Fix \((k,\lambda,\ell)\). Parameterize \(\widehat{\mathcal F}_k\) by the compact set
\[
 \mathbb B_{1,k}=\left\{u\in\mathbb R^{|J_k|}:\sum_{j\in J_k}|u_j|\le B\right\}.
\]
For fixed training data, the expression inside the supremum defining \(A_{k,\lambda,\ell}\) is a quadratic polynomial in \(u\), hence is continuous. Its supremum over \(\mathbb B_{1,k}\) equals its supremum over the countable dense subset \(\mathbb B_{1,k}\cap\mathbb Q^{|J_k|}\). For fixed rational \(u\), that expression is a finite sum of measurable random variables. Consequently \(A_{k,\lambda,\ell}\), being a countable supremum of measurable functions, is measurable.

Write
\[
 a_{k,\lambda}=\inf_{m\ge1}A_{k,\lambda,m}.
\]
This is a countable infimum of measurable functions. The scores are finite and bounded below by zero because the critic class contains \(0\), the dual supremum is nonnegative, and the regularization term is nonnegative. Hence the set
\[
 N_{k,\lambda}=\left\{\ell\ge1:A_{k,\lambda,\ell}\le a_{k,\lambda}+n^{-1}\right\}
\]
is nonempty by the defining property of an infimum. If \(L_{k,\lambda}=\min N_{k,\lambda}\), then for every \(m\ge1\),
\[
 \{L_{k,\lambda}=m\}
 =\{A_{k,\lambda,m}\le a_{k,\lambda}+n^{-1}\}
  \cap\bigcap_{j<m}\{A_{k,\lambda,j}>a_{k,\lambda}+n^{-1}\},
\]
so \(L_{k,\lambda}\) is measurable. Therefore \(\widehat h_{k,\lambda}=h_{k,L_{k,\lambda}}\) is measurable and, by construction,
\[
 A_{k,\lambda,L_{k,\lambda}}
 \le \inf_{m\ge1}A_{k,\lambda,m}+n^{-1}.
\]

It remains to treat validation. Conditional on \(\mathscr C\), the span of \(\widehat{\mathcal F}^{\mathrm A}\) is finite-dimensional: it is generated by the fixed finite-dimensional audit dictionary and finitely many learned images. The closure of the bounded absolute convex hull in that span is compact. The validation quadratic is continuous in finite-dimensional coordinates, and its supremum is unchanged on taking this closure. Choosing a countable dense subset of the compact coordinate set expresses every \(\widehat V_{k,\lambda}\) as a countable supremum of measurable finite-sample quadratics. Thus every validation score is measurable. Finally, \(\mathcal K\times\Lambda_n\) is finite by \(\texttt{ass:finite-candidates}\) and \(\texttt{def:grid-and-scale}\). The lexicographically least minimizer of its finitely many measurable scores is measurable. This proves all three assertions; the deterministic fallback is never invoked because \(N_{k,\lambda}\ne\varnothing\), but it is measurable as well.
