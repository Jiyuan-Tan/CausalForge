For fixed \(a,s,t,M\) with the separate conditions \(t>1/2\), \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), and \(a<2t(2s+1)\), along every triangular-array sequence with \(m/n\to\kappa\), the deterministic choices \(h=h_n\), \(J_\mu=J_{\mu,n}\), and \(J_r=J_{r,n}\) give
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}\Bigl\{\mathbb E_{\mathbb P_{n,m}}[(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau)^2]\Bigr\}^{1/2}\le C\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\]
Uniformly over the same sequence, the fold-trained dual extensions evaluated on their held-out source folds obey
\[
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\{\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\}^2\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h_n\}=O_P\!\left(1+\log(1/h_n)\mathbf 1\{a=1\}+h_n^{1-a}\mathbf 1\{a>1\}\right)
\]
and
\[
\frac{\max_{i:X_i\ge h_n}\{\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\}^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n\{\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\}^2\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h_n\}}=o_P(1),
\]
with the ratio defined as zero when the denominator is zero. The threshold \(t>1/2\) controls approximation of the one-dimensional density-ratio calibration sieve, whereas the product relation controls the distinct nuisance-product remainder. These out-of-sample stability bounds are conclusions to be derived from the primal program, normalized dual extension, spline geometry, and primitive density conditions, not model assumptions.
