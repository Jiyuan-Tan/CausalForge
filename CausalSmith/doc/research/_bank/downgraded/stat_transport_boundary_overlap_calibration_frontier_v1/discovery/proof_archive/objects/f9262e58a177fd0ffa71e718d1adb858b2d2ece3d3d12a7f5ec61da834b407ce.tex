\textbf{Proof.}
Fix arbitrary \(s>0\) and \(t>0\), and set \(a=0\).  By the definitions of the density factors, for every \(x\in(0,1]\),
\[
 f_S(x)=x^a g_S(x)=g_S(x),
 \qquad
 f_T(x)=g_T(x),
 \qquad
 r(x)=x^{-a}\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}.
\]
The source- and target-boundary-factor assumptions state that
\[
 c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+},
 \qquad
 c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+},
 \qquad x\in[0,1].
\]
Because \(c_{S,-}>0\), division is valid and gives
\[
 \frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
 \le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},
 \qquad 0<x\le1.
\]

Since \(a=0<1\), the first branch in the definition of \(h_n\) applies:
\[
 h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}.
\]
The inequality \(s>0\) ensures that \(s+1>0\), so exponent arithmetic yields
\[
 h_n^{s+1}
 =\left(n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\right)^{s+1}
 =n^{-1/2}.
\]
Also \(h_n\downarrow0\).  Applying Lemma \(\texttt{lem:riesz-threshold}\) in its \(a<1\) branch along this bandwidth gives
\[
 \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx\asymp1,
\]
and hence the integral is \(O(1)\).  Equivalently, the same conclusion follows directly from
\[
 0\le \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx
 =\int_{h_n}^1\frac{g_T(x)^2}{g_S(x)}\,dx
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}^2}{c_{S,-}}(1-h_n)
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}^2}{c_{S,-}},
\]
which also makes the uniform constant explicit.

It remains to prove positivity of the executable extension.  Fix a fold \(k\) for which both interior training measures have positive total mass, and put
\[
 I^T_{k,n}=\{j\in\mathcal T^T_k:X_j^T\ge h_n\}.
\]
Positive target interior mass implies \(I^T_{k,n}\ne\varnothing\) and \(|\mathcal T^T_k|>0\).  Positive source and target interior masses exclude the zero-mass fallback in Definition \(\texttt{def:ricot-weights}\), so either its exact-feasibility branch or its penalized branch is used.  On either branch the selected calibration and target-marginal multipliers are finite real vectors.  Thus, for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\) and every \(j\in I^T_{k,n}\),
\[
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{
 c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
 +\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
 \widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 +\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}
 {\eta_0}\right\}>0.
\]
Here the exponent is finite and \(\eta_0>0\).  Consequently, the bracket defining the raw KKT continuation is strictly positive:
\[
 \frac1{|\mathcal T^T_k|}
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal T^T_k}
 \mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h_n\}
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{
 c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
 +\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
 \widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 +\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}
 {\eta_0}\right\}>0.
\]
The outer exponent \(\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)\) is positive because \(\eta_0,\lambda_0>0\).  Therefore
\[
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0,
 \qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\]
At \(a=0\), the deterministic cap is
\[
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h_n^{-a}
 =\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}>0.
\]
The capped RiCOT extension is the minimum of this positive cap and the positive raw continuation.  Hence
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 =\min\!\left\{
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x),
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}
 \right\}>0,
 \qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\]

Every conclusion is deterministic conditional on the positive-interior-mass branch.  No step used \(t>1/2\) or \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\); the proof requires only the declared domains \(s>0\) and \(t>0\).  Finally, pointwise positivity alone gives no empirical norm bound, no nonzero realized residual sum, and no root-risk conclusion. \(\square\)
