For fixed \(a\ge0\), \(s>0\), \(t>0\), \(M\), \(\varepsilon\), \(c_{S,-}\), \(c_{S,+}\), \(c_{T,-}\), and \(c_{T,+}\), suppose \(M\ge a+1\) and \(c_{T,-}<1<c_{T,+}\). There is a constant \(c>0\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\) and \(m\),
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\]
At \(a=1\), the source term is \(c\sqrt{\log n/n}\); it is obtained from a multiscale sum of disjoint boundary-shell bumps and has no hidden polynomial loss.
