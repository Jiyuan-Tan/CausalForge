Let \(B_h=(c_{T,+}/c_{S,-})h^{-a}\), let \(u_k=\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\), and let \(w_k=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}=\min\{u_k,B_h\}\).  The boundary-factor assumptions give, for \(x\in[h,1]\),
\[
0<r(x)=x^{-a}\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}\le B_h.
\]
Because metric projection onto \([0,B_h]\) is nonexpansive relative to every point of that interval,
\[
0\le w_k(x)\le B_h,
\qquad
|w_k(x)-r(x)|\le |u_k(x)-r(x)|.
\]
For every held-out function \(v\), direct subtraction of the definitions gives the exact cap-defect identity
\[
\Delta_{w}(v)=\Delta_{u}(v)+\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\{u_{k_S(i)}(X_i)-B_h\}_+v(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}.
\]
Consequently, the raw training-atom spline equations do not imply calibration of the capped held-out weight.  For any probability measure \(Q\) and square-integrable \(v\), Cauchy--Schwarz and nonexpansiveness yield
\[
\left|\int\{w_k-r\}v\,dQ\right|
\le \|u_k-r\|_{L^2(Q)}\|v\|_{L^2(Q)}.
\]
The cap by itself yields only
\[
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_{k_S(i)}(X_i)^2\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\le B_h^2,
\]
whereas the claimed oracle order is \(1\), \(\log(1/h)\), or \(h^{1-a}\).  On a positive-interior-mass branch, strict convexity gives uniqueness of the primal plan, and the stipulated minimum-norm and lexicographic rules select the dual variables; on the zero-interior-mass branch all relevant outputs are deterministic.  Thus the construction is total and the displayed deterministic inequalities are proved.  What remains unproved is the quantitative theorem-specific assertion
\[
\|u_k-r\|_{L^2(P_S;[h_n,1])}^2=O_P\!\left(1+\log(1/h_n)\mathbf 1\{a=1\}+h_n^{1-a}\mathbf 1\{a>1\}\right)
\]
together with a uniform held-out maximal-leverage bound and a calibration approximation strong enough to control the nuisance-product remainder.  Those are properties of the optimizer and its continuation, not consequences of the pointwise cap.
