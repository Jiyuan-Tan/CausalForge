The proved class has a one-dimensional boundary, fixed \(a\ge0\), \(s>0\), and \(t>0\), known randomized-treatment propensity bounded by \(\varepsilon\), bounded outcomes, Hölder armwise responses and density factors, polynomial source depletion, and a target density bounded away from zero and infinity. The independent \(m^{-1/2}\) lower bound and the nonadaptation theorem retain strict slack \(c_{T,-}<1<c_{T,+}\); sequence results impose \(m/n\to\kappa\) only where stated. The formal contributions are the one-sided minimax lower phase diagram, the algebraically rate-equivalent truncated-oracle envelope, the exact finite-sample estimator certificate, fixed-class honest coverage, and the nonadaptation lower bound. No data-driven minimax upper bound, frontier-order expected length, unrestricted adaptive interval, or executable positive self-similar adaptation theorem is claimed. Multivariate boundary manifolds, observational treatment assignment, and sensitivity analysis for mean transportability remain outside scope.
