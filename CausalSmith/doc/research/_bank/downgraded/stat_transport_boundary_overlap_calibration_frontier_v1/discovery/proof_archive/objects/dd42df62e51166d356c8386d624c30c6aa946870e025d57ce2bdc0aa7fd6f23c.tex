For each fold \(k\), retain the untruncated order-\(p\) boundary local polynomial
\[
\widehat G_{k,h}=\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(X_i)\mathbf R_h(X_i)^\top+\lambda_nI_{p+1},
\qquad
\widehat\alpha_{k,h}=\widehat G_{k,h}^{-1}\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(X_i)Z_i.
\]
For the interior, define
\[
\widehat G^{\mu,(-k)}_{h,J}
=\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}X_i^{-a}\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)^\top,
\]
\[
\widehat s^{\mu,(-k)}_{h,J}
=\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}X_i^{-a}\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)Z_i,
\qquad
\widehat\beta^{\mu,(-k)}_{h,J}
=\left(\widehat G^{\mu,(-k)}_{h,J}+\lambda_nI_J\right)^{-1}\widehat s^{\mu,(-k)}_{h,J}.
\]
The inverse exists because \(\lambda_n>0\).  On a bad-Gram or empty-interior branch the interior coefficient is the zero vector.  Define the untruncated linear fit
\[
\widehat\mu^{(-k)}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbf R_h(x)^\top\widehat\alpha_{k,h},&0\le x<h,\\
\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)^\top\widehat\beta^{\mu,(-k)}_{h,J_{\mu,n}},&h\le x\le1.
\end{cases}
\]
Thus, conditionally on all source and target covariates and treatment assignments, \(\widehat\mu^{(-k)}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)\) is a completely specified linear function of the source pseudo-outcomes.
