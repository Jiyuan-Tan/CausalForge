For arbitrary \(h\in(0,1)\), define
\[
w_i=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\},
\]
and let
\[
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}
=\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_T(j))}(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}
+\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\{Z_i-\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_S(i))}(X_i)\}.
\]
Then the exact identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx
={}&\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left[\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx\right]\\
&+C_{n,m}(h)-\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu).
\end{aligned}
\]
The response-smoothness assumptions imply \(\mu\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\), and therefore
\[
|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu)|\le2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.
\]
The four source lines in \(\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}^2\) equal the sum of the within-fold sample variances of \(\widehat U^R+\widehat U^B\), so the displayed standard-error statistic is nonnegative. Moreover, if \(z_{1-\gamma/4}\) is the \((1-\gamma/4)\)-quantile of \(N(0,1)\), then every realized interval obeys
\[
\operatorname{length}(\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}})
\le2\{\widehat B_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}+\widehat E_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}
+2z_{1-\gamma/4}\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}.
\]
Finally, the standard one-dimensional Hölder entropy integral \(\int_0^1\epsilon^{-1/(2s)}\,d\epsilon\) is finite exactly when \(s>1/2\). These algebraic facts do not assert the uniform integrability, Gaussian approximation, or multiplier validity required for honesty.
