For \(j\in\mathcal V^T_k\) and \(i\in\mathcal V^S_k\), define the held-out target and interior-residual summands \[\widehat U^T_{j,h,J}=\widehat\mu_h^{(-k)}(X_j^T),\qquad \widehat U^R_{i,h,J}=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\{Z_i-\widehat\mu_h^{(-k)}(X_i)\}.\] The source-side linear effect of estimating the boundary polynomial in every fold whose training set contains source observation \(i\) is \[\widehat U^B_{i,h,J}=\frac{n}{m}\sum_{k:i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\frac{\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}}{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\left[\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_k}\mathbf 1\{X_j^T<h\}\widehat d_{k,h}(X_j^T)\mathbf R_h(X_j^T)^\top\widehat G_{k,h}^{-1}\mathbf R_h(X_i)\right]\{Z_i-\mathbf R_h(X_i)^\top\widehat\alpha_{k,h}\}.\] Thus \(n^{-1}\sum_i\widehat U^B_{i,h,J}\) is exactly the displayed first-order boundary local-polynomial training contribution to the target plug-in average, with the truncation derivative fixed by \(\widehat d_{k,h}\). For any fold-indexed numbers \((W_u)\), write \(\overline W_k^S=(N_k^S)^{-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}W_i\) and \(\overline W_k^T=(N_k^T)^{-1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_k}W_j\). Define the fully observable standard error by \[\begin{aligned}\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J}^2={}&\frac1{m^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^T}{N_k^T-1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_k}(\widehat U^T_{j,h,J}-\overline{\widehat U^T}_{k,h,J})^2\\&+\frac1{n^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^S}{N_k^S-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(\widehat U^R_{i,h,J}-\overline{\widehat U^R}_{k,h,J})^2\\&+\frac1{n^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^S}{N_k^S-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(\widehat U^B_{i,h,J}-\overline{\widehat U^B}_{k,h,J})^2\\&+\frac2{n^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^S}{N_k^S-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(\widehat U^R_{i,h,J}-\overline{\widehat U^R}_{k,h,J})(\widehat U^B_{i,h,J}-\overline{\widehat U^B}_{k,h,J}).\end{aligned}\] The four lines are respectively the target variance, interior source variance, boundary source variance, and their within-source covariance. There is no primitive source--target covariance because \(\mathbb P_{n,m}=P_S^{\otimes n}\otimes P_T^{\otimes m}\) is the product experiment; randomness of fold-trained coefficients is retained in every displayed summand and its asymptotic remainder is controlled in the honesty theorem. A term with fold size at most one is defined to be zero.
