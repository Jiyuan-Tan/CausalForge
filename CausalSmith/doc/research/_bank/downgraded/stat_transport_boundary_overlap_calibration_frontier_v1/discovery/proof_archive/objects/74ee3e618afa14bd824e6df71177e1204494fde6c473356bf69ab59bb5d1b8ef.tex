For \(h\in(0,1)\) and positive integers \(J_\mu,J_r\), define the observable realized-design discrepancy
\[
B_X(h,J_\mu,J_r)=
\sup_{\substack{v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\\ \|v\|_\infty\le1}}
\left|\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_\mu,J_r)v(X_i)-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m v(X_j^T)\right|.
\]
This is the support function of the symmetric convex Hölder ball evaluated at an observable signed empirical measure, hence is a well-defined observable convex verification object. No polynomial-time representation and no stochastic or frontier-order bound for \(B_X(h,J_\mu,J_r)\) is included in the construction.
