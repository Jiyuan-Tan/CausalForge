At \(h=h_n\), the weighted direct-series construction satisfies, uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\),
\[
 \mathbb P_{n,m}\{\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)^c\}\le C\exp\{-c\log^3(en)\},
\]
\[
 \|\widehat\mu-\mu\|_{L^2(g_S;[h_n,1])}
 =O_P\{\delta_{s,h_n}+\lambda_nJ_{\mu,n}\},
 \qquad
 \|\widehat q^{\mathrm{DS}}-q_0\|_{L^2(g_S;[h_n,1])}
 =O_P\{\delta_{t,h_n}+\lambda_nJ_{r,n}\}.
\]
The source validation weights have empirical squared norm \(O_P\{V_a(h_n)\}\) and maximum squared leverage \(o_P(1)\).  For a boundary spline cell the exact weighted second moment is bounded by \(CW_a(h,J)\); in particular it is of order \(J^{a-1}\) when \(0<a<1\) and \(hJ\to0\), rather than order \(J^{-1}\).
