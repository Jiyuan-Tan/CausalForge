For fixed \(a,s,t,M\) with \(M\ge a+1\) and \(t>0\), along every sequence with \(m/n\to\kappa\), there is \(c>0\) such that
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c\left\{m^{-1/2}+\begin{cases}
n^{-1/2},&a<1,\\
\sqrt{\log n/n},&a=1,\\
n^{-(s+1)/(2s+a+1)},&a>1.
\end{cases}\right\}.
\]
This lower phase diagram requires no strict slack in the target-density bounds. For the oracle boundary-local-polynomial linear weight \(\omega_{h_n}^o\), uniformly over \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\),
\[
|b_{h_n}(\mu;P)|\le b_{h_n}^o(P)\le C h_n^{s+1}
\]
and
\[
m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}\|\omega_{h_n}^o\|_{L^2(P_S)}
\asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
The bias allowance is class-sharp when \(a>0\). At \(a=0\), with \(\alpha=\min\{t,1\}\), the stronger uniform bound \(\sup_P b_{h_n}^o(P)\le C h_n^{s+1+\alpha}\) holds, but the order-\(h_n^{s+1}\) allowance remains of the same order as the regular source standard deviation. Thus the theorem gives a minimax lower phase diagram and a matching boundary-corrected oracle allowance--standard-deviation benchmark; it asserts neither a data-measurable minimax upper bound nor frontier attainment.
