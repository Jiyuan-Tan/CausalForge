\textbf{Proof.}
Trial randomization, consistency, and the known propensity give
\[
\mathbb E(Z\mid X=x)=\mu_1(x)-\mu_0(x)=\mu(x),
\qquad |Z|\le\varepsilon^{-1}.
\tag{5}
\]
On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n,J_n)\), the normal equations and the definition of \(\Gamma_i\) give the exact identity
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{pl}}_{h_n,J_n}=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)Z_i.
\tag{6}
\]
Add and subtract \(\sum_i\Gamma_i\mu(X_i)\) and the target empirical mean of \(\mu\). Conditional on all covariates, (5) and independence of the source observations imply
\[
\mathbb E\left[\left\{\sum_i\Gamma_i(Z_i-\mu(X_i))\right\}^2\middle|X_1,\ldots,X_n,X_1^T,\ldots,X_m^T\right]
\le\varepsilon^{-2}\sum_i\Gamma_i^2
\le \frac{CV_a(h_n)}n.
\tag{7}
\]
The localization lemma bounds the root mean square of the remaining noise-free discrepancy by
\[
C\{h_n^{s+1}+J_n^{-(s+t)}+n^{-1/2}V_a(h_n)^{1/2}+m^{-1/2}\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Off the good branch the estimator is zero and \(|\tau|\le1\), so its root-mean-square contribution is at most the square root of the exponentially small branch-failure probability.

By the definition of \(J_n\),
\[
J_n^{-(s+t)}\le\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{9}
\]
The oracle-truncation envelope gives
\[
h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}V_a(h_n)^{1/2}\asymp\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{10}
\]
Minkowski's inequality applied to (7)--(10) proves the uniform root-risk bound. The probability, coefficient-norm, and maximum-leverage conclusions are the first two conclusions of the localization lemma. \(\square\)
