\textbf{Proof.}
The broadened source-only lower phase diagram in \(\mathrm{lem:source\text{-}only\text{-}frontier\text{-}lower}\) gives a constant \(c_S>0\), independent of \(n,m\), such that
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c_S\rho_n(a,s).
\]
That result is proved on a subexperiment with common target law and uses no strict target-density slack and no nuisance-sieve smoothness threshold.

It remains to obtain the independent target-sample floor. Use the target-density alternatives constructed in \(\mathrm{lem:boundary\text{-}bump}\), keeping their source law common. Reduce the fixed perturbation multiplier \(\delta_1\), if necessary, so that the Kullback--Leibler divergence between the two complete observed-data laws is at most a fixed \(\varkappa<2\). Their parameter separation is still
\[
\Delta_m\ge c_Tm^{-1/2}
\]
for a constant \(c_T>0\). Applying \(\mathrm{lem:two\text{-}point\text{-}testing\text{-}risk}\) to any estimator \(\widehat\tau\) and then taking the infimum over estimators gives
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)
\ge \frac{c_T}{2\sqrt2}
\left(1-\sqrt{\varkappa/2}\right)^{1/2}m^{-1/2}
=c_T'm^{-1/2}.
\]
Both alternatives lie in the same broadened model because the construction used only \(t>0\).

Combining the two inequalities and using \(\max\{u,v\}\ge(u+v)/2\) yields
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)
\ge c\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\]
At \(a=1\), the source-only helper uses its multiscale sign mixture and gives \(\rho_n(1,s)=\sqrt{\log n/n}\) directly, so no polynomial factor is lost. \(\square\)
