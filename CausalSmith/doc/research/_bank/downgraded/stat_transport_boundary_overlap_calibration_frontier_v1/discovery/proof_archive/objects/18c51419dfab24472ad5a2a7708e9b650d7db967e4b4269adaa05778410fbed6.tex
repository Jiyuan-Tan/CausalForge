Fix integers \(n,m\ge2\), a bandwidth \(h\in(0,1)\), and \(\gamma\in(0,1/2)\). For every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), the unchanged spliced estimator and the observable coefficients in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}linear\text{-}coefficients}\) satisfy the exact identity
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})Z_i
\]
and the exact error decomposition
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau
={}&\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\mu(X_i)-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\right\}\\
&+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)-\tau\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The middle term is bounded in absolute value by
\[
B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\le\overline B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}),
\]
where \(\overline B_X\) is the finite certified jet-LP radius in \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}envelope}\). Consequently,
\[
\mathbb P_{n,m}\!\left\{\left|\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau\right|
\le \overline B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_T(m)\right\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
For any realized covariate design with \(\overline B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\le b\) and \(\sum_i\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2\le g^2\), the displayed certificate radius is at most
\[
b+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)}\,g+\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)/m}.
\]
This is a finite-sample execution certificate. It makes no stochastic-order, localization, leverage, minimax-attainment, or expected-length claim for \(B_X\), \(\overline B_X\), or the coefficient norm.
