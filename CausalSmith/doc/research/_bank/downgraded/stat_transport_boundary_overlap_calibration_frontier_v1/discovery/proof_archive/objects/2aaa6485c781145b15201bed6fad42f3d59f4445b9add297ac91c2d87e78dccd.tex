For \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), let \(P_S\) and \(P_T\) denote its source and target component laws and define
\[
G_h^{\circ}(P)=\int_0^h\mathbf R_h(x)\mathbf R_h(x)^\top f_S(x)\,dx,
\qquad
q_h^{\circ}(P)=\int_0^h\mathbf R_h(x)f_T(x)\,dx.
\]
Whenever \(G_h^{\circ}(P)\) is invertible, define
\[
w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)=\mathbf 1\{x<h\}\mathbf R_h(x)^\top
\{G_h^{\circ}(P)\}^{-1}q_h^{\circ}(P),
\qquad
\omega_h^{\circ}(x;P)=w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)+r(x)\mathbf 1\{x\ge h\}.
\]
For \(v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\) with \(\|v\|_\infty\le1\), define the signed boundary approximation bias and its class allowance by
\[
\mathfrak b_h(v;P)=\int_0^h\{w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)f_S(x)-f_T(x)\}v(x)\,dx,
\qquad
b_h^{\circ}(P)=\sup_{\substack{v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\\\|v\|_\infty\le1}}|\mathfrak b_h(v;P)|.
\]
These are oracle population objects because they use \(f_S\) and \(f_T\). Their invertibility, polynomial reproduction, norm orders, and bias bounds are properties to be proved, not parts of this construction.
