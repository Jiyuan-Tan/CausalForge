On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)\), the direct-series estimator has the expansion
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}-\tau
=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_{i,h_n}\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}
+\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\{\mu(X_j^T)-\tau\}+R_{n,m},
\]
where
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\{\mathbb E(R_{n,m}^2\mathbf 1_{\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)})\}^{1/2}
\le C\{h_n^{s+1}+\delta_{s,h_n}\delta_{t,h_n}+\lambda_n(J_{\mu,n}+J_{r,n})\}.
\]
Moreover \(\sum_i\Gamma_{i,h_n}^2\asymp_P n^{-1}V_a(h_n)\) and \(\max_i\Gamma_{i,h_n}^2/\sum_i\Gamma_{i,h_n}^2=o_P(1)\).
