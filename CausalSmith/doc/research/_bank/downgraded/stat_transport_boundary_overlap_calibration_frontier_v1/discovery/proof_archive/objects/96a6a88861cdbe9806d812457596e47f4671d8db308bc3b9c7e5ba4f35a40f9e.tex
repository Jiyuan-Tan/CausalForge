For fixed \(a,s,t,M\) with \(M\ge a+1\) and \(t>0\), let \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), write \(P=(P_S,P_T)\) for its pair of one-observation laws, and let \(h\in(0,1)\). The matrix \(G_h^{\circ}(P)\) is positive definite, and for every polynomial \(\ell\) of degree at most \(p\),
\[
\int_0^h w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)\ell(x)f_S(x)\,dx
=\int_0^h\ell(x)f_T(x)\,dx.
\]
Uniformly over the model class,
\[
\{q_h^{\circ}(P)\}^{\top}\{G_h^{\circ}(P)\}^{-1}q_h^{\circ}(P)\asymp h^{1-a},
\qquad
|\mathfrak b_h(\mu;P)|\le b_h^{\circ}(P)\le C h^{s+1},
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\omega_h^{\circ}(\cdot;P)\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2
&=\{q_h^{\circ}(P)\}^{\top}\{G_h^{\circ}(P)\}^{-1}q_h^{\circ}(P)
+\int_h^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx\\
&\asymp
\begin{cases}
1,&a<1,\\
\log(1/h),&a=1,\\
h^{1-a},&a>1.
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
\]
For every fixed \(a>0\), the allowance is class-sharp in the sense that, for an admissible member law and all sufficiently small \(h\), \(b_h^{\circ}(P)\ge c h^{s+1}\). At \(a=0\), if \(f_S=f_T\) on \([0,h]\), both \(\mathfrak b_h(\mu;P)\) and \(b_h^{\circ}(P)\) may vanish. Consequently, for the deterministic regime bandwidth \(h_n\),
\[
m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}\|\omega_{h_n}^{\circ}(\cdot;P)\|_{L^2(P_S)}
\asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
This is a population boundary-local-polynomial linear-weight benchmark. It is not a data-measurable estimator and does not establish a minimax upper bound.
