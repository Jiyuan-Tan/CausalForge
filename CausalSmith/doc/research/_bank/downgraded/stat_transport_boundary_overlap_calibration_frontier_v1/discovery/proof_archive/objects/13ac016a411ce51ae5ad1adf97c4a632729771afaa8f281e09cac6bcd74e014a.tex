\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\).  The constants below may depend on the fixed model constants but not on the member law.  By \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\), as \(h\downarrow0\),
\[
 \left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2
 \asymp
 \begin{cases}
  1,&a<1,\\
  \log(1/h),&a=1,\\
  h^{1-a},&a>1.
 \end{cases}
\tag{1}
\]
The comparison is uniform over \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), because its proof uses only the fixed positive bounds on \(g_S\) and \(g_T\).  Every branch of \(h_n\) lies in \((0,1)\) and converges to zero for all sufficiently large \(n\), so (1) applies.

Suppose first that \(a<1\).  The definition of \(h_n\) gives
\[
 h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},
 \qquad
 n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
 \asymp n^{-1/2}.
\tag{2}
\]
Hence the sum of the boundary-bias allowance and the source standard-deviation term is comparable to \(\rho_n(a,s)=n^{-1/2}\).

Suppose next that \(a=1\).  For \(n\ge3\),
\[
 h_n^{s+1}=\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
 \log(1/h_n)
 =\frac{\log n-\log\log n}{2(s+1)}
 \asymp\log n.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (1) and (4) imply
\[
 n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
 \asymp\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus both source terms have order \(\rho_n(1,s)=\sqrt{\log n/n}\).

Finally suppose that \(a>1\), and put \(D=2s+a+1>0\).  Then
\[
 h_n^{s+1}=n^{-(s+1)/D}=\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{6}
\]
By (1),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
 &\asymp n^{-1/2}h_n^{(1-a)/2}\\
 &=n^{-1/2+(a-1)/(2D)}
 =n^{-(s+1)/D}
 =\rho_n(a,s),
 \end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
where the penultimate identity follows from \(-D+a-1=-2(s+1)\).

In each regime, (2), (3)--(5), or (6)--(7) supplies constants \(0<c\le C<\infty\) such that
\[
 c\rho_n(a,s)
 \le h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}
 \left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
 \le C\rho_n(a,s)
\tag{8}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\), uniformly over the model class.  Adding the same nonnegative term \(m^{-1/2}\) to all three expressions preserves two-sided comparability: the lower inequality follows with constant \(\min\{1,c\}\), and the upper inequality with \(\max\{1,C\}\).  Consequently,
\[
 m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}
 \left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
 \asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
This is precisely the three-branch balance in \(\mathrm{def:frontier\text{-}rate}\).  Positivity of \(c_{S,-}\), \(c_{T,-}\), and \(h_n\), already used in \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\), supplies every support and division condition. \(\square\)
