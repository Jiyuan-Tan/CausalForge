If \(A_1,\ldots,A_N\) are independent centered self-adjoint matrices of dimension \(d\), \(\|A_i\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le L\) almost surely, and \(\|\sum_i\mathbb E(A_i^2)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le v\), then for every \(x>0\),
\[
\mathbb P\left\{\left\|\sum_{i=1}^NA_i\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}\ge x\right\}
\le2d\exp\left\{-\frac{x^2/2}{v+Lx/3}\right\}.
\]
