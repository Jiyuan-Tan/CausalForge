For each \(k\in\{1,\ldots,K_0\}\), use the source indices in \(\mathcal T^S_k\) and set
\[
\widehat G_{k,h}=\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(X_i)\mathbf R_h(X_i)^\top+n^{-2}I_{p+1},
\qquad
\widehat\alpha_{k,h}=\widehat G_{k,h}^{-1}\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(X_i)Z_i.
\]
For the interior spline, \(J_{\mu,n}\) is the number of knot intervals and
\[
d_{\mu,n}=J_{\mu,n}+q.
\]
Let
\[
\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)
=\bigl(N_{1,h,J_{\mu,n}}(x),\ldots,N_{d_{\mu,n},h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)\bigr)^\top
\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mu,n}}
\]
be the full standard normalized degree-\(q\) B-spline basis for the endpoint-repeated equally spaced knots. Using the same training indices with \(X_i\ge h\), let \(\widehat\beta_{k,h,J_{\mu,n}}\) be the unique minimizer over \(\mathbb R^{d_{\mu,n}}\) of
\[
\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}
\{Z_i-\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_i)^\top\beta\}^2+n^{-2}\|\beta\|_2^2.
\]
Define the untruncated fit
\[
\widehat\mu^{(-k)}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbf R_h(x)^\top\widehat\alpha_{k,h},&0\le x<h,\\
\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)^\top\widehat\beta_{k,h,J_{\mu,n}},&h\le x\le1.
\end{cases}
\]
Empty local or interior samples return the unique ridge solution. The positive ridges and ordered standard bases make every fold fit unique. No clipping map or kink derivative is part of the construction.
