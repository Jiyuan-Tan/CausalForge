On the event \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h)\cap\{\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}>0\}\), define
\[
\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
=\left[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
\pm
\operatorname{cv}_{1-\gamma/2}\!\left(
\frac{\widehat B_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}}
{\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}}
\right)
\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
\right].
\]
On the complementary bad-Gram, empty-cell, or zero-standard-error event, set
\[
\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=[-1,1].
\]
The fallback contains \(\tau\) by the parameter-space bound and is used only on the exponentially rare event quantified by the localized-series theorem.
