For a law \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), define
\[
G_h^o=\int_0^h\mathbf R_h(x)\mathbf R_h(x)^\top f_S(x)\,dx,
\qquad
q_h^o=\int_0^h\mathbf R_h(x)f_T(x)\,dx,
\]
\[
w_h^{BLP}(x)=\mathbf 1\{x<h\}\mathbf R_h(x)^\top(G_h^o)^{-1}q_h^o,
\qquad
\omega_h^o(x)=w_h^{BLP}(x)+r(x)\mathbf 1\{x\ge h\}.
\]
For \(v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\) with \(\|v\|_\infty\le1\), also define
\[
b_h(v;P)=\int_0^h\{w_h^{BLP}(x)f_S(x)-f_T(x)\}v(x)\,dx,
\qquad
b_h^o(P)=\sup_{\substack{v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\\ \|v\|_\infty\le1}}|b_h(v;P)|.
\]
These are population-law oracle objects. Positive definiteness, polynomial reproduction, the bias bound, and the norm orders are conclusions of \(\mathrm{lem:oracle\text{-}truncation\text{-}envelope}\), not parts of the construction.
