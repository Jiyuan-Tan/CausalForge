Write
\[
w_i=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}.
\]
For the interior part of the estimator, exact addition and subtraction of the empirical true-regression averages gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx
={}&\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx\right\}\\
&+C_{n,m}(h)-\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu).
\end{aligned}
\]
The armwise Hölder assumptions give \(\mu\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\), so
\[
|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu)|\le2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.
\]
Thus the corrected bias envelope contains every term in this exact discrepancy. If uniformly
\[
\widehat\tau-\tau=b_P+L_{n,m}+r_{n,m},
\qquad
|b_P|\le B_{n,m},
\qquad
r_{n,m}=o_P(\sigma_{n,m}),
\]
if \(L_{n,m}/\sigma_{n,m}\) has Kolmogorov distance \(o(1)\) from \(N(0,1)\), if \(\widehat{\mathrm{se}}/\sigma_{n,m}\to1\), and if the envelope event fails with probability at most \(\gamma/2+o(1)\), then the definition of \(\operatorname{cv}_{1-\gamma/2}\) gives coverage at least \(1-\gamma-o(1)\). Finally, for \(s>1/2\), the one-dimensional Hölder entropy calculation is finite because
\[
\int_0^1\epsilon^{-1/(2s)}\,d\epsilon<\infty.
\]
The stated stochastic hypotheses remain to be derived from the empirical transport construction.
