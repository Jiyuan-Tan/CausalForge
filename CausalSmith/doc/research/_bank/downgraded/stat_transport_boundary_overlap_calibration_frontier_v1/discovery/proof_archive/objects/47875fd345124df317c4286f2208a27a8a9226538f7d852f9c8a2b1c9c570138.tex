\textbf{Proof.}
For \(n,m\ge2\), the regime bandwidth satisfies \(0<h_n<1\), every source training complement is nonempty, and every target training complement used by a held-out observation is nonempty. The ridge inverses, selected transport solution, capped held-out evaluations, and coefficients are therefore finite and measurable. The measurability argument at the end of \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\) also makes \(B_X(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\) observable.

By \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}aware\text{-}interval}\), the event
\[
\left|\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau\right|
\le B_X(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2}
+\sqrt{\frac{2\log(4/\gamma)}m}
\]
is exactly the event \(\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}\). The finite-sample probability inequality in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\) bounds its probability below by \(1-\gamma\) for every member of the same model class. Taking the infimum over that class proves the coverage claim. The length identity follows directly by subtracting the two endpoints in the interval definition. No step bounds the random quantities \(B_X\) or \(\sum_i\Gamma_i^2\), so the proof supplies none of the excluded rate or approximation conclusions. \(\square\)
