\textbf{Proof.}
Fix arbitrary \(s>0\) and \(t>0\), fix a law in the current model class, and set \(a=0\). By the definitions of the density factors, for every \(x\in(0,1]\),
\[
f_S(x)=x^ag_S(x)=g_S(x),
\qquad
f_T(x)=g_T(x),
\qquad
r(x)=x^{-a}\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}.
\tag{1}
\]
The source- and target-boundary-factor assumptions give
\[
c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+},
\qquad
c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+},
\qquad x\in[0,1].
\tag{2}
\]
Since \(c_{S,-}>0\), division is valid and yields
\[
\frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
\le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}
\le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},
\qquad 0<x\le1.
\tag{3}
\]

Because \(a=0<1\), the first branch in the definition of \(h_n\) gives
\[
h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}.
\tag{4}
\]
The condition \(s>0\) makes \(s+1>0\), so
\[
h_n^{s+1}
=\left(n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\right)^{s+1}
=n^{-1/2}.
\tag{5}
\]
Moreover, \(h_n\downarrow0\). Applying \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\) in its \(a<1\) regime along this sequence gives
\[
\int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx\asymp1,
\tag{6}
\]
and hence the integral is \(O(1)\). The comparison constants depend only on the fixed boundary-factor constants.

Fix a fold \(k\) for which both interior training measures have positive total mass, and put
\[
I^T_{k,n}=\{j\in\mathcal T^T_k:X_j^T\ge h_n\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Positive target interior mass implies \(I^T_{k,n}\ne\varnothing\) and \(|\mathcal T^T_k|>0\). Positive source and target interior masses exclude the zero-mass fallback in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\), so the construction uses either its exact-feasibility branch or its penalized branch. On either branch, the selected calibration and target-marginal multipliers are finite real vectors. Therefore, for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\) and \(j\in I^T_{k,n}\),
\[
\exp\!\left\{-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
+\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
+\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right\}>0.
\tag{8}
\]
Indeed, the cost and spline coordinates are finite, the selected multipliers are real, and \(\eta_0>0\). The bracket defining the raw continuation contains at least one strictly positive summand, while its outer exponent \(\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)\) is positive because \(\eta_0,\lambda_0>0\). Hence
\[
\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0,
\qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\tag{9}
\]
When \(a=0\), the downstream cap equals
\[
\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h_n^{-a}=\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}>0.
\tag{10}
\]
The capped extension is the minimum of the positive quantities in (9) and (10). Therefore
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0,
\qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\tag{11}
\]
Every conclusion is deterministic conditional on the positive-interior-mass branch. No inequality coupling \(s\) and \(t\), and no threshold on \(t\) beyond \(t>0\), was used. Positivity alone supplies no empirical norm bound, no nonzero realized residual sum, and no root-risk conclusion. \(\square\)
