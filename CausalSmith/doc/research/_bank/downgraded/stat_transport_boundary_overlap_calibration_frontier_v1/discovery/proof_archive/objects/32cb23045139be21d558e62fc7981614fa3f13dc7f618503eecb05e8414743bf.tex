Under the primitive density, smoothness, and sample-balance conditions in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\), with \(h=h_n\) and \(J=J_n\), there are constants \(c,C>0\) such that
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n,J_n)^c\}\le C\exp\{-c\log^2(en)\}.
\]
On the good branch, \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{pl}}_{h_n,J_n}=\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)Z_i\),
\[
\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)^2\le C V_a(h_n)/n,
\qquad
\max_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)^2\big/\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)^2=o(1),
\]
and the root mean square of
\[
\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)\mu(X_i)-m^{-1}\sum_j\mu(X_j^T)
\]
is at most \(C\{h_n^{s+1}+J_n^{-(s+t)}+n^{-1/2}V_a(h_n)^{1/2}+m^{-1/2}\}\).
