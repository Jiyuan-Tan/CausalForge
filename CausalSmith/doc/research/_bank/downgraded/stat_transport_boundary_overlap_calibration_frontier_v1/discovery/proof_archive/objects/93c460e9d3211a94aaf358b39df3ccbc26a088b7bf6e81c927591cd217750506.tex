The source density is \(f_S(x)=x^ag_S(x)\), with \(c_{S,-}\le g_S\le c_{S,+}\), and the target density is \(f_T(x)=g_T(x)\), with \(c_{T,-}\le g_T\le c_{T,+}\). The source-only lower submodel uses \(g_S=a+1\), \(g_T=1\), and the normalization-compatible condition \(M\ge a+1\), with no strict target-bound slack. Strict inequalities \(c_{T,-}<1<c_{T,+}\) are used only for the optional independent target-density perturbation.
