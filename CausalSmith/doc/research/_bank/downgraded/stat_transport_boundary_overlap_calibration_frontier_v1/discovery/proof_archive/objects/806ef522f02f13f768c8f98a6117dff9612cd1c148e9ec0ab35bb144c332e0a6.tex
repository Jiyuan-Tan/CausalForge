Let \(\mathcal F\) be a sigma-field. If \(W_1,\ldots,W_N\) are conditionally independent given \(\mathcal F\), satisfy \(\mathbb E(W_i\mid\mathcal F)=0\), and obey \(a_i\le W_i\le b_i\) almost surely for finite \(\mathcal F\)-measurable \(a_i,b_i\), then, for every \(x>0\),
\[
\mathbb P\left(\left|\sum_{i=1}^N W_i\right|\ge x\,\middle|\,\mathcal F\right)
\le2\exp\left\{-\frac{2x^2}{\sum_{i=1}^N(b_i-a_i)^2}\right\}
\]
almost surely, with the right side interpreted as zero when the denominator is zero and \(x>0\).
