For every \(n,m\ge2\) and every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), the unchanged spliced estimator at \(h=h_n\) has the exact observable linear representation
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})Z_i.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau
={}&\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\mu(X_i)-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\right\}\\
&+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)-\tau\right\},
\end{aligned}
\]
and the absolute value of the middle line is at most \(B_X(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\). Consequently, for every \(\gamma\in(0,1/2)\),
\[
\mathbb P_{n,m}\!\left\{
\left|\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau\right|
\le B_X(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2}
+\sqrt{\frac{2\log(4/\gamma)}m}
\right\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Thus small realized values of the observable discrepancy and coefficient norm certify execution on the observed design. No probability bound at the truncated-oracle order, root-mean-squared attainment, empirical weight-norm or leverage bound, or inherited calibration identity for the capped weights is asserted.
