Use the source and target training blocks \(\mathcal T^S_1\) and \(\mathcal T^T_1\), of sizes \(n_{\mathrm{tr}}\) and \(m_{\mathrm{tr}}\).  For \(J=J_{r,n}\), define
\[
 \widehat G^{q}_{h,J}=\frac1{n_{\mathrm{tr}}}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_1}\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}X_i^{-a}
 \mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)^\top,
 \qquad
 \widehat g^{q}_{h,J}=\frac1{m_{\mathrm{tr}}}
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal T^T_1}\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_j^T).
\]
Put
\[
 \widehat\beta^{q}_{h,J}=(\widehat G^{q}_{h,J}+\lambda_nI_J)^{-1}\widehat g^{q}_{h,J},
 \qquad
 \widehat q^{\mathrm{DS}}_{h,J}(x)=
 \Pi_{[q_-,q_+]}\!\left\{\mathbf b_{h,J}(x)^\top\widehat\beta^{q}_{h,J}\right\},
\]
where \(\Pi_{[q_-,q_+]}(z)=\min\{q_+,\max\{q_-,z\}\}\).  Define
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{DS}}_{h,J}(x)=x^{-a}\widehat q^{\mathrm{DS}}_{h,J}(x),
 \qquad h\le x\le1.
\]
The inverse is ridge-totalized for every realized training sample; the estimator and interval decide whether to use it through the separate observable event.  The construction contains no transport plan, optimal-transport cost, dual multiplier, training-atom density, or off-support KKT continuation.  It directly estimates the nonsingular ratio \(q_0=g_T/g_S\) by a finite quadratic Galerkin system and then restores the known factor \(x^{-a}\).
