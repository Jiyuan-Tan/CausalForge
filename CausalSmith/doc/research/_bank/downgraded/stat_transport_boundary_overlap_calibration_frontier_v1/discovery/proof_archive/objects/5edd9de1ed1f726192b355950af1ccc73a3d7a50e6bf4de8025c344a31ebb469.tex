For fixed \((a,s,t,M)\) with \(s>1/2\), \(t>1/2\), \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), and \(a<2t(2s+1)\), along every triangular-array sequence satisfying \(m/n\to\kappa\), the direct-series interval \(\mathcal I_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) obeys
\[
 \liminf_{n,m\to\infty}\inf_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
 \mathbb P_{n,m}\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}\ge1-\gamma
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
 \mathbb E_{\mathbb P_{n,m}}\!\left[\operatorname{length}
 \{\mathcal I_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}\right]
 \le C\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\]
Writing \(\epsilon_i=Z_i-\mu(X_i)\), the complete realized linear term is
\[
 L_{n,m}=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_{i,h_n}\epsilon_i
 +\frac1{N^T_1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_1}\{\mu(X_j^T)-\tau\}.
\]
Uniformly over the class, \(L_{n,m}/\{\operatorname{Var}(L_{n,m})\}^{1/2}\) has Kolmogorov distance \(o(1)\) from \(N(0,1)\), and
\[
 \frac{\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}^2}
 {\operatorname{Var}(L_{n,m})}\longrightarrow_P1.
\]
The source part uses the full coefficient \(\Gamma_{i,h_n}\), so the sandwich includes every covariance created by the boundary and interior linear smoothers.  On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)^c\), or when the standard error is zero, the interval is the bounded parameter space \([-1,1]\).  The result includes \(a=1\), where the source scale is \(\sqrt{\log n/n}\); no multiplier-process approximation is asserted or needed.
