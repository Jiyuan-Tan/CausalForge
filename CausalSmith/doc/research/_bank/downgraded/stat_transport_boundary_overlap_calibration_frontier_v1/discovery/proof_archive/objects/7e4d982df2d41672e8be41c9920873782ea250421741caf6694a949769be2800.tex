For \(n,m\ge2\), \(h\in(0,1)\), and fold \(k\), define the interior ridge Gram matrix
\[
\widehat H_{k,h,J_{\mu,n}}
=\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{q\in\mathcal T^S_k}
\mathbf 1\{X_q\ge h\}\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_q)\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_q)^\top+n^{-2}I_{J_{\mu,n}}
\]
and, for \(x\in[0,1]\), the observable regression coefficient
\[
L_{k i}(x;h,J_{\mu,n})=
\frac{\mathbf 1\{i\in\mathcal T^S_k\}}{|\mathcal T^S_k|}
\begin{cases}
\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(x)^\top\widehat G_{k,h}^{-1}\mathbf R_h(X_i),&0\le x<h,\\
\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)^\top\widehat H_{k,h,J_{\mu,n}}^{-1}\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_i),&h\le x\le1.
\end{cases}
\]
The positive ridges make both inverses unique. Define
\[
w_\ell(h,J_{r,n})=
\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(\ell))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_\ell)\mathbf 1\{X_\ell\ge h\}
\]
and
\[
\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
=\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m L_{k_T(j)i}(X_j^T;h,J_{\mu,n})
+\frac1n w_i(h,J_{r,n})
-\frac1n\sum_{\ell=1}^n w_\ell(h,J_{r,n})L_{k_S(\ell)i}(X_\ell;h,J_{\mu,n}).
\]
These formulas uniquely determine \(\Gamma_i\) from the observed source and target covariates, the fixed folds, and the unchanged capped downstream RiCOT weights. They do not impose or inherit the raw transport plan's spline-calibration equations.
