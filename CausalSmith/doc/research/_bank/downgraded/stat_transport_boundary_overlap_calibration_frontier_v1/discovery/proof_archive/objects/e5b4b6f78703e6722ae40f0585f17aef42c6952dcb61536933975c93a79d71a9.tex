\textbf{Proof.}
By \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}aware\text{-}interval}\), the event \(\tau\in\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) is exactly
\[
\left|\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau\right|
\le B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
+R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_T(m).
\tag{1}
\]
For every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\) assigns probability at least \(1-\gamma\) to (1).  The lower bound does not depend on the member law.  Taking the infimum over the same model class therefore yields
\[
\inf_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}
\ge1-\gamma.
\]
This conclusion continues to hold for the broadened class obtained by removing the two quarantined density-rate conditions, because the preceding theorem does not use them.

The interval is symmetric about \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) and has half-length \(B_X+R_S+R_T\).  Its realized length is consequently exactly
\[
2\{B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_T(m)\}.
\]
On a design satisfying \(B_X\le b\) and \(\sum_i\Gamma_i^2\le g^2\), \(\mathrm{def:foldwise\text{-}inference\text{-}statistics}\) gives
\[
R_S\le\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)}\,g,
\qquad
R_T(m)=\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)/m}.
\]
Substitution into the exact length proves the final bound.  All three conclusions are finite-sample statements; none entails an expected-length rate, a Gaussian or multiplier approximation, variance consistency, leverage control, or empirical frontier attainment. \(\square\)
