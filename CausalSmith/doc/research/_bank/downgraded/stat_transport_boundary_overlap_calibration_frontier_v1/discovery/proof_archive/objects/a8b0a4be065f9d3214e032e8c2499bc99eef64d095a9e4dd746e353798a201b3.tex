Define the fixed-class interval
\[
\mathcal I^{\mathrm{pl}}_{h,J}=\begin{cases}
\left[\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{pl}}_{h,J}\pm\{\overline B_X(h,J)+R_S(h,J)+R_T(m)+n^{-2}\}\right],&\mathcal E_{n,m}(h,J),\\
[-1,1],&\mathcal E_{n,m}(h,J)^c.
\end{cases}
\]
Every endpoint is a computable map of the realized source and target samples, the known randomization probability bound, and the fixed Hölder class.  The fallback is used before any inverse is evaluated.
