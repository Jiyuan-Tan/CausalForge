For arbitrary fixed \(t>0\), suppose \(M\ge a+1\) and \(c_{T,-}<1<c_{T,+}\). There are constants \(c,C>0\) such that, for every sufficiently small \(h\), the perturbations generated by \(b_h\) yield two observed-data laws \(\mathbb P_{+,h},\mathbb P_{-,h}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\) whose source marginals satisfy
\[
\operatorname{KL}(P_{+,h}^{S,\otimes n},P_{-,h}^{S,\otimes n})\le Cnh^{2s+a+1},
\qquad
|\tau(\mathbb P_{+,h})-\tau(\mathbb P_{-,h})|\ge ch^{s+1}.
\]
A separate smooth target-density perturbation yields two full-data laws in \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), with a common observed source marginal, whose observed target projections satisfy
\[
\operatorname{KL}(P_{T,+}^{\otimes m},P_{T,-}^{\otimes m})\le C
\]
and whose values of \(\tau\) differ by at least \(cm^{-1/2}\).
