Fix \(a,s,t,M\) with \(M\ge a+1\), put \(p=\lceil s\rceil-1\) and \(\beta=s-p\in(0,1]\), and use the convention
\[
\mathcal H^s(L)=\left\{v\in C^p[0,1]:
\max_{0\le r\le p}\|v^{(r)}\|_\infty\le L,
\ [v^{(p)}]_{\beta}\le L\right\}.
\]
For every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\) and every sufficiently small \(h>0\), the oracle matrix \(G_h^o\) is positive definite, and the boundary local-polynomial weight reproduces every \(\ell\in\mathcal P_p\):
\[
\int_0^h w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x)\ell(x)f_S(x)\,dx
=\int_0^h\ell(x)f_T(x)\,dx.
\]
Uniformly over the model class,
\[
|b_h(\mu;P)|\le b_h^o(P)\le C h^{s+1}.
\]
For every fixed \(a>0\), this allowance is class-sharp: for some \(c>0\) and every sufficiently small \(h\),
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}b_h^o(P)\ge c h^{s+1}.
\]
No per-law lower equivalence is asserted; in particular, when \(a=0\) and \(f_S=f_T\) on \([0,h]\), one has \(w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}=1\) and \(b_h^o(P)=0\). The boundary energy and the full oracle-weight norm obey, uniformly over the model class,
\[
(q_h^o)^\top(G_h^o)^{-1}q_h^o\asymp h^{1-a},
\]
\[
\|\omega_h^o\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2
=(q_h^o)^\top(G_h^o)^{-1}q_h^o
+\int_h^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx
\asymp
\begin{cases}
1,&a<1,\\
\log(1/h),&a=1,\\
h^{1-a},&a>1.
\end{cases}
\]
Consequently, for the deterministic regime bandwidth \(h_n\), the actual oracle bias is at most \(C h_n^{s+1}\), while the explicit worst-case allowance satisfies
\[
m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}
+n^{-1/2}\|\omega_{h_n}^o\|_{L^2(P_S)}
\asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
This is a boundary-corrected oracle local-polynomial linear-weight benchmark, not a data-measurable estimator or a minimax upper bound.
