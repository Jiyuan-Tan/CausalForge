The proved scope is a one-dimensional boundary, known randomized-treatment propensity, bounded outcomes, fixed \(a,s,t,M\), \(t>1/2\), the separate nuisance-product inequality, and \(M\ge a+1\) for nonempty source-density normalization. The minimax class itself is finite-sample and does not contain sample balance; \(m/n\to\kappa\) is used only to convert the source-only converse into the displayed two-sample lower order without strict target-density slack. At \(a=0\), bounded-ratio and oracle arithmetic are proved, but empirical root-risk attainment is not. No matching data-driven minimax upper bound, fixed-class Wald coverage theorem, or unrestricted adaptive interval is claimed. The self-similar adaptation handle is not executable as frozen. Multivariate boundaries, observational treatment assignment, and transportability sensitivity analysis remain outside scope.
