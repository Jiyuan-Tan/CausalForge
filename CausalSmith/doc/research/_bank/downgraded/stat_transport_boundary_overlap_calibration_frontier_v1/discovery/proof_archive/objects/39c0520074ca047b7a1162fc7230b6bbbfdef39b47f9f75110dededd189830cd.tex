For a member law \(P=\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), define
\[
G_h^{\mathrm o}(P)=\int_0^h\mathbf R_h(x)\mathbf R_h(x)^\top f_S(x)\,dx,
\qquad
q_h^{\mathrm o}(P)=\int_0^h\mathbf R_h(x)f_T(x)\,dx.
\]
The oracle boundary-local-polynomial weight, the boundary-corrected full oracle weight, and its bias functional are
\[
w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)=\mathbf 1\{x<h\}\mathbf R_h(x)^\top
\{G_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\}^{-1}q_h^{\mathrm o}(P),
\qquad
\omega_h^{\mathrm o}(x;P)=w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)+r(x)\mathbf 1\{x\ge h\},
\]
\[
b_h(v;P)=\int_0^h\{w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)f_S(x)-f_T(x)\}v(x)\,dx,
\qquad
b_h^{\mathrm o}(P)=
\sup_{\substack{v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\\ \|v\|_\infty\le1}}|b_h(v;P)|.
\]
These are population objects depending on the unknown law. They define an oracle boundary-local-polynomial linear-weight benchmark, not a data-measurable estimator and not a minimax upper bound.
