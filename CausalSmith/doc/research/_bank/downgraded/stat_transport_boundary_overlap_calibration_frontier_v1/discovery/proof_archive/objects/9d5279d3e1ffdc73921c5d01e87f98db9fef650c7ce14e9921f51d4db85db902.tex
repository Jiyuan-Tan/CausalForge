Writing
\[
w_i=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\},
\]
the exact interior identity is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx
={}&\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx\right\}\\
&+C_{n,m}(h)-\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu).
\end{aligned}
\]
Since \(\mu_0,\mu_1\in\mathcal H^s(M)\), one has \(\mu\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\) and therefore
\[
|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu)|\le2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.
\]
Thus the corrected bias envelope contains every term in the exact held-out interior discrepancy. If a uniform expansion \(\widehat\tau-\tau=b_P+L_{n,m}+o_P(\sigma_{n,m})\), the bound \(|b_P|\le B_{n,m}\), variance consistency, a uniform normal approximation for \(L_{n,m}/\sigma_{n,m}\), and an envelope failure probability at most \(\gamma/2+o(1)\) are established, the definition of \(\operatorname{cv}_{1-\gamma/2}\) yields coverage at least \(1-\gamma-o(1)\). Finally,
\[
\int_0^1\epsilon^{-1/(2s)}\,d\epsilon<\infty
\qquad\text{if and only if}\qquad s>1/2,
\]
which verifies the stated one-dimensional Hölder entropy threshold but not the needed stochastic approximation.
