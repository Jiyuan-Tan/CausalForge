Let \(G_h^o,q_h^o,w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}},\omega_h^o,b_h(v;P)\), and \(b_h^o(P)\) be the oracle boundary-local-polynomial objects in \(\mathrm{def:oracle\text{-}boundary\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial\text{-}weight}\). For every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\) and every sufficiently small \(h>0\), \(G_h^o\) is positive definite, and for every \(\pi\in\mathcal P_p\),
\[
\int_0^h w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x)\pi(x)f_S(x)\,dx
=\int_0^h\pi(x)f_T(x)\,dx.
\]
Uniformly over the model class,
\[
(q_h^o)^\top(G_h^o)^{-1}q_h^o\asymp h^{1-a},
\qquad
\|\omega_h^o\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2
=(q_h^o)^\top(G_h^o)^{-1}q_h^o
+\int_h^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx,
\]
and
\[
|b_h(\mu;P)|\le b_h^o(P)\le C h^{s+1}.
\]
If \(a>0\) and \(M\ge a+1\), this allowance is sharp over the class: for all sufficiently small \(h\),
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}b_h^o(P)\ge c h^{s+1}.
\]
If \(a=0\), writing \(\alpha=\min\{t,1\}\), the stronger uniform bound \(\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(0,s,t)}b_h^o(P)\le C h^{s+1+\alpha}\) holds; in particular the order-\(h^{s+1}\) allowance is not class-sharp in that regular-overlap endpoint, and it may vanish, for example when \(f_S=f_T\). For the deterministic regime bandwidth \(h_n\), the actual oracle bias is at most \(C h_n^{s+1}\), while
\[
m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}\|\omega_{h_n}^o\|_{L^2(P_S)}
\asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
This is a law-dependent boundary-local-polynomial oracle linear-weight benchmark, not a data-measurable estimator or a minimax upper bound.
