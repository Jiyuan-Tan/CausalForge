For fixed \(a,s,t,M\) with \(t>1/2\), \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), and \(a<2t(2s+1)\), along every triangular-array sequence with \(m/n\to\kappa\), the deterministic choices \(h=h_n\) and \(J=J_n\) give
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\left\{\mathbb E_{\mathbb P_{n,m}}[(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{pl}}_{h_n,J_n}-\tau)^2]\right\}^{1/2}
\le C\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\]
Moreover, the estimator equals \(\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)Z_i\) on an observable event whose complement has probability at most \(C\exp\{-c\log^2(en)\}\), and on that event
\[
\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)^2\le C V_a(h_n)/n,
\qquad
\frac{\max_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)^2}{\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_n)^2}=o(1),
\]
with the ratio defined as zero when its denominator is zero. Thus the pure source outcome-regression plug-in attains the regular, critical, and nonregular frontier without estimating either density or their ratio.
