For \(k\in\{1,\ldots,K_0\}\), \(h\in(0,1)\), and a positive integer \(J_\mu\), define
\[
\widehat H_{k,h,J_\mu}
=\frac1{|\mathcal T_k^S|}\sum_{u\in\mathcal T_k^S}
\mathbf 1\{X_u\ge h\}\mathbf b_{h,J_\mu}(X_u)\mathbf b_{h,J_\mu}(X_u)^\top+n^{-2}I_{J_\mu},
\]
and, for \(\ell\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\), define the observable regression coefficient
\[
L_{k,h,J_\mu}(x,\ell)=\frac{\mathbf 1\{\ell\in\mathcal T_k^S\}}{|\mathcal T_k^S|}
\begin{cases}
\mathbf 1\{X_\ell\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(x)^\top\widehat G_{k,h}^{-1}\mathbf R_h(X_\ell),&0\le x<h,\\
\mathbf 1\{X_\ell\ge h\}\mathbf b_{h,J_\mu}(x)^\top\widehat H_{k,h,J_\mu}^{-1}\mathbf b_{h,J_\mu}(X_\ell),&h\le x\le1.
\end{cases}
\]
When \(\mathcal T_k^S\) is empty, every empirical average in this display is defined to be zero and \(L_{k,h,J_\mu}(x,\ell)=0\); this is the zero-response ridge convention already specified for empty training samples in \(\mathrm{def:regression\text{-}fit}\). For each source index \(i\), put
\[
w_i(h,J_r)=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_r}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}.
\]
The observable linear coefficient of \(Z_\ell\) in the existing spliced estimator is
\[
\Gamma_\ell(h,J_\mu,J_r)
=\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^mL_{k_T(j),h,J_\mu}(X_j^T,\ell)
+\frac1n w_\ell(h,J_r)
-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n w_i(h,J_r)L_{k_S(i),h,J_\mu}(X_i,\ell).
\]
All matrices in the display are invertible because of the positive ridge. The deterministic branch rules and tie breaking in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\) make every \(w_i(h,J_r)\), and hence every \(\Gamma_\ell(h,J_\mu,J_r)\), a measurable function of the source and target covariates alone. Collecting coefficients in the two ridge normal equations gives the exact algebraic identity
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_\mu,J_r}=\sum_{\ell=1}^n\Gamma_\ell(h,J_\mu,J_r)Z_\ell.
\]
