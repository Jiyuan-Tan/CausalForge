For fixed \((a,s,t,M)\) with \(s>1/2\), \(t>1/2\), \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), and \(a<2t(2s+1)\), along every triangular-array sequence satisfying \(m/n\to\kappa\), the direct-series bias-aware interval obeys
\[
\liminf_{n,m\to\infty}\inf_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Its maximal expected length is bounded by
\[
C\left\{
m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)
+\delta_{s,h_n}\delta_{t,h_n}
+\lambda_n(J_{\mu,n}+J_{r,n})
\right\}.
\]
Uniformly over the class, \(\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}^2\) divided by the variance of the complete linear term
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_{i,h_n}\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}
+\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\{\mu(X_j^T)-\tau\}
\]
converges in probability to one, and the corresponding studentized linear term has Kolmogorov distance \(o(1)\) from \(N(0,1)\).  On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)^c\), or when the estimated standard error is zero, the interval is the bounded parameter space \([-1,1]\).  Hence the critical case \(a=1\) has the oracle scale \(\sqrt{\log n/n}\), with the explicit nuisance-product addition above; the expected length reduces to the oracle frontier precisely in the matching subregimes stated in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\).
