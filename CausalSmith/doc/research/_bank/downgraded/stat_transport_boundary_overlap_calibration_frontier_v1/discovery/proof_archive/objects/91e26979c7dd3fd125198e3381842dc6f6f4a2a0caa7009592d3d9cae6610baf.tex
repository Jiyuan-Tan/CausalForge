For every \(n,m\ge1\), every \(h\in(0,1)\), and every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), the existing spliced estimator has the exact observable linear representation
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})Z_i.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau
={}&\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\mu(X_i)-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\right\}\\
&+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)-\tau\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The middle term is bounded in absolute value by \(B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\). Uniformly over the model class, the fully observable interval \(\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) satisfies the finite-sample guarantee
\[
\inf_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
In particular, on any realized design for which \(B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\le b\) and \(\sum_i\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2\le g^2\), its half-width is at most
\[
b+\varepsilon^{-1}g\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)}+\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)/m}.
\]
This is only a realized-design execution certificate: it makes no claim that \(B_X\), the coefficient norm, or the interval length has the oracle order \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\).
