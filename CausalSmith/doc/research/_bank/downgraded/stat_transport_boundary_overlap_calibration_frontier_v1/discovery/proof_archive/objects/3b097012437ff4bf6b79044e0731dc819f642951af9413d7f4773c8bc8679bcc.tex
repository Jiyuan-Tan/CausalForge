For the oracle truncated weight \(r_h=r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h\}\), the source variance is bounded by \(Cn^{-1}\int_h^1x^{-a}\,dx\), and the omitted boundary contribution has deterministic modulus at most \(C h^{s+1}\). Hence the oracle bias--standard-deviation envelope is \[m^{-1/2}+h^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}\begin{cases}1,&a<1,\\ \sqrt{\log(1/h)},&a=1,\\ h^{(1-a)/2},&a>1,\end{cases}\] which at \(h=h_n\) equals \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\). This verifies the early-kill rate calculation, but it does not establish that the empirical entropic transport dual attains the oracle envelope.
