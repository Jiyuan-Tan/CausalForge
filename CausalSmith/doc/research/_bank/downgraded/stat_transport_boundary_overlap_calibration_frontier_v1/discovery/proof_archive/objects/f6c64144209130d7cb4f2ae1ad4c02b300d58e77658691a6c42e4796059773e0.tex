Let \(c_{\mathrm{spl},-}>0\) and \(c_{\mathrm{bd},-}>0\) be the registered computable lower stability constants for the fixed normalized spline and boundary-polynomial bases.  Define \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h)\) to be the intersection of the following observable conditions:
\[
 N^S_1\ge2,
 \qquad N^T_1\ge2,
 \qquad |\mathcal T^S_1|\ge2,
 \qquad |\mathcal T^T_1|\ge2,
\]
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(\widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_h)
 \ge c_{\mathrm{bd},-}h^{a+1},
 \qquad
 \lambda_{\max}(\widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_h)
 \le c_{\mathrm{bd},-}^{-1}h^{a+1},
\]
and, for each \(J\in\{J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}\}\),
\[
 \frac{c_{\mathrm{spl},-}}{J}
 \le\lambda_{\min}(\widehat G^{\mu}_{h,J})
 \le\lambda_{\max}(\widehat G^{\mu}_{h,J})
 \le\frac{c_{\mathrm{spl},-}^{-1}}{J},
\]
where \(\widehat G^{\mu}_{h,J}\) denotes the same weighted source Gram formula for either dimension and \(\widehat G^{q}_{h,J_{r,n}}=\widehat G^{\mu}_{h,J_{r,n}}\).  If a displayed matrix has the wrong finite dimension or a sample size is zero, the event is false.  No population moment, unknown regression, density, fitted-rate bound, or unspecified ``bad Gram'' predicate enters the branch decision.
