Let \(W_1,\ldots,W_N\) be independent centered real random variables satisfying \(A_i\le W_i\le B_i\) almost surely. Then, for every \(x>0\),
\[
\mathbb P\left\{\left|\sum_{i=1}^N W_i\right|\ge x\right\}
\le2\exp\left\{-\frac{2x^2}{\sum_{i=1}^N(B_i-A_i)^2}\right\},
\]
with the evident convention that a zero denominator makes the sum identically zero.
