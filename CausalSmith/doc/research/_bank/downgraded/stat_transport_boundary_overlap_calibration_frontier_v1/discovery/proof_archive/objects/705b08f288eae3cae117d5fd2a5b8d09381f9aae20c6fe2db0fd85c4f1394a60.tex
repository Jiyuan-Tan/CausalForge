For every \(h\in(0,1)\), define the observable fold-varying discrepancy
\[
C_{n,m}(h)=\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_T(j))}(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_S(i))}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}.
\]
Define
\[
\widehat B_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=2C_{\mathrm{LP}}Mh^{s+1}+|C_{n,m}(h)|+2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.
\]
The middle term is required by the exact held-out interior decomposition; it is not generally equal to a calibration residual of one common fitted function. The factor \(2M\) is the Hölder radius inherited by \(\mu=\mu_1-\mu_0\).
