\[
\liminf_{\min(n,m)\to\infty}
\inf_{Q\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s_1,t)\cup\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s_2,t)}
Q\{\tau(Q)\in\mathcal C_{n,m}\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
There is a constant \(c>0\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n,m\), some law \(Q_{0,n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s_2,t)\) satisfies
\[
\mathbb E_{Q_{0,n,m}}|\mathcal C_{n,m}|
\ge c\left\{m^{-1/2}+n^{-(s_1+1)/(2s_1+a+1)}\right\}.
