\[
\widehat B_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=2C_{\mathrm{LP}}Mh^{s+1}+2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.

The factor \(2M\) is the Hölder radius inherited by \(\mu=\mu_1-\mu_0\) from the two armwise radius-\(M\) balls. The regression dimension is displayed because the interval is attached to \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\), while the calibration support function itself depends on \(J_{r,n}\) through its fitted transport weight.
