Let \(B_h=(c_{T,+}/c_{S,-})h^{-a}\), let \(u(x)=\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)\), and let \(w(x)=\min\{u(x),B_h\}\). The boundary-factor assumptions imply \(0<r(x)\le B_h\) on \([h,1]\). Projection onto the closed interval \([0,B_h]\) is nonexpansive relative to every point of that interval, hence
\[
0\le w(x)\le B_h,
\qquad
|w(x)-r(x)|\le |u(x)-r(x)|.
\]
For every held-out function \(v\), the cap changes the raw calibration discrepancy by the exact, observable remainder
\[
\Delta_{w}(v)=\Delta_{u}(v)+\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\{u(X_i)-B_h\}_{+}v(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}.
\]
Consequently exact spline calibration of the raw training-atom marginal does not transfer to \(w\). For any probability measure \(Q\) and any square-integrable \(v\), Cauchy--Schwarz and the projection inequality give the cap-compatible bias bound
\[
\left|\int\{w(x)-r(x)\}v(x)\,dQ(x)\right|
\le \|u-r\|_{L^2(Q)}\|v\|_{L^2(Q)}.
\]
The cap also gives only the coarse deterministic empirical bound \(n^{-1}\sum_iw(X_i)^2\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\le B_h^2\); it does not imply the oracle-order bound \(O_P(V_h)\), where \(V_h=1+\mathbf 1\{a=1\}\log(1/h)+\mathbf 1\{a>1\}h^{1-a}\), nor maximum-leverage decay. Under the proposed zero-mass branch, the finite-support feasible sets are compact, the generalized Kullback--Leibler term with \(\eta_0>0\) makes the primal criterion strictly convex on densities relative to the empirical product measure, and therefore the primal plan is unique. The exact-feasibility event is Borel, the unique argmin is a measurable function of the empirical atoms, and minimum-norm selection from the closed convex KKT solution set is measurable; composition with the exponential map and projection makes the capped fold map measurable. These measurability facts do not provide a quantitative held-out localization rate.
