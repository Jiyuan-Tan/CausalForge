The formal results cover a one-dimensional compact boundary, known randomized-treatment propensity bounded in \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\), bounded outcomes, H\"older response and density factors, polynomial source depletion, and an independent target covariate sample. The lower phase diagram holds for fixed \(t>0\), and its no-target-slack version is stated along proportional sample-size sequences. The oracle weight is population-defined and the class-sharp bias-allowance statement is restricted to fixed \(a>0\); special laws, including equal source and target boundary densities at \(a=0\), may have zero actual bias. The finite-sample interval is honest for the fixed declared class and bandwidth, with realized rather than frontier-order length. No measurable minimax upper, empirical frontier attainment, Gaussian approximation, variance consistency, multivariate boundary result, observational-treatment extension, or positive adaptive interval is claimed.
