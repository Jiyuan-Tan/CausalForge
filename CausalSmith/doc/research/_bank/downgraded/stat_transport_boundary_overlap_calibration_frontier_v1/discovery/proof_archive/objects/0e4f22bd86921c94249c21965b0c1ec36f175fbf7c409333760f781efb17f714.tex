For each fold \(k\), let \(\mathbf b_{h,J}\) be the normalized local B-spline basis on \([h,1]\).  Define
\[
\widehat G^{q,(-k)}_{h,J}
=\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_k}
\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}X_i^{-a}\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)^\top,
\qquad
\widehat g^{q,(-k)}_{h,J}
=\frac1{|\mathcal T^T_k|}\sum_{j\in\mathcal T^T_k}
\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_j^T).
\]
Let \(c_\star>0\) be the lower fixed-degree spline stability constant obtained from \(c_{S,-}\).  The fold is on its good branch when both interior training samples are nonempty and
\[
\lambda_{\min}\!\left(\widehat G^{q,(-k)}_{h,J}\right)\ge \frac{c_\star}{J}.
\]
On this branch put
\[
\widehat\beta^{q,(-k)}_{h,J}
=\left(\widehat G^{q,(-k)}_{h,J}+\lambda_n I_J\right)^{-1}\widehat g^{q,(-k)}_{h,J},
\qquad
\widehat q^{\mathrm{DS},(-k)}_{h,J}(x)
=\Pi_{[q_-,q_+]}\!\left\{\mathbf b_{h,J}(x)^\top\widehat\beta^{q,(-k)}_{h,J}\right\}.
\]
On the bad-Gram or empty-interior branch define \(\widehat q^{\mathrm{DS},(-k)}_{h,J}(x)=(q_-+q_+)/2\).  In both branches define the held-out direct-series weight
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{DS},(-k)}_{h,J}(x)=x^{-a}\widehat q^{\mathrm{DS},(-k)}_{h,J}(x),
\qquad h\le x\le1.
\]
The construction contains no transport plan, dual potential, empirical-support continuation, or inherited calibration equation.  The deterministic choices are \(h=h_n\), \(J=J_{r,n}\), and \(\lambda_n=n^{-2}\).
