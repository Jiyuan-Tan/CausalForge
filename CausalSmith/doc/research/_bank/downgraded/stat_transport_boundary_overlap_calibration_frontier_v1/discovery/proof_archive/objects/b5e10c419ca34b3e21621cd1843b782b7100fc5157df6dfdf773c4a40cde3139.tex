At \(a=0\), the factor bounds give
\[
\frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}\le r(x)\le\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},
\qquad
h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},
\qquad
\int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx=O(1).
\]
The exponential dual formula is strictly positive at every held-out interior point whenever both training interior measures are nonzero. Conditional on the still-unproved localization \(\sup_{x\in[h_n,1]}\widehat r(x)=O_P(1)\), boundedness of \(Z\) gives conditional source variance \(O_P(n^{-1})\), and the exact orthogonal decomposition reduces the remaining mean term to the nuisance product. Hence the proposition follows from the unresolved empirical part of \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\), but not from positivity alone.
