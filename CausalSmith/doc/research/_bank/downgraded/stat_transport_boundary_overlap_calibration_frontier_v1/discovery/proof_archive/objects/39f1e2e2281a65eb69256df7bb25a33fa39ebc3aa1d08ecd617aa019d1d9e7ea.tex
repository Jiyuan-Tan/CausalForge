The oracle and rate-arithmetic portion closes. Put
\[
V_h=1+\mathbf 1\{a=1\}\log(1/h)+\mathbf 1\{a>1\}h^{1-a}.
\]
Then \(\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h\}\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2\asymp V_h\), the boundary approximation bias is \(O(h^{s+1})\), and the oracle envelope is
\[
m^{-1/2}+h^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}V_h^{1/2}.
\]
At \(h=h_n\) this equals \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\). The repaired deterministic series dimensions give
\[
\delta_{\mu,n}=J_{\mu,n}^{-s}+\sqrt{J_{\mu,n}/n}\asymp n^{-s/(2s+1)},
\]
and
\[
\delta_{r,n}=J_{r,n}^{-t}+\sqrt{J_{r,n}\log J_{r,n}/n}\asymp(\log n/n)^{t/(2t+1)}.
\]
Thus the declared product condition implies \(\delta_{\mu,n}\delta_{r,n}=o(n^{-1/2})\). Also \(nh_n^{a+1}/\log J_{r,n}\to\infty\) in all three regimes: for \(a<1\) its polynomial exponent is \(1-(a+1)/\{2(s+1)\}>0\); for \(a=1\) it is \(n^{s/(s+1)}\) up to logarithms; and for \(a>1\) it is \(n^{2s/(2s+a+1)}\) up to logarithms. These facts do not imply the missing quantitative KKT localization for the empirical plan.
