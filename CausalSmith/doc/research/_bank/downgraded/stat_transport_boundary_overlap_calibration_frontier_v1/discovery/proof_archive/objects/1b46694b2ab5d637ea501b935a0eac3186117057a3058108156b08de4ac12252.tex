There is a universal constant \(C\) such that, for independent centered real variables \(W_1,\ldots,W_N\) with \(0<\sigma^2=\sum_i\mathbb E(W_i^2)<\infty\) and \(\sum_i\mathbb E|W_i|^3<\infty\),
\[
\sup_z\left|\mathbb P\left\{\sigma^{-1}\sum_iW_i\le z\right\}-\Phi(z)\right|
\le C\sigma^{-3}\sum_i\mathbb E|W_i|^3.
\]
