When \(a=0\), the boundary-factor assumptions give, for every \(x\in[0,1]\),
\[
\frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}\le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}\le\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}.
\]
The definition of \(h_n\) gives
\[
h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}},\qquad h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},
\]
and \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\) gives
\[
\int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx=O(1).
\]
If both fold-training interior measures are nonzero, the sum inside the displayed exponential dual continuation in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\) contains at least one strictly positive term and its positive power is strictly positive. Hence the extension is positive at every held-out \(x\in[h_n,1]\). This proves all deterministic and positivity assertions in the proposition. Positivity alone gives no upper bound on the squared weights, so the three stochastic orders and the risk conclusion remain downstream of \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\).
