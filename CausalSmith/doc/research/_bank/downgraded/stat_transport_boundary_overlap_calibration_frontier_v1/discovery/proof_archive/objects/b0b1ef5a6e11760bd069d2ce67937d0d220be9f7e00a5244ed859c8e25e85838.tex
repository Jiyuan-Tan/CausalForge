\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(s>0\) and \(t>0\), fix a law satisfying the proposition's assumptions, and set \(a=0\). By the definitions of the density factors, for every \(x\in(0,1]\),
\[
f_S(x)=x^a g_S(x)=g_S(x),
\qquad
f_T(x)=g_T(x),
\qquad
r(x)=x^{-a}\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}.
\]
The source- and target-boundary-factor assumptions give
\[
c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+},
\qquad
c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+},
\qquad x\in[0,1].
\]
Because \(c_{S,-}>0\), the denominator \(g_S(x)\) is strictly positive. Dividing the two pairs of bounds therefore yields, for every \(0<x\le1\),
\[
\frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
\le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}
\le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}.
\]

Since \(a=0<1\), the first branch in the definition of \(h_n\) applies and gives
\[
h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}.
\]
The condition \(s>0\) implies \(s+1>0\), so elementary exponent arithmetic gives
\[
h_n^{s+1}
=\left(n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\right)^{s+1}
=n^{-1/2}.
\]
Moreover, \(h_n\downarrow0\) as \(n\to\infty\). Applying the \(a<1\) branch of Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\) along this sequence gives
\[
\int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx\asymp1,
\]
uniformly over the model class. Hence the displayed integral is \(O(1)\), with finite comparison constants determined only by the fixed source and target boundary-factor constants.

It remains to prove positivity of the executable extension. Fix a fold \(k\) for which both interior training measures have positive total mass, and define
\[
I^T_{k,n}=\{j\in\mathcal T^T_k:X_j^T\ge h_n\}.
\]
Positive target interior mass implies \(I^T_{k,n}\ne\varnothing\) and \(|\mathcal T^T_k|>0\). Positive source and target interior masses exclude the zero-mass fallback in Definition \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\), so either its exact-feasibility branch or its penalized branch is used. On either branch, the selected calibration and target-marginal multipliers are finite real vectors. Thus, for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\) and \(j\in I^T_{k,n}\),
\[
\exp\!\left[-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
+\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
+\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right]>0.
\]
Indeed, the cost and spline coordinates are finite, the selected multipliers are real, and \(\eta_0>0\). Consequently the bracket in the raw continuation is a sum containing at least one strictly positive term:
\[
\frac{1}{|\mathcal T^T_k|}
\sum_{j\in I^T_{k,n}}
\exp\!\left[-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
+\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
+\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right]>0.
\]
The outer exponent \(\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)\) is strictly positive because \(\eta_0,\lambda_0>0\). Therefore
\[
\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0,
\qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\]
When \(a=0\), the deterministic downstream cap is
\[
\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h_n^{-a}=\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}>0.
\]
The capped RiCOT extension is the minimum of this positive cap and the positive raw continuation. Thus, for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\),
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
=\min\!\left\{
\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x),
\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}
\right\}>0.
\]

Every conclusion is deterministic conditional on the positive-interior-mass branch. The argument uses the density-factor bounds, the bandwidth definition, the Riesz threshold, and the RiCOT construction, but no additional smoothness inequality involving \(s\) or \(t\). It therefore holds for all declared \(s>0\) and \(t>0\). Positivity alone supplies no empirical norm bound, no nonzero realized residual sum, and no root-risk conclusion. \(\square\)
