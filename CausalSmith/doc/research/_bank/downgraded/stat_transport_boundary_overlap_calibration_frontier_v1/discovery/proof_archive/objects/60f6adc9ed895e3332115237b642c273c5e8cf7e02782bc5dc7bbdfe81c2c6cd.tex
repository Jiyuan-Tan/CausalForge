For fixed \(a,s,t,M\) with \(M\ge a+1\) and \(t>0\), along every sequence with \(m/n\to\kappa\), there is \(c>0\) such that
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c\left\{m^{-1/2}+\begin{cases}
n^{-1/2},&a<1,\\
\sqrt{\log n/n},&a=1,\\
n^{-(s+1)/(2s+a+1)},&a>1.
\end{cases}\right\}.
\]
This lower phase diagram requires no strict slack in the target-density bounds. Moreover, uniformly over \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\),
\[
m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
\asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
Thus the proved minimax lower frontier matches the exact truncated-oracle envelope; no data-driven minimax upper bound is asserted.
