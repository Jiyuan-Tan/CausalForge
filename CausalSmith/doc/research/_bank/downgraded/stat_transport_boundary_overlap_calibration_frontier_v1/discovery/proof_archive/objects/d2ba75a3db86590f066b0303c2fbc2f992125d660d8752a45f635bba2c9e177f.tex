For \(j\in\mathcal V^T_k\) and \(i\in\mathcal V^S_k\), define
\[
\widehat U^T_{j,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k)}(X_j^T),
\qquad
\widehat U^R_{i,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\{Z_i-\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k)}(X_i)\}.
\]
Because the boundary fit is untruncated, its ordinary ridge/local-polynomial linear summand is
\[
\widehat U^B_{i,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\frac{n}{m}\sum_{k:i\in\mathcal T^S_k}\frac{\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}}{|\mathcal T^S_k|}\left[\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_k}\mathbf 1\{X_j^T<h\}\mathbf R_h(X_j^T)^\top\widehat G_{k,h}^{-1}\mathbf R_h(X_i)\right]\{Z_i-\mathbf R_h(X_i)^\top\widehat\alpha_{k,h}\}.
\]
For fold-indexed numbers \((W_u)\), write \(\overline W_k^S=(N_k^S)^{-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}W_i\) and \(\overline W_k^T=(N_k^T)^{-1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_k}W_j\). Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}^2={}&\frac1{m^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^T}{N_k^T-1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_k}(\widehat U^T_{j,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\overline{\widehat U^T}_{k,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}})^2\\
&+\frac1{n^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^S}{N_k^S-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(\widehat U^R_{i,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\overline{\widehat U^R}_{k,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}})^2\\
&+\frac1{n^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^S}{N_k^S-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(\widehat U^B_{i,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\overline{\widehat U^B}_{k,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}})^2\\
&+\frac2{n^2}\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\frac{N_k^S}{N_k^S-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(\widehat U^R_{i,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\overline{\widehat U^R}_{k,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}})(\widehat U^B_{i,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\overline{\widehat U^B}_{k,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}).
\end{aligned}
\]
The four lines are the target variance, interior source variance, boundary source variance, and within-source covariance. There is no primitive source--target covariance because \(\mathbb P_{n,m}=P_S^{\otimes n}\otimes P_T^{\otimes m}\). A term with fold size at most one is zero.
