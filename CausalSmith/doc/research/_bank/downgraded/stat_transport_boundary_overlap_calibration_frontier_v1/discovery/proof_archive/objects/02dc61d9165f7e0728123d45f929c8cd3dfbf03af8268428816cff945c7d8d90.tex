Deterministic five-fold regression uses ridge-regularized order-\(p\) boundary local polynomials and the interior spline dimension \(J_{\mu,n}=\lceil n^{1/(2s+1)}\rceil\). Interior calibration uses the separate dimension \(J_{r,n}=\lceil(n/\log n)^{1/(2t+1)}\rceil\), fixed transport penalties \(\eta_0,\lambda_0\), slack penalty \(\xi_n=n^2\), and a normalized exponential dual continuation evaluated at held-out source covariates. The construction is measurable and executable. Its oracle rate arithmetic is proved, but held-out fitted-weight stability, leverage, the fold-varying discrepancy rate, variance consistency, Gaussian approximation, and multiplier validity are not.
