Use the source training block \(\mathcal T^S_1\), whose size is denoted by \(n_{\mathrm{tr}}=|\mathcal T^S_1|\).  Define the unregularized boundary and interior matrices
\[
 \widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_h=\frac1{n_{\mathrm{tr}}}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_1}\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}
 \mathbf R_h(X_i)\mathbf R_h(X_i)^\top,
\]
\[
 \widehat G^{\mu}_{h,J}=\frac1{n_{\mathrm{tr}}}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_1}\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}X_i^{-a}
 \mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)\mathbf b_{h,J}(X_i)^\top.
\]
Put
\[
 \widehat\alpha_h=(\widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_h+\lambda_nI_{p+1})^{-1}
 \frac1{n_{\mathrm{tr}}}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_1}
 \mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(X_i)Z_i,
\]
\[
 \widehat\beta^{\mu}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}=
 (\widehat G^{\mu}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}+\lambda_nI_{J_{\mu,n}})^{-1}
 \frac1{n_{\mathrm{tr}}}\sum_{i\in\mathcal T^S_1}
 \mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}X_i^{-a}\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_i)Z_i
\]
and define the linear, untruncated splice
\[
 \widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 \mathbf R_h(x)^\top\widehat\alpha_h,&0\le x<h,\\
 \mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)^\top\widehat\beta^{\mu}_{h,J_{\mu,n}},&h\le x\le1.
 \end{cases}
\]
The positive ridge makes this fit unique for every realized training sample; the estimator and interval decide whether to use it through the separate observable event.  For variance estimation, \(\widehat\mu^{(-i)}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}\) denotes the same ridge formula with source observation \(i\) deleted from its training sum when \(i\in\mathcal T^S_1\), and equals \(\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}\) when \(i\in\mathcal V^S_1\).
