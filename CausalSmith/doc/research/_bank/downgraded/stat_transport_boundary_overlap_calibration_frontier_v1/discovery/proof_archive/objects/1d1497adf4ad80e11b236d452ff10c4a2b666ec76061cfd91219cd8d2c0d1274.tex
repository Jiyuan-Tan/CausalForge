Write \(w_i=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\).  Addition and subtraction of the true response \(\mu\) gives the exact interior identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx
={}&\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx\right\}\\
&+C_{n,m}(h)-\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu).
\end{aligned}
\]
The two response-smoothness assumptions imply \(\mu=\mu_1-\mu_0\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\).  Homogeneity of the H\"older ball therefore gives the pointwise bound
\[
|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu)|
\le 2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.
\]
Thus the observable middle term in \(\widehat B_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) is required by the fold-varying decomposition, and the calibration term in that envelope bounds the last term even after capping.  Moreover, the one-dimensional H\"older entropy integral used by a possible Gaussian approximation is finite in the asserted range because
\[
\int_0^1\epsilon^{-1/(2s)}\,d\epsilon<\infty
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad s>\frac12.
\]
Finally, directly from the definition of \(\operatorname{cv}_{1-\gamma/2}\), if \(G\sim N(b,1)\) and \(|b|\le u\), then
\[
P\{|G|\le\operatorname{cv}_{1-\gamma/2}(u)\}\ge1-\gamma/2.
\]
These calculations reduce coverage to, but do not prove, a uniform asymptotically linear representation, consistency of the four-term variance statistic, a triangular-array Lindeberg bound, conditional approximation of the joint calibration--boundary supremum, and uniform integrability at the claimed rate.  None of those stochastic conclusions follows from the algebraic envelope alone, and the prerequisite held-out optimizer localization is precisely the unresolved part of \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\).
