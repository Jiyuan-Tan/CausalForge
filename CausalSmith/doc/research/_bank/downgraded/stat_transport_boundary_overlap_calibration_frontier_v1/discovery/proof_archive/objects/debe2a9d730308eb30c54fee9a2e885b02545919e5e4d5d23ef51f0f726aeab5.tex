Let \(C_{\mathrm{class}}\) be the explicit maximum of the fixed-degree spline approximation, Gram-inverse, and order-\(p\) boundary local-polynomial constants over the compact ranges determined by \(M\), \(\varepsilon\), \(c_{S,-}\), \(c_{S,+}\), \(c_{T,-}\), \(c_{T,+}\), and \(K_0\).  Define the analytic fixed-class bias radius
\[
\widehat B_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
=C_{\mathrm{class}}\left\{
h^{s+1}+\delta_{s,h}\delta_{t,h}
+\lambda_nJ_{\mu,n}+\lambda_nJ_{r,n}
\right\}.
\]
This quantity is deterministic once \((n,m,a,s,t,M)\) and the declared class constants are prespecified.  The approximation terms \(J_{\mu,n}^{-s}\) and \(J_{r,n}^{-t}\) already occur in \(\delta_{s,h}\) and \(\delta_{t,h}\).
