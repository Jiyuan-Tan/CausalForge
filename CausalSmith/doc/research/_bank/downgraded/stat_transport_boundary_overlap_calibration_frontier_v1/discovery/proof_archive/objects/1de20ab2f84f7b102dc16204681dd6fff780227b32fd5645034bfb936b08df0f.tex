Let \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h)\) be the intersection, over the fixed folds, of the following covariate-only events: both interior training samples are nonempty; every source weighted spline Gram matrix of dimensions \(J_{\mu,n}\) and \(J_{r,n}\) has eigenvalues between \(c_\star/J\) and \(C_\star/J\); every target and source knot cell has its count within one half and twice its population mean whenever that mean is at least \(C_\star\log(en)\); and the rescaled boundary Gram matrix \(h^{-(a+1)}\widehat G_{k,h}\) has eigenvalues between \(c_\star\) and \(C_\star\).  Here \(0<c_\star<C_\star<\infty\) are fixed computable constants determined only by the class constants, the fixed number of folds, and the fixed spline and local-polynomial degrees.  This definition records a diagnostic event only.  Its high probability, the localized nuisance bounds, and the leverage bounds are conclusions of \(\mathrm{lem:localized\text{-}direct\text{-}series\text{-}control}\), not assumptions.
