\textbf{Proof.}
Total variation gives
\[
Q_0\{\theta_1\in\mathcal C\}
\ge Q_1\{\theta_1\in\mathcal C\}-\eta
\ge1-\alpha_1-\eta.
\]
Therefore, under \(Q_0\), the probability that \(\mathcal C\) contains both \(\theta_0\) and \(\theta_1\) is at least \(\{1-\alpha_0-\alpha_1-\eta\}_+\). On that event an interval has length at least \(\Delta\). Taking expectations proves the claim. \(\square\)
