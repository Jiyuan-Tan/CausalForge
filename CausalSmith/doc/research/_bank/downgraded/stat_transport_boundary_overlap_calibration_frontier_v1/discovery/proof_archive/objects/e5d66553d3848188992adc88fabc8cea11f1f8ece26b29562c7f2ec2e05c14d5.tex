Put \(q_-=c_{T,-}/c_{S,+}\), \(q_+=c_{T,+}/c_{S,-}\), and \(q_0(x)=g_T(x)/g_S(x)\).  For \(h\in(0,1)\), define
\[
 V_a(h)=1+\int_h^1x^{-a}\,dx,
 \qquad
 N_h=\frac{n}{V_a(h)},
 \qquad
 \lambda_n=n^{-3}.
\]
For an integer \(J\ge1\), define the worst weighted knot-cell second moment
\[
 W_a(h,J)=\int_h^{\min\{1,h+(q+1)/J\}}x^{-a}\,dx.
\]
For \(u>0\), let
\[
 \mathsf J_u(h)=\max\!\left\{1,\left\lfloor
 \min\!\left[
 \left\{\frac{N_h}{\log^3(en)}\right\}^{1/(2u+1)},
 \left\{\frac{n}{V_a(h)\log^3(en)}\right\}^{1/2},
 \frac{nh^a}{\log^3(en)}
 \right]\right\rfloor\right\}
\]
and
\[
 \delta_{u,h}=\mathsf J_u(h)^{-u}
 +\sqrt{\frac{\mathsf J_u(h)V_a(h)\log(en)}{n}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{\mathsf J_u(h)\log(em)}{m}}.
\]
The estimator uses \(J_{\mu,n}=\mathsf J_s(h_n)\) and \(J_{r,n}=\mathsf J_t(h_n)\).  The two caps after the approximation--variance choice are finite-sample stability caps: they make, respectively, the weighted-variance and weighted-envelope Bernstein exponents at least a constant multiple of \(\log^3(en)\).  These quantities are deterministic functions of the declared sample sizes, boundary exponent, smoothness indices, and bandwidth; none is a member-property condition.
