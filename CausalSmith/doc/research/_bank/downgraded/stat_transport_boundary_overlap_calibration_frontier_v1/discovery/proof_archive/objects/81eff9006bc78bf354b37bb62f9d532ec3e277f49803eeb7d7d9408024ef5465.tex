For fixed \(a,s,t,M\) satisfying \(t>1/2\), \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), and \(a<2t(2s+1)\), along every triangular-array sequence with \(m/n\to\kappa\), the deterministic direct-series choices \(h=h_n\), \(J_\mu=J_{\mu,n}\), and \(J_r=J_{r,n}\) obey
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
 \Bigl\{\mathbb E_{\mathbb P_{n,m}}
 [\{\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau\}^2]\Bigr\}^{1/2}\\
&\qquad\le C\left\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)
 +\delta_{s,h_n}\delta_{t,h_n}
 +\lambda_n(J_{\mu,n}+J_{r,n})
 +\delta_{s,h_n}\sqrt{\frac{\log(en)}m}
 +\delta_{s,h_n}\sqrt{\frac{V_a(h_n)\log(en)}n}\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The observable failure event satisfies
\[
 \sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
 \mathbb P_{n,m}\{\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)^c\}
 \le C\exp\{-c\log^3(en)\}.
\]
On the source validation block, uniformly over the same class,
\[
 \frac1{N^S_1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_1}
 \{\widehat r^{\mathrm{DS}}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\}^2
 \mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h_n\}=O_P\{V_a(h_n)\},
\]
and the maximum summand divided by the corresponding sum is \(o_P(1)\), with the ratio defined as zero if the sum is zero.  If additionally \(s>1/2\), the stability caps in \(\mathsf J_s(h_n)\) and \(\mathsf J_t(h_n)\) are asymptotically inactive and the displayed remainder after \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\) is \(o\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}\); hence the original matched upper frontier holds in that subregime.  Without \(s>1/2\), the displayed formula is the proved upper side of the honest minimax bracket and no matching claim is made.
