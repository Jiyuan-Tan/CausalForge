Under the additional normalization and target-slack restrictions stated in the proposed claim, \(\mathrm{thm:minimax\text{-}lower}\) gives
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.

Independently, \(\mathrm{lem:oracle\text{-}truncation\text{-}envelope}\) proves that the boundary bias plus the truncated-oracle source standard deviation has the same three-regime order. This establishes a matching oracle benchmark, but an oracle ratio is not one estimator measurable with respect to \(\mathcal D_{n,m}\). The minimax upper inequality therefore remains equivalent to producing and controlling the data-driven witness asserted in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\).
