For fixed \(a,s,t,M,\varepsilon,c_{S,-},c_{S,+},c_{T,-},c_{T,+}\) with \(t>1/2\) and \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), there is \(c>0\) such that \[\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.\] At \(a=1\), the source term is \(c\sqrt{\log n/n}\); it is obtained by a multiscale mixture of boundary bumps and has no hidden polynomial loss.
