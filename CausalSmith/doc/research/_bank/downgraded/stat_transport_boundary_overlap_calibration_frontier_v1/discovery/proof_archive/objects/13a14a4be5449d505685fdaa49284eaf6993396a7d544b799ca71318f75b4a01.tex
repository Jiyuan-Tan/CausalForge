On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h,J)\), define the fixed-design Hölder optimal-recovery discrepancy
\[
B_X(h,J)=\sup_{f\in\mathcal H^s(2M)}
\left|\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^mf(X_j^T)-\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J)f(X_i)\right|.
\]
Let \(\mathcal Z_r\) be the ordered union of the observed source and target covariates and the dyadic mesh \(\{\ell2^{-r}:0\le\ell\le2^r\}\).  On \(\mathcal Z_r\), introduce variables for the values of a candidate function and its derivatives through order \(p\). Impose, for every ordered pair of mesh points and every derivative order, the two Taylor inequalities with remainder \(2M|x-z|^{s-v}/(p-v)!\), together with the radius bounds defining \(\mathcal H^s(2M)\). Maximizing the two signs of the displayed linear functional is a finite linear program. Denote its larger optimum by \(U_r(h,J)\).  Starting at \(r=0\), refine until
\[
2M\{1+\sum_i|\Gamma_i(h,J)|\}2^{-rs}\le n^{-2},
\]
breaking a tie at the smallest such \(r\), and set
\[
\overline B_X(h,J)=U_r(h,J)+2M\{1+\sum_i|\Gamma_i(h,J)|\}2^{-rs}.
\]
The Taylor remainder makes \(B_X(h,J)\le\overline B_X(h,J)\le B_X(h,J)+n^{-2}\). The program has \(O\{(p+1)(n+m+2^r)\}\) variables and \(O\{(p+1)(n+m+2^r)^2\}\) linear constraints.  Off the good branch set both quantities equal to one.
