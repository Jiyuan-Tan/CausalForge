Under the source- and target-boundary-factor assumptions, the corrected construction in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\) is a Borel-measurable map of \(\mathcal D_{n,m}\) for every fixed \((n,m,h,J)\), including folds with zero interior mass and samples with tied covariates. On a nonzero fold, write \(S=\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\), \(T=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), \(\beta=\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}\), \(\widetilde r=\widehat r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\), \(C_h=(c_{T,+}/c_{S,-})h^{-a}\), \(\widehat r=\min\{\widetilde r,C_h\}\), and \(d_{\mathrm{cap}}=(\widetilde r-C_h)_+\). Then, pointwise on \([h,1]\),
\[
0\le r\le C_h,\qquad |\widehat r-r|\le|\widetilde r-r|,
\qquad 0\le d_{\mathrm{cap}}\le|\widetilde r-r|.
\]
If \(\delta=0\) in the exact branch and \(\delta=\widehat\delta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) in the penalized branch, the training-sample calibration identity is
\[
\int\beta\widehat r\,dS-\int\beta\,dT
=\delta-\int\beta d_{\mathrm{cap}}\,dS.
\]
Thus exact spline calibration belongs to the raw first-marginal density and need not survive capping. If \(\Delta^{\mathrm{raw}}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) denotes the held-out residual obtained from \(\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}\) by replacing the capped weight by the raw continuation, then, for every bounded measurable \(v\),
\[
\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)-\Delta^{\mathrm{raw}}_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)
=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n d_{\mathrm{cap}}^{(-k_S(i))}(X_i)v(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\},
\]
and the absolute value of the right-hand side is at most
\[
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n
\left|\widehat r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)-r(X_i)\right|
|v(X_i)|\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}.
\]
In particular, capping is an oracle-centered contraction and converts raw localization into a cap-remainder bound, but it does not itself supply such localization.
