For every \(s>0\) and \(t>0\), in the special case \(a=0\),

\frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}\le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}\le\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},\qquad 0<x\le1,
\]
\(h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\), and
\[
h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},\qquad \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx=O(1).
\]
For every fold whose source and target interior training measures are both nonzero, the dual extension \(\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)\) is strictly positive for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\). These deterministic facts do not by themselves assert an empirical norm bound, a nonzero realized residual sum, or a root-risk bound.
