\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\widehat\mu_i=\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_S(i))}(X_i)\) and \(\widehat\mu_j^T=\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_T(j))}(X_j^T)\). The interior estimator is
\[
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}
=\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu_j^T\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}
+\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i(Z_i-\widehat\mu_i).
\]
Add and subtract the two empirical terms involving the true \(\mu\). The result is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}-\int_h^1\mu f_T
={}&\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i(Z_i-\mu(X_i))
+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\int_h^1\mu f_T\right\}\\
&+\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu_j^T\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}
-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\widehat\mu_i\\
&-\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}
-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\mu(X_i)\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The third line is \(C_{n,m}(h)\), while the bracket on the fourth line is \(\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu)\). This proves the exact decomposition.

Because \(\mu=\mu_1-\mu_0\) and each \(\mu_d\) belongs to \(\mathcal H^s(M)\), the triangle inequality for the Hölder norm gives \(\mu\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\). Homogeneity then gives
\[
|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu)|
\le2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.
\]

For each source fold, put \(R_i=\widehat U^R_i-\overline{\widehat U^R}_k\) and \(B_i=\widehat U^B_i-\overline{\widehat U^B}_k\). The last three lines of the standard-error definition have numerator
\[
\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(R_i^2+B_i^2+2R_iB_i)
=\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}(R_i+B_i)^2\ge0.
\]
Adding the nonnegative target-fold line proves \(\widehat{\mathrm{se}}^2\ge0\).

For \(u\ge0\), set \(c=u+z_{1-\gamma/4}\). Uniformly over \(|b|\le u\),
\[
P\{|N(b,1)|>c\}
\le P\{N(0,1)>c-b\}+P\{N(0,1)<-c-b\}
\le\frac\gamma4+\frac\gamma4=\frac\gamma2.
\]
Hence \(\operatorname{cv}_{1-\gamma/2}(u)\le u+z_{1-\gamma/4}\). In the positive-standard-error branch, multiplication by \(\widehat{\mathrm{se}}\) gives the stated length bound; in the zero-standard-error branch the interval length is exactly \(2(\widehat B+\widehat E)\), so the same inequality holds.

Finally,
\[
\int_0^1\epsilon^{-1/(2s)}\,d\epsilon
=\begin{cases}
\dfrac{2s}{2s-1},&s>1/2,\\
+\infty,&0<s\le1/2.
\end{cases}
\]
This proves the entropy assertion and completes the lemma. \(\square\)
