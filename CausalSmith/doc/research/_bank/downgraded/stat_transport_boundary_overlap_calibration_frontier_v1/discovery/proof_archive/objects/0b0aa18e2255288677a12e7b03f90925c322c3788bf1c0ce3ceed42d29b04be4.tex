For each fold \(k\), form \(\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\) and \(\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\) from the training complements. Let \(\mathcal F^\circ_{h,J_{r,n},k}\) be the finite plans \(\Pi\) that have strictly positive density relative to \(\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\otimes\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), satisfy \(\Pi_2=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), and satisfy
\[
\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)\,d\Pi_1(x)=\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z).
\]
If \(\mathcal F^\circ_{h,J_{r,n},k}\ne\varnothing\), define \(\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) as the unique minimizer over its closure of
\[
\int c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,z)\,d\Pi+\eta_0\operatorname{KL}(\Pi\,\|\,\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\otimes\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h)+\lambda_0\operatorname{KL}(\Pi_1\,\|\,\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h).
\]
Otherwise minimize the same strictly convex criterion subject to \(\Pi_2=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), plus
\[
\xi_n\left\|\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}\,d\Pi_1-\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z)\right\|_2^2.
\]
Generalized Kullback--Leibler divergence is used for finite measures. Let \(\widehat\theta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) and \(\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},j}\) be the associated KKT multipliers for nonconstant calibration and the target-marginal equations; among all dual solutions choose the pair of smallest Euclidean norm, with lexicographic tie-breaking. For every \(x\in[h,1]\), define
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=\left[\frac1{|\mathcal T^T_k|}\sum_{j\in\mathcal T^T_k}\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}\exp\left\{-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)+\widehat\theta_{h,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)+\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right\}\right]^{\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)}.
\]
This is the KKT continuation of \(d\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},1}/d\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\), agrees with that density at source training atoms, and is positive and measurable at held-out points. If either interior training measure is zero, set the extension to zero. The deterministic schedules are \(\eta_n=\eta_0\), \(\lambda_n=\lambda_0\), \(\xi_n=n^2\), and \(J=J_{r,n}\); the boundary-splice bandwidth remains \(h=h_n\) and is not used as the calibration dimension.
