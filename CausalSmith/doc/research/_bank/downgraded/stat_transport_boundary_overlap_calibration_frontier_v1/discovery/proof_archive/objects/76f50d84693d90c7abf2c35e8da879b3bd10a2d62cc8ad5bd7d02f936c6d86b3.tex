For \(h\in(0,1)\) and positive integers \(J_\mu,J_r\), define
\[
\mathcal I_{h,J_\mu,J_r}=\left[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_\mu,J_r}
\ \pm\ \left\{
B_X(h,J_\mu,J_r)
+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_\mu,J_r)^2}
+\sqrt{\frac{2\log(4/\gamma)}m}
\right\}\right].
\]
The interval is defined for every realized sample, including every fallback branch of \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\). It is a finite-sample execution certificate; its definition makes no assertion that its observable half-width has the oracle frontier order.
