Conditionally on \(\mathcal D_{n,m}\), let \(\mathbb P^*\) make the source and target multipliers mutually independent standard-normal variables. For \(v\in\mathcal H^s(1)\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb G^{\mathrm{cal},*}_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)={}&\frac1m\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_k}\zeta_j^T\left[v(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\frac1{N_k^T}\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal V^T_k}v(X_\ell^T)\mathbf 1\{X_\ell^T\ge h\}\right]\\
&-\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}\zeta_i^S\left[\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)v(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}-\frac1{N_k^S}\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal V^S_k}\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_\ell)v(X_\ell)\mathbf 1\{X_\ell\ge h\}\right].
\end{aligned}
\]
Define
\[
\mathbb G^{\mathrm{bd},*}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^{K_0}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_k}\zeta_i^S\left[\widehat U^B_{i,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\frac1{N_k^S}\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal V^S_k}\widehat U^B_{\ell,h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\right]
\]
and
\[
\widehat E_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\inf\left\{e\ge0:\mathbb P^*\left(\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}\left|2M\mathbb G^{\mathrm{cal},*}_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)+\mathbb G^{\mathrm{bd},*}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\right|\le e\,\middle|\,\mathcal D_{n,m}\right)\ge1-\gamma/2\right\}.
\]
Empty-fold averages are zero. The same source multipliers appear in both source pieces, retaining their covariance, and all target multipliers are independent of all source multipliers.
