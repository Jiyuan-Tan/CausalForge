The critical source lower bound cannot be obtained from one bump: at \(a=1\), a single-scale construction gives only \(n^{-1/2}\), so the logarithmic factor relies on the proved multiscale sign mixture. On the upper side, the current transport program supplies an exact algebraic carrier and finite-sample Hoeffding coverage, not empirical frontier attainment. Joint control of the capped held-out continuation, calibration residual, boundary and interior regression error, realized Hölder discrepancy, and coefficient norm remains open. The quarantined conditions \(t>1/2\) and \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\) are plausible ingredients for that open localization problem, but they are not model-class restrictions and do not by themselves establish optimizer stability or a rate.
