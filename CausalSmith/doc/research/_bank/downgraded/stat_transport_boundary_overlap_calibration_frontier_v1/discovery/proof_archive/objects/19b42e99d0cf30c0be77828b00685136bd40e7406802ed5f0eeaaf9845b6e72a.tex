Let \(C_{\mathrm{bias}}\) be the registered computable class constant.  Define
\[
 \widehat B_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
 =C_{\mathrm{bias}}\left[
 h^{s+1}+\delta_{s,h}\delta_{t,h}
 +\lambda_n(J_{\mu,n}+J_{r,n})
 +\delta_{s,h}\sqrt{\frac{\log(en)}{m}}
 +\delta_{s,h}\sqrt{\frac{V_a(h)\log(en)}{n}}
 \right].
\]
This is a deterministic, directly evaluable fixed-class envelope once \((a,s,t,M,c_{S,-},c_{S,+},c_{T,-},c_{T,+},\varepsilon)\) and \((n,m)\) are specified.  The first term is the integrated boundary local-polynomial remainder, the second is the de-singularized Galerkin product, the third is the ridge perturbation, and the last two dominate the held-out target and source empirical-process remainders.  The envelope contains no unknown population bias and no fitted-rate assumption.
