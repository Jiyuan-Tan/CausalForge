\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a member of the model and set \(a=0\).  The numerical restrictions \(t>1/2\) and
\(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\) are not used in the argument below.  By the definitions of
the density factors, for every \(x\in(0,1]\),
\[
 f_S(x)=x^a g_S(x)=g_S(x),
 \qquad
 f_T(x)=g_T(x),
 \qquad
 r(x)=x^{-a}\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}.
\]
The source- and target-boundary-factor assumptions give
\[
 c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+},
 \qquad
 c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+},
 \qquad x\in[0,1].
\]
Because \(c_{S,-}>0\), the denominator \(g_S(x)\) is strictly positive.  Dividing the
two pairs of bounds therefore yields, for every \(0<x\le1\),
\[
 \frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
 \le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}.
\]

Since \(a=0<1\), the first branch in the definition of \(h_n\) applies and gives
\[
 h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}.
\]
The declared smoothness index satisfies \(s>0\), hence \(s+1>0\), and elementary
exponent arithmetic gives
\[
 h_n^{s+1}
 =\left(n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\right)^{s+1}
 =n^{-1/2}.
\]
Applying the \(a<1\) branch of Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\) with
\(h=h_n\) gives
\[
 \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx\asymp1,
\]
uniformly over the model class, and thus the displayed integral is \(O(1)\).  The
comparison constants are finite and depend only on the fixed source and target
boundary-factor constants.

It remains to establish positivity of the executable extension.  Fix a fold \(k\) for
which both interior training measures have positive total mass and define
\[
 I^T_{k,n}=\{j\in\mathcal T^T_k:X_j^T\ge h_n\}.
\]
Positive target interior mass implies \(I^T_{k,n}\ne\varnothing\) and
\(|\mathcal T^T_k|>0\).  Positive source and target interior masses exclude the
zero-mass fallback in Definition \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\), so either its
exact-feasibility branch or its penalized branch is used.  On either branch the
selected calibration and target-marginal multipliers are finite real vectors.  Hence,
for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\) and \(j\in I^T_{k,n}\),
\[
 \exp\!\left[-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
 +\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
   \widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 +\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right]>0.
\]
Indeed, the cost and spline coordinates are finite, the selected multipliers are real,
and \(\eta_0>0\).  Consequently the bracket in the raw continuation is a sum containing
at least one strictly positive term:
\[
 \frac{1}{|\mathcal T^T_k|}
 \sum_{j\in I^T_{k,n}}
 \exp\!\left[-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
 +\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
   \widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 +\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right]>0.
\]
The outer exponent \(\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)\) is strictly positive because
\(\eta_0,\lambda_0>0\).  Therefore
\[
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0,
 \qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\]
When \(a=0\), the deterministic downstream cap is
\[
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h_n^{-a}=\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}>0.
\]
The capped RiCOT extension is the minimum of this positive cap and the positive raw
continuation.  Thus, for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\),
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 =\min\!\left\{
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x),
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}
 \right\}>0.
\]

Every conclusion above is deterministic conditional on the positive-interior-mass
branch and follows from the density-factor bounds, the bandwidth definition, the Riesz
threshold, and the RiCOT construction.  In particular, positivity alone supplies no
empirical norm bound, no nonzero realized residual sum, and no root-risk conclusion.
Moreover, because neither smoothness inequality involving \(t\) was used, the same
deterministic conclusions remain valid for every declared \(s>0\) and \(t>0\) whenever
the other stated premises hold. \(\square\)
