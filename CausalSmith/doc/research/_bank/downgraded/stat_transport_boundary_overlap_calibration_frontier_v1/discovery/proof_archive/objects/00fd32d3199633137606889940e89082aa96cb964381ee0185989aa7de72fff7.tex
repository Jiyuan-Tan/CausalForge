Put \(p=\lceil s\rceil-1\) and \(\beta=s-p\in(0,1]\), and use the convention
\[
\mathcal H^s(L)=\left\{v\in C^p[0,1]:
\max_{0\le r\le p}\|v^{(r)}\|_\infty\le L,
\ [v^{(p)}]_{\beta}\le L\right\}.
\]
For integers \(n,m\ge2\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), define the observable H\"older design discrepancy
\[
B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})=
\sup_{\substack{v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\\ \|v\|_\infty\le1}}
\left|\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})v(X_i)-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^mv(X_j^T)\right|.
\]
Let \(z_1,\ldots,z_N\) be the distinct sites among \(X_1,\ldots,X_n,X_1^T,\ldots,X_m^T\), and set
\[
c_\ell=\sum_{i:X_i=z_\ell}\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
-\frac1m\#\{j:X_j^T=z_\ell\},
\qquad \ell=1,\ldots,N.
\]
For each sign \(\sigma\in\{-1,1\}\), maximize
\[
\sigma\sum_{\ell=1}^N c_\ell u_{\ell,0}
\]
over the \(N(p+1)\) real variables \(u_{\ell,r}\), \(1\le\ell\le N\), \(0\le r\le p\), subject to
\[
|u_{\ell,0}|\le1,
\qquad |u_{\ell,r}|\le2M\quad(1\le r\le p),
\]
and, for every \(\ell,k\in\{1,\ldots,N\}\) and \(0\le r\le p\),
\[
\left|u_{\ell,r}-\sum_{q=r}^p
u_{k,q}\frac{(z_\ell-z_k)^{q-r}}{(q-r)!}\right|
\le
2M\frac{\Gamma(\beta+1)}{\Gamma(s-r+1)}|z_\ell-z_k|^{s-r}.
\]
Each absolute-value constraint is implemented as two linear inequalities. Define
\[
\overline B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
\]
as the maximum of the two optimal values. Thus \(\overline B_X\) is computed by two finite linear programs with \(N(p+1)\) variables and \(O(N^2p)\) linear inequalities. The domination \(B_X\le\overline B_X\), equality for \(0<s\le1\), and the conservative outer-bound interpretation for \(s>1\) are proved in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\); they are not assumed by this construction. No stochastic-order or frontier-order claim is part of the definition.
