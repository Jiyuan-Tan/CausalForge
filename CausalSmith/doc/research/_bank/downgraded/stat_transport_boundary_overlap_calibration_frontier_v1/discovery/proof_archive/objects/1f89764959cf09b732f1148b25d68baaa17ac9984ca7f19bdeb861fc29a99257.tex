\textbf{Proof.}
By \(\mathrm{lem:source\text{-}only\text{-}frontier\text{-}lower}\), for the fixed \(a,s,t,M\) and the other fixed model constants there are \(c_0>0\) and \(n_0\) such that, for every \(n\ge n_0\) and every \(m\),
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c_0\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{1}
\]
That lemma uses subexperiments with \(f_T=1\). The declared ranges \(c_{T,-}\le 1\le c_{T,+}\) therefore place those same subexperiments in the model even when either target-density bound is attained. Hence (1) requires no strict slack in the target-density bounds.

We next convert (1) into the displayed two-sample lower bound. By \(\mathrm{ass:sample\text{-}balance}\),
\[
 \frac{m}{n}\longrightarrow\kappa\in(0,\infty).
\]
Consequently, there are constants \(c_\kappa,C_\kappa>0\) and \(n_1\) such that
\[
 c_\kappa n\le m\le C_\kappa n
 \qquad(n\ge n_1).
\tag{2}
\]
The lower inequality in (2) implies
\[
 m^{-1/2}\le c_\kappa^{-1/2}n^{-1/2}.
\tag{3}
\]
In each of the three regimes, \(n^{-1/2}\) is at most the source rate \(\rho_n(a,s)\), eventually. Indeed, if \(a<1\), then \(\rho_n(a,s)=n^{-1/2}\). If \(a=1\), then
\[
 \rho_n(1,s)=\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}\ge n^{-1/2}
\tag{4}
\]
for \(n\ge e\). Finally, if \(a>1\), then positivity of \(s\) gives
\[
 \frac{s+1}{2s+a+1}<\frac12,
\]
and hence
\[
 \rho_n(a,s)=n^{-(s+1)/(2s+a+1)}\ge n^{-1/2}.
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (3)--(5) yield a constant \(C_1<\infty\), depending only on the fixed asymptotic ratio and regime parameters, for which
\[
 m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\le C_1\rho_n(a,s)
\tag{6}
\]
eventually. Combining (1) and (6),
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)
 \ge \frac{c_0}{C_1}\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Substituting the three branches of \(\rho_n(a,s)\) from \(\mathrm{def:frontier\text{-}rate}\) into (7) proves the stated regular, critical, and nonregular minimax lower phase diagram.

It remains to establish the benchmark assertion. The already established \(\mathrm{lem:oracle\text{-}truncation\text{-}envelope}\) states, uniformly over \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), that
\[
 m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}
 +n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
 \asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{8}
\]
Here \(h_n^{s+1}\) is the deterministic boundary-truncation bias order and the final term on the left of (8) is the exact standard-deviation order of the truncated oracle source representer. Thus (8) is an equal-order deterministic oracle envelope. It does not define, localize, or analyze any estimator measurable with respect to \(\mathcal D_{n,m}\), and therefore it is not a minimax upper bound. Equations (7) and (8) prove precisely the claimed lower frontier and its truncated-oracle benchmark. \(\square\)
