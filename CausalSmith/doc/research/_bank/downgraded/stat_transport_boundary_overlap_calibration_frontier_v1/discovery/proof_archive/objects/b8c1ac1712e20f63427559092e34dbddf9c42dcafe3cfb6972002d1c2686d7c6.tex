For every \(n,m\ge2\), every \(\gamma\in(0,1/2)\), and every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), the finite-sample interval in \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}aware\text{-}interval}\) satisfies
\[
\mathbb P_{n,m}\left\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\right\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Its realized length is exactly
\[
2\left\{
B_X(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2}
+\sqrt{\frac{2\log(4/\gamma)}m}
\right\}.
\]
In particular, on any realized covariate design satisfying \(B_X(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\le b\) and \(\sum_i\Gamma_i(h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2\le g^2\), its length is at most \(2\{b+\varepsilon^{-1}g\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)}+\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)/m}\}\). This is a fixed-class realized-design certificate; it asserts no stochastic order for \(b\) or \(g\), no expected-length rate, and no Gaussian, multiplier, variance-consistency, or empirical frontier-attainment conclusion.
