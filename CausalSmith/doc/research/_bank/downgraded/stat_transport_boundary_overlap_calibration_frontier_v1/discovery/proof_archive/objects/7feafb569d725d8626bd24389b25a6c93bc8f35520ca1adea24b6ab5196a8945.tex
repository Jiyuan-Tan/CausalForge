The oracle and deterministic tuning calculations do close. Define
\[
V_h=1+\mathbf 1\{a=1\}\log(1/h)+\mathbf 1\{a>1\}h^{1-a}.
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\), uniformly over the model class,
\[
\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h\}\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2\asymp V_h.
\]
The boundary approximation bias and the oracle source standard deviation therefore give the envelope
\[
m^{-1/2}+h^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}V_h^{1/2}.
\]
At \(h=h_n\), \(\mathrm{lem:oracle\text{-}truncation\text{-}envelope}\) identifies this expression with \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\). The two deterministic sieve dimensions satisfy
\[
J_{\mu,n}^{-s}+\sqrt{J_{\mu,n}/n}\asymp n^{-s/(2s+1)}
\]
and
\[
J_{r,n}^{-t}+\sqrt{J_{r,n}\log J_{r,n}/n}\asymp(\log n/n)^{t/(2t+1)}.
\]
Consequently, the declared product-smoothness inequality makes the product of these two envelopes \(o(n^{-1/2})\). Also \(nh_n^{a+1}/\log J_{r,n}\to\infty\) in every regime. These statements establish the target rate arithmetic but neither identify the empirical transport weight with the truncated oracle ratio nor control its held-out leverage.

The attempted cellwise spline localization reveals why the preceding effective boundary count is insufficient. For \(a>1\), writing \(D=2s+a+1\), the expected source count on a first-knot support has the polynomial orders
\[
\frac{nh_n^a}{J_{\mu,n}}\asymp n^{(2s+1)/D-1/(2s+1)}
\]
and
\[
\frac{nh_n^a}{J_{r,n}}\asymp n^{(2s+1)/D-1/(2t+1)}(\log n)^{1/(2t+1)}.
\]
Either quantity may converge to zero under the frozen assumptions, because those assumptions impose no relation between \(a\) and the sieve resolutions. Thus a Bernstein-plus-uniform-Gram route cannot yield the asserted localization from the available premises.
