After replacing the infinite zero-standard-error convention by its finite continuous extension and restricting the global Hölder empirical-process claim to \(s>1/2\), the algebraic coverage reduction is valid: on an event where the absolute bias is at most \(\widehat B_{h,J}+\widehat E_{h,J}\) and the centered term is asymptotically standard normal, the definition of \(\operatorname{cv}_{1-\gamma/2}\), followed by a union bound over two events of failure probability at most \(\gamma/2\), gives coverage at least \(1-\gamma-o(1)\). What remains is the uniform stochastic theorem establishing those two events for the empirical optimizer and the displayed foldwise variance statistic.
