Let \(Q_0\) and \(Q_1\) be probability laws on a common measurable space. Then \(\operatorname{TV}(Q_1,Q_0)\le\{\operatorname{KL}(Q_1,Q_0)/2\}^{1/2}\). Consequently, if real parameters \(\theta_0,\theta_1\) have separation \(\Delta=|\theta_1-\theta_0|>0\) and \(\operatorname{KL}(Q_1,Q_0)\le\varkappa<2\), every real-valued statistic \(T\) satisfies
\[
\max_{k\in\{0,1\}}\{\mathbb E_{Q_k}(T-\theta_k)^2\}^{1/2}
\ge\frac{\Delta}{2\sqrt2}\left(1-\sqrt{\varkappa/2}\right)^{1/2}.
\]
