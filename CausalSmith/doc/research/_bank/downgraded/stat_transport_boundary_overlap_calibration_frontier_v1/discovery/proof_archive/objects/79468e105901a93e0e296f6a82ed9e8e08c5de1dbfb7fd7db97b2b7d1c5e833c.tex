The phase diagram is presently a lower-bound and oracle benchmark, not a validated risk guarantee for the empirical RiCOT estimator. The critical logarithm at \(a=1\) is structural: it is generated by many boundary scales, not by one bump. For cardiomyopathy transport, the result can diagnose when source recruitment near a risk boundary makes root precision impossible, but the proposed estimator and interval should remain research procedures until their empirical stability and coverage proofs close.
