\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\) with \(\|v\|_\infty\le1\) and put \(u_{\ell,r}=v^{(r)}(z_\ell)\). The coordinate bounds in the linear programs follow directly from the H\"older-ball definition. For \(0\le r\le p\), Taylor's formula applied to \(v^{(r)}\) about \(z_k\), with the \(\beta\)-H\"older bound on \(v^{(p)}\), gives
\[
\left|v^{(r)}(z_\ell)-\sum_{q=r}^p
v^{(q)}(z_k)\frac{(z_\ell-z_k)^{q-r}}{(q-r)!}\right|
\le
2M\frac{\Gamma(\beta+1)}{\Gamma(s-r+1)}|z_\ell-z_k|^{s-r}.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus every H\"older trace is feasible. Grouping coincident observed sites gives the exact identity
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i v(X_i)-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^mv(X_j^T)
=\sum_{\ell=1}^Nc_\ell u_{\ell,0}.
\tag{2}
\]
Maximizing (2) and its negative first over H\"older traces and then over the larger jet polytope proves \(B_X\le\overline B_X\).

There are \(N(p+1)\) jet coordinates. The coordinate bounds contribute \(O(N(p+1))\) scalar inequalities, and the ordered pairs \((\ell,k)\), derivative indices \(r\), and two signs in (1) contribute \(O(N^2(p+1))\) inequalities.

If \(0<s\le1\), then \(p=0\) and the jet restrictions are exactly
\[
|u_{\ell,0}|\le1,
\qquad
|u_{\ell,0}-u_{k,0}|\le2M|z_\ell-z_k|^s.
\tag{3}
\]
The metric \(d_s(x,y)=|x-y|^s\) satisfies the triangle inequality. The McShane formula extends every trace satisfying (3) to a function on \([0,1]\) with H\"older constant \(2M\); composing this extension with the one-Lipschitz projection onto \([-1,1]\) preserves the trace, its H\"older constant, and the sup-norm bound. Therefore the feasible traces coincide with the H\"older traces and equality holds. For \(s>1\), only necessity of (1) was used, so the program is honestly a relaxation. \(\square\)
