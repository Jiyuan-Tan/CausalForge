For fixed \(a,s,t,M,\varepsilon,c_{S,-},c_{S,+},c_{T,-},c_{T,+}\) with \(t>0\) and \(M\ge a+1\), there is \(c>0\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\) and every \(m\),
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c\rho_n(a,s).
\]
No strict inequality in the target-density bounds is required. At \(a=1\), the source lower bound is \(c\sqrt{\log n/n}\) and is generated by a multiscale sign mixture whose target covariate law is common to all mixture components.
