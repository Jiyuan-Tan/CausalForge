For \(n,m\ge2\), \(h\in(0,1)\), and the observable coefficients in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}linear\text{-}coefficients}\), define
\[
B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})=
\sup_{\substack{v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\\ \|v\|_\infty\le1}}
\left|
\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})v(X_i)
-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m v(X_j^T)
\right|.
\]
This is an observable support-function calculation: equivalently, it is the larger optimum of the two linear optimal-recovery programs obtained by choosing either sign of the displayed affine functional, with optimization variable \(v\) and explicit constraints \(v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\) and \(\|v\|_\infty\le1\). The feasible set is fixed, convex, and uniformly bounded, so the value is finite. No polynomial-time guarantee and no stochastic-order claim are part of the definition.
