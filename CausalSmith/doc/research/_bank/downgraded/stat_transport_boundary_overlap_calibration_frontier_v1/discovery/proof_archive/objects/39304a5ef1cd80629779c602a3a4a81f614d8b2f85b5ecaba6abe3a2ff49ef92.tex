Let \(q_0=g_T/g_S\), and let \(\Pi_J\) be orthogonal projection in \(L^2(g_S(x)\,dx)\) on \([h,1]\) onto \(\mathcal B_{h,J}\). Then
\[
\int_h^1\{\Pi_J\mu(x)-\mu(x)\}f_T(x)\,dx
=\int_h^1\{q_0(x)-\Pi_Jq_0(x)\}\{\Pi_J\mu(x)-\mu(x)\}g_S(x)\,dx,
\]
and, uniformly over the model class, the absolute value is at most \(CJ^{-(s+t)}\).
