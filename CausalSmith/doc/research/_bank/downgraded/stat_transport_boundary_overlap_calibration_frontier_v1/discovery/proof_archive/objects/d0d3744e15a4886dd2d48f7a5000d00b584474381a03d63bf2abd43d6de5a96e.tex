For each fold \(k\), form \(\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\) and \(\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\) from the training complements. Put
\[
d_{r,n}=J_{r,n}+q,
\qquad
\mathbf b_{h,J_{r,n}}=(N_1,\ldots,N_{d_{r,n}})^\top,
\]
where \((N_1,\ldots,N_{d_{r,n}})\) is the standard left-to-right normalized degree-\(q\) B-spline basis for the \(J_{r,n}\) endpoint-repeated equally spaced knot intervals on \([h,1]\). Define the equivalent calibration coordinates
\[
\boldsymbol\phi_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=\bigl(1,N_2(x),\ldots,N_{d_{r,n}}(x)\bigr)^\top,
\qquad
\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=\bigl(N_2(x),\ldots,N_{d_{r,n}}(x)\bigr)^\top
\in\mathbb R^{d_{r,n}-1}.
\]
The partition-of-unity identity gives
\[
N_1(x)=1-\sum_{\ell=2}^{d_{r,n}}N_\ell(x),
\]
so \(\boldsymbol\phi_{h,J_{r,n}}\) and \(\mathbf b_{h,J_{r,n}}\) span the same spline space.

If either interior training measure has zero total mass, define \(\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}=0\), set the calibration vector in \(\mathbb R^{d_{r,n}-1}\) and every target-marginal multiplier to zero, and set both the raw continuation and the capped downstream weight to zero on \([h,1]\). This branch precedes all optimization below.

Suppose now that both interior training measures have positive total mass. Let \(\mathcal F^\circ_{h,J_{r,n},k}\) be the finite plans \(\Pi\) that have strictly positive density relative to \(\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\otimes\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), satisfy \(\Pi_2=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), and satisfy
\[
\int\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)\,d\Pi_1(x)
=\int\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z).
\]
Because \(\Pi_2=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\) implies
\[
\Pi_1([h,1])=\Pi_2([h,1])=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h([h,1]),
\]
the separate target-mass constraint supplies the constant coordinate. Together with the preceding \(d_{r,n}-1\) equations and the identity for \(N_1\), it is equivalent to calibration of the full space \(\mathcal B_{h,J_{r,n}}\).

If \(\mathcal F^\circ_{h,J_{r,n},k}\ne\varnothing\), define \(\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) as the unique minimizer over its closure of
\[
\int c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,z)\,d\Pi
+\eta_0\operatorname{KL}(\Pi\,\|\,\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\otimes\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h)
+\lambda_0\operatorname{KL}(\Pi_1\,\|\,\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h).
\]
Otherwise minimize the same strictly convex criterion subject to \(\Pi_2=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), plus
\[
\xi_n\left\|
\int\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)\,d\Pi_1(x)
-\int\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z)
\right\|_2^2.
\]
Generalized Kullback--Leibler divergence is used for finite measures. In the exact-feasibility branch, let
\[
\widehat\theta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\in\mathbb R^{d_{r,n}-1}
\]
and \(\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},j}\) be the KKT multipliers for the nonconstant calibration and target-marginal equations; among all joint dual solutions choose the pair of smallest Euclidean norm, with lexicographic tie-breaking. In the penalized branch, set
\[
\widehat\delta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}=
\int\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)\,d\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},1}(x)
-\int\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z),
\qquad
\widehat\theta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}=2\xi_n\widehat\delta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}
\in\mathbb R^{d_{r,n}-1},
\]
and let \(\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},j}\) be a KKT multiplier for the target-marginal equations; among compatible target-marginal multipliers choose the one of smallest Euclidean norm, with lexicographic tie-breaking. For every \(x\in[h,1]\), define the raw KKT continuation
\[
\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=
\left[
\frac1{|\mathcal T^T_k|}\sum_{j\in\mathcal T^T_k}\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}
\exp\left\{-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
+\widehat\theta_{h,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}\widetilde{\boldsymbol\phi}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)
+\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right\}
\right]^{\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)}
\]
and the capped downstream weight
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)
=\min\left\{\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x),\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h^{-a}\right\}.
\]
Only \(\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) is the KKT continuation of \(d\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},1}/d\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\), and only it agrees with that density at source training atoms. The downstream weight \(\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) is its pointwise projection onto \([0,(c_{T,+}/c_{S,-})h^{-a}]\). It is positive, measurable, and bounded at held-out points, but after projection it need not satisfy the raw plan's exact spline-calibration equations. The cap does not truncate the oracle ratio on \([h,1]\), because \(r(x)=x^{-a}g_T(x)/g_S(x)\le(c_{T,+}/c_{S,-})h^{-a}\). The deterministic schedules are \(\eta_n=\eta_0\), \(\lambda_n=\lambda_0\), \(\xi_n=n^2\), and \(J=J_{r,n}\); the boundary-splice bandwidth remains \(h=h_n\) and is not used as the calibration knot-interval count.

Both the raw continuation and the capped downstream weight are measurable functions of the observed covariates. In the execution certificate the capped downstream weight enters only through the observable coefficients \(\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\). The cap still does not inherit the raw plan's exact spline-calibration equations, and no held-out localization, coefficient-norm rate, leverage bound, or frontier attainment is claimed.
