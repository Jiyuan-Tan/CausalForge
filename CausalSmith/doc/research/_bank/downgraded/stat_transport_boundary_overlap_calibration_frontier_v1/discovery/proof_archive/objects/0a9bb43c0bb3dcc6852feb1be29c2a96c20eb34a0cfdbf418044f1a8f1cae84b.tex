Fix integers \(n,m\ge2\), a bandwidth \(h\in(0,1)\), and \(\gamma\in(0,1/2)\). The interval \(\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) in \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}aware\text{-}interval}\) satisfies the finite-sample fixed-class coverage guarantee
\[
\inf_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Its realized length is exactly
\[
2\{\overline B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_T(m)\}.
\]
In particular, on any realized covariate design for which \(\overline B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\le b\) and \(\sum_i\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2\le g^2\), its length is at most
\[
2\left\{b+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)}\,g+\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)/m}\right\}.
\]
The bias radius is computable as two finite linear programs with \(N(p+1)\) variables and \(O\{N^2(p+1)\}\) inequalities. No expected-length rate, Gaussian approximation, multiplier approximation, variance consistency, leverage control, or empirical frontier attainment is asserted.
