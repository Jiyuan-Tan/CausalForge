For a nonzero fold, let \(\widetilde r\) be the raw KKT continuation, \(C_h=(c_{T,+}/c_{S,-})h^{-a}\), and \(\widehat r=\min\{\widetilde r,C_h\}\). The corrected finite program, its unique primal optimizer, its minimum-norm dual selection, the raw continuation, and the capped downstream weight are Borel measurable. Moreover,
\[
|\widehat r-r|\le|\widetilde r-r|,
\qquad
0\le\widetilde r-\widehat r\le|\widetilde r-r|,
\]
and exact or penalized raw spline calibration is changed by precisely the overshoot moment \(\int\beta(\widetilde r-C_h)_+\,d\widehat P_h^S\). The held-out cap remainder has the corresponding empirical bound proved in \(\mathrm{lem:capped\text{-}ricot\text{-}map\text{-}and\text{-}remainder}\). Together with \(\mathrm{lem:oracle\text{-}truncation\text{-}envelope}\), this reduces attainment to a raw-continuation localization and generalization theorem; it does not prove that theorem.
