At each triangular-array index \((n,m)\), the observed law is \(\mathbb P_{n,m}=P_S^{\otimes n}\otimes P_T^{\otimes m}\), induced by full-data laws \(F^S_{n,m}\) and \(F^T_{n,m}\) through \(\pi_S\) and \(\pi_T\). The finite-index class imposes no sample-ratio condition; \(m/n\to\kappa\) is used only by results stated along proportional sequences.
