The folded-normal critical value is only the final decision-theoretic step. The frozen core contains no theorem converting the random empirical transport potential into moment and leverage bounds, and the attainment target that must supply those bounds remains open. Moreover, the boundary summands reuse observations across training complements, so an independent-fold central limit theorem cannot be invoked without a Hoeffding-projection or conditional triangular-array argument. The same dependence must be retained in the multiplier approximation and in the covariance term of the variance estimator.
