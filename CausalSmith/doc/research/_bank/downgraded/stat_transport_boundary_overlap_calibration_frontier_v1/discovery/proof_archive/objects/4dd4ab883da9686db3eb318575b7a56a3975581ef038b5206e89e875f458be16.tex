For the oracle truncated weight \(r_h=r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h\}\), the source standard deviation is bounded by \(Cn^{-1/2}\{\int_h^1x^{-a}\,dx\}^{1/2}\), while a radius-\(M\) boundary local-polynomial approximation contributes at most \(Ch^{s+1}\). Therefore the oracle envelope is \[m^{-1/2}+h^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}\begin{cases}1,&a<1,\\ \sqrt{\log(1/h)},&a=1,\\ h^{(1-a)/2},&a>1,\end{cases}\] and \(h=h_n\) gives \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\). This proves the early-kill rate calculation but not empirical RiCOT stability.
