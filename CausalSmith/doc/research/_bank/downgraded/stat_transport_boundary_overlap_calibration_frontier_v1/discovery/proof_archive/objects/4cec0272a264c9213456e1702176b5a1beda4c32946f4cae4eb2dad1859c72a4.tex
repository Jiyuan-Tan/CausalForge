The executable estimator uses deterministic five-fold ridge boundary local polynomials, a full \(J_{\mu,n}+q\)-coordinate interior regression spline, and a capped continuation of a semi-unbalanced entropic transport program calibrated in \(J_{r,n}+q-1\) explicit nonconstant coordinates. These finite-dimensional programs define observable coefficients \(\Gamma_i\). The paper proves their exact algebraic carrier and a bounded-outcome Hoeffding certificate, but assumes no fitted-weight rate and proves no held-out localization, coefficient-norm rate, leverage bound, calibration-discrepancy rate, or optimizer-stability rate.
