On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h,J)\), define the bounded-source and target Hoeffding radii
\[
R_S(h,J)=\varepsilon^{-1}\left\{2\log(4/\gamma)\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J)^2\right\}^{1/2},
\qquad
R_T(m)=\left\{2\log(4/\gamma)/m\right\}^{1/2}.
\]
These are explicit functions of the realized covariates and the known trial-randomization lower bound.  No standard-error estimate, covariance estimate, Gaussian approximation, multiplier process, or studentization is used.  Set both radii to one off the good branch.
