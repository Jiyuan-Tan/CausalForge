The primitive Hölder and density bounds control the oracle ratio but do not control the empirical entropic optimizer between random spline knots. The original unrestricted claim also conflicts with the boundary occupancy of its own calibration schedule: for \(a>1\), with \(D=2s+a+1\),
\[
\frac{n h_n^a}{J_{r,n}}\asymp n^{(2s+1)/D-1/(2t+1)}(\log n)^{1/(2t+1)}.
\]
This tends to zero when \(a>2t(2s+1)\). There are then many near-boundary spline supports having target observations and held-out source observations but no source training observation. In the penalized branch their calibration discrepancy is of order \(J_{r,n}^{-1}\), and the corrected gradient potential has order \(\xi_n/J_{r,n}=n^2/J_{r,n}\); the dual continuation can therefore be exponentially large on those held-out supports. Thus the declared conditions cannot yield the unrestricted stability assertion, and within the narrowed regime a new optimizer-localization argument is still required.
