Let
\[
V_h=1+\mathbf 1\{a=1\}\log(1/h)+\mathbf 1\{a>1\}h^{1-a}.
\]
Uniformly over the model class, the established Riesz calculation yields
\[
\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h\}\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2\asymp V_h,
\]
so the oracle envelope is
\[
m^{-1/2}+h^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}V_h^{1/2}.
\]
At \(h=h_n\) this equals \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\). The separate sieves satisfy
\[
J_{\mu,n}^{-s}+\sqrt{J_{\mu,n}/n}\asymp n^{-s/(2s+1)},
\qquad
J_{r,n}^{-t}+\sqrt{J_{r,n}\log J_{r,n}/n}
\asymp(\log n/n)^{t/(2t+1)}.
\]
The declared product-smoothness relation makes the product of these envelopes \(o(n^{-1/2})\). In addition, the boundary support occupancy obeys
\[
\frac{n h_n^a}{J_{r,n}}\asymp
n^{(2s+1)/(2s+a+1)-1/(2t+1)}(\log n)^{1/(2t+1)},
\]
which diverges polynomially exactly when \(a<2t(2s+1)\), is only logarithmic at equality, and vanishes when \(a>2t(2s+1)\).
