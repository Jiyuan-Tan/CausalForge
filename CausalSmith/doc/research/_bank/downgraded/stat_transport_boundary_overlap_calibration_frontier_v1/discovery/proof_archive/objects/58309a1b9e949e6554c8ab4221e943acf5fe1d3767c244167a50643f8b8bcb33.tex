\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(i\), condition on \(\mathcal F\), and write \(d_i=b_i-a_i\). The secant-line bound for the convex function \(u\mapsto e^{\lambda u}\), followed by the constraint \(\mathbb E(W_i\mid\mathcal F)=0\), gives the usual two-point upper envelope for \(\mathbb E(e^{\lambda W_i}\mid\mathcal F)\). Writing \(p_i=-a_i/d_i\in[0,1]\), that envelope is
\[
(1-p_i)e^{-\lambda p_i d_i}+p_i e^{\lambda(1-p_i)d_i}.
\]
For \(q\in[0,1]\), direct differentiation of the logarithm of
\((1-q)e^{-qu}+q e^{(1-q)u}\) twice in \(u\), together with its value and first derivative at \(u=0\), yields
\[
\log\{(1-q)e^{-qu}+q e^{(1-q)u}\}\le \frac{u^2}{8}.
\]
Consequently
\[
\mathbb E(e^{\lambda W_i}\mid\mathcal F)\le
\exp\{\lambda^2d_i^2/8\}.
\]
Conditional independence therefore implies
\[
\mathbb E\left[\exp\left\{\lambda\sum_{i=1}^N W_i\right\}\middle|\mathcal F\right]
\le\exp\left\{\frac{\lambda^2}{8}\sum_{i=1}^Nd_i^2\right\}.
\]
Conditional Markov inequality gives, for every \(\lambda>0\),
\[
\mathbb P\left(\sum_iW_i\ge x\,\middle|\mathcal F\right)
\le\exp\left\{-\lambda x+\frac{\lambda^2}{8}\sum_i d_i^2\right\}.
\]
When \(\sum_i d_i^2>0\), minimizing at \(\lambda=4x/\sum_i d_i^2\) gives \(\exp\{-2x^2/\sum_i d_i^2\}\). Applying the same argument to \(-W_i\) and using the union bound proves the display. If every \(d_i=0\), conditional centering forces every \(W_i=0\), which proves the stated convention. \(\square\)
