The project proves the lower phase diagram with explicit boundary-bump and critical multiscale witnesses, exhibits the rate-equivalent deterministic truncated-oracle envelope, constructs the boundary-spliced capped-RiCOT statistic, and proves an exact finite-sample execution certificate and fixed-class honest interval. It also proves a nonadaptation lower bound and isolates self-similarity as a necessary ingredient for future positive adaptation. The estimator-side empirical localization needed for a data-driven minimax upper remains unresolved.
