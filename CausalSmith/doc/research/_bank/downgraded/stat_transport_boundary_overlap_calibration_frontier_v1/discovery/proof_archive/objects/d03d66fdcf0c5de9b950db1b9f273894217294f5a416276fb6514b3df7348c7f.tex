\textbf{Proof.}
Fix arbitrary \(s>0\), \(t>0\), and integers \(n\ge2\) and \(m\ge1\).  Let \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(0,s,t)\), and fix an arbitrary realization of \(\mathcal D_{n,m}\).  By the definitions of the density factors and the restriction \(a=0\), for every \(x\in(0,1]\),
\[
 f_S(x)=x^a g_S(x)=g_S(x),
 \qquad
 f_T(x)=g_T(x),
 \qquad
 r(x)=x^{-a}\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}.
\]
The source- and target-boundary-factor assumptions give
\[
 c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+},
 \qquad
 c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+}.
\]
Because \(c_{S,-}>0\), division is valid, and hence
\[
 \frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
 \le r(x)
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},
 \qquad 0<x\le1.
\]

Since \(a=0<1\), the first branch in the definition of \(h_n\) yields
\[
 h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}.
\]
The condition \(s>0\) implies \(s+1>0\), so
\[
 h_n^{s+1}
 =\left(n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\right)^{s+1}
 =n^{-1/2}.
\]
Moreover, the same boundary-factor bounds give the direct uniform estimate
\[
 0\le
 \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx
 =\int_{h_n}^1\frac{g_T(x)^2}{g_S(x)}\,dx
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}^2}{c_{S,-}}(1-h_n)
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}^2}{c_{S,-}}.
\]
Thus the integral is \(O(1)\), uniformly over the displayed model class.  This is also the \(a<1\) branch of \(\mathrm{lem{:}riesz\text{-}threshold}\), specialized to \(a=0\).

It remains to prove the realization-wise positivity assertion.  Fix a fold \(k\) for which both interior training measures have positive total mass, and set
\[
 I^T_{k,n}=\{j\in\mathcal T^T_k:X_j^T\ge h_n\}.
\]
Positive target interior mass implies \(I^T_{k,n}\ne\varnothing\) and \(|\mathcal T^T_k|>0\).  Positive source and target interior masses exclude the zero-mass fallback in \(\mathrm{def{:}ricot\text{-}weights}\), so its exact-feasibility branch or its penalized branch applies.  On either branch, the selected calibration and target-marginal multipliers are finite real vectors.  Therefore, for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\) and every \(j\in I^T_{k,n}\),
\[
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
 +\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
 \widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 +\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right\}>0.
\]
Here the exponent is finite because the transport cost and spline coordinates are finite, the selected multipliers are real, and \(\eta_0>0\).  Since the defining sum for the raw continuation contains at least one such strictly positive summand,
\[
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0,
 \qquad x\in[h_n,1],
\]
where the outer power is strictly positive because \(\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)>0\).  At \(a=0\), the deterministic downstream cap equals
\[
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h_n^{-a}
 =\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}>0.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 =\min\!\left\{
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x),
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}
 \right\}>0,
 \qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\]

All conclusions are deterministic conditional on the realized covariates and the positive-interior-mass branch.  No inequality coupling \(s\) and \(t\), and no lower threshold on \(t\), was used.  Strict positivity supplies neither an empirical norm bound nor a nonzero realized residual sum nor a root-risk bound. \(\square\)
