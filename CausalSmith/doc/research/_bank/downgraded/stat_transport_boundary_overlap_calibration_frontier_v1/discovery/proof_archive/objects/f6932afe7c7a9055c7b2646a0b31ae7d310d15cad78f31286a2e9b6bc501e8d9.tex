Fix \(a>1\), \(t>1/2\), and \(0<s_1<s_2\) such that \(s_\ell/(2s_\ell+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\) for \(\ell\in\{1,2\}\). Any confidence interval with asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s_1,t)\cup\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s_2,t)\) has, at some law in \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s_2,t)\), expected length at least \(c\{m^{-1/2}+n^{-(s_1+1)/(2s_1+a+1)}\}\).
