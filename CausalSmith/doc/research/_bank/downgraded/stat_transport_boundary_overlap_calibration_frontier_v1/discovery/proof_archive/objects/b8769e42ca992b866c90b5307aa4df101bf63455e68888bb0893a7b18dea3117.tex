For integers \(n,m\ge2\), define the observable source and target Hoeffding radii
\[
R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
=\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2},
\qquad
R_T(m)=\sqrt{\frac{2\log(4/\gamma)}{m}}.
\]
The only source dispersion diagnostic used by the certificate is the exact observable coefficient norm \(\{\sum_i\Gamma_i^2\}^{1/2}\). No empirical variance estimator, covariance estimator, studentization, leverage condition, Gaussian approximation, or multiplier approximation is part of this construction.
