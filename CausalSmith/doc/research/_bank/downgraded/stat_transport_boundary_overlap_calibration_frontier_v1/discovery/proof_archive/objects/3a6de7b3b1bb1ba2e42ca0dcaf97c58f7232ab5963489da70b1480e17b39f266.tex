\textbf{Proof.}
Write
\[
E_{n,m}=m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}
+n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}.
\tag{1}
\]
We evaluate its last two terms in the three regimes using \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\).

If \(a<1\), then \(h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\), and hence
\[
h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},
\qquad
n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}\asymp n^{-1/2}.
\tag{2}
\]
Thus \(E_{n,m}\asymp m^{-1/2}+n^{-1/2}=m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\).

If \(a=1\), then \(h_n=(\log n/n)^{1/\{2(s+1)\}}\). Therefore
\[
h_n^{s+1}=\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}},
\qquad
\log(1/h_n)=\frac{\log n-\log\log n}{2(s+1)}\asymp\log n.
\tag{3}
\]
The norm conclusion of \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\) and (3) give
\[
n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
\asymp\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}.
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently \(E_{n,m}\asymp m^{-1/2}+\sqrt{\log n/n}=m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(1,s)\).

If \(a>1\), put \(D=2s+a+1>0\). The bandwidth is \(h_n=n^{-1/D}\), so
\[
h_n^{s+1}=n^{-(s+1)/D}=\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{5}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
&\asymp n^{-1/2}h_n^{(1-a)/2}\\
&=n^{-1/2+(a-1)/(2D)}\\
&=n^{-(s+1)/D}=\rho_n(a,s),
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
because \(D-(a-1)=2(s+1)\). Equations (1)--(6) prove
\[
E_{n,m}\asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
The two-sided constants are uniform over \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), since the constants in \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\) depend only on the fixed boundary-factor bounds and \(a\). This is an oracle bias--standard-deviation identity and does not construct a data-measurable estimator. \(\square\)
