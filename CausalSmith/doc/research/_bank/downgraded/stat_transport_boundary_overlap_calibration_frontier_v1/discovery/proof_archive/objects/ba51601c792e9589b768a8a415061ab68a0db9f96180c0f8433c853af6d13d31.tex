For fixed spline degree and equally spaced knots on \([h,1]\), the normalized local B-spline basis has uniformly bounded overlap and a partition of unity; its Lebesgue Gram matrix has eigenvalues comparable to \(J^{-1}\); and every radius-one Hölder function of order \(u\) has a spline approximant with uniform error at most \(C J^{-u}\), where \(C\) depends only on \(u\) and the degree.
