For \(n,m\ge2\), define the finite-sample execution-certificate interval
\[
\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
=\left[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
\ \pm\ 
\left\{
B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})
+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2}
+\sqrt{\frac{2\log(4/\gamma)}m}
\right\}
\right].
\]
All quantities in the center and radius are observable functions of \(\mathcal D_{n,m}\) and fixed model or design constants. The definition contains no Gaussian, multiplier, studentization, or expected-length approximation.
