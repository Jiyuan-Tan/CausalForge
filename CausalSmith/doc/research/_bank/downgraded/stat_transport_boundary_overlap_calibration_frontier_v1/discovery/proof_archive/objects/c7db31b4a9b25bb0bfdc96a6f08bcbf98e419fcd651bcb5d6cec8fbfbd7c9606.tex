For integers \(n,m\ge2\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), define the finite-sample Hoeffding certificate interval
\[
\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
=\left[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
\pm\left\{\overline B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_T(m)\right\}
\right].
\]
The certified bias radius \(\overline B_X\) is the maximum of the two finite jet linear programs in \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}envelope}\). Every term is therefore an explicit function of the observed data and the fixed class constants. The interval has no studentization or zero-standard-error branch.
