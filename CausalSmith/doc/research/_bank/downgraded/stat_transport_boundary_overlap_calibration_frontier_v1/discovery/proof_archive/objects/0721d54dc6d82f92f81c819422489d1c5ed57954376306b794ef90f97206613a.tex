The multiscale handle in \(\mathrm{def:adaptive\text{-}handle}\) does not, as presently constructed, define a single interval-valued statistic. It specifies neither measurable exponent estimators, thresholds, a selection rule, nor tie breaking. Consequently the frozen construction has no coverage probability or expected length to evaluate and does not yield the proposed adaptive guarantee. A positive result requires a new executable construction before self-similarity can be used to prove adaptation.
