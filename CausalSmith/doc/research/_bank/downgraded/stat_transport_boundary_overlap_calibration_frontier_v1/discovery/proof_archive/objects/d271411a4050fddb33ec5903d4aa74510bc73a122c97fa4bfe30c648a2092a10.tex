Let \(n_{\mathrm{ev}}=N^S_1\) and \(m_{\mathrm{ev}}=N^T_1\).  On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h)\), define the honest held-out estimator
\[
 \widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}
 =\frac1{m_{\mathrm{ev}}}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_1}
 \widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_j^T)
 +\frac1{n_{\mathrm{ev}}}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V^S_1}
 \mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\widehat r^{\mathrm{DS}}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)
 \{Z_i-\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_i)\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h)^c\), set \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=0\).  The source and target validation blocks are disjoint from every fit entering their displayed evaluations.  Because \(K_0=5\) is fixed, discarding the other validation rotations changes constants but no rate.  The fallback belongs to the parameter space and bounds the loss on the exponentially rare failure branch.
