For integers \(n,m\ge2\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), define the observable H\"older design discrepancy
\[
B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})=
\sup_{\substack{v\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\\ \|v\|_\infty\le1}}
\left|\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})v(X_i)-\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^mv(X_j^T)\right|.
\]
Equivalently, this is the maximum of the two linear optimization problems obtained by multiplying the expression inside the absolute value by \(+1\) and \(-1\), over the convex H\"older feasible set. Thus it is an exact covariate-measurable verification object. No polynomial-time evaluation claim and no stochastic-order or frontier-order claim is part of the definition.
