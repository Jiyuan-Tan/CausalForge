At \(h=h_n\), the good event and the direct-series nuisance fits satisfy, uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\),
\[
\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)^c\}
\le C\exp\{-c\underline N_n\},
\]
with \(\underline N_n\) as in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\), and
\[
\|\widehat\mu-\mu\|_{L^2(g_S;[h_n,1])}=O_P\{\delta_{s,h_n}+\lambda_nJ_{\mu,n}\},
\qquad
\|\widehat q^{\mathrm{DS}}-q\|_{L^2(g_S;[h_n,1])}=O_P\{\delta_{t,h_n}+\lambda_nJ_{r,n}\}.
\]
The held-out direct-series weights have empirical squared norm \(O_P\{V_a(h_n)\}\), and their maximum squared leverage divided by their summed squares is \(o_P(1)\).
