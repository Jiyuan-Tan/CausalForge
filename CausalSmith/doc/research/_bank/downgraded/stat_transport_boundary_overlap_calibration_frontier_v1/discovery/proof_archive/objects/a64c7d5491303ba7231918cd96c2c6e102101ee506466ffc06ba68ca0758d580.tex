For every \(P=\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), \(G_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\) is positive definite and \(w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}\) reproduces every polynomial \(q\) of degree at most \(p\):
\[
\int_0^h w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)q(x)f_S(x)\,dx
=\int_0^h q(x)f_T(x)\,dx.
\]
Uniformly over the model class as \(h\downarrow0\),
\[
b_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\le C h^{s+1},
\qquad
\int_0^h|w_h^{\mathrm{BLP}}(x;P)|f_S(x)\,dx\le Ch,
\]
\[
\{q_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\}^\top\{G_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\}^{-1}q_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\asymp h^{1-a},
\]
and
\[
\|\omega_h^{\mathrm o}(\cdot;P)\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2
=\{q_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\}^\top\{G_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\}^{-1}q_h^{\mathrm o}(P)
+\int_h^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx.
\]
For every fixed \(a>0\), the bias allowance is class-sharp:
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}b_h^{\mathrm o}(P)\ge c h^{s+1}
\]
for all sufficiently small \(h\) whenever the displayed fixed constants admit the constant-factor density witness. At \(a=0\) the uniform upper bound remains valid, but no per-law or class-wide lower equivalence is asserted; in particular, equal source and target densities give zero oracle bias.
