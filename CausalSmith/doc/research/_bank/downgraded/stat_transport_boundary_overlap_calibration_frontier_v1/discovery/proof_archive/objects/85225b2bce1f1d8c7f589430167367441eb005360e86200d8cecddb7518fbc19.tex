Under \(t>1/2\) and \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), in the special case \(a=0\),
\[
\frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}\le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}\le\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},\qquad 0<x\le1,
\]
\(h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\), and
\[
h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},\qquad \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx=O(1).
\]
These deterministic facts do not by themselves assert an empirical Gram-matrix bound, a leverage bound, a root-risk bound, or a confidence-interval guarantee.
