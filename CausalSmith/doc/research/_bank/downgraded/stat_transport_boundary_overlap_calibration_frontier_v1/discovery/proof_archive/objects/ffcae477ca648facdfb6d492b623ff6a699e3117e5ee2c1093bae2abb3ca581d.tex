On \(\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)\), the estimator admits the exact realized expansion
\[
 \widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau
 =\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_{i,h_n}\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}
 +\frac1{N^T_1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V^T_1}\{\mu(X_j^T)-\tau\}+R_{n,m}.
\]
The remainder retains the boundary approximation, target empirical, source empirical, Galerkin-product, and ridge terms, and satisfies
\[
 \sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
 \{\mathbb E(R_{n,m}^2\mathbf1_{\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)})\}^{1/2}
 \le\widehat B_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}.
\]
Moreover \(\sum_i\Gamma_{i,h_n}^2\asymp_Pn^{-1}V_a(h_n)\) and \(\max_i\Gamma_{i,h_n}^2/\sum_i\Gamma_{i,h_n}^2=o_P(1)\).
