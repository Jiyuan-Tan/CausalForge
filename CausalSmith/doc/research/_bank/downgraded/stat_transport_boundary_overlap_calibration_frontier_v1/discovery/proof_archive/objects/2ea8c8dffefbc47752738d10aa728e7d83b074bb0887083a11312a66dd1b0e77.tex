\textbf{Proof.}
We first derive the testing reduction used below.  Let \(Q_0,Q_1\) be two observed-data laws with parameter separation
\(\Delta=|\tau(Q_1)-\tau(Q_0)|\), and let \(T\) be any estimator.  On the event
\(E=\{|T-\tau(Q_0)|<\Delta/2\}\), the error relative to \(\tau(Q_1)\) is at least \(\Delta/2\); on \(E^c\), the error relative to \(\tau(Q_0)\) is at least \(\Delta/2\).  Therefore
\[
 \max_{v\in\{0,1\}}\mathbb E_{Q_v}
   [\{T-\tau(Q_v)\}^2]
 \ge \frac{\Delta^2}{8}
 \{Q_0(E^c)+Q_1(E)\}
 \ge \frac{\Delta^2}{8}
 \{1-\operatorname{TV}(Q_0,Q_1)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
For completeness, the log-sum inequality on a Hahn set for \(Q_0-Q_1\), followed by
\[
 p\log\frac pq+(1-p)\log\frac{1-p}{1-q}\ge2(p-q)^2,
\]
gives the elementary bound
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_0,Q_1)
 \le\sqrt{\operatorname{KL}(Q_0,Q_1)/2}.
\tag{2}
\]
Thus a pair with sufficiently small constant relative entropy forces root mean-squared risk at least a fixed multiple of its separation.

All source constructions below use
\[
 f_S(x)=(a+1)x^a,
 \qquad f_T(x)=1,
\tag{3}
\]
and the Bernoulli full-data extension checked in \(\mathrm{lem:boundary\text{-}bump}\).  In particular, the factors in (3) are constant and satisfy the density smoothness condition for every fixed \(t>0\).  The target sample law is common within every source pair, so it contributes zero relative entropy.

\emph{Regular regime \(a<1\).}
Choose a nonnegative nonzero \(\phi\in C_c^\infty((1/2,1))\) and set
\[
 u_n(x)=\delta n^{-1/2}\phi(x),
 \qquad
 \mu_{0,\pm}(x)=\frac12,
 \qquad
 \mu_{1,\pm}(x)=\frac12\pm u_n(x).
\tag{4}
\]
For a sufficiently small fixed \(\delta>0\), the support, H\"older-radius, outcome-range, treatment-positivity, and residual-variance checks in the boundary-bump proof put both laws in \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\).  Since the support of \(\phi\) is separated from zero, the Bernoulli calculation in that lemma gives
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(Q_{+,n},Q_{-,n})
 \le Cn\int_0^1u_n(x)^2f_S(x)\,dx
 \le C\delta^2,
\tag{5}
\]
whereas the uniform target density gives
\[
 |\tau(Q_{+,n})-\tau(Q_{-,n})|
 =2\delta n^{-1/2}\int_0^1\phi(x)\,dx
 \ge c n^{-1/2}.
\tag{6}
\]
Reducing \(\delta\) and applying (1)--(2) proves the source lower bound \(cn^{-1/2}\).

\emph{Nonregular regime \(a>1\).}
Apply \(\mathrm{lem:boundary\text{-}bump}\) at
\[
 h=c_0n^{-1/(2s+a+1)},
\tag{7}
\]
where \(c_0>0\) is fixed and small enough that the lemma applies and
\(Cc_0^{2s+a+1}\) is below a fixed testing constant.  The source relative entropy is then at most that constant, while the target-parameter separation is at least
\[
 cc_0^{s+1}n^{-(s+1)/(2s+a+1)}.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (1)--(2) prove the source lower bound in the third branch of \(\rho_n(a,s)\).

\emph{Critical regime \(a=1\).}
A single boundary bump gives only order \(n^{-1/2}\), so we accumulate equal target contributions over logarithmically many disjoint boundary shells.  Fix a nonnegative nonzero function
\(\psi\in C_c^\infty((5/4,7/4))\).  For sufficiently large \(n\), let
\[
 J_n=\left\lfloor\frac{\log_2 n}{2(s+1)}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad h_j=2^{-j},\quad 1\le j\le J_n,
 \qquad A_n=\frac{\delta}{\sqrt{nJ_n}},
\tag{9}
\]
and define
\[
 u_n(x)=A_n\sum_{j=1}^{J_n}h_j^{-1}\psi(x/h_j),
 \qquad
 \mu_{0,\pm}(x)=\frac12,
 \qquad
 \mu_{1,\pm}(x)=\frac12\pm u_n(x).
\tag{10}
\]
The shell supports are pairwise disjoint and lie in \([0,1]\).  H\"older scaling and the geometric series imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \lVert u_n\rVert_{\mathcal H^s}
 &\le C_\psi A_n\sum_{j=1}^{J_n}h_j^{-(s+1)}
 \le C_\psi' A_nh_{J_n}^{-(s+1)}\\
 &\le \frac{C_\psi'\delta}{\sqrt{J_n}},
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
because \(2^{J_n(s+1)}\le\sqrt n\).  Thus a sufficiently small fixed \(\delta\) makes both alternatives satisfy the response H\"older balls, the outcome support, and residual nondegeneracy for all large \(n\).

At \(a=1\), (3) gives \(f_S(x)=2x\).  Disjointness and the substitution \(x=h_ju\) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
 n\int_0^1u_n(x)^2f_S(x)\,dx
 &=2nA_n^2\sum_{j=1}^{J_n}
   \int h_j^{-2}\psi(x/h_j)^2x\,dx\\
 &=2nA_n^2J_n\int_{5/4}^{7/4}\psi(u)^2u\,du
 \le C_\psi\delta^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
The Bernoulli relative-entropy bound therefore gives
\(\operatorname{KL}(Q_{+,n},Q_{-,n})\le C\delta^2\).  Every shell has the same target integral, so
\[
\begin{aligned}
 |\tau(Q_{+,n})-\tau(Q_{-,n})|
 &=2\int_0^1u_n(x)\,dx\\
 &=2A_nJ_n\int_{5/4}^{7/4}\psi(u)\,du
 \ge c\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
After reducing \(\delta\), (1)--(2) prove the critical lower bound.  Equivalently, the testing prior is the equal two-point mixture of the two multiscale boundary-shell alternatives.  Since \(J_n\asymp\log n\), (12)--(13) show explicitly that the gain is exactly \(\sqrt{\log n}\) and contains no polynomial loss.

The target-density pair in \(\mathrm{lem:boundary\text{-}bump}\) has a common source law, target-sample relative entropy bounded by an arbitrarily small constant, and parameter separation at least \(c_Tm^{-1/2}\).  Hence (1)--(2) give
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c_T'm^{-1/2}.
\tag{14}
\]
The three source constructions give, uniformly in \(m\),
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c_S\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{15}
\]
Because \(\max\{u,v\}\ge(u+v)/2\), (14)--(15) imply
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)
 \ge c\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\tag{16}
\]
The critical construction was stated for all sufficiently large \(n\).  For the finitely many remaining positive indices, a fixed small interior perturbation gives a positive multiple of \(n^{-1/2}\); decreasing \(c\) in (16) covers those indices as well.  Every constant may depend on the fixed primitive parameters but not on \(n\) or \(m\). \(\square\)
