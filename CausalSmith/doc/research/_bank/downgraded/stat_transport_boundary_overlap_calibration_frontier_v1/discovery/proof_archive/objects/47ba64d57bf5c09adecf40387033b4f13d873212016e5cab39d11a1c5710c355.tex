\textbf{Proof.}
By \(\mathrm{lem:source\text{-}only\text{-}frontier\text{-}lower}\), for the fixed model constants there are \(c_0>0\) and \(n_0\) such that, for every \(n\ge n_0\) and every \(m\),
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c_0\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{1}
\]
The lower-bound subexperiments in that lemma use \(f_T=1\). Because the declared target-factor ranges permit \(c_{T,-}=1\), \(c_{T,+}=1\), or both, those subexperiments remain members of the model when a target-density bound is attained. Thus (1) uses no strict target-density slack.

By \(\mathrm{ass:sample\text{-}balance}\), there are constants \(c_\kappa,C_\kappa>0\) and \(n_1\) such that
\[
c_\kappa n\le m\le C_\kappa n,
\qquad n\ge n_1.
\tag{2}
\]
In particular,
\[
m^{-1/2}\le c_\kappa^{-1/2}n^{-1/2}.
\tag{3}
\]
For \(a<1\), \(\rho_n(a,s)=n^{-1/2}\). For \(a=1\),
\[
\rho_n(1,s)=\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}\ge n^{-1/2}
\tag{4}
\]
eventually. For \(a>1\), positivity of \(s\) and \(a-1\) gives
\[
\frac{s+1}{2s+a+1}<\frac12,
\qquad
\rho_n(a,s)=n^{-(s+1)/(2s+a+1)}\ge n^{-1/2}.
\tag{5}
\]
Consequently, for an eventual constant \(C_0<\infty\),
\[
m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\le C_0\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (1) and (6) yields
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)
\ge \frac{c_0}{C_0}\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Substitution of the three branches in \(\mathrm{def:frontier\text{-}rate}\) proves the displayed lower phase diagram.

The corrected \(\mathrm{lem:oracle\text{-}truncation\text{-}envelope}\) proves two distinct facts. First, the actual bias and its lawwise worst-case H\"older envelope obey
\[
|\mathfrak b_{h_n}(\mu;P)|\le b_{h_n}^{\circ}(P)\le C h_n^{s+1}
\tag{8}
\]
uniformly over member laws; this is an upper bound, not a per-law equivalence. Second, its explicit deterministic worst-case allowance and exact source-weight norm obey
\[
m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}
+n^{-1/2}\|\omega_{h_n}^{\circ}(\cdot;P)\|_{L^2(P_S)}
\asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\tag{9}
\]
That lemma also constructs, for every \(a>0\), admissible laws and H\"older witnesses for which the boundary allowance has order \(h_n^{s+1}\), while showing why special equal-density laws at \(a=0\) can have zero actual oracle bias. Thus (9) is an equal-order, class-sharp allowance in the nontrivial depletion regimes, not an assertion that every law has nonzero boundary bias.

Finally, \(G_{h_n}^{\circ}(P)\), \(q_{h_n}^{\circ}(P)\), \(w_{h_n}^{\mathrm{BLP}}(\cdot;P)\), and \(\omega_{h_n}^{\circ}(\cdot;P)\) depend on the population source and target densities. They are therefore oracle objects rather than maps of \(\mathcal D_{n,m}\). Equations (7)--(9) prove only the one-sided minimax lower phase diagram and the boundary-corrected oracle benchmark; they do not provide a data-measurable minimax upper bound or frontier attainment. \(\square\)
