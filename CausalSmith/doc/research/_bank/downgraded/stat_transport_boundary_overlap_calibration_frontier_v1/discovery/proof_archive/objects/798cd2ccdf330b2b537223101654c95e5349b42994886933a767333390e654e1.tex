\textbf{Proof.}
Suppose that \(a=0\).  By the definitions \(f_S(x)=x^a g_S(x)\), \(f_T(x)=g_T(x)\), and \(r(x)=x^{-a}g_T(x)/g_S(x)\),
\[
 f_S(x)=g_S(x),\qquad f_T(x)=g_T(x),\qquad
 r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}.
\]
The source- and target-boundary-factor assumptions state, for every \(x\in[0,1]\),
\[
 c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+},
 \qquad
 c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+}.
\]
All four constants are strictly positive.  Dividing the target bounds by the source bounds therefore gives
\[
 \frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
 \le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},
 \qquad 0\le x\le1.
\]

Because \(a=0<1\), the first branch in the definition of \(h_n\) applies, and hence
\[
 h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}},
 \qquad
 h_n^{s+1}
 =\left(n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\right)^{s+1}
 =n^{-1/2}.
\]
The already-established Riesz-threshold lemma, again in its \(a<1\) branch, yields
\[
 \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx=O(1).
\]
Equivalently, the same conclusion follows directly and uniformly from
\[
 0\le \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx
 =\int_{h_n}^1\frac{g_T(x)^2}{g_S(x)}\,dx
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}^2}{c_{S,-}}(1-h_n)
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}^2}{c_{S,-}}.
\]

Fix a fold for which both interior training measures are nonzero.  To distinguish the two objects in the corrected RiCOT construction, denote by
\[
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
\]
the bracketed KKT exponential continuation, raised to the power \(\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)\), before the outer cap is applied.  Nonzero target interior training measure means that the defining sum contains at least one index \(j\in\mathcal T^T_k\) with \(X_j^T\ge h_n\).  Every such summand is a strictly positive exponential, the selected dual vectors are finite real vectors, and
\[
 \frac{\eta_0}{\eta_0+\lambda_0}>0.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0
 \qquad\text{for every }x\in[h_n,1].
\]
At \(a=0\), the deterministic downstream cap equals
\[
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h_n^{-a}
 =\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}>0.
\]
The actual downstream weight is the pointwise projection specified in the corrected definition,
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 =\min\left\{
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x),
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}
 \right\},
\]
so it too is strictly positive on \([h_n,1]\).  This argument uses no assertion that the capped weight agrees with the training-atom marginal density or retains exact spline calibration; those properties concern only the raw continuation before projection.

Finally, the conditions \(t>1/2\) and \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\) are compatible with, but not needed for, these deterministic calculations.  A pointwise upper bound and positivity do not control the empirical squared norm, do not preclude cancellation in a realized weighted residual sum, and do not establish a root-mean-squared risk bound.  Thus the proposition asserts none of those downstream conclusions. \(\square\)
