Let \(W_1,\ldots,W_N\) be independent real random variables with \(\mathbb E W_i=0\) and \(W_i\in[\alpha_i,\beta_i]\) almost surely. For every \(\delta\in(0,1)\),
\[
\mathbb P\!\left\{\left|\sum_{i=1}^N W_i\right|>
\sqrt{\frac12\log(2/\delta)\sum_{i=1}^N(\beta_i-\alpha_i)^2}\right\}\le\delta.
\]
The same statement holds conditionally on a sigma-field when conditional independence, conditional centering, and the displayed conditional bounds hold almost surely.
