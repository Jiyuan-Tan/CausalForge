Under \(t>1/2\) and \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), in the special case \(a=0\),
\[
\frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
\le \frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}
\le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}},
\qquad 0\le x\le1,
\]
and \(r(x)=g_T(x)/g_S(x)\) for \(0<x\le1\). Moreover, \(h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\), and
\[
h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},\qquad
\int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx=O(1).
\]
These deterministic facts do not by themselves assert an empirical norm bound, a nonzero realized residual sum, or a root-risk bound.
