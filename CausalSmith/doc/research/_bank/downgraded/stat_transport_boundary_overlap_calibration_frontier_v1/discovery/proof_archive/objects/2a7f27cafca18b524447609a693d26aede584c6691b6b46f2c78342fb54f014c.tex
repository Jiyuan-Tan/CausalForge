For \(n,m\ge2\) and \(h\in(0,1)\), the realized linear-coefficient diagnostic is
\[
\left(
\bigl(\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})\bigr)_{i=1}^n,
\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})^2\right\}^{1/2}
\right).
\]
It is a measurable function of the observed source and target covariates and the fixed tuning constants. The second coordinate is the exact coefficient norm entering the bounded-outcome concentration radius; it is not asserted to estimate an asymptotic standard error or to have the truncated-oracle order.
