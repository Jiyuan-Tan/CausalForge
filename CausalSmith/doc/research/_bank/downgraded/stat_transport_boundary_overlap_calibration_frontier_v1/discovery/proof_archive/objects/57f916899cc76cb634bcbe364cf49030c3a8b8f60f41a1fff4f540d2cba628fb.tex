Let \(Q_0,Q_1\) be probability laws, let \(\theta_0,\theta_1\in\mathbb R\) have separation \(\Delta=|\theta_1-\theta_0|\), and let \(\mathcal C\) be a random interval. If \(Q_k\{\theta_k\in\mathcal C\}\ge1-\alpha_k\) for \(k\in\{0,1\}\) and \(\operatorname{TV}(Q_1,Q_0)\le\eta\), then
\[
\mathbb E_{Q_0}|\mathcal C|\ge\Delta\{1-\alpha_0-\alpha_1-\eta\}_+.
