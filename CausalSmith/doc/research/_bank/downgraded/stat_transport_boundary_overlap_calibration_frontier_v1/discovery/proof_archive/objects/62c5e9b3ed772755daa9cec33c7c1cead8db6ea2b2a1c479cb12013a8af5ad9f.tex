Under \(t>1/2\) and \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), in the special case \(a=0\), \(r(x)=g_T(x)/g_S(x)\) is uniformly bounded, \(h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\), and
\[
h_n^{s+1}=n^{-1/2},\qquad \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx=O(1).
\]
The dual extension in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\) is strictly positive on every held-out \(x\in[h_n,1]\) whenever both training interior measures are nonzero, so the residual correction in \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) does not collapse to zero. Consequently its boundary bias, source stochastic term, and target empirical term are respectively \(O(n^{-1/2})\), \(O_P(n^{-1/2})\), and \(O_P(m^{-1/2})\), giving the ordinary two-sample root-risk reduction \(O(m^{-1/2}+n^{-1/2})\).
