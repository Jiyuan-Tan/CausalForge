\textbf{Proof.}
The proof of \(\mathrm{lem:source\text{-}only\text{-}frontier\text{-}lower}\) gives a constant \(c_0>0\), independent of \(n\) and \(m\), such that for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)\ge c_0\rho_n(a,s).
\]
Its subexperiments have \(f_T=1\).  Since the declared parameter ranges imply \(c_{T,-}\le1\le c_{T,+}\), this construction remains admissible when either target-density bound is attained.  This proves the asserted absence of a strict target-density-slack requirement.  As shown in that lemma, the density factors are constant, so the converse itself does not use either nuisance-smoothness threshold appearing among the present statement's hypotheses.

By \(\mathrm{ass:sample\text{-}balance}\), \(m/n\to\kappa\in(0,\infty)\).  Hence there are constants \(c_\kappa,C_\kappa>0\) and an integer \(n_0\) such that
\[
 c_\kappa n\le m\le C_\kappa n
\]
for \(n\ge n_0\), and consequently
\[
 m^{-1/2}\le c_\kappa^{-1/2}n^{-1/2}.
\]
In each overlap regime, \(n^{-1/2}\) is no larger than \(\rho_n(a,s)\), up to a fixed constant.  Indeed,
\[
 \rho_n(a,s)=n^{-1/2}\quad\text{if }a<1,
\]
while for \(a=1\),
\[
 \rho_n(1,s)=\sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}\ge n^{-1/2}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Finally, if \(a>1\), then
\[
 \frac{s+1}{2s+a+1}<\frac12,
\]
so
\[
 \rho_n(a,s)=n^{-(s+1)/(2s+a+1)}\ge n^{-1/2}.
\]
It follows that, eventually,
\[
 m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\le C_1\rho_n(a,s)
\]
for a constant \(C_1<\infty\) depending only on the fixed parameters and \(\kappa\).  Therefore
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,m}(a,s,t)
 \ge \frac{c_0}{C_1}\{m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\}.
\]
Substituting the three branches of \(\rho_n(a,s)\) proves the displayed lower phase diagram with \(c=c_0/C_1\).

The already established \(\mathrm{lem:oracle\text{-}truncation\text{-}envelope}\) gives, uniformly over \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\),
\[
 m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}
 +n^{-1/2}\left\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h_n\}\right\|_{L^2(P_S)}
 \asymp m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s).
\]
This is an order identity for a deterministic boundary-approximation term and the exact truncated source representer.  It supplies no sample-measurable estimator and therefore no minimax upper bound.  The conclusion is exactly the claimed lower phase diagram together with its truncated-oracle order benchmark. \(\square\)
