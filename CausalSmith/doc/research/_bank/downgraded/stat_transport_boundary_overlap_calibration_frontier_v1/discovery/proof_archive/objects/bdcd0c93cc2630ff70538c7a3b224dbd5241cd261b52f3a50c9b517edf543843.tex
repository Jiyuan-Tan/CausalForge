Define the pure boundary-spliced outcome-regression plug-in estimator by
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{pl}}_{h,J}=
\begin{cases}
m^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu_{h,J}(X_j^T),&\mathcal E_{n,m}(h,J)\text{ holds},\\
0,&\mathcal E_{n,m}(h,J)^c\text{ holds}.
\end{cases}
\]
On the good branch, substitution of the two empirical normal equations gives the exact observable identity
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{pl}}_{h,J}=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J)Z_i.
\]
Thus the estimator uses source outcomes only through a preconditioned least-squares regression and averages that regression over the target covariates.  It contains no source residual correction.
