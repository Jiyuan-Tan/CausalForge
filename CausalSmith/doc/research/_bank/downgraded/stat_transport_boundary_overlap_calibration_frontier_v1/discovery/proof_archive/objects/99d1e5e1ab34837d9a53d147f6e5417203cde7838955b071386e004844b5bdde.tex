The note proves the three-regime minimax lower phase diagram and its critical multiscale witness, derives the matching deterministic boundary-local-polynomial oracle allowance and standard-deviation envelope, and gives an executable capped calibrated-transport estimator with an exact linear identity. Its finite-sample fixed-class interval uses a specified jet linear program with \(N(p+1)\) variables and \(O\{N^2(p+1)\}\) constraints. It also proves the explicit smoothness-nonadaptation lower bound while leaving positive adaptation as an open research direction rather than a formal construction.
