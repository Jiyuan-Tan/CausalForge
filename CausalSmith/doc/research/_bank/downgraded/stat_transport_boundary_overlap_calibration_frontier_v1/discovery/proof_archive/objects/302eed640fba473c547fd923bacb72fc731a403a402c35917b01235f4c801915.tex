The source density is \(f_S(x)=x^ag_S(x)\), with \(c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+}\), and the target density is \(f_T(x)=g_T(x)\), with \(c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+}\). The factor functions belong to \(\mathcal H^t(M)\) for an arbitrary fixed \(t>0\). Neither \(t>1/2\) nor the nuisance-product inequality is a member property of the proved model class.
