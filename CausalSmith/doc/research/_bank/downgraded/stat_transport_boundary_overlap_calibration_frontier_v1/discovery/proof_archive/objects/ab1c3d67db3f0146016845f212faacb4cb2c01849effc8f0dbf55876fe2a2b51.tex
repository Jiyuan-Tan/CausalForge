The phase transition is an information statement, not an achievement claim for the fitted transport weights. Below \(a=1\), the interior Riesz representer is square integrable; at \(a=1\), information accumulates across boundary scales and produces the logarithm; above \(a=1\), H\"older extrapolation and source noise balance at a nonparametric bandwidth. The oracle boundary polynomial removes lower-order boundary terms, so the remaining bias is at most order \(h^{s+1}\); that allowance is worst-case sharp for fixed \(a>0\), although a special law can have zero actual oracle bias. In a RICOT cardiomyopathy analysis, the finite jet program and coefficient norm reveal the realized certificate radius, but they do not certify frontier-order expected length.
