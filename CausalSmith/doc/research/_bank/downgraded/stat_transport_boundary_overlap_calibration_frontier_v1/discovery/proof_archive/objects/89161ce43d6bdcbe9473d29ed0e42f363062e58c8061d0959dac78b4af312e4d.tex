Put \(q_-=c_{T,-}/c_{S,+}\), \(q_+=c_{T,+}/c_{S,-}\), and \(q(x)=g_T(x)/g_S(x)\).  For \(h\in(0,1)\), define
\[
N_h=n\mathbf 1\{a<1\}+nh^a\mathbf 1\{a\ge1\},
\qquad
V_a(h)=1+\int_h^1x^{-a}\,dx,
\qquad
\lambda_n=n^{-2}.
\]
For \(u>0\), let
\[
\mathsf J_u(h)=\max\left\{1,\left\lceil\left(\frac{N_h}{\log(en)}\right)^{1/(2u+1)}\right\rceil\right\},
\]
and define
\[
\delta_{u,h}=\mathsf J_u(h)^{-u}
+\sqrt{\frac{\mathsf J_u(h)\log(en)}{N_h}}
+\sqrt{\frac{\mathsf J_u(h)\log(em)}{m}}.
\]
The deterministic dimensions used by the estimator are
\[
J_{\mu,n}=\mathsf J_s(h_n),
\qquad
J_{r,n}=\mathsf J_t(h_n).
\]
The quantity \(N_h\) is an effective concentration size, not an additional sample and not a member-property condition.
