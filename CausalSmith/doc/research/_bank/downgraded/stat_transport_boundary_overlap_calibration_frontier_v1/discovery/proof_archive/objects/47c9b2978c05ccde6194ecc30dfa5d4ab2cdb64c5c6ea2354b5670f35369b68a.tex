\textbf{Proof.}
By \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}aware\text{-}interval}\), the event \(\tau\in\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) is exactly
\[
\left|\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau\right|
\le B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_T(m).
\tag{1}
\]
For every \(\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\), \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\) assigns probability at least \(1-\gamma\) to (1).  This law-by-law bound uses the same fixed class constants for every member of \(\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)\).  Taking the infimum over that class therefore gives
\[
\inf_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\}
\ge1-\gamma.
\]

The interval is symmetric about \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\) and has half-length \(B_X+R_S+R_T\).  Its realized length is consequently exactly
\[
2\{B_X(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_S(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})+R_T(m)\}.
\]
If the realized covariate design satisfies \(B_X\le b\) and \(\sum_i\Gamma_i^2\le g^2\), then the definitions in \(\mathrm{def:foldwise\text{-}inference\text{-}statistics}\) give
\[
R_S\le\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)}\,g,
\qquad
R_T(m)=\sqrt{2\log(4/\gamma)/m}.
\]
Substitution into the exact length formula proves the stated realized-design upper bound.  Since no averaging or stochastic-order assertion for \(B_X\) or \(\sum_i\Gamma_i^2\) was used, the proof entails none of the expected-length, Gaussian-approximation, localization, leverage, variance-consistency, or frontier-attainment claims expressly excluded by the theorem. \(\square\)
