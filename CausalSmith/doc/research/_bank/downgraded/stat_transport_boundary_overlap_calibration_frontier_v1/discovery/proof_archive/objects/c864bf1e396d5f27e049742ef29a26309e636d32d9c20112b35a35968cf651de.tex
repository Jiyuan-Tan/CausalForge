For the transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy consumer motivated by RiCOT, the exponent \(a\) measures how rapidly trial representation disappears near a clinically relevant risk boundary while the real-world population retains patients there. The lower phase diagram is a recruitment and precision limit: changing the weighting optimizer alone cannot beat it. The fixed-class interval is immediately interpretable as a realized-design certificate, but its width is not claimed to achieve the phase diagram until held-out capped-weight localization and coefficient-norm control are proved.
