\textbf{Proof.}
Assume \(t>1/2\), \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), and \(a=0\).  By the definitions of the density factors, for every \(x\in(0,1]\),
\[
 f_S(x)=x^a g_S(x)=g_S(x),
 \qquad
 f_T(x)=g_T(x),
 \qquad
 r(x)=x^{-a}\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}.
\]
The source-boundary-factor and target-boundary-factor assumptions give
\[
 c_{S,-}\le g_S(x)\le c_{S,+}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 c_{T,-}\le g_T(x)\le c_{T,+},
 \qquad x\in[0,1].
\]
Because \(c_{S,-}>0\), division by \(g_S(x)\) is valid.  Thus, for every point in the declared domain \((0,1]\) of \(r\),
\[
 \frac{c_{T,-}}{c_{S,+}}
 \le r(x)=\frac{g_T(x)}{g_S(x)}
 \le \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}.
\]

Since \(a=0<1\), the first branch of the definition of the regime bandwidth gives
\[
 h_n=n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}.
\]
The declared smoothness satisfies \(s>0\), so \(s+1>0\), and elementary exponent arithmetic yields
\[
 h_n^{s+1}
 =\left(n^{-1/\{2(s+1)\}}\right)^{s+1}
 =n^{-1/2}.
\]
Moreover, \(h_n\downarrow0\).  Applying the \(a<1\) branch of Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:riesz\text{-}threshold}\) along this sequence gives
\[
 \int_{h_n}^1\frac{f_T(x)^2}{f_S(x)}\,dx\asymp1,
\]
and in particular the integral is \(O(1)\).  Its comparison constants depend only on the fixed source and target boundary-factor constants.

It remains to prove positivity of the executable extension.  Fix a fold \(k\) for which both interior training measures have positive total mass, and put
\[
 I^T_{k,n}=\{j\in\mathcal T^T_k:X_j^T\ge h_n\}.
\]
Positivity of the target interior training mass implies \(I^T_{k,n}\ne\varnothing\) and \(|\mathcal T^T_k|>0\).  Positivity of both interior masses excludes the zero-mass fallback in Definition \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\); hence either its exact-feasibility branch or its penalized branch is used.  On either branch, the selected calibration and target-marginal multipliers are finite real vectors.  Therefore, for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\) and every \(j\in I^T_{k,n}\),
\[
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
 +\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
   \widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 +\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right\}>0.
\]
Indeed, the cost and spline coordinates are finite, the multipliers are real, and \(\eta_0>0\).  Rewriting the indicator-weighted sum in the raw continuation over \(I^T_{k,n}\), its bracket is consequently strictly positive:
\[
 \frac1{|\mathcal T^T_k|}
 \sum_{j\in I^T_{k,n}}
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,X_j^T)
 +\widehat\theta_{h_n,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}
   \widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 +\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n},j}}{\eta_0}\right\}>0.
\]
The exponent \(\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)\) is positive because \(\eta_0,\lambda_0>0\).  It follows from the defining formula that
\[
 \widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)>0,
 \qquad x\in[h_n,1].
\]
Under \(a=0\), the downstream cap equals
\[
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h_n^{-a}
 =\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}>0.
\]
The capped RiCOT extension is the minimum of this positive cap and the positive raw continuation.  Hence
\[
 \widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x)
 =\min\!\left\{\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT},(-k)}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(x),
 \frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}\right\}>0
\]
for every \(x\in[h_n,1]\).

All conclusions proved above are deterministic consequences of the boundary-factor bounds, the bandwidth definition, the Riesz-threshold lemma, and the positive-mass branch of the RiCOT construction.  None supplies an empirical norm bound, a nonzero realized residual sum, or a root-risk bound.  The restrictions involving \(s\) and \(t\) are compatible with, but are not otherwise needed for, these deterministic conclusions. \(\square\)
