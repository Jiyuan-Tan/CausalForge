\textbf{Proof.}
The interval in \(\mathrm{def:bias\text{-}aware\text{-}interval}\) is centered at the unchanged estimator and has radius equal to the right-hand side of the probability inequality in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\). That theorem therefore gives
\[
\mathbb P_{n,m}\left\{\tau\in\mathcal I_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}\right\}\ge1-\gamma
\]
for each member of the model class. Taking an infimum over the class preserves this finite-sample lower bound.

The length of an interval written as \([\widehat\tau\pm R]\) is \(2R\), which gives the second display directly from the definition. If the realized design obeys \(B_X\le b\) and \(\sum_i\Gamma_i^2\le g^2\), monotonicity of the square root gives the asserted upper bound after substitution. No averaging over the design and no limit approximation enters any step, so none of the excluded rate or approximation claims follows. \(\square\)
