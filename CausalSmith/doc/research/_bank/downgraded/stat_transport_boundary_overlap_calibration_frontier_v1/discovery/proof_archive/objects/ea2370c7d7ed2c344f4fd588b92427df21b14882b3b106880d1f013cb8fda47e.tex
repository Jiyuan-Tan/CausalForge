For each fold \(k\), interpret a training-complement empirical measure with an empty denominator as the zero measure, and form \(\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\) and \(\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\). Represent each nonzero empirical measure on its increasingly ordered distinct atoms, aggregating the masses of tied observations. Write \(\mathsf S_k\) and \(\mathsf T_k\) for the resulting source and target atom sets and \(s_{k,x}=\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\{x\}\) and \(t_{k,z}=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\{z\}\) for their masses.

If either interior empirical measure has zero total mass, set \(\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}=0\), set the calibration and target-marginal dual vectors to zero in their ordered coordinate spaces, and set
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=
\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=0,
\qquad h\le x\le1.
\]
No optimization problem is entered on this branch.

On the complementary branch, let \(\mathcal F^\circ_{h,J_{r,n},k}\) be the finite plans \(\Pi\) on \(\mathsf S_k\times\mathsf T_k\) that have strictly positive density relative to \(\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\otimes\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), satisfy \(\Pi_2=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), and satisfy
\[
\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)\,d\Pi_1(x)=\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z).
\]
If \(\mathcal F^\circ_{h,J_{r,n},k}\ne\varnothing\), define \(\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) as the lexicographically first minimizer over its closure of
\[
\int c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,z)\,d\Pi+\eta_0\operatorname{KL}(\Pi\,\|\,\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\otimes\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h)+\lambda_0\operatorname{KL}(\Pi_1\,\|\,\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h).
\]
Otherwise define it as the lexicographically first minimizer of the same criterion, over plans on \(\mathsf S_k\times\mathsf T_k\) satisfying \(\Pi_2=\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\), plus
\[
\xi_n\left\|\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}\,d\Pi_1-\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z)\right\|_2^2.
\]
Generalized Kullback--Leibler divergence is used for finite measures. For the exact branch, fix the sign of the multipliers by adding
\[
\theta^\top\left\{\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}\,d\Pi_1-\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h\right\}
+\sum_{z\in\mathsf T_k}\psi_z\{\Pi_2\{z\}-t_{k,z}\}
\]
to the Lagrangian. Among all compatible exact-branch multiplier pairs, choose the pair \((\widehat\theta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}},\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}})\) of smallest Euclidean norm in the ordered atom coordinates. In the penalized branch, set
\[
\widehat\delta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}=
\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)\,d\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},1}(x)
-\int\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(z)\,d\widehat P^{T,(-k)}_h(z),
\qquad
\widehat\theta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}=2\xi_n\widehat\delta^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}},
\]
and choose the compatible target-marginal multiplier \(\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}\) of smallest Euclidean norm in the ordered target-atom coordinates.

For every \(x\in[h,1]\), define the raw KKT continuation
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=
\left[\sum_{z\in\mathsf T_k}t_{k,z}\exp\left\{-\frac{c_{\mathrm{OT}}(x,z)+\widehat\theta_{h,J_{r,n}}^{(-k)\top}\widetilde{\mathbf b}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)+\widehat\psi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},z}}{\eta_0}\right\}\right]^{\eta_0/(\eta_0+\lambda_0)}
\]
and the capped downstream weight
\[
\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x)=
\min\left\{\widehat r^{\mathrm{raw},(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n}}(x),\frac{c_{T,+}}{c_{S,-}}h^{-a}\right\}.
\]
Only the raw continuation agrees, at source training atoms, with \(d\widehat\Pi^{(-k)}_{h,J_{r,n},1}/d\widehat P^{S,(-k)}_h\). The capped downstream weight need not satisfy exact spline calibration. The cap is a class-known envelope for the oracle ratio on \([h,1]\), because \(r(x)=x^{-a}g_T(x)/g_S(x)\le(c_{T,+}/c_{S,-})h^{-a}\). The transport and relaxation levels are fixed at \(\eta_0\) and \(\lambda_0\), the slack penalty is \(\xi_n=n^2\), and the calibration dimension is \(J_{r,n}\); the boundary-splice bandwidth remains \(h=h_n\) and is not used as the calibration dimension.
