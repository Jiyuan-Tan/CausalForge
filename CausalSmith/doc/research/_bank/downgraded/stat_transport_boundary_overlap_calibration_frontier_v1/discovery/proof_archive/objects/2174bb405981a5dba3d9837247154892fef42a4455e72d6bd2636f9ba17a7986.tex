Using the fold maps \(k_S\) and \(k_T\) and the unique training-complement fits from \(\mathrm{def:regression\text{-}fit}\) and \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\), define
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=
\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_T(j))}(X_j^T)
+\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\{Z_i-\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_S(i))}(X_i)\}.
\]
Every displayed evaluation uses a fit trained without that observation's deterministic fold. The capped downstream weight is evaluated as a function at held-out \(X_i\), not as an empirical-support lookup; only the raw \(\widetilde r^{\mathrm{KKT}}\) has KKT-continuation and training-atom density status. If an interior training measure is empty, its weight contribution is zero by \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\). The indices \(J_{\mu,n}\) and \(J_{r,n}\) are respectively regression and calibration knot-interval counts, not coefficient dimensions. The full regression coefficient dimension is \(J_{\mu,n}+q\), the full calibration spline-space dimension is \(J_{r,n}+q\), and the explicit nonconstant calibration vector has dimension \(J_{r,n}+q-1\) because the target-marginal mass equation supplies the constant coordinate. Only \(h\) is the boundary-splice bandwidth.

After the covariates are fixed, the foldwise ridge fits are linear in the source pseudo-outcomes. With the observable coefficients defined in \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}linear\text{-}coefficients}\), algebraic expansion of the unchanged display gives
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_i(h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n})Z_i.
\]
This equality is proved in \(\mathrm{thm:ricot\text{-}attainment}\); it records the estimator's certificate carrier without modifying its folds, fits, weights, or formula.
