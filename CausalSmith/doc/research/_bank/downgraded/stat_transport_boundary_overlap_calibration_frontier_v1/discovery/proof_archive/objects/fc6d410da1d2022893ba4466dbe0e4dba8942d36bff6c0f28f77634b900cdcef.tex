For arbitrary capped held-out weights \(w_i=\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\), addition and subtraction give the exact interior decomposition
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{int}}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx
={}&\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nw_i\{Z_i-\mu(X_i)\}\\
&+\left\{\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\mu(X_j^T)\mathbf 1\{X_j^T\ge h\}-\int_h^1\mu(x)f_T(x)\,dx\right\}\\
&+C_{n,m}(h)-\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu).
\end{aligned}
\]
The response-smoothness assumptions imply \(\mu\in\mathcal H^s(2M)\), hence
\[
|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(\mu)|\le2M\sup_{v\in\mathcal H^s(1)}|\Delta_{h,J_{r,n}}(v)|.
\]
Accordingly, the displayed bias envelope contains the exact fold-varying interior discrepancy, including the remainder created by capping. The one-dimensional Hölder entropy integral used by a possible Gaussian approximation is finite in the stated regime because
\[
\int_0^1\epsilon^{-1/(2s)}\,d\epsilon<\infty
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad s>1/2.
\]
These deterministic identities do not establish an asymptotically linear representation, variance consistency, a triangular-array Lindeberg condition, conditional multiplier validity, uniform integrability, or the expected-length rate.
