Using the fold maps \(k_S\) and \(k_T\) and the unique training-complement fits from \(\mathrm{def:regression\text{-}fit}\) and \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\), define
\[
\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=
\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_T(j))}(X_j^T)
+\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\widehat r^{\mathrm{RICOT},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\{Z_i-\widehat\mu_{h,J_{\mu,n}}^{(-k_S(i))}(X_i)\}.
\]
Every displayed evaluation uses a fit trained without that observation's deterministic fold. The dual extension is evaluated as a function at held-out \(X_i\), not as an empirical-support lookup. If an interior training measure is empty, its weight contribution is zero by \(\mathrm{def:ricot\text{-}weights}\). The regression and calibration dimensions are respectively \(J_{\mu,n}\) and \(J_{r,n}\); only \(h\) is the boundary-splice bandwidth.
