Define
\[
V_h=1+\mathbf 1\{a=1\}\log(1/h)+\mathbf 1\{a>1\}h^{1-a}.
\]
Uniformly over the model class,
\[
\|r\mathbf 1\{X\ge h\}\|_{L^2(P_S)}^2\asymp V_h,
\]
and hence the oracle envelope is
\[
m^{-1/2}+h^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}V_h^{1/2}.
\]
At \(h=h_n\), this is \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\). The separate sieve dimensions satisfy
\[
J_{\mu,n}^{-s}+\sqrt{J_{\mu,n}/n}\asymp n^{-s/(2s+1)}
\]
and
\[
J_{r,n}^{-t}+\sqrt{J_{r,n}\log J_{r,n}/n}
\asymp(\log n/n)^{t/(2t+1)}.
\]
The declared product-smoothness relation makes the product of these two envelopes \(o(n^{-1/2})\), and \(nh_n^{a+1}/\log J_{r,n}\to\infty\). These calculations do not identify the fitted held-out transport weights with the oracle ratio or control their maximum leverage.
