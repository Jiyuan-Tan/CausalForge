For \(i\in\mathcal T^S_k\), define the conditional linear-smoother coefficient
\[
L_{i,k,h}(x)=\frac1{|\mathcal T^S_k|}
\begin{cases}
\mathbf 1\{X_i\le2h\}\mathbf R_h(x)^\top\widehat G_{k,h}^{-1}\mathbf R_h(X_i),&x<h,\\
\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}X_i^{-a}\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(x)^\top
\left(\widehat G^{\mu,(-k)}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}+\lambda_nI\right)^{-1}\mathbf b_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_i),&x\ge h,
\end{cases}
\]
and put \(L_{i,k,h}(x)=0\) when \(i\notin\mathcal T^S_k\).  The complete conditional coefficient of \(Z_i\) in the estimator is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Gamma_{i,h}={}&\frac1n\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h\}\widehat r^{\mathrm{DS},(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_i)
+\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^mL_{i,k_T(j),h}(X_j^T)\\
&-\frac1n\sum_{\ell=1}^n\mathbf 1\{X_\ell\ge h\}\widehat r^{\mathrm{DS},(-k_S(\ell))}_{h,J_{r,n}}(X_\ell)L_{i,k_S(\ell),h}(X_\ell).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_{i,h}Z_i\) exactly, conditionally on all covariates and treatment assignments.  Set
\[
\widehat\epsilon_i=Z_i-\widehat\mu^{(-k_S(i))}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_i),
\qquad
\overline\mu_T=\frac1m\sum_{j=1}^m\widehat\mu^{(-k_T(j))}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_j^T),
\]
and define the sandwich statistic
\[
\widehat{\mathrm{se}}_{h,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}^2
=\sum_{i=1}^n\Gamma_{i,h}^2\widehat\epsilon_i^2
+\frac1{m(m-1)}\sum_{j=1}^m
\left\{\widehat\mu^{(-k_T(j))}_{h,J_{\mu,n}}(X_j^T)-\overline\mu_T\right\}^2,
\]
with the target term set to zero when \(m=1\).  Because \(\Gamma_{i,h}\) is the full source coefficient, this single sandwich sum automatically contains every covariance among the direct interior, interior-regression, and boundary-regression pieces.
