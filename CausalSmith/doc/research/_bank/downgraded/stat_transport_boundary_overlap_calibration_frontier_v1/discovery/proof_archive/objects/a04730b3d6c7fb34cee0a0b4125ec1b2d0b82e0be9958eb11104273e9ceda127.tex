For fixed \(a,s,t,M\) with \(t>1/2\), \(s/(2s+1)+t/(2t+1)>1/2\), and \(a<2t(2s+1)\), along every triangular-array sequence with \(m/n\to\kappa\), the de-singularized direct-series estimator with \(h=h_n\), \(J_\mu=J_{\mu,n}\), and \(J_r=J_{r,n}\) satisfies
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\left\{\mathbb E_{\mathbb P_{n,m}}
\left[(\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{sp}}_{h_n,J_{\mu,n},J_{r,n}}-\tau)^2\right]\right\}^{1/2}
\le C\left\{
m^{-1/2}+h_n^{s+1}+n^{-1/2}V_a(h_n)^{1/2}
+\delta_{s,h_n}\delta_{t,h_n}
+\lambda_n(J_{\mu,n}+J_{r,n})
\right\}.
\]
The first three terms are \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\).  Moreover,
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_{n,m}\in\mathcal M_{n,m}(a,s,t)}
\mathbb P_{n,m}\{\mathcal E_{n,m}(h_n)^c\}
\le C\exp\{-c\,\underline N_n\},
\]
where
\[
\underline N_n=\min\left\{
\frac{N_{h_n}}{J_{\mu,n}},
\frac{N_{h_n}}{J_{r,n}},
\frac{m}{J_{\mu,n}},
\frac{m}{J_{r,n}},
nh_n^{a+1}
\right\}/\log(en)
\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Uniformly over the same sequence, the direct-series held-out weights obey
\[
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n
\{\widehat r^{\mathrm{DS},(-k_S(i))}_{h_n,J_{r,n}}(X_i)\}^2
\mathbf 1\{X_i\ge h_n\}=O_P\{V_a(h_n)\},
\]
and their maximum squared leverage divided by their summed squares is \(o_P(1)\).  The upper bound matches \(m^{-1/2}+\rho_n(a,s)\) when \(a<1\); it also matches at \(a=1\) if \(t(2s-1)\ge1\), and for \(a>1\) if \(t(2s-1)>1\).  Outside those subregimes the displayed nuisance-product term is the proved honest gap, so no matched empirical frontier is claimed.
