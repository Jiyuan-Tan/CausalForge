The data are a twelve-dimensional Gaussian sufficient statistic with known variance under a public deterministic mask.  Positive strata are entrywise bounded, have exact total and anchor rank, and have a specified positive smallest anchor singular value; rank zero is separate.  The exact finite-grid theorem requires \(L_{k,N}>0\) for every represented stratum because those values are denominators.  The continuum profile interval is honest without a positivity or mesh assumption and has an explicit rank-zero certificate.  No claim is made that finite-grid factors converge to the continuum optimum or that the profile interval is within a universal factor of every positive-rank frontier.
