The frozen primitives do yield one rank-and-strength-free honest interval and a
nontrivial rank-zero certificate.  Namely,
\[
C_{\mathrm{box}}(Y)=[-B_{\max},B_{\max}]
\]
uses no unknown rank, strength, or orbit amplitude and is honest over
\(\mathcal U\), because every member of a positive-rank stratum satisfies
\(\lVert M\rVert_\infty\leq B_{\max}\), while the rank-zero target is zero.
It has expected length \(2B_{\max}\) on every stratum.  Together with
\(\mathrm{thm:union-zero-lower}\), this proves
\[
1\leq
\frac{E_{P_0}\operatorname{len}C_{\mathrm{box}}(Y)}
{\mathfrak L_0^{\mathcal U}(n)}
\leq\frac{2}{1-2\alpha}.
\]
What is not determined is a uniform comparison of this or a test-inversion
interval with \(\mathfrak L_{r,s}^{\mathcal U}(n)\) over all positive-rank
strengths; those frontiers may become much smaller when \(n s^2/\sigma^2\) is
large.
