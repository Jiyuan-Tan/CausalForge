\textit{Proof.}
Fix \(M\in\mathcal M_r(s)\).  By the exact-rank assumptions,
\(\operatorname{rank}(M)=\operatorname{rank}(A)=r\).  Since \(A\) is a
submatrix of both \([A\ F]\) and \([A^{\mathsf T}\ G^{\mathsf T}]^{\mathsf T}\),
\[
r\leq \operatorname{rank}[A\ F]\leq \operatorname{rank}(M)=r,
\qquad
r\leq \operatorname{rank}\!\begin{bmatrix}A\\G\end{bmatrix}
\leq \operatorname{rank}(M)=r.
\]
Consequently \(\operatorname{col}(F)\subseteq\operatorname{col}(A)\) and
\(\operatorname{row}(G)\subseteq\operatorname{row}(A)\).  Thus there are
matrices \(X,Y_0\in\mathbb R^{2\times2}\) with \(F=AX\) and \(G=Y_0A\).
The Moore--Penrose identities give the particular choices
\(X=A^+F\) and \(Y_0=GA^+\).  Invertible block row and column operations now give
\[
\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\-Y_0&I\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}A&F\\G&D\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}I&-X\\0&I\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\0&D-Y_0AX\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The left side has rank \(r\), whereas the right side has rank
\(r+\operatorname{rank}(D-Y_0AX)\).  Hence \(D=Y_0AX=GA^+F\).
Substitution into the definition of the causal target yields
\[
\tau(M)=\frac14\mathbf 1_2^{\mathsf T}GA^+F\mathbf 1_2.
\]
All matrices on the right are contained in the observed vector
\(P_{\Omega}M=\operatorname{vec}(A,F,G)\); the positive-strength condition
\(s>0\) fixes the rank and ensures that the Moore--Penrose inverse is evaluated
on the asserted exact-rank stratum.  This proves the stated identifying
functional. \(\square\)
