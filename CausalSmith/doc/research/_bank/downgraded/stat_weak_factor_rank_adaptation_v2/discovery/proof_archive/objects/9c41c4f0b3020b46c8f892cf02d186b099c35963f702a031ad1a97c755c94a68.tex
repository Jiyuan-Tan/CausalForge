No compactification theorem presently identifies the limit of the finite-grid normalized programs with the continuum sharp factor.  Such a passage requires positivity and adequate semicontinuity of the normalized stratum frontiers, uniform coverage under mesh refinement, lower semicontinuity for connected actions, and a continuum deterministic-purification argument.  The profile interval bypasses those issues for honesty, but its bound \(\operatorname{len}C_{\mathrm{prof}}\leq2B_{\max}\) does not compare uniformly with \(\mathfrak L_{r,s}^{\mathcal U}(n)\), which may be much smaller for strong anchors.
