The finite legal-grid problem was reduced to a compact convex risk program, dualized with coverage multipliers \(\lambda_i\) and normalized risk multipliers \(w_i\), and purified.  For the continuum, direct Gaussian-ball inversion over the closed mean--target graph produced \(C_{\mathrm{prof}}\) and exact union coverage.  A mesh-limit argument was not asserted because the required compactification, positivity, semicontinuity, uniform-coverage, and continuum-purification steps are absent.
