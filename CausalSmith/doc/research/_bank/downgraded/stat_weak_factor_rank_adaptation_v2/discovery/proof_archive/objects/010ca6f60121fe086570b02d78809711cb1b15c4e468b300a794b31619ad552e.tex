Cai and Low, \cite{CaiLow2004AdaptiveCI}, develop ordered moduli and constant-factor confidence intervals for finite unions in Gaussian sequence models; those ideas motivate the finite-grid layer but do not prove continuum convergence or nuisance-wise simultaneous optimality here.  Evans, Hansen, and Stark, \cite{EvansHansenStark2005}, provide expected-measure decision-theoretic duality, but not the positive-stratum oracle comparison required by this union.  Cai and Guo, \cite{CaiGuo2017HDLinearCI}, supply fuzzy-hypothesis lower-bound technology rather than an attaining simultaneous inversion.  Schafer and Stark (2009) support computation on a finite \(\Gamma\)-grid only.  Carpentier, Klopp, Löffler, and Nickl, \cite{CarpentierKloppLofflerNickl2018}, study honest low-rank matrix confidence sets under different observation and loss structures.  Yan and Wainwright, \cite{YanWainwright2024MissingPanel}, Choi, Kwon, and Liao, \cite{ChoiKwonLiao2026UnknownRank}, and Armstrong, Weidner, and Zeleneev, \cite{ArmstrongWeidnerZeleneev2026}, provide panel-inference comparators under fixed rank, working-rank, or signal-side conditions; none gives the uniform expected-length ratio sought here.
