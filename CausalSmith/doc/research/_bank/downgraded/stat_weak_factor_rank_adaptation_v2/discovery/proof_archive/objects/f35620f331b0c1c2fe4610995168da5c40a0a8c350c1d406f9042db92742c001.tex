For a finite grid, let
\[
 \Theta_N=\{(\mu_i,t_i,k(i)):1\leq i\leq N\}
\]
mean that there is an \(M_i\in\mathcal U\) with \(\mu_i=P_\Omega M_i\) and \(t_i=\tau(M_i)\), and that \(k(i)=0\) for \(M_i\in\mathcal M_0\), while \(k(i)=(r,s)\) for \(M_i\in\mathcal M_r(s)\).  Put \(\mathcal T_N=\{t_i:1\leq i\leq N\}\) and
\[
 \mathcal A_N=\{[a,b]:a,b\in\mathcal T_N,\ a\leq b\}.
\]
The density of \(N(\mu_i,\sigma^2n^{-1}I_{12})\) is
\[
 f_i(y)=\left(\frac{n}{2\pi\sigma^2}\right)^6
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{n}{2\sigma^2}\lVert y-\mu_i\rVert_2^2\right\}.
\]
Let \(\mathcal P_N\) be the set of Borel kernels \(p=(p_A:A\in\mathcal A_N)\) satisfying \(p_A(y)\geq0\) and \(\sum_Ap_A(y)=1\).  Define
\[
 Q_i(p)=\int_{\mathbb R^{12}}\sum_{A\in\mathcal A_N}
 \mathbf 1\{t_i\in A\}p_A(y)f_i(y)\,dy,
 \qquad
 R_i(p)=\int_{\mathbb R^{12}}\sum_{A\in\mathcal A_N}
 \operatorname{len}(A)p_A(y)f_i(y)\,dy,
\]
and, for each represented stratum label \(k\),
\[
 L_{k,N}=\inf_{p\in\mathcal P_N:\,Q_j(p)\geq1-\alpha\ \forall j}
 \max_{i:k(i)=k}R_i(p).
\]
Call the grid normalization-legal when \(L_{k,N}>0\) for every represented \(k\).  On such a grid the simultaneous finite-grid oracle factor is
\[
 \kappa_N^*=\inf_{\substack{\kappa\geq0,\ p\in\mathcal P_N}}
 \left\{\kappa:Q_i(p)\geq1-\alpha,\quad
 R_i(p)\leq\kappa L_{k(i),N}\quad(1\leq i\leq N)\right\}.
\]
For the continuum construction put
\[
 \overline{\mathcal K}=\operatorname{cl}\{(P_\Omega M,\tau(M)):M\in\mathcal U\}
 \subset\mathbb R^{12}\times\mathbb R,
 \qquad
 \rho_\alpha=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\sqrt{q_{12,1-\alpha}},
\]
where \(q_{12,1-\alpha}\) is the \((1-\alpha)\)-quantile of a chi-square random variable with twelve degrees of freedom.  Define
\[
 T(y)=\{t:\text{there is }(\mu,t)\in\overline{\mathcal K}
 \text{ with }\lVert y-\mu\rVert_2\leq\rho_\alpha\},
 \qquad
 C_{\mathrm{prof}}(y)=
 \begin{cases}
 [\inf T(y),\sup T(y)],&T(y)\neq\varnothing,\\
 \{0\},&T(y)=\varnothing.
 \end{cases}
\]
The simultaneous-inversion handle consists of the finite-grid program \(\kappa_N^*\), its likelihood dual, and the continuum profile interval \(C_{\mathrm{prof}}\).
