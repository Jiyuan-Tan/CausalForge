The finite-grid dual does not by itself pass to the continuum.  The frozen primitives provide neither positivity and adequate semicontinuity of the normalized positive-stratum frontiers nor uniform coverage under mesh refinement, connected-action lower semicontinuity, and continuum deterministic purification.  The explicit profile interval proves honesty but only the absolute bound \(2B_{\max}\); no cited result converts that bound into a universal multiple of \(\mathfrak L_{r,s}^{\mathcal U}(n)\) uniformly in \(r,s\).
