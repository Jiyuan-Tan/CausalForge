\textit{Proof.}
If \(\mathcal G=\{g_0\}\), the vector \(b\) in the mixture dual has the single
coordinate \(b_{g_0}=c\).  Its objective is then
\[
E_{P_0}\min\{cL_{g_0}^{h,q}(Y),a,1\}-\alpha(a+c),
\]
which is exactly the sole term in the maximum defining
\(\mathfrak D_\alpha^{\mathrm{pair}}(h,q)\).  Hence the two duals coincide.
The three-action primal in the exact-dual proof then has one alternative
noncoverage constraint, and its dual threshold is a function of the single
Gaussian likelihood ratio.  This is precisely inversion of the corresponding
simple-null versus simple-alternative likelihood-ratio test. \(\square\)
