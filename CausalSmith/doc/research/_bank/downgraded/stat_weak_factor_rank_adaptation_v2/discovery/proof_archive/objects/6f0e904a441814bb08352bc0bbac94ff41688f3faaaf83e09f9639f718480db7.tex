Let \(q_{12,1-\alpha}\) be the chi-square quantile in Definition~\ref{def:simultaneous-inversion-handle}, and define the confidence-level constants
\[
 c_\alpha=\sqrt{q_{12,1-\alpha}}+\sqrt{12},\qquad
 d_\alpha=\frac{1-2\alpha}{2}\min\left\{1-2\alpha,\frac12\right\},
 \qquad
 K_\alpha=\max\left\{\frac{2}{1-2\alpha},\frac{16c_\alpha}{d_\alpha}\right\}.
\]
The positive answer to the simultaneous-union inversion question is the single interval
\[
 C^\star(Y;n,\sigma,B_{\max},\alpha)=C_{\mathrm{prof}}(Y).
\]
It is a data-only member of \(\mathfrak C_\alpha(\mathcal U)\), uses none of \(r,s,h,q\), has endpoints evaluable to arbitrary prescribed accuracy by the fixed-dimensional quantifier-elimination and bisection algorithm in Definition~\ref{def:simultaneous-inversion-handle}, and satisfies
\[
 B_{\max}(1-2\alpha)\leq\mathfrak L_0^{\mathcal U}(n)
 \leq E_{P_0}[\operatorname{len}C^\star(Y)]\leq2B_{\max},
\]
\[
 B_{\max}(1-2\alpha)\leq\mathfrak L_{1,s}^{\mathcal U}(n)
 \leq\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_1(s)}E_{P_M}[\operatorname{len}C^\star(Y)]
 \leq2B_{\max},
\]
and
\[
 d_\alpha B_{\max}\min\left\{1,\frac{B_{\max}\sigma}{\sqrt n\,s^2}\right\}
 \leq\mathfrak L_{2,s}^{\mathcal U}(n)
 \leq\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_2(s)}E_{P_M}[\operatorname{len}C^\star(Y)]
 \leq16c_\alpha B_{\max}\min\left\{1,\frac{B_{\max}\sigma}{\sqrt n\,s^2}\right\}.
\]
In particular,
\[
 \frac{E_{P_0}[\operatorname{len}C^\star(Y)]}{\mathfrak L_0^{\mathcal U}(n)}\leq K_\alpha
\]
and, simultaneously for \(r\in\{1,2\}\) and \(s\in(0,B_{\max}]\),
\[
 \frac{\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_r(s)}E_{P_M}[\operatorname{len}C^\star(Y)]}
 {\mathfrak L_{r,s}^{\mathcal U}(n)}\leq K_\alpha.
\]
All denominators in these displays are strictly positive.  Consequently the sharp smallest multiplicative excess displayed in the open question belongs to \([1,K_\alpha]\), and its upper bound is witnessed by the explicit interval \(C^\star\).  The constant depends only on the fixed confidence level \(\alpha\), and is uniform in \(n,\sigma,B_{\max},r,s\) and in every matrix in the corresponding stratum.
