\section{Results at a glance}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:orbit-exact-dual} identifies the exact finite-orbit expected-length functional, and Theorem~\ref{thm:strict-mixture-gap} proves that componentwise Gaussian moduli are insufficient.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-grid-simultaneous-dual} solves every normalization-legal finite grid by an explicit \((\lambda,w)\)-dual, proves primal and dual attainment, and yields a nonrandomized data-only interval.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:profile-union-honesty} gives a single rank-, strength-, and amplitude-free continuum interval with exact union coverage, evaluable endpoints, and rank-zero oracle factor at most \(2/(1-2\alpha)\).
\item The residual open-ended question is whether any continuum rule has a universal positive-stratum oracle factor and, if so, what the sharp simultaneous excess is.
\end{enumerate}
