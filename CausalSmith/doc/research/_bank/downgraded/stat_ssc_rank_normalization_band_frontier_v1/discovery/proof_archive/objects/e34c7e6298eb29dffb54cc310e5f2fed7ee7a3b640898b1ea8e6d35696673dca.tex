Put \(\nu=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\).  There is a constant \(c_{\mathrm{int}}<\infty\) such that, for the piecewise-linear interpolation rule in \(\mathrm{def:inference-grid}\), the difference between the continuous-time supremum error and its grid supremum is at most \(c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{\nu}\) uniformly over \(\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).  Consequently, under the corrected mesh condition \(c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{\nu}\le\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\), the explicit interpolation allowance is \(O(\rho_r)\), and continuous-time honesty is not obtained by replacing \(\mathcal I\) with a fixed finite grid.
