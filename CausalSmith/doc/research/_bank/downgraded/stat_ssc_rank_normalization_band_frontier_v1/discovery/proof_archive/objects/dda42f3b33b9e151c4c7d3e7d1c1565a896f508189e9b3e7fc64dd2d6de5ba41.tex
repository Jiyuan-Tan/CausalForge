For each fixed \(r\ge1\), there is \(a_0<\infty\) such that, at every evaluated triple with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\), the band \(\widehat B_r\) belongs to \(\mathcal H_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\). There is a constant \(c_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\), independent of \(N_0\), \(K_T\), and \(K_D\), such that at every such triple, \[\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)}E_P[w(\widehat B_r)]\le c_{\mathrm{up}}\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).\] Both assertions hold uniformly over every three-dimensional tail, with no balanced-growth or relative-rate restriction.
