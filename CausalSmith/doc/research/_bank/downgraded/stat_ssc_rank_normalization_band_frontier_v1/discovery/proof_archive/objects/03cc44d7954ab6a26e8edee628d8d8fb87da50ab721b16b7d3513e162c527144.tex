For fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1/2)\) and every fixed exact rank \(r\), there are \(a_0<\infty\) and constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) such that, uniformly over every evaluated triple with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\), without relative-growth restrictions,
\[
 W_1^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\asymp(N_0K_D)^{-1/2},
\]
and, for \(r\ge2\),
\[
 c\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)
 \le W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \le C\bar\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).
\]
Consequently \(W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\asymp\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) on every subregime satisfying \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D))\).  Without that subregime condition, the displayed bracket is the proved unrestricted conclusion and its tightness is not asserted.  Each infimum is over bands satisfying the coverage constraint at that same evaluated triple.
