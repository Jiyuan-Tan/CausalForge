The exact-rank higher-rank converse is of order \(\rho_r\), whereas the proved total-estimator upper envelope is \(\rho_r+\eta_D(K_D)\). The bracket is matched when \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\); unrestricted tightness remains unresolved, and random-quantile validity does not remove or lower-bound \(\eta_D\). The lower frontier is minimax over an exact-rank class and does not claim that every individual higher-rank law has a nonzero target-pre variance component. Exact rank is also not an approximation-bias guarantee.
