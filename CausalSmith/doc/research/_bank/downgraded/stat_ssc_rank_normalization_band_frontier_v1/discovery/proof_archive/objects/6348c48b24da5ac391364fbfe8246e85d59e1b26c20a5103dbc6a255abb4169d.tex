Fix a deterministic split of the patients in each donor-period cell into two groups whose sizes differ by at most one.  On the grid, compute the full-cell Kaplan--Meier curve \(\widehat S^{\mathrm{KM}}_{p,m}\) and the two split-sample curves \(\widehat S^{(1)}_{p,m}\) and \(\widehat S^{(2)}_{p,m}\).  Regard each donor's two-period curve as an element of \(L^2(\{0,1\}\times\mathcal I)\), center each split across donors, and form the symmetrized cross-fold covariance operator
\[
 \widehat\Gamma=
 \frac{1}{2N_0}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}
 \left\{\widehat Y_m^{(1)}\otimes\widehat Y_m^{(2)}+
 \widehat Y_m^{(2)}\otimes\widehat Y_m^{(1)}\right\}.
\]
For \(r\ge2\), let \(\widehat\Pi_d\) be the spectral projector onto the \(d=r-1\) largest eigenvalues of \(\widehat\Gamma\), with the declared deterministic tie rule, and use the corresponding cross-fold singular values and full-cell projected donor means to obtain \(\widehat b_0,\widehat b_1\).  Put \(\widehat s_p=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\widehat S^{\mathrm{KM}}_{p,m}\), define \(\widehat z_\star\) as the minimum-norm minimizer of
\[
 z\longmapsto\int_{\mathcal I}
 \left\{\widehat S^{\mathrm{KM}}_{0,\star}(t)-\widehat s_0(t)-
 \widehat b_0(t)^\top z\right\}^2dt,
\]
and set \(\widehat\theta_r=\widehat s_1+\widehat b_1^\top\widehat z_\star\).  For \(r=1\), define \(\widehat\theta_1\) to be the Kaplan--Meier curve obtained by pooling the \(N_0K_D\) donor-post records.  All integrals are computed from the piecewise-linear grid interpolants.

Differentiate this complete map, including the cross-fold spectral projector, with respect to every cellwise product-limit counting-process increment.  In the resulting empirical influence process replace the contribution of patient \((p,m,j)\) by \(\xi_{p,m,j}\) times that contribution.  Let \(c_r^\xi\) be the conditional \(q_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\)-quantile of the multiplier grid supremum, where
\[
 q_{N_0,K_T,K_D}=1-\alpha+
 \min\left\{\frac{\alpha}{2},\frac{\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)}{2}\right\}.
\]
With \(c_{\mathrm{int}}=c_{\mathrm{int}}(L,\bar\tau,\beta)\) from the interpolation proposition, define on the grid the endpoints \(\widehat\theta_r\pm c_r^\xi\), interpolate linearly, enlarge both sides by \(c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{\min\{\beta+1,2\}}\), intersect pointwise with \([0,1]\), and set both endpoints equal to one at time zero.  The resulting continuous band is \(\widehat B_r\).
