There is a constant \(c_{\mathrm{int}}=c_{\mathrm{int}}(L,\bar\tau,\beta)<\infty\) such that, for the piecewise-linear interpolant \(\widehat\theta_r\) in \(\mathrm{def:fpcr-multiplier-band}\),
\[
 0\le \|\widehat\theta_r-\theta\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}
 -\max_{g\in\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 \le c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{\min\{\beta+1,2\}}
\]
uniformly over \(\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).  Consequently the interpolation allowance under the corrected grid definition is \(O(\rho_r)\), so continuous-time honesty is not obtained by replacing \(\mathcal I\) with a fixed finite grid.
