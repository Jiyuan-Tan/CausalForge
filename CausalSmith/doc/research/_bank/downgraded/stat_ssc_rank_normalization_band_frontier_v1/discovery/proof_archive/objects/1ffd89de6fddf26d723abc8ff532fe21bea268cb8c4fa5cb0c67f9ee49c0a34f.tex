For fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1/2)\) and every fixed exact rank \(r\), there is \(a_0<\infty\) such that uniformly over every evaluated triple with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\), without relative-growth restrictions, \[W_1^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\asymp(N_0K_D)^{-1/2},\qquad W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\asymp K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\quad(r\ge2).\] Here each infimum is over bands satisfying the coverage constraint at that same evaluated triple.
