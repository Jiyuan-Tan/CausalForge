\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(c_T=K_T^{-1}\), let \(\widetilde z_m\) be the unoriented donor scores in the weak-pair construction, and put \(q_\alpha=1/2-\alpha>0\).

First, every \(Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\) has
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_m\zeta_{m,Q}=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_m\zeta_{m,Q}^2=1.                   \tag{1}
\]
The embedding proposition with \(d=1\) shows that the donor loading matrix with columns \((1,\zeta_{m,Q})^\top\) has full row rank and that \((1,\zeta_{\star,Q})^\top\) is in its span. Survival normalization and donor centering imply \(s_{p,Q}(0)=1\) and \(b_{p,Q}(0)=0\). Donor visibility makes some \(b_{p,Q}(t)\ne0\), so \((1,0)^\top\) and \((s_{p,Q}(t),b_{p,Q}(t))^\top\) are linearly independent. Thus the total factor rank is exactly two. By the cited scope lemma, these fixed-design laws satisfy the factorization-and-span part of Han--Shah Theorem~1.

Second, the weak pair belongs to this class. The centered arithmetic progression satisfies
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_m\widetilde z_m=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_m\widetilde z_m^2=1.                 \tag{2}
\]
Multiplication of both factors and loadings by \(\varsigma_T\) preserves every curve and enforces the canonical sign convention. In period zero the heterogeneous argument is \(c_T\widetilde z_m\) for a donor and \(c_Tu\bar h\) for the target; in period one it is \(\widetilde z_m\) and \(u\bar h\). These values lie in the numerical compatibility range. The four witness inequalities therefore give survival support in \([s_-,1]\), normalization, monotonicity, density floor, and the \(C^\beta\) hazard bound. The exponential continuation is valid, infinite censoring gives \(G=1\), and independence and consistency hold by construction. The donor and target loading bounds and factor upper bounds follow from the same compatibility inequalities.

Because the donor score has empirical second moment one, the nonzero joint contrast singular value and scalar pre-Gram are
\[
 \sigma_T=\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\}^{1/2}
 \|\psi_\circ\|_2,
 \qquad H_{0,T}=K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2. \tag{3}
\]
Hence \(\sigma_T\ge|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0\) while \(H_{0,T}\to0\), proving pair inclusion, exact rank two, donor visibility, and degenerating pre-Gram.

Third, fix a finite pre-period grid \(\mathcal T\) and put
\[
 a=(e^{-\lambda_\circ t_\ell})_\ell,
 \qquad q=(\psi_\circ(t_\ell))_\ell.
\]
The pre-period donor matrix is
\[
 M_{0,T}=a\mathbf1^\top+c_Ta_{\circ,0}q\widetilde z^\top. \tag{4}
\]
If \(a\) and \(q\) are linearly independent, (4) has rank two and, by the variational characterization,
\[
 s_1(M_{0,T})\ge\sqrt{N_0}\|a\|_2,
 \qquad s_2(M_{0,T})\le c_T|a_{\circ,0}|\sqrt{N_0}\|q\|_2. \tag{5}
\]
Since \(\|a\|_2\ge e^{-\lambda_\circ\bar\tau}\sqrt{T_0}\) and \(\|q\|_2\le\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty\sqrt{T_0}\), their ratio is at least a fixed positive constant times \(K_T\); Han--Shah Assumption~7 fails. If \(q=\kappa a\ne0\), the pre latent-factor direction is \((1,\varsigma_Tc_Ta_{\circ,0}\kappa)^\top\), while the post direction is \((1,\varsigma_Ta_{\circ,1}\kappa)^\top\); for large \(K_T\) the latter is outside the former span, so Assumption~8 fails. If \(q=0\), the finite grid sees none of the perturbation, so its target separation is zero even though the continuous-time separation below is nonzero. These cases are exhaustive and show no contradiction with Han--Shah Theorem~2.

Fourth, the observed laws differ only through the \(K_T\) target-pre failures. Their causal targets satisfy
\[
 \theta_u(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+u\bar h a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad
 \Delta_\star:=\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
 =2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0.          \tag{6}
\]
For one target-pre patient,
\[
 f_u(t)=\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
 -u c_Ta_{\circ,0}\bar h\psi_\circ'(t).                  \tag{7}
\]
The smooth zero extension makes both survival and density continuations identical at and after \(\bar\tau\), so there is no unmatched endpoint or tail mass. The failure-density floor, \(\log x\le x-1\), and equality of total mass give
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
 \le\frac{4c_T^2a_{\circ,0}^2\bar h^2}{c_f}
 \int_0^{\bar\tau}(\psi_\circ')^2.                       \tag{8}
\]
Additivity over the \(K_T\) target-pre records gives \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\|Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})\le C_0K_T^{-1}\). The revalidated Pinsker lemma yields
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le\{C_0/(2K_T)\}^{1/2}=O(K_T^{-1/2}).                  \tag{9}
\]

Finally, for \(u\in\{-,+\}\), let \(E_u\) be simultaneous coverage of \(\theta_u\). Honesty and pair inclusion give \(Q_u(E_u)\ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon\). Transfer of \(E_+\) to the minus law and a union bound imply
\[
 Q_-^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_-\cap E_+)
 \ge1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon-
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_+^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_-^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \ge q_\alpha                                             \tag{10}
\]
for all sufficiently large triples, because \(\varepsilon\le q_\alpha/4\). On the intersection the band contains both curves, so \(w(B)\ge\Delta_\star\). Therefore every honest band satisfies
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge q_\alpha\Delta_\star=:c_\star>0.                   \tag{11}
\]
The deterministic continuous band \([0,1]\) has coverage one and width one. This proves the minimax order-one boundary and all stated structural and finite-grid qualifications. \(\square\)
