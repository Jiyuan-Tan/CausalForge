\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\). At every sufficiently large triple, \(\mathrm{lem:uniform\mbox{-}linearization}\) gives
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}
 \le\alpha-\varepsilon/4,
 \qquad
 \sup_PP\{\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>\omega_r/2\}
 \le\varepsilon/4,                                      \tag{1}
\]
and \(\|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\). A union bound and the gridwise expansion yield
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\left\{
 \max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 \le c_r^\xi+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+\omega_r
 \right\}\ge1-\alpha.                                  \tag{2}
\]

Let \(q=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\). The interpolation proposition and the canonical grid give
\[
 \|\widehat\theta_r-\theta\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}
 \le\max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 +c_{\mathrm{int}}h^q,
 \qquad h^q\le\rho_r.                                   \tag{3}
\]
Projection onto \([0,1]\) cannot increase distance from \(\theta(g)\in[0,1]\). Both the rank-one and fallback branches are continuous because the construction interpolates the pooled product-limit curve, and linear interpolation of the endpoint curves preserves the grid enclosure up to (3). The radius \(A_r\) is exactly the right side of (2) plus the last term in (3). Hence
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)
 \le\theta(t)\le\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
 \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\}\ge1-\alpha.       \tag{4}
\]
This is stronger than \(1-\alpha-\varepsilon\), proving \(\widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r\).

Clipping endpoints can only reduce width, so
\[
 w(\widehat B_r)
 \le2c_r^\xi+2\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)
 +2\omega_r+2c_{\mathrm{int}}h^q.                        \tag{5}
\]
Write \(\widetilde c_r^\xi\) for the unguarded generalized-inverse multiplier quantile. For all sufficiently large triples, its conditional tail probability is at least \(\alpha/2\). Conditional Markov inequality gives
\[
 \widetilde c_r^\xi
 \le(2/\alpha)^{1/2}
 \{E_\xi\|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}^2\}^{1/2}. \tag{6}
\]
The two patient-martingale helpers give
\[
 \sup_PE_PE_\xi\|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_\infty^2\le C_Z\rho_r^2. \tag{7}
\]
Since \(c_r^\xi=\max\{\widetilde c_r^\xi,d_r(\alpha)\}\le\widetilde c_r^\xi+d_r(\alpha)\), Jensen's inequality and (6)--(7) show
\[
 \sup_PE_Pc_r^\xi\le C\rho_r.                            \tag{8}
\]
The linearization lemma gives \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\) uniformly on every three-dimensional tail, and (3) gives \(h^q\le\rho_r\). Taking expectations in (5) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\{\rho_r+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\}
 =C\bar\rho_r.                                           \tag{9}
\]
At rank one, \(\bar\rho_1=\rho_1=(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\). For fixed \(r\ge2\), (9) is \(O(\rho_r)\) only when \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\). No relative-growth comparison, multiplier-only coverage theorem, or unrestricted removal of \(\eta_D\) has been used. \(\square\)
