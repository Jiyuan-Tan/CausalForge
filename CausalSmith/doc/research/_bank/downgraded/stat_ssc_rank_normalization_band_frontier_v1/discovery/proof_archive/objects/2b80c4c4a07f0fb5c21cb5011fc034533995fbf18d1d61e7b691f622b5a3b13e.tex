\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(r\) and a sufficiently large evaluated triple.  By
\(\mathrm{thm:normalization\mbox{-}preserving\mbox{-}converse}\), every
admissible band has maximal expected width at least \(c\rho_r\).  Taking
the infimum over the same per-triple honesty class in
\(\mathrm{def:minimax\mbox{-}width}\) gives
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\ge c\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).         \tag{1}
\]
By \(\mathrm{thm:attaining\mbox{-}band}\), the explicit band
\(\widehat B_r\) is in that honesty class and obeys
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\bar\rho_r.                                           \tag{2}
\]
Using \(\widehat B_r\) as a single data-dependent witness in the minimax
infimum yields
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\le C\bar\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).     \tag{3}
\]
At rank one,
\(\bar\rho_1=\rho_1=(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\), so (1)--(3) prove
\[
 W_1^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\asymp(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}.            \tag{4}
\]
For fixed \(r\ge2\), (1)--(3) give the unrestricted bracket
\[
 c\{K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\}
 \le W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \le C\{K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}+\eta_D(K_D)\}.           \tag{5}
\]
Whenever \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\), the two sides of (5) match and hence
\(W_r^*\asymp\rho_r\).  The constants and the common lower-tail cutoff are
uniform over every three-dimensional tail because the two input theorems
have that property.  No comparison among the relative growth rates has
been introduced.  The infimum on each line is the per-triple infimum from
\(\mathrm{def:minimax\mbox{-}width}\). \(\square\)
