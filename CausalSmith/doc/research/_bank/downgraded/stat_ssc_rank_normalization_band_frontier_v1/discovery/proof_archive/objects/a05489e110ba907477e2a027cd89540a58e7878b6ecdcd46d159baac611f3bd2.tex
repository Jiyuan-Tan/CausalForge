Fix an observed control cell or deterministic half-fold containing \(k\) patients, put \(h_0=s_-g_-\), \(\ell_k=\max\{1,kh_0/8\}\), and let \(\widehat S_k^\dagger\) be the stabilized product-limit curve in the corrected \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\). For all sufficiently large \(k\): (i) \(\widehat S_k^\dagger\) equals the ordinary Kaplan--Meier curve on \(\{Y_k(\bar\tau)\ge\ell_k\}\); (ii)
\[
 P\{Y_k(\bar\tau)<kh_0/8\}
 \le \exp\{-49h_0k/128\}\le e^{-c_0k},
 \qquad c_0=h_0/3;
\]
(iii) changing one patient record changes the entire interpolated stabilized curve by at most \(C/k\), deterministically on every dataset; (iv) every individual observed multiplier contribution has supremum norm at most \(C/k\), deterministically; and (v), with \(\widetilde S^0=\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}S^0\), there are independent mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes \(\varphi_{k,j}\), a deterministic bias \(b_k^\dagger\), and a centered remainder \(q_k^\dagger\) such that
\[
 \widehat S_k^\dagger-\widetilde S^0
 =k^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^k\varphi_{k,j}+b_k^\dagger+q_k^\dagger,
 \qquad
 \|b_k^\dagger\|_\infty\le Ce^{-c_0k},
 \qquad
 E\|q_k^\dagger\|_\infty^2\le Ck^{-2}.
\]
For independent donor cells, their centered rare-cell contributions average with second moment at most \(CN_0^{-1}e^{-c_0k}\le C(N_0k^2)^{-1}\). All assertions allow ties and all realized risk sets; no all-cells-good event is imposed.
