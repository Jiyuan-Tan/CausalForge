\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(c_T=K_T^{-1}\), let \(\widetilde z_m\) be the unoriented donor scores in the weak-pair construction, and put \(q_\alpha=1/2-\alpha>0\).

Every \(Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\) has
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_m\zeta_{m,Q}=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_m\zeta_{m,Q}^2=1.                 \tag{1}
\]
The embedding proposition with \(d=1\) shows that the donor loading matrix with columns \((1,\zeta_{m,Q})^\top\) has full row rank and that \((1,\zeta_{\star,Q})^\top\) is in its span. Survival normalization and donor centering imply \(s_{p,Q}(0)=1\) and \(b_{p,Q}(0)=0\). Donor visibility makes some \(b_{p,Q}(t)\ne0\), so the total factor rank is exactly two. The cited scope lemma places these fixed-design laws inside the factorization-and-span part of Han--Shah Theorem~1.

The centered arithmetic progression in the weak pair satisfies
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_m\widetilde z_m=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_m\widetilde z_m^2=1.               \tag{2}
\]
Multiplication of factors and loadings by \(\varsigma_T\) preserves every curve and enforces the canonical sign convention. The witness-compatibility inequalities give survival support in \([s_-,1]\), normalization, monotonicity, the density floor, the \(C^\beta\) hazard bound, and all loading and factor envelopes. Infinite censoring gives \(G=1\), while independence and consistency hold by construction. Because the donor score has empirical second moment one,
\[
 \sigma_T=\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\}^{1/2}
 \|\psi_\circ\|_2,
 \qquad H_{0,T}=K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2. \tag{3}
\]
Hence \(\sigma_T\ge|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0\) while \(H_{0,T}\to0\), proving pair inclusion, exact rank two, donor visibility, and degenerating pre-period Gram.

For a finite pre-period grid put \(a=(e^{-\lambda_\circ t_\ell})_\ell\) and \(q=(\psi_\circ(t_\ell))_\ell\). The pre-period donor matrix is
\[
 M_{0,T}=a\mathbf1^\top+c_Ta_{\circ,0}q\widetilde z^\top. \tag{4}
\]
If \(a\) and \(q\) are linearly independent, (4) has rank two and
\[
 s_1(M_{0,T})\ge\sqrt{N_0}\|a\|_2,
 \qquad s_2(M_{0,T})\le c_T|a_{\circ,0}|\sqrt{N_0}\|q\|_2. \tag{5}
\]
Their ratio is at least a fixed positive constant times \(K_T\), so Han--Shah Assumption~7 fails. If \(q=\kappa a\ne0\), the pre-period and post-period latent-factor directions are not collinear for all large \(K_T\), so Assumption~8 fails. If \(q=0\), the finite grid sees none of the perturbation. These cases are exhaustive and show why the result does not contradict Han--Shah Theorem~2.

The observed laws differ only through the \(K_T\) target-pre failures, while
\[
 \theta_u(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+u\bar h a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad
 \Delta_\star=2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0. \tag{6}
\]
For one target-pre patient,
\[
 f_u(t)=\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
 -u c_Ta_{\circ,0}\bar h\psi_\circ'(t).                \tag{7}
\]
The smooth zero extension gives equal endpoint survival and identical continuation densities. The density floor and \(\log x\le x-1\) give
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
 \le\frac{4c_T^2a_{\circ,0}^2\bar h^2}{c_f}
 \int_0^{\bar\tau}(\psi_\circ')^2.                     \tag{8}
\]
Additivity over patients and the revalidated Pinsker lemma yield
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le\{C_0/(2K_T)\}^{1/2}=O(K_T^{-1/2}).                 \tag{9}
\]
Let \(E_u\) be simultaneous coverage of \(\theta_u\). Honesty, total-variation transfer, and a union bound give, for all sufficiently large triples,
\[
 Q_-^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_-\cap E_+)
 \ge1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon-
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_+^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_-^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \ge q_\alpha.                                          \tag{10}
\]
On the intersection \(w(B)\ge\Delta_\star\), so
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge q_\alpha\Delta_\star=:c_\star>0.                 \tag{11}
\]
The deterministic band \([0,1]\) has coverage one and width one. This proves the order-one boundary. The lower-bound subclass transfers converses upward, but the functional-PCR upper over \(\mathfrak P_2\) does not transfer to this larger weak-pre-Gram class. \(\square\)
