\textbf{Proof.}
The theorem \(\mathrm{thm:known\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}weak\mbox{-}pre\mbox{-}gram\mbox{-}boundary}\) constructs, at every sufficiently large triple, two laws
\[
 \{Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\}
 \subset\mathfrak Q_{\le2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)                  \tag{1}
\]
with a fixed target separation and proves that every band honest over those two laws has maximal expected width at least a fixed \(c_\star>0\).  A band satisfying the displayed honesty condition of the present theorem is honest under each law in (1).  Moreover,
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\le2}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge\max_{u\in\{-1,+1\}}E_{Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}}w(B)
 \ge c_\star.                                             \tag{2}
\]
This proves the lower bound.  Conversely, \(B^{\mathrm{lo}}\equiv0\) and \(B^{\mathrm{up}}\equiv1\) cover every survival curve and have width one, so the closure minimax order is exactly one.

The obstruction in (2) already has known exact total rank two and, by the primary theorem, its joint donor contrast singular value is at least \(|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0\).  Thus unknown-rank selection is not the source of the failure; it is a corollary of weak pre-period identification.  To make the oracle comparison without strengthening the unrestricted higher-rank result, consider for example the diagonal subregime \(N_0=K_T=K_D=k\).  There \(\eta_D(k)=O(\rho_2(k,k,k))\), so \(\mathrm{thm:sharp\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}frontier}\) gives the vanishing known-rank widths \(W_1^*\asymp\rho_1\) and \(W_2^*\asymp\rho_2\), while (2) stays bounded below.  Hence no single band honest over the no-common-pre-Gram closure can attain both known-rank oracle rates.  Outside the matching subregime, only the bracket in the sharp-frontier theorem is used and no unrestricted equivalence is claimed. \(\square\)
