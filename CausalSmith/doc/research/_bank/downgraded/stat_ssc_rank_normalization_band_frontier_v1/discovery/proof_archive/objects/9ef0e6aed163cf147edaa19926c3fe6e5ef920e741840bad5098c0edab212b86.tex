At \(r=1\), \(d=0\), so every contrast vector and loading product in the FPCR formula is the empty sum and no target-pre record can enter \(\widehat\theta_1\).  If \(\widehat s_1\) is defined as the pooled donor-post Kaplan--Meier curve, the estimator and its patient-multiplier derivative are exactly the classical pooled construction on the grid, followed by the declared interpolation rule.  The frozen definition does not actually specify pooled estimation: averaging the separately computed cellwise Kaplan--Meier curves is generally different from pooled Kaplan--Meier when censoring laws vary by donor.  The proposed construction makes the pooled branch explicit.
