\textbf{Proof.}
The corrected attaining theorem supplies one band in the per-triple honesty class with worst-case width at most \(C\bar\rho_r\).  Taking the infimum in \(\mathrm{def:minimax\mbox{-}width}\) gives
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\le C\bar\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).
\]
The normalization-preserving converse gives
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\ge c\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).
\]
At rank one, \(\bar\rho_1=\rho_1=(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\), so the bounds match.  At every fixed rank \(r\ge2\), they also match whenever \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\).  No relative-growth restriction was used for the bracket itself. \(\square\)
