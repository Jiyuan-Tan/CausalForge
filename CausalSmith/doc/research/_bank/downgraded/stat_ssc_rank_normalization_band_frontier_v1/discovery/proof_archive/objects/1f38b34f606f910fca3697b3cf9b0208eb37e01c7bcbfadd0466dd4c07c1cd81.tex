Put \(q=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\), \(M_r=\lceil\bar\tau\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)^{-1/q}\rceil\), and
\[
 \mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}
 =\left\{g_\ell=\frac{\ell\bar\tau}{M_r}:0\le\ell\le M_r\right\}.
\]
Define the adjacent-knot mesh by
\[
 h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}=\max_{0\le\ell<M_r}(g_{\ell+1}-g_\ell)
 =\frac{\bar\tau}{M_r}.
\]
Use piecewise-linear interpolation between adjacent knots. Then \(h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^q\le\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) and \(|\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}|=M_r+1\le2+\bar\tau\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)^{-1/q}\).
