There are constants \(a_0<\infty\) and \(c_{\mathrm{low}}>0\) such that, at every evaluated triple with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\), every \(B\in\mathcal H_1(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\) satisfies \[\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_1(N_0,K_T,K_D)}E_P[w(B)]\ge c_{\mathrm{low}}(N_0K_D)^{-1/2},\] and, for every fixed \(r\ge2\), every \(B\in\mathcal H_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\) satisfies \[\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)}E_P[w(B)]\ge c_{\mathrm{low}}\{K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\}.\] The donor channel uses a normalization-preserving post-donor hazard pair of size \((N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\). The target channel embeds the explicit rank-two pair from \(\mathrm{lem:rank-two-pair}\) and adds fixed orthogonal contrast directions for \(r>2\). All pairs preserve valid survival laws, endpoint normalization, exact primitive rank, the nonzero contrast-spectrum and pre-contrast Gram bounds, and uniformly bounded total variation.
