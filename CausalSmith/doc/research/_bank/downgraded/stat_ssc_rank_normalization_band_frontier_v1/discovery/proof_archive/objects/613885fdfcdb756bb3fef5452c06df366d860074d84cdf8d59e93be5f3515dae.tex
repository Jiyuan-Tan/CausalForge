\textbf{Proof.}
Fix the exact rank \(r\) and take an evaluated triple above the larger of the
two lower-tail cutoffs in
\(\mathrm{thm:attaining\mbox{-}band}\) and
\(\mathrm{thm:normalization\mbox{-}preserving\mbox{-}converse}\).  Write
\(n_D=N_0K_D\).  The converse theorem states that every
\(B\in\mathcal H_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\) satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_1(N_0,K_T,K_D)}E_P\{w(B)\}
 \ge c_1n_D^{-1/2}                                          \tag{1}
\]
when \(r=1\), and, for each fixed \(r\ge2\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)}E_P\{w(B)\}
 \ge c_r\{K_T^{-1/2}+n_D^{-1/2}\}
 =c_r\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).                                  \tag{2}
\]
Here and below the constants may depend on the fixed rank and fixed model
constants, but not on the three sample sizes.  Taking the infimum of (1) or
(2) over the same per-triple admissible class appearing in
\(\mathrm{def:minimax\mbox{-}width}\) gives
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \ge c_r\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).                               \tag{3}
\]

For the reverse inequality, the attaining theorem supplies one explicit,
data-dependent band \(\widehat B_r\), common to all laws at the evaluated
triple, such that
\[
 \widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha),
 \qquad
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)}
 E_P\{w(\widehat B_r)\}
 \le C_r\bar\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).                           \tag{4}
\]
Using this single band as a candidate in the infimum defining \(W_r^*\)
yields
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \le C_r\bar\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).                           \tag{5}
\]
This is a genuine minimax upper bound because \(\widehat B_r\) is a
measurable function of the observed sample and does not depend on the
unknown member \(P\).

At rank one, the definitions give
\[
 \bar\rho_1=\rho_1=(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}.
\]
Thus (3)--(5) imply
\[
 W_1^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \asymp(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}.                                    \tag{6}
\]
For every fixed \(r\ge2\), the same inequalities give the unrestricted
bracket
\[
 c_r\{K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\}
 \le W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \le C_r\{K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}+\eta_D(K_D)\}.         \tag{7}
\]
If \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D))\) on a subregime, then
\(\bar\rho_r=O(\rho_r)\), so (7) reduces to
\(W_r^*\asymp\rho_r\) there.  Without that condition, (7), rather than a
stronger equivalence, is the conclusion.

Both input theorems hold uniformly once
\(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\) exceeds a fixed cutoff, and neither imposes a
comparison among the growth rates of \(N_0\), \(K_T\), and \(K_D\).
Taking the larger cutoff therefore proves all asserted uniformity claims.
Every infimum used above is the coverage-constrained infimum at the same
evaluated triple, exactly as required by
\(\mathrm{def:minimax\mbox{-}width}\). \(\square\)
