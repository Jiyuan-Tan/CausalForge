Put \(d=r-1\).  For \(d\ge1\), let \(z_m^{(N,d)}\) be obtained, for each \(N\ge2d+1\), by empirical Gram--Schmidt from the explicitly centered columns
\[
 q_{k,m}^{(N)}=\{m-(N+1)/2\}^k-N^{-1}\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}\{\ell-(N+1)/2\}^k,
 \qquad 1\le k\le d,
\]
and define \(R_d=\sup_{N\ge2d+1}\max_{1\le m\le N}\|z_m^{(N,d)}\|_2\), with \(R_0=0\).  Let \(\Phi_d\) be the deterministic \(L^2(\mathcal I)\)-orthonormalization of \(\psi_\circ,t\psi_\circ,\ldots,t^{d-1}\psi_\circ\), with first coordinate \(\psi_\circ/\|\psi_\circ\|_2\), and put \(A_{p,d}=a_{\circ,p}\|\psi_\circ\|_2\Phi_d\).  The fixed primitive constants obey
\[
 g_-\le1,
 \qquad
 R_d\le M_z,
 \qquad
 \gamma_-^2\le(a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2)\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\le\gamma_+^2,
 \qquad
 \kappa_-\le a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\le\kappa_+,
\]
\[
 \max_{p\in\{0,1\}}\sup_{t\in\mathcal I}\|A_{p,d}(t)\|_2\le M_b,
 \qquad
 2|a_{\circ,0}|h_\circ c_f^{-1/2}
 \left\{\int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2dt\right\}^{1/2}
 \le\frac34(1/2-\alpha).
\]
Writing \(v_\circ=|a_{\circ,1}|/4\), for every \(p\in\{0,1\}\), every \(x\in\mathbb R^d\) with \(\|x\|_2\le R_d\), every \(|v|\le v_\circ\), and every \(t\in\mathcal I\), the curve
\[
 \ell_{p,x,v}(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+A_{p,d}(t)^\top x+mathbf 1\{p=1\}v\psi_\circ(t)
\]
satisfies
\[
 s_-\le\ell_{p,x,v}(t)\le1,
 \qquad
 -\partial_t\ell_{p,x,v}(t)\ge0,
 \qquad
 \inf_{t\in\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')}[-\partial_t\ell_{p,x,v}(t)]\ge c_f,
 \qquad
 \left\|-\frac{\partial_t\ell_{p,x,v}}{\ell_{p,x,v}}\right\|_{C^\beta(\mathcal I)}\le L.
\]
For \(d=0\), the same four inequalities hold with the empty factor term, \(x=0\), and every \(|v|\le v_\circ\).
