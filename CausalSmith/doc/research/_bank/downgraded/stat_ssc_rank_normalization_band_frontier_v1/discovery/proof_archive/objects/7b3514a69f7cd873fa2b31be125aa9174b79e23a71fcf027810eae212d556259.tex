For each sample-size triple, define
\[
\mathfrak Q_{\leq 2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)
=\left\{Q_{N_0,K_T,K_D}:Q_{N_0,K_T,K_D}
\text{ has the properties below}\right\}.
\]
Every member satisfies
\(\mathrm{ass:control\mbox{-}consistency}\),
\(\mathrm{ass:patient\mbox{-}independence}\),
\(\mathrm{ass:independent\mbox{-}censoring}\),
\(\mathrm{ass:censor\mbox{-}overlap}\),
\(\mathrm{ass:event\mbox{-}overlap}\), and
\(\mathrm{ass:hazard\mbox{-}smoothness}\), with the common constants appearing in
those assumptions.  Set, canonically,
\[
s_{p,Q}(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}S^0_{p,m}(t).
\]
There is a contrast indicator \(e_Q\in\{0,1\}\).  If \(e_Q=0\), then
\[
S^0_{p,m}(t)=s_{p,Q}(t)
\quad\text{for all }p\in\{0,1\},\ m\in\mathcal M,\ t\in\mathcal I.
\]
If \(e_Q=1\), there are scalar continuous factor curves \(b_{p,Q}\) and scalar
loadings \(\zeta_{m,Q}\) such that
\[
S^0_{p,m}(t)=s_{p,Q}(t)+b_{p,Q}(t)\zeta_{m,Q},
\quad
N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}=0,
\quad
N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}^2=1.
\]
The sign is selected canonically by requiring the first nonzero value in a fixed
countable dense sequence of continuous linear probes of
\((b_{0,Q},b_{1,Q})\) to be positive.  The invariant upper and loading bounds are
\[
0<\int_{\mathcal I}\{b_{0,Q}(t)^2+b_{1,Q}(t)^2\}\,dt\leq\gamma_+^2,
\qquad
0<\int_{\mathcal I}b_{0,Q}(t)^2\,dt\leq\kappa_+,
\]
\[
\max_{p\in\{0,1\}}\sup_{t\in\mathcal I}|b_{p,Q}(t)|\leq M_b,
\qquad
\max_{m\in\mathcal D}|\zeta_{m,Q}|\leq M_z,
\qquad
|\zeta_{\star,Q}|\leq M_\star.
\]
Thus every heterogeneous member has a unique target-pre coefficient, but the
definition imposes no common positive lower bound on its scalar pre-period Gram
\(\int_{\mathcal I}b_{0,Q}^2\).  All rank labels in this definition refer to the
separately normalized contrast indicator \(e_Q\), not to the fixed-rank law symbol
\(\mathfrak P_r\).
