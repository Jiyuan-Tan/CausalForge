\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), \(q_\beta=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\), and
\(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).  By the corrected
canonical grid,
\[
 h^{q_\beta}\le\rho_r,
 \qquad |\mathcal G|\le2+\bar\tau\rho_r^{-1/q_\beta}.       \tag{1}
\]
The interpolated-population stability lemma and the canonical target
coordinate lemma imply
\[
 \theta^{\mathrm{pop}}_{r,\mathcal G}
 =\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\theta,
 \qquad
 \theta^{\mathrm{pop}}_{r,\mathcal G}(g)=\theta(g)
 \quad(g\in\mathcal G).                                    \tag{2}
\]
They also place the interpolated population operator and pre-Gram a fixed
distance inside the three regular thresholds.

If \(r=1\), the normalization-collapse lemma makes every donor-post failure
hazard equal while allowing donor-specific censoring.  Apply
\(\mathrm{lem:pooled\mbox{-}noniid\mbox{-}km\mbox{-}linearization}\) to the
pooled, then interpolated, product-limit branch.  Define
\[
 \phi_{D,1,m,j}=\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}},
 \qquad \phi_{T,j}=0,                                      \tag{3}
\]
and assign the exponentially small pooled exhaustion bias to \(Q_1\).
That lemma gives the required expansion, with \(\mathfrak b_1=0\), and
\[
 E\|Q_1\|_{infty,\mathcal G}^2
 \le C\{n_D^{-1}+e^{-c_0n_D}\}^2.                         \tag{4}
\]
Its explicit pooled multiplier is precisely the corrected rank-one
multiplier in the band definition.

If \(r\ge2\), apply
\(\mathrm{lem:gridwise\mbox{-}fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}transfer}\).
The derivative of the functional-PCR map applied to each target-pre
martingale defines \(\phi_{T,j}\), and the derivative applied to each
donor-period martingale defines \(\phi_{D,p,m,j}\), including the
deterministic odd--even fold weights.  Patient independence makes the two
arrays mutually independent and mean zero.  The lemma yields, simultaneously
at all grid knots,
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta
 =K_T^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_T}\phi_{T,j}
  +n_D^{-1}\sum_{p,m,j}\phi_{D,p,m,j}
  +\mathfrak b_r+Q_r,                                      \tag{5}
\]
\[
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le\eta_D(K_D),
 \qquad E\|Q_r\|_{infty,\mathcal G}^2\le Cv_r^2.         \tag{6}
\]
Equations (3)--(6) are the asserted expansion in both rank regimes.

The rank-one pooled multiplier lemma and the higher-rank gridwise transfer
lemma both give, with the convention that the target channel is absent at
rank one,
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Z_r\|_{infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+x\}
 \le1-q_{N_0,K_T,K_D}+C\{\chi_r/x+v_r^2/x^2\},            \tag{7}
\]
as well as
\[
 \sup_PE\|Q_r\|_{infty,\mathcal G}^2\le Cv_r^2.          \tag{8}
\]
Here \(\chi_r\) and \(v_r\) are exactly the quantities displayed in the
definition of \(\omega_r\).  Since
\[
 \omega_r=C_\omega\{\varepsilon^{-1}\chi_r
                    +\varepsilon^{-1/2}v_r\},              \tag{9}
\]
Markov's inequality in (8), with a sufficiently large fixed \(C_\omega\),
gives
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Q_r\|_{infty,\mathcal G}>\omega_r/2\}
 \le\varepsilon/4.                                         \tag{10}
\]
For sufficiently large triples,
\(q_{N_0,K_T,K_D}=1-\alpha+\varepsilon/2\).  Substituting
\(x=\omega_r/2\) into (7) and enlarging \(C_\omega\) once more gives
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Z_r\|_{infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}
 \le\alpha-\varepsilon/4.                                 \tag{11}
\]
The ramp proof behind (7) remains valid at variance-zero coordinates and
therefore includes the normalization point \(t=0\).

It remains to check the asserted uniform small order.  Let
\(a=\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\).  The reciprocal of \(\varepsilon\) grows no
faster than an iterated logarithm of \(a\).  Since
\[
 \frac{K_T^{-1/6}\log^2(e+K_T)}{\varepsilon}\to0,
 \qquad
 \frac{n_D^{-1/6}\log^2(e+n_D)}{\varepsilon}\to0,         \tag{12}
\]
and
\[
 \frac{K_T^{-1/2}}{\sqrt\varepsilon}\to0,
 \quad
 \frac{K_D^{-1/2}}{\sqrt\varepsilon}\to0,
 \quad
 \frac{n_D^{-1/2}}{\sqrt\varepsilon}\to0,
 \quad
 \frac{\eta_D(K_D)}{\varepsilon}\to0,                    \tag{13}
\]
each channel in (9), divided by its corresponding summand of \(\rho_r\),
tends to zero.  Exponential terms do likewise.  Negative powers and
exponentials dominate an iterated logarithm uniformly above \(a\), so
(12)--(13) are uniform on every three-dimensional tail and do not compare
\(K_T\) with \(n_D\).  Thus \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\).  Equation (2) explains
why the internal \(L^2\) interpolation perturbation is used only for
spectrum and Gram stability, while the off-grid target discrepancy is left
for the final continuous-time interpolation allowance. \(\square\)
