Set \(x_m=\{m-(N_0+1)/2\}/\sqrt{(N_0^2-1)/12}\), so \(N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}x_m=0\), \(N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}x_m^2=1\), and \(\max_m|x_m|<\sqrt 3\). Choose the declared fixed constants \(a_{\circ,0},a_{\circ,1},\lambda_\circ,h_\circ\) in the interior of the class bounds, with \(h_\circ\) small enough for the displayed density floor. For \(u\in\{-1,+1\}\), put \(s_p(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}\), \(b_p(t)=a_{\circ,p}\psi_\circ(t)\), \(z_m=x_m\) for donors, and \(z_\star^{(u)}=u h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\). Define \(S_{p,m}^{(u)}(t)=s_p(t)+b_p(t)z_m\) on \(\mathcal I\), continue it after \(\bar\tau\) by \(S_{p,m}^{(u)}(\bar\tau)e^{-\lambda_\circ(t-\bar\tau)}\), and take \(C_{p,m,j}=\infty\). The constants are chosen so the densities \(f_{p,m}^{(u)}=-\partial_tS_{p,m}^{(u)}\) obey \(f_{p,m}^{(u)}\ge c_f\) on the support of \(\psi_\circ'\). The resulting observed-data laws are \(P_u\).
