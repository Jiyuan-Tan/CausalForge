Begin with the compatible all-unit affine representation supplied by \(\mathrm{ass:normalized\mbox{-}factor\mbox{-}model}\), and set \(s_p(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}S^0_{p,m}(t)\). Center its donor loadings. Because \(\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal C_D)=d\), both the compatible joint-period factor map and the centered donor loading matrix have rank \(d\). Their donor covariance is positive definite; covariance-normalize the loadings and apply the inverse normalization to the factors. Write the singular-value decomposition of the resulting primitive centered operator as
\[
 \mathcal C_D=\sum_{k=1}^d\sigma_k u_k\otimes v_k,
\]
ordered by decreasing \(\sigma_k\) with deterministic sign and tie-breaking rules, and define
\[
 z_{m,k}=\sqrt{N_0}v_{k,m},
 \qquad b_{p,k}(t)=\sigma_k u_{k,p}(t).
\]
Then \(N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m=0\) and \(N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_mz_m^\top=I_d\). The covariance-normalized compatible coordinates and these donor-SVD coordinates differ by one common orthogonal matrix. Transport the compatible target coordinate through that orthogonal matrix. Equivalently, target-pre compatibility gives \(S^0_{0,\star}-s_0\in\operatorname{span}\{b_{0,1},\ldots,b_{0,d}\}\), and the positive canonical pre-period Gram
\[
 H_0=\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)b_0(t)^\top\,dt
\]
makes the transported coordinate unique, with
\[
 z_\star=H_0^{-1}\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)\{S^0_{0,\star}(t)-s_0(t)\}\,dt.
\]
The compatible all-unit representation then gives \(S^0_{p,m}(t)=s_p(t)+b_p(t)^\top z_m\) for both periods and every unit, including \(m=\star\). Survival normalization gives \(s_p(0)=1\) and \(b_p(0)=0\). When \(d=0\), rank compatibility forces every contrast to vanish and all vectors, sums, and Gram matrices use the zero-dimensional convention.
