For every fixed declared pair \(\gamma_->0\) and \(\kappa_->0\), the prior cross-fold perturbation bound gives a deterministic threshold \(\tau_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\downarrow0\) with
\[
 (N_0K_D)^{-1/2}+N_0^{-1/2}K_D^{-1}+\eta_D(K_D)
 =o(\tau_{N_0,K_T,K_D})
\]
and \(\tau_{N_0,K_T,K_D}<\min\{\gamma_-^2,\kappa_-\}/4\) eventually.  Hence the thresholded spectrum and pre-Gram select the fixed rank-two oracle with error chargeable to \(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\); this is pointwise in the fixed strength constants, not uniform over their triangular union.

There is also a normalization-preserving obstruction on the proposed triangular closure.  Use one bounded centered donor loading column with empirical Gram one, take \(b_{1,n}=A_{1,1}\), \(b_{0,n}=c_nA_{0,1}\), and take two target loadings \(z_{\star}^{(+)}=\bar h\) and \(z_{\star}^{(-)}=-\bar h\), where \(\bar h>0\) is fixed inside the loading and survival interior supplied by \(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\).  The two laws have identical donor distributions and differ only in the target-pre cohort.  Their pre-Gram is \(c_n^2a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2>0\), their joint contrast singular value stays bounded away from zero because the post factor is fixed, and their target separation is the fixed number
\[
 2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty.
\]
The same density calculation as in \(\mathrm{lem:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}pair}\) gives
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_{n,+}\|P_{n,-})
 \le C K_Tc_n^2.
\]
Choosing \(c_n=o(K_T^{-1/2})\) makes total variation tend to zero.  Therefore simultaneous honesty at the two laws forces, by the coverage-intersection argument, a fixed positive expected width at at least one law.  Thus a singular-value test may detect donor rank perfectly while the target loading remains unlearnable, and no band can have the shrinking fixed-strength rank-two width uniformly over a closure with no common pre-Gram lower bound.

Finally, the literal metric \(D_\theta(P,\mathfrak P_1)=\inf_{Q\in\mathfrak P_1}\|\theta(P)-\theta(Q)\|_\infty\) is degenerate on the baseline-rich frozen model: for the displayed alternatives one can choose a homogeneous law \(Q\) whose post survival curve equals \(\theta(P)\), giving \(D_\theta(P,\mathfrak P_1)=0\).  A nontrivial positive testing theorem therefore needs a reviewed nuisance-fiber restriction, such as comparison only with rank-one laws sharing the donor-period mean curves, or an explicitly anchored target distance.  A second-singular-value distance cannot repair this defect because it is positive for target-invisible donor directions.
