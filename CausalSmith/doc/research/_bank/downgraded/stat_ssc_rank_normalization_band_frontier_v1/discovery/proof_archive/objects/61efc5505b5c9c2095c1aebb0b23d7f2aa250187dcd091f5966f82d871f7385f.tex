 \langle u_p^{\mathrm{HS}}(t),v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}\rangle
 =s_p(t)+b_p(t)^\top z_m=S^0_{p,m}(t).                   \tag{1}

Let \(V_D=[v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}:m\in\mathcal D]\). Donor centering and the loading-Gram identity give
\[
 \frac1{N_0}V_DV_D^\top
 =\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&I_d\end{pmatrix}=I_r.           \tag{2}
\]
Thus \(V_D\) has row rank \(r\), its columns span \(\mathbb R^r\), and \(v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}}\) is in their span. An explicit coefficient vector is
\[
 a_\star=V_D^\top(V_DV_D^\top)^{-1}v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}},
 \qquad V_Da_\star=v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}}.              \tag{3}
\]
