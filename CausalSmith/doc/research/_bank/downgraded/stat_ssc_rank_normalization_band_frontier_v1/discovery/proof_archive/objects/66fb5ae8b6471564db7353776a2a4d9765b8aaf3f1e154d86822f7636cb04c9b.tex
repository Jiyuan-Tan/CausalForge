Put \(p_{\mathcal G}=2|\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}|\), \(n_D=N_0K_D\), and
\[
 a_{r,n}=n_D^{-1/2}+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}
 \left\{K_T^{-1/2}+K_D^{-1/2}+\eta_D(K_D)
 +e^{-c_0K_T/2}+e^{-c_0n_D/2}\right\},
 \qquad c_0=s_-g_-/3.
\]
Let \(\Sigma_r\) be the covariance kernel of the oracle linear process \(Z_r\), let \(\widehat\Sigma_r\) be the conditional covariance kernel of \(\widehat Z_r^\xi\), and let \(d_r\) and \(\widehat d_r\) be the corresponding Gaussian canonical semimetrics. There is a measurable event \(\mathcal E_{r,n}\) such that, uniformly over \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\),
\[
 P(\mathcal E_{r,n}^c)\le C a_{r,n}^{1/2},
 \qquad
 \|\widehat\Sigma_r-\Sigma_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G^2}
 \le C\rho_r^2a_{r,n}^{1/2}
 \quad\hbox{on }\mathcal E_{r,n},
\]
and, on the same event, for all distinct \(g,g'\in\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\),
\[
 d_r(g,g')+\widehat d_r(g,g')
 \le C\rho_r|g-g'|^{1/2}.
\]
Moreover, for every \(u\in[h_{N_0,K_T,K_D},\bar\tau]\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\left\{
 \max_{\substack{g,g'\in\mathcal G\\|g-g'|\le u}}
 |Z_r(g)-Z_r(g')|>
 C\rho_r\left[\{u\log(e p_{\mathcal G})\}^{1/2}
 +n_{\mathrm{eff},r}^{-1/2}\log(e p_{\mathcal G})\right]
 \right\}\le p_{\mathcal G}^{-4}.
\]
All constants are independent of the sample-size triple, and no coordinate-variance lower bound is imposed.
