Closed results concern known fixed total rank, independent right censoring, a fixed horizon, smooth hazards, bounded loadings and factors, and per-triple honesty with the declared vanishing tolerance.  Rank one has the sharp donor-patient width.  Higher fixed ranks have the proved \(\rho_r\)-to-\(\bar\rho_r\) bracket and match on the stated subregime.  Unknown-rank adaptation remains a distinct follow-on problem: fixed separated classes admit eventual threshold selection, but uniform adaptation over a closure with no common pre-Gram lower bound is impossible at shrinking width, and the target-metric CKLN program requires an anchored distance before a quantitative positive theorem can be stated.
