\textbf{Proof.}
Here \(r=2\) and \(d=1\).  Empirical Gram--Schmidt in
\(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}lecam\mbox{-}pair}\) gives
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m=0,
 \qquad
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m^2=1,
 \qquad
 \max_m|z_m|<\sqrt3\le R_1\le M_z.                         \tag{1}
\]
Since \(A_{p,1}=a_{\circ,p}\psi_\circ\), the centered donor operator has
exactly one nonzero singular value
\[
 \left\{(a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2)
              \|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\right\}^{1/2},             \tag{2}
\]
and the pre-factor Gram is
\[
 a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2.                          \tag{3}
\]
The numerical inequalities in
\(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) place (2)
and (3) in the required spectrum and Gram intervals and verify the donor
loading and factor-envelope bounds.

Since \(M_\star>0\), for all sufficiently large \(K_T\),
\[
 h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\le\min\{M_\star,R_1\}.                  \tag{4}
\]
Apply the four pointwise compatibility inequalities with \(x=z_m\) for
donors and \(x=z_\star^{(u)}\) for the target.  They give valid
nonincreasing survival curves, event overlap, the failure-density floor on
\(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), and the \(C^\beta\) hazard bound.
Every factor curve and \(\psi_\circ\) vanish at zero, so
\(S_{p,m}^{(u)}(0)=1\).  Taking censoring identically infinite gives
\(G=1\), independent censoring, and censor overlap.  The product construction
gives patient independence and control consistency.  Equations (1)--(4)
verify every remaining defining property of
\(\mathfrak P_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).  Thus
\(P_-,P_+\in\mathfrak P_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).

Only the target loading changes with \(u\), and the target-post control
cohort is not observed.  Hence the observed laws differ only through the
\(K_T\) target-pre records.  The first factor coordinate gives
\[
 \theta_+(t)-\theta_-(t)
 =2a_{\circ,1}h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\psi_\circ(t),               \tag{5}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
 =2|a_{\circ,1}|h_\circ\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty K_T^{-1/2}.    \tag{6}
\]
The two target-pre densities satisfy
\[
 f_{0,\star}^{(+)}(t)-f_{0,\star}^{(-)}(t)
 =-2a_{\circ,0}h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\psi_\circ'(t).             \tag{7}
\]
They agree off \(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), and on that support
\(f_{0,\star}^{(-)}\ge c_f\).  The identity
\(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)=\int f_+\log(f_+/f_-)\), the inequality
\(\log x\le x-1\), and \(\int(f_+-f_-)=0\) imply
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
 \le\int\frac{(f_+-f_-)^2}{f_-}.
\]
Patient independence and (7) consequently give
\[
 \begin{split}
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_+\|P_-)
 &\le K_T\int\frac{(f_{0,\star}^{(+)}-f_{0,\star}^{(-)})^2}
                         {f_{0,\star}^{(-)}}\,dt\\
 &\le\frac{4a_{\circ,0}^2h_\circ^2}{c_f}
       \int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2\,dt.           \tag{8}
 \end{split}
\]
Moreover,
\((\sqrt{x}-\sqrt y)^2\le(x-y)^2/y\) for \(x,y>0\).  Applying this
pointwise in (7), multiplying the Hellinger affinities of the independent
records, and using total variation bounded by the corresponding Hellinger
distance yields
\[
 \|P_+-P_-\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
 \le2|a_{\circ,0}|h_\circ c_f^{-1/2}
       \left\{\int_0^{\bar\tau}(\psi_\circ')^2\right\}^{1/2}. \tag{9}
\]
The last inequality in the explicit compatibility assumption makes (9) a
fixed constant strictly below the amount required in the two-point width
argument.  Exact rank, spectrum, Gram, and normalization were established
in (1)--(4) and do not depend on the sign of the target loading. \(\square\)
