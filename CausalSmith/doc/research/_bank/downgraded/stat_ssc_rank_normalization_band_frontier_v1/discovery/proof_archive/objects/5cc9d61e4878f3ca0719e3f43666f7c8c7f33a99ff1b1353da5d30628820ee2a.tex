Suppose \(r=1\), put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), \(\bar G_D(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}G_{1,m}(t)\), and \(H_D(t)=s_1(t)\bar G_D(t)\). For the interpolated pooled donor-post product-limit estimator in the corrected \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\), there are independent mean-zero processes
\[
 \phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}(t)
 =-s_1(t)\int_0^tH_D(u)^{-1}\,dM_{m,j}(u)
\]
such that, on the inference grid,
\[
 \widehat\theta_1-\theta
 =n_D^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\sum_{j=1}^{K_D}
   \mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}
  +b_{D,n_D}+q_{D,n_D},
\]
where \(\|b_{D,n_D}\|_\infty\le Ce^{-c n_D}\), \(E q_{D,n_D}=0\), and \(E\|q_{D,n_D}\|_\infty^2\le Cn_D^{-2}\), uniformly over the rank-one class although the censoring laws may differ across donors. If \(\widehat Z_1^\xi\) is the explicit pooled multiplier in the corrected band definition and \(J=|\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}|\), then
\[
 \sup_PE_PE_\xi\|\widehat Z_1^\xi\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}^2
 \le Cn_D^{-1},
\]
its conditional covariance consistently estimates the covariance of the first-order sum uniformly on the grid, and, with \(\chi_1=n_D^{-2/3}\log^2(e+n_D)\), every conditional \(q_0\)-quantile \(c_{1,q_0}^\xi\) satisfies the additive random-quantile inequality
\[
 \sup_PP\left\{left\|n_D^{-1}\sum_{m,j}
 \mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}
 \right\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>c_{1,q_0}^\xi+x\right\}
 \le1-q_0+C\chi_1/x,
 \qquad x>0.
\]
The assertion allows variance-zero grid coordinates, including time zero.
