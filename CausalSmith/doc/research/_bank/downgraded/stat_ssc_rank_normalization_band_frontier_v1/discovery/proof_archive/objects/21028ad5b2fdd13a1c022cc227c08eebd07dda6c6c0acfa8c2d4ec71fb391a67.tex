\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\).  We first construct the donor channel.  Keep the
centered polynomial donor scores, all factor curves, all pre-period laws,
and target loading zero fixed, and replace only the post baseline by
\[
 s_1^{(u)}(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
              +u c n_D^{-1/2}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad u\in\{-1,+1\}.                                      \tag{1}
\]
Choose the fixed \(c>0\) sufficiently small as specified below.  For every
sufficiently large triple, \(cn_D^{-1/2}\le v_\circ\).  The pointwise
inequalities in
\(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) verify
survival validity, event overlap, the failure-density floor, and hazard
smoothness.  Adding a common post-period curve does not alter the centered
donor operator, so exact rank and its nonzero singular values are unchanged.
The pre-factor Gram, loading bounds, factor envelope, and endpoint
normalization are likewise unchanged.  With censoring identically infinite,
the censoring, consistency, and sampling conditions hold.

The two donor-channel laws differ only in the \(n_D\) donor-post records,
and their target curves have separation
\[
 \Delta_D=2c\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty n_D^{-1/2}.                \tag{2}
\]
For a donor-post record, the density difference is
\(-2cn_D^{-1/2}\psi_\circ'\).  The density floor on the support of this
difference and the same calculation as in
\(\mathrm{lem:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}pair}\) give
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{D,+}\|Q_{D,-})
 \le 4c^2c_f^{-1}\int_0^{\bar\tau}(\psi_\circ')^2,
 \qquad
 \|Q_{D,+}-Q_{D,-}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\le C_Dc.                 \tag{3}
\]
Choose \(c\) so that the last quantity is at most
\(\frac34(1/2-\alpha)\).  This construction applies at rank one under the
zero-dimensional convention and at every fixed higher rank using the fixed
orthogonal backbone from
\(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}lecam\mbox{-}pair}\).

For the target channel at rank \(r\ge2\), use \(P_{r,+}\) and \(P_{r,-}\)
from that fixed-rank construction.  Centering and empirical
Gram--Schmidt give
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_mz_m=0,
 \qquad
 N_0^{-1}\sum_mz_mz_m^\top=I_d.                             \tag{4}
\]
The joint-period factor columns are orthogonal with common squared norm
\((a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2)\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\), so the centered
operator has exactly \(d=r-1\) nonzero singular values, all in the
permitted interval.  Similarly,
\[
 \int_{\mathcal I}A_{0,d}(t)A_{0,d}(t)^\top\,dt
 =a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2 I_d.                      \tag{5}
\]
The row bound in the compatibility assumption gives donor incoherence.
Since \(M_\star>0\), eventually
\[
 h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\le\min\{M_\star,R_d\}.                  \tag{6}
\]
The remaining class properties follow from the explicit compatibility
inequalities.  Only the first target-loading coordinate changes, so
\[
 \Delta_T
 =2|a_{\circ,1}|h_\circ\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty K_T^{-1/2},    \tag{7}
\]
and the target-pair lemma gives
\[
 \|P_{r,+}-P_{r,-}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
 \le2|a_{\circ,0}|h_\circ c_f^{-1/2}\|\psi_\circ'\|_2
 \le\frac34(1/2-\alpha).                                    \tag{8}
\]
Equations (4)--(6) show explicitly that the added orthogonal directions for
\(r>2\) preserve endpoint normalization, exact primitive rank, spectrum,
and pre-contrast Gram bounds.

We now convert either pair into a width bound.  Let \(Q_-,Q_+\) be one of
the two pairs, let \(\theta_-,\theta_+\) be its targets, and set
\[
 \zeta=\alpha+\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha).
\]
For \(u\in\{-,+\}\), honesty of \(B\) under \(Q_u\) gives
\[
 Q_u\{\theta_u(t)\in B(t)\text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge1-\zeta.                                                 \tag{9}
\]
Transfer the \(+\) event from \(Q_+\) to \(Q_-\) by total variation and
intersect it with the \(-\) event in (9).  This gives
\[
 Q_-\{\theta_-,\theta_+\in B\}
 \ge1-2\zeta-\|Q_--Q_+\|_{\mathrm{TV}}.                     \tag{10}
\]
On the event in (10), at a time maximizing
\(|\theta_+-\theta_-|\), one has
\[
 w(B)\ge\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty.                       \tag{11}
\]
The coverage schedule satisfies
\[
 \varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
 \le\frac14(1/2-\alpha),
 \qquad
 1-2\zeta\ge\frac32(1/2-\alpha).                            \tag{12}
\]
Equations (3), (8), (10), and (12) therefore make the probability in
(10) at least \(\frac34(1/2-\alpha)>0\) for both channels.  Taking
expectations in (11) and using (2) and (7) yields constants
\(c_D,c_T>0\), depending only on the fixed model constants and \(\alpha\),
such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(B)\ge c_Dn_D^{-1/2},          \tag{13}
\]
and, when \(r\ge2\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(B)\ge c_TK_T^{-1/2}.          \tag{14}
\]
For \(r=1\), (13) is the asserted bound.  For \(r\ge2\), use
\(\max\{a,b\}\ge(a+b)/2\) in (13)--(14), and decrease the constant, to
obtain the asserted sum.  All membership and total-variation bounds hold
at every sufficiently large evaluated triple and do not impose a
relative-growth condition. \(\square\)
