\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).  At every
sufficiently large triple, \(\mathrm{lem:uniform\mbox{-}linearization}\)
gives
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}
 \le\alpha-\varepsilon/4,                                  \tag{1}
\]
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>\omega_r/2\}
 \le\varepsilon/4,
 \qquad
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}
 \le\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D).                        \tag{2}
\]
A union bound, followed by the gridwise expansion, yields
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\left\{
 \max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 \le c_r^\xi+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+\omega_r
 \right\}\ge1-\alpha.                                     \tag{3}
\]

Let \(q=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\).  The interpolation proposition and the
corrected canonical grid give
\[
 \|\widehat\theta_r-\theta\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}
 \le\max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
   +c_{\mathrm{int}}h^q,
 \qquad h^q\le\rho_r.                                      \tag{4}
\]
Both the rank-one and fallback estimators are continuous because the
corrected definition applies \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\) to the pooled
product-limit curve.  Projection onto \([0,1]\) cannot increase distance
from \(\theta(g)\in[0,1]\), and linear interpolation of the endpoint
curves preserves the gridwise enclosure up to the deterministic term in
(4).  The radius \(A_r\) is exactly the right side of (3) plus that term.
Therefore
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)
 \le\theta(t)\le\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
 \text{ for all }t\in\mathcal I\}\ge1-\alpha.              \tag{5}
\]
This is stronger than the required \(1-\alpha-\varepsilon\), proving
\(\widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\).

Clipping can only reduce width, so
\[
 w(\widehat B_r)
 \le2c_r^\xi+2\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)
      +2\omega_r+2c_{\mathrm{int}}h^q.                    \tag{6}
\]
For sufficiently large triples the conditional quantile level is at most
\(1-\alpha/2\).  The pooled multiplier lemma at rank one and the gridwise
functional-PCR transfer lemma at higher rank give
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_PE_\xi
 \|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_{infty,\mathcal G}^2\le C\rho_r^2. \tag{7}
\]
Conditional Markov inequality at tail probability at least \(\alpha/2\)
gives
\[
 c_r^\xi\le(2/\alpha)^{1/2}
 \{E_\xi\|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_{infty,\mathcal G}^2\}^{1/2}.
\]
Jensen's inequality and (7) imply
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pc_r^\xi\le C\rho_r.           \tag{8}
\]
The linearization lemma gives \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\) uniformly on every
three-dimensional tail, and (4) gives \(h^q\le\rho_r\).  Taking
expectations in (6), using (8), and increasing the common lower-tail cutoff
if necessary proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\{\rho_r+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\}
 =C\bar\rho_r.                                              \tag{9}
\]
At rank one, \(\bar\rho_1=\rho_1=(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\).  For fixed
\(r\ge2\), (9) is \(O(\rho_r)\) only on subregimes satisfying
\(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\).  No step compares the relative growth of the
three sample sizes, and no unrestricted removal of \(\eta_D\) has been
claimed. \(\square\)
