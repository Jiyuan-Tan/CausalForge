For fixed declared lower bounds \(\gamma_->0\) and \(\kappa_->0\), a deterministic threshold \(\tau_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\downarrow0\) satisfying
\[
 (N_0K_D)^{-1/2}+N_0^{-1/2}K_D^{-1}
       +\eta_D(K_D)=o(\tau_{N_0,K_T,K_D})
\]
separates exact rank one from every fixed rank-two class once
\(\tau_{N_0,K_T,K_D}<\min\{\gamma_-^2,\kappa_-\}/4\).  A sample split can charge the resulting selection error to \(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\), after which switching between the two oracle bands has no first-order width cost on that fixed separated pair of classes.  This does not settle uniform adaptation over triangular rank-two sequences with \(\delta_2\downarrow0\), nor the exact excess-width testing modulus at that boundary.
