For each sample-size triple, define
\[
 \mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}(N_0,K_T,K_D)
 =\left\{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D):
 e_Q=1,\ 
 \left[\int_{\mathcal I}\{b_{0,Q}(t)^2+b_{1,Q}(t)^2\}\,dt\right]^{1/2}
 \ge \frac12|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2\right\}.
\]
Here \(e_Q\), \(b_{0,Q}\), and \(b_{1,Q}\) are the separately normalized contrast indicator and factor curves in \(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}at\mbox{-}most\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}closure}\).  Thus this is a law class, not the image of a particular witness: every member has known contrast indicator one and donor-visible invariant joint contrast energy, while no common positive lower bound is imposed on \(\int_{\mathcal I}b_{0,Q}^2\).
