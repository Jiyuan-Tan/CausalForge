Let \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\), and let \((\bar b_p,\bar z_m)\) be any donor-centered compatible \(d\)-coordinate representation of the normalized factor model. After covariance normalization of its donor loadings, the donor-SVD factors and loadings in \(\mathrm{def:canonical\mbox{-}factorization}\) equal the compatible factors and loadings up to a common orthogonal transform. If \(d>0\), then \(S^0_{0,\star}-s_0\) belongs to the span of the canonical \(b_0\), the matrix \(H_0=\int_{\mathcal I}b_0b_0^\top\) is positive definite, and
\[
 z_\star=H_0^{-1}\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)
 \{S^0_{0,\star}(t)-s_0(t)\}\,dt
\]
is the unique compatible target coordinate. For \(d=0\), compatibility forces \(S^0_{p,m}=s_p\) for all units.
