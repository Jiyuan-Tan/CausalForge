For every evaluated triple, \(\mathfrak P_{\le2}^{\triangle}(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) is the class of laws satisfying \(\mathrm{ass:control\mbox{-}consistency}\), \(\mathrm{ass:patient\mbox{-}independence}\), \(\mathrm{ass:independent\mbox{-}censoring}\), \(\mathrm{ass:censor\mbox{-}overlap}\), \(\mathrm{ass:event\mbox{-}overlap}\), \(\mathrm{ass:hazard\mbox{-}smoothness}\), \(\mathrm{ass:normalized\mbox{-}factor\mbox{-}model}\), \(\mathrm{ass:triangular\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}at\mbox{-}most\mbox{-}two}\), \(\mathrm{ass:donor\mbox{-}incoherence}\), \(\mathrm{ass:target\mbox{-}loading\mbox{-}bound}\), and \(\mathrm{ass:factor\mbox{-}envelope}\), using one scalar contrast coordinate and the zero-augmentation convention for homogeneous laws.  Thus rank-one laws are included by setting \(b_0=b_1=0\), while rank-two triangular arrays may have their unique nonzero contrast singular value or their pre-period Gram converge to zero because no common positive lower bound is imposed.
