Uniformly over \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) and all sequences with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\to\infty\), the functional-PCR estimator has an expansion in \(\ell^\infty(\mathcal I)\),
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta
 =\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}K_T^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_T}\phi_{T,j}
 +(N_0K_D)^{-1}\sum_{p,m,j}\phi_{D,p,m,j}+R_r,
 \qquad
 \|R_r\|_\infty\le\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+o_P(\rho_r).
\]
The displayed sums are independent mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes, the target process \(\phi_{T,j}\) is identically zero when \(r=1\), and the patient-multiplier analogue consistently estimates the law of the centered first-order supremum normalized by \(\rho_r\), without a restriction on the relative growth of \(K_T\) and \(N_0K_D\).
