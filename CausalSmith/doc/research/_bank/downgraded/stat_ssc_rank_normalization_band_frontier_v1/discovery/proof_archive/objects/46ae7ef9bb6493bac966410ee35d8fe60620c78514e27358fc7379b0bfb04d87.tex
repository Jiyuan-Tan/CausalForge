For an observed control cell with \(k\) patients and \(k\) large enough, its linearly interpolated Kaplan--Meier curve admits the uniform decomposition
\[
 \widehat S^{\mathrm{KM}}_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m}
 =k^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{k}\varphi_{p,m,j}+b_{p,m,k}+q_{p,m,k}
 \quad\hbox{in }\ell^\infty(\mathcal I),
\]
where the \(\varphi_{p,m,j}\) are independent, mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes, \(\|b_{p,m,k}\|_\infty\le C_0e^{-c_0k}\), \(E q_{p,m,k}=0\), and \(E\|q_{p,m,k}\|_\infty^2\le C_0k^{-2}\), uniformly over the model class.  Here \(c_0=s_-g_-/3\), and \(C_0\) may be chosen as the conservative fixed constant used in the definition of \(\eta_D\).  For independent donor cells, averaging the \(q_{p,m,k}\) terms therefore has order \(O_P(N_0^{-1/2}k^{-1})=o_P((N_0k)^{-1/2})\), whereas averaging their bias terms need not improve the bound \(C_0e^{-c_0k}\).
