For every fixed rank \(r\) under consideration and all sufficiently large \(N_0\), the fixed constants defining \(\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) admit an uncensored normalized factor law \(P_r^\circ\) for which the event-survival, hazard-Hölder, nonzero contrast-spectrum, pre-contrast Gram, donor-loading, target-loading, and factor-envelope inequalities all hold with a fixed positive slack; when \(r\ge2\), at least one post-period factor curve of \(P_r^\circ\) is not identically zero.
