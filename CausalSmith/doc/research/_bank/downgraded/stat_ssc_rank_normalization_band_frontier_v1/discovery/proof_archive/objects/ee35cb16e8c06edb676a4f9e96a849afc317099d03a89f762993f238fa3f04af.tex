With the factor space treated as known, the product-limit martingale identity and the overlap bounds give independent mean-zero target and donor influence sums of orders \(K_T^{-1/2}\) and \((N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\).  The frozen raw principal-component map, however, has an errors-in-variables bias.  In the scalar snapshot \(\widehat X_m=b_0z_m+U_m\), \(\widehat Y_m=b_1z_m+V_m\), with \(E(U_m^2\mid z_m)=\sigma_U^2/K_D\), its slope converges to
\[
 \frac{E(\widehat X_m\widehat Y_m)}{E(\widehat X_m^2)}
 =\frac{b_0b_1}{b_0^2+\sigma_U^2/K_D},
\]
whose attenuation is generally of order \(K_D^{-1}\).  Along \(N_0=K_D^3\) and \(K_T=K_D^4\), this exceeds \(\rho_r\asymp K_D^{-2}\).  The proposed cross-fold operator has expectation equal to the signal covariance and removes this displayed attenuation term.  What remains is a uniform functional perturbation expansion for that corrected projector, including cellwise product-limit bias and a quantitative multiplier comparison error small relative to the prescribed \(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\) on every three-dimensional tail.
