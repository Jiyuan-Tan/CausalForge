For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_1(N_0,K_T,K_D)\), the canonical contrast dimension is \(d=0\), so \(S^0_{p,m}(t)=s_p(t)\) for all \(p\in\{0,1\}\), \(m\in\mathcal M\), and \(t\in\mathcal I\), and \(\theta(t)=s_1(t)\). Along any regular submodel whose target-pre marginal law is fixed and whose post-control law perturbs \(\theta\), the target-pre score is identically zero. More generally, for fixed \(r\ge2\), along any normalization-preserving regular submodel of \(\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) of the form \(z_{\star,\eta}=z_\star+\eta v\), with all donor coordinates and factor curves fixed, the target derivative is
\[
 \left.\frac{d}{d\eta}\theta_\eta(t)\right|_{\eta=0}=b_1(t)^\top v.
\]
It is nonzero exactly when \(b_1^\top v\) is not the zero function.
