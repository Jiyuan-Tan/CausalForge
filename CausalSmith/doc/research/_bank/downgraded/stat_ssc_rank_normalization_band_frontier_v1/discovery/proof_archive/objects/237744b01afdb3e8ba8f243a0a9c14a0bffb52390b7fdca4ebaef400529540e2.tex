Let \(\theta_Q(t)=S^0_{1,\star}(t)\) for \(Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).  There are constants \(a_0<\infty\) and \(c_\star>0\), independent of \(N_0,K_T,K_D\), such that, whenever \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\),
\[
 c_\star\le
 \inf_{B:\ \inf_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}
 Q\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta_Q(t)\le B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
 \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)}
 \ \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Q\{w(B)\}
 \le1.
\]
Hence the minimax expected supremum width over the donor-visible known-rank-two class has sharp order one.  Every member of this class has normalized contrast indicator one, full-row-rank donor loadings, and target loading in the donor span; in the fixed-design experiment it is therefore inside the factorization-and-span model used by Han--Shah Theorem~1.

The weak-pre-Gram pair \(\mathbb W_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) in \(\mathrm{def:weak\mbox{-}pre\mbox{-}gram\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}pair}\) satisfies
\[
 \{Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\}
 \subset\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}(N_0,K_T,K_D),
 \qquad
 \sigma_T=\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\}^{1/2}\|\psi_\circ\|_2,
 \qquad
 H_{0,T}=K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2,
\]
so \(\sigma_T\ge|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0\) while \(H_{0,T}\to0\).  Its target separation is a fixed positive constant and
\(\operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})=O(K_T^{-1/2})\).

For a finite pre-period grid \(\mathcal T=\{t_1,\ldots,t_{T_0}\}\), put \(a=(e^{-\lambda_\circ t_\ell})_\ell\) and \(q=(\psi_\circ(t_\ell))_\ell\).  If \(a\) and \(q\) are linearly independent, the pair's pre-period donor matrix has rank two and the ratio of its largest to smallest nonzero singular value is at least a fixed positive constant times \(K_T\), so Han--Shah Assumption~7 fails for all sufficiently large \(K_T\).  If \(q\ne0\) is collinear with \(a\), the pre-period latent-factor span is one-dimensional and Han--Shah Assumption~8 fails for all sufficiently large \(K_T\).  If \(q=0\) on the grid, Assumptions~7--8 may hold, but the grid observes none of the perturbation and Han--Shah Theorem~2 does not address the pair's nonzero continuous-time target separation between grid points.  Thus the lower bound does not contradict their Theorem~2, and the functional-PCR upper bound over \(\mathfrak P_2\) does not transfer to this larger weak-pre-Gram class.
