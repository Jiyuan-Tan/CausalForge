For fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1/2)\) and every fixed exact rank \(r\), there is \(a_0<\infty\) such that, uniformly over every evaluated triple with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\) and without relative-growth restrictions,
\[
 W_1^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\asymp(N_0K_D)^{-1/2},
\]
and, for \(r\ge2\),
\[
 c\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)
 \le W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \le C\bar\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D).
\]
The higher-rank bounds match, and hence recover \(W_r^*\asymp\rho_r\), on every subregime satisfying \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\).  Here each infimum is over bands satisfying the coverage constraint at that same evaluated triple.
