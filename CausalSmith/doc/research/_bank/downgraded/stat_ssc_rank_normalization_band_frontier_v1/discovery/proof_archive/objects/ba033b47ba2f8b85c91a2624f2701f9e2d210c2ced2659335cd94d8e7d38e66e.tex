By the minimax definition, an admissible band with worst-case expected width at most \(C\rho_r\) gives \(W_r^*\le C\rho_r\).  Conversely, the two channel bounds give \(W_r^*\ge c\rho_r\), because \(\max\{a,b\}\ge(a+b)/2\).  Hence the frontier theorem follows algebraically once the attaining theorem and the normalization-preserving converse are established.  The current core does not yet establish those two premises.
