When \(d\ge1\), let \(\varphi_{1,d},\ldots,\varphi_{d,d}\) be the deterministic Gram--Schmidt orthonormalization in \(L^2(\mathcal I)\) of \(\psi_\circ(t),t\psi_\circ(t),\ldots,t^{d-1}\psi_\circ(t)\), with \(\varphi_{1,d}=\psi_\circ/\|\psi_\circ\|_2\), and put \(\Phi_d=(\varphi_{1,d},\ldots,\varphi_{d,d})^\top\).  Let \(R_d<\infty\) be the supremum over integers \(N_0\ge2d+1\) of the maximum row norm of the empirical-orthonormal array obtained by Gram--Schmidt orthonormalizing the centered vectors \(m-(N_0+1)/2,\ldots,\{m-(N_0+1)/2\}^d\) in \(N_0^{-1}\sum_m xy\).  The declared numerical constants satisfy
\[
 g_-\le1,\qquad M_z\ge R_d,\qquad
 \gamma_-^2\le(a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2)\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\le\gamma_+^2,
 \qquad
 \kappa_-\le a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\le\kappa_+,
\]
\[
 \max_{p\in\{0,1\}}\sup_{t\in\mathcal I}
 \bigl\|a_{\circ,p}\|\psi_\circ\|_2\Phi_d(t)\bigr\|_2\le M_b,
 \qquad
 2c_f<\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ\bar\tau}.
\]
Writing \(A_{p,d}(t)=a_{\circ,p}\|\psi_\circ\|_2\Phi_d(t)\) and \(v_\circ=|a_{\circ,1}|/4\), for every \(p\in\{0,1\}\), every \(x\in\mathbb R^d\) with \(\|x\|_2\le R_d\), every \(|v|\le v_\circ\mathbf 1\{p=1\}\), and every \(t\in\mathcal I\), the function
\[
 \ell_{p,x,v}(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+A_{p,d}(t)^\top x+v\psi_\circ(t)
\]
satisfies
\[
 s_-\le\ell_{p,x,v}(t)\le1,qquad
 -\partial_t\ell_{p,x,v}(t)\ge0,qquad
 \left\|-\frac{\partial_t\ell_{p,x,v}}{\ell_{p,x,v}}\right\|_{C^\beta(\mathcal I)}\le L.
\]
For \(d=0\), the same three displayed inequalities hold for \(e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+v\psi_\circ(t)\) for every \(|v|\le v_\circ\).
