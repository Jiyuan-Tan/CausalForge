Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\) be independent mean-zero random vectors in \(\mathbb R^p\) with finite third Euclidean moments, and let \(Y_i\) be independent centered Gaussian vectors with \(E(Y_iY_i^\top)=E(X_iX_i^\top)\). For every \(a>0\), there is a coupling of \(\sum_iX_i\) and \(\sum_iY_i\) such that, with \(B=a^{-3}\sum_iE\|X_i\|_2^3\),
\[
 \Pr\left\{\left\|\sum_iX_i-\sum_iY_i\right\|_2>3a\right\}
 \le C B\{1+|\log B|\},
\]
where \(C<\infty\) is universal. No nonsingularity or coordinate-variance lower bound is required.
