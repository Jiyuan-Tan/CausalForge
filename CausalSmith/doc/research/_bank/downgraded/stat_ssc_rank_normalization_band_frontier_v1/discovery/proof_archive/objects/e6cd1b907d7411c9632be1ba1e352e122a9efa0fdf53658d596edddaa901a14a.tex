In the general adaptation problem \(\mathcal A_0\subset\mathcal A\) with loss \(D\), Carpentier, Klopp, L\"offler, and Nickl define the minimax testing radius \(\rho_n\) by uniformly consistent tests of \(\mathcal A_0\) against \(\{f\in\mathcal A:D(f,\mathcal A_0)\ge L\rho_n\}\).  Their Theorem~2.1 states that if \(r_n(\mathcal A_0)=o(\rho_n)\), honest adaptation is impossible and every honest confidence set has expected \(D\)-diameter at least a constant multiple of \(\rho_n\) somewhere on \(\mathcal A_0\), under the displayed error-level restriction.
