For fixed positive contrast-spectrum and pre-Gram lower bounds, thresholds tending to zero eventually recover the fixed-rank oracle, even when their numerical values are not supplied to the procedure.  This pointwise eventual statement is not uniform over a triangular union of all positive strengths.  In that union, donor post curves can reveal a rank-two direction while an arbitrarily weak pre-period factor hides the target loading; an honest band must then remain wide.
