\textbf{Proof.}
For the panel in the premise, the asserted factor identity is immediate:
\[
 \left\langle u_p^{\mathrm{HS}}(t),v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}\right\rangle
 =s_p(t)+b_p(t)^\top z_m=S^0_{p,m}(t).                 \tag{1}
\]
Let \(V_D=[v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}:m\in\mathcal D]\).  Donor centering and
the empirical loading-Gram identity give
\[
 \frac1{N_0}V_DV_D^\top
 =\begin{pmatrix}
 1 & N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m^\top\\
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m &
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_mz_m^\top
 \end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&I_d\end{pmatrix}=I_r.        \tag{2}
\]
Consequently \(V_DV_D^\top=N_0I_r\) is invertible.  Thus \(V_D\) has
row rank \(r\), its columns span \(\mathbb R^r\), and in particular
\(v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}}=(1,z_\star^\top)^\top\) is in their span.  An
explicit coefficient vector is
\[
 a_\star
 =V_D^\top(V_DV_D^\top)^{-1}v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}},
 \qquad
 V_Da_\star=v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}}.                    \tag{3}
\]

It remains to justify the specialization assertion, including the target
column.  Let \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).  The normalized-factor
compatibility condition and exact centered rank give a compatible
\(d=r-1\) dimensional affine panel.  By
\(\mathrm{lem:canonical\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}coordinate}\), the donor
singular-value coordinates in
\(\mathrm{def:canonical\mbox{-}factorization}\) agree with any such
compatible normalized coordinates up to an orthogonal change of basis,
and the common target coordinate is
\[
 z_\star
 =H_0^{-1}\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)
       \{S^0_{0,\star}(t)-s_0(t)\}\,dt,
 \qquad
 H_0=\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)b_0(t)^\top\,dt.          \tag{4}
\]
The positive pre-period Gram makes (4) unique, and the compatibility
identity transports the same coordinate to period one.  Hence the
canonical panel, including \(m=\star\), satisfies all three displayed
premises of the proposition.  Equations (1)--(3) therefore apply.
Finally,
\(\mathrm{lem:han\mbox{-}shah\mbox{-}theorem\mbox{-}one\mbox{-}scope}\)
identifies factorization and donor-span inclusion as the relevant
Han--Shah Theorem~1 conditions.  The argument is one-way: it does not
attribute this note's normalization, rank, spectrum, pre-Gram,
smoothness, or envelope restrictions to the larger published model.
\(\square\)
