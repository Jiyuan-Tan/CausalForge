Fix \(r\ge2\), put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), and use the stabilized full-cell and odd--even half-fold inputs in the corrected \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\). Uniformly over \(\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\), the aggregate functional-PCR estimator has the gridwise expansion
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta
 =K_T^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_T}\phi_{T,j}
 +n_D^{-1}\sum_{p,m,j}\phi_{D,p,m,j}
 +\mathfrak b_r+Q_r,
\]
where the patient processes are independent and mean zero,
\[
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le\eta_D(K_D),
 \qquad E\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}^2\le C\upsilon_r^2,
\]
with \(\upsilon_r\) as in the corrected definition of \(\omega_r\). The aggregate spectral/pre-Gram threshold failure probability is at most \(Ce^{-c_\dagger n_D}\), without a union over donor cells. On the regular branch, composition of the first two Fr\'echet derivatives of the coordinate-free prediction map with the stabilized inputs gives deterministic patient envelopes \(C/K_T\) for target-pre patients and \(C/(N_0K_D)\) for donor patients on every dataset. On fallback, the ordinary pooled derivative is used. Consequently, for the oracle linear process \(Z_r\), empirical multiplier process \(\widehat Z_r^\xi\), and their covariance kernels \(\Sigma_r\) and \(\widehat\Sigma_r\),
\[
 \sup_PE_P\|Z_r\|_\infty^2
 +\sup_PE_PE_\xi\|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_\infty^2
 \le C_Z\{K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\}\le C_Z\rho_r^2,
\]
\[
 \|\widehat\Sigma_r-\Sigma_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G^2}
 =o_P(\rho_r^2)
\]
uniformly on every three-dimensional tail. The fixed explicit \(C_Z\) is the one displayed in the corrected band definition. The moment bounds are continuous-time bounds and incur no \(\sqrt{\log|\mathcal G|}\) factor; the guarded coverage theorem does not assume a multiplier-only random-quantile approximation.
