\textbf{Proof.}
For the algebraic assertion, the affine identity gives, for every period, unit, and follow-up time,
\[
 \left\langle u_p^{\mathrm{HS}}(t),v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}\right\rangle
 =s_p(t)+b_p(t)^\top z_m
 =S^0_{p,m}(t).                                           \tag{1}
\]
Let \(V_D=[v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}:m\in\mathcal D]\).  Donor centering and covariance normalization yield
\[
 \frac1{N_0}V_DV_D^\top
 =\begin{pmatrix}
  1&N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m^\top\\
  N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m&
  N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_mz_m^\top
 \end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&I_d\end{pmatrix}
 =I_r.                                                     \tag{2}
\]
Consequently \(V_DV_D^\top=N_0I_r\) is invertible, so \(V_D\) has row rank \(r\) and its columns span \(\mathbb R^r\).  In particular,
\[
 a_\star:=V_D^\top(V_DV_D^\top)^{-1}v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}}
 \quad\text{satisfies}\quad
 V_Da_\star=v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}},                       \tag{3}
\]
which proves donor-span inclusion.

Now let \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).  The normalized factor-model assumption supplies an all-unit compatible affine representation, and exact centered rank fixes its contrast dimension at \(d=r-1\).  The canonical factorization centers and covariance-normalizes the donor loadings.  The canonical-target-coordinate lemma shows that target-pre compatibility is preserved by this normalization and that the positive pre-period Gram gives the unique shared target coordinate.  Thus the canonical coordinates satisfy all premises used in (1)--(3), including for \(m=\star\).  The cited Han--Shah scope lemma identifies finite-dimensional survival factorization and donor-span inclusion as the structural conditions used by their Theorem~1.  Hence every law in \(\mathfrak P_r\) is a specialization of that structural model.  This is only a one-way inclusion: none of the canonical normalization, exact-rank, spectrum, pre-Gram, smoothness, or loading-envelope restrictions has been attributed to Han and Shah. \(\square\)
