Suppose \(r=1\), put \(n_D=N_0K_D\),
\[
 \bar G_D(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}G_{1,m}(t),
 \qquad H_D(t)=s_1(t)\bar G_D(t).
\]
For the continuously interpolated pooled donor-post product-limit estimator in the corrected \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\), there are independent mean-zero processes
\[
 \phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}(t)
 =-s_1(t)\int_0^tH_D(u)^{-1}\,dM_{m,j}(u)
\]
such that, on the inference grid,
\[
 \widehat\theta_1-\theta
 =n_D^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\sum_{j=1}^{K_D}
 \mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}
 +b_{D,n_D}+q_{D,n_D},
\]
where
\[
 \|b_{D,n_D}\|_\infty\le Ce^{-cn_D},
 \qquad Eq_{D,n_D}=0,
 \qquad E\|q_{D,n_D}\|_\infty^2\le Cn_D^{-2},
\]
uniformly over the rank-one class although the censoring laws may differ across donors. If \(\widehat Z_1^\xi\) is the explicit pooled multiplier and \(\Sigma_1\) and \(\widehat\Sigma_1\) are respectively the covariance kernel of the first-order sum and the conditional covariance kernel of that multiplier, then
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_1}E_P
 \left\|n_D^{-1}\sum_{m,j}
 \mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}\right\|_
 {\infty,\mathcal I}^2
 +\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_1}E_PE_\xi
 \|\widehat Z_1^\xi\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}^2
 \le Cn_D^{-1},
\]
and
\[
 n_D\|\widehat\Sigma_1-\Sigma_1\|_{\infty,\mathcal I^2}
 \longrightarrow0
\]
in probability uniformly over \(\mathfrak P_1\). These assertions allow variance-zero coordinates, including time zero. No random-quantile coverage assertion is made.
