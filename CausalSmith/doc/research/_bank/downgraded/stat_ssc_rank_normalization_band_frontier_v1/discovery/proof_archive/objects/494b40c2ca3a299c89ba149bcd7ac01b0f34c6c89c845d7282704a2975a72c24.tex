Suppose a corrected estimator supplies a coupling \(\widehat  heta_r-\theta=Z_r+R_r\) with a conditional multiplier copy \(Z_r^\xi\), a deterministic sequence \(a\mapsto q(a)\downarrow0\) satisfying both the coupling and remainder error bounds uniformly on the tail \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a\), and \(q(a)\le\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).  If its conditional critical value \(c_{1-\alpha}^\xi\) obeys \(E c_{1-\alpha}^\xi\le C\rho_r\), then the multiplier quantile inequality and the corrected interpolation bound give
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\theta(t)\in\widehat B_r(t)
 \text{ for all }t\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le2C\rho_r+2c_{\mathrm{int}}h^{\min\{\beta+1,2\}}=O(\rho_r).
\]
Thus the final coverage and width step is elementary once a quantitative, unrestricted-growth linearization is available; the frozen qualitative \(o_P(\rho_r)\) assertion alone does not imply comparison error below the prescribed vanishing schedule.
