For an evaluated triple and an allocation \(q_{\mathrm{sel}}\in(0,1/4)\), split every donor-post cell into odd and even patients and let \(\widehat U_m^{(a)}(t)\) be the interpolated fold-\(a\) Kaplan--Meier curve centered across donors, \(a\in\{1,2\}\).  Define the normalization-preserving cross-fold statistic
\[
 T_D=\sup_{g\in\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}}
 \left|N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}
       \widehat U_m^{(1)}(g)\widehat U_m^{(2)}(g)\right|.
\]
Let \(\widehat H_0\) be the scalar pre-period Gram produced by the thresholded rank-two functional-PCR fit.  With \(q_\beta=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\), put
\[
 u_D(q_{\mathrm{sel}})=C_A\left\{
   \frac{1}{K_D\sqrt{N_0q_{\mathrm{sel}}}}
   +\eta_D(K_D)^2+h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{2q_\beta}\right\},
\]
\[
 u_G(q_{\mathrm{sel}})=C_Aq_{\mathrm{sel}}^{-1/2}\left\{
   (N_0K_D)^{-1/2}+N_0^{-1/2}K_D^{-1}
   +\eta_D(K_D)+h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{q_\beta}\right\},
\]
where \(C_A<\infty\) is a fixed model-envelope constant, and define
\[
 \Psi_{\mathrm{adapt}}
 =\mathbf 1\{T_D>u_D(q_{\mathrm{sel}}),\ \widehat H_0>u_G(q_{\mathrm{sel}})\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{test}}(q_{\mathrm{sel}})
 =C_A\left\{u_D(q_{\mathrm{sel}})^{1/2}
             +\eta_D(K_D)+h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{q_\beta}\right\}.
\]
The corresponding Carpentier--Klopp--L\"offler--Nickl test-plus-oracle construction has three branches.  If \(\Psi_{\mathrm{adapt}}=0\), use the rank-one donor-post center and enlarge its band by \(L_A\rho_{\mathrm{test}}(q_{\mathrm{sel}})\).  If \(\Psi_{\mathrm{adapt}}=1\) and the thresholded rank-two spectrum and pre-Gram are regular, use the rank-two martingale-multiplier band with the numerical lower bounds in its fallback rule replaced by \(u_D(q_{\mathrm{sel}})\) and \(u_G(q_{\mathrm{sel}})\).  If donor rank is detected but the pre-Gram threshold fails, return the full band \([0,1]\).  Interpolate endpoints and impose their common value one at zero.  The resulting band is denoted by \(B_{\mathrm{adapt}}\).

The proof program is the finite-triple composite test
\[
 H_0:P\in\mathfrak P_1(N_0,K_T,K_D)
 \quad\hbox{against}\quad
 H_1:P\in\mathfrak P_{\le2}^{\triangle}(N_0,K_T,K_D),\quad
 D_\theta(P,\mathfrak P_1)\ge L_A\rho_{\mathrm{test}}(q_{\mathrm{sel}}),
\]
with the sum of type-I and type-II errors allocated to \(q_{\mathrm{sel}}\), together with an oracle inequality for the thresholded center that explicitly includes the target-near-null and weak-pre-Gram branches.  A lower certificate must use a normalization-preserving Le Cam or fuzzy pair below the proposed testing radius.  The expected-width calculation on the null is
\[
 E_Pw(B_{\mathrm{adapt}})
 \le C\{\rho_1+\rho_{\mathrm{test}}(q_{\mathrm{sel}})\}
 +P_P\{\Psi_{\mathrm{adapt}}=1\}\,C\bar\rho_2,
 \qquad P\in\mathfrak P_1,
\]
with the weak-pre-Gram fallback probability and its unit-width contribution displayed separately.  This construction is a program to be verified from the censored survival experiment; the matrix-completion residual-energy calculation is not imported as a survival-data theorem.
