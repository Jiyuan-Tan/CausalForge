\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(Z_r\) denote the sum of the two centered first-order processes in the corrected linearization lemma and set \(q=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\).  The lemma gives
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta=Z_r+R_r,\qquad
 \|R_r\|_\infty\le\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+o_P(\rho_r),
\]
uniformly on every three-dimensional tail.  Its multiplier comparison, the conditional quantile level
\[
 1-\alpha+\min\{\alpha/2,\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)/2\},
\]
and the usual smoothing inequality for distribution functions imply, for all sufficiently large triples,
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}
 P\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le c_r^\xi+o(\rho_r)\}
 \ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha).
\]
The quantitative comparison error in the lemma is polynomial up to logarithms, whereas \(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\) decreases only as an inverse iterated logarithm.  Thus the displayed inequality is uniform on the entire tail rather than only along a balanced subsequence.

The corrected endpoints include \(\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\), so the deterministic part of the remainder is covered.  The interpolation proposition and the mesh condition give
\[
 \|\widehat\theta_r-\theta\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}
 \le \max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|+c_{\mathrm{int}}h^q,\qquad
 h^q\le\rho_r.
\]
Projection onto \([0,1]\) cannot increase the distance to the survival curve \(\theta\), and both curves equal one at zero.  Combining these facts proves
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}
 P\{\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta(t)\le\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
 \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\}
 \ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha),
\]
so \(\widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r\).

Clipping the endpoints to \([0,1]\) can only reduce width.  Therefore
\[
 w(\widehat B_r)
 \le2c_r^\xi+2c_{\mathrm{int}}h^q
 +2\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D).
\]
The bounded martingale envelopes and the entropy calculation in the linearization proof give \(\sup_PE_Pc_r^\xi\le C\rho_r\).  Taking expectations, using \(h^q\le\rho_r\), and recalling the definition of \(\bar\rho_r\) yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\{\rho_r+\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\}
 =C\bar\rho_r.
\]
All constants depend only on the fixed rank, coverage level, and fixed class constants, not on the three sample sizes. \(\square\)
