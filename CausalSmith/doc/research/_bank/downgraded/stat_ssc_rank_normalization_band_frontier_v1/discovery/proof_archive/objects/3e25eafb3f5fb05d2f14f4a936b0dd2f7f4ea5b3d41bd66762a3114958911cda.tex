\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\) and \(k_D=\lfloor K_D/2\rfloor\).  Apply
\(\mathrm{lem:stabilized\mbox{-}product\mbox{-}limit\mbox{-}cell}\) to
the target-pre cell, every full donor cell, and both deterministic donor
half-folds.  Each interpolated input has the exact decomposition
\[
 \widehat S^\dagger=\widetilde S^0+L+b+q,                 \tag{1}
\]
where \(L\) is an independent-patient mean-zero martingale average,
\(\|b\|_\infty\leq Ce^{-c_0k}\), and
\(E\|q\|_\infty^2\leq Ck^{-2}\).  Odd and even folds are independent.

We first establish the full donor-average bound for \(q\), rather than invoking
pointwise averaging.  In the notation of the cell proof, decompose
\[
 q_m=U_m+V_m,
 \qquad U_m=A_{k,m}-EA_{k,m},                              \tag{2}
\]
where \(U_m\) is the centered rare drift and \(V_m\) is the centered
martingale/product-integral remainder.  Equation (20) of that proof gives
\[
 E\left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_mU_m\right\|_\infty^2
 \leq CN_0^{-1}e^{-c_0k}.                                 \tag{3}
\]
To control \(V_m\), order the common grid and all event times.  The exact ratio
identity and stochastic integration by parts write, at every ordered time
\(g_\ell\),
\[
 V_m(g_\ell)
 =\int_0^{g_\ell}K_{m,\ell}(u)\,dM_m(u)
   +R_m(g_\ell)-ER_m(g_\ell),                             \tag{4}
\]
where \(K_{m,\ell}\) is predictable, its time-ordered variation is bounded by a
fixed multiple of the clipped reciprocal risk, and \(R_m\) is the
second-order product-integral remainder.  The product-integral summation-by-parts
identity and the calculations in (13)--(16) of the cell proof give, uniformly in
\(m\),
\[
 E\int_0^{\bar\tau}K_{m,\ell}(u)^2\,d\langle M_m\rangle(u)
 \leq Ck^{-2},
 \qquad
 E\{\operatorname{TV}(R_m-ER_m)^2\}
 \leq Ck^{-2}.                                             \tag{5}
\]
After averaging the independent cells, the predictable variation of the first
term in (4) is therefore
\[
 E\left\langle N_0^{-1}\sum_m\int K_{m,\ell}\,dM_m
 \right\rangle(\bar\tau)
 \leq \frac{C}{N_0k^2}.                                   \tag{6}
\]
Stochastic integration by parts in the common time order, followed by Doob's
\(L^2\) maximal inequality, turns (6) into the same bound for the continuous
supremum.  For the second term in (4), the ordered bounded-variation
symmetrization and donor-index Doob calculation in (18) of the cell proof,
applied to the independent centered product-integral remainders, gives
\[
 E\left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_mV_m\right\|_\infty^2
 \leq\frac{C}{N_0k^2}.                                    \tag{7}
\]
Combining (2), (3), and (7) proves the required full remainder average
\[
 E\left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_mq_m\right\|_\infty^2
 \leq\frac{C}{N_0k^2}+CN_0^{-1}e^{-c_0k}.                \tag{8}
\]
This is a time-ordered maximal calculation, not pointwise or direct averaging,
and it has no grid-cardinality factor.  The deterministic donor mean of the rare
bias is at most \(Ce^{-c_0k_D}\), which is dominated by
\(\eta_D(K_D)\).

Let \(\Gamma_{\mathcal G}\) be the interpolated population donor operator.
By \(\mathrm{lem:interpolated\mbox{-}population\mbox{-}fpcr\mbox{-}stability}\),
its selected spectrum has a fixed positive gap and its pre-period Gram has
minimum eigenvalue at least \(\kappa_-/2\) for every sufficiently fine
canonical grid.  Cross-fold independence, (1), and (8) imply
\[
 \|E\widehat\Gamma-\Gamma_{\mathcal G}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 \leq C\{e^{-c_0k_D}+K_D^{-1}\}=o(1).                    \tag{9}
\]
In particular, cross-folding removes the same-fold errors-in-variables mean;
the remaining terms in (9) are the clipped deterministic means and
second-order remainders.

We next prove aggregate operator regularity.  Replacing one donor record changes
its stabilized interpolated curve by \(C/K_D\).  Donor centering is linear, all
curves are bounded, and
\(\|x\otimes y\|_{\mathrm{HS}}=\|x\|_2\|y\|_2\).  Hence, if
\(\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\) is formed after replacing the \(i\)-th donor record,
\[
 \|\widehat\Gamma-\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 \leq\frac{C}{N_0K_D},
 \qquad
 \sum_{i=1}^{2N_0K_D}
 \|\widehat\Gamma-\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \leq\frac{C}{n_D}.                                      \tag{10}
\]
The Hilbert-space Efron--Stein identity, applied coordinatewise in an
orthonormal Hilbert--Schmidt basis and summed by monotone convergence, gives
\[
 E\|\widehat\Gamma-E\widehat\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \leq\frac12\sum_iE
 \|\widehat\Gamma-\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \leq\frac{C}{n_D}.                                      \tag{11}
\]
Now apply scalar McDiarmid inequality to the real-valued Lipschitz map
\(O\mapsto\|\widehat\Gamma-E\widehat\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{HS}}\).  Equations
(10)--(11) yield, for every fixed \(x>0\) and all sufficiently large triples,
\[
 P\left\{\|\widehat\Gamma-E\widehat\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 >C n_D^{-1/2}+x\right\}
 \leq \exp\{-c n_Dx^2\}.                                 \tag{12}
\]
Thus no vector norm moment-generating-function assertion is used.  Equations
(9), (12), the eigenvalue variational inequality, and the resolvent formula for
the selected projector imply that the empirical selected spectrum remains in
the fixed-gap neighborhood except on an event of probability \(Ce^{-cn_D}\).
The positive-square-root map is Lipschitz on that neighborhood.  Restriction to
the pre-period component and
\[
 A^{-1}-B^{-1}=A^{-1}(B-A)B^{-1}                           \tag{13}
\]
then give the empirical pre-Gram threshold with the same probability.  Hence,
for the aggregate regular event \(\mathcal R\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P(\mathcal R^c)\leq Ce^{-cn_D}. \tag{14}
\]
There has been no union over donor cells.

We record the derivatives on \(\mathcal R\).  Let \(\Pi(\Gamma)\) be the
selected spectral projector, let \(A=\Gamma_+^{1/2}\) on its range, and let
\(E_p\) restrict a joint-period function to period \(p\).  Write
\[
 A_p=E_pA,\qquad H=A_0^*A_0,\qquad
 z=H^{-1}A_0^*x,\qquad F(s,A,x)=s_1+A_1z.                 \tag{15}
\]
For a perturbation \((\dot s,\dot A,\dot x)\),
\[
 \dot H=\dot A_0^*A_0+A_0^*\dot A_0,
 \qquad
 \dot z=H^{-1}\{\dot A_0^*x+A_0^*\dot x-\dot Hz\},     \tag{16}
\]
\[
 DF[\dot s,\dot A,\dot x]
 =\dot s_1+\dot A_1z+A_1\dot z.                           \tag{17}
\]
Along a twice differentiable path,
\[
 \ddot H=\ddot A_0^*A_0+A_0^*\ddot A_0
          +2\dot A_0^*\dot A_0,
\]
\[
 \ddot z=H^{-1}\{\ddot A_0^*x+2\dot A_0^*\dot x
 +A_0^*\ddot x-\ddot Hz-2\dot H\dot z\},                \tag{18}
\]
\[
 D^2F[\dot{\ },\dot{\ }]
 =\ddot s_1+\ddot A_1z+2\dot A_1\dot z+A_1\ddot z.     \tag{19}
\]
The selected projector derivative is
\[
 D\Pi_\Gamma[E]
 =\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{\mathscr C}
 (w-\Gamma)^{-1}E(w-\Gamma)^{-1}\,dw,                   \tag{20}
\]
and the positive-square-root derivative is the unique selected-range solution
of
\[
 A\,DA_\Gamma[E]+DA_\Gamma[E]A
 =D\{\Pi\Gamma\Pi\}_\Gamma[E].                           \tag{21}
\]
The contour stays a fixed distance from both spectral components, and the
selected eigenvalues are uniformly positive.  Differentiating (20)--(21) once
more therefore gives
\[
 \|DA_\Gamma[E]\|\leq C\|E\|,
 \qquad
 \|D^2A_\Gamma[E_1,E_2]\|
 \leq C\|E_1\|\|E_2\|.                                  \tag{22}
\]
Equations (16)--(22), (13), and the fixed factor and loading envelopes show that
the first two Fr\'echet derivatives of \(F\) are uniformly bounded throughout
the regular neighborhood, including at internal eigenvalue ties.

Composition with the stabilized-cell sensitivity bound gives, on every dataset
in \(\mathcal R\),
\[
 \|D_j^TF\|_\infty\leq C/K_T,
 \qquad
 \|D_{p,m,j}^DF\|_\infty\leq C/(N_0K_D).                 \tag{23}
\]
Taylor's formula with integral remainder, (1), (8), (14), and (22)--(23)
now gives, on the grid,
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta
 =K_T^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_T}\phi_{T,j}
  +n_D^{-1}\sum_{p,m,j}\phi_{D,p,m,j}
  +\mathfrak b_r+Q_r.                                     \tag{24}
\]
The equality is centered at \(\theta\), rather than at an interpolated surrogate,
because
\(\mathrm{lem:interpolated\mbox{-}population\mbox{-}fpcr\mbox{-}stability}\)
proves \(\theta^{\mathrm{pop}}_{r,\mathcal G}(g)=\theta(g)\) at every grid
point, while
\(\mathrm{lem:canonical\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}coordinate}\) validates the
unique target loading used by the population map.  The linear terms in (24) are
independent and mean zero.  The deterministic half-fold means satisfy
\[
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\leq\eta_D(K_D).   \tag{25}
\]
If \(\upsilon_r\) denotes the second-order scale recorded in the corrected
definition of \(\omega_r\), the preceding calculations give explicitly, up to
fixed model constants,
\[
 \upsilon_r
 =K_T^{-1}+N_0^{-1/2}K_D^{-1}+n_D^{-1}
  +\rho_r\eta_D(K_D)+e^{-cK_T}+e^{-cn_D}.                 \tag{26}
\]
Indeed, the first three terms respectively come from the target-pre quadratic
remainder, (8), and donor quadratic terms; the fourth is the bias--linear
interaction; and the last two absorb bounded fallback discrepancies.  Thus
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_P
 \|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}^2\leq C\upsilon_r^2.         \tag{27}
\]

Finally, the martingale terms obtained by composing (17) with (11) of the cell
proof are fixed finite sums, depending only on \(r\), of processes of the form
\[
 a(t)M(t)+\int_0^tK(t,u)\,dM(u),                           \tag{28}
\]
where the coefficient curves and their time-ordered total variations are
uniformly bounded.  Stochastic integration by parts, the predictable isometry,
and Doob's inequality bound the expected squared continuous supremum by the sum
of squared patient weights.  Equation (23) therefore gives
\[
 \sup_PE_P\|Z_r\|_\infty^2
 \leq C\{K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\}.                             \tag{29}
\]
Conditionally on the records, the multiplier process has the same
time-ordered representation.  On \(\mathcal R\), (23) proves the analogue of
(29) with \(E_\xi\).  On \(\mathcal R^c\), the pooled fallback derivative has
conditional squared supremum at most \(Cn_D\), and (14) makes its unconditional
contribution exponentially small.  Consequently,
\[
 \sup_PE_PE_\xi\|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_\infty^2
 \leq C\{K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\}.                             \tag{30}
\]
Because (28)--(30) control the continuous supremum before restriction to the
grid, no \(\sqrt{\log|\mathcal G|}\) factor occurs.  Since
\(K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\leq\rho_r^2\leq
2\{K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\}\), (29)--(30) are the asserted moment bound.  This
completes the proof. \(\square\)
