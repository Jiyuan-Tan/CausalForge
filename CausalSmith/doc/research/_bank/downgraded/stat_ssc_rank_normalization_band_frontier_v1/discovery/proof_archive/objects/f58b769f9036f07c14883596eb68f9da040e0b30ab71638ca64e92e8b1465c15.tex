\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(z_m=z_m^{(N_0,1)}\), \(c_T=K_T^{-1}\), and \(q_\alpha=1/2-\alpha>0\).

\emph{Step 1: centering, normalization, and survival validity.}
The centered arithmetic progression satisfies
\[
 \frac1{N_0}\sum_{m=1}^{N_0}z_m=0,
 \qquad
 \frac1{N_0}\sum_{m=1}^{N_0}z_m^2=1,                    \tag{1}
\]
because
\(N_0^{-1}\sum_m\{m-(N_0+1)/2\}^2=(N_0^2-1)/12\).
For a donor pre-period curve, the factor term can be written as
\(a_{\circ,0}\psi_\circ x\) with \(x=c_Tz_m\); for the target it has \(x=c_Tu\bar h\).  The corresponding post-period choices are \(x=z_m\) and \(x=u\bar h\).  For all sufficiently large triples, \(c_T\le1\), and the definition of \(R_1\) together with \(\bar h<R_1\) gives \(|x|\le R_1\) in every case.  The four displayed curve conditions in \(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) therefore give endpoint normalization, monotonicity, event overlap, the failure-density floor on \(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), and the uniform \(C^\beta\) hazard bound.  Infinite censoring gives \(G\equiv1\), which satisfies censoring overlap since \(g_-\le1\).  Patient independence, independent censoring, and control consistency hold by construction.

The donor loading and target loading bounds follow from (1), \(R_1\le M_z\), and \(\bar h<M_\star\).  The factor envelope and both upper Gram bounds follow from the compatibility inequalities and \(c_T\le1\).  The contrast indicator is one because \(a_{\circ,1}\ne0\), \(\psi_\circ\not\equiv0\), and the donor scores in (1) are nonconstant.  Thus both laws satisfy every member property in \(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}at\mbox{-}most\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}closure}\), so
\[
 Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}\in\mathfrak Q_{\le2}(N_0,K_T,K_D),
 \qquad u\in\{-1,+1\}.                                  \tag{2}
\]

\emph{Step 2: exact rank, donor visibility, and the published-model boundary.}
Set
\[
 u_{p,T}(t)=(s_{p,T}(t),b_{p,T}(t))^\top,
 \qquad v_m=(1,z_m)^\top,
 \qquad v_\star^{(u)}=(1,u\bar h)^\top.                  \tag{3}
\]
Equation (1) and \(\mathrm{prop:han\mbox{-}shah\mbox{-}structural\mbox{-}embedding}\) show that the donor matrix \(V_D=[v_m:m\in\mathcal D]\) has full row rank two and that each \(v_\star^{(u)}\) lies in its column span.  The functions in (3) span two dimensions: at \(t=0\) they equal \((1,0)^\top\), while at any interior time with \(\psi_\circ(t)>0\), the second post-period coordinate \(a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t)\) is nonzero.  Hence the total factor rank is exactly two, not merely at most two.  This verifies directly the bilinear factor and donor-span conditions recorded in \(\mathrm{lem:han\mbox{-}shah\mbox{-}theorem\mbox{-}one\mbox{-}scope}\); fixed treatment assignment makes selection conditional on the deterministic latent factors degenerate.

Since the centered donor scores have empirical second moment one, the unique nonzero singular value of the joint-period centered contrast operator is
\[
 \sigma_T
 =\left\{\int_{\mathcal I}(b_{0,T}^2+b_{1,T}^2)\right\}^{1/2}
 =\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\}^{1/2}
   \|\psi_\circ\|_2
 \ge |a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0.                 \tag{4}
\]
In contrast,
\[
 H_{0,T}=\int_{\mathcal I}b_{0,T}^2
 =K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\longrightarrow0. \tag{5}
\]
Thus donor heterogeneity is uniformly visible even though the target-relevant pre-period direction becomes weak.

To compare with the additional hypothesis of Han--Shah Theorem~2, take any finite pre-period grid \(\mathcal T=\{t_1,\ldots,t_{T_0}\}\), put \(a=(e^{-\lambda_\circ t_\ell})_{\ell=1}^{T_0}\) and \(q=(\psi_\circ(t_\ell))_{\ell=1}^{T_0}\), and write its donor matrix as
\[
 M_{0,T}=a\mathbf1^\top+c_Ta_{\circ,0}qz^\top.           \tag{6}
\]
If (6) is rank deficient, its condition number is infinite.  Otherwise, its largest and second singular values obey
\[
 s_1(M_{0,T})\ge\|M_{0,T}\mathbf1/\sqrt{N_0}\|_2
 =\sqrt{N_0}\|a\|_2,
 \qquad
 s_2(M_{0,T})\le c_T|a_{\circ,0}|\sqrt{N_0}\|q\|_2,    \tag{7}
\]
where the second inequality follows by comparing (6) with its displayed rank-one baseline part.  Since
\(\|a\|_2\ge e^{-\lambda_\circ\bar\tau}\sqrt{T_0}\) and
\(\|q\|_2\le\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty\sqrt{T_0}\), (7) gives
\[
 \operatorname{cond}(M_{0,T})
 \ge \frac{e^{-\lambda_\circ\bar\tau}}
 {|a_{\circ,0}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty}K_T.             \tag{8}
\]
Thus the fixed condition-number bound in Han--Shah Assumption~7 excludes the pair.  The lower construction is inside their Theorem~1 structural model but outside the additional well-conditioned finite-grid regime supporting their Theorem~2.

\emph{Step 3: fixed target separation and vanishing information.}
All donor laws are identical under the two signs, and no target-post cohort is observed.  Hence the observed laws differ only through the \(K_T\) target-pre failure times.  The two causal targets are
\[
 \theta_u(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+u\bar h a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t),
\]
so
\[
 \Delta_\star:=\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
 =2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0.          \tag{9}
\]
For one target-pre patient, the two densities differ only on \(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), where
\[
 f_u(t)=\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
        -u c_Ta_{\circ,0}\bar h\psi_\circ'(t).           \tag{10}
\]
The compatibility density floor gives \(f_-(t)\ge c_f\) wherever the numerator below is nonzero.  From \(\log x\le x-1\) and equality of the two total masses,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
 &\le\int\frac{\{f_+(t)-f_-(t)\}^2}{f_-(t)}\,dt\\
 &\le\frac{4c_T^2a_{\circ,0}^2\bar h^2}{c_f}
       \int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2dt.
 \end{aligned}                                             \tag{11}
\]
Independence and product additivity of relative entropy therefore yield a fixed \(C_0<\infty\) with
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\|Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le C_0K_Tc_T^2=C_0K_T^{-1}.                             \tag{12}
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:pinsker\mbox{-}kl\mbox{-}tv}\),
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le\{C_0/(2K_T)\}^{1/2}.                                \tag{13}
\]
Increase \(a_0\) so that (13) is at most \(q_\alpha/2\) whenever \(K_T\ge a_0\), and so all preceding membership checks hold whenever the minimum sample size is at least \(a_0\).

\emph{Step 4: honesty forces constant width.}
Let
\[
 E_u=\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta_u(t)\le B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
       \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\}.
\]
The assumed pairwise honesty gives
\(Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_u)\ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon\), where
\(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).
Transfer \(E_+\) to \(Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}\) by total variation and intersect with \(E_-\).  Using \(\varepsilon\le q_\alpha/4\) and (13),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_-\cap E_+)
 &\ge1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon
      -\operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})\\
 &\ge2q_\alpha-q_\alpha/2-q_\alpha/2=q_\alpha.          \tag{14}
 \end{aligned}
\]
On \(E_-\cap E_+\), the same band contains both target curves, so (9) implies \(w(B)\ge\Delta_\star\).  Thus
\[
 \max_{u\in\{-1,+1\}}E_{Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}}w(B)
 \ge E_{Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}}w(B)
 \ge q_\alpha\Delta_\star=:c_\star>0.                   \tag{15}
\]
The deterministic band with endpoints zero and one covers every survival curve and has width one, proving the matching constant upper bound.  Finally, if an observation-model class contains both laws, uniform honesty over that class implies the pairwise honesty used in (14), and its maximal risk dominates the left side of (15).  This proves upward transfer of the converse.  An upper bound is in the opposite quantifier direction: the functional-PCR theorem is uniform only over \(\mathfrak P_2\), whose fixed pre-Gram lower bound excludes (5), so no upper theorem for the broader Han--Shah structural class follows. \(\square\)
