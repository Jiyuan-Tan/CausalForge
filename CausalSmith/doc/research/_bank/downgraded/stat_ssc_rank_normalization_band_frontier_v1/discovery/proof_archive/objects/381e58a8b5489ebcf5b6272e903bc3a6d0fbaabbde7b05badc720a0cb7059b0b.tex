Put
\[
 Z_r=\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}K_T^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_T}\phi_{T,j}+(N_0K_D)^{-1}\sum_{p,m,j}\phi_{D,p,m,j}.
\]
Uniformly over \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) and all sequences with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\to\infty\), the functional-PCR estimator has the expansion \(\widehat\theta_r-\theta=Z_r+R_r\) in \(\ell^\infty(\mathcal I)\), where the two displayed patient sums are independent mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes and \(\phi_{T,j}\equiv0\) when \(r=1\).  The remainder can be written \(R_r=\mathfrak b_r+Q_r\), with
\[
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_\infty\le \mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D),
 \qquad
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)}P\!\left\{\|Q_r\|_\infty>\frac12\omega_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\right\}
 \le\frac14\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
\]
at every sufficiently large evaluated triple.  Moreover, if \(c_r^\xi\) is the conditional multiplier quantile in \(\mathrm{def:fpcr-multiplier-band}\), then at every such triple
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)}P\!\left\{
 \|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}}>c_r^\xi+\frac12\omega_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \right\}
 \le \alpha-\frac14\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha).
\]
Finally, \(\omega_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)=o(\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D))\) uniformly on every three-dimensional tail, without any restriction on the relative growth of \(K_T\) and \(N_0K_D\).
