\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(q_\alpha=1/2-\alpha>0\).  We construct two members of the closure for
every sufficiently large triple.

\emph{Step 1: a separately normalized pair.}
Specialize the deterministic ingredients of
\(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}lecam\mbox{-}pair}\) to one contrast
coordinate.  Then
\[
A_{p,1}(t)=a_{\circ,p}\psi_\circ(t).
\]
The explicit donor column is
\[
z_m^{(N_0,1)}
=\frac{m-(N_0+1)/2}{\sqrt{(N_0^2-1)/12}}.
\]
The identities
\[
N_0^{-1}\sum_{m=1}^{N_0}z_m^{(N_0,1)}=0,
\qquad
N_0^{-1}\sum_{m=1}^{N_0}\{z_m^{(N_0,1)}\}^2=1
\tag{1}
\]
follow from the centered arithmetic-progression sum and
\(N_0^{-1}\sum_m\{m-(N_0+1)/2\}^2=(N_0^2-1)/12\).

Choose once and for all
\[
0<\bar h<\min\{R_1,M_\star\},
\qquad c_n=K_T^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
For \(u\in\{-1,+1\}\), set
\[
s_{p,n}(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t},
\qquad b_{0,n}(t)=c_nA_{0,1}(t),
\qquad b_{1,n}(t)=A_{1,1}(t),
\tag{3}
\]
use the donor loadings in (1), and put \(\zeta_{\star,n}^{(u)}=u\bar h\).
Thus, on \(\mathcal I\),
\[
S_{p,m}^{(u)}=s_{p,n}+b_{p,n}z_m^{(N_0,1)},
\qquad
S_{p,\star}^{(u)}=s_{p,n}+b_{p,n}\zeta_{\star,n}^{(u)}.
\tag{4}
\]
Continue the curves after \(\bar\tau\) by the exponential continuation in
\(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}lecam\mbox{-}pair}\), take every censoring
time to be infinity, and take all observed patient records independently.  Denote
the resulting laws by \(Q_{n,u}\).

We verify membership rather than treating (3)--(4) as proof of it.  For the pre
curves, (4) can be written as
\(e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+A_{0,1}(t)x\) with
\(x=c_nz_m^{(N_0,1)}\) for a donor and
\(x=c_nu\bar h\) for the target.  For the post curves the same representation
holds with \(x=z_m^{(N_0,1)}\) or \(x=u\bar h\).  By (2), by the definition of
\(R_1\), and by
\(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\), all these
choices obey \(|x|\leq R_1\).  That assumption therefore gives endpoint
normalization, monotonicity, event overlap, the failure-density floor, and the
required Hölder hazard bound.  Infinite censoring gives \(G\equiv1\), which obeys
censoring overlap because the same assumption states \(g_-\leq1\).  Independence,
independent censoring, and control consistency hold by construction.

The loading bounds follow from (1), (2), and \(R_1\leq M_z\).  The factor bounds
follow from the corresponding bounds in
\(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\), because
\(0<c_n\leq1\).  More explicitly, the unique joint contrast energy and the
pre-period Gram are
\[
\int_{\mathcal I}(b_{0,n}^2+b_{1,n}^2)
=(c_n^2a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2)\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2,
\qquad
H_{0,n}=c_n^2a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2.
\tag{5}
\]
The first quantity is at least
\(a_{\circ,1}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2>0\) and is at most \(\gamma_+^2\); the
second is positive for every triple and tends to zero.  Consequently
\(Q_{n,u}\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)\), the donor contrast rank is one,
and its joint singular value is uniformly bounded below even though its pre-Gram
has no positive uniform lower bound.

\emph{Step 2: fixed target separation and vanishing information.}
The donor laws are identical under the two signs.  The target-post cohort is not
observed.  Hence the observed laws differ only through the \(K_T\) target-pre
failure times.  Their causal target curves are
\[
\theta_u(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+u\bar h a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t),
\]
and therefore
\[
\Delta_\star:=\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
=2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0.
\tag{6}
\]
For one target-pre patient, the two densities differ only on
\(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), where
\[
f_u(t)=\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
-u c_n a_{\circ,0}\bar h\psi_\circ'(t).
\tag{7}
\]
The density floor in
\(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\), applied with
\(x=-c_n\bar h\), gives \(f_-(t)\geq c_f\) wherever the numerator below is
nonzero.  The elementary inequality \(\log x\leq x-1\) yields the chi-square
upper bound on relative entropy,
\[
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
&\leq\int\frac{\{f_+(t)-f_-(t)\}^2}{f_-(t)}\,dt\\
&\leq\frac{4c_n^2a_{\circ,0}^2\bar h^2}{c_f}
\int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2\,dt.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Product additivity of relative entropy and equality of every other observed-data
factor now give, for a fixed finite constant \(C_0\),
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{n,+}\|Q_{n,-})
\leq C_0K_Tc_n^2=C_0K_T^{-1}\longrightarrow0.
\tag{9}
\]
By the cited Pinsker inequality
\(\mathrm{lem:pinsker\mbox{-}kl\mbox{-}tv}\),
\[
\operatorname{TV}(Q_{n,+},Q_{n,-})
\leq\{C_0/(2K_T)\}^{1/2}.
\tag{10}
\]
Choose \(a_0\) large enough that the right side of (10) is at most
\(q_\alpha/2\) whenever \(K_T\geq a_0\), and also so that the construction above
is available whenever \(N_0\geq a_0\).

\emph{Step 3: honesty forces fixed width.}
For \(u\in\{-1,+1\}\), let
\[
E_u=\left\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\leq\theta_u(t)\leq
B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)\text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\right\}.
\]
Uniform honesty at the evaluated triple gives
\(Q_{n,u}(E_u)\geq1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).
Total-variation transfer and the union bound under \(Q_{n,-}\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_{n,-}(E_-\cap E_+)
&\geq1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
-\operatorname{TV}(Q_{n,+},Q_{n,-})\\
&\geq 2q_\alpha-\frac{q_\alpha}{2}-\frac{q_\alpha}{2}
=q_\alpha.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
The second line uses the defining bound
\(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\leq q_\alpha/4\) and (10).  On
\(E_-\cap E_+\), both target curves lie in the same band, so (6) gives
\(w(B)\geq\Delta_\star\).  Hence
\[
\sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}}E_Qw(B)
\geq E_{Q_{n,-}}w(B)
\geq q_\alpha\Delta_\star=:c_\star>0.
\tag{12}
\]
This constant depends only on fixed primitive constants and \(\alpha\), not on the
three sample sizes.  Conversely, the deterministic band
\(B^{\mathrm{lo}}\equiv0\), \(B^{\mathrm{up}}\equiv1\) covers every survival curve
and has width one.  Thus the minimax order over the closure is exactly one.  Since
the known-rank oracle widths in \(\mathrm{thm:sharp\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}frontier}\)
vanish on every three-dimensional tail, no uniformly honest selector over this
closure can attain both oracle widths.  The lower pair has a fixed joint contrast
singular value by (5), so a donor singular-value test cannot remove the obstruction.
\(\square\)
