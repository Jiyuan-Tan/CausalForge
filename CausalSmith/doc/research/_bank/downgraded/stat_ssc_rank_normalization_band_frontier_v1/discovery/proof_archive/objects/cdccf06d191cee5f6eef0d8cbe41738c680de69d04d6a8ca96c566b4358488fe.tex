Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), and use the canonical grid and corrected multiplier construction. On the regular spectral and pre-Gram event, the rotation-invariant functional-PCR prediction map from the interpolated cellwise product-limit inputs to \(\mathbb R^{|\mathcal G|}\) is twice Fr\'echet differentiable, with first and second derivative envelopes bounded uniformly over the exact-rank class. Its individual target-pre and donor-patient derivative weights are bounded respectively by \(C/K_T\) and \(C/n_D\). The complement of the regular event is uniformly negligible at the nonlinear-remainder scale. Consequently, for every fixed \(r\ge2\), the empirical functional-PCR estimator has a gridwise expansion
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta=Z_r+\mathfrak b_r+Q_r,
 \qquad
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le\eta_D(K_D),
\]
with the two independent patient channels displayed in \(\mathrm{lem:uniform\mbox{-}linearization}\), and
\[
 \sup_PE_P\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}^2\le Cv_r^2,
 \qquad
 \sup_PE_PE_\xi\|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_{infty,\mathcal G}^2\le C\rho_r^2,
\]
where \(v_r\) is the deterministic quantity in the definition of \(\omega_r\). If \(c_r^\xi\) is the conditional quantile in the band definition and \(\chi_r\) is the corresponding comparison quantity in the definition of \(\omega_r\), then
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Z_r\|_{infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+x\}
 \le1-q_{N_0,K_T,K_D}+C\{\chi_r/x+v_r^2/x^2\},
 \qquad x>0.
\]
In particular, for sufficiently large triples and sufficiently large fixed \(C_\omega\), the two finite-tail inequalities asserted in \(\mathrm{lem:uniform\mbox{-}linearization}\) hold and \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\) uniformly on every three-dimensional tail. No positive lower variance or anti-concentration condition is required.
