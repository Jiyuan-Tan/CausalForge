For \(N_0\ge3\), let
\[
 z_m^{(N_0,1)}=
 \frac{m-(N_0+1)/2}{\sqrt{(N_0^2-1)/12}},
 \qquad m\in\mathcal D,
\]
and fix \(\bar h=\frac12\min\{h_\circ,R_1,M_\star\}>0\), where \(R_1\) and \(M_\star\) are the fixed constants in \(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:target\mbox{-}loading\mbox{-}bound}\).  Put \(c_T=K_T^{-1}\).  For \(u\in\{-1,+1\}\), define
\[
 s_{p,T}(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t},
 \qquad
 b_{0,T}(t)=c_Ta_{\circ,0}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad
 b_{1,T}(t)=a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad
 z_\star^{(u)}=u\bar h,
\]
and, on \(\mathcal I\),
\[
 S_{p,m}^{(u)}(t)=s_{p,T}(t)+b_{p,T}(t)z_m^{(N_0,1)},
 \qquad
 S_{p,\star}^{(u)}(t)=s_{p,T}(t)+b_{p,T}(t)z_\star^{(u)}.
\]
Continue every survival curve after \(\bar\tau\) by its value at \(\bar\tau\) times \(e^{-\lambda_\circ(t-\bar\tau)}\), set all censoring times equal to infinity, and sample all observed patients independently with the cell sizes \(K_{p,m}\).  Denote the induced laws by \(Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}\), and set
\[
 \mathbb W_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)
   =(Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}).
\]
This construction only names the two laws; survival validity, class inclusion, exact rank, donor-span inclusion, singular values, pre-period weakness, target separation, and divergence are properties to be proved.
