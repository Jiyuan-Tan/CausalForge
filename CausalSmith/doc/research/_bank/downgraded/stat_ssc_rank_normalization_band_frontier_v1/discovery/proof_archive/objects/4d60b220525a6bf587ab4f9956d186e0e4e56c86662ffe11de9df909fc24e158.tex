\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).  At every sufficiently
large evaluated triple, \(\mathrm{lem:uniform\mbox{-}linearization}\) gives
\[
 \sup_PP\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}
 \le\alpha-\varepsilon/4,
 \qquad
 \sup_PP\{\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>\omega_r/2\}
 \le\varepsilon/4,                                         \tag{1}
\]
and
\(\|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}
\le\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\).  A union bound in the gridwise
expansion therefore yields
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\left\{
 \max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 \le c_r^\xi+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+\omega_r
 \right\}\ge1-\alpha.                                     \tag{2}
\]

Let \(q_\beta=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\).  The interpolation proposition and
the canonical grid give
\[
 \|\widehat\theta_r-\theta\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}
 \le\max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 +c_{\mathrm{int}}h^{q_\beta},
 \qquad h^{q_\beta}\le\rho_r.                              \tag{3}
\]
Projection onto \([0,1]\) cannot increase distance from the survival curve.
Every estimator branch is continuous after interpolation, and linear
interpolation of the band endpoints preserves the grid enclosure with the
allowance in (3).  The corrected radius is
\[
 A_r=\widetilde c_r^\xi+\tau_{r,n}
 +\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+\omega_r
 +c_{\mathrm{int}}h^{q_\beta}.                             \tag{4}
\]
Thus (2)--(4) imply
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)
 \le\theta(t)\le\widehat B_r^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
 \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\}\ge1-\alpha.          \tag{5}
\]
This is stronger than the defining \(1-\alpha-\varepsilon\) constraint, so
\(\widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r\).

Clipping endpoints can only reduce width.  From (4),
\[
 w(\widehat B_r)
 \le2\widetilde c_r^\xi+2\tau_{r,n}
 +2\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+2\omega_r
 +2c_{\mathrm{int}}h^{q_\beta}.                            \tag{6}
\]
The random-quantile theorem gives
\(\sup_PE_P\widetilde c_r^\xi\le C\rho_r\) and
\(\tau_{r,n}=o(\rho_r)\), uniformly on every three-dimensional tail.
The linearization lemma gives \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\), and (3) gives
\(h^{q_\beta}\le\rho_r\).  Taking expectations in (6) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\{\rho_r+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\}
 =C\bar\rho_r.                                             \tag{7}
\]
At rank one this is \(C(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\).  At fixed rank at least two it
is \(O(\rho_r)\) precisely on the stated subregimes; the proof makes no
unrestricted removal of \(\eta_D\) and uses no relative-growth condition.
\(\square\)
