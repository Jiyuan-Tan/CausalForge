The fixed-rank headline remains conditional on exact known rank and fixed positive spectrum and pre-Gram bounds.  Its unrestricted higher-rank upper rate is \(\bar\rho_r\), not \(\rho_r\), unless \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\).  For unknown rank, the literal global distance \(\inf_{Q\in\mathfrak P_1}\|\theta(P)-\theta(Q)\|_\infty\) is degenerate because the homogeneous model can vary its baseline curve; a positive composite-testing theorem needs a reviewed nuisance-fiber or anchored target distance.  A sharp survival-specific testing radius has not yet been proved.
