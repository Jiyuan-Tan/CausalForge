Suppose \(r=1\), put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), \(\bar G_D(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}G_{1,m}(t)\), and \(H_D(t)=s_1(t)\bar G_D(t)\). For the continuously interpolated ordinary pooled donor-post product-limit estimator in the corrected \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\), there are independent mean-zero censoring-martingale processes \(\phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}\), a deterministic bias \(b_{D,n_D}\), and a centered remainder \(q_{D,n_D}\) such that, on the inference grid,
\[
 \widehat\theta_1-\theta
 =n_D^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\sum_{j=1}^{K_D}
 \phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}+b_{D,n_D}+q_{D,n_D},
\]
\[
 \|b_{D,n_D}\|_\infty\le Ce^{-c_0n_D},
 \qquad E\|q_{D,n_D}\|_\infty^2\le C\{n_D^{-1}+e^{-c_0n_D}\}^2,
\]
uniformly over the rank-one class although donor censoring laws may differ. If \(Z_1=n_D^{-1}\sum_{m,j}\phi_{m,j}^{\mathrm{pool}}\) and \(\widehat Z_1^\xi\) is the explicit pooled aggregate-risk multiplier, then
\[
 \sup_PE_P\|Z_1\|_\infty^2\le Cn_D^{-1},
 \qquad
 \sup_PE_PE_\xi\|\widehat Z_1^\xi\|_\infty^2\le Cn_D^{-1}.
\]
The assertions include the normalization point and require no common censoring law.
