The known-rank analysis uses cellwise Kaplan--Meier curves, covariance-normalized functional PCR, and patient-level counting-process multipliers.  The proposed unknown-rank program replaces a bare singular-value switch by a cross-fold donor-energy test, a thresholded pre-period Gram check, a target-near-null enlargement, and a full-band fallback when donor rank is visible but the target loading is unstable.
