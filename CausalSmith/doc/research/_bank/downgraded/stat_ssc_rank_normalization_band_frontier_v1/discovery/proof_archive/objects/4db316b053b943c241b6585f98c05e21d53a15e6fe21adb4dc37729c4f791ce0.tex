Suppose an affine survival panel satisfies
\[
 S^0_{p,m}(t)=s_p(t)+b_p(t)^\top z_m,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_mz_m^\top=I_d,
 \qquad d=r-1.
\]
Set \(u_p^{\mathrm{HS}}(t)=(s_p(t),b_p(t)^\top)^\top\in\mathbb R^r\) and \(v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}=(1,z_m^\top)^\top\in\mathbb R^r\). Then \(S^0_{p,m}(t)=\langle u_p^{\mathrm{HS}}(t),v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}\rangle\), the donor loading matrix \(V_D=[v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}:m\in\mathcal D]\) has full row rank \(r\), and \(v_\star^{\mathrm{HS}}\in\operatorname{span}\{v_m^{\mathrm{HS}}:m\in\mathcal D\}\). In particular, every \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) is a specialized subclass member of the Han--Shah Theorem~1 survival factor model with donor-span inclusion; this implication does not assert that Han and Shah impose the canonical normalization, exact centered rank, spectrum, pre-Gram, smoothness, or loading-envelope restrictions defining \(\mathfrak P_r\).
