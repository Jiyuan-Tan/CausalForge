With the factor space treated as known, the product-limit martingale identity and the bounded at-risk denominators supplied by survival and censoring overlap yield independent mean-zero target and donor influence sums of orders \(K_T^{-1/2}\) and \((N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\), respectively.  The obstacle is the estimated raw principal-component space.  In the scalar rank-two snapshot, write a noisy donor-pre score as \(\widehat X_m=b_0z_m+U_m\) with \(E(U_m\mid z_m)=0\) and \(E(U_m^2\mid z_m)=\sigma_U^2/K_D\), and the donor-post score as \(\widehat Y_m=b_1z_m+V_m\).  Raw least-squares FPCR has probability limit
\[
 \frac{E(\widehat X_m\widehat Y_m)}{E(\widehat X_m^2)}
 =\frac{b_0b_1}{b_0^2+\sigma_U^2/K_D},
\]
so its attenuation bias is generically of order \(K_D^{-1}\).  Along \(N_0=K_D^3\) and \(K_T=K_D^4\), the claimed \(\rho_r\) is of order \(K_D^{-2}\), and this bias cannot be absorbed by an \(o_P(\rho_r)\) remainder.  Cross-fold deattenuation removes this displayed first-order bias, but a uniform functional perturbation and multiplier theorem for that corrected map remains to be proved.
