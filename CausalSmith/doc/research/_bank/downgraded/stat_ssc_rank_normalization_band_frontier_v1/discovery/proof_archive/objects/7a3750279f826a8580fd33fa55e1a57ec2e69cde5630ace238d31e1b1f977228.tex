\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\).  If \(r=1\), normalization collapse makes the target-pre influence block identically zero.  Apply \(\mathrm{lem:pooled\mbox{-}noniid\mbox{-}km\mbox{-}linearization}\), set \(\mathfrak b_1=0\), and absorb its exponentially small deterministic product-limit bias into \(Q_1\).  If \(r\ge2\), apply \(\mathrm{lem:stabilized\mbox{-}gridwise\mbox{-}fpcr\mbox{-}transfer}\).  In both cases the patient blocks are independent and mean zero by patient independence and their censoring-martingale constructions, and we obtain, simultaneously on the grid,
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta=Z_r+\mathfrak b_r+Q_r,
 \qquad
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}
 \le\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D),                     \tag{1}
\]
with
\[
 E\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}^2\le C\upsilon_r^2.      \tag{2}
\]
The interpolated-population stability lemma proves
\[
 \theta^{\mathrm{pop}}_{r,\mathcal G}
 =\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\theta,
 \qquad
 \theta^{\mathrm{pop}}_{r,\mathcal G}(g)=\theta(g)
 \quad(g\in\mathcal G).                                \tag{3}
\]

By definition, \(\omega_r=C_\omega\varepsilon^{-1/2}\upsilon_r\).  Markov's inequality and (2) give
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>\omega_r/2\}
 \le4C\upsilon_r^2/\omega_r^2
 \le\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)/4                \tag{4}
\]
once the fixed \(C_\omega\) is chosen sufficiently large.  The random-quantile theorem gives
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le c_r^\xi\}
 \ge1-\alpha+\delta_{r,n}(\alpha)/2.                   \tag{5}
\]
For sufficiently large triples, \(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)<\alpha\), hence \(\delta_{r,n}(\alpha)=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)/2\).  Enlarging the threshold in (5) by \(\omega_r/2\) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}
 \le\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)/4.         \tag{6}
\]

It remains to check the buffer rate.  At rank one,
\[
 \frac{\upsilon_1}{\rho_1}
 \le C\{n_D^{-1/2}+n_D^{1/2}e^{-c_\dagger n_D/2}\}.   \tag{7}
\]
For \(r\ge2\), using \(\rho_r\ge K_T^{-1/2}\) and \(\rho_r\ge n_D^{-1/2}\),
\[
 \frac{\upsilon_r}{\rho_r}
 \le C\left\{K_T^{-1/2}+K_D^{-1/2}+\eta_D(K_D)
 +n_D^{-1/2}+K_T^{1/2}e^{-c_\dagger K_T/2}
 +n_D^{1/2}e^{-c_\dagger n_D/2}\right\}.               \tag{8}
\]
Multiplication by \(\varepsilon^{-1/2}\), which grows only as a square root of an iterated logarithm, preserves convergence to zero uniformly on every three-dimensional tail.  Thus \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\) without a relative-growth condition.  The expansion is gridwise by construction; \(\mathrm{prop:grid\mbox{-}interpolation}\) carries the only off-grid discrepancy through \(c_{\mathrm{int}}h^{\min\{\beta+1,2\}}\). \(\square\)
