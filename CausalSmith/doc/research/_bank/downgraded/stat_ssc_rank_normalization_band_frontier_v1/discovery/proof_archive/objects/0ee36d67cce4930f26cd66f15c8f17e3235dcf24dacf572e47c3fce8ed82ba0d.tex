Set
\[
 x_m=\frac{m-(N_0+1)/2}{\sqrt{(N_0^2-1)/12}},\qquad m\in\mathcal D.
\]
Then \(N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}x_m=0\), \(N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}x_m^2=1\), and \(\max_m|x_m|<\sqrt3\).  For \(u\in\{-1,+1\}\), put \(s_p(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}\), \(b_p(t)=a_{\circ,p}\psi_\circ(t)\), \(z_m=x_m\) for \(m\in\mathcal D\), and \(z_\star^{(u)}=u h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\).  Define \(S_{p,m}^{(u)}(t)=s_p(t)+b_p(t)z_m\) on \(\mathcal I\), continue it for \(t>\bar\tau\) by \(S_{p,m}^{(u)}(\bar\tau)e^{-\lambda_\circ(t-\bar\tau)}\), take \(C_{p,m,j}=\infty\), and let \(P_u\) be the induced observed-data law.  This construction only names the pair; class membership, density bounds, separation, and divergence are properties to be proved.
