Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), let \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\) be piecewise-linear interpolation on \(\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\), and center the functional-PCR map at the interpolated population panel \(\widetilde S^0=\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}S^0\) defined in \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\). Uniformly over \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) and all sequences with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\to\infty\), there are independent mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes \(\phi_{T,j}\) and \(\phi_{D,p,m,j}\), a deterministic grid function \(\mathfrak b_r\), and a remainder \(Q_r\) such that, simultaneously for \(g\in\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\),
\[
 \widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)=Z_r(g)+\mathfrak b_r(g)+Q_r(g),
 \qquad
 Z_r=\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}K_T^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_T}\phi_{T,j}
     +n_D^{-1}\sum_{p,m,j}\phi_{D,p,m,j},
\]
where \(\phi_{T,j}\equiv0\) when \(r=1\) and
\[
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}
 \le \mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D).
\]
The interpolated population comparison equals \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\theta\), and hence equals \(\theta\) at every grid point. For every sufficiently large evaluated triple, the finite-tail buffer and the conditional multiplier critical value in \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\) obey
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>\omega_r/2\}
 \le\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)/4,
\]
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}
 \le\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)/4.
\]
Moreover \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\) uniformly on every three-dimensional tail without a restriction on the relative growth of \(K_T\) and \(N_0K_D\). The expansion and both tail inequalities are gridwise; the interpolation perturbation inside the \(L^2\) factor map is used to establish spectrum and Gram stability, while the discrepancy between \(S^0\) and \(\widetilde S^0\) away from the grid is carried only by \(c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{\min\{\beta+1,2\}}\) in the continuous-time band.
