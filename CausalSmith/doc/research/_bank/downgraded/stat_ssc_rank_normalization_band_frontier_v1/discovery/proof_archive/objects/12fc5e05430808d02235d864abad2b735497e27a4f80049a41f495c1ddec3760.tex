For an observed control cell with \(k\) patients and \(k\) large enough, let \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\) denote piecewise-linear interpolation on \(\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\).  Its linearly interpolated Kaplan--Meier curve admits the uniform decomposition
\[
 \widehat S^{\mathrm{KM}}_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m}
 =k^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{k}\varphi_{p,m,j}+b_{p,m,k}+q_{p,m,k}+a_{p,m,\mathcal G}
 \quad\hbox{in }\ell^\infty(\mathcal I),
\]
where the \(\varphi_{p,m,j}\) are independent, mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes, \(\|b_{p,m,k}\|_\infty\le C_0e^{-c_0k}\), \(E q_{p,m,k}=0\), and \(E\|q_{p,m,k}\|_\infty^2\le C_0k^{-2}\), uniformly over the model class.  The deterministic interpolation remainder is
\[
 a_{p,m,\mathcal G}=\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}S^0_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m},
 \qquad
 \|a_{p,m,\mathcal G}\|_\infty
 \le C_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{q_\beta},
 \qquad q_\beta=\min\{\beta+1,2\},
\]
where \(C_{\mathrm{int}}=C_{\mathrm{int}}(L,\bar\tau,\beta)<\infty\).  Here \(c_0=s_-g_-/3\), and \(C_0\) may be chosen as the conservative fixed constant used in the definition of \(\eta_D\).  For independent donor cells,
\[
 \left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}q_{p,m,k}\right\|_\infty
 =O_P(N_0^{-1/2}k^{-1})
 =o_P((N_0k)^{-1/2}),
\]
whereas averaging the bias terms or interpolation remainders need not improve their respective deterministic bounds.  For the deterministic odd--even donor split in \(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\), the smaller half has size \(\lfloor K_D/2\rfloor\), so its exact exponential allowance is \(C_0\exp\{-c_0\lfloor K_D/2\rfloor\}\), the corresponding term in \(\eta_D\).
