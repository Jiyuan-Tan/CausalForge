\textbf{Proof.}
Put
\[
 c_T=K_T^{-1},
 \qquad \bar h=\frac12\min\{h_\circ,R_1,M_\star\}>0,
 \qquad q_\alpha=\frac12-\alpha>0.                       \tag{1}
\]
For an arbitrary \(Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\), the scalar donor loadings satisfy
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}^2=1. \tag{2}
\]
Thus, for \(V_{D,Q}=[(1,\zeta_{m,Q})^\top:m\in\mathcal D]\),
\[
 N_0^{-1}V_{D,Q}V_{D,Q}^\top=I_2.                       \tag{3}
\]
The donor loading matrix has full row rank, its columns span \(\mathbb R^2\), and \((1,\zeta_{\star,Q})^\top\) belongs to that donor span.  The visibility lower bound makes the joint contrast nonzero.  By the cited Han--Shah scope lemma, every member therefore satisfies the factorization-and-span structure of their Theorem~1; no positive pre-period Gram is attributed to that theorem.

For the weak pair, direct summation gives
\[
 \sum_{m=1}^{N_0}\left\{m-\frac{N_0+1}{2}\right\}=0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{m=1}^{N_0}\left\{m-\frac{N_0+1}{2}\right\}^2
 =\frac{N_0(N_0^2-1)}{12},
\]
and hence
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_m\widetilde z_m=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_m\widetilde z_m^2=1.              \tag{4}
\]
Multiplying both factors and loadings by \(\varsigma_T\) preserves their products and imposes the canonical sign.

For a donor, the pre curve is the witness curve \(\ell_{0,x,0}\) with \(x=c_T\widetilde z_m\), and the post curve is \(\ell_{1,x,0}\) with \(x=\widetilde z_m\).  For the target the corresponding coordinates are \(c_Tu\bar h\) and \(u\bar h\).  Since \(c_T\le1\), \(\max_m|\widetilde z_m|\le R_1\), and \(\bar h\le R_1/2\wedge M_\star/2\), the explicit witness-compatibility assumption supplies survival normalization, values in \([s_-,1]\), monotonicity, the density floor, H\"older hazard regularity, and the factor envelope.  Infinite censoring gives \(G=1\ge g_-\); consistency, independent censoring, and mutual patient independence hold by construction.  Thus both laws belong to \(\mathfrak Q_{\le2}\).

Using (4), their joint contrast energy and pre-period Gram are
\[
 \sigma_T=\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\}^{1/2}
 \|\psi_\circ\|_2,
 \qquad
 H_{0,T}=K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2.      \tag{5}
\]
Therefore \(\sigma_T\ge|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0\), while \(H_{0,T}\to0\), and the visibility threshold is satisfied.  Consequently
\[
 \{Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\}
 \subset\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}.                   \tag{6}
\]

For a fixed pre-period grid, put
\[
 a=(e^{-\lambda_\circ t_\ell})_{\ell=1}^{T_0},
 \qquad q=(\psi_\circ(t_\ell))_{\ell=1}^{T_0}.
\]
The donor pre-period matrix is
\[
 M_{0,T}=a\mathbf1^\top+c_Ta_{\circ,0}q\widetilde z^\top. \tag{7}
\]
The right vectors are orthogonal by (4).  If \(a\) and \(q\) are linearly independent, (7) has rank two and
\[
 s_1(M_{0,T})\ge\sqrt{N_0}\|a\|_2,
 \qquad
 s_2(M_{0,T})\le c_T|a_{\circ,0}|\sqrt{N_0}\|q\|_2,
\]
so
\[
 \frac{s_1(M_{0,T})}{s_2(M_{0,T})}
 \ge K_T\frac{\|a\|_2}{|a_{\circ,0}|\|q\|_2}.          \tag{8}
\]
The condition number diverges and Han--Shah Assumption~7 fails.  If \(q=\kappa a\ne0\), the pre latent time direction is \((1,\varsigma_Tc_Ta_{\circ,0}\kappa)^\top\), whereas the post direction is \((1,\varsigma_Ta_{\circ,1}\kappa)^\top\).  They differ for all large \(K_T\), so Assumption~8 fails.  If \(q=0\), the finite grid sees no perturbation although \(\psi_\circ\) is nonzero between grid points.  These exhaustive cases show that the lower pair does not contradict Han--Shah Theorem~2.

All observed donor cells agree under the two signs and the target-post cell is unobserved.  The observed laws differ only through the \(K_T\) independent target-pre failures.  Their target curves satisfy
\[
 \Delta_\star=\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
 =2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0.        \tag{9}
\]
On \([0,\bar\tau]\), one target-pre density is
\[
 f_u(t)=\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
 -u c_Ta_{\circ,0}\bar h\psi_\circ'(t).                \tag{10}
\]
The densities agree outside \(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), and the witness condition gives \(f_-\ge c_f\) on that support.  The inequality \(\log x\le x-1\) and equality of total masses give
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
 \le\int\frac{(f_+-f_-)^2}{f_-}
 \le\frac{4c_T^2a_{\circ,0}^2\bar h^2}{c_f}
 \int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2dt.             \tag{11}
\]
Product additivity over the target-pre patients yields
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\|Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le C_0/K_T.                                           \tag{12}
\]
The Pinsker inequality, correctly located at Tsybakov's Lemma~2.5(i), gives
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le(C_0/(2K_T))^{1/2}=O(K_T^{-1/2}).                   \tag{13}
\]

Let \(B\) satisfy the theorem's honesty constraint, and let \(E_u\) be simultaneous containment of \(\theta_u\).  Honesty, total-variation transfer, and the union bound give
\[
 Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_-\cap E_+)
 \ge1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
 -\operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}). \tag{14}
\]
Because \(\varepsilon\le q_\alpha/4\), (13)--(14) are at least \(q_\alpha\) for every sufficiently large triple.  On \(E_-\cap E_+\), \(w(B)\ge\Delta_\star\).  Using (6),
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge q_\alpha\Delta_\star=:c_\star>0.                 \tag{15}
\]
Taking the infimum proves the lower bound.  The deterministic band \([0,1]\) is honest and has width one, proving the upper bound.

Thus donor-visible known rank two and donor-span inclusion coexist with order-one minimax honest width when target-relevant pre-period strength vanishes.  This is a generic information-theoretic boundary: donor-rank visibility, or a donor-only retained-rank decision, cannot by itself certify a shrinking honest band.  Agarwal--Shah--Shen is used only as PCR/HSVT prediction-preprocessing context and is not attributed an honest-band claim. \(\square\)
