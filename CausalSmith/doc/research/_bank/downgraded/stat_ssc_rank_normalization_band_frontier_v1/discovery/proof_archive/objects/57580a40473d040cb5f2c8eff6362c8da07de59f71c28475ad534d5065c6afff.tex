For a law satisfying \(\mathrm{ass:normalized\mbox{-}factor\mbox{-}model}\), first set \(s_p(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}S^0_{p,m}(t)\). Write the singular-value decomposition of the primitive centered donor operator as
\[
 \mathcal C_D=\sum_{k=1}^d\sigma_k u_k\otimes v_k,
\]
ordered by decreasing \(\sigma_k\), with deterministic sign and tie-breaking rules. Define the donor-SVD coordinates by
\[
 z_{m,k}=\sqrt{N_0}v_{k,m},
 \qquad
 b_{p,k}(t)=\sigma_k u_{k,p}(t),
 \qquad
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_mz_m^\top=I_d.
\]
For \(d>0\), put
\[
 H_0=\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)b_0(t)^\top\,dt
 \quad\hbox{and define}\quad
 z_\star=H_0^{-1}\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)
 \{S^0_{0,\star}(t)-s_0(t)\}\,dt.
\]
For \(d=0\), define \(z_\star\) to be the unique zero-dimensional vector. The construction is conditional on the already imposed compatible affine factorization for all \(m\in\mathcal M\); \(\mathrm{lem:canonical\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}coordinate}\) proves that covariance normalization changes any compatible donor coordinates only by an orthogonal transform, that \(H_0\) is positive definite under \(\mathrm{ass:pre\mbox{-}contrast\mbox{-}gram}\), and that the displayed formula is the unique coefficient satisfying \(S^0_{0,\star}-s_0=b_0^\top z_\star\). Endpoint normalization gives \(s_p(0)=1\) and \(b_p(0)=0\).
