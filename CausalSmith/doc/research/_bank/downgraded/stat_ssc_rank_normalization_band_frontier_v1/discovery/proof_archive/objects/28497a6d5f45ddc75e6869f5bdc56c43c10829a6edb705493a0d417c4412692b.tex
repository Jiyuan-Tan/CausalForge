\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\) and \(k_D=\lfloor K_D/2\rfloor\).  Apply
\(\mathrm{lem:stabilized\mbox{-}product\mbox{-}limit\mbox{-}cell}\) to
the target-pre cell, every full donor cell, and both deterministic donor
half-folds.  Each interpolated input has the exact decomposition
\[
 \widehat S^\dagger=\widetilde S^0+L+b+q,                \tag{1}
\]
where \(L\) is an independent-patient mean-zero martingale average,
\(\|b\|_\infty\leq Ce^{-c_0k}\), and
\(E\|q\|_\infty^2\leq Ck^{-2}\).  The odd and even folds are independent.

We first prove the full donor-average bound for \(q\).  Suppress the period
and fold labels temporarily.  The exact cell representation gives
\[
 q_m=U_m+V_m,
 \qquad
 U_m=A_{k,m}-EA_{k,m},
 \qquad
 V_m(t)=-S_m^0(t)\int_0^tD_m(u)\,dM_m(u),                \tag{2}
\]
where \(U_m\) is the centered clipped-risk drift and the predictable kernel
is explicitly
\[
 D_m(u)=\frac{R_m(u-)}{Y_m^\dagger(u)}-\frac{1}{kH_m(u)}.
                                                                    \tag{3}
\]
Thus no additional kernel or product-integral remainder is being introduced.
The cell calculation, repeated from (13)--(16) there, uses
\[
 D_m=\frac{R_m-1}{Y_m}
     -\frac{Y_m-kH_m}{kH_mY_m}
\]
on \(\{Y_m(\bar\tau)\geq kh_0/2\}\), and uses
\(|D_m|\leq C/k\) plus the Chernoff bound on its complement.  Consequently,
\[
 E\int_0^{\bar\tau}D_m(u)^2Y_m(u)\lambda_m^0(u)\,du
 \leq Ck^{-2}.                                           \tag{4}
\]
This is the extra \(k^{-1}\) carried by the product-limit remainder.  For
comparison, the first-order cell kernel is \((kH_m)^{-1}\), and hence its
predictable quadratic variation is only bounded by
\[
 E\int_0^{\bar\tau}\frac{Y_m(u)\lambda_m^0(u)}
 {k^2H_m(u)^2}\,du\leq Ck^{-1}.                          \tag{5}
\]
Thus averaging the first-order cell curves has squared-supremum order
\(C/(N_0k)\), whereas averaging the remainders in (2) will have order
\(C/(N_0k^2)\).  These are different objects.

Define the martingale
\[
 A_m(t)=\int_0^tD_m(u)\,dM_m(u).
\]
Independence across donor cells, (4), and Doob's inequality give
\[
 E\sup_{t\in\mathcal I}
 \left|N_0^{-1}\sum_mA_m(t)\right|^2
 \leq \frac{C}{N_0k^2}.                                 \tag{6}
\]
Because \(S_m^0(0)=1\) and \(S_m^0\) is deterministic under a fixed law,
integration by parts is exact:
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_mV_m(t)
 =-N_0^{-1}\sum_mA_m(t)
  +N_0^{-1}\sum_m\int_0^tA_m(u-)\,d\{1-S_m^0(u)\}.     \tag{7}
\]
We control the second term without asserting bounded variation of the
martingale remainder itself.  Put
\[
 F_m(t)=\int_0^tA_m(u-)\,d\{1-S_m^0(u)\}.
\]
The processes \(F_m\) are independent.  Since \(EA_m(u)=0\) for every
\(u\), deterministic Stieltjes Fubini gives \(EF_m(t)=0\) for every \(t\).
At each fixed \(t\), expanding the donor square therefore cancels every
cross term.  To retain that cancellation after taking a supremum, apply the
following time-ordered calculation.  For independent centered
bounded-variation processes \(G_m\), symmetrization, the Jordan decomposition,
an auxiliary draw from each positive or negative variation measure, and
Doob's inequality for the donor-index partial sums give
\[
 E\left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_mG_m\right\|_\infty^2
 \leq\frac{16}{N_0^2}\sum_mE\{\operatorname{TV}(G_m)^2\}. \tag{8}
\]
Indeed, after conditioning on the paths and auxiliary times, ordering those
times converts every nested indicator \(\mathbf 1\{T_m\leq t\}\) into a
partial-sum martingale; the positive and negative variation components are
then combined by Cauchy--Schwarz.  This proves (8), rather than assuming a
maximal inequality for arbitrary paths.

Here the total variation is controlled directly by the integrator:
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(F_m)
 \leq \|A_m\|_\infty\operatorname{TV}(S_m^0)
 \leq\|A_m\|_\infty.                                    \tag{9}
\]
Equations (4), (8), (9), and Doob's inequality yield
\[
 E\sup_{t\in\mathcal I}
 \left|N_0^{-1}\sum_m
 \int_0^tA_m(u-)\,d\{1-S_m^0(u)\}\right|^2
 \leq\frac{C}{N_0k^2}.                                  \tag{10}
\]
This is precisely the finite-variation integration-by-parts cross-term.
Combining (6), (7), and (10) gives
\[
 E\left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_mV_m\right\|_\infty^2
 \leq\frac{C}{N_0k^2}.                                  \tag{11}
\]
The last assertion of the cell lemma, proved by the same time-ordered
bounded-variation argument on the rare drift itself, gives
\[
 E\left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_mU_m\right\|_\infty^2
 \leq CN_0^{-1}e^{-c_0k}.                               \tag{12}
\]
Therefore the complete centered cell remainder satisfies
\[
 E\left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_mq_m\right\|_\infty^2
 \leq\frac{C}{N_0k^2}+CN_0^{-1}e^{-c_0k}.               \tag{13}
\]
Interpolation is a supremum-norm contraction, so (13) holds on continuous
time after interpolation.  No grid-cardinality factor and no all-cells-good
event have entered.  The deterministic half-fold bias is bounded by
\(Ce^{-c_0k_D}\), which is dominated by \(\eta_D(K_D)\).

Let \(\Gamma_{\mathcal G}\) be the interpolated population donor operator.
By
\(\mathrm{lem:interpolated\mbox{-}population\mbox{-}fpcr\mbox{-}stability}\),
its selected spectrum has a fixed positive gap and its pre-period Gram has
minimum eigenvalue at least \(\kappa_-/2\) for every sufficiently fine
canonical grid.  Cross-fold independence, (1), and (13) give
\[
 \|E\widehat\Gamma-\Gamma_{\mathcal G}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 \leq C\{e^{-c_0k_D}+K_D^{-1}\}=o(1).                   \tag{14}
\]
In particular, cross-folding removes the same-fold errors-in-variables mean;
the displayed terms are the clipped deterministic means and a conservative
bound for the centered second-order terms.

We next establish aggregate operator regularity.  By the deterministic
sensitivity conclusion of the cell lemma, replacing one donor record changes
its stabilized interpolated curve by \(C/K_D\).  Donor centering is linear,
all input curves are bounded, and
\(\|x\otimes y\|_{\mathrm{HS}}=\|x\|_2\|y\|_2\).  Hence, if
\(\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\) is formed after replacing donor record \(i\),
\[
 \|\widehat\Gamma-\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 \leq\frac{C}{N_0K_D},
 \qquad
 \sum_{i=1}^{2N_0K_D}
 \|\widehat\Gamma-\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \leq\frac{C}{n_D}.                                     \tag{15}
\]
Applying the Hilbert-space Efron--Stein identity coordinatewise in an
orthonormal Hilbert--Schmidt basis and summing by monotone convergence gives
\[
 E\|\widehat\Gamma-E\widehat\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \leq\frac12\sum_iE
 \|\widehat\Gamma-\widehat\Gamma^{(i)}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \leq\frac{C}{n_D}.                                     \tag{16}
\]
The scalar map
\(O\mapsto\|\widehat\Gamma-E\widehat\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{HS}}\) has the
same bounded differences as (15).  Jensen's inequality and (16), followed by
scalar McDiarmid inequality, therefore imply
\[
 P\left\{\|\widehat\Gamma-E\widehat\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
 >Cn_D^{-1/2}+x\right\}
 \leq e^{-cn_Dx^2},
 \qquad x>0.                                             \tag{17}
\]
Equations (14), (17), the variational inequality for eigenvalues, and the
resolvent identity for the selected projector imply that the empirical
selected spectrum remains in the fixed-gap neighborhood except on an event
of probability \(Ce^{-cn_D}\).  The positive-square-root map is Lipschitz on
that neighborhood.  Restriction to the pre-period component and
\[
 A^{-1}-B^{-1}=A^{-1}(B-A)B^{-1}                          \tag{18}
\]
give the empirical pre-Gram threshold with the same probability.  Thus, for
the aggregate regular event \(\mathcal R\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P(\mathcal R^c)\leq Ce^{-cn_D}. \tag{19}
\]
This is one aggregate concentration bound; there is no union over donor cells.

We now differentiate the coordinate-free prediction map on \(\mathcal R\).
Let \(\Pi(\Gamma)\) be the selected spectral projector, let
\(A=\Gamma_+^{1/2}\) on its selected range, and let \(E_p\) restrict a
joint-period function to period \(p\).  Write
\[
 A_p=E_pA,\qquad H=A_0^*A_0,\qquad
 z=H^{-1}A_0^*x,\qquad F(s,A,x)=s_1+A_1z.                \tag{20}
\]
For a differentiable path, direct differentiation gives
\[
 \dot H=\dot A_0^*A_0+A_0^*\dot A_0,
 \qquad
 \dot z=H^{-1}\{\dot A_0^*x+A_0^*\dot x-\dot Hz\},     \tag{21}
\]
\[
 \dot F=\dot s_1+\dot A_1z+A_1\dot z.                  \tag{22}
\]
Differentiating once more gives
\[
 \ddot H=\ddot A_0^*A_0+A_0^*\ddot A_0
          +2\dot A_0^*\dot A_0,
\]
\[
 \ddot z=H^{-1}\{\ddot A_0^*x+2\dot A_0^*\dot x
 +A_0^*\ddot x-\ddot Hz-2\dot H\dot z\},               \tag{23}
\]
\[
 \ddot F=\ddot s_1+\ddot A_1z+2\dot A_1\dot z+A_1\ddot z. \tag{24}
\]
The selected-projector derivative is
\[
 D\Pi_\Gamma[E]
 =\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{\mathscr C}
 (w-\Gamma)^{-1}E(w-\Gamma)^{-1}\,dw,                  \tag{25}
\]
where \(\mathscr C\) separates the selected spectrum from zero.  The
positive-square-root derivative is the unique selected-range solution of
\[
 A\,DA_\Gamma[E]+DA_\Gamma[E]A
 =D\{\Pi\Gamma\Pi\}_\Gamma[E].                          \tag{26}
\]
The contour distance, selected eigenvalue lower bound, and pre-Gram lower bound
are fixed by the centered-spectrum and pre-contrast-Gram assumptions.
Differentiating (25)--(26) once more and using (18) therefore gives
\[
 \|DA_\Gamma[E]\|\leq C\|E\|,
 \qquad
 \|D^2A_\Gamma[E_1,E_2]\|
 \leq C\|E_1\|\|E_2\|.                                 \tag{27}
\]
Equations (21)--(27), the factor envelope, and the target-loading bound show
that the first two Fr\'echet derivatives of \(F\) are uniformly bounded on the
regular neighborhood, including at internal eigenvalue ties.

Composition with the stabilized-cell sensitivity bound gives, on every
dataset in \(\mathcal R\),
\[
 \|D_j^TF\|_\infty\leq C/K_T,
 \qquad
 \|D_{p,m,j}^DF\|_\infty\leq C/(N_0K_D).                \tag{28}
\]
Taylor's formula with integral remainder, (1), (13), (19), and (27)--(28)
now gives, simultaneously on the grid,
\[
 \widehat\theta_r-\theta
 =K_T^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{K_T}\phi_{T,j}
  +n_D^{-1}\sum_{p,m,j}\phi_{D,p,m,j}
  +\mathfrak b_r+Q_r.                                    \tag{29}
\]
The population center in (29) is the causal curve \(\theta\) at every grid
point: the interpolated-population stability lemma proves
\(\theta^{\mathrm{pop}}_{r,\mathcal G}(g)=\theta(g)\), while
\(\mathrm{lem:canonical\mbox{-}target\mbox{-}coordinate}\) validates the
unique target loading used by that population map.  The linear terms in (29)
are independent and mean zero.  The deterministic half-fold means satisfy
\[
 \|\mathfrak b_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\leq\eta_D(K_D).  \tag{30}
\]
Writing \(\upsilon_r\) for the second-order scale in the corrected definition
of \(\omega_r\), the preceding bounds give, up to a fixed model-class
constant,
\[
 \upsilon_r
 =K_T^{-1}+N_0^{-1/2}K_D^{-1}+n_D^{-1}
  +\rho_r\eta_D(K_D)+e^{-cK_T}+e^{-cn_D}.                \tag{31}
\]
Here the exponential constants may be decreased so that the square roots of
the target-cell and aggregate fallback probabilities are absorbed by the last
two displayed terms.  The first three terms in (31) are respectively the
target-pre quadratic remainder, the donor average in (13), and the donor
quadratic Taylor term; the fourth is the bias--linear interaction.  Therefore
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_P
 \|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}^2\leq C\upsilon_r^2.        \tag{32}
\]

Finally, composing (22) with the patient martingale influences produces a
fixed finite sum, depending only on \(r\), of processes of the form
\[
 a(t)M(t)+\int_0^tK(t,u)\,dM(u),                         \tag{33}
\]
where the coefficient curves and their time-ordered total variations are
bounded by the overlap, smoothness, spectral-gap, loading-envelope, and
pre-Gram constants.  Stochastic integration by parts, the predictable
isometry, and Doob's inequality bound the expected squared continuous
supremum by the sum of squared patient weights.  Equation (28) gives
\[
 \sup_PE_P\|Z_r\|_\infty^2
 \leq C\{K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\}.                            \tag{34}
\]
Conditionally on the records, the multiplier process has the same ordered
representation.  On \(\mathcal R\), (28) proves the analogue of (34) with
\(E_\xi\).  On \(\mathcal R^c\), the ordinary pooled fallback derivative
has conditional squared supremum at most \(Cn_D\); (19) and
\(n_De^{-cn_D}\leq Cn_D^{-1}\) absorb that contribution.  Consequently,
\[
 \sup_PE_PE_\xi\|\widehat Z_r^\xi\|_\infty^2
 \leq C\{K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\}.                            \tag{35}
\]
The constant in (34)--(35) is a deterministic function only of the fixed
model constants and \(r\), so it may be dominated by the explicit guard
constant registered in the corrected band definition.  Because (33)--(35)
control the continuous supremum before restriction to the grid, no
\(\sqrt{\log|\mathcal G|}\) factor occurs.  Since
\[
 K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\leq\rho_r^2
 \leq2\{K_T^{-1}+n_D^{-1}\},
\]
the asserted moment bound follows.  This completes the proof. \(\square\)
