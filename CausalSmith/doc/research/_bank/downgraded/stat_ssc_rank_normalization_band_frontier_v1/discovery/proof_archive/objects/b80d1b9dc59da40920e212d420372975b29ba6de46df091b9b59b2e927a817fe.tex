Put \(q=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\), \(a_r=\min\{\bar\tau,\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)^{1/q}\}\), and
\[
 M_r=\left\lceil\frac{\bar\tau}{a_r}\right\rceil,
 \qquad
 \mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}
 =\left\{\frac{\ell\bar\tau}{M_r}:0\le\ell\le M_r\right\}.
\]
Use piecewise-linear interpolation between adjacent knots and define the mesh by
\[
 h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}=\frac{\bar\tau}{M_r}.
\]
Then \(0,\bar\tau\in\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\),
\[
 2^{-q}\min\{\bar\tau^q,\rho_r\}
 \le h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^q
 \le\min\{\bar\tau^q,\rho_r\},
 \qquad
 |\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}|
 \le2+\bar\tau\rho_r^{-1/q}.
\]
Thus the interpolation error is rate matched and the grid dimension is explicitly polynomial in \(\rho_r^{-1}\).
