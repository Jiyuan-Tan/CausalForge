For \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\le2}^{\triangle}(N_0,K_T,K_D)\), define the target-metric distance to the rank-one model by
\[
 D_\theta(P,\mathfrak P_1)
 =\inf_{Q\in\mathfrak P_1(N_0,K_T,K_D)}
   \|\theta(P)-\theta(Q)\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}.
\]
This is the literal target-space distance used in the Carpentier--Klopp--L\"offler--Nickl testing reduction; it is distinct from a singular-value distance and from a distance between observed-data laws.
