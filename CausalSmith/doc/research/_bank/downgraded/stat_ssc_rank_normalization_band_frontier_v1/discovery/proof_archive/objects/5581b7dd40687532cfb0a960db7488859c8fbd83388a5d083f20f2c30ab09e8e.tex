Set
\[
 s_p(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}S^0_{p,m}(t).
\]
Start from the model's target-compatible all-unit affine representation, center its donor loadings, covariance-normalize those loadings, and apply the inverse normalization to the factor curves. Write the singular-value decomposition of the resulting primitive centered operator as
\[
 \mathcal C_D=\sum_{k=1}^d\sigma_k u_k\otimes v_k,
\]
ordered by decreasing \(\sigma_k\) with deterministic sign and tie-breaking rules, and define
\[
 z_{m,k}=\sqrt{N_0}v_{k,m},
 \qquad b_{p,k}(t)=\sigma_k u_{k,p}(t).
\]
Thus the construction uses the donor-centered normalization
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_mz_m^\top=I_d.
\]
Let
\[
 H_0=\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)b_0(t)^\top\,dt.
\]
Conditional on the model's target-pre factor compatibility
\(S^0_{0,\star}-s_0\in\operatorname{span}\{b_{0,1},\ldots,b_{0,d}\}\), define the shared target coordinate by
\[
 z_\star=H_0^{-1}\int_{\mathcal I}b_0(t)
 \{S^0_{0,\star}(t)-s_0(t)\}\,dt.
\]
The positive canonical pre-period Gram makes this coordinate unique. Transporting the compatible all-unit representation through the same covariance normalization and orthogonal donor-SVD transform gives
\[
 S^0_{p,m}(t)=s_p(t)+b_p(t)^\top z_m
\]
for both periods and every donor or target unit. Survival normalization gives \(s_p(0)=1\) and \(b_p(0)=0\). When \(d=0\), compatibility forces every contrast to vanish and all vectors, sums, and Gram matrices use the zero-dimensional convention.
