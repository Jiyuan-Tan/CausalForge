\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(r\) and an evaluated triple above the larger cutoff in the attaining
theorem and the normalization-preserving converse.  Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\).
The converse states that every admissible band satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_1}E_Pw(B)\ge c_1n_D^{-1/2}
 \quad(r=1),                                                \tag{1}
\]
and, for fixed \(r\ge2\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(B)
 \ge c_r\{K_T^{-1/2}+n_D^{-1/2}\}=c_r\rho_r.             \tag{2}
\]
Taking the infimum over the same per-triple admissible class used in the
definition of \(W_r^*\) gives
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\ge c_r\rho_r.                  \tag{3}
\]

For the reverse inequality, the attaining theorem supplies one explicit
measurable band \(\widehat B_r\), common to every law at the evaluated
triple, for which
\[
 \widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha),
 \qquad
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)\le C_r\bar\rho_r. \tag{4}
\]
Using this data-dependent, non-oracle band as a candidate in the infimum
defining \(W_r^*\) yields
\[
 W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\le C_r\bar\rho_r.              \tag{5}
\]
At rank one, \(\bar\rho_1=\rho_1=n_D^{-1/2}\), so (3)--(5) give
\[
 W_1^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)\asymp(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}.          \tag{6}
\]
For fixed \(r\ge2\), they give the unrestricted bracket
\[
 c_r\{K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\}
 \le W_r^*(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 \le C_r\{K_T^{-1/2}+(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}+\eta_D(K_D)\}.       \tag{7}
\]
If \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\), the two sides of (7) have the same order.
Without that subregime condition, (7) is the proved conclusion and does not
assert tightness.  Both input theorems are uniform on three-dimensional
tails and impose no relative-growth restriction, so the same is true of
(6)--(7).  Every infimum is evaluated under the coverage constraint at that
same sample-size triple. \(\square\)
