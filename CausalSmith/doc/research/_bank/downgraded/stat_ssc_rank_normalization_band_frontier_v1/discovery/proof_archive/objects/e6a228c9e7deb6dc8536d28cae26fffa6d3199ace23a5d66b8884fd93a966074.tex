\textbf{Proof.}
For an evaluated triple, let \(\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\) denote the
donor-visible known-rank-two class used in
\(\mathrm{thm:donor\mbox{-}visible\mbox{-}known\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}minimax\mbox{-}boundary}\).
That theorem proves both the inclusion
\[
 \mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}(N_0,K_T,K_D)
 \subseteq \mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)                \tag{1}
\]
and the following fixed lower bound.  There are constants
\(a_{\mathrm{vis}}<\infty\) and \(c_{\mathrm{vis}}>0\), independent of
the three sample sizes, such that every band \(B\) satisfying
\[
 \inf_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}
 Q\!\left\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta_Q(t)
             \le B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
             \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\right\}
 \ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)             \tag{2}
\]
obeys
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Q\{w(B)\}
 \ge c_{\mathrm{vis}}.                                    \tag{3}
\]
The sibling theorem derives (1)--(3) from its normalization-preserving
weak-pre-Gram pair; in particular, it proves the pair's class membership,
canonical reorientation, full-law likelihood bound, and fixed target
separation rather than assuming those properties.

Now suppose \(B\) satisfies the honesty premise of the present theorem.
By (1), restricting its coverage infimum to the smaller class gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \inf_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}
 Q\!\left\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta_Q(t)
             \le B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
             \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\right\}
 &\ge
 \inf_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}}
 Q\!\left\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta_Q(t)
             \le B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
             \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\right\}.
                                                                    \tag{4}
 \end{aligned}
\]
Thus (2) holds.  Applying (3), and enlarging the supremum by (1), yields
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}}E_Q\{w(B)\}
 \ge
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Q\{w(B)\}
 \ge c_{\mathrm{vis}}.                                    \tag{5}
\]
Taking \(a_0=a_{\mathrm{vis}}\) and \(c_\star=c_{\mathrm{vis}}\) proves
the asserted lower bound.

For the matching upper bound, take the deterministic band
\[
 B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)=0,
 \qquad
 B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)=1,
 \qquad t\in\mathcal I.                                   \tag{6}
\]
Every target in the closure is a survival curve with values in \([0,1]\),
so (6) has coverage one under every \(Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}\), and
\(w(B)=1\).  Hence the closure minimax maximal expected width is at most
one.  Combining this fact with (5) proves that its sharp order is one.

We finally make the oracle-rate implication explicit.  Along the diagonal
sequence \(N_0=K_T=K_D=k\),
\[
 \rho_1(k,k,k)=k^{-1},
 \qquad
 \rho_2(k,k,k)=k^{-1/2}+k^{-1},                            \tag{7}
\]
and the exponential definition of \(\eta_D\) gives
\[
 \eta_D(k)=C_\eta e^{-c_0\lfloor k/2\rfloor}
 =O\{\rho_2(k,k,k)\}.                                     \tag{8}
\]
Therefore \(\mathrm{thm:sharp\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}frontier}\) shows that
the known-rank oracle widths on the strong fixed-rank classes satisfy
\[
 W_1^*(k,k,k;\alpha)\asymp k^{-1},
 \qquad
 W_2^*(k,k,k;\alpha)\asymp k^{-1/2}+k^{-1},               \tag{9}
\]
and both vanish.  A rank-adaptive band honest over
\(\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}\) and attaining the rank-two oracle order uniformly
on its rank-two stratum would in particular have
\(\sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Q\{w(B)\}\to0\)
along this diagonal, contradicting (3).

By construction of \(\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\), every member has
contrast indicator one and invariant joint contrast energy at least
\(\tfrac12|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0\).  Under the unit empirical
variance normalization of its scalar donor loadings, this energy is the
sole nonzero singular value of the centered donor contrast operator.
Thus the obstruction in (3) is already present on an exact rank-two class
with donor-visible rank bounded away from zero.  It is the absence of a
uniform positive pre-period factor-strength bound, not failure to detect
donor rank, that prevents a uniformly shrinking honest band. \(\square\)
