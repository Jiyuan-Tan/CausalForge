Let \(\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)\) be the separately normalized
rank-at-most-two closure in
\(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}at\mbox{-}most\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}closure}\).  There are
constants \(a_0<\infty\) and \(c_\star>0\), independent of
\(N_0,K_T,K_D\), such that, whenever
\(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\geq a_0\), every random continuous-time band \(B\)
satisfying
\[
\inf_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)}
Q\left\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\leq\theta_Q(t)\leq B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
\text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\right\}
\geq1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
\]
also satisfies
\[
\sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)}E_Q\{w(B)\}\geq c_\star.
\]
The constant band with endpoints zero and one has width one and is honest, so the
corresponding minimax maximal expected width is between \(c_\star\) and \(1\).
Hence its sharp order is one.  In particular, over the no-common-pre-Gram closure
there is no single rank-selection band attaining the vanishing known-rank oracle
widths \(\rho_1\) and \(\rho_2\).  This remains true on a rank-two subfamily whose
joint donor contrast singular value is bounded away from zero: donor-rank
detectability alone does not make the target loading estimable.
