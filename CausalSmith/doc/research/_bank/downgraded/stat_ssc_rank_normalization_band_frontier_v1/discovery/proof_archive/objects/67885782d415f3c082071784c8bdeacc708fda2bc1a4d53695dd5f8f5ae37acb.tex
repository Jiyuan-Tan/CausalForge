For \(N_0\ge3\), put
\[
 \widetilde z_m=
 \frac{m-(N_0+1)/2}{\sqrt{(N_0^2-1)/12}},
 \qquad m\in\mathcal D,
 \qquad
 \bar h=\frac12\min\{h_\circ,R_1,M_\star\}>0,
 \qquad c_T=K_T^{-1},
\]
where \(R_1\) and \(M_\star\) are the fixed constants in \(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:target\mbox{-}loading\mbox{-}bound}\).  Define raw factors
\[
 \widetilde b_{0,T}(t)=c_Ta_{\circ,0}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad
 \widetilde b_{1,T}(t)=a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t).
\]
Let \(\varsigma_T\in\{-1,+1\}\) be the sign which makes the first nonzero value in the fixed countable dense sequence of probes from \(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}at\mbox{-}most\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}closure}\), applied to \((\varsigma_T\widetilde b_{0,T},\varsigma_T\widetilde b_{1,T})\), positive.  Set
\[
 s_{p,T}(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t},
 \qquad b_{p,T}=\varsigma_T\widetilde b_{p,T},
 \qquad z_m=\varsigma_T\widetilde z_m,
 \qquad z_\star^{(u)}=\varsigma_Tu\bar h,
 \qquad u\in\{-1,+1\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal I\), put
\[
 S_{p,m}^{(u)}(t)=s_{p,T}(t)+b_{p,T}(t)z_m,
 \qquad
 S_{p,\star}^{(u)}(t)=s_{p,T}(t)+b_{p,T}(t)z_\star^{(u)}.
\]
Continue every survival curve after \(\bar\tau\) by its value at \(\bar\tau\) times \(e^{-\lambda_\circ(t-\bar\tau)}\), set all censoring times equal to infinity, and sample all observed patients independently with the cell sizes \(K_{p,m}\).  Denote the induced laws by \(Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}\), and set
\[
 \mathbb W_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)
 =(Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}).
\]
This construction only names the laws.  Survival validity, class membership, exact rank, donor visibility, donor-span inclusion, target separation, and likelihood distance are properties to be proved.
