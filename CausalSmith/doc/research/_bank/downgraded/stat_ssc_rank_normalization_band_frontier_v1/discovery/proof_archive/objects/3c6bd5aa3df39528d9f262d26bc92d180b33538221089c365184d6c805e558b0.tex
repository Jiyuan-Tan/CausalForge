\textbf{Proof.}
At \(r=1\), \(d=0\).  By
\(\mathrm{lem:normalization\mbox{-}collapse}\), every factor and loading
product is an empty sum and no target-pre record enters either the causal
target or the estimator.  The estimator definition therefore sets
\(\widehat\theta_1\) exactly equal to the Kaplan--Meier curve formed by
pooling the \(N_0K_D\) donor-post patients.  Its empirical derivative is
the ordinary pooled product-limit martingale derivative.  Attaching one
independent multiplier to each patient consequently gives exactly the
patient-level simultaneous multiplier process in the rank-one branch.

The correction \(\mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\) vanishes, and
\(\mathrm{lem:uniform\mbox{-}linearization}\) gives
\(\omega_1=o((N_0K_D)^{-1/2})\).  Finally,
\(\mathrm{prop:grid\mbox{-}interpolation}\) supplies the stated
continuous-time interpolation allowance, and
\(\mathrm{thm:attaining\mbox{-}band}\) supplies its simultaneous honesty.
Thus the rank-one branch is precisely the pooled donor-post patient-level
Kaplan--Meier martingale-multiplier construction asserted in the
proposition. \(\square\)
