For \(r=1\), pool the \(N_0K_D\) donor-post patient records, set \(\widehat\theta_1\) equal to their pooled Kaplan--Meier estimator on \(\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\), and do not use target-pre records.  For \(r\ge2\), split the patients in every donor-period cell into two deterministic folds.  From the foldwise Kaplan--Meier curves form centered operators \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_D^{(1)}\) and \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_D^{(2)}\), and estimate the donor loading space by the first \(d=r-1\) eigenvectors of the deattenuated cross-Gram operator
\[
 \widehat K_D^{\mathrm{cross}}
 =\frac12\left\{(\widehat{\mathcal C}_D^{(1)})^*\widehat{\mathcal C}_D^{(2)}
 +(\widehat{\mathcal C}_D^{(2)})^*\widehat{\mathcal C}_D^{(1)}\right\}.
\]
Normalize those eigenvectors to have donor empirical Gram \(I_d\), estimate each fold's factor curves by projection of the opposite fold on these loadings, estimate \(z_\star\) by minimum-norm least squares from the target-pre Kaplan--Meier curve and the cross-fitted pre-factor curves, and average the two fold-swapped predictions \(\widehat s_1+\widehat b_1^\top\widehat z_\star\).  Linearize this cross-fitted map jointly in the patient counting-process martingales.  Replace each patient martingale increment by \(\xi_{p,m,j}\) times its estimated increment and take the conditional \((1-\alpha)\)-quantile of the resulting grid supremum.  Add and subtract that quantile and the deterministic allowance \(c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{\min\{\beta+1,2\}}\), interpolate linearly, clip to \([0,1]\), and impose the common endpoint value one at time zero.  The fold split, eigenvalue ordering, signs, generalized inverses, and quantile convention are deterministic, making \((\widehat\theta_r,\widehat B_r)\) a measurable map of the observed records and multipliers.
