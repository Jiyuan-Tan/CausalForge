Fix \(r\ge1\), put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), \(m=n_{\mathrm{eff},r}\), and let
\(a_{r,n}\) be the deterministic covariance-transfer schedule in
\(\mathrm{def:fpcr\mbox{-}multiplier\mbox{-}band}\).  Let \(\Sigma_r\) be
the covariance kernel of the oracle patient linear process \(Z_r\), let
\(\widehat\Sigma_r\) be the conditional covariance kernel of
\(\widehat Z_r^\xi\), and let \(d_r\) and \(\widehat d_r\) be their canonical
Gaussian semimetrics.  There is a measurable event \(\mathcal E_{r,n}\),
contained in the regular-margin event \(\mathcal M_{r,n}\) when \(r\ge2\),
such that, uniformly over \(P\in\mathfrak P_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)\),
\[
 P(\mathcal E_{r,n}^c)\le C a_{r,n}^{1/2},
 \qquad
 \|\widehat\Sigma_r-\Sigma_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal I^2}
 \le C\rho_r^2a_{r,n}^{1/2}\quad\hbox{on }\mathcal E_{r,n},
\]
and, for all grid points \(g,g'\),
\[
 d_r(g,g')^2+\widehat d_r(g,g')^2
 \le C\rho_r^2|g-g'|\quad\hbox{on }\mathcal E_{r,n}.
\]
Moreover the oracle summands have deterministic envelopes \(Cn_D^{-1}\) for
rank one, and \(CK_T^{-1}\) and \(Cn_D^{-1}\) for target-pre and donor
patients when \(r\ge2\).  If \(p_{\mathcal G}=2|\mathcal G|\), then for every
\(u\in[h_{N_0,K_T,K_D},\bar\tau]\),
\[
 P\left\{
 \max_{\substack{g,g'\in\mathcal G\\|g-g'|\le u}}
 |Z_r(g)-Z_r(g')|>
 C\rho_r\left[\{u\log(ep_{\mathcal G})\}^{1/2}
 +m^{-1/2}\log(ep_{\mathcal G})\right]\right\}
 \le 2p_{\mathcal G}^{-4}.
\]
The empirical semimetric assertion uses the explicitly defined branchwise
multiplier coefficients; it does not assert differentiability of the total
hard-switched map across a cutoff, and it permits singular covariance and
zero-variance coordinates.
