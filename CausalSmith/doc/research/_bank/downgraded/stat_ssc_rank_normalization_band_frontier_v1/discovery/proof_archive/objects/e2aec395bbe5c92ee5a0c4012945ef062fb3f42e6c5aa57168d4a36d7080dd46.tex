Han and Shah establish identification and finite-grid consistency for Synthetic Survival Control but do not provide uniformly honest continuous-time bands, patient-level propagation through functional PCR, or a minimax converse; their PETAL interval resamples donors only.  CKLN characterize generic confidence-set adaptation through testing and oracle estimation, but their matrix-completion experiment does not supply the censored-survival target-metric test, near-null control, or pre-Gram stability needed here.
