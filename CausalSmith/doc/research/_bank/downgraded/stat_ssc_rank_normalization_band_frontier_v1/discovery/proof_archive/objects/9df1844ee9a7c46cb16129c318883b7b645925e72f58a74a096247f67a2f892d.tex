\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(c_T=K_T^{-1}\), let \(\widetilde z_m\) be the unoriented donor scores in the weak-pair construction, put
\[
 \bar h=\frac12\min\{h_\circ,R_1,M_\star\}>0,
 \qquad q_\alpha=\frac12-\alpha>0,                       \tag{1}
\]
and suppress the fixed sign \(\varsigma_T\) whenever it cancels from a product.

We first verify the structural assertions for the whole class.  Every \(Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\) has contrast indicator one and normalized scalar loadings satisfying
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}=0,
 \qquad
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}^2=1.          \tag{2}
\]
Applying the algebraic part of the structural-embedding proposition with \(d=1\), the donor loading matrix with columns \((1,\zeta_{m,Q})^\top\) has full row rank two, and \((1,\zeta_{\star,Q})^\top\) lies in its column span.  The visibility lower bound makes the joint contrast factor nonzero.  Thus the total factor rank is two, and the cited scope lemma places every fixed-design member inside the factorization-and-span model used by Han--Shah Theorem~1.

We next validate the two laws in \(\mathbb W_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).  Direct summation of the centered arithmetic progression gives
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\widetilde z_m=0,
 \qquad
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\widetilde z_m^2=1.       \tag{3}
\]
Multiplying both the factors and loadings by \(\varsigma_T\) preserves every survival curve and enforces the prescribed sign convention.  For a donor, the pre-period curve is the witness curve with scalar coordinate \(c_T\widetilde z_m\), and the post-period curve is the witness curve with coordinate \(\widetilde z_m\).  For the target under sign \(u\in\{-1,+1\}\), the corresponding coordinates are \(c_Tu\bar h\) and \(u\bar h\).  Because \(c_T\le1\), \(\max_m|\widetilde z_m|\le R_1\), and \(\bar h\le\min\{R_1,M_\star\}/2\), the explicit witness-compatibility assumption gives normalization at zero, values in \([s_-,1]\), monotonicity, the failure-density floor on \(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), and the stated Hölder hazard bound.  The same assumption gives the factor envelope, and the loading bounds follow from the preceding inequalities.  Infinite censoring gives \(G=1\ge g_-\); consistency, independent censoring, and mutual patient independence hold by construction.  The exponential continuation is common to the two signs because the smooth zero extension of \(\psi_\circ\) vanishes at \(\bar\tau\).

Since the donor score in (3) has empirical second moment one, the invariant joint contrast singular value and pre-period Gram are
\[
 \sigma_T=\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\}^{1/2}
             \|\psi_\circ\|_2,
 \qquad
 H_{0,T}=K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2.       \tag{4}
\]
Thus \(\sigma_T\ge |a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0\), while \(H_{0,T}\to0\).  Moreover, the visibility threshold in the class definition is satisfied because \(\sigma_T\ge |a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2\), which is twice that threshold.  Equations (3)--(4), together with the law checks above, prove
\[
 \{Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\}
 \subset\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}(N_0,K_T,K_D).       \tag{5}
\]

We also verify the comparison with the later Han--Shah conditions.  For a finite pre-period grid, set
\[
 a=(e^{-\lambda_\circ t_\ell})_{\ell=1}^{T_0},
 \qquad q=(\psi_\circ(t_\ell))_{\ell=1}^{T_0}.
\]
The pre-period donor matrix is
\[
 M_{0,T}=a\mathbf1^\top+c_Ta_{\circ,0}q\widetilde z^\top. \tag{6}
\]
The two right vectors in (6) are orthogonal by (3).  If \(a\) and \(q\) are linearly independent, then \(M_{0,T}\) has rank two.  Evaluating its operator norm on \(N_0^{-1/2}\mathbf1\) and using the rank-one perturbation inequality for its second singular value gives
\[
 s_1(M_{0,T})\ge\sqrt{N_0}\|a\|_2,
 \qquad
 s_2(M_{0,T})\le c_T|a_{\circ,0}|\sqrt{N_0}\|q\|_2.    \tag{7}
\]
Hence the ratio of the largest to the smallest nonzero singular value is at least a fixed positive constant times \(K_T\), and Assumption~7 fails for all sufficiently large \(K_T\).  If \(q=\kappa a\ne0\), every pre-period latent vector is proportional to
\[
 (1,\varsigma_Tc_Ta_{\circ,0}\kappa)^\top,              \tag{8}
\]
whereas the post-period vectors on the same grid are proportional to
\((1,\varsigma_Ta_{\circ,1}\kappa)^\top\).  Since \(a_{\circ,1}\ne0\), these directions differ for all sufficiently large \(K_T\), so Assumption~8 fails.  If \(q=0\), the finite grid observes none of the contrast perturbation; even if the two later assumptions happen to hold, they do not control the fixed nonzero continuous-time separation between grid points.  These exhaustive cases show that the lower pair does not contradict Han--Shah Theorem~2.

It remains to prove the minimax bound.  All observed donor cells are identical under the two signs, and the target-post cell is unobserved.  Hence the observed laws differ only through the \(K_T\) target-pre failure times.  Their causal target curves are
\[
 \theta_u(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+u\bar h a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad
 \Delta_\star:=\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
 =2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0.         \tag{9}
\]
On \([0,\bar\tau]\), the one-patient target-pre density is
\[
 f_u(t)=\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
        -u c_Ta_{\circ,0}\bar h\psi_\circ'(t).          \tag{10}
\]
Outside the support of \(\psi_\circ'\) the two densities agree, and after \(\bar\tau\) their continuations agree.  On that support, the witness density floor gives \(f_-\ge c_f\).  Therefore the elementary inequality \(\log x\le x-1\) and equality of the total masses imply
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
 &\le \int\frac{(f_+-f_-)^2}{f_-}\,dt\\
 &\le \frac{4c_T^2a_{\circ,0}^2\bar h^2}{c_f}
       \int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2\,dt.
 \end{aligned}                                           \tag{11}
\]
Patient independence and additivity of relative entropy over product laws now give
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\|Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le \frac{C_0}{K_T}.                                    \tag{12}
\]
The revalidated Pinsker lemma yields
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})
 \le\{C_0/(2K_T)\}^{1/2}=O(K_T^{-1/2}).                 \tag{13}
\]

Let \(B\) be any band satisfying the displayed honesty constraint, and let \(E_u\) denote simultaneous coverage of \(\theta_u\).  By honesty and total-variation transfer,
\[
 Q_-^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_-\cap E_+)
 \ge 1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
      -\operatorname{TV}(Q_+^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_-^{\mathrm{wk}}). \tag{14}
\]
The definition of the honesty tolerance gives \(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\le q_\alpha/4\).  By (13), for all sufficiently large \(K_T\), the total variation is at most \(q_\alpha/2\).  Since \(1-2\alpha=2q_\alpha\), (14) is at least \(q_\alpha\).  On \(E_-\cap E_+\), the band contains both target curves at every time, and hence \(w(B)\ge\Delta_\star\).  Consequently
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge Q_-^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_-\cap E_+)\Delta_\star
 \ge q_\alpha\Delta_\star=:c_\star>0.                 \tag{15}
\]
The constant band with lower endpoint zero and upper endpoint one is continuous, has coverage one for every survival curve, and has width one.  It supplies the matching upper bound.  Thus the minimax expected supremum width has sharp order one.  The converse transfers upward from the explicit subclass, while the functional-PCR upper theorem over the smaller positive-pre-Gram class \(\mathfrak P_2\) does not transfer to \(\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\). \(\square\)
