For any declared fixed positive contrast and pre-Gram lower bounds, a deterministic threshold tending to zero can consistently distinguish rank one from that separated rank-two class, and sample splitting can charge the selection error to the common honesty tolerance.  Uniformity over all unknown positive bounds is a different problem because the union contains sequences whose contrast or pre-Gram strength approaches zero.  A complete answer requires a formally specified separation sequence and a two-point experiment linking that sequence simultaneously to singular-value testing error, target-loading instability, excess expected width, and the schedule \(\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).  Those objects are not fixed by the present adaptation handle.
