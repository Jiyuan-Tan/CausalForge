\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\). The normalization-preserving converse and the attaining theorem apply to the same per-triple honesty class. Therefore
\[
 W_1^*\ge cn_D^{-1/2},\qquad
 W_r^*\ge c\{K_T^{-1/2}+n_D^{-1/2}\}=c\rho_r\quad(r\ge2), \tag{1}
\]
and the single explicit, data-driven band \(\widehat B_r\) gives
\[
 W_r^*\le\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\bar\rho_r.                                        \tag{2}
\]
Because \(\bar\rho_1=\rho_1=n_D^{-1/2}\), (1)--(2) prove the rank-one equivalence. For fixed \(r\ge2\), they give exactly
\[
 c\{K_T^{-1/2}+n_D^{-1/2}\}
 \le W_r^*\le
 C\{K_T^{-1/2}+n_D^{-1/2}+\eta_D(K_D)\}.                 \tag{3}
\]
If \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\), the two sides match in order. Otherwise (3) is only the asserted bracket. Neither input theorem imposes relative growth, and both evaluate coverage and width at the same triple. \(\square\)
