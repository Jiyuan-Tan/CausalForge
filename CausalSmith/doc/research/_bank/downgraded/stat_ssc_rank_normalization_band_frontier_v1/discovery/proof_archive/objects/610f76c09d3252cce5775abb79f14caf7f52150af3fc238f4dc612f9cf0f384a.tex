Let \(\mathbb W_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)=(Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}})\) be the weak-pre-Gram pair in \(\mathrm{def:weak\mbox{-}pre\mbox{-}gram\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}pair}\).  There are constants \(a_0<\infty\) and \(c_\star>0\), independent of \(N_0,K_T,K_D\), such that every triple with \(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\) has the following properties.

First, both laws belong to \(\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D)\), have known exact total factor rank two, have a full-row-rank donor loading matrix, and have the target loading in the donor loading span.  Thus the pair is a normalization-preserving subclass of the Han--Shah Theorem~1 structural survival-factor model.  Its sole joint donor-contrast singular value is
\[
 \left\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\right\}^{1/2}
 \|\psi_\circ\|_2
 \ge |a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0,
\]
whereas its pre-period contrast Gram is
\[
 H_{0,T}=K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\longrightarrow0.
\]
For every finite pre-period grid, the corresponding donor survival matrix is either rank deficient or has condition number at least a fixed positive constant times \(K_T\).  Hence this pair is excluded by the finite-grid pre-matrix well-conditioning used in Han--Shah Theorem~2.

Second, the targets have fixed separation
\[
 \|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
 =2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0,
\]
while \(\operatorname{TV}(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}})=O(K_T^{-1/2})\).  Consequently every random continuous-time band \(B\) satisfying
\[
 \min_{u\in\{-1,+1\}}Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}
 \left\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta_u(t)\le B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
 \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\right\}
 \ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)
\]
obeys
\[
 \max_{u\in\{-1,+1\}}E_{Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}}\{w(B)\}\ge c_\star.
\]
The constant band \([0,1]\) is honest with width one, so the minimax maximal expected width on this known-rank-two subexperiment has sharp order one.  Subclass inclusion transfers this converse to every larger observation-model class containing the pair, including the Han--Shah Theorem~1 structural class under the same sampling experiment, but it does not transfer the functional-PCR upper bound from \(\mathfrak P_2\) to that larger class.
