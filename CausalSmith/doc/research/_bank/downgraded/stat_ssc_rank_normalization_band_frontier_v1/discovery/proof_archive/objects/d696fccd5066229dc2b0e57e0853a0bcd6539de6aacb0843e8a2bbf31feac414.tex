\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a sufficiently large evaluated triple and abbreviate \(q=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\).  Let
\[
 \mathcal A_Q=\{\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le\omega_r/2\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_Z=\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}\le c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}.
\]
The uniform-linearization lemma and a union bound give
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}P(\mathcal A_Q\cap\mathcal A_Z)
 \ge1-\alpha.                                           \tag{1}
\]
On this event, at every grid knot,
\[
 |\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 \le \widetilde c_r^\xi+\tau_{r,n}
 +\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+\omega_r.              \tag{2}
\]
Projection onto \([0,1]\) is nonexpansive for the true survival value, and both the target and the corrected zero-knot center equal one at zero.  Hence (2) also holds with \(\widetilde\theta_r(g)\) in place of \(\widehat\theta_r(g)\).  Since \(\widetilde\theta_r\) is the piecewise-linear interpolant of these projected grid values, the interpolation argument in \(\mathrm{prop:grid\mbox{-}interpolation}\) yields
\[
 \|\widetilde\theta_r-\theta\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}
 \le \widetilde c_r^\xi+\tau_{r,n}
 +\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+\omega_r
 +c_{\mathrm{int}}h^q=A_r.                              \tag{3}
\]
The corrected definition takes pointwise continuous clipping of the interval \([\widetilde\theta_r-A_r,\widetilde\theta_r+A_r]\) to \([0,1]\).  Because \(0\le\theta\le1\), (3) implies simultaneous containment for every \(t\in\mathcal I\).  Equation (1) is stronger than the required \(1-\alpha-\varepsilon\) coverage, so \(\widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r\).

Pointwise clipping cannot increase width, whence
\[
 w(\widehat B_r)\le2A_r.                                \tag{4}
\]
The random-quantile theorem gives \(E\widetilde c_r^\xi\le C\rho_r\) and \(\tau_{r,n}=o(\rho_r)\).  The uniform-linearization lemma gives \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\), while the grid definition gives \(h^q\le\rho_r\).  Taking expectations in (4), uniformly over the class, therefore yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\{\rho_r+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\}
 =C\bar\rho_r.                                         \tag{5}
\]
All tail cutoffs and constants are uniform and no relative-growth comparison was used.  At rank one \(\bar\rho_1=\rho_1=(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\); at higher rank (5) is \(O(\rho_r)\) precisely on the stated subregimes. \(\square\)
