\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_mz_m=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_mz_m^2=1,
 \qquad \max_m|z_m|<\sqrt3\le R_1\le M_z.

Because \(A_{p,1}=a_{\circ,p}\psi_\circ\), the sole nonzero singular value of the centered donor operator is
\[
 \{(a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2)\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\}^{1/2},

and the pre-factor Gram is \(a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\).  The compatibility inequalities place these quantities in the required intervals and bound the loading and factor envelopes.  Since \(M_\star>0\) and \(R_1>0\), for all sufficiently large \(K_T\),
\[
 h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\le\min\{M_\star,R_1\}.

Taking censoring identically infinite gives \(G=1\), independent censoring, and censor overlap; the product construction gives patient independence and control consistency.  f_{0,\star}^{(+)}(t)-f_{0,\star}^{(-)}(t)

 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_+\|P_-)
