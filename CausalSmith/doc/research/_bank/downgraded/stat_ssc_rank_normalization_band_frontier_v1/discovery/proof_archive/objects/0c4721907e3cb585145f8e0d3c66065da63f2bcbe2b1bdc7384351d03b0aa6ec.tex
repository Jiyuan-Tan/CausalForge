\textbf{Proof.}
By definition,
\[
 \mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}(N_0,K_T,K_D)
 \subseteq\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}(N_0,K_T,K_D).             \tag{1}
\]
Thus every band honest over the closure is honest over the donor-visible known-rank-two subclass.  The preceding minimax-boundary theorem and monotonicity of the supremum under class inclusion imply
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge c_\star.                                           \tag{2}
\]
The constant band \([0,1]\) is honest over the closure and has width one, so (2) proves that the closure's minimax maximal expected width is bounded above and below by positive constants.

To compare this boundary with the known-rank oracle frontiers without imposing a relative-growth condition on the theorem itself, consider only the diagnostic balanced sequence
\[
 N_0=K_T=K_D=k\longrightarrow\infty.                    \tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
 \rho_1=k^{-1},
 \qquad
 \rho_2=k^{-1/2}+k^{-1},
 \qquad
 \eta_D(k)=O(e^{-ck})=O(\rho_2).                        \tag{4}
\]
The sharp fixed-rank frontier theorem therefore gives vanishing minimax widths of orders \(\rho_1\) and \(\rho_2\) on the two positive-pre-Gram exact-rank classes along (3), while (2) forces order-one width for every band honest over the no-common-pre-Gram closure.  Hence no single rank-selection band can attain both shrinking known-rank oracle widths uniformly over that closure.

Finally, the lower pair used in (2) has contrast indicator one and, by the boundary theorem,
\[
 \sigma_T\ge |a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0            \tag{5}
\]
uniformly in the sample sizes, even though its pre-period Gram tends to zero.  Thus the obstruction persists when donor rank is visibly two: detecting donor rank does not identify the target loading. \(\square\)
