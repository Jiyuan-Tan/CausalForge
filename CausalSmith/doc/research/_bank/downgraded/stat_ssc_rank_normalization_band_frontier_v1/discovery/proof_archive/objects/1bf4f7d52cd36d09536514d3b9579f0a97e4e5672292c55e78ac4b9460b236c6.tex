Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), \(c_0=s_-g_-/3\), and
\[
 \upsilon_r=
 \mathbf 1\{r\ge2\}\left\{K_T^{-1}+N_0^{-1/2}K_D^{-1}
 +\rho_r\eta_D(K_D)+e^{-c_0K_T/2}+e^{-c_0n_D/2}\right\}
 +n_D^{-1}+e^{-c_0n_D/2}.
\]
Define
\[
 \omega_r(N_0,K_T,K_D;\alpha)
 =C_\omega\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)^{-1/2}\upsilon_r,
\]
where the fixed \(C_\omega<\infty\) depends only on the model constants and is chosen to dominate the nonlinear Taylor remainder and aggregate fallback contributions.
