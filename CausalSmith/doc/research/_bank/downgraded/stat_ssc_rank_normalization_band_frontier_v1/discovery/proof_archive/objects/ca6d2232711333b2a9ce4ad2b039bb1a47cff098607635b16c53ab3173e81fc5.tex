\textbf{Proof.}
Here \(r=2\) and \(d=1\). Empirical Gram--Schmidt in \(\mathrm{def:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}lecam\mbox{-}pair}\) gives
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m=0,
 \qquad
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_m^2=1,
 \qquad
 \max_m|z_m|<\sqrt3\le R_1\le M_z.
\]
Since \(A_{p,1}=a_{\circ,p}\psi_\circ\), the centered donor operator has one nonzero singular value
\[
 \{(a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2)\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\}^{1/2},
\]
and the pre-factor Gram equals \(a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2\). The numerical inequalities in \(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) put these quantities in the required intervals and verify the donor-loading and factor-envelope bounds.

Because \(M_\star>0\), for all sufficiently large \(K_T\),
\[
 h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\le\min\{M_\star,R_1\}.
\]
The four pointwise compatibility inequalities, with \(x=z_m\) for donors and \(x=z_\star^{(u)}\) for the target, then give valid nonincreasing survival curves, event overlap, the failure-density floor on \(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), and the \(C^\beta\) hazard bound. Every factor curve vanishes at zero, so \(S_{p,m}^{(u)}(0)=1\). Taking censoring identically infinite gives \(G=1\), independent censoring, and censor overlap. The product construction gives patient independence and control consistency. Thus both laws belong to \(\mathfrak P_2(N_0,K_T,K_D)\).

Only the target loading changes with \(u\), and the target-post control cohort is unobserved. Hence the observed laws differ only in the \(K_T\) target-pre records. The first factor coordinate gives
\[
 \theta_+(t)-\theta_-(t)
 =2a_{\circ,1}h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\psi_\circ(t),
\]
which proves the stated supremum separation. The two target-pre densities satisfy
\[
 f_{0,\star}^{(+)}(t)-f_{0,\star}^{(-)}(t)
 =-2a_{\circ,0}h_\circ K_T^{-1/2}\psi_\circ'(t).
\]
They agree off \(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\). On that support, \(f_{0,\star}^{(-)}\ge c_f\). The inequality \(\log x\le x-1\), followed by patient independence, therefore gives
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P_+\|P_-)
 \le K_T\int\frac{(f_{0,\star}^{(+)}-f_{0,\star}^{(-)})^2}{f_{0,\star}^{(-)}}\,dt
 \le\frac{4a_{\circ,0}^2h_\circ^2}{c_f}
 \int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2\,dt.
\]
Finally, \((\sqrt f_+-\sqrt f_-)^2\le(f_+-f_-)^2/f_-\), multiplicativity of Hellinger affinity, and total variation bounded by Hellinger distance show
\[
 \|P_+-P_-\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
 \le2|a_{\circ,0}|h_\circ c_f^{-1/2}\|\psi_\circ'\|_2.
\]
The last compatibility inequality makes this a fixed constant strictly below the amount allowed in the two-point converse. Exact rank, spectrum, Gram, and normalization were already verified and are unchanged by the target-loading sign. \(\square\)
