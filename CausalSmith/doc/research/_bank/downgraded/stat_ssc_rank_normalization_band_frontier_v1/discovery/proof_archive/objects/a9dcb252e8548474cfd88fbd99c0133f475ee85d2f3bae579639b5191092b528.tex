The rank-one donor channel can be constructed without a factor backbone.  Choose a constant hazard \(\lambda_0\) strictly inside the hazard and survival bounds, put \(n_D=N_0K_D\), and in the donor-post cells use hazards \(\lambda_0\pm c n_D^{-1/2}\), leaving every other observed cell fixed and taking no censoring.  Both laws have exact rank one and endpoint value one.  At any fixed \(t_0\in(0,\bar\tau]\), their target curves differ by at least \(c_1n_D^{-1/2}\) for all sufficiently large \(n_D\), while the product-law Kullback--Leibler divergence is bounded by \(c_2c^2\).  For two laws \(Q_0,Q_1\), target separation \(\Delta\), and an honest band with error \(\eta=\alpha+\varepsilon\), change of measure gives under \(Q_0\)
\[
 Q_0\{\theta_0,\theta_1\in B\}\ge1-2\eta-\|Q_0-Q_1\|_{\mathrm{TV}}.
\]
On this event \(w(B)\ge\Delta\).  Choosing \(c\) so that total variation is smaller than a fixed fraction of \(1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon\) proves the rank-one lower bound.  The same two-point inequality, combined with the algebra in the rank-two partial result, proves the \(K_T^{-1/2}\) channel once membership of the fixed rank-two construction is secured.  The maximum of the donor and target lower bounds is at least one half their sum.
