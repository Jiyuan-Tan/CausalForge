There is a constant \(c_{\mathrm{int}}<\infty\) such that, for the interpolation rule in \(\mathrm{def:inference-grid}\), the difference between the continuous-time supremum error and its grid supremum is at most \(c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^\beta\) uniformly over \(\mathfrak P_r\). Consequently the explicit interpolation allowance is \(O(\rho_r)\) and continuous-time honesty is not obtained by silently replacing \(\mathcal I\) with a fixed finite grid.
