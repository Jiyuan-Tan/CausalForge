Set \(s_p(t)=N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}S^0_{p,m}(t)\). Write the singular-value decomposition of the primitive centered operator as \(\mathcal C_D=\sum_{k=1}^d\sigma_k u_k\otimes v_k\), ordered by decreasing \(\sigma_k\) with deterministic sign and tie-breaking rules. Define \(z_{m,k}=\sqrt{N_0}v_{k,m}\) and \(b_{p,k}(t)=\sigma_k u_{k,p}(t)\), so \(N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}z_mz_m^\top=I_d\). Define the shared target coordinate \(z_\star\) by the unique coefficient equation \(S^0_{0,\star}-s_0=b_0^\top z_\star\). Then \(S^0_{p,m}(t)=s_p(t)+b_p(t)^\top z_m\), \(s_p(0)=1\), and \(b_p(0)=0\). When \(d=0\), all vectors, sums, and Gram matrices in this construction are zero-dimensional.
