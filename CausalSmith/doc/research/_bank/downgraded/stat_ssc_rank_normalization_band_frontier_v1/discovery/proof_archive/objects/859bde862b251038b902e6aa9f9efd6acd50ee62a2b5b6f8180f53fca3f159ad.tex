\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(c_T=K_T^{-1}\), let \(\widetilde z_m\) be the unoriented donor
scores in
\(\mathrm{def:weak\mbox{-}pre\mbox{-}gram\mbox{-}rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}pair}\),
and put \(q_\alpha=1/2-\alpha>0\).

\emph{Step 1: every law in the class has the claimed structural rank and
span properties.}
For \(Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\), the definition gives
\(e_Q=1\) and
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}=0,
 \qquad
 N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}\zeta_{m,Q}^2=1.       \tag{1}
\]
Apply the algebraic part of
\(\mathrm{prop:han\mbox{-}shah\mbox{-}structural\mbox{-}embedding}\)
with \(d=1\), \(r=2\), and \(z_m=\zeta_{m,Q}\).  It follows from (1)
that the donor loading matrix with columns \((1,\zeta_{m,Q})^\top\) has
full row rank and that \((1,\zeta_{\star,Q})^\top\) is in its column
span.

This is genuinely a two-coordinate factorization.  Indeed, survival
normalization at zero, donor centering, and the nonzero donor loading
variance in (1) imply
\[
 s_{p,Q}(0)=1,
 \qquad b_{p,Q}(0)=0.                                  \tag{2}
\]
The donor-visibility lower bound implies that \(b_{p,Q}(t)\ne0\) for at
least one \((p,t)\).  The two factor vectors \((1,0)^\top\) from (2) and
\((s_{p,Q}(t),b_{p,Q}(t))^\top\) at such a point are linearly
independent.  Thus the normalized contrast indicator is one and the total
factor rank is two.  By
\(\mathrm{lem:han\mbox{-}shah\mbox{-}theorem\mbox{-}one\mbox{-}scope}\),
these fixed-design laws satisfy the factorization-and-donor-span part of
the Han--Shah Theorem~1 model.  No positive uniform lower bound on their
pre-period contrast Gram has been used.

\emph{Step 2: the weak pair belongs to the class.}
For \(N_0\ge3\), the centered arithmetic progression in the construction
satisfies
\[
 \frac1{N_0}\sum_{m=1}^{N_0}\widetilde z_m=0,
 \qquad
 \frac1{N_0}\sum_{m=1}^{N_0}\widetilde z_m^2=1,       \tag{3}
\]
because
\(N_0^{-1}\sum_m\{m-(N_0+1)/2\}^2=(N_0^2-1)/12\).
Multiplication of both factor and loadings by \(\varsigma_T\) preserves
(3), preserves every survival curve, and enforces the sign convention in
\(\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}\).

For a donor in period zero, the heterogeneous term has the form
\(a_{\circ,0}\psi_\circ x\) with
\(x=c_T\widetilde z_m\); for the target it has
\(x=c_Tu\bar h\).  In period one the corresponding choices are
\(x=\widetilde z_m\) and \(x=u\bar h\).  Once \(K_T\ge1\), these
arguments have absolute value at most \(R_1\), because
\(\max_m|\widetilde z_m|\le R_1\) and
\(\bar h\le R_1/2\).  Therefore the four inequalities in
\(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) give, on
\(\mathcal I\), survival support in \([s_-,1]\), endpoint normalization,
monotonicity, the failure-density floor on
\(\operatorname{supp}(\psi_\circ')\), and the stated \(C^\beta\) hazard
bound.  The exponential continuation is nonincreasing, remains in
\([0,1]\), and converges to zero.  Infinite censoring gives \(G\equiv1\),
which satisfies censoring overlap since \(g_-\le1\).  Independence,
independent censoring, and control consistency hold by construction.

The donor loading bound follows from \(R_1\le M_z\); the target loading
bound follows from \(\bar h\le M_\star/2\).  Since \(c_T\le1\), the
factor envelope and both upper energy bounds follow from the corresponding
inequalities in the witness-compatibility condition.  Their lower bounds
required by \(\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}\) are strict because
\(a_{\circ,0}\ne0\), \(a_{\circ,1}\ne0\), and
\(\psi_\circ\not\equiv0\).  Thus both constructed laws belong to
\(\mathfrak Q_{\leq2}\).

Because the donor scores have empirical second moment one, the sole
nonzero joint contrast singular value and the scalar pre-period Gram are
\[
 \sigma_T
 =\left\{\int_{\mathcal I}
       \bigl(b_{0,T}(t)^2+b_{1,T}(t)^2\bigr)\,dt\right\}^{1/2}
 =\{K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2+a_{\circ,1}^2\}^{1/2}
       \|\psi_\circ\|_2,
 \qquad
 H_{0,T}=K_T^{-2}a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2.    \tag{4}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \sigma_T\ge |a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2
 \ge\frac12|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_2>0,
 \qquad H_{0,T}\longrightarrow0.                     \tag{5}
\]
Equations (3)--(5) prove inclusion of both laws in
\(\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}\), exact rank two, donor visibility, and
the asserted degenerating pre-period Gram.

\emph{Step 3: the three finite-grid cases are exhaustive.}
Fix \(\mathcal T=\{t_1,\ldots,t_{T_0}\}\) and define
\[
 a=(e^{-\lambda_\circ t_\ell})_{\ell=1}^{T_0},
 \qquad q=(\psi_\circ(t_\ell))_{\ell=1}^{T_0}.
\]
The simultaneous orientation cancels in the product of factor and
loading, so the target-pre donor matrix is
\[
 M_{0,T}=a\mathbf1^\top+c_Ta_{\circ,0}q\widetilde z^\top.              \tag{6}
\]
The right vectors \(\mathbf1\) and \(\widetilde z\) are orthogonal by
(3).  If \(a\) and \(q\) are linearly independent, (6) has rank two.
Writing \(s_1(M_{0,T})\ge s_2(M_{0,T})>0\) for its nonzero singular
values, the variational characterization and the rank-one approximation
obtained by deleting the second term in (6) give
\[
 s_1(M_{0,T})
 \ge\|M_{0,T}\mathbf1/\sqrt{N_0}\|_2
 =\sqrt{N_0}\|a\|_2,
 \qquad
 s_2(M_{0,T})
 \le c_T|a_{\circ,0}|\sqrt{N_0}\|q\|_2.              \tag{7}
\]
Since
\(\|a\|_2\ge e^{-\lambda_\circ\bar\tau}\sqrt{T_0}\) and
\(\|q\|_2\le\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty\sqrt{T_0}\),
\[
 \frac{s_1(M_{0,T})}{s_2(M_{0,T})}
 \ge
 \frac{e^{-\lambda_\circ\bar\tau}}
 {|a_{\circ,0}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty}K_T.            \tag{8}
\]
Thus no fixed conditioning constant in Han--Shah Assumption~7 can hold
for this pair when \(K_T\) is sufficiently large.

If \(q=\kappa a\ne0\), the pre-period latent factor vectors on the grid
are multiples of
\((1,\varsigma_Tc_Ta_{\circ,0}\kappa)^\top\), whereas the post-period
vectors are multiples of
\((1,\varsigma_Ta_{\circ,1}\kappa)^\top\).  The former span is
one-dimensional, and the latter direction is outside it for all
sufficiently large \(K_T\), because \(a_{\circ,1}\ne0\) while
\(c_Ta_{\circ,0}\to0\).  Hence Han--Shah Assumption~8 fails.  Finally,
if \(q=0\), (6) has rank one, its largest-to-smallest nonzero singular
value ratio is one, and both pre- and post-period heterogeneous factors
vanish on the grid.  Assumptions~7--8 may then hold, but the grid sees none
of \(\psi_\circ\), while \(\psi_\circ\) is nonzero between its boundary
zeros.  A finite-grid conclusion therefore does not cover the nonzero
continuous-time target separation below.  Linear independence,
nonzero collinearity, and \(q=0\) exhaust all possibilities.

\emph{Step 4: the full observed laws are asymptotically indistinguishable
despite fixed target separation.}
All donor cohorts have the same law under the two signs, and the target
post-control cohort is unobserved.  The observed laws can therefore differ
only through the \(K_T\) target-pre failure times.  The two causal target
curves are
\[
 \theta_u(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
             +u\bar h a_{\circ,1}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad u\in\{-1,+1\},
\]
and hence
\[
 \Delta_\star:=\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty
 =2\bar h|a_{\circ,1}|\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty>0.       \tag{9}
\]
For one target-pre patient, the density on \((0,\bar\tau)\) is
\[
 f_u(t)=\lambda_\circ e^{-\lambda_\circ t}
       -u c_Ta_{\circ,0}\bar h\psi_\circ'(t).         \tag{10}
\]
The smooth zero extension has
\(\psi_\circ(0)=\psi_\circ(\bar\tau)=0\) and
\(\psi_\circ'(0)=\psi_\circ'(\bar\tau)=0\).  Thus the two survivals
match at \(\bar\tau\), their exponential continuations have identical
density, and there is no unmatched endpoint or tail mass.  Equation (10)
therefore describes the entire difference of the one-patient laws.  On
the support of that difference,
\(\mathrm{ass:explicit\mbox{-}witness\mbox{-}compatibility}\) gives
\(f_-(t)\ge c_f\).  Using \(\log x\le x-1\) and equality of total mass,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_+\|f_-)
 &\le \int\frac{\{f_+(t)-f_-(t)\}^2}{f_-(t)}\,dt\\
 &\le \frac{4c_T^2a_{\circ,0}^2\bar h^2}{c_f}
       \int_0^{\bar\tau}\{\psi_\circ'(t)\}^2\,dt.
 \end{aligned}                                                       \tag{11}
\]
Patient independence and additivity of relative entropy now imply, for a
constant \(C_0<\infty\) depending only on the fixed primitives,
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}
 \bigl(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}}\|Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}\bigr)
 \le C_0K_Tc_T^2=C_0K_T^{-1}.                         \tag{12}
\]
The corrected cited Pinsker inequality
\(\mathrm{lem:pinsker\mbox{-}kl\mbox{-}tv}\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{TV}
 \bigl(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}\bigr)
 \le\{C_0/(2K_T)\}^{1/2}=O(K_T^{-1/2}).              \tag{13}
\]
Choose \(a_0\ge3\) large enough that (13) is at most \(q_\alpha/2\),
and that every preceding large-\(K_T\) statement holds, whenever
\(\min\{N_0,K_T,K_D\}\ge a_0\).

\emph{Step 5: uniform honesty forces constant expected width.}
For \(u\in\{-1,+1\}\), let
\[
 E_u=\{B^{\mathrm{lo}}(t)\le\theta_u(t)\le B^{\mathrm{up}}(t)
       \text{ for every }t\in\mathcal I\}.
\]
For any band satisfying the displayed honesty constraint, pair inclusion
gives
\(Q_{2,u}^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_u)\ge1-\alpha-\varepsilon\), where
\(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\).  Transfer of
\(E_+\) to the minus law by total variation, followed by the union bound,
yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}(E_-\cap E_+)
 &\ge1-2\alpha-2\varepsilon
      -\operatorname{TV}
       \bigl(Q_{2,+}^{\mathrm{wk}},Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}\bigr)\\
 &\ge2q_\alpha-q_\alpha/2-q_\alpha/2=q_\alpha.       \tag{14}
 \end{aligned}
\]
Here the definition of the finite-triple tolerance gives
\(\varepsilon\le q_\alpha/4\), and (13) gives the other error bound.
On \(E_-\cap E_+\), the same band contains both curves pointwise, so (9)
implies \(w(B)\ge\Delta_\star\).  Therefore
\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathfrak Q_{2,\mathrm{vis}}}E_Qw(B)
 \ge E_{Q_{2,-}^{\mathrm{wk}}}w(B)
 \ge q_\alpha\Delta_\star=:c_\star>0.                \tag{15}
\]
Taking the infimum over honest bands proves the lower bound.  Conversely,
the deterministic continuous band \([0,1]\) contains every survival curve
and has width one, proving the upper bound.  All constants depend only on
the fixed primitive constants and \(\alpha\), not on the sample-size
triple or its relative growth.  Hence the minimax order is exactly one.
This obstruction concerns any attempted upgrade from donor rank detection
alone to a shrinking honest continuous-time band; it does not attribute
such a rank-adaptive-band claim to Han and Shah, and it does not contradict
their later quantitatively conditioned finite-grid result. \(\square\)
