\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\). The uniform linearization gives, for every sufficiently large evaluated triple,
\[
 P\{\|Z_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>c_r^\xi+\omega_r/2\}
 \le\alpha-\varepsilon/4,\qquad
 P\{\|Q_r\|_{\infty,\mathcal G}>\omega_r/2\}\le\varepsilon/4, \tag{1}
\]
uniformly in \(P\), while \(\|\mathfrak b_r\|_\infty\le\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\). A union bound in the expansion yields
\[
 P\left\{\max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 \le c_r^\xi+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+\omega_r\right\}
 \ge1-\alpha.                                             \tag{2}
\]
The interpolation proposition gives, with \(q_\beta=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\),
\[
 \|\widehat\theta_r-\theta\|_{\infty,\mathcal I}
 \le\max_{g\in\mathcal G}|\widehat\theta_r(g)-\theta(g)|
 +c_{\mathrm{int}}h^{q_\beta},\qquad h^{q_\beta}\le\rho_r. \tag{3}
\]
Projection onto \([0,1]\) cannot increase distance from the survival curve. Linear interpolation of the band endpoints preserves the grid enclosure plus (3), and all branches are measurable and continuous. Thus the radius in the corrected definition implies simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\alpha\), which is stronger than the defining \(1-\alpha-\varepsilon\) constraint. Hence \(\widehat B_r\in\mathcal H_r\).

Clipping endpoints can only shorten the band, so
\[
 w(\widehat B_r)\le2\widetilde c_r^\xi+2\tau_{r,n}
 +2\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)+2\omega_r
 +2c_{\mathrm{int}}h^{q_\beta}.                           \tag{4}
\]
The grid-quantile theorem gives \(\sup_PE\widetilde c_r^\xi\le C\rho_r\) and \(\tau_{r,n}=o(\rho_r)\); the linearization gives \(\omega_r=o(\rho_r)\); and (3) bounds interpolation by \(C\rho_r\). Taking expectations in (4), and increasing \(a_0\) so all small-order terms are bounded by fixed multiples of \(\rho_r\), proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(\widehat B_r)
 \le C\{\rho_r+\mathbf1\{r\ge2\}\eta_D(K_D)\}=C\bar\rho_r. \tag{5}
\]
At rank one this is \(C(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\). At fixed higher rank it is \(O(\rho_r)\) only under the stated \(\eta_D=O(\rho_r)\) condition. \(\square\)
