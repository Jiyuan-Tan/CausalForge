Put \(q=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\) and
\[
 J_r=\max\left\{1,
 \left\lceil\bar\tau\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)^{-1/q}\right\rceil
 \right\}.
\]
Define the canonical quasi-uniform grid and its adjacent-knot mesh by
\[
 \mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}
 =\left\{\frac{\ell\bar\tau}{J_r}:0\le\ell\le J_r\right\},
 \qquad h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}=\frac{\bar\tau}{J_r}.
\]
Use piecewise-linear interpolation between adjacent knots. Then
\[
 h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{q}\le\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D),
 \qquad
 |\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}|
 \le2+\bar\tau\rho_r(N_0,K_T,K_D)^{-1/q}.
\]
