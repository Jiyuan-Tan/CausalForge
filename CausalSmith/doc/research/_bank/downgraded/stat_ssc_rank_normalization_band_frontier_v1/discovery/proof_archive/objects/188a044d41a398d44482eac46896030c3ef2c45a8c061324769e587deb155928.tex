Carpentier, Klopp, L\"offler, and Nickl, Theorem~2.2 and Section~7.1, construct an honest adaptive confidence set when a uniformly consistent composite test has separation no larger than the small-model estimation rate and an estimator satisfies their two-model oracle inequality.  The construction centers at the oracle estimator and chooses the radius \(r_n(\mathcal A)\Psi_n+r_n(\mathcal A_0)(1-\Psi_n)\); its coverage proof separately treats the null, alternatives far from the null, and alternatives within the testing radius.
