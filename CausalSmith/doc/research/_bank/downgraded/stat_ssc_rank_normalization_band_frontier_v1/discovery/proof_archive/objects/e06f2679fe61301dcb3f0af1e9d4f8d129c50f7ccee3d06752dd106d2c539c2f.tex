For an observed control cell with \(k\) patients and \(k\) large enough, let \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}\) be the deterministic piecewise-linear interpolation operator on \(\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}\), and set \(\widetilde S^0_{p,m}=\mathcal L_{\mathcal G}S^0_{p,m}\).  Its linearly interpolated Kaplan--Meier curve admits the uniform decomposition
\[
 \widehat S^{\mathrm{KM}}_{p,m}-\widetilde S^0_{p,m}
 =k^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{k}\varphi_{p,m,j}+b_{p,m,k}+q_{p,m,k}
 \quad\hbox{in }\ell^\infty(\mathcal I),
\]
where the \(\varphi_{p,m,j}\) are independent, mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes, \(\|b_{p,m,k}\|_\infty\le C_0e^{-c_0k}\), \(E q_{p,m,k}=0\), and \(E\|q_{p,m,k}\|_\infty^2\le C_0k^{-2}\), uniformly over the model class.  Here \(c_0=s_-g_-/3\), and \(C_0\) may be chosen as the conservative fixed constant used in the definition of \(\eta_D\).  Moreover, with \(q_\beta=\min\{\beta+1,2\}\),
\[
 \widetilde S^0_{p,m}(g)=S^0_{p,m}(g)\quad(g\in\mathcal G_{N_0,K_T,K_D}),
 \qquad
 \|\widetilde S^0_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m}\|_\infty
 \le c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{q_\beta},
\]
and
\[
 \|\widetilde S^0_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m}\|_{L^2(\mathcal I)}
 \le \bar\tau^{1/2}c_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{q_\beta},
\]
where \(c_{\mathrm{int}}=c_{\mathrm{int}}(L,\bar\tau,\beta)<\infty\).  Thus the exact expansion about \(S^0_{p,m}\) contains the additional deterministic term \(a_{\mathcal G,p,m}=\widetilde S^0_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m}\), which is zero on the grid and is controlled only by the displayed interpolation bounds.  For independent donor cells, averaging the \(q_{p,m,k}\) terms has order \(O_P(N_0^{-1/2}k^{-1})=o_P((N_0k)^{-1/2})\), whereas averaging the bias or interpolation terms need not improve their respective uniform bounds.
