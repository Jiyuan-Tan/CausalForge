For an observed control cell with \(k\) patients and \(k\) large enough, let \(\widehat S^{\mathrm{PL}}_{p,m}\) be its product-limit Kaplan--Meier step curve and let \(I_{\mathcal G}\) denote piecewise-linear interpolation from the inference grid.  Uniformly over the model class,
\[
 \widehat S^{\mathrm{PL}}_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m}
 =k^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{k}\varphi_{p,m,j}+b_{p,m,k}+q_{p,m,k}
 \quad\hbox{in }\ell^\infty(\mathcal I),
\]
where the \(\varphi_{p,m,j}\) are independent, mean-zero censoring-martingale influence processes,
\[
 \|b_{p,m,k}\|_\infty\le C_0e^{-c_0k},\qquad
 E q_{p,m,k}=0,\qquad
 E\|q_{p,m,k}\|_\infty^2\le C_0k^{-2},
 \qquad c_0=s_-g_-/3.
\]
Consequently the linearly interpolated curve has the exact decomposition
\[
 I_{\mathcal G}\widehat S^{\mathrm{PL}}_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m}
 =k^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^{k}I_{\mathcal G}\varphi_{p,m,j}
  +I_{\mathcal G}b_{p,m,k}+I_{\mathcal G}q_{p,m,k}+a_{p,m},
 \qquad
 a_{p,m}=I_{\mathcal G}S^0_{p,m}-S^0_{p,m},
\]
with, for some fixed \(C_{\mathrm{int}}=C_{\mathrm{int}}(L,\bar\tau,\beta)<\infty\), \(\|a_{p,m}\|_\infty\le C_{\mathrm{int}}h_{N_0,K_T,K_D}^{\min\{\beta+1,2\}}\).  The constant \(C_0\) may be chosen as the conservative fixed constant used in the definition of \(\eta_D\).  For independent donor cells,
\[
 \left\|N_0^{-1}\sum_{m\in\mathcal D}q_{p,m,k}\right\|_\infty
 =O_P(N_0^{-1/2}k^{-1})
 =o_P((N_0k)^{-1/2})
\]
as \(k\to\infty\), whereas averaging the \(b_{p,m,k}\) terms need not improve \(C_0e^{-c_0k}\).  In particular, for either deterministic half-fold in the donor construction the exponential allowance is \(C_0\exp\{-c_0\lfloor K_D/2\rfloor\}\).
