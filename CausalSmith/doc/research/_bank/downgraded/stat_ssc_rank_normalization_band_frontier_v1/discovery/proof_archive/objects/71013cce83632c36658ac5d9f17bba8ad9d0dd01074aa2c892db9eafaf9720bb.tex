The project proves the known-fixed-rank width frontier for the entire post-control survival trajectory, subject to one explicit higher-rank finite-cell remainder.  Exact rank one removes the target loading and gives \(W_1^*\asymp(N_0K_D)^{-1/2}\).  At every fixed rank \(r\ge2\), the proved unrestricted result is the bracket \(c\rho_r\le W_r^*\le C\bar\rho_r\), which matches when \(\eta_D(K_D)=O(\rho_r)\).  The separate unknown-rank question is not solved by a singular-value switch: a CKLN-style test-plus-oracle theorem requires a nested triangular closure, target-relevant separation, a near-null branch, and a thresholded pre-Gram estimator.  On a closure with no common pre-Gram lower bound, an explicit normalization-preserving pair keeps donor rank visible while making the target-loading sign contiguous, forcing nonvanishing width and ruling out uniform oracle-rate adaptation.
