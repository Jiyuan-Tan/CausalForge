Han and Shah's Theorem~1 structural model requires a finite-dimensional survival factorization \(S^{(0)}_{p,m}(t)=\langle u_p(t),v_m\rangle\) and, separately, inclusion of each treated-unit factor \(v_\star\) in the linear span of the donor factors.  The well-conditioned finite-grid pre-period matrix and latent time-period span restrictions are introduced later as Assumptions~7 and~8 for their Theorem~2, not as conditions of their Theorem~1.
