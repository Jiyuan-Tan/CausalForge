\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(n_D=N_0K_D\). For the donor channel, keep the centered donor scores, factor curves, pre-period laws, and target loading fixed, and replace only the post baseline by
\[
 s_1^{(u)}(t)=e^{-\lambda_\circ t}+u c n_D^{-1/2}\psi_\circ(t),
 \qquad u\in\{-1,+1\}.                                    \tag{1}
\]
For fixed sufficiently small \(c>0\) and all large triples, the explicit witness-compatibility inequalities verify survival validity, overlap, the density floor, and hazard smoothness. Adding a common post curve leaves the centered operator, exact rank, spectrum, pre-Gram, loadings, factor envelope, and endpoint normalization unchanged. Take censoring identically infinite. The laws differ only in the \(n_D\) donor-post records, their target separation is
\[
 \Delta_D=2c\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty n_D^{-1/2},            \tag{2}
\]
and the density difference and floor give
\[
 D_{\mathrm{KL}}(Q_{D,+}\|Q_{D,-})
 \le4c^2c_f^{-1}\int_0^{\bar\tau}(\psi_\circ')^2,
 \qquad \operatorname{TV}(Q_{D,+},Q_{D,-})\le C_Dc.       \tag{3}
\]
Choose \(c\) so the last quantity is at most \(\tfrac34(1/2-\alpha)\). The construction applies at rank one under the zero-dimensional convention and at higher fixed rank on the orthogonal witness backbone.

For the target channel when \(r\ge2\), use \(P_{r,+}\) and \(P_{r,-}\) from the fixed-rank construction. Empirical Gram--Schmidt gives
\[
 N_0^{-1}\sum_mz_m=0,
 \qquad N_0^{-1}\sum_mz_mz_m^\top=I_d.                    \tag{4}
\]
Its orthogonal factor columns give the required joint spectrum and
\[
 \int_{\mathcal I}A_{0,d}(t)A_{0,d}(t)^\top dt
 =a_{\circ,0}^2\|\psi_\circ\|_2^2I_d.                    \tag{5}
\]
The compatibility inequalities verify all remaining class conditions. Only the first target-loading coordinate changes, so
\[
 \Delta_T=2|a_{\circ,1}|h_\circ\|\psi_\circ\|_\infty K_T^{-1/2}, \tag{6}
\]
and \(\mathrm{lem:rank\mbox{-}two\mbox{-}pair}\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(P_{r,+},P_{r,-})
 \le2|a_{\circ,0}|h_\circ c_f^{-1/2}\|\psi_\circ'\|_2
 \le\tfrac34(1/2-\alpha).                                 \tag{7}
\]

Let \(Q_-,Q_+\) be either pair, with targets \(\theta_-,\theta_+\), and put \(\zeta=\alpha+\varepsilon_{N_0,K_T,K_D}(\alpha)\). Honesty gives \(Q_u\{\theta_u\in B\}\ge1-\zeta\). Total-variation transfer and the union bound yield
\[
 Q_-\{\theta_-,\theta_+\in B\}
 \ge1-2\zeta-\operatorname{TV}(Q_-,Q_+).                  \tag{8}
\]
Since \(\varepsilon\le\tfrac14(1/2-\alpha)\), (3), (7), and (8) make this probability at least \(\tfrac34(1/2-\alpha)>0\). On the intersection, \(w(B)\ge\|\theta_+-\theta_-\|_\infty\). Thus
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(B)\ge c_Dn_D^{-1/2},
 \qquad
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_r}E_Pw(B)\ge c_TK_T^{-1/2}\quad(r\ge2). \tag{9}
\]
For \(r\ge2\), \(\max\{a,b\}\ge(a+b)/2\) combines the channels. No relative-growth condition was used. \(\square\)
