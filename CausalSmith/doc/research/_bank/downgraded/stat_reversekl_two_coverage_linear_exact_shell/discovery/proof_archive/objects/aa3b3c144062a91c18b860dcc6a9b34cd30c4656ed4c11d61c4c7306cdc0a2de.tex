Let
\[
a_n=d\log(en)+\log(2n^2).
\]
The fast alternative in \(\text{thm:erm-plugin-upper}\) supplies the measurable learner \(\widehat\pi_{\mathrm{ERM}}\), whose worst-case expected regularized regret over the fixed nonempty shell is at most
\[
600\kappa_\eta D\frac{a_n}{n}+\frac{n^{-2}}2\min\{2,\eta/2\}
=600\kappa_\eta D\frac{a_n}{n}+b_\eta n^{-2}.
\]
Indeed, the minimum of the two good-event bounds in that theorem is no larger than its first argument, and \(\delta_n=n^{-2}\). By the infimum over learners in \(\text{def:minimax-risk}\), it follows that
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,\mathfrak E}\bigl(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\bigr)
\le 600\kappa_\eta D\frac{a_n}{n}+b_\eta n^{-2}.
\]

For every integer \(n\ge1\), direct expansion gives
\[
a_n=d+(d+2)\log n+\log2
\le(d+2)\log(2en).
\]
Write \(Q=Q_{d,D,\eta}\), fix \(0<\epsilon<1\), and set
\[
L=\log\!\left(\frac{8eQ}{\epsilon}\right),
\qquad
n_\epsilon=\left\lceil\frac{8QL}{\epsilon}\right\rceil.
\]
Since \(Q\ge1\) and \(L\ge\log(8e)>1\), one has \(QL/\epsilon>1\), and therefore
\[
\frac{8QL}{\epsilon}\le n_\epsilon
\le\frac{8QL}{\epsilon}+1
\le\frac{9QL}{\epsilon}.
\]
We next bound the logarithm in the prediction radius. The function \(x\mapsto x-\log(9x/4)\) is increasing for \(x>1\) and is positive at \(x=\log(8e)\). Consequently \(\log(9L/4)\le L\), and hence
\[
\log(2en_\epsilon)
\le\log\!\left(\frac{18eQL}{\epsilon}\right)
=L+\log(9L/4)
\le2L.
\]
Using \(600\kappa_\eta D(d+2)\le Q\) and the lower bound on \(n_\epsilon\), we obtain
\[
600\kappa_\eta D\frac{a_{n_\epsilon}}{n_\epsilon}
\le600\kappa_\eta D(d+2)\frac{2L}{8QL/\epsilon}
\le\frac{\epsilon}{4}.
\]
Similarly, because \(b_\eta\le Q\), \(Q\ge1\), \(L>1\), and \(0<\epsilon<1\),
\[
b_\eta n_\epsilon^{-2}
\le\frac{b_\eta\epsilon^2}{64Q^2L^2}
\le\frac{\epsilon}{64}.
\]
Thus
\[
\mathfrak R_{n_\epsilon,\mathfrak E}
\bigl(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\bigr)
\le\frac{17}{64}\epsilon<\epsilon.
\]
The definition in \(\text{def:sample-complexity}\) now yields
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le n_\epsilon
=\left\lceil\frac{8Q_{d,D,\eta}}{\epsilon}
\log\!\left(\frac{8eQ_{d,D,\eta}}{\epsilon}\right)\right\rceil,
\]
which is the asserted explicit bound.

Finally, with \(d,D,\eta\) and \(\mathfrak E\) fixed, the upper estimate on \(n_\epsilon\) gives
\[
0\le
\frac{\epsilon^2}{d}N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le\frac{9Q_{d,D,\eta}}d\,\epsilon
\log\!\left(\frac{8eQ_{d,D,\eta}}{\epsilon}\right)
\longrightarrow0
\qquad(\epsilon\downarrow0),
\]
since \(\epsilon\log(1/\epsilon)\to0\). If positive constants \(c(C,D,\eta)\) and \(\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\) satisfied
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\ge c(C,D,\eta)\frac d{\epsilon^2}
\]
for every \(0<\epsilon\le\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\), then the left-hand side of the preceding limit would be at least \(c(C,D,\eta)>0\) throughout a punctured neighborhood of zero, a contradiction. The argument used no feature-design property beyond membership in the nonempty finite fixed-design shell, so it applies in particular to every genuinely shared-feature off-diagonal experiment.
