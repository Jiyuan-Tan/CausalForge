The varying-design feasible index set is \(\{(1,1)\}\cup\{(C,D):1<D\le C<e^\eta\}\). The equality \(D=1\) forces \(C=1\), and the point \((1,1)\) has zero minimax risk and \(N^\star(\epsilon;d,1,1,\eta)=1\). Every nonempty fixed-design shell has the computable upper bound \(N^\star(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\le\widetilde O(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\})\). For \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\) and \(2D-1<e^\eta\), the exact shared-feature family proves \(N^\star(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\ge c(C,D,\eta)dD\eta/\epsilon\) for sufficiently small \(\epsilon\). Canonical tabular fixed designs force \(D=C\), while shared-feature designs can realize \(D<C\).
