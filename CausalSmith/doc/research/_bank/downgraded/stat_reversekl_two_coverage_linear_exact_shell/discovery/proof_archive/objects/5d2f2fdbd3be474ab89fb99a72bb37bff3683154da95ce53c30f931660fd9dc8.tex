Fix \(d\ge4\), \(\eta>0\), and the public experiment \(\mathfrak E\). By \(\text{lem:fixed-experiment-shell-certificate}\), the shell \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\) is nonempty if and only if at least one of the finitely many displayed exponential nonlinear-semidefinite systems is feasible. Indeed, a shell member supplies the branch indexed by its positive-mass context support, its reward coefficient, and a unit eigenvector for the largest generalized eigenvalue; conversely, a feasible branch supplies a bounded realizable law whose likelihood-ratio constraints give \(C_P=C\), whose semidefinite and unit-kernel constraints give \(D_P=D\), and whose positive-definiteness constraint gives \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\succ0\). This proves the first assertion.

All remaining assertions follow from \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\), as we now spell out. If \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,1,1,\eta)\ne\varnothing\), that theorem gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,1,1,\eta)=1
\]
for every \(\epsilon>0\).

Suppose next that a nontrivial fixed-design shell is nonempty. The explicit learner used in \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) is the measurable idealized global-optimization learner of \(\text{thm:clean-temperature-feature-upper}\). Its selection and Gibbs normalization are measurable in \((\mathcal D_n,\mathfrak E)\), so it belongs to \(\mathcal L_n(\mathfrak E)\), and its construction uses neither the law-specific context distribution \(\rho\) nor the shell labels \(C\) and \(D\). If \(\eta\le2\epsilon\), the deterministic Gibbs regret bound used there is at most \(\eta/2\le\epsilon\) already at sample size one. Since sample sizes belong to \(\mathbb N\),
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
If \(\eta>2\epsilon\), put
\[
q_\epsilon=dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\},
\qquad
\ell_\epsilon=1+\log(eq_\epsilon)+\log\{1+(1+\eta)q_\epsilon\},
\]
and
\[
n_0=\left\lceil10^8q_\epsilon\ell_\epsilon\right\rceil.
\]
The explicit inversion established in \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) shows that this learner satisfies
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n_0}}
\left[J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi_{\mathrm{stab}})\right]
\le\epsilon.
\]
Taking the infimum over all learners in the definition of \(\mathfrak R_{n_0,\mathfrak E}\), and then applying the definition of sample complexity, gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le n_0
=\widetilde O\!\left(dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right).
\]
Thus this upper bound is valid on every nonempty fixed-design exact shell and does not involve pointwise coverage \(C\).

Fix now \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\). The experiment-nonuniqueness part of \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) supplies finite public experiments \(\mathfrak E_0\) and \(\mathfrak E_1\), both having nonempty exact shells at these same indices, for which
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1
\qquad(\epsilon>0),
\]
whereas there are constants \(c(C,D,\eta)>0\) and \(\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)>0\) such that
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_1}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\ge c(C,D,\eta)\frac{dD\eta}{\epsilon}
\qquad(0<\epsilon\le\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)).
\]
That informative construction is valid throughout \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), including when \(2D-1\ge e^\eta\). Because its constant is permitted to depend on \((C,D,\eta)\), its parameter-invariant rate content is \(\Omega_{C,D,\eta}(d/\epsilon)\), rather than a lower bound uniform in \(D\) or \(\eta\). The two experiments therefore show that \((d,C,D,\eta)\) does not determine the fixed-design minimax frontier.

Let \(L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\) be any lower bound asserted uniformly over all public experiments with a nonempty exact shell at the displayed indices. Applying it to \(\mathfrak E_0\) yields
\[
L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
Hence every such index-only uniform lower bound is at most one.

Finally, fix a specified finite public experiment with a nonempty shell and hold \((d,C,D,\eta)\) fixed. The final conclusion of \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) gives directly
\[
\lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}
\frac{\epsilon^2}{d}N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=0.
\]
Equivalently, this also follows from the preceding explicit inversion: for all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), one has \(\eta>2\epsilon\) and \(\eta\epsilon<1\), whence \(q_\epsilon=dD\eta/\epsilon\) and
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
=O_{d,D,\eta}\!\left(\epsilon^{-1}\log(1/\epsilon)\right).
\]
Multiplication by \(\epsilon^2/d\) gives an upper bound of order \(\epsilon\log(1/\epsilon)\), which tends to zero. Thus no positive quadratic lower branch can hold for every sufficiently small accuracy on a specified finite experiment. Combining these conclusions proves the theorem.
