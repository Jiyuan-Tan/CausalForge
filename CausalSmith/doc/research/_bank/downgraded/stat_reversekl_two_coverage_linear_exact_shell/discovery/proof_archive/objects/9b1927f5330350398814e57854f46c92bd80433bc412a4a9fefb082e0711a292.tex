The varying-design feasible index set is \(\{(1,1)\}\cup\{(C,D):1<D\le C<e^\eta\}\). Every nonempty shell with \(D=1\) has \(C=1\), and \((1,1)\) has zero minimax risk and sample complexity one. For every nonempty fixed-design shell, the explicit lower-envelope Gibbs learner satisfies \(N^\star(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\le\widetilde O(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\})\). On the interior region \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\) and \(2D-1<e^\eta\), the exact shared-feature family gives the local fast lower bound \(N^\star(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\ge c(C,D,\eta)dD\eta/\epsilon\) for sufficiently small \(\epsilon\). Canonical tabular features force \(D=C\), whereas shared features can realize off-diagonal pairs.
