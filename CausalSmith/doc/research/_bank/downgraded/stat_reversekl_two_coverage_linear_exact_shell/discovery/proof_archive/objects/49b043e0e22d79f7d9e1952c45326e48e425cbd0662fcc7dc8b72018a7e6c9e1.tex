Fix \(d\ge4\), \(\eta>0\), and the public experiment \(\mathfrak E\). By \(\text{lem:fixed-experiment-shell-certificate}\), the shell \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\) is nonempty if and only if at least one of the finitely many displayed exponential nonlinear-semidefinite systems is feasible. This proves the feasibility assertion.

All remaining claims follow from \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) and \(\text{thm:clean-temperature-feature-upper}\). If \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,1,1,\eta)\ne\varnothing\), the former theorem gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,1,1,\eta)=1
\]
for every \(\epsilon>0\). Suppose a nontrivial fixed-design shell is nonempty. The stabilized learner is a measurable member of \(\mathcal L_n(\mathfrak E)\) and uses neither the law-specific \(\rho\) nor the shell labels \(C\) and \(D\). If \(\eta\le2\epsilon\), its deterministic regret cap is at most \(\eta/2\le\epsilon\) at sample size one, so
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
If \(\eta>2\epsilon\), define
\[
q_\epsilon=dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\},
\qquad
\ell_\epsilon=1+\log(eq_\epsilon)+\log\{1+(1+\eta)q_\epsilon\},
\]
\[
n_0=\left\lceil10^8q_\epsilon\ell_\epsilon\right\rceil.
\]
By \(\text{thm:clean-temperature-feature-upper}\),
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n_0}}
\left[J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi_{\mathrm{stab}})\right]
\le\epsilon.
\]
Taking the infimum over learners and applying the sample-complexity definition gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le n_0
=\widetilde O\!\left(dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right).
\]
Thus the clean upper bound holds on every nonempty exact shell and does not involve \(C\).

For every \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) supplies finite public experiments \(\mathfrak E_0\) and \(\mathfrak E_1\), both with nonempty exact shells at the same indices, such that
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1
\qquad(\epsilon>0),
\]
while for some positive \(c(C,D,\eta)\) and \(\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\),
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_1}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\ge c(C,D,\eta)\frac{dD\eta}{\epsilon},
\qquad 0<\epsilon\le\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta).
\]
The informative experiment is valid even when \(2D-1\ge e^\eta\). Because its constant is pointwise in \((C,D,\eta)\), its invariant content is \(\Omega_{C,D,\eta}(d/\epsilon)\), not a lower bound uniform in \(D\) or \(\eta\). The two experiments show that the index tuple does not determine the minimax frontier. Applying any uniform index-only lower bound to \(\mathfrak E_0\) shows that it is at most one.

Finally, the same theorem proves that for every specified finite experiment with a nonempty exact shell and fixed indices,
\[
\lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}\frac{\epsilon^2}{d}N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=0.
\]
Indeed, outside the boundary case this also follows directly from the explicit inversion, which becomes \(O_{d,D,\eta}(\epsilon^{-1}\log(1/\epsilon))\) for sufficiently small \(\epsilon\). Hence no positive quadratic lower branch can persist throughout all sufficiently small accuracies. This proves every assertion.
