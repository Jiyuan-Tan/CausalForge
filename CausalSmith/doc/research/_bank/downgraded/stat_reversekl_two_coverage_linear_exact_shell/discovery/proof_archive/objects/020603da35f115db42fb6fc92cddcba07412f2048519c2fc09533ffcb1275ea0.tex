Let \(W_i=(X_i,A_i)\), let \(\varepsilon_i=Y_i-r_\theta(W_i)\), and write \(\widehat h=\widehat f-r_\theta\). Conditional on \((W_i)_{i=1}^n\), the variables \(\varepsilon_i\) are independent, have conditional mean zero, and lie in \([-1,1]\). Let \(V_n\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) be the subspace of all evaluation vectors \((h(W_i))_{i=1}^n\) with \(h\in\operatorname{span}(\phi)\), and let \(\Pi_n\) be its orthogonal projector. Its rank is at most \(d\).

The ERM basic inequality is
\[
\|\widehat h\|_n^2
\le\frac2n\sum_{i=1}^n\varepsilon_i\widehat h(W_i).
\]
Because the evaluation vector of \(\widehat h\) lies in \(V_n\), Cauchy--Schwarz and \(2uv\le u^2/2+2v^2\) give
\[
\|\widehat h\|_n^2
\le\frac12\|\widehat h\|_n^2+\frac2n\|\Pi_n\varepsilon\|_2^2,
\]
whence
\[
\|\widehat h\|_n^2\le\frac4n\|\Pi_n\varepsilon\|_2^2.
\]
Take a one-half net of the unit sphere of \(V_n\) with at most \(5^d\) elements. Applying \(\text{lem:bounded-projection-concentration}\) with \(\zeta=(4n^2)^{-1}\) yields, with conditional probability at least \(1-(4n^2)^{-1}\),
\[
\|\Pi_n\varepsilon\|_2^2
\le 8\{d\log5+\log(8n^2)\}.
\]
Indeed, the norm is at most twice the largest inner product with a net point. Therefore
\[
\|\widehat f-r_\theta\|_n^2
\le c_1\frac{d+\log(2n^2)}n
\le c_2a_n.
\]
Choosing \(c_0\ge c_2\) proves \(r_\theta\in\mathcal Q_n\) on this event.

It remains to pass from empirical to logging prediction error uniformly over the confidence set. The prediction polytope \(\mathcal F\) lies in a linear space of dimension at most \(d\) and in the unit ball of the supremum norm. A maximal \(u\)-separated subset of \(\mathcal F\) has disjoint supremum-norm balls of radius \(u/2\) inside the ball of radius \(1+u/2\). Comparing volume in the affine span gives
\[
N_{\mathcal F}(u)\le(1+2/u)^d.
\]
Apply \(\text{lem:zhao-uniform-square-comparison}\) with \(\mathcal G=\mathcal F\), \(\mu=\rho\times\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\), \(u=1/n\), and failure probability \((4n^2)^{-1}\). For \(n\ge2\), simultaneously for all \(f,g\in\mathcal F\),
\[
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}(f-g)^2
\le2\|f-g\|_n^2+c_3a_n.
\]
The case \(n=1\) follows after enlarging the universal constant because all prediction differences are bounded by one.

On the intersection of the two events, take any \(f\in\mathcal Q_n\). The triangle inequality gives
\[
\|f-r_\theta\|_n
\le\|f-\widehat f\|_n+\|\widehat f-r_\theta\|_n
\le2\sqrt{c_0a_n}.
\]
Consequently
\[
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}(f-r_\theta)^2
\le8c_0a_n+c_3a_n=Ka_n.
\]
The two failure probabilities sum to at most \((2n^2)^{-1}=\delta_n/2\), completing the proof.
