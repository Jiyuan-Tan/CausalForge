Fix \(d\ge4\), \(\eta>0\), and the public experiment \(\mathfrak E\). By \(\text{lem:fixed-experiment-shell-certificate}\), a law in \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\) exists if and only if at least one of the displayed finitely many branches has a feasible triple \((\vartheta,\rho,u)\). Its likelihood-ratio constraints impose \(C_P=C\), its semidefinite and unit-kernel constraints impose \(D_P=D\), and its positive-definiteness constraint imposes \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\succ0\). Conversely, every member of the exact shell supplies such a branch by taking \(I\) to be its positive-mass context support, \(\vartheta=\theta\), and \(u\) to be a unit eigenvector associated with the largest generalized eigenvalue. This proves the fixed-experiment nonemptiness equivalence.

If the boundary shell is nonempty, \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,1,1,\eta)=1
\]
for every \(\epsilon>0\).

Now suppose that a nontrivial shell is nonempty. Invoke \(\text{thm:clean-temperature-feature-upper}\). Its learner selects, inside the data-dependent compact set \(\mathcal Q_n\), a global minimizer of the empirical worst Gibbs-weighted squared radius and deploys the corresponding Gibbs policy. The measurable-selection argument in that theorem makes this an explicit measurable idealized global-optimization learner in \(\mathcal L_n(\mathfrak E)\). Its definition uses only \((\mathcal D_n,\mathfrak E)\), and hence uses neither the law-specific \(\rho\) nor the shell labels \(C,D\).

The deterministic Gibbs regret cap in \(\text{thm:clean-temperature-feature-upper}\) is \(\eta/2\). Hence, if \(\eta\le2\epsilon\), the risk at sample size one is at most \(\epsilon\), and the fact that sample sizes range over \(\mathbb N\) gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
If \(\eta>2\epsilon\), define
\[
q_\epsilon=dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\},
\qquad
\ell_\epsilon=1+\log(eq_\epsilon)+\log\{1+(1+\eta)q_\epsilon\},
\]
\[
n_0=\left\lceil10^8q_\epsilon\ell_\epsilon\right\rceil.
\]
The explicit inversion in \(\text{thm:clean-temperature-feature-upper}\), including integer rounding and the \(n^{-2}\min\{2,\eta/2\}\) exceptional-event contribution, yields
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n_0}}
\left[J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi_{\mathrm{stab}})\right]
\le\epsilon.
\]
Since the minimax risk is the infimum over all learners in \(\mathcal L_{n_0}(\mathfrak E)\),
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\le n_0.
\]
This is the asserted
\[
\widetilde O\!\left(dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right)
\]
upper bound, with universal multiplicative constants. In particular, \(C\) occurs neither in the learner nor in the guarantee.

It remains to determine what the four indices can say about lower difficulty. Fix \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\). By \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\), there are finite public experiments \(\mathfrak E_0\) and \(\mathfrak E_1\), both with nonempty exact shells at these indices, such that
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1
\]
for every \(\epsilon>0\), whereas for positive constants \(c(C,D,\eta)\) and \(\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\),
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_1}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\ge c(C,D,\eta)\frac{dD\eta}{\epsilon}
\]
whenever \(0<\epsilon\le\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\). The informative construction applies throughout \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), and therefore includes pairs satisfying \(2D-1\ge e^\eta\). Because the constant is allowed to depend on \((C,D,\eta)\), the invariant lower-rate content is \(\Omega_{C,D,\eta}(d/\epsilon)\), not a uniform lower dependence on \(D\) or \(\eta\). The pair \((\mathfrak E_0,\mathfrak E_1)\) proves that \((d,C,D,\eta)\) does not determine a unique fixed-experiment frontier.

For completeness, fix one finite \(\mathfrak E\) and hold \((d,C,D,\eta)\) fixed. For all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), one has \(\eta>2\epsilon\) and \(\eta\epsilon<1\), so
\[
q_\epsilon=dD\eta/\epsilon.
\]
Both logarithms in \(\ell_\epsilon\) are then \(O_{d,D,\eta}(\log(1/\epsilon))\). The explicit upper bound therefore implies
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
=O_{d,D,\eta}\!\left(\epsilon^{-1}\log(1/\epsilon)\right)
\]
and hence
\[
0\le
\frac{\epsilon^2}{d}N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
=O_{d,D,\eta}\!\left(\epsilon\log(1/\epsilon)\right)
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus no positive lower bound of order \(d/\epsilon^2\), equivalently of order \(dD/\epsilon^2\) at fixed \(D\), can hold for every sufficiently small \(\epsilon\) on a specified finite experiment.

Finally, let \(L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\) be any lower bound asserted uniformly over all public experiments having a nonempty exact shell at these indices. Applying it to \(\mathfrak E_0\) forces
\[
L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
No nontrivial index-only uniform lower frontier can therefore diverge as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). This proves every assertion of the corrected theorem.
