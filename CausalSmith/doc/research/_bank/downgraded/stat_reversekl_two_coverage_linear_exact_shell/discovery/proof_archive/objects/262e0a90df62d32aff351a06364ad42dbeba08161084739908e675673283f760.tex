The strongest closed result remains the complementary envelope from \(\text{thm:erm-plugin-upper}\) and \(\text{thm:localized-upper}\): \(N^\star_{\mathfrak E}\le\widetilde O\!\left(dD\min\{d\eta/\epsilon,d/\epsilon^2,\kappa_\eta/\epsilon,(1+e^{2\eta})^2/\epsilon^2\}\right)\). It already answers the question positively on the universal low-temperature subregime \(0<\eta\le1\): from \(\kappa_\eta=\eta\int_0^1 t e^{2\eta t}\,dt\le(e^2/2)\eta\) and \((1+e^{2\eta})^2\le(1+e^2)^2\), the empirical-risk-minimizer Gibbs plug-in satisfies \(N^\star_{\mathfrak E}\le\widetilde O\!\left(dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right)\) there with universal constants and without using \(C\) or \(D\). The unresolved range is unbounded temperature.
