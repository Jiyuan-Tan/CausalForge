The set \(\mathcal F\), equipped with the supremum norm on the finite set \(S\), lies in the unit ball of a linear space of dimension at most \(d\). A maximal \(v\)-separated subset is a \(v\)-net. The disjoint supremum-norm balls of radius \(v/2\) around its points lie in a ball enlarged by the factor \(1+v/2\). Comparing finite-dimensional volumes gives
\[
N_{\mathcal F}(v)\le(1+2/v)^d.
\]

If \(\|g-g'\|_\infty\le v\), put \(g_t=(1-t)g+tg'\). Differentiating the finite Gibbs formula gives
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\pi_{g_t}(a\mid x)
=\eta\pi_{g_t}(a\mid x)\left[(g'-g)(x,a)-\mathbb E_{\pi_{g_t}}(g'-g)(x,A)\right].
\]
After summing absolute values and integrating over \(t\),
\[
\|\pi_g(\cdot\mid x)-\pi_{g'}(\cdot\mid x)\|_1\le2\eta v.
\]
If also \(\|f-f'\|_\infty\le v\), then \(g-f\) and \(g'-f'\) take values in \([-1,1]\), and hence
\[
\|H_{g,f}-H_{g',f'}\|_\infty\le(2\eta+4)v.
\]
Choose \(v=\{n(2\eta+4)\}^{-1}\). A product of two \(v\)-nets for \(\mathcal F\) is therefore a \(1/n\)-net for the class \(\{H_{g,f}:g,f\in\mathcal F\}\), and its cardinality \(M\) satisfies
\[
M\le\{1+4n(\eta+2)\}^{2d}.
\]

We next establish the required fixed-function deviation directly. Let \(W_1,\ldots,W_n\in[0,1]\) be independent with common mean \(\mu\). For \(|\lambda|<3\), the power series for the exponential and \(k!\ge2\cdot3^{k-2}\) for \(k\ge2\) imply, for every \(|w|\le1\),
\[
e^{\lambda w}\le1+\lambda w+\frac{\lambda^2w^2}{2(1-|\lambda|/3)}.
\]
Apply this with \(w=W_i-\mu\), use \(\mathbb E(W_i-\mu)=0\) and \(\mathbb E(W_i-\mu)^2\le\mu\), and then use \(1+s\le e^s\). Exponential Markov bounds, followed by minimization in \(\lambda\), give, for every \(t>0\),
\[
\Pr\left\{\left|P_nW-\mu\right|>\sqrt{\frac{2\mu t}{n}}+\frac{t}{3n}\right\}\le2e^{-t}.
\]
Since \(\sqrt{2\mu t/n}\le\mu/2+t/n\), the complementary event implies
\[
\mu\le2P_nW+\frac{8t}{3n},
\qquad
P_nW\le2\mu+\frac{8t}{3n}.
\]

Apply these inequalities to every member of the finite net and choose \(t=\log(4M/\zeta)\). A union bound gives both comparisons on the net with probability at least \(1-\zeta\). For arbitrary \(H_{g,f}\), choose a net member \(H_0\) within \(1/n\) in supremum norm. Replacing \(P_\rho H_0\) and \(P_nH_0\) by their counterparts for \(H_{g,f}\) costs at most \(3/n\) in either two-sided comparison. Finally,
\[
\log(4M/\zeta)
\le\log(4/\zeta)+2d\log\{1+4n(\eta+2)\},
\]
which gives exactly \(b_n(\zeta)\) and proves the simultaneous assertion.
