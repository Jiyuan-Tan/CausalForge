Fix a context \(x\) and restrict attention to the support of \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\cdot\mid x)\). Write \(p=\pi_r(\cdot\mid x)\), \(q=\pi_g(\cdot\mid x)\), and
\[
c=\eta^{-1}\log\mathbb E_p e^{\eta\Delta}.
\]
The two Gibbs formulas give
\[
\log\frac{q(a)}{p(a)}=\eta\{\Delta(a)-c\}.
\]
For \(\psi(t)=\log\mathbb E_p e^{t\Delta}\), convexity and differentiability give
\[
\mathbb E_p\Delta=\psi'(0)\le\frac{\psi(\eta)-\psi(0)}{\eta}=c\le\psi'(\eta)=\mathbb E_q\Delta.
\]
The conditional regularized regret is
\[
\eta^{-1}\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert p)=\mathbb E_q\Delta-c
\le\mathbb E_q\Delta-\mathbb E_p\Delta
\le\sqrt{\mathbb E_q\Delta^2}+\sqrt{\mathbb E_p\Delta^2}.
\]
Averaging over \(X\sim\rho\) and applying Jensen's inequality to the two square roots proves the slow bound \(\sqrt A+\sqrt B\).

For the fast bound, first note the scalar inequality
\[
(u-v)\log(u/v)\le\frac{u+v}{2}\{\log(u/v)\}^2
\qquad(u,v>0).
\]
Indeed, after writing \(u/v=e^t\), it is equivalent to \(2\tanh(|t|/2)\le|t|\), which follows because the derivative of \(s-2\tanh(s/2)\) is \(\tanh^2(s/2)\ge0\) and the function vanishes at zero. Summing the scalar inequality over actions yields
\[
\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert p)+\operatorname{KL}(p\Vert q)
\le\frac{\eta^2}{2}\left\{\mathbb E_q(\Delta-c)^2+\mathbb E_p(\Delta-c)^2\right\}.
\]
Because \(c\) lies between \(\mathbb E_p\Delta\) and \(\mathbb E_q\Delta\),
\[
c^2\le\max\{(\mathbb E_p\Delta)^2,(\mathbb E_q\Delta)^2\}
\le\mathbb E_p\Delta^2+\mathbb E_q\Delta^2.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\mathbb E_q(\Delta-c)^2+\mathbb E_p(\Delta-c)^2
\le6\left\{\mathbb E_q\Delta^2+\mathbb E_p\Delta^2\right\}.
\]
The first KL divergence is no larger than the Jeffreys divergence, so division by \(\eta\) shows that the conditional regret is at most
\[
3\eta\left\{\mathbb E_q\Delta^2+\mathbb E_p\Delta^2\right\}.
\]
Averaging over contexts proves the fast bound \(3\eta(A+B)\), and taking the smaller of the two valid bounds proves the lemma.
