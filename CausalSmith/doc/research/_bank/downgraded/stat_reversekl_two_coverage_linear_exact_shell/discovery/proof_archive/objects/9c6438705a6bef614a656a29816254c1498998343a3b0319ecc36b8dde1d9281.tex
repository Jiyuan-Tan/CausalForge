For a fixed public experiment, shell nonemptiness is characterized by \(\text{lem:fixed-experiment-shell-certificate}\). Across public experiments with identical \((d,C,D,\eta)\), the frontier is nonunique: \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) gives a zero-risk experiment and an informative experiment with local lower bound \(c(C,D,\eta)dD\eta/\epsilon\), including pairs with \(2D-1\ge e^\eta\). Consequently a uniform matching \(\Omega(dD\epsilon^{-2})\) branch based only on the four indices is false, while \(\text{thm:localized-upper}\) supplies the computable bound \(\widetilde O(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\})\) for every nonempty fixed-experiment shell.
