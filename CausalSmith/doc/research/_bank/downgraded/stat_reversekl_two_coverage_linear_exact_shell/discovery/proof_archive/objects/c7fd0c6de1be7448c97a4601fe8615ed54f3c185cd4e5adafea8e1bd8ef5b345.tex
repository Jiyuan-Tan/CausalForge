Order the coordinates of \(\mathbb R^{|S|}\) by \((x,a)\in S\). For \(\phi(x,a)=e_{(x,a)}\), the covariance definitions give
\[
\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}
=\operatorname{diag}_{(x,a)\in S}\{\rho(x)\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a\mid x)\},
\qquad
\Lambda_P^\star
=\operatorname{diag}_{(x,a)\in S}\{\rho(x)\pi_P^\star(a\mid x)\}.
\]
Nonsingularity of \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\) implies that every displayed logging diagonal entry is strictly positive. Whitening is therefore coordinatewise, and
\[
\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}^{-1/2}\Lambda_P^\star\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}^{-1/2}
=\operatorname{diag}_{(x,a)\in S}
\left\{\frac{\pi_P^\star(a\mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a\mid x)}\right\}.
\]
The largest eigenvalue of a diagonal matrix is its largest diagonal entry. Because the support is finite and every logging cell has positive probability, this maximum is exactly the essential supremum defining \(C_P\). Hence \(D_P=C_P\). Imposing \(C_P=C\) is therefore equivalent to imposing \(D_P=C\), so \(\mathcal M(d,C,C,\eta)\) is precisely the canonical tabular reverse-KL shell.
