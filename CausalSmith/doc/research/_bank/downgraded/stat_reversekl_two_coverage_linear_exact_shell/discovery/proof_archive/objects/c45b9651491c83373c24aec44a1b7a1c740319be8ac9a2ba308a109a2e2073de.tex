Fix \(x\in\mathcal X\), and restrict all action distributions to the nonempty support \(\mathcal A_x^+=\{a:\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a\mid x)>0\}\). Write \(p=\pi_r(\cdot\mid x)\), \(q=\pi_g(\cdot\mid x)\), and \(\Delta(a)=g(x,a)-r(x,a)\). Both \(p\) and \(q\) are strictly positive on \(\mathcal A_x^+\). Their Gibbs formulas imply
\[
\frac{q(a)}{p(a)}=\frac{e^{\eta\Delta(a)}}{\mathbb E_p e^{\eta\Delta}},
\qquad
\log\frac{q(a)}{p(a)}=\eta\{\Delta(a)-c\},
\qquad
c=\eta^{-1}\log\mathbb E_p e^{\eta\Delta}.
\]
Let \(m_p=\mathbb E_p\Delta\) and \(m_q=\mathbb E_q\Delta\). For \(\psi(t)=\log\mathbb E_p e^{t\Delta}\), finite support makes \(\psi\) differentiable and convex, and exponential tilting gives \(\psi'(0)=m_p\) and \(\psi'(\eta)=m_q\). Hence the secant-slope inequalities for a differentiable convex function give
\[
m_p=\psi'(0)\le\frac{\psi(\eta)-\psi(0)}{\eta}=c\le\psi'(\eta)=m_q.
\]
The Gibbs variational identity, obtained by substituting the Gibbs formula for \(p\) into the conditional regularized welfare, yields
\[
J_{\eta,r,x}(p)-J_{\eta,r,x}(q)
=\eta^{-1}\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert p)
=m_q-c.
\]
Therefore
\[
J_{\eta,r,x}(p)-J_{\eta,r,x}(q)
\le m_q-m_p
\le |m_q|+|m_p|
\le\sqrt{\mathbb E_q\Delta^2}+\sqrt{\mathbb E_p\Delta^2}.
\]
This is the conditional slow inequality.

For the fast inequality, first observe that for all \(u,v>0\),
\[
(u-v)\log(u/v)\le\frac{u+v}{2}\{\log(u/v)\}^2.
\]
Indeed, on writing \(u/v=e^t\), the inequality reduces to \(2\tanh(|t|/2)\le |t|\); the difference between the right and left sides vanishes at zero and has derivative \(\tanh^2(s/2)\ge0\) at \(s=|t|\). Apply this inequality action by action with \(u=q(a)\) and \(v=p(a)\), and use the displayed likelihood-ratio formula. It follows that the conditional Jeffreys divergence satisfies
\[
\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert p)+\operatorname{KL}(p\Vert q)
\le\frac{\eta^2}{2}\left\{
\mathbb E_p(\Delta-c)^2+\mathbb E_q(\Delta-c)^2
\right\}.
\]
Define
\[
S(t)=\mathbb E_p(\Delta-t)^2+\mathbb E_q(\Delta-t)^2.
\]
The function \(S\) is convex. Since \(c\in[m_p,m_q]\), convexity implies \(S(c)\le\max\{S(m_p),S(m_q)\}\). Direct expansion at either endpoint gives the same value,
\[
S(m_p)=S(m_q)
=\mathbb E_p\Delta^2+\mathbb E_q\Delta^2-2m_pm_q.
\]
The elementary inequality \(-2m_pm_q\le m_p^2+m_q^2\), followed by Jensen's inequality under \(p\) and \(q\), therefore yields
\[
S(c)
\le2\left\{\mathbb E_p\Delta^2+\mathbb E_q\Delta^2\right\}.
\]
Since \(\operatorname{KL}(p\Vert q)\ge0\), the preceding two displays and the conditional Gibbs identity show
\[
J_{\eta,r,x}(p)-J_{\eta,r,x}(q)
\le\eta\left\{\mathbb E_p\Delta^2+\mathbb E_q\Delta^2\right\}.
\]
Finally average both conditional inequalities over \(X\sim\rho\). The fast inequality becomes \(J_{\eta,r}(\pi_r)-J_{\eta,r}(\pi_g)\le\eta(A+B)\). For the slow inequality, Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
\mathbb E_\rho\sqrt{\mathbb E_{\pi_r(\cdot\mid X)}\Delta^2}\le\sqrt A,
\qquad
\mathbb E_\rho\sqrt{\mathbb E_{\pi_g(\cdot\mid X)}\Delta^2}\le\sqrt B.
\]
Thus \(J_{\eta,r}(\pi_r)-J_{\eta,r}(\pi_g)\le\sqrt A+\sqrt B\) as well. Taking the smaller of the two bounds proves the proposed sharpened statement.
