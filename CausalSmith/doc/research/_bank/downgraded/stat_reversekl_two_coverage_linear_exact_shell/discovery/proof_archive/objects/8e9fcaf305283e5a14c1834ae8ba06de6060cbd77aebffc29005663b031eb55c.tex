Take a singleton context support and actions \(\{0,1\}\). Put
\[
q=e^{-\eta},\qquad
\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(1)=q,\qquad
\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(0)=1-q,
\]
\[
r(0)=r(1)=0,\qquad g(0)=0,\qquad g(1)=1.
\]
Because \(r\) is constant, \(\pi_r=\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\). Hence
\[
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_r}(g-r)^2=q=e^{-\eta}.
\]
The Gibbs normalizer for \(g\) is
\[
Z_g=1-q+qe^\eta=2-q,
\]
so, writing \(p=\pi_g(1)\),
\[
p=\frac1{2-q}\ge\frac12.
\]
Direct substitution into the regularized objective, equivalently the Gibbs variational identity, gives
\[
J_{\eta,r}(\pi_r)-J_{\eta,r}(\pi_g)
=\frac1\eta\operatorname{KL}\!\left(\operatorname{Bern}(p)\,\middle\Vert\,\operatorname{Bern}(q)\right).
\]
Let \(H(p)=-p\log p-(1-p)\log(1-p)\) be binary entropy. Since \(-\log q=\eta\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{KL}\!\left(\operatorname{Bern}(p)\,\middle\Vert\,\operatorname{Bern}(q)\right)
&=-H(p)+\eta p-(1-p)\log(1-q)\\
&\ge \eta p-H(p)\\
&\ge \frac\eta2-\log2.
\end{aligned}
\]
For \(\eta\ge4\), division by \(\eta\) gives
\[
J_{\eta,r}(\pi_r)-J_{\eta,r}(\pi_g)
\ge\frac12-\frac{\log2}{\eta}\ge\frac14.
\]
If the asserted universal \(K\) existed, this example would imply
\[
\frac14\le K\min\{\eta e^{-\eta},e^{-\eta/2}\},
\]
whose right-hand side tends to zero as \(\eta\to\infty\). This contradiction proves the final assertion.
