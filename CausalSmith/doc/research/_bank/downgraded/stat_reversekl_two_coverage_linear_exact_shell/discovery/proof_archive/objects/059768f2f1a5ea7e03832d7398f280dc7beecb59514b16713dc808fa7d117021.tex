The exact identities \(J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\pi)=\eta^{-1}\mathbb E_\rho\operatorname{KL}(\pi\Vert\pi_P^\star)\) and \(\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}h^2\le D_P\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}h^2\) are proved as \(\text{lem:gibbs-regret-identity}\) and \(\text{lem:feature-coverage-domination}\). A corrected, computable, coordinate-invariant all-context Gibbs completion is proposed.
