Order the coordinates of \(\mathbb R^{|S|}\) by \((x,a)\in S\). For canonical features,
\[
\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}=\operatorname{diag}_{(x,a)\in S}\{\rho(x)\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a\mid x)\},
\qquad
\Lambda_P^\star=\operatorname{diag}_{(x,a)\in S}\{\rho(x)\pi_P^\star(a\mid x)\}.
\]
Nonsingularity of \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\) implies that every displayed logging diagonal entry is positive. Consequently whitening is coordinatewise and
\[
\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}^{-1/2}\Lambda_P^\star\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}^{-1/2}
=\operatorname{diag}_{(x,a)\in S}\left\{\frac{\pi_P^\star(a\mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a\mid x)}\right\}.
\]
Its largest eigenvalue is the maximum of these finitely many ratios, which is their essential supremum. Thus \(D_P=C_P\). Imposing the exact equality \(C_P=C\) is therefore equivalent to imposing \(D_P=C\), and the exact shell with indices \((C,C)\) is precisely the canonical tabular reverse-KL shell.
