Fix \(d\ge4\), \(\eta>0\), and the finite public experiment \(\mathfrak E\). By \(\text{def:common-experiment}\), the supports, feature map, reference policy, and inverse temperature are public, while \(\rho\) remains law-specific. By \(\text{lem:fixed-experiment-shell-certificate}\), the shell \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\) is nonempty if and only if at least one branch of the displayed finite exponential nonlinear-semidefinite system is feasible.

Suppose a nontrivial shell is nonempty. The learner of \(\text{thm:clean-temperature-feature-upper}\) is a measurable element of \(\mathcal L_n(\mathfrak E)\) and uses only \((\mathcal D_n,\mathfrak E)\), not \(\rho\), \(C\), or \(D\). If \(\eta\le2\epsilon\), that theorem gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
If \(\eta>2\epsilon\), put
\[
q_\epsilon=dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\},
\qquad
\ell_\epsilon=1+\log(eq_\epsilon)+\log\{1+(1+\eta)q_\epsilon\}.
\]
Its explicit inversion gives risk at most \(\epsilon\) at
\[
n_0=\left\lceil10^8q_\epsilon\ell_\epsilon\right\rceil,
\]
and therefore
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le n_0
=\widetilde O\!\left(dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right).
\]
On a nonempty boundary shell, \(\text{thm:zero-risk-boundary}\) gives \(N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,1,1,\eta)=1\).

Now fix \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\). By \(\text{thm:fixed-design-feasibility-and-frontier-nonuniqueness}\), there is a finite public experiment \(\mathfrak E_0\) with a nonempty exact shell and
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1
\qquad(\epsilon>0).
\]
At the same indices, \(\text{prop:informative-fast-shell-all-indices}\) supplies a finite public experiment \(\mathfrak E_1\) and positive constants \(c(C,D,\eta)\) and \(\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\) such that
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_1}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\ge c(C,D,\eta)\frac{dD\eta}{\epsilon},
\qquad 0<\epsilon\le\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta).
\]
The informative proposition assumes only \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), so it includes pairs satisfying \(2D-1\ge e^\eta\). Since its constant may depend on \((C,D,\eta)\), its parameter-invariant content is the pointwise local rate \(\Omega_{C,D,\eta}(d/\epsilon)\), rather than uniform lower dependence on \(D\) or \(\eta\). The two experiments prove that \((d,C,D,\eta)\) does not determine fixed-experiment sample complexity.

If \(L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\) is any lower bound asserted uniformly over all public experiments with a nonempty exact shell at the displayed indices, applying it to \(\mathfrak E_0\) forces
\[
L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
Thus no such index-only bound can exceed one or diverge as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).

Finally, fix a specified finite experiment with a nonempty exact shell and hold \((d,C,D,\eta)\) fixed. The boundary case is immediate because its sample complexity equals one. In the nontrivial case, for all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), one has \(\eta>2\epsilon\) and \(\eta\epsilon<1\), so
\[
q_\epsilon=dD\eta/\epsilon,
\qquad
\ell_\epsilon=O_{d,D,\eta}(\log(1/\epsilon)).
\]
The explicit upper inversion therefore gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
=O_{d,D,\eta}\left(\epsilon^{-1}\log(1/\epsilon)\right).
\]
Consequently
\[
0\le\frac{\epsilon^2}{d}N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
=O_{d,D,\eta}\left(\epsilon\log(1/\epsilon)\right)\longrightarrow0.
\]
A positive \(d/\epsilon^2\) lower bound, or a positive \(dD/\epsilon^2\) lower bound with fixed \(D\), therefore cannot hold for every sufficiently small \(\epsilon\).
