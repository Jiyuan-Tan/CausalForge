When \(d=5\), \(k=\lfloor(5-1)/2\rfloor=2\), and the orthonormal basis used by the construction is \(e_0,u_1,w_1,u_2,w_2\); no optional \(z\) coordinate is present. For each \(j\in\{1,2\}\), the coordinate representations of the two informative action features in the \((u_j,w_j)\) plane are
\[
\phi(x_j,+)=2^{-1/2}(1,1),
\qquad
\phi(x_j,-)=2^{-1/2}(1,-1).
\]
Both vectors have nonzero components in both latent directions \(u_j\) and \(w_j\). They are distinct, nonorthogonal to each of those directions, and neither is any canonical basis vector. These feature identities are common to every sign-indexed law in \(\mathcal H_{5,C,D,\eta,\epsilon}\); only the coefficient signs change. Hence every admissible member is genuinely shared-feature rather than a canonical tabular-coordinate instance.
