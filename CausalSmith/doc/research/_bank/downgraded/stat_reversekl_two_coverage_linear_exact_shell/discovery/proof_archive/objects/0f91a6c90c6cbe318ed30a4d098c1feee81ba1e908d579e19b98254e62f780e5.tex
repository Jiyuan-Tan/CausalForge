The varying-design feasible index set is \(\{(1,1)\}\cup\{(C,D):1<D\le C<e^\eta\}\); \((1,1)\) has zero risk; every nonempty fixed-design shell has the computable upper bound \(\widetilde O(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\})\); and the interior region \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), \(2D-1<e^\eta\), has the local fast lower bound \(\Omega(dD\eta/\epsilon)\).
