Choose the universal radius constant \(c_0\) large enough for \(\text{lem:bounded-linear-localization}\), and put
\[
a_n=\frac{d\log(en)+\log(2n^2)}{n}.
\]
The learner is a finite-dimensional prediction-space construction. The empirical risk minimizer, the compact confidence set, every coordinate minimum, and the Gibbs normalization are determined by \(\mathcal D_n\) and the public experiment \(\mathfrak E\). Fixed sequential lexicographic tie breaking makes these finite-dimensional optimization maps Borel measurable. Thus \(\widehat\pi\in\mathcal L_n(\mathfrak E)\). Its formula contains neither \(C\) nor \(D\).

Fix \(P\in\mathcal M(d,C,D,\eta)\). By \(\text{lem:bounded-linear-localization}\), there is an event \(\mathcal E_n\) with \(P^{\otimes n}(\mathcal E_n)\ge1-\delta_n/2\) on which
\[
r_\theta\in\mathcal Q_n,
\qquad
\sup_{f\in\mathcal Q_n}\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}(f-r_\theta)^2\le K a_n
\]
for a universal \(K\). Compactness of \(\mathcal Q_n\) makes every coordinate minimum attainable. On \(\mathcal E_n\), define
\[
\Delta_n(x,a)=\underline r_n(x,a)-r_\theta(x,a).
\]
Truth inclusion gives the global sign condition
\[
-1\le\Delta_n(x,a)\le0
\qquad\text{for every }(x,a)\in S.
\]

Write \(\Lambda=\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\), which is positive definite by the shell assumptions, and define the leverage
\[
\ell(x,a)=\phi(x,a)^\top\Lambda^{-1}\phi(x,a).
\]
For each \((x,a)\), choose an attaining function \(f_{x,a}\in\mathcal Q_n\). If \(h_{x,a}=f_{x,a}-r_\theta\), the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality in the \(\Lambda\)-inner product gives
\[
\Delta_n(x,a)^2=h_{x,a}(x,a)^2
\le \ell(x,a)\,\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}h_{x,a}(X,A)^2
\le K a_n\ell(x,a).
\]
Thus the nonlinear lower-envelope error has the valid quadratic majorant
\[
b_n(x,a)^2=\phi(x,a)^\top M_n\phi(x,a),
\qquad
M_n=K a_n\Lambda^{-1}\succeq0.
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:quadratic-majorant-transfer}\) and then the trace bound by the largest eigenvalue yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}\Delta_n^2
&\le K a_n\operatorname{tr}(\Lambda^{-1}\Lambda_P^\star)\\
&=K a_n D_{2,P}\\
&\le K a_n dD_P
=K a_n dD.
\end{aligned}
\]
Notice that the logging expectation of this pointwise majorant is
\[
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}b_n^2
=K a_n\operatorname{tr}(\Lambda^{-1}\Lambda)=K a_n d.
\]
Since \(a_n\) itself is of order \(d/n\) up to logarithms, the coordinatewise envelope costs \(d^2/n\), not \(d/n\). This is the precise extra dimension factor absent from the original claim.

The Gibbs identity in \(\text{lem:gibbs-regret-identity}\) distinguishes the causal welfare target from the learned policy. Applying \(\text{lem:gibbs-pessimism-conversion}\) to \(r_\theta\), \(\underline r_n\), and \(\Delta_n\) gives simultaneously
\[
J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi)
\le \mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}[-\Delta_n]
\le \sqrt{\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}\Delta_n^2}
\]
and
\[
J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi)
\le\frac{\eta}{2}\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}\Delta_n^2.
\]
Consequently, on \(\mathcal E_n\),
\[
J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi)
\le
\min\left\{\frac{K\eta a_ndD}{2},\sqrt{Ka_ndD}\right\}.
\]
This is
\[
\widetilde O\left(\min\left\{\frac{\eta d^2D}{n},\sqrt{\frac{d^2D}{n}}\right\}\right).
\]

It remains to integrate the failure event. Both \(r_\theta\) and \(\underline r_n\) take values in \([0,1]\). If \(\pi_u\) and \(\pi_v\) are Gibbs policies generated by two such potentials, then on the support of \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\),
\[
\log\frac{\pi_u(a\mid x)}{\pi_v(a\mid x)}
=\eta\{u(x,a)-v(x,a)\}+\log\frac{Z_v(x)}{Z_u(x)}\le2\eta.
\]
The Gibbs-regret identity therefore bounds every realized regret by two. Since \(P^{\otimes n}(\mathcal E_n^c)\le\delta_n/2\), its contribution is at most \(\delta_n\). The event and all constants are uniform over the shell, so
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M(d,C,D,\eta)}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}
\left[J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi)\right]
\le
\widetilde O\left(
\min\left\{\frac{\eta d^2D}{n},\sqrt{\frac{d^2D}{n}}\right\}
\right)+\delta_n.
\]

By \(\text{thm:feasible-index-region}\), every nonempty shell has \(D\ge1\), so \(d^2D\ge16\).

Finally, choose \(n\) to make either the fast term or the slow term at most \(\epsilon/2\). This requires, up to the same logarithms,
\[
n\ge K' d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}.
\]
The failure contribution is also absorbed. Indeed, the Gibbs Bregman calculation bounds regret deterministically by \(\min\{2,\eta/2\}\). If \(\eta\le2\epsilon\), this is already at most \(\epsilon\); if \(\eta>2\epsilon\) on the fast branch, \((\eta/2)n^{-2}\le\epsilon\) after increasing \(K'\); and on the slow branch \(2n^{-2}\le\epsilon\). The definition of \(N^\star\) now gives
\[
N^\star(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le\widetilde O\left(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right)
\]
whenever the displayed right-hand side is at least two.
