Choose \(b\in\mathbb R^d\) with \(h(x,a)=\phi(x,a)^\top b\). Then
\[
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}h^2=b^\top\Lambda_P^\star b,
\qquad
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}h^2=b^\top\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}b.
\]
Because \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\succ0\), put \(u=\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}^{1/2}b\). The definition of the largest eigenvalue gives
\[
b^\top\Lambda_P^\star b
=u^\top(\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}^{-1/2}\Lambda_P^\star\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}^{-1/2})u
\le D_P\|u\|_2^2
=D_Pb^\top\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}b.
\]
