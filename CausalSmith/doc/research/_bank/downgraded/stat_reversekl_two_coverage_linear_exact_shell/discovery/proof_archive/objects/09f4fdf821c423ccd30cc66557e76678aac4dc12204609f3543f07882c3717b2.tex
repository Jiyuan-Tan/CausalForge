Choose \(c_0=64\) and put
\[
a_n=d\log(en)+\log(2n^2).
\]
The construction is a finite-dimensional prediction-space procedure. The empirical risk minimizer, the compact confidence set, each of the finitely many coordinate minima, and the Gibbs normalization are determined by \(\mathcal D_n\) and the public experiment \(\mathfrak E\). The stipulated sequential lexicographic tie breaking makes these optimization maps Borel measurable. Hence \(\widehat\pi\in\mathcal L_n(\mathfrak E)\). Its formula contains neither \(C\) nor \(D\).

Fix \(P\in\mathcal M(d,C,D,\eta)\). By \(\text{lem:bounded-linear-localization}\), there is an event \(\mathcal E_n\) satisfying \(P^{\otimes n}(\mathcal E_n)\ge1-\delta_n/2\) on which
\[
r_\theta\in\mathcal Q_n,
\qquad
\sup_{f\in\mathcal Q_n}\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}(f-r_\theta)^2
\le 600\frac{a_n}{n}.
\]
Compactness of \(\mathcal Q_n\) makes every coordinate minimum attainable. On \(\mathcal E_n\), define
\[
\Delta_n(x,a)=\underline r_n(x,a)-r_\theta(x,a).
\]
Truth inclusion and the range restriction defining \(\mathcal F\) imply
\[
-1\le\Delta_n(x,a)\le0
\qquad\text{for every }(x,a)\in S.
\]

Write \(\Lambda=\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\), which is positive definite. For each \((x,a)\), choose an attaining function \(f_{x,a}\in\mathcal Q_n\), and put \(h_{x,a}=f_{x,a}-r_\theta\). If \(b_{x,a}\in\mathbb R^d\) represents this linear contrast, Cauchy--Schwarz in the \(\Lambda\)-inner product gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_n(x,a)^2
&=\{\phi(x,a)^\top b_{x,a}\}^2\\
&\le \{b_{x,a}^\top\Lambda b_{x,a}\}
   \{\phi(x,a)^\top\Lambda^{-1}\phi(x,a)\}\\
&=\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}h_{x,a}^2
   \{\phi(x,a)^\top\Lambda^{-1}\phi(x,a)\}\\
&\le 600\frac{a_n}{n}\phi(x,a)^\top\Lambda^{-1}\phi(x,a).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the nonlinear lower-envelope error has the quadratic majorant \(\Delta_n(x,a)^2\le\phi(x,a)^\top M_n\phi(x,a)\), where
\[
M_n=600\frac{a_n}{n}\Lambda^{-1}\succeq0.
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:quadratic-majorant-transfer}\) yields
\[
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}\Delta_n^2
\le600\frac{a_n}{n}\operatorname{tr}(\Lambda^{-1}\Lambda_P^\star)
=600\frac{a_n}{n}D_{2,P}.
\]
The whitened matrix \(\Lambda^{-1/2}\Lambda_P^\star\Lambda^{-1/2}\) is positive semidefinite and all its eigenvalues are at most \(D_P\). Therefore
\[
D_{2,P}=\operatorname{tr}(\Lambda^{-1}\Lambda_P^\star)
\le dD_P=dD,
\]
and hence
\[
\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}\Delta_n^2
\le600\frac{a_ndD}{n}.
\]
This is the point at which the extra factor \(d\) enters: the logging radius \(a_n/n\) is of order \(d/n\), while averaging the leverage majorant costs \(D_{2,P}\le dD_P\). No linear covariance domination has been applied directly to the nonlinear envelope.

The policy \(\widehat\pi\) is the Gibbs policy generated by \(r_\theta+\Delta_n=\underline r_n\). By \(\text{lem:gibbs-pessimism-conversion}\), on \(\mathcal E_n\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi)
&\le\min\left\{\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}[-\Delta_n],
\frac\eta2\mathbb E_{\rho,\pi_P^\star}\Delta_n^2\right\}\\
&\le\min\left\{\sqrt{600a_ndD/n},\,300\eta a_ndD/n\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The first inequality is the slow endpoint conversion and the second term is the fast endpoint conversion.

It remains to control \(\mathcal E_n^c\). For any two potentials \(r,g:S\to[0,1]\), let \(\pi_r,\pi_g\) be their Gibbs policies. On the support of \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\),
\[
\log\frac{\pi_g(a\mid x)}{\pi_r(a\mid x)}
=\eta\{g(x,a)-r(x,a)\}+\log\frac{Z_r(x)}{Z_g(x)}\le2\eta,
\]
because both log normalizers lie in \([0,\eta]\). The identity in \(\text{lem:gibbs-regret-identity}\) therefore gives
\[
0\le J_{\eta,r}(\pi_r)-J_{\eta,r}(\pi_g)\le2.
\]
Taking \(r=r_\theta\) and \(g=\underline r_n\), the contribution of \(\mathcal E_n^c\) is at most \(2(\delta_n/2)=\delta_n\). The event and constants are uniform over the shell, so
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M(d,C,D,\eta)}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}[J_{\eta,P}(\pi_P^\star)-J_{\eta,P}(\widehat\pi)]
\le
\widetilde O\left(\min\left\{\frac{\eta d^2D}{n},\sqrt{\frac{d^2D}{n}}\right\}\right)+\delta_n.
\]

We now invert the bound. First observe that every nonempty shell has \(D\ge1\). If \(\theta=0\), then \(\pi_P^\star=\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) and \(D=1\). If \(\theta\ne0\), fix \(x\) and let \(R=r_\theta(x,A)\) under \(A\sim\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\cdot\mid x)\). For an independent copy \(R'\),
\[
2\operatorname{Cov}(R^2,e^{\eta R})
=\mathbb E[(R^2-R'^2)(e^{\eta R}-e^{\eta R'})]\ge0,
\]
since both displayed functions are nondecreasing on \([0,1]\). Consequently
\[
\mathbb E_{\pi_P^\star(\cdot\mid x)}R^2
=\frac{\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}[R^2e^{\eta R}]}{\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}[e^{\eta R}]}
\ge\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}R^2.
\]
After integrating over \(x\), the Rayleigh quotient in direction \(\theta\) is at least one. Nonsingularity of \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\) makes its denominator positive, so \(D_P\ge1\). Thus \(d^2D\ge16\).

For the failure-probability inversion it is useful to record the sharper deterministic bound
\[
J_{\eta,r}(\pi_r)-J_{\eta,r}(\pi_g)\le\frac\eta2.
\]
Indeed, at each \(x\), for \(F_x(t)=\log\sum_a\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(a\mid x)e^{\eta\{r(x,a)+t(g-r)(x,a)\}}\),
\[
\eta^{-1}\operatorname{KL}(\pi_g(\cdot\mid x)\Vert\pi_r(\cdot\mid x))
=\eta^{-1}\{F_x(0)-F_x(1)+F_x'(1)\}
=\eta^{-1}\int_0^1tF_x''(t)\,dt.
\]
Because \(F_x''(t)=\eta^2\operatorname{Var}_{\pi_t}(g-r)\le\eta^2\), the claim follows after averaging over \(x\).

If \(\eta\le2\epsilon\), this deterministic bound already gives risk at most \(\epsilon\). Otherwise choose \(n\), up to universal logarithmic factors and integer rounding, of order
\[
d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}.
\]
On the fast branch, the first displayed deterministic bound makes the failure contribution at most \(\eta\delta_n/4\), which is absorbed because \(d^2D\ge16\) and \(\eta>2\epsilon\). On the slow branch, the bound by two makes the failure contribution at most \(\delta_n\), also absorbed. The good-event fast or slow term, respectively, is at most a fixed fraction of \(\epsilon\) after increasing the universal constant and the logarithmic factor. Since the minimax infimum is no larger than the risk of the displayed measurable learner, \(\text{def:sample-complexity}\) gives
\[
N^\star(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le\widetilde O\left(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right)
\]
whenever the displayed right-hand side is at least two.

Finally, \(\text{prop:empirical-ball-dimension-witness}\) supplies exact-shell deterministic-reward experiments on which this empirical-ball coordinatewise-envelope learner has regret bounded away from zero at \(n=d^2\), although the old \(dD/n\) and \(\sqrt{dD/n}\) expressions vanish up to logarithms. Hence the extra factor is unavoidable for this learner. The proposition does not exclude a different measurable learner or confidence geometry.
