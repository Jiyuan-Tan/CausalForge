By \(\text{thm:erm-plugin-upper}\), the sequentially selected empirical-risk-minimizer Gibbs policy \(\widehat\pi_{\mathrm{ERM}}\) is a measurable member of \(\mathcal L_n(\mathfrak E)\), and its defining formula uses neither \(C\) nor \(D\). We use only the fast, quadratic branch of that theorem. It gives the sufficient sample-size order
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le \widetilde O\!\left(dD\frac{\kappa_\eta}{\epsilon}\right),
\]
with universal constants in the stated range.

For \(0<\eta\le1\), the definition of \(\kappa_\eta\) and monotonicity of the exponential give
\[
\frac{\kappa_\eta}{\eta}
=\int_0^1 t e^{2\eta t}\,dt
\le \int_0^1 t e^{2t}\,dt
=\kappa_1
=\frac{e^2+1}{4}.
\]
Hence the same learner satisfies
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le \widetilde O\!\left(dD\frac{\eta}{\epsilon}\right),
\]
where the multiplicative constant \(\kappa_1\) is numerical and therefore universal. Finally, because \(0<\epsilon<1\) and \(0<\eta\le1\),
\[
\frac{\eta}{\epsilon}\le\frac1\epsilon\le\frac1{\epsilon^2},
\qquad\text{so}\qquad
\min\left\{\frac{\eta}{\epsilon},\frac1{\epsilon^2}\right\}
=\frac{\eta}{\epsilon}.
\]
Substitution proves
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le\widetilde O\!\left(dD\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right).
\]
The logarithmic factors and the proviso that the displayed sample-size order be at least two are exactly those of \(\text{thm:erm-plugin-upper}\). This is the strongest direct universal-constant consequence of its fast plug-in branch on the normalized regime \(0<\eta\le1\); no assertion for unbounded temperature is used or obtained.
