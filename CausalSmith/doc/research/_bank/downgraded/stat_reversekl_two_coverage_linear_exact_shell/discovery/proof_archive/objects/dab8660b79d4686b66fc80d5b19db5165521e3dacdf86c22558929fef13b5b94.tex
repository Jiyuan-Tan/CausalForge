Write
\[
w_x(a)=\frac{e^{\eta r_\theta(x,a)}}{\sum_b\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(b\mid x)e^{\eta r_\theta(x,b)}}.
\]
For every \(x\), \(\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\cdot\mid x)}w_x(A)=1\), so its essential supremum is at least one and \(C\ge1\). Since \(0\le r_\theta\le1\), the numerator is at most \(e^\eta\) and the denominator is at least one, giving \(C\le e^\eta\). If equality held at a supported cell, that cell would have reward one and the denominator would have to equal one. Its strictly positive reference mass would then make the denominator strictly larger than one, a contradiction. Hence \(C<e^\eta\) whenever \(C>1\).

The inequality \(w_x(a)\le C\) implies the Loewner inequality
\[
\Lambda_P^\star\preceq C\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}},
\]
so \(D\le C\). To prove the missing lower inequality, suppose first that \(\theta\ne0\). At a fixed context let \(R=r_\theta(x,A)\) under the reference action law and let \(R'\) be an independent copy. Exponential tilting gives
\[
\mathbb E_{\pi_P^\star}R^2-\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}R^2
=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}_{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}(R^2,e^{\eta R})}{\mathbb E_{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}}e^{\eta R}},
\]
and
\[
2\operatorname{Cov}(R^2,e^{\eta R})
=\mathbb E[(R^2-R'^2)(e^{\eta R}-e^{\eta R'})]\ge0,
\]
because both functions are increasing on \([0,1]\). After averaging over contexts,
\[
\theta^\top\Lambda_P^\star\theta\ge\theta^\top\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\theta>0.
\]
The Rayleigh quotient characterization therefore yields \(D\ge1\). If \(\theta=0\), every reward is zero, the two policies and covariances coincide, and again \(D=1\).

If \(D=1\), then \(\Lambda_P^\star\preceq\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}\). The preceding nonnegative quadratic difference in the direction \(\theta\) must be zero. Every contextwise covariance in its integral is nonnegative, so it is zero for \(\rho\)-almost every context. Strict monotonicity of \(r\mapsto r^2\) and \(r\mapsto e^{\eta r}\) on \([0,1]\) shows from the independent-copy display that the reward is constant on the reference support at each such context. The Gibbs tilt is then trivial, so \(\pi_P^\star=\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) and \(C=1\). The converse \(C=1\Rightarrow D=1\) is the same mean-one likelihood-ratio argument used at the zero-risk boundary. This proves the necessary region.

For sufficiency, fix an orthonormal basis \(e_0,\ldots,e_{d-1}\) and two actions \(\{1,0\}\). Put
\[
q_K=\frac{e^\eta/K-1}{e^\eta-1}\in(0,1),\qquad 1<K<e^\eta.
\]
Take \(\theta=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}e_j\). At a context \(x_C\), let the action-one feature be \(e_0\), the action-zero feature be zero, and give action one reference probability \(q_C\). At contexts \(x_j\), \(1\le j\le d-1\), use the analogous pair \(e_j,0\) and reference probability \(q_D\). At each such context the rewards are one and zero, the Gibbs likelihood ratio of action one is respectively \(C\) or \(D\), and the other ratio is respectively \(C/e^\eta\) or \(D/e^\eta\).

When \(C>D\), add a context \(x_A\) at which both actions have feature \(e_0\), and set its context mass relative to that of \(x_C\) equal to
\[
A=\frac{q_C(C-D)}{D-1}.
\]
Both actions then have reward one and the Gibbs policy equals the reference policy at \(x_A\). If \(C=D\), omit \(x_A\). Give every displayed context a positive raw mass and normalize, while preserving the ratio \(A\) between the two \(e_0\) contexts. In direction \(e_0\), the target-to-logging covariance ratio is
\[
\frac{q_CC+A}{q_C+A}=D.
\]
In every direction \(e_j\), \(j\ge1\), it is also \(D\). Hence the logging covariance is positive definite and the generalized-eigenvalue index is exactly \(D\), while the pointwise index is exactly \(C\). This constructs every interior pair. For \((C,D)=(1,1)\), take \(d\) positive-mass contexts, one action, features \(e_j\), and \(\theta=0\).

Finally, with canonical features the two covariance matrices are diagonal and their coordinatewise ratios are the policy likelihood ratios, forcing \(D=C\). The construction just given uses the same feature direction \(e_0\) at both \(x_C\) and \(x_A\) and realizes \(D<C\), so fixed-experiment feasibility genuinely depends on the public design.
