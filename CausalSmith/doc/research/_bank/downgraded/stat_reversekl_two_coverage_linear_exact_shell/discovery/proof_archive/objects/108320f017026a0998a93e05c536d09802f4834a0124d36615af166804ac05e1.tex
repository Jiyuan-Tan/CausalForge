Fix \(d\ge4\), \(\eta>0\), and \(\mathfrak E\). By \(\text{lem:fixed-experiment-shell-certificate}\), the shell \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\) is nonempty if and only if at least one branch of its displayed finite exponential nonlinear-semidefinite system is feasible. This proves the asserted fixed-experiment feasibility characterization.

Suppose next that the shell is nonempty. On the boundary \((C,D)=(1,1)\), \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) gives
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,1,1,\eta)=1.
\]
For a nontrivial shell, \(\text{thm:localized-upper}\) supplies a measurable lower-envelope Gibbs learner whose inversion yields
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le\widetilde O\!\left(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right)
\]
in the stated range. This bound contains no pointwise-coverage factor \(C\), although it contains the additional dimension factor \(d\).

It remains to determine what can be concluded from the four numerical indices alone. Fix \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\). By \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\), there are public experiments \(\mathfrak E_0\) and \(\mathfrak E_1\), both having nonempty exact shells at these same indices, such that
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1
\]
for every \(\epsilon>0\), while for some \(c(C,D,\eta)>0\) and \(\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)>0\),
\[
N^\star_{\mathfrak E_1}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\ge c(C,D,\eta)\frac{dD\eta}{\epsilon}
\]
whenever \(0<\epsilon\le\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\). The informative construction holds on the whole region \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), so it also covers \(2D-1\ge e^\eta\).

Let \(L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\) be any lower bound claimed uniformly over all public experiments with a nonempty exact shell at these indices. Applying the claim to \(\mathfrak E_0\) forces
\[
L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
\le N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
\]
Hence no such lower bound can diverge as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\), and in particular no uniform \(\Omega(dD\epsilon^{-2})\) branch can be valid. The two experiments also prove directly that a single frontier indexed only by \((d,C,D,\eta)\) cannot represent fixed-design minimax sample complexity. Finally, neither cited theorem supplies a slow lower construction for a specified informative experiment or a clean-temperature \(dD\) upper rate, so the theorem makes neither assertion.
