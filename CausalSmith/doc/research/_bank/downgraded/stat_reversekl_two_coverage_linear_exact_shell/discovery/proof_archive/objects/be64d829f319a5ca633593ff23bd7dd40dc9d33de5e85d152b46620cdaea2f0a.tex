Fix \(d\ge4\), \(\eta>0\), and \(\mathfrak E\). The equivalence between nonemptiness of \(\mathcal M_{\mathfrak E}(d,C,D,\eta)\) and feasibility of one of the displayed finite systems is exactly \(\text{lem:fixed-experiment-shell-certificate}\): its branches enumerate the positive support of the law-specific context distribution and a cell attaining \(C\), while its normalization, reward-range, positive-definiteness, likelihood-ratio, semidefinite, and null-eigenvector constraints are respectively equivalent to compatibility with the shell and the exact equalities \(C_P=C\) and \(D_P=D\).
+
+If \((C,D)=(1,1)\) and the shell is nonempty, \(\text{thm:zero-risk-boundary}\) gives \(\pi_P^\star=\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) for every member and shows that the constant reference-policy learner has zero risk. Hence \(N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,1,1,\eta)=1\).
+
+Now suppose the fixed-design shell is nonempty and nontrivial. By \(\text{thm:localized-upper}\), the measurable lower-envelope Gibbs learner has worst-case expected regularized regret at most
+\[
+\widetilde O\!\left(\min\left\{\frac{\eta d^2D}{n},\sqrt{\frac{d^2D}{n}}\right\}\right)+n^{-2}.
+\]
+The inversion proved in that theorem and the definition of sample complexity therefore give
+\[
+N^\star_{\mathfrak E}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
+\le\widetilde O\!\left(d^2D\min\{\eta/\epsilon,\epsilon^{-2}\}\right)
+\]
+whenever the displayed right-hand side is at least two.
+
+By \(\text{thm:feasible-index-region}\), every nonboundary pair that can occur when the public experiment is allowed to vary satisfies \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), and every such pair is realizable. Fix any pair in this range. The two constructions in \(\text{thm:fixed-design-index-insufficiency}\) supply experiments \(\mathfrak E_0\) and \(\mathfrak E_1\) at exactly the same \((d,C,D,\eta)\). For \(\mathfrak E_0\), the publicly computable Gibbs optimum is common to the whole shell, so the theorem gives
+\[
+N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1
+\]
+for every \(\epsilon>0\). For \(\mathfrak E_1\), it gives constants \(c(C,D,\eta)>0\) and \(\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)>0\) such that
+\[
+N^\star_{\mathfrak E_1}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)
+\ge c(C,D,\eta)\frac{dD\eta}{\epsilon}
+\]
+for \(0<\epsilon\le\epsilon_0(C,D,\eta)\). That construction is valid throughout \(1<D\le C<e^\eta\), without the restriction \(2D-1<e^\eta\); hence it also covers the stated complementary regime.
+
+The two displays prove that the four indices cannot determine one fixed-design minimax frontier. More explicitly, let \(L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\) be any lower bound asserted uniformly over all public experiments having a nonempty shell at those indices. Applying it to \(\mathfrak E_0\) forces
+\[
+L(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)\le
+N^\star_{\mathfrak E_0}(\epsilon;d,C,D,\eta)=1.
+\]
+Thus such a bound cannot diverge as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\), which refutes in particular a uniform \(\Omega(dD\epsilon^{-2})\) branch. The cited upper theorem contains no \(C\), but the argument supplies neither a matching experiment-specific converse nor a learner with the sharper \(dD\) dimension dependence. Accordingly those claims are not consequences of the established bounds, as asserted.
