The calibration identities give the two target action laws
\[
s_+=(a,b,c),\qquad s_-=(b,a,c),\qquad a=pD=\frac14,\quad b=ae^{-2\eta\gamma},\quad c=1-a-b.
\]
The conditional regularized regret under sign \(\sigma\) is \(\eta^{-1}\operatorname{KL}(u\Vert s_\sigma)\). For two probability vectors \(p,q\), direct Lagrange minimization over \(u\) gives
\[
\inf_u\{\operatorname{KL}(u\Vert p)+\operatorname{KL}(u\Vert q)\}=-2\log\sum_i\sqrt{p_iq_i}.
\]
Here the affinity is
\[
2\sqrt{ab}+c=1-a(1-e^{-\eta\gamma})^2.
\]
This proves the equality. Its argument is at least \(1-a=3/4\), which proves the upper bound. In particular, this paired family cannot provide a separation proportional to \(\eta^{-1}\log\cosh(\eta\gamma)\) uniformly for large \(\eta\gamma\).
