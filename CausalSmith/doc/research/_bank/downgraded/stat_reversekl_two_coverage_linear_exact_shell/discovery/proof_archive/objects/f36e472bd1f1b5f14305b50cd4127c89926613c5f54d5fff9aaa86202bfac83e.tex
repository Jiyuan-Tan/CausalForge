For each \(d\), take contexts \(x_1,\ldots,x_d\), two actions \(\{1,0\}\), and \(\rho(x_j)=1/d\). Let \((e_j)_{j=1}^d\) be the canonical basis and set
\[
\phi(x_j,1)=e_j,
\qquad
\phi(x_j,0)=\frac12e_j,
\qquad
\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(1\mid x_j)=\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(0\mid x_j)=\frac12.
\]
Take \(\theta_j=1/2\) for every \(j\), and let the reward be deterministic at its conditional mean. Thus
\[
r_\theta(x_j,1)=\frac12,
\qquad
r_\theta(x_j,0)=\frac14.
\]
The features are bounded and
\[
\Lambda_{\mathrm{ref}}
=\frac1d\left(\frac12+\frac18\right)I_d
=\frac{5}{8d}I_d\succ0.
\]
Because \(\eta_0(r_\theta(x_j,1)-r_\theta(x_j,0))=\log3\), the target Gibbs probabilities are
\[
\pi_{P_d}^\star(1\mid x_j)=\frac34,
\qquad
\pi_{P_d}^\star(0\mid x_j)=\frac14.
\]
Therefore
\[
C_{P_d}=\max\left\{\frac{3/4}{1/2},\frac{1/4}{1/2}\right\}=\frac32,
\]
and
\[
\Lambda_{P_d}^\star
=\frac1d\left(\frac34+\frac1{16}\right)I_d
=\frac{13}{16d}I_d.
\]
It follows that every generalized eigenvalue equals
\[
\frac{13/16}{5/8}=\frac{13}{10},
\]
so \(P_d\in\mathcal M(d,C_0,D_0,\eta_0)\).

Let \(N_j\) be the number of logged observations with context \(x_j\). Since \(N_j\) is binomial with mean \(n_d/d=d\), the count bound in \(\text{lem:multinomial-max-count}\) gives
\[
P_d^{\otimes n_d}\left(\max_{1\le j\le d}N_j\le3d\right)=1-o(1).
\]
Call this event \(\mathcal B_d\). If \(N_j>0\), deterministic realizability makes the empirical least-squares coefficient in direction \(e_j\) equal to \(1/2\). If \(N_j=0\), changing that coefficient does not change empirical loss. In either case, on \(\mathcal B_d\) there is an empirical-risk minimizer modified only in coordinate \(j\) to have coefficient zero whose empirical squared distance from \(\widehat f\) is at most
\[
\frac14\widehat\Sigma_{n,jj}
\le\frac14\frac{N_j}{n_d}
\le\frac{3}{4d}.
\]
On the other hand, the confidence radius is
\[
c_0\frac{d\log(en_d)+\log(2n_d^2)}{n_d},
\]
which is asymptotic to a positive constant times \(\log d/d\) and therefore exceeds \(3/(4d)\) for all sufficiently large \(d\). Thus, separately for every coordinate and hence for both actions at every context,
\[
\underline r_{n_d}(x_j,1)=\underline r_{n_d}(x_j,0)=0
\]
on \(\mathcal B_d\). The deployed Gibbs policy then equals \(\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\) at every context.

By the Gibbs-regret identity, on \(\mathcal B_d\),
\[
J_{\eta_0,P_d}(\pi_{P_d}^\star)-J_{\eta_0,P_d}(\widehat\pi)
=\frac1{\eta_0}\operatorname{KL}\left((1/2,1/2)\Vert(3/4,1/4)\right)
=\frac{\log(4/3)}{8\log3}.
\]
Regret is nonnegative off \(\mathcal B_d\), so taking expectations proves the displayed lower bound. Finally,
\[
\frac{dD_0}{n_d}=O(d^{-1}),
\qquad
\sqrt{\frac{dD_0}{n_d}}=O(d^{-1/2}),
\]
and multiplying either expression by any fixed polylogarithmic factor still makes it vanish. This contradicts the original rate for this learner.
