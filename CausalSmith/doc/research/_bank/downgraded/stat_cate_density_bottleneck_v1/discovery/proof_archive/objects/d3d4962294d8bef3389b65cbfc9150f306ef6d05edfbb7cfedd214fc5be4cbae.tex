Under the preceding conditions, uniformly over compact subsets of \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\),
\[
n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\lesssim R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\lesssim \overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}.
\]
Writing \(p=(\alpha+\beta)/d\) and \(q=\gamma/(2\gamma+d)\), one has \(0<p<q\) on \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), and hence, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
This bracket does not determine a polynomial minimax exponent and does not establish a positive-band slowdown relative to the density-qualified benchmark.
