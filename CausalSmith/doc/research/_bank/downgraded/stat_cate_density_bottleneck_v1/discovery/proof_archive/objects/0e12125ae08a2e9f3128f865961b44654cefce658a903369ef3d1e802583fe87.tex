For every \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), define
\[
p=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{d},\qquad
t=\frac{1}{1+d/(2\gamma)+d/(4s)},\qquad
q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d}.
\]
Then \(0<p<t<q<1/2\) and \(r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\).  Consequently, the additional premise \(R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c n^{-p}\) for all sufficiently large \(n\) would imply
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\geq c n^{t-p}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Without that unproved premise, the available positive-band bracket does not determine the limit of \(R_n^{\mathrm{band}}/r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}\).
