Let \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\) be compact.  Since
\(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\),
\(\mathrm{thm:density\mbox{-}coupled\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\) gives constants
\(c_K>0\) and \(n_{1,K}\) such that
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c_Kn^{-q},
\qquad q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d},
\qquad n\geq n_{1,K},
\tag{1}
\]
uniformly over \(K\).  Only the oracle pointwise-regression branch of that
theorem is invoked.

The theorem \(\mathrm{thm:close\mbox{-}pair\mbox{-}kbrw\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}bound}\)
supplies the single total measurable estimator
\(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{pair}}(x_0)\) and constants \(C_K<\infty\) and
\(n_{2,K}\) such that
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}}
\mathbb E_P\left|
\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{pair}}(x_0)-\tau_P(x_0)
\right|
\leq C_Kn^{-t},
\qquad
t=\left(1+\frac d{2\gamma}+\frac d{4s}\right)^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
Substituting this data-driven estimator into the infimum defining
\(R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\) gives
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq C_Kn^{-t}
\qquad(n\geq n_{2,K}).
\tag{3}
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\),
\(t<q\).  Hence \(\mathrm{def:kbrw\mbox{-}rate}\) implies
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}.
\tag{4}
\]
Combining (1), (3), and (4), and taking
\(n_0=\max\{n_{1,K},n_{2,K}\}\), proves
\[
c_Kn^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C_Kn^{-t}=C_Kr_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}.
\]
Because \(t<q\), the endpoints have distinct polynomial exponents.  The upper
endpoint proves benchmark-rate achievability, whereas the bracket cannot identify
the exact fixed-positive-band minimax exponent.
