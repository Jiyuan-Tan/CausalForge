Fix a compact set \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).  By
\(\mathrm{lem:oracle\mbox{-}regression\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\), there
are constants \(c_K>0\) and \(n_{0,K}\) such that, uniformly over
\((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in K\),
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c_K n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
\qquad(n\geq n_{0,K}).
\]
That lemma proves the inequality by two full-data laws in
\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}\) having the same uniform covariate density,
the same constant propensity \(1/2\), bounded Rademacher potential outcomes,
and CATEs separated at \(x_0\) by a \(\gamma\)-smooth bump of height
\(c_*n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\).  Thus its constants have exactly the compact
uniformity required here.  Because this pair keeps the design density fixed,
the argument proves only the displayed oracle pointwise-regression branch; it
does not prove a density-induced branch of order
\(n^{-(\alpha+\beta)/d}\).
