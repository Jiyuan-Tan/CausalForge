On
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\cap
\{(\alpha,\beta,\gamma):0<\beta<\alpha\leq1\},
\]
the total close-pair estimator satisfies
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}}
\mathbb E_P\left|
\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{pair}}(x_0)-\tau_P(x_0)
\right|\leq Cn^{-t}=Cr_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}},
\]
and hence
\[
cn^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t},
\qquad t<q.
\]
Thus the estimator-side logarithmic and rank-cap bottlenecks are removed on this region, but the exact exponent remains between the two displayed endpoints.
