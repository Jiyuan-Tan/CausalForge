The available routes are the unequal-resolution density, propensity, and
outcome-regression perturbation program \(\mathfrak H_n^{\mathrm{dens}}\), and
same-sample stabilized Gram corrections at asymmetric nuisance resolutions.
The former still lacks simultaneous verification of normalization, positive-band
membership, H\"older membership, target separation, and bounded \(n\)-sample
testing distance.  The latter currently motivates an estimator program but has
no proved risk bound at the required resolutions.  Importing the published
support-hole lower construction was rejected because it violates the fixed
positive lower density bound.
