Let \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\) be compact.  Since
\(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), the density-coupled lower-bound
theorem gives, uniformly on \(K\),
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c_K n^{-q}.
\tag{1}
\]
The close-pair KBRW upper-bound theorem supplies one total measurable
estimator whose maximal mean absolute error is at most \(C_Kn^{-t}\).
Substituting that single estimator into the infimum in
\(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}risk}\) gives
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq C_Kn^{-t}.
\tag{2}
\]
The low-region-exponent-ordering lemma gives \(t<q\), and
\(\mathrm{def:kbrw\mbox{-}rate}\) then gives
\(r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}\).  Combining (1) and
(2), and taking the maximum of the two uniform sample-size thresholds, proves
the theorem.  Since \(t<q\), the two polynomial endpoints differ, so the
bracket alone cannot identify the exact exponent.
