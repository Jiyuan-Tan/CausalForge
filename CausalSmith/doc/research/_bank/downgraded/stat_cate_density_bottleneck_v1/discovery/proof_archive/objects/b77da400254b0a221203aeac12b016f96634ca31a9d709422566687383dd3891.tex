Let \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\) be compact.  Since \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), \(\mathrm{thm:density\mbox{-}coupled\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\) gives constants \(c_K>0\) and \(n_{1,K}\) such that, uniformly over \(K\),
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c_Kn^{-q},
\qquad n\geq n_{1,K}.
\tag{1}
\]
The conclusion used here is only that theorem's oracle pointwise-regression branch.  On the other hand, \(\mathrm{thm:close\mbox{-}pair\mbox{-}kbrw\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}bound}\) supplies one total measurable estimator whose maximal mean-absolute error is at most \(C_Kn^{-t}\).  Substituting that single data-driven estimator into the infimum in \(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}risk}\) yields
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq C_Kn^{-t},
\qquad n\geq n_{2,K}.
\tag{2}
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\), \(t<q\).  Therefore \(\mathrm{def:kbrw\mbox{-}rate}\) gives
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}.
\tag{3}
\]
Combining (1)--(3), and taking \(n_0=\max(n_{1,K},n_{2,K})\), proves the displayed bracket uniformly over \(K\).  Because \(t<q\), the lower and upper polynomial exponents differ; the bracket therefore proves benchmark-rate achievability but does not determine the exact positive-band minimax exponent.
