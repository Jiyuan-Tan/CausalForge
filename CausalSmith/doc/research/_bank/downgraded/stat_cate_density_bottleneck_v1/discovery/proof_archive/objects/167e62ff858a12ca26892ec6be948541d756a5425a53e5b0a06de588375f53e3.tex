On
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}
=\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\cap
\{(\alpha,\beta,\gamma):0<\beta<\alpha\leq1\},
\]
the established close-pair upper theorem and the oracle lower theorem give,
uniformly over compact subsets,
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C n^{-t}=C r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}},
\qquad
t=\left(1+\frac d{2\gamma}+\frac d{4s}\right)^{-1},
\qquad
q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d},
\qquad t<q.
\]
Thus benchmark-rate achievability under the fixed density band is settled on
this region and the logarithmic loss of the broader rank-capped upper bound is
absent there.  The exact exponent is not identified because the converse has
exponent \(q\), whereas the achieved upper endpoint has exponent \(t\).
On the remainder of \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), the proved general
bracket remains
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p},
\qquad p=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{d}<q.
\]
Finally, \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\) itself requires \(\beta<\alpha\), so
the opposite ordering \(\alpha<\beta\) is outside that defined region and
cannot be characterized as a subcase of it without first specifying the
corresponding regime.
