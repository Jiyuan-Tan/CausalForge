Split \(O_{1:n}\) into independent folds. On separate nuisance folds, fit locally supported partition-series estimates of \(\pi_P\) and \(\mu_{a,P}\) with dimension \(k_n=\lfloor n/(\log n)^2\rfloor\), truncate fitted propensities to \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\), and clip empirical Gram eigenvalues to the fixed interval \([f_{-}/2,2f_{+}]\). On the evaluation fold, regress the cross-fitted R-score on polynomials of total degree \(\lfloor\gamma\rfloor\) with kernel support \(\lVert X-x_0\rVert_\infty\le h_n\). Average the fold-specific intercepts and truncate the result to \([-2,2]\); the resulting scalar is \(\widehat{\tau}^{\mathrm{cap}}_n(x_0)\).
