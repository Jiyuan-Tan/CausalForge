Starting from an interior uniform density, I combined a flat outer CATE bump, inner propensity and regression bumps, paired Rademacher signs, and mean-zero density perturbations.  The exact collision balance \(n^2h^{d+2t+td/\gamma}\asymp1\) yields
\[
\delta=h^t=n^{-1/\{1+d/(2\gamma)+d/(2t)\}},
\]
which is the KBRW exponent because \(t=2s\), not the proposed \(n^{-t/d}\) exponent.  The positive background density prevents the support-hole cancellation used by the flat-top construction.  Higher-order moment matching was considered, but no explicit prior was found for which the target survives the required matching.
