Fix \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\) and apply
\(\mathrm{thm:close\mbox{-}pair\mbox{-}band\mbox{-}frontier}\) to the compact
singleton containing this triple.  There are constants \(C<\infty\) and \(n_0\)
such that
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t}
\qquad(n\geq n_0).
\tag{1}
\]
The lemma \(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\)
gives \(t<q\).  Therefore \(\mathrm{def:kbrw\mbox{-}rate}\) gives
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
Dividing (1) by (2) yields
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}\leq C
\qquad(n\geq n_0).
\]
This argument uses only the upper endpoint of the close-pair frontier theorem;
it supplies no reverse comparison and hence no matching positive-band converse.
