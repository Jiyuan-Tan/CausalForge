The symbol \(\mathfrak H_n^{\mathrm{dens}}\) names the following candidate
positive-band testing program.  Start from the uniform density, which lies
strictly inside \([f_{-},f_{+}]\), partition a shrinking cube around \(x_0\)
into paired cells, place normalized mean-zero signed wavelet perturbations in
\(f_P\), couple their signs with flat-top perturbations of \(\pi_P\) and
\(\mu_{a,P}\), and mix over the paired signs.  The cell widths and amplitudes
are to be chosen subject to H\"older membership, density normalization, the
fixed two-sided density band, target separation, and an \(n\)-sample testing-distance
bound.  This notation specifies only the candidate perturbation-and-mixture
program; model membership, separation, and indistinguishability are analytical
properties that require separate theorem proofs.
