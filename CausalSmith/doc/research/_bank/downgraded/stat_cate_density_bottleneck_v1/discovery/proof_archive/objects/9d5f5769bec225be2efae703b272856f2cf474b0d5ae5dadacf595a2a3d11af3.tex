The asserted inequality is exactly the conclusion of
\(\mathrm{lem:oracle\mbox{-}regression\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\).  That
lemma constructs two laws with uniform covariate density, constant propensity
\(1/2\), bounded Rademacher potential outcomes, and CATEs separated at
\(x_0\) by a \(\gamma\)-smooth bump of height
\(c_*n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\).  It verifies membership in
\(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}\), bounds the one-observation squared Hellinger
distance by \(C n^{-1}\), tensorizes the affinity, and applies the two-point
testing inequality for mean absolute loss.  Its constants are uniform on each
compact subset of \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), which gives the quantifiers
in the proposed statement.  Since the pair keeps the density fixed, the proof
establishes only the oracle branch and makes no claim about
\(n^{-(\alpha+\beta)/d}\).
