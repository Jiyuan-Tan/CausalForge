Apply the close-pair frontier theorem to the compact singleton containing the
fixed smoothness triple.  It gives
\(R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t}\).  The low-region exponent-ordering
lemma gives \(t<q\), so the KBRW definition yields
\(r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}\).  Division by this
strictly positive benchmark proves the displayed bounded ratio.  The argument
uses only the upper endpoint of the frontier theorem and therefore makes no
claim that its lower endpoint matches \(n^{-t}\).
