Fix \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\).  Apply \(\mathrm{thm:close\mbox{-}pair\mbox{-}band\mbox{-}frontier}\) to the compact singleton containing this triple.  There are \(C<\infty\) and \(n_0\) such that
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t}
\qquad(n\geq n_0).
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\), \(t<q\).  Hence \(\mathrm{def:kbrw\mbox{-}rate}\) gives
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
Dividing (1) by the strictly positive quantity in (2) proves
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}\leq C
\qquad(n\geq n_0).
\]
Only the upper endpoint of the frontier theorem is used, so this argument supplies no matching positive-band converse.
