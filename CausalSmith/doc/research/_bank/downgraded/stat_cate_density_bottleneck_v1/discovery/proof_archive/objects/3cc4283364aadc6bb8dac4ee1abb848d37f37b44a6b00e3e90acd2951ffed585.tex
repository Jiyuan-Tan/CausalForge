Fix \(d,L,\varepsilon,f_-,f_+\), and \(x_0\).  Uniformly over compact
subsets of \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\), there are constants
\(0<c\leq C<\infty\) and \(n_0\) such that, for every \(n\geq n_0\),
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C n^{-t}=C r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}},
\qquad
t=\left(1+\frac d{2\gamma}+\frac d{4s}\right)^{-1},
\quad q=\frac\gamma{2\gamma+d}.
\]
Here \(t<q\).  Thus benchmark-rate achievability is unconditional on this
region, while the displayed bracket does not determine the exact positive-band
minimax exponent.
