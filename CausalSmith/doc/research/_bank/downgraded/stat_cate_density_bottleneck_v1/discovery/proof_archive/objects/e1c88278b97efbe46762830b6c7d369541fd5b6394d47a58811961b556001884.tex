On the current region \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), where \(\alpha>\beta\), put
\[
p=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{d},\qquad
t=\frac{1}{1+d/(2\gamma)+d/(4s)},\qquad
q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d}.
\]
The proved exponent ordering is \(0<p<t<q<1/2\), the benchmark is \(r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\), and the strongest presently derived band-only result is
\[
cn^{-q}\le R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\le Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
cn^{t-q}\le
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\le Cn^{t-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
The first endpoint tends to zero and the second to infinity.  These proved inequalities therefore do not determine the exact minimax rate, whether the fixed positive-band unknown density causes any slowdown relative to the benchmark, or whether the logarithmic factor is removable.  The current class also imposes \(\alpha>\beta\), so it supplies no conclusion for the requested \(\alpha<\beta\) orderings.
