On
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}
=\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\cap
\{(\alpha,\beta,\gamma):0<\beta<\alpha\leq1\},
\]
the established close-pair upper theorem and oracle lower theorem give, uniformly
over compact subsets,
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C n^{-t}=C r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}},
\qquad
t=\left(1+\frac d{2\gamma}+\frac d{4s}\right)^{-1},
\qquad
q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d},
\qquad t<q.
\]
Thus benchmark-rate achievability without density estimation or density
smoothness is settled on this nonempty region, and the logarithmic loss of the
broader rank-capped upper bound is absent there.  The distinct endpoints leave
the exact exponent unidentified.  On the remainder of
\(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), the proved bracket is
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p},
\qquad p=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{d}<q.
\]
Finally, \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\) requires \(\beta<\alpha\), so the
opposite ordering \(\alpha<\beta\) is outside the currently defined region.
