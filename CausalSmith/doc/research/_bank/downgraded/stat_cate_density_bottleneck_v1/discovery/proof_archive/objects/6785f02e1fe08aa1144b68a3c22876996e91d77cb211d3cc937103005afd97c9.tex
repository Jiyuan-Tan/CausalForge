Fix \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).  The low-region exponent-ordering lemma gives
\[
0<p<t<q<\frac12.
\tag{1}
\]
By the definition of the benchmark and (1),
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t},
\tag{2}
\]
because \(t<q\) implies \(n^{-t}>n^{-q}\) for \(n>1\).  Under the additional premise in the proposition, division by (2) gives
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\ge cn^{t-p}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
since \(t-p>0\).

Without that additional premise, apply the proved positive-band bracket to the compact singleton containing the fixed smoothness triple.  It gives only
\[
cn^{-q}\le R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\le Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Dividing by (2) yields
\[
cn^{t-q}\le
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\le Cn^{t-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
By (1), the lower numerical endpoint tends to zero and the upper numerical endpoint tends to infinity.  Therefore the available bracket does not determine the limit of the risk ratio, as claimed.
