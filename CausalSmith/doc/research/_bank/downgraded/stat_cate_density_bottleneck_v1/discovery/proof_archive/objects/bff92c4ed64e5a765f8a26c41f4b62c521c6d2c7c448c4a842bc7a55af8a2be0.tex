The strongest currently proved result on the displayed close-pair region is
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t}=Cr_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}},
\qquad
t=\left(1+\frac d{2\gamma}+\frac d{4s}\right)^{-1}
<q=\frac\gamma{2\gamma+d}.
\]
The upper endpoint is attained by the total close-pair estimator under only \(f_-\leq f_P\leq f_+\), without estimating or smoothing \(f_P\).  The available lower endpoint is only the oracle pointwise-regression branch.  Thus benchmark-rate achievability is settled, whereas the exact band-only exponent and a matching positive-band converse are not.
