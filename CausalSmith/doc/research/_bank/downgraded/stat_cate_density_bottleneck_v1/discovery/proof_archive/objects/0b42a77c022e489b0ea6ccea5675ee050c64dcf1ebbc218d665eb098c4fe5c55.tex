Fix \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\).  Apply the
close-pair frontier theorem to the compact singleton containing this
smoothness triple.  It gives \(R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t}\).  The
low-region-exponent-ordering lemma gives \(t<q\), so
\(\mathrm{def:kbrw\mbox{-}rate}\) yields
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}>0.
\]
Division by this strictly positive benchmark proves
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}\leq C
\]
for every \(n\) beyond the frontier theorem's threshold.  The argument uses
only the upper endpoint of that theorem and therefore makes no claim that its
lower endpoint matches \(n^{-t}\).
