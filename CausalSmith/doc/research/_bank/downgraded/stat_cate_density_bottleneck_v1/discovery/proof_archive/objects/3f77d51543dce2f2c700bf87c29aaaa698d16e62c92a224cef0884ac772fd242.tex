Fix a compact set \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).  The theorem
\(\mathrm{thm:density\mbox{-}coupled\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\) gives
constants \(c_K>0\) and \(n_{1,K}\) such that
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c_K n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
\qquad(n\geq n_{1,K})
\tag{1}
\]
uniformly over \(K\).  On the other hand,
\(\mathrm{thm:rank\mbox{-}capped\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}bound}\) supplies the
single total measurable sample map
\(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)\) and constants \(C_K<\infty\) and
\(n_{2,K}\) for which
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}}
\mathbb E_P\left|
\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)-\tau_P(x_0)
\right|
\leq C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}.
\tag{2}
\]
Substituting this one data-driven estimator for \(T_n\) in
\(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}risk}\) yields
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\qquad(n\geq n_{2,K}).
\tag{3}
\]
Equations (1) and (3), with
\(n_0=\max\{n_{1,K},n_{2,K}\}\), prove the first bracket.

Put
\[
p=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{d},
\qquad
q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d}.
\]
The lemma
\(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\) gives
\(0<p<q\) throughout \(K\).  Since \(K\) is compact and the inequalities
are strict, \(q-p\) is bounded below by a positive constant on \(K\).
Consequently, uniformly over \(K\),
\[
\frac{n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}}{n^{-q}}
=n^{q-p}(\log n)^{2p}\longrightarrow\infty.
\tag{4}
\]
By \(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}rate}\), equation (4) implies
that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}
=n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\tag{5}
\]
Combining (1), (3), and (5) proves
\[
c_Kn^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C_Kn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Because \(p<q\), the endpoints have distinct polynomial exponents.  Hence
this bracket determines neither an exact polynomial minimax exponent nor a
positive-band slowdown relative to the density-qualified benchmark.
