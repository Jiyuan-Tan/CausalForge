Fix \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), and define
\(p,t,q\) as in the proposition.  The lemma
\(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\) gives
\[
0<p<t<q<\frac12.
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\mathrm{def:kbrw\mbox{-}rate}\) and (1),
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}
=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}
=n^{-t},
\tag{2}
\]
because \(t<q\) implies \(n^{-t}>n^{-q}\) for \(n>1\).  If, in addition,
\(R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c n^{-p}\) for all sufficiently large \(n\),
then division by (2) gives
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\geq c n^{t-p}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
where the convergence follows from \(t-p>0\) in (1).

It remains to verify the proposition's statement about what follows without
that additional premise.  Apply
\(\mathrm{thm:band\mbox{-}density\mbox{-}polynomial\mbox{-}frontier}\) to
the compact singleton containing the fixed smoothness triple.  For all
sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\tag{3}
\]
Dividing (3) by (2) yields
\[
c n^{t-q}\leq
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\leq Cn^{t-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\tag{4}
\]
By (1), the lower numerical endpoint in (4) tends to zero and the upper
numerical endpoint tends to infinity.  Thus (4) imposes neither a common
finite limit nor divergence on every sequence satisfying it; in particular,
the constant sequence and sequences tending to zero or infinity all lie
between its endpoints eventually after harmless fixed rescaling.  Hence the
available positive-band bracket does not determine the limit of the risk
ratio.
