Fix a compact set \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).  By
\(\mathrm{thm:density\mbox{-}coupled\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\), there
are constants \(c_K>0\) and \(n_{0,L}\) such that
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c_K n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
\qquad(n\geq n_{0,L}).
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\mathrm{thm:rank\mbox{-}capped\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}bound}\), there
are constants \(C_K<\infty\) and \(n_{0,U}\) for which the single
measurable sample map \(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)\) satisfies
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}}
\mathbb E_P\left|
\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)-\tau_P(x_0)
\right|
\leq C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\qquad(n\geq n_{0,U}).
\tag{2}
\]
The infimum in \(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}risk}\) ranges over all measurable
estimators, so substituting this one sample map into that infimum gives
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}.
\tag{3}
\]
Taking \(n\geq\max\{n_{0,L},n_{0,U}\}\), equations (1) and (3) prove the
first bracket, with constants uniform on \(K\).

Write
\[
p=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{d},
\qquad
q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d}.
\]
The lemma
\(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\) gives
\(0<p<q\) at every point of \(K\).  Since \(p\) and \(q\) are continuous
and \(K\) is compact, there is a constant \(\delta_K>0\) such that
\(q-p\geq\delta_K\) on \(K\).  For \(n\geq e\),
\[
\frac{n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}}{n^{-q}}
=n^{q-p}(\log n)^{2p}
\geq n^{\delta_K}>1.
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently, the definition of \(\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}\) and (4)
give, uniformly on \(K\),
\[
\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}
=n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Combining this identity with (1) and
(3) yields
\[
c_Kn^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C_Kn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Because \(p<q\), the two endpoints have different polynomial exponents.
The inequalities therefore neither identify a polynomial minimax exponent
nor supply the missing lower bound of order \(n^{-p}\) that would establish
a slowdown relative to the density-qualified benchmark.
