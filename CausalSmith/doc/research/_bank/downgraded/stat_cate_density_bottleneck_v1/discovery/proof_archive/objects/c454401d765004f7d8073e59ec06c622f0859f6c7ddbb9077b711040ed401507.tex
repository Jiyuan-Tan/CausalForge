Fix \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).  The
low-region exponent-ordering lemma gives
\[
0<p<t<q<\frac12.
\tag{1}
\]
By the definition of the benchmark and (1),
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t}.
\tag{2}
\]
Under the additional premise in the proposition, division by (2) gives
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\geq cn^{t-p}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
because \(t-p>0\).

Without that additional premise, the broad positive-band bracket applied to
the compact singleton containing the fixed smoothness triple gives only
\[
cn^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
After division by (2),
\[
cn^{t-q}\leq
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\leq Cn^{t-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
The lower numerical endpoint tends to zero and the upper numerical endpoint
tends to infinity, so this broad bracket does not determine the risk-ratio
limit.
