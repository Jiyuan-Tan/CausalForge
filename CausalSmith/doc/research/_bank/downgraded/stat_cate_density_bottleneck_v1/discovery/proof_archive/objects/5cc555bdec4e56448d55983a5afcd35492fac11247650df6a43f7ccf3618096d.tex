Fix \(d,L,\varepsilon,f_{-},f_{+},x_0\).  Uniformly over compact subsets of \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), there are constants \(c>0\) and \(n_0\) such that, for every \(n\geq n_0\),
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c\,n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}.
\]
This conclusion is the oracle pointwise-regression branch; it does not assert a density-induced \(n^{-(\alpha+\beta)/d}\) branch under the fixed positive density band.
