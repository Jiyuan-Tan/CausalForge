Consider a finite sequence \(\{X_k\}\) of independent, random, self-adjoint matrices with dimension \(d\). Assume that each random matrix satisfies \(\mathbb E X_k=0\) and \(\lambda_{\max}(X_k)\leq R\) almost surely. Then, for all \(t\geq0\),
\[
\Pr\!\left\{\lambda_{\max}\!\left(\sum_k X_k\right)\geq t\right\}
\leq d\exp\!\left\{\frac{-t^2/2}{\sigma^2+Rt/3}\right\},
\qquad
\sigma^2:=\left\|\sum_k\mathbb E(X_k^2)\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
