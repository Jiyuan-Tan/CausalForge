The established lemma
\(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\) gives
\(0<p<t<q<1/2\).  By the definition of the benchmark,
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t},
\]
because \(t<q\) implies \(n^{-t}>n^{-q}\).  Under the additional premise
stated in the proposition, division by \(n^{-t}\) gives
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\ge c n^{t-p}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
because \(t-p>0\).

Without that premise, the proved positive-band bracket gives only
\[
c n^{-q}\le R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\le Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Dividing by \(r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\) yields
\[
c n^{t-q}\le
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\le Cn^{t-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
The left endpoint tends to zero and the right endpoint tends to infinity, so
these inequalities do not determine the limit of the ratio.
