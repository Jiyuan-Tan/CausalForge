I optimized the frozen rate definitions and proved the exponent-ordering lemma.  On every compact \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), if \(p=(\alpha+\beta)/d\) and \(q=\gamma/(2\gamma+d)\), then \(\inf_K(q-p)>0\); hence for all sufficiently large \(n\), uniformly on \(K\),
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{band}}=n^{-p},\qquad
\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}=n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
The minimax-witness step for the upper inequality was also checked, but it is usable only after the corrected estimator theorem is proved.
