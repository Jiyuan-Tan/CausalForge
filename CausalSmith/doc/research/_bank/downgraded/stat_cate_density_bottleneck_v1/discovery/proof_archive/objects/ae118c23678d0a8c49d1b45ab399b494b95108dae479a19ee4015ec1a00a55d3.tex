The close-pair estimator fits neither the propensity, outcome regressions, nor the design density; it uses all unordered sample pairs with explicit midpoint and distance bandwidths.  On the broader low-smoothness region, the fallback uses cross-fitted partition-series nuisance regressions capped at \(k_n=\lfloor n/(\log n)^2\rfloor\).  Neither method estimates \(f_P\) or assumes a density modulus.
