The established lemma
\(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\) gives
\(0<p<t<q<1/2\).  By the definition of the benchmark,
\[
r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\vee n^{-q}=n^{-t},
\]
because \(t<q\) implies \(n^{-t}>n^{-q}\).  Under the displayed additional
premise in the proposed statement, division by \(n^{-t}\) gives
\[
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\geq c n^{t-p}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
since \(t-p>0\).

On the other hand, the proved positive-band bracket is only
\[
c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq Cn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Dividing by \(r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}=n^{-t}\) yields
\[
c n^{t-q}\leq
\frac{R_n^{\mathrm{band}}}{r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}}
\leq Cn^{t-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
The left endpoint tends to zero and the right endpoint tends to infinity.
Thus these proved inequalities imply no limit for the ratio, which is the
final assertion of the proposed statement.
