Fix a compact set \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).  The theorem
\(\mathrm{thm:density\mbox{-}coupled\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\) gives constants
\(c_K>0\) and \(n_{1,K}\) such that
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}geq c_K n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
\qquad(n\geq n_{1,K})
\tag{1}
\]
uniformly over \(K\).  On the other hand,
\(\mathrm{thm:rank\mbox{-}capped\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}bound}\) supplies one total
measurable sample map \(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)\) whose maximal
mean-absolute error is at most \(C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}\).  Substitution
of this single data-driven estimator into the infimum in
\(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}risk}\) yields
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}leq C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\qquad(n\geq n_{2,K}).
\tag{2}
\]
Equations (1)--(2), with \(n_0=\max\{n_{1,K},n_{2,K}\}\), prove the first
displayed bracket.

Put
\[
p=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{d},
\qquad q=\frac{\gamma}{2\gamma+d}.
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:low\mbox{-}region\mbox{-}exponent\mbox{-}ordering}\),
\(0<p<q\) throughout \(K\), with the gap bounded away from zero by compactness.
Therefore
\[
\frac{n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}}{n^{-q}}
=n^{q-p}(\log n)^{2p}\longrightarrow\infty
\]
uniformly over \(K\).  From \(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}rate}\), for
all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}
=n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\tag{3}
\]
Combining (1)--(3) gives
\[
c_Kn^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C_Kn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Since \(p<q\), these two inequalities leave a nondegenerate interval of possible
polynomial exponents.  They consequently establish neither that the upper
endpoint is minimax nor that an unknown density in a fixed positive band causes
a slowdown relative to the density-qualified benchmark.
