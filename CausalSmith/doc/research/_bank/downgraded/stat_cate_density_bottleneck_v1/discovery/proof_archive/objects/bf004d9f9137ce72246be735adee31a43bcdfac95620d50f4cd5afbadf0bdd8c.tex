Fix a compact set \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).  The
density-coupled lower-bound theorem supplies constants \(c_K>0\) and
\(n_{0,L}\) such that
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\geq c_Kn^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
\qquad(n\geq n_{0,L}).
\tag{1}
\]
The rank-capped upper-bound theorem supplies constants \(C_K<\infty\) and
\(n_{0,U}\), together with the single measurable sample map
\(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)\), whose maximal mean absolute error is
at most \(C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}\).  Substituting that map into
the infimum over estimators in the definition of \(R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\)
gives
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq C_K\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\qquad(n\geq n_{0,U}).
\tag{2}
\]
Taking the larger threshold proves the first bracket uniformly on \(K\).

Put \(p=(\alpha+\beta)/d\) and
\(q=\gamma/(2\gamma+d)\).  The low-region exponent-ordering lemma gives
\(0<p<q\).  Hence
\[
\frac{n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}}{n^{-q}}
=n^{q-p}(\log n)^{2p}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
so the definition of the upper rate gives, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}=n^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\tag{3}
\]
Combining (1)--(3) proves
\[
c_Kn^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\leq C_Kn^{-p}(\log n)^{2p}.
\]
Since the exponents \(p\) and \(q\) differ, this broad-region bracket does
not identify a polynomial minimax exponent.  In particular, by itself it
does not establish that an unknown density in the fixed positive band causes
a slowdown relative to the density-qualified benchmark.
