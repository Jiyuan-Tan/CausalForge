\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}=\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\cap
\{(\alpha,\beta,\gamma):0<\beta<\alpha\leq1\},
\qquad
t=\left(1+\frac d{2\gamma}+\frac d{4s}\right)^{-1}.
\]
Uniformly over compact subsets of \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\), the close-pair estimator satisfies
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}}
\mathbb E_P\left|\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{pair}}(x_0)-\tau_P(x_0)\right|
\lesssim n^{-t}=r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}.
\]
On \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\), the broader rank-capped fallback remains
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{band},n}}
\mathbb E_P\left|\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)-\tau_P(x_0)\right|
\lesssim\overline r_n^{\mathrm{band}}.
\]
If \(\pi_P,\mu_{0,P},\mu_{1,P}\) are supplied, the direct plug-in \(\mu_{1,P}(x_0)-\mu_{0,P}(x_0)\) has zero error.  Separately, oracleizing the local \(R\)-normal equations while retaining their evaluation sample gives mean absolute error \(O(n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)})\)
