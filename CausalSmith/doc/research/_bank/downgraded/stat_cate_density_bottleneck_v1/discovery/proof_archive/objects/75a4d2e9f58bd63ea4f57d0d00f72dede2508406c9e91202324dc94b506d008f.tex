Let \(K\subset\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\) be compact.  By
\(\mathrm{lem:oracle\mbox{-}regression\mbox{-}lower\mbox{-}bound}\), there are
constants \(c_K>0\) and \(n_{0,K}\) such that, simultaneously for every
\((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\in K\), every \(n\geq n_{0,K}\), and every measurable
estimator appearing in \(\mathrm{def:band\mbox{-}risk}\),
\[
R_n^{\mathrm{band}}
\geq c_K n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}.
\]
This invocation is legitimate because the lemma uses exactly the i.i.d. sampling,
two-sided density band, overlap, boundedness, H\"older, and causal identification
conditions declared in the present theorem's dependency closure.  Its two-point
subexperiment fixes the covariate density and the propensity while perturbing a
\(\gamma\)-smooth conditional-mean bump at \(x_0\).  Hence the displayed lower
bound is the oracle pointwise-regression branch.  In particular, neither the
candidate program \(\mathfrak H_n^{\mathrm{dens}}\) nor a lower bound of order
\(n^{-(\alpha+\beta)/d}\) is used or obtained.
