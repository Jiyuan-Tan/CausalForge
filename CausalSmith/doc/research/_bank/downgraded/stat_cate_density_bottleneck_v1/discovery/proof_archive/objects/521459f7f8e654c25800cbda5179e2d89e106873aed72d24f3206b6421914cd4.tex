The note constructs a total pair-difference \(U\)-statistic estimator and proves the compact-uniform risk bound \(h^\gamma+\ell^{\alpha+\beta}+(nh^d)^{-1/2}+(n^2h^d\ell^d)^{-1/2}\), optimized at \(n^{-t}=r_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}\).  It combines this estimator with the oracle pointwise-regression lower branch to give the honest bracket \(c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t}\), while retaining the broader rank-capped fallback outside the close-pair region.
