For \(n\geq3\), partition the indices deterministically into three folds \(I_1,I_2,I_3\) whose sizes differ by at most one, and use the three cyclic choices \((I_{r},I_{r+1},I_{r+2})\) as two nuisance folds and one evaluation fold.  For \(v\in\{\alpha,\beta\}\), let \(\mathcal B_{v,n}\) be the Lebesgue-orthonormal basis obtained by partitioning \([0,1]^d\) into the largest regular tensor grid for which the cellwise tensor polynomials of total degree \(\lceil v\rceil-1\) have total dimension at most \(k_n\).  On \(I_r\), estimate \(\pi_P\) by least squares of \(A\) on \(\mathcal B_{\alpha,n}\); on the independent fold \(I_{r+1}\), estimate a second copy of \(\pi_P\) in the same way and estimate \(\eta_P(x)=\mathbb E_P[Y\mid X=x]\) by least squares of \(Y\) on \(\mathcal B_{\beta,n}\).  In each nuisance least-squares fit, replace the empirical Gram matrix \(G=U\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_j)U^\top\) by
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathrm{nuis}}(G)=U\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\min\{2f_+,\max\{f_-/2,\lambda_j\}\}\right)U^\top.
\]
Clip the two propensity predictions to \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\) and the outcome prediction to \([-1,1]\); denote them by \(\widehat\pi_{r,a}\), \(\widehat\pi_{r,b}\), and \(\widehat\eta_r\).

Let \(\mathcal C=[-1/2,1/2]^d\), let \(b_\gamma\) be the vector of Lebesgue-orthonormal polynomials on \(\mathcal C\) of total degree at most \(\lceil\gamma\rceil-1\), and put \(b_{i,h}=b_\gamma((X_i-x_0)/h_n)\) and \(K_{i,h}=h_n^{-d}\mathbf1\{(X_i-x_0)/h_n\in\mathcal C\}\).  On \(I_{r+2}\), form
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat Q_r&=\frac1{|I_{r+2}|}\sum_{i\in I_{r+2}}K_{i,h}b_{i,h}b_{i,h}^\top
\{A_i-\widehat\pi_{r,a}(X_i)\}\{A_i-\widehat\pi_{r,b}(X_i)\},\\
\widehat S_r&=\frac1{|I_{r+2}|}\sum_{i\in I_{r+2}}K_{i,h}b_{i,h}
\{A_i-\widehat\pi_{r,a}(X_i)\}\{Y_i-\widehat\eta_r(X_i)\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Define \(\mathsf C_{\mathrm{loc}}\) by spectral clipping to
\([\varepsilon^2f_-/2,2f_+]\), put
\(\widehat\theta_r=\mathsf C_{\mathrm{loc}}(\widehat Q_r)^{-1}\widehat S_r\),
and take the fold estimate \(b_\gamma(0)^\top\widehat\theta_r\).  The estimator
\(\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{cap}}(x_0)\) is the truncation to \([-2,2]\) of the average of the three cyclic fold estimates.  For \(n<3\), define it to be zero.  Spectral clipping is the matrix functional in the displays and is independent of the choice of eigenvectors, so this construction specifies a measurable value on singular matrices and eigenvalue ties.
