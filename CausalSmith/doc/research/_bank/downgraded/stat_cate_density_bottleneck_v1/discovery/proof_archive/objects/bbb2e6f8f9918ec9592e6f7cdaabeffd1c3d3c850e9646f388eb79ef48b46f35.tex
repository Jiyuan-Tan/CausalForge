On \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\), midpoint localization and sufficiently close covariate pairs turn treatment and outcome differences into a local \(R\)-normal equation whose nuisance remainder is of order \(\ell^{\alpha+\beta}\).  The two-sided density band alone gives population rank and the pairwise Hoeffding variance calculation, so density smoothness is unnecessary for benchmark-rate achievability.
