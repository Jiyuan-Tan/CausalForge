A total close-pair local \(R\)-estimator attains the Kennedy--Balakrishnan--Robins--Wasserman benchmark rate on the nonempty region \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{pair}}\) using only the fixed density band \(f_{-}\leq f_P\leq f_{+}\), with no density estimator or density smoothness.  The proved positive-band minimax bracket is \(c n^{-q}\leq R_n^{\mathrm{band}}\leq Cn^{-t}=Cr_n^{\mathrm{KBRW}}\) with \(t<q\), so exact minimaxity and a matching positive-band converse remain open.  A broader rank-capped estimator gives the stated logarithmically inflated upper bound on \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{low}}\).
