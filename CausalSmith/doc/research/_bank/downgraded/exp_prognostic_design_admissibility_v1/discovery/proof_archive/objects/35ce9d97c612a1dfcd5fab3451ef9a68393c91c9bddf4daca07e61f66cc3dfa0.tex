The universal regret bounds need not coincide outside the named equality geometries.  No polynomial-time algorithm, quality estimator, sampling theory, or growing-population limit is claimed.  Closed forms beyond the general finite programs are restricted to homogeneous even blocks, matched pairs as their corollary, and unequal partitions with exactly two blocks.
