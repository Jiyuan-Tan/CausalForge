For the fixed finite population, the causal target remains the SATE and the design risk of the unbiased difference-in-means estimator is optimized over the full balanced mean-zero assignment class. The score-quality shell has an exact rank-one semidefinite lift, yielding an attaining sign-pair mixture, a matching full-class converse, and a computable admissibility threshold. If \(p=\dim(S)\), improvement over complete randomization is possible only when the squared quality exceeds \(\gamma(S)\geq p/(n-1)\), with equality exactly for equal-leverage score spaces. Three rank-one four-unit examples realize \(\gamma=1/3\), \(8/9\), and \(1\), proving that score geometry adds material information beyond dimension. A finite robust semidefinite formulation also gives the exact additive regret and an attaining single design when \(\rho\) is unknown, with closed forms for homogeneous even-block and matched-pair geometries.
