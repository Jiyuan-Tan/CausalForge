The paper combines a rank-one-exact semidefinite adversary, minimax duality over balanced sign mixtures, a compressed spectral threshold, and a finite robust regret program. It delivers the exact oracle value, attaining design, matching converse, admissibility dichotomy, the universal bound \(\gamma(S)\geq\dim(S)/(n-1)\) with an equal-leverage equality characterization, a strict singleton-versus-rest countergeometry, and an attaining unknown-quality minimax-regret design.
