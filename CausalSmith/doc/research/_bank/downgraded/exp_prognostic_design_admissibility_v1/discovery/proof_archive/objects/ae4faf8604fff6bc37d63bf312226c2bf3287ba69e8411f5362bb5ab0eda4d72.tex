\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(s=\rho^2\), \(q_s(A)=q_{\sqrt{s}}(A;S)\), and \(v(s)=v^*(\sqrt{s};S)\).  For a unit vector \(u\in H_n\), put \(t(u)=u^\top P_Su\).  Compactness of every spherical shell and monotonicity of \(v\) give
\[
\sup_{0\leq s\leq1}\{q_s(A)-v(s)\}
=\sup_{\substack{u\in H_n\\\|u\|_2=1}}
\{u^\top Au-v(t(u))\}.
\tag{1}
\]
For the lower inequality, set \(s=t(u)\).  For the reverse inequality, choose a maximizer \(u_s\) for \(q_s(A)\).  Since \(t(u_s)\geq s\) and \(v\) is nonincreasing,
\[
q_s(A)-v(s)\leq u_s^\top Au_s-v(t(u_s)).
\]

Every \(B\in\mathcal C_n\) is \(A(b)\) for some \(b\in\Delta_m\).  The scalar dual in \(\text{thm:cone-lift-dual}\) therefore yields
\[
v(t)=
\inf_{\substack{b\in\Delta_m,\ \eta\geq0,\ \ell\in\mathbb R\\
\ell I_{n-1}-Q_H^\top\{A(b)+\eta P_S\}Q_H\succeq0}}
\{\ell-\eta t\}.
\tag{2}
\]
At \(t=1\), this is interpreted by the compactified compressed-spectrum limit from \(\text{thm:cone-lift-dual}\) and \(\text{def:regret-handle}\); equality of values does not require a finite multiplier.  Substituting (2) into (1), using the negative-of-an-infimum identity, and minimizing over \(a\in\Delta_m\) proves the displayed robust representation.

We next prove the value bounds.  For fixed \(A\in\mathcal C_n\), define
\[
L=\lambda_{\max}(A\mid H_n),
\qquad
K=\lambda_{\max}(P_SAP_S\mid S),
\qquad
r=\Delta_{\mathrm{reg}}(A;S).
\]
At the two quality endpoints, \(q_0(A)=L\), \(v(0)=c_n\), \(q_1(A)=K\), and \(v(1)=\kappa(S)\).  Hence
\[
r\geq L-c_n,
\qquad
r\geq K-\kappa(S).
\tag{3}
\]
Let \(T=H_n\cap S^\perp\), so \(\dim(T)=n-1-p\).  Taking traces of the two diagonal compressions and bounding their eigenvalues by \(K\) and \(L\), respectively, gives
\[
n=\operatorname{tr}(A\mid H_n)
\leq pK+(n-1-p)L.
\tag{4}
\]
By (3), \(K\leq r+\kappa(S)\) and \(L\leq r+c_n\).  Substitution in (4), followed by \((n-1)c_n=n\), yields
\[
r\geq\frac{p}{n-1}\{c_n-\kappa(S)\}.
\tag{5}
\]
For the upper bound, choose complete randomization.  Its loss is \(c_n\) for every \(s\), while \(v(s)\) is nonincreasing from \(c_n\) to \(\kappa(S)\).  Therefore
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{reg}}(c_nP_H;S)=c_n-\kappa(S),
\tag{6}
\]
which proves the upper bound and identifies its witness.  If \(\kappa(S)=c_n\), (5)--(6) give zero regret.

We verify the asserted equality geometry without assuming it.  Suppose \(S=S_B\) comes from \(B\) equal blocks of even size, and put
\[
p=B-1,
\qquad
h=n-B,
\qquad
\beta=\frac{p}{n-1},
\qquad
\delta=\frac{n}{h}.
\]
Averaging within blocks and across blocks turns every covariance into \(aP_S+b(P_H-P_S)\) without increasing any shell loss; its trace identity is \(pa+hb=n\).  The shell loss is
\[
\max\{a,\,sa+(1-s)b\}.
\]
For \(0\leq a\leq c_n\), the trace identity gives \(b\geq a\) and
\[
sa+(1-s)b
=\delta(1-s)+a\left\{s-\frac{p}{h}(1-s)\right\}.
\]
The coefficient of \(a\) changes sign at \(s=\beta\); hence the minimum occurs at \(a=c_n\) when \(s\leq\beta\) and at \(a=0\) when \(s\geq\beta\).  Values \(a>c_n\) already have loss at least \(c_n\).  Therefore
\[
v(s)=\min\{c_n,\delta(1-s)\},
\qquad
\kappa(S_B)=0,
\qquad
c_n=\delta(1-\beta).
\tag{7}
\]
The two endpoints in (7) are feasible: complete randomization gives \(c_nP_H\), and independent complete randomization within the even blocks gives \(\delta(P_H-P_S)\).  Their mixture with weight \(\beta\) on complete randomization has loss
\[
\beta c_n+(1-\beta)\delta(1-s).
\]
Subtracting the two branches in (7) shows that its regret is maximized at \(s=0\) and \(s=1\), with common value \(c_n\beta\).  Equation (5), with \(\kappa=0\), gives the matching lower bound \(c_n\beta\).  Thus every homogeneous even-block space attains the universal lower bound.

It remains to justify attainment and the design interpretation.  If \(S=H_n\), then \(q_s(A)=\lambda_{\max}(A\mid H_n)\) for every \(s\), so continuity is immediate.  Otherwise \(T=H_n\cap S^\perp\) has positive dimension.  Every unit vector can be written as
\[
u=\sqrt{t}\,x+\sqrt{1-t}\,y,
\qquad
x\in S,
\qquad
y\in T,
\qquad
\|x\|_2=\|y\|_2=1,
\]
with \(t=u^\top P_Su\).  Writing \(t=s+(1-s)w\) reduces the shell maximization to a continuous quadratic function on the fixed compact set consisting of \(w\in[0,1]\) and the two unit spheres.  Hence \(q_s(A)\) is jointly continuous in \((A,s)\).  Compact minimization over \(\mathcal C_n\) makes \(v(s)\) continuous, and (1) then makes \(\Delta_{\mathrm{reg}}(A;S)\) continuous in \(A\).  Since \(\mathcal C_n\) is compact, an outer minimizer exists.  By \(\text{lem:balanced-covariance-realizability}\), its cut-hull weights can be sign-symmetrized into a mean-zero balanced design \(d_{\mathrm{reg}}\).

Finally, \(\text{thm:exact-oracle-saddle}\) and \(\text{lem:exact-design-risk}\) give, for every \(d\in\mathcal D_n\),
\[
\sup_{\rho\in[0,1]}
\left\{
\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal Y(\rho,M;S)}
\mathbb E_d[(\widehat\tau_d-\tau_n)^2]-V^*(\rho;S)
\right\}
=\frac{4M}{n}\Delta_{\mathrm{reg}}(A_d;S)
\geq R^*(n,S),
\]
with equality for the minimizing design.  The only randomness is the assignment, and the causal estimand remains the fixed-population SATE \(\tau_n\). \(\square\)
