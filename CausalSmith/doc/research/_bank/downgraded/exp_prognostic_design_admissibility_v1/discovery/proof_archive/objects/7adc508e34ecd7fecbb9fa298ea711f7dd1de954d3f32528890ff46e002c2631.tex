Fix \(\rho\in[0,1]\) and abbreviate \(\mathcal X_\rho(S)\) by \(K\).  Both \(K\) and \(\mathcal C_n\) are nonempty compact convex sets, and \((A,X)\mapsto\operatorname{tr}(AX)\) is bilinear.  For completeness, the needed minimax equality can be seen by a finite separation argument.  Enumerate the balanced sign-pairs by representatives \(z^{(1)},\ldots,z^{(m)}\), and put \(g_j(X)=z^{(j)\top}Xz^{(j)}\).  If
\(r>\max_{X\in K}\min_j g_j(X)\), then the compact convex image
\[
\{(g_1(X)-r,\ldots,g_m(X)-r):X\in K\}
\]
is disjoint from the nonnegative orthant.  Strong separation supplies a nonzero nonnegative vector \(a\); after normalization \(a\in\Delta_m\), and
\[
\max_{X\in K}\sum_{j=1}^m a_jg_j(X)<r.
\]
Letting \(r\) decrease to the right-hand max--min value proves the reverse of the elementary weak-minimax inequality.  Consequently,
\[
\min_{a\in\Delta_m}\max_{X\in K}
\sum_{j=1}^m a_jz^{(j)\top}Xz^{(j)}
=\max_{X\in K}\min_{1\leq j\leq m}z^{(j)\top}Xz^{(j)}.
\]
The left side is \(\min_{A\in\mathcal C_n}\max_{X\in K}\operatorname{tr}(AX)\), and sign reversal does not change the right side.  The cone-lift theorem and the definition of \(v^*(\rho;S)\) now give the first display in the theorem.

Introducing a scalar \(t\), the identity
\[
t\leq\min_{z\in\mathcal Z_n}z^\top Xz
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
t\leq z^\top Xz\ \text{for every }z\in\mathcal Z_n
\]
gives the stated finite semidefinite program.  Compactness gives primal and minimizing covariance optimizers.  The separating vector \(a\), equivalently the dual weights for the finitely many cut constraints, represents an optimizing covariance as a convex combination of \(z^{(j)}z^{(j)\top}\).  By balanced-covariance realizability, symmetrizing these weights produces an attaining design \(d^*_{\rho,S}\in\mathcal D_n\).

It remains to translate the spectral game into causal mean squared error.  For any \(\mu\in\mathcal Y(\rho,M;S)\), write \(\mu=\sqrt{nM}\,u\).  Then \(u\in H_n\), \(\|u\|_2=1\), and \(u^\top P_Su\geq\rho^2\).  The exact design-risk lemma and the rank-one-exact lift yield, for every \(d\in\mathcal D_n\),
\[
\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal Y(\rho,M;S)}
\mathbb E_d[(\widehat\tau_d-\tau_n)^2]
=\frac{4M}{n}q_\rho(A_d;S)
\geq\frac{4M}{n}v^*(\rho;S)=V^*(\rho;S).
\]
For the symmetrized design whose covariance minimizes the game, equality holds.  Thus the causal target remains the fixed SATE \(\tau_n\); \(V^*(\rho;S)\) is the exact design-risk criterion for its unbiased estimator, not a redefinition of that causal estimand.
