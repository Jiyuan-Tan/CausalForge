For every \(A\in\mathcal C_n\) and \(\rho\in[0,1]\), the nonconvex spherical-shell adversary has the rank-one-exact lift and scalar dual \[q_\rho(A;S)=\max_{X\in\mathcal X_\rho(S)}\operatorname{tr}(AX)=\min_{\eta\geq0}\left\{\lambda_{\max}(A+\eta P_S\mid H_n)-\eta\rho^2\right\}.\] Moreover, the lifted maximum admits a rank-one optimizer.
