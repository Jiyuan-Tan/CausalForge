Existing design-stage results explain that prognostic quality is unavailable before randomization and that an unindexed minimax criterion collapses to complete randomization, but they do not determine the exact quality frontier of an arbitrary external-score subspace within the realizable fixed-count covariance hull.  They also do not quantify the finite-population regret of one quality-agnostic design by universal sharp-endpoint bounds or solve the parity-sensitive unequal two-block family over the full balanced design class.
