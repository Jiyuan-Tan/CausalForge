HarshawSavjeSpielmanZhang2024 supplies the design-based covariance identity and motivates tuning by predictive quality, while Section 8 of its supplement states that the relevant quality ratio is unavailable at design time. Kallus2018OptimalBalance develops the zero-sum mixed-design architecture and the no-free-lunch benchmark under unstructured outcomes. Kallus2021Randomization, Section 5, defines a generic mixed-strategy optimal design for a specified conditional-mean class and gives a largest-eigenvalue criterion for an ellipsoidal class. The theorem-level delta here is the rank-one-exact solution of the nonellipsoidal lower score-energy shell over the fixed-count balanced cut hull, recovery of an attaining sign-pair law, the if-and-only-if quality frontier, the leverage-exact dimension price, and the exact robust regret of a quality-agnostic design. HuberMaric2019, p. 5, Theorem 2, concerns a cut-polytope image for symmetric Bernoulli correlations; it neither imposes fixed treatment counts nor studies causal risk. Bai2022MatchedPairs gives a scalar-prognosis endpoint within stratified superpopulation designs, not a converse over all fixed-population balanced laws. KapelnerKriegerSklarAzriel2022 shows that rerandomization tuning depends on unknown response features, motivating the oracle-relative regret criterion resolved here. SchulerWalshHallWalshFisher2022 documents deterioration of externally trained scores under domain shift. SchindlBranson2024 compares quadratic-form rerandomization rules but leaves comparison with other design classes outside its scope. GuoLiangToulis2025 uses a Gaussianized covariance subclass and local optimization; the present program retains the full discrete covariance hull and accepts exponential worst-case computation.
