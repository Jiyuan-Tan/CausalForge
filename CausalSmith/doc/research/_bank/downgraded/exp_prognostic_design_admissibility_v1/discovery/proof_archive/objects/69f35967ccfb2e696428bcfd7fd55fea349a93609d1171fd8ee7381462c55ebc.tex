\paragraph{Full score space.}
Suppose first that \(S=H_n\).  If \(u\in H_n\) and \(\|u\|_2=1\), then \(u^\top P_Su=1\), so the score-quality constraint is automatic for every \(\rho\in[0,1]\).  Hence, by the definition of the fixed-design loss,
\[
q_\rho(A;H_n)=\lambda_{\max}(A\mid H_n).
\tag{1}
\]
Every \(A\in\mathcal C_n\) is a convex combination of matrices \(zz^\top\) with \(z\in\mathcal Z_n\).  Thus \(A\succeq0\), \(A\mathbf 1_n=0\), every diagonal entry of \(A\) is one, and \(\operatorname{tr}(A)=n\).  Since \(H_n\) has dimension \(n-1\), the largest-eigenvalue--average-eigenvalue inequality gives
\[
\lambda_{\max}(A\mid H_n)
\geq \frac{\operatorname{tr}(A\mid H_n)}{n-1}
=\frac{n}{n-1}=c_n.
\tag{2}
\]
The complete-randomization covariance is \(A_{\mathrm{CRD}}=c_nP_H\in\mathcal C_n\), and its restriction to \(H_n\) is \(c_nI_{H_n}\).  Equations \((1)\)--\((2)\) and the definition of \(v^*\) therefore imply
\[
v^*(\rho;H_n)=c_n
\qquad\text{for every }\rho\in[0,1].
\tag{3}
\]
After multiplication by \(4M/n\), equation \((3)\) equals \(4M/(n-1)=V_{\mathrm{CRD}}(\rho;H_n)\).  Hence there is no \(\rho<1\) at which strict improvement occurs, and the definition of \(\mathfrak A_n\) yields \(H_n\notin\mathfrak A_n\).

\paragraph{Exact pair-space specialization.}
The exact pair-space frontier lemma, which supplies a matching converse over the full covariance hull, gives for every even \(n\geq4\)
\[
v^*(\rho;S_{\mathrm{pair}})
=\min\left\{\frac{n}{n-1},\,2(1-\rho^2)\right\},
\qquad
\kappa(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0,
\tag{4}
\]
and
\[
\gamma(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)},
\qquad
\rho^*(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=
\sqrt{\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)}}.
\tag{5}
\]
It also identifies the endpoint covariances: complete randomization attains the first term in \((4)\), while independent fair orientations within the fixed pairs attain the second.  Thus this proposition is the \(m=2\), \(B=n/2\) specialization of the homogeneous even-block calculation, rather than a restriction to a matched-design subclass.

For \(n=4\), equations \((4)\)--\((5)\), together with \(S_4=S_{\mathrm{pair}}\), reduce to
\[
v^*(\rho;S_4)=\min\left\{\frac43,\,2(1-\rho^2)\right\},
\quad
\kappa(S_4)=0,
\quad
\gamma(S_4)=\frac13,
\quad
\rho^*(S_4)=\frac1{\sqrt3}.
\tag{6}
\]
Indeed, equality of the two branches in \((6)\) is equivalent to
\[
\frac43=2(1-\rho^2)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\rho^2=\frac13.
\tag{7}
\]
Consequently complete randomization attains the minimum for \(\rho\leq1/\sqrt3\), and the within-pair design attains it for \(\rho\geq1/\sqrt3\); both attain it at equality.

It remains to verify that the displayed four-unit law is exactly that endpoint design and has the displayed covariance.  Put
\[
a=(1,-1,1,-1)^\top,
\qquad
b=(1,-1,-1,1)^\top.
\]
The support of \(d_{\mathrm{pair},4}\) is \(\{a,-a,b,-b\}\), with probability \(1/4\) on each point.  Each support point is balanced, and the support is closed under sign reversal with equal weights, so \(d_{\mathrm{pair},4}\in\mathcal D_4\).  Direct averaging gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_{d_{\mathrm{pair},4}}[zz^\top]
&=\frac12aa^\top+\frac12bb^\top\\
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1&0&0\\
-1&1&0&0\\
0&0&1&-1\\
0&0&-1&1
\end{pmatrix}
=A_{\mathrm{pair},4}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
The four assignments in \((8)\) are precisely the outcomes of two independent fair pair orientations.  Therefore the second endpoint covariance in the pair-space frontier lemma is the displayed \(A_{\mathrm{pair},4}\), proving the attainment assertion in \((6)\).

For general even \(n\), equation \((4)\) gives \(\kappa(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0<c_n\).  The admissibility-frontier theorem then yields \(S_{\mathrm{pair}}\in\mathfrak A_n\).  Finally, at \(n=8\), \(M=1\), and \(\rho=1\), equation \((4)\) and the exact risk scaling give
\[
V^*(1;S_{\mathrm{pair}})
=\frac{4M}{n}v^*(1;S_{\mathrm{pair}})
=\frac48\min\left\{\frac87,0\right\}=0,
\tag{9}
\]
whereas the complete-randomization benchmark is
\[
V_{\mathrm{CRD}}(1;S_{\mathrm{pair}})
=\frac{4M}{n-1}=\frac47.
\tag{10}
\]
As boundary checks, \((4)\) gives \(v^*(0;S_{\mathrm{pair}})=c_n\), because \(c_n<2\), and \(v^*(1;S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0\), consistent respectively with no quality information and with a covariance that annihilates the score space.
