The results are finite-population and design-based: potential outcomes are fixed and assignment is the only source of randomness. The exact oracle value, attaining design, full-class converse, admissibility dichotomy, dimension-price equality characterization, threshold computation, three exact rank-one geometry checks, endpoint reductions, finite-dimensional unknown-quality regret program, and attaining regret design are established. Homogeneous even blocks, including matched pairs, admit closed-form oracle and regret values. Polynomial-time computation is not claimed; enumeration of the balanced cut hull can be exponential, and the arbitrary-subspace regret formula is an exact finite optimization rather than a universal closed form. Growing-rank asymptotics and sampling-based inference are outside scope.
