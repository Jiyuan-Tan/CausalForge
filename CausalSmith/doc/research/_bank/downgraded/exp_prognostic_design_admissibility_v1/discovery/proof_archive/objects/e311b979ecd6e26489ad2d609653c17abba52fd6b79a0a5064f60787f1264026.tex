The quantity \(\rho\) is an oracle index for realized outcome--score alignment, not a feature computable from the score matrix before randomization. The score matrix nevertheless determines a rigorous design-stage screen. Balancing a \(p\)-dimensional score space cannot improve the worst-case SATE risk until the guaranteed score-energy fraction exceeds \(p/(n-1)\). Equal leverage makes this necessary dimension price exact, but rank is not sufficient: at rank one and \(n=4\), the pair, intermediate, and singleton-versus-rest geometries have \(\gamma=1/3\), \(8/9\), and \(1\). When no defensible value of \(\rho\) can be chosen, the additive-regret theorem supplies one quality-agnostic assignment mixture with a matching minimax certificate.
