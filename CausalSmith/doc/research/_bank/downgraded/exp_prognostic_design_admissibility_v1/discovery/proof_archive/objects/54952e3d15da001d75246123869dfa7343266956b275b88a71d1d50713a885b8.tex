If \(S=H_n\), then \(v^*(\rho;S)=c_n\) for every \(\rho\in[0,1]\), so \(S\notin\mathfrak A_n\). Define the singleton-versus-rest score geometry and its normalized basis by
\[
S_{\mathrm{out}}=\operatorname{span}\{P_H(1,0,\ldots,0)^\top\},
\qquad
u_{\mathrm{out}}=\frac{P_H(1,0,\ldots,0)^\top}{\|P_H(1,0,\ldots,0)^\top\|_2}.
\]
For every \(z\in\mathcal Z_n\),
\[
(u_{\mathrm{out}}^\top z)^2=c_n.
\]
Consequently,
\[
v^*(\rho;S_{\mathrm{out}})=c_n\quad(0\leq\rho\leq1),
\qquad
\kappa(S_{\mathrm{out}})=c_n,
\qquad
\gamma(S_{\mathrm{out}})=1,
\qquad
S_{\mathrm{out}}\notin\mathfrak A_n.
\]
At \(n=4\), this rank-one geometry has \(\dim(S_{\mathrm{out}})/(n-1)=1/3\) but \(\gamma(S_{\mathrm{out}})=1\). In contrast, for the equal-leverage rank-one basis \(u_4=2^{-1}(1,1,-1,-1)^\top\) and \(S_4=\operatorname{span}\{u_4\}=S_{\mathrm{pair}}\), the displayed balanced sign-pair mixture \(d_{\mathrm{pair},4}\) has covariance \(A_{\mathrm{pair},4}\) and satisfies
\[
v^*(\rho;S_4)=\min\left\{\frac43,\ 2(1-\rho^2)\right\},
\qquad
\kappa(S_4)=0,
\qquad
\gamma(S_4)=\frac13,
\qquad
\rho^*(S_4)=\frac1{\sqrt3}.
\]
Complete randomization attains the first branch for \(\rho\leq1/\sqrt3\), while \(d_{\mathrm{pair},4}\) attains the second branch for \(\rho\geq1/\sqrt3\). More generally, independent fair orientations within fixed pairs give \(\kappa(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0\), so \(S_{\mathrm{pair}}\in\mathfrak A_n\). At \(n=8\) and \(M=1\), \(V^*(1;S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0\) while \(V_{\mathrm{CRD}}(1;S_{\mathrm{pair}})=4/7\).
