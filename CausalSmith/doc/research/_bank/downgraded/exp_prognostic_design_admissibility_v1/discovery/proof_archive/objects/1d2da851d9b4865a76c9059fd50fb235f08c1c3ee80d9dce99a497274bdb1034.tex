The paper proves the exact oracle saddle and implementability result, the identity \(\gamma(S)=U(\operatorname{diag}P_S)\) with its sharp leverage and equal-leverage consequences, the universal regret bracket with complete-randomization and endpoint-trace certificates, and exact unequal two-block formulas for both even--even and odd--odd partitions.  Homogeneous even blocks and matched pairs receive closed-form oracle values, regret-optimal mixtures, and matching converses over the full design class.
