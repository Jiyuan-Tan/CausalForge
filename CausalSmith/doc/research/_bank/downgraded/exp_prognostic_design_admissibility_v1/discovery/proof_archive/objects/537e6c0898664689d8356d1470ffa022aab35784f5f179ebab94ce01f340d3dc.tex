The following conditions are equivalent:
\[
(i)\ S\in\mathfrak A_n,
\qquad
(ii)\ \kappa(S)<c_n,
\qquad
(iii)\ \gamma(S)<1.
\]
When these conditions hold, \(\rho^*(S)=\sqrt{\gamma(S)}\); otherwise \(\rho^*(S)=1\).  Let \(p=\dim(S)\), \(\ell_i=(P_S)_{ii}\), and \(\beta=p/(n-1)\).  For \(t\in[\beta,1]\), put
\[
d_i(t)=\frac{n-1}{n}t-\ell_i,
\]
and define the leverage envelope
\[
U(\ell)=\inf\left\{t\in[\beta,1]:d_i(t)\geq0\ \text{for every }i,\quad
2\max_i\sqrt{d_i(t)}\leq\sum_{i=1}^n\sqrt{d_i(t)}\right\}.
\]
Then the explicit two-sided leverage bounds are
\[
\beta\leq \frac{n}{n-1}\max_i\ell_i
\leq\gamma(S)\leq U(\ell)\leq1.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\gamma(S)=\beta
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{diag}(P_S)=\frac{p}{n}(1,\ldots,1)^\top.
\]
Thus \(U(\ell)<1\) is a score-matrix-only sufficient certificate for \(S\in\mathfrak A_n\), while \(n\max_i\ell_i/(n-1)=1\) certifies \(S\notin\mathfrak A_n\).  The envelope is exact for the three four-unit rank-one examples: it gives \(\gamma(S_4)=1/3\), \(\gamma(S_{\mathrm{mid}})=8/9\), and \(\gamma(S_{\mathrm{out}})=1\).  It is an upper envelope, not a claim that leverage determines \(\gamma(S)\) for every subspace.

Consequently,
\[
\{\rho^*(S)\}^2\geq\frac{p}{n-1},
\qquad
V^*(\rho;S)<V_{\mathrm{CRD}}(\rho;S)
\Longrightarrow
\rho^2>\gamma(S)\geq\frac{n}{n-1}\max_i\ell_i.
\]
Given a basis matrix for \(S\), the leverage envelope and both leverage screens are computable directly from \(P_S\) by a one-dimensional algebraic search.  If the screens do not decide admissibility, enumeration of \(\mathcal Z_n\), semidefinite optimization, and one-dimensional bisection compute \(\kappa(S)\), \(\rho^*(S)\), and an \(\varepsilon\)-optimal attaining assignment mixture from the score matrix and \(n\).
