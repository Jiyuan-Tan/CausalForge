Let \(\mathcal P=(G_1,\ldots,G_B)\) be a partition of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\), where \(B\geq2\), and put \(m_j=|G_j|\).  For every \(\rho\in[0,1]\), within-block orbit averaging gives the exact finite reduction
\[
v^*(\rho;S_{\mathcal P})
=\min_{G\in\mathcal G_{\mathcal P}}
q_\rho\!\left(A_{\mathcal P}(G);S_{\mathcal P}\right).
\]
For every \(G\in\mathcal G_{\mathcal P}\), the matrix \(A_{\mathcal P}(G)\) belongs to \(\mathcal C_n\) and is the covariance of a mean-zero law in \(\mathcal D_n\), obtained by within-block permutation and sign symmetrization.  Conversely, for every \(A\in\mathcal C_n\), there is a \(G\in\mathcal G_{\mathcal P}\) such that
\[
q_\rho\!\left(A_{\mathcal P}(G);S_{\mathcal P}\right)
\leq q_\rho(A;S_{\mathcal P}).
\]
Thus the displayed finite moment-polytope problem is an attaining construct-and-determine formulation with a matching converse over the full balanced covariance hull.  Moreover,
\[
\kappa(S_{\mathcal P})=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
m_j\text{ is even for every }j=1,\ldots,B.
\]

Now suppose that the partition has \(B\) equal blocks of an even size \(m=n/B\).  Write
\[
p=B-1,
\qquad
h=n-B,
\qquad
\delta_m=\frac{m}{m-1}=\frac{n}{n-B},
\qquad
\beta=\frac{p}{n-1}.
\]
Then the exact oracle value and frontier invariants are
\[
v^*(\rho;S_B)
=\min\left\{c_n,\,\delta_m(1-\rho^2)\right\},
\qquad
\kappa(S_B)=0,
\qquad
\gamma(S_B)=\beta,
\qquad
\rho^*(S_B)=\sqrt{\beta}.
\]
Complete randomization attains the first branch.  Independent complete randomization within every block has covariance \(\delta_m(P_H-P_S)\), where here \(P_S\) projects onto \(S_B\), and attains the second branch.  The mixture placing weight \(\beta\) on complete randomization has covariance
\[
A_{B,\mathrm{mix}}
=\beta c_nP_H+(1-\beta)\delta_m(P_H-P_S)\in\mathcal C_n
\]
and attains the exact full-class regret
\[
\min_{A\in\mathcal C_n}\Delta_{\mathrm{reg}}(A;S_B)
=\frac{np}{(n-1)^2},
\qquad
R^*(n,S_B)=\frac{4Mp}{(n-1)^2}.
\]
The regret constraints at \(\rho=0\) and \(\rho=1\) are jointly binding, and the same two endpoint constraints give a matching lower bound for every \(A\in\mathcal C_n\).

In particular, if \(n=2k\), the pair partition is the case \(B=k\), \(m=2\), and hence
\[
v^*(\rho;S_{\mathrm{pair}})
=\min\left\{c_n,\,2(1-\rho^2)\right\},
\qquad
\kappa(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0,
\qquad
\gamma(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)},
\qquad
\rho^*(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=\sqrt{\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)}}.
\]
The complete-randomization covariance attains the first branch, and independent fair within-pair orientations define a law in \(\mathcal D_n\) attaining the second branch.
