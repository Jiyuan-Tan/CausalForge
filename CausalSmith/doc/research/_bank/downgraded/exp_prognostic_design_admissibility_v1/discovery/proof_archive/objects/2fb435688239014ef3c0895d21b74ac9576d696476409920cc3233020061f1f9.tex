The quantity \(\rho\) is an oracle index for realized outcome--score alignment, not a feature computable from the score matrix before randomization. The score matrix nevertheless determines a rigorous design-stage screen. Balancing a \(p\)-dimensional score space cannot improve the worst-case SATE risk until the guaranteed score-energy fraction exceeds \(p/(n-1)\). Equal leverage makes this necessary dimension price exact; it is not a universal sufficiency claim based on rank alone, because the singleton-versus-rest rank-one space has \(\gamma=1\) and is design-useless. When no defensible value of \(\rho\) can be chosen, the additive-regret theorem supplies one quality-agnostic assignment mixture with a matching minimax certificate.
