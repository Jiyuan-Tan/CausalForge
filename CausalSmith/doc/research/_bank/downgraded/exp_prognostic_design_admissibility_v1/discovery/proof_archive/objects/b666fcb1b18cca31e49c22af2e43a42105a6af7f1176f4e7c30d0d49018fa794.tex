For \(n=8\) and \(S=S_{\mathrm{pair}}\), the matched-pair design has zero risk on vectors constant within pairs, yet exact enumeration also produces a fixed mean vector with within-pair variation on which its risk is \(1.75\) times the complete-randomization risk. Thus \(V^*(1;S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0\) does not justify matched pairs at lower quality, and no theorem may infer global dominance from perfect-score performance. The dimension floor is only necessary: even at rank one, the intermediate four-unit geometry raises the squared-quality threshold to \(8/9\), while singleton-versus-rest raises it to one.
