\textbf{Proof.}
This statement is the pair-partition corollary of \(\text{lem:pair-space-exact-frontier}\), not an independent design theorem.  Set \(B=k=n/2\) and block size \(m=2\) in its homogeneous even-block formulas.  Then
\[
p=k-1,\qquad \delta_m=2,\qquad
\beta=\frac{k-1}{2k-1}=\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)}=\alpha_n.
\]
The lemma immediately gives the oracle value, \(\kappa=0\), \(\gamma=\alpha_n\), and \(\rho^*=\sqrt{\alpha_n}\).  Its attaining within-block law is exactly independent fair orientation within each pair, with covariance \(2(P_H-P_S)\).  Mixing it with complete randomization with weight \(\alpha_n\) gives
\[
A_{\mathrm{mix}}=\alpha_nc_nP_H+(1-\alpha_n)2(P_H-P_S),
\]
and the lemma's endpoint optimality certificate gives
\[
\min_A\Delta_{\mathrm{reg}}(A;S_{\mathrm{pair}})
=c_n\alpha_n=\frac{n(n-2)}{2(n-1)^2}.
\]
Multiplication by \(4M/n\) yields the asserted \(R^*\).

When \(n=4\), complete randomization assigns weights \((1/3,1/3,1/3)\) to the three balanced sign-pairs, and the within-pair law assigns \((0,1/2,1/2)\), after ordering the omitted pair first.  Their mixture with complete-randomization weight \(1/3\) is
\[
\frac13(1/3,1/3,1/3)+\frac23(0,1/2,1/2)
=(1/9,4/9,4/9).
\]
The normalized regret is \(c_4\alpha_4=(4/3)(1/3)=4/9\), and \(4M/n=M\), so \(R^*(4,S_4)=4M/9\). \(\square\)
