The following conditions are equivalent:
\[
(i)\ S\in\mathfrak A_n,
\qquad
(ii)\ \kappa(S)<c_n,
\qquad
(iii)\ \gamma(S)<1.
\]
When these conditions hold, \(\rho^*(S)=\sqrt{\gamma(S)}\); otherwise \(\rho^*(S)=1\). Let \(p=\dim(S)\). Then
\[
\gamma(S)\geq \frac{p}{n-1},
\]
and equality holds if and only if the score projector has equal leverage,
\[
\operatorname{diag}(P_S)=\frac{p}{n}(1,\ldots,1)^\top.
\]
Consequently, for every \(S\),
\[
\{\rho^*(S)\}^2\geq\frac{p}{n-1},
\]
where the convention \(\rho^*(S)=1\) applies off \(\mathfrak A_n\), and
\[
V^*(\rho;S)<V_{\mathrm{CRD}}(\rho;S)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\rho^2>\gamma(S)\geq\frac{p}{n-1}.
\]
Given a basis matrix for \(S\) and a tolerance \(\varepsilon>0\), enumeration of \(\mathcal Z_n\), semidefinite optimization, and one-dimensional bisection compute \(\kappa(S)\), \(\rho^*(S)\), and an \(\varepsilon\)-optimal attaining assignment mixture using only the score matrix and \(n\). The equality case in the dimension bound is decidable directly from the diagonal of the score projector.
