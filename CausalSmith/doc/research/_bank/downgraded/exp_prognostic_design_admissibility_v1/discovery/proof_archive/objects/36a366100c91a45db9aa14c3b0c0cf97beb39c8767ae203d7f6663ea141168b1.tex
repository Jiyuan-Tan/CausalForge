For a fixed finite population and an external score subspace known before assignment, the paper solves the oracle design problem over the full balanced mean-zero assignment class and gives an attaining randomization with a matching converse for the design-unbiased difference in means.  The first improving squared quality is determined exactly from the score matrix: \(\gamma(S)\) is both an affine spectral minimum and the one-dimensional polygon functional \(U(\operatorname{diag}P_S)\), so \(\rho^*(S)=\sqrt{\gamma(S)}\) on the admissible class.  A single design chosen without knowing quality has an exact finite, generally exponential, robust program and satisfies the universal bracket \(\frac{p}{n-1}(c_n-\kappa(S))\leq\frac{n}{4M}R^*(n,S)\leq c_n-\kappa(S)\).  Closed forms, attaining mixtures, and full-class certificates are given for homogeneous even blocks, matched pairs, and every unequal two-block partition, including both parity cases.
