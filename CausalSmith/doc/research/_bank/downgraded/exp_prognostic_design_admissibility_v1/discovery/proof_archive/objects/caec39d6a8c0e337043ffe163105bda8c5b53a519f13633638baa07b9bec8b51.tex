Let \(n=2k\) with \(k\geq2\), and put \(s=\rho^2\).  For the pair-partition score space, the exact oracle value over the full covariance hull is
\[
v^*(\rho;S_{\mathrm{pair}})
=\min\left\{c_n,\,2(1-\rho^2)\right\}.
\]
The complete-randomization covariance \(A_{\mathrm{CRD}}\) attains the first branch.  The law obtained by drawing independent fair signs \(\epsilon_1,\ldots,\epsilon_k\) and setting \(z_{2j-1}=\epsilon_j\), \(z_{2j}=-\epsilon_j\) belongs to \(\mathcal D_n\) and attains the second branch.  Consequently,
\[
\kappa(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=0,
\qquad
\gamma(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)},
\qquad
\rho^*(S_{\mathrm{pair}})=\sqrt{\frac{n-2}{2(n-1)}}.
\]
