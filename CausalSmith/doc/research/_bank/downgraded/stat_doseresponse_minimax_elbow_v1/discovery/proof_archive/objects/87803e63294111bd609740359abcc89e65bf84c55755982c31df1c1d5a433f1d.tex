The designated density \(p_X\) is nonnegative and integrates to one. Integrating the pointwise inequalities \(c\le\pi_P(a\mid x)\le1/c\), supplied by the two positivity conditions in Definition~\texttt{def:holder-class}, against \(p_X(x)\,dx\) therefore gives, for every \(a\in[0,1]\),
\[
c=c\int_{[0,1]^d}p_X(x)\,dx
\le \int_{[0,1]^d}\pi_P(a\mid x)p_X(x)\,dx
=f_A(a)
\le \frac1c\int_{[0,1]^d}p_X(x)\,dx
=\frac1c.
\]
No smoothness, lower bound, or upper bound on \(p_X\) is used.
