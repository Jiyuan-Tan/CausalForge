Put \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), and \(S_0=s\wedge1\), and define
\[
E_\mu=\left(2+A_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1}\right)^{-1},
\qquad
E_\pi=\left(2+B_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1}\right)^{-1}.
\]
The optimized separated-pilot exponent is
\[
R_{\mathrm{sep}}=
\max_{0\le\gamma\le1}
\min\left\{
2\alpha\gamma,
1-\gamma,
2\left[\min\{A_0\gamma,E_\mu\}+\min\{B_0\gamma,E_\pi\}\right]
\right\},
\]
and the optimized regressogram exponent is
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=
\left[
1+\frac1{2A_0}+\frac d{4S_0}
+\left(\frac12-\frac{B_0}{A_0}\right)_+
\min\left\{\frac1{2B_0},\frac d{2S_0}\right\}
\right]^{-1},
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\}.
\]
Lemma~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket} establishes constants independent of \(n\) such that
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\). In either residual region, \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}<2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\). Thus, if an exact polynomial exponent \(\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\) exists, it must satisfy
\[
R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)
\le\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1}.
\]
The exponent \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) when \(B_0\ge A_0/2\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sep}}\), \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\), and \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\). These are estimator-side invariance statements, not residual minimax \(\beta\)-invariance. At \((\alpha,\beta,d,s)=(1,1,1,1/5)\), the prescribed higher-order population exponent is \(R_{\mathrm{HOIF}}=8/13\), whereas \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{1/2,4/11\}=1/2\), and the required rank obeys \(k\asymp n^{10/13}=o(n)\). This explicit surviving-band gap remains unresolved because uniform empirical design-stability control has not been established under the frozen class.
