The random variable \(U\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(Z_i:i\in I_\mu)\), whereas \(V\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(Z_i:i\in I_{\mathrm{aux}})\). These sigma-fields are independent under Assumption~\texttt{ass:iid}, because the folds are deterministic and disjoint. The population functions and norms occurring in \(U\) and \(V\) are nonrandom. Hence \(U\) and \(V\) are independent, and Lemma~\texttt{lem:separate-slice-pilot-mse} gives
\[
\mathbb E_P[U^2V^2]=\mathbb E_P[U^2]\mathbb E_P[V^2]\le Cu^2v^2.
\]
