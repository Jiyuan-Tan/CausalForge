For every admissible residual tuple and fixed \(0<c<1\), Lemmas~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket} and~\texttt{lem:extreme-low-s-fuzzy-lower} imply that there are constants \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\in\mathbb N\), independent of \(n\), such that, for all \(n\ge n_0\),
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}},
\qquad
r_{\mathrm{low}}
=
\min\left\{
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},
\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}
\right\}.
\]
The two valid inequalities force \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\). Consequently, if an exact exponent \(\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\) exists, then
\[
R_{\mathrm{ach}}
\le
\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)
\le
r_{\mathrm{low}}.
\]
At every tuple where the two explicit endpoints happen to agree, this bracket itself proves the exact rate with \(\rho=R_{\mathrm{ach}}=r_{\mathrm{low}}\). The fuzzy converse strictly improves the oracle floor exactly when
\[
\frac ds>4+\frac4\alpha-\frac{2}{\alpha\vee\beta}.
\]
The converse exponent \(r_{\mathrm{low}}\) is independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\le\alpha\). On the achievable side, \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sep}}\), \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\), and \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\). These one-sided invariance facts do not prove minimax \(\beta\)-invariance unless the bracket endpoints match. As a boundary check outside the fixed \(0<c<1\) question, Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-oracle-rate} proves that when \(c=1\),
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,1,t_0)
\asymp
n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\]
for every \(\beta,s,d\).
