Let \(B_{\mu j}\) be a regression-pilot cube and put \(q_{\mu j}=P_X(B_{\mu j})\). If \(Q_{\mu j}\) denotes the probability that an observation falls in this cube and satisfies \(\lvert A-t_0\rvert\le b_\mu\), positivity and the interior restriction on \(b_\mu\) give
\[
2cb_\mu q_{\mu j}\le Q_{\mu j}\le 2b_\mu q_{\mu j}/c.
\]
Within a cube, the slice \(x\mapsto\mu_P(t_0,x)\) oscillates by at most \(C\delta_\mu^{S_0}\). Within the treatment window, \(a\mapsto\mu_P(a,x)\) differs from its value at \(t_0\) by at most \(Cb_\mu^{A_0}\), uniformly in \(x\). Thus the conditional cell bias of the raw regression average is at most \(C(\delta_\mu^{S_0}+b_\mu^{A_0})\).

Let \(R_{\mu j}\) be its selected count. It is binomial with parameters \((N_\mu,Q_{\mu j})\), where \(N_\mu\asymp n\). For a binomial variable \(R\sim\operatorname{Bin}(N,Q)\),
\[
\mathbb E\left[\frac{1}{R+1}\right]
=\frac{1-(1-Q)^{N+1}}{(N+1)Q}
\le\frac{1}{(N+1)Q},
\]
and \(R^{-1}\mathbf 1\{R>0\}\le2/(R+1)\). Since the response is bounded, the nonempty-cell variance, integrated with weight \(q_{\mu j}\), is at most \(Cq_{\mu j}/(nQ_{\mu j})\). Summing over at most \(C\delta_\mu^{-d}\) cells gives \(C/(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)\). Empty cells obey the same order because
\[
\sum_jq_{\mu j}P(R_{\mu j}=0)
\le\sum_jq_{\mu j}e^{-2cN_\mu b_\mu q_{\mu j}}
\le \frac{C}{nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d},
\]
where \(xe^{-rx}\le1/(er)\) is used cellwise. Therefore
\[
\mathbb E_PU^2\le C\left\{\delta_\mu^{2S_0}+b_\mu^{2A_0}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1}\right\}\le Cu^2.
\]

For the density pilot, let \(B_{\pi j}\) be a cube, \(q_{\pi j}=P_X(B_{\pi j})\), and \(N_{\pi j}\) the number of \(I_{\mathrm{aux}}\)-observations in that cube. Conditional on the covariates, the treatment-window count is a sum of Bernoulli variables whose success probabilities lie between \(2cb_\pi\) and \(2b_\pi/c\). Division by \(2b_\pi(N_{\pi j}\vee1)\) consequently has conditional variance at most \(C/[b_\pi(N_{\pi j}\vee1)]\). The same reciprocal-binomial identity, now for \(N_{\pi j}\sim\operatorname{Bin}(N_\pi,q_{\pi j})\), yields after integration and summation the bound \(C/(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)\). The cell bias is at most \(C(\delta_\pi^{S_0}+b_\pi^{B_0})\), by the two slice Hölder conditions. Clipping cannot increase the squared error because \(\pi_P(t_0\mid x)\in[c,1/c]\subset[c/2,2/c]\). Hence
\[
\mathbb E_PV^2\le C\left\{\delta_\pi^{2S_0}+b_\pi^{2B_0}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1}\right\}\le Cv^2.
\]
No lower bound or smoothness condition on \(p_X\) was used.
