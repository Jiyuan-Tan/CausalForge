Write \(N=|I_{\mathrm{lp}}|\), \(Q_i=K_h(A_i)S_h(A_i)S_h(A_i)^\top\), and \(G=\int_{-1}^1K(u)S(u)S(u)^\top\,du\).  The population calculation in \(\mathrm{lem:design-adaptive-local-polynomial-bias}\) gives
\[
D_h=\mathbb E_PQ_i\succeq cG,
\qquad
\lambda_{\min}(D_h)\ge c\lambda_{\min}(G)=2\lambda_*.
\]
Put \(m=p+1\).  Since \(S\) and \(K\) are bounded on \([-1,1]\), every entry of \(Q_i-\mathbb E_PQ_i\) is bounded by \(C/h\).  The upper marginal-density bound in \(\mathrm{lem:marginal-treatment-positivity}\) also gives
\[
\mathbb E_P[Q_{i,rs}^2]
\le \frac{1}{ch}\int_{-1}^1K(u)^2|S_r(u)S_s(u)|^2\,du
\le \frac Ch.
\]
Apply \(\mathrm{lem:bounded-sum-concentration}\) entrywise at the fixed threshold \(\lambda_*/m\), and use the iid observations on the deterministic fold.  A union bound over the \(m^2\) entries gives
\[
P\{\|\widehat D_h-D_h\|_{\mathrm{op}}>\lambda_*\}
\le2m^2\exp(-c_0Nh).
\]
Weyl's inequality shows that the bad-Gram event is contained in the event on the left.  The deterministic proportional split has \(N\ge\kappa n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Absorbing the finitely many remaining \(n\) into \(C\), and taking \(h_0\le\min(t_0,1-t_0)\), proves the asserted bound.
