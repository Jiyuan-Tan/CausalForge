Condition on the two pilot folds and first replace the empirical \(I_X\)-fold marginalizations by
\[
\widetilde m(a)=P_X\widehat\mu(a,\cdot),
\qquad
\widetilde f(a)=P_X\widehat\pi(a\mid\cdot).
\]
Let \(\widetilde\xi\) denote the corresponding pseudo-outcome.  By \(\mathrm{lem:exact-double-robust-remainder}\),
\[
\mathbb E_P[\widetilde\xi\mid A=a]=\theta_P(a)+B(a).
\]
The population local-polynomial functional applied to \(\theta_P\) differs from \(\theta_P(t_0)\) by at most \(Ch^\alpha\), by \(\mathrm{lem:design-adaptive-local-polynomial-bias}\).  The same proof shows that its equivalent kernel has uniformly bounded \(L_1(f_A)\)-norm, so the contribution of \(B\) is at most \(C\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\).  On the good-Gram event, \(\mathrm{lem:localized-pseudo-outcome-variance}\) bounds the conditional mean-square stochastic error by \(C/(nh)\), and replacement of the population marginalizations by the independent empirical \(I_X\)-fold versions contributes at most \(C/n\).  Clipping cannot increase squared error because \(\theta_P(t_0)\in[-M_Y,M_Y]\).  On the bad-Gram event the squared error is at most \(4M_Y^2\), while \(\mathrm{lem:empirical-local-polynomial-good-gram}\) bounds its probability by \(Ce^{-c_1nh}\).  Consequently,
\[
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2\mid I_\mu,I_{\mathrm{aux}}]
\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\left\{\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\right\}^2+\frac1{nh}+\frac1n+e^{-c_1nh}\right].
\]
The constant-extension lemma gives
\[
\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\le C(U+h^{A_0})(V+h^{B_0}).
\]
Because the pilots use independent folds,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_P[(U+h^{A_0})^2(V+h^{B_0})^2]
&=\mathbb E_P[(U+h^{A_0})^2]\,\mathbb E_P[(V+h^{B_0})^2]\\
&\le C(u+h^{A_0})^2(v+h^{B_0})^2,
\end{aligned}
\]
where the last inequality uses \(\mathrm{lem:separate-slice-pilot-mse}\).  Averaging over the pilot folds and then taking the supremum over the class proves the displayed master-risk inequality.  The tuning restrictions ensure that every preceding binomial and concentration bound is used in its declared regime.
