Let \(\bar\alpha=\alpha\wedge1\), \(\bar\beta=\beta\wedge1\), and \(\bar s=s\wedge1\). Define \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\) as the value of
\[
\max_{u,v,r\ge0}r
\]
subject to
\[
r\le2\bar\alpha u,\qquad
r\le4\bar s v,\qquad
r\le2\bar s v+2\bar\beta u,\qquad
r\le1-u-dv.
\]
Then, for every fixed admissible tuple, there are \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}(\alpha,\beta,s,d)}
\]
for every \(n\ge n_0\). In addition, every admissible separated tuning satisfies
\[
\mathcal M_n\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\{(u_\mu+h^{\alpha\wedge1})(v_\pi+h^{\beta\wedge1})\}^2+(nh)^{-1}+n^{-1}+e^{-c_1nh}\right],
\]
where
\[
u_\mu=\delta_\mu^{s\wedge1}+b_\mu^{\alpha\wedge1}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1/2},
\qquad
v_\pi=\delta_\pi^{s\wedge1}+b_\pi^{\beta\wedge1}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1/2}.
\]
