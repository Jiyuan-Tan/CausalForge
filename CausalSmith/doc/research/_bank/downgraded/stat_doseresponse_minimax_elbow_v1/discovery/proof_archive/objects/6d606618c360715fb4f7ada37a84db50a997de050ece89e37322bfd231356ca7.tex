Assume \((\alpha,\beta,s,d)\in\Omega\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:oracle-region-tuning} supplies deterministic bandwidths and grid widths in Definition~\texttt{def:beta-free-handle} for which Lemma~\texttt{lem:localized-polynomial-master-risk} gives, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)}
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2]
\le C_+^0r_n(\alpha).
\]
Definition~\texttt{def:beta-free-handle} makes \(\widehat\theta\) a Borel measurable function of the sample, so it belongs to \(\mathfrak T_n\). Taking the infimum in Definition~\texttt{def:minimax-mse} proves the upper bound for sufficiently large \(n\). For the finitely many remaining sample sizes, the zero estimator has risk at most \(M_Y^2\); enlarging \(C_+^0\) to a finite constant \(C_+\) extends the upper inequality to every \(n\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:all-beta-oracle-lower-floor} gives a constant \(C_->0\), independent of \(n\), for which
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)\ge C_-r_n(\alpha).
\]
Enlarging \(C_+\) if necessary gives \(C_-\le C_+\), proving the exact order.

For the numerical comparison, suppose \(\beta<\alpha\). Then \(\alpha\wedge\beta=\beta\), and strict monotonicity of \(x\mapsto2x/(2x+1)\) gives
\[
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1}>
\frac{2\beta}{2\beta+1}
=\frac{2(\alpha\wedge\beta)}{2(\alpha\wedge\beta)+1}.
\]
Lemma~\texttt{lem:bonvini-kennedy-treatment-smoothing-bound} records that the number on the right is obtained by balancing \(h^\beta\), the treatment-smoothing component of Bonvini and Kennedy's conditional estimator-risk bound, with its leading variance order \((nh)^{-1}\). The two guarantees are unlike: the present theorem is a uniform minimax statement over \(\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)\), whereas their result is a conditional risk bound for a specified higher-order estimator under its own conditions. The displayed inequality is therefore a numerical contrast; it proves neither a strict tightening of their theorem nor domination of their estimator.
