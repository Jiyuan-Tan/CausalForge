Throughout both residual regions, for every fixed \(u,v>0\) with \(u+dv<1\),
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le \mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le C n^{-R(u,v)},
\]
where, with \(\bar\alpha=\alpha\wedge1\), \(\bar\beta=\beta\wedge1\), and \(\bar s=s\wedge1\),
\[
R(u,v)=\min\{2\bar\alpha u,4\bar s v,2\bar s v+2\bar\beta u,1-u-dv,1\}.
\]
The separate-pilot construction also gives
\[
\mathcal M_n\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\{(u_\mu+h^{\alpha\wedge1})(v_\pi+h^{\beta\wedge1})\}^2+(nh)^{-1}+n^{-1}+e^{-c_1nh}\right],
\]
with
\[
u_\mu=\delta_\mu^{s\wedge1}+b_\mu^{\alpha\wedge1}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1/2},
\qquad
v_\pi=\delta_\pi^{s\wedge1}+b_\pi^{\beta\wedge1}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1/2}.
\]
Thus achievability is explicit and computable, but exact tightness and residual-region \(\beta\)-invariance remain unresolved.
