A univariate Hölder function of order \(q\) is \((q\wedge1)\)-Hölder with controlled constant: this is the defining inequality for \(q\le1\), and follows from the bounded first derivative and the mean-value theorem for \(q>1\). Since the pilots are constant in \(a\), the triangle inequality gives the two norm bounds. Substitution in \(\mathrm{lem:exact\text{-}double\text{-}robust\text{-}remainder}\) gives
\[
\lvert B(a)\rvert\le C(U+Lh^{A_0})(V+Lh^{B_0}),
\]
uniformly on the treatment window.
