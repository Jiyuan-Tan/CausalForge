At \(c=1\), positivity forces \(\pi_P(a\mid x)=1\), and Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-oracle-rate} proves, for every \(\beta,s,d\),
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,1,t_0)
\asymp n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}.
\]
For \(0<c<1\), put
\[
r_0=\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},
\qquad
r_{\mathrm f}=\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s},
\qquad
r_{\mathrm{low}}=\min\{r_0,r_{\mathrm f}\}.
\]
Combining Lemmas~\texttt{lem:all-beta-oracle-lower-floor}, \texttt{lem:extreme-low-s-fuzzy-lower}, and \texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket} gives, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}.
\]
Hence any exact exponent must satisfy
\[
R_{\mathrm{ach}}
\le \rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)
\le r_{\mathrm{low}}.
\]
The new fuzzy floor improves the oracle floor exactly when \(d/s>4+4/\alpha-2/(\alpha\vee\beta)\). Its exponent is independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\le\alpha\), while for \(\beta>\alpha\) it is only a \(\beta\)-dependent lower certificate and does not prove that the minimax exponent depends on \(\beta\). The frozen achieved exponent \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) when \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), and all three achieved exponents \(R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}},R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\); these remain estimator-side facts. At \((\alpha,\beta,d,s)=(1,1,1,1/5)\), the proved achievable exponent is \(1/2\), whereas the unproved population higher-order balance suggests \(8/13\) with \(k\asymp n^{10/13}=o(n)\), so rank singularity does not explain the surviving-band gap.
