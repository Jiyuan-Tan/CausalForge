Let
\[
\psi(u)=
\begin{cases}
\exp\!\left(1-\dfrac{1}{1-u^2}\right),&\lvert u\rvert<1,\\
0,&\lvert u\rvert\ge1,
\end{cases}
\]
so that \(\psi(0)=1\), and let \(C_{\psi,\alpha}\) bound its Hölder-\(\alpha\) norm. Put \(b=M_Y\). Choose fixed \(\lambda,\kappa>0\) so that
\[
\lambda C_{\psi,\alpha}\le L,
\qquad
\lambda\kappa^\alpha\le b/2,
\qquad
\kappa<\min(t_0,1-t_0),
\]
and impose the further smallness condition below. Set \(h_n=\kappa n^{-1/(2\alpha+1)}\). For \(\zeta\in\{-1,1\}\), let \(X\) and \(A\) be independent uniforms and, conditionally on \((A=a,X=x)\), let \(Y\in\{-b,b\}\) with
\[
P_\zeta(Y=b\mid A=a,X=x)
=
\frac{1+\mu_\zeta(a,x)/b}{2},
\qquad
\mu_\zeta(a,x)=\zeta\lambda h_n^\alpha
\psi\!\left(\frac{a-t_0}{h_n}\right).
\]
The choices above make these probabilities valid and put \(\mu_\zeta\) in the required Hölder-\(\alpha\) ball. At \(t_0\) it is constant in \(x\). The designated densities \(p_X=1\) and \(\pi_{P_\zeta}=1\) satisfy positivity and every finite Hölder order. Thus both laws belong to the model and
\[
\theta_{P_\zeta}(t_0)=\zeta\lambda h_n^\alpha.
\]
Both outcome probabilities under \(P_-\) are at least \(1/4\), whence
\[
\chi^2(P_+,P_-)
\le
\frac{8}{b^2}\int_0^1\mu_+(a,x)^2\,da
\le
C_0\frac{\lambda^2}{b^2}h_n^{2\alpha+1},
\qquad
C_0=8\int\psi(u)^2du.
\]
Independence gives
\[
1+\chi^2(P_+^n,P_-^n)
\le
\exp\!\left(C_0\lambda^2\kappa^{2\alpha+1}/b^2\right).
\]
Choose \(\kappa\) small enough that the right side is at most \(5/4\). Then \(\operatorname{TV}(P_+^n,P_-^n)\le\tfrac12\sqrt{\chi^2(P_+^n,P_-^n)}\le1/4\). For any estimator \(T\), use the test \(\phi=\mathbf1\{T\ge0\}\). The squared error is at least \(\lambda^2h_n^{2\alpha}\) on \(\{\phi=0\}\) under \(P_+^n\) and on \(\{\phi=1\}\) under \(P_-^n\). Therefore
\[
\max_{\zeta\in\{-1,1\}}
\mathbb E_{P_\zeta}[(T-\theta_{P_\zeta}(t_0))^2]
\ge
\frac{\lambda^2h_n^{2\alpha}}2
\{1-\operatorname{TV}(P_+^n,P_-^n)\}
\ge
\frac{3\lambda^2\kappa^{2\alpha}}8
n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}.
\]
Taking the infimum over all Borel estimators proves the claim.
