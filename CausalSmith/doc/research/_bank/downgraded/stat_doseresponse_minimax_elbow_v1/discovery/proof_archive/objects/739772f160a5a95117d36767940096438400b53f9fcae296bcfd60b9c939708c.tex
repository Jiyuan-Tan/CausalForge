Let \(\bar\alpha=\alpha\wedge1\), \(\bar\beta=\beta\wedge1\), and \(\bar s=s\wedge1\). If \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\) is the value of \(\max_{u,v,r\ge0}r\) subject to \(r\le2\bar\alpha u\), \(r\le4\bar s v\), \(r\le2\bar s v+2\bar\beta u\), and \(r\le1-u-dv\), then for every fixed admissible tuple there are constants \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\), independent of \(n\), such that \[C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}(\alpha,\beta,s,d)}\] for every \(n\ge n_0\).
