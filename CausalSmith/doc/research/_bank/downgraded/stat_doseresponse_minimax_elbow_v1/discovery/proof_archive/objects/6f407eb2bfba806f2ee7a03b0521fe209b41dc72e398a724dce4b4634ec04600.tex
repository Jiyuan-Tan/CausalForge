Lemma~\texttt{lem:optimized-histogram-rate-bracket} proves, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le \mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}},
\]
where
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=
\left[
1+\frac{1}{2(\alpha\wedge1)}+\frac{d}{4(s\wedge1)}
+\left\{\frac12-\frac{\beta\wedge1}{\alpha\wedge1}\right\}_+
\min\left\{\frac{1}{2(\beta\wedge1)},\frac{d}{2(s\wedge1)}\right\}
\right]^{-1}.
\]
This upper exponent is independent of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), but it is strictly smaller than \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\) for every admissible tuple, so it does not resolve the exact deficient frontier.
