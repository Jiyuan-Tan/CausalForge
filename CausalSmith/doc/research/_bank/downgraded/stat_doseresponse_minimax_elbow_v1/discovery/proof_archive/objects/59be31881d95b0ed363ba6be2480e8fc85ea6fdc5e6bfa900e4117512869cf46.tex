Let \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), and \(S_0=s\wedge1\), and let \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) be the value of
\[
\max_{u,v,r\ge0}r
\]
subject to
\[
r\le2A_0u,\qquad r\le4S_0v,\qquad r\le2S_0v+2B_0u,\qquad r\le1-u-dv.
\]
Then, for every fixed admissible tuple, there are constants \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}(\alpha,\beta,s,d)}
\]
for every \(n\ge n_0\). In addition, every admissible separated tuning satisfies the explicit master envelope
\[
\mathcal M_n\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\{(u_\mu+h^{\alpha\wedge1})(v_\pi+h^{\beta\wedge1})\}^2+(nh)^{-1}+n^{-1}+e^{-c_1nh}\right],
\]
where
\[
u_\mu=\delta_\mu^{s\wedge1}+b_\mu^{\alpha\wedge1}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1/2},
\qquad
v_\pi=\delta_\pi^{s\wedge1}+b_\pi^{\beta\wedge1}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1/2}.
\]
