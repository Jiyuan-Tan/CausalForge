The frozen H\"older-seminorm convention and the bounded first derivative when the order exceeds one imply, uniformly in \(x\),
\[
|\mu_P(a,x)-\mu_P(t_0,x)|\le L|a-t_0|^{\bar\alpha},
\qquad
|\pi_P(a\mid x)-\pi_P(t_0\mid x)|\le L|a-t_0|^{\bar\beta}.
\]
These are increment bounds, not zeroth-order supremum bounds. Because both fitted pilots are constant in \(a\), Minkowski's inequality gives, whenever \(|a-t_0|\le h\),
\[
\|v_a\|_2\le U+Lh^{\bar\alpha},
\qquad
\|e_a\|_2\le V+Lh^{\bar\beta}.
\]
Applying Lemma~\texttt{lem:exact-double-robust-remainder} and expanding the product yields
\[
|B(a)|\le C(c)\|v_a\|_2\|e_a\|_2
\le C\{UV+Uh^{\bar\beta}+Vh^{\bar\alpha}+h^{\bar\alpha+\bar\beta}\}.
\]
Taking the supremum over the local window proves the claim.
