Put \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), \(S_0=s\wedge1\), \(E_\mu=(2+A_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1}\), and \(E_\pi=(2+B_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1}\). Define
\[
R_{\mathrm{sep}}=\max_{0\le\gamma\le1}\min\left\{2\alpha\gamma,1-\gamma,2\left[\min\{A_0\gamma,E_\mu\}+\min\{B_0\gamma,E_\pi\}\right]\right\},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=\left[1+\frac1{2A_0}+\frac d{4S_0}+\left(\frac12-\frac{B_0}{A_0}\right)_+\min\left\{\frac1{2B_0},\frac d{2S_0}\right\}\right]^{-1},
\qquad R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\}.
\]
Then, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}.
\]
The separated-pilot exponent equals \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\) exactly on \(\Omega\), while the histogram exponent is always strictly smaller; hence the bracket remains strict in both residual regions. The regressogram exponent is \(\beta\)-free when \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), and the full achievable exponent \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) is \(\beta\)-free for \(\beta\ge1\), but exact residual minimax \(\beta\)-invariance is not proved.
