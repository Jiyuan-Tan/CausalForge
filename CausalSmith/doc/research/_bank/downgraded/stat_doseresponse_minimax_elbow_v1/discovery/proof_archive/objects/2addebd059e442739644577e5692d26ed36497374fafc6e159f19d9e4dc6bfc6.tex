For all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}},
\]
where
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=\left[1+\frac{1}{2(\alpha\wedge1)}+\frac{d}{4(s\wedge1)}+\left\{\frac12-\frac{\beta\wedge1}{\alpha\wedge1}\right\}_+\min\left\{\frac{1}{2(\beta\wedge1)},\frac{d}{2(s\wedge1)}\right\}\right]^{-1}.
\]
This achievable exponent is independent of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), but \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}<2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\), so the exact deficient-region exponent remains unresolved.
