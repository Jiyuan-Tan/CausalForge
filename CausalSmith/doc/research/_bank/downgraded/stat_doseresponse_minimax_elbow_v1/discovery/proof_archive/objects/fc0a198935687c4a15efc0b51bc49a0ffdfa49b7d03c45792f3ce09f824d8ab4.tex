Put \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), and \(S_0=s\wedge1\), and define
\[
E_\mu=(2+A_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1},\qquad E_\pi=(2+B_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{sep}}=\max_{0\le\gamma\le1}\min\left\{2\alpha\gamma,1-\gamma,2\left[\min\{A_0\gamma,E_\mu\}+\min\{B_0\gamma,E_\pi\}\right]\right\},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=\left[1+\frac1{2A_0}+\frac d{4S_0}+\left(\frac12-\frac{B_0}{A_0}\right)_+\min\left\{\frac1{2B_0},\frac d{2S_0}\right\}\right]^{-1},
\qquad R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\},
\]
and
\[
r_{\mathrm{low}}=\min\left\{\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}\right\}.
\]
For every fixed residual tuple and \(0<c<1\), the frozen achievability and converse lemmas give constants \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}
\qquad(n\ge n_0).
\]
Consequently \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\), and any exact polynomial exponent, if it exists, must obey
\[
R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\le r_{\mathrm{low}}.
\]
On every tuple for which the two explicit endpoints coincide, the exact exponent exists and equals their common value. At \(c=1\), separately, Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-oracle-rate} gives the exact exponent \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\) for all \(\beta,s,d\); this is a degenerate boundary result rather than a resolution for fixed \(0<c<1\).
