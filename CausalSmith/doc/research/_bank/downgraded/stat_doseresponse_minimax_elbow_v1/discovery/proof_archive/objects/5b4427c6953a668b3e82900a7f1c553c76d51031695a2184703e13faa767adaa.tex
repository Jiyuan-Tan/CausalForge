A univariate Hölder function of order \(q\) in the frozen ball is \((q\wedge1)\)-Hölder with a constant controlled by its radius: this is the defining remainder inequality for \(q\le1\), and follows from the bounded first derivative and the mean-value theorem for \(q>1\). Since the pilot is constant in \(a\), the triangle inequality gives
\[
\lVert v_a\rVert_2
\le\lVert\widetilde\mu(t_0,\cdot)-\mu_P(t_0,\cdot)\rVert_2
+\lVert\mu_P(t_0,\cdot)-\mu_P(a,\cdot)\rVert_2
\le U+Lh^{\bar\alpha}.
\]
The same argument gives \(\lVert e_a\rVert_2\le V+Lh^{\bar\beta}\). Substitution into the exact remainder bound yields
\[
\lvert B(a)\rvert
\le C(U+Lh^{\bar\alpha})(V+Lh^{\bar\beta}),
\]
and expansion proves the display. The last term is structural: even if \(U=V=0\), the off-slice product of the two slice discrepancies need not vanish.
