For a regression-pilot cube \(B_{\mu j}\), put \(q_j=P_X(B_{\mu j})\) and \(Q_j=P\{X\in B_{\mu j},\lvert A-t_0\rvert\le b_\mu\}\). Positivity and the interior restriction give
\[
2cb_\mu q_j\le Q_j\le2b_\mu q_j/c.
\]
The slice Hölder condition bounds within-cell variation by \(C\delta_\mu^{S_0}\), and treatment-direction Hölder continuity bounds window bias by \(Cb_\mu^{A_0}\). If \(R_j\sim\operatorname{Bin}(N_\mu,Q_j)\) is the selected count, then
\[
\mathbb E[(R_j+1)^{-1}]=\frac{1-(1-Q_j)^{N_\mu+1}}{(N_\mu+1)Q_j}\le\frac1{(N_\mu+1)Q_j},
\]
and \(R_j^{-1}\mathbf1\{R_j>0\}\le2/(R_j+1)\). Bounded outcomes therefore make the integrated nonempty-cell variance at most \(C/(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)\). Empty cells have the same order because there are at most \(C\delta_\mu^{-d}\) cells and \(q_j e^{-2cN_\mu b_\mu q_j}\le C/(nb_\mu)\). Thus
\[
\mathbb E_PU^2\le C\{\delta_\mu^{2S_0}+b_\mu^{2A_0}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1}\}\le Cu^2.
\]
For the density pilot, condition on the covariates in a cube. Its window count is a sum of Bernoulli variables with probabilities between \(2cb_\pi\) and \(2b_\pi/c\), so division by \(2b_\pi(N_{\pi j}\vee1)\) has conditional variance at most \(C/[b_\pi(N_{\pi j}\vee1)]\). The same reciprocal-binomial calculation and empty-cell bound give \(C/(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)\). The bias is at most \(C(\delta_\pi^{S_0}+b_\pi^{B_0})\). Clipping cannot increase error because the target lies in \([c,1/c]\subset[c/2,2/c]\). Hence
\[
\mathbb E_PV^2\le C\{\delta_\pi^{2S_0}+b_\pi^{2B_0}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1}\}\le Cv^2.
\]
No lower density bound for \(p_X\) is used.
