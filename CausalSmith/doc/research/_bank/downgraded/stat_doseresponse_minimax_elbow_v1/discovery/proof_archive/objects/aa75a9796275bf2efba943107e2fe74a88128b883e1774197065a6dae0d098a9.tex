The counts, clipping, the eigenvalue indicator, inversion on the good-Gram set, and the assigned value on its complement make the estimator in \(\mathrm{def:beta-free-handle}\) Borel measurable.  Hence it is an element of \(\mathfrak T_n\).  For every admissible tuning tuple,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal M_n
&=\inf_{\widehat\theta\in\mathfrak T_n}\sup_{P\in\mathcal P}
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2]\\
&\le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P}
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta_{h,b_\mu,\delta_\mu,b_\pi,\delta_\pi}-\theta_P(t_0))^2].
\end{aligned}
\]
Applying \(\mathrm{lem:beta-free-master-risk}\) to the right-hand side gives the asserted envelope.
