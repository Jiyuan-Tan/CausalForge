The regression pilot, and hence \(U\), is a Borel function only of observations in \(I_\mu\). The density pilot, and hence \(V\), is a Borel function only of observations in the disjoint fold \(I_{\mathrm{aux}}\). Under iid sampling, the sigma-fields generated by these two deterministic disjoint folds are independent. Thus \(U\) and \(V\) are independent and
\[
\mathbb E_P[U^2V^2]=\mathbb E_P[U^2]\mathbb E_P[V^2].
\]
Applying the two second-moment inequalities in \(\mathrm{lem:separate\text{-}slice\text{-}pilot\text{-}mse}\) proves the stated bound. No fourth-moment assertion is used.
