For every residual tuple, the proved bracket is
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le \mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}},
\]
where
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=
\left[1+\frac1{2(\alpha\wedge1)}+\frac d{4(s\wedge1)}+
\left\{\frac12-\frac{\beta\wedge1}{\alpha\wedge1}\right\}_+
\min\left\{\frac1{2(\beta\wedge1)},\frac d{2(s\wedge1)}\right\}\right]^{-1}.
\]
For the infeasible population projection, the approximation and second-order variance terms are \(O(k^{-4s/d})\) and \(O\{k/(nh)^2\}\), respectively, and their formal balance reaches the oracle floor when \(s\ge d/4\). The unresolved step is a uniform data-driven realization under no regularity assumption on \(p_X\), not the population bias--variance algebra.
