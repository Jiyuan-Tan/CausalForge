Write \(m_\mu=|I_\mu|\) and \(m_\pi=|I_{\mathrm{aux}}|\). By the deterministic fold construction in Definition~\texttt{def:beta-free-handle}, there are constants \(0<a_-\le a_+<\infty\), independent of \(n\), such that \(a_-n\le m_\mu,m_\pi\le a_+n\). Put \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), and \(S_0=s\wedge1\).

We first analyze the outcome pilot. For an outcome-pilot cell \(B_{\mu j}\), define
\[
p_j=P_X(B_{\mu j}),\qquad q_j=P\{X\in B_{\mu j},\ |A-t_0|\le b_\mu\}.
\]
Because \(b_\mu\le\min(t_0,1-t_0)\), the treatment window has length \(2b_\mu\). The two positivity bounds in \(\mathrm{def:holder-class}\) therefore give
\[
2b_\mu c p_j\le q_j\le \frac{2b_\mu}{c}p_j.
\]
Cells with \(p_j=0\) make no contribution to the \(L_2(P_X)\) risk. If \(p_j>0\), let
\[
\eta_j=\mathbb E_P[Y\mid X\in B_{\mu j},\ |A-t_0|\le b_\mu].
\]
For \(x,x'\in B_{\mu j}\) and \(|a-t_0|\le b_\mu\), the treatment-direction and fixed-slice covariate-direction Hölder conditions imply
\[
|\mu_P(a,x')-\mu_P(t_0,x)|
\le |\mu_P(a,x')-\mu_P(t_0,x')|
   +|\mu_P(t_0,x')-\mu_P(t_0,x)|
\le C\{b_\mu^{A_0}+\delta_\mu^{S_0}\}.
\]
Averaging this inequality under the conditional law defining \(\eta_j\) yields, uniformly for \(x\in B_{\mu j}\),
\[
|\eta_j-\mu_P(t_0,x)|\le C\{b_\mu^{A_0}+\delta_\mu^{S_0}\}.
\]
Let \(N_j\) be the number of observations in \(I_\mu\) falling in the cell-window event. Then \(N_j\sim\operatorname{Bin}(m_\mu,q_j)\). Conditional on \(N_j=k>0\), the pilot is the average of \(k\) conditionally iid outcomes with mean \(\eta_j\), and its conditional variance is at most \(M_Y^2/k\). Moreover, for any \(N\sim\operatorname{Bin}(m,q)\),
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\frac{\mathbf 1\{N>0\}}{N}\right]
\le 2\mathbb E\!\left[\frac1{N+1}\right]
=\frac{2\{1-(1-q)^{m+1}\}}{(m+1)q}
\le\frac{2}{(m+1)q},
\]
and \(P(N=0)=(1-q)^m\le e^{-mq}\). Combining these bounds with the bounded squared-error bound on the event \(N_j=0\), and using the trivial bounded bound when \(m_\mu q_j<1\), gives
\[
P(N_j=0)+\mathbb E\!\left[\frac{\mathbf 1\{N_j>0\}}{N_j}\right]
\le C\min\left\{1,\frac1{m_\mu q_j}\right\}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\int_{B_{\mu j}}\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\mu_0(x)-\mu_P(t_0,x))^2]\,dP_X(x)
\le Cp_j\{b_\mu^{2A_0}+\delta_\mu^{2S_0}\}
 +Cp_j\min\left\{1,\frac1{m_\mu b_\mu p_j}\right\}.
\]
The regular grid in \(\mathrm{def:beta-free-handle}\) has \(J_\mu\le C_d'\delta_\mu^{-d}\) cells. With the summand interpreted as zero when \(p_j=0\),
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{J_\mu}p_j\min\left\{1,\frac1{m_\mu b_\mu p_j}\right\}
=
\sum_{j=1}^{J_\mu}\min\left\{p_j,\frac1{m_\mu b_\mu}\right\}
\le \min\left\{\sum_jp_j,\frac{J_\mu}{m_\mu b_\mu}\right\}
\le \frac{C}{nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d}.
\]
Summing over the cells proves
\[
\mathbb E_PU^2
\le C\left\{\delta_\mu^{2S_0}+b_\mu^{2A_0}+\frac1{nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d}\right\}
\le C\left\{\delta_\mu^{S_0}+b_\mu^{A_0}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1/2}\right\}^2
=Cu^2.
\]

We next analyze the density pilot. Define the window-averaged conditional density
\[
r_b(x)=\frac1{2b_\pi}\int_{t_0-b_\pi}^{t_0+b_\pi}\pi_P(a\mid x)\,da.
\]
For \(x,x'\) in the same density-pilot cell, the two relevant Hölder conditions give
\[
|r_b(x')-\pi_P(t_0\mid x)|
\le C\{b_\pi^{B_0}+\delta_\pi^{S_0}\}.
\]
Fix such a cell, write \(p_j=P_X(B_{\pi j})\), and let \(N_{\pi j}\sim\operatorname{Bin}(m_\pi,p_j)\) be its covariate count. Conditional on \(N_{\pi j}=k>0\) and on the \(k\) covariates in the cell, the unclipped ratio \(R_{\pi j}/(2b_\pi k)\) has mean equal to the average of their \(r_b\)-values. Since each treatment-window probability is at most \(2b_\pi/c\), its conditional variance is at most
\[
\frac1{4b_\pi^2k^2}\sum_{\ell=1}^k\frac{2b_\pi}{c}
\le\frac{C}{b_\pi k}.
\]
The target \(\pi_P(t_0\mid x)\) lies in \([c,1/c]\subset[c/2,2/c]\), so projection onto \([c/2,2/c]\) is a contraction relative to that target. Both the clipped estimator and the target are uniformly bounded. Thus the conditional squared error is bounded by
\[
C\{b_\pi^{2B_0}+\delta_\pi^{2S_0}\}
\quad +C\min\left\{1,\frac1{b_\pi k}\right\}
\]
when \(k>0\), and by a constant when \(k=0\). The preceding binomial identity, considered separately according as \(m_\pi b_\pi p_j<1\) or \(m_\pi b_\pi p_j\ge1\), therefore yields
\[
P(N_{\pi j}=0)
\quad +\mathbb E\!\left[\mathbf 1\{N_{\pi j}>0\}
\min\left\{1,\frac1{b_\pi N_{\pi j}}\right\}\right]
\le C\min\left\{1,\frac1{m_\pi b_\pi p_j}\right\}.
\]
It follows that
\[
\int_{B_{\pi j}}\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\pi_0(x)-\pi_P(t_0\mid x))^2]\,dP_X(x)
\le Cp_j\{b_\pi^{2B_0}+\delta_\pi^{2S_0}\}
 +Cp_j\min\left\{1,\frac1{m_\pi b_\pi p_j}\right\}.
\]
Since the independent density grid has \(J_\pi\le C_d'\delta_\pi^{-d}\), the same summation argument, using only \(\sum_jp_j=1\), gives
\[
\mathbb E_PV^2
\le C\left\{\delta_\pi^{2S_0}+b_\pi^{2B_0}+\frac1{nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d}\right\}
\le C\left\{\delta_\pi^{S_0}+b_\pi^{B_0}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1/2}\right\}^2
=Cv^2.
\]
All constants are uniform in \(P\) and \(n\), depending only on the frozen class constants, the fixed fold proportions, and the regular-grid constants. Taking \(C_0\) large enough to meet the stated effective-cell conditions completes the proof. No lower bound or smoothness condition on \(p_X\), and no off-slice covariate smoothness, has been used.
