Let \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), and \(S_0=s\wedge1\). By Lemma~\texttt{lem:oracle-region-tuning}, membership of \((\alpha,\beta,s,d)\) in \(\Omega\) supplies deterministic choices of the five bandwidths in Definition~\texttt{def:beta-free-handle} for which Lemma~\texttt{lem:localized-polynomial-master-risk} gives
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)}\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2]\le C r_n(\alpha).
\]
The estimator is one Borel measurable, fully data-driven member of \(\mathfrak T_n\), so taking the infimum over \(\mathfrak T_n\) proves the minimax upper bound for all sufficiently large \(n\). For the finitely many smaller sample sizes, the zero estimator has risk at most \(M_Y^2\), and enlarging \(C_+\) extends the upper inequality to every \(n\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:all-beta-oracle-lower-floor} proves the matching lower bound over the identical class.

It remains only to compare exponents. If \(\beta<\alpha\), then \(\alpha\wedge\beta=\beta\), and strict monotonicity of \(x\mapsto2x/(2x+1)\) on \((0,\infty)\) gives
\[
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1}>\frac{2(\alpha\wedge\beta)}{2(\alpha\wedge\beta)+1}.
\]
Lemma~\texttt{lem:bonvini-kennedy-treatment-smoothing-bound} identifies the latter as the exponent obtained by balancing the treatment-smoothing term in that conditional-risk bound with \((nh)^{-1}\). Our statement concerns the minimax risk over the present class, whereas that lemma records an estimator-specific conditional bound, so the strict comparison is not a contradiction.
