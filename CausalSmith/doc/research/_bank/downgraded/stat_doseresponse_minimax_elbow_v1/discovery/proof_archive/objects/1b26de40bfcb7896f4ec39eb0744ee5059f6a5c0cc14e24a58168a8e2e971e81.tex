Fix an admissible parameter tuple and consider the law \(P_0\) in \(\mathrm{def:nonempty-witness}\).  The uniform law of \(X\) has the designated Borel density \(p_X(x)=1\), and the conditional uniform law of \(A\) given \(X\) has the jointly Borel density \(\pi_{P_0}(a\mid x)=1\).  Thus \(p_X\le pbar\), because \(pbar\ge1\), and \(c\le1\le1/c\), because \(0<c\le1\).  Since \(Y=0\), the displayed function \(\mu_{P_0}(a,x)=0\) is a jointly Borel version of \(\mathbb E_{P_0}[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\), it takes values in \([-M_Y,M_Y]\), and \(|Y|\le M_Y\) almost surely.  Every positive-order derivative and every Hölder remainder of the constant functions \(0\) and \(1\) vanishes.  Hence the treatment-direction and fixed-slice covariate-direction Hölder conditions hold for every positive radius \(L\), under the frozen convention in \(\mathrm{def:holder-class}\).  The displayed potential outcomes also obey \(Y(a)=0\), although causal assumptions are not part of membership in the statistical class.  All defining member properties in \(\mathrm{def:holder-class}\) are therefore satisfied, so \(P_0\in\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)\).  This proves nonemptiness.
