Write \(N=|I_{\mathrm{lp}}|\), \(Q_i=K_h(A_i)S_h(A_i)S_h(A_i)^\top\), and
\[
G=\int_{-1}^1K(u)S(u)S(u)^\top\,du.
\]
The population calculation in Lemma~\texttt{lem:design-adaptive-local-polynomial-bias} gives
\[
D_h=\mathbb E_PQ_i\succeq cG,
\qquad
\lambda_{\min}(D_h)\ge c\lambda_{\min}(G)=2\lambda_*.
\]
Put \(m=\lfloor\alpha\rfloor+1\). Since \(S\) and \(K\) are bounded on \([-1,1]\), each entry of \(Q_i-\mathbb E_PQ_i\) is bounded by \(C/h\). The upper marginal-density bound from Lemma~\texttt{lem:marginal-treatment-positivity} also gives
\[
\mathbb E_P[Q_{i,rs}^2]
\le \frac{1}{ch}\int_{-1}^1K(u)^2|S_r(u)S_s(u)|^2\,du
\le \frac Ch.
\]
Conditional on the deterministic fold, the \(Q_i\) are iid by Assumption~\texttt{ass:iid}. Apply Lemma~\texttt{lem:bounded-sum-concentration} entrywise to the centered sums at average threshold \(\lambda_*/m\). A union bound over the \(m^2\) entries, together with \(\|H\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le m\max_{r,s}|H_{rs}|\), yields
\[
P\{\|\widehat D_h-D_h\|_{\mathrm{op}}>\lambda_*\}
\le2m^2\exp(-c_0Nh).
\]
Weyl's inequality and \(\lambda_{\min}(D_h)\ge2\lambda_*\) imply that the bad-Gram event is contained in the event on the left. The proportional split gives \(N\ge\kappa n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\). Enlarging the leading constant to cover the finitely many remaining \(n\), and choosing \(h_0\le\min(t_0,1-t_0)\), proves the claim.
