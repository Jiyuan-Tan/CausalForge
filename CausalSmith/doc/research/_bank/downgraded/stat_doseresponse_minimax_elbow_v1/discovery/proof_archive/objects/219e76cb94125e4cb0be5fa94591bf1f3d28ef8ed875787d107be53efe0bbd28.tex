Use the regular-grid cardinality condition in the corrected \(\mathrm{def:beta-free-handle}\), and write \(m_\mu=|I_\mu|\asymp n\) and \(m_\pi=|I_{\mathrm{aux}}|\asymp n\).  For an outcome-pilot cell \(B_j\), put \(p_j=P_X(B_j)\) and
\[
q_j=P\{X\in B_j,\ |A-t_0|\le b_\mu\}.
\]
Two-sided positivity yields \(2b_\mu c p_j\le q_j\le2b_\mu p_j/c\).  If \(p_j>0\), let \(\eta_j=\mathbb E_P[Y\mid X\in B_j,|A-t_0|\le b_\mu]\).  For \(x\in B_j\), treatment-direction smoothness and fixed-slice covariate smoothness give
\[
|\eta_j-\mu_P(t_0,x)|\le C\{b_\mu^{A_0}+\delta_\mu^{S_0}\}.
\]
Conditional on a positive cell-window count \(N_j=k\), the pilot is an average of \(k\) bounded outcomes, so its conditional variance about \(\eta_j\) is at most \(M_Y^2/k\).  For a binomial variable,
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\frac{\mathbf1\{N_j>0\}}{N_j}\right]
\le\frac{2}{(m_\mu+1)q_j},
\qquad
P(N_j=0)\le e^{-m_\mu q_j}.
\]
Combining these bounds with the trivial bounded-error estimate when \(m_\mu q_j<1\) gives
\[
\int_{B_j}\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\mu_0(x)-\mu_P(t_0,x))^2]\,dP_X(x)
\le Cp_j\{b_\mu^{2A_0}+\delta_\mu^{2S_0}\}
+Cp_j\min\!\left\{1,\frac1{m_\mu b_\mu p_j}\right\}.
\]
There are at most \(C\delta_\mu^{-d}\) cells, so summing gives
\[
\mathbb E_PU^2\le C\left\{\delta_\mu^{2S_0}+b_\mu^{2A_0}+\frac1{nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d}\right\}\le Cu^2.
\]
For the density pilot, define
\[
r_b(x)=\frac1{2b_\pi}\int_{t_0-b_\pi}^{t_0+b_\pi}\pi_P(a\mid x)\,da.
\]
For \(x,x'\) in one cell, \(|r_b(x')-\pi_P(t_0\mid x)|\le C\{b_\pi^{B_0}+\delta_\pi^{S_0}\}\).  Conditional on \(N_{\pi j}=k>0\) and the \(k\) covariates in the cell, the un-clipped estimator has mean \(k^{-1}\sum_{\ell=1}^kr_b(X_\ell)\) and variance at most \(C/(b_\pi k)\), because each window probability is at most \(2b_\pi/c\).  Projection onto \([c/2,2/c]\) is a contraction relative to the target \(\pi_P(t_0\mid x)\in[c,1/c]\).  Repeating the preceding binomial and cell-summation argument, now with at most \(C\delta_\pi^{-d}\) cells, yields
\[
\mathbb E_PV^2\le C\left\{\delta_\pi^{2S_0}+b_\pi^{2B_0}+\frac1{nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d}\right\}\le Cv^2.
\]
No lower bound on the cell probabilities or on \(p_X\) is used.
