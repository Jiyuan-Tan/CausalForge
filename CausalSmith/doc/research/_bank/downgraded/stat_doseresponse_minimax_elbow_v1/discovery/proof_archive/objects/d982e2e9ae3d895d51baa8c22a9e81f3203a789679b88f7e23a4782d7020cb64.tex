The random variable \(U\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(Z_i:i\in I_\mu)\), whereas \(V\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(Z_i:i\in I_{\mathrm{aux}})\).  These sigma-fields are independent by iid sampling because the folds are disjoint and deterministic.  The population functions and norms occurring in \(U\) and \(V\) are nonrandom.  Hence \(U\) and \(V\) are independent, and \(\mathrm{lem:separate-slice-pilot-mse}\) gives
\[
\mathbb E_P[U^2V^2]=\mathbb E_P[U^2]\mathbb E_P[V^2]\le Cu^2v^2.
\]
