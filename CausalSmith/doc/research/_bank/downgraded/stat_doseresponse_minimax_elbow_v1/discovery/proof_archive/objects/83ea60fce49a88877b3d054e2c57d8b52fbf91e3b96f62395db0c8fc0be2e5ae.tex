The designated density \(p_X\) is nonnegative and integrates to one. Integrating the pointwise inequalities \(c\le\pi_P(a\mid x)\le1/c\) against \(p_X(x)\,dx\) therefore gives
\[
c=c\int p_X(x)\,dx\le f_A(a)\le\frac1c\int p_X(x)\,dx=\frac1c.
\]
This argument uses only two-sided treatment positivity.
