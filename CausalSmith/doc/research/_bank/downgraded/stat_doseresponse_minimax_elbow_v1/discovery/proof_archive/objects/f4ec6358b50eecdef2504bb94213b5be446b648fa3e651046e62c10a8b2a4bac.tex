For every residual tuple and fixed \(0<c<1\), the established results give an explicit bracket
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}},
\]
where
\[
r_{\mathrm{low}}=
\min\left\{
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},
\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}
\right\}
\]
and \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) is the explicit maximum of the separated-pilot and histogram exponents in Lemma~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket}. Thus any exact exponent, if it exists, must lie between \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) and \(r_{\mathrm{low}}\), and it is identified on the endpoint-equality subregions. Separately, at the explicitly degenerate boundary corner \(c=1\), Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-oracle-rate} gives the exact exponent \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\) for every \(\beta,s,d\); this does not resolve the fixed-interior-overlap question.
