Put \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), \(S_0=s\wedge1\),
\[
E_\mu=\left(2+A_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1}\right)^{-1},
\qquad
E_\pi=\left(2+B_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1}\right)^{-1},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{sep}}=
\max_{0\le\gamma\le1}
\min\left\{2\alpha\gamma,1-\gamma,
2\left[\min\{A_0\gamma,E_\mu\}+\min\{B_0\gamma,E_\pi\}\right]\right\},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=
\left[1+\frac1{2A_0}+\frac d{4S_0}
+\left(\frac12-\frac{B_0}{A_0}\right)_+
\min\left\{\frac1{2B_0},\frac d{2S_0}\right\}
\right]^{-1},
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\},
\]
and
\[
r_{\mathrm{low}}=
\min\left\{
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},
\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}
\right\}.
\]
For every admissible tuple in either residual region and every fixed \(0<c<1\), Lemmas~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket} and~\texttt{lem:extreme-low-s-fuzzy-lower} give constants \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\in\mathbb N\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}
\qquad(n\ge n_0).
\]
Necessarily \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\). If an exact exponent \(\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\) exists, then
\[
R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\le r_{\mathrm{low}}.
\]
At every tuple where the two displayed endpoints agree, the exact rate follows with \(\rho=R_{\mathrm{ach}}=r_{\mathrm{low}}\). The fuzzy converse improves the oracle floor exactly when
\[
\frac ds>4+\frac4\alpha-\frac{2}{\alpha\vee\beta}.
\]
The exponent \(r_{\mathrm{low}}\) is independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\le\alpha\). The exponent \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sep}}\), \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\), and \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\). These one-sided invariance facts establish minimax \(\beta\)-invariance only at tuples where the bracket closes. In addition, Lemma~\texttt{lem:population-piecewise-polynomial-projection} proves the population higher-order remainder bound \(Ck^{-2s/d}\) and kernel second moment bound \(C_{d,s}k\), while Lemma~\texttt{lem:prescribed-low-s-projection-rank-obstruction} proves that the prescribed full-rank empirical projection is singular for all sufficiently large \(n\) when \(s<\alpha d/\{4(\alpha+1)\}\), without excluding stabilized alternatives.
