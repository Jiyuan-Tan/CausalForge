Fix an admissible parameter tuple and consider \(P_0\) from Definition~\texttt{def:nonempty-witness}. The uniform law of \(X\) has the designated Borel density \(p_X(x)=1\), and the conditional uniform law of \(A\) given \(X\) has the jointly Borel density \(\pi_{P_0}(a\mid x)=1\). Since \(0<c\le1\), one has \(c\le1\le1/c\). Because \(Y=0\), the function \(\mu_{P_0}(a,x)=0\) is a jointly Borel version of \(\mathbb E_{P_0}[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\), takes values in \([-M_Y,M_Y]\), and satisfies \(\lvert Y\rvert\le M_Y\) almost surely. Every positive-order derivative and every Hölder remainder of the constant functions zero and one vanishes. Thus all treatment-direction and fixed-slice covariate-direction Hölder conditions hold for every positive radius \(L\) under the convention in Definition~\texttt{def:holder-class}. Consequently every member property in that definition is satisfied, so
\[
P_0\in\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0).
\]
The class, and hence the supremum in Definition~\texttt{def:minimax-mse}, is nonempty.
