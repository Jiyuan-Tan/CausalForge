The estimator in \(\mathrm{def:beta\text{-}free\text{-}handle}\) is one Borel measurable, data-driven member of \(\mathfrak T_n\); it does not depend on the unknown law. The uniform risk inequality in \(\mathrm{lem:beta\text{-}free\text{-}master\text{-}risk}\) therefore bounds the supremum risk of this single admissible estimator. By the definition of \(\mathcal M_n\) as the infimum of those supremum risks, the same right-hand side is an upper bound for \(\mathcal M_n\). The independent-pilot product step is inherited through that lemma and no fourth-moment argument is introduced.
