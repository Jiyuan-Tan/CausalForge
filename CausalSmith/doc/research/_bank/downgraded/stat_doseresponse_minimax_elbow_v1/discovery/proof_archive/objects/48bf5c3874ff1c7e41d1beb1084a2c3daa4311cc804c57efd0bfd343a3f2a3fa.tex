Let \(P_0\) be the displayed witness. Its \(X\)-marginal is Lebesgue measure on \([0,1]^d\), so the designated density \(p_X(x)=1\) is Borel, integrates to one, and satisfies \(p_X(x)\le pbar\) because \(pbar\ge1\). Conditional on \(X=x\), the treatment is uniform, so \(\pi_{P_0}(a\mid x)=1\) is a jointly Borel conditional density. Since \(0<c\le1\), \[c\le1\le 1/c.\] The outcome is identically zero, hence \(\mu_{P_0}(a,x)=0\) is a jointly Borel version of \(\mathbb E_{P_0}[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\), takes values in \([-M_Y,M_Y]\), and \(\lvert Y\rvert\le M_Y\) almost surely. Every derivative and every Hölder remainder of the two constant functions \(a\mapsto0\) and \(a\mapsto1\) is zero; the same is true of the constant slices \(x\mapsto0\) and \(x\mapsto1\). Thus all four Hölder conditions hold for every positive order and radius under the convention in \(\mathrm{def:holder\text{-}class}\). All version, boundedness, positivity, density-envelope, and smoothness member properties in that definition are therefore satisfied. Hence \(P_0\in\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)\), proving that the class is nonempty.
