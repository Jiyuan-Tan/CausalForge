For \(0<\lambda<3/R\), the power-series inequality
\[
e^x\le1+x+\frac{x^2}{2(1-\lvert x\rvert/3)}
\]
and centering give \(\mathbb E e^{\lambda W_i}\le\exp\{\lambda^2\mathbb E[W_i^2]/[2(1-\lambda R/3)]\}\). Independence and exponential Markov inequality yield the upper tail. Choosing \(\lambda=t/(V+Rt/3)\), repeating for \(-W_i\), and adding the tails proves the display.
