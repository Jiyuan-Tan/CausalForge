Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-minimal-class-oracle-rate} is stated over the larger class \(\mathcal Q_1(\alpha,d,M_Y,L)\), which imposes no covariate-direction H\"older condition and no treatment-density smoothness condition. That lemma proves that every frozen class at \(c=1\) is contained in \(\mathcal Q_1(\alpha,d,M_Y,L)\). Therefore Definition~\texttt{def:minimax-mse} and the upper half of that lemma give constants \(C_+<\infty\) and \(n_0\in\mathbb N\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,1,t_0)
\le C_+r_n(\alpha)
\qquad(n\ge n_0).
\]
Lemma~\texttt{lem:all-beta-oracle-lower-floor}, applied at \(c=1\), gives a constant \(C_->0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,1,t_0)
\ge C_-r_n(\alpha)
\qquad(n\ge1).
\]
Enlarging \(C_+\) if necessary ensures \(C_-\le C_+\). Definition~\texttt{def:oracle-rate} gives \(r_n(\alpha)=n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\), proving the displayed exact rate for every \(\beta,s,d\). The proof does not use membership in \(\Omega\). The strict-containment assertion in Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-minimal-class-oracle-rate} also verifies the requested minimal-hypothesis point: the same boundary rate holds over a class strictly broader than the frozen one, without \(x\mapsto\mu_P(t_0,x)\) being H\"older.
