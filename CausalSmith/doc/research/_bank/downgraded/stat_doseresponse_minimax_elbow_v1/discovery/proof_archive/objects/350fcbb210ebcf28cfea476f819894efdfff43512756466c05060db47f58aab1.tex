Write \(f_A(a)=P_X\pi_P(a\mid\cdot)\). Positivity gives \(c\le f_A(a)\le1/c\). Hence, after \(a=t_0+hu\),
\[
D_h=\int K(u)S(u)S(u)^\top f_A(t_0+hu)\,du\succeq c\int K(u)S(u)S(u)^\top\,du.
\]
The fixed matrix on the right is positive definite because \(K\) is positive on an interval and a nonzero polynomial cannot vanish there. Thus \(D_h^{-1}\) is uniformly bounded. If \(q(a)=\gamma^\top S_h(a)\), direct multiplication by \(D_h^{-1}\) gives exact reproduction. Integrating the uniform treatment-direction Hölder inequalities for \(\mu_P\) against \(P_X\) shows that \(\theta_P(a)=P_X\mu_P(a,\cdot)\) is Hölder-\(\alpha\). Let \(T_{t_0}\) be its admissible Taylor polynomial. Polynomial reproduction cancels \(T_{t_0}\), while \(\int\lvert W_h(a)\rvert f_A(a)\,da\le C\), so
\[
\left\lvert\int W_h(a)\theta_P(a)f_A(a)\,da-\theta_P(t_0)\right\rvert
\le Ch^\alpha.
\]
The oracle pseudo-outcome has conditional mean \(\theta_P(a)\) because its residual term has conditional mean zero given \((A,X)\), completing the proof.
