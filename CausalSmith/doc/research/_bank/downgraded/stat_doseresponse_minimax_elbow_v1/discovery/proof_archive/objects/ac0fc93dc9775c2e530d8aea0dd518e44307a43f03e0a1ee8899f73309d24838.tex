Fix a class member. By the design-adaptive bias lemma, the oracle population local-polynomial intercept differs from \(\theta_P(t_0)\) by at most \(Ch^\alpha\). For the pilot pseudo-outcome, the exact remainder and constant-extension lemmas give, conditionally on the pilots,
\[
\sup_{\lvert a-t_0\rvert\le h}\lvert B(a)\rvert
\le C(U+h^{A_0})(V+h^{B_0}).
\]
Squaring and expanding, then using \(\mathbb EU^2\le Cu^2\), \(\mathbb EV^2\le Cv^2\), and the independent-fold identity \(\mathbb E[U^2V^2]\le Cu^2v^2\), yields
\[
\mathbb E\sup_{\lvert a-t_0\rvert\le h}\lvert B(a)\rvert^2
\le C\{(u+h^{A_0})(v+h^{B_0})\}^2.
\]
This is the step at which pilot independence is essential; no fourth moment is used.

On the good-Gram event, empirical polynomial reproduction removes the admissible Taylor polynomial exactly. The Taylor remainder contributes \(Ch^{2\alpha}\), the centered pseudo-outcome term contributes \(C/(nh)\), and empirical marginalization contributes \(C/n\), by the localized-variance lemma. The good-Gram complement has probability at most \(Ce^{-c_1nh}\). Both the final clipped estimator and the target lie in \([-M_Y,M_Y]\), so its risk contribution is at most \(Ce^{-c_1nh}\). Clipping is nonexpansive relative to the target. Combining these bounds and taking the supremum over the class proves the display, with constants uniform in \(P\).
