Fix \(P\) in the declared class and condition on the two pilot folds. First replace the empirical \(I_X\)-fold marginalizations by their population counterparts
\[
\widetilde m(a)=P_X\widehat\mu(a,\cdot),
\qquad
\widetilde f(a)=P_X\widehat\pi(a\mid\cdot),
\]
and let \(\widetilde\xi\) be the resulting pseudo-outcome. By \(\mathrm{lem:exact\text{-}double\text{-}robust\text{-}remainder}\),
\[
\mathbb E_P[\widetilde\xi\mid A=a]=\theta_P(a)+B(a).
\]
The population local-polynomial functional applied to \(\theta_P\) differs from \(\theta_P(t_0)\) by at most \(Ch^\alpha\), by \(\mathrm{lem:design\text{-}adaptive\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial\text{-}bias}\). The equivalent-kernel calculation in that lemma also gives a uniformly bounded \(L_1(f_A)\)-norm, so the contribution of \(B\) is bounded by
\[
C\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|.
\]
On the good-Gram event, \(\mathrm{lem:localized\text{-}pseudo\text{-}outcome\text{-}variance}\) bounds the conditional mean-square stochastic error by \(C/(nh)\), and replacing the population marginalizations by the independent empirical \(I_X\)-fold marginalizations contributes at most \(C/n\). Clipping cannot increase squared error because \(\theta_P(t_0)\in[-M_Y,M_Y]\). On the bad-Gram event the squared error is at most \(4M_Y^2\), while \(\mathrm{lem:empirical\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial\text{-}good\text{-}gram}\) bounds its probability by \(Ce^{-c_1nh}\). Consequently,
\[
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2\mid I_\mu,I_{\mathrm{aux}}]
\le C\left[
h^{2\alpha}+
\left\{\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\right\}^2+
\frac1{nh}+\frac1n+e^{-c_1nh}
\right].
\]
Put
\[
U=\|\widehat\mu_0-\mu_P(t_0,\cdot)\|_{L_2(P_X)},
\qquad
V=\|\widehat\pi_0-\pi_P(t_0\mid\cdot)\|_{L_2(P_X)}.
\]
The constant-extension bound in \(\mathrm{lem:constant\text{-}extension\text{-}off\text{-}slice\text{-}control}\) gives
\[
\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|
\le C(U+h^{A_0})(V+h^{B_0}).
\]
Since the two pilots are trained on independent folds, \(\mathrm{lem:independent\text{-}pilot\text{-}product}\) and Cauchy--Schwarz give exact factorization followed by
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_P[(U+h^{A_0})^2(V+h^{B_0})^2]
&=
\mathbb E_P[(U+h^{A_0})^2]\,
\mathbb E_P[(V+h^{B_0})^2]\\
&\le C(u+h^{A_0})^2(v+h^{B_0})^2,
\end{aligned}
\]
where \(\mathrm{lem:separate\text{-}slice\text{-}pilot\text{-}mse}\) bounds the two second moments. Averaging over the pilot folds and taking the supremum over \(P\) proves the displayed master-risk inequality. The restrictions \(nh\ge C_0\), \(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d\ge C_0\), and \(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d\ge C_0\) place every binomial and concentration estimate in its stated regime. All constants depend only on the fixed model, kernel, grid, and fold-proportion constants, never on \(n\).
