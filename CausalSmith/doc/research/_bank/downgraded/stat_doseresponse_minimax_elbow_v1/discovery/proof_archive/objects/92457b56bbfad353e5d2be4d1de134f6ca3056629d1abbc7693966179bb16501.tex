For every admissible residual tuple and fixed \(0<c<1\), there exist positive constants \(C_{\mathrm{low}},C_{\mathrm{up}}\) and \(n_0\), independent of \(n\), such that for all \(n\ge n_0\), \[ C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}\le \mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}. \] Consequently, any exact polynomial exponent must satisfy \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\). The fuzzy converse strictly improves the oracle floor exactly when \[ \frac ds>4+\frac4\alpha-\frac{2}{\alpha\vee\beta}. \] Its exponent, and hence \(r_{\mathrm{low}}\), is independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\le\alpha\). On the achievable side, \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sep}}\), \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\), and \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\). These are rigorous endpoint facts, but they do not imply minimax \(\beta\)-invariance unless the bracket is matched.
