Fix \(0<\beta<\beta^{\prime}<\infty\) and a parameter tuple for which the rough member satisfies \((\alpha,\beta,s,d)\in\Omega\). By \(\mathrm{thm:oracle\text{-}high\text{-}s}\), there are \(C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\), neither depending on \(n\), such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)},\qquad n\ge n_0.
\]
The one-sided class-monotonicity lemma applies because \(\beta<\beta^{\prime}\), and therefore
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0).
\]
The all-beta lower-floor lemma, applied to the smoother class indexed by \(\beta^{\prime}\), supplies \(C_{\mathrm{low}}>0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\ge C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}.
\]
Combining the three displays proves
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}.
\]
In particular, the lower bound makes the denominator below positive and division gives
\[
1\le
\frac{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
\le \frac{C_{\mathrm{up}}}{C_{\mathrm{low}}}.
\]
The upper bound is deliberately proved on the larger, rougher class, whereas the lower floor is applied to the smoother class. Thus no oracle-region condition on \(\beta^{\prime}\) is needed. The constants may depend on the fixed pair \((\beta,\beta^{\prime})\) and the remaining fixed class parameters, but not on \(n\); neither uniformity over a continuum of smoothness orders nor convergence of the displayed ratio to one is asserted.
