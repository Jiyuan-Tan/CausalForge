For every admissible residual tuple and fixed \(0<c<1\), Lemmas~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket} and~\texttt{lem:extreme-low-s-fuzzy-lower} imply constants \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\in\mathbb N\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}
\qquad(n\ge n_0),
\]
where
\[
r_{\mathrm{low}}
=
\min\left\{
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},
\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}
\right\}.
\]
Necessarily \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\). If an exact exponent \(\rho\) exists, then \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\); wherever the endpoints agree, the exact rate is proved with \(\rho=R_{\mathrm{ach}}=r_{\mathrm{low}}\). The fuzzy converse improves the oracle floor exactly when \(d/s>4+4/\alpha-2/(\alpha\vee\beta)\). The corrected Bernstein constant \(3/104\) changes none of these exponents or regions. The lower exponent is independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\le\alpha\); \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) when \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\); and all displayed achievable exponents are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\). These one-sided facts prove minimax \(\beta\)-invariance only where the bracket closes. As a boundary check, Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-minimal-class-oracle-rate} proves the oracle exponent at \(c=1\) over a class strictly broader than the frozen class and requiring no covariate-direction H\"older restriction.
