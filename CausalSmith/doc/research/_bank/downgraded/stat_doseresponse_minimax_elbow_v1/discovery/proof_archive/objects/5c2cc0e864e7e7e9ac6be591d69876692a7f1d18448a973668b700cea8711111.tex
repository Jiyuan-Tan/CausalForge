The variable \(U\) is measurable with respect to the observations in \(I_\mu\), whereas \(V\) is measurable with respect to the observations in the disjoint deterministic fold \(I_{\mathrm{aux}}\). Iid sampling makes these two sigma-fields independent. Therefore
\[
\mathbb E_P[U^2V^2]=\mathbb E_P[U^2]\mathbb E_P[V^2]\le Cu^2v^2,
\]
where the last inequality uses \(\mathrm{lem:separate\text{-}slice\text{-}pilot\text{-}mse}\).
