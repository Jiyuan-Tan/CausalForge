For all sufficiently large \(n\), the proved bracket is \[C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}},\] where \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\}\) is the explicit exponent in Lemma~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket}; hence any exact exponent must satisfy \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\le2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\). The regressogram exponent is independent of \(\beta\) when \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), and all displayed achievable exponents are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\), but these are estimator-side facts rather than minimax invariance. At \((\alpha,\beta,d,s)=(1,1,1,1/5)\), \(R_{\mathrm{HOIF}}=8/13\), strictly above the proved \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}=1/2\), with \(k\asymp n^{10/13}=o(n)\); this surviving-band gap is not a rank impossibility.
