Fix \(P\in\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)\) and condition on the two pilot folds. Replace the empirical \(I_X\)-fold marginalizations by
\[
\widetilde m(a)=P_X\widehat\mu(a,\cdot),
\qquad
\widetilde f(a)=P_X\widehat\pi(a\mid\cdot),
\]
and let \(\widetilde\xi\) be the resulting pseudo-outcome. Lemma~\texttt{lem:exact-double-robust-remainder} gives
\[
\mathbb E_P[\widetilde\xi\mid A=a]=\theta_P(a)+B(a).
\]
Lemma~\texttt{lem:design-adaptive-local-polynomial-bias} bounds the population local-polynomial bias contributed by \(\theta_P\) by \(Ch^\alpha\). Its proof also gives a uniformly bounded equivalent-kernel \(L_1(f_A)\)-norm, so the contribution of \(B\) is at most \(C\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:localized-pseudo-outcome-variance} contributes \(C/(nh)+C/n\) on the good-Gram event. Clipping cannot increase loss because \(\theta_P(t_0)\in[-M_Y,M_Y]\), and Lemma~\texttt{lem:empirical-local-polynomial-good-gram} bounds the complementary contribution by \(Ce^{-c_1nh}\). Therefore
\[
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2\mid I_\mu,I_{\mathrm{aux}}]
\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\left\{\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\right\}^2+\frac1{nh}+\frac1n+e^{-c_1nh}\right].
\]
Let
\[
U=\|\widehat\mu_0-\mu_P(t_0,\cdot)\|_{L_2(P_X)},
\qquad
V=\|\widehat\pi_0-\pi_P(t_0\mid\cdot)\|_{L_2(P_X)}.
\]
Lemma~\texttt{lem:constant-extension-off-slice-control} gives
\[
\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|
\le C(U+h^{A_0})(V+h^{B_0}).
\]
Because the pilots use independent folds, Lemmas~\texttt{lem:independent-pilot-product} and~\texttt{lem:separate-slice-pilot-mse}, together with Cauchy--Schwarz, imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_P[(U+h^{A_0})^2(V+h^{B_0})^2]
&=\mathbb E_P[(U+h^{A_0})^2]\,\mathbb E_P[(V+h^{B_0})^2]\\
&\le C(u+h^{A_0})^2(v+h^{B_0})^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Averaging over the pilot folds and taking the supremum over \(P\) proves the displayed risk bound. The stated count, bandwidth, and interior-window restrictions are exactly those required by the constituent lemmas, and all their constants are uniform over the frozen model class.
