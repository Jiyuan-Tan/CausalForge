Write
\[
G_h=\{\lambda_{\min}(\widehat D_h)\ge\lambda_*\},
\qquad N=|I_{\mathrm{lp}}|,
\]
and condition on \(I_\mu,I_{\mathrm{aux}}\). Put \(\bar f=P_X\widehat\pi_0\), \(\bar m=P_X\widehat\mu_0\), and
\[
\bar\xi_i=
\frac{\bar f}{\widehat\pi_0(X_i)}\{Y_i-\widehat\mu_0(X_i)\}+\bar m.
\]
The clipping in Definition~\texttt{def:beta-free-handle} makes \(\bar\xi_i\) uniformly bounded. Define
\[
D_h=\mathbb E_P[K_h(A)S_h(A)S_h(A)^\top],
\qquad
\gamma_h=D_h^{-1}\mathbb E_P[K_h(A)S_h(A)\bar\xi].
\]
The preceding design lemma gives \(\|D_h^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}+\|\gamma_h\|\le C\). Exact normal-equation algebra yields, on \(G_h\),
\[
\widehat\gamma_h-\gamma_h
=
\widehat D_h^{-1}\frac1N\sum_{i\in I_{\mathrm{lp}}}
K_h(A_i)S_h(A_i)\{\bar\xi_i-S_h(A_i)^\top\gamma_h\}.
\]
The summands are conditionally iid and centered, with second moments bounded by \(C/h\); also \(\|\widehat D_h^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le1/\lambda_*\) on \(G_h\). Therefore
\[
\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\mathbf1_{G_h}\lvert e_0^\top(\widehat\gamma_h-\gamma_h)\rvert^2
\,\middle|\,I_\mu,I_{\mathrm{aux}}\right]
\le\frac{C}{Nh}\le\frac{C}{nh}.
\]
For empirical marginalizations, set
\[
\Delta_m=P_{I_X}\widehat\mu_0-P_X\widehat\mu_0,
\quad
\Delta_f=P_{I_X}\widehat\pi_0-P_X\widehat\pi_0,
\quad
q_i=\frac{Y_i-\widehat\mu_0(X_i)}{\widehat\pi_0(X_i)}.
\]
Conditional independence and boundedness give \(\mathbb E_P(\Delta_m^2+\Delta_f^2\mid I_\mu,I_{\mathrm{aux}})\le C/n\). Since \(S_h(a)^\top e_0=1\),
\[
e_0^\top(\widehat\gamma_h^{\mathrm{emp}}-\widehat\gamma_h^{\mathrm{pop}})
=
\Delta_m+\Delta_fT_h,
\qquad
T_h=e_0^\top\widehat D_h^{-1}P_{I_{\mathrm{lp}}}(K_hS_hq).
\]
Let \(J=\sum_{i\in I_{\mathrm{lp}}}\mathbf1\{K_h(A_i)>0\}\). If \(\lfloor\alpha\rfloor=0\), \(T_h\) is a kernel-weighted average of bounded \(q_i\). Otherwise, invertibility on \(G_h\) implies \(J\ge\lfloor\alpha\rfloor+1\ge2\) and
\[
\mathbf1_{G_h}|T_h|\le C\mathbf1_{\{J\ge2\}}J/(Nh).
\]
When \(Nh\le1\), the binomial factorial moment \(\mathbb E[J(J-1)]\le C(Nh)^2\) bounds the truncated second moment of \(T_h\). When \(Nh>1\), centering about \(D_h^{-1}\mathbb E_P[K_hS_hq]\) and repeating the preceding variance calculation gives the same uniform bound. Independence of \(I_X\) and \(I_{\mathrm{lp}}\) now yields
\[
\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\mathbf1_{G_h}\lvert e_0^\top(\widehat\gamma_h^{\mathrm{emp}}-
\widehat\gamma_h^{\mathrm{pop}})\rvert^2\right]
\le C/n.
\]
This proves both assertions.
