Put \(A=A_0\), \(B=B_0\), \(S=S_0\), \(C=2\alpha+1\), \(z=z_*\), and \(q=\alpha-z\). Feasibility in Definition~\texttt{def:oracle-threshold} gives \(0\le z\le A\), \(0\le q\le B\), and \(z+q=\alpha\). Choose the implicit constants in the five tuning relations small enough that the fixed upper restrictions in Lemma~\texttt{lem:beta-free-master-risk} hold. Under the displayed powers,
\[
\delta_\mu^S\asymp b_\mu^A\asymp n^{-kz},
\qquad
\delta_\pi^S\asymp b_\pi^B\asymp n^{-kq}.
\]
By Definition~\texttt{def:oracle-region}, \(S\ge g(\alpha,\beta,d)\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:separated-threshold-optimization} and Definition~\texttt{def:oracle-threshold} imply
\[
S\ge \frac{dz}{C-z/A-2z},
\qquad
S\ge \frac{dq}{C-q/B-2q},
\]
with positive denominators. The first inequality is equivalent to
\[
1-kz/A-kdz/S\ge2kz,
\]
and hence
\[
(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1/2}
\asymp n^{-\{1-kz/A-kdz/S\}/2}
\le Cn^{-kz}.
\]
The same calculation with \(q\) gives
\[
(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1/2}\le Cn^{-kq}.
\]
Thus \(u=O(n^{-kz})\) and \(v=O(n^{-kq})\). Since \(z\le A\) and \(q\le B\),
\[
h^A=O(n^{-kz}),
\qquad
h^B=O(n^{-kq}),
\]
and therefore
\[
\{(u+h^A)(v+h^B)\}^2
=O(n^{-2k(z+q)})=O(n^{-2k\alpha}).
\]
Moreover,
\[
h^{2\alpha}\asymp n^{-2k\alpha},
\qquad
(nh)^{-1}\asymp n^{-(1-k)}=n^{-2k\alpha},
\]
while \(n^{-1}=O(n^{-2k\alpha})\) because \(2k\alpha<1\), and \(e^{-c_1nh}=o(n^{-2k\alpha})\) because \(nh\asymp n^{1-k}\to\infty\).

It remains only to check the cell-count restrictions. If \(z>0\), the preceding exponent inequality gives \(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d\gtrsim n^{2kz}\to\infty\); if \(z=0\), its exact exponent is \(1-kz/A-kdz/S=1\), so it also diverges. The identical argument applies to the density count, separating the cases \(q>0\) and \(q=0\). Hence all restrictions hold for sufficiently large \(n\), and substitution into Lemma~\texttt{lem:localized-polynomial-master-risk} proves the claimed order.
