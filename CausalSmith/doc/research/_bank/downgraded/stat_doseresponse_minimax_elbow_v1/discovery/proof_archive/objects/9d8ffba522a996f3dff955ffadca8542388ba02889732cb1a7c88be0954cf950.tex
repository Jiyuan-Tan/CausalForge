Let \(0<\beta<\beta^{\prime}<\infty\).  On the unit interval, the frozen componentwise Hölder convention gives the constant-one embedding of the order-\(\beta^{\prime}\) ball into the order-\(\beta\) ball.  Indeed, when the integer parts agree, \(|a-a'|^{\beta^{\prime}-\lfloor\beta\rfloor}\le |a-a'|^{\beta-\lfloor\beta\rfloor}\); when the integer parts differ, repeated mean-value inequalities and the bounded higher derivatives give the required lower-order modulus, again because \(|a-a'|\le1\).  Thus every designated \(a\mapsto\pi_P(a\mid x)\) satisfying \(\mathrm{ass:pi-a-holder}\) at order \(\beta^{\prime}\) also satisfies it at order \(\beta\), with the same radius.  All other conditions in \(\mathrm{def:holder-class}\) are identical.  This proves
\[
\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\subseteq
\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0).
\]
For any fixed estimator, taking the supremum of its squared risk over the larger class cannot decrease the result.  Taking the infimum over the common decision class \(\mathfrak T_n\), as in \(\mathrm{def:minimax-mse}\), preserves the inequality and gives the asserted minimax monotonicity.
