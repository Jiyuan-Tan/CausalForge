Lemma~\texttt{lem:beta-class-one-sided-monotonicity} gives the class inclusion at orders \(\beta^{\prime}>\beta\), and hence, for every \(n\),
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0).
\]
Because \((\alpha,\beta,s,d)\in\Omega\), Theorem~\texttt{thm:oracle-high-s} applied only at the rougher order \(\beta\) supplies constants \(C_{\mathrm{up}}^0<\infty\) and \(n_0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}^0r_n(\alpha)
\qquad(n\ge n_0).
\]
Independently, Lemma~\texttt{lem:all-beta-oracle-lower-floor}, applied at order \(\beta^{\prime}\), supplies \(C_{\mathrm{low}}>0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\ge C_{\mathrm{low}}r_n(\alpha)
\qquad(n\ge1).
\]
Set \(C_{\mathrm{up}}=\max\{C_{\mathrm{up}}^0,C_{\mathrm{low}}\}\). Substituting \(r_n(\alpha)=n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\) gives the asserted three inequalities. The first one makes the denominator below positive, so division yields
\[
1\le
\frac{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)}
{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)}
\le\frac{C_{\mathrm{up}}}{C_{\mathrm{low}}}.
\]
The upper theorem was used only for \(\beta\); at \(\beta^{\prime}\) only the all-order lower floor was used. Thus no oracle-region condition on \(\beta^{\prime}\) is required.
