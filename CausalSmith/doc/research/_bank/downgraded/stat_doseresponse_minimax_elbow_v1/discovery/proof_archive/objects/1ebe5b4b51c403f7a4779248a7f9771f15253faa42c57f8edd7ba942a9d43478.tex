The marginal treatment density is \(f_a=P_X\pi_{P,a}\), so positivity gives \(c\le f_a\le1/c\). Bayes' formula and \(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=x]=\mu_P(a,x)\) yield
\[
\mathbb E_P[\widetilde\xi\mid A=a]
=-\frac{\widetilde f_a}{f_a}P_X\!\left[\frac{v_a\pi_{P,a}}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right]+P_X\mu_{P,a}+P_Xv_a.
\]
Since \(\widetilde f_a=f_a+\bar e_a\) and
\[
P_Xv_a-P_X\!\left[\frac{v_a\pi_{P,a}}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right]
=P_X\!\left[\frac{v_ae_a}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right],
\]
the identity follows. The inequalities \(\widetilde\pi_a\ge c/2\), \(f_a\ge c\), and \(\pi_{P,a}\le1/c\), followed by Cauchy--Schwarz, bound the two terms by \((2/c)\lVert v_a\rVert_2\lVert e_a\rVert_2\) and \((2/c^3)\lVert v_a\rVert_2\lVert e_a\rVert_2\), respectively.
