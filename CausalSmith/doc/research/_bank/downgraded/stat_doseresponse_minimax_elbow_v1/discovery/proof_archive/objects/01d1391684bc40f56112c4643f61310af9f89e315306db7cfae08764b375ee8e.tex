Fix an admissible parameter tuple and consider the law \(P_0\) in Definition~\texttt{def:nonempty-witness}. The uniform law of \(X\) has the designated Borel density \(p_X(x)=1\), and the conditional uniform law of \(A\) given \(X\) has the jointly Borel density \(\pi_{P_0}(a\mid x)=1\). Because \(0<c\le 1\), the two positivity inequalities \(c\le1\le1/c\) hold. Since \(Y=0\), the displayed function \(\mu_{P_0}(a,x)=0\) is a jointly Borel version of \(\mathbb E_{P_0}[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\), takes values in \([-M_Y,M_Y]\), and satisfies \(\lvert Y\rvert\le M_Y\) almost surely. Every positive-order derivative and every Hölder remainder of the constant functions zero and one vanishes. Hence the treatment-direction and fixed-slice covariate-direction Hölder conditions hold for every positive radius \(L\), under the convention in Definition~\texttt{def:holder-class}. The displayed potential outcomes also obey \(Y(a)=0\), although causal assumptions are not member properties of the statistical class. Thus every member property in Definition~\texttt{def:holder-class} is satisfied, and
\[
P_0\in\mathcal P(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0).
\]
The class, and therefore the supremum in the minimax functional, is nonempty.
