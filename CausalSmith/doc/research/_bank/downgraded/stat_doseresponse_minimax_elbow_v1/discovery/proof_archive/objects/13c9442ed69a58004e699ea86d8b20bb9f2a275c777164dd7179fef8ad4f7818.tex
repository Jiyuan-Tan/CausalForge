Fix \(0<\beta<\beta^{\prime}<\infty\) and suppose that the rough tuple \((\alpha,\beta,s,d)\) belongs to \(\Omega\). The restricted oracle-rate theorem \(\mathrm{thm:oracle\text{-}high\text{-}s}\), applied to the class with exponent \(\beta\), supplies a constant \(C_{\mathrm{up}}(\beta)<\infty\), independent of \(n\), such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}(\beta)n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}.
\]
The one-sided class-monotonicity lemma applies because \(\beta<\beta^{\prime}\), and gives
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\ge
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0).
\]
Finally, the all-beta oracle lower-floor lemma applies to the smoother class with exponent \(\beta^{\prime}\). Thus there is \(C_{\mathrm{low}}>0\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\ge C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}.
\]
Combining the three displays yields
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le \mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le \mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}(\beta)n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}.
\]
In particular, division by the positive lower bound for the smoother risk gives
\[
1\le
\frac{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
\le \frac{C_{\mathrm{up}}(\beta)}{C_{\mathrm{low}}}.
\]
All constants may depend on the fixed parameter pair and on the remaining fixed class constants, but not on \(n\). No condition on \(\beta^{\prime}\) beyond \(\beta^{\prime}>\beta\) is needed: the upper bound was proved on the larger, rougher class, while the lower floor holds for every finite smoothness order.
