Let \(N=|I_{\mathrm{lp}}|\), \(Q_i=K_h(A_i)S_h(A_i)S_h(A_i)^\top\), and \(G=\int K(u)S(u)S(u)^\top du\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:design-adaptive-local-polynomial-bias} gives
\[
D_h=\mathbb E_PQ_i\succeq cG,
\qquad
\lambda_{\min}(D_h)\ge c\lambda_{\min}(G)=2\lambda_*.
\]
Put \(m=\lfloor\alpha\rfloor+1\). Boundedness and compact support of \(K\) and \(S\) imply that every entry of \(Q_i-\mathbb E_PQ_i\) is bounded by \(C/h\), while \(f_A\le1/c\) gives \(\mathbb E_P[Q_{i,rs}^2]\le C/h\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:bounded-sum-concentration}, applied entrywise at average threshold \(\lambda_*/m\), and a union bound give
\[
P\{\|\widehat D_h-D_h\|_{\mathrm{op}}>\lambda_*\}
\le2m^2\exp(-c_0Nh).
\]
Weyl's inequality shows that the bad-Gram event is contained in this event. The fold size satisfies \(N\asymp n\); adjustment of the leading constant for finitely many small \(n\) proves the claim.
