Writing \(\bar\alpha=\alpha\wedge1\), \(\bar\beta=\beta\wedge1\), and \(\bar s=s\wedge1\), the proved lemma \(\mathrm{lem:deficient\text{-}explicit\text{-}rate\text{-}bracket\) gives \[C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}(\alpha,\beta,s,d)},\] where \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is the value of the explicit three-variable linear program in that lemma. Independently, the separated-pilot construction gives \[\mathcal M_n\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\{(u_\mu+h^{\alpha\wedge1})(v_\pi+h^{\beta\wedge1})\}^2+(nh)^{-1}+n^{-1}+e^{-c_1nh}\right],\] with \(u_\mu=\delta_\mu^{s\wedge1}+b_\mu^{\alpha\wedge1}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1/2}\) and \(v_\pi=\delta_\pi^{s\wedge1}+b_\pi^{\beta\wedge1}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1/2}\). Thus the achievable side is explicit and computable throughout the residual regions, while exact tightness and beta-invariance there remain open.
