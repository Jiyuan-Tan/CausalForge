By \(\mathrm{lem:beta\text{-}class\text{-}one\text{-}sided\text{-}monotonicity}\),
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0).
\]
The hypothesis \((\alpha,\beta,s,d)\in\Omega\) permits application of \(\mathrm{thm:oracle\text{-}high\text{-}s}\) to the rough class, and therefore there is an \(n\)-independent constant \(C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}r_n(\alpha).
\]
Independently, \(\mathrm{lem:all\text{-}beta\text{-}oracle\text{-}lower\text{-}floor}\), applied at the smoother order \(\beta^{\prime}\), gives an \(n\)-independent constant \(C_{\mathrm{low}}>0\) such that
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\ge C_{\mathrm{low}}r_n(\alpha).
\]
Substitution of \(r_n(\alpha)=n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\) from \(\mathrm{def:oracle\text{-}rate}\) proves the displayed chain, after taking \(n_0\) large enough for the rough-class oracle upper bound. The lower bound makes the denominator in the claimed ratio strictly positive. Dividing the chain by the smoother-class minimax risk gives
\[
1\le
\frac{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
\le \frac{C_{\mathrm{up}}}{C_{\mathrm{low}}}.
\]
Only the all-order lower-floor lemma, rather than the oracle upper theorem, was applied at \(\beta^{\prime}\). Hence no membership condition in \(\Omega\) is imposed on the smoother tuple.
