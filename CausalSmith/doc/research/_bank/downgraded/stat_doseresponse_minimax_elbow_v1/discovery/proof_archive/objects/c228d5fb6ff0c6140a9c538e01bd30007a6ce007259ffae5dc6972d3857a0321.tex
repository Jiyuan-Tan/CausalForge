Let \(E_\mu=(2+A_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1}\) and \(E_\pi=(2+B_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1}\), and define
+\[
+R_{\mathrm{sep}}=
+\max_{0\le\gamma\le1}
+\min\left\{
+2\alpha\gamma,
+1-\gamma,
+2\left[\min\{A_0\gamma,E_\mu\}+\min\{B_0\gamma,E_\pi\}\right]
+\right\},
+\]
+\[
+R_{\mathrm{hist}}=
+\left[
+1+\frac1{2A_0}+\frac d{4S_0}
++\left(\frac12-\frac{B_0}{A_0}\right)_+
+\min\left\{\frac1{2B_0},\frac d{2S_0}\right\}
+\right]^{-1},
+\qquad
+R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\}.
+\]
+Lemma~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket} establishes constants independent of \(n\) for which
+\[
+C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
+\le
+\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
+\le
+C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}
+\]
+for all sufficiently large \(n\). In either residual region, \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}<2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\). Hence any exact polynomial exponent must lie in the interval
+\[
+R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)
+\le\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1}.
+\]
+The regressogram exponent \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) when \(B_0\ge A_0/2\), and \(R_{\mathrm{sep}}\), \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\), and \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\) are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\). These are estimator-side invariance statements, not a proof of residual minimax \(\beta\)-invariance.
