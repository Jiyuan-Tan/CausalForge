The population Gram is at least
\[
c\int_{-1}^1K(u)S(u)S(u)^\top\,du,
\]
whose least eigenvalue is \(2\lambda_*\). Each empirical summand has operator norm at most \(C/h\) and second operator moment at most \(C/h\). Apply \(\mathrm{lem:bounded\text{-}sum\text{-}concentration}\) to quadratic forms on a fixed \(1/4\)-net of the unit sphere in dimension \(p+1\). A union bound and the standard net inequality yield
\[
P\{\lVert\widehat D_h-D_h\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}>\lambda_*\}\le Ce^{-c_1nh}.
\]
Weyl's inequality gives the claimed good-Gram event.
