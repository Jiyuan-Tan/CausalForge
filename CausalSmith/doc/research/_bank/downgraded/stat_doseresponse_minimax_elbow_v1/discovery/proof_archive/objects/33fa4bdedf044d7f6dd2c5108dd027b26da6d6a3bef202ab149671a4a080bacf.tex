The marginal treatment density is \(f_a=P_X\pi_{P,a}\), so Lemma~\texttt{lem:marginal-treatment-positivity} gives \(c\le f_a\le1/c\). Bayes' formula and \(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=x]=\mu_P(a,x)\) yield
\[
\mathbb E_P[\widetilde\xi\mid A=a]
=-\frac{\widetilde f_a}{f_a}P_X\!\left[\frac{v_a\pi_{P,a}}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right]+P_X\mu_{P,a}+P_Xv_a.
\]
Since \(\widetilde f_a=f_a+\bar e_a\) and
\[
P_Xv_a-P_X\!\left[\frac{v_a\pi_{P,a}}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right]
=P_X\!\left[v_a\left(1-\frac{\pi_{P,a}}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right)\right]
=P_X\!\left[\frac{v_ae_a}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right],
\]
the asserted identity follows exactly. For the bound, \(\widetilde\pi_a\ge c/2\), \(f_a\ge c\), and \(\pi_{P,a}\le1/c\). Cauchy--Schwarz therefore gives
\[
\left|P_X\!\left[\frac{v_ae_a}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right]\right|
\le\frac2c\|v_a\|_2\|e_a\|_2
\]
and
\[
\left|\frac{\bar e_a}{f_a}P_X\!\left[\frac{v_a\pi_{P,a}}{\widetilde\pi_a}\right]\right|
\le\frac{2}{c^3}\|e_a\|_2\|v_a\|_2.
\]
Adding these inequalities proves the conclusion.
