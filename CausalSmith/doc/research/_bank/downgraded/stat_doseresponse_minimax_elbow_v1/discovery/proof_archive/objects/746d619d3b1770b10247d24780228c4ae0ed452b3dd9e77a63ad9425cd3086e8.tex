The random variable \(U\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(Z_i:i\in I_\mu)\), whereas \(V\) is measurable with respect to \(\sigma(Z_i:i\in I_{\mathrm{aux}})\). Because the folds are deterministic and disjoint, these sigma-fields are independent under \(\mathrm{ass:iid}\). The population functions and norms occurring in \(U\) and \(V\) are nonrandom. Hence \(U\) and \(V\) are independent, and \(\mathrm{lem:separate\text{-}slice\text{-}pilot\text{-}mse}\) gives
\[
\mathbb E_P[U^2V^2]
=
\mathbb E_P[U^2]\mathbb E_P[V^2]
\le Cu^2v^2.
\]
