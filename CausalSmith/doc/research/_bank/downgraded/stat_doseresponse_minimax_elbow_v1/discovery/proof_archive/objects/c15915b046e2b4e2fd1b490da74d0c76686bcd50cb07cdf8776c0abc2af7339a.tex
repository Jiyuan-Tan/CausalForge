For every residual tuple and fixed \(0<c<1\), there are positive constants independent of \(n\) such that \[C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}},\] where \[r_{\mathrm{low}}=\min\left\{\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}\right\},\qquad R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\}.\] Hence any exact exponent, if it exists, must satisfy \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\). The endpoints do not coincide in general, and the note does not characterize the tuples at which they might coincide. Separately, \(c=1\) is a degenerate boundary corner with exact exponent \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\); it does not answer the fixed-\(0<c<1\) question.
