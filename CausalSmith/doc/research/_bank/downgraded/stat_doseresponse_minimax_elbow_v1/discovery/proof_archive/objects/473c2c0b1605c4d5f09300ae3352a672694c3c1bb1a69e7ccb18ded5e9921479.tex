For every admissible tuning, the explicit separate-pilot estimator satisfies
\[
\mathcal M_n\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\{(u+h^{\alpha\wedge1})(v+h^{\beta\wedge1})\}^2+(nh)^{-1}+n^{-1}+e^{-cnh}\right],
\]
where
\[
u=\delta_\mu^{s\wedge1}+b_\mu^{\alpha\wedge1}+(nb_\mu\delta_\mu^d)^{-1/2},
\quad
v=\delta_\pi^{s\wedge1}+b_\pi^{\beta\wedge1}+(nb_\pi\delta_\pi^d)^{-1/2}.
\]
Together with \(\mathcal M_n\ge Cn^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\) and the frozen explicit histogram-window upper bound, this gives a computable two-sided rate bracket throughout both open regions.
