Put \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), \(S_0=s\wedge1\), \(E_\mu=(2+A_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1}\), and \(E_\pi=(2+B_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1})^{-1}\). Define
\[
R_{\mathrm{sep}}=\max_{0\le\gamma\le1}\min\left\{2\alpha\gamma,1-\gamma,2\left[\min\{A_0\gamma,E_\mu\}+\min\{B_0\gamma,E_\pi\}\right]\right\},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}=\left[1+\frac1{2A_0}+\frac d{4S_0}+\left(\frac12-\frac{B_0}{A_0}\right)_+\min\left\{\frac1{2B_0},\frac d{2S_0}\right\}\right]^{-1},
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\},
\]
and
\[
r_{\mathrm{low}}=\min\left\{\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}\right\}.
\]
For every admissible residual tuple and fixed \(0<c<1\), there are constants \(0<C_{\mathrm{low}}\le C_{\mathrm{up}}<\infty\) and \(n_0\in\mathbb N\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}
\qquad(n\ge n_0).
\]
Consequently \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\), and any exact exponent must lie in \([R_{\mathrm{ach}},r_{\mathrm{low}}]\). Wherever the endpoints agree, the exact rate is established. The fuzzy converse improves the oracle floor exactly when \(d/s>4+4/\alpha-2/(\alpha\vee\beta)\); its exponent is independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) when \(\beta\le\alpha\). The exponent \(R_{\mathrm{hist}}\) is independent of \(\beta\) when \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), and all three achievable exponents are independent of the numerical value of \(\beta\) on \(\beta\ge1\). These one-sided invariances imply minimax \(\beta\)-invariance only where the bracket closes. At the degenerate boundary \(c=1\), the exact exponent is \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\) for every \(\beta,s,d\), including arbitrary Borel \(P_X\), but this does not settle any fixed interior \(0<c<1\) tuple.
