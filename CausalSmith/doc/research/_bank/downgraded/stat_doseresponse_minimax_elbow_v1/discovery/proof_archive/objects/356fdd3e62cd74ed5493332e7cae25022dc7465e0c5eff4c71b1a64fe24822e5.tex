After the substitution \(a=t_0+hu\),
\[
D_h=\int_{-1}^1K(u)S(u)S(u)^\top f_A(t_0+hu)\,du.
\]
For any vector \(v\), marginal positivity implies
\[
v^\top D_hv\ge c\int_{-1}^1K(u)\{v^\top S(u)\}^2\,du.
\]
The last integral is positive for every nonzero \(v\), because \(K\) is positive on an interval and a nonzero polynomial cannot vanish on an interval. Compactness of the unit sphere therefore supplies a positive, \(h\)-independent lower eigenvalue. Thus \(D_h\) is invertible.

If \(q(a)=\gamma^\top S_h(a)\) has degree at most \(p\), then
\[
\int W_h(a)q(a)f_A(a)\,da
=e_0^\top D_h^{-1}\left\{\int K_h(a)S_h(a)S_h(a)^\top f_A(a)\,da\right\}\gamma
=e_0^\top\gamma=q(t_0).
\]
By the oracle pseudo-outcome identity,
\[
\vartheta_h=\int W_h(a)\theta_P(a)f_A(a)\,da.
\]
Let \(T_{t_0}\) be the Taylor polynomial allowed by the order-\(\alpha\) Hölder convention; it has degree at most \(p\), and on \(\lvert a-t_0\rvert\le h\),
\[
\lvert\theta_P(a)-T_{t_0}(a)\rvert\le C L\lvert a-t_0\rvert^\alpha.
\]
Polynomial reproduction cancels \(T_{t_0}\). Moreover, the upper density bound, the bounded kernel and polynomial basis, and the uniform inverse bound imply
\[
\int\lvert W_h(a)\rvert f_A(a)\,da\le C.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\lvert\vartheta_h-\theta_P(t_0)\rvert
\le\int\lvert W_h(a)\rvert\lvert\theta_P(a)-T_{t_0}(a)\rvert f_A(a)\,da
\le C h^\alpha.
\]
No modulus of continuity of \(f_A\) occurs: the design density is absorbed exactly into \(D_h\).
