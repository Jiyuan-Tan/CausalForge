Fix \(P\) and condition on the two pilot folds. Replace the empirical \(I_X\)-fold marginalizations by
\[
\widetilde m(a)=P_X\widehat\mu(a,\cdot),
\qquad
\widetilde f(a)=P_X\widehat\pi(a\mid\cdot),
\]
and let \(\widetilde\xi\) be the resulting pseudo-outcome. Lemma~\texttt{lem:exact-double-robust-remainder} gives
\[
\mathbb E_P[\widetilde\xi\mid A=a]=\theta_P(a)+B(a).
\]
The population local-polynomial functional has bias at most \(Ch^\alpha\) on \(\theta_P\), by Lemma~\texttt{lem:design-adaptive-local-polynomial-bias}; its bounded equivalent-kernel \(L_1(f_A)\)-norm bounds the contribution of \(B\) by \(C\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:localized-pseudo-outcome-variance} contributes \(C/(nh)+C/n\) on the good-Gram event. Clipping cannot increase loss because \(\theta_P(t_0)\in[-M_Y,M_Y]\), and Lemma~\texttt{lem:empirical-local-polynomial-good-gram} bounds the complementary contribution by \(Ce^{-c_1nh}\). Therefore
\[
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2\mid I_\mu,I_{\mathrm{aux}}]
\le C\left[h^{2\alpha}+\left\{\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|\right\}^2+\frac1{nh}+\frac1n+e^{-c_1nh}\right].
\]
Let
\[
U=\|\widehat\mu_0-\mu_P(t_0,\cdot)\|_{L_2(P_X)},
\qquad
V=\|\widehat\pi_0-\pi_P(t_0\mid\cdot)\|_{L_2(P_X)}.
\]
Lemma~\texttt{lem:constant-extension-off-slice-control} gives
\[
\sup_{|a-t_0|\le h}|B(a)|
\le C(U+h^{A_0})(V+h^{B_0}).
\]
The pilots use independent folds, so Lemmas~\texttt{lem:independent-pilot-product} and~\texttt{lem:separate-slice-pilot-mse} imply
\[
\mathbb E_P[(U+h^{A_0})^2(V+h^{B_0})^2]
\le C(u+h^{A_0})^2(v+h^{B_0})^2.
\]
Average over the pilot folds and take the supremum over \(P\). The displayed count and bandwidth restrictions are exactly those needed by the pilot and concentration lemmas, completing the proof.
