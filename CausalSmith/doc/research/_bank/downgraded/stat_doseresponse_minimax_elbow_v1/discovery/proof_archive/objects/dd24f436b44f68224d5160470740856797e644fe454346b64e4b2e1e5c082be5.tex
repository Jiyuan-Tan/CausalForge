By \(\mathrm{lem:beta-class-one-sided-monotonicity}\),
\[
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0).
\]
The condition \((\alpha,\beta,s,d)\in\Omega\) applies \(\mathrm{thm:oracle-high-s}\) to the rough class and yields its upper bound \(C_{\mathrm{up}}r_n(\alpha)\).  Independently, \(\mathrm{lem:all-beta-oracle-lower-floor}\) applies to the smoother class at \(\beta^{\prime}\) and yields its lower bound \(C_{\mathrm{low}}r_n(\alpha)\).  Using \(r_n(\alpha)=n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}\) gives the claimed chain.  The positive lower bound allows division by the smoother-class minimax risk, and hence
\[
1\le
\frac{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
{\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta^{\prime},s,d,M_Y,L,pbar,c,t_0)}
\le\frac{C_{\mathrm{up}}}{C_{\mathrm{low}}}.
\]
Only the lower-floor lemma, not the oracle upper theorem, was applied at \(\beta^{\prime}\).  Thus no oracle-region condition on \(\beta^{\prime}\) is used.
