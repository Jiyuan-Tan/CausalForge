The counts, clipping operations, eigenvalue indicator, inversion on the good-Gram set, and assigned value on its complement make the estimator in \(\mathrm{def:beta\text{-}free\text{-}handle}\) Borel measurable. Hence it belongs to \(\mathfrak T_n\) in \(\mathrm{def:estimator\text{-}class}\). For every admissible tuning tuple, \(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}mse}\) therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal M_n
&=
\inf_{\widehat\theta\in\mathfrak T_n}
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P}
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta-\theta_P(t_0))^2]\\
&\le
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P}
\mathbb E_P[(\widehat\theta_{h,b_\mu,\delta_\mu,b_\pi,\delta_\pi}-\theta_P(t_0))^2].
\end{aligned}
\]
Applying \(\mathrm{lem:beta\text{-}free\text{-}master\text{-}risk}\) to the right-hand side yields exactly the asserted envelope.
