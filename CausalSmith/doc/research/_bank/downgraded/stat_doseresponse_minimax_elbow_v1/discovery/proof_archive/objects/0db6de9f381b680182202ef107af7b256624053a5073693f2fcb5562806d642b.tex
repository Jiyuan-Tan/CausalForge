Put \(A_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(B_0=\beta\wedge1\), and \(S_0=s\wedge1\), and define
\[
E_\mu=\left(2+A_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1}\right)^{-1},
\qquad
E_\pi=\left(2+B_0^{-1}+dS_0^{-1}\right)^{-1},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{sep}}
=
\max_{0\le\gamma\le1}
\min\left\{
2\alpha\gamma,
1-\gamma,
2\left[\min\{A_0\gamma,E_\mu\}+\min\{B_0\gamma,E_\pi\}\right]
\right\},
\]
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}
=
\left[
1+\frac1{2A_0}+\frac d{4S_0}
+\left(\frac12-\frac{B_0}{A_0}\right)_+
\min\left\{\frac1{2B_0},\frac d{2S_0}\right\}
\right]^{-1},
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{ach}}=\max\{R_{\mathrm{sep}},R_{\mathrm{hist}}\},
\]
and
\[
r_{\mathrm{low}}
=
\min\left\{
\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+1},
\frac{8}{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s}
\right\}.
\]
For every fixed residual tuple and \(0<c<1\), Lemma~\texttt{lem:optimized-deficient-rate-bracket} supplies one Borel estimator whose uniform risk is at most \(C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}\). Lemmas~\texttt{lem:all-beta-oracle-lower-floor} and~\texttt{lem:extreme-low-s-fuzzy-lower} give the two lower bounds with exponents \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\) and \(8/\{4+2/(\alpha\vee\beta)+d/s\}\); taking their maximum yields, after reducing the constant if necessary,
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-r_{\mathrm{low}}}
\le
\mathcal M_n(\alpha,\beta,s,d,M_Y,L,c,t_0)
\le
C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{ach}}}
\qquad(n\ge n_0).
\]
The coexistence of these inequalities forces \(R_{\mathrm{ach}}\le r_{\mathrm{low}}\). If an exact polynomial exponent exists, it must satisfy
\[
R_{\mathrm{ach}}
\le
\rho(\alpha,\beta,s,d)
\le
r_{\mathrm{low}}.
\]
On every tuple for which the two explicit endpoints coincide, the exact exponent exists and equals their common value. In particular, wherever the equality holds with common value \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\), the resulting exact exponent is independent of \(\beta\); the same conclusion holds uniformly on any residual subregion on which that equality persists. At the excluded boundary \(c=1\), Lemma~\texttt{lem:unit-positivity-oracle-rate} separately gives the exact \(\beta\)-free exponent \(2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)\) for all \(\beta,s,d\).
