Clipping gives \(\lvert\widetilde\mu\rvert\le M_Y\), \(c/2\le\widetilde\pi\le2/c\), and therefore a uniformly bounded pseudo-outcome. On the good-Gram event, \(\lVert\widehat D_h^{-1}\rVert\le\lambda_*^{-1}\). Conditional on the training folds and the treatment values, the centered residual contributions are independent, and their coefficient vector has conditional variance bounded by
\[
\frac{C}{N_{\mathrm{lp}}^2}\sum_{i\in I_{\mathrm{lp}}}K_h(A_i)^2\lVert S_h(A_i)\rVert^2.
\]
Its expectation is \(O((nh)^{-1})\) because \(K_h\) is supported on a window of probability \(O(h)\) and has squared magnitude \(O(h^{-2})\).

Conditional on the pilot folds, the errors \(\widehat m-P_X\widetilde\mu\) and \(\widehat f-P_X\widetilde\pi\) are empirical averages of bounded functions on the independent fold \(I_X\), so each has conditional second moment \(O(n^{-1})\). Their induced pseudo-outcome perturbation is a bounded linear combination of these two errors. The good-event local-polynomial map has bounded squared \(L_1\) operator norm, since \(\mathbb E K_h(A)^2=O(h^{-1})\) and \(nh\) is bounded below. Independence of \(I_X\) and \(I_{\mathrm{lp}}\) then gives the additional \(C/n\) term.
