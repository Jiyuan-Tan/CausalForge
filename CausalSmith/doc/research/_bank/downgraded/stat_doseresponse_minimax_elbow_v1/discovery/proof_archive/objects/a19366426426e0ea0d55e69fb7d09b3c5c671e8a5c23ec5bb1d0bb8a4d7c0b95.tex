With \(a_0=\alpha\wedge1\), \(b_0=\beta\wedge1\), and \(q_0=s\wedge1\), define
\[
R_{\mathrm{hist}}
=
\left[
1+\frac{1}{2a_0}+\frac{d}{4q_0}
+
\left(\frac12-\frac{b_0}{a_0}\right)_+
\min\left\{\frac{1}{2b_0},\frac{d}{2q_0}\right\}
\right]^{-1}.
\]
Then, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
C_{\mathrm{low}}n^{-2\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}
\le\mathcal M_n
\le C_{\mathrm{up}}n^{-R_{\mathrm{hist}}}.
\]
This exponent is the exact optimum of the frozen histogram estimator's two tuning parameters. It is independent of \(\beta\) whenever \(\beta\wedge1\ge(\alpha\wedge1)/2\), where it reduces to \(\{1+[2(\alpha\wedge1)]^{-1}+d/[4(s\wedge1)]\}^{-1}\), but it remains strictly smaller than the universal lower-floor exponent, so this is an honest loose bracket rather than an exact minimax rate.
