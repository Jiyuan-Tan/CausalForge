\textbf{Proof.}
Every local balanced vector \(v_k\) is orthogonal to \(\mathbf1_4\). Hence every \(s\in\mathscr S\subset\mathcal Z_n\) belongs to
\[
\mathcal H=\bigoplus_{\ell=1}^q\mathbf1_4^\perp,
\qquad
\dim(\mathcal H)=3q=d.
\tag{1}
\]
Also, every coordinate of \(s\) is either \(-1\) or \(1\), so
\[
\operatorname{tr}(ss^\top)=s^\top s=n.
\tag{2}
\]
The vector space of self-adjoint operators on the \(d\)-dimensional space \(\mathcal H\) has dimension \(D=d(d+1)/2\). By (2), all matrices \(ss^\top\) lie in the affine trace hyperplane of that space, whose dimension is at most \(D-1\).

Let \(\alpha^\star\) be the sign-pair mass vector of an optimizer and write
\[
Q^\star=\sum_{s\in\mathscr S}\alpha_s^\star ss^\top.
\tag{3}
\]
We now give the finite support reduction directly. Suppose (3) has more than \(D\) positive coefficients. The associated matrices are affinely dependent in the trace hyperplane, so there are real numbers \(c_s\), not all zero and supported only where \(\alpha_s^\star>0\), such that
\[
\sum_sc_s=0,
\qquad
\sum_sc_sss^\top=0.
\tag{4}
\]
The first identity implies that the nonzero \(c_s\)'s have both signs. Put
\[
\varepsilon=\min_{s:c_s>0}\frac{\alpha_s^\star}{c_s}>0,
\qquad
\alpha'_s=\alpha_s^\star-\varepsilon c_s.
\tag{5}
\]
Then \(\alpha'_s\ge0\) for every \(s\), at least one formerly positive coefficient becomes zero, and (4) gives
\[
\sum_s\alpha'_s=1,
\qquad
\sum_s\alpha'_sss^\top=Q^\star.
\tag{6}
\]
Iterating this elimination terminates with a mass vector \(\widetilde\alpha\) having at most \(D\) positive entries and representing the same second moment. Since there are only \(m=6^q/2\) sign pairs, the support bound is \(\min\{m,D\}\).

Define the blinded law
\[
\widetilde\pi(s)=\widetilde\pi(-s)=\frac{\widetilde\alpha_s}{2},
\qquad s\in\mathscr S.
\tag{7}
\]
It belongs to \(\Pi_n\), uses at most twice the number of positive sign-pair masses, and (6) proves \(Q_{\widetilde\pi}=Q^\star\). Every generalized risk and every constraint of program \((\mathrm P)\) depends on the design only through this second moment, by \(\mathrm{thm:higher\text{-}rank\text{-}menu\text{-}exact\text{-}program}\). Therefore \(\widetilde\pi\) has exactly the same class risks and commitment value. The argument begins with an already-computed \(Q^\star\); it neither finds that optimizer nor supplies a polynomial-time column-generation method, so it proves only the stated support-existence result. \(\square\)
