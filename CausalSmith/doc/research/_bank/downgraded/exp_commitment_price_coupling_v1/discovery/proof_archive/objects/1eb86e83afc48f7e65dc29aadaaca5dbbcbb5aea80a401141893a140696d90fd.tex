For any array \(p=(p_{\ell k})\) satisfying \(p_{\ell k}\ge0\) and \(\sum_{k=1}^3p_{\ell k}=1\), independently for each block \(\ell\), draw a direction \(k\) with probability \(p_{\ell k}\), draw an independent symmetric sign \(\varepsilon_\ell\in\{-1,1\}\), and set \(Z_{[\ell]}=\varepsilon_\ell v_k\). Denote the resulting assignment law by \(\pi_p\).
