The homogeneous two-class menu is the specialization of \(\mathrm{def:block-specific-spectral-menu}\) with \(J_{\mathrm{blk}}=2\) and, for every \(\ell\),
\[
(d_{1\ell1},d_{1\ell2},d_{1\ell3})
=(c_1,c_1+s_1a,c_1+s_1b),
\qquad
(d_{2\ell1},d_{2\ell2},d_{2\ell3})
=(c_2+s_2a,c_2,c_2+s_2b).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
u_{1\ell}=E_\ell\{\sqrt{c_1}h_1+\sqrt{c_1+s_1a}h_2+\sqrt{c_1+s_1b}h_3\},
\]
\[
u_{2\ell}=E_\ell\{\sqrt{c_2+s_2a}h_1+\sqrt{c_2}h_2+\sqrt{c_2+s_2b}h_3\},
\qquad
U_j=[u_{j1},\ldots,u_{jq}],
\qquad
K_j=U_jU_j^\top.
\]
