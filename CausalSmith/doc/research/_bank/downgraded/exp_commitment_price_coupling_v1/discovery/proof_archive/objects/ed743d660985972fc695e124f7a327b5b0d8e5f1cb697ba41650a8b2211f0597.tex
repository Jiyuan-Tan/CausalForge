For each response scenario \(j\), set \(\operatorname{RE}_j(\pi)=r_j^\star/R_j(\pi)\) and \(\eta_{\mathrm{REL}}=\max_{\pi\in\Pi_n}\min_{j\in\{1,2\}}\operatorname{RE}_j(\pi)\). Ryan, Esserman, and Li (2023, equation (8) and Algorithm 1) supply the published variance-ratio normalization and its maximin rule. This paper applies that rule at the criterion level to its own two-class matched-quadruple variance menu by taking \(r_j^\star\) as the scenario oracle variance and \(R_j(\pi)\) as the committed variance; no claim is made that the matched-quadruple menu is a literal submodel of their cluster-randomized treatment-effect-heterogeneity variance formulas.
