\textbf{Proof.}
Under \(\mathrm{def:block-geometry}\), class \(1\) has its smallest squared loading \(c_1\) on \(v_1\), and class \(2\) has its smallest squared loading \(c_2\) on \(v_2\).  Applying \(\mathrm{prop:block-specific-single-class-reduction}\) gives the independent-sign product laws
\[
R_1=\frac{4c_1}{n^2}=r_1^\star,
\qquad
R_2=\frac{4c_2}{n^2}=r_2^\star. \tag{1}
\]
Because \(a,b,s_j>0\), \(\mathrm{thm:quadruple-menu-frontier}\) gives \(\Delta_j>0\); hence either one-class gain fraction equals one.  For the two-class problem, the same theorem gives
\[
\lambda^\star
=1-\frac3{a+b}\min\left\{\frac a2,b\right\}<1, \tag{2}
\]
since the subtracted quantity is strictly positive.  This proves both the oracle reductions and the strict commitment loss from simultaneous class uncertainty. \(\square\)
