The rank-one proof was first extended by replacing each largest eigenvalue with normalized trace and asking for simultaneous equality.  This yields an exact equality condition for each fixed class and design but not a menu optimum, because the active classes, gain denominators, and relaxation optimizer are unspecified.  A second route treated the feasible assignment second moment as a semidefinite variable; it does not close because the convex hull of blinded block-balanced assignment outer products and the proposed trace relaxation are not defined in \(\mathcal H_{\mathrm{mat}}\).  A third route sought a universal matrix-discrepancy constant, but arbitrary ranks and scalings, absent a declared normalization and block-degree condition, leave no invariant quantity against which such a constant can be sharp.
