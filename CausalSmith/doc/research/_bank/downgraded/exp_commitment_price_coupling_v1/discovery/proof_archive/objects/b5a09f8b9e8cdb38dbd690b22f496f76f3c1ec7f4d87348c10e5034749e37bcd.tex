For the homogeneous two-class specialization in \(\mathrm{def:block-geometry}\), set
\[
B_j=R_j(\pi_0),
\qquad
r_j^\star=\min_{\pi\in\Pi_n}R_j(\pi),
\qquad
\Delta_j=B_j-r_j^\star,
\qquad
g_j(\pi)=\frac{B_j-R_j(\pi)}{\Delta_j},
\]
and
\[
\lambda^\star=\max_{\pi\in\Pi_n}\min_{j\in\{1,2\}}g_j(\pi).
\]
This is the two-class specialization of the finite-class commitment criterion; the displayed gain fractions are used only where \(\Delta_j>0\).
