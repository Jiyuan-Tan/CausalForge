Fix \(J_{\mathrm{blk}}\ge1\).  For every \(1\le j\le J_{\mathrm{blk}}\) and \(1\le\ell\le q\), let \(G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\in\mathbb S_+^4\) satisfy \(G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\mathbf 1_4=0\), and require \(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}}:=\bigoplus_{\ell=1}^qG^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\ne0\).  Put
\[
H=[h_1\ h_2\ h_3],\qquad
\widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}=H^\top G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}H,
\qquad
S^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}=(\widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell})^{1/2},
\]
and, for \(p_\ell=(p_{\ell1},p_{\ell2},p_{\ell3})\in\Delta_3\),
\[
L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)
=4S^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\operatorname{Diag}(p_{\ell1},p_{\ell2},p_{\ell3})S^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}.
\]
The associated response ellipsoid is
\[
\mathcal M_j^{\mathrm{bd}}
=\{x\in\operatorname{range}(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}}):
x^\top(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^\dagger x\le1\}.
\]
Local blocks and different classes may have arbitrary ranks and need not commute.  The direct-sum restriction is part of this menu.
