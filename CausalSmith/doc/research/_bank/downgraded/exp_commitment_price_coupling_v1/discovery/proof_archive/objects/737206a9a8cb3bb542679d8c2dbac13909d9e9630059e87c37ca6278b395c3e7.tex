The polynomial LP theorem is exact for arbitrary positive squared loadings only within the rank-one-per-block construction \(u_{j\ell}=E_\ell\sum_k\sqrt{d_{j\ell k}}h_k\). It is not a theorem for arbitrary positive-semidefinite block kernels, arbitrary within-block spectra, or noncommuting higher-rank geometries. Those cases generally retain spectral matrix constraints and are handled by the separate exponential-column SDP; the polynomial support-existence result there does not by itself give a polynomial-time solver. Gain normalization also requires the explicit boundary condition \(b_j>\bar r_j\) for every active class.
