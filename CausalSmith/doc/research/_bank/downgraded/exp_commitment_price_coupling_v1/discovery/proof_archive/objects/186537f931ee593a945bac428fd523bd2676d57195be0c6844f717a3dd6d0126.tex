The causal target is always the fixed finite-population average treatment effect \(\tau_N\). The LP value is a design-selection guarantee: it is the largest fraction of every active class's variance reduction, measured from block complete randomization to that class's oracle, that one precommitted law can retain simultaneously. The theorem is actionable because the inputs are the known loadings \(d_{j\ell k}\), the optimization is an explicit polynomial-size LP, and its optimizer is converted directly into the assignment law used in the experiment.
