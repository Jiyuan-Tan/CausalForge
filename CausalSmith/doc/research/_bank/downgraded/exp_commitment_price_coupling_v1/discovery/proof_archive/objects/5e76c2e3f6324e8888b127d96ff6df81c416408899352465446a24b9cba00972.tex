The block-diagonal restriction in \(\mathrm{thm:block-diagonal-psd-finite-class-frontier}\) is necessary.  In the smallest two-block case \(q=2\), \(n=8\), put \(w=E_1h_1-E_2h_1\) and \(G=ww^\top\).  There is a blinded law \(\pi\in\Pi_n\) whose two block directions both equal \(v_1\) and whose block signs are identical, for which
\[
\frac1{n^2}\lambda_{\max}(G^{1/2}Q_\pi G^{1/2})=0.
\]
The independent-sign product law with the same local direction marginals instead satisfies
\[
\frac1{n^2}\lambda_{\max}(G^{1/2}Q_{\pi_p}G^{1/2})=\frac8{n^2}>0.
\]
Thus marginal-preserving independent-sign productization can strictly increase risk for a centered positive-semidefinite kernel with cross-block coupling.
