The construction \(\mathcal H_{\mathrm{mat}}\) enumerates one representative of each sign pair in \(\mathcal Z_n\), solves the exact mass-simplex semidefinite program for \(\lambda_{\mathrm{gen}}^\star\) for any finite menu of nonzero positive-semidefinite matrices, compares it with the normalized trace linear program, certifies trace exactness by active-class isotropy or certifies the exact optimum by the dual, and implements an optimizer as a blinded mixture.  A support-reduction step in the \(3q\)-dimensional block-balanced assignment subspace yields an equivalent optimizer with at most \(\min\{6^q/2,3q(3q+1)/2\}\) sign-pair masses.  The construction is exact for unrestricted \(\Pi_n\), but its mass-simplex program has exponentially many columns.  The block-diagonal positive-semidefinite menu instead admits the local-LMI program in \(\mathrm{thm:block-diagonal-psd-finite-class-frontier}\); discrepancy rounding is reserved for additional product-law, deterministic-law, or prescribed-support restrictions.
