For any positive-semidefinite Gram matrix \(K\) of rank \(r\ge1\) and any feasible second moment \(Q\succeq0\), let \(A=K^{1/2}QK^{1/2}\). Then \(A\succeq0\), \(\operatorname{rank}(A)\le r\), and
\[
\lambda_{\max}(A)\ge\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}r. \tag{1}
\]
After padding the nonnegative eigenvalues of \(A\) to \(r\) entries, equality holds if and only if all padded eigenvalues are equal, equivalently
\[
A=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}rP_{\operatorname{range}(K)}. \tag{2}
\]
Consequently, at any proposed common design, condition (2) for every active class is necessary for simultaneous exactness of the classwise normalized trace bounds and is sufficient for replacing the active spectral objectives by their trace values at that design. In the solved block menu this becomes \(C_j=\{\operatorname{tr}(C_j)/q\}I_q\), and the regime-specific independent-sign product laws satisfy it. The unresolved step is joint feasibility, and then the optimal loss, for a general noncommuting menu.
