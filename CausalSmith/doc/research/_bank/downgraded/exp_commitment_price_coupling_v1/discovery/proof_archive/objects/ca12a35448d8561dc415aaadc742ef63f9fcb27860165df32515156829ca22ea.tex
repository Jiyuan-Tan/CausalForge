For \(n=4q\) units randomized within matched quadruples, two rank-\(q\) response ellipsoids have different oracle assignment directions and a third shared direction. The main theorem derives the exact finite-sample, piecewise commitment frontier: \(\lambda^\star=(a-2b)/(a+b)\) for \(a\ge2b\) and \(\lambda^\star=(2b-a)/\{2(a+b)\}\) for \(a\le2b\). Observable trace bounds certify optimality; independent signed assignments on the shared direction attain the upper-elbow branch, an equal mixture of the two competing directions attains the lower-elbow branch, and a one-parameter family is optimal at the elbow. A second theorem applies the variance-ratio maximin normalization informed by Ryan--Esserman--Li equation (8) and Algorithm 1 to this paper's own variance menu and proves criterion-level optimizer separation on an open parameter region; it does not assert a model-level embedding. The higher-rank noncommuting trace-integrality gap remains open.
