Fix \(q\ge1\), put \(n=4q\), and fix the finite block-diagonal positive-semidefinite menu in \(\mathrm{def:block-diagonal-psd-menu}\).  For every blinded law \(\pi\in\Pi_n\), every block marginal is sign-symmetric:
\[
\Pr_\pi\{Z_{[\ell]}=v_k\}
=\Pr_\pi\{Z_{[\ell]}=-v_k\}
=\frac12p_{\ell k}(\pi).
\]
Let \(\pi_p\) be the independent-direction, independent-symmetric-sign product law with the same array \(p_{\ell k}(\pi)\). Then \(\pi_p\in\Pi_n\),
\[
[Q_{\pi_p}]_{\ell\ell}=[Q_\pi]_{\ell\ell}
=4H\operatorname{Diag}(p_{\ell1},p_{\ell2},p_{\ell3})H^\top,
\qquad
[Q_{\pi_p}]_{\ell r}=0\quad(\ell\ne r).
\]
For each class \(j\), the diagonal principal block of \((G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}Q_\pi(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}\), expressed in the \(H\)-coordinates, is \(L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\). Consequently,
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)
=\frac1{n^2}\lambda_{\max}\{(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}Q_\pi(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}\}
\ge\frac1{n^2}\max_{1\le\ell\le q}\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\},
\]
whereas
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi_p)
=\frac1{n^2}\max_{1\le\ell\le q}\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\}
\le R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi).
\]
Thus productization Pareto-dominates the original law simultaneously for arbitrary local ranks and noncommuting local class kernels.  If \(\mu\in\mathcal M_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), then \(\widehat\tau\) is design-unbiased for the causal estimand \(\tau_N\) and \(\operatorname{Var}_\pi(\widehat\tau)\le R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)\).

Moreover,
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi_0)=\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}},
\qquad
\min_{\pi\in\Pi_n}R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)
=\rho_j^{\mathrm{bd}}
=\frac1{n^2}\max_{1\le\ell\le q}
\min_{p_\ell\in\Delta_3}\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\}.
\]
The \(J_{\mathrm{blk}}q\) values \(\omega^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\) are computable by constant-size semidefinite programs
\[
\min_{p_\ell\in\Delta_3,\ u\in\mathbb R}
\{u:L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\preceq uI_3\}.
\]
Assume \(\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}>0\) for every \(1\le j\le J_{\mathrm{blk}}\). Then the exact finite-sample commitment value is
\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{\mathrm{bd}}^\star
=\max_{t,\,(p_{\ell k})}
\left\{t:
p_{\ell k}\ge0,\quad
\sum_{k=1}^3p_{\ell k}=1,\quad
L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)
\preceq n^2(\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}-\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}t)I_3
\ \text{for every }j,\ell
\right\}.}
\]
This program has \(3q+1\) scalar variables, \(q\) simplex equalities, and \(J_{\mathrm{blk}}q\) linear matrix inequalities of order \(3\), in addition to nonnegativity. Every optimizer produces through \(\mathrm{def:block-product-design}\) a blinded product law attaining \(\lambda_{\mathrm{bd}}^\star\). Zero direction probabilities are allowed. The conclusion is restricted to block-diagonal kernels; no productization claim is made for a kernel coupling distinct quadruples.
