\textbf{Proof.}
For the stated product law, equation (9) in \(\mathrm{thm:block-specific-finite-class-frontier}\) gives
\[
C_j^{\mathrm{blk}}
=4\operatorname{diag}_{\ell}
\left(\min_{1\le k\le3}d_{j\ell k}\right).
\]
The spectral-risk identity in that theorem therefore yields
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{blk}}
=\frac4{n^2}\max_\ell\min_kd_{j\ell k}
=\frac{4\bar r_j}{n^2}
=(r_j^{\mathrm{blk}})^\star.
\]
When \(b_j>\bar r_j\), the gain denominator is positive.  Oracle minimality implies \(R_j^{\mathrm{blk}}(\pi)\ge(r_j^{\mathrm{blk}})^\star\) for every design, and hence
\[
g_j^{\mathrm{blk}}(\pi)
\le
\frac{B_j^{\mathrm{blk}}-(r_j^{\mathrm{blk}})^\star}
{\Delta_j^{\mathrm{blk}}}
=1.
\]
The displayed product law attains equality.  Thus any finite-menu value below one comes from the requirement to use one law simultaneously across classes, not from nonattainment of an individual oracle. \(\square\)
