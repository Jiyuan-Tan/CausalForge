For the specified two-class matched-quadruple menu, the project derives the piecewise exact frontier \(\lambda^\star=(a-2b)/(a+b)\) for \(a\ge2b\) and \(\lambda^\star=(2b-a)/\{2(a+b)\}\) for \(a\le2b\). It certifies regime-specific optimal laws by matching trace and scalar score-matrix bounds: the shared-direction law above the elbow, the equal oracle-direction mixture below it, and a one-parameter optimal family at the elbow. The optimal laws improve both class risks over block complete randomization exactly off the elbow. The project also proves a criterion-level optimizer separation from the Ryan--Esserman--Li variance-ratio maximin normalization on a common feasible menu.
