For the menu in \(\mathrm{def:block-diagonal-psd-menu}\), define
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)
=\sup_{x\in\mathcal M_j^{\mathrm{bd}}}n^{-2}x^\top Q_\pi x,
\qquad
\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}
=\frac1{n^2}\max_{1\le\ell\le q}
\lambda_{\max}\!\left(\frac43G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\right),
\]
\[
\omega^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}
=\min_{p_\ell\in\Delta_3}\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\},
\qquad
\rho_j^{\mathrm{bd}}
=\frac1{n^2}\max_{1\le\ell\le q}\omega^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell},
\qquad
\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}=\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}-\rho_j^{\mathrm{bd}}.
\]
When \(\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}>0\) for every \(1\le j\le J_{\mathrm{blk}}\), put
\[
g_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)
=\frac{\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}-R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)}{\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}},
\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{bd}}^\star
=\max_{\pi\in\Pi_n}\min_{1\le j\le J_{\mathrm{blk}}}g_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi).
\]
