Kallus (2018, Theorem 1 and Sections 1 and 7) makes one response-function class the primitive of a minimax balancing problem, and Kallus (2021, Sections 1 and 4--6) characterizes one-class minimax randomization through assignment covariance. Those papers inform the ellipsoidal class risk and the one-class oracle boundary. The present result differs by proving a simultaneous productization theorem for any finite menu of heterogeneous block-specific rank-one loadings and by reducing its exact commitment frontier to a polynomial-size LP. Tommasi, Rodr\'iguez-D\'iaz, and L\'opez-Fidalgo (2023, Theorem 3) supply established generic binding-weight equivalence and remain methodological background rather than the source of the productization certificate. Ryan, Esserman, and Li (2023, Section 3.2, equation (8), and Algorithm 1) supply the variance-ratio maximin comparator applied to the homogeneous two-class corollary; the optimizer-separation theorem is criterion-level and does not claim their cluster-randomized heterogeneity model as a submodel. Harshaw, S\"avje, Spielman, and Zhang (2024, Sections 1, 8, and 9) motivate covariance and discrepancy questions under additional restrictions. Genuine higher-rank or noncommuting block kernels remain governed here by the separate exact finite SDP, not by the new LP.
