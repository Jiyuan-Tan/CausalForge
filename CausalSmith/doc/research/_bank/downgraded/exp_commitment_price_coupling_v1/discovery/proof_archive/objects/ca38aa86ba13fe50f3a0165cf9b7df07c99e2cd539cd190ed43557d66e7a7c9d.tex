Above the elbow \(a=2b\), the common direction is sufficiently inexpensive that committing to it is optimal. Below the elbow, sharing is too costly, and the best protection alternates equally between the two class-specific oracle directions. Exactly at the elbow, these strategies and block complete randomization tie, so the guaranteed retained gain is zero. This is a design-based statement about fixed potential outcomes: assignment is the only randomness, and the theorem optimizes a finite-population variance guarantee rather than asserting an asymptotic sampling law.
