\textbf{Proof.}
Apply \(\mathrm{thm:heterogeneous-block-psd-finite-class-frontier}\) with \(L=q\), \(N=n=4q\), \(b_\ell=4\),
\[
\mathcal A_\ell=\{\pm v_1,\pm v_2,\pm v_3\},
\qquad
a_{\ell r}=v_r,
\qquad
m_\ell=3,
\qquad
H_\ell=H=[h_1\ h_2\ h_3].
\tag{1}
\]
The supports and blinded design classes in (1) are exactly \(\mathrm{def:block-assignments}\) and \(\mathrm{def:design-space}\); the specialized independent-block law is \(\mathrm{def:block-product-design}\), and its uniform member is \(\mathrm{def:baseline-design}\).  Since \(v_r=2h_r\), orthonormality gives \(H^\top v_r=2e_r\).  Hence the generalized covariance and local score specialize to
\[
\Sigma_\ell(p_\ell)
=\sum_{r=1}^3p_{\ell r}v_rv_r^\top
=4H\operatorname{Diag}(p_{\ell1},p_{\ell2},p_{\ell3})H^\top,
\tag{2}
\]
\[
L_{j\ell}(p_\ell)
=4S^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}
\operatorname{Diag}(p_{\ell1},p_{\ell2},p_{\ell3})
S^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell},
\tag{3}
\]
which is precisely \(L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\) in \(\mathrm{def:block-diagonal-psd-menu}\).  The global direct sums, ellipsoids, risks, uniform-baseline values, local oracle values, gain denominators, and commitment value in the generalized construction therefore coincide term by term with \(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), \(\mathcal M_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), \(R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), \(\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), \(\omega_{j\ell}^{\mathrm{bd}}\), \(\rho_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), \(\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), and \(\lambda_{\mathrm{bd}}^\star\) from \(\mathrm{def:block-diagonal-commitment-value}\).

The sign-symmetry identity, preservation of every diagonal covariance block, deletion of all cross-block blocks, spectral risk identity, principal-block lower certificate, and simultaneous Pareto dominance now follow from the generalized theorem after substitutions (1)--(3).  Its fixed-array calculation agrees with \(\mathrm{def:estimator}\); alternatively, \(\mathrm{lem:variance-and-spectral-risk}\) gives the same identity under the three declared design-based assumptions.  Thus \(\widehat\tau\) is unbiased for \(\tau_N\), and membership \(\mu\in\mathcal M_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\) gives the claimed variance bound.

At the uniform masses \(p_{\ell r}=1/3\), (3) gives the displayed \(\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\).  The generalized block-separable oracle formula gives the displayed \(\rho_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\) and its order-three local semidefinite programs.  Under \(\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}>0\) for all classes, its commitment program becomes exactly the boxed program in the statement.  Since \(m_\ell=3\) and \(b_\ell-1=3\), it has \(3q+1\) scalar variables, \(q\) simplex equalities, and \(J_{\mathrm{blk}}q\) order-three linear matrix inequalities.  The generalized attaining law is exactly \(\pi_p\).  Boundary masses remain permitted, and the generalized theorem's direct-sum boundary gives the stated prohibition on cross-block kernels. \(\square\)
