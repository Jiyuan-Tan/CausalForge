For any fixed positive-semidefinite Gram matrix \(K\) of rank \(r\ge1\) and any feasible assignment second moment \(Q\succeq0\), put \(A=K^{1/2}QK^{1/2}\).  Then \(A\succeq0\), \(\operatorname{rank}(A)\le r\), and
\[
\lambda_{\max}(A)\ge\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}r. \tag{1}
\]
Indeed, after padding the nonnegative eigenvalues of \(A\) to \(r\) terms, their maximum is at least their average.  Equality in (1) holds if and only if all \(r\) padded eigenvalues are equal, equivalently
\[
A=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}r P_{\operatorname{range}(K)}, \tag{2}
\]
where \(P_{\operatorname{range}(K)}\) is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(K)\).  Thus a necessary condition for any menu-wide trace relaxation to be exact at a proposed common design is equality (2) for every class whose trace constraint is active; it is sufficient for replacing those active classwise spectral objectives by their trace terms at that design.  In the solved rank-one block menu, the score representation converts (2) to \(C_j=\{\operatorname{tr}(C_j)/q\}I_q\), and the independent-sign product witnesses satisfy it.  What remains unproved is existence of one feasible covariance satisfying the analogous active-class conditions for a general noncommuting menu, and the sharp loss when none exists.
