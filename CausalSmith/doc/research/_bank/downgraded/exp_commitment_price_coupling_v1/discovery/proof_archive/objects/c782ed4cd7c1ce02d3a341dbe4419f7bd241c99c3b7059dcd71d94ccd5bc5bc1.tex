Because \(K^{1/2}\) and \(K\) have the same range,
\[
\operatorname{range}(A)\subseteq\operatorname{range}(K).
\]
Let \(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_r\) be the eigenvalues of the restriction of \(A\) to \(\operatorname{range}(K)\), with zero eigenvalues included. Positive semidefiniteness gives \(\xi_h\ge0\), and therefore
\[
\lambda_{\max}(A)=\max_{1\le h\le r}\xi_h
\ge \frac1r\sum_{h=1}^r\xi_h
=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}r.
\]
Equality between the maximum and the average of \(r\) nonnegative numbers holds exactly when all of them are equal. Thus equality holds exactly when the restriction of \(A\) to \(\operatorname{range}(K)\) is \(\operatorname{tr}(A)I/r\). Since \(A\) vanishes on \(\operatorname{range}(K)^\perp\), this is equivalent to
\[
A=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}rP_K.
\]
