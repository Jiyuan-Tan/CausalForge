Let \(K,Q\in\mathbb S_+^n\), let \(r=\operatorname{rank}(K)\ge1\), let \(P_K\) be the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(K)\), and put \(A=K^{1/2}QK^{1/2}\). Then
\[
\lambda_{\max}(A)\ge \frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}r.
\]
Equality holds if and only if
\[
A=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}rP_K.
\]
