For \(n=4q\) units randomized within matched quadruples, two rank-\(q\) response ellipsoids have different oracle directions and a third shared direction. The exact finite-sample commitment frontier has a phase transition: \(\lambda^\star=(a-2b)/(a+b)\) for \(a\ge2b\), while \(\lambda^\star=(2b-a)/\{2(a+b)\}\) for \(a\le2b\). Independent symmetric block signs attain the frontier using only the shared direction above the elbow and an equal mixture of the two oracle directions below it; at \(a=2b\), the value is zero and block complete randomization is maximin. Matching trace and scalar-eigenvalue bounds certify optimality. A criterion-level application of the Ryan--Esserman--Li variance-ratio maximin rule yields a different optimizer on an open parameter region, without asserting that this matched-quadruple menu is a literal submodel of their cluster-randomized variance formulas. The higher-rank noncommuting menu remains open.
