Kallus (2018, Theorem 1 and Sections 1 and 7) makes a response-function class the primitive of a minimax balancing problem, while Kallus (2021, Sections 1 and 4--6) studies optimal randomization for one chosen response class through assignment covariance. These papers inform the risk construction and the one-class oracle reduction; the present result computes the exact commitment loss for a specified two-class matched-quadruple menu. Ryan, Esserman, and Li (2023, Section 3.2, equation (8), and Algorithm 1) define a variance-ratio relative efficiency and maximize its worst scenario. This paper applies that published normalization rule to its own finite variance menu and proves criterion-level optimizer separation; it does not claim that the menu is a literal submodel of their cluster-randomized treatment-effect-heterogeneity formulas or a substantive nonrepresentability result against their model. Tommasi, Rodr\'iguez-D\'iaz, and L\'opez-Fidalgo (2023, Theorem 3) provide established generic maximin binding-weight equivalence, which is methodological background rather than a contribution here. Harshaw, S\"avje, Spielman, and Zhang (2024, Sections 1, 8, and 9) motivate the matrix-discrepancy handle for the still-open higher-rank problem, and Bai (2023, Theorem 2.1) supplies a complete-randomization benchmark without addressing this structured two-geometry commitment price.
