\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(z=(z_{[1]},\ldots,z_{[q]})\).  Fix \(\ell\) and \(k\).  Global blinding and the bijection \(z\mapsto-z\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Pr_\pi\{Z_{[\ell]}=v_k\}
&=\sum_{z:z_{[\ell]}=v_k}\pi(z)
=\sum_{z:z_{[\ell]}=v_k}\pi(-z)\\
&=\sum_{w:w_{[\ell]}=-v_k}\pi(w)
=\Pr_\pi\{Z_{[\ell]}=-v_k\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
Their sum is \(p_{\ell k}(\pi)\).  Since \(v_k=2h_k\),
\[
[Q_\pi]_{\ell\ell}
=\mathbb E_\pi[Z_{[\ell]}Z_{[\ell]}^\top]
=\sum_{k=1}^3p_{\ell k}v_kv_k^\top
=4H\operatorname{Diag}(p_{\ell1},p_{\ell2},p_{\ell3})H^\top.
\tag{2}
\]

Construct \(\pi_p\) as in \(\mathrm{def:block\text{-}product\text{-}design}\).  Its mass at directions \((k_1,\ldots,k_q)\) and signs \((\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_q)\) is
\[
2^{-q}\prod_{\ell=1}^qp_{\ell k_\ell},
\tag{3}
\]
which is invariant under simultaneous sign reversal.  Thus \(\pi_p\in\Pi_n\), including when some masses vanish.  Equation (2) also holds under \(\pi_p\), and independence with symmetric signs gives, for \(\ell\ne r\),
\[
[Q_{\pi_p}]_{\ell r}
=\mathbb E_{\pi_p}[Z_{[\ell]}Z_{[r]}^\top]=0.
\tag{4}
\]

Because \(H^\top H=I_3\), \(HH^\top\) projects onto \(\mathbf1_4^\perp\).  The assumptions \(G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\succeq0\) and \(G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\mathbf1_4=0\) imply
\[
G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}
=H\widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}H^\top,
\qquad
(G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell})^{1/2}
=HS^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}H^\top.
\tag{5}
\]
The square root preserves direct sums, so
\[
(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}
=\bigoplus_{\ell=1}^q(G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell})^{1/2}.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (2), (5), and (6), the \(\ell\)-th diagonal principal block of
\(A_j(\pi)=(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}Q_\pi(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}\) is
\[
[A_j(\pi)]_{\ell\ell}
=H L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)H^\top.
\tag{7}
\]
The nonzero eigenvalues in (7) are those of \(L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\).  A Rayleigh quotient on vectors supported in block \(\ell\) gives
\[
\lambda_{\max}\{A_j(\pi)\}
\ge\max_\ell\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Under \(\pi_p\), (4) makes \(A_j(\pi_p)\) the direct sum of (7), and hence
\[
\lambda_{\max}\{A_j(\pi_p)\}
=\max_\ell\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\}.
\tag{9}
\]

For possibly singular \(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), spectral decomposition gives
\[
\mathcal M_j^{\mathrm{bd}}
=\{(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})^{1/2}u:
\|P_{\operatorname{range}(G_j^{\mathrm{bd}})}u\|_2\le1\}.
\tag{10}
\]
Rayleigh--Ritz applied to (10) yields
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)
=n^{-2}\lambda_{\max}\{A_j(\pi)\}.
\tag{11}
\]
Equations (8)--(11) prove simultaneous Pareto productization for arbitrary local ranks and without commutativity across class kernels.

The fixed-outcome, no-interference, assignment-only-randomness, balance, and blinding assumptions are exactly those used in \(\mathrm{lem:variance\text{-}and\text{-}spectral\text{-}risk}\), which gives
\[
\mathbb E_\pi\widehat\tau=\tau_N,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_\pi(\widehat\tau)=n^{-2}\mu^\top Q_\pi\mu.
\tag{12}
\]
If \(\mu\in\mathcal M_j^{\mathrm{bd}}\), (10)--(12) imply the stated variance bound.  Thus the causal target remains separate from the robust design criterion.

Under \(\pi_0\), each direction has mass \(1/3\), while independent symmetric signs kill cross-block moments.  Equations (5), (9), and (11) give
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi_0)
=\frac1{n^2}\max_\ell
\lambda_{\max}\!\left(\frac43\widehat G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\right)
=\frac1{n^2}\max_\ell
\lambda_{\max}\!\left(\frac43G^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}\right)
=\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}.
\tag{13}
\]
For every design, (8)--(11) imply
\[
R_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi)
\ge\frac1{n^2}\max_\ell\omega^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}.
\tag{14}
\]
Continuity on the compact simplex gives a minimizer of every local problem.  The product law using these blockwise minimizers attains the right side of (14), because
\[
\min_{(p_\ell)\in\Delta_3^q}
\max_\ell\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\}
=\max_\ell\min_{p_\ell\in\Delta_3}
\lambda_{\max}\{L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)\}.
\tag{15}
\]
This equality follows by choosing each block variable independently at its local minimum.  It proves the oracle formula.  The epigraph identity
\(\lambda_{\max}(L)\le u\iff L\preceq uI_3\) proves the displayed constant-size semidefinite representation.

Assume now \(\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}>0\) for every class in the minimum.  Productization cannot increase any risk, so it cannot decrease any retained gain.  We may optimize over \(p\in\Delta_3^q\).  For a common scalar \(t\), (9)--(11) give
\[
g_j^{\mathrm{bd}}(\pi_p)\ge t
\iff
L^{\mathrm{bd}}_{j\ell}(p_\ell)
\preceq n^2(\beta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}-\delta_j^{\mathrm{bd}}t)I_3
\quad\text{for every }\ell.
\tag{16}
\]
Maximizing \(t\) gives the asserted local-LMI program.  Compactness of \(\Delta_3^q\) and continuity of the minimum gain give an optimizer, while (3), (9), and (11) show that its product law attains the same value.  The program has \(3q\) masses, one scalar \(t\), \(q\) simplex equalities, and \(J_{\mathrm{blk}}q\) order-three LMIs.  No positive lower bound on direction masses is required.

For the smallest case \(q=1\), the global score consists of the single principal block (7), so (8) is equality for every law and the local program exactly exhausts the three-direction simplex.  The proof's boundary is (6): a kernel coupling different quadruples need not have a block-diagonal square root, and no productization claim is made there.  \(\square\)
