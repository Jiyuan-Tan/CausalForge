The frozen core does not define the normalized trace relaxation's objective, normalization, variables, constraints, block ranks, or the higher-rank response ellipsoids.  Likewise, \(\lambda_{\mathrm{gen}}^\star\) is described only by an informal replacement operation, although the rank-one formula uses class-specific baselines and oracle gains that must be positive.  Different legitimate choices of those missing data produce different relaxation values and integrality gaps.  Moreover, individual spectra cannot determine simultaneous attainability for noncommuting matrices because their relative eigenspaces and the feasible assignment covariance set matter.  Consequently, a universal sharp constant or an attaining law cannot be derived without silently selecting a new problem, while assuming a rounding gap would assume the crux.
