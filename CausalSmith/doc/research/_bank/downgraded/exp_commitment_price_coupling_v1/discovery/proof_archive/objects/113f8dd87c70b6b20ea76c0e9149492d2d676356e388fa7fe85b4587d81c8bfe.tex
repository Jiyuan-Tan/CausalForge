The exact frontier uses a deliberately structured rank-\(q\) menu with identical four-unit geometry across predeclared blocks. The shared-direction law is certified as a commitment optimizer only when \(a>2b\); below the elbow the certified optimizer mixes the two competing directions equally. The trace certificate need not be sharp for higher-rank noncommuting block kernels, and resolving that integrality gap is the explicit open question. The generic saddle or binding-weight equivalence is established by Tommasi, Rodr\'iguez-D\'iaz, and L\'opez-Fidalgo (2023, Theorem 3) and carries no novelty claim.
