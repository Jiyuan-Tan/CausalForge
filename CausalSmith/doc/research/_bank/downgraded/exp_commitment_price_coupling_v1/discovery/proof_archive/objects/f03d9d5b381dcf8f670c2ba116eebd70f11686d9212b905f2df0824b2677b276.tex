Embed the menu as the two-scenario maximin variance subproblem of Ryan, Esserman, and Li (2023, equation (8) and Algorithm 1) by setting \(\sigma_j^{2*}=r_j^\star\) and \(\sigma_j^2(\pi)=R_j(\pi)\). Then their exact component criterion is \(\operatorname{RE}_j(\pi)=r_j^\star/R_j(\pi)\). If \(\kappa_2>\kappa_1\), its maximin value on the same implementable design space is \[\eta_{\mathrm{REL}}=\left[1+\frac{ab\kappa_1\kappa_2}{\kappa_1(a-b)+\kappa_2b}\right]^{-1}.\] Every relative-efficiency optimizer has average direction frequencies \[p_1=0,\qquad p_2=\frac{b(\kappa_2-\kappa_1)}{\kappa_1(a-b)+\kappa_2b},\qquad p_3=1-p_2,\] and an independent-block, independent-sign law with these frequencies attains the value. Every gain-normalized commitment optimizer instead has \(p_3=1\). Hence the optimizer sets are disjoint for the open parameter region \(a>2b>0\) and \(\kappa_2>\kappa_1>0\), even though both criteria use the same two oracle variances, committed variances, and feasible balanced assignment laws.
