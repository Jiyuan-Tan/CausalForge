Fix \(x\in[-1,1]^d\) and \(0<h\le1\). In each coordinate, truncating the interval \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) at at most one face of \([-1,1]\) leaves
\[
\frac h2\le \ell_j=R_j-L_j\le h.
\]
Multiplying over coordinates gives
\[
2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
\]
Under the affine change of variables \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\), one has \(du=V_{x,h}\,dz\). Since \(f_P=2^{-d}\), the tensor spline basis is orthonormal on \([0,1]^d\), and \(b_{x,h,k}=V_{x,h}^{-1/2}B_k\circ T_{x,h}\) on the window,
\[
\Omega_{x,h,k}
=2^{-d}V_{x,h}^{-1}\int_{W_{x,h}}B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du
=2^{-d}I_k.
\]
The same change of variables and orthonormality of \(\rho_p\) yield
\[
\int_{W_{x,h}}K_{x,h}(u)r_{x,h,p}(u)r_{x,h,p}(u)^\top\,du=I_{J_p}.
\]
Strict overlap implies \(c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\le\pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}\le1/4\). Multiplication by the uniform density therefore gives
\[
2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
\preceq Q_{x,h}
\preceq 2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
\]
For the corrected choices \(q_-=2^{-d-1}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\) and \(q_+=2^{-d-1}\), the population matrix lies in the closed convex spectral interval \(\mathcal C=\{M:q_-I\preceq M\preceq q_+I\}\). The spectral map \(T_{[q_-,q_+]}\) is the Frobenius projection onto \(\mathcal C\). Hence, with \(S=(\widetilde Q+\widetilde Q^\top)/2\),
\[
\|\widehat Q-Q\|_F=\|\Pi_{\mathcal C}(S)-\Pi_{\mathcal C}(Q)\|_F\le\|S-Q\|_F.
\]
Its eigenvalues are deterministically in \([q_-,q_+]\), so \(\|\widehat Q^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le q_-^{-1}\). The resolvent identity gives
\[
\|\widehat Q^{-1}-Q^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le \|\widehat Q^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\,\|\widehat Q-Q\|_{\mathrm{op}}\,\|Q^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le q_-^{-2}\|S-Q\|_F.
\]
Finally, the corrected known-density construction sets \(\widehat\Omega=\Omega=2^{-d}I_k\), so its inverse error is exactly zero. All displayed statements are deterministic; no unsupported good-event assertion is needed.
