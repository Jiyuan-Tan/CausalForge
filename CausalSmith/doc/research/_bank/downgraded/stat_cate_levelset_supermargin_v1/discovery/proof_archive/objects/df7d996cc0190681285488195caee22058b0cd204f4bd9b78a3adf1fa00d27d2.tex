By \(\mathrm{thm:calibrated-frontier-feasibility}\), the calibrated feasible super-margin frontier is
\[
 \mathcal F(\kappa)=\{(1,\gamma):\gamma>d\},
\]
and, for every feasible pair, \(\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\). In particular, the class in the displayed minimax risk is nonempty and has an interior-crossing member.

Fix \(\gamma>d\). The achievability statement \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-upper-transfer}\) gives, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[d_H(\widehat G_n^{\mathrm{LP}},G_P;P)\right]
 \le C_+(r_n^*)^2.
\]
Because \(\widehat G_n^{\mathrm{LP}}\) is one sample-measurable estimator, independent of the unknown member law, taking the infimum over all measurable set estimators in \(\mathrm{def:minimax-risk}\) yields the upper bound.

Conversely, \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-transverse-two-branch-converse}\) gives
\[
 \inf_{\widehat G_n}\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[d_H(\widehat G_n,G_P;P)\right]
 \ge C_-(r_n^*)^2
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\), in each branch of \(\mathrm{def:rate}\). Substitution of \(\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\) proves the lower bound for \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\). Enlarging \(C_+\), decreasing \(C_-\), and increasing \(n_0\), if necessary, makes the same constants valid throughout the two cases. Since the calibrated feasible exponent is \(\xi=1\), the rate \((r_n^*)^2\) is exactly \((r_n^*)^{1+\xi}\).
