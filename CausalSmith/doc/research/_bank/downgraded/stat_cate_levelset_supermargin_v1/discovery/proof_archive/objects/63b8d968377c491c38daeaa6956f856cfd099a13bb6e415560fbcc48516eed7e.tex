Let \((\xi,\gamma)\in\mathcal F(\kappa)\). By \(\mathrm{def:feasible-frontier}\), \(\xi\gamma>d\) and there exists \(P\in\mathcal M(\xi,\gamma;\kappa)\). The calibrated margin-compatibility lemma gives \(\xi=1\) and \(\gamma>d\). Therefore
\[
\mathcal F(\kappa)\subseteq\{(1,\gamma):\gamma>d\}.
\]
Conversely, for every \(\gamma>d\), \(\mathrm{thm:calibrated-witness}\) supplies an observed law in \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\). By \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\), this is a member of \(\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)\), and \(1\cdot\gamma>d\). Hence \((1,\gamma)\in\mathcal F(\kappa)\), proving
\[
\mathcal F(\kappa)=\{(1,\gamma):\gamma>d\}.
\]
Finally, every feasible pair has \(\xi=1\), so
\[
\mathcal M(\xi,\gamma;\kappa)=\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)
\]
by the defining identity in \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\).
