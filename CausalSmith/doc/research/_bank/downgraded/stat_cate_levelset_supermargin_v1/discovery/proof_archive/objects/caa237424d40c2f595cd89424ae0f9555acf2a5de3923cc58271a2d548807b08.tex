Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\). By \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\), this law is a member of \(\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)\), so it satisfies \(\mathrm{ass:bounded-density}\). Under \(\mathrm{def:calibration}\), the lower and upper density constants coincide:
\[
c_f=C_f=2^{-d}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
2^{-d}\le f_P(x)\le2^{-d}
\]
for Lebesgue-almost every \(x\in\mathcal X\), and hence \(f_P(x)=2^{-d}=F_0(x)\) almost everywhere on \(\mathcal X\). Since \(P_X(\mathcal X)=1\), the density may be represented by its zero extension outside \(\mathcal X\). On the support, \(f_P/F_0=1\) almost everywhere; therefore every density-ratio error relative to \(F_0\) is zero and the essential supremum of the ratio is one.
