Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\), put \(r=r_n^*\), and write \(\Delta(x)=|\tau_P(x)-\theta|\). By the definitions of the two upper sets,
\[
x\in\widehat G_n^{\mathrm{LP}}\triangle G_P
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
|\widehat\tau_n(x)-\tau_P(x)|\ge\Delta(x).
\]
Choose a fixed \(A\) larger than the constant in \(\mathrm{lem:lp-exponential-error}\). The contribution from \(0<\Delta(X)\le Ar\) is, by the upper margin condition with \(\xi=1\),
\[
\mathbb E_P\!\left[\Delta(X)\mathbf 1\{0<\Delta(X)\le Ar\}\right]
\le Ar\,C_mAr\le Cr^2.
\]
For \(j\ge0\), let
\[
E_j=\{2^jAr<\Delta(X)\le2^{j+1}Ar\}
\]
and first retain indices for which \(2^{j+1}Ar\le\eta/4\). The pointwise exponential inequality and the margin bound give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_P\!\left[\Delta(X)\mathbf 1\{E_j\}
 \mathbf 1\{X\in\widehat G_n^{\mathrm{LP}}\triangle G_P\}\right]
&\le 2^{j+1}Ar\,P_X\{\Delta(X)\le2^{j+1}Ar\}\,Ce^{-c2^j}\\
&\le C(2^jr)^2e^{-c2^j}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The series \(\sum_{j\ge0}4^je^{-c2^j}\) is finite, so these shells contribute at most \(Cr^2\). There is at most one terminal truncated shell between the last such dyadic endpoint and \(\eta/4\). On it, \(2^jr\) is bounded below by a fixed positive constant, and the same pointwise inequality gives contribution at most \(C\exp\{-c/r\}=o(r^2)\). On \(\eta/4<\Delta(X)\le\eta/2\), misclassification forces error at least \(\eta/4\); the first assertion of \(\mathrm{lem:lp-exponential-error}\), used with \(z\asymp1/r\), again gives \(C\exp\{-c/r\}\).

On the remaining set \(\Delta(X)>\eta/2\), bounded outcomes and calibration bound \(\Delta\) by a fixed constant. The second assertion of \(\mathrm{lem:lp-exponential-error}\) therefore gives a contribution at most \(C\exp\{-c/r\}=o(r^2)\). Integrating the pointwise bounds with respect to the common covariate law is justified by Tonelli's theorem, since the integrand is nonnegative. The constants are uniform in \(P\), so taking the supremum proves the claim in both branches.
