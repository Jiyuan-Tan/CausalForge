Fix \(x\in[-1,1]^d\) and \(0<h\le1\). In each coordinate, truncating \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) at a face of the cube removes at most half of the interval. Therefore
\[
h/2\le\ell_j=R_j-L_j\le h,
\qquad
2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(f_P=2^{-d}\) almost everywhere. Under the change of variables \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\), one has \(du=V_{x,h}\,dz\). Orthonormality of \(B_k\) and the definition of the localized basis give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Omega_{x,h,k}
&=2^{-d}V_{x,h}^{-1}\int_{W_{x,h}}B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du\\
&=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}B_k(z)B_k(z)^\top\,dz
=2^{-d}I_k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The same change of variables and orthonormality of the shifted-Legendre basis yield
\[
\int_{W_{x,h}}K_{x,h}(u)r_{x,h,p}(u)r_{x,h,p}(u)^\top\,du=I_{J_p}.
\]
Strict overlap implies
\[
c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\le\pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}\le\frac14.
\]
Multiplying the preceding identity by the uniform density and these scalar bounds proves
\[
2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
\preceq Q_{x,h}
\preceq2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
\]
The known-density construction sets \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=2^{-d}I_k\), so it equals \(\Omega_{x,h,k}\) identically.

Put \(q_-=2^{-d-1}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\), \(q_+=2^{-d-1}\), and
\[
\mathcal C=\{M=M^\top:q_-I_{J_p}\preceq M\preceq q_+I_{J_p}\}.
\]
The population bounds place \(Q_{x,h}\) in \(\mathcal C\). Spectral clipping is the Frobenius-metric projection onto this closed convex set. Thus, with \(S=(\widetilde Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}+\widetilde Q^{(\ell)\top}_{x,h})/2\), projection nonexpansiveness gives
\[
\|\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\|_F\le\|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\]
Every eigenvalue of \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}\) lies in \([q_-,q_+]\), whence \(\|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le q_-^{-1}\). The population lower bound also gives \(\|Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le q_-^{-1}\). The resolvent identity now yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
&\le\|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\|\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\|Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\\
&\le q_-^{-2}\|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\end{aligned}
\]
All assertions are deterministic.
