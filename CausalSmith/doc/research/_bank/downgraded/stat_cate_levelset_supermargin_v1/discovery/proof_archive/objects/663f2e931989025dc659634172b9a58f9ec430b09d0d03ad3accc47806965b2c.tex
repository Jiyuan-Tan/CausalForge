Fix \(x\in[-1,1]^d\) and \(0<h\le1\). In each coordinate, truncating \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) at a face of \([-1,1]\) removes at most half its length. Hence
\[
 h/2\le \ell_j=R_j-L_j\le h,
 \qquad
 2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(f_P=2^{-d}\) almost everywhere. Under the change of variables \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\), one has \(du=V_{x,h}\,dz\). The orthonormality of \(B_k\) on \([0,1]^d\) and the definition of \(b_{x,h,k}\) therefore give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Omega_{x,h,k}
 &=2^{-d}V_{x,h}^{-1}\int_{W_{x,h}}
 B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du\\
 &=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}B_k(z)B_k(z)^\top\,dz
 =2^{-d}I_k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The same change of variables and the orthonormality of the shifted-Legendre basis yield
\[
 \int_{W_{x,h}}K_{x,h}(u)r_{x,h,p}(u)r_{x,h,p}(u)^\top\,du=I_{J_p}.
\]
Strict overlap implies
\[
 c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\le \pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}\le\frac14.
\]
Multiplying the preceding identity by the uniform density and these scalar bounds proves
\[
 2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
 \preceq Q_{x,h}
 \preceq 2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
\]
The corrected known-density construction sets \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=2^{-d}I_k\), so \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=\Omega_{x,h,k}\) identically.

Put
\[
 q_-=2^{-d-1}c_\pi(1-c_\pi),
 \qquad q_+=2^{-d-1},
\]
and let \(\mathcal C=\{M=M^\top:q_-I_{J_p}\preceq M\preceq q_+I_{J_p}\}\). The displayed bounds place \(Q_{x,h}\) in \(\mathcal C\). Spectral clipping \(T_{[q_-,q_+]}\) is the Frobenius-metric projection onto this closed convex set. Thus, for
\[
 S=\{\widetilde Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}+\widetilde Q^{(\ell)\top}_{x,h}\}/2,
\]
nonexpansiveness of a metric projection gives
\[
 \|\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\|_F
 =\|\Pi_{\mathcal C}(S)-\Pi_{\mathcal C}(Q_{x,h})\|_F
 \le \|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\]
Every eigenvalue of \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}\) is in \([q_-,q_+]\), so
\[
 \|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le q_-^{-1}.
\]
The lower population bound also gives \(\|Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le q_-^{-1}\). Applying the resolvent identity and \(\|M\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\|M\|_F\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 &\le \|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \|\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \|Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\\
 &\le q_-^{-2}\|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\end{aligned}
\]
All conclusions are deterministic, so no empirical good event or complement estimate is needed.
