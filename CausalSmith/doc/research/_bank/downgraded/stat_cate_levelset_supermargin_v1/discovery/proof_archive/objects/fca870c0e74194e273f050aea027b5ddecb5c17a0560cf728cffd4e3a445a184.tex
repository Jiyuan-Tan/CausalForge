Fix a law in the calibrated transverse model, a point \(x\in\mathcal X=[-1,1]^d\), and \(0<h\le 1\). For each coordinate, \([L_j,R_j]=[-1,1]\cap[x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\). Truncation cannot increase length, so \(\ell_j\le h\). Moreover, at least one of the two distances \(x_j+1\) and \(1-x_j\) is at least one, and hence at least \(h/2\). The corresponding half of \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) is therefore not truncated, which gives \(\ell_j\ge h/2\). Multiplication over \(j=1,\ldots,d\) yields
\[
 2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
\]

By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(f_P=2^{-d}\) almost everywhere on \(\mathcal X\). Make the change of variables \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\) on \(W_{x,h}\); its Jacobian is \(du=V_{x,h}\,dz\). The definition of \(b_{x,h,k}\), followed by orthonormality of \(B_k\) on \([0,1]^d\), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Omega_{x,h,k}
 &=2^{-d}\int_{W_{x,h}}V_{x,h}^{-1}
 B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du\\
 &=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}B_k(z)B_k(z)^\top\,dz
 =2^{-d}I_k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The corrected known-density construction sets \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=2^{-d}I_k\) on every training complement. Consequently,
\[
 \widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=\Omega_{x,h,k}.
\]

Next fix \(w\in\mathbb R^{J_p}\). Using \(K_{x,h}=V_{x,h}^{-1}\mathbf 1_{W_{x,h}}\), the same change of variables, and \(r_{x,h,p}(u)=\rho_p(T_{x,h}(u))\), we obtain
\[
 w^\top Q_{x,h}w
 =2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}
 \{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2
 \pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\{1-\pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\}\,dz.
\]
Strict overlap implies
\[
 c_\pi(1-c_\pi)
 \le \pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}
 \le \frac14
 \qquad (u\in\mathcal X).
\]
Since \(\rho_p\) is orthonormal on \([0,1]^d\),
\[
 \int_{[0,1]^d}\{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2\,dz=\|w\|_2^2.
\]
It follows for every \(w\) that
\[
 2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\|w\|_2^2
 \le w^\top Q_{x,h}w
 \le 2^{-d}\frac14\|w\|_2^2,
\]
which is equivalent to
\[
 2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
 \preceq Q_{x,h}
 \preceq 2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
\]

Let
\[
 S=\frac{\widetilde Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}+\widetilde Q^{(\ell)\top}_{x,h}}2,
 \qquad a=q_-=2^{-d-1}c_\pi(1-c_\pi),
 \qquad b=q_+=2^{-d-1}.
\]
On every fold on which the raw matrix is formed, the definition gives \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}=T_{[a,b]}(S)\). Thus every eigenvalue of \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}\) belongs to \([a,b]\), and in particular
\[
 \|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le a^{-1}=q_-^{-1}.
\]
If the construction invokes its stated fallback because a raw matrix is nonfinite or a fold has fewer than two observations, no matrix inverse is evaluated on that fold.

It remains to prove the perturbation inequality. The population bounds already obtained imply
\[
 2aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq \frac b2 I_{J_p},
\]
so in particular \(aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq bI_{J_p}\) and \(\|Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le a^{-1}\). We now verify directly that clipping cannot increase Frobenius distance from such a matrix. Write \(S=V\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{J_p})V^\top\) and put \(Q'=V^\top Q_{x,h}V\). Every diagonal entry \(Q'_{rr}\), being a Rayleigh quotient of \(Q_{x,h}\), lies in \([a,b]\). For the scalar projection \(p_{[a,b]}(s)=\min\{b,\max\{a,s\}\}\),
\[
 |p_{[a,b]}(\lambda_r)-Q'_{rr}|
 \le |\lambda_r-Q'_{rr}|.
\]
The off-diagonal entries of both \(V^\top SV\) and \(V^\top T_{[a,b]}(S)V\) are zero. Summing the squared diagonal and off-diagonal differences therefore gives
\[
 \|\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\|_F
 \le \|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\]
Finally, the inverse identity
\[
 [\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}
 = [\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}
 (Q_{x,h}-\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h})Q_{x,h}^{-1}
\]
and submultiplicativity imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 &\le a^{-2}\|\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\\
 &\le a^{-2}\|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F
 =q_-^{-2}\|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves all the asserted bounds.
