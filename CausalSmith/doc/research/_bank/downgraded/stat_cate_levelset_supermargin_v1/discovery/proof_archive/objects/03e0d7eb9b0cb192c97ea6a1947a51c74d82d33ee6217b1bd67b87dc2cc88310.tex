The calibrated witness shows that the model is nonempty, and \(\mathrm{thm:identified-version}\) identifies its target modulo irrelevant null sets.

For the upper bound, the corrected construction is one law-independent, sample-measurable estimator. By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(\mathrm{lem:local-gram}\), \(\mathrm{lem:sieve-approximation}\), and \(\mathrm{lem:estimator-construction}\), it satisfies uniformly all conditions of the cited level-set upper theorem. Applying \(\mathrm{lem:bkk-level-set-upper-cited}\) with \(\xi=1\) gives
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)}
\mathbb E_P[d_H(\widehat G_n^{\mathrm{LP}},G_P;P)]
\le C_1(r_n^*)^2
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\), in both branches of \(r_n^*\). Taking the infimum over all measurable set estimators preserves this upper bound.

For the lower bound, \(\mathrm{lem:transverse-assouad-lower}\) constructs, within the same calibrated model, a direct Assouad experiment in the high-smoothness branch and a fuzzy Assouad experiment in the low-smoothness branch. It yields
\[
\inf_{\widehat G_n}\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)}
\mathbb E_P[d_H(\widehat G_n,G_P;P)]
\ge c_1(r_n^*)^2.
\]
Choose \(c_1\) smaller if necessary and \(C_1\) larger if necessary so that \(0<c_1<C_1<\infty\). Their dependence is only on the fixed model parameters, not on \(n\). This proves the two inequalities and the asserted attainment.
