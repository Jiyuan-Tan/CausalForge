Fix \(q\in\{\alpha,\beta,\bar s\}\). By \(\mathrm{def:average-smoothness}\), \(\bar s=(\alpha+\beta)/2\le \max\{\alpha,\beta\}\). Hence \(q\le \max\{\alpha,\beta\}\), and the choice in \(\mathrm{def:spline-basis}\) gives
\[
r_B\ge \left\lceil\max\{\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\gamma'\}\right\rceil+1>q.
\]
Put \(s_q=\lceil q\rceil-1\). Thus \(s_q\le r_B-1\), so the univariate spline space of order \(r_B\) reproduces every polynomial of degree at most \(s_q\), and its tensor product reproduces every coordinatewise polynomial of degree at most \(s_q\).

We first record the elementary approximation property of this tensor spline space. Write \(\Delta=m^{-1}\) for the grid width and let \(\{N_\nu\}\) denote the usual nonnegative tensor-product B-splines before orthonormalization. There is a local linear quasi-interpolant
\[
Q_m f=\sum_\nu \lambda_\nu(f)N_\nu
\]
with the following three properties: it reproduces coordinatewise polynomials of degree at most \(s_q\); each \(\lambda_\nu\) uses only values of \(f\) in the union of at most \(r_B\) adjacent cells meeting \(\operatorname{supp}(N_\nu)\); and
\[
|Q_m f(x)|\le C_{d,r_B,q}\sup_{\|u-x\|_\infty\le C_{d,r_B}\Delta}|f(u)|.
\]
For completeness, these properties follow directly by choosing, on each univariate support, \(s_q+1\) equally spaced grid points and selecting the coefficients of \(\lambda_\nu\) so that the identities hold for the monomials \(1,z,\ldots,z^{s_q}\). After rescaling a support to a fixed interval, the resulting Vandermonde matrix is fixed and nonsingular, so its inverse has a norm independent of \(m\) and of the support index. At the two boundaries one uses the first or last \(s_q+1\) grid points. Tensorizing these univariate functionals proves polynomial reproduction, locality, and the displayed stability inequality, with constants depending only on \(d,r_B,q\).

Let \(g\in\mathsf H_q(1;[0,1]^d)\). For each \(x\in[0,1]^d\), the Taylor-remainder condition in \(\mathrm{def:holder-ball}\) supplies a polynomial \(P_x\) of total degree at most \(s_q\) such that
\[
|g(u)-P_x(u)|\le C_{d,q}\|u-x\|_\infty^q
\]
whenever \(u\in[0,1]^d\). Polynomial reproduction and local stability therefore give
\[
\begin{aligned}
|Q_mg(x)-g(x)|
&=|Q_m(g-P_x)(x)-(g-P_x)(x)|\\
&\le C_{d,r_B,q}\sup_{\|u-x\|_\infty\le C_{d,r_B}\Delta}|g(u)-P_x(u)|\\
&\le C'_{d,r_B,q}\Delta^q.
\end{aligned}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\|Q_mg-g\|_{L_2([0,1]^d)}\le C'_{d,r_B,q}m^{-q}.
\]
Orthonormalizing the tensor B-splines changes their coordinates but not their linear span. Hence there is a coefficient vector \(v_q\in\mathbb R^k\) for which \(Q_mg=v_q^\top B_k\). Since \(k=m^d\),
\[
\inf_v\|g-v^\top B_k\|_{L_2([0,1]^d)}
\le \|g-v_q^\top B_k\|_{L_2([0,1]^d)}
\le C'_{d,r_B,q}k^{-q/d}.
\]
The set \(\{\alpha,\beta,\bar s\}\) is finite. Taking \(C_{\mathrm{app}}\) to be the maximum of the three constants proves the asserted bound simultaneously for all three values of \(q\). The calibration and bounded-density conditions merely identify the normalized pushforward domain and do not alter this deterministic approximation calculation.

Applying the bound with \(q=\alpha\) and \(q=\beta\), the product of the two approximation errors has order
\[
k^{-\alpha/d}k^{-\beta/d}=k^{-(\alpha+\beta)/d}.
\]
Finally, \(\alpha+\beta=2\bar s\) by \(\mathrm{def:average-smoothness}\), so this order is \(k^{-2\bar s/d}\), as claimed.
