Under calibration, \(\mathcal X=[-1,1]^d\), \(c_f=C_f=2^{-d}\), and every member of \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\) satisfies \(\mathrm{ass:bounded-density}\). Consequently
\[
2^{-d}\le f_P(x)\le2^{-d}
\]
for Lebesgue-almost every \(x\in\mathcal X\), so \(f_P(x)=2^{-d}=F_0(x)\) almost everywhere. Extending the densities by zero outside \(\mathcal X\) preserves equality. Hence \(f_P/F_0=1\) wherever \(F_0>0\), the density-ratio error is zero, and its essential supremum is one.
