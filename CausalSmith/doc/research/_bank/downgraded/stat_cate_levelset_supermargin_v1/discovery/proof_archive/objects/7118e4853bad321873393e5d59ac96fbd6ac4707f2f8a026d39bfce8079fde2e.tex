Fix a law \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\), a point \(x\in\mathcal X=[-1,1]^d\), and \(0<h\le 1\). For each \(j\in\{1,\ldots,d\}\), the coordinate window is
+\[
+[L_j,R_j]=[-1,1]\cap[x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2].
+\]
+Truncation gives \(\ell_j=R_j-L_j\le h\). At least one of the distances \(x_j+1\) and \(1-x_j\) is at least one, hence at least \(h/2\). The corresponding half of \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) is not truncated, so \(\ell_j\ge h/2\). Multiplication over the coordinates yields
+\[
+2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
+\]
+
+By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(f_P=2^{-d}\) Lebesgue-almost everywhere on \(\mathcal X\). Under \(\mathrm{def:window-basis}\), the affine change of variables \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\) maps \(W_{x,h}\) bijectively to \([0,1]^d\) and has Jacobian \(du=V_{x,h}\,dz\). Therefore \(\mathrm{def:spline-basis}\), \(\mathrm{def:local-matrices}\), and the orthonormality of \(B_k\) give
+\[
+\begin{aligned}
+\Omega_{x,h,k}
+&=2^{-d}\int_{W_{x,h}}V_{x,h}^{-1}B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du\\
+&=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}B_k(z)B_k(z)^\top\,dz
+=2^{-d}I_k.
+\end{aligned}
+\]
+The known-density construction in \(\mathrm{def:boundary-adapted-estimator}\) sets \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=2^{-d}I_k\) on every training complement. Hence
+\[
+\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=\Omega_{x,h,k}.
+\]
+
+Next fix \(w\in\mathbb R^{J_p}\). Using \(K_{x,h}=V_{x,h}^{-1}\mathbf 1_{W_{x,h}}\), \(r_{x,h,p}(u)=\rho_p(T_{x,h}(u))\), and the same change of variables, we obtain
+\[
+w^\top Q_{x,h}w
+=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}
+\{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2
+\pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\{1-\pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\}\,dz.
+\]
+Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:strict-overlap}\) implies
+\[
+c_\pi(1-c_\pi)
+\le \pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}
+\le \frac14
+\qquad (u\in\mathcal X).
+\]
+Since the shifted-Legendre basis \(\rho_p\) is orthonormal on \([0,1]^d\),
+\[
+\int_{[0,1]^d}\{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2\,dz=\lVert w\rVert_2^2.
+\]
+Consequently,
+\[
+2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\lVert w\rVert_2^2
+\le w^\top Q_{x,h}w
+\le 2^{-d}\frac14\lVert w\rVert_2^2.
+\]
+Because this holds for every \(w\),
+\[
+2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
+\preceq Q_{x,h}
+\preceq 2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
+\]
+
+Put
+\[
+S=\frac{\widetilde Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}+\widetilde Q^{(\ell)\top}_{x,h}}2,
+\qquad
+a=q_-=2^{-d-1}c_\pi(1-c_\pi),
+\qquad
+b=q_+=2^{-d-1}.
+\]
+Whenever the raw fold matrix is formed, \(\mathrm{def:boundary-adapted-estimator}\) gives \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}=T_{[a,b]}(S)\). Thus every eigenvalue of \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}\) lies in \([a,b]\), so it is invertible and
+\[
+\bigl\lVert[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\bigr\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
+\le a^{-1}=q_-^{-1}.
+\]
+If the construction invokes its stated fallback because a raw matrix is nonfinite or a fold contains fewer than two observations, no matrix inverse is evaluated on that fold.
+
+It remains to establish the inverse perturbation bound. The population Gram bounds imply
+\[
+2aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq (b/2)I_{J_p}.
+\]
+In particular, \(aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq bI_{J_p}\) and \(\lVert Q_{x,h}^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le a^{-1}\). We first show that spectral clipping cannot increase Frobenius distance from \(Q_{x,h}\). Write
+\[
+S=V\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{J_p})V^\top,
+\qquad Q'=V^\top Q_{x,h}V.
+\]
+Every diagonal entry \(Q'_{rr}\) is a Rayleigh quotient of \(Q_{x,h}\), and therefore belongs to \([a,b]\). For the scalar projection \(p_{[a,b]}(s)=\min\{b,\max\{a,s\}\}\),
+\[
+|p_{[a,b]}(\lambda_r)-Q'_{rr}|
+\le |\lambda_r-Q'_{rr}|.
+\]
+In the basis given by \(V\), both \(S\) and \(T_{[a,b]}(S)\) have zero off-diagonal entries, whereas the off-diagonal entries of \(Q'\) are unchanged in the two squared Frobenius distances. Summing the diagonal and off-diagonal squared differences, and using orthogonal invariance of the Frobenius norm, gives
+\[
+\lVert\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F
+\le \lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F.
+\]
+Finally, the resolvent identity gives
+\[
+[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}
+=[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}
+(Q_{x,h}-\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h})Q_{x,h}^{-1}.
+\]
+Submultiplicativity, the two inverse-norm bounds, \(\lVert M\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le\lVert M\rVert_F\), and the clipping contraction just proved yield
+\[
+\begin{aligned}
+\bigl\lVert[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}\bigr\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
+&\le a^{-2}\lVert\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\\
+&\le a^{-2}\lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F\\
+&=q_-^{-2}\lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F.
+\end{aligned}
+\]
+This proves every assertion.
