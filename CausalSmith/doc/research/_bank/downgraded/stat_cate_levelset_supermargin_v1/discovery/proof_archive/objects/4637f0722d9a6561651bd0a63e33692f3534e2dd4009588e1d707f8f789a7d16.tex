For all sufficiently large \(n\),
+\[
+c n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
+\le \mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)
+\le C n^{-2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}/\{2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}+d\}}.
+\]
+When \(\beta\ge\gamma\), the high-smoothness branch applies, both endpoints equal \((r_n^*)^2\) in order, and therefore \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\). When \(\beta<\gamma\), this bracket is the strongest result currently proved on the calibrated uniform-design class.
