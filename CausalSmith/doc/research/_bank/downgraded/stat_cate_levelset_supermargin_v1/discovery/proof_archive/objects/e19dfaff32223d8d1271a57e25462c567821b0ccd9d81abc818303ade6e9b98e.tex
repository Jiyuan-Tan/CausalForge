Under \(\mathrm{def:transverse-witness}\), \(X\) is uniform on \([-1,1]^d\), and the three auxiliary uniforms are conditionally independent given \(X\). Therefore \(A\) is conditionally independent of \((Y^0,Y^1)\), \(P(A=1\mid X=x)=1/2\), and \(Y=Y^A\) by construction. Moreover,
\[
\mu_0(x)=P(U_0\le 1/2)=\frac12,
\qquad
\mu_1(x)=P(U_1\le 1/2+cx_1)=\frac12+cx_1.
\]
The constraint \(0<c<1/4\) makes both Bernoulli probabilities lie in \([1/4,3/4]\), and \(Y,Y^0,Y^1\in[0,1]\subset[-M_Y,M_Y]\) under calibration. Hence
\[
\pi(x)=\frac12,\qquad \tau_{P^{\mathrm{tr}}}(x)=\mu_1(x)-\mu_0(x)=cx_1.
\]
The uniform density is \(2^{-d}=c_f=C_f\), and \(c_\pi=1/4\) verifies strict overlap. Constant functions \(1/2\) lie in the calibrated propensity and control-regression Hölder balls. The linear function \(x\mapsto cx_1\) is in \(\mathsf H_\gamma(1;[-1,1]^d)\) for every \(\gamma>0\): its derivatives of order at least two vanish, and its sup-norm and first-derivative norm are bounded by a fixed multiple of \(c<1/4\). Since \(\eta=c\) and \(|cx_1|\le c\),
\[
\mathcal D_{P^{\mathrm{tr}}}(c)=[-1,1]^d,
\]
so the off-band condition is vacuous for every \(\gamma'>0\).

For \(0<t\le c\), the first coordinate \(X_1\) is uniform on \([-1,1]\), whence
\[
P_X\{0<|\tau_{P^{\mathrm{tr}}}(X)-\theta|\le t\}
=P\{0<|X_1|\le t/c\}=\frac{t}{c}.
\]
This simultaneously verifies the lower and upper margin conditions with \(\xi=1\) and \(c_m=C_m=c^{-1}\). Finally, the points with first coordinates \(-1/2\) and \(1/2\), and all other coordinates zero, lie in the interior and have CATEs of opposite sign. All member conditions in \(\mathrm{def:smooth-model}\) are therefore satisfied. By \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\), the induced observed law belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\) for every \(\gamma>d\).
