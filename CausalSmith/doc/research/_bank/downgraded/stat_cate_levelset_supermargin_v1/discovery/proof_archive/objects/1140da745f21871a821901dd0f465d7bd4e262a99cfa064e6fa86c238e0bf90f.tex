For every fixed calibrated \(\kappa\), every \(\gamma>d\), and all sufficiently large \(n\), the surviving results prove
\[
c_{\mathrm{lb}}n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\le \mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\le C_{\mathrm{ub}}n^{-2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}/\{2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}+d\}}.
\]
Consequently, if \(\beta\ge\gamma\), the high-smoothness branch applies and \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\).
