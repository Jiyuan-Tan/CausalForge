Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\), \(x\in\mathcal X=[-1,1]^d\), and \(0<h\le1\). For each \(j\in\{1,\ldots,d\}\), \(\mathrm{def:window-basis}\) gives
\[
[L_j,R_j]=[-1,1]\cap[x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2].
\]
Truncation immediately gives \(\ell_j=R_j-L_j\le h\). The two distances from \(x_j\) to the endpoints of \([-1,1]\) sum to two, so at least one is at least one and hence at least \(h/2\). The corresponding half of \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) is not truncated. Therefore
\[
 h/2\le \ell_j\le h.
\]
Multiplying these inequalities over \(j=1,\ldots,d\) yields
\[
 2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
\]

By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(f_P=2^{-d}\) Lebesgue-almost everywhere on \(\mathcal X\). The affine change of variables \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\) maps \(W_{x,h}\) bijectively onto \([0,1]^d\) and has Jacobian \(du=V_{x,h}\,dz\). Hence \(\mathrm{def:spline-basis}\), \(\mathrm{def:local-matrices}\), and the orthonormality of \(B_k\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Omega_{x,h,k}
&=2^{-d}\int_{W_{x,h}}V_{x,h}^{-1}B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du\\
&=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}B_k(z)B_k(z)^\top\,dz
 =2^{-d}I_k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The known-density construction in \(\mathrm{def:boundary-adapted-estimator}\) sets \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=2^{-d}I_k\) on every training complement. Consequently,
\[
 \widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=\Omega_{x,h,k}.
\]

We next bound the local Gram matrix. Fix \(w\in\mathbb R^{J_p}\). Using \(K_{x,h}=V_{x,h}^{-1}\mathbf 1_{W_{x,h}}\), \(r_{x,h,p}(u)=\rho_p(T_{x,h}(u))\), the uniform density, and the same change of variables,
\[
 w^\top Q_{x,h}w
 =2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}
 \{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2
 \pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\{1-\pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\}\,dz.
\]
Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:strict-overlap}\) implies
\[
 c_\pi(1-c_\pi)
 \le \pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}
 \le \frac14
 \qquad(u\in\mathcal X).
\]
Since the shifted-Legendre basis \(\rho_p\) is orthonormal on \([0,1]^d\),
\[
 \int_{[0,1]^d}\{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2\,dz=\lVert w\rVert_2^2.
\]
It follows that
\[
 2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\lVert w\rVert_2^2
 \le w^\top Q_{x,h}w
 \le 2^{-d}\frac14\lVert w\rVert_2^2.
\]
Because this holds for every \(w\),
\[
 2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
 \preceq Q_{x,h}
 \preceq 2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
\]

Put
\[
 S=\frac{\widetilde Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}+\widetilde Q^{(\ell)\top}_{x,h}}2,
 \qquad a=q_-=2^{-d-1}c_\pi(1-c_\pi),
 \qquad b=q_+=2^{-d-1}.
\]
Whenever the raw fold matrix is formed, \(\mathrm{def:boundary-adapted-estimator}\) gives \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}=T_{[a,b]}(S)\). Thus every eigenvalue of \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}\) lies in \([a,b]=[q_-,q_+]\), and
\[
 \bigl\lVert[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\bigr\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le a^{-1}=q_-^{-1}.
\]
If the construction invokes its stated fallback because a raw matrix is nonfinite or a fold has fewer than two observations, it returns \(\theta\) on that fold and does not evaluate a matrix inverse.

It remains to prove the inverse perturbation inequality. The population Gram bounds already established imply
\[
 2aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq (b/2)I_{J_p}.
\]
In particular, \(aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq bI_{J_p}\) and \(\lVert Q_{x,h}^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le a^{-1}\). Spectral clipping cannot increase Frobenius distance from such a matrix. Indeed, write
\[
 S=V\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{J_p})V^\top,
 \qquad Q'=V^\top Q_{x,h}V.
\]
Every diagonal entry \(Q'_{rr}\) is a Rayleigh quotient of \(Q_{x,h}\), so \(Q'_{rr}\in[a,b]\). For the scalar projection \(p_{[a,b]}(s)=\min\{b,\max\{a,s\}\}\),
\[
 |p_{[a,b]}(\lambda_r)-Q'_{rr}|
 \le |\lambda_r-Q'_{rr}|.
\]
In the basis determined by \(V\), both \(S\) and \(T_{[a,b]}(S)\) are diagonal, while the off-diagonal entries of their respective differences from \(Q'\) coincide. Summing the squared diagonal and off-diagonal entries and using orthogonal invariance of the Frobenius norm gives
\[
 \lVert\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F
 \le \lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F.
\]
Finally, the resolvent identity is
\[
 [\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}
 = [\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}
 (Q_{x,h}-\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h})Q_{x,h}^{-1}.
\]
Submultiplicativity, the two inverse-norm bounds, \(\lVert M\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le\lVert M\rVert_F\), and the clipping contraction yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \bigl\lVert[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}\bigr\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 &\le a^{-2}\lVert\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\\
 &\le a^{-2}\lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F\\
 &=q_-^{-2}\lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves all assertions.
