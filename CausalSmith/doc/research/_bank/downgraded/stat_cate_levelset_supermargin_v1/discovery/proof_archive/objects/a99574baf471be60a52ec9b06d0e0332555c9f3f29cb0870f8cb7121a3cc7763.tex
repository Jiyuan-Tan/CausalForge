Suppose first that \((\xi,\gamma)\in\mathcal F(\kappa)\). By \(\mathrm{def:feasible-frontier}\), \(\xi\gamma>d\) and there exists \(P\in\mathcal M(\xi,\gamma;\kappa)\). The compatibility lemma \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-margin-compatibility}\) therefore gives \(\xi=1\) and \(\gamma>d\). This proves
\[
\mathcal F(\kappa)\subseteq\{(1,\gamma):\gamma>d\}.
\]

Conversely, fix \(\gamma>d\). By \(\mathrm{thm:calibrated-witness}\), the observed marginal induced by \(P^{\mathrm{tr}}\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\). By \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\), this class equals \(\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)\). It is therefore nonempty, and \(1\cdot\gamma>d\); hence \((1,\gamma)\in\mathcal F(\kappa)\). Thus
\[
\mathcal F(\kappa)=\{(1,\gamma):\gamma>d\}.
\]
For any point on this frontier, \(\xi=1\), and the identity \(\mathcal M(\xi,\gamma;\kappa)=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\) follows directly from \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\).
