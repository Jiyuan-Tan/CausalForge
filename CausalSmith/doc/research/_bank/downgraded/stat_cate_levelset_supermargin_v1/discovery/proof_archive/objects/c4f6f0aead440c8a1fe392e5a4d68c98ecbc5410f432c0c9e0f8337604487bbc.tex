Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\), \(x\in\mathcal X=[-1,1]^d\), and \(0<h\le1\). For each \(j\in\{1,\ldots,d\}\), \(\mathrm{def:window-basis}\) gives
\[
 [L_j,R_j]=[-1,1]\cap[x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2].
\]
Truncation immediately gives \(\ell_j=R_j-L_j\le h\). At least one of \(x_j+1\) and \(1-x_j\) is at least one, and hence at least \(h/2\). The corresponding half of \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) is not truncated, so \(\ell_j\ge h/2\). Multiplying these inequalities over \(j\) yields
\[
 2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
\]

By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(f_P=2^{-d}\) Lebesgue-almost everywhere on \(\mathcal X\). The affine transformation \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\) maps \(W_{x,h}\) bijectively onto \([0,1]^d\) and has Jacobian \(du=V_{x,h}\,dz\). Therefore \(\mathrm{def:spline-basis}\), \(\mathrm{def:local-matrices}\), and the orthonormality of \(B_k\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Omega_{x,h,k}
 &=2^{-d}\int_{W_{x,h}}V_{x,h}^{-1}B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du\\
 &=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}B_k(z)B_k(z)^\top\,dz
 =2^{-d}I_k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The known-density construction in \(\mathrm{def:boundary-adapted-estimator}\) sets \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=2^{-d}I_k\) on every training complement. Hence
\[
 \widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=\Omega_{x,h,k}.
\]

Next fix \(w\in\mathbb R^{J_p}\). Using \(K_{x,h}=V_{x,h}^{-1}\mathbf 1_{W_{x,h}}\), \(r_{x,h,p}(u)=\rho_p(T_{x,h}(u))\), the uniform density, and the same change of variables gives
\[
 w^\top Q_{x,h}w
 =2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}\{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2
 \pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\{1-\pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\}\,dz.
\]
Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:strict-overlap}\) implies
\[
 c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\le\pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}\le\frac14
 \qquad(u\in\mathcal X).
\]
Because the shifted-Legendre basis \(\rho_p\) is orthonormal on \([0,1]^d\),
\[
 \int_{[0,1]^d}\{w^\top\rho_p(z)\}^2\,dz=\lVert w\rVert_2^2.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\lVert w\rVert_2^2
 \le w^\top Q_{x,h}w
 \le 2^{-d}\frac14\lVert w\rVert_2^2.
\]
Since this holds for every \(w\),
\[
 2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
 \preceq Q_{x,h}
 \preceq 2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
\]

Put
\[
 S=\frac{\widetilde Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}+\widetilde Q^{(\ell)\top}_{x,h}}2,
 \qquad a=q_-=2^{-d-1}c_\pi(1-c_\pi),
 \qquad b=q_+=2^{-d-1}.
\]
For every fold on which the raw matrix is formed, \(\mathrm{def:boundary-adapted-estimator}\) gives \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}=T_{[a,b]}(S)\). Thus every eigenvalue of \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}\) lies in \([a,b]\), so the matrix is invertible and
\[
 \bigl\lVert[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\bigr\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le a^{-1}=q_-^{-1}.
\]
If the construction invokes its stated fallback because a raw matrix is nonfinite or a fold has fewer than two observations, no matrix inverse is evaluated on that fold, so the bound covers every inverse used by the estimator.

It remains to prove the inverse perturbation inequality. The population Gram bounds already established imply
\[
 2aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq(b/2)I_{J_p}.
\]
In particular, \(aI_{J_p}\preceq Q_{x,h}\preceq bI_{J_p}\) and \(\lVert Q_{x,h}^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le a^{-1}\). Spectral clipping cannot increase Frobenius distance from such a matrix. Indeed, write
\[
 S=V\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{J_p})V^\top,
 \qquad Q'=V^\top Q_{x,h}V.
\]
Every diagonal entry \(Q'_{rr}\) is a Rayleigh quotient of \(Q_{x,h}\), hence belongs to \([a,b]\). For the scalar projection \(p_{[a,b]}(s)=\min\{b,\max\{a,s\}\}\),
\[
 |p_{[a,b]}(\lambda_r)-Q'_{rr}|\le|\lambda_r-Q'_{rr}|.
\]
In the basis defined by \(V\), both \(S\) and \(T_{[a,b]}(S)\) have zero off-diagonal entries, while the off-diagonal contributions from \(Q'\) are identical in the two squared Frobenius distances. Summing the squared entries and using orthogonal invariance gives
\[
 \lVert\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F
 \le\lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F.
\]
Finally, the resolvent identity is
\[
 [\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}
 = [\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}
 (Q_{x,h}-\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h})Q_{x,h}^{-1}.
\]
Submultiplicativity, the two inverse-norm bounds, \(\lVert M\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le\lVert M\rVert_F\), and the clipping contraction yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \bigl\lVert[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}\bigr\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 &\le a^{-2}\lVert\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\\
 &\le a^{-2}\lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F\\
 &=q_-^{-2}\lVert S-Q_{x,h}\rVert_F.
\end{aligned}
\]
All displayed conclusions are deterministic conditional on the realized folds; \(\mathrm{ass:iid-sampling}\) supplies the sampling setup in which the fold statistics are defined. This proves the lemma.
