By \(\mathrm{thm:calibrated-frontier-feasibility}\), the calibrated feasible super-margin laws have \(\xi=1\) and \(\gamma>d\). For each such fixed \(\gamma\), the first bracket and the assertion for \(\beta\ge\gamma\) are exactly \(\mathrm{prop:calibrated-direct-rate-bracket}\). If \(\beta<\gamma\), then \(\min\{\beta,\gamma\}=\beta\), so substituting this identity into the upper endpoint of that proposition gives
\[
c_{\mathrm{lb}}n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
\le\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)
\le C_{\mathrm{ub}}n^{-2\beta/(2\beta+d)}.
\]
Renaming the two fixed comparison constants as \(c\) and \(C\) gives the statement. This last display is a partial bracket corollary; it does not assert that either endpoint is sharp when \(\beta<\gamma\).
