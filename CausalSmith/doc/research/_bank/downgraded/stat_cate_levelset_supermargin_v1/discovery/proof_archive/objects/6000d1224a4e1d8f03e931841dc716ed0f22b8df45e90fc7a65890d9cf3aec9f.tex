The proved proposition \(\mathrm{prop:calibrated-direct-rate-bracket}\) gives, for all sufficiently large \(n\), \[c n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\le \mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\le C n^{-2\beta/(2\beta+d)}\] whenever \(\beta<\gamma\). It also settles the complementary regime \(\beta\ge\gamma\), where \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\). In the residual \(\beta<\gamma\) regime, both remaining high-smoothness configurations and every low-smoothness case remain unresolved.
