For every fixed calibrated \(\kappa\), every \(\gamma>d\), and all sufficiently large \(n\), \(c n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\le\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\le C n^{-2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}/\{2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}+d\}}\). Consequently, if \(\beta\ge\gamma\), the high-smoothness branch applies and \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\). The unresolved regime is exactly \(\beta<\gamma\).
