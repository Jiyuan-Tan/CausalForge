For \(x_j\in[-1,1]\) and \(0<h\le1\), the interval \([x_j-h/2,x_j+h/2]\) has length \(h\). Intersecting it with \([-1,1]\) can remove at most one half of the interval because its center remains in \([-1,1]\). Thus
\[
\frac h2\le\ell_j\le h,
\qquad
2^{-d}h^d\le V_{x,h}=\prod_{j=1}^d\ell_j\le h^d.
\]

By \(\mathrm{lem:uniform-design}\), \(X\) has density \(2^{-d}\) on \(\mathcal X\). The change of variables \(z=T_{x,h}(u)\) has Jacobian \(V_{x,h}\). Using \(\mathrm{def:spline-basis}\) and the orthonormality of \(B_k\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Omega_{x,h,k}
&=2^{-d}\int_{W_{x,h}}V_{x,h}^{-1}
B_k(T_{x,h}(u))B_k(T_{x,h}(u))^\top\,du\\
&=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}B_k(z)B_k(z)^\top\,dz
=2^{-d}I_k.
\end{aligned}
\]
The same change of variables, now using \(K_{x,h}=V_{x,h}^{-1}\mathbf 1_{W_{x,h}}\), gives
\[
Q_{x,h}=2^{-d}\int_{[0,1]^d}
\rho_p(z)\rho_p(z)^\top
\pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\{1-\pi(T_{x,h}^{-1}(z))\}\,dz.
\]
Strict overlap implies
\[
c_\pi(1-c_\pi)\le\pi(u)\{1-\pi(u)\}\le\frac14.
\]
Because \(\rho_p\) is orthonormal on \([0,1]^d\), integration of \(\rho_p\rho_p^\top\) gives \(I_{J_p}\), and therefore
\[
2^{-d}c_\pi(1-c_\pi)I_{J_p}
\preceq Q_{x,h}
\preceq2^{-d}I_{J_p}/4.
\]

The known-density construction in \(\mathrm{def:boundary-adapted-estimator}\) sets \(\widehat\Omega^{(-\ell)}_{x,h,k}=2^{-d}I_k\), which equals the population matrix just computed. Write
\[
S=\frac{\widetilde Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}+\widetilde Q^{(\ell)\top}_{x,h}}2.
\]
By definition, \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}=T_{[q_-,q_+]}(S)\). Hence every eigenvalue of \(\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}\) lies in \([q_-,q_+]\), and
\[
\|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le q_-^{-1}.
\]
The spectrum of \(Q_{x,h}\) lies inside the same clipping interval because its lower endpoint is at least \(2q_-\) and its upper endpoint is at most \(2^{-d}/4<q_+\). Spectral clipping is the metric projection in Frobenius norm onto the closed convex set of symmetric matrices with spectrum in \([q_-,q_+]\). Since \(Q_{x,h}\) belongs to that set,
\[
\|\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}-Q_{x,h}\|_F
\le\|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\]
Finally, the resolvent identity gives
\[
[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}
=[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}
(Q_{x,h}-\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h})Q_{x,h}^{-1}.
\]
Both inverse operator norms are at most \(q_-^{-1}\), so
\[
\|[\widehat Q^{(\ell)}_{x,h}]^{-1}-Q_{x,h}^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le q_-^{-2}\|S-Q_{x,h}\|_F.
\]
This proves every asserted deterministic bound.
