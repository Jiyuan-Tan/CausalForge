Fix the calibrated value of \(\kappa\). We first prove the nontrivial inclusion. Let \((\xi,\gamma)\in\mathcal F(\kappa)\). By \(\mathrm{def:feasible-frontier}\),
+\[
+\xi\gamma>d
+\qquad\text{and}\qquad
+\mathcal M(\xi,\gamma;\kappa)\ne\varnothing.
+\]
+Choose \(P\in\mathcal M(\xi,\gamma;\kappa)\). The calibration-specific compatibility result \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-margin-compatibility}\) applies to this member and gives
+\[
+\xi=1
+\qquad\text{and}\qquad
+\gamma>d.
+\]
+Therefore
+\[
+\mathcal F(\kappa)\subseteq\{(1,\gamma):\gamma>d\}.
+\tag{1}
+\]
+
+For the reverse inclusion, fix any \(\gamma>d\). By \(\mathrm{thm:calibrated-witness}\), the observed marginal induced by \(P^{\mathrm{tr}}\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\). By \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\),
+\[
+\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)=\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa).
+\]
+Consequently \(\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)\ne\varnothing\). Since also \(1\cdot\gamma>d\), \(\mathrm{def:feasible-frontier}\) yields
+\[
+(1,\gamma)\in\mathcal F(\kappa).
+\]
+Together with (1), this proves
+\[
+\mathcal F(\kappa)=\{(1,\gamma):\gamma>d\}.
+\]
+
+Finally, if \((\xi,\gamma)\in\mathcal F(\kappa)\), the equality just proved gives \(\xi=1\) and \(\gamma>d\). Substitution into \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\) gives
+\[
+\mathcal M(\xi,\gamma;\kappa)
+=\mathcal M(1,\gamma;\kappa)
+=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa).
+\]
+Every step in both inclusions uses \(\mathrm{def:calibration}\), either through \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-margin-compatibility}\) or through \(\mathrm{thm:calibrated-witness}\). Thus the displayed frontier is a compatibility result for the fixed calibrated constants, not a claim about an uncalibrated law class.
