Under \(\mathrm{def:transverse-witness}\), \(X\) is uniform on \([-1,1]^d\), and \(U_A,U_0,U_1\) are mutually independent conditionally on \(X\). Hence \(A\) is conditionally independent of \((Y^0,Y^1)\), \(P(A=1\mid X=x)=1/2\), and \(Y=Y^A\) by construction. Moreover,
\[
\mu_0(x)=P(U_0\le1/2)=\frac12,
\qquad
\mu_1(x)=P(U_1\le1/2+cx_1)=\frac12+cx_1.
\]
Because \(0<c<1/4\), the Bernoulli probabilities lie in \([1/4,3/4]\), and the binary variables \(Y,Y^0,Y^1\) lie in \([-M_Y,M_Y]=[-1,1]\). Thus
\[
\pi(x)=\frac12,
\qquad
\tau_{P^{\mathrm{tr}}}(x)=\mu_1(x)-\mu_0(x)=cx_1.
\]
The uniform density is \(2^{-d}=c_f=C_f\), and \(c_\pi=1/4\) verifies strict overlap. The constant functions \(1/2\) lie in the calibrated propensity and control-regression Hölder balls. The linear function \(x\mapsto cx_1\) lies in \(\mathsf H_\gamma(1;[-1,1]^d)\) for every \(\gamma>d\): its derivatives of order at least two vanish, while its sup-norm and first derivative are bounded by the calibrated radius. Since \(\eta=c\) and \(|cx_1|\le c\),
\[
\mathcal D_{P^{\mathrm{tr}}}(c)=[-1,1]^d,
\]
so the off-band condition is vacuous for every \(\gamma'>0\).

For \(0<t\le c\), the first coordinate is uniform on \([-1,1]\), and therefore
\[
P_X\{0<|\tau_{P^{\mathrm{tr}}}(X)-\theta|\le t\}
=P\{0<|X_1|\le t/c\}=\frac{t}{c}.
\]
This verifies both margin inequalities with \(\xi=1\) and \(c_m=C_m=c^{-1}\). Finally, the interior points \((-1/2,0,\ldots,0)\) and \((1/2,0,\ldots,0)\) have CATEs of opposite sign. Every member condition in \(\mathrm{def:smooth-model}\) is therefore satisfied. By \(\mathrm{def:transverse-model}\), the observed marginal belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\) for every \(\gamma>d\).
