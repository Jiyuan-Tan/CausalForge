The two displayed inequalities follow immediately from \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-direct-assouad-lower}\) and \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-direct-regression-upper}\). If \(\beta\ge\gamma\), then \(\min\{\beta,\gamma\}=\gamma\), so their powers of \(n\) are equal. Moreover,
\[
 \bar s=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\ge\frac\gamma2
 >\frac d{4\{1+d/(2\gamma)\}},
\]
where the strict inequality uses \(\gamma>d\). Hence \(\mathrm{def:rate}\) is in its high-smoothness branch and \(r_n^*=n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\). Substitution proves \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\) in this subregime.
