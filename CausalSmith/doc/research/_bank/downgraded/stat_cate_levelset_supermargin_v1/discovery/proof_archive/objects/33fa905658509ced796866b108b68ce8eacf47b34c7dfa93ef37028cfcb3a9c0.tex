For all sufficiently large \(n\), \(c_{\mathrm{lb}}n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\le\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\le C_{\mathrm{ub}}n^{-2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}/\{2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}+d\}}\). Hence, when \(\beta\ge\gamma\), the high-smoothness branch applies and \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\); only the case \(\beta<\gamma\) remains unresolved.
