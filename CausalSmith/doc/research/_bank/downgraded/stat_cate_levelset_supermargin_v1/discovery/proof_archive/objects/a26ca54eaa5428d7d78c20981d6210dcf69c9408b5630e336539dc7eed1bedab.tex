For all sufficiently large \(n\), \[c_{\mathrm{lb}}n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\le\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\le C_{\mathrm{ub}}n^{-2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}/\{2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}+d\}}.\] Hence, if \(\beta\ge\gamma\), the high-smoothness branch applies, \(r_n^*=n^{-\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\), and \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\). If \(\beta<\gamma\), the strongest certified conclusion is \[c_{\mathrm{lb}}n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}\le\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\le C_{\mathrm{ub}}n^{-2\beta/(2\beta+d)}.\]
