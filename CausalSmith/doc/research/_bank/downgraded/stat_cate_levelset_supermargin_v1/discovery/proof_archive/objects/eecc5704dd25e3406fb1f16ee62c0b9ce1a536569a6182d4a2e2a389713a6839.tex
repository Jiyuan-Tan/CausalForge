Under \(\mathrm{def:calibration}\), one has \(\mathcal X=[-1,1]^d\) and \(c_f=C_f=2^{-d}\). Every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{tr}}(\gamma;\kappa)\) satisfies \(\mathrm{ass:bounded-density}\), hence
\[
2^{-d}\le f_P(x)\le 2^{-d}
\]
for Lebesgue-almost every \(x\in\mathcal X\). Thus \(f_P(x)=2^{-d}=F_0(x)\) almost everywhere on \(\mathcal X\). Both zero extensions vanish off \(\mathcal X\). Consequently \(f_P/F_0=1\) wherever \(F_0>0\), so the density-ratio error is zero and the essential supremum of the ratio is one.
