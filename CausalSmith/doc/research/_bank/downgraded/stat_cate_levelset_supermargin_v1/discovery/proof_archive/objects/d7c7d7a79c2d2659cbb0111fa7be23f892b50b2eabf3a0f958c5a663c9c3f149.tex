The formal proposition \(\mathrm{prop:calibrated-direct-rate-bracket}\) proves, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 c n^{-2\gamma/(2\gamma+d)}
 \le \mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)
 \le C n^{-2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}/\{2\min\{\beta,\gamma\}+d\}}.
\]
The lower bound is a direct exact-margin Assouad bound over a finite submodel of the frozen law class. The upper bound is attained by the explicit armwise local-polynomial estimator defined in \(\mathrm{lem:calibrated-direct-regression-upper}\). When \(\beta\ge\gamma\), the bracket closes, this parameter region is necessarily in the high-smoothness branch, and \(\mathfrak R_n(1,\gamma;\kappa)\asymp(r_n^*)^2\). For \(\beta<\gamma\), the bracket is honest but generally loose; whether its lower endpoint can be raised to \((r_n^*)^2\), or its upper endpoint sharpened to that scale, is the residual question.
