Write \(a_d=c_{E,d}V_{E,d}\), \(b_d=c_{O,d}V_{O,d}\), \(u_d=\sqrt{a_d}\), \(v_d=\sqrt{b_d}\), and
\[
\alpha_d^*=\frac{u_d}{u_d+v_d}.
\]
Finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) and the displayed uniform lower and upper bounds furnish constants \(0<\eta_0<1/2\) and \(C_0<\infty\) such that
\[
\eta_0\le\alpha_d^*\le1-\eta_0,\qquad
\max\{c_{E,d},c_{O,d},V_{E,d},V_{O,d}\}\le C_0
\]
for every \(d\).

On the event on which the asserted uniform pilot error is at most a sufficiently small deterministic constant, truncation is inactive except possibly when it changes an estimated variance by at most \(\underline v_m\). The map
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto
\frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}}
{\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}y}}
\]
has uniformly bounded first derivatives on a fixed neighborhood of the finite collection \(\{(V_{E,d},V_{O,d}):d\in\mathcal D\}\). The mean-value theorem therefore gives
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}|\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*|
=O_P(r_{V,m}+\underline v_m)=:O_P(t_m),
\qquad t_m=o(1).
\]
In particular,
\[
P\!\left(\min_{d\in\mathcal D}
\min\{\widehat\alpha_{d,m},1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m}\}
\ge\eta_0/2\right)\longrightarrow1.
\]
On this event, every floor count is positive for all sufficiently large \(B\); hence the stipulated zero-count extension affects an event of probability tending to zero.

Let \(F_d(\alpha)=a_d/\alpha+b_d/(1-\alpha)\). Since
\[
F_d'(\alpha_d^*)=0,\qquad
F_d''(\alpha)=\frac{2a_d}{\alpha^3}+\frac{2b_d}{(1-\alpha)^3},
\]
the second derivatives are uniformly bounded on \([\eta_0/2,1-\eta_0/2]\). Taylor's theorem and the budget-profile identity give
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}
|F_d(\widehat\alpha_{d,m})-F_d(\alpha_d^*)|
=O_P(t_m^2).
\]
For \(x\ge2\), the elementary inequality
\[
0\le\frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x\le\frac2{x^2}
\]
shows uniformly on the same interior event that replacing continuous counts by floor counts changes the budget-normalized value by \(O(B^{-1})\). Since \(F_d(\alpha_d^*)=\mathcal V_d^*(P)\), combining the last two displays proves
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
+\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
\right\}-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
=O_P(t_m^2+B^{-1}),
\]
under the declared total convention.
