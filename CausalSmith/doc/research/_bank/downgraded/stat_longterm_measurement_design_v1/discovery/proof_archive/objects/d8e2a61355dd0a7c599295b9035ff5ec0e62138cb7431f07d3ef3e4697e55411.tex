Set \(t_m:=r_{V,m}+\underline v_m\) and
\[
 Z_m:=\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\bigl\{
 |\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
 |\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\bigr\}.
\]
The stated pilot rate gives \(Z_m=O_P(r_{V,m})\).  Let
\[
 A_d(x,y):=
 \frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}}
 {\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}y}}.
\]
The elementary inequality
\[
 |(x\vee\underline v_m)-V|
 \le |x-V|+\underline v_m
\]
and the mean-value theorem, applied on a common positive compact rectangle, imply
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}
 |\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*(P)|=O_P(t_m).                            \tag{1}
\]
The compact rectangle is common because \(\mathcal D\) is finite and all stated costs and variance components are positive and finite.  Since every \(\alpha_d^*(P)\) is strictly between zero and one, there is an \(a_0\in(0,1/2)\) for which all population shares lie in \([2a_0,1-2a_0]\).  Equation (1) and \(t_m=o(1)\) imply
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{a_0\le\widehat\alpha_{d,m}\le1-a_0
       \text{ for every }d\in\mathcal D\right\}\longrightarrow1.       \tag{2}
\]

For fixed \(d\), the budget-profile theorem gives
\[
 \mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)
 =\frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{\alpha}
  +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{1-\alpha}.
\]
On \([a_0,1-a_0]\),
\[
 \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\alpha^2}\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)
 =\frac{2c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{\alpha^3}
  +\frac{2c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{(1-\alpha)^3}
\]
is uniformly bounded over \(d\in\mathcal D\).  Moreover,
\(\partial_\alpha\mathcal V_d(\alpha_d^*(P);P)=0\).  Taylor's theorem, (1), and finiteness of the family yield
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}
 \left|\mathcal V_d(\widehat\alpha_{d,m};P)-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
 =O_P(t_m^2).                                                               \tag{3}
\]

For every \(x\ge2\),
\[
 0\le \frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x
 \le\frac2{x^2}.                                                          \tag{4}
\]
On the event in (2), the two continuous source counts are at least a positive constant times \(B\), uniformly in \(d\).  Thus they exceed two for all sufficiently large \(B\), and applying (4) to each count gives
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|
 B\left\{
 \frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
 \right\}
 -\mathcal V_d(\widehat\alpha_{d,m};P)
 \right|=O_P(B^{-1}).                                                       \tag{5}
\]
Combining (3) and (5) proves the displayed rate.  The vanishing first derivative at \(\alpha_d^*(P)\) is exactly why the allocation-estimation contribution in (3) is quadratic.
