Under the sampling and maintained estimator-level conditions of Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}postselection\text{-}wald}\), substitute this equality into that theorem's studentized limit and coverage statement.  This yields
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)}{\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1),
 \qquad
 P\{\tau\in I_B\}\longrightarrow1-\eta.
\]
The total-event convention is unchanged because the substitution affects only the centering target.
