Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), \(d\in\mathcal D\), and \(0<\alpha<1\), and set
\[
 x_E=\frac{\alpha B}{c_{E,d}},\qquad
 x_O=\frac{(1-\alpha)B}{c_{O,d}}.
\]
The asserted lower bound on \(B\) makes \(x_E,x_O\ge2\).  For every \(x\ge2\),
\[
 0\le \frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x
 =\frac{x-\lfloor x\rfloor}{x\lfloor x\rfloor}
 \le\frac2{x^2}, \tag{1}
\]
because \(0\le x-\lfloor x\rfloor<1\) and \(\lfloor x\rfloor\ge x/2\).  Applying (1) to the two source counts gives the exact decomposition
\[
 B\left\{\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_{E,B}(d,\alpha)}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_{O,B}(d,\alpha)}\right\}
 =\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)+\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
\]
and the bound
\[
 0\le\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
 \le\frac2B\left\{
 \frac{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2}{\alpha^2}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2}{(1-\alpha)^2}\right\}. \tag{2}
\]
For fixed \(d\) and \(\alpha\), the right-hand side tends to zero.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}designwise\text{-}gradient}\) identifies the unscaled expression in braces as the variance of the two-source gradient of the observed bridge contrast.

Put \(a_d=c_{E,d}V_{E,d}\) and \(b_d=c_{O,d}V_{O,d}\).  Positive costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) imply \(a_d,b_d>0\).  Direct algebra gives, for \(0<\alpha<1\),
\[
 \frac{a_d}{\alpha}+\frac{b_d}{1-\alpha}
 -(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2
 =\frac{\{(1-\alpha)\sqrt{a_d}-\alpha\sqrt{b_d}\}^2}
 {\alpha(1-\alpha)}\ge0. \tag{3}
\]
Equality holds exactly when
\[
 \alpha=\frac{\sqrt{a_d}}{\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d}}
 =\frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}}
 {\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}}
 =\alpha_d^*(P).
\]
This proves uniqueness and
\[
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2.
\]
On \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\), the observed contrast equals the common causal target, so the same calculation profiles its designwise gradient variance.  If that gradient is additionally canonical for \(\tau\), its variance is the local semiparametric efficiency lower bound; the fixed-system Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang result supplies that interpretation under its separately stated normalization and premises.
