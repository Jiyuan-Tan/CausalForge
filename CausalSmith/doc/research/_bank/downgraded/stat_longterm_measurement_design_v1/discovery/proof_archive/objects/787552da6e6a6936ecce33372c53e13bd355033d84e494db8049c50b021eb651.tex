For each \(d\in\mathcal D\), define the unadjusted observational residual prediction risk
\[
 R_d(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(X,Z_{M_d})}
 \mathbb E_P[(Y-f(X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O]
\]
and the treatment-aware observational residual prediction risk
\[
 R_d^A(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(A,X,Z_{M_d})}
 \mathbb E_P[(Y-f(A,X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O].
\]
The two generic population rules retain every tie:
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}R_d(P),
 \qquad
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}R_d^A(P).
\]
Their best-tie arm-normalized source-score ratios are
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.
\]
For comparison only, define
\[
 n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)=\frac{B}{c_{E,d}+c_{O,d}},
 \qquad
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 =\frac{R_d(P)}{n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)}
 =\frac{(c_{E,d}+c_{O,d})R_d(P)}{B}.
\]
This secondary quantity defines neither population selector and neither approximation ratio.  The subscript records only the residual-risk-per-budget shape and does not attribute either proxy-selection workflow or any guarantee to Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm.
