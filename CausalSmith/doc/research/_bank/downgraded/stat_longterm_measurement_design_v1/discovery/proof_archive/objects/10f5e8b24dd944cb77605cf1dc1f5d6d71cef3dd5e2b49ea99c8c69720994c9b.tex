For each \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), choose
\[
 0<\delta_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 <\min\{\delta_\epsilon,r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\},
\]
where \(\delta_\epsilon\) is the radius used in \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) is furnished by Lemma~\texttt{lem:ikmw-normalized-witness-stability}. Define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\bigcup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \left\{P\text{ in the binary constrained finite-cell model}:
 \|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<\delta_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\right\}.
\]
This is a relatively open subregion of \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) in the binary constrained finite-cell model.
