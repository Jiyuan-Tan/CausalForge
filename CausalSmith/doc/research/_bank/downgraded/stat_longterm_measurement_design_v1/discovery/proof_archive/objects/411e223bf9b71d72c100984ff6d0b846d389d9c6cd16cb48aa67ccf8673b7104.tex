Fix \((\chi,\epsilon)\).  On the relative interior of \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\), every observed cell of \((A,X,Z_{M_d},Y)\) has positive probability.  Hence
\[
 R_d^A(P)=
 \mathbb E_P\{\operatorname{Var}_P(Y\mid A,X,Z_{M_d},G=O)\mid G=O\}
\]
is a finite sum of cell probabilities and conditional-moment ratios with positive denominators, and is continuous in the complete latent-cell vector.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}treatment\text{-}aware\text{-}sharp\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) gives a strict center gap
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}^A(P_\epsilon)<R_{d_{\mathrm e}}^A(P_\epsilon).
\]
Thus a relative neighborhood of the center preserves \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\).

The arm-normalized and IKM-normalized profiles are continuous and strictly positive on the regular balls already supplied by Lemmas~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) and \(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\).  Therefore both treatment-aware best-tie ratios are continuous there.  Since each center ratio is positive, further relative neighborhoods satisfy
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_\epsilon),
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon),
\]
respectively.  Intersecting these finitely many neighborhoods with the two pre-existing balls, and replacing each radius by the minimum of the corresponding positive radii, loses none of the earlier conclusions and proves all assertions.
