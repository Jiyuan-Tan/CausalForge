For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) and \(d\in\mathcal D\), the observed-data bridge contrast \(\theta_d(P):=\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}\) is identified.  Along every regular two-source submodel its pathwise gradient has source components \(\phi_{E,d}\) and \(\phi_{O,d}\), so independent sample sizes \(n_E,n_O\) give gradient variance \(V_{E,d}/n_E+V_{O,d}/n_O\).  The frozen assumptions do not in general imply \(\theta_d(P)=\tau\): there is a finite-support law in \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) for which \(\theta_d(P)=0\) and \(\tau=-3/25\).  Thus the displayed gradient is not a canonical gradient for the causal target \(\tau\) on the entire frozen class.
