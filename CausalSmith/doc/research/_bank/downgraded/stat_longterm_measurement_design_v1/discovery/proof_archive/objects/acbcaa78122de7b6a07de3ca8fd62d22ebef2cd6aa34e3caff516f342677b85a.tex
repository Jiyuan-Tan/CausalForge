At every center, the treatment probability in each source is one half.  From Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}design\text{-}value}\),
\[
 \psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})\phi_{g,d}=\frac12\phi_{g,d},
 \qquad
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{g,d}quad(g\in\{E,O\}). \tag{27}
\]
Taking square roots in the profile and then squaring gives
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                      \tag{28}
\]
The factor in (28) is common to both designs.  The strict arm-normalized ordering in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) therefore gives \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and the factor cancels from the best-tie ratio.

For stability, all finite-cell probabilities are positive at the center.  As proved in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\), regularity, common identification, and the raw-risk choice persist on a sufficiently small constrained-model ball.  On that ball the finite bridge matrices remain nonsingular; their inverses, the score second moments, the source treatment probabilities, and hence every IKM-normalized value are continuous in the cell vector.  The IKM optimizer gap is strict at the center, and the denominator of the ratio is positive.  Shrinking the radius preserves the optimizer and makes the ratio exceed half its center value.

Every law in the ball satisfies the common causal-identification equality.  Under the additional fixed-system bridge uniqueness, bijectivity, and local-model premises recorded in the cited Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\), its IKM-normalized profile is the corresponding profiled causal efficiency bound.
