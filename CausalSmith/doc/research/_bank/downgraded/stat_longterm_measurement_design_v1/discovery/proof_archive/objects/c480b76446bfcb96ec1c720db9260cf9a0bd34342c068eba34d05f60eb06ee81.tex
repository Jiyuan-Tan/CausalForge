For every \(d\in\mathcal D\), fit the two observed bridge equations by cross-fitting on \(\mathcal P_m\), estimate \(V_{E,d}\) and \(V_{O,d}\) by foldwise score second moments, form \(\widehat{\mathcal V}_{d,m}^*=(\sqrt{c_{E,d}\widehat V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}\widehat V_{O,d}})^2\), select a deterministic-tie-broken \(\widehat d_m\in\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\widehat{\mathcal V}_{d,m}^*\), compute \(\widehat\alpha_{d,m}=\sqrt{c_{E,d}(\widehat V_{E,d}\vee\underline v_m)}/[\sqrt{c_{E,d}(\widehat V_{E,d}\vee\underline v_m)}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}(\widehat V_{O,d}\vee\underline v_m)}]\), set \(\widehat\alpha_m=\widehat\alpha_{\widehat d_m,m}\), and purchase \(N_{E,B}=\lfloor\widehat\alpha_mB/c_{E,\widehat d_m}\rfloor\) experimental units and \(N_{O,B}=\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_m)B/c_{O,\widehat d_m}\rfloor\) follow-up units.
