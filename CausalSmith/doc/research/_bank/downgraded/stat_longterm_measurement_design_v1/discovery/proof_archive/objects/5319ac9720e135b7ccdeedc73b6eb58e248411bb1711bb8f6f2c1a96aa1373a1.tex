Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  Because \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, the predictive optimizer set \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) is also finite and nonempty.  Positive costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), together with Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\), give for every \(d\in\mathcal D\)
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+
         \sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2<\infty.        \tag{1}
\]
Substituting (1) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the displayed identity.  Its numerator minimizes the same positive collection over the subset \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\), whereas its denominator minimizes over all of \(\mathcal D\).  Consequently the ratio is at least one; finiteness and positivity follow from finiteness of the catalogue and (1).

For the actual best-tie rule, an approximation constant \(C\) must satisfy
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P).     \tag{2}
\]
The denominator in (2) is strictly positive.  Division shows that (2) holds if and only if \(C\ge\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  Hence the smallest such constant is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For the pairwise assertion, let
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)=
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.       \tag{3}
\]
The indexing set in (3) is nonempty because it contains every diagonal pair.  It is finite, and every denominator is positive by (1), so the maximum is finite.  If all prediction-order implications hold with constant \(C\), applying them to each pair in (3) gives \(C\ge C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)\).  Conversely, the definition of the maximum gives
\(\mathcal V_d^*(P)\le C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)\) for every prediction-ordered pair.  Therefore (3) is the least pairwise constant.

Finally, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) states that for every prescribed finite \(\chi>1\), the corresponding binary region obeys
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty,       \tag{4}
\]
although at every center the uniquely prediction-selected design has paired cost \(5\chi\) and the efficient design has paired cost \(5\).  Their paired-cost ratio is therefore exactly \(\chi\).  Equation (4) rules out any finite primitive uniform guarantee on that regular common-identification region.  Thus a finite catalogue gives the exact lawwise enumeration above, but no uniform predictive-efficiency approximation factor.
