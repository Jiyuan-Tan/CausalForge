Fix \(\chi>1\), \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and write \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\) for the binary center in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}witness}\). For a center design with early and late proxy signals \(x,y\in(0,1)\), the population least-squares coefficients in the Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm residual-variance criterion are
\[
 \beta_1(x,y)=\frac{x(1-y^2)}{1-x^2y^2},
 \qquad
 \beta_2(x,y)=\frac{y(1-x^2)}{1-x^2y^2},
\]
and the resulting residual variance is
\[
 \sigma^2(x,y)=\frac{(1-x^2)(1-y^2)}{1-x^2y^2}=R(x,y).
\]
Thus, under the linear cost specialization \(c(M_d,n)=n(c_{E,d}+c_{O,d})\), the CLW population criterion is exactly \(\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\). The center is a member of \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
\begin{aligned}
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\frac{3}{10\chi}\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac{3}{10\chi}<\frac3{10}<\frac35
 =R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon}),\\
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 &=\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B).
\end{aligned}                                                     \tag{1}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
Moreover, for all sufficiently large \(B\), the integer-budget version has the same unique choice:
\[
 \arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}
 \frac{R_d(P_{\chi,\epsilon})}
 {\left\lfloor B/(c_{E,d}+c_{O,d})\right\rfloor}
 =\{d_{\mathrm p}\},                                      \tag{2}
\]
where the assertion is made only after both denominators are positive. The selected design is costlier in each source by \(5(\chi-1)/2\), and
\[
 V_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{40\chi}{10\chi-3},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14),
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\frac{3872}{25}.
\]
At the balanced center,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon}),          \tag{3}
\]
and their exact common value is
\[
 \frac{25}{3872}
 \left\{
 \sqrt{\frac{20\chi(5\chi-1)}{10\chi-3}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{32(5\chi+1)}{75(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14)}
 \right\}^{2}.                                             \tag{4}
\]
It is larger than \((\epsilon^{-2}+14)/726\). For all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), minimizing the exact common ratio over \(\chi>1\) gives
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)\sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3}
\]
and
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}+o(\epsilon^{2/3}), \tag{5}
\]
with the identical display for each ratio in (3).
