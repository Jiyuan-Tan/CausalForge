For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), \(d\in\mathcal D\), and fixed \(0<\alpha<1\), as \(B\to\infty\) the floor-count variance of the designwise arm-normalized bridge-functional gradient satisfies \(B\{V_{E,d}/n_{E,B}(d,\alpha)+V_{O,d}/n_{O,B}(d,\alpha)\}=\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)+\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)\), where \(0\le\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)\le 2B^{-1}\{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2/\alpha^2+V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2/(1-\alpha)^2\}\) once \(B\ge2\max\{c_{E,d}/\alpha,c_{O,d}/(1-\alpha)\}\). Hence the floor-count variance converges to \(\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)\). The unique minimizer and minimum of this limiting profile are \(\alpha_d^*(P)=\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}/(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})\) and \(\mathcal V_d^*(P)=(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2\).  This algebraic arm-normalized profile is not identified with the absolute efficiency bound printed in Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorems~5 and~7 without the explicit source-score normalization.  At the binary center laws, the printed-score profile is exactly \(\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)/4\), so it has the same allocation and design ordering while its absolute value is one quarter as large.
