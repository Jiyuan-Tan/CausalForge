Let \(\mathcal I_{E,B}\) and \(\mathcal I_{O,B}\) be the experimental and observational main-study index sets, of sizes \(N_{E,B}\) and \(N_{O,B}\). Use a fixed integer \(L\ge2\) and a deterministic balanced observational-only partition \(\mathcal I_{O,B}=\bigsqcup_{\ell=1}^L\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}\). For every \(d\in\mathcal D\), train \(\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}\) on \(\mathcal I_{O,B}\setminus\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}\), and train \(\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}\) on \(\mathcal I_{E,B}\cup(\mathcal I_{O,B}\setminus\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell})\). Assign any degenerate fit a fixed measurable value.

For \(a\in\{0,1\}\), define
\[
 e_{B,a}=\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}}\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\},
 \qquad
 o_{B,\ell,a}=\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}}\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\},
\]
\[
 \widehat p_{E,a}=e_{B,a}/N_{E,B},
 \qquad
 \widehat p_{O,\ell,a}=o_{B,\ell,a}/|\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}|,
\]
and
\[
 \widehat\mu_{d,a}^{(-\ell)}
 =\frac1{e_{B,a}}\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}}\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\}
 \widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{3d,i},S_{2d,i},a,X_i).
\]
Every displayed quantity is assigned zero if one of its displayed denominators is zero. Whenever the required denominators are positive, set
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat\mu_{B,d}(a)
 &=\frac1L\sum_{\ell=1}^L\widehat\mu_{d,a}^{(-\ell)}\\
 &\quad+\frac1L\sum_{\ell=1}^L\frac1{o_{B,\ell,a}}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}}\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\}
 \widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{2d,i},S_{1d,i},a,X_i)\\
 &\hspace{35mm}\times
 \{Y_i-\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{3d,i},S_{2d,i},a,X_i)\},
\end{aligned}
\]
and \(\widehat\tau_B(d,\alpha)=\widehat\mu_{B,d}(1)-\widehat\mu_{B,d}(0)\). For \(i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat\phi_{E,d,B,i}
 =\frac1L\sum_{\ell=1}^L\sum_{a=0}^1(-1)^{1-a}
 \frac{\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\}}{\widehat p_{E,a}}
 \{&\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{3d,i},S_{2d,i},a,X_i)
 -\widehat\mu_{d,a}^{(-\ell)}\},
\end{aligned}
\]
and, for \(i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat\phi_{O,d,B,i}
 &=\sum_{a=0}^1(-1)^{1-a}
 \frac{\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\}}{\widehat p_{O,\ell,a}}
 \widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{2d,i},S_{1d,i},a,X_i)\\
 &\hspace{22mm}\times
 \{Y_i-\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{3d,i},S_{2d,i},a,X_i)\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every summand with a zero empirical arm probability is zero. Set
\[
 \widetilde V_{E,d,B}=N_{E,B}^{-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}}\widehat\phi_{E,d,B,i}^2,
 \qquad
 \widetilde V_{O,d,B}=N_{O,B}^{-1}\sum_{\ell=1}^L\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}}\widehat\phi_{O,d,B,i}^2,
\]
with value zero when the corresponding source count is zero. At \((d,\alpha)=(\widehat d_m,\widehat\alpha_m)\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf D_B
 ={}&\{N_{E,B}\ge1,N_{O,B}\ge1\}
 \cap\bigcap_{a=0}^1\{e_{B,a}\ge1\}\\
 &{}\cap\bigcap_{\ell=1}^L\bigcap_{a=0}^1\{o_{B,\ell,a}\ge1\},
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
 \widehat s_B^2=\mathbf 1\{\mathsf D_B\}B\left\{
 \frac{\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{E,B}}
 +\frac{\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{O,B}}\right\},
 \qquad
 \widehat s_B=(\widehat s_B^2)^{1/2},
\]
and \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}=\mathsf D_B\cap\{\widehat s_B^2>0\}\). Let \(s_{0,B}\) be the nonnegative square root of \(B\{V_{E,\widehat d_m}/N_{E,B}+V_{O,\widehat d_m}/N_{O,B}\}\) on positive source counts and zero otherwise. On \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}\), set \(\widehat\tau_B=\widehat\tau_B(\widehat d_m,\widehat\alpha_m)\), \(\mathsf T_B=\sqrt B\{\widehat\tau_B-\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\}/\widehat s_B\), and
\[
 I_B=\left[\widehat\tau_B-z_{1-\eta/2}\widehat s_B/\sqrt B,
 \widehat\tau_B+z_{1-\eta/2}\widehat s_B/\sqrt B\right].
\]
On \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}^c\), set \(\widehat\tau_B=0\), \(\widehat s_B=0\), \(\mathsf T_B=0\), and \(I_B=[0,0]\). Under common identification, \(\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)=\tau\), so the numerator of \(\mathsf T_B\) is equivalently \(\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)\). These conventions make every estimator, score moment, statistic, and interval a fixed measurable map on the entire sample space.
