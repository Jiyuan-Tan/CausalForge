Fix \(\chi>1\).  For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), jointly decrease \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) as permitted by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}exact\text{-}integer\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\).  Let \(\delta_\epsilon\) be the radius in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\), choose
\[
 0<\widetilde\delta_\epsilon
 <\min\{\delta_\epsilon,r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\},
\]
and define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\bigcup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \left\{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi):
 \|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<\widetilde\delta_\epsilon\right\}. \tag{1}
\]
The radii retain the conclusions used to define \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\), together with the strict IKM optimizer ordering and the exact-integer IKM-ratio stability from Lemmas~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\) and \(\mathrm{lem{:}exact\text{-}integer\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\).  This is a relatively open subset of \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\).
