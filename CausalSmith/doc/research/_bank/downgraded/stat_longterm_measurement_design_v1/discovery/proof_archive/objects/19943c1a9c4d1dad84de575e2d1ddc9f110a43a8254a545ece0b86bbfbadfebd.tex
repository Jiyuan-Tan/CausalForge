Under the IKM score and profile normalization, every binary center law satisfies
\[
\psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
\qquad
\psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},
\qquad
V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
\qquad
V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d},
\qquad
\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon)
\]
for \(g\in\{E,O\}\) and \(d\in\mathcal D\). Consequently \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\) and \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)\). For each fixed \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), there is \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\) such that every law \(P\) in the binary constrained finite-cell model with \(\|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) is regular, both admissible bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
On this neighborhood, the IKM-normalized profile is the profiled local causal semiparametric efficiency bound under the fixed-system conditions in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\).
