The empirical treatment-aware selector result is deliberately finite-cell: it uses bounded binary outcomes, uniformly positive cells along a fixed-\(\chi\) triangular path, and a split-sample saturated regression. The general post-selection Wald theorem remains conditional on the declared selected-path bridge-learning, multiplier, and variance conditions outside the separate fixed-dimensional attainability subclass. No uniform claim is made for arbitrary predictive algorithms, continuous high-dimensional catalogues, or laws approaching empty treatment-proxy cells.
