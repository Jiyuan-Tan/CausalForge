At every center the treatment probability in each source is one half. Hence, for \(g\in\{E,O\}\),
\[
 \psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{g,d},
 \qquad
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{g,d}.                   \tag{27}
\]
Taking square roots in the profile and then squaring gives
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                      \tag{28}
\]
The common factor leaves the strict optimizer and best-tie ratio unchanged, proving the center assertions.

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) supplies a constrained-model ball on which regularity, common identification, CLW admissibility, and raw-risk choice persist. On that ball the finite bridge inverses, source treatment probabilities, score moments, and IKM profiles are continuous. The IKM optimizer gap is strict at the center and its ratio denominator is positive. Shrinking preserves the optimizer and makes the ratio exceed one half of its center value. Under the additional fixed-system premises of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\), the IKM profile has the asserted causal-efficiency interpretation; no such interpretation is inferred from (27)--(28) alone.
