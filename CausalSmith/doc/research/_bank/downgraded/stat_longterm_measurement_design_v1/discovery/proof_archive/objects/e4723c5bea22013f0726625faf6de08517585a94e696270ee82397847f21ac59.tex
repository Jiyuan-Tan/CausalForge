At every center the treatment probability in each source is one half. Hence, for \(g\in\{E,O\}\),
\[
 \psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac{1}{2}\phi_{g,d},
 \qquad
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac{1}{4}V_{g,d}.               \tag{33}
\]
Taking square roots in the profile and then squaring gives
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{1}{4}\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                  \tag{34}
\]
The common factor leaves the strict optimizer and best-tie ratio unchanged. Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) therefore prove all center assertions.

The latter lemma supplies a constrained-model ball on which regularity, common identification, and raw-risk choice persist. On that ball, source treatment probabilities, finite bridge inverses, source-score moments, and IKM profiles are continuous functions of the positive finite-cell coordinates. The IKM optimizer gap is strict at the center and its ratio denominator is positive. Shrinking the ball preserves the optimizer and makes the ratio exceed one half of its center value. Under the additional fixed-system premises of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\), that cited result identifies the IKM profile as the profiled local causal efficiency bound. No efficiency interpretation is inferred from (33)--(34) without those premises.
