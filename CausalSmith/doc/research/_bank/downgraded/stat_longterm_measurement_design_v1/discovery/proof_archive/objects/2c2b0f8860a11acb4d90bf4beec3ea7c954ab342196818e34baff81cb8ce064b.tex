At every binary center, \(P(A=1\mid G=E)=P(A=1\mid G=O)=1/2\). The definitions of the IKM-normalized scores therefore give, design by design,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d}.
\]
Taking conditional second moments gives
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d}. \tag{1}
\]
The positive homogeneity of the profiled formula, or direct substitution into Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}design\text{-}value}\), now yields
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\left\{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}\right\}^2
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon). \tag{2}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) shows that \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is the unique arm-normalized minimizer and \(d_{\mathrm p}\) the unique raw-risk minimizer. Equation (2) preserves the first ordering, and its common factor cancels from the best-tie ratio. Hence
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon). \tag{3}
\]

For stability, start with the regularity and common-identification ball furnished by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\). Within this finite-cell ball, source-arm probabilities, bridge coefficients, score second moments, and therefore all IKM-normalized profiled values are continuous functions of the positive cell vector. At the center, the raw-risk gap, the IKM-profile gap, and the positive ratio in (3) are strict. Shrinking the ball preserves regularity, both bridge contrasts equal to \(\tau\), the two unique optimizer assignments, and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
This supplies \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\). Finally, when the fixed-system premises stated in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) hold, that cited result identifies the IKM score as the canonical gradient for \(\tau\). Profiling its two source moments over the budget share therefore gives the asserted local causal semiparametric efficiency bound on this neighborhood.
