Fix \(\chi>1\), and let \(\epsilon_m\in(0,10^{-3})\) be any deterministic sequence with \(\epsilon_m\downarrow0\).  Draw the independent full-catalogue pilot \(\mathcal P_m\) from the triangular center law \(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}\), and construct \(\widehat R_{d,m}^A\) and \(\widehat d_m^A\) by Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}treatment\text{-}aware\text{-}pilot\text{-}selector}\).  Then
\[
 \max_{d\in\{d_{\mathrm p},d_{\mathrm e}\}}
 \left|\widehat R_{d,m}^A-R_d^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})\right|
 =O_P(m^{-1/2}),                                                              \tag{1}
\]
and
\[
 P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}\{\widehat d_m^A=d_{\mathrm p}\}\longrightarrow1.      \tag{2}
\]
Moreover, under the joint law of the pilot and the deterministic center parameters,
\[
 \frac{\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m^A}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 {\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_d^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 \longrightarrow\infty\quad\text{in probability},                           \tag{3}
\]
and
\[
 \frac{\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m^A,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 {\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 \longrightarrow\infty\quad\text{in probability}.                           \tag{4}
\]
Thus the failure is feasible, not merely an oracle-population artifact: a cross-fitted saturated finite-cell estimator consistently implements the treatment-aware prediction selector, yet its selected source-score and IKM-normalized design-value ratios diverge.
