First fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\). Full-Gram nonsingularity and finite moments make every \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\) finite. The budget-profile theorem and nondegeneracy give \(0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)<\infty\). Source-arm positivity gives \(0<1-p_{g,1}<1\), so every IKM profile is also finite and positive. The finite named selector is nonempty. Direct substitution proves (1)--(2); the numerator minimizes over a nonempty subset of the denominator's index set, establishing the interval and minimal best-tie constants.

For either positive profile \(w_d\), set
\[
 C_{\mathrm{ord}}^{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}\frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}.       \tag{34}
\]
The index set is finite and contains each diagonal pair. Every constant satisfying all named order implications must dominate every indexed ratio, and the maximum itself satisfies all implications. This proves (3). Replacing the continuous criterion by the integer one gives the same argument on the positive-count domain. Evaluating the ordered pairs uses at most \(|\mathcal D|^2\) comparisons.

Now, independently, fix any \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). The generic unadjusted identity is Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}catalogue\text{-}predictive\text{-}guarantee}\); the same positivity and substitution prove the treatment-aware and IKM identities. Repeating (34) with the appropriate generic risk proves their pairwise constants. No step assumes this generic \(P\) lies in the CLW domain.

The joint impossibility theorem proves unbounded ratios on the corrected regions. The sharp-elbow and treatment-aware-elbow lemmas prove equality of all six center ratios and the expansion (5). Exact enumeration at a fixed law therefore supplies no law-independent named or generic constant.
