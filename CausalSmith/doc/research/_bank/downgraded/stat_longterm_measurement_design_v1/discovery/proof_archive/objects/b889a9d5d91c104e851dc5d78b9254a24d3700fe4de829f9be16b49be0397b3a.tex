Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). Positive costs, Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) give
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2
 <\infty                                                    \tag{35}
\]
for every design. Since \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, both the global minimizer set and \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) are nonempty. Substitution of (35) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the identity. Its numerator minimizes over a subset of the denominator's index set, so the ratio is at least one; (35) makes it finite.

The actual best-tie selected-value inequality holds with constant \(C\) exactly when \(C\) is at least the displayed ratio, proving minimality. For the pairwise implications, every valid \(C(P)\) must dominate \(\mathcal V_d^*(P)/\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)\) for each ordered pair satisfying \(R_d(P)\le R_{d'}(P)\). Conversely, the maximum of these finitely many positive ratios dominates every such pair and therefore makes every implication true. This proves the exact pairwise constant and its finiteness.

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) supply the secondary generic raw-risk conclusions. Each binary region contains every one of its centers, and the center ratio exceeds \((\epsilon^{-2}+14)/726\); hence the supremum is infinite as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). At a center, the selected design has paired cost \(5\chi\), the optimal design has paired cost \(5\), and their ratio is \(\chi\). The same sharp-elbow lemma proves the minimizing scale and the displayed expansion for both generic center ratios. Thus exact fixed-law enumeration does not imply a law-uniform guarantee.
