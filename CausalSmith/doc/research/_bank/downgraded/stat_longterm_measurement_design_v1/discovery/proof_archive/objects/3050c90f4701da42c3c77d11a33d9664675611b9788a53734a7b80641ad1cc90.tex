For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), the CLW linear-cost population selector has the exact finite-catalogue ratios
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d'}V_{E,d'}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d'}V_{O,d'}})^2}
 \in[1,\infty),                                           \tag{1}
\]
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 \in[1,\infty).                                           \tag{2}
\]
Each is the smallest constant for its actual best-tie selector. The exact pairwise-order constant for either positive profile \(w_d\) is
\[
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}.                                      \tag{3}
\]
The generic unadjusted and treatment-aware squared-risk rules retain the corresponding exact lawwise constants already displayed in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}catalogue\text{-}predictive\text{-}guarantee}\) and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}treatment\text{-}aware\text{-}predictive\text{-}operator\text{-}certificate}\). Finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) therefore makes all these constants computable by enumeration.

This lawwise finiteness is not uniform. For every fixed \(\chi>1\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)=\infty,
 \qquad
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)=\infty.          \tag{4}
\]
At every center, these two ratios and the four generic ratios agree, and any one of their common values satisfies
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                      \tag{5}
\]
Thus the finite catalogue yields an exact lawwise audit but no finite uniform bridge-efficiency guarantee for the CLW linear-cost population rule transported through measured support. The generic squared-risk separations remain secondary corollaries.
