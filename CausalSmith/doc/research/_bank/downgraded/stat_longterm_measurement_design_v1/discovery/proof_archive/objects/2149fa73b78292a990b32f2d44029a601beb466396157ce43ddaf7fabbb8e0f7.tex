For each regular design \(d\), define the source scores on the Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang normalization by
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})\phi_{O,d},
\]
and define their source-conditional second moments by
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\mathbb E_P[(\psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}})^2\mid G=E]
 =(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},
\]
\[
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\mathbb E_P[(\psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}})^2\mid G=O]
 =(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}.
\]
The profiled IKM-normalized value, its optimizer set, and the best-tie raw population prediction-risk ratio are
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)
 =\left(
 \sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 +\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \right)^2,
\]
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P),
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}.
\]
These objects are defined only as normalization-specific score moments and profiles. Their interpretation as causal efficiency bounds is supplied design by design by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) after its causal-identification and regularity premises have been verified.
