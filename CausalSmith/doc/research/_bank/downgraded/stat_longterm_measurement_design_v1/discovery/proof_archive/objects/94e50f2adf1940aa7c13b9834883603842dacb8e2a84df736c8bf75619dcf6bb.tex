Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), \(d\in\mathcal D\), and \(0<\alpha<1\), and set
\[
 x_E=\frac{\alpha B}{c_{E,d}},\qquad
 x_O=\frac{(1-\alpha)B}{c_{O,d}}.
\]
The asserted lower bound on \(B\) makes \(x_E,x_O\ge2\). For every \(x\ge2\),
\[
 0\le \frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x
 =\frac{x-\lfloor x\rfloor}{x\lfloor x\rfloor}
 \le\frac2{x^2}, \tag{1}
\]
because \(0\le x-\lfloor x\rfloor<1\) and \(\lfloor x\rfloor\ge x/2\). Applying (1) to the two source counts gives the exact decomposition
\[
 B\left\{\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_{E,B}(d,\alpha)}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_{O,B}(d,\alpha)}\right\}
 =\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)+\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
\]
and
\[
 0\le\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
 \le\frac2B\left\{
 \frac{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2}{\alpha^2}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2}{(1-\alpha)^2}\right\}. \tag{2}
\]
For fixed \(d\) and \(\alpha\), the right-hand side tends to zero. The left-hand side is the budget-scaled source-score variance; by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}designwise\text{-}gradient}\), it is also the budget-scaled gradient variance along every bridge-differentiable submodel.

Put \(a_d=c_{E,d}V_{E,d}\) and \(b_d=c_{O,d}V_{O,d}\). Positive costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) imply \(a_d,b_d>0\). Direct algebra gives
\[
 \frac{a_d}{\alpha}+\frac{b_d}{1-\alpha}
 -(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2
 =\frac{\{(1-\alpha)\sqrt{a_d}-\alpha\sqrt{b_d}\}^2}
 {\alpha(1-\alpha)}\ge0. \tag{3}
\]
Equality holds exactly when
\[
 \alpha=\frac{\sqrt{a_d}}{\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d}}
 =\frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}}
 {\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}}
 =\alpha_d^*(P).
\]
This proves uniqueness and
\[
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2.
\]
On \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\), the observed contrast equals the common causal target, but the gradient interpretation still requires the declared bridge-differentiable submodel restriction. If that gradient is additionally canonical for \(\tau\), its variance is the local semiparametric efficiency lower bound; the separately recorded fixed-system Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang result supplies that interpretation under its own normalization and premises. Thus the calculation itself is a cost profile of source-score variance and does not, by algebra alone, assert a new cross-design canonical-efficiency theorem.
