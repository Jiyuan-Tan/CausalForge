For \(d\in\mathcal D\), write \(H_d=(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X)\) and \(L_d=(S_{2d},S_{1d},A,X)\).  For each \((d,a)\), fix ex ante a common finite dimension \(r_{d,a}\) and known measurable column vectors
\[
 b_{h,d,a}(H_d)\in\mathbb R^{r_{d,a}},
 \qquad b_{q,d,a}(L_d)\in\mathbb R^{r_{d,a}}.
\]
On an observational training sample in arm \(a\), define
\[
 \widehat M_{d,a}
 =\mathbb P_{O,a}^{\mathrm{tr}}
   [b_{q,d,a}(L_d)b_{h,d,a}(H_d)^{\mathsf T}],
 \qquad
 \widehat r_{h,d,a}
 =\mathbb P_{O,a}^{\mathrm{tr}}[b_{q,d,a}(L_d)Y],
\]
and, using an experimental training sample in the same arm, define
\[
 \widehat r_{q,d,a}
 =\mathbb P_{E,a}^{\mathrm{tr}}[b_{h,d,a}(H_d)].
\]
Whenever \(\widehat M_{d,a}\) is nonsingular, put
\[
 \widehat\beta_{h,d,a}=\widehat M_{d,a}^{-1}\widehat r_{h,d,a},
 \qquad
 \widehat\beta_{q,d,a}=(\widehat M_{d,a}^{\mathsf T})^{-1}
                         \widehat r_{q,d,a},                   \tag{1}
\]
\[
 \widehat h_d(H_d)=b_{h,d,A}(H_d)^{\mathsf T}
                    \widehat\beta_{h,d,A},
 \qquad
 \widehat q_d(L_d)=b_{q,d,A}(L_d)^{\mathsf T}
                    \widehat\beta_{q,d,A}.                   \tag{2}
\]
On a singular empirical matrix or an empty arm, assign the corresponding coefficient vector the value zero.  Apply (1)--(2) on every training complement in the fixed cross-fitting partitions, for every \(d\) in the full-catalogue pilot and only for the selected \(d\) in the main study.  Form \(\widehat V_{E,d},\widehat V_{O,d}\) and \(\widetilde V_{E,d,B},\widetilde V_{O,d,B}\) as the empirical second moments of the exact arm-normalized plug-in scores specified in Definitions~\(\mathrm{def{:}pilot\text{-}selector}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}selected\text{-}wald\text{-}variance}\).  This is a fixed-dimensional sample-moment construction; no basis dimension grows with \(m\) or \(B\).
