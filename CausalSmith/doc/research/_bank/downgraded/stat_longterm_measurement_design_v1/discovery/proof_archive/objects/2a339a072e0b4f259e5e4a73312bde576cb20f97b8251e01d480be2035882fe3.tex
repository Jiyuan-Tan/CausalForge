For a fixed short-term-outcome system and source-conditional law \(P\), let \(n=n_E+n_O\) and define the artificial superpopulation law \(P_n^{\circ}\) by
\[
 P_n^{\circ}(G=E)=\frac{n_E}{n},
 \qquad
 P_n^{\circ}(G=O)=\frac{n_O}{n},
 \qquad
 P_n^{\circ}(\cdot\mid G=g)=P(\cdot\mid G=g).
\]
Every norm in the statement below is \(\|\cdot\|_2=\|\cdot\|_{L_2(P_n^{\circ})}\).  Use a fixed number \(L_0\ge2\) of observational-only cross-fitting folds, independent i.i.d. observations within the two mutually independent sources, \(n_E,n_O\to\infty\), and \(n_E/n_O\to\lambda\in(0,\infty)\).  Suppose Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~4 statements 1 and 2, their square-integrable latent outcome and selection bridge premises, the corresponding observed moment equations, and both parts of their Assumption~8 hold with nuisance limits equal to the true bridges \(h_0\) and \(q_0\).  Thus, for every \(1\le\ell\le L_0\),
\[
 \|\mathsf T_0(\widehat h^{(-\ell)}-h_0)\|_2=O_P(\delta_{h,n}),
 \qquad
 \|\widehat h^{(-\ell)}-h_0\|_2=O_P(\rho_{h,n}),
\]
\[
 \|\mathsf T_0^\star(\widehat q^{(-\ell)}-q_0)\|_2=O_P(\delta_{q,n}),
 \qquad
 \|\widehat q^{(-\ell)}-q_0\|_2=O_P(\rho_{q,n}),
\]
all four rates tend to zero, and
\[
 \min\{\delta_{h,n}\rho_{q,n},\rho_{h,n}\delta_{q,n}\}
 =o(n^{-1/2}).
\]
Define the two score moments printed in their Theorem~5, kept distinct from this paper's arm-normalized \(V_{E,d}\) and \(V_{O,d}\), by
\[
 V_E^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \left\{\frac{A-P(A=1\mid G=E)}{P(A=1\mid G=E)}
 \bigl(h_0(S_3,S_2,A,X)-\mu_0(A)\bigr)\right\}^2
 \mathrel{\Big|}G=E\right]
\]
and
\[
 V_O^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \left\{\frac{A-P(A=1\mid G=O)}{P(A=1\mid G=O)}
 q_0(S_2,S_1,A,X)
 \bigl(Y-h_0(S_3,S_2,A,X)\bigr)\right\}^2
 \mathrel{\Big|}G=O\right].
\]
If these two moments are finite and strictly positive, their Theorem~5 states that
\[
 \sqrt n\,(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{DR}}-\tau_0)
 \rightsquigarrow
 \mathcal N\!\left(0,
 \frac{1+\lambda}{\lambda}V_E^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 +(1+\lambda)V_O^{\mathrm{IKM}}\right).
\]
This cited statement records the source's printed normalization only.  It neither identifies \(V_g^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) with this paper's \(V_{g,d}\) nor supplies the arm-normalized fixed-path expansion used by the post-selection theorem.
