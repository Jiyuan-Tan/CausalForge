Write
\[
a_d=c_{E,d}V_{E,d},\quad b_d=c_{O,d}V_{O,d},\quad
\alpha_d^*=\frac{\sqrt{a_d}}{\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d}},\quad
t_m=r_{V,m}+\underline v_m.
\]
Finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) and the displayed uniform positive lower and finite upper bounds furnish constants \(\eta_0\in(0,1/2)\) and \(C_0<\infty\) such that
\[
\eta_0\le\alpha_d^*\le1-\eta_0
\]
and all four costs and variance components are at most \(C_0\), uniformly in \(d\).

On an event of probability tending to one, every pilot variance estimate lies in a fixed neighborhood of its strictly positive target.  Replacing an estimate by its truncation at \(\underline v_m\) changes it by at most its estimation error plus \(\underline v_m\).  The maps
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto
\frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}}
{\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}y}}
\]
have uniformly bounded first derivatives on that neighborhood, since there are only finitely many designs.  The mean-value theorem and the assumed pilot rate therefore give
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}|\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*|
=O_P(t_m)=o_P(1).                                                    \tag{1}
\]
In particular, the event
\[
\mathsf A_m=\left\{\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\min
\{\widehat\alpha_{d,m},1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m}\}\ge\eta_0/2\right\}
\]
has probability tending to one.  On \(\mathsf A_m\), both floor counts are positive for every \(d\) once \(B\) is sufficiently large.  Consequently the declared zero extension differs from the ordinary expression only on an event of probability tending to zero.

Let
\[
F_d(\alpha)=\frac{a_d}{\alpha}+\frac{b_d}{1-\alpha}.
\]
The budget-profile theorem gives \(F_d(\alpha_d^*)=\mathcal V_d^*(P)\), and
\[
F_d'(\alpha_d^*)=0,\qquad
F_d''(\alpha)=\frac{2a_d}{\alpha^3}+\frac{2b_d}{(1-\alpha)^3}.
\]
The second derivatives are uniformly bounded on \([\eta_0/2,1-\eta_0/2]\).  Taylor's theorem and (1) hence imply
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}
|F_d(\widehat\alpha_{d,m})-\mathcal V_d^*(P)|=O_P(t_m^2).           \tag{2}
\]
For \(x\ge2\),
\[
0\le\frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x\le\frac2{x^2}.
\]
Uniformly on \(\mathsf A_m\), applying this inequality to both counts shows that their floor replacement changes the budget-normalized value by \(O(B^{-1})\).  Combining this fact with (2), and retaining the declared total convention off \(\mathsf A_m\), proves
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
+\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
\right\}-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
=O_P(t_m^2+B^{-1}).
\]
