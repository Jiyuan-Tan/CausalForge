For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), consider the finite family of binary-support laws that retain the witness equalities \(A=(T+1)/2\), \(Z(a)=(W_1,W_2,W_3,W_4)\), \(Y(a)=U\), constant \(X,S_{2d}\), the two fixed role assignments, and the strictly positive factorization
\[
 P(G=g,U=u,T=t,W_1=w_1,\ldots,W_4=w_4)
 =\pi_g\nu_u
 \bigl\{\mathbf 1\{g=E\}\lambda_t+\mathbf 1\{g=O\}e_u(t)\bigr\}
 \prod_{j=1}^4 k_{j,u}(w_j).
\]
There is \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{reg}}>0\) such that every law \(P\) in this constrained family with \(\|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{reg}}\) belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  Hence the regular locus is a relative neighborhood of \(P_\epsilon\) in the constrained family; regularity is not imposed by intersecting the neighborhood with \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).
