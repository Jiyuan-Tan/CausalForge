For a binary treatment with \(p_{g,0}=1-p_{g,1}\), compare the multiplier in the IKM score with the arm-normalized multiplier in \(\phi_{g,d}\). If \(A=1\), then
\[
 \frac{A-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}}
 =\frac{p_{g,0}}{p_{g,1}}
 =p_{g,0}\frac1{p_{g,1}},
\]
whereas if \(A=0\), then
\[
 \frac{A-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}}
 =-1
 =p_{g,0}\left(-\frac1{p_{g,0}}\right).
\]
The residual factor is identical in the two formulas, separately for the experimental and observational sources. Hence
\[
 \psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})\phi_{g,d},
 \qquad
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})^2V_{g,d}. \tag{1}
\]
At \(P_\epsilon\), treatment is balanced in both sources, so (1) gives the first two displayed equalities. Substituting the second equality into the closed profiled formula gives
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}
 +\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}\right)^2
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon). \tag{2}
\]
The common positive factor in (2) preserves every design ordering and cancels from every approximation ratio. Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) therefore gives \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\) and \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)\).

On that neighborhood, treatment probabilities, bridge coefficients, source second moments in (1), and costs are continuous functions of the positive latent-cell vector. Therefore every \(\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*\) is continuous. At the center, the predictive and normalized-efficiency optimizer inequalities are strict, and the denominator of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) is positive. A finite intersection of neighborhoods preserving these strict inequalities, regularity, and the two latent bridge equations yields a radius \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\). Continuity of the ratio permits a further shrinkage so that its value exceeds one half of its center value. Finally, the narrowed cited IKM lemma applies after the binary-stability proof has verified causal identification, unique square-integrable latent bridges, operator bijectivity, and finite nonzero score moments.
