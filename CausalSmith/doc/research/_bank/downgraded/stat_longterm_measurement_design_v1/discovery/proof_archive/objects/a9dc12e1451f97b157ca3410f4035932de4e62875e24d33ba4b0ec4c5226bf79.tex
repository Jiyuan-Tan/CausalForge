For the full-catalogue pilot selector,
\[
0\le\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)-\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
\le2\varepsilon_m
\]
deterministically, and
\[
\{\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\}
\subseteq\{2\varepsilon_m\ge\gamma(P)\}.
\]
Thus
\[
P\{\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\}
\le P\{2\varepsilon_m\ge\gamma(P)\}\longrightarrow0,
\]
with the right-hand event empty when every design is optimal. Under
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\{|\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
|\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\}=O_P(r_{V,m}),
\qquad r_{V,m}=o(1),
\qquad \underline v_m=o(1),
\]
one has
\[
\varepsilon_m=O_P(r_{V,m}),
\qquad
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}|\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*(P)|
=O_P(r_{V,m}),
\]
and, after assigning the entire floor-count expression for a design the value zero whenever either floor count is zero,
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
+\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
\right\}-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
=O_P(r_{V,m}^2+B^{-1})=o_P(1).
\]
The deterministic tie rule selects one empirical minimizer, while consistency means membership in the full population optimizer set \(D^*(P)\).
