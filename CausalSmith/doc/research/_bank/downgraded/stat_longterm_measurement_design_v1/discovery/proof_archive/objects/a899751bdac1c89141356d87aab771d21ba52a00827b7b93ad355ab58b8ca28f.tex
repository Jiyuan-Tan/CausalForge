The long-term causal target and fixed-system bridge theory follow Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang (2025), whose measured short-term system and sampling ratio are exogenous. The surrogate-index literature studies prediction or efficiency gains for a given surrogate system, and existing optimal-measurement work addresses different linear front-door, Gaussian instrumental-variable, or fixed-variable two-phase designs. The headline benchmark here is the generic raw population prediction-risk rule, not a workflow attributed to any cited paper. The paired-source cost-weighted diagnostic is retained only as a secondary calculation motivated by residual-risk-per-budget reasoning.
