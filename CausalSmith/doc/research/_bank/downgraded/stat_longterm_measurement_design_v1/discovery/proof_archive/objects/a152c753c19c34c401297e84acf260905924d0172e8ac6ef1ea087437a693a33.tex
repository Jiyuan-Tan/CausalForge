Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  Positive costs, the nondegeneracy assumption, and finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) imply that every profiled value is finite and strictly positive.  The budget-profile theorem gives, for each \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+
 \sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2. \tag{1}
\]
The predictive optimizer set is a nonempty subset of the finite set \(\mathcal D\).  Substitution of (1) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) gives the displayed identity.  Its numerator is at least its denominator, and both are finite and positive.  Consequently
\[
 1\le\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)<\infty. \tag{2}
\]

The actual best-tie inequality is
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C(P)\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P). \tag{3}
\]
Dividing (3) by its strictly positive right-hand design minimum shows that (3) holds if and only if \(C(P)\ge\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  Thus the least valid best-tie constant is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For the pairwise statement, define the proof-intermediate finite set
\[
 \mathcal A_P=\left\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)\right\}.
\]
It is nonempty because it contains every diagonal pair.  For a fixed \((d,d')\in\mathcal A_P\), positivity of \(\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)\) makes the required conclusion equivalent to
\[
 C(P)\ge
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}. \tag{4}
\]
Therefore the maximum of these finitely many ratios is sufficient for every pairwise implication.  It is also necessary: a maximizing pair belongs to \(\mathcal A_P\), and (4) for that pair forces any valid constant to be at least the maximum.  This proves the sharp enumerated formula.

The unequal-cost predictive-impossibility theorem supplies centers \(P_\epsilon\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) for which \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)\to\infty\).  Hence
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty,
\]
so no finite best-tie constant is uniform on that region.  In particular, finiteness of the catalogue proves only the exact fixed-law enumeration above; it cannot supply a primitive uniform predictive-efficiency guarantee.
