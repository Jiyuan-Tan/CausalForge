For each regular design \(d\), define the two scores printed in Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~5 by
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\frac{A-p_{E,1}}{p_{E,1}}
 \{h_d(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X)-\mu_{d,A}\},
\]
\[
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\frac{A-p_{O,1}}{p_{O,1}}
 q_d(S_{2d},S_{1d},A,X)
 \{Y-h_d(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X)\},
\]
and let
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\mathbb E_P[(\psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}})^2\mid G=E],
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\mathbb E_P[(\psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}})^2\mid G=O].
\]
Define the printed-score budget profile and its profiled value by
\[
 \mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM}}(\alpha;P)
 =\frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}{\alpha}
 +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}{1-\alpha},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM},*}(P)
 =\inf_{0<\alpha<1}\mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM}}(\alpha;P).
\]
Retain ties in
\[
 D^{\mathrm{IKM},*}(P)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM},*}(P),
\]
and define
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}
 \mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM},*}(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^{\mathrm{IKM},*}(P)}.
\]
