Fix the source-faithful transported comparison from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}clw\text{-}population\text{-}design\text{-}rule}\): treatment is completely randomized with equal arm allocation, and the two arms have the same affine-regression residual variance.  These are restrictions on the transported comparison, not on the persistent-confounding bridge law.  Map the published support \(\mathcal I(\gamma)\) only to \(M_d\); the role assignment \(r_d\) remains preattached by this note.

Choose a finite-dimensional nonredundant affine encoding \(X_{\mathrm{nr}}\), with constant and reference columns removed, and define
\[
 \xi_d=(1,X_{\mathrm{nr}}^\top,Z_{M_d}^\top)^\top,
 \qquad
 \Gamma_d(P)=\mathbb E_P(\xi_d\xi_d^\top\mid G=O),
 \qquad
 g_d(P)=\mathbb E_P(\xi_dY\mid G=O).                       \tag{1}
\]
The domain \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\) consists of laws \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) such that, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P(Y^2+\|\xi_d\|^2\mid G=O)<\infty,
 \qquad \det\Gamma_d(P)\ne0,                              \tag{2}
\]
and the transported balanced comparison has the arm-homoskedastic residual required by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}clw\text{-}population\text{-}design\text{-}rule}\).  The determinant condition concerns the full Gram matrix on the nonredundant basis; no pseudoinverse is used.  Put
\[
 \beta_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)=\Gamma_d(P)^{-1}g_d(P),
 \qquad
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)=
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \{Y-\xi_d^\top\beta_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\}^2\mid G=O
 \right].                                                   \tag{3}
\]
Under the linear paired-unit cost
\[
 c(M_d,n)=nC_d,
 \qquad C_d=c_{E,d}+c_{O,d},                                \tag{4}
\]
define the continuous and exact integer criteria by
\[
 n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)=\frac{B}{C_d},
 \qquad
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)=\frac{C_dr_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{B}, \tag{5}
\]
\[
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=
 \begin{cases}
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)/\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor,&\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor>0,\\
 +\infty,&\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor=0.
 \end{cases}                                                \tag{6}
\]
Retain all ties in
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B),
 \qquad
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B). \tag{7}
\]
The continuous and exact-integer arm-normalized best-tie ratios are
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.             \tag{8}
\]
The IKM-normalized continuous and integer ratios are defined in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}design\text{-}value}\).  Equations (3)--(8) transport only the population affine-linear criterion.  The exact finite-catalogue empirical affine least-squares counterpart of equation~(3.6) is defined separately in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}clw\text{-}empirical\text{-}linear\text{-}cost\text{-}selector}\).  Nothing here attributes persistent-confounding analysis, sequential bridge-role choice, or bridge-value results to Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm, and no claim concerns their approximate OGA procedure or their original randomized-trial theorem.
