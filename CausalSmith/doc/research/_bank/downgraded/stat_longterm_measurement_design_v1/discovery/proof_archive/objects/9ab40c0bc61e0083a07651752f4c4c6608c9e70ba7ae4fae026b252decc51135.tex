For either displayed profile, positivity follows from source-arm positivity, positive costs, and finite nondegenerate source moments. The family of ordered pairs in (1) is finite and contains every diagonal pair. If a constant satisfies all treatment-aware order implications, it must dominate \(w_d/w_{d'}\) for every pair in that finite set and hence must dominate their maximum. Conversely, the maximum dominates each such ratio and therefore satisfies every implication. This proves (1).

The treatment-aware selector set is nonempty. Dividing its best attainable selected value by \(\min_{d'}w_{d'}\) shows directly that the smallest best-tie selector constant is the corresponding ratio in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}predictive\text{-}benchmark}\). A selected minimizer and a global profile minimizer form an ordered pair because the selected design minimizes \(R_d^A(P)\); hence the selector constant is bounded by (1). Taking suprema over a law class proves the uniform equivalence. This is the same finite-order argument used for the unadjusted rule in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}sharp\text{-}predictive\text{-}operator\text{-}certificate}\), now with the treatment-aware order relation.

For the quadratic-form certificate, the upper envelope gives
\[
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)=F_d\{x_d(P)\}
 \le\Lambda_+^A(P)R_d^A(P),
\]
whereas the lower envelope gives
\[
 \mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)=F_{d'}\{x_{d'}(P)\}
 \ge\Lambda_-^A(P)R_{d'}^A(P).
\]
Whenever \(R_d^A(P)\le R_{d'}^A(P)\), division yields the second inequality in (2); the first was proved by the ordered-pair argument. Finally,
\[
 F_d(x)=\left\{\sqrt{c_{E,d}Q_{E,d}(x)}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}Q_{O,d}(x)}\right\}^2
\]
is not generally quadratic. A single largest generalized eigenvalue can provide at most a one-sided full-space upper envelope; it neither supplies the positive lower envelope nor ensures that a law-realized ordered pair saturates both bounds. This proves the final assertion.
