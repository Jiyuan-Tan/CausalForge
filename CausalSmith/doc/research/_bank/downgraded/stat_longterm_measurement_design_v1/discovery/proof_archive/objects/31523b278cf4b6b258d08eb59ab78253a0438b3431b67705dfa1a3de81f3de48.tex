The within-source empirical second moment of the foldwise plug-in version of the exact arm-normalized score \(\phi_{g,d}\).  It uses the factors \(\mathbf 1\{A=a\}/\widehat p_{g,a}\) for both treatment arms and replaces \(h_d\), \(q_d\), \(p_{g,a}\), and \(\mu_{d,a}\) by the corresponding off-fold estimates.  Only the observational sample is split into \(L\ge2\) folds; every experimental observation is evaluated with each observationally trained outcome-bridge fit.
