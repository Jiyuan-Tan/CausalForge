Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  The family \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty.  Positive unit costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) imply, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 0<c_{E,d}V_{E,d}<\infty,
 \qquad
 0<c_{O,d}V_{O,d}<\infty.
 \tag{1}
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) therefore gives
\[
 v_d:=\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}
       +\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2
 \in(0,\infty).
 \tag{2}
\]
By Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}predictive\text{-}benchmark}\), the diagnostic optimizer set \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) is a nonempty subset of the finite set \(\mathcal D\), and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}v_d}
        {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}v_{d'}}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Substitution of (2) into (3) is the displayed fixed-catalogue identity.  Because the numerator minimizes over a nonempty subset of the denominator's index set, it is at least the denominator.  Because all values in (2) are finite and strictly positive and both index sets are finite and nonempty, the ratio in (3) belongs to \([1,\infty)\).

For the actual best-tie rule, a constant \(C\ge0\) is valid precisely when
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}v_d
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}v_{d'}.
 \tag{4}
\]
The denominator in (4) is strictly positive by (2), so division proves that the least valid constant is exactly the ratio in (3), namely \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For the pairwise comparison, define
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)
 =\max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{v_d}{v_{d'}}.
 \tag{5}
\]
The index set in (5) contains every diagonal pair, hence is nonempty; it is finite because \(\mathcal D\) is finite.  Equation (2) makes every ratio in (5) finite and strictly positive, so \(C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)<\infty\).  If a constant \(C(P)\) makes every diagnostic-order implication valid, then for every pair indexed in (5), \(v_d/v_{d'}\le C(P)\); taking the maximum yields \(C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)\le C(P)\).  Conversely, the definition of the maximum gives \(v_d\le C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)v_{d'}\) for every indexed pair.  Thus (5) is exactly the least constant for all pairwise implications.

It remains to prove that these lawwise finite constants have no uniform counterpart.  Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves for every prescribed \(\chi>1\) that the associated binary causal region satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty,
 \tag{6}
\]
while at each center law the uniquely diagnostic-selected design has paired cost \(5\chi\) and the arm-normalized source-score-optimal design has paired cost \(5\).  Their paired-cost ratio is therefore exactly \(\chi\).

Finally, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) optimizes the exact center ratio over \(\chi>1\).  It proves that, for all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), every minimizing sequence satisfies
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},
 \tag{7}
\]
and that
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}
   \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac1{726}
   +\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
   +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).
 \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]
Equations (3)--(5) establish exact pointwise enumeration throughout the observable bridge-law class.  Equations (6)--(8) retain the binary causal subclasses and show that neither finiteness of the catalogue nor optimization of the witness's prescribed cost disadvantage yields a uniform diagnostic-to-source-score approximation guarantee.  This proves the proposed statement.
