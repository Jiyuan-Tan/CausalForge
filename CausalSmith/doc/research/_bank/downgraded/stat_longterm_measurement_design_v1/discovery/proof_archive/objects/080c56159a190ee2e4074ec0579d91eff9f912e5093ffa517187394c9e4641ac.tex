For \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) and \(B>0\), define the tie-preserving Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm linear-cost population selector by
\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B).
\]
Its arm-normalized and IKM-normalized bridge-design approximation ratios are
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
=\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
{\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
\qquad
\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)
=\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
{\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}.
\]
Because the common factor \(B^{-1}\) cancels, the continuous selector and both ratios are independent of the numerical value of \(B\), although \(B\) is retained to display the budget normalization.
