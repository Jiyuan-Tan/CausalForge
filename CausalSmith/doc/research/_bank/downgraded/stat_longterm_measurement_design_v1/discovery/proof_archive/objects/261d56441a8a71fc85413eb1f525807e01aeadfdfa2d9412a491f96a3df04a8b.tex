For the unequal-cost binary witness, every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\) gives a law \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) for which both designs identify \(\tau\) and are regular. Although \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is more expensive, with \(c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=6>5=c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}\), it is the unique minimizer of this paper's Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm-shaped paired-source prediction objective, while \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is the unique minimizer of the causal design value. More precisely,
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{448}{2025\epsilon^2}+\frac{6272}{2025},\qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)=\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1}).
\]
Every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular for both designs, \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty\). The same divergence holds on the relatively open range of cap-feasible cost vectors satisfying
\[
 1<\frac{c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}}
 {c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}}<\frac{48}{35},
\]
for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\). Thus the failure is neither an equal-cost artifact nor an identification failure.
