Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  The full-catalogue pilot \(\mathcal P_m\) is independent of the main study.  Within the pilot and within the main study, the experimental and observational source samples are mutually independent and consist of independent and identically distributed observations from their respective source-conditional laws.  For each \(d\in\mathcal D\), write \(H_d=(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X)\) and \(L_d=(S_{2d},S_{1d},A,X)\).  Suppose the following fixed-dimensional conditions hold.

For each \((d,a)\), there are known, bounded, finite-dimensional bridge bases \(b^h_{d,a}(H_d)\) and \(b^q_{d,a}(L_d)\), and known bounded test bases \(z^h_{d,a}(L_d)\) and \(z^q_{d,a}(H_d)\), whose dimensions do not depend on \(m\) or \(B\), such that
\[
 h_d(H_d)=b^h_{d,a}(H_d)^\top\beta_{d,a},
 \qquad
 q_d(L_d)=b^q_{d,a}(L_d)^\top\gamma_{d,a}
 \quad\text{on }\{A=a\}.                                    \tag{1}
\]
The square population moment matrices
\[
 M^h_{d,a}=E_P\{z^h_{d,a}(L_d)b^h_{d,a}(H_d)^\top\mid A=a,G=O\},
 \qquad
 M^q_{d,a}=E_P\{z^q_{d,a}(H_d)b^q_{d,a}(L_d)^\top\mid A=a,G=O\} \tag{2}
\]
have smallest singular values bounded below by a constant \(c_0>0\), uniformly over the finite set of \((d,a)\); the corresponding basis Gram matrices have eigenvalues in \([c_0,c_0^{-1}]\).  The exact moment equations are
\[
 M^h_{d,a}\beta_{d,a}
 =E_P\{z^h_{d,a}(L_d)Y\mid A=a,G=O\},
 \qquad
 M^q_{d,a}\gamma_{d,a}
 =E_P\{z^q_{d,a}(H_d)\mid A=a,G=E\}.                         \tag{3}
\]
There are \(\delta>0\) and \(C_0<\infty\) such that all four bases are bounded by \(C_0\), \(E_P(|Y|^{4+\delta}\mid G=O)\le C_0\), and every source and source-arm probability is at least \(c_0\).  Pilot source-arm sizes, main-study source-arm sizes, and every training and evaluation fold size are proportional to \(m\) or \(B\), as appropriate, with fixed \(L\).

On each training sample, form the empirical conditional analogues of (2)--(3) and set
\[
 \widehat\beta_{d,a}=\widehat M_{d,a}^{h,-1}\widehat g^h_{d,a},
 \qquad
 \widehat\gamma_{d,a}=\widehat M_{d,a}^{q,-1}\widehat g^q_{d,a}, \tag{4}
\]
with value zero when a displayed empirical matrix is singular.  Use the resulting fold-external linear bridge fits in the pilot and main study, and use the empirical plug-in source-score second moments specified in Definitions~\(\mathrm{def{:}pilot\text{-}selector}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}selected\text{-}wald\text{-}variance}\).

Then, uniformly over the finite catalogue and the fixed folds,
\[
 \|\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d\|_{2,B}^{\circ}
 +\|T_d(\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d)\|_{2,O,q,d}
 =O_P(B^{-1/2}),                                               \tag{5}
\]
\[
 \|\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d\|_{2,O,q,d}
 +\|T_d^\star(\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d)\|_{2,B}^{\circ}
 =O_P(B^{-1/2}).                                               \tag{6}
\]
Both weak--strong products are \(O_P(B^{-1})=o_P(B^{-1/2})\), the multiplier expression in Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}postselection\text{-}multiplier\text{-}condition}\) is \(O_P(B^{-1/2})=o_P(1)\), and
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\bigl\{
 |\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
 |\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\bigr\}=O_P(m^{-1/2}),
 \qquad
 \max\bigl\{
 |\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}-V_{E,\widehat d_m}|,
 |\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}-V_{O,\widehat d_m}|\bigr\}
 =O_P(B^{-1/2}).                                               \tag{7}
\]
Consequently the observational-norm weak-outcome and strong-selection conditions, the retained artificial-law strong-outcome and weak-selection conditions, and all product, multiplier, pilot-variance, and main-study-variance conditions used by Theorems~\(\mathrm{thm{:}pilot\text{-}oracle\text{-}regret}\) and \(\mathrm{thm{:}postselection\text{-}wald}\) are verified on this fixed-dimensional subclass, with \(r_{V,m}=m^{-1/2}\), \(r_{\widetilde V,B}=B^{-1/2}\), and all four bridge rates of order \(B^{-1/2}\).  Hence the oracle-regret, selected-design limit law, and Wald-coverage conclusions of those theorems hold without separately maintaining those rate conditions on this subclass.  No such conclusion is asserted for all of \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).
