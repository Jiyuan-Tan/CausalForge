Let \(m_O\) be the number of observational pilot records.  At every law on the triangular center path, \(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}(G=O)=1/2\), and hence
\[
 \frac{m_O}{m}=\frac12+O_P(m^{-1/2}),
 \qquad P(m_O<m/4)\longrightarrow0.                       \tag{46}
\]
Conditional on the source labels, the ordered observational records are independent and identically distributed from the observational source law, and the deterministic training and validation halves are independent.  For fixed \(\chi\), all cells of \((A,X,Z_{M_d})\) under both designs have probabilities bounded below by a positive constant uniformly over \(0<\epsilon_m<10^{-3}\).  This follows from the strictly positive treatment kernels, the fixed signal \(y_\chi\in(0,1)\), the two fixed one-half signals, and the fact that the weak signal lies in \((0,10^{-3})\).  The number of designs and cells is fixed, and \(|Y|=1\).

Let
\[
 f_d^A(v)=\mathbb E_{P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}}(Y\mid A,X,Z_{M_d}=v,G=O)
\]
be the oracle finite-cell regression.  Binomial concentration for the training cell counts, followed by Chebyshev's inequality for each bounded cell mean, gives
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max_v
 |\widehat f_{d,m}^A(v)-f_d^A(v)|=O_P(m^{-1/2}).            \tag{47}
\]
The zero-denominator convention is harmless because the probability of any empty training cell tends to zero exponentially, uniformly over the finitely many cells.

Conditional on the training half, every validation loss is bounded by \(4\).  The conditional variance bound, (46), and a finite union bound give
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|
 \widehat R_{d,m}^A-
 \mathbb E\{(Y-\widehat f_{d,m}^A(A,X,Z_{M_d}))^2
 \mid\text{training},G=O\}
 \right|=O_P(m^{-1/2}).                                   \tag{48}
\]
The conditional-mean projection identity gives, design by design,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\mathbb E\{(Y-\widehat f_{d,m}^A(A,X,Z_{M_d}))^2
 \mid\text{training},G=O\}\\
 &\qquad=R_d^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})
 +\mathbb E\{(\widehat f_{d,m}^A-f_d^A)^2
 \mid\text{training},G=O\}.
\end{aligned}                                               \tag{49}
\]
The last term is \(O_P(m^{-1})\) uniformly in \(d\) by (47).  Equations (48)--(49) prove (1).

The center risk gap is bounded away from zero uniformly in \(m\).  Indeed, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}treatment\text{-}aware\text{-}sharp\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) gives
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm e}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})
 =\frac{585}{1024},
 \qquad
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})
 <\frac3{10\chi}<\frac3{10}.
\]
Thus the gap exceeds \(585/1024-3/10>0\), and (1) implies
\[
 P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}\{\widehat d_m^A=d_{\mathrm p}\}\longrightarrow1,
\]
regardless of the deterministic rate at which \(\epsilon_m\downarrow0\).

On this selection event, each achieved ratio equals its population center ratio at \(d_{\mathrm p}\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) gives, under both normalizations,
\[
 \frac{\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 {\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 =\frac{\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p},\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 {\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e},\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 >\frac{\epsilon_m^{-2}+14}{726}\longrightarrow\infty.  \tag{50}
\]
For any finite threshold, (50) eventually exceeds it and the selection event has probability tending to one.  This proves both convergences in (3).  Independence of the pilot and main study is exactly Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}independent\text{-}pilot}\); the construction itself uses no bridge learner or growing sieve.
