Fix any prescribed \(\chi>1\) and use Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}witness}\). For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), both admissible bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), the unique raw population prediction-risk optimizer is \(d_{\mathrm p}\), and the unique arm-normalized design-value optimizer is \(d_{\mathrm e}\). More precisely,
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{3}{10\chi}\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac{3}{10\chi}<\frac3{10}<\frac35
 =R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
The paired costs and the secondary cost-weighted prediction objectives satisfy
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi,
 \qquad
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}=5,
\]
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon;B).
\]
Moreover,
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +\frac{896}{75(10\chi-3)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac{3872}{25},
\]
and, as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) with \(\chi\) fixed,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}).
\]
For each fixed pair \((\chi,\epsilon)\), there is a radius \(r_\epsilon>0\) such that every law \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\) with \(\|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<r_\epsilon\) is regular, has the same strict raw-risk and arm-normalized optimizer assignments, has both bridge contrasts equal to \(\tau\), and satisfies \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)>\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)/2\).
