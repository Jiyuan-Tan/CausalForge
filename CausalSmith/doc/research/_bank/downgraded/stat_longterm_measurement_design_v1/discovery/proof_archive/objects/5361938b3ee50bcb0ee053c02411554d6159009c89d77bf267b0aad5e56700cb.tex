Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and abbreviate the explicitly indexed center \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}\) by the legacy notation \(P_\epsilon\) used in the statement. Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) verifies directly that this center belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), that both bridge contrasts and \(\tau\) equal zero, and that \(d_{\mathrm p}\) and \(d_{\mathrm e}\) are respectively the unique predictive and arm-normalized value optimizers. It also gives
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi,
 \qquad
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}=5,
\]
the prediction comparison in the statement, and the exact moment formulas
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +\frac{896}{75(10\chi-3)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac{3872}{25}.
\]
Writing \(a_\chi\) and \(b_{\chi,\epsilon}\) as in that lemma, its exact ratio formula expands as
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{25}{3872}
 \{a_\chi+b_{\chi,\epsilon}+2\sqrt{a_\chi b_{\chi,\epsilon}}\}.
\]
For fixed \(\chi\), the three terms have orders \(O_\chi(1)\),
\[
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14),
\]
and \(O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1})\), respectively. Therefore
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}),
\]
which is the asserted expansion.

It remains to prove the neighborhood assertion. In the relative-interior coordinates of \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\), every relevant conditional probability and finite moment is continuous. At \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}\), the early- and late-proxy conditional matrices and the two bridge operators have nonzero determinants, all support probabilities are positive, and both source score variances are positive, as established in the proof of the sharp-elbow lemma. Determinants, support inequalities, and positive variances remain nonzero on a sufficiently small relative neighborhood. There the bridge solutions are inverses of finite matrices, so the bridges, score variances, prediction risks, and profiled values are continuous.

The structural factorization defining \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\) preserves consistency, experimental randomization, observational latent exchangeability, proxy conditional independence, source invariance, and \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\). On a sufficiently small neighborhood the binary late-proxy matrix remains invertible, so there is a latent outcome bridge \(h^\dagger\) with
\[
 \mathbb E[h^\dagger(L)\mid U]=U.
\]
Experimental randomization and source invariance then yield, for both treatment levels,
\[
 \mu_{d,a}
 =\mathbb E[h^\dagger(L)\mid A=a,G=E]
 =\mathbb E[U]
 =\mathbb E[Y(a)\mid G=O].                            \tag{1}
\]
Thus both designwise contrasts remain equal to \(\tau=0\).

The two optimizer inequalities are strict at the center and their criteria are continuous. The ratio is also a continuous positive function throughout the regular neighborhood. Shrinking the radius if necessary therefore preserves both unique optimizers and gives
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
The resulting radius is the required \(r_\epsilon>0\).
