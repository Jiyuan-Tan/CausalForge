For a fixed short-term-outcome system, write \(\tau_0\) for its causal average long-term treatment effect and let \(n=n_E+n_O\).  Use a fixed number \(L_0\ge2\) of observational cross-fitting folds, independent i.i.d. observations within the two mutually independent sources, \(n_E,n_O\to\infty\), and \(n_E/n_O\to\lambda\in(0,\infty)\).  Suppose the conditions of Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~4 statements 1 and 2 both hold.  Explicitly, the causal and completeness conditions used by their Theorems~1 and 2 hold; their Assumptions~6 and 7 furnish square-integrable latent outcome and selection bridges \(h_0\) and \(q_0\); the corresponding observed conditional-moment equations hold; and both parts of their Assumption~8 hold with nuisance limits equal to \(h_0\) and \(q_0\).  Let \(\mathsf T_0\) and \(\mathsf T_0^\star\) be the conditional-expectation operator and adjoint defined immediately before their Assumption~8.  Thus, for every fold \(1\le\ell\le L_0\),
\[
 \|\mathsf T_0(\widehat h^{(-\ell)}-h_0)\|_2=O_P(\delta_{h,n}),
 \qquad
 \|\widehat h^{(-\ell)}-h_0\|_2=O_P(\rho_{h,n}),
\]
\[
 \|\mathsf T_0^\star(\widehat q^{(-\ell)}-q_0)\|_2=O_P(\delta_{q,n}),
 \qquad
 \|\widehat q^{(-\ell)}-q_0\|_2=O_P(\rho_{q,n}),
\]
where all four deterministic rates tend to zero and
\[
 \min\{\delta_{h,n}\rho_{q,n},\rho_{h,n}\delta_{q,n}\}
 =o(n^{-1/2}).
\]
Write \(V_E\) and \(V_O\) for the experimental and observational source-score second moments displayed in their Theorem~5, and assume
\[
 0<\min\{V_E,V_O\}\le\max\{V_E,V_O\}<\infty.
\]
Then
\[
 \sigma^2=\frac{1+\lambda}{\lambda}V_E+(1+\lambda)V_O
\]
is finite and strictly positive, and their cross-fitted doubly robust estimator satisfies
\[
 \sqrt n\,(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{DR}}-\tau_0)
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2).
\]
In particular, the published theorem is centered at the causal target \(\tau_0\) under its two identification premises; it does not assert an observed-bridge-centered limit on a larger model where those premises may fail.
