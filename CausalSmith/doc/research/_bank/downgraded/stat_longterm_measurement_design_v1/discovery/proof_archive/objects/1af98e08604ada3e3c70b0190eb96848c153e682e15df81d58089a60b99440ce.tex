Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\).  At every binary center, treatment is balanced in both sources, so
\[
 p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=\frac12.                              \tag{1}
\]
The definitions of the IKM-normalized scores and moments therefore give, for either design,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},          \tag{2}
\]
and hence
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d}.                \tag{3}
\]
Using Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}design\text{-}value}\), (3) implies
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 &=\left(
 \sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}+
 \sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}
 \right)^2\\
 &=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                 \tag{4}
\end{aligned}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) says that \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes the right-hand side of (4), while \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is the unique predictive optimizer.  Thus
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).                   \tag{5}
\]

For the stability assertion, first restrict to the radius supplied by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\).  On that neighborhood every law is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and \(d_{\mathrm p}\) remains uniquely prediction-selected.  In finite-cell coordinates, the treatment probabilities, arm-normalized score moments, and therefore
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}            \tag{6}
\]
are continuous.  The normalized profile is consequently continuous and strictly positive.  At the center its optimizer ordering is strict by (4), and its best-tie ratio is positive.  A further reduction of the radius therefore preserves
\(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\) and ensures
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).       \tag{7}
\]
Call the resulting positive radius \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\).

Finally, on this finite-support neighborhood the support probabilities are interior, the outcome and selection bridges exist uniquely, the conditional-expectation operator is bijective, the bridge contrast equals the causal target, and the score moments are finite.  Thus whenever the remaining fixed-system local-model premises stated in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) are imposed, that lemma identifies the normalized profile as the profiled local causal semiparametric efficiency bound.  This is precisely the conditional efficiency interpretation asserted here.
