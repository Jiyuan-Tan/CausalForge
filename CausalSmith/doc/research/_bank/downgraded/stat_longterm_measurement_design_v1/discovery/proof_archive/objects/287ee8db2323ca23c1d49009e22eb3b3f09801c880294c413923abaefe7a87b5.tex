The counterexample isolates an objective mismatch. CLW's criterion correctly rewards a subset that predicts \(Y\) per dollar in its intended randomized-trial calculation. In the long-term bridge catalogue, however, that same subset can contain an early proxy whose signal for the latent confounder is arbitrarily weak. Prediction remains excellent because the late proxy is strong, while the selection bridge scales as \(\epsilon^{-1}\) and its variance as \(\epsilon^{-2}\). Design-value minimization detects this loss; residual prediction per dollar does not.
