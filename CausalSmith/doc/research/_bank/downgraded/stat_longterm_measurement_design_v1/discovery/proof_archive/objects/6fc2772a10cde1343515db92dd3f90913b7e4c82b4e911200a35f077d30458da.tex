Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) and \(d\in\mathcal D\).  A regular two-source parametric path \(\{P_t:|t|<t_0\}\) through \(P_0=P\), with source-conditional scores \(\ell_E\) and \(\ell_O\), is called a \emph{bridge-differentiable submodel for design \(d\) at \(P\)} when the following conditions hold.  For all sufficiently small \(t\), \(P_t\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) and its design-\(d\) outcome bridge \(h_{d,t}\) is unique; after choosing a common dominating measure, there is \(\dot h_d\in L_2(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X;P)\) such that
\[
 \left\|\frac{h_{d,t}-h_{d,0}}{t}-\dot h_d\right\|_{L_2(P)}\longrightarrow0;
\]
and the conditional bridge equation may be differentiated in \(L_2(P)\) at \(t=0\), giving
\[
 T_d\dot h_d
 =\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \{Y-h_d(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X)\}\ell_O
 \mathrel{\Big|}S_{2d},S_{1d},A,X,G=O
 \right].
\]
This definition is a restriction on paths, not a consequence of pointwise bijectivity of \(T_d\).
