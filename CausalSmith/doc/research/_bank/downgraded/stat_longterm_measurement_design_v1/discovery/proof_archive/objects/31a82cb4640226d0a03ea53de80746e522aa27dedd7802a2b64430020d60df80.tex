Fix \(\chi>1\). Along the triangular center sequence, every observational cell of \((A,X,Z_{M_d})\) has probability bounded below by a positive constant depending only on \(\chi\), uniformly in \(m\). The observational treatment kernels lie in \([3/8,5/8]\), the early signal lies in \((0,10^{-3})\), the late signal \(y_\chi\) is fixed below one, and the other signals equal \(1/2\). Hence every training and validation cell count is of order \(m\) with probability tending to one; a zero denominator occurs with exponentially small probability.

Let
\[
 f_{d,m}^*(v)=\mathbb E_{P_{\chi,epsilon_m}}(Y\mid A,X,Z_{M_d}=v,G=O).
\]
Since \(Y\in\{-1,1\}\), saturated cell means are bounded. The finite-cell variance calculation gives
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max_v
 |\widehat f_{d,m}^A(v)-f_{d,m}^*(v)|=O_P(m^{-1/2}).         \tag{35}
\]
Conditional on the independent training half, each validation squared loss is bounded by four, so
\[
 \max_d\left|\widehat R_{d,m}^A-
 \mathbb E\!\left[\{Y-\widehat f_{d,m}^A(A,X,Z_{M_d})\}^2
 \mid G=O,\mathcal I_{m,O}^{\mathrm{tr}}\right]\right|
 =O_P(m^{-1/2}).                                            \tag{36}
\]
Conditional-expectation orthogonality gives the exact excess-risk identity
\[
 \mathbb E\!\left[
 \{\widehat f_{d,m}^A-f_{d,m}^*\}^2
 \mid G=O,\mathcal I_{m,O}^{\mathrm{tr}}
 \right]=O_P(m^{-1}),                                      \tag{37}
\]
where (35) and the finite number of cells give the order. Combining (36)--(37) proves the uniform \(m^{-1/2}\) risk rate.

The treatment-aware elbow lemma gives uniformly
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}^A(P_{\chi,epsilon_m})<\frac3{10},
 \qquad
 R_{d_{\mathrm e}}^A(P_{\chi,epsilon_m})=\frac{585}{1024}.
\]
The population gap is bounded away from zero, so the uniform empirical error implies selection of \(d_{\mathrm p}\) with probability tending to one. On that event, each ratio in the theorem equals its deterministic \(d_{\mathrm p}\) center ratio, and the sharp-elbow lemma gives
\[
 \frac{\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*}{\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*}
 =\frac{\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p},\mathrm{IKM}}^*}
 {\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e},\mathrm{IKM}}^*}
 >\frac{\epsilon_m^{-2}+14}{726}\longrightarrow\infty.  \tag{38}
\]
Thus both achieved ratios diverge in probability. Pilot independence makes the rule feasible before main-study measurement, and no inverse-bridge learner enters this selection proof.
