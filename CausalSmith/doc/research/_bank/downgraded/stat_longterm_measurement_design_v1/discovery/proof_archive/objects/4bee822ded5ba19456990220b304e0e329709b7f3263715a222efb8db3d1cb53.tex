Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). The family \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty. Positive unit costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) imply, for every \(d\),
\[
 0<c_{E,d}V_{E,d}<\infty,
 \qquad 0<c_{O,d}V_{O,d}<\infty.
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) therefore gives
\[
 v_d:=\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2\in(0,\infty). \tag{1}
\]
The corrected predictive-benchmark definition gives
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}v_d}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}v_{d'}}. \tag{2}
\]
Substitution of (1) proves the displayed identity. The numerator minimizes over a nonempty subset of the denominator's index set, so the ratio is at least one; finiteness and positivity give a finite ratio. Dividing the best-tie inequality by the positive denominator in (2) shows that its least constant is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For the pairwise comparison, set
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)
 =\max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,R_d(P)\le R_{d'}(P)}}
 \frac{v_d}{v_{d'}}. \tag{3}
\]
The index set contains all diagonal pairs and is finite; (1) makes every ratio finite. Any constant valid for all raw-risk-order implications must dominate every ratio in (3), hence their maximum. Conversely, the maximum itself makes all implications valid. This proves exactness of (3).

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves, for every prescribed \(\chi>1\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty, \tag{4}
\]
while the uniquely raw-risk-selected center design has paired cost \(5\chi\) and the source-score-optimal design has paired cost \(5\). Their ratio is \(\chi\). Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) further proves
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3} \tag{5}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}+o(\epsilon^{2/3}). \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]
Equations (1)--(3) establish exact lawwise enumeration. Equations (4)--(6) show that neither finiteness of the catalogue nor optimization of the witness's cost disadvantage gives a uniform raw-risk-to-source-score approximation guarantee. This proves the theorem.
