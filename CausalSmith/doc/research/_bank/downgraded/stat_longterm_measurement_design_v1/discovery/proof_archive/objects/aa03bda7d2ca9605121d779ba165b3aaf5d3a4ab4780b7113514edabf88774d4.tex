For each \(d\in\mathcal D\), define the two generic unrestricted observational prediction risks
\[
 R_d(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(X,Z_{M_d})}
 \mathbb E_P[(Y-f(X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O],
\]
\[
 R_d^A(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(A,X,Z_{M_d})}
 \mathbb E_P[(Y-f(A,X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O].                \tag{1}
\]
Their tie-preserving population selectors and arm-normalized source-score ratios are
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}R_d(P),
 \qquad
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}R_d^A(P),
\]
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.           \tag{2}
\]
Distinctly, on \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\), the transported Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm population rule uses the affine-linear residual variance \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\), not either unrestricted risk.  Under \(c(M_d,n)=nC_d\), its continuous and integer criteria, selectors, and arm-normalized ratio are those in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}clw\text{-}linear\text{-}cost\text{-}selector}\):
\[
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)=\frac{C_dr_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{B},
 \qquad
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 =\frac{r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor}.      \tag{3}
\]
The support is mapped only to \(M_d\), and this note's role assignment remains preattached.  No equality between \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\) and \(R_d(P)\) or \(R_d^A(P)\) is imposed away from the binary centers where it is proved.  The two unrestricted-risk rules in (1)--(2) are secondary comparisons and are not attributed to a named published proxy-selection workflow.
