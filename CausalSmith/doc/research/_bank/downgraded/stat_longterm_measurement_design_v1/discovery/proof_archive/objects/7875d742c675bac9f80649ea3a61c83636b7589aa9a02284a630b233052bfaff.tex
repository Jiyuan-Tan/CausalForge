First fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\) and \(B>0\). The named continuous CLW population rule has the exact finite-catalogue ratios
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d'}V_{E,d'}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d'}V_{O,d'}})^2}
 \in[1,\infty),                                            \tag{1}
\]
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 \in[1,\infty).                                            \tag{2}
\]
Each ratio is the smallest constant for its actual best-tie selector. For either positive profile \(w_d\), the least constant making every named-rule order implication valid is
\[
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}\frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}.       \tag{3}
\]
On the positive-count integer domain, the same formula with \(\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}\) gives the exact integer-selector constant. Computing every criterion, selector, and pairwise maximum takes at most \(O(|\mathcal D|^2)\) arithmetic comparisons after the designwise moments have been supplied.

Separately, for every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), the generic unrestricted unadjusted and treatment-aware population squared-risk rules have the exact identities
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},            \tag{4}
\]
with the analogous two IKM-normalized identities and the corresponding exact pairwise maxima ordered by \(R_d(P)\) or \(R_d^A(P)\). These generic assertions do not require \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\).

The jointly shrunk binary regions have unbounded best-tie ratios for the named CLW rule and, secondarily, for both generic rules under both profiles. At every center all six ratios agree and
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}),                                       \tag{5}
\]
where \(\rho\) can be any of the six center ratios. Thus finiteness gives an exact computable lawwise enumeration, not a law-uniform named or generic guarantee.
