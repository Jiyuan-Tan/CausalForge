Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang establish identification, fixed-system asymptotic normality, and local efficiency for a given short-term-outcome system and sampling ratio. The surrogate-index literature likewise compares estimators for a prespecified surrogate system. Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm motivate prediction-based covariate choice in a different experimental setting; the paired-source cost-weighted objective here is this paper's transplant, not a guarantee attributed to their procedure. Work on optimal measurement or two-phase design either uses linear front-door or Gaussian instrumental-variable structure or holds the measured variable set fixed. The present contribution instead makes the proxy subset, sequential role assignment, and source allocation joint design variables, gives an exact curvature-plus-floor decomposition for feasible allocation, and proves a sharp uniform predictive-efficiency elbow of \(1/726\) over every finite prescribed cost disadvantage, even with sourcewise cost dominance of the prediction-selected design.
