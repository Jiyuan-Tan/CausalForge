At \(P_\epsilon\), symmetry of the Rademacher latent variable gives \(p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=1/2\). Lemma~\texttt{lem:ikmw-arm-score-normalization} therefore gives the two quarter-scaling identities. Lemma~\texttt{lem:binary-witness-stability} gives the strict paper-profile optimizer \(d_{\mathrm e}\), the strict predictive optimizer \(d_{\mathrm p}\), and the divergent center ratio. Multiplication of every design value by the same positive constant preserves both the minimizer and the ratio.

It remains to justify the neighborhood claim for the printed-score profile rather than import the paper-profile radius.  In the constrained finite-cell model, strict cell positivity persists locally.  The early and late binary proxy-channel determinants are nonzero at \(P_\epsilon\), so they remain nonzero locally.  The two latent bridge systems and the observed conditional-expectation system are therefore solved by inverses of finite matrices whose entries vary continuously with the cell vector.  Hence the bridge coefficients, the treated probabilities \(p_{g,1}\), and all finite source-score second moments vary continuously.  The causal calculation also persists: \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), the proxy kernels are source invariant, and the latent outcome-bridge equation yields \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=0=\tau\) for both designs.  Thus the hypotheses of the cited fixed-system efficiency theorem hold throughout a sufficiently small neighborhood, and \(\mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM},*}\) is the profiled local causal-efficiency value there.

At the center the predictive and printed-score efficiency inequalities are strict, and the denominator of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) is positive. Continuity therefore supplies a positive radius on which both optimizer assignments persist and the ratio exceeds half its center value. Shrinking the radius further, if necessary, preserves all regularity and causal-identification properties proved above.
