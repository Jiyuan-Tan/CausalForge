Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\) and \(B>0\).  The moment and full-Gram conditions make every \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\), and hence both named criteria, finite whenever its integer count is positive.  Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) imply \(0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)<\infty\).  Source-arm positivity gives \(0<(1-p_{g,1})^2<1\), so the IKM profiles are also finite and positive.  The finite named selector is nonempty.  Substitution of the profile formulas gives (1)--(2).  Each numerator minimizes over a nonempty subset of the denominator's index set, proving that each ratio is finite and at least one.  The actual best-tie inequality holds exactly for constants no smaller than the displayed ratio.

For either profile \(w_d\), set
\[
 C_{\mathrm{ord}}^{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}\frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}.       \tag{42}
\]
Every constant satisfying all named-rule order implications must dominate each indexed ratio; conversely, (42) dominates every such pair.  This proves (3).  On the positive-count integer domain, the identical finite argument with \(\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}\) proves the exact integer constant.  Computing the criteria and minima takes \(O(|\mathcal D|)\) comparisons, and enumerating every ordered pair takes at most \(O(|\mathcal D|^2)\).

Now fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  The unadjusted identity is Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}catalogue\text{-}predictive\text{-}guarantee}\).  Direct substitution proves the treatment-aware identity in (4) and both IKM analogues.  Repeating (42) with \(R_d(P)\) or \(R_d^A(P)\) proves their exact pairwise maxima without imposing the narrower CLW domain.

Finally, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}two\text{-}rule\text{-}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves unbounded ratios for the named continuous rule and both generic rules on the jointly shrunk regions.  Lemmas~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) and \(\mathrm{lem{:}treatment\text{-}aware\text{-}sharp\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) prove equality of all six center ratios and the common expansion (5).  Hence the finite enumerations are exact and computable law by law but furnish no law-uniform guarantee.
