Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  Positive unit costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), together with Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\), give
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2
 <\infty                                                     \tag{29}
\]
for every design.  Since \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, both the global minimizer set and \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) are nonempty.  Substitution of (29) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the displayed identity.  Its numerator minimizes over a subset of the denominator's index set, so the ratio is at least one; positivity and finiteness in (29) make it finite.

Let
\[
 C_{\mathrm{sel}}
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.
\]
The best selected value is at most \(C\) times the global optimum exactly when \(C\ge C_{\mathrm{sel}}\); hence this ratio is the smallest best-tie constant.  For all pairwise implications, any valid constant must dominate every ratio indexed by a pair satisfying \(R_d(P)\le R_{d'}(P)\), and their finite maximum itself dominates each such ratio.  This proves the exact pairwise formula.
