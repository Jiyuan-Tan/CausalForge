Fix the prescribed \(\chi>1\). For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), let \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\) be furnished by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\), and let \(\delta_\epsilon\) be the radius used in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\). Choose
\[
 0<\widetilde\delta_\epsilon
 <\min\{\delta_\epsilon,r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\}
\]
and define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\bigcup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \left\{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi):
 \|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<\widetilde\delta_\epsilon\right\}. \tag{1}
\]
Then \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\subseteq\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and is relatively open in \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\).
