At every binary center, treatment is balanced in both sources, so \(p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=1/2\). The definitions immediately give, for either design,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},
\]
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d}. \tag{1}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon). \tag{2}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) shows that \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes the right-hand side, while \(d_{\mathrm p}\) uniquely minimizes raw prediction risk. The common factor in (2) cancels from the ratio.

Restrict first to the regularity radius from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\). Within that finite-cell neighborhood, treatment probabilities, bridge coefficients, source moments, profiled values, and raw risks are continuous. In particular,
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}
\]
are continuous. Since the raw-risk and normalized-profile optimizer gaps at the center are strict and the center ratio is positive, a further radius reduction gives \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\) on which regularity and common identification persist, both optimizer assignments remain unchanged, and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Under the fixed-system causal-identification, local-model, canonical-gradient, and source-ratio premises recorded in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\), that lemma identifies the unprofiled IKM variance as the local causal efficiency bound. Profiling its positive source components by the algebra of Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) gives the stated conditional interpretation.
