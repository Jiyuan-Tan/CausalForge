Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and write \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\) for the binary center in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}witness}\).  Then \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), both designwise bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac{3}{10\chi}\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac{3}{10\chi}<\frac3{10}<\frac35
 =R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon}).                                      \tag{1}
\]
For the treatment-aware rule,
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 \le R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})<\frac3{10}
 <\frac{585}{1024}
 =R_{d_{\mathrm e}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon}).                                  \tag{2}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.                          \tag{3}
\]
The prediction-selected design is costlier in each source:
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =\frac{5(\chi-1)}2>0.                                                        \tag{4}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 V_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{40\chi}{10\chi-3},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14),
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\frac{3872}{25}.           \tag{5}
\]
At every center the four best-tie ratios coincide and have the exact value
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac{25}{3872}
 \left\{
 \sqrt{\frac{20\chi(5\chi-1)}{10\chi-3}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{32(5\chi+1)}{75(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14)}
 \right\}^{2}.                                                               \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]
Each ratio in (6) is at least
\[
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14)
 >\frac{\epsilon^{-2}+14}{726}.                                               \tag{7}
\]
For all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), the infimum over \(\chi>1\) of any ratio in (6) is attained.  If \(\chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)\) is any minimizer and \(k_\star=\sqrt[3]{18}/15\), then
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)\sim k_\star\epsilon^{-2/3}.                 \tag{8}
\]
On writing \(\chi=k\epsilon^{-2/3}\), the coefficient of \(\epsilon^{2/3}\) inside the braces-squared expression before multiplication by \(25/3872\) is
\[
 q(k)=2\sqrt{\frac{32k}{15}}+\frac8{75k},                                    \tag{9}
\]
and \(q\) has the unique minimizer \(k_\star\).  Simultaneously for all four ratios,
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}),                                                          \tag{10}
\]
where \(\rho\) may denote any one of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}\), \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A\), \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\), or \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}\).  In particular, the common first-order limit in (10) is \(1/726\).
