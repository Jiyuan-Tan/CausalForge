By Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}common\text{-}identification\text{-}class}\), \(\theta_d(P)=\tau\) for every \(d\in\mathcal D\).  Therefore \(\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)=\tau\) for every pilot realization.  Substituting this identity into the statistic and coverage conclusions of Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}postselection\text{-}wald}\) gives
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)}{\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1),
 \qquad P\{\tau\in I_B\}\to1-\eta.
\]
The total event and its zero extension are unchanged.
