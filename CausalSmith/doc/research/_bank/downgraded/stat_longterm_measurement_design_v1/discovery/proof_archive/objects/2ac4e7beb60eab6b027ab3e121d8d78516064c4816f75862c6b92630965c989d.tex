We now prove the neighborhood assertion. The center is in the relative interior of the finite-cell parameterization of \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\). Positivity of cells, source and treatment overlap, and nonzero determinants of the finite conditional-expectation matrices are open conditions. Matrix inversion is continuous where the determinant is nonzero, so the two bridges, their contrasts, and their source-score variances are continuous on a sufficiently small ball. The variances remain finite and strictly positive there. The constrained model keeps \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), randomized treatment in source \(E\), source-invariant proxy kernels, and conditional proxy independence. Thus its causal restrictions persist, and the bridge argument using completeness gives both bridge contrasts equal to the common \(\tau=0\) throughout the ball.

The two raw risks are continuous finite-cell conditional-variance functionals. The profiled values are continuous compositions of the positive costs and source-score variances. Since both optimizer gaps at \(P_\epsilon\) are strict, a sufficiently small radius preserves the two unique optimizer assignments. Finally, the denominator defining \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}\) stays positive and both numerator and denominator are continuous. Because \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)>0\), shrinking the radius once more ensures
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Taking the minimum of these finitely many positive radii gives the asserted \(r_\epsilon\).
