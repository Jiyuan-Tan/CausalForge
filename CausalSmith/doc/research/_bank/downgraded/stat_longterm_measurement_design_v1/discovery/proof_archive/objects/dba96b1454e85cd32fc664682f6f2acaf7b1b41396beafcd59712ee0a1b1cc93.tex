The admissible catalogue is finite and fixed ex ante, every candidate design is required to satisfy bridge existence, completeness, operator bijectivity, overlap, and moment conditions, and the pilot is separately funded and independent of the main study. Uniform nuisance and variance-estimation rates are maintained estimation conditions, not consequences of catalogue finiteness. Relative openness is asserted only within the constrained binary finite-cell model. The unscaled arm-normalized profile is the gradient value for the identified bridge contrast on the full class; causal Wald coverage and absolute semiparametric-efficiency language require common identification and the fixed-system IKM premises. The impossibility theorem rules out a uniform guarantee from predictive selection alone for every finite \(\chi>1\); it does not say that prediction and efficiency can never agree under additional primitive comparison restrictions.
