After the independently funded pilot chooses \(\widehat d_m\) and \(\widehat\alpha_m\), the main study measures only \(M_{\widehat d_m}\). Let \(\mathcal I_{E,B}\) and \(\mathcal I_{O,B}\) have sizes \(N_{E,B}\) and \(N_{O,B}\), and partition \(\mathcal I_{O,B}=\bigsqcup_{\ell=1}^L\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}\) into deterministic balanced folds. Train only \(\widehat h_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}\) and \(\widehat q_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}\), excluding observational fold \(\ell\) from both fits and using the experimental sample only for the selection-bridge fit. For \(a\in\{0,1\}\), define the selected-design arm counts, empirical arm probabilities, bridge means, residual corrections, and scores exactly by
\[
e_{B,a}=\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}}\mathbf1\{A_i=a\},
\qquad
o_{B,\ell,a}=\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}}\mathbf1\{A_i=a\},
\]
\[
\widehat\mu_{\widehat d_m,a}^{(-\ell)}
=\frac1{e_{B,a}}\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}}\mathbf1\{A_i=a\}
\widehat h_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{3\widehat d_m,i},S_{2\widehat d_m,i},a,X_i),
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\mu_{B,\widehat d_m}(a)
={}&\frac1L\sum_{\ell=1}^L\widehat\mu_{\widehat d_m,a}^{(-\ell)}\\
&+\frac1L\sum_{\ell=1}^L\frac1{o_{B,\ell,a}}
\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}}\mathbf1\{A_i=a\}\widehat q_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}
\{Y_i-\widehat h_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}\},
\end{aligned}
\]
with every function evaluated at its selected-design arguments, and set \(\widehat\tau_B(\widehat d_m,\widehat\alpha_m)=\widehat\mu_{B,\widehat d_m}(1)-\widehat\mu_{B,\widehat d_m}(0)\). For \(i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\phi_{E,\widehat d_m,B,i}
=\frac1L\sum_{\ell=1}^L\sum_{a=0}^1(-1)^{1-a}
\frac{\mathbf1\{A_i=a\}}{e_{B,a}/N_{E,B}}
\bigl\{&\widehat h_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}
(S_{3\widehat d_m,i},S_{2\widehat d_m,i},a,X_i)
-\widehat\mu_{\widehat d_m,a}^{(-\ell)}\bigr\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
For \(i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\phi_{O,\widehat d_m,B,i}
={}&\sum_{a=0}^1(-1)^{1-a}
\frac{\mathbf1\{A_i=a\}}{o_{B,\ell,a}/|\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}|}
\widehat q_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}(S_{2\widehat d_m,i},S_{1\widehat d_m,i},a,X_i)\\
&\quad\times\bigl\{Y_i-\widehat h_{\widehat d_m,B}^{(-\ell)}
(S_{3\widehat d_m,i},S_{2\widehat d_m,i},a,X_i)\bigr\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Set
\[
\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}
=\frac1{N_{E,B}}\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{E,B}}
\widehat\phi_{E,\widehat d_m,B,i}^2,
\qquad
\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}
=\frac1{N_{O,B}}\sum_{\ell=1}^L\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{O,B,\ell}}
\widehat\phi_{O,\widehat d_m,B,i}^2.
\]
Let \(\mathsf D_B\) require positive selected source, arm, and fold-arm counts. On \(\mathsf D_B\), define
\[
\widehat s_B^2=B\left\{\frac{\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{E,B}}+\frac{\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{O,B}}\right\},
\quad
s_{0,B}^2=B\left\{\frac{V_{E,\widehat d_m}}{N_{E,B}}+\frac{V_{O,\widehat d_m}}{N_{O,B}}\right\},
\]
and \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}=\mathsf D_B\cap\{\widehat s_B^2>0\}\). On \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}\), use the selected estimator, statistic centered at \(\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\), and Wald interval; on its complement set the estimator, standard error, and statistic to zero and the interval to \([0,0]\). Every zero denominator is totalized at zero. No unselected main-study fit or score variance is part of this measurable map.
