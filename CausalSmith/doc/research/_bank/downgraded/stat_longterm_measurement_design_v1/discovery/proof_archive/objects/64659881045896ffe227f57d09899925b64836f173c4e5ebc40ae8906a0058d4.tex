For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), if \(\mathcal D=\{d\}\), the main study fixes positive counts \(n_E,n_O\), and \(B=c_{E,d}n_E+c_{O,d}n_O\), then
\[
 \frac{1}{B}\mathcal V_d\!\left(\frac{c_{E,d}n_E}{B};P\right)
 =\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.
\]
The right-hand side is the fixed-system two-source pathwise-gradient variance for the identified observed-data bridge contrast \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}\). If, in addition, \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=\tau\) and \((\phi_{E,d},\phi_{O,d})\) is the canonical gradient for \(\tau\) in the Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang fixed-system model under its bridge-existence, bridge-uniqueness, operator-bijectivity, moment, and interior sampling-ratio conditions, then this quantity is their fixed-system causal semiparametric efficiency bound. Without those additional causal-identification and canonical-gradient conditions, only the bridge-functional gradient-variance identity is asserted.
