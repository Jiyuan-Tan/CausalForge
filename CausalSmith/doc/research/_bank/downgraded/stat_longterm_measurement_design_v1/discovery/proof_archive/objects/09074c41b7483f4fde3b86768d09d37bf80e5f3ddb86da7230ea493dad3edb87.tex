Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\).  The radii \(r_\epsilon\) and \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) in Lemmas~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) and \(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\) may be jointly decreased, without losing any of their conclusions or those of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}predictive\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\), so that every law \(P\) in the resulting \(r_\epsilon\)-ball satisfies
\[
 2C_{d_{\mathrm p}}r_{d_{\mathrm p}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)
 <C_{d_{\mathrm e}}r_{d_{\mathrm e}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P),   \tag{1}
\]
and hence, for every \(B\ge5\chi=C_{d_{\mathrm p}}\),
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_\epsilon;B).               \tag{2}
\]
Every law in the resulting \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\)-ball also satisfies
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM,int}}(P;B)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon;B) \tag{3}
\]
for every \(B\ge5\chi\).  At the center itself, (2)--(3) hold for every \(B\ge5\chi\), and both integer ratios equal their corresponding continuous center ratios.
