Fix an arbitrary \(\chi>1\). Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) proves that every center \(P_\epsilon\) belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), that both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and that
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi>5
 =c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}, \tag{1}
\]
while
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon;B). \tag{2}
\]
It also proves the exact observational variance formula and the expansion
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}), \tag{3}
\]
whose leading coefficient is strictly positive for every finite \(\chi>1\). Thus \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is uniquely prediction-selected, \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is uniquely arm-normalized-value optimal, and the arm-normalized ratio diverges as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\) gives at every balanced center
\[
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{g,d},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon). \tag{4}
\]
Hence the same unique optimizer \(d_{\mathrm e}\) minimizes the IKM-normalized profile, the factor \(1/4\) cancels from the ratio, and (3) is also the asserted IKM-ratio expansion. Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\), whose premises are verified in the normalized-stability lemma, supplies the absolute local causal-efficiency interpretation design by design.

Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\) chooses each ball strictly inside the radius furnished by the first stability lemma. Consequently every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}. \tag{5}
\]
Every center lies in its own ball. Letting \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) in (3) therefore proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty. \tag{6}
\]
Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}region}\) shrinks the same balls within the normalized-stability radii. Thus every law in \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) is regular, has both bridge contrasts equal to \(\tau\), and obeys the predictive and normalized-efficiency optimizer assignments. The shrunken region also retains every center, so (3)--(4) give
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty. \tag{7}
\]

It remains to verify the cost-manifold claim. The center primitive cost vector is
\[
 \left(1,2,\frac{5\chi-3}{4},\frac{5\chi-3}{4},
 \frac12,\frac12\right),
 \qquad \kappa=\frac{5\chi-3}{2}. \tag{8}
\]
Because \(\chi>1\), all entries are positive, \(b_E<b_O\), the predictive design exactly meets the cap, and the efficient design has catalogue cost \(1<\kappa\). Thus (8) lies in \(\mathcal C_\kappa\), and (1) shows that its paired-cost ratio is exactly \(\chi\).

At \(\epsilon=0\), interpreted as the continuous boundary limit of the prediction risks, the two cost-weighted prediction criteria at the center cost vector are \(3/2\) and \(3\). Their gap is strict. The paired-cost gap \(5\chi-5\) is also strict. Both criteria are jointly continuous in the primitive costs and \(\epsilon\), and both paired costs are continuous in the primitive costs. Hence, relative to \(\mathcal C_\kappa\), there is a neighborhood of (8) on which \(d_{\mathrm p}\) remains strictly more expensive and uniquely prediction-selected for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\).

On a sufficiently small such neighborhood, \(c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\) is bounded away from zero and the efficient-design costs remain bounded. Equation (12) of the binary stability lemma then yields
\[
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 \ge c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 \asymp_\chi \epsilon^{-2},
\]
whereas \(\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_\epsilon)\) stays finite and positive. The arm-normalized ratio therefore diverges. Balanced treatment at every center makes (4) independent of the positive cost vector, so the IKM-normalized ratio diverges as well. Since \(\chi>1\) was arbitrary, this proves the claim for every prescribed finite cost disadvantage.
