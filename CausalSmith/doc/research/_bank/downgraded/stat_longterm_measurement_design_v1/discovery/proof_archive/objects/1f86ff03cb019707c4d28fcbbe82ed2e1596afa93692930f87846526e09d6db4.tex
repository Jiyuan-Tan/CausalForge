Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). For the independently funded full-catalogue pilot selector,
\[
0\le\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)-\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_d^*(P)\le2\varepsilon_m,
\qquad
\{\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\}\subseteq\{2\varepsilon_m\ge\gamma(P)\}.
\]
Conditional on the maintained full-catalogue pilot variance condition in Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}pilot\text{-}gradient\text{-}variance\text{-}rate}\), together with \(r_{V,m}=o(1)\) from Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}pilot\text{-}variance\text{-}rate\text{-}vanishes}\) and \(\underline v_m=o(1)\),
\[
\varepsilon_m=O_P(r_{V,m}),
\qquad
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}|\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*(P)|=O_P(r_{V,m}),
\]
and \(P\{\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\}\to0\), with the event empty when \(D^*(P)=\mathcal D\). With the stipulated zero value when either floor count vanishes,
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
+\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
\right\}-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
=O_P(r_{V,m}^2+B^{-1})=o_P(1).
\]
The selector consistently chooses the complete optimizer set under these maintained pilot conditions; no causal interpretation is needed for this observable bridge-functional regret statement. The theorem does not derive the maintained variance condition from a primitive bridge learner.
