\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2
 <\infty                                                     \tag{29}
\]
for every design. Since \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, both the global minimizer set and \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) are nonempty. Substitution of (29) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the identity. Its numerator minimizes over a subset of the denominator's index set, so the ratio is at least one; (29) makes it finite.
