Fix \(\chi>1\).  For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), decrease the radius \(r_\epsilon\), if necessary, as permitted by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}exact\text{-}integer\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\), and set \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{opt}}=r_\epsilon\).  Choose \(0<\delta_\epsilon<r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{opt}}\) and define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}
 =\bigcup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \left\{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi):
 \|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<\delta_\epsilon\right\}. \tag{1}
\]
The radii are chosen simultaneously to retain the regularity, common-identification, CLW-admissibility, continuous named-rule, two generic prediction-rule, arm-normalized bridge-ordering, and exact-integer margin conclusions of Lemmas~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\), \(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}predictive\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\), and \(\mathrm{lem{:}exact\text{-}integer\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\).  The set is relatively open in the constrained model \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\); no ambient-simplex openness is part of the definition.
