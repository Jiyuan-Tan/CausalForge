First fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\) and \(B>0\). The finite-moment and full-Gram conditions make each \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\), and hence each named criterion, finite. Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) give \(0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)<\infty\). Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}source\text{-}arm\text{-}positivity}\) implies \(0<(1-p_{g,1})^2<1\), so every IKM profile is also finite and strictly positive. The finite named selector is nonempty. Direct substitution of the budget-profile formula into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}\) proves (1), and (2) is its IKM analogue. In each case the numerator minimizes over a nonempty subset of the denominator's index set, giving a finite ratio at least one. The selected-value inequality holds precisely for constants no smaller than that ratio, proving best-tie minimality.

For either positive profile \(w_d\), define the proof intermediate
\[
 C_{\mathrm{ord}}^{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}\frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}.       \tag{42}
\]
The index set is finite and contains every diagonal pair. Any constant satisfying all named-rule order implications must dominate every indexed ratio, whereas (42) itself satisfies every implication. This proves (3). On the domain where all compared integer counts are positive, replacing the continuous criterion by \(\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}\) proves the integer claim verbatim. Computing the \(|\mathcal D|\) criteria and minima is linear in \(|\mathcal D|\); enumerating all ordered pairs uses at most \(|\mathcal D|^2\) comparisons.

Now, independently, fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). The generic unadjusted identity and its positivity are Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}catalogue\text{-}predictive\text{-}guarantee}\). The same direct substitution proves the treatment-aware identity in (4) and both IKM identities. Repeating (42) with \(R_d(P)\) or \(R_d^A(P)\) as the ordering criterion proves the exact generic pairwise maxima. This argument uses only observable-class positivity and does not place the generic law in the narrower CLW domain.

Finally, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}two\text{-}rule\text{-}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves unbounded ratios for the named rule and both generic rules on the jointly shrunk regions. Lemmas~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) and \(\mathrm{lem{:}treatment\text{-}aware\text{-}sharp\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) prove equality of all six center ratios and the common expansion (5). Thus the enumerations are exact and computable at each fixed law but yield no law-uniform constant.
