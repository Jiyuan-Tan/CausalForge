For the unequal-cost binary construction, every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\) gives \(P_{\epsilon}\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), the predictive optimizer is \(d_{\mathrm p}\), and the design-value optimizer is \(d_{\mathrm e}\). Moreover,
\[
V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{448}{2025\epsilon^2}+\frac{6272}{2025},\qquad
\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\epsilon})=\frac{3872}{25},\qquad
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\epsilon})=\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1}).
\]
For each fixed \(\epsilon\) there is a radius \(r_\epsilon>0\) such that every law in the constrained fixed-support regular model within cell distance \(r_\epsilon\) of \(P_\epsilon\) has the same two strict optimizer assignments and has \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)>\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)/2\).
