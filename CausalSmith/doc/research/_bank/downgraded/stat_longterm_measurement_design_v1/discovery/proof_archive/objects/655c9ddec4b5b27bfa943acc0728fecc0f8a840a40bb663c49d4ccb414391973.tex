For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), if \(\mathcal D=\{d\}\), the main study fixes positive counts \(n_E,n_O\), and \(B=c_{E,d}n_E+c_{O,d}n_O\), then
\[
 \frac1B\mathcal V_d\!\left(\frac{c_{E,d}n_E}{B};P\right)
 =\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.
\]
The right-hand side is the fixed-system two-source pathwise-gradient variance for \(\theta_d(P)\). If \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\) and the fixed-system Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang canonical-gradient conditions hold, it is also their causal semiparametric efficiency bound for \(\tau\); absent those additional conditions only the observable bridge-functional identity is asserted.
