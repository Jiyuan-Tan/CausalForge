Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and write \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\) for the binary center in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}witness}\).  Then \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\cap\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\), both designwise bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and, for a center design with proxy signals \(x,y\in(0,1)\), the CLW affine linear regression coefficients and residual variance are
\[
 \beta_1=\frac{x(1-y^2)}{1-x^2y^2},
 \qquad
 \beta_2=\frac{y(1-x^2)}{1-x^2y^2},
 \qquad
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac{(1-x^2)(1-y^2)}{1-x^2y^2}.                        \tag{1}
\]
Only at these centers, the last quantity also equals \(R_d(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\).  Hence
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 r_{d_{\mathrm p}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac{3}{10\chi}
 \frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))},\\
 r_{d_{\mathrm e}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\frac35,
 \end{aligned}                                             \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B). \tag{3}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)=\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\); moreover \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\) for all sufficiently large \(B\).  The selected design is costlier in each source:
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =\frac{5(\chi-1)}2>0.                                    \tag{4}
\]
Nevertheless \(D^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and
\[
 V_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{40\chi}{10\chi-3},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right),
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\frac{3872}{25}. \tag{5}
\]
All four center ratios agree:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 &=\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac{25}{3872}
 \left\{
 \sqrt{\frac{20\chi(5\chi-1)}{10\chi-3}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{32(5\chi+1)}{75(10\chi-3)}
 \left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right)}
 \right\}^{2}.                                             \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]
Consequently every ratio in (6) is at least
\[
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14)
 >\frac{\epsilon^{-2}+14}{726}.                            \tag{7}
\]
For all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), minimizing the exact common ratio over \(\chi>1\) is attained.  Every minimizer satisfies
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},              \tag{8}
\]
and, for any of the four ratios \(\rho\) in (6),
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                      \tag{9}
\]
Equivalently, after writing \(\chi=k\epsilon^{-2/3}\), the order-\(\epsilon^{2/3}\) coefficient before multiplication by \(25/3872\) is
\[
 q(k)=2\sqrt{\frac{32k}{15}}+\frac8{75k},
\]
whose unique minimizer is \(\sqrt[3]{18}/15\).
