Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) and the singleton design \(d\).  Put
\[
 \alpha=\frac{c_{E,d}n_E}{B}.
\]
Since \(B=c_{E,d}n_E+c_{O,d}n_O\) and both counts and costs are positive,
\[
 0<\alpha<1,
 \qquad
 1-\alpha=\frac{c_{O,d}n_O}{B}.
\]
Substitution into the definition of the observable design-value profile gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \frac1B\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)
 &=\frac1B\left\{
 \frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{c_{E,d}n_E/B}
 +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{c_{O,d}n_O/B}\right\}\\
 &=\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.
\end{aligned}
\]
By Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}designwise\text{-}gradient}\), the right side is exactly the fixed-count variance of the two-source pathwise gradient for the observed bridge contrast \(\theta_d(P)\).  If \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\), the separate causal-identification premise gives \(\theta_d(P)=\tau\).  If the fixed-system canonical-gradient premises also hold for this source decomposition, the canonical-gradient variance is the causal semiparametric efficiency lower bound; Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) records that fixed-system conclusion under its stated normalization and premises.  Without both the causal-identification and canonical-gradient premises, only the observable algebraic and pathwise-gradient identity proved above is asserted.
