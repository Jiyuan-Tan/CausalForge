Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) supplies, for every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), membership \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), regularity of both designs, the strict predictive and arm-normalized profile orderings, and
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{448}{2025\epsilon^2}+\frac{6272}{2025},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1}).              \tag{1}
\]
Its bridge calculation also gives \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=0=\tau\) for both designs.

At every center law, treatment is balanced in both sources.  The definition of the IKM-normalized scores therefore gives, for \(g\in\{E,O\}\),
\[
 \psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =(1-p_{g,1})\phi_{g,d}=\frac12\phi_{g,d},
 \qquad
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{g,d}.                    \tag{2}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                       \tag{3}
\]
The common positive factor in (3) preserves the unique optimizer \(d_{\mathrm e}\) and cancels from the ratio. Equations (1)--(3) prove all center-law normalization claims. The bridge construction verifies causal identification, unique square-integrable latent and observed bridges, operator bijectivity, and finite source moments. Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) therefore applies design by design: the normalized profile, rather than the unscaled arm-normalized profile, has the published absolute local causal-efficiency interpretation.

For the original relative-open region, Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\) chooses each radius below the regularity and arm-normalized optimizer-stability radii. Thus every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
Every center law belongs to its own ball, and (1) diverges, so
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty.
                                                                    \tag{4}
\]

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\) and Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}region}\) shrink each original ball below a radius preserving the normalized strict ordering. Hence \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\subseteq\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is relatively open; every law in it is regular, and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
For completeness, causal identification throughout this subregion is not an extra assumption. In the binary constrained model \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), so \(\tau=0\). Invertibility of the late proxy channel constructs \(h_{d,a}\) with \(\mathbb E(h_{d,a}\mid U,A=a,G=O)=U\); experimental randomization and source invariance then give \(\mu_{d,a}=\mathbb E(U\mid G=O)\), independent of \(a\). Thus every admissible bridge contrast equals \(\tau\). The normalized-stability lemma also supplies the fixed-system efficiency interpretation on the shrunken balls. Since the construction retains every center \(P_\epsilon\), (1)--(3) yield
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty.             \tag{5}
\]

It remains to verify the open cost range. Put
\[
 C_{\mathrm p}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}},
 \qquad
 C_{\mathrm e}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}.
\]
The binary conditional-variance calculation in the stability lemma gives
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{7(1-\epsilon^2)}{16-9\epsilon^2},
 \qquad
 R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon)=\frac35.
\]
Therefore the strict predictive ordering is equivalent to
\[
 \frac{C_{\mathrm p}}{C_{\mathrm e}}
 <\frac{3(16-9\epsilon^2)}{35(1-\epsilon^2)}.               \tag{6}
\]
The right-hand side tends to \(48/35\). Every fixed cap-feasible positive cost vector with
\[
 1<\frac{C_{\mathrm p}}{C_{\mathrm e}}<\frac{48}{35}
\]
therefore makes \(d_{\mathrm p}\) strictly more expensive and uniquely prediction-selected for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\). For any such fixed costs, the \(d_{\mathrm e}\) arm-normalized profile stays bounded, whereas
\[
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 \ge c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
The paper ratio diverges. Balanced treatment still gives the common quarter-scaling in (2)--(3), independently of the positive costs, so the IKM-normalized causal-efficiency ratio diverges as well. All inequalities are strict and continuous in the positive costs, making the displayed range relatively open in the cap-feasible cost region. This completes the proof.
