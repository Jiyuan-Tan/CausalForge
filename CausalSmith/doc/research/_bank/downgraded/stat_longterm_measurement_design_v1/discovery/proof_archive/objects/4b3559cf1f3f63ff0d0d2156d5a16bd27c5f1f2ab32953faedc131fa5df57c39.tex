Order the full-catalogue pilot records by their original indices and let
\[
 \mathcal I_{m,O}=\{i\in\{1,\ldots,m\}:G_i=O\},
 \qquad m_O=|\mathcal I_{m,O}|.
\]
For every \(d\in\mathcal D\), define the exact observational-pilot affine residual quadratic
\[
 \widehat r_{d,m}^{\mathrm{lin}}=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle
 \min_{b\in\mathbb R^{\dim(\xi_d)}}
 \frac1{m_O}\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{m,O}}
 \{Y_i-\xi_{d,i}^\top b\}^2,&m_O>0,\\[6pt]
 0,&m_O=0.
 \end{cases}                                                \tag{1}
\]
This finite convex quadratic is evaluable even when its empirical Gram matrix is singular.  Define
\[
 \widehat\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d,m}(B)
 =\frac{C_d\widehat r_{d,m}^{\mathrm{lin}}}{B},
\]
\[
 \widehat\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d,m}^{\mathrm{int}}(B)=
 \begin{cases}
 \widehat r_{d,m}^{\mathrm{lin}}/\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor,
 &\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor>0,\\
 +\infty,&\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor=0.
 \end{cases}                                                \tag{2}
\]
Let \(\widehat d_{\mathrm{CLW},m}(B)\) and
\(\widehat d_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{int}}(B)\) be the deterministically tie-broken minimizers of the two criteria in (2) over \(\mathcal D\).  Their exact-integer achieved ratios are
\[
 \widehat\rho_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=
 \frac{\mathcal V_{\widehat d_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{int}}(B)}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
\]
\[
 \widehat\rho_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{IKM,int}}(P;B)=
 \frac{\mathcal V_{\widehat d_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{int}}(B),\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}. \tag{3}
\]
This is the exact finite-catalogue affine least-squares minimizer corresponding to Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm equation~(3.6), transported with support mapped only to \(M_d\).  It does not implement or analyze approximate OGA.
