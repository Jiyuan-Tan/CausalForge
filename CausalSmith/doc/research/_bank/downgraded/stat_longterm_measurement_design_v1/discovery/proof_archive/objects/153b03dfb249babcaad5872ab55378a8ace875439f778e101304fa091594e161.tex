It remains to prove the joint neighborhood statement. The center lies in the relative interior of the finite-cell parameterization. Strict positivity of cells, source and treatment overlap, and nonzero determinants of the finite conditional-expectation matrices are open conditions. Matrix inversion is continuous away from zero determinant, so the bridges, bridge contrasts, and source-score variances are continuous on a sufficiently small ball. The constrained family keeps \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), experimental randomization, source-invariant proxy kernels, and conditional proxy independence. Thus all causal restrictions persist, and both bridge contrasts remain equal to the common \(\tau=0\).

The residual risks are finite-cell conditional-variance functionals and hence continuous rational functions of the strictly positive cell vector. Costs are fixed on \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\), so both CLW criteria are continuous. The profiled bridge values are continuous compositions of positive costs and source variances. Since the CLW, raw-risk, and bridge-value optimizer gaps are all strict at the center, one common positive radius preserves every ordering. Positivity of the relevant minima makes both approximation ratios continuous. Shrinking once more gives the two displayed one-half bounds.

Finiteness of the two-design catalogue therefore gives a finite law-specific budget threshold beyond which the integer selector also uniquely chooses \(d_{\mathrm p}\). This proves every assertion.
