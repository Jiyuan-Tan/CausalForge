Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) and suppose that \(\mathcal D=\{d\}\), that \(n_E,n_O\) are positive, and that
\[
 B=c_{E,d}n_E+c_{O,d}n_O.
\]
Membership in \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) makes \(d\) regular. The costs are strictly positive, so \(B>0\). Define
\[
 \alpha=\frac{c_{E,d}n_E}{B}.
\]
Positivity of both counts and both costs gives \(0<\alpha<1\), and the budget identity gives
\[
 1-\alpha
 =\frac{B-c_{E,d}n_E}{B}
 =\frac{c_{O,d}n_O}{B}. \tag{1}
\]
Because the design is regular, the definition of the design-value profile and (1) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \frac{1}{B}\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)
 &=\frac{1}{B}\left\{
   \frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{\alpha}
   +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{1-\alpha}
 \right\} \\
 &=\frac{1}{B}\left\{
   \frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{c_{E,d}n_E/B}
   +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{c_{O,d}n_O/B}
 \right\} \\
 &=\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.
\end{aligned}\tag{2}
\]
The nondegeneracy assumption makes the two variance components finite and positive. The designwise-gradient lemma identifies the last expression in (2), without any causal reinterpretation, as the independent two-source gradient variance for the identified bridge contrast \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}\). Thus (2) proves the unconditional algebraic part of the proposition.

Now impose the proposition's additional conditions: \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=\tau\), and \((\phi_{E,d},\phi_{O,d})\) is the canonical gradient for \(\tau\) in the fixed-system model, with the bridge, operator, moment, and interior sampling-ratio premises required by the fixed-system efficiency lemma. That lemma identifies the canonical-gradient variance in (2) with the Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang causal semiparametric efficiency lower bound. This proves the conditional efficiency interpretation. If either causal identification or canonicality is absent, the counterexample in the designwise-gradient lemma prevents that final relabelling, while the exact algebraic identity (2) remains valid.
