Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  Finiteness and nonemptiness of \(\mathcal D\) imply the same for \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  Positive costs, nondegenerate source variances, and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) give
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2<\infty
 \qquad(d\in\mathcal D). \tag{1}
\]
Substitution into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the displayed identity.  Its numerator minimizes over a subset of the denominator's design set, so the ratio is at least one, and (1) makes it finite.

A best-tie constant \(C\) is valid exactly when
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P).
\]
The denominator is positive, so division shows that the least such constant is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  For pairwise comparisons, let
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)=
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}. \tag{2}
\]
The indexing set contains all diagonal pairs and is finite; every denominator is positive by (1).  Hence (2) is finite.  Any constant satisfying all pairwise implications must dominate each indexed ratio and therefore their maximum.  Conversely, the maximum itself satisfies every indexed implication.  This proves its exact minimality.

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves, for each prescribed \(\chi>1\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty,
\]
even though the uniquely diagnostic-selected center design has paired cost \(5\chi\), compared with \(5\) for the efficient design.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) further proves the asserted minimizing scale and sharp envelope after optimizing over \(\chi\).  Therefore a finite catalogue supplies the exact lawwise enumeration above, but no primitive uniform efficiency guarantee for this diagnostic.
