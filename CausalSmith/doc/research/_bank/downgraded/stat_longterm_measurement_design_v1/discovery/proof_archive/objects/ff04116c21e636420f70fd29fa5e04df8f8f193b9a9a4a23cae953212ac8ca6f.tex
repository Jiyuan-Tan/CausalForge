Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and write \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\) for the binary center in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}witness}\), with the dependence on \(\chi\) made explicit. Then \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), both designwise bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac{3}{10\chi}\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac{3}{10\chi}<\frac3{10}<\frac35
 =R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon}).
\]
Consequently \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\) and \(D^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\). The raw-risk-selected design is costlier in each source:
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =\frac{5(\chi-1)}2>0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 V_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{40\chi}{10\chi-3},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right),
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\frac{3872}{25},
\]
and the exact center ratio is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac{25}{3872}
 \left\{
 \sqrt{\frac{20\chi(5\chi-1)}{10\chi-3}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{32(5\chi+1)}{75(10\chi-3)}
 \left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right)}
 \right\}^{2}.                                             \tag{1}
\end{aligned}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 \ge
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}
 \left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right)
 >\frac{\epsilon^{-2}+14}{726},                            \tag{2}
\]
with the identical inequalities for \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\), and
\[
 \inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 >\frac{\epsilon^{-2}+14}{726}.                            \tag{3}
\]
For all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), the infimum in (3) is attained. If \(\chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)\) is any minimizer and
\[
 k_\star=\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15},
\]
then
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim k_\star\epsilon^{-2/3}.                              \tag{4}
\]
Equivalently, on writing \(\chi=k\epsilon^{-2/3}\), the coefficient of \(\epsilon^{2/3}\) inside the braces-squared expression before multiplication by \(25/3872\) is
\[
 q(k)=2\sqrt{\frac{32k}{15}}+\frac{8}{75k},                \tag{5}
\]
and \(q\) has the unique minimizer \(k_\star\). The sharp second-order uniform elbow is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                      \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]
In particular, the first-order limit in (6) is \(1/726\).
