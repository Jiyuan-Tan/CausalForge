Fix the prescribed \(\chi>1\). For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), choose the radius \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\) furnished by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\) after the joint shrinking permitted by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}predictive\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\), and let \(\delta_\epsilon\) be the radius used in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\). Choose
\[
 0<\widetilde\delta_\epsilon
 <\min\{\delta_\epsilon,r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\}
\]
and define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 =\bigcup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \left\{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi):
 \|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<\widetilde\delta_\epsilon\right\}. \tag{1}
\]
Every member belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\), and, for every \(B>0\), the strict continuous CLW selector, both generic unrestricted-risk selectors, and the strict IKM-normalized optimizer are stable throughout every displayed ball. The named IKM ratio \(\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)\) and both generic IKM ratios each exceed one half of their corresponding center value after the stated shrinking. Thus \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\subseteq\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and is relatively open in \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\).
