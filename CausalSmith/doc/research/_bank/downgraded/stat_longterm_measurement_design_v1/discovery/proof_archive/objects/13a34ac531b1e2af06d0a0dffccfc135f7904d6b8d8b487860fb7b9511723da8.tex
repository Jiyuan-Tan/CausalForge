For each \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), choose a positive radius \(\delta_\epsilon<r_\epsilon\), where \(r_\epsilon\) is furnished by the binary witness stability lemma.  Restrict to the affine fixed-support model having the same finite support and structural equality restrictions as the binary witness, equip its complete latent-cell probability vector with the maximum norm, and define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}
 =\bigcup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \left\{P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}:
 P\text{ lies in that constrained fixed-support model and }
 \|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<\delta_\epsilon\right\}.
\]
This is a union of open balls relative to the constrained regular model.  It is not asserted to be open in the ambient unrestricted probability simplex, because the fixed-support and causal equality restrictions define a lower-dimensional subset.
