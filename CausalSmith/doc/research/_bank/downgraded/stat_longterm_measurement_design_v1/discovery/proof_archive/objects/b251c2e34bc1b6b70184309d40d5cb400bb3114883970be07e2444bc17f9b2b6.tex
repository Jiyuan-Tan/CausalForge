Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\). For a binary center design having early and late proxy signals \(x,y\in(0,1)\), its treatment-aware observational prediction risk is
\[
 \mathcal R_A(x,y)=
 \frac{15(1-x^2)(1-y^2)(16-x^2-y^2-14x^2y^2)}
 {\{16(1+xy)^2-(x+y)^2\}\{16(1-xy)^2-(x-y)^2\}}. \tag{1}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm e}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\frac{585}{1024},
 \qquad
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 \le R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 <\frac{3}{10\chi}<\frac3{10}<\frac{585}{1024}. \tag{2}
\]
Thus
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
\]
and, at every center,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon}),
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon}). \tag{3}
\]
Hence the exact ratio, the lower bound \((\epsilon^{-2}+14)/726\), the optimizing scale \(\chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)\sim(\sqrt[3]{18}/15)\epsilon^{-2/3}\), and the sharp second-order expansion in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) hold unchanged for both treatment-aware ratios.
