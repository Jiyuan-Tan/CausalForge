Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang analyze identification, estimation, and efficiency for a fixed short-term outcome system and sampling ratio; the present note treats the measured subset, sequential role assignment, and budget allocation as design variables.  Surrogate-index and surrogate-efficiency work generally compares performance with versus without a given surrogate system rather than optimizing a finite proxy catalogue under measurement costs.  Existing optimal-measurement results for linear front-door, Gaussian instrumental-variable, or fixed-variable two-phase designs address different models.  The two squared-risk rules studied here are generic population benchmarks---one excludes treatment and one includes it---and are not attributed as proxy-selection workflows to any named paper.  Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm appears only in the label of a secondary residual-risk-per-paired-budget diagnostic, not as the source of either rule or of any claimed guarantee.
