Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  By finiteness and nonemptiness of \(\mathcal D\), the predictive optimizer set \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) is nonempty.  Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), positivity of all costs, and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) imply
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2<\infty,
 \qquad d\in\mathcal D.                                    \tag{1}
\]
Substituting (1) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) gives the displayed identity. Since \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\subseteq\mathcal D\), the numerator is at least the denominator; finiteness of the family and (1) make both positive and finite. Hence \(1\le\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)<\infty\).

Write
\[
 V_{\min}(P)=\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P),
 \qquad
 V_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P).
\]
The actual best-tie inequality is \(V_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\le C V_{\min}(P)\). Because \(V_{\min}(P)>0\), it holds exactly when
\[
 C\ge \frac{V_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}{V_{\min}(P)}
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P).                                 \tag{2}
\]
Thus the least admissible constant is precisely the stated ratio.

For the pairwise assertion, let
\[
 \mathcal A(P)=\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)\}.
\]
This is a nonempty finite set because it contains every diagonal pair. For a constant \(C\), all displayed implications hold if and only if
\[
 C\ge\frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}
 \quad\hbox{for every }(d,d')\in\mathcal A(P).
\]
Every denominator is positive by (1), so the least such constant is the maximum over \(\mathcal A(P)\), exactly as displayed in the theorem.

Finally, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) supplies center laws in \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) for which \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)\to\infty\) as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). Hence
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty.
\]
Thus finite enumeration yields an exact constant at each fixed law but no law-uniform primitive guarantee. It does not resolve the separate generalized-eigenvalue operator-comparability question.
