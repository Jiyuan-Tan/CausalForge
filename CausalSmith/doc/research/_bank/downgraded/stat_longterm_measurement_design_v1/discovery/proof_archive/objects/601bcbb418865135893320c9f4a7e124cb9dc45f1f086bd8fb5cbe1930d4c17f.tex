Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  Positive costs, Assumption \(\texttt{ass:nondegenerate-components}\), and finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) imply that every profiled design value is finite and strictly positive.  The budget-profile theorem gives, for each \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+
 \sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2. \tag{1}
\]
The predictive optimizer set is a nonempty subset of the finite set \(\mathcal D\).  Substituting (1) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) gives the displayed identity.  Its numerator is at least its denominator, and both quantities are finite and strictly positive.  Therefore
\[
 1\le\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)<\infty. \tag{2}
\]

The actual best-tie inequality with constant \(C(P)\) is
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C(P)\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P). \tag{3}
\]
Dividing (3) by the strictly positive design minimum shows that (3) holds if and only if \(C(P)\ge\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  Hence the smallest valid best-tie constant is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For the pairwise assertion, define the finite nonempty set
\[
 \mathcal A_P=\left\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 \le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)\right\}.
\]
It is nonempty because it contains every diagonal pair.  For a fixed \((d,d')\in\mathcal A_P\), positivity of \(\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)\) makes the desired consequent equivalent to
\[
 C(P)\ge\frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}. \tag{4}
\]
The maximum of the finitely many ratios in (4) is therefore sufficient for every displayed implication.  It is also necessary: a maximizing pair belongs to \(\mathcal A_P\), and the implication for that pair forces every valid constant to be at least its ratio.  This proves the sharp finite-enumeration formula and its fixed-law finiteness.

Finally, Theorem~\texttt{thm:predictive-impossibility} supplies center laws \(P_\epsilon\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) for which \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)\to\infty\).  Consequently
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty.
\]
Thus catalogue finiteness yields the exact law-specific constants above but cannot yield a finite uniform predictive-efficiency guarantee on the stated regular region.
