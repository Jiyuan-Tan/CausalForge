Put \(p=p_{g,1}\), so \(p_{g,0}=1-p\). Let \(R_{g,d}(A)\) denote the residual factor following the treatment weight: it is \(h_d(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X)-\mu_{d,A}\) for \(g=E\), and it is \(q_d(S_{2d},S_{1d},A,X)\{Y-h_d(S_{3d},S_{2d},A,X)\}\) for \(g=O\). The paper's score can be written
\[
 \phi_{g,d}
 =\left\{\frac{\mathbf 1\{A=1\}}p
 -\frac{\mathbf 1\{A=0\}}{1-p}\right\}R_{g,d}(A).
\]
Because \(A\in\{0,1\}\), the printed treatment weight satisfies the pointwise identity
\[
 \frac{A-p}{p}
 =(1-p)\left\{\frac{\mathbf 1\{A=1\}}p
 -\frac{\mathbf 1\{A=0\}}{1-p}\right\}.
\]
The overlap assumptions ensure that every denominator is nonzero. Multiplication by the common residual proves \(\psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})\phi_{g,d}\). Squaring and taking the source-conditional expectation gives \(V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})^2V_{g,d}\). If both treated probabilities equal \(1/2\), substitution in the two budget profiles gives the factor \(1/4\) pointwise in \(\alpha\); taking the infimum preserves the same positive factor.
