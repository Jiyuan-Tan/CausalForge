Fix \(\chi>1\), let \(\epsilon_m\in(0,10^{-3})\) satisfy \(\epsilon_m\downarrow0\), and suppose the independently funded full-catalogue pilot consists of independent and identically distributed draws from \(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}\), independently of the main study.  Apply Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}clw\text{-}empirical\text{-}linear\text{-}cost\text{-}selector}\).  Then
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}
 \left|\widehat r_{d,m}^{\mathrm{lin}}
 -r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})\right|
 =O_P(m^{-1/2}).                                           \tag{1}
\]
For every fixed \(B\ge5\chi\),
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}
 \left|\widehat\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d,m}^{\mathrm{int}}(B)
 -\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m};B)\right|
 =O_P(m^{-1/2}),                                           \tag{2}
\]
with the analogous conclusion for the continuous criteria, and
\[
 P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}\!\left\{
 \widehat d_{\mathrm{CLW},m}(B)
 =\widehat d_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{int}}(B)
 =d_{\mathrm p}\right\}\longrightarrow1.                \tag{3}
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \widehat\rho_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{int}}
 (P_{\chi,\epsilon_m};B)\longrightarrow_P+\infty,
 \qquad
 \widehat\rho_{\mathrm{CLW},m}^{\mathrm{IKM,int}}
 (P_{\chi,\epsilon_m};B)\longrightarrow_P+\infty.       \tag{4}
\]
No rate relation between \(m\) and \(\epsilon_m\) is required.  The boundary \(B=5\chi\) is included.  No conclusion is asserted for \(B<5\chi\), where the prediction-selected design can have zero exact budget count, or at the singular limit \(\epsilon=0\).  This theorem analyzes the exact finite-catalogue affine least-squares minimizer corresponding to Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm equation~(3.6), not their approximate OGA procedure.
