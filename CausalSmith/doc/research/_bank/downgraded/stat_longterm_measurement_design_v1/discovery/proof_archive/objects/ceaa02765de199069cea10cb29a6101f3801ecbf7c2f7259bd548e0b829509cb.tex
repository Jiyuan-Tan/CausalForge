Let \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\).  By Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}common\text{-}identification\text{-}class}\),
\[
 \theta_d(P)=\tau
 \qquad\text{for every }d\in\mathcal D.
\]
Therefore \(\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)=\tau\) for every pilot realization.  Substitute this identity into the statistic and coverage conclusions of Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}postselection\text{-}wald}\).  The total event and the zero extension are unchanged, which gives
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)}{\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1),
 \qquad
 P\{\tau\in I_B\}\longrightarrow1-\eta.
\]
