The paper treats measurement design as the argument of a budget-normalized two-source source-score variance.  On explicitly declared bridge-differentiable submodels this is a pathwise-gradient variance; pointwise bridge bijectivity alone is not used to infer path stability.  Conditional on the separately maintained pilot variance condition, a full-catalogue pilot selects the measured subset, sequential roles, and budget share with an oracle-regret certificate.  Conditional on all listed selected-path learner, product, multiplier, pilot, and main-study variance conditions, a selected-design-only cross-fitted estimator has a studentized limit law for the observable bridge contrast; the causal ATE conclusion is a separate corollary on \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\).  The paper's newly defined paired-source residual-risk diagnostic has no finite uniform efficiency guarantee on a relatively open regular and commonly identifying binary region.  This is not a claim about predictive proxy selection generally or about a named published selector.
