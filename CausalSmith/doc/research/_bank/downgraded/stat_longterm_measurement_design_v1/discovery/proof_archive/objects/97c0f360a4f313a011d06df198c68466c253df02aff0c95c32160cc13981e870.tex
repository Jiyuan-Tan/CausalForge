Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang provide identification, fixed-system estimation, and a fixed-system semiparametric efficiency result for a given short-term outcome system and source ratio.  This note uses their result only under its separately stated causal-identification, regularity, canonical-gradient, and fixed-ratio premises.  Surrogate-efficiency work generally compares availability of a fixed surrogate vector, while optimal-measurement papers in other causal models optimize different design objects or two-phase probabilities for a fixed variable set.  Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm motivate a residual-prediction-risk-per-budget calculation in a randomized-trial design problem; this note defines its own paired-source residual-risk diagnostic of that shape and attributes neither a proxy-selection workflow nor an efficiency guarantee to their paper.  The binary impossibility theorem refutes only that newly defined diagnostic, not predictive proxy selection generally.  The pilot and Wald results are also not claimed to follow from generic double-machine-learning citations: they are conditional on the eight sanctioned maintained conditions, whose primitive attainability is left open.
