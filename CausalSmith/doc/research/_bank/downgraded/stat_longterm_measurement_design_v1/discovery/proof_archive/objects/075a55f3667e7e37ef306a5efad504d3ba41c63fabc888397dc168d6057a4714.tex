For the unequal-cost binary witness, every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\) gives a law \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) for which both designs identify \(\tau\) and are regular. Although \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is more expensive, with \(c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=6>5=c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}\), it uniquely minimizes this paper's Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm-shaped paired-source prediction objective, whereas \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes the paper's arm-normalized bridge-gradient value. More precisely,
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{448}{2025\epsilon^2}+\frac{6272}{2025},\qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)=\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1}).
\]
At every center law, \(p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=1/2\), so the Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang printed-score profile is exactly one quarter of the paper profile.  Consequently \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is also the unique local causal-efficiency optimizer there and the printed-score predictive approximation ratio equals \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)\). Every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular for both designs, \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty\).  There is also a relatively open subregion \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\subseteq\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) on which every design identifies \(\tau\), the printed-score local causal-efficiency optimizer is \(d_{\mathrm e}\), the predictive optimizer is \(d_{\mathrm p}\), and \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty\). The same center-path divergence holds on the relatively open range of cap-feasible cost vectors satisfying
\[
 1<\frac{c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}}
 {c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}}<\frac{48}{35},
\]
for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\). Thus the failure is neither an equal-cost artifact nor an identification failure; the paper's unscaled profile is not asserted to be the anchor's absolute efficiency bound.
