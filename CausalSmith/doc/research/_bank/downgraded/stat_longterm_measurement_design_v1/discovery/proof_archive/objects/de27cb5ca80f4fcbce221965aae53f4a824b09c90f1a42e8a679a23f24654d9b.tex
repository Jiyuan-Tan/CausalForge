For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), the exact fixed-catalogue diagnostic identity is
\[
 1\le\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d'}V_{E,d'}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d'}V_{O,d'}})^2}<\infty.
\]
The smallest constant in the actual best-tie inequality is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\). The least constant that makes every pairwise implication
\(\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)\Rightarrow \mathcal V_d^*(P)\le C(P)\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)\)
valid is
\[
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.
\]
Every displayed constant is finite at a fixed \(P\), but \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty\). Thus finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) yields an exact law-specific enumeration, not a primitive uniform predictive-efficiency guarantee.
