Lemma~\texttt{lem:binary-witness-stability} proves center-law regularity, causal identification, strict optimizer assignments, and the displayed variance and approximation-ratio expansions. In particular,

 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=6>5
 =c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}},

yet the cost-aware predictive objective uniquely selects \(d_{\mathrm p}\), while the paper's arm-normalized bridge-gradient value uniquely selects \(d_{\mathrm e}\). The normalization lemma gives

 \mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM},*}(P_\epsilon)

because both source treatment probabilities equal \(1/2\). The cited fixed-system theorem applies after the stability proof's verification of causal identification, unique square-integrable bridges, bijectivity, and finite moments.  P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D},\qquad

 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)

The separately defined smaller region \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) uses the continuity radius for the printed-score profile. Lemma~\texttt{lem:ikmw-normalized-open-impossibility} therefore proves the relative-open causal-efficiency version without conflating the two normalizations.

It remains to verify the cost-vector range. For proxy signals \(x,y\), direct Bayes averaging gives
\[
 R(x,y)=\frac{(1-x^2)(1-y^2)}{1-x^2y^2}.
\]
Writing \(C_{\mathrm p}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\) and \(C_{\mathrm e}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}\), the strict predictive ordering is equivalent to
\[
 \frac{C_{\mathrm p}}{C_{\mathrm e}}
 <\frac{R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon)}{R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon)}
 =\frac{3(16-9\epsilon^2)}{35(1-\epsilon^2)}. \tag{3}
\]
The right side tends to \(48/35\). Therefore every fixed cap-feasible positive cost vector satisfying
\[
 1<\frac{C_{\mathrm p}}{C_{\mathrm e}}<\frac{48}{35}
\]
makes \(d_{\mathrm p}\) strictly more expensive and uniquely prediction-selected for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\). For such fixed costs, the value of \(d_{\mathrm e}\) remains bounded while
\[
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 \ge c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Thus \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is eventually the unique paper-profile optimizer and the ratio diverges. Equation (1) gives the identical conclusion for the anchor-normalized causal-efficiency profile along the center path. Strictness and continuity in positive costs make the displayed cost range relatively open. This proves the proposed theorem.
