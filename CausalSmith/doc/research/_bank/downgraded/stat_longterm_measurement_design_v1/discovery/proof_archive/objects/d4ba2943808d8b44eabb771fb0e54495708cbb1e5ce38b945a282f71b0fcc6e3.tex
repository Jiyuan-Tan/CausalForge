Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), and write
\[
 v_d=\mathcal V_d^*(P),
 \qquad
 \widehat v_d=\widehat{\mathcal V}_{d,m}^*,
 \qquad
 v_* =\min_{d\in\mathcal D}v_d.
\]
Choose \(d^*\in D^*(P)\).  By the definition of \(\varepsilon_m\) and the minimizing property of \(\widehat d_m\),
\[
 v_{\widehat d_m}
 \le \widehat v_{\widehat d_m}+\varepsilon_m
 \le \widehat v_{d^*}+\varepsilon_m
 \le v_*+2\varepsilon_m.                                \tag{1}
\]
Since \(v_*\le v_{\widehat d_m}\),
\[
 0\le v_{\widehat d_m}-v_*\le2\varepsilon_m.            \tag{2}
\]
If \(\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\), the definition of \(\gamma(P)\) gives
\[
 \gamma(P)\le v_{\widehat d_m}-v_*\le2\varepsilon_m.    \tag{3}
\]
When every design is optimal, the event on the left is empty; otherwise finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) gives \(\gamma(P)>0\).

Set
\[
 Z_m=\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\{|\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
 |\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\}.
\]
The maintained pilot condition and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}pilot\text{-}variance\text{-}rate\text{-}vanishes}\) give \(Z_m=O_P(r_{V,m})=o_P(1)\).  By the pilot construction and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\),
\[
 \widehat v_d=G_d(\widehat V_{E,d},\widehat V_{O,d}),
 \qquad
 v_d=G_d(V_{E,d},V_{O,d}),
\]
where
\[
 G_d(x,y)=(\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}y})^2.
\]
Positive population variances, positive costs, and finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) place all population variance pairs in a compact subset of \((0,\infty)^2\); with probability tending to one, all estimated pairs lie in a slightly larger such compact set.  The finitely many gradients \(\nabla G_d\) are uniformly bounded there.  The mean-value theorem therefore gives
\[
 \varepsilon_m=\max_d|\widehat v_d-v_d|=O_P(r_{V,m})=o_P(1). \tag{4}
\]
Equations (3)--(4) imply \(P\{\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\}\to0\).

The same compact-set argument applied to
\[
 A_d(x,y)=\frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}}
 {\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}y}}
\]
shows that the variance truncation is inactive with probability tending to one and
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}|\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*(P)|
 =O_P(r_{V,m}).                                           \tag{5}
\]
Finally, the already-established Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}second\text{-}order\text{-}allocation\text{-}loss}\), whose premises are exactly the positive finite costs and variances, the maintained pilot variance rate, its vanishing rate, and the pilot selector, gives
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
+\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
\right\}-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
=O_P(r_{V,m}^2+B^{-1})=o_P(1).
\]
The deterministic tie rule was used only to choose an empirical minimizer in (1); the conclusion is therefore membership in the complete optimizer set, not convergence to an arbitrarily selected population minimizer.  Every stochastic conclusion is explicitly conditional on the maintained pilot source-score variance condition.
