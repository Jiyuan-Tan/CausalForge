For \(d\in\mathcal D\), put
\[
 a_d=c_{E,d}V_{E,d},\qquad b_d=c_{O,d}V_{O,d},
 \qquad
 F_d(\alpha)=\frac{a_d}{\alpha}+\frac{b_d}{1-\alpha}.
\]
Then
\[
 \alpha_d^*(P)=\frac{\sqrt{a_d}}{\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d}},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)=(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2,
\]
and, for every \(0<\widehat\alpha_{d,m}<1\),
\[
 F_d(\widehat\alpha_{d,m})-\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 (\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*(P))^2}
 {\widehat\alpha_{d,m}(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})}.       \tag{1}
\]
Let
\[
 n_E=\left\lfloor\frac{\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B}{c_{E,d}}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad
 n_O=\left\lfloor\frac{(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B}{c_{O,d}}\right\rfloor.
\]
Under the convention that the entire integer-allocation expression is zero if either count vanishes, define on \(\{n_E\ge1,n_O\ge1\}\)
\[
 \mathcal R_{\mathrm{floor},d}
 =B\left(\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}\right)
 -F_d(\widehat\alpha_{d,m}).                            \tag{2}
\]
Then \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{floor},d}\ge0\), and the realized positive-count loss decomposes exactly as
\[
 B\left(\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}\right)
 -\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 (\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*(P))^2}
 {\widehat\alpha_{d,m}(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})}
 +\mathcal R_{\mathrm{floor},d}.                       \tag{3}
\]
Whenever both continuous counts exceed one,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 0\le\mathcal R_{\mathrm{floor},d}
 <{}&\frac{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2}
 {\widehat\alpha_{d,m}(\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B-c_{E,d})}\\
 &+\frac{V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2}
 {(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})
 \{(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B-c_{O,d}\}}.             \tag{4}
\end{aligned}
\]
