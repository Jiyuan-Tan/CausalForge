Fix \(\chi>1\), and write the binary center as \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\). Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) proves center regularity and common identification, as well as

 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}

The same lemma gives

 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)

so the more expensive design \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is uniquely prediction-selected. It also proves that \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes the arm-normalized profile and that

 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad

 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}

The same lemma optimizes the exact rather than fixed-\(\chi\) formula. It proves that every minimizing cost-disadvantage factor satisfies

 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)

\end{aligned}

This proves the asserted least-divergent scale and sharp second-order elbow.

Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\) places every ball inside the radius furnished by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\). Hence each \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and

 \qquad

Every center belongs to its own ball. Letting \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) in either (5) at fixed \(\chi\) or (7) proves

Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}region}\) shrinks the same balls inside the normalized-stability radii without removing their centers.  \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty.       \tag{10}

Under its stated fixed-system premises, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) supplies the absolute local causal-efficiency interpretation of the normalized profile.

It remains to verify the cost-manifold assertion. The center primitive vector and cap are

 \qquad \kappa=\frac{5\chi-3}{2}.                     \tag{11}
\]
All entries are positive, \(b_E<b_O\), the catalogue cost of \(d_{\mathrm p}\) equals the cap, and the catalogue cost of \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is \(1<\kappa\). Thus (11) belongs to \(\mathcal C_\kappa\). The paired costs have ratio \(\chi\), and (1) shows strict cost dominance in each source.

These strict criterion gaps and both strict sourcewise cost gaps are continuous in the primitive costs and in \(\epsilon\). A relatively open neighborhood of (11) in \(\mathcal C_\kappa\) therefore preserves sourcewise cost dominance and, uniformly for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\), unique prediction selection of \(d_{\mathrm p}\).

On a sufficiently small such cost neighborhood, \(c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\) is bounded away from zero and both costs of \(d_{\mathrm e}\) are bounded above. Equation (3), whose score variance is independent of the costs, gives

 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 \ge c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 \asymp_\chi\epsilon^{-2},                            \tag{12}

whereas \(\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\) remains finite and positive. Hence the arm-normalized ratio diverges. Treatment is balanced at every center, so (4) continues to hold for every positive primitive cost vector, and the IKM-normalized ratio diverges as well. This proves every assertion.
