Fix \(\chi>1\), \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and \(B>0\), and write \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\). For a binary center design with early and late signals \(x,y\in(0,1)\), the nonredundant affine regressor is \(\xi_d=(1,W_x,W_y)^\top\), its full Gram matrix and determinant are
\[
 \Gamma_d(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&xy\\0&xy&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \det\Gamma_d(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=1-x^2y^2>0,              \tag{1}
\]
and its population affine projection has intercept zero and coefficients
\[
 \beta_1=\frac{x(1-y^2)}{1-x^2y^2},
 \qquad
 \beta_2=\frac{y(1-x^2)}{1-x^2y^2}.                       \tag{2}
\]
Consequently the distinct CLW affine residual and the unrestricted unadjusted risk agree at the center, with
\[
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =R_d(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =R(x,y)=\frac{(1-x^2)(1-y^2)}{1-x^2y^2}.                 \tag{3}
\]
Thus \(P_{\chi,epsilon}\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\cap\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\), both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,epsilon};B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,epsilon};B). \tag{4}
\]
At the same centers,
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =\frac{3}{10\chi}\frac{1-\epsilon^2}
 {1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac{3}{10\chi}<\frac3{10}<\frac35
 =R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_{\chi,epsilon}).                  \tag{5}
\]
Hence
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_{\chi,epsilon};B)
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D^*(P_{\chi,epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.              \tag{6}
\]
There is a finite \(B_0(P_{\chi,epsilon})\) such that
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P_{\chi,epsilon};B)
 =\{d_{\mathrm p}\}
 \quad\text{for every }B\ge B_0(P_{\chi,epsilon}).      \tag{7}
\]
The selected design is costlier in each source:
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =\frac{5(\chi-1)}2>0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 V_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{40\chi}{10\chi-3},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14),
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,epsilon})=\frac{3872}{25}.
\]
All four named-CLW and unadjusted-risk center ratios agree:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_{\chi,epsilon};B)
 &=\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,epsilon};B)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,epsilon})\\
 &=\frac{25}{3872}
 \left\{
 \sqrt{\frac{20\chi(5\chi-1)}{10\chi-3}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{32(5\chi+1)}{75(10\chi-3)}
 (\epsilon^{-2}+14)}
 \right\}^{2}.                                            \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]
Every ratio in (8) exceeds \((\epsilon^{-2}+14)/726\). For all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), its infimum over \(\chi>1\) is attained; every minimizer satisfies
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},             \tag{9}
\]
and the sharp optimized elbow is
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}),                                      \tag{10}
\]
where \(\rho\) is any of the four center ratios in (8).
