Let \(m\to\infty\) and \(B\to\infty\), assume \(\underline v_m=o(1)\), and assume that \(\mathcal P_m\) is independent of the main-study observations used to construct \(\widehat\tau_B\).  Conditional on the pilot, assume that the two purchased main-study source samples are mutually independent and i.i.d. within source.  For every \(d\in\mathcal D\), assume the causal identification premises of Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~4 statements 1 and 2: their Assumptions~6 and 7 furnish latent outcome and selection bridges equal to \(h_d\) and \(q_d\), respectively, the corresponding observed conditional-moment equations hold, and both identification routes identify the common causal target \(\tau\).  Under the main-study law, suppose that for fixed \(L\), uniformly over \(d\in\mathcal D\) and \(1\le\ell\le L\),
\[
 \|\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d\|_{w,h,d}=O_P(\delta_{h,B}),
 \qquad
 \|\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d\|_{2,O,h,d}=O_P(\rho_{h,B}),
\]
\[
 \|\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d\|_{w,q,d}=O_P(\delta_{q,B}),
 \qquad
 \|\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d\|_{2,O,q,d}=O_P(\rho_{q,B}),
\]
where all four deterministic rates are \(o(1)\) and
\[
 \min\{\delta_{h,B}\rho_{q,B},\rho_{h,B}\delta_{q,B}\}
 =o(B^{-1/2}).
\]
Require these four rate assertions and the product-rate assertion to hold along every deterministic sequence of source counts with an interior limiting source ratio.  Under the full-catalogue pilot law, suppose
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\{|
 \widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,|
 \widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\}=O_P(r_{V,m}),
 \qquad r_{V,m}=o(1).
\]
Under the joint main-study law, suppose
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D,\,g\in\{E,O\}}
 |\widetilde V_{g,d,B}-V_{g,d}|=O_P(r_{\widetilde V,B}),
 \qquad r_{\widetilde V,B}=o(1).
\]
For \(\widehat d_m\) and \(\widehat\alpha_m\), form \(N_{E,B},N_{O,B}\) and
\[
 \widehat s_B^2
 =B\left\{
 \frac{\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{E,B}}
 +\frac{\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{O,B}}
 \right\}.
\]
Then
\[
 B\left\{
 \frac{V_{E,\widehat d_m}}{N_{E,B}}
 +\frac{V_{O,\widehat d_m}}{N_{O,B}}
 \right\}
 -\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
 \qquad
 \widehat s_B^2-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
\]
and
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)}{\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
For
\[
 I_B=\left[
 \widehat\tau_B-z_{1-\eta/2}\frac{\widehat s_B}{\sqrt B},
 \widehat\tau_B+z_{1-\eta/2}\frac{\widehat s_B}{\sqrt B}
 \right],
\]
we have \(P\{\tau\in I_B\}\to1-\eta\).
