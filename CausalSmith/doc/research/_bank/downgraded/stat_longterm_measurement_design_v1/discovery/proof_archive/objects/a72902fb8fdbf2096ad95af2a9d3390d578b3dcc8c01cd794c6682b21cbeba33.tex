All center-law claims, including regularity, common identification, the distinct CLW regression calculation, the strict CLW and unrestricted-risk orderings, the score variances, and the exact diverging ratios, were proved in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\).  We prove the neighborhood claim without identifying the two risk functionals off center.

The center lies in the relative interior of the finite-cell parameterization.  Strict positivity of the latent cells, source and treatment overlap, and nonzero determinants of all finite conditional-expectation matrices are open conditions.  Matrix inversion is continuous away from zero determinant.  Hence the bridges, their contrasts, and the source-score variances are continuous on a sufficiently small relative ball.  The factorization defining \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\) continues to impose \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), experimental randomization, source-invariant proxy kernels, and conditional proxy independence.  Thus all causal restrictions persist, and both bridge contrasts remain equal to \(\tau=0\).

For the named criterion, let
\[
 \Gamma_d(P)=\mathbb E_P[\xi_d\xi_d^\top\mid G=O],
 \qquad g_d(P)=\mathbb E_P[\xi_dY\mid G=O].
\]
Each entry is linear in the positive finite-cell vector.  On the open set where \(\det\Gamma_d(P)\ne0\),
\[
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)
 =\mathbb E_P[Y^2\mid G=O]-g_d(P)^\top\Gamma_d(P)^{-1}g_d(P) \tag{26}
\]
is continuous.  This proves both persistence of CLW admissibility and continuity of the actual CLW objective.  Separately, the unrestricted risks are finite-cell conditional-variance functionals and are continuous rational functions of the positive cell vector.  The profiled bridge values are continuous compositions of the positive costs and score variances.

All CLW, unrestricted-risk, and bridge-value optimizer gaps are strict at the center.  Finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) permits one common radius preserving every ordering.  The relevant denominators remain positive, so the ratios are continuous.  Shrinking once more gives both one-half inequalities.  This establishes (5)--(6) from continuity of (26), not from an off-center equality with \(R_d(P)\).

Finally fix a law in the resulting ball.  Its continuous CLW gap is strict.  The floor bound (16) in the sharp-elbow proof holds designwise with \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\) in place of the center residual and shows
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 \longrightarrow C_dr_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P).
\]
Finiteness of the catalogue supplies a finite law-specific \(B_0(P)\) beyond which the integer selector uniquely chooses \(d_{\mathrm p}\).  This proves the lemma.
