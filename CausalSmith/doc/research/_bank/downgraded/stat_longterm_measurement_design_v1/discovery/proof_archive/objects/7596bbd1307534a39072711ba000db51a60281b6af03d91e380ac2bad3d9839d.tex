Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  For \(j\in\{0,A\}\), define
\[
 p_{d,0}=R_d(P),
 \qquad
 p_{d,A}=\inf_f\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \{Y-f(A,X,Z_{M_d})\}^2\mid G=O\right],
\]
where the second infimum is over square-integrable predictors, and let
\[
 \mathcal D_j(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}p_{d,j}.
\]
For \(s\in\{\mathrm{arm},\mathrm{IKM}\}\), put
\[
 w_{d,\mathrm{arm}}=\mathcal V_d^*(P),
 \qquad
 w_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}=\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P),
\]
and define
\[
 \mathcal O_{j,P}=\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:p_{d,j}\le p_{d',j}\},
 \qquad
 C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{ord}}(P)
 =\max_{(d,d')\in\mathcal O_{j,P}}
 \frac{w_{d,s}}{w_{d',s}},
\]
\[
 C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{sel}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_j(P)}w_{d,s}}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}w_{d',s}}.
\]
Then all \(w_{d,s}\) are finite and strictly positive and, for every \((j,s)\),
\[
 1\le C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{sel}}(P)
 \le C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{ord}}(P)<\infty.
\]
The constant \(C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{ord}}(P)\) is the least \(C\) for which
\[
 p_{d,j}\le p_{d',j}\Longrightarrow w_{d,s}\le Cw_{d',s}
 \quad\text{for every }d,d'\in\mathcal D,
\]
and \(C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{sel}}(P)\) is the least constant for the corresponding best-tie selector.  Hence, on any nonempty subclass, a finite uniform constant exists if and only if the supremum of the appropriate pointwise least constant is finite.  In particular, these conclusions hold simultaneously for the unadjusted and treatment-aware standard observational squared-risk rules, and for both the arm-normalized and IKM-normalized source-score profiles.

Suppose further, for one fixed pair \((j,s)\), that a numerical realization of \(\mathsf H_{\mathrm{comp}}\) supplies, for each \(d\), a cone containing a transported direction \(x_d(P)\), a positive quadratic form \(Q_{R,d}^{j}\), and nonnegative source-score quadratic forms \(Q_{E,d}^{s},Q_{O,d}^{s}\), such that
\[
 p_{d,j}=Q_{R,d}^{j}\{x_d(P)\},
 \qquad
 w_{d,s}=F_{d,s}\{x_d(P)\},
\]
where
\[
 F_{d,s}(x)=
 \left\{\sqrt{c_{E,d}Q_{E,d}^{s}(x)}
 +\sqrt{c_{O,d}Q_{O,d}^{s}(x)}\right\}^{2}.
\]
If the infimum and supremum of \(F_{d,s}/Q_{R,d}^{j}\), taken over the stated cones and then over the finite design family, give constants \(0<\Lambda_{j,s,-}(P)\le\Lambda_{j,s,+}(P)<\infty\), then
\[
 C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{sel}}(P)
 \le C_{j,s}^{\mathrm{ord}}(P)
 \le\frac{\Lambda_{j,s,+}(P)}{\Lambda_{j,s,-}(P)}.
\]
This envelope is the weakest two-sided quadratic-form certificate on those cones.  Its profiled numerator satisfies
\[
 F_{d,s}(x)=\inf_{0<\alpha<1}
 \left\{
 \frac{c_{E,d}Q_{E,d}^{s}(x)}{\alpha}
 +\frac{c_{O,d}Q_{O,d}^{s}(x)}{1-\alpha}
 \right\},
\]
so, without additional common-space and saturation conditions, the sharp selector constant need not be a largest generalized eigenvalue.
