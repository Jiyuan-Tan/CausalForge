Fix \(P\), \(d\), and \(0<\alpha<1\), and put
\[
 x_E=\frac{\alpha B}{c_{E,d}},\qquad
 x_O=\frac{(1-\alpha)B}{c_{O,d}}.
\]
The assumed lower bound on \(B\) makes \(x_E,x_O\ge2\), so both floor counts are positive.  For every \(x\ge2\),
\[
 0\le \frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x
 =\frac{x-\lfloor x\rfloor}{x\lfloor x\rfloor}
 \le \frac{2}{x^2}, \tag{1}
\]
because \(0\le x-\lfloor x\rfloor<1\) and \(\lfloor x\rfloor\ge x/2\).  Applying (1) to the two counts gives the defining equality
\[
 B\left\{\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_{E,B}(d,\alpha)}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_{O,B}(d,\alpha)}\right\}
 =\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)+\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
\]
and the bound
\[
 0\le\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
 \le\frac2B\left\{
 \frac{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2}{\alpha^2}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2}{(1-\alpha)^2}\right\}. \tag{2}
\]
The right side of (2) tends to zero for fixed \(d\) and \(\alpha\), proving convergence to the design-value profile.

Set \(a_d=c_{E,d}V_{E,d}>0\) and \(b_d=c_{O,d}V_{O,d}>0\).  For \(0<\alpha<1\), direct completion of the square gives
\[
 \frac{a_d}{\alpha}+\frac{b_d}{1-\alpha}
 -(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2
 =\frac{\{(1-\alpha)\sqrt{a_d}-\alpha\sqrt{b_d}\}^2}
        {\alpha(1-\alpha)}\ge0. \tag{3}
\]
Equality in (3) holds exactly when
\[
 (1-\alpha)\sqrt{a_d}=\alpha\sqrt{b_d},
\]
namely at the unique point
\[
 \alpha=\frac{\sqrt{a_d}}{\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d}}
 =\alpha_d^*(P).
\]
Substitution into (3) gives
\[
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)=(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2.
\]
