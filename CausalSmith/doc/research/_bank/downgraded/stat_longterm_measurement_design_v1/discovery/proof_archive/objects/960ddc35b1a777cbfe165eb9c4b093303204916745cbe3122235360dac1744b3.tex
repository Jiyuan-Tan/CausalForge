Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  By Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}regular\text{-}law\text{-}class}\), this law also belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  The family \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, and so is \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  Positive costs, Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) give, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+
          \sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2<\infty.       \tag{1}
\]
Substitution of (1) into Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}predictive\text{-}benchmark}\) proves
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d'}V_{E,d'}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d'}V_{O,d'}})^2}.
 \tag{2}
\]
The numerator in (2) minimizes over a nonempty subset of the design set used by the denominator, so the ratio is at least one; (1) makes it finite.

For the actual best-tie diagnostic rule, a constant \(C\) is valid exactly when
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P).
 \tag{3}
\]
The denominator is strictly positive by (1).  Dividing (3) by it shows that the least valid constant is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For the pairwise assertion, put
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)=
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.        \tag{4}
\]
The indexing set in (4) is nonempty because it contains every diagonal pair, and it is finite.  Every ratio in it is finite and positive by (1), so \(C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)<\infty\).  Any constant satisfying every diagnostic-order implication must dominate every ratio indexed in (4), and hence their maximum.  Conversely, the maximum in (4) dominates every indexed ratio and therefore satisfies all the implications.  This proves the claimed exact minimality.

It remains to establish the nonuniform assertions on the binary causal subclass.  Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves, for every fixed \(\chi>1\), that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty,                         \tag{5}
\]
while at every center law the uniquely diagnostic-selected design has paired cost \(5\chi\) and the efficient design has paired cost \(5\).  Thus their paired-cost ratio is exactly \(\chi\).

Finally, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) optimizes the exact center ratio over all \(\chi>1\).  It proves that, for all sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), every minimizer satisfies
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},           \tag{6}
\]
and that
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}
   \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
   +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                    \tag{7}
\end{aligned}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) give the exact finite law-specific enumeration.  Equations (5)--(7) show that this pointwise finiteness cannot be promoted to a primitive uniform guarantee and that optimizing the cost disadvantage leaves the leading divergence \(1/(726\epsilon^2)\).  This proves every assertion.
