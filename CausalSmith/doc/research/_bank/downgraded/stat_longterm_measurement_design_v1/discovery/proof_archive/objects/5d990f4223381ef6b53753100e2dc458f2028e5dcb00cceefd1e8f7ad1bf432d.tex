Fix an arbitrary \(\chi>1\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) proves that every center \(P_\epsilon\) belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi>5
 =c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}},             \tag{1}
\]
while
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon;B).   \tag{2}
\]
The same lemma gives
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}+O_\chi(1)       \tag{3}
\]
and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
  +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}).                              \tag{4}
\]
The leading coefficient in (4) is strictly positive.  Hence \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is uniquely prediction-selected, \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is uniquely arm-normalized-value optimal, and the ratio diverges as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\) gives at every center
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d},qquad
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                  \tag{5}
\]
Thus \(d_{\mathrm e}\) also uniquely minimizes the IKM-normalized profile, and the common factor in (5) cancels from the approximation ratio.  Equations (4)--(5) prove the asserted normalized expansion.  Under the fixed-system premises, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) supplies the designwise absolute causal-efficiency interpretation.

Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\) chooses every ball inside the radius furnished by the first stability lemma.  Hence every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.                     \tag{6}
\]
Every center belongs to its own ball.  Letting \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) in (4) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty.                      \tag{7}
\]
Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}region}\) shrinks the same balls inside the normalized-stability radii, without removing their centers.  The normalized stability lemma therefore gives the corresponding regularity, common-identification, and strict optimizer assertions throughout \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\), while (4)--(5) give
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty.       \tag{8}
\]

It remains to verify the cost-manifold claim.  The center primitive cost vector and cap are
\[
 \left(1,2,\frac{5\chi-3}{4},\frac{5\chi-3}{4},
 \frac12,\frac12\right),
 \qquad \kappa=\frac{5\chi-3}{2}.                     \tag{9}
\]
All entries are positive, \(b_E<b_O\), the catalogue cost of \(d_{\mathrm p}\) equals the cap, and the catalogue cost of \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is \(1<\kappa\).  Thus (9) belongs to \(\mathcal C_\kappa\).  Equation (1) shows that its paired-cost ratio is exactly \(\chi\).

At \(\epsilon=0\), interpreted as the continuous boundary value of the prediction risks, the two cost-weighted prediction criteria at (9) equal \(3/2\) and \(3\), respectively.  Their gap and the paired-cost gap \(5(\chi-1)\) are strict.  The paired costs and prediction criteria are jointly continuous in the primitive costs and \(\epsilon\).  Therefore a relatively open neighborhood of (9) in \(\mathcal C_\kappa\) preserves the cost ordering and, uniformly for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\), preserves unique prediction selection of \(d_{\mathrm p}\).

On a sufficiently small such cost neighborhood, \(c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\) is bounded away from zero and both efficient-design costs are bounded above.  Formula (3), whose score variance is independent of the costs, then gives
\[
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 \ge c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 \asymp_\chi\epsilon^{-2},                            \tag{10}
\]
whereas \(\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_\epsilon)\) stays finite and strictly positive.  The arm-normalized ratio diverges.  Treatment is balanced at every center, so (5) holds for every positive primitive cost vector and the IKM-normalized ratio diverges as well.  Since \(\chi>1\) was arbitrary, all assertions follow.  The last sentence of the theorem is exactly the distinction supplied by Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\): the former profiles the bridge-gradient variance, while the latter supplies absolute causal-efficiency meaning under its fixed-system premises.
