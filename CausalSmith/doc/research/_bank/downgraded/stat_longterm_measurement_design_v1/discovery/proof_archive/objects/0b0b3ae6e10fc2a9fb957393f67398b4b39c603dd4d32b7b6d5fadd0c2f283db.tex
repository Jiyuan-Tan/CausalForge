Under Assumption~3.1 of Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm, for a completely randomized treatment with arm-homoskedastic residual variance, Lemma~3.1 and equations~(3.3)--(3.5) show that, under equal treatment allocation, choosing the sample size \(n\) and coefficient support \(\mathcal I(\gamma)\) to minimize the conditional mean-squared error subject to \(c(\mathcal I(\gamma),n)\le B\) is equivalent to minimizing \(\sigma^2(\gamma)/n\) under the same constraint, where \(\sigma^2(\gamma)\) is the residual variance from predicting the outcome by the selected covariates. Their equation~(3.6) is the corresponding pre-experimental empirical squared-error problem.
