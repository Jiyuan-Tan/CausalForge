For each \(d\in\mathcal D\), compute the observational residual prediction risk
\[
R_d(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(X,Z_{M_d})}\mathbb E_P[(Y-f(X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O]
\]
and define the newly introduced paired-source diagnostic
\[
\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)=\frac{(c_{E,d}+c_{O,d})R_d(P)}{B}.
\]
Let \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) retain every minimizer of this diagnostic, and define
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=
\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
{\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.
\]
The notation records that the diagnostic is shaped by a residual-risk-per-budget calculation; it is not attributed as a proxy-selection workflow or guarantee of Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm.
