Fix \(\chi>1\). For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), the center \(P_\epsilon\) is regular, both admissible bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_\epsilon;B)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
The paired costs and exact CLW linear-cost criteria satisfy
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi,
 \qquad c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}=5,
\]
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon;B).
\]
Moreover,
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +\frac{896}{75(10\chi-3)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac{3872}{25},
\]
and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}+O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}).
\]
For each fixed pair \((\chi,\epsilon)\), there is \(r_\epsilon>0\) such that every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\) with \(\|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<r_\epsilon\) is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and the same strict CLW, raw-risk, and arm-normalized optimizer assignments hold. On this ball,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_\epsilon;B),
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
For every law in the ball, the floor-budget CLW criterion also uniquely selects \(d_{\mathrm p}\) for all sufficiently large \(B\), with the threshold allowed to depend on the law.
