Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\). Finiteness and nonemptiness of \(\mathcal D\) make \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) nonempty. Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), positivity of costs, and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) give
\[
0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
=\bigl(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\bigr)^2
<\infty,\qquad d\in\mathcal D.
\]
Substitution into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the displayed identity. Its numerator is at least its denominator because \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\subseteq\mathcal D\); both are positive and finite.

Write \(V_{\min}=\min_{d'}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)\) and \(V_{\mathrm{pred}}=\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)\). The best-tie inequality \(V_{\mathrm{pred}}\le CV_{\min}\) holds exactly when \(C\ge V_{\mathrm{pred}}/V_{\min}=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\). For the pairwise assertion, the finite nonempty set
\[
\mathcal A(P)=\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:
\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)\}
\]
contains every diagonal pair. All implications hold exactly when
\[
C\ge\frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}
\quad\hbox{for every }(d,d')\in\mathcal A(P),
\]
so their least constant is the stated maximum.

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) supplies center laws \(P_\epsilon\) for which
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
=\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1})\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\) includes every center \(P_\epsilon\) in \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\). Hence \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty\). The result is an exact finite enumeration at each law and does not resolve the separate primitive operator-comparability question.
