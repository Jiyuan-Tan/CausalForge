Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), \(d\in\mathcal D\), and \(0<\alpha<1\), and put
\[
x_E=\frac{\alpha B}{c_{E,d}},\qquad x_O=\frac{(1-\alpha)B}{c_{O,d}}.
\]
The asserted lower bound on \(B\) makes \(x_E,x_O\ge2\).  For every \(x\ge2\),
\[
0\le \frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x
=\frac{x-\lfloor x\rfloor}{x\lfloor x\rfloor}
\le \frac2{x^2},                                                   \tag{1}
\]
because \(0\le x-\lfloor x\rfloor<1\) and \(\lfloor x\rfloor\ge x/2\).  Applying (1) to the two source counts and using the definition of \(\mathfrak r_{d,B}\) gives
\[
B\left\{\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_{E,B}(d,\alpha)}+
\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_{O,B}(d,\alpha)}\right\}
=\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)+\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
\]
and
\[
0\le\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)
\le \frac2B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2}{\alpha^2}+
\frac{V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2}{(1-\alpha)^2}\right\}.                    \tag{2}
\]
For fixed \(d\) and \(\alpha\), the right-hand side of (2) tends to zero, proving the asserted convergence.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}designwise\text{-}gradient}\) identifies the unscaled brace as the variance of the two-source gradient of \(\theta_d(P)\).

Set \(a_d=c_{E,d}V_{E,d}\) and \(b_d=c_{O,d}V_{O,d}\).  Positive costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) imply \(a_d,b_d>0\).  For \(0<\alpha<1\), direct algebra gives
\[
\frac{a_d}{\alpha}+\frac{b_d}{1-\alpha}
-(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2
=\frac{\{(1-\alpha)\sqrt{a_d}-\alpha\sqrt{b_d}\}^2}
{\alpha(1-\alpha)}\ge0.                                           \tag{3}
\]
Equality in (3) holds exactly when
\[
\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{a_d}}{\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d}}
=\frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}}
{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}}
=\alpha_d^*(P).
\]
Thus this minimizer is unique and
\[
\mathcal V_d^*(P)
=\bigl(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\bigr)^2.
\]
If \(\theta_d(P)=\tau\) and the displayed gradient is canonical for \(\tau\), the defining variance-lower-bound property of the canonical gradient makes the same budget-normalized quantity the local semiparametric efficiency bound for \(\tau\).  This last interpretation uses precisely the two additional conditions stated in the theorem and is not needed for the preceding algebra.
