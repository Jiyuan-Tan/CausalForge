Fix the prescribed \(\chi>1\). For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), let \(r_\epsilon>0\) be furnished by Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\), and set \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{opt}}:=r_\epsilon\). Choose \(0<\delta_\epsilon<r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{opt}}\) and define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}
 =\bigcup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \left\{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi):
 \|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<\delta_\epsilon\right\}. \tag{1}
\]
Every member of every ball is regular, has both bridge contrasts equal to \(\tau\), and retains the strict raw prediction-risk and arm-normalized source-score optimizer assignments furnished by the stability lemma. Thus \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\subseteq\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) and is relatively open in \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\). No claim of openness in the ambient unrestricted probability simplex is made, because the factorization, support, consistency, and causal equality restrictions define a lower-dimensional model.
