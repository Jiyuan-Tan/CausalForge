Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  Because \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}observable\text{-}bridge\text{-}law\text{-}class}\), Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), and positivity of the unit costs imply, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+
          \sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2<\infty,                 \tag{1}
\]
where the equality is Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\).  Moreover \(R_d(P)<\infty\), because \(Y\in L_2\) and the admissible predictor \(f=0\) gives a finite risk.  Hence the finite minimizer set \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) is nonempty.

Substituting (1) into Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}predictive\text{-}benchmark}\) proves the displayed identity for \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  Its numerator minimizes over a nonempty subset of the set used by its denominator.  The ratio is consequently at least one; (1) makes both numerator and denominator finite and the denominator strictly positive.

For the best-tie rule, a constant \(C\) is valid precisely when
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P).
\]
Division by the positive denominator in (1) shows that the least such \(C\) is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For all pairwise implications define
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)=
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.                \tag{2}
\]
The indexing set in (2) is finite and nonempty because it contains every diagonal pair.  Every denominator is strictly positive by (1), so \(C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)<\infty\).  Any constant satisfying all of the pairwise implications must dominate every ratio indexed in (2), hence must dominate their maximum.  Conversely, the maximum in (2) dominates each indexed ratio and therefore satisfies every implication.  This proves the claimed exact minimality.  Notice that no latent causal restriction was used in this argument; the observable bridge class is the correct universal scope.

The remaining clauses concern the stronger binary causal subclasses.  Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves for each prescribed \(\chi>1\) that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty,
\]
while the diagnostic-selected center design has paired cost \(5\chi\) and the efficient design has paired cost \(5\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) proves, without changing those causal subclasses,
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).
\end{aligned}
\]
These facts establish both the lawwise finite enumeration and the absence of a primitive uniform guarantee for this paper's paired-source residual-risk diagnostic.
