Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) proves that the center belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), both bridge contrasts and \(\tau\) are zero, and the two strict optimizer assignments are
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.                     \tag{1}
\]
It also gives the paired costs and diagnostic values stated here.  Its exact moment calculations yield
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}(\epsilon^{-2}+14)
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +\frac{896}{75(10\chi-3)},                              \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac{3872}{25}. \tag{3}
\]
For fixed \(\chi\), expanding the exact square in that lemma gives
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}),                                \tag{4}
\]
because the observational square term is of order \(\epsilon^{-2}\), the cross term is of order \(\epsilon^{-1}\), and the experimental square term is bounded.

It remains to prove the neighborhood statement.  The center is an interior point of the finite latent-cell parameterization in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}witness}\).  Within that constrained model, consistency, latent exchangeability, experimental randomization, external validity, source support, and the sequential-outcome restriction follow identically from the factorization and the potential-variable construction.  Also \(Y(1)-Y(0)=0\) identically.  The two bridge contrasts remain equal because, whenever the bridge matrices are nonsingular, the outcome bridge recovers the latent conditional mean and experimental randomization averages it to the same value in both arms.

At the center, every support and overlap inequality is strict.  The two binary completeness matrices and the two conditional-expectation matrices have nonzero determinants, as shown in the sharp-elbow proof.  Determinants and cell probabilities are continuous, so all support, completeness, and bijectivity conditions persist on a sufficiently small relative cell neighborhood.  On that neighborhood, the bridge coefficients are obtained by inverses of finite nonsingular matrices.  They, the source score moments, the prediction risks, and the profiled design values are therefore continuous functions of the cell vector.  Positivity and finiteness of all source moments also persist.

The diagnostic gap and the arm-normalized design-value gap at the center are strict by (1), and the center ratio is positive.  Continuity therefore supplies \(r_\epsilon>0\) such that every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\) with \(\|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<r_\epsilon\) is regular, retains the two optimizer assignments in (1), has both bridge contrasts equal to \(\tau\), and obeys
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
This proves the stability claim.
