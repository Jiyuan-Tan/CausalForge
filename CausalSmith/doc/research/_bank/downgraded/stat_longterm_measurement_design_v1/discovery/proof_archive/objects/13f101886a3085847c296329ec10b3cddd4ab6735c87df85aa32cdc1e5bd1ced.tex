For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), \(d\in\mathcal D\), and fixed \(0<\alpha<1\), as \(B\to\infty\),
\[
B\left\{\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_{E,B}(d,\alpha)}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_{O,B}(d,\alpha)}\right\}
=\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)+\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P),
\]
where, once \(B\ge2\max\{c_{E,d}/\alpha,c_{O,d}/(1-\alpha)\}\),
\[
0\le\mathfrak r_{d,B}(\alpha;P)\le \frac2B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2}{\alpha^2}+
\frac{V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2}{(1-\alpha)^2}\right\}.
\]
Thus the floor-count source-score variance converges to \(\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)\). Along every bridge-differentiable submodel in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}bridge\text{-}differentiable\text{-}submodel}\), this source-score variance is the gradient variance of \(\theta_d(P)\). The unique minimizer and minimum are
\[
\alpha_d^*(P)=\frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}}{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}},
\qquad
\mathcal V_d^*(P)=\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2.
\]
For \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\), this profiles the gradient variance of the common causal target only along those bridge-differentiable submodels. It is a local causal semiparametric efficiency bound only when the designwise gradient is additionally canonical for \(\tau\), in particular under the fixed-system Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang conditions recorded separately.
