Fix any prescribed \(\chi>1\).  Under the IKM score and profile normalization, every binary center law satisfies, for \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},
 \qquad V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d},
\]
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).
\]
Consequently
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon),
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_\epsilon).
\]
For each fixed \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), there is \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\) such that every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\) with \(\|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\),
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
\]
and each of the two IKM-normalized ratios exceeds one half of its center value.  On this neighborhood, the IKM-normalized profile is the profiled local causal semiparametric efficiency bound under the fixed-system conditions in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\).
