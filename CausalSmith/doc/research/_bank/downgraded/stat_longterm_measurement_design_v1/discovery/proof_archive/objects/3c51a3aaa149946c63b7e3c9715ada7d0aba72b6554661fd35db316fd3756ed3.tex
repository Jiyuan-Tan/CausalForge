The binary witness stability lemma proves regularity, causal identification on this constant-\((X,S_2)\) subclass, the strict optimizer assignments, and the exact variance and ratio expansions at every center.  The adaptive-radius definition of \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and the last assertion of that lemma then imply, for every member of the region,
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},\qquad
 D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
Every center belongs to its own relative ball.  Since
\(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)=49/(9801\epsilon^2)+O(\epsilon^{-1})\), taking \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) proves the unbounded supremum.

It remains only to verify the open cost range.  For an arbitrary cap-feasible cost vector on the same stochastic construction, write
\[
 C_{\mathrm p}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}},qquad
 C_{\mathrm e}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}.
\]
By (3) in the stability proof, predictive selection favors \(d_{\mathrm p}\) exactly when
\[
 \frac{C_{\mathrm p}}{C_{\mathrm e}}
 <\frac{R_{d_{\mathrm e}}}{R_{d_{\mathrm p}}}
 =\frac{3(16-9\epsilon^2)}{35(1-\epsilon^2)}. \tag{1}
\]
The right side is strictly increasing in \(\epsilon^2\) and tends to \(48/35\) at zero.  Hence every vector satisfying the displayed strict cost-ratio condition makes \(d_{\mathrm p}\) predictive-optimal for all sufficiently small \(\epsilon>0\), even though \(C_{\mathrm p}>C_{\mathrm e}\).  Positivity of \(c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\) and the exact \(V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\) formula force \(\mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*\) to diverge at order \(\epsilon^{-2}\), whereas the value of \(d_{\mathrm e}\) remains finite.  Thus \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is efficiency-optimal for sufficiently small \(\epsilon\), and the ratio diverges.  Strict positivity, the cap inequalities, and both strict cost-ratio inequalities define a relatively open set of feasible cost vectors.  The explicit vector in the corrected witness has ratio \(6/5\), which lies strictly between \(1\) and \(48/35\).
