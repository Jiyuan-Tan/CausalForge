Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\).  Treatment is balanced in both sources at every center, so
\[
 p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=\frac12.
\]
The definitions of the IKM-normalized scores and moments give, for either design,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},
 \qquad
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d}. \tag{1}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon). \tag{2}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) shows that \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes the right-hand side, while \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is uniquely diagnostic-selected.  The common factor in (2) cancels from the ratio, proving the center assertions.

Restrict first to the regularity radius supplied by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\).  Within that finite-cell neighborhood, treatment probabilities, bridge coefficients, score moments, and profiled values are continuous: the bridge coefficients are inverses of finite nonsingular conditional-expectation matrices, and the remaining objects are finite sums and continuous square-root operations on strictly positive arguments.  The IKM moments
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}
\]
are therefore continuous as well.  Since both optimizer gaps at the center are strict and the center ratio is positive, a further radius reduction gives \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\) on which regularity and common identification persist,
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
\]
and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
On this neighborhood, once the fixed-system causal-identification, local-model, canonical-gradient, and source-ratio premises in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) hold design by design, that lemma identifies the unprofiled IKM variance as the local causal efficiency bound.  Profiling its two positive source components over the budget share by the same algebra as Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) gives \(\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)\).
