For binary treatment, the multiplier printed in the IKM score is \((1-p_{g,1})\) times the arm-normalized multiplier. Indeed, when \(A=1\),
\[
 \frac{A-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}}=\frac{1-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}},
\]
whereas when \(A=0\),
\[
 \frac{A-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}}=-1
 =(1-p_{g,1})\left(-\frac1{1-p_{g,1}}\right).
\]
The remaining residual factors agree source by source. Hence
\[
 \psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})\phi_{g,d},
 \qquad
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})^2V_{g,d}. \tag{1}
\]
At every center \(P_\epsilon\), treatment is balanced in both sources. Equation (1) proves the four score and variance identities, and
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}
 +\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}\right)^2
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon). \tag{2}
\]
The common positive factor preserves the design ordering and cancels from the ratio. Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) therefore gives
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon). \tag{3}
\]

Fix \((\chi,\epsilon)\). The finite-cell inversion in the binary stability lemma constructs unique square-integrable outcome and selection bridges throughout a relative neighborhood. In the constrained model, \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), so \(\tau=0\); source invariance and the outcome-bridge equation give \(\mu_{d,1}=\mu_{d,0}=\mathbb E(U)\) for each design. Thus both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\). Treatment probabilities, bridge coefficients, prediction risks, source moments in (1), and both profiled values are continuous functions of the strictly positive cell vector. Regularity and the predictive and normalized-efficiency inequalities are strict at the center, and the ratio denominator is positive. Intersecting the finitely many neighborhoods that preserve these properties gives \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\); shrinking once more by continuity gives
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Finally, the binary stability lemma verifies causal identification, unique square-integrable bridges, operator bijectivity, finite moments, and interior source sampling. Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) therefore applies design by design. For positive \(a,b\),
\[
 \inf_{0<\alpha<1}\left\{\frac a\alpha+\frac b{1-\alpha}\right\}
 =(\sqrt a+\sqrt b)^2,
\]
as follows by completing the square. Taking \(a=c_{E,d}V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) and \(b=c_{O,d}V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) shows that profiling the cited two-source lower bound over the cost-feasible allocation yields exactly \(\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)\), proving the last assertion.
