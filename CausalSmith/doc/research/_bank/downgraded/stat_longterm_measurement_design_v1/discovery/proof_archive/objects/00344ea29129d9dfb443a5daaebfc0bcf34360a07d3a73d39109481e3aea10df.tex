The note defines a nonredundant full-Gram transport of the CLW population affine-linear criterion, proves an exact continuous and integer-budget measurement rule, and constructs a regular unequal-cost binary region with an unbounded named-rule bridge-value ratio and a sharp optimized \(1/(726\epsilon^2)\) leading divergence. It separately retains generic unrestricted-risk counterexamples and supplies the positive bridge-value criterion, pilot oracle-regret control, square-root source allocation, and conditional post-selection Wald theory.
