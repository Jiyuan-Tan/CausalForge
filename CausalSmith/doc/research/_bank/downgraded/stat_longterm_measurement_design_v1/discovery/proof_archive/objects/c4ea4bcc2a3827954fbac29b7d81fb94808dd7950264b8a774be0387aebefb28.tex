For each fixed \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), the radii \(r_\epsilon\) and \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) in Lemmas~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) and \(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\) may be jointly decreased, without losing any conclusion of those lemmas, so that every law in the corresponding smaller finite-cell ball also satisfies
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_\epsilon),
\]
and, in the IKM-normalized ball,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Thus the radii used to form \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) can preserve both strict prediction-risk orderings simultaneously with the arm-normalized and IKM-normalized optimizer orderings.
