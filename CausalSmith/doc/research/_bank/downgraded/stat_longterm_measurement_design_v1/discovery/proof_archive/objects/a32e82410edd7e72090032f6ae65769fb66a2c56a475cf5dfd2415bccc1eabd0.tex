For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) and \(d\in\mathcal D\), \(\tau=\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}\) is identified, and under independent sample sizes \(n_E,n_O\) its canonical gradient decomposes into the two sample components \(\phi_{E,d}/n_E\) and \(\phi_{O,d}/n_O\), giving local variance \(V_{E,d}/n_E+V_{O,d}/n_O\).
