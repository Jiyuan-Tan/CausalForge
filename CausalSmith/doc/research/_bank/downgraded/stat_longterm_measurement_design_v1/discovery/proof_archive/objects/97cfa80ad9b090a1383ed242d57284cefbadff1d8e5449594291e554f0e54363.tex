For the unequal-cost binary witness, every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\) gives a law \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) for which both designs identify \(\tau\) and are regular. Although \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is more expensive, with \(c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=6>5=c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}\), it is the unique minimizer of this paper's Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm-shaped paired-source prediction objective, while \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is the unique minimizer of both the paper's arm-normalized profile and the Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang-normalized causal efficiency profile. More precisely,
\[
V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{448}{2025\epsilon^2}+\frac{6272}{2025},
\qquad V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
\qquad V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d},
\]
\[
\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon),
\qquad
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)=\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1}).
\]
Every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular for both designs, \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty\). On the relatively open subregion \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\subseteq\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\), every design remains regular and identifies \(\tau\), \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty.
\]
The same center-path divergence, for both ratios, holds for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\) on the relatively open subset of the induced cap-feasible catalogue-cost manifold
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal C_\kappa=\biggl\{(b_E,b_O,c_1,\ldots,c_K):{}&0<b_E<b_O,\quad c_j>0,\\
&\sum_{j\in M_d}c_j\le\kappa\quad(d\in\mathcal D),\\
&c_{E,d}=b_E+\sum_{j\in M_d}c_j,\quad
c_{O,d}=b_O+\sum_{j\in M_d}c_j\quad(d\in\mathcal D)\biggr\}
\end{aligned}
\]
on which
\[
1<\frac{c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}}{c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}}<\frac{48}{35}.
\]
Thus the failure is neither an equal-cost artifact nor an identification failure, and the absolute causal-efficiency interpretation uses the IKM-normalized profile rather than the unscaled arm-normalized profile.
