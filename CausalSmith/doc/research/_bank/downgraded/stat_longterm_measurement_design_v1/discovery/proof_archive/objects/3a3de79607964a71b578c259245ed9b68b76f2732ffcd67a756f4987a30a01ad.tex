For each regular design \(d\), define
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})\phi_{O,d},
\]
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}.             \tag{1}
\]
The profiled IKM-normalized value and its tie-preserving optimizer set are
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)
 =\left\{
 \sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 +\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \right\}^2,
 \qquad
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P). \tag{2}
\]
The best-tie IKM ratios for the named continuous CLW selector and the two generic unrestricted-risk selectors are, respectively,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}, \tag{3}
\]
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}. \tag{4}
\]
These are normalization-specific source-score profiles. Their interpretation as causal efficiency bounds is supplied design by design only under Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) after its causal-identification and regularity premises have been verified. The named selector in (3) transports only the CLW population affine-linear criterion; neither (3) nor (4) attributes persistent-confounding analysis or bridge-role selection to a published paper.
