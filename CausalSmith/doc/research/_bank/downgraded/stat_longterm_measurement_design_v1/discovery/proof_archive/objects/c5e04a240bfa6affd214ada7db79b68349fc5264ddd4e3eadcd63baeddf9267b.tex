Fix \(\chi>1\).  For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), the center \(P_\epsilon\) belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\cap\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\), both admissible bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.                       \tag{1}
\]
The paired costs and exact center CLW criteria satisfy
\[
 C_{d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi,
 \qquad C_{d_{\mathrm e}}=5,
\]
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon;B).       \tag{2}
\]
The unrestricted risks have the same center ordering, but are kept distinct from the linear residuals off center.  Moreover,
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +\frac{896}{75(10\chi-3)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac{3872}{25}, \tag{3}
\]
and, as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) with \(\chi\) fixed,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}).                                  \tag{4}
\]
For each fixed \((\chi,\epsilon)\), there is \(r_\epsilon>0\) such that every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{bin}}(\chi)\) with \(\|P-P_\epsilon\|_{\mathrm{cell}}<r_\epsilon\) belongs to \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\cap\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\), has both bridge contrasts equal to \(\tau\), and satisfies
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)=\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},                         \tag{5}
\]
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P_\epsilon;B),
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon). \tag{6}
\]
For every fixed law in this ball there is a finite \(B_0(P)\) such that
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}
 \qquad(B\ge B_0(P)).                                     \tag{7}
\]
Thus the named-rule neighborhood statement follows from continuity of the distinct finite-dimensional Gram-matrix functional \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\), not from continuity of \(R_d(P)\).
