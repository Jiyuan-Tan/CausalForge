Use \(\mathsf H_{\mathrm{comp}}\) to compare the source-score quadratic forms underlying \(\mathcal V_d^*(P)\) with either observational prediction criterion, \(R_d(P)\) or \(R_d^A(P)\).  These source-score forms have a gradient interpretation only along the bridge-differentiable paths declared for design \(d\).  For each risk rule the exact target is its lawwise order-restricted constant and its best-tie selector ratio, respectively \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) or \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)\).  The handle does not assert that a generalized eigenvalue is sharp and does not give an unconditional gradient or causal-efficiency interpretation.
