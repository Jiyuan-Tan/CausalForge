An independent full-catalogue pilot supports two distinct measurable rules: the proposed bridge-value selector fits both bridges and their source-score variances for every admissible design, whereas the Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm comparator computes each design's observational affine OLS residual and divides it by the continuous or exact floor-budget count.  The proposed rule fixes \(\widehat d_m\), its square-root budget share, and the integer experimental and follow-up counts; the independent main study then measures only the selected catalogue subset in both sources and observes \(Y\) only in observational follow-up.
