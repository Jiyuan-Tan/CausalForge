Fix \(\chi>1\), let \(\epsilon_m\in(0,10^{-3})\) satisfy \(\epsilon_m\downarrow0\), and suppose the independently funded full-catalogue pilot consists of independent and identically distributed draws from \(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}\), independently of the main study. Apply the split-sample saturated finite-cell rule in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}treatment\text{-}aware\text{-}pilot\text{-}selector}\). Then
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}
 |\widehat R_{d,m}^A-R_d^A(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})|=O_P(m^{-1/2}), \tag{1}
\]
\[
 P_{\chi,\epsilon_m}\{\widehat d_m^A=d_{\mathrm p}\}\longrightarrow1. \tag{2}
\]
Moreover, the achieved arm-normalized and IKM-normalized profile ratios diverge in probability:
\[
 \frac{\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m^A}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 {\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_d^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 \longrightarrow_P\infty,
 \qquad
 \frac{\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m^A,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 {\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon_m})}
 \longrightarrow_P\infty. \tag{3}
\]
Thus the treatment-aware population impossibility is attained by a feasible pilot selector using only bounded binary outcomes and saturated finite-cell regression; no inverse-problem learner or growing sieve is used.
