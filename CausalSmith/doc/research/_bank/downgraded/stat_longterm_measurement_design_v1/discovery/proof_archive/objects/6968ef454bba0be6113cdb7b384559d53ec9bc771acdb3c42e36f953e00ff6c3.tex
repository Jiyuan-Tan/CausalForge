Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) and suppose that \(\mathcal D=\{d\}\), that \(n_E,n_O\) are positive, and that
\[
 B=c_{E,d}n_E+c_{O,d}n_O.
\]
Membership in \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) makes \(d\) regular.  Since both unit costs and both counts are strictly positive, \(B>0\).  Put
\[
 \alpha_0=\frac{c_{E,d}n_E}{B}.
\]
Then \(0<\alpha_0<1\), and the budget identity gives
\[
 1-\alpha_0
 =\frac{B-c_{E,d}n_E}{B}
 =\frac{c_{O,d}n_O}{B}.                                      \tag{1}
\]
The budget-profile theorem, applied to this regular design at \(\alpha_0\), gives
\[
 \mathcal V_d(\alpha_0;P)
 =\frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{\alpha_0}
  +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{1-\alpha_0}.                         \tag{2}
\]
Substitution of \(\alpha_0=c_{E,d}n_E/B\) and (1) into (2) yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \frac{1}{B}\mathcal V_d(\alpha_0;P)
 &=\frac{1}{B}\left\{
   \frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{c_{E,d}n_E/B}
   +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{c_{O,d}n_O/B}
   \right\}\\
 &=\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.                  \tag{3}
\end{aligned}
\]
The nondegeneracy assumption ensures that the two variance components in (3) are finite and positive.  By the designwise-gradient lemma, \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}\) is an identified observed-data functional, its two source-gradient components are \(\phi_{E,d}\) and \(\phi_{O,d}\), and independence of samples of sizes \(n_E\) and \(n_O\) makes the variance of that gradient exactly the right-hand side of (3).  This proves the unconditional identity and its bridge-functional interpretation.

For the final conditional assertion, additionally suppose that \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=\tau\) and that \((\phi_{E,d},\phi_{O,d})\) is the canonical gradient for \(\tau\) in the stated fixed-system model.  The semiparametric efficiency bound is, by the defining projection characterization of the canonical gradient in the product two-source tangent space, the variance of that canonical gradient.  Independence of the two sources and their fixed counts decomposes this variance as
\[
 \frac{\mathbb E_P[\phi_{E,d}^2\mid G=E]}{n_E}
 +\frac{\mathbb E_P[\phi_{O,d}^2\mid G=O]}{n_O}
 =\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O},                  \tag{4}
\]
which equals (3).  The listed bridge-existence, bridge-uniqueness, operator-bijectivity, moment, and interior sampling-ratio conditions specify the fixed-system regime in which the proposition assumes this causal identification and canonicality.  If either additional conclusion is absent, the designwise-gradient lemma supplies only the observed bridge-functional gradient statement (and indeed shows that the frozen class alone need not satisfy \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=\tau\)); hence no unconditional causal-efficiency relabelling has been used.
