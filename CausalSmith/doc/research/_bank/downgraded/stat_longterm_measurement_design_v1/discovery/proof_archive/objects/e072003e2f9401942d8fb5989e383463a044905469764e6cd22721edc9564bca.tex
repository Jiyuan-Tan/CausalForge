Fix the source-faithful transported comparison from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}clw\text{-}population\text{-}design\text{-}rule}\): the comparison experiment uses complete randomization with equal treatment allocation and its two treatment arms have the same affine-regression residual variance.  These are restrictions on the transported comparison design, not additional restrictions on the persistent-confounding bridge law.  Map the published support \(\mathcal I(\gamma)\) only to the measured subset \(M_d\); the sequential role assignment \(r_d\) remains fixed by this note and is not attributed to Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm.

Choose once and for all a finite-dimensional nonredundant affine encoding \(X_{\mathrm{nr}}\) of the always-measured covariates, with every constant and reference column removed, and put
\[
 \xi_d=(1,X_{\mathrm{nr}}^\top,Z_{M_d}^\top)^\top,
 \qquad
 \Gamma_d(P)=\mathbb E_P(\xi_d\xi_d^\top\mid G=O),
 \qquad
 g_d(P)=\mathbb E_P(\xi_dY\mid G=O).                       \tag{1}
\]
The CLW-admissible transported domain \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\) consists of the laws \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) for which, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\), the coordinates in (1) are real-valued,
\[
 \mathbb E_P(Y^2+\|\xi_d\|^2\mid G=O)<\infty,
 \qquad
 \det\Gamma_d(P)\ne0,                                    \tag{2}
\]
and the transported balanced comparison has the arm-homoskedastic residual required by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}clw\text{-}population\text{-}design\text{-}rule}\).  Condition (2) is imposed on the full Gram matrix of the nonredundant affine basis.  It neither uses a pseudoinverse nor encodes any selector ordering or bridge-value comparison.

For \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\), define
\[
 \beta_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)=\Gamma_d(P)^{-1}g_d(P),
 \qquad
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)
 =\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \{Y-\xi_d^\top\beta_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\}^2\mid G=O
 \right].                                                  \tag{3}
\]
Under the linear per-unit cost specialization
\[
 c(M_d,n)=nC_d,
 \qquad C_d=c_{E,d}+c_{O,d},                               \tag{4}
\]
the continuous and integer-budget criteria are
\[
 n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)=\frac{B}{C_d},
 \qquad
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 =\frac{r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)}
 =\frac{C_dr_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{B},                      \tag{5}
\]
\[
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 =\frac{r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor}      \tag{6}
\]
when the denominator in (6) is positive, with value \(+\infty\) otherwise.  Retain all ties in
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B),
 \qquad
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B). \tag{7}
\]
The best-tie arm-normalized and IKM-normalized bridge-value ratios of the continuous named selector are
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}. \tag{8}
\]
Equations (3)--(8) transport only the population affine-linear criterion.  They do not assert that Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm study persistent confounding, choose sequential bridge roles, or prove anything about this note's bridge values.  No result here evaluates their original randomized-trial theorem, their empirical equation~(3.6), or their OGA approximation.
