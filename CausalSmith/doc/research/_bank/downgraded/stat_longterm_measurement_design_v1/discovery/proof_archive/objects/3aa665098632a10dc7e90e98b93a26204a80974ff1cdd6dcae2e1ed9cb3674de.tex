The first two conclusions and the half-center lower bound follow directly from the radius defining \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) and Lemma~\texttt{lem:ikmw-normalized-witness-stability}. Every center \(P_\epsilon\) lies in its own ball. Hence
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 \ge
 \sup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\sup_{0<\epsilon<10^{-3}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\infty,
\]
where the middle equality is the quarter-scaling identity and the last equality is the center expansion in Lemma~\texttt{lem:binary-witness-stability}. Relative openness follows because the region is a union of relative open balls in the named finite-cell model.
