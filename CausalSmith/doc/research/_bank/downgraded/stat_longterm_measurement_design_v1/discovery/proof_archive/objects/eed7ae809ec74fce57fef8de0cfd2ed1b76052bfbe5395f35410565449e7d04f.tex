For each \(d\in\mathcal D\), compute the unadjusted and treatment-aware observational residual prediction risks
\[
R_d(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(X,Z_{M_d})}\mathbb E_P[(Y-f(X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O],
\qquad
R_d^A(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(A,X,Z_{M_d})}\mathbb E_P[(Y-f(A,X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O].
\]
The corresponding generic population rules retain every tie:
\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}R_d(P),
\qquad
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}R_d^A(P),
\]
and their arm-normalized best-tie ratios are
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=
\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
{\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)},
\qquad
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=
\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
{\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.
\]
For the Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm population data-collection rule, specialize their cost function to the valid linear form
\[
c(M_d,n)=n(c_{E,d}+c_{O,d}),
\]
map their selected support only to the measured subset \(M_d\), and leave the role assignment \(r_d\) preattached by this note. The largest continuous budget-feasible size and the resulting population criterion are
\[
n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)=\frac{B}{c_{E,d}+c_{O,d}},
\qquad
\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
=\frac{R_d(P)}{n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)}
=\frac{(c_{E,d}+c_{O,d})R_d(P)}{B}.
\]
Thus \(\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}\) is the exact CLW linear-cost population criterion under this support mapping, not merely a shape analogy. This specialization neither attributes the preattached bridge roles to CLW nor changes their randomized-trial optimality result.
