For the selected design \(\widehat d_m\) and feasible pilot share \(\widehat\alpha_m\), use the realized counts \(N_{E,B}=\lfloor\widehat\alpha_mB/c_{E,\widehat d_m}\rfloor\) and \(N_{O,B}=\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_m)B/c_{O,\widehat d_m}\rfloor\).  Use a fixed observational-only fold count \(L\ge2\).  For each main-study observation, form the foldwise plug-in version of the exact arm-normalized score \(\phi_{E,\widehat d_m}\) or \(\phi_{O,\widehat d_m}\), replacing \(h_{\widehat d_m}\), \(q_{\widehat d_m}\), \(p_{g,a}\), and \(\mu_{\widehat d_m,a}\) by their corresponding off-fold estimates; the experimental observations are not split, but are evaluated with every observationally trained outcome-bridge fit.  Let \(\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}\) and \(\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}\) be the within-source empirical second moments of precisely these plug-in scores.  On positive source counts set \(\widehat s_B^2=B\{\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}/N_{E,B}+\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}/N_{O,B}\}\), and take \(\widehat s_B=(\widehat s_B^2)^{1/2}\) to be the nonnegative square root; use fixed measurable extensions when a required denominator is zero.
