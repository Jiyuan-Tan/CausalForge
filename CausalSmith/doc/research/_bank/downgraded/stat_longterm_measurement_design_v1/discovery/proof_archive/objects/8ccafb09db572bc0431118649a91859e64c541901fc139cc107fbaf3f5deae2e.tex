For a fixed short-term-outcome system and a design \(d\), let \(V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) and \(V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) denote the two source-score second moments printed in Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~5 for the causal target \(\tau\). Suppose the conditions of their Theorem~7 hold: their Assumptions~1--4 and~6 define the local semiparametric model; at the law under evaluation, their Assumptions~6 and~7 admit unique square-integrable outcome and selection bridges; the corresponding conditional-expectation operator is bijective; and \(V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) and \(V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) are finite. Then their Theorem~7 identifies the printed two-source gradient as the canonical gradient and gives the local efficiency lower bound
\[
 \sigma_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^2
 =\lim (n_E+n_O)\left(
 \frac{V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}{n_E}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}{n_O}
 \right)
\]
along any sequence for which the displayed limit exists. For attainment, additionally impose the sampling and estimation premises of their Theorem~5: independent identically distributed observations within the two sources, \(n_E,n_O\to\infty\), \(n_E/n_O\) converging to a number in \((0,\infty)\), both identification-and-consistency premises in statements~1 and~2 of their Theorem~4, cross-fitting, and both parts of their Assumption~8 with nuisance limits equal to the true bridges, all weak- and strong-metric errors vanishing, and the minimum of the outcome-weak--selection-strong and outcome-strong--selection-weak error products equal to \(o((n_E+n_O)^{-1/2})\). Under these additional premises, their Theorem~5 gives asymptotic normality with variance \(\sigma_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^2\), so their doubly robust estimator attains the Theorem~7 lower bound for \(\tau\). Equivalently, on the original estimator scale, the leading variance is \(V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}/n_E+V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}/n_O\).
