Predictive sufficiency and bridge efficiency are different geometric objects. Treatment adjustment improves or preserves prediction risk because it enlarges the projection space, but it does not control the inverse sensitivity of the early proxy inside the selection bridge. In the witness, the late proxy makes \(d_{\mathrm p}\) highly predictive, even after conditioning on treatment, while the early signal \(\epsilon\) drives its observational score variance at order \(\epsilon^{-2}\). The exact design-value gap therefore answers the measurement question that prediction risk cannot. The IKM-normalized profile is interpreted as an absolute causal efficiency bound only under the separately stated fixed-system canonical-gradient premises.
