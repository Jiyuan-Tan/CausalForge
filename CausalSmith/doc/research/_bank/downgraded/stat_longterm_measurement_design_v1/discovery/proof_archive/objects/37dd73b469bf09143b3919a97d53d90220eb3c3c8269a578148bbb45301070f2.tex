Fix a short-term-outcome design \(d\) and the causal target \(\tau\).  Let \(V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) and \(V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) denote exactly the two source-conditional score second moments printed in Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~5.  Under the identification, sampling, cross-fitting, nuisance-convergence, and weak--strong product-rate conditions of that theorem, if \(n_E,n_O\to\infty\) and \(n_E/n_O\to\lambda\in(0,\infty)\), their doubly robust estimator is asymptotically normal with variance
\[
 \frac{1+\lambda}{\lambda}V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
 +(1+\lambda)V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
\]
on the \(\sqrt{n_E+n_O}\) scale.  If, in addition, the outcome and selection bridges are unique and the conditional-expectation operator in their Assumption~8 is bijective, their Theorem~7 states that this same variance is the local semiparametric efficiency lower bound for \(\tau\).  Equivalently, on the unscaled estimator scale the source contribution is
\[
 \frac{V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}{n_E}
 +\frac{V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}{n_O}.
\]
No equality with this paper's arm-normalized moments \(V_{E,d}\) and \(V_{O,d}\) is part of the cited result.
