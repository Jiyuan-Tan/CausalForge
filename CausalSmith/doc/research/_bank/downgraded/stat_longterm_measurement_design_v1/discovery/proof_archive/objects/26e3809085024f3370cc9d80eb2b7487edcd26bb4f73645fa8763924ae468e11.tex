The paper derives the optimal square-root allocation, the profiled design value, and an exact finite-budget decomposition: continuous miss-allocation costs exactly \(\mathcal V_d^*(P)(\widehat\alpha-\alpha_d^*(P))^2/\{\widehat\alpha(1-\widehat\alpha)\}\), and positive integer counts add an explicit nonnegative floor remainder with a reciprocal-count bound. This yields the sharp \(O_P(r_{V,m}^2+B^{-1})\) pilot allocation loss and oracle-set selection. The negative result is uniform over all finite \(\chi>1\): the prediction-selected center design is costlier in each source by \(5(\chi-1)/2\), its ratio is at least \(\{(5\chi+1)/(363(10\chi-3))\}(\epsilon^{-2}+14)\), and the uniform first-order constant is exactly \(1/726\), the upper direction being attained along \(\chi=\epsilon^{-2/3}\). Thus the exact finite-catalogue constant is only a law-specific enumeration, while a full-catalogue pilot consistently targets the correct design-value gap.
