Fix one of the two profiles in the statement and write its values as \(w_d\).  For the arm-normalized profile, strict positivity and finiteness follow from Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), positive unit costs, and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\).  For the IKM-normalized profile, Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}source\text{-}arm\text{-}positivity}\) gives \(0<1-p_{g,1}<1\); hence
\[
  0<V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}
   =(1-p_{g,1})^2V_{g,d}<\infty,
\]
and Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}design\text{-}value}\) gives \(0<w_d<\infty\).  Thus, for either profile, all ratios below are well defined and finite.

Define
\[
  \mathcal O_P^A
  =\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:R_d^A(P)\le R_{d'}^A(P)\},
  \qquad
  C_{mathrm{ord}}^A(P;w)
  =\max_{(d,d')\in\mathcal O_P^A}\frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}.       \tag{18}
\]
The set is nonempty and finite because \(\mathcal D\) is nonempty and finite and every diagonal pair belongs to it.  If \(C\ge C_{mathrm{ord}}^A(P;w)\), every treatment-aware order implication follows immediately from (18).  Conversely, if every implication holds with \(C\), division by \(w_{d'}>0\) for each pair in \(\mathcal O_P^A\), followed by maximization, gives \(C\ge C_{mathrm{ord}}^A(P;w)\).  This proves (1) and its minimality.  It is the treatment-aware instance of the finite-order argument proved in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}sharp\text{-}predictive\text{-}operator\text{-}certificate}\).

Let
\[
  \bar w=\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}w_{d'},
  \qquad
  w_{mathrm{pred}}^A
  =\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}w_d.
\]
The least best-tie selector constant is \(w_{mathrm{pred}}^A/\bar w\).  By Definitions~\(\mathrm{def{:}predictive\text{-}benchmark}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}design\text{-}value}\), this ratio is \(\rho_{mathrm{pred}}^A(P)\) for the arm-normalized profile and \(\rho_{mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)\) for the IKM-normalized profile.  Choose \(d_A\) attaining \(w_{mathrm{pred}}^A\) within \(\mathcal D_{mathrm{pred}}^A(P)\), and choose \(d_0\) attaining \(\bar w\).  Since \(R_{d_A}^A(P)\le R_{d_0}^A(P)\), the pair \((d_A,d_0)\) occurs in (18), and therefore
\[
  1\le\frac{w_{mathrm{pred}}^A}{\bar w}
  \le C_{mathrm{ord}}^A(P;w)<\infty.                       \tag{19}
\]
The uniform-law-class equivalence follows pointwise: a finite uniform constant bounds the corresponding pointwise least ratio and hence its supremum, while a finite supremum itself supplies a uniform constant.

For the quadratic certificate, let
\[
  r_d^A(x)=\frac{F_d(x)}{Q_{R,d}^A(x)}.
\]
Positivity of \(Q_{R,d}^A\) on the stated cones permits division.  The definitions of \(\Lambda_-^A(P)\) and \(\Lambda_+^A(P)\) imply, at every transported direction \(x_d(P)\),
\[
  \Lambda_-^A(P)R_d^A(P)
  \le\mathcal V_d^*(P)
  \le\Lambda_+^A(P)R_d^A(P).                              \tag{20}
\]
For any pair with \(R_d^A(P)\le R_{d'}^A(P)\), (20) gives
\[
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le\Lambda_+^A(P)R_d^A(P)
 \le\Lambda_+^A(P)R_{d'}^A(P)
 \le\frac{\Lambda_+^A(P)}{\Lambda_-^A(P)}
       \mathcal V_{d'}^*(P).                               \tag{21}
\]
Maximizing (21) over the treatment-aware order relation, and applying (19) with \(w_d=\mathcal V_d^*(P)\), proves (2).

Finally, changing the denominator from \(Q_{R,d}\) to \(Q_{R,d}^A\) does not change the profiled numerator \(F_d\).  Identity (9) and the explicit parallelogram-law failure (17) in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}sharp\text{-}predictive\text{-}operator\text{-}certificate}\) therefore apply unchanged.  A largest generalized eigenvalue for a fixed allocation supplies only an upper full-space quadratic envelope.  The exact treatment-aware constant additionally requires a positive lower envelope and equality along law-realized directions whose treatment-aware risks have the required ordering.  Absent those saturation conditions, (2) can be strict, so neither the pairwise sharp constant nor the best-tie sharp constant is generally a largest generalized eigenvalue.
