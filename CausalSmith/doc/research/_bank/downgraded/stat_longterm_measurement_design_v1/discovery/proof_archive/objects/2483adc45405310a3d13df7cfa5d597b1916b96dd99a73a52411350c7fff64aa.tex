Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) supplies a relative finite-cell ball on which regularity, CLW admissibility, common identification, the continuous named ordering, the raw-risk ordering, the arm-normalized optimizer ordering, and their one-half ratio bounds hold.  Its proof expresses
\[
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)=
 \mathbb E_P(Y^2\mid G=O)-g_d(P)^\top\Gamma_d(P)^{-1}g_d(P),
\]
where all moments are continuous functions of the positive cell vector and the full Gram determinants stay nonzero.  Thus both sides of (51) are continuous.  Shrinking the ball preserves the strict margin (51).  Intersecting with the smaller ball allowed by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}predictive\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\) loses none of these properties.

For every \(B\ge C_{d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi\), set \(z_d=B/C_d\).  Then \(z_d\ge1\), and the elementary floor inequalities \(z/\lfloor z\rfloor<2\) and \(z/\lfloor z\rfloor\ge1\) give
\[
 \frac{B}{\lfloor B/C_{d_{\mathrm p}}\rfloor}<2C_{d_{\mathrm p}},
 \qquad
 \frac{B}{\lfloor B/C_{d_{\mathrm e}}\rfloor}\ge C_{d_{\mathrm e}}. \tag{52}
\]
Multiplying by the two positive residuals and using the preserved margin yields
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 <B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B).
\]
Because \(\mathcal D=\{d_{\mathrm p},d_{\mathrm e}\}\), the exact integer selector is \(\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\).  The continuous selector is the same singleton throughout the ball, so the two arm-normalized best-tie ratios are identical.  The inherited one-half bound proves (2).
