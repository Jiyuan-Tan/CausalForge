Fix \(\chi>1\).  For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\) and every \(B\ge5\chi\), the exact integer-budget CLW selector at \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}\) is \(\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), whereas \(D^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\).  The eight center ratios
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}},\quad
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}},\quad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}},\quad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A,
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}},\quad
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM,int}},\quad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}},\quad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}
\]
all equal
\[
 \rho_\chi(\epsilon)=\frac{25}{3872}
 \left\{
 \sqrt{\frac{20\chi(5\chi-1)}{10\chi-3}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{32(5\chi+1)}{75(10\chi-3)}
 (\epsilon^{-2}+14)}
 \right\}^{2}.                                             \tag{1}
\]
This common value exceeds \((\epsilon^{-2}+14)/726\) and, for fixed \(\chi\),
\[
 \rho_\chi(\epsilon)=
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}).                                  \tag{2}
\]
For every \(B\ge5\chi\), the suitably shrunk regions satisfy
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=\infty,
 \qquad
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM,int}}(P;B)=\infty.      \tag{3}
\]
The optimized integer envelope is understood with joint budget feasibility:
\[
 \inf_{\substack{\chi>1\\ B\ge5\chi}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 =\inf_{\substack{\chi>1\\ B\ge5\chi}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM,int}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon};B)
 =\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_\chi(\epsilon),                     \tag{4}
\]
and every minimizing \(\chi\) satisfies
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},
\]
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_\chi(\epsilon)
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                      \tag{5}
\]
Thus (5) applies along any budget schedule large enough to satisfy \(B_\epsilon\ge5\chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)\); it is not an unrestricted optimization over \(\chi\) at one fixed finite budget.  The named empirical counterpart is treated separately and no result concerns approximate OGA, an arbitrary predictive method, the original randomized-trial theorem, persistent-confounding analysis by Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm, or bridge-role choice by that paper.
