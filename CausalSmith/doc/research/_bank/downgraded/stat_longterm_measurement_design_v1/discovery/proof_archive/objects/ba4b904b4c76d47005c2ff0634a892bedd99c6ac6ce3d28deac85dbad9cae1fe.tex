Let \(\mathcal D\) be finite and nonempty. Suppose the costs and population variance components obey
\[
0<\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\min\{c_{E,d},c_{O,d},V_{E,d},V_{O,d}\}
\le
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\{c_{E,d},c_{O,d},V_{E,d},V_{O,d}\}<\infty.
\]
Suppose
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\bigl\{
|\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
|\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\bigr\}=O_P(r_{V,m}),
\qquad r_{V,m}=o(1),
\qquad \underline v_m\downarrow0,
\]
and define \(\widehat\alpha_{d,m}\) by the full-catalogue pilot selector. Then, along every joint sequence \(m\to\infty\) and \(B\to\infty\),
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
+\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
\right\}-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
=O_P\!\left((r_{V,m}+\underline v_m)^2+B^{-1}\right).
\]
In particular, allocation estimation enters the profiled value only at second order.
