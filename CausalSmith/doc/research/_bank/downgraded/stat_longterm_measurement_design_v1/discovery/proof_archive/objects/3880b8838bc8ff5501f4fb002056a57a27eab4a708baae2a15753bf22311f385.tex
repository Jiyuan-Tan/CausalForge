For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) and \(d\in\mathcal D\), the observed-data bridge contrast \(\theta_d(P):=\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}\) is identified. Along every regular two-source submodel of the observable bridge model, its pathwise gradient has source components \(\phi_{E,d}\) and \(\phi_{O,d}\). Hence independent source sample sizes \(n_E,n_O\) give gradient variance
\[
 \frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.
\]
No latent causal restriction, completeness condition, or equality with \(\tau\) is used or asserted.
