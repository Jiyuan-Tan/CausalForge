For \(d\in\mathcal D\), write
\[
 C_d=c_{E,d}+c_{O,d},
 \qquad
 \xi_d=(1,X^\top,Z_{M_d}^\top)^\top.
\]
The transported CLW-admissible domain \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\) is the set of laws \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) for which, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\), \(X\) and \(Z_{M_d}\) have fixed finite-dimensional real encodings,
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y^2+\|\xi_d\|^2\mid G=O]<\infty,
 \qquad
 \Gamma_d(P):=\mathbb E_P[\xi_d\xi_d^\top\mid G=O]
\]
is nonsingular.  On this domain define
\[
 \beta_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)
 =\Gamma_d(P)^{-1}\mathbb E_P[\xi_dY\mid G=O],
 \qquad
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)
 =\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \{Y-\xi_d^\top\beta_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\}^2\mid G=O
 \right].                                                     \tag{1}
\]
The support chosen in the Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm rule is transported only to \(M_d\); the sequential role assignment \(r_d\) is fixed ex ante by this note.  Under the linear per-unit cost specialization \(c(M_d,n)=nC_d\), define the continuous feasible size and population criterion
\[
 n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)=\frac{B}{C_d},
 \qquad
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
 =\frac{r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)}
 =\frac{C_dr_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{B},                       \tag{2}
\]
and set the criterion to \(+\infty\) off \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\).  The continuous population selector retains ties:
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B).   \tag{3}
\]
When \(\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor\ge1\), the exact integer-budget criterion is
\[
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 =\frac{r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)}{\lfloor B/C_d\rfloor};       \tag{4}
\]
it is \(+\infty\) when the floor count is zero or the law is off the transported domain, and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 =\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B).                \tag{5}
\]
Finally, on the transported domain define the arm-normalized bridge-design loss
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 \mathcal V_d^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.             \tag{6}
\]
Equations (1)--(6) define only the transported population rule and its exact floor-budget counterpart.  They do not identify \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\) with the unrestricted conditional-expectation risks \(R_d(P)\) or \(R_d^A(P)\).
