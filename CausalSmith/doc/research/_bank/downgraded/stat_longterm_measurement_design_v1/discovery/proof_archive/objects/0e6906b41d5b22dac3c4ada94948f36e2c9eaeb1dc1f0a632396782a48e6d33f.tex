For binary treatment, the multiplier printed in the IKM score equals \((1-p_{g,1})\) times the arm-normalized multiplier.  Indeed, for \(A=1\),
\[
\frac{A-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}}=\frac{1-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}},
\]
whereas for \(A=0\),
\[
\frac{A-p_{g,1}}{p_{g,1}}=-1
=(1-p_{g,1})\left(-\frac1{1-p_{g,1}}\right).
\]
The remaining residual factors coincide source by source.  Therefore
\[
\psi_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})\phi_{g,d},\qquad
V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{g,1})^2V_{g,d}.                       \tag{1}
\]
At every center \(P_\epsilon\), treatment is balanced in both sources.  Equation (1) proves the four score and variance identities, and
\[
\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
=\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}\right)^2
=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                               \tag{2}
\]
The common positive factor preserves the design ordering and cancels from the ratio.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) thus gives
\[
D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},\qquad
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).
\]

Fix \(\epsilon\).  The finite-cell inversion in the binary stability lemma constructs unique square-integrable outcome and selection bridges throughout a relative neighborhood.  In this constrained model \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), so \(\tau=0\); the same inversion and source invariance give \(\mu_{d,1}=\mu_{d,0}=\mathbb E(U)\) for each design, so both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\).  Treatment probabilities, bridge coefficients, prediction risks, source moments in (1), and both profiled values are continuous functions of the strictly positive cell vector.  Regularity and the predictive and normalized-efficiency inequalities are strict at the center, and the ratio denominator is positive.  Intersecting the finitely many neighborhoods preserving these properties gives \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\); shrinking once more by continuity gives
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
>\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Finally, the binary construction verifies causal identification, unique square-integrable bridges, operator bijectivity, finite moments, and the interior source-ratio conditions.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) therefore applies design by design and identifies the IKM-normalized profile on this neighborhood as the profiled local causal semiparametric efficiency bound.
