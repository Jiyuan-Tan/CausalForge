Let \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\).  By Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}common\text{-}identification\text{-}class}\),
\[
 \theta_d(P)=\tau\qquad\text{for every }d\in\mathcal D.
\]
In particular, \(\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)=\tau\) for every pilot realization.  Therefore the studentized statistic in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}postselection\text{-}wald}\) is exactly
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)}{\widehat s_B}
\]
on \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}\), with the same zero extension on its complement.  The theorem's normal limit consequently gives the first conclusion.  Likewise, on \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}\), its coverage event \(\{\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\in I_B\}\) equals \(\{\tau\in I_B\}\); the complement of \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}\) has probability tending to zero.  Hence the coverage conclusion of that theorem gives
\[
 P\{\tau\in I_B\}\longrightarrow1-\eta.
\]
