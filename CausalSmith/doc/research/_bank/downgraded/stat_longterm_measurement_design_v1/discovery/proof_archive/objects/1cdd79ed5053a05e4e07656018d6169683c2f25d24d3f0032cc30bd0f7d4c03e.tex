The actionable population criterion is the design-value gap \(\Delta_P(d,d')\), computed from the two source-score variances and known costs. The CLW affine predictor can fit the long-term outcome extremely well because the late proxy is strong, while the same design's weak early proxy makes the selection bridge unstable; predictive fit therefore misses the information bottleneck that governs the observational correction. The counterexample is not an identification failure: every admissible design is regular and all designwise bridge contrasts equal the common causal target on the witness region.
