The long-term bridge and surrogate-efficiency literatures treat the measured proxy system and source ratio as fixed.  Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm optimize affine predictive residual variance and sample size in a randomized-trial collection problem, but neither that work nor the fixed-system bridge literature supplies an efficiency guarantee for transporting this objective to costly sequential proxy designs, an exact integer-budget comparison, or an analysis of its finite-catalogue empirical least-squares minimizer under persistent confounding.
