Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). Conditional on the independent full-catalogue pilot, suppose the purchased experimental and observational samples are mutually independent and independent and identically distributed within source. Use the selected-design-only estimator, score moments, total event, variance estimator, statistic, and interval of Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}selected\text{-}wald\text{-}variance}\). Impose, as explicit estimator-level conditions, Assumptions~\(\mathrm{ass{:}uniform\text{-}outcome\text{-}weak\text{-}rate}\)--\(\mathrm{ass{:}uniform\text{-}bridge\text{-}product\text{-}rate}\), the foldwise multiplier condition \(\mathrm{ass{:}postselection\text{-}multiplier\text{-}condition}\), the full-catalogue pilot variance conditions, and the selected-design main-study variance conditions. Let \(m\to\infty\), \(B\to\infty\), and \(\underline v_m=o(1)\). Then
\[
 P(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}})\longrightarrow1,
\]
\[
 s_{0,B}^2-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
 \qquad
 \widehat s_B^2-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
\]
and the selected observable contrast obeys
\[
 \mathsf T_B=
 \frac{\sqrt B\{\widehat\tau_B-\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\}}{\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1),
 \qquad
 P\{\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\in I_B\}\longrightarrow1-\eta.
\]
All nuisance-rate, multiplier, and variance-consistency requirements are maintained estimator conditions of this theorem; they are not conclusions of the observable law class or of the cited fixed-design theory.
