Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\).  By Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}common\text{-}identification\text{-}class}\),
\[
 \theta_d(P)=\tau
 \qquad\text{for every }d\in\mathcal D. \tag{1}
\]
Since the pilot selector satisfies \(\widehat d_m\in\mathcal D\) for every realization, (1) implies the pathwise identity
\[
 \theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)=\tau. \tag{2}
\]
Under the sampling and maintained estimator-level conditions in the proposition, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}postselection\text{-}wald}\) gives
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B\{\widehat\tau_B-\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\}}
 {\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1),
 \qquad
 P\{\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\in I_B\}\longrightarrow1-\eta, \tag{3}
\]
where the studentized statistic in (3) uses the theorem's declared zero extension off \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{tot}}\).  Substitution of (2) into the first conclusion of (3) yields
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)}{\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1),
\]
with exactly the same zero extension.  Moreover, (2) makes the events \(\{\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\in I_B\}\) and \(\{\tau\in I_B\}\) identical.  Substitution into the second conclusion of (3) therefore gives
\[
 P\{\tau\in I_B\}\longrightarrow1-\eta.
\]
This proves both assertions.
