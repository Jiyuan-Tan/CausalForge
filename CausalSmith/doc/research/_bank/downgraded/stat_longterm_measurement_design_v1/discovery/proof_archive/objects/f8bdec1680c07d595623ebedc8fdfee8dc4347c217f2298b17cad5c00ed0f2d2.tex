Under the stated budget identity and allocation,
\[
 \alpha=\frac{c_{E,d}n_E}{B},\qquad
 1-\alpha=\frac{B-c_{E,d}n_E}{B}=\frac{c_{O,d}n_O}{B}.
\]
Therefore the definition of the design-value profile gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \frac1B\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)
 &=\frac1B\left\{
 \frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{c_{E,d}n_E/B}
 +\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{c_{O,d}n_O/B}\right\}\\
 &=\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The designwise-gradient lemma identifies the last expression as the fixed-system local semiparametric efficiency bound.  This is an exact algebraic reduction; no asymptotic floor approximation is involved.
