Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang establish identification, fixed-system asymptotic normality, and local efficiency for a given short-term-outcome system and sampling ratio. The surrogate-index literature likewise compares estimators for a prespecified surrogate system. Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm motivate prediction-based covariate choice in a different experimental setting; the paired-source cost-weighted objective used here is this paper's transplant, not a guarantee attributed to their procedure. Work on optimal measurement or two-phase design either uses linear front-door or Gaussian instrumental-variable structure or holds the measured variable set fixed. The present contribution instead makes the proxy subset, sequential role assignment, and source allocation joint design variables and proves that predictive fit has no finite uniform efficiency approximation guarantee, even when its selected design bears any prescribed finite cost disadvantage.
