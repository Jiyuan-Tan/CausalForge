Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  Since \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) is finite and nonempty.  Positive costs, nondegenerate source moments, and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) give, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2<\infty. \tag{1}
\]
Substitution of (1) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the displayed identity.  Its numerator minimizes the same positive collection over the subset \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\), whereas its denominator minimizes over all of \(\mathcal D\).  The ratio is therefore at least one and finite.

For the actual best-tie rule, a constant \(C\) is valid exactly when
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P).       \tag{2}
\]
The denominator is strictly positive by (1), so division shows that (2) holds if and only if \(C\ge\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  This proves sharpness of the best-tie constant.

For the pairwise claim, set
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)=
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.         \tag{3}
\]
The indexing set contains every diagonal pair and is finite.  All denominators are positive, so (3) is finite.  If every diagnostic-order implication holds with constant \(C\), applying it to each indexed pair gives \(C\ge C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)\).  Conversely, the definition of the maximum gives the implication for every indexed pair with \(C=C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)\).  Thus (3) is exactly the least pairwise constant.

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves that for every prescribed \(\chi>1\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty, \tag{4}
\]
although every witness design is regular and commonly identifying and the uniquely diagnostic-selected design has paired cost \(5\chi\), compared with five for the efficient design.  Its paired-cost ratio is therefore exactly \(\chi\).

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) strengthens (4) by optimizing the exact center ratio over \(\chi\).  It gives
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3}             \tag{5}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                    \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus even the cost-disadvantage sequence that minimizes this counterexample leaves the ratio asymptotic to \(1/(726\epsilon^2)\).  Equations (4)--(6) rule out a primitive uniform diagnostic-efficiency guarantee.  Finiteness of the catalogue yields the exact lawwise enumeration (1)--(3), but nothing stronger.
