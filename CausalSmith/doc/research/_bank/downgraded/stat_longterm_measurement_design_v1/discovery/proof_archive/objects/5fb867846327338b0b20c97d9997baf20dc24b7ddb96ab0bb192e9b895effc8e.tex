Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). For either positive design-value profile
\[
 w_d=\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 w_d=\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P),
\]
the least constant making every treatment-aware prediction-order implication
\[
 R_d^A(P)\le R_{d'}^A(P)\Longrightarrow w_d\le Cw_{d'}
 \qquad(d,d'\in\mathcal D)
\]
valid is
\[
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,R_d^A(P)\le R_{d'}^A(P)}}
 \frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}. \tag{1}
\]
For the arm-normalized profile, the smallest constant for the actual best-tie selector is \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)\); for the IKM-normalized profile it is \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)\). Each is at most (1) for its profile and is finite at a fixed regular law. Consequently, a uniform constant over a nonempty law class exists exactly when the supremum of the corresponding best-tie ratio is finite.

If a numerical realization of \(\mathsf H_{\mathrm{comp}}\) represents \(R_d^A(P)\) by a positive quadratic form \(Q_{R,d}^A\{x_d(P)\}\) and represents the arm-normalized value by the profiled source-score form \(F_d\{x_d(P)\}\), then the two-sided envelope
\[
 0<\Lambda_-^A(P):=\min_d\inf_x\frac{F_d(x)}{Q_{R,d}^A(x)}
 \le
 \max_d\sup_x\frac{F_d(x)}{Q_{R,d}^A(x)}=:\Lambda_+^A(P)<\infty
\]
implies
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)
 \le\max_{\substack{d,d':\,R_d^A(P)\le R_{d'}^A(P)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}
 \le\frac{\Lambda_+^A(P)}{\Lambda_-^A(P)}. \tag{2}
\]
As for the unadjusted rule, this envelope is generally conservative and the sharp constant is not generally a largest generalized eigenvalue, because profiling the two source terms produces a nonquadratic numerator and equality additionally requires saturation by law-realized, treatment-aware-risk-ordered directions.
