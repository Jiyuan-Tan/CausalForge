Put \(\alpha=c_{E,d}n_E/B\). Since \(B=c_{E,d}n_E+c_{O,d}n_O\),
\[
1-\alpha=\frac{c_{O,d}n_O}{B}.
\]
Substitution into the profile gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac1B\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P)
&=\frac1B\left\{
\frac{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}{c_{E,d}n_E/B}
+\frac{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}{c_{O,d}n_O/B}\right\}\\
&=\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}designwise\text{-}gradient}\) supplies the fixed-system pathwise-gradient interpretation for the observed bridge contrast. If the additional causal equality and canonical-gradient premises in the proposition hold, the defining property of a canonical gradient makes this same variance the local semiparametric lower bound for \(\tau\). Without them, the algebra and bridge-functional gradient identity remain valid but no causal-efficiency conclusion follows.
