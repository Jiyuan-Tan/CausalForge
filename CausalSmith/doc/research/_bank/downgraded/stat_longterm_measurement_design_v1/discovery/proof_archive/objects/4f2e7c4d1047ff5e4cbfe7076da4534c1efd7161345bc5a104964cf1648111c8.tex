The impossibility theorem concerns standalone minimization of raw population prediction risk over the declared finite design family. It does not attribute that rule to a published paper and does not refute every predictive proxy-selection method. The cost-weighted paired-source diagnostic is a secondary calculation. Universal lawwise enumeration is over \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\); the binary nonuniformity witnesses lie in regular common-identification causal subclasses. Post-selection inference targets the selected observed bridge contrast on the observable class and targets the long-term average treatment effect only on \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\). Broader nonparametric learner attainability is left open.
