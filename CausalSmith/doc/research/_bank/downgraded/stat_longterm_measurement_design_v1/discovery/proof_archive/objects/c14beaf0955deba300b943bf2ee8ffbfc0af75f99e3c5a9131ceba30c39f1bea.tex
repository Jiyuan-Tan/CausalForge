Let \(m\to\infty\) and \(B\to\infty\), and let \(\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\) denote the pilot-selected observed-data bridge contrast identified in the narrowed designwise-gradient lemma.  Under all rate, variance-estimation, and independence hypotheses displayed in the current statement, form \(N_{E,B},N_{O,B}\) and \(\widehat s_B^2=B\{\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}/N_{E,B}+\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}/N_{O,B}\}\).  Then
\[
 B\left\{\frac{V_{E,\widehat d_m}}{N_{E,B}}+
 \frac{V_{O,\widehat d_m}}{N_{O,B}}\right\}
 -\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
 \qquad
 \widehat s_B^2-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
\]
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B\{\widehat\tau_B-\theta_{\widehat d_m}(P)\}}
 {\widehat s_B}\rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1),
\]
and the corresponding Wald interval has coverage tending to \(1-\eta\) for the pilot-selected bridge contrast.  On any subclass on which every admissible bridge contrast equals \(\tau\), these conclusions are the asserted causal limit law and coverage result for \(\tau\).
