Partition the independently funded full-catalogue pilot indices deterministically into two folds \(I_{m,1}\) and \(I_{m,2}\), whose sizes differ by at most one.  For \(d\in\mathcal D\), write \(C_{d,i}=(A_i,X_i,Z_{M_d,i})\).  For \(\ell\in\{1,2\}\) and a cell \(c\), define the fold-external saturated observational cell mean
\[
 \widehat m_{d,m}^{(-\ell)}(c)=
 \frac{\sum_{j\in I_{m,3-\ell}}\mathbf 1\{G_j=O,C_{d,j}=c\}Y_j}
 {\sum_{j\in I_{m,3-\ell}}\mathbf 1\{G_j=O,C_{d,j}=c\}},
\]
with value zero when the denominator is zero.  Let \(N_{O,m}=\sum_{i=1}^m\mathbf1\{G_i=O\}\), and define
\[
 \widehat R_{d,m}^A=
 \frac1{N_{O,m}}\sum_{\ell=1}^2\sum_{i\in I_{m,\ell}}
 \mathbf1\{G_i=O\}
 \left\{Y_i-\widehat m_{d,m}^{(-\ell)}(C_{d,i})\right\}^2,
\]
with value zero when \(N_{O,m}=0\).  The feasible treatment-aware empirical selector is the deterministic-tie-broken rule
\[
 \widehat d_m^A\in\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}\widehat R_{d,m}^A.
\]
The construction is used below only on the binary finite-cell family, where every displayed cell catalogue is finite.
