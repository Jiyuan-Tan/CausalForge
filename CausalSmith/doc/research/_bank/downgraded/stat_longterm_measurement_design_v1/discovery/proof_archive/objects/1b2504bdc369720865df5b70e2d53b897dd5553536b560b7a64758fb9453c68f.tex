Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  For each \(d\in\mathcal D\) and \(a\in\{0,1\}\), write \(H_{d,a}=(S_{3d},S_{2d},X)\) and \(L_{d,a}=(S_{2d},S_{1d},X)\) on the arm \(A=a\).  Suppose there is a fixed integer \(r_{d,a}\), independent of \(m\) and \(B\), and known basis vectors \(b_{h,d,a}:H_{d,a}\to\mathbb R^{r_{d,a}}\) and \(b_{q,d,a}:L_{d,a}\to\mathbb R^{r_{d,a}}\) satisfying the following primitive conditions uniformly over the finite set of pairs \((d,a)\):

\(\mathrm{(i)}\) There are coefficient vectors \(\beta_{h,d,a}\) and \(\beta_{q,d,a}\) such that
\[
 h_d(H_{d,a},a)=b_{h,d,a}(H_{d,a})^\top\beta_{h,d,a},
 \qquad
 q_d(L_{d,a},a)=b_{q,d,a}(L_{d,a})^\top\beta_{q,d,a}.
 \tag{1}
\]

\(\mathrm{(ii)}\) For some constants \(\underline p>0\) and \(0<\underline\lambda\le\overline\lambda<\infty\), \(p_{g,a}\ge\underline p\) for both sources and arms, the square matrix
\[
 M_{d,a}=\mathbb E_P\!left[
 b_{q,d,a}(L_{d,a})b_{h,d,a}(H_{d,a})^\top
 \mid A=a,G=O\right]
 \tag{2}
\]
has smallest singular value at least \(\underline\lambda\), and every source-and-arm Gram matrix of either basis has all its eigenvalues in \([\underline\lambda,\overline\lambda]\).

\(\mathrm{(iii)}\) For some \(\delta>0\) and \(C<\infty\), both basis vectors have Euclidean norm at most \(C\) almost surely in either source and
\[
 \max_{a\in\{0,1\}}
 \mathbb E_P[|Y|^{4+\delta}\mid A=a,G=O]\le C.
 \tag{3}
\]

\(\mathrm{(iv)}\) The pilot source-arm counts, the main-study source-arm counts, and every observational training and evaluation fold-arm count are, with probability tending to one, bounded above and below by positive constant multiples of \(m\) for the pilot and of \(B\) for the main study; \(L\) is fixed.

For an observational training fold, let \(\mathbb P_{O,-\ell,a}\) denote its empirical arm mean and let \(\mathbb P_{E,a}\) denote the experimental empirical arm mean.  Define the fixed-dimensional cross-fitted sample-moment estimators by
\[
 \widehat M_{d,a}^{(-\ell)}
 =\mathbb P_{O,-\ell,a}[b_{q,d,a}b_{h,d,a}^\top],
\]
\[
 \widehat\beta_{h,d,a}^{(-\ell)}
 =\{\widehat M_{d,a}^{(-\ell)}\}^{-1}
   \mathbb P_{O,-\ell,a}[b_{q,d,a}Y],
 \qquad
 \widehat\beta_{q,d,a}^{(-\ell)}
 =\{\widehat M_{d,a}^{(-\ell)\top}\}^{-1}
   \mathbb P_{E,a}[b_{h,d,a}],                              \tag{4}
\]
on the nonsingularity event, assigning zero coefficients otherwise, and set
\[
 \widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}=b_{h,d,a}^\top
 \widehat\beta_{h,d,a}^{(-\ell)},
 \qquad
 \widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}=b_{q,d,a}^\top
 \widehat\beta_{q,d,a}^{(-\ell)}                           \tag{5}
\]
on arm \(a\).  Use the same construction foldwise in the full-catalogue pilot and form the empirical plug-in source-score second moments in Definitions~\(\mathrm{def{:}pilot\text{-}selector}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}selected\text{-}wald\text{-}variance}\).

Then, uniformly over the finite catalogue and the fixed folds,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \max_{d,\ell}\bigl(&
 \|\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d\|_{2,B}^{\circ}
 +\|T_d(\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d)\|_{2,B}^{\circ}\\
 &+\|\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d\|_{2,B}^{\circ}
 +\|T_d^\star(\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d)\|_{2,B}^{\circ}
 \bigr)=O_P(B^{-1/2}),                                     \tag{6}
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
 \min\{\delta_{h,B}\rho_{q,B},\rho_{h,B}\delta_{q,B}\}
 =O_P(B^{-1})=o_P(B^{-1/2})                                \tag{7}
\]
when all four deterministic bridge-rate envelopes are chosen proportional to \(B^{-1/2}\).  Writing \(\Delta h_{B,\ell}=\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d\) and \(\Delta q_{B,\ell}=\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\max_{d,\ell}\bigl(&
\|\mathbf1\{G=O\}\Delta q_{B,\ell}(Y-h_d)\|_{2,B}^{\circ}
+\|\mathbf1\{G=O\}q_d\Delta h_{B,\ell}\|_{2,B}^{\circ}\\
&+\|\mathbf1\{G=O\}\Delta q_{B,\ell}\Delta h_{B,\ell}\|_{2,B}^{\circ}
\bigr)=O_P(B^{-1/2})=o_P(1).                               \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]
Finally,
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\{|widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
 |widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\}=O_P(m^{-1/2}),               \tag{9}
\]
and
\[
 \max_{g\in\{E,O\}}
 |\widetilde V_{g,\widehat d_m,B}-V_{g,\widehat d_m}|
 =O_P(B^{-1/2}).                                           \tag{10}
\]
Thus this explicit fixed-dimensional, non-growing-basis construction verifies the four uniform weak and strong bridge rates, the product rate, the multiplier condition, and the pilot and selected-main-study variance conditions at root sample-size rates on the stated finite-dimensional regime.  It does not assert these conclusions on all of \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).
