The design-value gap is an observable, computable selection criterion and the independent full-catalogue pilot yields oracle regret and feasible allocation. The negative side now covers both \(R_d(P)\) and \(R_d^A(P)\): both uniquely select \(d_{\mathrm p}\), whereas \(d_{\mathrm e}\) is arm-normalized and IKM-normalized profile-optimal. Their fixed-law constants are exactly enumerable but have no finite uniform guarantee; the sharp center envelope diverges as \(1/(726\epsilon^2)\). A held-out saturated finite-cell estimator of \(R_d^A(P)\) selects \(d_{\mathrm p}\) with probability tending to one along fixed-\(\chi\) triangular paths, so the impossibility is operational rather than an oracle-only artifact.
