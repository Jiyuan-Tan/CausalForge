Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and write \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\) for the binary center in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}witness}\), with the dependence on \(\chi\) made explicit. Then \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), both designwise bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), and \(D^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\). The prediction-selected design is costlier in each source:
\[
 c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}-c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}
 =\frac{5(\chi-1)}2>0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 V_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}=\frac{40\chi}{10\chi-3},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right),
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm e}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})=\frac{3872}{25},
\]
and the exact center ratio is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac{25}{3872}
 \left\{
 \sqrt{\frac{20\chi(5\chi-1)}{10\chi-3}}
 +\sqrt{\frac{32(5\chi+1)}{75(10\chi-3)}
 \left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right)}
 \right\}^{2}.                                             \tag{1}
\end{aligned}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 \ge
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}
 \left(\epsilon^{-2}+14\right)
 >\frac{\epsilon^{-2}+14}{726},                            \tag{2}
\]
with the identical inequalities for \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\), and
\[
 \inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 >\frac{\epsilon^{-2}+14}{726},                            \tag{3}
\]
\[
 \lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}\epsilon^2
 \inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}\epsilon^2
 \inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}.                                             \tag{4}
\]
