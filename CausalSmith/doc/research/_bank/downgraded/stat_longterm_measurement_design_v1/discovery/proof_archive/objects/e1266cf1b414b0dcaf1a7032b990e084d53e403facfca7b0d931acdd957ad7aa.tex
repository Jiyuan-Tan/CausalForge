Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\). By Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), every \(V_{E,d}\) and \(V_{O,d}\) is finite and strictly positive; all costs are finite and strictly positive by construction. The budget-profile theorem therefore gives, for every \(d\in\mathcal D\),
\[
 v_d:=\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2
 \in(0,\infty). \tag{1}
\]
The family \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty, as is its predictive optimizer subset. Hence both minima in the displayed ratio exist and are strictly positive and finite. Since \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\subseteq\mathcal D\),
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}v_d
 \ge \min_{d\in\mathcal D}v_d,
\]
which proves the diagnostic identity and its bounds.

Let a best-tie predictive rule be allowed to choose the predictive minimizer having the smallest \(v_d\). Its performance inequality with constant \(C\) is exactly
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}v_d
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}v_{d'}.
\]
Division by the positive denominator shows that this holds if and only if \(C\ge\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\). Thus the smallest best-tie constant is the stated ratio.

For the pairwise claim, define the finite nonempty set
\[
 \mathcal O_P=\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)\}.
\]
It contains every diagonal pair. The implication for all \((d,d')\in\mathcal O_P\) is equivalent, after division by \(v_{d'}>0\), to
\[
 C(P)\ge\max_{(d,d')\in\mathcal O_P}\frac{v_d}{v_{d'}}.
\]
Therefore the displayed maximum is the least valid pairwise constant, and finiteness follows from finite enumeration.

Finally, Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) proves for every prescribed finite \(\chi>1\) that the corresponding region \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) contains all center laws and that \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)\to\infty\) as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\), while the prediction-selected center design has paired-cost ratio \(\chi\). Consequently
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty.
\]
This proves that the fixed-law enumeration supplies no finite uniform primitive guarantee.
