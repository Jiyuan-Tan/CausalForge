Fix \(\chi>1\) and a binary center \(P_\epsilon\).  Treatment is balanced in both sources, so
\[
 p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=\frac12.                                 \tag{1}
\]
The definitions of the normalized scores and moments give, for either design,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},            \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d}.                  \tag{3}
\]
Consequently
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 &=\left\{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}
 +\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}\right\}^2\\
 &=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                   \tag{4}
\end{aligned}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) shows that \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes the arm-normalized profile, while \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is uniquely diagnostic-selected.  The common factor in (4) preserves the optimizer and cancels from the ratio, proving
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).                     \tag{5}
\]

For the neighborhood assertion, first restrict to the radius supplied by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\).  Every law there is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and \(d_{\mathrm p}\) remains uniquely diagnostic-selected.  Within the finite-cell parameterization, treatment probabilities, bridge coefficients, arm-normalized moments, and therefore
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}             \tag{6}
\]
are continuous.  Indeed, the bridge coefficients are inverses of finite nonsingular conditional-expectation matrices throughout a sufficiently small regular neighborhood.  The normalized profiled values are continuous and strictly positive.  The center optimizer ordering in (4) is strict, and the center ratio is positive.  Shrinking the radius therefore preserves \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\) and ensures
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Call the resulting positive radius \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\).

On this neighborhood, regularity and common identification have already been verified.  Under the additional fixed-system local-model, unique-bridge, bijectivity, finite-moment, and positive source-ratio premises stated in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\), that lemma identifies the IKM-normalized fixed-allocation variance as the local causal efficiency lower bound.  Profiling that explicit two-source bound over the interior budget share by the square identity in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) gives \(\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)\), proving the final conditional interpretation.
