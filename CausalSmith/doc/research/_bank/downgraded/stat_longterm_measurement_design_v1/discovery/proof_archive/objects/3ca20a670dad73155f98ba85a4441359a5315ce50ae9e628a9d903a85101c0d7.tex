For both the generic unadjusted population prediction-risk rule and the generic treatment-aware observational prediction-risk rule, the following impossibility holds.  For every prescribed finite cost-disadvantage factor \(\chi>1\), the unequal-cost binary witness has paired costs \(5\chi\) for \(d_{\mathrm p}\) and \(5\) for \(d_{\mathrm e}\).  For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), the center law \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) is regular for both designs, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\), and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P_\epsilon)=D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
The strict risk calculations are
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{3}{10\chi}\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac3{10}<\frac35=R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon),
\]
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}^A(P_\epsilon)<\frac3{10}
 <\frac{585}{1024}=R_{d_{\mathrm e}}^A(P_\epsilon),
\]
and the secondary paired-cost diagnostic obeys
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3=B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon;B).
\]
Furthermore,
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}+O_\chi(1),
 \qquad
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d},
\]
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon),
\]
and all four center ratios coincide:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 &=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)\\
 &=\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}+O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}).
\end{aligned}
\]
Across the center family, every minimizing cost-disadvantage factor satisfies
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},
\]
and, simultaneously for the four ratios,
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}+o(\epsilon^{2/3}).       \tag{1}
\]
Every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), both prediction selectors equal \(\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), and \(D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\).  Moreover,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\infty.
\]
On \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\), every design remains regular and identifies \(\tau\), both prediction selectors equal \(\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty.
\]
For each \(\chi>1\), the center cost vector belongs to \(\mathcal C_\kappa\), has paired-cost ratio exactly \(\chi\), and has a relatively open cost neighborhood on which \(d_{\mathrm p}\) remains strictly more expensive and uniquely selected by both prediction rules for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\), while both arm-normalized and balanced-center IKM-normalized ratios diverge.  Thus neither standard population squared-risk rule has a finite uniform source-score guarantee.  No proxy-selection workflow is attributed to a published paper, and no claim is made about every predictive method.  The IKM-normalized profile has its absolute causal-efficiency interpretation only under the separately stated fixed-system premises; the arm-normalized profile is a source-score design value on the full regular class and a gradient design value only on bridge-differentiable submodels.
