Positive costs and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\) imply \(a_d,b_d>0\). For \(0<\alpha<1\), direct algebra gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 F_d(\alpha)-(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2
 &=\frac{\{(1-\alpha)\sqrt{a_d}-\alpha\sqrt{b_d}\}^2}
 {\alpha(1-\alpha)}\\
 &=\frac{(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2
 (\alpha-\alpha_d^*(P))^2}{\alpha(1-\alpha)}.         \tag{5}
\end{aligned}
\]
The first equality follows by expanding the square. For the second, use
\[
 (1-\alpha)\sqrt{a_d}-\alpha\sqrt{b_d}
 =\sqrt{a_d}-\alpha(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})
 =(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})(\alpha_d^*(P)-\alpha).
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) identifies the minimum as \(\mathcal V_d^*(P)=(\sqrt{a_d}+\sqrt{b_d})^2\). Substitution of \(\alpha=\widehat\alpha_{d,m}\) in (5) proves (1).

Put
\[
 x_E=\frac{\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B}{c_{E,d}},
 \qquad
 x_O=\frac{(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B}{c_{O,d}}.
\]
On the positive-count event, subtracting the continuous expression source by source in (2) gives the exact identity
\[
 \mathcal R_{\mathrm{floor},d}
 =BV_{E,d}\left(\frac1{\lfloor x_E\rfloor}-\frac1{x_E}\right)
 +BV_{O,d}\left(\frac1{\lfloor x_O\rfloor}-\frac1{x_O}\right). \tag{6}
\]
Since \(\lfloor x\rfloor\le x\), both summands are nonnegative. Adding (1) and (2) proves (3).

If \(x>1\), then \(\lfloor x\rfloor>x-1>0\), and therefore
\[
 0\le\frac1{\lfloor x\rfloor}-\frac1x
 =\frac{x-\lfloor x\rfloor}{x\lfloor x\rfloor}
 <\frac1{x(x-1)}.                                      \tag{7}
\]
Applying (7) to \(x_E\) and \(x_O\) in (6) gives
\[
 BV_{E,d}\frac1{x_E(x_E-1)}
 =\frac{V_{E,d}c_{E,d}^2}
 {\widehat\alpha_{d,m}(\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B-c_{E,d})}
\]
and
\[
 BV_{O,d}\frac1{x_O(x_O-1)}
 =\frac{V_{O,d}c_{O,d}^2}
 {(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})
 \{(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B-c_{O,d}\}}.
\]
Summing these strict bounds proves (4). The total convention outside the positive-count event is exactly the convention stated in the lemma and does not alter any of the pointwise identities on that event.
