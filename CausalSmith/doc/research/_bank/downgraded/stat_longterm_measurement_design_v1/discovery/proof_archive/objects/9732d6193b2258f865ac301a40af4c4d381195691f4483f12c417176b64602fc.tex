For the generic raw population prediction-risk rule, the following impossibility holds. For every prescribed finite cost-disadvantage factor \(\chi>1\), the generalized unequal-cost binary witness has paired costs \(5\chi\) for \(d_{\mathrm p}\) and \(5\) for \(d_{\mathrm e}\). For every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), the center law \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) is regular for both designs and both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau=0\). Nevertheless, \(d_{\mathrm p}\) uniquely minimizes raw prediction risk, while \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes both the arm-normalized source-score profile and the IKM-normalized profile. More precisely,
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{3}{10\chi}\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2(1-3/(10\chi))}
 <\frac{3}{10\chi}<\frac3{10}<\frac35
 =R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon),
\]
and the secondary paired-cost diagnostic obeys
\[
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon;B)
 =\frac32\frac{1-\epsilon^2}{1-\epsilon^2y_\chi^2}
 <\frac32<3
 =B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon;B).
\]
Furthermore,
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{64}{75(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}+O_\chi(1),
 \qquad
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d},
\]
\[
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon),
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}).
\]
Across the center family, let \(\chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)\) be any cost-disadvantage factor minimizing the exact ratio for sufficiently small \(\epsilon\). Then
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{opt}}(\epsilon)
 \sim \frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},
\]
and the least-divergent sharp elbow is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})\\
 &=\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).
\end{aligned}
\]
Every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular for both designs, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty.
\]
On the relatively open subregion \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\subseteq\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\), every design remains regular and identifies \(\tau\), \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty.
\]
For each prescribed \(\chi>1\), the center cost vector belongs to \(\mathcal C_\kappa\), has paired-cost ratio exactly \(\chi\), and has a relatively open neighborhood in \(\mathcal C_\kappa\) on which \(d_{\mathrm p}\) remains strictly more expensive and uniquely raw-risk-selected for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\), while both the arm-normalized and balanced-center IKM-normalized ratios diverge along the center-law path. Thus standalone selection by raw population prediction risk has no finite uniform source-score guarantee, even when its chosen design bears any prescribed finite cost disadvantage; even the least-divergent cost-disadvantage sequence grows at order \(\epsilon^{-2/3}\), and its ratio retains the leading divergence \(1/(726\epsilon^2)\). The secondary cost-weighted diagnostic selects the same center design, but it is not the headline rule. No raw-risk proxy-selection workflow is attributed to a published paper, and no claim is made about every predictive method. The IKM-normalized profile has its absolute causal-efficiency interpretation only under the separately stated fixed-system premises; the arm-normalized profile is a source-score design value on the full regular class and a gradient design value only on bridge-differentiable submodels.
