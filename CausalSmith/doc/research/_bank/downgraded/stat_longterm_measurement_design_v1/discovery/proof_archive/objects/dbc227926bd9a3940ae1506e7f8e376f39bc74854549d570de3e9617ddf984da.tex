Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), and abbreviate
\[
 v_d=\mathcal V_d^*(P),\qquad
 \widehat v_d=\widehat{\mathcal V}_{d,m}^*,\qquad
 v_*=\min_{d\in\mathcal D}v_d.
\]
Choose any \(d^*\in D^*(P)\).  By the definition of \(\varepsilon_m\) and the minimizing property of \(\widehat d_m\),
\[
 v_{\widehat d_m}
 \le \widehat v_{\widehat d_m}+\varepsilon_m
 \le \widehat v_{d^*}+\varepsilon_m
 \le v_*+2\varepsilon_m. \tag{1}
\]
Since \(v_*\le v_{\widehat d_m}\), (1) proves
\[
 0\le v_{\widehat d_m}-v_*\le2\varepsilon_m. \tag{2}
\]
If \(\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\), the definition of \(\gamma(P)\) and (2) give
\[
 \gamma(P)\le v_{\widehat d_m}-v_*\le2\varepsilon_m. \tag{3}
\]
This proves the two deterministic assertions.  When \(D^*(P)=\mathcal D\), the event on the left of (3) is empty.  Otherwise, finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) implies \(\gamma(P)>0\).

For the stochastic assertions, set
\[
 Z_m=\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\bigl\{
 |\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
 |\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\bigr\}.
\]
Assumptions~\(\mathrm{ass{:}pilot\text{-}gradient\text{-}variance\text{-}rate}\) and \(\mathrm{ass{:}pilot\text{-}variance\text{-}rate\text{-}vanishes}\) imply
\[
 Z_m=O_P(r_{V,m})=o_P(1). \tag{4}
\]
By Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), positivity of the costs, and finiteness of \(\mathcal D\), there are constants \(0<\nu_-<\nu_+<\infty\) such that all population variance components and all costs lie between these constants.  Hence, with probability tending to one, every estimated variance pair belongs to a common compact rectangle contained in \((0,\infty)^2\).

For each \(d\), define
\[
 G_d(x,y)=\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}y}\right)^2.
\]
Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) and Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}pilot\text{-}selector}\) give
\[
 v_d=G_d(V_{E,d},V_{O,d}),
 \qquad
 \widehat v_d=G_d(\widehat V_{E,d},\widehat V_{O,d}).
\]
The gradients of the finitely many maps \(G_d\) are uniformly bounded on the common compact rectangle.  The mean-value theorem and (4) therefore yield
\[
 \varepsilon_m=\max_{d\in\mathcal D}|\widehat v_d-v_d|
 =O_P(r_{V,m})=o_P(1). \tag{5}
\]
If not every design is optimal, (3), (5), and \(\gamma(P)>0\) imply
\[
 P\{\widehat d_m\notin D^*(P)\}
 \le P\{2\varepsilon_m\ge\gamma(P)\}\longrightarrow0. \tag{6}
\]
Together with the already noted empty-event case, this proves selection consistency for the complete optimizer set.

Likewise, put
\[
 A_d(x,y)=
 \frac{\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}}
 {\sqrt{c_{E,d}x}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}y}}.
\]
Because \(\underline v_m=o(1)\), the variance truncation in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}pilot\text{-}selector}\) is inactive with probability tending to one: on the event where every variance estimate exceeds \(\nu_-/2\) and \(\underline v_m<\nu_-/2\), the truncated and untruncated formulas coincide.  The finitely many gradients of \(A_d\) are uniformly bounded on the same compact rectangle.  Consequently,
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}
 |\widehat\alpha_{d,m}-\alpha_d^*(P)|
 =O_P(r_{V,m}). \tag{7}
\]

Finally, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}second\text{-}order\text{-}allocation\text{-}loss}\) applies because its variance, rate, truncation, cost, and nondegeneracy premises are exactly (4), \(r_{V,m}=o(1)\), \(\underline v_m\downarrow0\), positivity of the finite cost family, and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\).  It gives, along every joint sequence \(m\to\infty\) and \(B\to\infty\),
\[
\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\left|B\left\{
\frac{V_{E,d}}{\lfloor\widehat\alpha_{d,m}B/c_{E,d}\rfloor}
+\frac{V_{O,d}}{\lfloor(1-\widehat\alpha_{d,m})B/c_{O,d}\rfloor}
\right\}-\mathcal V_d^*(P)\right|
=O_P(r_{V,m}^2+B^{-1})=o_P(1). \tag{8}
\]
The lemma uses precisely the stipulated zero extension on a vanishing-probability event where a floor count is zero.  Equations (2)--(8) prove every assertion.  The stochastic steps used the maintained pilot variance condition explicitly; no property of the observable law class alone was used to infer that estimator-level condition.
