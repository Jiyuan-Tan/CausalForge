The diagnostic impossibility is specific to the paired-source residual-risk objective defined in this note and does not establish a negative result for predictive proxy selection generally or for any published selector.  The operational learner theorem assumes fixed known bases, exact bridge specification, bounded regressors, uniformly nonsingular moment and Gram matrices, and finite \((4+\delta)\) outcome moments.  It does not cover growing sieves, weak singular values, approximate specification, nonunique bridges, or data-adaptive basis discovery.  Those extensions require separate inverse-problem theory.
