Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{CLW}}\).  The transported CLW population rule has the exact finite-catalogue constants
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d'}V_{E,d'}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d'}V_{O,d'}})^2}
 \in[1,\infty),                                           \tag{1}
\]
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)
 =\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 \in[1,\infty).                                           \tag{2}
\]
Each is the smallest constant for its actual best-tie selector.  For either positive profile \(w_d\), the least constant making every named-rule order implication valid is
\[
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le
 \Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)}}\frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}.         \tag{3}
\]
The same formula with \(\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}\) gives the exact integer-budget pairwise constant whenever at least one design has positive floor count.  Thus all named-rule constants are computable using at most \(|\mathcal D|^2\) comparisons after the finite list of Gram-matrix residuals and bridge profiles has been evaluated.

The two unrestricted conditional-expectation selectors remain distinct secondary rules.  On \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), their exact lawwise arm-normalized constants are \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) and \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)\); the treatment-aware IKM constant is
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}. \tag{4}
\]
For either generic rule and either positive profile, the least pairwise constant is the corresponding maximum of \(w_d/w_{d'}\) over prediction-risk-ordered pairs.

Exact lawwise enumeration does not yield a law-independent guarantee.  For every fixed \(\chi>1\), the jointly shrunk binary regions have unbounded named CLW ratios and, secondarily, unbounded generic ratios.  At every center all six ratios agree, and for any one of them
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                      \tag{5}
\]
Thus the transported CLW population linear-risk rule has no finite uniform arm-normalized or IKM-normalized bridge-design guarantee on the regular common-identification region.  This statement does not identify \(r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\) with either unrestricted risk away from the centers and does not address CLW's empirical equation~(3.6) procedure or approximate solver.
