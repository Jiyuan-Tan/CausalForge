All center assertions follow from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\).  We prove the neighborhood claims while keeping the CLW affine residual and unrestricted risk distinct away from the centers.

The center is in the relative interior of the finite-cell parameterization.  Strict cell positivity, source and treatment overlap, and nonzero determinants of the finite completeness and bridge-operator matrices are open conditions.  Matrix inversion is continuous away from zero determinant.  The constrained factorization continues to impose consistency, experimental randomization, latent observational exchangeability, source-invariant proxy kernels, source support, and conditional proxy independence.  It therefore preserves all causal and sequential restrictions on a sufficiently small relative ball.  Bridge coefficients, source-score moments, and positive profiles are rational functions of positive cell probabilities and nonsingular finite matrices, so they are continuous.  A further shrinking preserves finite positive component variances.

Common identification is structural in this constrained family.  Because \(Y(1)=Y(0)=U\), \(\tau=0\).  For each arm, the bridge equation, sequential restriction, and early-proxy completeness imply
\[
 \mathbb E_P\{h_d(S_{3d},a)\mid U,A=a,G=O\}
 =\mathbb E_P(Y\mid U,A=a,G=O)=U.
\]
Source invariance and experimental randomization then yield
\[
 \mu_{d,a}
 =\mathbb E_P\{h_d(S_{3d},a)\mid A=a,G=E\}
 =\mathbb E_P(U),
\]
independently of \(a\).  Hence \(\theta_d(P)=0=\tau\) throughout the ball.

Every entry of
\[
 \Gamma_d(P)=\mathbb E_P(\xi_d\xi_d^\top\mid G=O),
 \qquad g_d(P)=\mathbb E_P(\xi_dY\mid G=O)
\]
is continuous in the cell vector.  The full determinant remains nonzero after shrinking, and
\[
 r_d^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)=
 \mathbb E_P(Y^2\mid G=O)
 -g_d(P)^\top\Gamma_d(P)^{-1}g_d(P)                       \tag{32}
\]
is continuous.  Separately,
\[
 R_d(P)=\mathbb E_P\{\operatorname{Var}_P(Y\mid X,Z_{M_d},G=O)\mid G=O\}
\]
is a finite sum of cell masses and conditional-moment ratios with positive denominators, hence continuous without being equated to (32).

The named CLW, raw-risk, and bridge-value gaps are strict at the center.  Finiteness of \(\mathcal D\) permits one common radius preserving all three orderings, and continuity of their positive ratio denominators permits a further shrinking that proves the two one-half bounds.

The same shrinking may impose the stronger, budget-free margin
\[
 2C_{d_{\mathrm p}}r_{d_{\mathrm p}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)
 <C_{d_{\mathrm e}}r_{d_{\mathrm e}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P),   \tag{33}
\]
because at the center its left side is strictly less than \(3\), while its right side equals \(3\).  For every law in this smaller ball and every \(B\ge C_{d_{\mathrm p}}=5\chi\), the elementary floor inequalities used in the sharp-elbow proof imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm p}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)
 &<2C_{d_{\mathrm p}}r_{d_{\mathrm p}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)\\
 &<C_{d_{\mathrm e}}r_{d_{\mathrm e}}^{\mathrm{lin}}(P)
 \le B\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d_{\mathrm e}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B).
\end{aligned}                                               \tag{34}
\]
Thus the exact integer selector is \(\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\) for every \(B\ge5\chi\).  In particular, the weaker statement follows with \(B_0(P)=5\chi\).  This proves every asserted conclusion.
