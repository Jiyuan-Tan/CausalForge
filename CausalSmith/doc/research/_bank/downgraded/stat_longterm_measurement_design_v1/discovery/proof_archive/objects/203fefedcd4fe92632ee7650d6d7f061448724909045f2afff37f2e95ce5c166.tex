For each \(d\in\mathcal D\), compute the observational residual prediction risk
\[
R_d(P)=\inf_{f\in L_2(X,Z_{M_d})}\mathbb E_P[(Y-f(X,Z_{M_d}))^2\mid G=O].
\]
The generic raw population prediction-risk rule retains every tie:
\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\arg\min_{d\in\mathcal D}R_d(P).
\]
Its best-tie profiled design-value ratio is
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=
\frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)}
{\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.
\]
For comparison only, define the continuous paired-source count and the secondary cost-weighted residual-risk diagnostic
\[
n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)=\frac{B}{c_{E,d}+c_{O,d}},
\qquad
\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)
=\frac{R_d(P)}{n_{\mathrm{CLW},B}(d)}
=\frac{(c_{E,d}+c_{O,d})R_d(P)}{B}.
\]
This secondary quantity does not define \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) or \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\). The subscript records only the residual-risk-per-budget shape and does not attribute a proxy-selection workflow or guarantee to Carneiro--Lee--Wilhelm.
