Let \(m\to\infty\) and \(B\to\infty\), assume \(\underline v_m=o(1)\), and assume that \(\mathcal P_m\) is independent of the main-study observations.  Conditional on the pilot, suppose the purchased experimental and observational samples are mutually independent and consist of independent and identically distributed observations from their respective source laws.  For every \(d\in\mathcal D\), suppose both identification routes in statements 1 and 2 of Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~4 hold for the common causal target \(\tau\): their causal and completeness premises hold, their Assumptions~6 and 7 furnish square-integrable latent outcome and selection bridges \(h_d\) and \(q_d\), and the corresponding observed conditional-moment equations hold.

The nuisance-rate premise is the following sequential version of their Assumption~8.  For every deterministic sequence \(d_B\in\mathcal D\) and \(\alpha_B\in(0,1)\) satisfying
\[
 |\alpha_B-\alpha_{d_B}^*(P)|\longrightarrow0,
\]
put
\[
 e_B=\left\lfloor\frac{\alpha_BB}{c_{E,d_B}}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad
 o_B=\left\lfloor\frac{(1-\alpha_B)B}{c_{O,d_B}}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad n_B=e_B+o_B.
\]
For all sufficiently large \(B\), define the artificial two-source superpopulation \(P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ}\) by
\[
 P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ}(G=E)=\frac{e_B}{n_B},
 \qquad
 P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ}(G=O)=\frac{o_B}{n_B},
 \qquad
 P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ}(\,\cdot\mid G=g)=P(\,\cdot\mid G=g).
\]
For fixed \(L\), uniformly over \(1\le\ell\le L\), require along every such deterministic sequence that
\[
 \bigl\|T_{d_B}(\widehat h_{d_B,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_{d_B})\bigr\|_{L_2(P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ})}
 =O_P(\delta_{h,B}),
 \qquad
 \bigl\|\widehat h_{d_B,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_{d_B}\bigr\|_{L_2(P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ})}
 =O_P(\rho_{h,B}),
\]
\[
 \bigl\|T_{d_B}^{\star}(\widehat q_{d_B,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_{d_B})\bigr\|_{L_2(P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ})}
 =O_P(\delta_{q,B}),
 \qquad
 \bigl\|\widehat q_{d_B,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_{d_B}\bigr\|_{L_2(P_{B,d_B,\alpha_B}^{\circ})}
 =O_P(\rho_{q,B}).
\]
Assume that all four deterministic rates are \(o(1)\) and
\[
 \min\{\delta_{h,B}\rho_{q,B},\rho_{h,B}\delta_{q,B}\}=o(B^{-1/2}).
\]
Under the full-catalogue pilot law, suppose
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\bigl\{
 |\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
 |\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\bigr\}=O_P(r_{V,m}),
 \qquad r_{V,m}=o(1).
\]
Under the joint main-study law, suppose
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\bigl\{
 |\widetilde V_{E,d,B}-V_{E,d}|,
 |\widetilde V_{O,d,B}-V_{O,d}|\bigr\}
 =O_P(r_{\widetilde V,B}),
 \qquad r_{\widetilde V,B}=o(1).
\]

Let
\[
 \mathsf A_{m,B}=\{N_{E,B}\ge1,\ N_{O,B}\ge1\}.
\]
On \(\mathsf A_{m,B}\), define
\[
 s_{0,B}^2=B\left\{
 \frac{V_{E,\widehat d_m}}{N_{E,B}}+
 \frac{V_{O,\widehat d_m}}{N_{O,B}}\right\},
 \qquad
 \widehat s_B^2=B\left\{
 \frac{\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{E,B}}+
 \frac{\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}}{N_{O,B}}\right\},
\]
take \(\widehat s_B\) to be the positive square root, and define every ratio, studentized statistic, and interval below by these formulas.  On \(\mathsf A_{m,B}^{c}\), give all division-based quantities and \(I_B\) arbitrary fixed measurable values.  Then \(P(\mathsf A_{m,B})\to1\),
\[
 s_{0,B}^2-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
 \qquad
 \widehat s_B^2-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1),
\]
and
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B(\widehat\tau_B-\tau)}{\widehat s_B}
 \rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
For
\[
 I_B=\left[
 \widehat\tau_B-z_{1-\eta/2}\frac{\widehat s_B}{\sqrt B},
 \widehat\tau_B+z_{1-\eta/2}\frac{\widehat s_B}{\sqrt B}
 \right]
\]
on \(\mathsf A_{m,B}\), where \(z_{1-\eta/2}\) is the \((1-\eta/2)\)-quantile of the standard normal law, one has
\[
 P\{\tau\in I_B\}\longrightarrow1-\eta.
