Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) supplies, for every \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), membership \(P_\epsilon\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\), regularity of both designs, the strict predictive and arm-normalized profile orderings, and
\[
 V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}
 =\frac{448}{2025\epsilon^2}+\frac{6272}{2025},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1}). \tag{1}
\]
Its latent bridge calculation also gives \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=0=\tau\) for both designs.

By Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}stability}\), balanced treatment in both sources implies
\[
 V_{g,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{g,d},
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon). \tag{2}
\]
The common positive factor in (2) preserves the unique optimizer \(d_{\mathrm e}\) and cancels from the ratio, proving all center-law normalization claims. Because causal identification, unique latent bridges, operator bijectivity, and finite source moments were verified before using the narrowed cited fixed-system lemma, \(\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*\), not the unscaled paper profile, has the absolute local causal-efficiency interpretation.

For the original relative-open region, Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\) chooses each radius below the regularity and arm-normalized optimizer-stability radii. Thus every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular,
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
Every center law belongs to its own ball, and (1) diverges, so
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty. \tag{3}
\]

Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}ikmw\text{-}region}\) shrinks each of those balls below the radius in the normalized-stability lemma. Hence \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\subseteq\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is relatively open; every law in it is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.
\]
The construction retains every center \(P_\epsilon\), so (1)--(2) yield
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty. \tag{4}
\]

It remains to verify the open cost range. Put
\[
 C_{\mathrm p}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm p}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}},
 \qquad
 C_{\mathrm e}=c_{E,d_{\mathrm e}}+c_{O,d_{\mathrm e}}.
\]
The binary conditional-variance calculation in the stability lemma gives
\[
 R_{d_{\mathrm p}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\frac{7(1-\epsilon^2)}{16-9\epsilon^2},
 \qquad
 R_{d_{\mathrm e}}(P_\epsilon)=\frac35.
\]
Therefore the strict predictive ordering is equivalent to
\[
 \frac{C_{\mathrm p}}{C_{\mathrm e}}
 <\frac{3(16-9\epsilon^2)}{35(1-\epsilon^2)}. \tag{5}
\]
The right side tends to \(48/35\). Every fixed cap-feasible positive cost vector with
\[
 1<\frac{C_{\mathrm p}}{C_{\mathrm e}}<\frac{48}{35}
\]
therefore makes \(d_{\mathrm p}\) strictly more expensive and uniquely prediction-selected for all sufficiently small positive \(\epsilon\). For any such fixed costs, the \(d_{\mathrm e}\) arm-normalized profile stays bounded, whereas
\[
 \mathcal V_{d_{\mathrm p}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 \ge c_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}V_{O,d_{\mathrm p}}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
The paper ratio diverges. Balanced treatment still gives the common quarter-scaling in (2), independently of the positive costs, so the IKM-normalized causal-efficiency ratio diverges as well. All inequalities are strict and continuous in the positive costs, making the displayed range relatively open in the cap-feasible cost region. This completes the proof of the proposed statement.
