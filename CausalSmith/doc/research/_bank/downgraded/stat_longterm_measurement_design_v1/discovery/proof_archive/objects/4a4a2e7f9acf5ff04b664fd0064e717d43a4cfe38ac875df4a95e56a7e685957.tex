Choose the radii defining \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) after the joint shrinking in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}predictive\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\). Then every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular, identifies the common contrast \(\tau\), and satisfies

 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},

\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)

Every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) additionally satisfies \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\) and

 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=\infty. \tag{2}
\]
At every center, all four ratios agree. Writing \(P\) for the center indexed by \((\chi,\epsilon)\), for each fixed \(\chi>1\) their common ratio has the expansion

 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}),
 \qquad
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}
 =\frac1{726}+\frac5{726(10\chi-3)} >\frac1{726}, \tag{3}
\]
and the same equality holds for each of the other three ratios. Thus \(1/726\) is not the fixed-\(\chi\) leading coefficient. Rather, after optimizing the exact common ratio over \(\chi>1\), any minimizing value of \(\chi\) satisfies
\[
 \chi\sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3}, \tag{4}

and the optimized envelope obeys
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}). \tag{5}
\]
The same optimized-envelope display holds for each of the other three center ratios. The paired-cost disadvantage at the center is the prescribed finite factor \(\chi\).
