For a fixed short-term-outcome system, suppose first that the conditions of Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem 7 hold: Assumptions 1--4 and 6 define the local semiparametric model; at the law under evaluation, Assumptions 6 and 7 admit unique square-integrable outcome and selection bridges; the corresponding conditional-expectation operator \(T_d\) is bijective; and the two source-score second moments \(V_{E,d}\) and \(V_{O,d}\) are finite. Then their Theorem 7 identifies the two-source gradient as the canonical gradient and gives the local efficiency lower bound
\[
 \sigma_d^2
 =\lim (n_E+n_O)\left(\frac{V_{E,d}}{n_E}+\frac{V_{O,d}}{n_O}\right)
\]
along any sequence for which the displayed limit exists. For attainment by the doubly robust estimator, additionally impose the sampling and estimation premises of their Theorem 5: independent i.i.d. observations within the two sources, \(n_E,n_O\to\infty\), \(n_E/n_O\) converging to a number in \((0,\infty)\), both identification-and-consistency premises in statements 1 and 2 of their Theorem 4, cross-fitting, and both parts of their Assumption 8 with the nuisance limits equal to the true bridges, all weak- and strong-metric errors vanishing, and the minimum of the outcome-weak--selection-strong and outcome-strong--selection-weak error products equal to \(o((n_E+n_O)^{-1/2})\). Under these additional premises, their Theorem 5 gives asymptotic normality with variance \(\sigma_d^2\), so the estimator attains the Theorem 7 local semiparametric efficiency lower bound. Equivalently, on the original estimator scale, the leading variance is \(V_{E,d}/n_E+V_{O,d}/n_O\).
