Let \(m\to\infty\) and \(B\to\infty\), assume \(\underline v_m=o(1)\), and assume that \(\mathcal P_m\) is independent of the main-study observations used to construct \(\widehat\tau_B\). Under the joint main-study law, suppose that for fixed \(L\), uniformly over \(d\in\mathcal D\) and \(1\le\ell\le L\), \(\|\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d\|_{w,h,d}=O_P(\delta_{h,B})\), \(\|\widehat h_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-h_d\|_{2,O,h,d}=O_P(\rho_{h,B})\), \(\|\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d\|_{w,q,d}=O_P(\delta_{q,B})\), and \(\|\widehat q_{d,B}^{(-\ell)}-q_d\|_{2,O,q,d}=O_P(\rho_{q,B})\), where all four deterministic rates are \(o(1)\) and \(\min\{\delta_{h,B}\rho_{q,B},\rho_{h,B}\delta_{q,B}\}=o(B^{-1/2})\). Under the full-catalogue pilot law, suppose \(\max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\{|\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,|\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\}=O_P(r_{V,m})\) with \(r_{V,m}=o(1)\). Under the joint main-study law, suppose \(\max_{d\in\mathcal D,\,g\in\{E,O\}}|\widetilde V_{g,d,B}-V_{g,d}|=O_P(r_{\widetilde V,B})\) with \(r_{\widetilde V,B}=o(1)\). For \(\widehat d_m\) and the feasible share \(\widehat\alpha_m\), form the realized counts \(N_{E,B},N_{O,B}\) and define \(\widehat s_B^2=B\{\widetilde V_{E,\widehat d_m,B}/N_{E,B}+\widetilde V_{O,\widehat d_m,B}/N_{O,B}\}\) from the selected foldwise source-score second moments. Then \(B\{V_{E,\widehat d_m}/N_{E,B}+V_{O,\widehat d_m}/N_{O,B}\}-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1)\), \(\widehat s_B^2-\mathcal V_{\widehat d_m}^*(P)=o_P(1)\), and
\[
 \frac{\sqrt B\{\widehat\tau_B-(\mu_{\widehat d_m,1}-\mu_{\widehat d_m,0})\}}
 {\widehat s_B}\rightsquigarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
Let \(z_{1-\eta/2}\) be the \((1-\eta/2)\)-quantile of the standard normal law and define
\[
 I_B=\left[
 \widehat\tau_B-z_{1-\eta/2}\frac{\widehat s_B}{\sqrt B},
 \widehat\tau_B+z_{1-\eta/2}\frac{\widehat s_B}{\sqrt B}
 \right].
\]
Then \(P\{\mu_{\widehat d_m,1}-\mu_{\widehat d_m,0}\in I_B\}\to1-\eta\). If, in addition, \(\mu_{d,1}-\mu_{d,0}=\tau\) for every \(d\in\mathcal D\), then the same studentized limit is centered at \(\tau\) and \(P\{\tau\in I_B\}\to1-\eta\).
