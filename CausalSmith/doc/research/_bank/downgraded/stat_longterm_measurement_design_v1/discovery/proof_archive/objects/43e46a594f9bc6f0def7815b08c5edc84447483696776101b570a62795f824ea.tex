There exists a finite-support law \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\) with one admissible design such that all frozen causal, completeness, bridge, operator, support, and nondegeneracy conditions hold, but
\[
 \theta_d(P)=0,
 \qquad
 \tau=-\frac3{25}.
\]
Hence \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\not\subseteq\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{id}}\), and the observed bridge contrast cannot be relabelled as the long-term average treatment effect on the entire causal class.
