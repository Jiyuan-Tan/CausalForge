Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) and use the fixed-dimensional sample-moment construction in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}finite\text{-}dimensional\text{-}bridge\text{-}learners}\).  Suppose that there are constants \(c,C,\delta>0\), independent of \(d\in\mathcal D\) and \(a\in\{0,1\}\), such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the source and arm probabilities are bounded below by \(c\);
\item the bases are uniformly bounded by \(C\), \(\mathbb E_P[|Y|^{4+\delta}\mid A=a,G=O]\le C\), and every pilot source-arm sample size and every main-study training and evaluation fold size is proportional to its total sample size with probability tending to one;
\item the bridges are exactly specified by fixed coefficients,
\[
 h_d(H_d)=b_{h,d,A}(H_d)^{\mathsf T}\beta_{h,d,A},
 \qquad
 q_d(L_d)=b_{q,d,A}(L_d)^{\mathsf T}\beta_{q,d,A};
\]
\item for
\[
 M_{d,a}=\mathbb E_P[b_{q,d,a}(L_d)b_{h,d,a}(H_d)^{\mathsf T}mid A=a,G=O],
\]
the least singular value of every \(M_{d,a}\), and the least eigenvalue of every source-arm Gram matrix of either basis, is at least \(c\), while all corresponding largest eigenvalues are at most \(C\).
\end{enumerate}
The matrices \(M_{d,a}\) are the finite-basis representations of the designwise conditional-moment operators.  Then the cross-fitted sample-moment bridge estimators satisfy, uniformly over the finite catalogue, treatment arms, and fixed folds,
\[
 \|\widehat h_d-h_d\|_2+
 \|T_d(\widehat h_d-h_d)\|_2+
 \|\widehat q_d-q_d\|_2+
 \|T_d^\star(\widehat q_d-q_d)\|_2
 =O_P(n^{-1/2}),                                             \tag{1}
\]
where \(n=m\) for pilot fits and \(n\asymp B\) for main-study fits.  In particular, on every deterministic selected-design path used by Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}postselection\text{-}wald}\),
\[
 \max_{1\le\ell\le L}
 \min\!\left\{
 \|T_d\Delta h_\ell\|_2\|\Delta q_\ell\|_2,
 \|\Delta h_\ell\|_2\|T_d^\star\Delta q_\ell\|_2
 \right\}=O_P(B^{-1})=o_P(B^{-1/2}),                         \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\max_{1\le\ell\le L}\bigl(&
 \|\mathbf1\{G=O\}\Delta q_\ell(Y-h_d)\|_2
 +\|\mathbf1\{G=O\}q_d\Delta h_\ell\|_2\\
 &+\|\mathbf1\{G=O\}\Delta q_\ell\Delta h_\ell\|_2
 \bigr)=O_P(B^{-1/2})=o_P(1).                               \tag{3}
\end{aligned}
\]
The empirical plug-in source-score variances obey
\[
 \max_{d\in\mathcal D}\max\bigl\{
 |\widehat V_{E,d}-V_{E,d}|,
 |\widehat V_{O,d}-V_{O,d}|\bigr\}=O_P(m^{-1/2}),
 \qquad
 \max_{g\in\{E,O\}}
 |\widetilde V_{g,\widehat d_m,B}-V_{g,\widehat d_m}|
 =O_P(B^{-1/2}).                                             \tag{4}
\]
Consequently, on this fixed finite-dimensional subclass only, the four weak and strong bridge conditions hold with rates \(B^{-1/2}\), the bridge-product condition holds at rate \(B^{-1}\), the multiplier condition is \(o_P(1)\), and the pilot and main score-variance conditions hold with rates \(m^{-1/2}\) and \(B^{-1/2}\).  These are verified consequences of the primitive construction on this subclass, not properties asserted for all of \(\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).
