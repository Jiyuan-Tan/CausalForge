Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  The family \(\mathcal D\) is finite and nonempty.  Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), positive unit costs, and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) imply
\[
 0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 =\left(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+
 \sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\right)^2<\infty
 \qquad(d\in\mathcal D).                                \tag{38}
\]
The selector \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) is a nonempty subset of \(\mathcal D\).  Substitution of (38) into its defining best-tie ratio proves the displayed identity.  Since the numerator minimizes over a subset of the denominator's index set, the ratio is at least one; finiteness and strict positivity in (38) make it finite.

Let \(C\) be a constant in the actual best-tie inequality.  That inequality holds exactly when
\[
 \min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}\mathcal V_d^*(P)
 \le C\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P).
\]
Division by the positive denominator shows that the smallest such \(C\) is exactly \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).

For the pairwise assertion, put
\[
 C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)=
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,
 R_d(P)\le R_{d'}(P)}}
 \frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}.          \tag{39}
\]
The maximum is over a nonempty finite set, since every diagonal pair is included, and every ratio is finite by (38).  Any constant satisfying every stated implication must dominate each ratio in (39), hence must be at least \(C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)\).  Conversely, the definition of the maximum makes every implication valid with \(C=C_{\mathrm{pair}}(P)\).  This proves sharpness of the pairwise constant.

Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}predictive\text{-}impossibility}\) and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) show, for every fixed \(\chi>1\), that each center belongs to its defining ball in \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\), the raw-risk selector uniquely chooses the design whose paired cost is \(\chi\) times that of \(d_{\mathrm e}\), and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 >\frac{\epsilon^{-2}+14}{726}.
\]
Letting \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) proves the asserted supremum is infinite.  The sharp-elbow optimization also proves that every minimizing factor has the stated cube-root scale and that both the arm-normalized and IKM-normalized optimized envelopes have the displayed second-order expansion.  Therefore finiteness of the catalogue gives exact lawwise enumeration but no uniform primitive guarantee.
