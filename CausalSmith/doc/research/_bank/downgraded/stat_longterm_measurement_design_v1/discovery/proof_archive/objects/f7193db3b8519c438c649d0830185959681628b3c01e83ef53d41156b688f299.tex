Fix \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}\).  Finiteness and nonemptiness of \(\mathcal D\) make \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) nonempty.  Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}nondegenerate\text{-}components}\), positivity of costs, and Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\) give
\[
0<\mathcal V_d^*(P)
=\bigl(\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}}\bigr)^2<\infty
\quad(d\in\mathcal D).                                             \tag{1}
\]
Substitution of (1) into the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\) proves the displayed identity.  Its numerator is at least its denominator because \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\subseteq\mathcal D\), and both are positive and finite.

Let
\[
W_{\min}=\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P),
\qquad
W_{\mathrm{pred}}=\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)}
\mathcal V_d^*(P).
\]
The best-tie inequality \(W_{\mathrm{pred}}\le C W_{\min}\) holds exactly when \(C\ge W_{\mathrm{pred}}/W_{\min}=\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)\).  For the pairwise assertion, the finite nonempty set
\[
\mathcal A(P)=\{(d,d')\in\mathcal D^2:
\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d}(P;B)\le\Psi_{\mathrm{CLW},d'}(P;B)\}
\]
contains every diagonal pair.  All the implications hold exactly when
\[
C\ge\frac{\mathcal V_d^*(P)}{\mathcal V_{d'}^*(P)}
\quad\text{for every }(d,d')\in\mathcal A(P),
\]
so the least such constant is the maximum displayed in the theorem.

Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\) supplies center laws satisfying
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)
=\frac{49}{9801\epsilon^2}+O(\epsilon^{-1})\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}binary\text{-}region}\) includes every center \(P_\epsilon\) in \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\).  Hence
\(\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)=\infty\).  Thus the finite enumeration is exact law by law but cannot be promoted to a uniform primitive guarantee on that region.
