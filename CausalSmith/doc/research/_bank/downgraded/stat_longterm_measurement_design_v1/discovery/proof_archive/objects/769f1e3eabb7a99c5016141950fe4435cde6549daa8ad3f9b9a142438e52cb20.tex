At a center, complete randomization in source \(E\), symmetry in source \(O\), and \(P(T=1\mid G=g)=1/2\) give \(p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=1/2\).  Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ikmw\text{-}normalized\text{-}design\text{-}value}\) therefore yields, design by design,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},
\]
and hence
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d}.
\]
Substitution into the profiled formula gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)
 &=\left\{
 \sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}
 \right\}^2\\
 &=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_\epsilon).                    \tag{35}
\end{aligned}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) gives \(D^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\), \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\), and positive finite profiles.  The common factor in (35) preserves the optimizer and cancels from the best-tie ratio, proving
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).                       \tag{36}
\]

For the neighborhood assertion, start with the radius supplied by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\).  On that relative finite-cell ball, regularity, common identification, and the strict raw-risk selector identity already hold.  Source arm probabilities are finite sums of positive cells and hence continuous.  The bridge coefficients are obtained from nonsingular finite matrices, so they, the two source-score moments, and the IKM-normalized profiles are continuous functions of the cell vector.  Their denominators remain strictly positive after shrinking.  Since the IKM optimizer gap in (36) is strict, there is a smaller radius on which \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\).  Since the center ratio is positive and finite, continuity permits a further shrinking such that
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Taking the intersection of these finitely many relative neighborhoods defines a positive \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) and preserves every inherited conclusion.

Finally, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\) states that, whenever its additional fixed-system regularity, canonical-gradient, sampling-ratio, and bridge-estimation premises hold for the design under evaluation, the IKM source moments form the local causal semiparametric efficiency bound and the profiled cost allocation profiles that bound.  Applying that conditional statement designwise proves the last sentence; no such absolute interpretation is inferred merely from finite-cell continuity.
