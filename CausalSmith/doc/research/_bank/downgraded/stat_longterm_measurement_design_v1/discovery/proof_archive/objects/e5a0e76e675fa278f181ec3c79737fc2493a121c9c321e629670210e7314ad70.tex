For every regular binary-treatment law and design \(d\), the source scores printed in Imbens--Kallus--Mao--Wang Theorem~5 and the paper's arm-normalized scores obey, pointwise,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},
\]
and
\[
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})\phi_{O,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}.
\]
Consequently, if \(p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=1/2\), then for every \(0<\alpha<1\),
\[
 \mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM}}(\alpha;P)=\frac14\mathcal V_d(\alpha;P),
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_d^{\mathrm{IKM},*}(P)=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P).
\]
