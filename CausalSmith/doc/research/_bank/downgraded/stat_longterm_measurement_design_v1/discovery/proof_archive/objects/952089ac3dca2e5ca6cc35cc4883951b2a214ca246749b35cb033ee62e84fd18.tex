Fix \(\chi>1\) and \(0<\epsilon<10^{-3}\), and write \(P_{\chi,\epsilon}=P_\epsilon\). Treatment is balanced in both sources at every center, so
\[
 p_{E,1}=p_{O,1}=\frac12.                              \tag{1}
\]
The definitions of the IKM-normalized scores and moments consequently give, for either design,
\[
 \psi_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{E,d},
 \qquad
 \psi_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac12\phi_{O,d},          \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=\frac14V_{O,d}.                \tag{3}
\]
Therefore, using Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}budget\text{-}profile}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 &=\left\{\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}/4}
 +\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}/4}\right\}^2\\
 &=\frac14\mathcal V_d^*(P_{\chi,\epsilon}).           \tag{4}
\end{aligned}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}sharp\text{-}uniform\text{-}binary\text{-}elbow}\) shows that \(d_{\mathrm e}\) uniquely minimizes the right-hand side of (4), while \(d_{\mathrm p}\) is uniquely diagnostic-selected. Hence
\[
 D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P_\epsilon)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\},
 \qquad
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon)
 =\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P_\epsilon).                   \tag{5}
\]

For the neighborhood assertion, first restrict to the radius supplied by Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}binary\text{-}witness\text{-}stability}\). Every law there is regular, both bridge contrasts equal \(\tau\), and \(d_{\mathrm p}\) remains uniquely diagnostic-selected. In finite-cell coordinates, the treatment probabilities, arm-normalized score moments, and hence
\[
 V_{E,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{E,1})^2V_{E,d},
 \qquad
 V_{O,d}^{\mathrm{IKM}}=(1-p_{O,1})^2V_{O,d}           \tag{6}
\]
are continuous. Indeed, on the regular neighborhood the bridge solutions are inverses of finite nonsingular conditional-expectation matrices; consequently their coefficients, all score moments, and all profiled values are continuous functions of the finite-cell probabilities. The normalized profiles are strictly positive. Their center ordering is strict by (4), and their best-tie ratio is positive. A further reduction of the radius therefore preserves \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\) and ensures
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 >\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P_\epsilon).
\]
Call the resulting radius \(r_\epsilon^{\mathrm{IKM}}>0\).

On this finite-support neighborhood the support probabilities are interior, the outcome and selection bridges exist uniquely, the conditional-expectation operator is bijective, both bridge contrasts equal the causal target, and the score moments are finite. For a fixed interior allocation, the resulting source-count ratio has a positive finite limit. Under the remaining fixed-system local-model premises stated in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}ikmw\text{-}fixed\text{-}system\text{-}efficiency}\), that cited lemma identifies the corresponding IKM-normalized variance as the local causal semiparametric efficiency bound. Profiling that explicit bound over the interior budget share gives \(\mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)\). This proves the final conditional interpretation.
