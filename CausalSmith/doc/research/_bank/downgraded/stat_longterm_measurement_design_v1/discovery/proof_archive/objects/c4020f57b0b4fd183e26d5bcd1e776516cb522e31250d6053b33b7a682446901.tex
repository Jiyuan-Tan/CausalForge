For every \(P\in\mathfrak P_{\mathcal D}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), both the unadjusted and treatment-aware population squared-risk rules have exact finite-catalogue constants. In addition to the identity in Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}catalogue\text{-}predictive\text{-}guarantee}\),
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d}V_{E,d}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d}V_{O,d}})^2}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}
 (\sqrt{c_{E,d'}V_{E,d'}}+\sqrt{c_{O,d'}V_{O,d'}})^2}\in[1,\infty), \tag{1}
\]
and
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)=
 \frac{\min_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)}
 \mathcal V_{d,\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}
 {\min_{d'\in\mathcal D}\mathcal V_{d',\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)}\in[1,\infty). \tag{2}
\]
Each ratio is the smallest constant for its actual best-tie selector. For either positive profile \(w_d\), the least constant for every treatment-aware pairwise implication is
\[
 \max_{\substack{d,d'\in\mathcal D:\,R_d^A(P)\le R_{d'}^A(P)}}\frac{w_d}{w_{d'}}. \tag{3}
\]
These are exact lawwise enumerations, not primitive uniform guarantees. For every fixed \(\chi>1\), the jointly shrunk binary regions have unbounded best-tie ratios for both prediction rules and both profiles in their respective regions. Across the center family, all four ratios agree and obey the same sharp expansion
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,\epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}+o(\epsilon^{2/3}), \tag{4}
\]
where \(\rho\) may be any of the four center ratios. Thus treatment adjustment in the prediction objective does not produce a finite uniform source-score or IKM-normalized guarantee.
