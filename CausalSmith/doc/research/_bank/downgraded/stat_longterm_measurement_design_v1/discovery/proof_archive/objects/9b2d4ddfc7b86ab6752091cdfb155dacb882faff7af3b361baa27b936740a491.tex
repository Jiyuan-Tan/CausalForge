Fix \(B>0\), and choose the radii defining \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) after the joint shrinking in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}predictive\text{-}binary\text{-}stability}\). Then every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is regular, CLW-admissible, and common-identifying, and
\[
 \mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\mathcal D_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)
 =\{d_{\mathrm p}\},
 \qquad D^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}.                         \tag{1}
\]
For every fixed \(P\) in this region, \(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{int}}(P;B)=\{d_{\mathrm p}\}\) for all \(B\ge B_0(P)\), and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}(P;B)
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}(P)
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}}\rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^A(P)
 =\infty.                                                   \tag{2}
\]
Every \(P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}\) additionally satisfies \(D_{\mathrm{IKM}}^*(P)=\{d_{\mathrm e}\}\) and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{CLW}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P;B)
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{bin}}^{\mathrm{IKM}}}
 \rho_{\mathrm{pred}}^{A,\mathrm{IKM}}(P)
 =\infty.                                                   \tag{3}
\]
At every center all six ratios in (2)--(3) agree. For fixed \(\chi>1\), their common value satisfies
\[
 \rho(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =\frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}\epsilon^{-2}
 +O_\chi(\epsilon^{-1}),
 \qquad
 \frac{5\chi+1}{363(10\chi-3)}
 =\frac1{726}+\frac5{726(10\chi-3)}>\frac1{726}.          \tag{4}
\]
After optimizing the exact common center ratio over \(\chi>1\), every minimizer satisfies
\[
 \chi\sim\frac{\sqrt[3]{18}}{15}\epsilon^{-2/3},         \tag{5}
\]
and each of the six optimized center envelopes obeys
\[
 \epsilon^2\inf_{\chi>1}\rho(P_{\chi,epsilon})
 =\frac1{726}+\frac{5\sqrt[3]{12}}{968}\epsilon^{2/3}
 +o(\epsilon^{2/3}).                                       \tag{6}
\]
