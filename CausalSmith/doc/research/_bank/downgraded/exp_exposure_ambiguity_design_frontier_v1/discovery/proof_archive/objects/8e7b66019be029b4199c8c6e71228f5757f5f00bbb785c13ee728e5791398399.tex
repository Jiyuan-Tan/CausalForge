Fix \(\bar p\in(0,1)\). For every \(B\ge1\) such that \(n=2B\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\), write \(m_{2B}=\lfloor 2B\bar p\rfloor\), so \(1\le m_{2B}\le2B-1\). The two menus in \(\mathrm{def{:}persistent\text{-}witness}\) have cardinality two, every unit retains distinct endpoint exposures, and \(\rho_B(\Omega_{2B})=\{10,01\}\). For each mapping, \(H_{2B}(\widetilde e^\kappa)\) is the labeled type blow-up of \(H_2(e^{(\kappa)})\): vertices are \(((j,a),k)\), and two such vertices are adjacent exactly when no \(q\in\{10,01\}\) simultaneously realizes their two base endpoint exposures under \(e^{(\kappa)}\). In particular, the feasible endpoint-exposure vector sets of \(\widetilde e^-\) and \(\widetilde e^+\) coincide, and the identity map \(((j,a),k)\mapsto((j,a),k)\) is an explicit labeled graph isomorphism; the same identity matches the shared \(\widetilde e^0\) graphs. Thus the two mapping-wise graph multisets agree for every admissible \(B\), but \(V_{2B}^\star(\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{B,-})=1\) and \(V_{2B}^\star(\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{B,+})=5/4\). The lower bounds are valid for every exact-budget design and every measurable estimator, and the upper bounds are attained by explicit exact-budget designs and block-averaged bounded-outcome estimators. Consequently, for every fixed \(\bar p\in(0,1)\), all sufficiently large even sizes are admissible and the absolute risk gap \(1/4\) persists along them.
