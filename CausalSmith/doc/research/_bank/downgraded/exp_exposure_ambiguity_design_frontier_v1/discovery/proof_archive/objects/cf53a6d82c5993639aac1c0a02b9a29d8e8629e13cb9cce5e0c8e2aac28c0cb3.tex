For every admissible exact-budget instance, the trivial action and one extremal target pair for each positive-diameter observation fiber give a fractional diameter cover with congestion at most \(|\Omega_n|\). Therefore
\[
 \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)
 \le |\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi),
 \qquad
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le L_n(\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le\frac12|\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi).
\]
More generally, whenever an equivariant action on the menu-induced sign experiment and a fractional diameter cover of congestion \(\kappa_n\) are supplied, \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\) gives a quotient linear program that exactly computes \(\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)^2\), an explicit invariant design and midpoint estimator, and a \(\sqrt{\kappa_n}\)-factor bracket for \(L_n(\mathcal E,\bar p)\). Polynomially many polynomially encoded orbits and cover entries give a polynomial-size certificate. The unrestricted nonlocal construction proves that mapping-wise labeled conflict-graph multisets do not determine the frontier without a locality restriction. In graph-local subclasses, \(G_n\) can affect the result only through the resulting menu \(\mathcal E(G_n)\) and certificates for its sign experiment.
