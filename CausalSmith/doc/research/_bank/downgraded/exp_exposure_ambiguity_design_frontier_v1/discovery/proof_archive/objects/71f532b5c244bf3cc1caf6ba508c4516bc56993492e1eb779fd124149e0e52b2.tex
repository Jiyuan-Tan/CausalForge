\textbf{Proof.}
All sets are finite and \(\Omega_n\ne\varnothing\) by admissibility. The action sends the fiber \(\{\alpha:s_{\alpha z}=s\}\) bijectively to \(\{\alpha':s_{\alpha',gz}=\phi_{g,z}(s)\}\), while preserving targets. Hence
\[
 d_n(gz,\phi_{g,z}(s))=d_n(z,s), \tag{1}
\]
and the bijectivity of \(\phi_{g,z}\) gives
\[
 s_{g\alpha,gz}=s_{g\beta,gz}
 \Longleftrightarrow s_{\alpha z}=s_{\beta z}. \tag{2}
\]
Thus transformed representative covers remain valid.

Define
\[
 F_{\alpha\beta}(\pi)=|t_\alpha-t_\beta|^2
 \sum_z\pi_z\mathbf1\{s_{\alpha z}=s_{\beta z}\}.
\]
Then \(C_n(\pi)^2=\max_{\alpha,\beta}F_{\alpha\beta}(\pi)\). Average any \(\pi\) over the group to obtain \(\bar\pi\). By (2), target invariance, and a change of variables,
\[
 F_{\alpha\beta}(\bar\pi)
 =|\Gamma_n|^{-1}\sum_gF_{g^{-1}\alpha,g^{-1}\beta}(\pi)
 \le C_n(\pi)^2. \tag{3}
\]
Thus \(C_n(\bar\pi)\le C_n(\pi)\), and a continuous minimum on the simplex has an invariant optimizer. An invariant design is uniquely encoded by orbit masses \(x_j\), with \(\pi_z=x_j/|O_j|\) on \(O_j\). For a pair representative,
\[
 F_{\alpha_r\beta_r}(\pi)=\Delta_r^2\sum_ja_{rj}x_j. \tag{4}
\]
Pair-orbit invariance makes the maximum of (4) over \(r\) exactly \(C_n(\pi)^2\). Therefore
\[
 \lambda_n=\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)^2. \tag{5}
\]

If two schedules collide at \(z\), their target separation is bounded by the diameter of that fiber. Squaring and averaging gives \(F_{\alpha\beta}(\pi)\le U_n(\pi)^2\), hence \(C_n(\pi)\le U_n(\pi)\). Let \(\pi^\star\) be the invariant lift of an optimizer in (5). Multiply the valid cover for a fixed schedule by \(\pi_z^\star\) and sum over \(z\). Each pair term is at most \(C_n(\pi^\star)^2\), and the total weight is at most \(\kappa_n\), so
\[
 U_n(\pi^\star)^2\le\kappa_n C_n(\pi^\star)^2
 =\kappa_n\lambda_n. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (5)--(6) prove both optimized-modulus comparisons.

Apply \(\mathrm{thm{:}collision\text{-}geometry\text{-}sandwich}\) and \(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}saddle}\) to \(\pi^\star\):
\[
 \frac12\sqrt{\lambda_n}
 \le L_n(\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le\frac12U_n(\pi^\star)
 \le\frac12\sqrt{\kappa_n\lambda_n}. \tag{7}
\]
The lifted design is explicit, and the midpoint rule from the sandwich theorem followed by multilinear extension attains the last term. The displayed LP dimensions follow by counting its variables and constraints. Uniformly bounded \(\kappa_n\) gives a uniform constant ratio. The sharpness and unrestricted nonrepresentability conclusions follow from the two named dependent results.

Nothing in (1)--(7) requires a graph action: the action is on the menu-induced finite experiment. An ambient graph may supply such an action only after it has constrained or organized the menu; holding the menu fixed leaves every object in the proof unchanged by \(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}saddle}\). \(\square\)
