Fix \(\bar p\in(0,1)\).  For \(B\ge1\) such that \(n=2B\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\), label the units \((j,a)\) with \(j\in\{1,\ldots,B\}\) and \(a\in\{1,2\}\), take the empty graph, and set \(m_{2B}=\lfloor2B\bar p\rfloor\).  Define \(\rho_B:\mathcal A_{2B}\to\{00,10,01,11\}\) by
\[
 \rho_B(z)=
 \begin{cases}
 00,&z=\mathbf 0_{2B},\\
 11,&z=\mathbf 1_{2B},\\
 10,&z\notin\{\mathbf 0_{2B},\mathbf 1_{2B}\}\text{ and }z_{(1,1)}=1,\\
 01,&z\notin\{\mathbf 0_{2B},\mathbf 1_{2B}\}\text{ and }z_{(1,1)}=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
For \(\kappa\in\{0,-,+\}\), set
\[
 \widetilde e^{\kappa}_{(j,a),2B}(z)
 =e^{(\kappa)}_{a,2}(\rho_B(z)),
\]
and define \(\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{B,-}=\{\widetilde e^0,\widetilde e^-\}\) and \(\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{B,+}=\{\widetilde e^0,\widetilde e^+\}\).
