Fix \(\bar p\in(0,1)\) and \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\), and write \(\mathfrak A_n=\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\). Suppose a finite group \(\Gamma_n\) acts on \(\Omega_n\) and on \(\mathfrak A_n\) such that, for every \(g\in\Gamma_n\) and \(z\in\Omega_n\), there is a bijection \(\phi_{g,z}:\mathcal S_n\to\mathcal S_n\) satisfying
\[
 t_{g\alpha}=t_\alpha,
 \qquad
 s_{g\alpha,gz}=\phi_{g,z}(s_{\alpha z})
 \quad\text{for every }\alpha\in\mathfrak A_n.
\]
Let \(O_1,\ldots,O_K\) be the assignment orbits and \(P_1,\ldots,P_R\) the orbits of unordered pairs of sign schedules. Choose \(p_r=\{\alpha_r,\beta_r\}\in P_r\), put \(\Delta_r=|t_{\alpha_r}-t_{\beta_r}|\), and define
\[
 a_{rj}=\frac{1}{|O_j|}\sum_{z\in O_j}
 \mathbf 1\{s_{\alpha_r z}=s_{\beta_r z}\}.
\]
Assume that there is a fractional diameter cover of congestion \(\kappa_n\ge1\): for one representative \(\alpha_h\) of every \(\Gamma_n\)-orbit in \(\mathfrak A_n\), there are unordered pairs \(p_{h\ell}=\{\beta_{h\ell},\gamma_{h\ell}\}\) and weights \(q_{h\ell}\ge0\) such that \(\sum_\ell q_{h\ell}\le\kappa_n\) and, for every \(z\in\Omega_n\),
\[
 d_n(z,s_{\alpha_h z})^2
 \le
 \sum_\ell q_{h\ell}
 |t_{\beta_{h\ell}}-t_{\gamma_{h\ell}}|^2
 \mathbf 1\{s_{\beta_{h\ell}z}=s_{\gamma_{h\ell}z}\}.
\]
For other schedules use the cover obtained by applying the same group element to every pair in the representative cover. Define the quotient linear-program value
\[
 \lambda_n=\min_{x_1,\ldots,x_K,\lambda}
 \left\{\lambda:
 x_j\ge0,\ \sum_{j=1}^Kx_j=1,\
 \Delta_r^2\sum_{j=1}^K a_{rj}x_j\le\lambda
 \text{ for }r=1,\ldots,R\right\}.
\]
Then
\[
 \inf_\pi C_n(\pi)=\sqrt{\lambda_n},
 \qquad
 \inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)
 \le \sqrt{\kappa_n}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi),
\]
and the exact ambiguity frontier obeys the computable bracket
\[
 \frac12\sqrt{\lambda_n}
 \le L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le \frac12\sqrt{\kappa_n\lambda_n}.
\]
An optimizer \(x^\star\) lifts to the explicit invariant design assigning mass \(x_j^\star/|O_j|\) to every \(z\in O_j\); the observation-fiber midpoint rule and its multilinear extension have worst-case root mean squared error at most \(\sqrt{\kappa_n\lambda_n}/2\). Thus, if \(\sup_n\kappa_n<\infty\), the optimized collision moduli match in order. If the orbit tables and fractional cover have polynomial encoding length, the displayed certificate is a linear program with \(K+1\) variables, \(R\) loss inequalities, \(K\) nonnegativity inequalities, and one simplex equality, and is polynomial-size whenever \(K\) and \(R\) are polynomial in \(n\). This is a sufficient structural compression criterion, not an exhaustive characterization: on the unrestricted class, mapping-wise conflict-graph multisets fail to determine the frontier by \(\mathrm{thm{:}persistent\text{-}nonrepresentability}\), while the existence of an analogous asymptotic separation for natural graph-local menus is not decided here.
