\textbf{Proof.}
Take both group actions to be trivial. Then every sign schedule is its own orbit representative. Fix such a schedule \(\alpha\). For each \(z\in\Omega_n\) with \(d_n(z,s_{\alpha z})>0\), finiteness of \(\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\) supplies schedules \(\beta_z,\gamma_z\) in the fiber \(\{\eta:s_{\eta z}=s_{\alpha z}\}\) attaining its maximum and minimum target. Put weight one on the unordered pair \(\{\beta_z,\gamma_z\}\). Assign no pair when the diameter is zero. There are at most \(|\Omega_n|\) positive weights, so their sum is at most \(|\Omega_n|\).

At any fixed \(z_0\), the term indexed by \(z_0\), when present, satisfies
\[
 |t_{\beta_{z_0}}-t_{\gamma_{z_0}}|^2
 \mathbf 1\{s_{\beta_{z_0}z_0}=s_{\gamma_{z_0}z_0}\}
 =d_n(z_0,s_{\alpha z_0})^2. \tag{1}
\]
All other terms are nonnegative. If the left side diameter is zero, the required inequality is automatic. Thus these pairs and weights satisfy the fractional-diameter-cover condition with congestion at most \(|\Omega_n|\). Applying \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\) with \(\kappa_n=|\Omega_n|\) gives
\[
 \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)
 \le |\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
\]
and the two displayed bounds for \(L_n(\mathcal E,\bar p)\). The construction uses only finiteness and nonemptiness of the exact-budget assignment set and sign-schedule set; it requires no assignment-level positivity, rank, graph action, or locality. \(\square\)
