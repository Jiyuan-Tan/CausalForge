Fix \(\bar p\in(0,1)\) and \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\). The finite programs \(\mathsf P_n\) and \(\mathsf Q_n\) attain equal optimal objectives. With \(L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)\) equal to the square root of this common objective, one has the exact identity \(V_n^\star=L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)\). The recovery in \(\mathrm{def{:}primal\text{-}recovery}\) yields \(\Theta_n^\star\in\mathcal D_n(\bar p)\) and an arbitrary-outcome estimator \(\widehat\theta_n^\star\) satisfying \(\sup_{y\in\mathcal Y_n(\mathcal E)}\operatorname{RMSE}_n(\Theta_n^\star,\widehat\theta_n^\star;y)=L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)\). Every \(\Theta\in\mathcal D_n(\bar p)\) and every measurable \(\widehat\theta\) have worst-case root mean squared error at least \(L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)\).
