Fix \(\bar p\in(0,1)\) and \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\).  For the trivial group actions on \(\Omega_n\) and \(\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\), the fractional-diameter-cover hypothesis of \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\) holds with congestion at most \(|\Omega_n|\).  Consequently,
\[
 \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)
 \le |\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi),
\]
and
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le \frac12|\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi).
\]
