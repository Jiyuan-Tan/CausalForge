Fix a design vector \(\pi\) and an arbitrary estimator.  For schedules \(\alpha,\beta\), let
\[
 E_{\alpha\beta}=\{z\in\Omega_n:s_{\alpha z}=s_{\beta z}\}.
\]
On this event the estimator receives the same observation under the two schedules.  If its common value is \(a_z\), then the algebraic identity
\[
 (a_z-t_\alpha)^2+(a_z-t_\beta)^2
 =2\left(a_z-\frac{t_\alpha+t_\beta}{2}\right)^2
  +\frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
 \ge\frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
\]
holds.  After multiplying by \(\pi_z\) and summing on \(E_{\alpha\beta}\), the sum of the two schedule risks is at least
\[
 \frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
 \sum_{z\in E_{\alpha\beta}}\pi_z.
\]
Their maximum risk is at least half this sum, hence their maximum root mean squared error is at least
\[
 \frac12|t_\alpha-t_\beta|
 \left(\sum_{z\in E_{\alpha\beta}}\pi_z\right)^{1/2}.
\]
Maximizing over the pair and then infimizing over the design and estimator proves
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\le V_n^\star.
\]

For the upper bound, fix \(\pi\).  For each nonempty observation fiber
\[
 F_{zs}=\{\alpha\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E):s_{\alpha z}=s\},
\]
define the sign rule to be the midpoint
\[
 \delta^{\mathrm{bin}}(z,s)=
 \frac12\left(\max_{\alpha\in F_{zs}}t_\alpha+
                    \min_{\alpha\in F_{zs}}t_\alpha\right),
\]
and assign any value in \([-2,2]\) to an empty fiber.  For every schedule \(\alpha\),
\[
 |\delta^{\mathrm{bin}}(z,s_{\alpha z})-t_\alpha|
 \le\frac12d_n(z,s_{\alpha z}).
\]
Consequently its squared risk is at most
\[
 \frac14\sum_{z\in\Omega_n}\pi_zd_n(z,s_{\alpha z})^2,
\]
and its worst-case sign root mean squared error is at most \(U_n(\pi)/2\).  The sign reduction and multilinear extension give the same upper bound for every bounded response table.  Taking the infimum over \(\pi\) proves
\[
 V_n^\star\le\frac12\inf_\pi U_n(\pi).
\]

Write \(C_n^\circ=\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\) and \(U_n^\circ=\inf_\pi U_n(\pi)\).  The two inequalities say
\[
 C_n^\circ\le2V_n^\star\le U_n^\circ.
\]
Therefore \(U_n^\circ\to0\) implies \(V_n^\star\to0\), and \(V_n^\star\to0\) implies \(C_n^\circ\to0\).  Likewise, \(\liminf C_n^\circ>0\) implies \(\liminf V_n^\star>0\), while \(\liminf V_n^\star>0\) implies \(\liminf U_n^\circ>0\).  These are precisely the four asserted sequence implications; no converse beyond them is used.
