Fix a probability vector \(\pi\) on \(\Omega_n\) and an arbitrary estimator.  By \(\mathrm{lem{:}sign\text{-}reduction}\), every sign schedule belongs to \(\mathcal Y_n(\mathcal E)\), so restricting the adversary to sign schedules is valid for a lower bound.  For \(\alpha,\beta\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\), define
\[
 E_{\alpha\beta}=\{z\in\Omega_n:s_{\alpha z}=s_{\beta z}\}.
\]
At each \(z\in E_{\alpha\beta}\), the assignment and observed sign vector coincide under the two schedules.  The estimator therefore takes one common value, say \(a_z\), and the algebraic identity
\[
 (a_z-t_\alpha)^2+(a_z-t_\beta)^2
 =2\left(a_z-\frac{t_\alpha+t_\beta}{2}\right)^2
   +\frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
 \ge\frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
\]
holds.  Multiply by \(\pi_z\) and sum over \(E_{\alpha\beta}\).  The sum of the two full design-based mean squared errors is at least
\[
 \frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
 \sum_{z\in E_{\alpha\beta}}\pi_z.
\]
The larger mean squared error is at least half this sum.  Thus the worst-case root mean squared error under the fixed design and estimator is at least
\[
 \frac12|t_\alpha-t_\beta|
 \left(\sum_{z\in E_{\alpha\beta}}\pi_z\right)^{1/2}.
\]
Maximizing over \(\alpha,\beta\), then infimizing over estimators and over probability vectors \(\pi\), proves
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\le V_n^\star.
\]

For the upper bound, continue to fix \(\pi\).  For each nonempty observation fiber
\[
 F_{zs}=\{\alpha\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E):s_{\alpha z}=s\},
\]
define the finite sign rule
\[
 \delta^{\mathrm{bin}}(z,s)
 =\frac12\left(
    \max_{\alpha\in F_{zs}}t_\alpha
    +\min_{\alpha\in F_{zs}}t_\alpha
   \right),
\]
and choose any value in \([-2,2]\) on an empty fiber.  Finiteness of \(\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\) guarantees that both extrema exist.  For every \(\alpha\) and \(z\), the midpoint identity and \(\mathrm{def{:}collision\text{-}moduli}\) give
\[
 \left|\delta^{\mathrm{bin}}(z,s_{\alpha z})-t_\alpha\right|
 \le\frac12d_n(z,s_{\alpha z}).
\]
Consequently the mean squared error for schedule \(\alpha\) is at most
\[
 \frac14\sum_{z\in\Omega_n}\pi_zd_n(z,s_{\alpha z})^2,
\]
and the maximum sign-schedule root mean squared error is at most \(U_n(\pi)/2\).  The sign reduction and the multilinear extension in \(\mathrm{def{:}multilinear\text{-}extension}\) produce an explicit rule on \(\Omega_n\times[-1,1]^n\) with no larger worst-case risk over arbitrary bounded tables.  Because this construction is available for every \(\pi\),
\[
 V_n^\star\le\frac12\inf_\pi U_n(\pi).
\]

For the sequence conclusions, abbreviate
\[
 C_n^\circ=\inf_\pi C_n(\pi),
 \qquad
 U_n^\circ=\inf_\pi U_n(\pi).
\]
The two established inequalities say exactly
\[
 C_n^\circ\le2V_n^\star\le U_n^\circ.
\]
Therefore \(U_n^\circ\to0\) implies \(V_n^\star\to0\), and \(V_n^\star\to0\) implies \(C_n^\circ\to0\).  Likewise, \(\liminf_n C_n^\circ>0\) implies \(\liminf_nV_n^\star>0\), and \(\liminf_nV_n^\star>0\) implies \(\liminf_nU_n^\circ>0\).  All limits are along \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\) with \(n\to\infty\).  These are one-sided certificates on opposite sides; the proof does not assert a converse comparison between the two optimized moduli.

The exact-budget support is nonempty by \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\).  No assignment-level positivity, rank, graph-locality, or boundary regularity beyond \(\bar p\in(0,1)\) and \(1\le m_n\le n-1\) is used.
