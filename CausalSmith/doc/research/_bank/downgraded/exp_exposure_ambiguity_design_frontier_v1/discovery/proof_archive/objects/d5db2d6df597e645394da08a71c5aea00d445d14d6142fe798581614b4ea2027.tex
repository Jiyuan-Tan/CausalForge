Fix \(\bar p\in(0,1)\) and consider \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\) along a diverging sequence. The conditional compression result in \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\) already gives an exact quotient linear program and a \(\sqrt{\kappa_n}\)-factor bracket whenever an equivariant fractional diameter cover of congestion \(\kappa_n\) is supplied. The trivial-action construction in \(\mathrm{lem{:}universal\text{-}coarse\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover}\) gives congestion at most \(|\Omega_n|\), and hence
\[
 \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)
 \le |\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi),
\]
but this certificate can have exponential encoding length and its factor need not be uniform in \(n\). The residual problem is to identify and verify primitive graph, budget, and exposure-incidence conditions for natural graph-local menu sequences that yield polynomial-encoding orbit tables, polynomially many orbit variables and constraints, and fractional diameter covers with \(\sup_n\kappa_n<\infty\). More generally, establish or refute for such families a size-uniform reverse comparison
\[
 \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)\le K\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
\]
for some finite \(K\) independent of \(n\). Determine separately whether a natural graph-local sequence can have identical mapping-wise labeled conflict-graph multisets but ambiguity frontiers separated by a nonvanishing amount. The unrestricted nonlocal witness in \(\mathrm{thm{:}persistent\text{-}nonrepresentability}\) does not answer that local question, and no natural graph-local separation is asserted here.
