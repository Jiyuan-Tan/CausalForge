The strongest unconditional result currently available is the exact finite second-order-cone saddle
\[
 V_n^\star=L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)
 =\sqrt{\operatorname{val}(\mathsf P_n)}
 =\sqrt{\operatorname{val}(\mathsf Q_n)}
\]
together with the evaluable sandwich
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le\frac12\inf_\pi U_n(\pi).
\]
In particular, whenever \(\inf_\pi U_n(\pi)\le K\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\) for a fixed finite \(K\), either optimized modulus is a factor-\(K\) certificate for the other and the corresponding bounds determine the minimax frontier up to that factor.  The nonlocal solved menu satisfies \(C_n(\pi)=U_n(\pi)=2\) and \(V_n^\star=1\) for every design, proving that both universal factors \(1/2\) are sharp.  Moreover, for every fixed \(\bar p\in(0,1)\), the generalized common-selector construction in \(\mathrm{thm{:}persistent\text{-}nonrepresentability}\) applies at every admissible even size and preserves the multiset of mapping-wise conflict graphs while the two exact risks remain \(1\) and \(5/4\); hence their gap is \(1/4\) along every sufficiently large even subsequence.  These results rule out mapping-wise conflict-graph multisets as an exact or asymptotically risk-determining summary on the unrestricted class.  They do not provide a polynomial-size certificate for arbitrary menus and do not resolve whether a comparable separation occurs for natural graph-local menus.
