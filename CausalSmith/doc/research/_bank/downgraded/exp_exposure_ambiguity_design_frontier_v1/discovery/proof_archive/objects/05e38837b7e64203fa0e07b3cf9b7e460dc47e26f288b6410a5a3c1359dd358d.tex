The exact finite second-order-cone saddle and the computable sandwich give
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le\frac12\inf_\pi U_n(\pi).
\]
Thus, on any specified sequence satisfying \(\inf_\pi U_n(\pi)\le K\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\) for a fixed \(K\), either optimized modulus supplies a factor-\(K\) certificate for the exact risk.  On \(\mathcal E^{\mathrm{nl}}\), both moduli equal two under every design and the exact risk is one, proving both constants sharp.  The generalized persistent witness supplies a fixed \(1/4\) risk separation along all sufficiently large admissible even sizes for every fixed \(\bar p\in(0,1)\), despite identical mapping-wise conflict-graph multisets.  These results do not provide a polynomial-size certificate in general, a structural criterion forcing order matching, or a natural graph-local asymptotic separation.
