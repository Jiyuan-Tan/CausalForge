\textbf{Proof.}
Use the trivial group actions, so every sign schedule is its own orbit representative.  Fix \(\alpha\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\).  For each \(z\in\Omega_n\) with
\(d_n(z,s_{\alpha z})>0\), finiteness of the observation fiber permits schedules \(\beta_z\) and \(\gamma_z\) in that fiber attaining its maximum and minimum target.  Give the unordered pair \(\{\beta_z,\gamma_z\}\) weight one.  Assign no pair to cells of diameter zero.  The total weight is at most \(|\Omega_n|\).

At any fixed \(u\in\Omega_n\), the pair selected for \(u\), when present, satisfies
\[
 s_{\beta_u u}=s_{\gamma_u u}=s_{\alpha u},
 \qquad
 |t_{\beta_u}-t_{\gamma_u}|
 =d_n(u,s_{\alpha u}).
\]
Its term alone therefore equals the left side of the fractional-cover inequality at \(u\); every other term is nonnegative.  If the diameter is zero, the inequality is immediate.  Thus the required cover exists with congestion \(\kappa_n\le|\Omega_n|\).

Apply \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\) with this cover.  It gives
\[
 \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)
 \le |\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
\]
and, using its frontier bracket,
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le\frac12|\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi).
\]
The only boundary condition needed is the nonemptiness of \(\Omega_n\), guaranteed by \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\). \(\square\)
