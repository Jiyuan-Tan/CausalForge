The strongest unconditional result is the computable finite sandwich
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\le L_n(G_n,\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le\frac12\inf_\pi U_n(\pi),
\]
together with the exact finite second-order-cone saddle for \(L_n\).  Consequently, on any explicitly specified sequence for which \(\inf_\pi U_n(\pi)\le K\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\) with fixed \(K\), either optimized modulus is a factor-\(K\) certificate for the exact risk.  The persistent construction proves a fixed asymptotic separation in what mapping-wise conflict graphs can represent when nonlocal maps are allowed; the graph-local calibration gives exact risk \(1/n\), and the no-interference calibration gives order \(n^{-1/2}\).  This does not resolve the requested structural characterization or the existence of a natural local separation.
