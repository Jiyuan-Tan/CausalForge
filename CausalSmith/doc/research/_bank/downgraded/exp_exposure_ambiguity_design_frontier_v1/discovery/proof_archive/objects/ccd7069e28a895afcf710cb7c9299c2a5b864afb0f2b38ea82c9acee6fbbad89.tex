\textbf{Proof.}
Take the trivial group, so every assignment and every sign schedule is its own orbit.  Fix \(\alpha\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\).  For each \(z\in\Omega_n\), the fiber
\[
 F_{\alpha z}
 =\{\beta\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E):s_{\beta z}=s_{\alpha z}\}
\]
is finite and nonempty because it contains \(\alpha\).  If \(d_n(z,s_{\alpha z})>0\), choose schedules \(\beta^+_{\alpha z},\beta^-_{\alpha z}\in F_{\alpha z}\) attaining the maximum and minimum target values in the fiber.  Then
\[
 s_{\beta^+_{\alpha z},z}=s_{\beta^-_{\alpha z},z}=s_{\alpha z},
 \qquad
 |t_{\beta^+_{\alpha z}}-t_{\beta^-_{\alpha z}}|
 =d_n(z,s_{\alpha z}). \tag{1}
\]
Give the unordered pair \(\{\beta^+_{\alpha z},\beta^-_{\alpha z}\}\) unit weight.  If the same pair is selected at several assignments, aggregate its unit weights; assignments with zero fiber diameter require no pair.  The total weight is at most \(|\Omega_n|\).  At any \(x\in\Omega_n\) of positive fiber diameter, the pair selected for \(x\) collides at \(x\), so its contribution equals \(d_n(x,s_{\alpha x})^2\) by (1), while all other contributions are nonnegative.  Therefore
\[
 d_n(x,s_{\alpha x})^2
 \le
 \sum_\ell q_{\alpha\ell}
 |t_{\beta_{\alpha\ell}}-t_{\gamma_{\alpha\ell}}|^2
 \mathbf 1\{s_{\beta_{\alpha\ell}x}=s_{\gamma_{\alpha\ell}x}\}. \tag{2}
\]
For zero diameter, (2) is immediate.  Thus every schedule has the required fractional cover with congestion at most \(|\Omega_n|\).  The support condition \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\) makes \(\Omega_n\) nonempty, so \(|\Omega_n|\ge1\), as required by the cover convention.  Apply \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\) with this trivial action and congestion \(|\Omega_n|\).  Its optimized-modulus comparison and exact-frontier bracket give the two displayed conclusions. \(\square\)
