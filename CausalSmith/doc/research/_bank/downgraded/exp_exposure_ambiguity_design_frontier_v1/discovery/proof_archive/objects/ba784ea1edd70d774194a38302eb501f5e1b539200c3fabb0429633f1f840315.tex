For fixed \(\bar p\in(0,1)\), \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\), and a finite nonempty menu \(\mathcal E\), the mapping-known oracle benchmark is
\[
 \max_{e\in\mathcal E}V_n^\star(\{e\},\bar p),
\]
where \(V_n^\star(\{e\},\bar p)\) is the design-inclusive minimax root mean squared error in \(\mathrm{def{:}minimax\text{-}risk}\) with the adversarial menu restricted to the singleton \(\{e\}\). The design still ranges over all of \(\mathcal D_n(\bar p)\), and the estimator may depend on the known singleton mapping. Finiteness and nonemptiness of \(\mathcal E\) make the displayed maximum well defined.
