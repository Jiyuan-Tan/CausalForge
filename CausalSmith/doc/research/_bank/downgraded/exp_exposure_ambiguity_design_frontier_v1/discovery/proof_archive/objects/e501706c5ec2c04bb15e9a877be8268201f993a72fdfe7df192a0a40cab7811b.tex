Fix a probability vector \(\pi\) on \(\Omega_n\), and let \(\widehat\theta\) be any estimator.  It is enough for the lower bound to restrict the adversary to the sign schedules, which belong to \(\mathcal Y_n(\mathcal E)\) by \(\mathrm{lem{:}sign\text{-}reduction}\).  For \(\alpha,\beta\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\), put
\[
 E_{\alpha\beta}=\{z\in\Omega_n:s_{\alpha z}=s_{\beta z}\}.
\]
At any \(z\in E_{\alpha\beta}\), the observed assignment and sign-valued outcome vector are identical under the two schedules.  Thus the estimator has a common value, say \(a_z\), and the elementary identity
\[
 (a_z-t_\alpha)^2+(a_z-t_\beta)^2
 =2\left(a_z-\frac{t_\alpha+t_\beta}{2}\right)^2
   +\frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
 \ge \frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
\]
applies.  Multiplication by \(\pi_z\) and summation over \(E_{\alpha\beta}\) show that the sum of the two design-based mean squared errors is at least
\[
 \frac{(t_\alpha-t_\beta)^2}{2}
 \sum_{z\in E_{\alpha\beta}}\pi_z.
\]
The larger of the two mean squared errors is at least one half of their sum.  Hence the worst-case root mean squared error under this fixed \(\pi\) and this fixed estimator is at least
\[
 \frac12 |t_\alpha-t_\beta|
 \left(\sum_{z\in E_{\alpha\beta}}\pi_z\right)^{1/2}.
\]
Maximizing over \((\alpha,\beta)\), then infimizing first over estimators and then over probability vectors \(\pi\), gives
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)\le V_n^\star.
\]

For the reverse inequality, continue to fix \(\pi\).  For every nonempty observation fiber
\[
 F_{zs}=\{\alpha\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E):s_{\alpha z}=s\},
\]
define
\[
 \delta^{\mathrm{bin}}(z,s)
 =\frac12\left(\max_{\alpha\in F_{zs}}t_\alpha
                    +\min_{\alpha\in F_{zs}}t_\alpha\right),
\]
and give \(\delta^{\mathrm{bin}}(z,s)\) any value in \([-2,2]\) when the fiber is empty.  Finiteness of \(\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\) makes the extrema well-defined.  For every schedule \(\alpha\) and assignment \(z\), the midpoint property and \(\mathrm{def{:}collision\text{-}moduli}\) give
\[
 \left|\delta^{\mathrm{bin}}(z,s_{\alpha z})-t_\alpha\right|
 \le \frac12d_n(z,s_{\alpha z}).
\]
Therefore the mean squared error of schedule \(\alpha\) is at most
\[
 \frac14\sum_{z\in\Omega_n}\pi_zd_n(z,s_{\alpha z})^2,
\]
and the largest sign-schedule root mean squared error is at most \(U_n(\pi)/2\).  By \(\mathrm{lem{:}sign\text{-}reduction}\), the multilinear extension in \(\mathrm{def{:}multilinear\text{-}extension}\) has no larger worst-case risk over arbitrary bounded tables in \(\mathcal Y_n(\mathcal E)\).  Since this construction is available for every \(\pi\),
\[
 V_n^\star\le\frac12\inf_\pi U_n(\pi).
\]

For the sequence assertions, abbreviate
\[
 C_n^\circ=\inf_\pi C_n(\pi),
 \qquad
 U_n^\circ=\inf_\pi U_n(\pi).
\]
The two proved inequalities are exactly
\[
 C_n^\circ\le 2V_n^\star\le U_n^\circ.
\]
Thus \(U_n^\circ\to0\) implies \(V_n^\star\to0\), whereas \(V_n^\star\to0\) implies \(C_n^\circ\to0\).  Similarly, \(\liminf_n C_n^\circ>0\) implies \(\liminf_n V_n^\star>0\), whereas \(\liminf_n V_n^\star>0\) implies \(\liminf_n U_n^\circ>0\).  These are one-sided necessary and sufficient certificates on opposite sides; no converse relation between the two optimized moduli is asserted.
