For fixed \(\bar p\in(0,1)\), a one-shot exact-budget experiment at \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\) must estimate the all-treated-versus-all-control effect when a fixed bounded potential-outcome table obeys one unknown member of a finite exposure-mapping menu. The unrestricted exact frontier is \(V_n^\star(\mathcal E,\bar p)=L_n(\mathcal E,\bar p)\): a finite second-order cone primal and least-favourable-prior dual compute it and recover an attaining design and lossless multilinear rule. The known ambient graph \(G_n\) is ancillary once \(\mathcal E\) is fixed; in graph-local subclasses it affects the frontier only by defining, constraining, or organizing the menu \(\mathcal E(G_n)\). Derived mapping-wise conflict-graph multisets still fail to represent latent-menu risk: persistent menus with identical such multisets have exact risks \(1\) and \(5/4\), while singleton oracle risk is \(5/6\). Collision and fiber-diameter moduli give explicit opposite-side certificates, and uniformly low-congestion polynomial compression for natural graph-local menus remains open.
