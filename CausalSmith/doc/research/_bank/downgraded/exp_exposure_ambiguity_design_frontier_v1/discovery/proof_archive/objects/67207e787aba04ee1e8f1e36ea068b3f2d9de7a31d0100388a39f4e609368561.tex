For every admissible exact-budget instance, the trivial action and one extremal target pair for each positive-diameter observation fiber give a fractional diameter cover with congestion at most \(|\Omega_n|\). Hence
\[
 \inf_\pi U_n(\pi)
 \le |\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi),
 \qquad
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le L_n(\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le \frac12|\Omega_n|^{1/2}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi).
\]
More generally, under the explicit equivariance and fractional-diameter-cover hypotheses of \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\), its quotient linear program exactly computes \(\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)^2\), and its optimizer lifts to an explicit invariant design. The observation-fiber midpoint rule and multilinear extension give
\[
 \frac12\inf_\pi C_n(\pi)
 \le L_n(\mathcal E,\bar p)
 \le \frac12\sqrt{\kappa_n}\inf_\pi C_n(\pi).
\]
Thus \(\sup_n\kappa_n<\infty\) is a sufficient primitive certificate for a size-uniform reverse comparison, and polynomially many polynomially encoded assignment and pair orbits together with polynomially encoded cover data give a polynomial-size certificate. Finally, \(\mathrm{thm{:}persistent\text{-}nonrepresentability}\) proves that mapping-wise labeled conflict-graph multisets do not determine the frontier on the unrestricted nonlocal class. None of these proved facts establishes bounded congestion or a separating witness for an as-yet undefined graph-local family.
