Existing work treats known exposure structure, testing of exposure specifications, fixed assignment mechanisms, or correctly specified linear spillover operators. It does not compute the unrestricted one-shot exact-count design--estimator saddle over a latent finite menu of arbitrary bounded exposure-response tables. The ambient graph is not a missing primitive of this unrestricted saddle: once the analyst supplies \(\mathcal E\), the risk depends only on \((n,\mathcal E,\bar p)\). The remaining graph-local question is whether natural rules mapping \(G_n\) to menus \(\mathcal E(G_n)\) yield uniformly low-congestion, polynomially encoded compression or a local nonrepresentability separation.
