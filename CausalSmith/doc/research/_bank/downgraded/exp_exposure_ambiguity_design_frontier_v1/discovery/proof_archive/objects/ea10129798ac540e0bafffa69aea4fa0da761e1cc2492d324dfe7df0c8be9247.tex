At each admissible \(n\), the population, ambient observed graph \(G_n\), exposure menu \(\mathcal E\), and potential-outcome table are fixed; there is no superpopulation sampling. Once \(\mathcal E\) is fixed, \(G_n\) is ancillary to the unrestricted frontier. In graph-local subclasses it may determine or organize \(\mathcal E(G_n)\).
