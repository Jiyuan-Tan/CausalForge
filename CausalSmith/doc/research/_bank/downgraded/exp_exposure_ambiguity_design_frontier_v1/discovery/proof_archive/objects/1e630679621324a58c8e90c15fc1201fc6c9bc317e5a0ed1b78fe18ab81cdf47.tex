\textbf{Proof.}
Use trivial actions. Fix \(\alpha\). For every \(z\) with positive fiber diameter, finiteness supplies schedules \(\beta_z,\gamma_z\) in that fiber attaining its maximum and minimum target; give their unordered pair weight one. Use no pair for a zero-diameter cell. The total weight is at most \(|\Omega_n|\). At a fixed \(u\), the pair selected for \(u\), when present, collides there and contributes exactly
\[
 |t_{\beta_u}-t_{\gamma_u}|^2=d_n(u,s_{\alpha u})^2.
\]
All other terms are nonnegative, so the fractional-cover inequality holds. Applying \(\mathrm{thm{:}orbit\text{-}fractional\text{-}cover\text{-}compression}\) with \(\kappa_n\le|\Omega_n|\) proves both displays. This construction concerns the menu-induced sign experiment and uses no ambient-graph property. Nonemptiness of \(\Omega_n\) is guaranteed by \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\). \(\square\)
