For fixed \(\bar p\in(0,1)\) and \(n\in\mathcal N_{\bar p}\), \(\mathsf P_n\) has complete primitive data \((n,\mathcal E,\bar p)\) through \(\Omega_n\), \(\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\), \(s_{\alpha z}\), and \(t_\alpha\). It minimizes \(r\) over \(\pi_z\ge0\), \(w_{zs}\in\mathbb R\), and \(q_{\alpha z}\ge0\), subject to \(\sum_{z\in\Omega_n}\pi_z=1\), \(-2\pi_z\le w_{zs}\le2\pi_z\), \(\sum_{z\in\Omega_n}q_{\alpha z}\le r\) for every \(\alpha\in\mathcal B_n(\mathcal E)\), and the rotated-cone inequalities \(\pi_zq_{\alpha z}\ge(w_{z,s_{\alpha z}}-\pi_zt_\alpha)^2\) for every \((\alpha,z)\). The closed perspective convention forces \(w_{zs}=0\) when \(\pi_z=0\).
