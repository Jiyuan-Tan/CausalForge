For every known graph \(G\) and every supplied nonempty clique witness \(Q\subseteq V_n\), the directly evaluated lower certificate is
\[
R_2(G)\geq\frac{|Q|^2+n-|Q|}{n^2};
\]
a maximum-clique witness yields \(R_2(G)\geq\{\omega(G)^2+n-\omega(G)\}/n^2\).  More generally, the finite clique-partition lower functional is
\[
R_2(G)\geq\max_{\substack{\mathcal P\text{ a partition of }V_n\\G[B]\text{ complete for every }B\in\mathcal P}}\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\left(\frac{|B|}{n}\right)^2.
\]
Neither maximum-clique search nor maximization over clique partitions is claimed to be polynomial time.  On an equal block-clique graph, the computable two-sided functional is
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}
=\inf_{p,A,D}\sup_{u\in\Theta_{b,s}}\mathbb E\!\left[\left\{\delta_{p,A,D}-\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}(Y)\right\}^2\right].
\]
Equality \(R_2(G_{b,s})=V_{b,s}\) is certified only when the two equal-block endpoints agree; for \(b=1\), both equal one.  For \(G_n=K_{\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor}\sqcup(n-\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor)K_1\), the clique lower has order \(n^{-1}\vee n^{2\gamma-2}\), while the displayed published lower functional has order \(n^{-1}\vee n^{\gamma-3/2}\); their genuine lower-bound elbow is \(\gamma=1/2\).
