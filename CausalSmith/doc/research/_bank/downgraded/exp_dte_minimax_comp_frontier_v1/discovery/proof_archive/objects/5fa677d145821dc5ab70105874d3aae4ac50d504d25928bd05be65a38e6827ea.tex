Kandiros, Harshaw, and Sävje (2026, Proposition 2.1, Proposition 2.2, Appendix A.5, and Corollary D.1) provide the published network-risk monotonicity, conflict-graph representation, and displayed \(q=2\) DTE graph-functional bounds.  The clique-partition Rademacher prior adds an explicit all-procedure lower certificate, and the polynomial-clique family shows exactly where it improves that published lower display.  Kandiros, Pipis, Daskalakis, and Harshaw (2024) construct the realizable Conflict Graph Design randomization, modified Horvitz--Thompson estimator, and computable conservative upper guarantees; the present arbitrary-graph result is lower-only and does not turn those guarantees into a matching frontier.  Fatemi and Zheleva (2023) introduce CauseIS, whose maximal-independent-set-based DTE benchmarking can use the supplied-clique certificate.  Gabler (1990) and Rinott (2009) inform the separate equal-block invariant and decision-theoretic calculations, but do not provide this network-conflict posterior obstruction.
