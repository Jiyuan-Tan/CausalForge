The proved arbitrary-graph statement is an all-design, all-estimator Bayes lower bound obtained from any supplied clique partition, together with the witness-based clique-number corollary and the exact \(K_{\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor}\)-plus-isolates rate calibration.  It does not prove a two-sided computational frontier, efficient clique optimization, or polynomial certificate support.  The equal block-clique results remain a separate computable sandwich: exact within the invariant linear class, realizable as an unrestricted upper action, and exact for unrestricted risk only when the finite posterior endpoint matches it; the complete-clique case is exactly calibrated.
