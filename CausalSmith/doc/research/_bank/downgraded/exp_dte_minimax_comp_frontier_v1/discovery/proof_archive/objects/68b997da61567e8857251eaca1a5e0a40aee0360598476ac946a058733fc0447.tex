For every explicitly listed finite prior supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\), posterior cells give a finite, directly auditable Bayes lower certificate; for every explicitly listed realizable design--estimator pair, direct quadratic-risk evaluation gives an auditable upper certificate. On equal block cliques the proved certificates sharpen to
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s},
\]
with a realizable upper procedure, and endpoint equality gives an exact all-estimator saddle. The closed-form Rademacher lower certificate has \(4^b\) atoms, while the posterior-collision support bound contains \(|\mathfrak G_{b,s}|\); neither is uniformly polynomial in \(n\). No universal constant ratio or polynomial-support construction has been proved for arbitrary \(H_{\mathrm{DTE}}(G)\).
