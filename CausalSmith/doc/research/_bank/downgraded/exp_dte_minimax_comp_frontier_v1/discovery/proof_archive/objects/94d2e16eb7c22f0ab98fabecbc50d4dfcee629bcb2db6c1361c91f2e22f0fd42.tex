Take a primal--dual optimum of \(\text{def:mode-sdp-value}\), factor each nonzero dual mode matrix into at most two columns, and embed a column \((a_0,a_1)\) as \((a_0g_0,a_1g_0)\) for the constant mode, \((a_0g_{\mathrm B},a_1g_{\mathrm B})\) for the between-block mode, or \((0,a_1g_{\mathrm W})\) for the within-block mode. Normalize the columns within the two layer balls and absorb their squared scales into their weights. For every active orbit \(c\), replace each embedded vector \(v\) by the collision pair \(u_{c,v}^{\pm}=P_cv\pm(I-P_c)v\), whose members agree on all coordinates revealed by \(S_c\). Close the pairs under \(\mathfrak G_{b,s}\), discard zero-weight duplicates, and determine the nonnegative orbit weights \(q_h\) from the finite posterior-mean and complementary-slackness equations \(B_c(\Pi)=V_{b,s}\) for \(c\in\mathcal C^{\star}\). The resulting atom list is \(\mathcal A_{b,s}^{\star}\) and \(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star}=\sum_hq_h\delta_{u^{(h)}}\).
