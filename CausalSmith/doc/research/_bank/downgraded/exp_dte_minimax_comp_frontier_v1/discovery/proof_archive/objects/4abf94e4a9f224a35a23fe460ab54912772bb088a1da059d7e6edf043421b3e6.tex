\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(m=m_n=\lfloor\rho n\rfloor\). If \(m=0\), then \(G_n\) is the edgeless graph on \(n\) vertices; use the all-singleton clique partition. Its certificate is
\[
n\left(\frac1n\right)^2=\frac1n
=\left(\frac mn\right)^2+\frac{n-m}{n^2}.
\tag{1}
\]
If \(m\geq1\), use the nonempty vertex set of \(K_m\) as one block and each of the \(n-m\) isolated vertices as a singleton block. The clique-partition converse then gives the same formula,
\[
R_2(G_n)\geq
\left(\frac mn\right)^2+(n-m)\left(\frac1n\right)^2.
\tag{2}
\]
Thus (2), interpreted through (1) in the finite initial cases with \(m=0\), holds for every \(n\geq1\). Since \(m/n\to\rho\in(0,1)\), its right side converges to \(\rho^2\) and is therefore \(\Theta(1)\).

It remains to make the published-bound comparison only where the exact graph formulas apply. For every sufficiently large \(n\), one has \(m\geq1\), and then
\[
\alpha(G_n)=n-m+1,
\qquad
d_{\mathrm{avg}}(G_n)=\frac{m(m-1)}n,
\qquad
\lambda(G_n)=m-1.
\tag{3}
\]
Indeed, an independent set contains at most one clique vertex and may contain every isolated vertex; the only edges are the \(\binom m2\) clique edges; and the adjacency spectral radius of this disjoint union is the spectral radius \(m-1\) of \(K_m\). Because \(m/n\to\rho\) and \(1-m/n\to1-\rho>0\), equation (3) yields
\[
\frac1{\alpha(G_n)}=\Theta(n^{-1}),
\qquad
\frac{\sqrt{d_{\mathrm{avg}}(G_n)}}n=\Theta(n^{-1/2}),
\qquad
\frac{\lambda(G_n)+1}{n}=\frac mn=\Theta(1).
\tag{4}
\]
Applying \(\mathrm{lem{:}published\text{-}dte\text{-}graph\text{-}functional\text{-}bounds}\), the published lower display supplies order \(n^{-1/2}\) on this family, whereas (2) supplies order one. Its spectral upper display is also order one, so the new converse and that upper bound agree in order under precisely this fixed-\(\rho\), published \(q=2\) DTE calculation. \(\square\)
