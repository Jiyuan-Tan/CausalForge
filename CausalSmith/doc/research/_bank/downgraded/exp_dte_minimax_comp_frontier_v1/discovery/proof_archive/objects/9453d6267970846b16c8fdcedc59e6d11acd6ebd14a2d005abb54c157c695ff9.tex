The graph, population, and potential-outcome schedule are fixed, and the assignment is the only randomness.  The arbitrary-graph converse permits every randomization law and every measurable estimator.  On equal block cliques, the computable upper action draws the number of all-control blocks, chooses those blocks uniformly, and selects one treated label uniformly in each remaining block; this action is optimal within the invariant linear class and is an unrestricted upper-risk witness.
