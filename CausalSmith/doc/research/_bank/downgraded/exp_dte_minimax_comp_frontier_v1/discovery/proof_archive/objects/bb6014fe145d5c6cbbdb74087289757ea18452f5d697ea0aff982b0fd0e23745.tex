For every known graph \(G\) and every supplied nonempty clique witness \(Q\subseteq V_n\), the directly evaluated four-atom lower certificate is
\[
R_2(G)\geq\frac{|Q|}{n};
\]
a maximum-clique witness yields \(R_2(G)\geq\omega(G)/n\).  Every clique-partition certificate is subordinate because
\[
\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\left(\frac{|B|}{n}\right)^2\leq\frac{\omega(G)}n.
\]
Maximum-clique search is not claimed to be polynomial time.  On an equal block-clique graph, \(\omega(G_{b,s})/n=1/b\), and the computable two-sided functional remains
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}
=\inf_{p,A,D}\sup_{u\in\Theta_{b,s}}\mathbb E\!\left[\left\{\delta_{p,A,D}-\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}(Y)\right\}^2\right].
\]
Equality \(R_2(G_{b,s})=V_{b,s}\) is certified only when the equal-block endpoints agree; for \(b=1\), both equal one.  For \(G_n=K_{\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor}\sqcup(n-\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor)K_1\), \(\omega(G_n)=\max\{1,\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor\}\), so the four-atom lower and the cited spectral upper functional imply \(R_2(G_n)=\Theta(n^{\gamma-1})\) for every \(\gamma\in(0,1]\); the displayed published lower functional has the smaller order \(n^{-1}\vee n^{\gamma-3/2}\).
