For every explicitly listed finite prior supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\), exact enumeration of its finitely many posterior cells gives an auditable Bayes lower certificate.  Every explicitly listed realizable randomization and measurable design--estimator pair gives an auditable upper certificate.

The separately established four-atom rescaled clique certificate gives, for every supplied nonempty clique \(Q\subseteq V_n\),
\[
R_2(G)\geq \frac{|Q|}{n},
\]
and hence
\[
R_2(G)\geq \frac{\omega(G)}{n}.
\]
It dominates the earlier clique-partition certificate because every clique partition \(\mathcal P\) satisfies
\[
\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\frac{|B|^2}{n^2}
\leq
\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\frac{\omega(G)|B|}{n^2}
=\frac{\omega(G)}{n}.
\]
For \(G_n=K_{m_n}\sqcup(n-m_n)K_1\), where \(m_n=\lfloor\rho n^\gamma\rfloor\), the certificate is exactly \(m_n/n\).  Whenever \(n\geq(2/\rho)^{1/\gamma}\),
\[
\frac{\rho}{2}n^{\gamma-1}
\leq \frac{m_n}{n}
\leq \rho n^{\gamma-1},
\]
so the lower endpoint has order \(n^{\gamma-1}\) for every \(\gamma\in(0,1]\), matching the separately cited spectral upper order on this family.

For an equal block-clique graph, \(\omega(G_{b,s})=s\) and \(n=bs\), so this lower endpoint is \(1/b\).  Together with the established finite posterior certificate and invariant-linear upper procedure,
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}.
\]
The upper endpoint is attained by a realizable randomization and measurable estimator, and equality of the two computed endpoints certifies an exact all-estimator saddle.  These finite, directly checkable facts do not imply randomized polynomial-time separation, polynomial support, or a universal-factor upper guarantee for arbitrary graphs.
