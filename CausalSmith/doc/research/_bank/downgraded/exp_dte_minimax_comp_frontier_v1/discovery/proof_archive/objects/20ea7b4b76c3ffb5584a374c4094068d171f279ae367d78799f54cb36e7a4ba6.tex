For every known interference graph \(G\), any supplied clique partition gives an explicit finite-prior lower certificate for the unrestricted \(q=2\) direct-treatment-effect risk, valid against every assignment law and measurable estimator.  In particular, a supplied clique \(Q\) gives \(R_2(G)\geq\{|Q|^2+n-|Q|\}/n^2\), and a maximum-clique witness gives the corresponding \(\omega(G)\) bound; finding that witness efficiently is not claimed.  On \(K_{\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor}\) plus isolated vertices, the clique lower has order \(n^{-1}\vee n^{2\gamma-2}\), producing a genuine elbow at \(\gamma=1/2\) relative to the published displayed lower functional.  Separately, on equal block-clique graphs the finite mode SDP gives the exact invariant-linear value and a realizable upper-risk action, while the finite posterior construction gives a computable unrestricted lower endpoint.  Equality of those equal-block endpoints is claimed only when their finite check succeeds, and the arbitrary-graph finite-saddle question remains open.
