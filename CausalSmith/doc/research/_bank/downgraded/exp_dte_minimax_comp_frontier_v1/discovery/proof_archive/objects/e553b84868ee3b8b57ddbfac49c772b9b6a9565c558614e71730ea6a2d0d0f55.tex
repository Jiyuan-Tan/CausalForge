Fix \(\rho\in(0,1)\), let \(m_n=\lfloor\rho n\rfloor\), and let \(G_n\) be the disjoint union of \(K_{m_n}\) and \(n-m_n\) isolated vertices. The clique-partition certificate obtained from the large clique and singleton blocks is
\[
R_2(G_n)\geq
\left(\frac{m_n}{n}\right)^2+\frac{n-m_n}{n^2}
\longrightarrow\rho^2,
\]
so it is of order one. By contrast, the two terms in the published general DTE lower bound have orders \(n^{-1}\) and \(n^{-1/2}\), while its spectral upper bound is of order one. Thus the new lower certificate is strictly stronger in rate on this family and agrees in order with the cited spectral upper bound for this fixed-\(\rho\), published \(q=2\) DTE calculation only.
