\textbf{Proof.}
Apply \(\mathrm{prop{:}polynomial\text{-}clique\text{-}isolates\text{-}rate\text{-}calibration}\) with \(\gamma=1\).  Its exact finite formula is
\[
R_2(G_n)\geq\frac{m_n^2+n-m_n}{n^2}
=\left(\frac{m_n}{n}\right)^2+\frac{n-m_n}{n^2}.
\]
Because \(m_n/n\to\rho\) and \(0\leq(n-m_n)/n^2\leq1/n\), the right side converges to \(\rho^2\) and is \(\Theta(1)\).  The same proposition gives, at \(\gamma=1\),
\[
\frac1{\alpha(G_n)}=\Theta(n^{-1}),
\qquad
\frac{\sqrt{d_{\mathrm{avg}}(G_n)}}n=\Theta(n^{-1/2}),
\qquad
\frac{\lambda(G_n)+1}{n}=\Theta(1).
\]
Thus the published displayed lower functional is \(\Theta(n^{-1/2})\), whereas the clique certificate and cited spectral upper functional are both \(\Theta(1)\), exactly as claimed. \(\square\)
