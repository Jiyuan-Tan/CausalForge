For every explicitly listed finite prior supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\), exact enumeration of the finitely many posterior cells gives an auditable Bayes lower certificate, and every explicitly listed realizable design--estimator pair gives an auditable upper certificate. The established clique-partition converse gives the computable-witness inequality
\[
R_2(G)\geq\max_{\substack{\mathcal P\text{ a partition of }V_n\\G[B]\text{ complete for every }B\in\mathcal P}}\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\left(\frac{|B|}{n}\right)^2.
\]
For equal block-clique graphs, the established certificates give
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s},
\]
where the upper endpoint is attained by a realizable invariant randomization and measurable estimator. Equality of the two computed endpoints certifies an exact all-estimator saddle, and \(R_2(G_{1,s})=1\). All these certificates are finite and directly checkable, but no polynomial-time, polynomial-support, universal-factor guarantee on arbitrary graphs follows.
