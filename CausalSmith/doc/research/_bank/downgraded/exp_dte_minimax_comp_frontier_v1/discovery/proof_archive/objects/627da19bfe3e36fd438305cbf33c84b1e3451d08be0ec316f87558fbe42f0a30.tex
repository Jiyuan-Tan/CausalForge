For every known graph \(G\), the finite clique-partition lower functional is \[R_2(G)\geq\max_{\substack{\mathcal P\text{ a partition of }V_n\\G[B]\text{ complete for every }B\in\mathcal P}}\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\left(\frac{|B|}{n}\right)^2.\] On an equal block-clique graph, the computable two-sided functional is \[\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}=\inf_{p,A,D}\sup_{u\in\Theta_{b,s}}\mathbb E\!\left[\left\{\delta_{p,A,D}-\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}(Y)\right\}^2\right].\] Equality \(R_2(G_{b,s})=V_{b,s}\) is certified only when the two equal-block endpoints agree; for \(b=1\), both equal one. No polynomial-time guarantee is made for maximizing the arbitrary-graph clique-partition functional.
