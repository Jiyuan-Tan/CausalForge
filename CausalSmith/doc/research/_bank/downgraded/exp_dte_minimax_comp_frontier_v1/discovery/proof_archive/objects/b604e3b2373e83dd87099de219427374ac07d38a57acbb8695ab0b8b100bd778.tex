The combinatorial and symmetry parts are proved.  In each block a maximal compatible set is either all \(s\) control vertices or one treated vertex, giving \((s+1)^b\) sets.  Two such sets are in the same block-and-label permutation orbit exactly when they contain the same number \(c\) of all-control blocks, giving \(b+1\) orbits.  Averaging a design over the finite symmetry group and conditionally averaging its estimator over the preimages of an observation does not increase squared risk by Jensen's inequality, so an optimal invariant design and an invariant measurable estimator exist.  The remaining assertion that some optimal invariant estimator is linear with two coefficients \(A_c,D_c\) is not supplied by symmetry: invariant odd functions of the revealed multiset may be nonlinear.
