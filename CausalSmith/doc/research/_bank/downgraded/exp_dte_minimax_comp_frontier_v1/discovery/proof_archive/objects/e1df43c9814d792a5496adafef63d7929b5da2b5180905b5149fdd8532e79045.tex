Fix \(\rho\in(0,1)\) and \(\gamma\in(0,1]\).  For every \(n\geq1\), let \(m_n=\lfloor\rho n^{\gamma}\rfloor\), and let \(G_n\) be the disjoint union of \(K_{m_n}\) and \(n-m_n\) isolated vertices, interpreting \(K_0\) as the empty graph.  Then \(0\leq m_n\leq n-1\), and the clique-plus-singletons partition, interpreted as the all-singleton partition when \(m_n=0\), gives the exact finite certificate
\[
R_2(G_n)\geq L_n:=\frac{m_n^2+n-m_n}{n^2}.
\]
The exact graph quantities are
\[
\alpha(G_n)=
\begin{cases}
n,&m_n=0,\\
n-m_n+1,&m_n\geq1,
\end{cases}
\qquad
d_{\mathrm{avg}}(G_n)=\frac{m_n(m_n-1)}n,
\qquad
\lambda(G_n)=
\begin{cases}
0,&m_n=0,\\
m_n-1,&m_n\geq1.
\end{cases}
\]
For all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
L_n=\Theta\!\left(n^{-1}\vee n^{2\gamma-2}\right),
\]
the displayed lower functional in \(\mathrm{lem{:}published\text{-}dte\text{-}graph\text{-}functional\text{-}bounds}\) has order
\[
\max\left\{\frac1{\alpha(G_n)},\frac{\sqrt{d_{\mathrm{avg}}(G_n)}}n\right\}
=\Theta\!\left(n^{-1}\vee n^{\gamma-3/2}\right),
\]
and its spectral upper functional has order
\[
\frac{\lambda(G_n)+1}{n}=\Theta\!\left(n^{\gamma-1}\right).
\]
Thus the two lower certificates have the same order for \(0<\gamma\leq1/2\).  For \(1/2<\gamma\leq1\), the clique certificate improves the displayed published lower functional by the factor \(\Theta(n^{\gamma-1/2})\).  It matches the spectral upper functional in order only at \(\gamma=1\); for \(1/2<\gamma<1\), no matching arbitrary-graph upper bound is claimed.
