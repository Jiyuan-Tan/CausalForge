The graph \(H_{b,s}\) has \((s+1)^b\) maximal compatible exposure sets and exactly \(b+1\) block-and-unit permutation orbits \(\mathcal S_0,\ldots,\mathcal S_b\). Joint symmetrization of a design and a measurable estimator over block permutations, within-block label permutations, and simultaneous sign reversal does not increase worst-case squared risk. Consequently an invariant assignment law is parameterized by \(p\), but an invariant measurable estimator need not be linear. Explicitly, when \(b=1\) and \(s=2\), the all-control rule \((u_{1,0},u_{2,0})\mapsto(u_{1,0}+u_{2,0})^3\) is label-invariant and sign-equivariant but is not of the form \(\delta_{p,A,D}\). Thus \(\delta_{p,A,D}\) is an invariant linear subclass, not the entire invariant measurable class.
