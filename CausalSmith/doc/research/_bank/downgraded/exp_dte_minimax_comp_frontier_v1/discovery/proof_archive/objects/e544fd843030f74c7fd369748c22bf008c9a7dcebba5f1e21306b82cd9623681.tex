The minimax rate of estimating the Direct Treatment Effect \(\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}\) on a network \(G\) is bounded as
\[
\max\left\{\frac{1}{|\mathcal I(G)|},\frac{\sqrt{d_{\mathrm{avg}}(G)}}{n}\right\}
\lesssim R(G,\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}},2)
\lesssim\frac{\lambda(G)+1}{n},
\]
where \(\mathcal I(G)\) is the maximum independent set in \(G\), \(d_{\mathrm{avg}}(G)\) is its average degree, and \(\lambda(G)\) is the largest eigenvalue of its adjacency matrix.
