Set \(b=1\), so \(n=s\). We first bound the relaxed SDP from below without assuming rank-one conditional moments. The constant-mode constraint gives
\[
\lambda_0+\lambda_1\ge\operatorname{tr}K_0(W).
\]
Using the displayed formula for \(K_0(W)\) at \(b=1\), its trace is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}K_0(W)
&=2w^0_{00}-2w^0_{01}+w^0_{11}
  +2w^1_{00}+2sw^1_{02}+s^2w^1_{22}\\
&=w^0_{00}+(1,-1,0)W_0(1,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}
  +w^1_{00}+(1,0,s)W_1(1,0,s)^{\mathsf T}\\
&\ge w^0_{00}+w^1_{00}=1.
\end{aligned}
\]
The inequality uses only \(W_0,W_1\succeq0\), and the final equality is the SDP moment normalization. Consequently every feasible point has objective at least one.

For the reverse inequality, take \(p_1=1\), \(D_1=-1/s\), and any value of the unused coefficient \(A_1\). The constant-mode coefficient vector is \((0,-1)\), while the between-block and treated within-block modes are absent. Hence \(K_0=\operatorname{diag}(0,1)\), and \((\lambda_0,\lambda_1)=(0,1)\) is feasible. Therefore
\[
V_{1,s}=1.
\]

The corresponding all-control estimator is \(-s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,0}\). Its error is
\[
-s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,0}
-s^{-1}\sum_i(u_{i,1}-u_{i,0})
=-s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,1}.
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz and the treated-layer moment restriction,
\[
\left(s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,1}\right)^2
\le s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,1}^2\le1.
\]
Equality holds when the treated layer is constant with value one, so this procedure has worst-case risk exactly one.

For the lower certificate, let \(x_0,x_1\) be independent Rademacher variables and set \(u_{i,0}=x_0\) and \(u_{i,1}=x_1\) for every \(i\). Each of the four atoms lies on the boundary of both layer balls. Under the all-control orbit the observation reveals \(x_0\), and the posterior variance of the target \(x_1-x_0\) is \(\operatorname{Var}(x_1)=1\). Under every singleton-treated orbit the observation reveals \(x_1\), and the posterior variance is \(\operatorname{Var}(x_0)=1\). Posterior means are the Bayes actions, so the four-atom prior has posterior risk one in both orbits. Its minimum posterior risk therefore equals \(V_{1,s}\). Applying the conditional endpoint clause of \(\text{thm:block-clique-exact-saddle}\) also gives the unrestricted calibration \(R_2(G_{1,s})=1\).
