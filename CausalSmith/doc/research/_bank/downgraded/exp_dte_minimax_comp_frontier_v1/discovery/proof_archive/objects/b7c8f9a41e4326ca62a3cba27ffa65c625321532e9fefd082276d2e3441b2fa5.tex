For any explicitly listed finite prior \(\Pi\) supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\) and any explicitly listed realizable design--estimator procedure, posterior cells and quadratic risks give finite, directly auditable lower and upper certificates. On the equal block-clique family, \(\text{thm:block-clique-exact-saddle}\) proves the computable sandwich
\[
\min_{0\le c\le b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\le R_2(G_{b,s})\le V_{b,s},
\]
and gives a realizable procedure attaining the upper endpoint. If the finite cell calculation verifies equality of these endpoints, it produces an exact all-estimator saddle on that instance. This is the strongest established certificate: it is instance-conditional, its displayed atom bound is not polynomial uniformly in \(n\), and it provides no universal constant ratio away from a successful endpoint check.
