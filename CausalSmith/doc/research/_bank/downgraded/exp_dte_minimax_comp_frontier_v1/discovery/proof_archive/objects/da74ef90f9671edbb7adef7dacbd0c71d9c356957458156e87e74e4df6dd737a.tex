Set \(b=1\), so \(n=s\).  We first lower-bound the relaxed SDP without assuming rank-one conditional moments.  The constant-mode constraint implies
\[
\lambda_0+\lambda_1\geq\operatorname{tr}K_0(W).
\]
Using the displayed formula for \(K_0(W)\) at \(b=1\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}K_0(W)
&=2w^0_{00}-2w^0_{01}+w^0_{11}
  +2w^1_{00}+2sw^1_{02}+s^2w^1_{22}\\
&=w^0_{00}+(1,-1,0)W_0(1,-1,0)^{\mathsf T}
  +w^1_{00}+(1,0,s)W_1(1,0,s)^{\mathsf T}\\
&\geq w^0_{00}+w^1_{00}=1.
\end{aligned}
\]
The inequality uses \(W_0,W_1\succeq0\), and the last equality is the SDP moment normalization.  Every feasible point therefore has objective at least one.

For the reverse inequality, take \(p_1=1\), \(D_1=-1/s\), and any value of the unused coefficient \(A_1\).  The constant-mode coefficient vector is \((0,-1)\), while the between-block and treated within-block modes are absent.  Hence \(K_0=\operatorname{diag}(0,1)\), and \((\lambda_0,\lambda_1)=(0,1)\) is feasible.  It follows that
\[
V_{1,s}=1.
\]

The associated randomization assigns the unique block entirely to control, and its estimator is \(-s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,0}\).  Its error equals
\[
-\frac1s\sum_i u_{i,0}
-\frac1s\sum_i(u_{i,1}-u_{i,0})
=-\frac1s\sum_i u_{i,1}.
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz and the treated-layer moment restriction,
\[
\left(\frac1s\sum_i u_{i,1}\right)^2
\leq\frac1s\sum_i u_{i,1}^2
\leq1.
\]
Equality holds when the treated layer is constant with value one, so the worst-case risk is exactly one.

For the lower certificate, let \(x_0,x_1\) be independent Rademacher variables and set \(u_{i,0}=x_0\), \(u_{i,1}=x_1\) for every \(i\).  Each of the four atoms satisfies both layer constraints with equality.  Under the all-control orbit the observation reveals \(x_0\), and the posterior variance of the target \(x_1-x_0\) is \(\operatorname{Var}(x_1)=1\).  Under every singleton-treated orbit the observation reveals \(x_1\), and the posterior variance is \(\operatorname{Var}(x_0)=1\).  Posterior means are the Bayes actions, so the four-atom prior has posterior risk one on every maximal orbit.  The conditional endpoint clause of the block-clique theorem then yields the sanity check \(R_2(G_{1,s})=V_{1,s}=1\).
