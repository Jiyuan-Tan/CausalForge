\textbf{Construction and support.}
Let \((\xi_{r,k}:1\leq r\leq b,\ k\in\{0,1\})\) be mutually independent variables with
\[
\Pr(\xi_{r,k}=1)=\Pr(\xi_{r,k}=-1)=\frac12,
\]
and set \(u_{i,k}=\xi_{r,k}\) whenever \(i\in B_r\). There are \(2^{2b}=4^b\) equally weighted atoms. For either layer \(k\),
\[
\frac1n\sum_{i\in V_n}u_{i,k}^2
=\frac1{bs}\sum_{r=1}^b\sum_{i\in B_r}\xi_{r,k}^2
=1.
\]
Hence every atom belongs to \(\Theta_{b,s}\). By the target-coordinate reduction in \(\text{lem:published-conflict-calibration}\), each atom can be extended to a schedule in \(\mathcal M_2(G_{b,s})\): assign these values to the two target local-exposure patterns and assign a common fixed value, say zero, to every other local-exposure pattern. This extension obeys arbitrary neighborhood interference and keeps all non-target observations uninformative about the atom.

\textbf{Posterior risk on maximal patterns.}
For every atom,
\[
\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}(Y)
=\frac1{bs}\sum_{r=1}^b\sum_{i\in B_r}(\xi_{r,1}-\xi_{r,0})
=\frac1b\sum_{r=1}^b(\xi_{r,1}-\xi_{r,0}).
\]
Fix a maximal pattern in \(\mathcal S_c\), with all-control block set \(C\). If \(r\in C\), the observation reveals \(\xi_{r,0}\) and leaves \(\xi_{r,1}\) unobserved. If \(r\notin C\), the isolated-treated observation reveals \(\xi_{r,1}\) and leaves \(\xi_{r,0}\) unobserved. Exactly one independent Rademacher sign therefore remains unobserved in every block. Since conditioning on the other independent signs does not alter their variances,
\[
\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}(Y)\mid S_c,u_{S_c}\right)
=\frac1{b^2}\sum_{r=1}^b1
=\frac1b.
\]
Under squared loss the posterior mean minimizes conditional risk, and its minimized risk is this conditional variance. The value is independent of the observed signs and selected labels, so the posterior risk is exactly \(1/b\) on every member of every orbit \(\mathcal S_c\).

\textbf{Arbitrary binary assignments.}
Within a block, a binary assignment reveals the control target coordinates precisely when the block is all control, reveals one treated target coordinate precisely when the block has exactly one treated unit, and reveals no target coordinate when at least two units are treated. Because the prior is constant within each block and layer, the target observation reveals, respectively, \(\xi_{r,0}\), \(\xi_{r,1}\), or neither sign. It never reveals both signs in the same block. Thus at least one independent sign, entering the target with coefficient of absolute value \(1/b\), remains unobserved in every block. Conditional on the target data from any assignment \(z\),
\[
\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}(Y)\mid z,u_{R_z}\right)
=\frac{\#\{\text{unrevealed signs}\}}{b^2}
\geq\frac b{b^2}
=\frac1b.
\]
The fixed non-target outcomes add no information. Equivalently, a nonmaximal target observation is weakly less informative than a realizable maximal extension, and the latter has risk \(1/b\).

For every assignment law and measurable estimator, integrated squared error under this prior is at least the expected posterior variance and hence at least \(1/b\). The supremum over admissible schedules dominates this Bayes risk. Taking the infimum over assignment laws and estimators yields
\[
R_2(G_{b,s})\geq\frac1b.
\]
The theorem \(\text{thm:block-clique-exact-saddle}\) independently supplies
\[
\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}.
\]
Taking the maximum of the two valid lower endpoints proves
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}.
\]
No equality of the two endpoints is used.
