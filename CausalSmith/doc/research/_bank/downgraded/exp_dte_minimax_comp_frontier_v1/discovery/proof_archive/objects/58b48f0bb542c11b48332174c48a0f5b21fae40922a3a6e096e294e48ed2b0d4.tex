For every explicitly listed finite prior supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\), posterior-cell enumeration gives a finite, directly auditable Bayes lower certificate. For every explicitly listed realizable design--estimator pair, direct risk evaluation gives an auditable upper certificate. On equal block cliques the proved certificates give
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s},
\]
with a realizable upper procedure; equality of the computed endpoints certifies an exact all-estimator saddle, and \(R_2(G_{1,s})=1\). The closed-form Rademacher certificate has \(4^b\) atoms, while the posterior-collision support bound contains \(|\mathfrak G_{b,s}|\), so neither construction is uniformly polynomial in \(n\).
