\textbf{Established partial result and finite-population boundary check.}
For every explicitly listed finite prior supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\), exact enumeration of its finitely many posterior cells gives an auditable Bayes lower certificate.  Every explicitly listed realizable randomization and measurable design--estimator pair gives an auditable upper certificate.

By \(\text{prop:rescaled-clique-four-atom-certificate}\), every supplied nonempty clique \(Q\subseteq V_n\) yields
\[
R_2(G)\geq \frac{|Q|}{n},
\]
and maximizing this certificate over cliques gives
\[
R_2(G)\geq \frac{\omega(G)}{n}.
\]
This certificate dominates every clique-partition certificate because \(|B|\leq\omega(G)\) for each \(B\in\mathcal P\), while the blocks partition \(V_n\); hence
\[
\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\frac{|B|^2}{n^2}
\leq
\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\frac{\omega(G)|B|}{n^2}
=\frac{\omega(G)}{n}.
\]

Now let
\[
G_n=K_{m_n}\sqcup(n-m_n)K_1,
\qquad
m_n=\lfloor\rho n^\gamma\rfloor,
\]
where \(n\geq1\), \(\rho\in(0,1)\), and \(\gamma\in(0,1]\).  The finite population is nonempty.  If \(m_n=0\), then \(G_n\) consists of \(n\) isolated vertices and a singleton is a clique, so \(\omega(G_n)=1\).  If \(m_n\geq1\), the displayed complete component is a maximum clique, so \(\omega(G_n)=m_n\).  Therefore the supplied-clique certificate is exactly
\[
\frac{\omega(G_n)}{n}
=\frac{\max\{1,m_n\}}{n}
\qquad(n\geq1).
\]
In particular, it is not \(m_n/n\) at the boundary \(m_n=0\).  Whenever \(n\geq(2/\rho)^{1/\gamma}\), one has \(\rho n^\gamma\geq2\), and the elementary inequality \(\lfloor x\rfloor\geq x/2\) for \(x\geq2\) gives
\[
\frac{\rho}{2}n^{\gamma-1}
\leq \frac{m_n}{n}
=\frac{\max\{1,m_n\}}{n}
\leq \rho n^{\gamma-1}.
\]
Thus the finite correction preserves the conclusion that the clique lower endpoint is \(\Theta(n^{\gamma-1})\) for every fixed \(\rho\in(0,1)\) and \(\gamma\in(0,1]\).  By \(\text{prop:polynomial-clique-isolates-four-atom-calibration}\), this order matches the cited spectral upper functional on this family.

For an equal block-clique graph, \(\omega(G_{b,s})=s\) and \(n=bs\), so the same lower endpoint equals \(1/b\).  Combining it with \(\text{thm:block-clique-exact-saddle}\) gives the directly computable sandwich
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}.
\]
The upper endpoint is attained by a realizable randomization and measurable estimator, and equality of the two computed endpoints certifies an exact all-estimator saddle.  These finite, directly checkable facts do not establish randomized polynomial-time separation, polynomial support for both certificates, a two-sided value-preserving reduction, or a universal-factor upper guarantee on arbitrary DTE conflict graphs.  Those requirements remain open.
