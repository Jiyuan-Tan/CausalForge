For every explicitly listed finite prior supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\), exact posterior-cell enumeration gives an auditable Bayes lower certificate, and every explicitly listed realizable design--estimator pair gives an auditable upper certificate. Moreover, the established clique-partition converse yields \[R_2(G)\ge \max_{\substack{\mathcal P\text{ a partition of }V_n\\G[B]\text{ complete for every }B\in\mathcal P}}\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\left(\frac{|B|}{n}\right)^2.\] On equal block-clique graphs, the established certificates give \[\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\le c\le b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}\le R_2(G_{b,s})\le V_{b,s},\] where the upper endpoint is attained by a realizable invariant randomization and measurable estimator. Equality of the computed endpoints certifies an exact all-estimator saddle, and \(R_2(G_{1,s})=1\). These statements are finite and checkable, but their displayed support bounds and optimization domains do not imply polynomial time or polynomial support on arbitrary graphs.
