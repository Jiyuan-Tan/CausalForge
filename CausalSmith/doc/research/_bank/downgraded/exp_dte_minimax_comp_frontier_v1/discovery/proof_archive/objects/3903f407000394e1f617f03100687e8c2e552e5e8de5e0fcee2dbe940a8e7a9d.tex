Set \(b=1\), so \(n=s\).  First prove the SDP lower bound.  The constant-mode constraint implies
\[
\lambda_0+\lambda_1\ge\operatorname{tr}K_0(W).
\]
For \(c=0\), the first constant-mode coefficient is fixed at one; for \(c=1\), the second is fixed at minus one.  Since each conditional contribution is an outer product, its trace is at least \(w^c_{00}\).  Therefore
\[
\operatorname{tr}K_0(W)ge w^0_{00}+w^1_{00}=1,
\]
and every feasible point has objective at least one.

For the reverse inequality, take \(p_1=1\), \(D_1=-1/s\), and any value of the unused coefficient \(A_1\).  The constant-mode coefficient vector is \((0,-1)\), while the between-block and treated within-block modes are absent.  Thus \(K_0=\operatorname{diag}(0,1)\), and \((\lambda_0,\lambda_1)=(0,1)\) is feasible.  Hence \(V_{1,s}=1\).

The corresponding all-control estimator is \(-s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,0}\).  Its error is
\[
-s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,0}
-s^{-1}\sum_i(u_{i,1}-u_{i,0})
=-s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,1}.
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz and the treated-layer moment restriction,
\[
\left(s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,1}\right)^2
\le s^{-1}\sum_i u_{i,1}^2\le1.
\]
Thus the estimator attains worst-case risk at most one, and equality is attained by a layer-constant treated vector.

Finally, let \(x_0,x_1\) be independent Rademacher variables and set \(u_{i,0}=x_0\), \(u_{i,1}=x_1\) for every \(i\).  Every atom lies on the boundary of both layer balls.  Under the all-control orbit, the data reveal \(x_0\), so the posterior variance of \(x_1-x_0\) is \(\operatorname{Var}(x_1)=1\).  Under any singleton-treated orbit, the data reveal \(x_1\), so the posterior variance is \(\operatorname{Var}(x_0)=1\).  The posterior means are the Bayes actions, hence the four-atom prior has posterior risk one in both orbits.  This proves all three assertions and, as a separate sanity check, certifies the unrestricted value one without using the defective general lift.
