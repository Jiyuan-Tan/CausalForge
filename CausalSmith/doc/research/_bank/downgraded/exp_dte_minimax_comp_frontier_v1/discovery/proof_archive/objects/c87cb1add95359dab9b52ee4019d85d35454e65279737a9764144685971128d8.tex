A clique partition \(\mathcal P\) of \(G\) is a partition of \(V_n\) into nonempty blocks \(B\) such that every block induces a clique of \(G\). Its spanning clique subgraph is
\[
V(H_{\mathcal P})=V_n,
\qquad
E(H_{\mathcal P})=\bigcup_{B\in\mathcal P}\binom{B}{2}.
\]
In particular, \(H_{\mathcal P}\subseteq G\), and singleton blocks are allowed.
