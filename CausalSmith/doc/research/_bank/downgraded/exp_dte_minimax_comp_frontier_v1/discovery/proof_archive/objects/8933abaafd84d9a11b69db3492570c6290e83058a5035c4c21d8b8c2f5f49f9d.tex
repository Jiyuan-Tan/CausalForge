Let \(G\) be any simple graph on \(V_n\).  For every supplied nonempty clique \(Q\subseteq V_n\),
\[
R_2(G)\geq\frac{|Q|^2+n-|Q|}{n^2}.
\]
In particular, if \(Q\) is a maximum clique, then \(|Q|=\omega(G)\) and
\[
R_2(G)\geq\frac{\omega(G)^2+n-\omega(G)}{n^2}.
\]
The first bound is directly computable from the supplied witness \(Q\); no efficient algorithm for finding a maximum clique or evaluating \(\omega(G)\) is asserted.
