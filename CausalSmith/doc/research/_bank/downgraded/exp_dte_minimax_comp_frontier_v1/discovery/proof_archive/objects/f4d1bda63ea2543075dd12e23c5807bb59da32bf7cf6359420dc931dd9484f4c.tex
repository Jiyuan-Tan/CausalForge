The certificate measures unavoidable missing target-layer information rather than estimator bias: every clique block retains at least one independent sign after any assignment.  A clique of size \(q\) alone forces risk at least \((q^2+n-q)/n^2\).  For \(q\asymp n^{\gamma}\), this obstruction overtakes the published displayed lower functional only above \(\gamma=1/2\), by the factor \(n^{\gamma-1/2}\), and reaches the available spectral-upper order only at \(\gamma=1\).  Thus CauseIS comparisons on graphs with a supplied dense clique should report this lower benchmark without treating it as an efficiently optimized or two-sided minimax value.
