For every explicitly listed finite prior supported on \(\Theta_2(G)\), posterior-cell enumeration gives an auditable Bayes lower certificate, and every explicitly listed realizable design--estimator pair can be evaluated for an auditable upper certificate. The clique-partition result supplies the arbitrary-graph lower endpoint
\[
R_2(G)\geq
\max_{\substack{\mathcal P\text{ a clique partition of }G}}
\sum_{B\in\mathcal P}\left(\frac{|B|}{n}\right)^2,
\]
but its Rademacher prior may have \(4^{|\mathcal P|}\) atoms and no polynomial-time method for optimizing over clique partitions is claimed. On equal block cliques the proved certificates give
\[
\max\left\{\frac1b,\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\right\}
\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s},
\]
with a realizable upper procedure; equality of the computed endpoints certifies an exact all-estimator saddle, and \(R_2(G_{1,s})=1\). The posterior-collision support bound contains \(|\mathfrak G_{b,s}|\). Thus neither the lower constructions nor the upper construction yields the requested polynomial-support, polynomial-time, universal-constant certificate pair on arbitrary conflict graphs.
