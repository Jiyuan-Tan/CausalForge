\textbf{Construction and support.} Let \((\xi_{r,k}:1\leq r\leq b,\ k\in\{0,1\})\) be mutually independent variables with
\[
\Pr(\xi_{r,k}=1)=\Pr(\xi_{r,k}=-1)=\frac12,
\]
and, on each atom, set \(u_{i,k}=\xi_{r,k}\) for \(i\in B_r\). There are \(2^{2b}=4^b\) equally weighted atoms. For either layer \(k\),
\[
\frac1n\sum_{i\in V_n}u_{i,k}^2
=\frac1{bs}\sum_{r=1}^b\sum_{i\in B_r}\xi_{r,k}^2
=1.
\]
Hence every atom belongs to \(\Theta_{b,s}\) by the separate-layer Euclidean constraints. By \(\text{lem:published-conflict-calibration}\), these target vectors can be realized by schedules in the published \(q=2\) arbitrary-neighborhood-interference class, with every non-target outcome fixed, so the prior is admissible for the unrestricted minimax game.

\textbf{Posterior risk on a maximal orbit.} For every atom the causal target is
\[
\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}
=\frac1{bs}\sum_{r=1}^b\sum_{i\in B_r}(\xi_{r,1}-\xi_{r,0})
=\frac1b\sum_{r=1}^b(\xi_{r,1}-\xi_{r,0}).
\]
Fix a maximal pattern in \(\mathcal S_c\), with all-control block set \(C\). If \(r\in C\), the observation reveals \(\xi_{r,0}\) and leaves \(\xi_{r,1}\) unobserved. If \(r\notin C\), its isolated-treated observation reveals \(\xi_{r,1}\) and leaves \(\xi_{r,0}\) unobserved. Thus exactly one independent Rademacher sign remains unobserved in every block. Since revealed sign values do not change the variances of the remaining independent signs,
\[
\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}\mid S_c,u_{S_c}\right)
=\frac1{b^2}\sum_{r=1}^b1
=\frac1b.
\]
Under squared loss the posterior mean minimizes conditional risk, and the minimized risk is the displayed conditional variance. It is independent of the observed values and of the selected labels, so the posterior risk equals \(1/b\) on every member of every orbit \(\mathcal S_c\).

\textbf{Arbitrary binary assignments.} Within a block, a binary assignment reveals all control target coordinates only when the block is all control, reveals one treated target coordinate only when the block has exactly one treated unit, and reveals no target coordinate when the block has at least two treated units. Because the prior is block-constant within each layer, the target data reveal respectively \(\xi_{r,0}\), \(\xi_{r,1}\), or neither sign. They never reveal both signs from the same block. Therefore at least one independent sign contributing with coefficient of absolute value \(1/b\) remains unobserved in every block. Conditional on the target data from any binary assignment \(z\),
\[
\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\tau_{\mathrm{DTE}}\mid z,u_{R_z}\right)
=\frac{\#\{\text{unrevealed signs}\}}{b^2}
\geq\frac b{b^2}
=\frac1b.
\]
Equivalently, any nonmaximal target observation is weakly less informative than a realizable maximal extension, whose risk was computed above. Non-target outcomes are fixed across the prior and add no information.

For every assignment law and every measurable estimator, its integrated squared error under this prior is at least the expected posterior variance, hence at least \(1/b\). The supremum over admissible schedules dominates this Bayes risk; taking the infimum over designs and estimators gives
\[
R_2(G_{b,s})\geq\frac1b.
\]
The block-clique theorem separately gives
\[
\min_{0\leq c\leq b}B_c(\Pi_{b,s}^{\star})\leq R_2(G_{b,s})\leq V_{b,s}.
\]
Taking the maximum of the two valid lower endpoints proves the claimed sandwich.
