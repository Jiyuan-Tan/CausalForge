Let \(k=(2,2)\) and let the sole retained comparison cohort be \(c=3\).  The construction specifies a full-data law because every potential-outcome coordinate is the measurable random variable \(U_{it}/8\), the observed outcome is its consistency-selected coordinate, and the remaining variables have the displayed joint law.

First consider the cell probabilities.  Since \(X_i=x_0\) almost surely and \((G_i,S_i)\) is uniform on the four retained cells, for every \((a,s)\in\{2,3\}\times\{0,1\}\),
\[
p_{a,s}(x_0)=Q_0(G_i=a,S_i=s\mid X_i=x_0)=\frac14,
\qquad
\pi_{2,1}=Q_0(G_i=2,S_i=1)=\frac14.
\]
Thus every cell entering the pairwise score has conditional probability \(1/4>\varepsilon=1/8\).  In particular, all denominators in the score are positive.

We next verify the causal restrictions defining \(\mathcal M\).  Consistency holds because \(Y_{it}\) was set equal to \(Y_{it}(G_i,S_i)\).  For every enabling date \(a\), eligibility state \(s\), and time \(t\),
\[
Y_{it}(a,s)=\frac{U_{it}}8=Y_{it}(\infty,s).
\]
This identity implies no anticipation for eligible potential outcomes and no enabling effect on ineligible potential outcomes.  For the only retained group-period and comparison pair, independence of \((U_{i1},U_{i2})\) from \((G_i,S_i,X_i)\) gives, for every retained cell,
\[
E_{Q_0}\!\left[Y_{i2}(\infty,s)-Y_{i1}(\infty,s)
 \mid G_i=a,S_i=s,X_i=x_0\right]
=E\!\left[\frac{U_{i2}-U_{i1}}8\right]=0.
\]
Hence the eligible-minus-ineligible untreated trend is zero for both \(a=2\) and \(a=3\), which is exactly the sole pairwise conditional DDD restriction.  The baseline used here is the observed period \(g-1=1\).

The primitive moment bound also holds.  Indeed, almost surely,
\[
\max_{t\in\{1,2,3\}}|Y_{it}|^4=8^{-4}\le 1=C_4.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\Delta Y_{i,k}=\frac{U_{i2}-U_{i1}}8,
\qquad
m_{a,s,k}(x_0)=E_{Q_0}[\Delta Y_{i,k}\mid G_i=a,S_i=s,X_i=x_0]=0.
\]
Thus the true regressions have fourth moment zero, and the corresponding conditional residual fourth moment is finite; explicitly,
\[
E_{Q_0}\!\left[|\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(x_0)|^4
 \mid G_i=a,S_i=s,X_i=x_0\right]
=\frac{E[(U_{i2}-U_{i1})^4]}{8^4}
=\frac{8}{8^4}=\frac1{512}.
\]

It remains to check the score-spectrum restriction.  The four zero conditional means imply
\[
d_{k,3}(x_0)=0,
\qquad
\Theta_k(P_0)=0.
\]
On the event \(\{G_i=a,S_i=s\}\), the pairwise-score formula therefore reduces to
\[
R_{k,3}(O_i;\eta_0)
=\frac{p_{2,1}(x_0)}{\pi_{2,1}}
  \sigma_{a,s}^{2,3}\frac{1}{p_{a,s}(x_0)}\Delta Y_{i,k}
=4\sigma_{a,s}^{2,3}\frac{U_{i2}-U_{i1}}8.
\]
This variable has mean zero.  Since \(B\Theta(P_0)=0\), the sole centered-score coordinate is the same variable.  Each realized cell has \((\sigma_{a,s}^{2,3})^2=1\), while independence and the Rademacher moments give
\[
E[(U_{i2}-U_{i1})^2]
=E[U_{i2}^2]+E[U_{i1}^2]-2E[U_{i2}]E[U_{i1}]
=2.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\Omega
=E_{P_0}[\Psi(O_i)^2]
=\frac{16}{64}E[(U_{i2}-U_{i1})^2]
=\frac12.
\]
Here \(M=1\), so with \(\kappa=1/2\),
\[
\kappa I_1=\frac12 I_1\preceq \Omega=\frac12 I_1
\preceq 2I_1=\kappa^{-1}I_1.
\]
All member conditions in the definition of \(\mathcal M\) are therefore satisfied by \(Q_0\).  Hence \(Q_0\in\mathcal M\), and the primitive law class is nonempty.
