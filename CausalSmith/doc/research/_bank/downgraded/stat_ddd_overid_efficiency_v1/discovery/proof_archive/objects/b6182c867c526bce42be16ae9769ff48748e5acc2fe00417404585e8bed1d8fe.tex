Fix \(F\), \(T\), \(\mathcal K\), and \(M\). For every \(Q\in\mathcal M\) with \(P=Q_O\), impose the theorem-level foldwise independence, propensity and regression \(L_4(P_X)\) rates, and product-rate condition. No separate true- or fitted-regression envelope is imposed: the score-expansion lemma derives the required envelopes from the primitive outcome fourth moment, overlap, and the regression error rate. That lemma gives \(D_{i,j}=\widehat\Psi_{i,j}-\Psi_j(O_i)\) with \(\max_j n^{-1}\sum_iD_{i,j}^2=o_P(1)\). For every \(1\le j,q\le M\), \[\widehat\Omega^{\mathrm{raw}}_{jq}-\Omega_{jq}=\left\{n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi_j(O_i)\Psi_q(O_i)-\Omega_{jq}\right\}+n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nD_{i,j}\widehat\Psi_{i,q}+n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi_j(O_i)D_{i,q}.\] The oracle empirical-covariance fluctuation is \(o_P(1)\) by i.i.d. sampling and the derived fourth-moment bound. Cauchy--Schwarz gives \[\begin{aligned}|\widehat\Omega^{\mathrm{raw}}_{jq}-\Omega_{jq}|&\le\left|n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi_j(O_i)\Psi_q(O_i)-\Omega_{jq}\right|\\&\quad+\left(n^{-1}\sum_iD_{i,j}^2\right)^{1/2}\left(n^{-1}\sum_i\widehat\Psi_{i,q}^2\right)^{1/2}+\left(n^{-1}\sum_i\Psi_j(O_i)^2\right)^{1/2}\left(n^{-1}\sum_iD_{i,q}^2\right)^{1/2}=o_P(1).\end{aligned}\] Since \(M\) is fixed, \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le M\max_{j,q}|A_{jq}|\) passes entrywise convergence to operator norm. Because \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^M\) and positive-semidefinite projection is nonexpansive in Frobenius norm, \[\|\widehat\Omega-\Omega\|_{\mathrm{op}}=o_P(1),\qquad \|\widehat\Omega_\lambda-\Omega\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\|\widehat\Omega-\Omega\|_{\mathrm{op}}+\lambda_n=o_P(1).\]
