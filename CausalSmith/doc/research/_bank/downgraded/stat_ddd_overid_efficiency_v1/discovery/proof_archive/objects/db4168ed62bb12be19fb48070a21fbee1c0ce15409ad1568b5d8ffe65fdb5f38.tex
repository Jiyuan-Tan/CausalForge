For each \(k\), the definition of the componentwise loading gives
\[
\mathbf 1^{\prime}b_k^{\mathrm{sep}}=\omega_k.
\]
Stacking over blocks yields \(B^{\prime}b^{\mathrm{sep}}=\omega\), so \(b^{\mathrm{sep}}\) is feasible for the joint constrained problem. The columns of \(N\) are linearly independent and satisfy \(B^{\prime}N=0\). Because the nonempty, disjoint row supports of the columns of \(B\) give \(\operatorname{rank}(B)=|\mathcal K|\), and because \(N\) has \(M-|\mathcal K|\) columns, its columns form a basis of \(\ker(B^{\prime})\). Consequently every feasible loading has a unique representation
\[
b=b^{\mathrm{sep}}+N\gamma.
\]
Substitution into the quadratic criterion gives
\[
b^{\prime}\Omega b
=V_{\mathrm{sep}}
+2\gamma^{\prime}N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}
+\gamma^{\prime}N^{\prime}\Omega N\gamma.
\]
If \(N\) has positive column dimension, the score-spectrum assumption and the full column rank of \(N\) make \(N^{\prime}\Omega N\) positive definite. Completing the square gives the unique minimizer
\[
\gamma_*=-\bigl(N^{\prime}\Omega N\bigr)^{-1}N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}.
\]
If \(N\) has zero columns, the feasible set is the singleton \(\{b^{\mathrm{sep}}\}\), and the corresponding quadratic is interpreted as zero. In both cases Theorem \(\ref{thm:joint-system-limit}\) identifies the constrained minimizer as \(b^{\mathrm{joint}}\). Evaluating the completed square therefore gives
\[
V_{\mathrm{sep}}-V_{\mathrm{joint}}
=(N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}})^{\prime}
 (N^{\prime}\Omega N)^{-1}
 (N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}).
\]

The constrained first-order condition is \(N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{joint}}=0\). Since \(b^{\mathrm{sep}}-b^{\mathrm{joint}}\in\operatorname{col}(N)\), the cross term in the \(\Omega\)-norm expansion vanishes, and hence
\[
V_{\mathrm{sep}}-V_{\mathrm{joint}}
=(b^{\mathrm{sep}}-b^{\mathrm{joint}})^{\prime}
 \Omega(b^{\mathrm{sep}}-b^{\mathrm{joint}}).
\]
Positive definiteness of \(\Omega\) shows that the gap is zero if and only if \(N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}=0\), equivalently if and only if \(b^{\mathrm{sep}}=b^{\mathrm{joint}}\).

For a vector \(z\) partitioned conformably with the comparison sets, the canonical definition of \(N_k\) gives \(N_k^{\prime}z_k=0\) exactly when every coordinate of \(z_k\) equals its final coordinate. Taking \(z=\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}\) proves the blockwise-constancy criterion. Moreover,
\[
\Omega_{kk}b_k^{\mathrm{sep}}
=\frac{\omega_k}{\mathbf 1^{\prime}\Omega_{kk}^{-1}\mathbf 1}\mathbf 1,
\]
so the within-block contribution is already constant. Therefore nonconstancy of
\[
(\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}})_k
=\Omega_{kk}b_k^{\mathrm{sep}}
+\sum_{\ell\ne k}\Omega_{k\ell}b_\ell^{\mathrm{sep}}
\]
is exactly nonconstancy of the net cross-block covariance contribution across the comparison coordinates of block \(k\). This is the actual strict-gain mechanism; no covariate-varying conditional-precision premise is used.

Now fix a law and supplied learner sequences satisfying the pointwise conditions of Theorem \(\ref{thm:joint-system-limit}\). The score and covariance lemmas give
\[
\sqrt n\{\widehat\mu-B\Theta(P)\}
=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi(O_i)+o_P(1),
\qquad
\widehat\Omega_\lambda\to_P\Omega,
\qquad
\widehat\Omega\to_P\Omega.
\]
Each principal block \(\Omega_{kk}\) is positive definite. Continuity of block inversion and of its strictly positive normalizer gives \(\widehat b^{\mathrm{sep}}\to_Pb^{\mathrm{sep}}\), while the block definitions give \(B^{\prime}\widehat b^{\mathrm{sep}}=\omega\) exactly. Thus
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt n\{\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{sep}}-\Theta_\omega^{\mathrm{agg}}(P)\}
&=(\widehat b^{\mathrm{sep}})^{\prime}
  \sqrt n\{\widehat\mu-B\Theta(P)\}\\
&=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n
  (b^{\mathrm{sep}})^{\prime}\Psi(O_i)+o_P(1).
\end{aligned}
\]
Let \(Z_i=(b^{\mathrm{sep}})^{\prime}\Psi(O_i)\). Independent sampling and causal identification give independent, identically distributed, centered summands with variance \(V_{\mathrm{sep}}\). The moment derivation in Lemma \(\ref{lem:cross-fitted-score-expansion}\) gives \(E_P|Z_i|^4<\infty\). For every \(r>0\),
\[
E_P[Z_i^2\mathbf 1\{|Z_i|>r\sqrt n\}]
\le\frac{E_P[Z_i^4]}{r^2n}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Lemma \(\ref{lem:lindeberg-feller-central-limit}\) and the same remainder sandwich as in Theorem \(\ref{thm:joint-system-limit}\) yield
\[
\sqrt n\{\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{sep}}-\Theta_\omega^{\mathrm{agg}}(P)\}
\rightsquigarrow N(0,V_{\mathrm{sep}}).
\]
Theorem \(\ref{thm:joint-system-limit}\) supplies the corresponding pointwise limit with variance \(V_{\mathrm{joint}}\). Thus the population quadratic is the exact difference between the two asymptotic variances within the fixed constant score span.

Loading and covariance consistency now imply
\[
\widehat\Delta
=(\widehat b^{\mathrm{sep}}-\widehat b^{\mathrm{joint}})^{\prime}
 \widehat\Omega
 (\widehat b^{\mathrm{sep}}-\widehat b^{\mathrm{joint}})
\to_P
(b^{\mathrm{sep}}-b^{\mathrm{joint}})^{\prime}
 \Omega(b^{\mathrm{sep}}-b^{\mathrm{joint}})
=V_{\mathrm{sep}}-V_{\mathrm{joint}}.
\]
Because \(\widehat\Omega\) is positive semidefinite, \(\widehat\Delta\ge0\) for every realized sample. This establishes only descriptive consistency, not a rate or inferential procedure for the gap.

Finally, if \(\mathcal K=\{k\}\), then \(\omega_k=1\) and
\[
\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}
=\frac{\mathbf 1}{\mathbf 1^{\prime}\Omega^{-1}\mathbf 1},
\]
which is constant in the sole block. Hence \(N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}=0\), the loadings coincide, and the variance gap is zero. If \(g=2\), the outcome-change definition makes every relevant baseline \(g-1=1\). The constrained minimization used above is standard finite-dimensional GMM/SUR algebra; the result proved here is its exact mapping to the shared-cell staggered-DDD score system and its feasible descriptive diagnostic.
