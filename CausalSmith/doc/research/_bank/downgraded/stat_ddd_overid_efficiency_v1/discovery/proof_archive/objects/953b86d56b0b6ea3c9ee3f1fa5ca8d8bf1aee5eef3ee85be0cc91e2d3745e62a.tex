Fix \(F\), \(T\), \(\mathcal K\), and \(M\). For every \(Q\in\mathcal M\) with \(P=Q_O\), suppose the off-fold learners satisfy foldwise independence, \(\max_{f,a,s}\|\widehat p_{a,s,-f}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n)\), \(\max_{f,a,s,k}\|\widehat m_{a,s,k,-f}-m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(b_n)\), \(a_n\to0\), \(b_n\to0\), and \(a_nb_n=o(n^{-1/2})\). No separate true- or fitted-regression fourth-moment envelope is imposed. Conditional Jensen's inequality, the primitive outcome fourth-moment bound, and cell overlap imply, for every retained cell, \[\|m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}^4\le \frac{16C_4}{\varepsilon},\qquad \|\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)\|_{P(\cdot\mid G_i=a,S_i=s),4}^4\le\frac{256C_4}{\varepsilon}.\] The regression error rate and the triangle inequality then imply \(\max_{f,a,s,k}\|\widehat m_{a,s,k,-f}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(1)\). Four disjoint cells enter each retained pairwise score, so overlap and \(0<\varepsilon<1/4\) give \(p_{a,s}(x)\in[\varepsilon,1-3\varepsilon]\subset[\varepsilon/2,1-\varepsilon/2]\) for every retained cell. Hence \(C_\varepsilon(p_{a,s})=p_{a,s}\), the clipping map is one-Lipschitz, and \[\|\widetilde p_{a,s,-f}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}\le\|\widehat p_{a,s,-f}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n).\] For each residual term, clipping, the Lipschitz bound for \((u,v)\mapsto u/v\) on \([\varepsilon/2,1-\varepsilon/2]^2\), and H\"older's inequality yield \[\left\|\mathbf 1_{a,s}\left(\frac{\widetilde p_{g,1}}{\widetilde p_{a,s}}-\frac{p_{g,1}}{p_{a,s}}\right)(\Delta Y_k-m_{a,s,k})\right\|_{P,2}=O_P(a_n),\qquad \left\|\mathbf 1_{a,s}\frac{\widetilde p_{g,1}}{\widetilde p_{a,s}}(\widehat m_{a,s,k}-m_{a,s,k})\right\|_{P,2}=O_P(b_n).\] The fitted target-contrast term is bounded by the sum of its four regression errors. Conditional score bias is \(O_P(a_nb_n)=o_P(n^{-1/2})\); foldwise independence makes every centered validation-fold plug-in fluctuation \(o_P(1)\) after \(n^{-1/2}\) summation. The empirical target share is root-\(n\) consistent, lies in the interior clipping region with probability tending to one, and its ratio expansion supplies exactly the target-share term in \(\Psi\). Therefore, uniformly over the fixed score coordinates, \[\sqrt n\{\widehat\mu-B\Theta(P)\}=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi(O_i)+o_P(1),\qquad \max_{1\le j\le M}\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\{\widehat\Psi_{i,j}-\Psi_j(O_i)\}^2=o_P(1).\]
