By label-preserving equivalence, equivalent invariants agree exactly in \(\mathfrak C(P)\), \(N\), \(\omega\), every comparison label, every primitive residual label, and every signed incidence.  Since \(\mathfrak C(P)=\Omega(P)\), the blockwise formula for \(b^{\mathrm{sep}}(P)\) is computable from the invariant.  Therefore
\[
 N^{\prime}\mathfrak C(P)b^{\mathrm{sep}}(P)
\]
is constant on each label-preserving equivalence class.  The population part of Theorem \(\ref{thm:separate-joint-frontier}\) states that the variance gain is zero exactly when this vector is zero and is otherwise the strictly positive quadratic form
\[
 (N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}})^{\prime}
 (N^{\prime}\Omega N)^{-1}
 (N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}).
\]
This proves that \(\mathscr E_0\) is exactly the equality class and that every compatible class in its complement \(\mathscr E_+\) has strictly positive gain.  It also proves completeness, because every entry needed for the diagnostic is either retained by \(\mathfrak H(P)\) or obtained from retained entries by the displayed finite-dimensional matrix operations.

It remains to prove nonemptiness in the stated smallest architecture.  Apply Theorem \(\ref{thm:open-strict-gap-family}\) with
\[
 \rho=\frac12,
 \qquad
 \delta=\frac1{16}.
\]
These values satisfy \(0<|\rho|<\sqrt7/3\) and \(0<|\delta|<1/8\).  That theorem supplies a bounded compatible \(Q\in\mathcal M\) with the prescribed architecture and
\[
 \Omega=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 3/8&3/16&0\\
 3/16&3/8&3/32\\
 0&3/32&3/8
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 b^{\mathrm{sep}}=
 \begin{pmatrix}\omega_1/2\\ \omega_1/2\\ \omega_2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its exact variance gain is
\[
 \frac{3(1/2)^2\omega_2^2}{32}
 =\frac{3\omega_2^2}{128}>0.
\]
This is precisely the required witness.  In fact, the strict-gap-family theorem proves the stronger robustness fact that the witness belongs to a nondegenerate open family indexed by \((\rho,\delta)\), with \(\delta\ne0\) giving genuine post-adoption effects.
