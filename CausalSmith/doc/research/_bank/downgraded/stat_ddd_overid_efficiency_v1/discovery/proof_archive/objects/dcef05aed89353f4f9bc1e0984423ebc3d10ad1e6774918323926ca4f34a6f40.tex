For every triangular array of independent finite-variance real random variables \(Z_{n,1},\ldots,Z_{n,n}\), if \(\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(Z_{n,i})\to V\) and, for every \(r>0\), \(\sum_{i=1}^nE_P[Z_{n,i}^2\mathbf 1\{|Z_{n,i}|>r\}]\to0\), then \(\sum_{i=1}^n\{Z_{n,i}-E_PZ_{n,i}\}\rightsquigarrow N(0,V)\).
