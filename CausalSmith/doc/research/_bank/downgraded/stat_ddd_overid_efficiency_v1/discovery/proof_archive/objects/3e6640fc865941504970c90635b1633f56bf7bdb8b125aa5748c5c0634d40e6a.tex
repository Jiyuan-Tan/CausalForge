Index two score coordinates by \(j=(k,c)\) and \(q=(\ell,d)\), and write their target cohorts as \(g_k\) and \(g_\ell\).  By the identification lemma, their centered scores can be written as
\[
 \Psi_j=T_j+U_j,
 \qquad
 T_j=\frac{\mathbf 1\{G_i=g_k,S_i=1\}}{\pi_{g_k,1}}
       \{d_{k,c}(X_i)-\Theta_k(P)\},
\]
and
\[
 U_j=\sum_{a,s}\mathbf 1\{G_i=a,S_i=s\}
 L_{k,c}^{a,s}(X_i)\{\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)\},
\]
where the latter sum is only over the four cells entering coordinate \(j\).  Conditional residual centering gives \(E_P[U_j\mid G_i,S_i,X_i]=0\).  It follows both that \(E_P[T_jU_q]=E_P[U_jT_q]=0\) and that
\[
 E_P[T_jT_q]
 =E_P\!\left[
 \frac{\mathbf 1\{g_k=g_\ell\}p_{g_k,1}(X_i)
 \{d_{k,c}(X_i)-\Theta_k(P)\}
 \{d_{\ell,d}(X_i)-\Theta_\ell(P)\}}
 {\pi_{g_k,1}\pi_{g_\ell,1}}
 \right].
\]
For the residual product, indicators of two distinct cohort--eligibility cells have product zero.  If the same cell \((a,s)\) enters both coordinates, iterated expectation gives
\[
 E_P[U_jU_q]
 =E_P\!\left[
 \sum_{a,s}p_{a,s}(X_i)L_{k,c}^{a,s}(X_i)L_{\ell,d}^{a,s}(X_i)
 v_{a,s;k\ell}(X_i)
 \right],
\]
where the sum is precisely over cells shared by the two scores.  Adding the target and residual products reproduces entry \((j,q)\) of \(\Omega^{\mathrm{DDD}}\).  Since this holds for every pair of coordinates, \(\Omega=\Omega^{\mathrm{DDD}}\).  The same calculation proves the stated interpretation: distinct observed cells contribute zero by mutual exclusivity, while two changes observed in a reused cell contribute their conditional covariance \(v_{a,s;k\ell}\).
