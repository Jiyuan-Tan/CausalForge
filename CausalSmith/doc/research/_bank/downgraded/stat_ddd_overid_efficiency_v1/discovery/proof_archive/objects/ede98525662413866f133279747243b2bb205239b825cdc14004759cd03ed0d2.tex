Fix \(Q\in\mathcal M\), write \(P=Q_O\), and fix \(k=(g,t)\in\mathcal K\) and \(c\in\mathcal J_k\).  For \(a\in\{g,c\}\) and \(s\in\{0,1\}\), define the conditional untreated change
\[
 \mu^0_{a,s,k}(x)
 =E_Q\!\left[Y_{it}(\infty,s)-Y_{i,g-1}(\infty,s)
       \mid G_i=a,S_i=s,X_i=x\right].
\]
Because \(g-1<g\), consistency and no anticipation imply
\[
 m_{g,1,k}(x)
 =\mu^0_{g,1,k}(x)
  +E_Q\!\left[Y_{it}(g,1)-Y_{it}(\infty,1)
       \mid G_i=g,S_i=1,X_i=x\right].
\]
No ineligible spillover gives \(m_{g,0,k}(x)=\mu^0_{g,0,k}(x)\).  Since \(c>t\), no anticipation gives \(m_{c,1,k}(x)=\mu^0_{c,1,k}(x)\), and no ineligible spillover gives \(m_{c,0,k}(x)=\mu^0_{c,0,k}(x)\).  The pairwise conditional DDD restriction is
\[
 \mu^0_{g,1,k}(x)-\mu^0_{g,0,k}(x)
 =\mu^0_{c,1,k}(x)-\mu^0_{c,0,k}(x)
\]
for \(Q_X\)-almost every \(x\).  Substitution into the definition of \(d_{k,c}\) therefore yields
\[
 d_{k,c}(x)
 =E_Q\!\left[Y_{it}(g,1)-Y_{it}(\infty,1)
       \mid G_i=g,S_i=1,X_i=x\right].
\]
Overlap makes \(\pi_{g,1}>0\).  Iterated expectation and the identity
\(E_P[\mathbf 1\{G_i=g,S_i=1\}h(X_i)]=E_P[p_{g,1}(X_i)h(X_i)]\) now give
\[
 \theta_k(Q)
 =E_P[d_{k,c}(X_i)\mid G_i=g,S_i=1]
 =\frac{E_P[p_{g,1}(X_i)d_{k,c}(X_i)]}{\pi_{g,1}}.
\]
This value does not depend on \(c\).  Consequently its average over \(\mathcal J_k\) is \(\Theta_k(P)\).

It remains to verify the score identity.  Conditional on \((G_i,S_i,X_i)=(a,s,x)\), each residual \(\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(x)\) has mean zero.  Hence all residual terms in \(R_{k,c}\) have expectation zero.  The expectation of its target-cell term is
\[
 \frac{E_P[p_{g,1}(X_i)\{d_{k,c}(X_i)-\Theta_k(P)\}]}{\pi_{g,1}}
 =\theta_k(Q)-\Theta_k(P)=0.
\]
Thus \(E_P[R_{k,c}(O_i;\eta_0)]=\Theta_k(P)=\theta_k(Q)\).  Stacking the coordinate identities gives
\[
 E_P[R(O_i;\eta_0)]=B\Theta(P),\qquad
 E_P[\Psi(O_i)]=0.
\]
The definitions of the two aggregates then give \(\tau_\omega(Q)=\omega'\theta(Q)=\omega'\Theta(P)=\Theta_\omega^{\mathrm{agg}}(P)\).  Finally, when \(g=2\), the already used inequality \(g-1<g\) reads \(1<2\), and the observed change is exactly \(Y_{it}-Y_{i1}\), as claimed.
