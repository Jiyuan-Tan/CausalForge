Write \(D_{i,j}=\widehat\Psi_{i,j}-\Psi_j(O_i)\). Lemma \(\ref{lem:cross-fitted-score-expansion}\), whose proof derives rather than assumes the regression envelopes, gives
\[
 \max_{1\le j\le M}P_nD_{i,j}^2=o_P(1).
\]
The same derived moment bounds and overlap imply \(E_P|\Psi_j(O_i)|^4<\infty\) for every fixed coordinate: the target term is a bounded indicator times an \(L_4(P_X)\) contrast, and each residual term is a bounded loading times a residual with a finite cell-conditional fourth moment. Hence \(E_P\{\Psi_j^2\Psi_q^2\}<\infty\). Independence across units and Chebyshev's inequality give
\[
 P_n\{\Psi_j(O_i)\Psi_q(O_i)\}-\Omega_{jq}=o_P(1).
\]

Using \(\widehat\Psi_j=\Psi_j+D_j\), the identity
\[
 \widehat\Omega^{\mathrm{raw}}_{jq}-\Omega_{jq}
 =\{P_n(\Psi_j\Psi_q)-\Omega_{jq}\}
  +P_n(D_j\widehat\Psi_q)+P_n(\Psi_jD_q)
\]
is exact. Cauchy--Schwarz yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 |\widehat\Omega^{\mathrm{raw}}_{jq}-\Omega_{jq}|
 &\le |P_n(\Psi_j\Psi_q)-\Omega_{jq}|\\
 &\quad +(P_nD_j^2)^{1/2}(P_n\widehat\Psi_q^2)^{1/2}
 +(P_n\Psi_j^2)^{1/2}(P_nD_q^2)^{1/2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Again by independence and Chebyshev, \(P_n\Psi_j^2=O_P(1)\), and
\[
 P_n\widehat\Psi_q^2\le2P_n\Psi_q^2+2P_nD_q^2=O_P(1).
\]
Every term in the preceding bound is therefore \(o_P(1)\). Since \(M\) is fixed,
\[
 \|\widehat\Omega^{\mathrm{raw}}-\Omega\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le M\max_{j,q}|\widehat\Omega^{\mathrm{raw}}_{jq}-\Omega_{jq}|=o_P(1).
\]

It remains to account for projection and ridging. If \(\Pi\) is the Frobenius projection onto the closed convex cone \(\mathbb S_+^M\), the two projection variational inequalities imply
\[
 \|\Pi(A)-\Pi(C)\|_F^2
 \le\langle\Pi(A)-\Pi(C),A-C\rangle_F
 \le\|\Pi(A)-\Pi(C)\|_F\|A-C\|_F.
\]
Take \(C=\Omega\), noting that \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^M\) and \(\Pi(C)=C\). Fixed dimensionality gives
\[
 \|\widehat\Omega-\Omega\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le\|\widehat\Omega-\Omega\|_F
 \le\|\widehat\Omega^{\mathrm{raw}}-\Omega\|_F=o_P(1).
\]
Finally \(\lambda_n=n^{-1/4}\to0\), and hence
\[
 \|\widehat\Omega_\lambda-\Omega\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le\|\widehat\Omega-\Omega\|_{\mathrm{op}}+\lambda_n=o_P(1).
\]
