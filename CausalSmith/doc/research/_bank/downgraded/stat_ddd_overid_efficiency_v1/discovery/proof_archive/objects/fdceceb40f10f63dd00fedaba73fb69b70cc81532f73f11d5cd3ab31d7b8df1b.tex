The paper has two headline results and one substantive witness theorem, all within the finite constant span of the retained pairwise staggered-DDD scores. First, the everywhere-defined clipped, projected, ridged cross-fitted joint estimator has a derived influence-sum limit law and valid Wald coverage, conditional on explicit theorem-level nuisance rates. Second, an exact joint-versus-componentwise frontier characterizes the aggregate variance loss, and \(\widehat\Delta\) consistently estimates that loss. The open nonzero-effect \((\rho,\delta)\) family proves strict gain in the smallest three-period architecture. The covariance-enriched labelled invariant only preserves and evaluates the already-proved zero/nonzero diagnostic; it is bookkeeping, not an independent classification. No full semiparametric efficiency bound or uniform learner-achievability theorem is claimed.
