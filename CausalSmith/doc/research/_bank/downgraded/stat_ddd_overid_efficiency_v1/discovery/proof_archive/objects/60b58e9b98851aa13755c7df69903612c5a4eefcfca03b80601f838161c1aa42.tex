Lemma \(\ref{lem:fitted-covariance-consistency}\) gives \(\widehat\Omega\to_P\Omega\) and \(\widehat\Omega_{\lambda}\to_P\Omega\). The score-spectrum condition makes \(\Omega\) and all its principal blocks positive definite. Continuous inversion in the blockwise formula for \(\widehat b^{\mathrm{sep}}\) therefore gives
\[
\widehat b^{\mathrm{sep}}\to_Pb^{\mathrm{sep}}.
\]
Theorem \(\ref{thm:joint-system-limit}\) gives
\[
\widehat b^{\mathrm{joint}}\to_Pb^{\mathrm{joint}}.
\]
The positive-semidefinite projection in the estimator construction ensures \(\widehat\Omega\succeq0\), so \(\widehat\Delta\ge0\) for every sample. Applying the continuous-mapping theorem to the quadratic form yields
\[
\widehat\Delta
\to_P
(b^{\mathrm{sep}}-b^{\mathrm{joint}})^{\prime}
\Omega(b^{\mathrm{sep}}-b^{\mathrm{joint}}).
\]
The population identity in Theorem \(\ref{thm:separate-joint-frontier}\) identifies the limit with \(V_{\mathrm{sep}}-V_{\mathrm{joint}}\). This proves consistency. Because only consistency, and no convergence rate or limit distribution for the quadratic form, has been derived, the result supports descriptive use but not a test or confidence interval.
