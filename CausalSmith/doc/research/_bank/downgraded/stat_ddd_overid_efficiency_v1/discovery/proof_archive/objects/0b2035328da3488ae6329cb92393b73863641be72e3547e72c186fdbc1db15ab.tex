Because \(\mathcal K\ne\varnothing\) and every \(\mathcal J_k\ne\varnothing\), choose \(k=(g,t)\in\mathcal K\) and \(c\in\mathcal J_k\). By the definition of a retained comparison, \(c>t\ge g\), so \(c\ne g\). Hence the four distinct pairs
\[
(g,0),\quad(g,1),\quad(c,0),\quad(c,1)
\]
belong to \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{ret}}\), proving \(L_{\mathrm{ret}}\ge4\). Thus
\[
0<\varepsilon<L_{\mathrm{ret}}^{-1}\le1/4.
\]
Fix one of the four cells in this retained score. Conditional on \(X_i=x\), the other three cells are disjoint from it and each has probability at least \(\varepsilon\) by cell overlap. Since all conditional cell probabilities sum to at most one,
\[
p_{a,s}(x)\le1-3\varepsilon.
\]
The inequality \(1-3\varepsilon\le1-\varepsilon/2\) follows from \(\varepsilon>0\).

For Proposition \(\ref{prop:class-nonempty}\), the only target cohort is \(2\) and the only comparison cohort is \(3\), so
\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{ret}}=\{2,3\}\times\{0,1\},
\qquad L_{\mathrm{ret}}=4.
\]
For Theorem \(\ref{thm:open-strict-gap-family}\), the retained target cohort is \(2\) and the retained comparison cohorts are \(3\) and \(\infty\), so
\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{ret}}=\{2,3,\infty\}\times\{0,1\},
\qquad L_{\mathrm{ret}}=6.
\]
Finally \(1/8<1/6\), which verifies the strict-gap family's overlap constant under the corrected fixed-design domain.
