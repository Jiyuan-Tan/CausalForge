Fix a score coordinate \(j=(k,c)\), write \(k=(g,t)\), and abbreviate \(I_{a,s}=\mathbf 1\{G_i=a,S_i=s\}\), \(p_a^s=p_{a,s}\), \(m_a^s=m_{a,s,k}\), and \(\pi=\pi_{g,1}\). All constants below may depend on the fixed objects \(F,T,\mathcal K,M,\varepsilon,C_4\), but not on \(n\).

First derive, rather than assume, the regression envelopes. Since
\[
 |\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4
 \le 8\{|Y_{it}|^4+|Y_{i,g-1}|^4\}
 \le16\max_{u\in\mathcal T}|Y_{iu}|^4,
\]
the primitive fourth-moment condition gives \(E_P|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4\le16C_4\). Conditional Jensen's inequality gives, for a retained cell and almost every \(x\),
\[
 |m_a^s(x)|^4
 \le E_P[|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4\mid G_i=a,S_i=s,X_i=x].
\]
Overlap gives \(p_a^s(x)\ge\varepsilon\). Integrating the last display with respect to \(P_X\) therefore yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|m_a^s\|_{P_X,4}^4
 &\le \int E_P[|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4\mid a,s,X_i=x],dP_X(x)\\
 &\le \varepsilon^{-1}E_P[I_{a,s}|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4]
 \le16C_4/\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
\]
Also \(P(G_i=a,S_i=s)\ge\varepsilon\). Applying \(|u-v|^4\le8(|u|^4+|v|^4)\) and conditional Jensen once more gives
\[
 \|\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_a^s(X_i)\|_{P(\cdot\mid a,s),4}=O(1).
\]
The regression error hypothesis and the triangle inequality now imply, fold uniformly,
\[
 \|\widehat m_{a,s,k,-f}\|_{P_X,4}
 \le \|m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}
     +\|\widehat m_{a,s,k,-f}-m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}
 =O_P(1).
\]
Thus neither a true- nor a fitted-regression envelope is an additional premise.

Next consider clipping. The four events \((G_i,S_i)=(g,0),(g,1),(c,0),(c,1)\) are disjoint. Conditional overlap therefore implies, for each such cell and almost every \(x\),
\[
 \varepsilon\le p_a^s(x)\le1-3\varepsilon
 <1-\varepsilon/2.
\]
Thus \(C_\varepsilon(p_a^s)=p_a^s\). Projection onto an interval is one-Lipschitz, so
\[
 \|\widetilde p_a^s-p_a^s\|_{P_X,4}
 \le\|\widehat p_a^s-p_a^s\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n)
\]
fold by fold. On \([\varepsilon/2,1-\varepsilon/2]^2\), the map \((u,v)\mapsto u/v\) is Lipschitz with a constant depending only on \(\varepsilon\). Consequently,
\[
 \left\|\frac{\widetilde p_g^1}{\widetilde p_a^s}
       -\frac{p_g^1}{p_a^s}\right\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n).
\]
H"older's inequality and the derived residual fourth moment now imply
\[
 \left\|I_{a,s}\left(
 \frac{\widetilde p_g^1}{\widetilde p_a^s}
 -\frac{p_g^1}{p_a^s}\right)
 \{\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_a^s(X_i)\}\right\|_{P,2}=O_P(a_n),
\]
while bounded clipping gives
\[
 \left\|I_{a,s}\frac{\widetilde p_g^1}{\widetilde p_a^s}
 \{\widehat m_a^s(X_i)-m_a^s(X_i)\}\right\|_{P,2}=O_P(b_n).
\]
The fitted contrast error \(\widehat d_{k,c}-d_{k,c}\) is the signed sum of four regression errors and hence is \(O_P(b_n)\) in \(L_4(P_X)\).

Define the oracle numerator summand
\[
 A_j(O_i)=I_{g,1}d_{k,c}(X_i)
 +p_{g,1}(X_i)\sum_{a,s}\sigma_{a,s}^{g,c}
 \frac{I_{a,s}}{p_{a,s}(X_i)}
 \{\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)\},
\]
where the sum is over the four retained cells, and let \(\widehat A_{i,j}\) be its fitted version. The preceding bounds give, conditionally on each training sample,
\[
 \|\widehat A_{i,j}-A_j(O_i)\|_{P,2}=O_P(a_n+b_n)=o_P(1).
\]
There is also an exact orthogonality calculation. Put \(e_{a,s}=\widehat m_{a,s,k}-m_{a,s,k}\). Conditional residual centering gives
\[
 E_P[\widehat A_{i,j}-A_j(O_i)\mid X_i=x]
 =\sum_{a,s}\sigma_{a,s}^{g,c}
 \left\{p_{g,1}(x)-\widetilde p_{g,1}(x)
 \frac{p_{a,s}(x)}{\widetilde p_{a,s}(x)}\right\}e_{a,s}(x).
\]
The bracket has \(L_4(P_X)\) norm bounded by a constant times the relevant propensity errors. H"older's inequality therefore gives
\[
 \left|E_P[\widehat A_{i,j}-A_j(O_i)\mid\text{training sample}]\right|
 =O_P(a_nb_n)=o_P(n^{-1/2}).
\]

For a validation fold, condition on its training sample. The validation observations are independent of the fitted functions. Conditional Chebyshev's inequality and the preceding \(L_2\) bound show that
\[
 \sqrt n\,(P_{n,f}-P)\{\widehat A_{j,f}-A_j\}=o_P(1).
\]
Since \(F\) is fixed, summing over folds preserves \(o_P(1)\), and the conditional-bias display controls the noncentered part. Hence
\[
 \sqrt n\,P_n(\widehat A_j-A_j)=o_P(1).
\]

Let \(\bar I_g=P_nI_{g,1}\). Overlap gives \(\pi\in[\varepsilon,1-3\varepsilon]\). Bernoulli variance and Chebyshev's inequality give \(\bar I_g-\pi=O_P(n^{-1/2})\), so \(\widetilde\pi_{g,1}=\bar I_g\) with probability tending to one. Causal identification and residual centering give
\[
 E_PA_j=\pi\Theta_k(P),
 \qquad
 A_j(O_i)-\Theta_k(P)I_{g,1}=\pi\Psi_j(O_i).
\]
On the event where target-share clipping is inactive,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sqrt n\{\widehat\mu_j-\Theta_k(P)\}
 &=\frac{\sqrt n\,P_n\{A_j-\Theta_k(P)I_{g,1}\}}
         {\bar I_g}
   +\frac{\sqrt n\,P_n(\widehat A_j-A_j)}{\bar I_g}\\
 &=\frac{\pi}{\bar I_g}\frac1{\sqrt n}
   \sum_{i=1}^n\Psi_j(O_i)+o_P(1)\\
 &=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi_j(O_i)+o_P(1).
\end{aligned}
\]
The last step uses \(\bar I_g/\pi\to_P1\) and the finite variance of \(\Psi_j\), which follows from the moment bounds just derived and bounded inverse-propensity loadings. Since \(M\) is fixed, stacking gives
\[
 \sqrt n\{\widehat\mu-B\Theta(P)\}
 =\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi(O_i)+o_P(1).
\]

Finally compare the fitted and oracle influence coordinates. On the same probability-tending-to-one event, their difference is a finite sum of the denominator error \(\bar I_g^{-1}-\pi^{-1}\), the fitted-contrast error, the centering error \(\widehat\mu_j-\Theta_k(P)\), a propensity-ratio error times a true residual, and a bounded fitted ratio times a regression error. Conditional Markov's inequality, foldwise independence, and the preceding \(L_4\) bounds yield
\[
 P_n\{\widehat\Psi_{i,j}-\Psi_j(O_i)\}^2
 \le O_P(1)\left\{(\bar I_g-\pi)^2
 +|\widehat\mu_j-\Theta_k(P)|^2+a_n^2+b_n^2\right\}=o_P(1).
\]
Here empirical second moments of oracle residual terms are \(O_P(1)\) by the derived fourth moments, and the expansion already proved gives \(\widehat\mu_j-\Theta_k(P)=O_P(n^{-1/2})\). Taking the maximum over the fixed \(M\) coordinates proves
\[
 \max_{1\le j\le M}\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n
 \{\widehat\Psi_{i,j}-\Psi_j(O_i)\}^2=o_P(1).
\]
