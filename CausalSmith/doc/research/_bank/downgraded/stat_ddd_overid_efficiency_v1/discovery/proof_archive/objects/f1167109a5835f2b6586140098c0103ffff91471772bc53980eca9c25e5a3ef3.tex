Let \(Q_0\) be the full-data law specified in the proposition and let \(P_0=(Q_0)_O\).  Because \(X_i=x_0\) almost surely and \((G_i,S_i)\) is uniform on the four displayed cells, for every \(a\in\{2,3\}\) and \(s\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
p_{a,s}(x_0)=P_0(G_i=a,S_i=s\mid X_i=x_0)=\frac14.
\]
In particular, \(\pi_{2,1}=1/4\), and all four cells entering the unique retained comparison have conditional probability \(1/4>1/8=\varepsilon\).  Thus the overlap restriction holds.

The observed outcomes satisfy consistency by their construction.  Moreover, for every \(a\in\{2,3,\infty\}\), \(s\in\{0,1\}\), and \(u\in\{1,2,3\}\),
\[
Y_{iu}(a,s)=\frac{U_{iu}}8.
\]
Consequently, the potential outcomes do not depend on either \(a\) or \(s\).  The no-anticipation and no-ineligible-spillover equalities therefore hold pointwise.  Independence of the Rademacher variables from \((G_i,S_i)\), together with their zero means, gives, for every retained cell \((a,s)\),
\[
E_{Q_0}\!\left[Y_{i2}(\infty,s)-Y_{i1}(\infty,s)\mid G_i=a,S_i=s,X_i=x_0\right]
=E\!\left[\frac{U_{i2}-U_{i1}}8\right]=0.
\]
Hence both eligibility differences appearing in the pairwise conditional DDD restriction are zero, so that restriction holds as well.

For the unique index \(k=(2,2)\),
\[
\Delta Y_{i,k}=\frac{U_{i2}-U_{i1}}8,
\qquad
m_{a,s,k}(x_0)=0
\]
for each of the four retained cells.  It follows that \(d_{k,3}(x_0)=0\), \(\Theta_k(P_0)=0\), and \(B\Theta(P_0)=0\).  Substitution into the pairwise-score definition, using \(p_{2,1}(x_0)/\pi_{2,1}=1\) and \(p_{a,s}(x_0)=1/4\), shows that on cell \((a,s)\),
\[
R_{k,3}(O_i;\eta_0)
=4\sigma_{a,s}^{2,3}\frac{U_{i2}-U_{i1}}8.
\]
This variable has mean zero, so the sole centered-score coordinate \(\Psi(O_i)\) equals the same expression.  Since \((\sigma_{a,s}^{2,3})^2=1\) on every one of the four cells and the two Rademacher variables are independent,
\[
\Omega
=E_{P_0}[\Psi(O_i)^2]
=16E\!\left[\frac{(U_{i2}-U_{i1})^2}{64}\right]
=\frac14\{E[U_{i2}^2]+E[U_{i1}^2]-2E[U_{i2}]E[U_{i1}]\}
=\frac12.
\]
Here \(M=1\).  With \(\kappa=1/2\), this yields
\[
\kappa I_1=\frac12 I_1\preceq\Omega=\frac12 I_1\preceq 2I_1=\kappa^{-1}I_1.
\]
Finally, \(\max_{u\in\mathcal T}|Y_{iu}|=1/8\) almost surely, and therefore
\[
E_{P_0}\!\left[\max_{u\in\mathcal T}|Y_{iu}|^4\right]=8^{-4}\le 1=C_4.
\]
All retained true regressions vanish, so their fourth-moment bound is zero.  Thus every member condition in the definition of \(\mathcal M\) is satisfied, and \(Q_0\in\mathcal M\).
