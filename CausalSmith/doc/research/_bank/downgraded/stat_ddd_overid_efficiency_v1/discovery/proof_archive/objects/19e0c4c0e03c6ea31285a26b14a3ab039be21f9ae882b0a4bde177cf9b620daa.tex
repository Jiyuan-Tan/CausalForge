Let \(\mathfrak H(P)\) be a labelled invariant in the stated domain. Definition \(\ref{def:labelled-incidence-handle}\) retains the labelled coordinate blocks, \(\mathfrak C(P)=\Omega(P)\), \(N\), and \(\omega\). Positive definiteness of \(\Omega\) implies positive definiteness of every principal block, so
\[
 (b^{\mathrm{sep}})_k
 =\omega_k\frac{\Omega_{kk}^{-1}\mathbf 1}
 {\mathbf 1^{\prime}\Omega_{kk}^{-1}\mathbf 1}
\]
is well defined and evaluable; its denominator is strictly positive. Stacking the blocks and multiplying finite matrices evaluates
\[
 \Phi(\mathfrak H(P))
 :=N^{\prime}\mathfrak C(P)b^{\mathrm{sep}}(P).
\]

The value does not depend on the representative of a label-preserving equivalence class. Indeed, after the required label identification, let \(\Pi\) be the resulting block-preserving coordinate permutation. Then
\[
 \widetilde{\mathfrak C}=\Pi\mathfrak C\Pi^{\prime},
 \qquad
 \widetilde N=\Pi N,
 \qquad
 \widetilde b^{\mathrm{sep}}=\Pi b^{\mathrm{sep}}.
\]
The last identity follows blockwise from the loading formula, because \(\Pi\mathbf 1=\mathbf 1\) and inversion commutes with permutation conjugation. Therefore
\[
 \widetilde N^{\prime}\widetilde{\mathfrak C}
 \widetilde b^{\mathrm{sep}}
 =N^{\prime}\mathfrak Cb^{\mathrm{sep}}.
\]
Every finite vector is either zero or nonzero, so \(\mathscr E_0\) and \(\mathscr E_+\) are well defined, disjoint, and exhaustive.

For an invariant induced by a compatible law \(Q\in\mathcal M\), Theorem \(\ref{thm:separate-joint-frontier}\) gives, with \(u=N^{\prime}\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}\) and \(H=N^{\prime}\Omega N\),
\[
 V_{\mathrm{sep}}-V_{\mathrm{joint}}=u^{\prime}H^{-1}u
\]
when \(N\) has columns, with value zero in the zero-dimensional case. If \(N\) has columns, its full column rank and positive definiteness of \(\Omega\) give, for nonzero \(z\),
\[
 z^{\prime}Hz=(Nz)^{\prime}\Omega(Nz)>0.
\]
Thus the gain is zero exactly when \(u=0\), and is strictly positive exactly when \(u\ne0\). This proves the asserted classification.
