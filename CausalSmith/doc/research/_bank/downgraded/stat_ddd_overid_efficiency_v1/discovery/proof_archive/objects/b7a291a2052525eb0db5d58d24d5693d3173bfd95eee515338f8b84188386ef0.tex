The causal parameter is defined under the full-data law \(Q\), whereas every score, nuisance, covariance, loading, estimator, diagnostic, and limiting variance is a functional of the observed-data pushforward \(P=Q_O\). Identification is the bridge. Within the retained constant score span, a strict gain occurs exactly when cross-block covariance makes \(\Omega b^{\mathrm{sep}}\) nonconstant across comparison cohorts in some block. The open \((\rho,\delta)\) family shows that this mechanism persists with nonzero treatment effects; the labelled invariant merely lets a reader compute the same diagnostic from stored covariance and incidence data.
