Ortiz-Villavicencio and Sant'Anna (2025, Theorem 4.1, Corollary 4.1, and Remark 4.6) supply the pairwise staggered-DDD scores and componentwise constant-GMM estimator used as the comparator. Hsieh (2026, Section 6) derives fixed-weight stacked event-study asymptotic normality and correctly aggregates reused-unit covariance; the present result does not claim otherwise. Its distinction is optimization over the full retained score covariance for a prespecified scalar aggregate, the exact joint-versus-componentwise equality frontier, a consistent feasible loss diagnostic, and a complete labelled-invariant classification with an open strict-gap family. Chen, Sant'Anna, and Xie (2025) inform conditional covariance weighting for staggered two-way difference-in-differences, while Akbari, Kiyavash, and Ghassami (2025) treat the canonical two-period DDD design. Zellner (1962), Hansen (1982), and Ackerberg, Chen, Hahn, and Liao (2014) provide systems, finite-dimensional GMM, and semiparametric two-step geometry. These works are methodological inputs; the note's proved novelty remains restricted to the retained staggered-DDD score span.
