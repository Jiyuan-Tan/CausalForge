The optimum, limit law, and diagnostic concern only constant linear combinations of a fixed finite vector of retained pairwise doubly robust scores. They do not establish the semiparametric tangent-space efficiency bound for the full conditional-moment staggered-DDD model. Propensity and regression learner sequences and \(L_4(P_X)\) rates are explicit theorem-level conditional hypotheses; no model-specific learner construction or uniform achievability result over \(\mathcal M\) is proved. Consistency of \(\widehat\Delta\) is established, but no rate, test, or confidence interval for the gap is claimed.
