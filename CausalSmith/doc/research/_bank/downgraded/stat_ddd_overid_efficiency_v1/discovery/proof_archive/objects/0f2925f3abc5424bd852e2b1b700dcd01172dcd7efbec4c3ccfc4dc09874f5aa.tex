Fix a score coordinate \(j=(k,c)\), write \(k=(g,t)\), and abbreviate \(I_{a,s}=\mathbf 1\{G_i=a,S_i=s\}\). Constants below may depend on the fixed objects \(F,T,\mathcal K,M,\varepsilon,C_4\), but not on \(n\).

We first derive the moment envelopes that are no longer assumed. The elementary inequality \(|u-v|^4\le 8(|u|^4+|v|^4)\) and the primitive outcome bound give
\[
 E_P|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4
 \le 8E_P\{|Y_{it}|^4+|Y_{i,g-1}|^4\}
 \le16C_4.
\]
For a retained cell \(A_{a,s}=\{G_i=a,S_i=s\}\), conditional Jensen's inequality yields
\[
 |m_{a,s,k}(x)|^4
 \le E_P[|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4\mid A_{a,s},X_i=x].
\]
Since \(p_{a,s}(x)\ge\varepsilon\), integration with respect to \(P_X\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}^4
 &\le E_{P_X}E_P[|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4\mid A_{a,s},X_i]\\
 &\le\varepsilon^{-1}E_P[I_{a,s}|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4]
 \le16C_4/\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
\]
Also \(P(A_{a,s})=E_Pp_{a,s}(X_i)\ge\varepsilon\). Jensen's inequality under the conditional law given \(A_{a,s}\) and the same elementary inequality therefore imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 E_P[|\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)|^4\mid A_{a,s}]
 &\le 8E_P[|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4+|m_{a,s,k}(X_i)|^4\mid A_{a,s}]\\
 &\le16E_P[|\Delta Y_{i,k}|^4\mid A_{a,s}]
 \le256C_4/\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
\]
Finally, conditional on every training sample, the triangle inequality and the assumed regression error rate give
\[
 \|\widehat m_{a,s,k,-f}\|_{P_X,4}
 \le \|m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}
     +\|\widehat m_{a,s,k,-f}-m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}
 =O_P(1),
\]
uniformly over the finitely many folds and coordinates. Thus both deleted envelopes follow from the remaining primitives and the theorem-level error rate.

We next control clipping. The four retained events \((g,0),(g,1),(c,0),(c,1)\) are disjoint. Conditional overlap implies, for any one of them and almost every \(x\),
\[
 \varepsilon\le p_{a,s}(x)\le1-3\varepsilon
 <1-\varepsilon/2.
\]
Consequently \(C_\varepsilon(p_{a,s})=p_{a,s}\). Projection onto an interval is one-Lipschitz, so, fold by fold,
\[
 \|\widetilde p_{a,s}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}
 \le\|\widehat p_{a,s}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n).
\]
On \([\varepsilon/2,1-\varepsilon/2]^2\), the map \((u,v)\mapsto u/v\) is Lipschitz with a constant depending only on \(\varepsilon\). Hence
\[
 \left\|\frac{\widetilde p_{g,1}}{\widetilde p_{a,s}}
       -\frac{p_{g,1}}{p_{a,s}}\right\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n).
\]
H\"older's inequality and the derived residual fourth-moment bound now give
\[
 \left\|I_{a,s}\left(
 \frac{\widetilde p_{g,1}}{\widetilde p_{a,s}}
 -\frac{p_{g,1}}{p_{a,s}}\right)
 \{\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)\}\right\|_{P,2}=O_P(a_n).
\]
Bounded clipping similarly gives
\[
 \left\|I_{a,s}\frac{\widetilde p_{g,1}}{\widetilde p_{a,s}}
 \{\widehat m_{a,s,k}(X_i)-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)\}\right\|_{P,2}
 =O_P(b_n).
\]
The fitted contrast error \(\widehat d_{k,c}-d_{k,c}\) is the signed sum of four regression errors, and is therefore \(O_P(b_n)\) in \(L_4(P_X)\).

Define the oracle numerator summand
\[
 A_j(O_i)=I_{g,1}d_{k,c}(X_i)
 +p_{g,1}(X_i)\sum_{(a,s)\in\{g,c\}\times\{0,1\}}
 \sigma_{a,s}^{g,c}\frac{I_{a,s}}{p_{a,s}(X_i)}
 \{\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)\},
\]
and let \(\widehat A_{i,j}\) be its fitted counterpart in Definition \(\ref{def:joint-estimator}\). The preceding bounds imply, conditionally on each training sample,
\[
 \|\widehat A_{i,j}-A_j(O_i)\|_{P,2}=O_P(a_n+b_n)=o_P(1).
\]
For \(e_{a,s}=\widehat m_{a,s,k}-m_{a,s,k}\), residual centering gives the exact conditional-bias identity
\[
 E_P[\widehat A_{i,j}-A_j(O_i)\mid X_i=x]
 =\sum_{a,s}\sigma_{a,s}^{g,c}
 \left\{p_{g,1}(x)-\widetilde p_{g,1}(x)
 \frac{p_{a,s}(x)}{\widetilde p_{a,s}(x)}\right\}e_{a,s}(x).
\]
The bracket is bounded in \(L_4(P_X)\) by a constant times the relevant propensity errors. H\"older's inequality therefore gives
\[
 \left|E_P[\widehat A_{i,j}-A_j(O_i)\mid\text{training sample}]\right|
 =O_P(a_nb_n)=o_P(n^{-1/2}).
\]

For each validation fold, condition on its training sample. Foldwise independence makes the validation observations independent of the fitted functions. Conditional Chebyshev's inequality, the \(L_2\) bound, and fixed \(F\) show that the sum over folds of the centered terms \(\sqrt n(P_{n,f}-P)(\widehat A_{j,f}-A_j)\) is \(o_P(1)\). The bias display treats the noncentered terms. Thus
\[
 \sqrt n P_n(\widehat A_j-A_j)=o_P(1).
\]

Put \(\bar I_g=P_nI_{g,1}\) and \(\pi=\pi_{g,1}\). Overlap gives \(\pi\in[\varepsilon,1-3\varepsilon]\). Since \(I_{g,1}\) is Bernoulli, \(\bar I_g-\pi=O_P(n^{-1/2})\), and clipping of the empirical share is inactive with probability tending to one. Causal identification and residual centering give
\[
 E_PA_j=\pi\Theta_k(P),
 \qquad
 A_j(O_i)-\Theta_k(P)I_{g,1}=\pi\Psi_j(O_i).
\]
On the probability-tending-to-one event where clipping is inactive,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sqrt n\{\widehat\mu_j-\Theta_k(P)\}
 &=\frac{\sqrt nP_n\{A_j-\Theta_k(P)I_{g,1}\}}{\bar I_g}
   +\frac{\sqrt nP_n(\widehat A_j-A_j)}{\bar I_g}\\
 &=\frac{\pi}{\bar I_g}\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi_j(O_i)+o_P(1)\\
 &=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi_j(O_i)+o_P(1).
\end{aligned}
\]
The last line uses \(\bar I_g/\pi\to_P1\) and the fact that the mean-zero sum is \(O_P(1)\). Since \(M\) is fixed, stacking proves the first conclusion.

For the fitted-influence conclusion, subtract the fitted and oracle influence formulas on the same event. The difference is a finite sum of a target-share denominator error, a fitted-contrast error, a centering error, a propensity-ratio error times a true residual, and a bounded fitted ratio times a regression error. Conditional Markov's inequality and the bounds above yield
\[
 P_n\{\widehat\Psi_{i,j}-\Psi_j(O_i)\}^2
 \le O_P(1)\{(\bar I_g-\pi)^2+|\widehat\mu_j-\Theta_k(P)|^2+a_n^2+b_n^2\}=o_P(1).
\]
Here empirical second moments of the oracle residual terms are \(O_P(1)\) by the derived fourth-moment bounds, and the expansion already proved gives \(\widehat\mu_j-\Theta_k(P)=O_P(n^{-1/2})\). Taking the maximum over the fixed \(M\) coordinates proves the second conclusion.
