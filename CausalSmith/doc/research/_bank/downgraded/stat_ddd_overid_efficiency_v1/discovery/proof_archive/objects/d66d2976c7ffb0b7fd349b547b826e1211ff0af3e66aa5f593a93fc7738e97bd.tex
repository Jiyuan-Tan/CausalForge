Label-preserving equivalence classes of \(\mathfrak H(P)\) split exactly into the equality class
\[
 \mathscr E_0=\{[\mathfrak H(P)]:N'\mathfrak C(P)b^{\mathrm{sep}}(P)=0\}
\]
and its complement \(\mathscr E_+\), on which the joint variance gain is strictly positive whenever the invariant is induced by a compatible law in \(\mathcal M\).  This is a complete classification because all entries of the displayed diagnostic are components or computable functions of the labelled invariant.

The complement is nonempty even in the smallest architecture in the question.
