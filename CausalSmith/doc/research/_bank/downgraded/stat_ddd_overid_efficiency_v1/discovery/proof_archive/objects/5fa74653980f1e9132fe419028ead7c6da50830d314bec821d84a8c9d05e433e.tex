Fix \(F\), \(T\), \(\mathcal K\), and \(M\). For every \(Q\in\mathcal M\) with \(P=Q_O\), suppose the off-fold learners satisfy foldwise independence, the fitted-regression \(L_4(P_X)\) envelope, \(\max_{f,a,s}\|\widehat p_{a,s,-f}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n)\), \(\max_{f,a,s,k}\|\widehat m_{a,s,k,-f}-m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(b_n)\), \(a_n\to0\), \(b_n\to0\), and \(a_nb_n=o(n^{-1/2})\). Four disjoint cells enter each retained pairwise score, so overlap and \(0<\varepsilon<1/4\) give \(p_{a,s}(x)\in[\varepsilon,1-3\varepsilon]\subset[\varepsilon/2,1-\varepsilon/2]\) for every retained cell. Hence \(C_\varepsilon(p_{a,s})=p_{a,s}\), the clipping map is one-Lipschitz, and \[\|\widetilde p_{a,s,-f}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}\le\|\widehat p_{a,s,-f}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}=O_P(a_n).\] The outcome and true-regression fourth-moment envelopes give \(\|\Delta Y_{i,k}-m_{a,s,k}(X_i)\|_{P(\cdot\mid a,s),4}=O(1)\), and the fitted regressions are uniformly \(L_4\)-bounded by hypothesis. For each residual term, clipping, the Lipschitz bound for \((u,v)\mapsto u/v\) on \([\varepsilon/2,1-\varepsilon/2]^2\), and H\"older's inequality yield \[\left\|\mathbf 1_{a,s}\left(\frac{\widetilde p_{g,1}}{\widetilde p_{a,s}}-\frac{p_{g,1}}{p_{a,s}}\right)(\Delta Y_k-m_{a,s,k})\right\|_{P,2}\le C_\varepsilon\left(\|\widetilde p_{g,1}-p_{g,1}\|_{P_X,4}+\|\widetilde p_{a,s}-p_{a,s}\|_{P_X,4}\right)\|\Delta Y_k-m_{a,s,k}\|_{P(\cdot\mid a,s),4}=O_P(a_n),\] and \[\left\|\mathbf 1_{a,s}\frac{\widetilde p_{g,1}}{\widetilde p_{a,s}}(\widehat m_{a,s,k}-m_{a,s,k})\right\|_{P,2}\le C_\varepsilon\|\widehat m_{a,s,k}-m_{a,s,k}\|_{P_X,2}=O_P(b_n).\] The fitted target-contrast term is bounded by the sum of its four regression errors. Conditional score bias is bounded by \(C_\varepsilon\max_{p,m}\|\widehat p-p\|_{P_X,2}\|\widehat m-m\|_{P_X,2}=O_P(a_nb_n)=o_P(n^{-1/2})\); foldwise independence makes every centered validation-fold plug-in fluctuation \(o_P(1)\) after \(n^{-1/2}\) summation. The empirical target share is root-\(n\) consistent, lies in the interior clipping region with probability tending to one, and its ratio expansion supplies exactly the target-share term in \(\Psi\). Therefore, uniformly over the fixed score coordinates, \[\sqrt n\{\widehat\mu-B\Theta(P)\}=\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\Psi(O_i)+o_P(1),\qquad \max_{1\le j\le M}\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\{\widehat\Psi_{i,j}-\Psi_j(O_i)\}^2=o_P(1).\]
