\textbf{Proof.}
Simultaneous decomposition into \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1\}\) and \(\mathbf1^\perp\) reduces \(729\Omega\) to
\[
\begin{bmatrix}60&96\\96&186\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{bmatrix}108&-108\\-108&162\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Their determinants are \(1944\) and \(5832\), with positive leading entries, so \(\Omega\succ0\).  Rank zero therefore has both columns zero, while rank one has exactly one nonzero arm column.

For a focal-only normalized vector \(x\), set \(u=(\mathbf1^{\top}x)^2\in[0,9]\).  Direct substitution in the scalar projector quotient gives the two stated endpoint formulas and
\[
g_x^+(0)=\frac{21141-1225u}{1200(81-4u)},
\qquad
w^+(x)=\frac{9(1755-17u)}{400(81-4u)}.
\tag{1}
\]
Their derivatives are respectively negative and positive on \([0,9]\), so their relevant extrema are \(87/400\) and \(39/80\), both at \(u=0\).  For a comparison-only vector, the displayed endpoint formulas give
\[
g_y^+(0)=\frac{25u+12393}{300(4u+243)},
\qquad
w^+(y)=\frac{9(256u+2889)}{200(4u+243)}.
\tag{2}
\]
Again the derivatives are respectively negative and positive, giving \(17/100\) and \(107/200\).  At rank zero, the gain is zero and the loss is \(\max\{313/600,171/200\}=171/200\).  Comparing ranks zero and one proves the exact global singular bounds and both strict inequalities. \(\square\)
