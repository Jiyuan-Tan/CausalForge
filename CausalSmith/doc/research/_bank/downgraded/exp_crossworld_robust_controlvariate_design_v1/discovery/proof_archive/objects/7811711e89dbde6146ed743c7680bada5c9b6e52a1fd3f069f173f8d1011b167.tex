\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(K_0\) be the ordered field generated over \(\mathbb Q\) by the finitely many entries of \(\Pi\), \(\Omega\), \(Q(c_L)\), and \(Q(c_U)\), together with the other exactly encoded input constants.  The rational or real-algebraic input condition in def:certified-input-interface makes every element of \(K_0\) real algebraic.  By ass:exposure-positivity, every diagonal entry of \(\Pi\) is strictly positive.  Thus \(\Pi^{-1}\) exists, and its entries belong to \(K_0\).  The endpoint matrices \(Q(c_L)\) and \(Q(c_U)\) and the design covariance \(\Omega\) are positive semidefinite by their declared spaces.  These facts supply the support, division, and positivity conditions used below.

\emph{Normalized semialgebraic representation of the quotient.}
The object \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) is the note's projective quotient of Zhao's published all-block-separable pseudoinverse carrier.  By def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain, every quotient class has a representative for which each arm column is either zero or has squared Euclidean norm \(n\).  It therefore suffices to work on
\[
 \mathcal K
 =\left\{(b_1,b_0):
   \bigl(b_1=0\ \vee\ b_1^{\top}b_1=n\bigr),\quad
   \bigl(b_0=0\ \vee\ b_0^{\top}b_0=n\bigr)
  \right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
This is a union of four compact semialgebraic sets over \(K_0\).  To obtain literal quotient coordinates, for each nonzero arm select the first nonzero coordinate and require it to be positive.  The resulting finite collection of semialgebraic sign charts is a one-to-one cross-section of the independent-rescaling quotient.  Hence any semialgebraic subset described below on normalized representatives is also semialgebraic in these quotient charts.

The objective is constant on quotient fibers, including at singular ranks.  To verify this, let \(C=\Omega^{1/2}B\), and, for \(e\in\{L,U\}\), let
\[
 R_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}.
\]
The square roots are proof intermediates only; the algorithm below uses polynomial formulas in the encoded entries.  Since \(S_B=C^{\top}C\), cyclicity of trace and def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss give
\[
 g_B^+(c_e)
 =n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
 C(C^{\top}C)^+C^{\top}R_e
 \right].
\tag{2}
\]
If \(C=U\Sigma V^{\top}\) is a singular-value decomposition, then
\[
 C(C^{\top}C)^+C^{\top}
 =U\operatorname{diag}\!\bigl(\mathbf 1\{\sigma_j>0\}\bigr)U^{\top},
\]
which is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{col}(C)\).  Independent nonzero rescaling of the nonzero arm columns leaves that column space unchanged.  Thus (2), and consequently \(w^+(B)\), is quotient invariant at ranks zero, one, and two.  Moreover,
\[
 0\preceq C(C^{\top}C)^+C^{\top}\preceq I,
 \qquad R_e\succeq0.
\]
Taking traces after multiplying by \(R_e\), and using \(n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(R_e)=h(c_e)\), yields
\[
 0\le g_B^+(c_e)\le h(c_e),
 \qquad
 0\le \ell_B^+(c_e)\le h(c_e).
\tag{3}
\]
The attainable loss set is therefore bounded below.  It is nonempty and finite because the all-zero representative belongs to \(\mathcal K\), has gain zero, and has finite endpoint losses.

\emph{Exact rank-stratified graph.}
Write \(S=S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\).  Since \(\Omega\succeq0\), the matrix \(S\) is a \(2\times2\) positive-semidefinite matrix for every representative.  Its possible ranks and Moore--Penrose inverses are exactly
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\operatorname{rank}(S)&\text{semialgebraic condition}&S^+\\ \hline
0&\operatorname{tr}(S)=0&0,\\[2mm]
1&\operatorname{tr}(S)>0,\ \det(S)=0&
\displaystyle\frac{S}{\{\operatorname{tr}(S)\}^2},\\[3mm]
2&\det(S)>0&
\displaystyle\frac{\operatorname{adj}(S)}{\det(S)}.
\end{array}
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, positive semidefiniteness makes trace zero equivalent to rank zero.  At rank one, if the nonzero eigenvalue is \(\lambda=\operatorname{tr}(S)\) and \(S=\lambda uu^{\top}\) with \(\lVert u\rVert_2=1\), then \(S/\{\operatorname{tr}(S)\}^2=\lambda^{-1}uu^{\top}=S^+\).  At rank two, positive semidefiniteness and \(\det(S)>0\) give positive definiteness, so the last expression is the ordinary inverse.  Every denominator in (4) is strictly positive on its stated stratum, including arbitrarily close to a lower-rank boundary; at the boundary the appropriate lower-rank row is used instead.

For each row of (4), substitute the displayed rational expression into the two endpoint gains in def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss.  Introduce variables \(z_L,z_U,y\) for the two endpoint losses and their maximum, and impose
\[
 z_e=h(c_e)-g_B^+(c_e)\quad(e\in\{L,U\}),
 \qquad y\ge z_L,\qquad y\ge z_U,
 \qquad (y=z_L\ \vee\ y=z_U).
\tag{5}
\]
On rank one multiply the gain equalities by the strictly positive quantity \(\{\operatorname{tr}(S)\}^2\); on rank two multiply them by the strictly positive quantity \(\det(S)\); no denominator is present on rank zero.  Thus (1), the relevant row of (4), and (5) form a finite Boolean combination of polynomial equalities and strict or weak inequalities over \(K_0\).  Their union is the semialgebraic graph
\[
 \mathcal G
 =\{(b_1,b_0,y):(b_1,b_0)\in\mathcal K,\ y=w^+(B)\}.
\tag{6}
\]
This graph covers every representative and every rank, including equality boundaries, without evaluating an inverse at a zero denominator.

\emph{Exact value isolation.}
Define the upper attainable-value set
\[
 \mathcal T
 =\left\{t\in\mathbb R:
   \exists (b_1,b_0,y)\in\mathcal G\ \text{with}\ y\le t
  \right\}.
\tag{7}
\]
By (3), this set is nonempty and bounded below, and by the definitions of \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) and \(\mathcal G\),
\[
 \inf\mathcal T
 =\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
 =v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\tag{8}
\]
The formula in (7) is first order over a real closed field.  Apply lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination to eliminate \(b_1,b_0,z_L,z_U,y\).  It returns an equivalent quantifier-free description of \(\mathcal T\) using finitely many polynomial sign conditions over \(K_0\).  A one-dimensional polynomial sign pattern is constant on every interval avoiding the finitely many real roots of its nonzero polynomials.  Consequently every finite boundary point of \(\mathcal T\) is a root of one of those polynomials.  Equations (3) and (8) make \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) such a finite boundary point.  Therefore \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is algebraic over \(K_0\), and hence is a real-algebraic number.

The corresponding cylindrical algebraic decomposition terminates because it starts with finitely many variables and input polynomials.  Each projection stage forms finitely many coefficients, discriminants, and resultants; exact real-algebraic root isolation produces finitely many sign cells; and lifting performs the same finite operation through finitely many coordinates.  The equivalence supplied by lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination certifies the sign decomposition.  Refine an isolating interval for the algebraic boundary \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) until its exactly checked width is at most the exactly encoded positive number \(\varepsilon\).  This returns rational or real-algebraic numbers \(L_\varepsilon^+\) and \(U_\varepsilon^+\) satisfying
\[
 L_\varepsilon^+\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\le U_\varepsilon^+,
 \qquad
 U_\varepsilon^+-L_\varepsilon^+\le\varepsilon.
\tag{9}
\]
Strict positivity and exact encoding of \(\varepsilon\) ensure that the refinement test terminates.

\emph{Algebraic incumbent and finite certificate.}
Attainment was not used.  From the definition of an infimum and \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
 \exists [B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+
 \quad w^+(B)<v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon.
\tag{10}
\]
Adjoin the real-algebraic constant \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) to the coefficient field and apply CAD lifting to the formula in (10), represented by (1), (4), and (5).  Since the formula is nonempty, CAD returns a sample point with real-algebraic coordinates.  Its normalized representative defines a basis class \([B_\varepsilon]_+\) in the note's projective quotient of Zhao's published carrier, and
\[
 w^+(B_\varepsilon)<v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon,
 \qquad\text{hence}\qquad
 w^+(B_\varepsilon)\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon.
\tag{11}
\]
Likewise, in the finite quotient charts described after (1), the weak sublevel set
\[
 \{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:w^+(B)\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon\}
\]
is semialgebraic by the same rank-stratified formulas, and it is nonempty by (10).

Finally, retain the normalized quotient-chart list, the rank condition and pseudoinverse identity from (4) on each CAD cell, the projection polynomials and sign table, the algebraic sample establishing (11), and the root-isolation data establishing (9).  Exact algebraic arithmetic checks every record.  This is a finite sign, sample, and value-isolation certificate covering ranks zero, one, and two.  The procedure supplies an \(\varepsilon\)-incumbent whether or not the infimum is attained.  It claims neither convergence of outer sets to \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\), nor a uniform complexity bound, nor a semidefinite-program reduction. \(\square\)
