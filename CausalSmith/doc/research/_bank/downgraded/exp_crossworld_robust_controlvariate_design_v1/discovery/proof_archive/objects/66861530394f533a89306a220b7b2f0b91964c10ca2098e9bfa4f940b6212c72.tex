\textbf{Proof.}
Represent the block-separable published domain as the finite disjoint union
\[
 \mathscr D=\{0\}\sqcup\mathbb {RP}^{2}_{x}\sqcup\mathbb {RP}^{2}_{y}
 \sqcup\bigl(\mathbb {RP}^{2}_{x}\times\mathbb {RP}^{2}_{y}\bigr),
 \tag{26}
\]
corresponding respectively to ranks zero, focal-only rank one, comparison-only rank one, and rank two.  Each component is compact, so the finite disjoint union is compact.

At \(\xi_0\), lem:triangle-singular-strata-certificate gives \(\Omega(\xi_0)\succ0\).  Because the assignment space is finite, total-variation convergence of \(\nu\) implies entrywise convergence of \(\Omega\) and \(\Pi\).  Positive definiteness is open, so after restricting to a relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\), \(\Omega(\xi)\succ0\).  Exposure positivity at \(\xi_0\), finiteness of the six exposure probabilities, and continuity permit a further restriction on which all diagonal entries of \(\Pi(\xi)\) are uniformly bounded away from zero.  Consequently \(\Pi(\xi)^{-1}\) varies continuously there.

On each nonzero rank stratum of (26), positive definiteness of \(\Omega(\xi)\) gives a uniform positive lower bound for the nonzero eigenvalues of \(B^{\top}\Omega(\xi)B\) after unit-sphere normalization.  Its Moore--Penrose inverse therefore varies jointly continuously in \((\xi,B)\) on that stratum; on the rank-zero stratum it is identically zero.  By lem:restricted-marginal-extrema-continuity, \(c_L(\xi)\) and \(c_U(\xi)\) vary continuously with the ordered marginal pair.  The bounded first and second moments vary continuously under Wasserstein convergence on \([0,1]\), so def:moment-matrix makes both endpoint matrices continuous.  The finite-dimensional trace formulas now show that
\[
 (\xi,B)\longmapsto g_{B,\xi}^{+}(0)
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 (\xi,B)\longmapsto w_\xi^{+}(B)
 \tag{27}
\]
are jointly continuous on the fixed compact stratified domain \(\mathscr D\).

For completeness, we derive the optimizer stability needed below directly from (27).  Let \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\), and choose \(B_{0,j}\) from the zero-covariance argmax set at \(\xi_j\) so that it minimizes \(w_{\xi_j}^{+}\) over that argmax set.  Compactness of \(\mathscr D\) gives a convergent subsequence, still indexed by \(j\), with limit \(B_0\).  For every fixed \(B\in\mathscr D\), optimality gives
\[
 g_{B_{0,j},\xi_j}^{+}(0)\ge g_{B,\xi_j}^{+}(0).
\]
Passing to the limit using (27) proves that \(B_0\) is a zero-covariance maximizer at \(\xi_0\).  A subsequence realizing the lower limit therefore gives
\[
 \liminf_{j\to\infty}
 \min_{B_0\in\operatorname*{argmax}_B g_{B,\xi_j}^{+}(0)}w_{\xi_j}^{+}(B_0)
 \ge
 \min_{B_0\in\operatorname*{argmax}_B g_{B,\xi_0}^{+}(0)}w_{\xi_0}^{+}(B_0).
 \tag{28}
\]
The same compactness argument, now comparing minimizers of \(w_{\xi_j}^{+}\) with every fixed \(B\), proves continuity of the minimum value:
\[
 \min_B w_{\xi_j}^{+}(B)\longrightarrow\min_Bw_{\xi_0}^{+}(B).
 \tag{29}
\]
Subtracting (29) from (28) shows lower semicontinuity at \(\xi_0\) of the published-domain dominance gap appearing in the statement.

By thm:triangle-published-domain-separation-certificate, that gap at \(\xi_0\) equals
\[
 \delta=\frac{4912}{39525}>0.
\]
If no relative-open neighborhood had gap greater than \(\delta/2\), metrizability would give a sequence \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) whose gaps were at most \(\delta/2\), contradicting the lower semicontinuity just proved.  Hence some relative-open \(\mathcal U^{+}\subset\mathfrak X_{1/8}\) satisfies the displayed bound.  For a minimax optimizer \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and zero-covariance optimizer \(B_0\) at any \(\xi\in\mathcal U^{+}\),
\[
 w_\xi^{+}(B_0)-w_\xi^{+}(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
 \ge
 \min_{\widetilde B_0\in\operatorname*{argmax}_B g_{B,\xi}^{+}(0)}
 w_\xi^{+}(\widetilde B_0)-\min_Bw_\xi^{+}(B)
 >\frac{2456}{39525}>0.
\]
This proves the strict full-correspondence comparison. \(\square\)
