\textbf{Proof.}
Assumption ass:kappa-feasibility and prop:kappa-exhaustion make \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) a nonempty compact metrizable quotient.  Normalized representatives satisfy
\[
 \lVert B\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=\sqrt n,
 \qquad \lVert(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\tag{1}
\]
The bounded armwise strong law and the one-dimensional identity
\[
 \int_0^1|\widehat F_a^{-1}(u)-F_a^{-1}(u)|\,du
 =\int_0^1|\widehat F_a(y)-F_a(y)|\,dy
\tag{2}
\]
give almost surely convergence of the first two moments and of both quantile-coupling products.  Thus
\[
 \widehat c_L\to c_L,qquad \widehat c_U\to c_U,qquad
 \rho_m:=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}  o0.
\tag{3}
\]
Exposure positivity fixes a finite \(\Pi^{-1}\).  Trace duality, (1), and the gain formula give a finite fixed-design constant \(K\) such that
\[
 \sup_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|\le K\rho_m\to0
\tag{4}
\]
almost surely.  In particular,
\[
 |\widehat v_{\mathrm{mm}}-v_{\mathrm{mm}}|
 \le\sup_B|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|\to0.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(B_m\in\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm}}\), a convergent subsequence has a limit \(B_\infty\) by compactness.  For any \(B^\star\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), empirical optimality and (4) imply
\[
 w(B_m)\le w(B^\star)+2\sup_B|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|.
\]
Passing to the limit gives \(w(B_\infty)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\), so \(B_\infty\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  This is precisely the asserted outer limit. \(\square\)
