\textbf{Proof.}
The moment-matrix definition gives \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\).  Cyclicity of trace in def:gain-loss therefore gives
\[
g_B(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_BQ(0)\}
-c\,n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_BE)=a_B+b_Bc. \tag{1}
\]
Lemma lem:ht-affine-drift gives \(h(c)=h(0)+2c/n\).  Subtracting (1) yields
\[
\ell_B(c)=h(0)-a_B+(2/n-b_B)c.
\]
Taking the maximum at \(c_L,c_U\) proves
\[
w(B)=h(0)-a_B+p_\xi(b_B). \tag{2}
\]

By (2), minimization of \(w\) over the ordinary domain is equivalent to maximization of \(a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\), and hence
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
=h(0)-\max_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}\{a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\}. \tag{3}
\]
On the zero-covariance optimizer set, \(a_B=a^\star\), so
\[
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B_0)
=h(0)-a^\star+min_{B:\,a_B=a^\star}p_\xi(b_B). \tag{4}
\]
Subtracting (3) from (4) proves the displayed formula for \(\Delta_Z\).  This difference is positive exactly when some \(B\) satisfies
\[
a_B-p_\xi(b_B)>a^\star-min_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C),
\]
and rearranging gives the slope-penalty inequality in the statement.

Finally, thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer proves that \(g\), \(\ell\), and \(w\) are constant on every fiber of \(q_Z\), that \(q_Z\) is surjective, and that attained argmin and argmax sets pull back exactly.  Applying that theorem to (2)--(4) proves the matrix-domain transfer. \(\square\)
