Two arm-specific external or historical i.i.d. samples are mutually unpaired and independent of the later assignment.  They estimate \(F_1\), \(F_0\), their moments, and the two sharp empirical endpoint matrices, while the cross-world copula is neither estimated nor assumed.  For the all-rank objective, a finite strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable tolerance selects the first qualifying member of a fixed countable normalized rank-two chart enumeration; measurability is proved from the countable objective evaluations rather than inferred from compactness across rank changes.
