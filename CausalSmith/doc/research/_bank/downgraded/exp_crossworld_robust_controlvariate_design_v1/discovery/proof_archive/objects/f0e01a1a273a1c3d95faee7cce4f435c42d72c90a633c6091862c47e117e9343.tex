\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(K_0\) be the ordered field generated over \(\mathbb Q\) by the finitely many entries of \(\Pi\), \(\Omega\), \(Q(c_L)\), and \(Q(c_U)\), together with the other exactly encoded input constants.  The rational or real-algebraic input condition in def:certified-input-interface makes every element of \(K_0\) real algebraic.  By ass:exposure-positivity, every diagonal entry of \(\Pi\) is strictly positive.  Hence \(\Pi^{-1}\) exists and its entries belong to \(K_0\).  The endpoint matrices \(Q(c_L)\) and \(Q(c_U)\), as well as the design covariance \(\Omega\), are positive semidefinite by their declared spaces.  These facts provide every support, division, and positivity condition used below.

\emph{Normalized semialgebraic representation of the quotient.}
By def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain, every class in \(\mathcal B_Z^+\), the note's projective quotient of Zhao's published all-block-separable carrier, has a representative for which each arm column is either zero or has squared Euclidean norm \(n\).  It therefore suffices to work on
\[
\mathcal K=
\left\{(b_1,b_0):
\bigl(b_1=0\ \vee\ b_1^{\top}b_1=n\bigr),\quad
\bigl(b_0=0\ \vee\ b_0^{\top}b_0=n\bigr)
\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
This is a union of four compact semialgebraic sets over \(K_0\).  For literal quotient coordinates, for each nonzero arm choose its first nonzero coordinate and require that coordinate to be positive.  The finitely many resulting semialgebraic sign charts form a one-to-one cross-section of the independent-rescaling quotient.

The objective is constant on quotient fibers, including at singular ranks.  Let \(C=\Omega^{1/2}B\), and, for \(e\in\{L,U\}\), let
\[
R_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}.
\]
The square roots are proof intermediates only; the algorithm below uses polynomial formulas in the encoded entries.  Since \(S_B=C^{\top}C\), cyclicity of trace and def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss give
\[
g_B^+(c_e)
=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
C(C^{\top}C)^+C^{\top}R_e
\right].
\tag{2}
\]
If \(C=U\Sigma V^{\top}\) is a singular-value decomposition, then
\[
C(C^{\top}C)^+C^{\top}
=U\operatorname{diag}\!\bigl(\mathbf 1\{\sigma_j>0\}\bigr)U^{\top},
\]
which is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{col}(C)\).  Independent nonzero rescaling of either nonzero arm column preserves that column space.  Hence (2), and therefore \(w^+(B)\), is quotient invariant at ranks zero, one, and two.  Moreover,
\[
0\preceq C(C^{\top}C)^+C^{\top}\preceq I,
\qquad R_e\succeq0.
\]
Taking traces after multiplication by \(R_e\), and using \(n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(R_e)=h(c_e)\), yields
\[
0\le g_B^+(c_e)\le h(c_e),
\qquad
0\le \ell_B^+(c_e)\le h(c_e).
\tag{3}
\]
Equation (3) implies \(0\le w^+(B)\le H:=\max\{h(c_L),h(c_U)\}<\infty\) for every normalized representative.  Hence the attainable loss set is nonempty because the all-zero representative lies in \(\mathcal K\), and it is bounded below by \(0\) (indeed contained in \([0,H]\)); no finiteness-in-cardinality assertion is used.

\emph{Exact rank-stratified graph.}
Write \(S=S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\).  Since \(\Omega\succeq0\), the matrix \(S\) is a \(2\times2\) positive-semidefinite matrix.  Its possible ranks and spectral pseudoinverses are exactly
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\operatorname{rank}(S)&\text{semialgebraic condition}&S^+\\ \hline
0&\operatorname{tr}(S)=0&0,\\[2mm]
1&\operatorname{tr}(S)>0,\ \det(S)=0&
\displaystyle\frac{S}{\{\operatorname{tr}(S)\}^2},\\[3mm]
2&\det(S)>0&
\displaystyle\frac{\operatorname{adj}(S)}{\det(S)}.
\end{array}
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, positive semidefiniteness makes trace zero equivalent to rank zero.  At rank one, if the nonzero eigenvalue is \(\lambda=\operatorname{tr}(S)\) and \(S=\lambda uu^{\top}\) with \(\lVert u\rVert_2=1\), then \(S/\{\operatorname{tr}(S)\}^2=\lambda^{-1}uu^{\top}=S^+\).  At rank two, positive semidefiniteness and \(\det(S)>0\) imply positive definiteness, so the last expression is the ordinary inverse.  Every denominator in (4) is strictly positive on its stated stratum; at a boundary the appropriate lower-rank row applies.

For each row of (4), substitute the displayed rational expression into the two endpoint gains.  Introduce variables \(z_L,z_U,y\) and impose
\[
z_e=h(c_e)-g_B^+(c_e)\quad(e\in\{L,U\}),
\qquad y\ge z_L,
\qquad y\ge z_U,
\qquad (y=z_L\ \vee\ y=z_U).
\tag{5}
\]
On rank one multiply the gain equalities by the strictly positive quantity \(\{\operatorname{tr}(S)\}^2\); on rank two multiply them by the strictly positive quantity \(\det(S)\); no denominator occurs on rank zero.  Consequently (1), the applicable row of (4), and (5) form a finite Boolean combination of polynomial equalities and strict or weak inequalities over \(K_0\).  Their union is the semialgebraic graph
\[
\mathcal G=
\{(b_1,b_0,y):(b_1,b_0)\in\mathcal K,\ y=w^+(B)\}.
\tag{6}
\]
It covers every normalized representative and all three ranks, including equality boundaries, without evaluating an inverse at a zero denominator.

\emph{Exact value isolation.}
Define
\[
\mathcal T=
\left\{t\in\mathbb R:
\exists(b_1,b_0,y)\in\mathcal G\ \text{such that}\ y\le t
\right\}.
\tag{7}
\]
By (3), \(\mathcal T\) is nonempty and bounded below, and by the definitions of \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) and \(\mathcal G\),
\[
\inf\mathcal T
=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\tag{8}
\]
The formula in (7) is first order over a real closed field.  Apply lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination to eliminate \(b_1,b_0,z_L,z_U,y\).  The resulting quantifier-free description of \(\mathcal T\) uses finitely many polynomial sign conditions over \(K_0\).  A one-dimensional polynomial sign pattern is constant on every interval avoiding the finitely many real roots of its nonzero polynomials.  Every finite boundary point of \(\mathcal T\) is therefore a root of one of those polynomials.  By (3) and (8), \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is such a finite boundary point.  Hence \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is algebraic over \(K_0\), and thus is a real-algebraic number.

The corresponding cylindrical algebraic decomposition terminates: it begins with finitely many variables and polynomials; every projection stage forms finitely many coefficients, discriminants, and resultants; exact real-algebraic root isolation produces finitely many sign cells; and lifting repeats a finite operation through finitely many coordinates.  The equivalence in lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination certifies the sign decomposition.  Refine an isolating interval for \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) until its exactly checked width is at most the exactly encoded positive number \(\varepsilon\).  This returns rational or real-algebraic numbers \(L_\varepsilon^+\) and \(U_\varepsilon^+\) such that
\[
L_\varepsilon^+\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\le U_\varepsilon^+,
\qquad
U_\varepsilon^+-L_\varepsilon^+\le\varepsilon.
\tag{9}
\]
Strict positivity and exact encoding of \(\varepsilon\) make the refinement test terminate.

\emph{Algebraic incumbent and finite certificate.}
Attainment is unnecessary.  By the defining property of an infimum and \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
\exists [B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+
\quad
w^+(B)<v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon.
\tag{10}
\]
Adjoin the real-algebraic constant \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) to the coefficient field and apply CAD lifting to (10), represented by (1), (4), and (5).  Because the formula is nonempty, CAD returns a sample point with real-algebraic coordinates.  Its normalized representative defines a basis class \([B_\varepsilon]_+\) in the note's quotient \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) of Zhao's published carrier, and
\[
w^+(B_\varepsilon)<v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon,
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
w^+(B_\varepsilon)\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon.
\tag{11}
\]
Likewise, in the finite quotient charts following (1), the weak sublevel set
\[
\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:w^+(B)\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon\}
\]
is semialgebraic by the same rank-stratified formulas and is nonempty by (10).

Finally, retain the normalized quotient-chart list, the rank condition and pseudoinverse identity from (4) on every CAD cell, the projection polynomials and sign table, the algebraic sample establishing (11), and the root-isolation data establishing (9).  Exact algebraic arithmetic checks each item.  This is a finite sign, sample, and value-isolation certificate covering ranks zero, one, and two.  It supplies an \(\varepsilon\)-incumbent whether or not the infimum is attained and asserts neither outer-set convergence to \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\), a uniform complexity bound, nor a semidefinite-program reduction.  \(\square\)
