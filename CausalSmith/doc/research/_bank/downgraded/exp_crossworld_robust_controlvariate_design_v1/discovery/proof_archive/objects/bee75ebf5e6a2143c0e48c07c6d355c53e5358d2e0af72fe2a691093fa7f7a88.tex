\textbf{Proof.}
At \(\xi_0\), thm:triangle-completion-separation-certificate supplies the ordinary separation \(\delta>0\), the optimizer-wide slack \(\eta>0\), and the strict singular-stratum margins
\[
\frac{31}{80}-\frac{87}{400}=\frac{17}{100}>0,
\qquad
\frac{39}{80}-\frac{2613}{8432}=\frac{468}{2635}>0.               \tag{1}
\]
We first prove persistence of the rank conclusions.

On the finite assignment space, total-variation convergence of \(\nu\) implies entrywise convergence of \(\Pi\) and \(\Omega\).  Exposure positivity at \(\xi_0\) therefore makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) continuous on a relative-open neighborhood.  Wasserstein convergence of the bounded marginals gives convergence of their first and second moments, and lem:restricted-marginal-extrema-continuity gives convergence of the lower and upper covariance endpoints.  Def:moment-matrix then gives convergence of \(Q(c_L)\) and \(Q(c_U)\).  Because the triangle matrix \(\Omega(\xi_0)\) is positive definite, shrink the neighborhood so that \(\Omega(\xi)\succeq\rho I_{2n}\) for some \(\rho>0\) throughout it.

Under the normalization in def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain, rank zero is a singleton, each rank-one component is a radius-\(\sqrt n\) sphere modulo sign, and rank two is the product of two such quotients.  These are finitely many compact components distinguished by which arm column vanishes.  On a rank-one component the Moore--Penrose formula reduces to a scalar inverse whose denominator is at least \(n\rho\).  On the rank-two component, the two block-supported normalized columns are Euclidean-orthogonal and have norm \(\sqrt n\), so
\[
B^{\top}\Omega(\xi)B\succeq n\rho I_2.                            \tag{2}
\]
Thus the two endpoint gains and losses are jointly continuous in \((\xi,B)\) on each fixed compact component, uniformly on a compact neighborhood of \(\xi_0\).  Uniform continuity implies directly that the maximum or minimum over each component varies continuously with \(\xi\): the absolute difference of two such optimum values is bounded by the supremum norm difference of their objective functions.  The strict component gaps in (1) therefore persist after shrinking to a relative-open neighborhood.  Consequently every completion optimizer for either criterion has rank two there; in particular, the completion equality set for the residual criterion is nonempty there because each component has a minimizer and there are only finitely many components.

We next prove that these rank-two optimizers stay inside the \(\kappa\)-regular class.  If not, there would exist \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) and rank-two completion optimizers \(B_j\) for one of the two criteria with
\[
\lambda_{\min}\{n^{-1}S_{B_j}(\xi_j)\}<\kappa+\frac\eta2.        \tag{3}
\]
Compactness of the normalized rank-two component gives a subsequence \(B_j\to\overline B\).  Joint continuity of the criterion and comparison with every fixed rank-two basis show that \(\overline B\) is a rank-two optimizer for the same completion criterion at \(\xi_0\).  Thm:triangle-completion-separation-certificate identifies every such optimizer with the image of an ordinary optimizer and gives
\[
\lambda_{\min}\{n^{-1}S_{\overline B}(\xi_0)\}\ge\kappa+\eta,
\]
contradicting (3) after passage to the limit.  Hence every nearby completion optimizer has normalized eigenvalue at least \(\kappa+\eta/2\).

Lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification preserves both criteria under the ordinary preimage.  Since each global completion optimum is attained at a point of \(\mathcal B_\kappa(\xi)\), its value equals the corresponding optimum over \(\mathcal B_\kappa(\xi)\).  Both optimizer-set inclusions follow, and therefore
\[
\mathcal A_0^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_0(\xi)),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+(\xi)
=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)).                        \tag{4}
\]

Finally, the slack at \(\xi_0\) permits application of lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity.  Since \(\Delta(\xi_0)=\delta\), a sequential contradiction shows that a still smaller relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\) satisfies
\[
\Delta(\xi)>\frac\delta2=\frac{2456}{39525}
\qquad(\xi\in\mathcal U).                                       \tag{5}
\]
For every \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)\) and \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\), def:dominance-gap and the equality \(w_\xi(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\) give
\[
w_\xi(B_0)-w_\xi(B_{\mathrm{mm}})\ge\Delta(\xi)>\frac\delta2.
\]
Equation (4) and preservation of loss under \(\iota_\xi\) give the identical strict inequality for every completion optimizer pair. \(\square\)
