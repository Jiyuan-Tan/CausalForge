For algebraic \(\Omega\), \(\kappa\), and a rational pivot-chart box, feasibility of
\[
 \lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=n,
 \qquad \lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
 \qquad S_B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0
\]
is an existential formula over a real closed field.  In dimension two the last constraint is exactly the three polynomial inequalities
\[
 (S_B)_{11}-n\kappa\ge0,
 \quad (S_B)_{22}-n\kappa\ge0,
 \quad \det(S_B-n\kappa I_2)\ge0.
\]
Consequently CAD terminates with either an algebraic feasible sample, including on the equality boundary, or a finite sign-cell infeasibility certificate checkable by exact algebraic arithmetic.
