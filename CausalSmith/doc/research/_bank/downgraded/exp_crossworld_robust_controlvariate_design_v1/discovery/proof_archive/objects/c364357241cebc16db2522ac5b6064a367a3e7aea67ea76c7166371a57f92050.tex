\textbf{Proof.}
Assumption ass:known-exposure gives the deployed estimator in def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator its exposure-potential-outcome interpretation.  Put
\[
v_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B).
\]
The assumed optimizer containment implies
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=v_n,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\mathcal A_{0,Z},
\qquad
\Delta_Z=q_n-v_n.
\tag{1}
\]
Indeed, a global minimizer and every global zero-covariance maximizer are feasible in the smaller class.  Conversely, any smaller-class point attaining the corresponding global value belongs to the appropriate global optimizer set.

The robust regret and conditional zero-surrogate inequalities in thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer give
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Applying that theorem's feasible-estimator bridge once in each direction yields
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n-2R_n.
\tag{3}
\]
This establishes the finite end-to-end inequality.

Under the three displayed deterministic envelopes,
\[
C_n\mathbb E_PM_n
\le K_CK_M\frac{n^\beta}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}.
\tag{4}
\]
Substituting (4) and the bound on \(R_n\) into (3) gives
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge \Delta_Z
-(4L_n+2)K_CK_M\frac{n^\beta}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}
-2K_Rn^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus the stated finite-\(n\) strictness condition makes the right-hand side positive and proves \(V_0>V_{\mathrm{mm}}\).

For the asymptotic claim, multiply (3) by \(n\).  The explicitly retained premise
\[
nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0
\tag{6}
\]
and the assumed bound \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\) imply
\[
n(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0.
\tag{7}
\]
Lemma lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence and \(4\alpha+4\beta<1\) give \(nR_n\to0\).  Hence (3) yields
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n(V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\Delta_Z>0.
\]
The argument uses, rather than derives, optimizer containment, the bounded linear-error constants, and (6).  Every inner variance is conditional on the fixed potential-outcome array and is taken only over the known assignment law; the pilot and coupling expectations are ex ante evaluations.  The observable variance bridge remains confined to the positive-definite rank-two class. \(\square\)
