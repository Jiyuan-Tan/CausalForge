\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(K(\xi)=\mathcal B_\kappa(\xi)\), viewed inside the compact quotient of the product of the two normalized radius-\(\sqrt n\) spheres.  We first record joint continuity of the ingredients.

Because the assignment space is finite, total-variation convergence \(\nu_j\to\nu_0\) implies entrywise convergence of \(\Pi_j\) and \(\Omega_j\).  Exposure positivity at \(\xi_0\), together with finiteness of the \(2n\) diagonal entries, gives a neighborhood on which all exposure probabilities are bounded away from zero.  Hence \(\Pi_j^{-1}\to\Pi_0^{-1}\).

Wasserstein convergence on \([0,1]\) implies weak convergence.  By lem:restricted-marginal-extrema-continuity, the minimum and maximum of
\[
H\longmapsto\int y_1y_0\,dH(y_1,y_0)
\]
over \(H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)\) vary continuously with the ordered marginal pair.  Ass:frechet-covariance-domain identifies these extrema, after subtraction of \(\mu_1\mu_0\), with \(c_L\) and \(c_U\).  The first and second marginal moments also converge because \(y\) and \(y^2\) are bounded continuous functions on \([0,1]\).  Therefore \(c_L,c_U,Q(c_L)\), and \(Q(c_U)\) vary continuously with \(\xi\).

The map
\[
(\xi,B)\longmapsto
\lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega(\xi)B\right)
\tag{3}
\]
is continuous on the normalized ambient quotient.  Its \(\kappa\)-superlevel graph is closed.  On that graph,
\[
\lVert S_B^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\tag{4}
\]
The finite-dimensional trace formulas, the continuity just proved, and (4) show that
\[
(\xi,B)\longmapsto g_{B,\xi}(0)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(\xi,B)\longmapsto w_\xi(B)
\tag{5}
\]
are jointly continuous on the feasible graph.  These functions are invariant under the projective sign identifications, so (5) is well defined on the quotient.

We next establish sequential upper stability of the two optimizer correspondences at \(\xi_0\).  Suppose \(B_j\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)\) and, after passage to a subsequence in the compact ambient quotient, \(B_j\to B\).  Closedness of the feasible graph gives \(B\in K(\xi_0)\).  Choose \(B^\circ\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\).  The displayed slack hypothesis yields
\[
\lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}S_{B^\circ}(\xi_0)\right)\ge\kappa+\eta,
\]
so continuity of (3) makes the fixed basis \(B^\circ\) feasible for all sufficiently large \(j\).  Optimality and (5) then give
\[
w_{\xi_j}(B_j)\le w_{\xi_j}(B^\circ)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
w_{\xi_0}(B)\le w_{\xi_0}(B^\circ)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0).
\]
Feasibility of \(B\) supplies the reverse inequality, hence \(B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\).  The same argument, with the optimization inequality reversed and \(g_{B,\xi}(0)\) in place of \(w_\xi(B)\), proves that every limit of \(B_j\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)\) belongs to \(\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)\).

Each feasible set is nonempty by membership in \(\mathfrak X_\kappa\), compact by prop:kappa-exhaustion, and has continuous objectives by (5).  Thus both optimizer sets are nonempty and compact.  Define
\[
\phi_0(\xi)=\min_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)}w_\xi(B),
\qquad
\phi_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)=\max_{B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)}w_\xi(B).
\]
Take a subsequence realizing \(\liminf_j\phi_0(\xi_j)\), select a minimizer from each \(\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)\), and then take a convergent further subsequence.  Upper stability and (5) imply
\[
\liminf_{j\to\infty}\phi_0(\xi_j)\ge\phi_0(\xi_0).
\tag{6}
\]
Applying the analogous argument to a subsequence realizing the upper limit and to maximizers over \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)\) gives
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\phi_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)
\le\phi_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0).
\tag{7}
\]
By def:dominance-gap, \(\Delta=\phi_0-\phi_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Combining (6)--(7) yields
\[
\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)
\ge
\liminf_{j\to\infty}\phi_0(\xi_j)
-\limsup_{j\to\infty}\phi_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)
\ge\Delta(\xi_0),
\]
as claimed. \(\square\)
