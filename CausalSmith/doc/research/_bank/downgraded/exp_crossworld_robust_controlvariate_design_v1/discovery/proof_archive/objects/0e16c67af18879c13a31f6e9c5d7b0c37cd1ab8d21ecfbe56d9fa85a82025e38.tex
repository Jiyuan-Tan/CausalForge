\(\mathcal B_Z=\{[B]:B=\begin{bmatrix}b_1&0\\0&b_0\end{bmatrix},\ \lambda_{\min}(B^{\top}\Omega B)>0,\ b_1,b_0\in\mathbb R^n\}\), where \([B]\) quotients independent nonzero rescaling of the two columns.  Equivalently, \(\mathcal B_Z\) is the positive-definite rank-two restriction of Zhao's full published pseudoinverse class \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).  It is used for ordinary-inverse regularity and deployability results; it is not Zhao's full basis domain.
