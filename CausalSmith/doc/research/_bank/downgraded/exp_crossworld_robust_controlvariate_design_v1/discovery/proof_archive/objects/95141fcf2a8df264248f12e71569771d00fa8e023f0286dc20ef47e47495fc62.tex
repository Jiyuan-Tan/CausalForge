\textbf{Proof.}
If \(0<\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\), then \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_2}\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}\).  Minimizing the same continuous function over the larger compact set proves the first inequality.  Prop:kappa-exhaustion also gives
\[
 \mathcal B_Z=\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa.
\]
The infimum of a function over a union is the infimum of the infima over its nonempty members, so
\[
 \inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
 =\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\]
If \(B_Z^\star\) attains the right-hand infimum, prop:kappa-exhaustion supplies \(\kappa_0>0\) with \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_0}\).  For \(0<\kappa\le\kappa_0\), nesting yields
\[
 \inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
 \le\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
 \le w(B_Z^\star)
 =\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
\]
This proves equality throughout.  No step concerns the singular or pseudoinverse strata of the full published carrier. \(\square\)
