\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
v_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B).
\]
The assumed ordinary-domain minimax and zero-covariance optimizer sets are nonempty and lie in \(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\).  Therefore restricting the minimization and zero-covariance maximization to \(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\) does not change their values.  Moreover, every rate-class zero-covariance maximizer attains the full value \(a^\star\), while every full ordinary-domain zero-covariance maximizer belongs to the rate class by hypothesis.  Hence
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=v_n,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\mathcal A_{0,Z},
\qquad
\Delta_Z=q_n-v_n.
\tag{1}
\]
The last equality uses the infimum formulation proved in thm:ordinary-inverse-strict-improvement-characterization and does not require the slope-penalty infimum itself to be attained.

The robust-regret conclusion and the quantitative zero-selector conclusion of thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer give
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_n+o(n^{-1}),
\qquad
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)\ge q_n-o(n^{-1}).
\tag{2}
\]
Subtracting the two relations in (2) and using (1) yields
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\Delta_Z-o(n^{-1}).
\tag{3}
\]

For \(s\in\{0,\mathrm{mm}\}\), the feasible-estimator bridge in the same theorem states
\[
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}
\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
\right]
=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1}).
\tag{4}
\]
Substituting (4) twice into (3), multiplying by \(n\), and taking lower limits gives a lower bound equal to \(\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\Delta_Z\), which is strictly positive by hypothesis.  This is the claimed ordering of the two feasible independent-pilot Horvitz--Thompson deployments.  The calculation concerns ex ante expected conditional design variance throughout and invokes no sampling limit law. \(\square\)
