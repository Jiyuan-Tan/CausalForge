\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(X=S^{n-1}_{\sqrt n}\times S^{n-1}_{\sqrt n}\) for the product of the two normalized arm spheres, and identify the independent sign changes in the two factors.  By Proposition~\(\mathrm{prop{:}kappa\mbox{-}exhaustion}\), the subset
\[
 K=\left\{[B]\in X:\lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega B\right)\geq\kappa\right\}
 =\mathcal B_\kappa
\]
is compact.  Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}kappa\mbox{-}feasibility}\) makes \(K\) nonempty.  Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}exposure\mbox{-}positivity}\) makes every diagonal entry of \(\Pi\) positive, so \(\Pi^{-1}\) is defined.  Moreover, for every \(B\in K\),
\[
 S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2. \tag{1}
\]
Thus the ordinary inverse in each endpoint loss is defined throughout \(K\).

For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), let \(\ell_e(B)=\ell_B(c_e)\).  Matrix multiplication, trace, and the map \(S\mapsto S^{-1}\) are continuous, with the inverse map uniformly continuous on matrices satisfying (1).  Hence \(\ell_L\) and \(\ell_U\) are continuous on \(K\), and so is
\[
 w(B)=\max\{\ell_L(B),\ell_U(B)\}. \tag{2}
\]
Compactness therefore gives a minimizer and
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}=\min_{B\in K}w(B),
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\neq\varnothing. \tag{3}
\]

We next record the effective form of this continuity that is supplied by the input interface.  By continuity of \(B\mapsto n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega B\) and compactness of \(K\), there is a neighborhood \(N_K\) of \(K\) on which
\[
 \lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega B\right)\geq\kappa/2. \tag{4}
\]
On (4), inversion of the two-by-two matrix has a finite modulus of continuity; explicitly, the resolvent identity gives
\[
 \lVert S^{-1}-T^{-1}\rVert
 \leq \lVert S^{-1}\rVert\,\lVert S-T\rVert\,\lVert T^{-1}\rVert
 \leq \frac{4}{n^2\kappa^2}\lVert S-T\rVert. \tag{5}
\]
The effective coefficient-refinement moduli in \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\), together with (5), consequently imply the following: for every rational \(\alpha>0\), one can effectively reach a chart subdivision depth and an arithmetic precision such that every chart box \(C\) meeting \(K\) has diameter at most \(\alpha\), lies in \(N_K\), and is assigned outward-rounded rational bounds \(l_C,u_C\) satisfying
\[
 l_C\leq w(B)\leq u_C\quad(B\in C\cap K),
 \qquad
 u_C-l_C\leq\alpha. \tag{6}
\]
Indeed, the interval extensions of matrix multiplication, the inverse, trace, and maximum converge under box bisection and coefficient refinement; (4)--(5) exclude an inverse singularity on every sufficiently small box meeting \(K\).  This is precisely the effective refinement information required in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}certified\mbox{-}input\mbox{-}interface}\).

Fix the exactly encoded \(\varepsilon>0\), and choose an exactly encoded rational \(\alpha\) with
\[
 0<\alpha\leq\varepsilon. \tag{7}
\]
The finitely many signed pivot charts in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}branch\mbox{-}bound\mbox{-}handle}\) cover \(X\).  Bisect them to the finite common depth furnished by (6), increasing that depth if necessary so every resulting box has diameter at most \(\varepsilon\).  There are only finitely many boxes at this depth.  The exact sphere-and-\(\kappa\)-feasibility decision supplied by \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\) terminates on each box, so all boxes disjoint from \(K\) are certified infeasible and every box meeting \(K\), including a box whose feasible part lies entirely on
\[
 \lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega B\right)=\kappa, \tag{8}
\]
is recognized as feasible.  The outward interval computations in (6) also terminate at the requested precision.

For completeness, an exact feasible point can be named even when \(K\) has empty relative interior.  Starting from any box \(C\) recognized as meeting \(K\), bisect it and use the exact feasibility decision to select a closed child that still meets \(K\).  Iteration gives nested closed boxes \(C=C_0\supseteq C_1\supseteq\cdots\), each meeting \(K\), with diameters tending to zero.  Compactness and closedness imply
\[
 \bigcap_{j\geq0}(C_j\cap K)=\{B_C\}
 \quad\text{for some }B_C\in K. \tag{9}
\]
The finite rule producing \(C_j\) at requested precision is a certified inclusion-oracle name for \(B_C\), and every request terminates.  Thus (9) is an exact feasible representation authorized by Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}certified\mbox{-}output}\), not an appeal to a floating-point interior point.

For every feasible terminal box compute the bounds in (6), and set
\[
 L_\varepsilon=\min_{C:C\cap K\neq\varnothing}l_C. \tag{10}
\]
Choose a box \(C^\star\) attaining the finite minimum, take \(B_\varepsilon=B_{C^\star}\) from (9), and set
\[
 U_\varepsilon=u_{C^\star}. \tag{11}
\]
The bounds are rational because all interval endpoints are outward-rounded rationals.  Every \(B\in K\) belongs to at least one feasible terminal box, so (6) and (10) give \(L_\varepsilon\leq v_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Equations (6), (10), and (11) give
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}leq w(B_\varepsilon)leq U_\varepsilon,
 \qquad
 U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon
 =u_{C^\star}-l_{C^\star}leq\alpha\leq\varepsilon. \tag{12}
\]

It remains to verify the outer minimizer certificate and the pruning rule.  Discard a box only if the exact decision says that it is disjoint from \(K\), or if its valid lower bound satisfies \(l_C>U_\varepsilon\).  In the latter case, (6) and (12) imply, for every \(B\in C\cap K\),
\[
 w(B)\geq l_C>U_\varepsilon\geq w(B_\varepsilon)\geq v_{\mathrm{mm}}, \tag{13}
\]
so the box contains no global minimizer.  Therefore the union \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\) of the feasible parts of all unpruned boxes contains \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Every retained box has diameter at most \(\varepsilon\), whence
\[
 \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\subseteq\mathcal C_\varepsilon,
 \qquad h_\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon. \tag{14}
\]
In particular, a box meeting the equality boundary (8) cannot receive an infeasibility certificate and is discarded only if the strict objective test in (13) applies.

All chart covers and terminal box collections used above are finite.  Each exact feasibility query, arithmetic refinement, and rational comparison terminates by the defining properties of \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\).  Consequently the procedure stops after finitely many subdivisions and the finite records (6), (9)--(14), together with all infeasibility and pruning decisions, form the checkable certificate \(\mathscr K_\varepsilon\).  Finally,
\[
 0\leq U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon,
 \qquad 0\leq h_\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon,
\]
so both quantities converge to zero along every sequence \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\).  The argument selects an instance-dependent refinement depth and supplies no uniform complexity bound; it also makes no semidefinite-program reduction. \(\square\)
