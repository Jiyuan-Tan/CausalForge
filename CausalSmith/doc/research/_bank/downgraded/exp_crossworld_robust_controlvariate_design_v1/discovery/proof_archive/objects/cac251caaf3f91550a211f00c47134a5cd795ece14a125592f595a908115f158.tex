\textbf{Proof.}
By ass:kappa-feasibility, \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is nonempty.  By prop:kappa-exhaustion it is compact and is contained in \(\mathcal B_Z\).  Hence every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\) satisfies
\[
S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2\succ0.
\]
Ass:exposure-positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well-defined.  On the normalized representative set, the maps \(B\mapsto B^{\top}\Omega B\), \(S\mapsto S^{-1}\) on \(\{S:S\succeq n\kappa I_2\}\), matrix multiplication, and the trace are continuous.  Therefore, for each fixed endpoint \(e\in\{L,U\}\),
\[
B\longmapsto g_B(c_e)
=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
S_B^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega B
\right]
\]
is continuous.  Indeed, if \(T=\operatorname{diag}(t_1,t_0)\) with \(t_1t_0\ne0\), then
\[
S_{BT}=T^{\top}S_BT,
\qquad
S_{BT}^{-1}=T^{-1}S_B^{-1}T^{-\top},
\]
and substitution into the trace makes all four factors of \(T\) cancel.  Thus independent nonzero rescaling of the two columns leaves the gain unchanged, so the continuous map on normalized representatives descends to the projective quotient.  The endpoint quantities \(h(c_e)\) do not depend on \(B\).  Thus each \(B\mapsto\ell_B(c_e)=h(c_e)-g_B(c_e)\) is continuous.  The identity \(\max\{x,y\}=(x+y+|x-y|)/2\) then shows that
\[
w(B)=\max\{\ell_B(c_L),\ell_B(c_U)\},
\]
is continuous.  Equivalently, the same conclusion follows directly from the endpoint representation in lem:ht-affine-drift.  The map \(B\mapsto g_B(0)\) is continuous by the identical argument.

To verify attainment directly, let \(m=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)\) and choose \(B_j\in\mathcal B_\kappa\) with \(w(B_j)\le m+j^{-1}\).  Compactness supplies a convergent subsequence with limit \(B^\star\in\mathcal B_\kappa\), and continuity gives \(w(B^\star)=m\).  The same argument applied to \(-g_B(0)\) supplies a maximizer of \(g_B(0)\).  Consequently the correspondences \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(\mathcal A_0\) in def:argmax-correspondences are nonempty, and \(m=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Every \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) has \(w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\), whence
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\]
Moreover, every \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\) is an element of \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  The defining minimum therefore gives, pointwise in \(B_0\),
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}
=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
\le w(B_0).
\]
Taking the infimum over the nonempty set \(\mathcal A_0\) proves
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0}w(B_0).
\]

Finally, strict inequality cannot follow from the stated conditions.  Keep any design satisfying ass:exposure-positivity and ass:kappa-feasibility and choose the lawful degenerate marginals \(F_1=F_0=\delta_0\).  Then \(\mu_1=\mu_0=\sigma_1^2=\sigma_0^2=0\), \(c_L=c_U=0\), and def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(0)=0\).  Hence, for every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\),
\[
h(0)=g_B(0)=\ell_B(0)=w(B)=0.
\]
Thus \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\mathcal A_0=\mathcal B_\kappa\) and both sides of the dominance inequality equal zero.  The fixed-threshold rank-two certificate is therefore weak without an additional lawful strict-separation witness. \(\square\)
