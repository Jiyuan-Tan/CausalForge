\textbf{Proof.}
If \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\), then \(S_B\succ0\), both arm columns are nonzero, and \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)=2\).  On this stratum the completion equivalence relation is exactly independent nonzero rescaling of the two columns.  Hence \(\iota([B])=[B]_+\) is well defined and injective.  Conversely, a positive-semidefinite two-by-two matrix of rank two is positive definite, so every rank-two completion class has a unique preimage in \(\mathcal B_Z\).  Finally \(S_B^+=S_B^{-1}\) on rank two.  Substitution into the gain definitions proves equality of gains; subtraction from the common \(h(c)\) and maximization over the two endpoints prove equality of losses. \(\square\)
