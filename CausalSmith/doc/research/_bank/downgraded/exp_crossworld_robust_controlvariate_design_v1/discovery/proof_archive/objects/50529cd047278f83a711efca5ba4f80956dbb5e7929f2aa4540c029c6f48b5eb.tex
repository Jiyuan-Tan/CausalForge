For \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), let \(S_B^+\) be the Moore--Penrose inverse of \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\), and define
\[
g_B^+(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[S_B^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c)\Pi^{-1}\Omega B\right],
\qquad \ell_B^+(c)=h(c)-g_B^+(c),
\]
\[
w^+(B)=\max\{\ell_B^+(c_L),\ell_B^+(c_U)\},
\qquad v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+:=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B),
\]
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
=\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\},
\qquad \mathcal A_0^+=\operatorname*{argmax}_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}g_B^+(0).
\]
The equality set \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) may be empty because the Moore--Penrose loss need not be lower semicontinuous across rank drops.  These are objects of the note's novel completion, not formulas or a domain attributed to Zhao.
