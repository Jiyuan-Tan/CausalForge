Suppose ass:exposure-positivity holds and \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\).  Then the full published Moore--Penrose value equals the nonsingular-repair infimum and the vanishing-threshold value:
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 =\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
 =\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
   \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\]
The rank-two published minimizers are exactly
\[
 \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\mathcal B_Z
 =\bigcup_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+}}
 \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa),
\]
where \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\) denotes the fixed-threshold argmin.  The singular published minimizers form the separate explicit stratum
\[
 \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\{B:\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1\}
 =\{B\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1, w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\}.
\]
Thus singular ties are retained rather than silently removed; the theorem does not assert that the Moore--Penrose equality set is nonempty.
