\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(\mathcal K_n=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\).  Assumption ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility makes \(\mathcal K_n\) nonempty.  Its normalized representatives form a closed subset of a product of two spheres, and the residual sign quotient is compact.  The uniform lower bound \(B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2\) makes matrix inversion continuous throughout this set.  Consequently both empirical criteria are continuous in the basis and Borel measurable in the finite pilot sample, and their optimizer sets are nonempty and compact.  The fixed signed-chart lexicographic selector is measurable: successively minimizing each coordinate over the preceding compact optimizer set gives a compact-valued graph, and each inverse image of a rational open interval can be expressed through infima over a fixed countable dense subset.  This proves the existence and measurability asserted in def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator.  Assumption ass:known-exposure is inherited by the observable map, so the deployed outcome stack has the exposure-potential-outcome interpretation used below.

Let \(M_n,d_n,e_n\) be the displayed quantities in the theorem.  Lemma lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate gives, pathwise under the pilot law,
\[
d_n\le C_nM_n,
\qquad
e_n\le2C_nM_n.
\tag{1}
\]
Choose \(B_n^\star\in\mathcal K_n\) with \(w(B_n^\star)=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}\).  Empirical optimality yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
&\le\widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d_n\\
&\le\widehat w(B_n^\star)+d_n\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+2d_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
The population minimum gives the reverse inequality.  Taking pilot expectations and applying (1) proves
\[
0\le\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})-v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}
\le2C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{3}
\]

Conditional on the pilot, \(\widehat B_s\) is fixed and belongs to \(\mathcal K_n\).  Assumption ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence therefore permits application of lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence at this pilot-measurable basis.  Assumption ass:frechet-covariance-domain and thm:fixed-basis-endpoints give, for every fixed \(B\in\mathcal K_n\),
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_B(c_H)=w(B).
\tag{4}
\]
For two bounded real families, \(\lvert\sup_H f_H-\sup_H g_H\rvert\le\sup_H\lvert f_H-g_H\rvert\).  Apply this inequality to the uniform variance bridge in lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, use (4), and then integrate over the pilot.  This proves
\[
\lvert V_s-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)\rvert\le R_n,
\qquad s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Under the explicit triangular-sequence hypothesis
\[
nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0,
\tag{6}
\]
equation (3) gives robust regret \(o(n^{-1})\).  Lemma lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence gives \(nR_n\to0\), so (3), (5), and (6) prove the two stated first-order conclusions for the robust selector and both feasible-estimator bridges.

For the zero-covariance selector, choose \(B_n^0\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}\), which is possible by compactness and continuity.  Empirical optimality and (1) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)
&=g_{B_n^0}(0)-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\\
&\le2e_n
\le4C_nM_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
If the displayed deterministic linear error bound holds, then
\[
[q_n-w(\widehat B_0)]_+
\le L_n\{g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\}
\le4L_nC_nM_n.
\tag{8}
\]
Since \(x\ge q-[q-x]_+\) for real \(x,q\), taking expectations in (8) proves
\[
q_n-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\le4L_nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{9}
\]
If \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\), hypothesis (6) makes the right-hand side \(o(n^{-1})\), proving the stated consequence.  No primitive condition used above establishes such a uniformly bounded \(L_n\); (8)--(9) are explicitly conditional on that separate deterministic geometry.  Without it, (7) controls nominal-gain regret only and cannot supply a first-order worst-loss ordering.  All selectors remain in the regular rank-two class, where Zhao's pseudoinverse equals the ordinary inverse. \(\square\)
