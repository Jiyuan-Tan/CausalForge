\textbf{Proof.}
The optimizer-wide slack in the hypothesis is exactly the premise of lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity.  Suppose no relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) had positive gap.  Because the relative topology in def:lawful-instance-class is metrizable, for every integer \(j\ge1\) one could choose \(\xi_j\in\mathfrak X_\kappa\) with distance less than \(1/j\) from \(\xi_0\) and \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le0\).  Then \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\), while the lower-semicontinuity lemma gives
\[
0\ge\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)\ge\Delta(\xi_0)>0,
\]
a contradiction.  Hence some relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\) of \(\xi_0\) satisfies \(\Delta(\xi)>0\) throughout. \(\square\)
