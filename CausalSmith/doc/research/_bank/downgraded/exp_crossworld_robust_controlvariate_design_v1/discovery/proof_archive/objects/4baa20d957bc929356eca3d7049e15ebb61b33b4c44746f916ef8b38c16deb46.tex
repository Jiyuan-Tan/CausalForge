For every moment-complete input \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\) satisfying exposure positivity and \(\kappa\)-feasibility, and every exactly encoded \(\varepsilon>0\), the candidate projective interval branch-and-bound procedure terminates after finitely many subdivisions and returns a checkable certificate \(\mathscr K_{\varepsilon}\) such that
\[
L_{\varepsilon}\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_{\varepsilon})\le U_{\varepsilon},
\qquad
U_{\varepsilon}-L_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon,
\qquad
h_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\subseteq\mathcal C_{\varepsilon}.
\]
Here \(B_{\varepsilon}\in\mathcal B_\kappa\) is represented exactly, including when the feasible set has no relative interior and lies on \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega B)=\kappa\).  A chart box meeting that equality boundary is never discarded as infeasible; it may be discarded only when its certified objective lower bound exceeds the incumbent upper bound.  Consequently, along any sequence \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), the certified value gaps and retained-box mesh diameters converge to zero.  This is a finite, instance-dependent termination result; it asserts neither a uniform complexity bound nor a semidefinite-program reduction.
