\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\mathcal K_n=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\) and define
\[
 M_n=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
 \lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}},
 \quad
 d_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal K_n}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|,
 \quad
 e_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal K_n}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|.
 \tag{1}
\]
Lemma lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate gives the pathwise finite-sample inequalities
\[
 d_n\leq C_nM_n,
 \qquad
 e_n\leq2C_nM_n.
 \tag{2}
\]

The normalized representatives of \(\mathcal K_n\) form a closed subset of the product of two radius-\(\sqrt n\) spheres: the coordinate envelope and the eigenvalue inequality are closed.  The finite projective quotient is therefore compact.  Since \(w\) and \(g_B(0)\) are continuous on the uniformly positive-definite class, their minimum and maximum are attained.  Choose \(B_n^\star\in\mathcal K_n\) with \(w(B_n^\star)=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}\).  Empirical optimality of \(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}}\) gives, for every pilot realization,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
 &\leq\widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d_n\\
 &\leq\widehat w(B_n^\star)+d_n\\
 &\leq v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+2d_n.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{3}
\]
The reverse inequality \(w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\geq v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}\) follows from the definition of the population minimum.  Taking pilot expectations in (3) and using (2) proves the finite-\(n\) robust-selector regret certificate
\[
 0\leq
 \mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})-v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}
 \leq2C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
 \tag{4}
\]

Let \(R_n\) be the deterministic envelope constructed in lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, so
\[
 0\leq R_n\leq K_Rn^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}=o(n^{-1}).
 \tag{5}
\]
For \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\), define the actual feasible-estimator worst-coupling evaluation
\[
 V_s=
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
 \mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
 \operatorname{Var}_\nu
 (\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
 \right].
 \tag{6}
\]
Conditional on the pilot, \(\widehat B_s\) is fixed.  Assumption ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence makes this selected basis independent of both the main assignment and the main potential-outcome array.  The uniform finite ex ante envelope in lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence therefore applies at \(B=\widehat B_s\).  Assumption ass:frechet-covariance-domain and thm:fixed-basis-endpoints give
\[
 \sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_B(c_H)=w(B).
 \tag{7}
\]
The elementary inequality \(|\sup_Hf_H-\sup_Hg_H|\leq\sup_H|f_H-g_H|\), followed by integration over the pilot, now yields the finite feasible-estimator bridge
\[
 |V_s-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)|\leq R_n,
 \qquad s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}.
 \tag{8}
\]

The exact sensitivity condition says \(nC_nM_n\to0\) in \(L^1(P)\), hence
\[
 nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{9}
\]
Combining (4) and (9) proves
\[
 \mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
 =v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+o(n^{-1}).
 \tag{10}
\]
Equations (5), (8), and (10) give the displayed feasible Horvitz--Thompson coefficient bridge in the theorem for both selectors.

It remains to quantify the zero-covariance selector.  Choose \(B_n^0\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}\), so \(g_{B_n^0}(0)=g_n^\star\).  Empirical optimality of \(\widehat B_0\) and (1) imply
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)
 &\leq
 |g_{B_n^0}(0)-\widehat g_{B_n^0}(0)|
 +|\widehat g_{\widehat B_0}(0)-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)|\\
 &\leq2e_n.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{11}
\]
Under the displayed deterministic linear error bound in the theorem,
\[
 [q_n-w(\widehat B_0)]_+
 \leq L_n\{g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\}
 \leq2L_ne_n.
 \tag{12}
\]
Because \(x\geq q-[q-x]_+\), equations (2) and (12) give the finite-\(n\) pilot-regret inequality
\[
 q_n-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
 \leq4L_nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
 \tag{13}
\]
If \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\), equation (9) makes the right side of (13) \(o(n^{-1})\), which is precisely the theorem's zero-surrogate conclusion.  Without the deterministic linear error bound, (11) controls nominal-gain regret but supplies no \(n^{-1}\)-scale control of the worst-loss deficit, so outer argmax consistency alone cannot replace (12).  This completes the proof. \(\square\)
