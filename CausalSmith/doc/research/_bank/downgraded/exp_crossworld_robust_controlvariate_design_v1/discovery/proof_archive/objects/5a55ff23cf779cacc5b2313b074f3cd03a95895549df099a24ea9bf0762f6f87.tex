\textbf{Proof.}
At \(\xi_0\), the triangle published-domain separation theorem gives
\[
\Delta(\xi_0)=\delta=\frac{4912}{39525}>0
\tag{1}
\]
and gives optimizer-wide normalized eigenvalue slack
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\cup\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)}
\left\{\lambda_{\min}(3^{-1}S_B)-\kappa\right\}
=\eta=\frac5{216}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
It also gives the strict singular-stratum gaps
\[
\frac{31}{80}-\frac{87}{400}=\frac{17}{100}>0,
\qquad
\frac{39}{80}-\frac{2613}{8432}=\frac{468}{2635}>0.
\tag{3}
\]

We first show that (3) persists.  Because the assignment space is finite, total-variation convergence of \(\nu\) implies entrywise convergence of \(\Pi\) and \(\Omega\).  Exposure positivity at \(\xi_0\) makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) continuous in a neighborhood.  Wasserstein convergence of the bounded marginals implies convergence of their first two moments, and restricted-marginal extrema continuity gives convergence of \(c_L,c_U\).  Hence both endpoint moment matrices converge.  The matrix \(\Omega(\xi_0)\) is positive definite by the explicit isotypic calculation in the singular-strata certificate, so, after shrinking the neighborhood, \(\Omega(\xi)\) is uniformly positive definite.

On this neighborhood the published carrier is the fixed finite disjoint union of its compact rank-zero point, its two compact rank-one projective factors, and its compact rank-two product-projective factor.  The rank-stratified projector formula makes \(g_B^+(c)\) and \(w^+(B)\) jointly continuous in \((\xi,B)\) on each fixed-rank factor: uniform positive definiteness bounds the scalar inverse on rank one and the two-by-two inverse on rank two.  The optimum of either continuous criterion on each compact factor is therefore continuous in \(\xi\).  For completeness, this follows directly by sequences: choose factorwise optimizers along any convergent sequence \(\xi_j\to\xi\); compactness gives a convergent subsequence, joint continuity gives one optimal-value inequality, and evaluation at a fixed optimizer for \(\xi\) gives the reverse inequality.

The strict inequalities in (3) consequently persist on a relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U_1\) of \(\xi_0\).  On \(\mathcal U_1\), every published maximizer of \(g^+(0)\) and every published minimizer of \(w^+\) lies in the rank-two stratum.

We next retain the \(\kappa\)-slack.  Suppose, contrary to the claim, that there were a sequence \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) and published rank-two optimizers \(B_j^+\), for either criterion, whose ordinary preimages \(B_j=\iota_{\xi_j}^{-1}(B_j^+)\) satisfied
\[
\lambda_{\min}\!\left(3^{-1}S_{B_j}(\xi_j)\right)<\kappa+\frac\eta2.
\tag{4}
\]
Compactness of the normalized rank-two carrier supplies a convergent subsequence \(B_j\to B\).  Joint continuity and comparison with any fixed rank-two optimizer at \(\xi_0\) show that \(\iota_{\xi_0}(B)\) is a published optimizer at \(\xi_0\).  The exact correspondence identities in the triangle theorem therefore put \(B\) in \(\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)\) or \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\), according to the criterion.  Passing to the limit in (4) contradicts (2).  Thus, on a smaller relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U_2\subseteq\mathcal U_1\), every published optimizer is rank two and its ordinary preimage lies in \(\mathcal B_\kappa(\xi)\).

This proves the correspondence identities on \(\mathcal U_2\).  Indeed, consider maximization of \(g(0)\).  A published maximizer has a preimage feasible for the \(\kappa\)-problem, so the published and \(\kappa\)-restricted maximum values coincide.  Every \(\kappa\)-optimizer therefore also attains the published maximum, and the carrier identity preserves its gain.  Hence
\[
\mathcal A_0^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi\!\left(\mathcal A_0(\xi)\right).
\tag{5}
\]
The identical argument with inequalities reversed gives
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+(\xi)
=\iota_\xi\!\left(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\right).
\tag{6}
\]

It remains to retain the quantitative loss separation.  The hypotheses of the dominance-gap lower-semicontinuity lemma hold at \(\xi_0\) by (2).  If no relative-open neighborhood had \(\Delta(\xi)>\delta/2\), metrizability of the relative total-variation--Wasserstein topology would supply \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) with \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le\delta/2\).  Lower semicontinuity would then give
\[
\delta=\Delta(\xi_0)
\le\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)
\le\frac\delta2,
\]
a contradiction.  Intersect the resulting neighborhood with \(\mathcal U_2\) and call the intersection \(\mathcal U\).

For \(\xi\in\mathcal U\), \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\), and \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)\), the definition of \(\Delta\) gives
\[
\begin{split}
w_\xi(B_0)-w_\xi(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
&\ge
\inf_{\widetilde B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)}w_\xi(\widetilde B_0)
-\sup_{\widetilde B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)}
w_\xi(\widetilde B_{\mathrm{mm}})\\
&=\Delta(\xi)>\frac\delta2.
\end{split}
\tag{7}
\]
The carrier equalities (5)--(6) and preservation of \(w\) under \(\iota_\xi\) turn (7) into the identical inequality for every pair of published optimizers. \(\square\)
