Let \(E_1,E_0\) be compact metric spaces.  Under weak convergence of an ordered pair of marginal probability measures, the fixed-marginal coupling correspondence is continuous in the weak topology.  Consequently, for every continuous function \(\varphi:E_1\times E_0\to\mathbb R\), the minimum and maximum of \(H\mapsto\int\varphi\,dH\) over the coupling set vary continuously with the ordered marginal pair, and the associated optimizer correspondences are upper hemicontinuous.
