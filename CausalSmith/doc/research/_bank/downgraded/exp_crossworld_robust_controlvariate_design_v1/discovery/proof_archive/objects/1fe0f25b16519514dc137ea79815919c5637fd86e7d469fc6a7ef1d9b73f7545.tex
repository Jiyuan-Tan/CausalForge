Compute the empirical means, variances, and two empirical quantile-coupling covariances from \(\widehat F_1,\widehat F_0\), and substitute the resulting endpoint matrices into the published-class projector criterion \(w^+\).  The primary plug-in decision at tolerance \(\varepsilon>0\) is any published-class basis whose empirical worst-endpoint pseudoinverse loss is within \(\varepsilon\) of its infimum over \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).  On the compact fixed-threshold rank-two restriction, retain
\[
\widehat w(B)=\max\{\widehat\ell_B(\widehat c_L),\widehat\ell_B(\widehat c_U)\},
\quad
\widehat v_{\mathrm{mm}}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}\widehat w(B),
\quad
\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm}}=\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}\widehat w(B),
\]
and retain \(\widehat r(B)\), \(\widehat v\), and \(\widehat{\mathcal A}_R\) only as secondary diagnostics.  Exact full-class argmin or cross-rank outer-set consistency is not part of this construction.
