\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\mathbf1=\mathbf1_3\).  Simultaneous permutation decomposition into \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1\}\) and \(\mathbf1^{\perp}\) reduces \(729\Omega\) to
\[
M_{\parallel}=\begin{bmatrix}60&96\\96&186\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
M_{\perp}=\begin{bmatrix}108&-108\\-108&162\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Their leading diagonal entries are positive and
\[
\det(M_{\parallel})=1944>0,
\qquad
\det(M_{\perp})=5832>0.
\]
Thus \(\Omega\succ0\).  Consequently rank zero means both arm columns vanish, and rank one means exactly one arm column is nonzero.

On the focal-only stratum, normalize \(\lVert x\rVert_2^2=3\) and set
\[
u=(\mathbf1^{\top}x)^2.
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(0\le u\le9\).  Substitution of the displayed matrices into the scalar Rayleigh quotient in lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives
\[
g_x^+(c_L)=\frac{1195u+6561}{1200(81-4u)},
\qquad
g_x^+(c_U)=\frac{243(49-5u)}{400(81-4u)}.                           \tag{1}
\]
Because \(c_L=-1/4\), \(c_U=1/4\), and the gain is affine in \(c\),
\[
g_x^+(0)=\frac{g_x^+(c_L)+g_x^+(c_U)}2
=\frac{21141-1225u}{1200(81-4u)},
\]
and direct differentiation yields
\[
\frac{d}{du}g_x^+(0)
=-\frac{4887}{400(4u-81)^2}<0.                                    \tag{2}
\]
The uncontrolled endpoint values are
\[
h(c_L)=\frac{313}{600},
\qquad
h(c_U)=\frac{171}{200}.
\]
Using (1), the upper endpoint is always the larger residual loss and
\[
w^+(x)=\ell_{x,U}
=\frac{9(1755-17u)}{400(81-4u)},
\qquad
\frac{d}{du}\ell_{x,U}
=\frac{50787}{400(4u-81)^2}>0.                                    \tag{3}
\]
Every denominator in (1)--(3) is positive on \([0,9]\).  Hence the focal-only maximum zero-covariance gain is \(87/400\), and its minimum worst loss is \(39/80\), both attained at \(u=0\).

On the comparison-only stratum, normalize \(\lVert y\rVert_2^2=3\) and set \(u=(\mathbf1^{\top}y)^2\in[0,9]\).  The analogous substitution gives
\[
g_y^+(c_L)=\frac{1240u+729}{150(4u+243)},
\qquad
g_y^+(c_U)=\frac{81(48-5u)}{50(4u+243)},                            \tag{4}
\]
and hence
\[
g_y^+(0)=\frac{25u+12393}{300(4u+243)},
\qquad
\frac{d}{du}g_y^+(0)
=-\frac{14499}{100(4u+243)^2}<0.                                  \tag{5}
\]
Again the upper endpoint is the larger residual loss, now with
\[
w^+(y)=\ell_{y,U}
=\frac{9(256u+2889)}{200(4u+243)},
\qquad
\frac{d}{du}\ell_{y,U}
=\frac{113967}{50(4u+243)^2}>0.                                   \tag{6}
\]
Thus the comparison-only maximum gain is \(17/100\), and its minimum worst loss is \(107/200\), both at \(u=0\).

At rank zero, the projector is zero, so \(g_B^+(0)=0\), and
\[
w^+(B)=\max\{h(c_L),h(c_U)\}=\frac{171}{200}.
\]
Comparing rank zero with the two complete rank-one components gives the exact singular-stratum extrema
\[
\sup_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}g_B^+(0)
=\frac{87}{400}<\frac{31}{80},
\]
\[
\inf_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}w^+(B)
=\frac{39}{80}>\frac{2613}{8432}.
\]
The compact interval \(0\le u\le9\), the positive denominators, and the derivative signs include all boundary cases, so (1)--(6) are an exact semialgebraic certificate. \(\square\)
