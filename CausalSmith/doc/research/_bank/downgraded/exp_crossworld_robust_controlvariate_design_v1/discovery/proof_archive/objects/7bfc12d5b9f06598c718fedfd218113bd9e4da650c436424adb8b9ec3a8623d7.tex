Define \(\iota:\mathcal B_Z\to\mathcal B_Z^+\) by \(\iota([B])=[B]_+\).  This map is a well-defined bijection from \(\mathcal B_Z\) onto
\[
 \{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\operatorname{rank}(B^{\top}\Omega B)=2\}.
\]
For every \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\) and every \(c\in\mathcal C(F_1,F_0)\),
\[
 g_{\iota(B)}^+(c)=g_B(c),
 \qquad \ell_{\iota(B)}^+(c)=\ell_B(c),
 \qquad w^+(\iota(B))=w(B).
\]
