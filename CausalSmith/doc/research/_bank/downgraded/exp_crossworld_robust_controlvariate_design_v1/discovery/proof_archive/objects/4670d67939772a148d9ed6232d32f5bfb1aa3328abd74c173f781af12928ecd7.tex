Fix \(G,\nu,(f_i)_{i=1}^n\) satisfying ass:exposure-positivity and ass:kappa-feasibility.  Under ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot, define the secondary fixed-\(\kappa\), rank-two empirical value and correspondence by
\[
\widehat v_{\mathrm{mm},\kappa}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}\widehat w(B),
\qquad
\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm},\kappa}=\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}\widehat w(B).
\]
As \(m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty\),
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|\to0,
\qquad
\widehat v_{\mathrm{mm},\kappa}\to v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\]
almost surely, and every subsequential limit of \(B_m\in\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm},\kappa}\) belongs to \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) almost surely.  Equivalently, \(\limsup\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm},\kappa}\subseteq\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) in the projective quotient.  This theorem concerns only the compact regular rank-two restriction, not the primary full pseudoinverse-class infimum.
