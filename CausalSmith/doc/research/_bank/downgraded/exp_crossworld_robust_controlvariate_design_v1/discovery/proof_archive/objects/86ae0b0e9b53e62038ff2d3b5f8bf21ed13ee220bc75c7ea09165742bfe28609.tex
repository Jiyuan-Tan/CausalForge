For every \(\kappa>0\), the quotient \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is compact and satisfies \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\).  Conversely, every \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\) has normalized representatives belonging to \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) for some basis-dependent \(\kappa>0\), so
\[
\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa=\mathcal B_Z,
\qquad
q_Z^{-1}\!\left(\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa\right)=\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z.
\]
Thus the threshold classes exhaust the maximal well-defined ordinary-inverse block-separable domain implicit in Zhao's Equation (8), modulo the exact independent nonzero column-rescaling fibers of \(q_Z\).  Each fixed \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is a threshold-restricted subclass, although strict inclusion is not universal, and no singular or Moore--Penrose basis is covered.
