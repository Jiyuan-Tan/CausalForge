\textbf{Proof.}
By thm:triangle-minimax-separation-certificate,
\[
 \Delta(\xi_0)=\delta=\frac{4912}{39525}>0
\]
and every optimizer in \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\cup\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)\) has regularity slack \(\eta=5/216>0\).  Thus the hypotheses of lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity hold at \(\xi_0\).

Suppose no relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) satisfied \(\Delta(\xi)>\delta/2\) throughout.  Metrizability of the relative total-variation--Wasserstein topology in def:lawful-instance-class would then provide, for every positive integer \(j\), an instance \(\xi_j\in\mathfrak X_\kappa\) at distance less than \(1/j\) from \(\xi_0\) such that \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le\delta/2\).  Hence \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\), while lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity gives
\[
 \delta=\Delta(\xi_0)
 \le\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)
 \le\frac{\delta}{2},
\]
a contradiction.  Therefore some relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\subset\mathfrak X_\kappa\) of \(\xi_0\) satisfies
\[
 \Delta(\xi)>\frac{\delta}{2}=\frac{2456}{39525}
 \qquad(\xi\in\mathcal U).
\]

Fix \(\xi\in\mathcal U\), \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\), and \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)\).  By def:dominance-gap,
\[
 \begin{split}
 w_\xi(B_0)-w_\xi(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
 &\ge
 \inf_{\widetilde B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)}w_\xi(\widetilde B_0)
 -\sup_{\widetilde B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)}
 w_\xi(\widetilde B_{\mathrm{mm}})\\
 &=\Delta(\xi)>\frac{\delta}{2}>0.
 \end{split}
\]
Hence every minimax optimizer has strictly smaller worst-copula residual variance than every zero-covariance-surrogate optimizer throughout \(\mathcal U\). \(\square\)
