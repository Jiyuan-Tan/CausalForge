The exploratory Jiangxi PICC reanalysis record \(\mathcal J_{\mathrm{PICC}}\) is the following reproducible procedure.  Start from the Cai--de Janvry--Sadoulet files \(\texttt{0422survey.dta}\) and \(\texttt{0422allinforawnet.dta}\); retain the \(4902\) released households, clean the directed nominations to the \(16997\) valid ordered edges documented by Zhao, and retain cross-village nominations.  For \(k=1,2,3\), use Zhao's target population \(\mathcal I_k\) and effects \(\tau_{10},\tau_{21},\tau_{32}\), with sizes \(881,481,168\), under the disclosed independent Bernoulli \(1/2\) reconstruction necessitated by the unreleased original strata.  Split connected components of the overlap graph without cutting components, using only graph and population information; on each training fold estimate the two exposure-specific marginal laws and their Fr\'echet endpoints.  Under the same deterministic numerical budget and initialization list, compute a robust basis minimizing \(\widehat w\) and a surrogate basis maximizing \(\widehat g_B(0)\); record incumbent values and valid numerical lower bounds, without calling these high-dimensional gaps exact certificates.  On the held-out fold deploy both bases through def:observable-coefficient-map, swap folds, and aggregate with Zhao's fold-size weights.  For each target and each rule, report the point estimate, design-randomization standard error, both endpoint residual losses, their maximum, and the optimization gap.  Because these folds are internal to the experiment rather than an independent unpaired pilot, \(\mathcal J_{\mathrm{PICC}}\) is exploratory and is not covered by thm:plugin-correspondence.
