\textbf{Proof.}
Ass:frechet-covariance-domain makes both endpoint losses well typed on the feasible covariance interval, and ass:exposure-positivity is the positivity condition used by lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction.  Set
\[
V:=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B),
\qquad
\alpha:=\inf_{[C]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(C).
\tag{1}
\]
By the corrected infimum convention in def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss, \(V=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).

For every \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification gives
\[
w^+(\iota([C]))=w(C).
\tag{2}
\]
Because \(\iota([C])\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), equations (1)--(2) imply
\[
V\le \alpha.
\tag{3}
\]

For the reverse inequality, fix an arbitrary \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\).  If \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)=2\), lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification supplies a unique \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\) with \([B]_+=\iota([C])\).  Hence, by (1)--(2),
\[
\alpha\le w(C)=w^+(B).
\tag{4}
\]
If instead \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1\), lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction and the assumed nonemptiness of \(\mathcal B_Z\) give a sequence \([C_j]\in\mathcal B_Z\) satisfying
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}w(C_j)\le w^+(B).
\tag{5}
\]
The definition of \(\alpha\) gives \(\alpha\le w(C_j)\) for every \(j\).  Therefore
\[
\alpha\le\limsup_{j\to\infty}w(C_j)\le w^+(B).
\tag{6}
\]
Thus (4) or (6) yields \(\alpha\le w^+(B)\) for every completion point.  Taking the infimum over \([B]_+\) and combining with (3) proves
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=V=\alpha
=\inf_{[C]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(C).
\tag{7}
\]
Prop:kappa-value-exhaustion now gives
\[
\alpha
=\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B),
\tag{8}
\]
which proves the value identity.

We next identify the rank-two equality set without assuming it is nonempty.  Suppose first that
\([B]_+\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\iota(\mathcal B_Z)\).  The rank-two identification lemma gives a unique \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\) such that \([B]_+=\iota([C])\).  Equations (2) and (7) yield
\[
w(C)=w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\alpha,
\tag{9}
\]
so \([C]\) attains the ordinary-domain infimum.  By prop:kappa-exhaustion, \([C]\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}\) for some \(\kappa_C>0\).  Since \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\), equations (7) and (9) give
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le \min_{A\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}}w(A)
\le w(C)
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\tag{10}
\]
Consequently \([C]\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa_C)\) and the fixed-threshold minimum equals \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).

Conversely, if \([C]\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\) at a threshold satisfying
\[
\min_{A\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(A)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+,
\tag{11}
\]
then (2) and (11) imply \(w^+(\iota([C]))=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  Hence \(\iota([C])\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\iota(\mathcal B_Z)\).  The two inclusions prove the displayed rank-two equality-set formula.  The same argument also proves both conditional attainment assertions in the statement.

Finally, lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification says that the rank-two stratum of \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) is exactly \(\iota(\mathcal B_Z)\), while lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction shows that every completion rank belongs to \(\{0,1,2\}\).  Removing the rank-two stratum from the equality set \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) therefore gives exactly
\[
\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1,\ w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\}.
\tag{12}
\]
For a singular equality point, (5) supplies only an ordinary sequence whose losses approach no worse than that point; it does not supply an ordinary point attaining \(\alpha\).  Thus neither ordinary attainment nor completion attainment has been inferred from compactness across a rank drop.  This proves every asserted equality and conditional transfer. \(\square\)
