Under Zhao's convention that the inverse of every symmetric positive-semidefinite matrix is obtained by taking spectral reciprocals on positive eigenvalues and assigning zero to zero eigenvalues, Zhao's published unquotiented domain consists of all block-separable matrices
\[
B=\begin{bmatrix}b_1&0\\0&b_0\end{bmatrix},\qquad b_1,b_0\in\mathbb R^n,
\]
including ranks zero, one, and two.  Define \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) to be the note's exact scale-invariant projective quotient of that unquotiented domain.  Two representatives are equivalent when, separately in each arm, both corresponding columns are zero or one is a nonzero scalar multiple of the other.  Each class is represented with every nonzero arm column having squared norm \(n\), while a zero arm remains zero.  Thus \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) is the note's projective quotient of Zhao's published all-block-separable pseudoinverse domain, not a projective class named by Zhao.  Its rank-two positive-definite stratum is the image of \(\mathcal B_Z\) under \(\iota\).
