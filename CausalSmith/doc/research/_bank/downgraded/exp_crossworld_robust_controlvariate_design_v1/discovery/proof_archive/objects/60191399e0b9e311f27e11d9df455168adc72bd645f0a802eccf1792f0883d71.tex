\textbf{Proof.}
Fix the design.  By \(\texttt{ass:exposure-positivity}\), \(\Pi^{-1}\) is well defined.  For each \(e\in\{L,U\}\), the empirical endpoint covariance is attained by the comonotone or antimonotone quantile coupling \(\widehat H_e\) of the two empirical marginals.  If
\[
\mathcal Y=(Y_1(1),\ldots,Y_n(1),-Y_1(0),\ldots,-Y_n(0))^{\top}
\]
under \(\widehat H_e^{\otimes n}\), then the endpoint construction in \(\texttt{def:pilot-plugin}\) gives
\[
\widehat Q_e=\mathbb E_{\widehat H_e^{\otimes n}}(\mathcal Y\mathcal Y^{\top})\succeq0.
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\widehat A_e
=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}\widehat Q_e\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}
\succeq0.
\tag{2}
\]
For every carrier class, cyclicity of trace and the projector formula in \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\) write the empirical endpoint residual as
\[
\widehat\ell_{B,e}^+
=\widehat h_e-n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_B\widehat A_e),
\qquad e\in\{L,U\},
\tag{3}
\]
where \(\widehat h_e=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Q_e)\) is independent of \(B\).

We first show that the sequence \((C_k)_{k\ge1}\) is nonempty and dense in objective value on the maximal-rank stratum.  If \(r_\Omega=2\), choose a rank-two representative.  Positive definiteness of \(B^{\top}\Omega B\) is an open condition, rational directions are dense in every signed-pivot chart, and normalization is continuous away from zero.  Thus rational-support rank-two classes exist, and every rank-two class is a limit of a subsequence of \((C_k)\).  On this fixed-rank stratum the projector, and hence (3), is continuous.

If \(r_\Omega=1\), let \([B]_+\) have rank one.  At least one arm column, say the arm indexed by \(a\), has nonzero image
\[
z_a=\Omega^{1/2}B_ae_a\ne0.
\]
Choose nonzero rational vectors \(q_j\in\mathbb Q^n\) converging to that arm direction.  Continuity makes the corresponding images nonzero for all sufficiently large \(j\).  Form a block class with the normalized rational vector in arm \(a\) and the other arm equal to zero.  Its rank is one, because \(r_\Omega=1\) rules out rank two.  Its one-dimensional projector converges to the projector onto
\(\operatorname{span}(z_a)=\operatorname{range}(\Omega^{1/2}B)\).  These rational-support classes occur in \((C_k)\), so their values in (3) converge to the value at \(B\).  If \(r_\Omega=0\), every attainable projector is zero, and the repeated zero class prescribed by \(\texttt{def:pilot-plugin}\) is nonempty and has every carrier value.

It remains to compare lower-rank classes with the maximal-rank enumeration.  Fix \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\).  The approximation clause of \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\) supplies maximal-rank classes \([B_j]_+\) and a projector \(P_\infty\) such that
\[
P_{B_j}\longrightarrow P_\infty,
\qquad P_\infty-P_B\succeq0.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) and trace monotonicity imply, endpoint by endpoint and then after taking the maximum,
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\widehat w_m^+(B_j)
\le \widehat w_m^+(B).
\tag{5}
\]
For \(r_\Omega=2\), approximate each fixed \(B_j\) by an enumerated rational-support rank-two class; for \(r_\Omega=1\), use the rational one-arm approximation just proved; and for \(r_\Omega=0\), use the zero class.  Hence, for every \(\delta>0\), some \(C_k\) satisfies
\[
\widehat w_m^+(C_k)\le\widehat w_m^+(B)+\delta.
\tag{6}
\]
Taking the infimum over \(k\), then over \([B]_+\), and finally letting \(\delta\downarrow0\), gives
\[
\inf_{k\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
\le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B).
\tag{7}
\]
The reverse inequality holds because every \(C_k\) is a carrier class.  This proves equality of the infima without continuity across rank changes or attainment of the full-carrier infimum.

For measurability, fix \(k\).  Empirical means and variances are measurable functions of the pilot observations, and the empirical quantile-coupling cross moments are finite functions of order statistics.  Because \(C_k\), its rank, and its pseudoinverse are fixed by the design,
\[
X_k:=\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
\]
is pilot-measurable.  Therefore \(\rho_m=\inf_{j\ge1}X_j\) is measurable as a countable infimum.  Strict positivity and finiteness of \(\varepsilon_m\) make the qualifying set nonempty by the definition of an infimum.  Moreover,
\[
\{\widehat k_m=k\}
=\{X_k\le\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\}
\cap\bigcap_{j<k}\{X_j>\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\},
\tag{8}
\]
so \(\widehat k_m\) is measurable.  The countable-valued selector \(C_{\widehat k_m}\) is consequently measurable, has rank \(r_\Omega\), and satisfies the stated positive-tolerance inequality.  Since \(r_\Omega=2\) exactly when a rank-two class is achievable, the rank assertion follows.

This last construction is only a classical set-theoretic measurable selector: neither the countable infimum nor the first qualifying index comes with a finite stopping test for arbitrary real pilot inputs.  It uses no full-carrier minimizer or zero-tolerance selector and makes no agreement claim with the distinct exact-input cylindrical-algebraic selector. \(\square\)
