\textbf{Proof.}
Fix the design.  Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well-defined.  The empirical lower and upper endpoint matrices are positive semidefinite.  Indeed, each endpoint is obtained from an actual coupling of the two empirical marginals, and if
\[
 \mathcal Y=(Y_1(1),\ldots,Y_n(1),-Y_1(0),\ldots,-Y_n(0))^{\top},
\]
then the construction in def:pilot-plugin gives
\[
 \widehat Q_e=\mathbb E_{\widehat H_e^{\otimes n}}
 (\mathcal Y\mathcal Y^{\top})\succeq0,
 \qquad e\in\{L,U\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus
\[
 \widehat A_e=
 \Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}\widehat Q_e\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}
 \succeq0.
\tag{2}
\]

We first prove density of the enumerated rank-two values in the empirical full-carrier objective.  Let \([B^{\circ}]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\).  If \(\operatorname{rank}(S_{B^{\circ}})=2\), the ordinary inverse is continuous on a neighborhood of \(B^{\circ}\).  Rational directions are dense in every signed pivot chart, normalization is continuous away from zero, and positive definiteness is open.  Consequently, there is a subsequence of \((C_k)\) converging to \([B^{\circ}]_+\), and
\[
 \widehat w_m^+(C_k)\longrightarrow
 \widehat w_m^+(B^{\circ})
\tag{3}
\]
along that subsequence.

Suppose instead that \(\operatorname{rank}(S_{B^{\circ}})\le1\).  The condition \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\) supplies a block-separable representative \(C^{\circ}\) with \(S_{C^{\circ}}\succ0\).  Apply the perturbation used in lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction to
\[
 B_t^{\circ}=B^{\circ}+tC^{\circ}.
\]
Outside the finitely many roots of the nonzero polynomial
\[
 t\longmapsto
 \det\{(\Omega^{1/2}B_t^{\circ})^{\top}
          (\Omega^{1/2}B_t^{\circ})\},
\]
the perturbed class has rank two.  Choose \(t_j\downarrow0\) avoiding those roots and normalize its nonzero arm columns.  After passing to a subsequence, the associated rank-two projectors converge to a projector \(P_{\infty}\) whose range contains
\(\operatorname{range}(\Omega^{1/2}B^{\circ})\).  Hence
\[
 P_{\infty}-P_{B^{\circ}}\succeq0.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2), (4), and trace monotonicity give, for both endpoints,
\[
 \lim_j n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_{B_{t_j}^{\circ}}\widehat A_e)
 =n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_{\infty}\widehat A_e)
 \ge n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_{B^{\circ}}\widehat A_e).
\tag{5}
\]
The uncontrolled empirical endpoint term is independent of the basis.  Subtracting (5) from that term and taking the maximum over \(e\in\{L,U\}\) therefore yields
\[
 \limsup_{j\to\infty}
 \widehat w_m^+(B_{t_j}^{\circ})
 \le \widehat w_m^+(B^{\circ}).
\tag{6}
\]
Each class in (6) has rank two, so the chart-density and continuity argument leading to (3) shows that, for every \(\delta>0\), some enumerated \(C_k\) satisfies
\[
 \widehat w_m^+(C_k)
 \le \widehat w_m^+(B^{\circ})+\delta.
\tag{7}
\]
Equation (7) also follows from (3) in the rank-two case.  Taking first the infimum over \(k\), then the infimum over \([B^{\circ}]_+\), and finally letting \(\delta\downarrow0\) gives
\[
 \inf_{k\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
 \le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B).
\tag{8}
\]
The reverse inequality is immediate because every \(C_k\) lies in \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).  This proves the asserted equality of infima without continuity across rank changes and without full-carrier attainment.

It remains to prove measurability.  For fixed \(k\), empirical means and variances are measurable functions of the pilot observations, while empirical quantile-coupling cross moments are finite functions of order statistics and hence are measurable.  Matrix evaluation at the fixed positive-definite class \(C_k\) is continuous.  Therefore
\[
 X_k:=\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
\]
is pilot-measurable.  The countable infimum
\[
 \rho_m:=\inf_{j\ge1}X_j
\]
is measurable.  Strict positivity of the finite random variable \(\varepsilon_m\) and the defining property of an infimum imply that the qualifying set
\(\{k:X_k\le\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\}\) is nonempty.  For every \(k\ge1\),
\[
 \{\widehat k_m=k\}
 =\{X_k\le\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\}
  \cap\bigcap_{j<k}\{X_j>\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\},
\tag{9}
\]
so \(\widehat k_m\) is measurable.  The countable-valued map
\(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+=C_{\widehat k_m}\) is consequently measurable, and its defining inequality combined with (8) is the displayed full-carrier positive-tolerance guarantee. \(\square\)
