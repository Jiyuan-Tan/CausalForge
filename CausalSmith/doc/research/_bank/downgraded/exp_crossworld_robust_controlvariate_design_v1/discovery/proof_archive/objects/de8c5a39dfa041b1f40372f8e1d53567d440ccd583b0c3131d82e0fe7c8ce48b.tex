\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\xi_j=(\nu_j,F_{1j},F_{0j})\to\xi_0\).  Use normalized representatives in the fixed compact product of spheres modulo signs.  Because the assignment space is finite, total-variation convergence of \(\nu_j\) implies entrywise convergence \(\Pi_j\to\Pi_0\) and \(\Omega_j\to\Omega_0\).  Exposure positivity at \(\xi_0\) implies that all relevant diagonal entries stay uniformly away from zero eventually, so \(\Pi_j^{-1}\to\Pi_0^{-1}\).

Wasserstein convergence on \([0,1]\) implies weak convergence and convergence of first and second moments.  Lem:restricted-marginal-extrema-continuity, applied to \(\varphi(y_1,y_0)=y_1y_0\), gives convergence of the minimum and maximum cross moments over the coupling sets.  Subtracting the convergent products of marginal means yields
\[
 c_{Lj}\to c_{L0},\qquad c_{Uj}\to c_{U0}.
\]
Def:moment-matrix then gives \(Q_j(c_{ej})\to Q_0(c_{e0})\) for \(e\in\{L,U\}\).

For a normalized basis \(B\), put
\[
 \rho(\xi,B)=\lambda_{\min}\{n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega(\xi)B\}.
\]
This function is jointly continuous.  On every set \(\rho\ge\kappa\), exposure positivity and the inverse bound \(\lVert S_B^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\kappa)^{-1}\) make \(g_{B,\xi}(c_e)\), \(\ell_{B,\xi}(c_e)\), \(w_\xi(B)\), and \(g_{B,\xi}(0)\) jointly continuous.

Choose \(B^\star_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\).  The assumed slack gives \(\rho(\xi_0,B^\star_{\mathrm{mm}})\ge\kappa+\eta\); hence this fixed representative is feasible for all large \(j\).  Therefore
\[
 \limsup_j v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)
 \le\lim_j w_{\xi_j}(B^\star_{\mathrm{mm}})
 =v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0). \tag{1}
\]

For each \(j\), choose \(B_{0j}\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)\) minimizing \(w_{\xi_j}\) over that optimizer set.  Compactness supplies a subsequence, still indexed by \(j\), with \(B_{0j}\to\bar B_0\).  Closedness of \(\rho\ge\kappa\) makes \(\bar B_0\) feasible at \(\xi_0\).  Choose any \(C_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)\).  Its slack makes it feasible for all large \(j\), and empirical optimality gives
\[
 g_{B_{0j},\xi_j}(0)\ge g_{C_0,\xi_j}(0).
\]
Passing to the limit shows that \(\bar B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)\).  Joint continuity then yields
\[
 \liminf_j\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)}w_{\xi_j}(B)
 =w_{\xi_0}(\bar B_0)
 \ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)}w_{\xi_0}(B). \tag{2}
\]
The subsequence was chosen to realize the lower limit, so (2) holds for the original sequence.  Finally, def:dominance-gap and (1)--(2) imply
\[
 \liminf_j\Delta(\xi_j)
 \ge \inf_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)}w_{\xi_0}(B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)
 =\Delta(\xi_0).
\]
All inversions, moving-feasible-set comparisons, and boundary passages have thus been justified by positivity, compactness, and the stated optimizer-wide slack. \(\square\)
