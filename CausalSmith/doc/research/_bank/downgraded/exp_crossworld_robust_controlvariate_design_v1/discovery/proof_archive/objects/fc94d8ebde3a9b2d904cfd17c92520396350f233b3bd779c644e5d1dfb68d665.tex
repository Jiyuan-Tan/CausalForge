Under ass:zhao-dependency-neighborhood-rate, the exact directed-neighborhood size, covariance-column support, and centered moment moduli in def:rank-adaptive-stability-strata satisfy
\[
s_n\le d_n\le\overline d n^\alpha,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{3,n}\le8(2n)d_n^2,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{4,n}\le64(2n)^2d_n^2.
\]
The bounds use only Zhao's coordinate-versus-complement independence: a centered product vanishes when a once-occurring coordinate has every other distinct coordinate outside its own directed neighborhood.  They do not use separated-set dependency-graph independence or an inverse-neighborhood bound.
