Fix \(G,\nu,(f_i)_{i=1}^n\) satisfying ass:exposure-positivity.  Under ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot, define \(\widehat w_m^+\) by substituting the two empirical endpoint moment matrices into the projector formula for \(w^+\), and put
\[
d_m^+=\sup_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}|\widehat w_m^+(B)-w^+(B)|.
\]
As \(m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty\), \(d_m^+\to0\) almost surely and
\[
\left|\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\right|
\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
For every deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\ge0\), every empirical \(\varepsilon_m\)-minimizer \(\widehat B_m\) satisfies
\[
w^+(\widehat B_m)
\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\]
Consequently, if \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely, the published-class population regret converges to zero almost surely.  No cross-rank outer-argmin or point convergence is asserted without attainment and rank-separation conditions.
