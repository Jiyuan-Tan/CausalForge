\textbf{Proof.}
Ass:kappa-feasibility makes \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) nonempty, and prop:kappa-exhaustion makes it compact.  Ass:exposure-positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  For every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\),
\[
S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2,
\qquad
\lVert S_B^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\]
Inversion is therefore continuous on the entire feasible class.  The finite-dimensional formulas in def:gain-loss imply continuity of \(B\mapsto w(B)\) and \(B\mapsto g_B(0)\).  Hence the minimum defining \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and the maximum defining \(\mathcal A_0\) are attained, so both correspondences are nonempty and compact.

For arbitrary \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\), the defining minimization gives
\[
w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_0).
\]
The left-hand side is constant over \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Taking the supremum over \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\), followed by the infimum over \(B_0\), proves
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0}w(B_0).
\]
Strictness cannot follow from minimization alone.  Indeed, if \(F_1=F_0=\delta_0\), then \(c_L=c_U=0\) and \(Q(0)=0\); hence \(g_B(0)=w(B)=0\) for every feasible \(B\), so equality holds whenever \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is nonempty.  A strict conclusion therefore needs a separate lawful instance certificate. \(\square\)
