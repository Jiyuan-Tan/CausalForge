\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(\mathcal K_n=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\).  Assumption ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility makes \(\mathcal K_n\) nonempty.  Its normalized representatives form a closed subset of the product of the two radius-\(\sqrt n\) spheres, and quotienting the two residual sign actions preserves compactness.  The uniform bound
\[
B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2
\tag{1}
\]
makes ordinary inversion continuous on all of \(\mathcal K_n\).  Hence the population and empirical criteria are continuous in \(B\), and the empirical criteria are Borel measurable in the finite pilot sample.  Their optimizer sets are therefore nonempty and compact.  The fixed signed-chart lexicographic rule is measurable: in the finite signed-chart representation, first minimize the criterion over the compact feasible set, then successively minimize each representative coordinate over the compact set retained at the preceding step.  At every step the attained minimum is measurable because the infimum of a function that is measurable in the pilot and continuous in the basis equals the infimum over a fixed countable dense subset.  The coordinate restrictions have measurable graphs, so induction through the finitely many coordinates gives a measurable singleton.  This proves the existence and measurability required by def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator.  Assumption ass:known-exposure gives the deployed observed-outcome stack the exposure-potential-outcome interpretation used by the oracle bridge.

Let \(M_n,d_n,e_n\) be the quantities displayed in the theorem.  Lemma lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate gives, pathwise under the pilot law,
\[
d_n\le C_nM_n,
\qquad
e_n\le2C_nM_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Choose \(B_n^\star\in\mathcal K_n\) with \(w(B_n^\star)=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}\), which is possible by compactness and continuity.  Empirical optimality and (2) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
&\le \widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d_n\\
&\le \widehat w(B_n^\star)+d_n\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+2d_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
The definition of the population minimum gives the reverse inequality.  Taking pilot expectations in (3) and using (2) proves
\[
0\le
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})-v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}
\le2C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{4}
\]

Fix \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\).  Conditional on the pilot, \(\widehat B_s\) is fixed and lies in \(\mathcal K_n\).  Assumption ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence therefore permits the uniform conclusion of lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence to be applied at this pilot-measurable basis.  Assumption ass:frechet-covariance-domain and thm:fixed-basis-endpoints give, for every fixed \(B\in\mathcal K_n\),
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_B(c_H)=w(B).
\tag{5}
\]
For any two bounded real families \((u_H)_H\) and \((v_H)_H\),
\[
\left|\sup_Hu_H-\sup_Hv_H\right|
\le\sup_H|u_H-v_H|.
\tag{6}
\]
Apply (6) to the uniform variance bridge of lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, use (5), and then integrate over the pilot.  This proves the finite bound
\[
\left|V_s-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)\right|\le R_n,
\qquad s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Under the stated triangular-sequence premise,
\[
nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0,
\tag{8}
\]
equation (4) gives
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+o(n^{-1}).
\tag{9}
\]
Lemma lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence gives \(nR_n\to0\); consequently (7) gives
\[
V_s=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1})
\qquad(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}).
\tag{10}
\]

For the zero-covariance selector, choose \(B_n^0\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}\), again possible by compactness and continuity.  Empirical optimality and (2) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)
&=g_{B_n^0}(0)-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\\
&\le
|g_{B_n^0}(0)-\widehat g_{B_n^0}(0)|
+|\widehat g_{\widehat B_0}(0)-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)|\\
&\le2e_n
\le4C_nM_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
If the displayed deterministic linear error bound holds, then (11) gives
\[
[q_n-w(\widehat B_0)]_+
\le L_n\{g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\}
\le4L_nC_nM_n.
\tag{12}
\]
Since \(x\ge q-[q-x]_+\) for all real \(x,q\), taking pilot expectations in (12) proves
\[
q_n-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\le4L_nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{13}
\]
If \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\), (8) makes the right-hand side of (13) \(o(n^{-1})\).  Because \(q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B)\), this is the asserted zero-surrogate conclusion.  No primitive condition used above establishes a uniformly bounded sequence \((L_n)_n\); equations (12)--(13) are explicitly conditional on that deterministic geometry.  Without it, (11) controls nominal-gain regret only and supplies no first-order worst-loss ordering.  Every selected basis remains in the regular rank-two class, where Zhao's spectral pseudoinverse is the ordinary inverse. \(\square\)
