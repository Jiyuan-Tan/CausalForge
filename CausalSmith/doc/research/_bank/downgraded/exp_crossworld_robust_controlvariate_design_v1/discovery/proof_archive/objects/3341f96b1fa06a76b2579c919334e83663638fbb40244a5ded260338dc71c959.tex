At \(\xi_0\), the singular-stratum margins are

\qquad

Total-variation and Wasserstein convergence make \(\Pi\), \(\Omega\), and the two endpoint matrices continuous.  Since \(\Omega(\xi_0)\succ0\), it remains uniformly positive definite nearby.  On each normalized support component, the pseudoinverse criterion is either a scalar quotient or a uniformly nonsingular two-by-two inverse, so the component optima vary continuously.  The strict margins (1) therefore persist and exclude singular optimizer ties on a relative-open neighborhood.

Compactness and a contradiction argument using the triangle slack imply that every nearby rank-two optimizer has \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_B)\ge\kappa+\eta/2\).  Rank-two identification then gives
\[
\mathcal A_0^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_0(\xi)),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)).
\tag{2}
\]
Finally, lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity and \(\Delta(\xi_0)=\delta\) give, after shrinking the neighborhood, \(\Delta(\xi)>\delta/2\).  Definition of \(\Delta\), (2), and preservation of loss under \(\iota\) prove both ordinary and completion inequalities with the stated margin. \(\square\)
