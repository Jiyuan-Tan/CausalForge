Assume ass:known-exposure and ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility and the conditions of thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer, including its exact hypothesis \(nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\to0\) and its deterministic linear error bound for the zero-surrogate argmax.  Suppose the attained ordinary-domain optimizer sets satisfy
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}\cup\mathcal A_{0,Z}
\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}.
\]
With \(M_n,C_n,L_n,R_n\) as in that theorem and lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, define, for \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\),
\[
V_s=
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
\right].
\]
Then the finite-\(n\) end-to-end lower bound is
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge
\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n-2R_n.
\]
In particular, if explicit finite constants \(K_M,K_C,K_R\), independent of \(n\), certify
\[
\mathbb E_PM_n\le K_M\frac{n}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}},
\qquad
C_n\le K_Cn^{\beta-1},
\qquad
R_n\le K_Rn^{4\alpha+4\beta-2},
\]
then the directly checkable condition
\[
\Delta_Z>
(4L_n+2)K_CK_M
\frac{n^{\beta}}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}
+2K_Rn^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}
\]
implies \(V_0>V_{\mathrm{mm}}\) at that finite \(n\).  If, in addition,
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\Delta_Z>0,
\]
then
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n(V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}})>0.
\]
Thus a positive first-order ordinary-domain intercept--slope gap transfers to the feasible independent-pilot estimator, with an explicit finite-\(n\) diagnostic and the sharpened pilot term.  The conclusion concerns ex ante expected conditional design variance; it is neither a superpopulation sampling variance nor a Wald limit law.  Neither the optimizer-containment condition nor a primitive theorem yielding uniformly bounded \(L_n\) is proved here; both are separate checkable hypotheses.  The proposition concerns the positive-definite rank-two restriction only.
