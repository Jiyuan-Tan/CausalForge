\textbf{Proof.}
Fix the design.  Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  For each \(e\in\{L,U\}\), the empirical endpoint covariance is attained by the comonotone or antimonotone quantile coupling \(\widehat H_e\) of the two empirical marginals.  If
\[
\mathcal Y=(Y_1(1),\ldots,Y_n(1),-Y_1(0),\ldots,-Y_n(0))^{\top}
\]
under \(\widehat H_e^{\otimes n}\), the endpoint construction gives
\[
\widehat Q_e=\mathbb E_{\widehat H_e^{\otimes n}}(\mathcal Y\mathcal Y^{\top})\succeq0.
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\widehat A_e
=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}\widehat Q_e\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}
\succeq0.
\tag{2}
\]
For every carrier class, the empirical endpoint residual can therefore be written as
\[
\widehat\ell_{B,e}^+
=\widehat h_e-n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_B\widehat A_e),
\qquad e\in\{L,U\},
\tag{3}
\]
where \(\widehat h_e=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Q_e)\) is independent of \(B\).

We first show that \((C_k)\) is nonempty and dense in objective values on the maximal-rank stratum.  If \(r_\Omega=2\), positive definiteness of \(B^{\top}\Omega B\) is open, rational directions are dense in each signed pivot chart, and normalization is continuous away from zero.  Hence rational-support rank-two classes exist and every rank-two class is a limit of a subsequence of \((C_k)\).  The projector in (3) is continuous on this fixed-rank stratum.

If \(r_\Omega=1\), take a rank-one class.  At least one arm column has nonzero image under \(\Omega^{1/2}\), and that image spans the class's one-dimensional projector range.  Approximate that arm direction by nonzero rational vectors, normalize them, and set the other arm to zero.  Their images remain nonzero eventually, while the definition of \(r_\Omega=1\) rules out rank two.  The resulting one-dimensional projectors converge to the original range projector, and these classes occur in \((C_k)\).  If \(r_\Omega=0\), every attainable projector is zero and the repeated zero class prescribed in the definition is nonempty and has every carrier value.

Now fix \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\).  By \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\), there are maximal-rank classes \([B_j]_+\) and a projector \(P_\infty\) such that
\[
P_{B_j}\longrightarrow P_\infty,
\qquad P_\infty-P_B\succeq0.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) and trace monotonicity imply
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\widehat w_m^+(B_j)
\le \widehat w_m^+(B).
\tag{5}
\]
Approximate each fixed \(B_j\) by the enumerated rational-support classes just constructed.  Thus, for every \(\delta'>0\), some \(C_k\) satisfies
\[
\widehat w_m^+(C_k)\le\widehat w_m^+(B)+\delta'.
\tag{6}
\]
Taking infima and letting \(\delta'\downarrow0\) proves
\[
\inf_{k\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B).
\tag{7}
\]

For measurability, put \(X_k=\widehat w_m^+(C_k)\).  Empirical moments and empirical quantile-coupling cross moments are measurable functions of the pilot order statistics, while each \(C_k\) and its pseudoinverse are fixed by the design.  Hence every \(X_k\) is pilot-measurable, as is \(\rho_m=\inf_{j\ge1}X_j\).  Strict positivity of \(\varepsilon_m\) makes the qualifying set nonempty by the defining approximation property of an infimum.  Moreover,
\[
\{\widehat k_m=k\}
=\{X_k\le\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\}
\cap\bigcap_{j<k}\{X_j>\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\},
\tag{8}
\]
so \(\widehat k_m\) is measurable.  The selected class therefore has rank \(r_\Omega\) and satisfies the asserted positive-tolerance inequality.  Since \(r_\Omega\in\{0,1,2\}\), it equals two exactly when rank two is achievable.

The measurability argument uses the completed real number \(\rho_m\) and the set-theoretic approximation property of an infimum.  It gives no finite stage at which either \(\rho_m\) or the absence of a better value later in the enumeration is certified.  Thus the rule is a classical measurable existence selector, not a finite algorithm, and nothing in the argument identifies it with the separately defined exact-input cylindrical-algebraic selector. \(\square\)
