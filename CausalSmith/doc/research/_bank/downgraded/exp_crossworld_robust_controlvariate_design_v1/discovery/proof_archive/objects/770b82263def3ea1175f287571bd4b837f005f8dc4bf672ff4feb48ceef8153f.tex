Let \(\mathcal K=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), let \(\mathcal Z\) be the normalized positive-definite rank-two carrier, put \(a(B)=g_B(0)\),
\[
a^\star=\max_{B\in\mathcal K}a(B),
\quad
A_0=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal K}a(B),
\quad
v=\min_{B\in\mathcal K}w(B),
\quad
A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal K}w(B),
\quad
q=\inf_{B\in A_0}w(B).
\]
Fix the finite signed-pivot charts and their semialgebraic quotient distance \(d_{\mathrm{pr}}\).  A primitive geometry record \(\mathscr G_{\mathrm{CAD}}\) consists of exact rational or real-algebraic CAD, KKT, and interval-Hessian certificates with the following components.

First, for some \(\zeta_0,\zeta_{\mathrm{mm}}>0\), it certifies the strict inside/outside gaps
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K}a(B)
\le a^\star-\zeta_0,
\qquad
\inf_{B\in\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K}w(B)
\ge v+\zeta_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{G1}
\]
Second, it gives a finite semialgebraic stratified chart cover of \(A_0\), exact nominal KKT multipliers, linear independence of the active constraint gradients, strictly positive multipliers for active inequalities, and a strictly negative reduced Hessian for the nominal Lagrangian.  On
\[
A_q=\{C\in A_0:w(C)=q\},
\]
it additionally gives exact weights \(\theta_L(C),\theta_U(C)\ge0\), summing to one and supported only on endpoint losses attaining \(w(C)\), such that the weighted endpoint gradient plus the certified active-constraint multipliers has the feasible normal sign.  It gives uniform algebraic bounds on the endpoint Hessians and Taylor remainders and a positive \(w\)-gap on \(A_0\setminus A_q\).  These finite primitives certify constants \(\rho,\mu>0\) and \(M<\infty\) for the Taylor conclusions
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\mu d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2,
\qquad
[q-w(B)]_+\le M d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\quad\text{when }d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\le\rho.
\tag{G2}
\]
Third, on the compact away set \(d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\ge\rho\), it certifies
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\gamma>0,
\qquad
[q-w(B)]_+\le R<\infty.
\tag{G3}
\]

Every valid \(\mathscr G_{\mathrm{CAD}}\) proves
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=A_0,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=A_{\mathrm{mm}},
\]
and derives, rather than assumes, the global error bound
\[
[q-w(B)]_+
\le L\{a^\star-a(B)\}
\quad(B\in\mathcal K),
\qquad
L=\max\{M/\mu,R/\gamma\}.
\tag{G4}
\]
Consequently, if
\[
e=\sup_{\mathcal K}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|,
\qquad
d=\sup_{\mathcal K}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|,
\]
then any empirical nominal maximizer \(\widehat B_0\) and robust minimizer \(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}}\) satisfy
\[
w(\widehat B_0)\ge q-2Le,
\qquad
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\le v+2d.
\]
If their observable-versus-oracle variance errors are bounded by \(R_0\) and \(R_{\mathrm{mm}}\), respectively, then
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge(q-v)-2Le-2d-R_0-R_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{G5}
\]
Thus containment and the finite comparison constant are consequences of exact primitive certificates, not maintained hypotheses.
