\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(N=2n\), index exposure coordinates by \(j\), and put \(Z_j=D_j-\pi_j\).  Known exposure and the signed convention in \(Y\) give
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\widehat Y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}=Y_j,
\qquad
\{\Pi^{-1}(\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}-Y)\}_j
=\frac{Y_jZ_j}{\pi_j^2}.
\tag{1}
\]
Exposure positivity is exactly the condition used by the divisions in (1).

If \(S_B=0\), its spectral pseudoinverse is zero.  Moreover \(B^{\top}\Omega B=0\) and \(\Omega\succeq0\) imply \(\Omega^{1/2}B=0\), hence \(B^{\top}\Omega=0\).  Both the observable and oracle coefficients therefore vanish.  Substitution in def:observable-coefficient-map gives exactly \(n^{-1}\mathbf1^{\top}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  This proves every rank-zero assertion, including the absence of a control-variate gain.

Fix \(r\in\{1,2\}\) and \([B]_+\in\mathcal S_{r,n}(\lambda_r,\overline a)\).  Set
\[
G_B=\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)-\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y).
\]
Equation (1) yields, using the single coefficient name required throughout,
\[
G_B=\sum_{j=1}^{N}u_{B,j}Z_j,
\qquad
u_{B,j}=S_B^+B^{\top}\Omega e_j\frac{Y_j}{\pi_j^2}.
\tag{2}
\]
The positive-eigenvalue margin gives
\[
\lVert S_B^+\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\lambda_r)^{-1}.
\tag{3}
\]
Each covariance column has at most \(s_n\) nonzero entries, every covariance entry has absolute value at most one, and the basis coordinates have absolute value at most \(\overline a\).  Since \(|Y_j|\le1\),
\[
\sup_j\lVert B^{\top}\Omega e_j\rVert_2
\le\sqrt2\,\overline a s_n,
\qquad
\sup_j\lVert u_{B,j}\rVert_2\le U_{n,r}.
\tag{4}
\]
The centered basis statistic is
\[
X_B=n^{-1}B^{\top}(D-\mathbb E_\nu D)
=\sum_{j=1}^{N}x_{B,j}Z_j,
\qquad
x_{B,j}=n^{-1}B^{\top}e_j,
\tag{5}
\]
and \(\sup_j\lVert x_{B,j}\rVert_2\le X_n\).  The fourth-moment envelope in def:rank-adaptive-stability-strata, applied to each of the two coordinates and the inequality \((z_1^2+z_2^2)^2\le2z_1^4+2z_2^4\), gives
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\lVert G_B\rVert_2^4
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}U_{n,r}^4,
\qquad
\mathbb E_\nu\lVert X_B\rVert_2^4
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}X_n^4.
\tag{6}
\]
The feasible and oracle estimators satisfy
\[
\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}
=\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}+\Delta_B,
\qquad
\Delta_B=-G_B^{\top}X_B.
\tag{7}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz and (6) imply
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\Delta_B^2
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}U_{n,r}^2X_n^2.
\tag{8}
\]

Put \(T_B=\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}-\tau\).  The Horvitz--Thompson term is unbiased by (1), while the basis statistic is centered, so \(\mathbb E_\nu T_B=0\).  Direct expansion gives
\[
T_B=\sum_{j=1}^{N}t_{B,j}Z_j,
\qquad
t_{B,j}=n^{-1}\left\{\frac{Y_j}{\pi_j}
-B_{j\cdot}\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)\right\}.
\tag{9}
\]
The projector identity from lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives
\[
\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)^{\top}S_B\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)
\le Y^{\top}\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y.
\tag{10}
\]
There are at most \(2ns_n\) nonzero entries in the covariance sum, and every term has absolute value at most \(\pi_{\min,n}^{-2}\), so
\[
Y^{\top}\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y
\le2ns_n\pi_{\min,n}^{-2}.
\tag{11}
\]
The coefficient lies in the positive spectral subspace of \(S_B\).  Combining (10)--(11) with \(\lambda_+(S_B)\ge n\lambda_r\) yields
\[
\lVert\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)\rVert_2\le\Gamma_{n,r},
\qquad
\sup_j|t_{B,j}|\le T_{n,r}.
\tag{12}
\]
Expanding \(\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(T_B,\Delta_B\mid Y)\) in the two coefficient coordinates and applying the third-moment envelope gives
\[
|\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(T_B,\Delta_B\mid Y)|
\le2\mathfrak M_{3,n}T_{n,r}U_{n,r}X_n.
\tag{13}
\]
Because
\[
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(T_B+\Delta_B)-\operatorname{Var}_\nu(T_B)
=\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\Delta_B)+2\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(T_B,\Delta_B),
\]
equations (8) and (13) prove the displayed finite bound \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\).

For the oracle identity, put \(q_B=B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y\).  Since
\[
q_B\in\operatorname{range}\{(\Omega^{1/2}B)^{\top}\}
=\operatorname{range}(S_B),
\]
one has \(S_BS_B^+q_B=q_B\).  Completing the quadratic variance expression therefore gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}\mid Y)
=n^{-2}Y^{\top}\left\{\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}
-\Pi^{-1}\Omega BS_B^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\}Y.
\tag{14}
\]
Under ass:iid-potential-pairs, \(\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}(YY^{\top})=Q(c_H)\).  Taking the trace expectation in (14) and applying def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss proves \(\ell_B^+(c_H)\).

Now let \(\widetilde B\) satisfy the selected-stratum certificate (S).  Pilot independence permits conditioning on its realized class, but is not used to infer (S).  On the event of rank zero, the difference is exactly zero.  On the event \(\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widetilde B})=r\in\{1,2\}\), condition (S) authorizes the fixed-basis calculation with the displayed \(\lambda_r\) and \(\overline a\).  Integrating the resulting conditional inequalities over the pilot proves the selected-basis display.  For the encoded CAD output, thm:encoded-cad-pilot-selector-certificate supplies exact rank and positive-eigenvalue isolation; the theorem applies only after those records and the exact coordinate check verify (S).

For the dependency rates, a centered mixed moment vanishes whenever one distinct coordinate is separated in \(\mathcal G_n\) from all remaining coordinates.  Counting ordered contributing triples and quadruples and using \(|Z_j|\le1\) gives
\[
\mathfrak M_{3,n}\le8(2n)d_n^2,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{4,n}\le64(2n)^2d_n^2.
\tag{15}
\]
Nonadjacent coordinates are independent and hence uncorrelated, so \(s_n\le d_n\).  Substitution of \(d_n\le\overline d n^\alpha\), \(s_n\le d_n\), and \(\pi_{\min,n}\ge\underline\pi n^{-\beta}\) into the definition of \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\) yields a finite constant \(K_{\mathrm{ad}}\) with
\[
R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}
\le K_{\mathrm{ad}}
\{n^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}+n^{(7/2)\alpha+3\beta-2}\}.
\tag{16}
\]
Because \((7/2)\alpha+3\beta\le4\alpha+4\beta\), ass:zhao-first-order-rate implies \(\sup_r nR_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\to0\) for the two positively separated ranks.  At rank two, the definitions give \(\mathcal S_{2,n}(\kappa,\overline a)=\iota(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a})\), and the pseudoinverse is the ordinary inverse, recovering lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence.

Finally, every \(S(b_1,b_0)\) is positive semidefinite.  A positive-semidefinite \(2\times2\) matrix has rank two exactly when its determinant is positive, rank one exactly when its determinant is zero and trace positive, and rank zero exactly when its trace is zero.  Taking existential maxima proves the first three design-side equivalences.  If \(\Omega_{11}=\Omega_{00}=0\), positive semidefiniteness forces \(\Omega_{10}=\Omega_{01}=0\), so \(r_\Omega=0\).  Conversely a nonzero positive-semidefinite diagonal block has a vector with positive quadratic form.  This proves the final equivalence and the individual rank tests. \(\square\)
