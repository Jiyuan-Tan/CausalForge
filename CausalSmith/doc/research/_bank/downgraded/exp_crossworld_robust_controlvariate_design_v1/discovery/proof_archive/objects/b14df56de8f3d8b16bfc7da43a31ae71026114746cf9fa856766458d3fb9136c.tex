\textbf{Proof.}
Define
\[
E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\ I_n&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{HT}}=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}.
\]
By ass:product-coupling-zero and ass:frechet-covariance-domain, \(0\in\mathcal C(F_1,F_0)\), so \(Q(0)\) is well defined.  Comparing the four blocks in def:moment-matrix gives the affine identity
\[
Q(c)=Q(0)-cE. \tag{1}
\]

For each unit \(i\), ass:mutually-exclusive-exposures yields \(D_{i,1}D_{i,0}=0\) almost surely.  Therefore
\[
\Omega_{i,n+i}
=\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(D_{i,1},D_{i,0})
=\mathbb E_\nu[D_{i,1}D_{i,0}]
 -\mathbb E_\nu[D_{i,1}]\mathbb E_\nu[D_{i,0}]
=-\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}. \tag{2}
\]
Ass:exposure-positivity permits every division below.  Since \(\Omega\) is symmetric, (2) implies
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)
&=\sum_{i=1}^n
\left\{(A_{\mathrm{HT}})_{i,n+i}
       +(A_{\mathrm{HT}})_{n+i,i}\right\}\\
&=2\sum_{i=1}^n
\frac{\Omega_{i,n+i}}{\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}}
=-2n. \tag{3}
\end{aligned}
\]
Using (1), (3), linearity of the trace, and def:gain-loss,
\[
\begin{aligned}
h(c)
&=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_{\mathrm{HT}}Q(c)\}\\
&=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_{\mathrm{HT}}Q(0)\}
  -cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)\\
&=h(0)+\frac{2c}{n}.
\end{aligned}
\]
At either endpoint \(c_e\in\{c_L,c_U\}\), def:gain-loss consequently gives
\[
\ell_B(c_e)=h(0)+\frac{2c_e}{n}-g_B(c_e).
\]
Taking the maximum of these two quantities, as prescribed by def:robust-objective, proves
\[
w(B)=h(0)+\max\left\{
\frac{2c_L}{n}-g_B(c_L),
\frac{2c_U}{n}-g_B(c_U)
\right\}.
\]

Finally, suppose \(c_L<c_U\), put \(a_L=2c_L/n\) and \(a_U=2c_U/n\), and regard \((x_L,x_U)\) as a formal endpoint-gain pair.  Up to the common constant \(h(0)\), minimization of the residual uses
\[
\Phi(x_L,x_U)=\max\{a_L-x_L,a_U-x_U\},
\]
whereas maximization of \(\min\{x_L,x_U\}\) is equivalent to minimization of
\[
\Psi(x_L,x_U)=-\min\{x_L,x_U\}.
\]
If \(\Phi=\Psi+K\) for a constant \(K\) independent of \((x_L,x_U)\), then for every sufficiently large \(t\), evaluation at \((t,0)\) gives \(K=a_U\), while evaluation at \((0,t)\) gives \(K=a_L\).  This contradicts \(a_L<a_U\).  Hence the two endpoint criteria are not related by an additive constant independent of the formal gain pair, which is the claimed failure of algebraic equivalence. \(\square\)
