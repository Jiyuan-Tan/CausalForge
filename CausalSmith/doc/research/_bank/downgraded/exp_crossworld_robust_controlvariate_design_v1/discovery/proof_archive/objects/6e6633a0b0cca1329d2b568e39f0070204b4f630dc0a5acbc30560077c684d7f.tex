Let \(\mathcal K=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), let \(\mathcal Z\) be the normalized positive-definite rank-two carrier, and put \(a(B)=g_B(0)\),
\[
a^\star=\max_{B\in\mathcal K}a(B),
\quad A_0=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal K}a(B),
\quad v=\min_{B\in\mathcal K}w(B),
\quad A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal K}w(B),
\quad q=\min_{B\in A_0}w(B).
\]
Fix the finite signed-pivot charts and their semialgebraic quotient distance \(d_{\mathrm{pr}}\).  A primitive geometry record \(\mathscr G_{\mathrm{CAD}}\) consists only of finite rational or real-algebraic CAD signs, KKT data, derivative bounds, and interval remainders as follows.

First, it certifies numbers \(\zeta_0,\zeta_{\mathrm{mm}}>0\) such that
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K}a(B)
\le a^\star-\zeta_0,
\qquad
\inf_{B\in\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K}w(B)
\ge v+\zeta_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{G1}
\]

Second, it gives a finite semialgebraic stratification \(A_0=\bigcup_j V_j\).  On every signed-pivot chart meeting a stratum \(V_j\), it supplies: exact active constraints; nominal KKT multipliers; linear independence of active gradients; strictly positive multipliers for active inequalities; a certified tubular chart whose derivative splits feasible displacements into directions tangent to \(V_j\), feasible tangent directions transverse to \(V_j\), and active-normal coordinates; lower singular-value bounds for the transverse and normal coordinate map; and interval bounds for its first two derivatives and remainders.  The nominal Lagrangian Hessian is required to be strictly negative only on the feasible tangent directions transverse to \(V_j\), with a certified eigenvalue bounded above by a negative algebraic number.  No curvature is required along \(T_CV_j\), where \(a\) is constant.

Put \(A_q=\{C\in A_0:w(C)=q\}\).  The record certifies a relative semialgebraic neighborhood \(N_q\) of \(A_q\) in \(A_0\).  On every stratum contained in \(N_q\), it supplies exact weights \(\theta_L(C),\theta_U(C)\ge0\), summing to one and supported only on endpoint losses attaining \(w(C)\), and exact KKT normal data proving that the first feasible directional derivative of \(\theta_L(C)\ell_B(c_L)+\theta_U(C)\ell_B(c_U)\) is nonnegative.  It also supplies uniform interval bounds on the endpoint Hessians and Taylor remainders.  On the compact remainder \(A_0\setminus N_q\), it certifies a strictly positive \(w-q\) gap.  These are primitive finite checks; neither quadratic growth nor a global comparison inequality is included in the record.

Third, after the local tube radius is derived from those derivative bounds, CAD certifies on its compact complement constants \(\gamma>0\) and \(R<\infty\) satisfying
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\gamma,
\qquad [q-w(B)]_+\le R.
\tag{G2}
\]
Finally, the record contains an exactly feasible algebraic witness \(B_R\in\mathcal K\) and \(\zeta_\Delta>0\) with
\[
w(B_R)\le q-\zeta_\Delta.
\tag{G3}
\]

Every valid \(\mathscr G_{\mathrm{CAD}}\) derives constants \(\rho,\mu>0\) and \(M<\infty\) for
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\mu d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2,
\qquad
[q-w(B)]_+\le M d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\quad\text{if }d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\le\rho,
\tag{G4}
\]
proves
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=A_0,
\qquad \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=A_{\mathrm{mm}},
\qquad q-v\ge\zeta_\Delta>0,
\tag{G5}
\]
and derives the global comparison
\[
[q-w(B)]_+
\le L\{a^\star-a(B)\}
\quad(B\in\mathcal K),
\qquad L=\max\{M/\mu,R/\gamma\}.
\tag{G6}
\]

Consequently, if
\[
e=\sup_{\mathcal K}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|,
\qquad d=\sup_{\mathcal K}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|,
\]
then any empirical nominal maximizer \(\widehat B_0\) and robust minimizer \(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}}\) satisfy
\[
w(\widehat B_0)\ge q-2Le,
\qquad w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\le v+2d.
\]
Define \(V_0\) and \(V_{\mathrm{mm}}\) to be the observable ex ante conditional design-variance performances of these two selected bases.  If
\[
|V_0-w(\widehat B_0)|\le R_0,
\qquad
|V_{\mathrm{mm}}-w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})|\le R_{\mathrm{mm}},
\]
where the errors may be supplied by thm:rank-adaptive-oracle-observable-variance-bridge only on its certified stability strata, then
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge(q-v)-2Le-2d-R_0-R_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{G7}
\]
Thus containment, local growth, the global comparison constant, and the positive robust gap are derived from primitive finite certificates rather than assumed.
