\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\ I_n&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A_{\mathrm{HT}}=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}.
\]
By def:moment-matrix,
\[
Q(c)=Q(0)-cE.
\]
The expression \(Q(0)\) is well typed because ass:product-coupling-zero gives \(0\in\mathcal C(F_1,F_0)\); ass:frechet-covariance-domain further identifies this feasible domain with \([c_L,c_U]\).

For each unit \(i\), ass:mutually-exclusive-exposures gives \(D_{i,1}D_{i,0}=0\) almost surely.  Therefore
\[
\Omega_{i,n+i}
=\operatorname{Cov}_{\nu}(D_{i,1},D_{i,0})
=\mathbb E_{\nu}[D_{i,1}D_{i,0}]-\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}
=-\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}.
\]
Assumption ass:exposure-positivity permits division by both exposure probabilities.  Since \(A_{\mathrm{HT}}\) is symmetric and \(E\) exchanges the two exposure blocks,
\[
\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)
=2\sum_{i=1}^n(A_{\mathrm{HT}})_{i,n+i}
=2\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\Omega_{i,n+i}}{\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}}
=-2n.
\]
Linearity of trace now gives, for every feasible \(c\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
h(c)
&=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_{\mathrm{HT}}(Q(0)-cE)\}\\
&=h(0)-cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)\\
&=h(0)+\frac{2c}{n}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Using def:gain-loss and def:robust-objective, substitute this identity into
\(w(B)=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\{h(c_e)-g_B(c_e)\}\) to obtain
\[
w(B)=h(0)+\max\left\{
\frac{2c_L}{n}-g_B(c_L),
\frac{2c_U}{n}-g_B(c_U)
\right\}.
\]

It remains to verify the asserted algebraic non-equivalence.  Put \(a_e=2c_e/n\).  For a formal endpoint-gain pair \(x=(x_L,x_U)\in\mathbb R^2\), define
\[
\Phi(x)=\max\{a_L-x_L,a_U-x_U\},
\qquad
\Psi(x)=\max\{-x_L,-x_U\}=-\min\{x_L,x_U\}.
\]
Up to the basis-independent constant \(h(0)\), minimization of worst residual uses \(\Phi\), whereas maximization of \(r(B)\) is equivalent to minimization of \(\Psi\).  Suppose there were a constant \(K\), independent of \(x\), such that \(\Phi(x)=\Psi(x)+K\) for all \(x\).  With \(x=(t,0)\), letting \(t\to\infty\) gives \(K=a_U\).  With \(x=(0,t)\), letting \(t\to\infty\) gives \(K=a_L\).  Hence such a constant can exist only if \(a_L=a_U\), equivalently \(c_L=c_U\).  When \(c_L<c_U\), the two formal criteria are therefore not related by a basis-independent additive constant. \(\square\)
