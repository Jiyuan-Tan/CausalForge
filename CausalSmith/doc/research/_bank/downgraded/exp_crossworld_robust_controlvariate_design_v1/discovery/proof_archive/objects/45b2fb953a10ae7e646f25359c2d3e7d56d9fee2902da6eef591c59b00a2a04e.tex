\textbf{Proof.}
Exposure positivity makes every diagonal entry of \(\Pi\) strictly positive, so the inverse-probability matrices occurring in \(w\) are finite.  For every nonempty \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), the compactness proposition makes \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) compact.  On its normalized representatives,
\[
 S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2.
\]
Consequently, \(B\mapsto S_B^{-1}\) is continuous there.  The trace formulas defining \(g_B(c_e)\) and \(\ell_B(c_e)\), for \(e\in\{L,U\}\), are therefore continuous in \(B\), and so is their finite maximum \(w(B)\).  These functions are invariant under the residual independent sign changes, hence descend continuously to the quotient.  Thus every displayed minimum over a nonempty \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is attained.

If \(0<\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\), the eigenvalue constraints give
\[
 \mathcal B_{\kappa_2}\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}.
\]
Minimizing the same function over these nested nonempty sets yields
\[
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}}w(B)
 \le
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_2}}w(B).
\]

The exhaustion proposition gives the set identity
\[
 \mathcal B_Z=\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa,
\]
where empty threshold classes do not affect the union.  Therefore the elementary identity for an infimum over a union gives
\[
 \inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
 =
 \inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
 \inf_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
 =
 \inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\tag{1}
\]
The quotient-transfer theorem states that \(q_Z\) is surjective and that \(w\circ q_Z\) is constant on every fiber.  Hence
\[
 \inf_{B\in\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z}w(q_Z(B))
 =\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\tag{2}
\]
which proves all three value equalities.

Finally, suppose \(B_Z^\star\) attains the middle infimum.  By exhaustion there is \(\kappa_0>0\) such that \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_0}\).  For every \(0<\kappa\le\kappa_0\), nesting gives \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_\kappa\), while \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\).  If \(V_Z=\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)\), these two inclusions imply
\[
 V_Z
 \le \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
 \le w(B_Z^\star)
 =V_Z.
\]
Thus equality holds for every such \(\kappa\).  All arguments take place on the positive-definite rank-two ordinary-inverse restriction and its exact rescaling quotient; no singular pseudoinverse class is included. \(\square\)
