\textbf{Proof.}
\emph{Step 1: compact algebraic feasible set.}
Use normalized representatives and put
\[
 K_\kappa=\{(b_1,b_0):\lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
 \ S_B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0\}.
\]
Ass:kappa-feasibility makes this set nonempty, and prop:kappa-exhaustion makes its quotient compact.  On a radius-\(\sqrt n\) sphere some coordinate has absolute value at least one, so the finitely many signed pivot regions \(\{\pm b_j\ge1\}\) cover each projective factor.  Retaining the sphere equations and pivot sign gives a finite semialgebraic chart cover.  Lem:algebraic-box-feasibility-cad decides each rational chart-box intersection with \(K_\kappa\) and returns an algebraic point when nonempty, including on the equality boundary.

\emph{Step 2: an effective uniform loss modulus.}
For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), put
\[
 R_e=\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega,
 \quad S(B)=B^{\top}\Omega B,
 \quad T_e(B)=B^{\top}R_eB.
\]
Exact algebraic sign evaluation and exposure positivity provide positive lower bounds for the diagonal entries of \(\Pi\) and for \(\kappa\), and rational upper bounds \(M_\Omega\ge\lVert\Omega\rVert_2\) and \(M_e\ge\lVert R_e\rVert_2\).  Every normalized block matrix satisfies \(\lVert B\rVert_F=\sqrt{2n}\).  If \(d=\lVert B-C\rVert_F\), then
\[
 \lVert S(B)-S(C)\rVert_2\le K_Sd,
 \qquad \lVert T_e(B)-T_e(C)\rVert_2\le K_{T,e}d,
\]
where \(K_S=2\sqrt{2n}M_\Omega\) and \(K_{T,e}=2\sqrt{2n}M_e\).  On \(K_\kappa\),
\[
 S(B)\succeq n\kappa I_2,
 \qquad \lVert S(B)^{-1}\rVert_2\le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\]
The resolvent identity and \(\lvert\operatorname{tr}(XY)\rvert\le2\lVert X\rVert_2\lVert Y\rVert_2\) give
\[
 \lvert g_B(c_e)-g_C(c_e)\rvert\le L_ed,
 \quad
 L_e=n^{-2}\left\{\frac{2K_{T,e}}{n\kappa}
 +\frac{4nM_eK_S}{(n\kappa)^2}\right\}.
\]
The uncontrolled term is basis independent and the maximum is one-Lipschitz, hence
\[
 \lvert w(B)-w(C)\rvert\le L\lVert B-C\rVert_F,
 \qquad L=\max\{L_L,L_U\}. \tag{1}
\]
Algebraic isolation supplies a recorded rational \(\overline L\ge L\).

\emph{Step 3: finite exhaustive depth.}
Choose rational \(\alpha>0\) with \(4\alpha<\varepsilon\).  Uniform bisection reaches a finite depth at which every chart box \(C\) has rational diameter bound \(d_C\) with
\[
 d_C\le\varepsilon,
 \qquad \overline Ld_C\le\alpha. \tag{2}
\]
There are finitely many boxes.  CAD certifies each empty box and returns an exact algebraic sample \(B_C\) for each feasible box.  Algebraic isolation terminates with rationals \(\underline q_C,\overline q_C\) satisfying
\[
 w(B_C)-\alpha\le\underline q_C\le w(B_C)
 \le\overline q_C\le w(B_C)+\alpha. \tag{3}
\]
By (1)--(2),
\[
 \lambda_C:=\underline q_C-\overline Ld_C
 \le\inf_{B\in C\cap K_\kappa}w(B). \tag{4}
\]
Let \(U_\varepsilon=\min_C\overline q_C\), choose the corresponding algebraic sample as \(B_\varepsilon\), and set \(L_\varepsilon=\min_C\lambda_C\).  Compactness supplies a minimizer \(B^\star\).  A box containing it and (1)--(4) imply
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}-2\alpha\le L_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}
 \le w(B_\varepsilon)\le U_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}+2\alpha.
\]
Thus \(U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon\le4\alpha<\varepsilon\).

\emph{Step 4: outer boxes and local checking.}
Prune a feasible box only if \(\lambda_C>U_\varepsilon\).  By (4) such a box cannot contain a minimizer, whereas a box containing a minimizer has \(\lambda_C\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le U_\varepsilon\).  Let \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\) be the quotient image of all retained feasible boxes.  Then \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\subseteq\mathcal C_\varepsilon\), and (2) gives \(h_\varepsilon\le\varepsilon\).

The checker receives only finite CAD sign records, algebraic feasible coordinates, rational interval inclusions, norm and Lipschitz bounds, chart cover, pruning comparisons, and mesh.  The inequalities concerning \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) are consequences of those records, not inputs to the checker.  Every stage terminates after finitely many algebraic operations and subdivisions.  Repeating the construction for \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) makes both the certified value gap and mesh tend to zero. \(\square\)
