For a fixed design with \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\), \((C_k)_{k\ge1}\) is obtained by enumerating rational arm directions in all signed pivot charts, normalizing each nonzero arm column to squared norm \(n\), retaining the representatives with \(B^{\top}\Omega B\succ0\), and removing duplicate quotient classes in the fixed enumeration order.
