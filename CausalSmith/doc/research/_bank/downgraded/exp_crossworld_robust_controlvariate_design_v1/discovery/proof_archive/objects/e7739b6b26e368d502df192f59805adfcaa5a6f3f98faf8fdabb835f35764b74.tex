If \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\{B^\star\}\) in the projective quotient, \(B^\star\) is isolated by a positive loss gap outside every neighborhood, and exactly one of \(c_L,c_U\) attains \(w(B^\star)\), then every measurable selection \(\widehat B\in\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm}}\) converges almost surely to \(B^\star\).  Without the singleton condition, there exist bounded two-marginal instances satisfying the maintained assumptions for which the outer-set conclusion of thm:plugin-correspondence holds but some measurable plug-in selections fail to converge to a point.
