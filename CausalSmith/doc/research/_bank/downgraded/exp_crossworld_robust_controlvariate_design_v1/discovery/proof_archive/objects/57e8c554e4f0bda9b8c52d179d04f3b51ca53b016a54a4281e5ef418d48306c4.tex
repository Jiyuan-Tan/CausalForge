\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\mathcal K_n=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\).  Coordinate-bounded feasibility makes \(\mathcal K_n\) nonempty.  Normalization and the coordinate envelope make it compact, while \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\) makes inversion and all population and empirical criteria continuous on it.  Hence both optimizer sets are nonempty.  The fixed finite-chart lexicographic rule is pilot measurable: each empirical endpoint is a Borel function of finitely many order statistics; the objective is jointly Borel in the pilot and continuous in \(B\); its minimum is the infimum over a fixed countable dense subset; and successive lexicographic coordinate minima are limits of infima over the compact \(k^{-1}\)-near-optimizer sets.  Thus the two selectors in \(\texttt{def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator}\) exist and are measurable.

Use the proposed notation \(d_n^{\mathrm{pl}}\) for the plug-in loss error, leaving \(d_n\) exclusively for directed-neighborhood size.  Lemma \(\texttt{lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate}\) gives, pathwise under the pilot law,
\[
d_n^{\mathrm{pl}}\le C_nM_n,
\qquad
e_n\le2C_nM_n.
\tag{1}
\]
Choose \(B_n^\star\in\operatorname*{argmin}_{\mathcal K_n}w\).  Empirical optimality and (1) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
&\le\widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d_n^{\mathrm{pl}}\\
&\le\widehat w(B_n^\star)+d_n^{\mathrm{pl}}\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+2d_n^{\mathrm{pl}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
The population minimum gives the reverse inequality.  Taking pilot expectations in (2) proves
\[
0\le\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})-v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}
\le2C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{3}
\]

Conditional on the independent pilot, \(\widehat B_s\) is fixed in \(\mathcal K_n\).  The uniform observable-to-oracle lemma therefore applies.  By the sharp covariance-domain equality and \(\texttt{thm:fixed-basis-endpoints}\),
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_B(c_H)=w(B).
\tag{4}
\]
Apply \(|\sup_Hu_H-\sup_Hv_H|\le\sup_H|u_H-v_H|\) to the uniform bridge and then integrate over the pilot.  This gives
\[
|V_s-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)|\le R_n,
\qquad s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}.
\tag{5}
\]
The exact premise \(nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\to0\) makes the right side of (3) \(o(n^{-1})\), and the repaired directed-local bridge gives \(nR_n\to0\).  Equations (3) and (5) prove both first-order conclusions.

For the nominal selector, choose \(B_n^0\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}\).  Inserting and subtracting empirical gains and using empirical optimality gives
\[
g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)
\le2e_n\le4C_nM_n.
\tag{6}
\]
The displayed deterministic geometric inequality then implies
\[
[q_n-w(\widehat B_0)]_+
\le4L_nC_nM_n.
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(x\ge q-[q-x]_+\), taking pilot expectations in (7) gives
\[
q_n-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\le4L_nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{8}
\]
Boundedness of \(L_n\) and the exact sensitivity premise make (8) \(o(n^{-1})\).  Outer argmax consistency alone would not supply this rate; the displayed global error bound is exactly the extra geometric input used. \(\square\)
