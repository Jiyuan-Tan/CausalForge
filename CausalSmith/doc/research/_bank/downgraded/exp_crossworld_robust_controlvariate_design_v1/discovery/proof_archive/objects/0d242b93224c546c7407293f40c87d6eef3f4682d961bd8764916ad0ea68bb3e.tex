\textbf{Proof.}
Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) exist.  For \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\), def:zhao-basis-class gives \(S_B\succ0\), so \(S_B^{-1}\) exists.  Put
\[
 E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\I_n&0\end{bmatrix},
 \qquad A_B=\Pi^{-1}\Omega BS_B^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}.
\]
Since \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\), cyclicity and linearity of trace give
\[
 g_B(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_BQ(0)\}
 -cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_BE),
\]
so the gain is affine.  The calculation in lem:ht-affine-drift, repeated directly from mutual exclusivity, gives \(h(c)=h(0)+2c/n\).  Hence \(\ell_B(c)=h(c)-g_B(c)\) is affine.  Ass:known-exposure and ass:iid-potential-pairs supply the causal exposure and ex ante moment interpretations; the affine conclusion itself is the displayed matrix algebra.

By ass:frechet-covariance-domain,
\[
 \{\operatorname{Cov}_H(Y_i(1),Y_i(0)):H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)\}=[c_L,c_U].
\]
An affine function attains its extrema at endpoints, and equality of the covariance image with the closed interval gives endpoint-attaining couplings.  This proves both endpoint formulas and attainment.  Ass:product-coupling-zero supplies a feasible coupling with covariance zero, hence \(c_L\le0\le c_U\).

Finally write \(g_B(c)=\alpha_B+\beta_Bc\).  If \(\beta_B=0\), the gain is constant.  If \(c_L<0<c_U\) and \(\beta_B>0\), then \(g_B(c_L)<g_B(0)\); if \(\beta_B<0\), then \(g_B(c_U)<g_B(0)\).  Thus the zero-covariance value is the worst-coupling gain exactly when the slope vanishes. \(\square\)
