For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), set \(A_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\) and \(Z_B=\Omega^{1/2}B\).  Every \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\) satisfies
\[
 g_B^+(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e),
 \qquad P_B=Z_B(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top},
\]
where \(P_B\) is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(Z_B)\), \(A_e\succeq0\), and \(\operatorname{rank}(P_B)=\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\in\{0,1,2\}\).  On rank two, \([B]_+=\iota([C])\) for a unique \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), and the gains and losses agree.  Define
\[
r_\Omega=\max_{[C]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\operatorname{rank}(C^{\top}\Omega C).
\]
For every \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), there is a sequence \(([B_j]_+)_{j\ge1}\subseteq\mathcal B_Z^+\) with \(\operatorname{rank}(S_{B_j})=r_\Omega\) and a projector \(P_\infty\) such that
\[
P_{B_j}\longrightarrow P_\infty,
\qquad P_\infty-P_B\succeq0.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\liminf_{j\to\infty}g_{B_j}^+(c_e)\ge g_B^+(c_e)
\quad(e\in\{L,U\}),
\qquad
\limsup_{j\to\infty}w^+(B_j)\le w^+(B).
\]
If \(r_\Omega=2\), the approximating sequence may be chosen in \(\iota(\mathcal B_Z)\).
