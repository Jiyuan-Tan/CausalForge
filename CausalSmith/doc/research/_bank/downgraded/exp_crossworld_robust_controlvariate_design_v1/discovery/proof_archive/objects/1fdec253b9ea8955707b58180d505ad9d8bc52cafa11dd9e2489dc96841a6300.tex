Suppose ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate, ass:zhao-dependency-neighborhood-rate, ass:zhao-first-order-rate, ass:bounded-outcomes, and ass:iid-potential-pairs hold.  Uniformly over \(B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), let
\[
\gamma_B^*(Y)=(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y
\]
and let \(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or}}\) be the control-variate estimator using this fixed-array oracle coefficient.  Then
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}
n\left|
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid Y)
-\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or}}\mid Y)
\right|\longrightarrow0
\]
uniformly over all arrays with \(0\le Y_i(a)\le1\).  If \(c_H=\operatorname{Cov}_H\{Y_i(1),Y_i(0)\}\), then the oracle ex ante identity is exact:
\[
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or}}\mid Y)
=\ell_B(c_H).
\]
Consequently the same ex ante variance for the feasible Horvitz--Thompson-coefficient estimator equals \(\ell_B(c_H)+o(n^{-1})\), uniformly over the rate-admissible class.  The expectations over \(H^{\otimes n}\) in these displays are ex ante basis-performance averages, not additional randomness in the conditional design-based estimand.
