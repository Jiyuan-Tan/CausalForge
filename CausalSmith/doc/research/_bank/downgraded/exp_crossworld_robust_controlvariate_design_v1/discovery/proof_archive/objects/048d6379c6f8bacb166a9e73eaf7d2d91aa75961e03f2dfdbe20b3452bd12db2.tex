\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(N=2n\), index exposure coordinates by \(\ell\), and write \(Z_\ell=D_\ell-\pi_\ell\).  Known exposure and the signed convention in \(Y\) give
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\widehat Y_\ell^{\mathrm{obs}}=Y_\ell,
\qquad
\{\Pi^{-1}(\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}-Y)\}_\ell
=\frac{Y_\ell Z_\ell}{\pi_\ell^2}.
\tag{1}
\]
Exposure positivity is exactly what licenses these divisions.

If \(S_B=0\), then \(S_B^+=0\).  Since \(S_B=B^\top\Omega B=0\) and \(\Omega\succeq0\), one has \(\Omega^{1/2}B=0\), hence \(B^\top\Omega=0\).  Both the observable and oracle coefficients vanish.  Substitution in \(\texttt{def:observable-coefficient-map}\) gives exactly \(n^{-1}\mathbf1^\top\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}\), proving every rank-zero assertion.

Fix \(r\in\{1,2\}\) and \([B]_+\in\mathcal S_{r,n}(\lambda_r,\overline a)\).  Set
\[
G_B=\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)-\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y).
\]
Equation (1) yields
\[
G_B=\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}u_{B,\ell}Z_\ell,
\qquad
u_{B,\ell}:=S_B^+B^\top\Omega e_\ell\frac{Y_\ell}{\pi_\ell^2}.
\tag{2}
\]
No alternative coefficient name is introduced.  The positive-eigenvalue margin gives
\[
\lVert S_B^+\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\lambda_r)^{-1}.
\tag{3}
\]
Each covariance column has at most \(s_n\) nonzero entries, every covariance entry has absolute value at most one, and the basis coordinates are bounded by \(\overline a\).  Hence
\[
\sup_\ell\lVert B^\top\Omega e_\ell\rVert_2
\le\sqrt2\,\overline a s_n,
\qquad
\sup_\ell\lVert u_{B,\ell}\rVert_2\le U_{n,r}.
\tag{4}
\]
The centered basis statistic is
\[
X_B=n^{-1}B^\top(D-\mathbb E_\nu D)
=\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}x_{B,\ell}Z_\ell,
\qquad x_{B,\ell}=n^{-1}B^\top e_\ell,
\tag{5}
\]
and \(\sup_\ell\lVert x_{B,\ell}\rVert_2\le X_n\).  The fourth-moment envelope in \(\texttt{def:rank-adaptive-stability-strata}\), applied coordinatewise, gives
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\lVert G_B\rVert_2^4
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}U_{n,r}^4,
\qquad
\mathbb E_\nu\lVert X_B\rVert_2^4
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}X_n^4.
\tag{6}
\]
The feasible and oracle estimators satisfy
\[
\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}
=\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}+\Delta_B,
\qquad \Delta_B=-G_B^\top X_B.
\tag{7}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz and (6) imply
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\Delta_B^2
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}U_{n,r}^2X_n^2.
\tag{8}
\]

Put \(T_B=\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}-\tau\).  Equation (1) makes the Horvitz--Thompson term unbiased, and the basis statistic is centered, so \(\mathbb E_\nu T_B=0\).  Direct expansion gives
\[
T_B=\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}t_{B,\ell}Z_\ell,
\qquad
t_{B,\ell}=n^{-1}\left\{\frac{Y_\ell}{\pi_\ell}
-B_{\ell\cdot}\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)\right\}.
\tag{9}
\]
The projector identity in \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\) gives
\[
\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)^\top S_B\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)
\le Y^\top\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y.
\tag{10}
\]
There are at most \(Ns_n=2ns_n\) nonzero entries in the covariance sum, and every term is at most \(\pi_{\min,n}^{-2}\) in absolute value.  Thus
\[
Y^\top\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y
\le2ns_n\pi_{\min,n}^{-2}.
\tag{11}
\]
The coefficient lies in the positive spectral subspace of \(S_B\).  Combining (10)--(11) with \(\lambda_+(S_B)\ge n\lambda_r\) yields
\[
\lVert\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)\rVert_2\le\Gamma_{n,r},
\qquad
\sup_\ell|t_{B,\ell}|\le T_{n,r}.
\tag{12}
\]
Expanding \(\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(T_B,\Delta_B\mid Y)\) in the two coefficient coordinates and applying the third-moment envelope gives
\[
|\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(T_B,\Delta_B\mid Y)|
\le2\mathfrak M_{3,n}T_{n,r}U_{n,r}X_n.
\tag{13}
\]
The variance identity then combines (8) and twice (13) to give exactly the displayed \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\).

For the oracle identity, put \(q_B=B^\top\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y\).  Since
\[
q_B\in\operatorname{range}\{(\Omega^{1/2}B)^\top\}
=\operatorname{range}(S_B),
\]
one has \(S_BS_B^+q_B=q_B\).  Completing the quadratic variance expression gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}\mid Y)
=n^{-2}Y^\top\left\{\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}
-\Pi^{-1}\Omega BS_B^+B^\top\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\}Y.
\tag{14}
\]
Under the i.i.d. potential-pair assumption, \(\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}(YY^\top)=Q(c_H)\).  Taking the trace expectation in (14) and applying \(\texttt{def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss}\) proves \(\ell_B^+(c_H)\).

Now let \(\widetilde B\) satisfy the selected-stratum certificate (S).  Pilot independence permits conditioning on its realized class but does not imply (S).  On the rank-zero event the difference is exactly zero.  On \(\{\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widetilde B})=r\}\), condition (S) licenses the fixed-basis calculation with the displayed \(\lambda_r\) and \(\overline a\).  Integrating the conditional inequalities over the pilot proves the selected-basis display.  For the encoded CAD output, \(\texttt{thm:encoded-cad-pilot-selector-certificate}\) supplies exact rank and positive-eigenvalue isolation; the present theorem applies only after those records and the coordinate check verify (S).

It remains to derive the stated rates from the directed local condition.  Lemma \(\texttt{lem:directed-local-centered-moment-count}\) explicitly partitions the multiplicity types \(4\), \(3+1\), \(2+2\), \(2+1+1\), and \(1+1+1+1\), and enumerates the one-cycle, two-cycle, inward-star, chain, three-cycle, and four-cycle charges using only the singleton criterion.  It gives
\[
s_n\le d_n\le\overline d n^\alpha,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{4,n}\le64(2n)^2d_n^2,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{3,n}\le8(2n)d_n^2.
\tag{15}
\]
Substitution of (15) and \(\pi_{\min,n}\ge\underline\pi n^{-\beta}\) into \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\) gives a finite constant \(K_{\mathrm{ad}}\) such that
\[
R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}
\le K_{\mathrm{ad}}
\{n^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}+n^{(7/2)\alpha+3\beta-2}\},
\qquad r\in\{1,2\}.
\tag{16}
\]
The constant is uniform in the selected basis, its positive rank, the bounded array, and \(H\), for fixed positive lower bounds on \(\lambda_1,\lambda_2\) and fixed \(\overline a\).  Since \((7/2)\alpha+3\beta\le4\alpha+4\beta\), the first-order rate assumption implies \(\sup_r nR_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\to0\).  At rank two, the definitions give \(\mathcal S_{2,n}(\kappa,\overline a)=\iota(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a})\), and the pseudoinverse is the ordinary inverse, recovering \(\texttt{lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence}\).

Finally, every \(S(b_1,b_0)\) is positive semidefinite.  Such a \(2\times2\) matrix has rank two exactly when its determinant is positive, rank one exactly when its determinant is zero and its trace positive, and rank zero exactly when its trace is zero.  Taking existential maxima proves the first three design-side equivalences.  If \(\Omega_{11}=\Omega_{00}=0\), positive semidefiniteness forces \(\Omega_{10}=\Omega_{01}=0\), so \(r_\Omega=0\).  Conversely, a nonzero positive-semidefinite diagonal block has a vector with positive quadratic form.  This proves the final equivalence and the individual rank tests.  No step identifies optimizer classes across an uncontrolled rank change. \(\square\)
