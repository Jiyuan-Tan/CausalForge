\textbf{Proof.}
Positive definiteness of \(B^{\top}\Omega B\) forces both arm columns to be nonzero.  Hence the quotient map is well defined and surjective, and the defining projective relation gives
\[
q_Z(B)=q_Z(C)
\Longleftrightarrow
C=BT,qquad T=\operatorname{diag}(t_1,t_0),\quad t_1t_0\ne0.
\tag{1}
\]
For such \(T\),
\[
 S_{BT}=T^{\top}S_BT,qquad
 S_{BT}^{-1}=T^{-1}S_B^{-1}(T^{\top})^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
Substitution of (2) into the trace formula for \(g\), followed by cyclicity of trace, cancels every factor of \(T\).  Thus \(g_{q_Z(BT)}(c)=g_{q_Z(B)}(c)\).  Since \(h(c)\) is basis independent, the same is true for \(\ell\) and \(w\).  A function constant on the fibers of a surjection has the same infimum and supremum on \(K\) as its pullback on \(q_Z^{-1}(K)\); equality in the optimizing inequality also shows that every attained optimizer set pulls back exactly. \(\square\)
