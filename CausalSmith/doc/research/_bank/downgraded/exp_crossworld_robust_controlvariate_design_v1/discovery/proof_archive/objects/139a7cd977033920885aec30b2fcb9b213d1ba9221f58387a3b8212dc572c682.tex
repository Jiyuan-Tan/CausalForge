The causal estimand is the fixed finite-population contrast \(\tau\); the primary ex ante design criterion used to choose a control span is the distinct functional \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)\).  For arbitrary real pilot observations, \(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+=C_{\widehat k_m}\) is only a classical nonconstructive measurable existence selector and has no finite stopping rule.  For rational or real-algebraic empirical endpoint input, \(\widehat{\mathscr K}_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}\) finitely certifies an algebraic basis \(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}\), its selected rank, positive-eigenvalue isolating intervals, and an \(\varepsilon_m\)-value gap; agreement with the countable selector is not claimed.  Deployment uses \(\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}=(D_{i,1}Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}/\pi_{i,1},-D_{i,0}Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}/\pi_{i,0})_{i=1}^n\), \(\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)=S_B^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}\), and \(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}=n^{-1}\mathbf1^{\top}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}-\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)^{\top}\{n^{-1}B^{\top}D-\mathbb E_\nu[n^{-1}B^{\top}D]\}\).  Its oracle variance equals \(\ell_B^+(c_H)\).  The observable bridge is exact at rank zero and has the explicit error \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\) at rank one or two only when, almost surely on the selected-rank event, a spectral certificate verifies \(\lambda_+(S_B)\ge n\lambda_r\) for a displayed \(\lambda_r>0\) and an exact coordinate check verifies the displayed envelope; pilot independence alone supplies neither condition.
