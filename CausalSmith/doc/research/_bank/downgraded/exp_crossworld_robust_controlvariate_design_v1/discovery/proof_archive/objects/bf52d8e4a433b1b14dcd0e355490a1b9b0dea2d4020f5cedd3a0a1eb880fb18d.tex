\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
 E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\I_n&0\end{bmatrix},
 \qquad A_{\mathrm{HT}}=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}.
\]
Def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\).  The value at zero is well typed by ass:product-coupling-zero and ass:frechet-covariance-domain.  For each unit, mutual exclusivity gives
\[
 \Omega_{i,n+i}=\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(D_{i,1},D_{i,0})
 =-\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}.
\]
Exposure positivity permits division, and symmetry gives
\[
 \operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)
 =2\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\Omega_{i,n+i}}{\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}}=-2n.
\]
Therefore
\[
 h(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_{\mathrm{HT}}Q(c)\}
 =h(0)-cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)
 =h(0)+\frac{2c}{n}.
\]
Substitution into def:gain-loss and def:robust-objective proves the displayed formula for \(w(B)\).

For the non-equivalence, set \(a_e=2c_e/n\) and, for a formal gain pair \(x=(x_L,x_U)\), define
\[
 \Phi(x)=\max\{a_L-x_L,a_U-x_U\},
 \qquad \Psi(x)=-\min\{x_L,x_U\}.
\]
If \(\Phi=\Psi+K\) for a constant independent of \(x\), evaluation at \((t,0)\) and letting \(t\to\infty\) gives \(K=a_U\), whereas evaluation at \((0,t)\) gives \(K=a_L\).  Thus no such constant exists when \(c_L<c_U\). \(\square\)
