\textbf{Proof.}
By exposure positivity all inverse-probability expressions are defined.  The sharp covariance-domain assumption ensures that \(c_L\) and \(c_U\) are the endpoints of the entire feasible cross-world covariance interval, so the endpoint maximum in \(w^+\) has its stated worst-copula interpretation.  Put
\[
 V_+=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B),
 \qquad
 V_Z=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
\]

The rank-two stratum-identification lemma maps every \(C\in\mathcal B_Z\) to \(\iota(C)\in\mathcal B_Z^+\) and gives
\[
 w^+(\iota(C))=w(C).
\]
It follows at once that \(V_+\le V_Z\).  For the reverse inequality, fix any \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\).  If \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)=2\), the same lemma supplies a unique \(C\in\mathcal B_Z\) with \([B]_+=\iota(C)\), and hence
\[
 V_Z\le w(C)=w^+(B).
\tag{1}
\]
If \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1\), the rank-stratified projector-reduction lemma supplies a sequence \(B_j\in\mathcal B_Z\) satisfying
\[
 \limsup_{j\to\infty}w(B_j)\le w^+(B).
\]
Since \(V_Z\le w(B_j)\) for every \(j\),
\[
 V_Z
 \le\liminf_{j\to\infty}w(B_j)
 \le\limsup_{j\to\infty}w(B_j)
 \le w^+(B).
\tag{2}
\]
Equations \((1)\) and \((2)\) show that \(V_Z\) is a lower bound for \(w^+\) on all of \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).  Thus \(V_Z\le V_+\), and consequently
\[
 V_+=V_Z=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\tag{3}
\]
The fixed-threshold value-exhaustion proposition then gives
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 =\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
 =\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\tag{4}
\]

The displayed description of \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is precisely the equality set associated with the infimum in \((3)\); it may be empty because \((3)\) establishes a value equality, not attainment.  It remains to identify its rank-two part.  Suppose first that \(\iota(C)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  Rank-two loss equality and \((3)\) yield
\[
 w(C)=w^+(\iota(C))=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=V_Z.
\tag{5}
\]
The exhaustion proposition places \(C\) in some nonempty \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}\).  Because \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\), equation \((5)\) gives
\[
 V_Z
 \le\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}}w(B)
 \le w(C)
 =V_Z.
\]
Therefore the threshold value equals \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) and \(C\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa_C)\).

Conversely, suppose that for some nonempty \(\mathcal B_\kappa\),
\[
 C\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa),
 \qquad
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\]
Then rank-two loss equality gives
\[
 w^+(\iota(C))=w(C)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+,
\]
so \(\iota(C)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  The two implications prove
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
=\iota\!\left(
\bigcup_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,
\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing,\ 
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+}}
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\right).
\tag{6}
\]
In particular, any ordinary-domain minimizer \(C\) satisfies \((5)\), hence maps into the full equality set; and every rank-two full equality point satisfies the converse conclusion just proved.

Finally, the stratum-identification lemma says that the complement of \(\iota(\mathcal B_Z)\) in \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) consists exactly of the classes with \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1\).  Intersecting that complement with the defining equality for \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) yields
\[
 \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\setminus\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
 =\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1,\ w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\}.
\]
The singular approximation used in \((2)\) produces only a rank-two minimizing sequence.  It does not assert that the rank-two infimum is attained, nor that either the full equality set or its singular stratum is nonempty. \(\square\)
