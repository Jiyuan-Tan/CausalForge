\textbf{Proof.}
Fix a rational signed pivot-chart box and write the \(2n\) coordinates of \((b_1,b_0)\) as \(x\).  The normalization conditions are the polynomial equations
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n b_{1i}^2=n,
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^n b_{0i}^2=n.
\]
Every box restriction and signed pivot restriction is a polynomial weak inequality with rational coefficients.  Because \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\), every entry of \(S_B\) is a quadratic polynomial in \(x\) with rational or real-algebraic coefficients.

Write
\[
S_B-n\kappa I_2=
\begin{bmatrix}u&v\\v&z\end{bmatrix}.
\]
If this matrix is positive semidefinite, its one-by-one and two-by-two principal minors give
\[
u\ge0,\qquad z\ge0,\qquad uz-v^2\ge0. \tag{1}
\]
Conversely, suppose (1) holds.  If \(u>0\), then for every \((s,t)\in\mathbb R^2\),
\[
us^2+2vst+zt^2
=u\left(s+\frac vu t\right)^2
+\left(z-\frac{v^2}{u}\right)t^2\ge0.
\]
If \(u=0\), the determinant inequality forces \(v=0\), and the quadratic form equals \(zt^2\ge0\).  Thus (1) is also sufficient.  In particular, it retains rather than discards the equality boundary.

Intersection of the chart box with the normalized feasible set is therefore expressed by one existential first-order formula
\[
\exists x\;\Phi(x),
\]
where \(\Phi\) is a finite conjunction of polynomial equalities and weak inequalities over the real-algebraic coefficient field.  By lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination, Collins's EVAL/ELIM construction returns after finitely many eliminations a quantifier-free formula equivalent to this existential formula.  Exact real-algebraic sign isolation decides the returned formula.

For completeness, the witness part of the same cylindrical construction is obtained during lifting.  At each of the finitely many lifting levels, the projection polynomials specialize to finitely many univariate polynomials with real-algebraic coefficients.  Exact root isolation partitions the line into finitely many open sectors and root sections; choosing a rational point in a sector or the isolated real-algebraic root in a section gives an algebraic sample.  The number of variables, projection polynomials, roots, and resulting cells is finite, so recursive lifting terminates.  A cell whose sign vector satisfies \(\Phi\) returns its real-algebraic sample.  If no cell satisfies \(\Phi\), the finite sign-invariant cell list together with the quantifier-elimination trace is an infeasibility certificate.  Since weak inequalities admit root sections, the conclusion remains valid when all feasible samples lie on the sphere or \(\kappa\)-equality boundary. \(\square\)
