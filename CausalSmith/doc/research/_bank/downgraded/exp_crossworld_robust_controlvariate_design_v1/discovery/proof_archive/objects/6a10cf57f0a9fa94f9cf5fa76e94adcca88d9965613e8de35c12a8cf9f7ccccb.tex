\textbf{Proof.}
Assumption ass:known-exposure makes the deployed estimator in def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator agree with the exposure-potential-outcome stack.  Put
\[
v_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B).
\]
The assumed optimizer containment implies
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=v_n,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\mathcal A_{0,Z},
\qquad
\Delta_Z=q_n-v_n.
\tag{1}
\]
Indeed, each global optimizer is feasible in the smaller class, and any smaller-class optimizer attaining the same global value is itself a global optimizer.

The robust and conditional zero-surrogate inequalities in thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer give
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Applying the feasible-estimator bridge once in each direction yields
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n-2R_n.
\tag{3}
\]
Substitution of the three stated deterministic envelopes in (3) gives
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge \Delta_Z
-(4L_n+2)K_CK_M\frac{n^{\alpha+2\beta}}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}
-2K_Rn^{4\alpha+4\beta-2},
\]
so the displayed finite-\(n\) condition implies \(V_0>V_{\mathrm{mm}}\).

For the asymptotic assertion, multiply (3) by \(n\).  The exact sensitivity condition and \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\) make
\[
n(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0,
\]
and lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, using \(4\alpha+4\beta<1\), gives \(nR_n\to0\).  Therefore
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n(V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\Delta_Z>0.
\]
The argument uses the two explicitly stated conditional hypotheses, optimizer containment and the bounded linear-error constants; it does not infer either one from the design primitives.  \(\square\)
