\textbf{Proof.}
By ass:kappa-feasibility, \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is nonempty.  By prop:kappa-exhaustion, it is compact and is contained in \(\mathcal B_Z\).  Hence every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\) has
\[
S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2\succ0.
\]
In particular, the inverse in def:gain-loss exists throughout \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), and
\[
\lVert S_B^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le (n\kappa)^{-1}.
\]
Ass:exposure-positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  On normalized representatives, the maps
\[
B\longmapsto B^{\top}\Omega B,
\qquad
B\longmapsto B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c)\Pi^{-1}\Omega B
\]
are continuous for each fixed endpoint \(c\in\{c_L,c_U,0\}\).  Matrix inversion is continuous on the closed cone \(\{S:S\succeq n\kappa I_2\}\).  Therefore def:gain-loss makes \(B\mapsto g_B(c)\) continuous at the two endpoints and at zero.  Equivalently, lem:ht-affine-drift writes the endpoint residual criterion as
\[
w(B)=h(0)+\max\left\{\frac{2c_L}{n}-g_B(c_L),
                         \frac{2c_U}{n}-g_B(c_U)\right\},
\]
so \(B\mapsto w(B)\) is continuous as the maximum of two continuous functions.  These functions are invariant under the independent nonzero column rescalings defining the projective quotient, so the preceding continuity statements descend from normalized representatives to \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).

The extreme-value theorem now shows that both \(w\) and \(g_B(0)\) attain their extrema on the nonempty compact set \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  Thus, by def:argmax-correspondences,
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\ne\varnothing,
\qquad
\mathcal A_0\ne\varnothing.
\]
For every \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), def:robust-objective and def:argmax-correspondences give
\[
w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\]
Moreover, every \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\) is an element of \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), so the defining minimum gives
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_0).
\]
Taking the infimum over the nonempty set \(\mathcal A_0\) proves
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le \inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0}w(B_0).
\]

It remains to verify that strictness is not forced by the assumptions.  Fix any design satisfying ass:exposure-positivity and ass:kappa-feasibility, and take \(F_1=F_0=\delta_0\).  Then \(\mu_1=\mu_0=\sigma_1^2=\sigma_0^2=0\), while \(c_L=c_U=0\).  Def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(0)=0\).  Hence def:gain-loss gives, for every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\),
\[
h(0)=g_B(0)=\ell_B(0)=w(B)=0.
\]
Thus \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\mathcal A_0=\mathcal B_\kappa\), and the asserted inequality is equality.  Therefore a separate lawful witness is necessary for strict dominance. \(\square\)
