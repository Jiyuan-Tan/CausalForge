\textbf{Proof.}
The hypotheses are exactly those of lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity, and \(\Delta(\xi_0)>0\).  If no relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) had positive gap throughout, metrizability of the total-variation--Wasserstein product topology would provide a sequence \(\xi_j\in\mathfrak X_\kappa\) with \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) and \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le0\) for every \(j\).  The lemma would then imply
\[
0\ge\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)\ge\Delta(\xi_0)>0,
\]
a contradiction.  Therefore a relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\subset\mathfrak X_\kappa\) with \(\Delta(\xi)>0\) for every \(\xi\in\mathcal U\) exists. \(\square\)
