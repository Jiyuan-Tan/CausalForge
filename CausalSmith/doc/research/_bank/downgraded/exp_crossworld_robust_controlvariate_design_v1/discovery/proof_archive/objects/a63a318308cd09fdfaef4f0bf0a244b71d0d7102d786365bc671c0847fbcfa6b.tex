\textbf{Proof.}
For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), the empirical endpoint matrix is the second-moment matrix generated by the empirical comonotone or antimonotone coupling, so
\[
\widehat Q_e\succeq0.
\tag{1}
\]
Every coefficient entering the encoded input is rational or real algebraic, and exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  Thus
\[
\widehat w_m^+(B)=
\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
\left\{\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}
-\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\}
\widehat Q_e\right]
\tag{2}
\]
has exactly the rank-stratified semialgebraic form in \(\texttt{thm:zhao-pseudoinverse-rank-stratified-epsilon-solver}\).  Applying that theorem to the empirical endpoint matrices proves finite termination and returns an algebraic basis and value-isolation record satisfying
\[
\widehat L_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}
\le\widehat v_m^+
\le\widehat U_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+},
\quad
\widehat U_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}
-\widehat L_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}
\le\varepsilon_m,
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+})
\le\widehat v_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{4}
\]

It remains to justify the augmented rank and spectral fields.  The entries of \(\widehat S_m^{\mathrm{CAD}}\) are real algebraic and the matrix is positive semidefinite.  For a positive-semidefinite \(2\times2\) matrix \(S\),
\[
\operatorname{rank}(S)=
\begin{cases}
0,&\operatorname{tr}(S)=0,\\
1,&\operatorname{tr}(S)>0\text{ and }\det(S)=0,\\
2,&\det(S)>0.
\end{cases}
\tag{5}
\]
Indeed, its eigenvalues are nonnegative, their sum is its trace, and their product is its determinant.  Exact ordered real-algebraic arithmetic decides all signs and equality cases in (5).  The eigenvalues are the roots of \(\chi_m\).  Exact algebraic arithmetic determines multiplicities and equality with zero.  For each positive distinct root, rational bisection using exact algebraic comparisons terminates with an interval contained in \((0,\infty)\) that contains that root and no other distinct root.  This produces the claimed finite spectral record.  The proof uses the finite algebraic encoding essentially and never evaluates the countable selector. \(\square\)
