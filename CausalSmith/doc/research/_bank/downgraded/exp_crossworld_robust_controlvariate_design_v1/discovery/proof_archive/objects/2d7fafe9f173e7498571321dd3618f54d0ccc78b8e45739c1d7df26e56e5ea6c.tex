\textbf{Proof.}
The triangle certificate gives \(\Delta(\xi_0)=\delta>0\) and optimizer-wide slack \(\eta>0\).  By thm:relative-open-persistence there is a relative-open neighborhood with positive gap.  To retain half the exact margin, argue by contradiction: if every neighborhood contained \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) with \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le\delta/2\), lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity would give
\[
 \delta=\Delta(\xi_0)\le\liminf_j\Delta(\xi_j)\le\delta/2,
\]
impossible.  Hence some relative-open \(\mathcal U\) satisfies \(\Delta(\xi)>\delta/2\).  For any indicated optimizers,
\[
 w_\xi(B_0)-w_\xi(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
 \ge\inf_{C_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)}w_\xi(C_0)
 -\sup_{C\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)}w_\xi(C)
 =\Delta(\xi)>\delta/2,
\]
which proves the strict comparison. \(\square\)
