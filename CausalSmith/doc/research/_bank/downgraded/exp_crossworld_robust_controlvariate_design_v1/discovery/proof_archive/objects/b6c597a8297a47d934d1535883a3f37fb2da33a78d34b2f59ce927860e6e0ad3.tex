\textbf{Proof.}
For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), congruence and exposure positivity give
\[
A_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\succeq0.
\]
With \(Z_B=\Omega^{1/2}B\), cyclicity of trace gives
\[
n^2g_B^+(c_e)
=\operatorname{tr}\{Z_B(Z_B^\top Z_B)^+Z_B^\top A_e\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The singular-value decomposition shows that \(P_B=Z_B(Z_B^\top Z_B)^+Z_B^\top\) is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(Z_B)\), with rank \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\).  The rank-two assertion follows from lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification.

For a singular representative \(B^\circ\), choose any rank-two representative \(C^\circ\) and put \(B_t^\circ=B^\circ+tC^\circ\).  The determinant of \((Z_{B^\circ}+tZ_{C^\circ})^\top(Z_{B^\circ}+tZ_{C^\circ})\) is a nonzero polynomial, so there is \(t_j\downarrow0\) avoiding its finitely many roots.  Normalize the two nonzero columns and call the resulting ordinary classes \(B_j\).  A convergent subsequence of their rank-two projectors has limit \(P_\infty\) whose range contains \(\operatorname{range}(Z_{B^\circ})\); hence \(P_\infty-P_B\succeq0\).  Since \(A_e\succeq0\), (1) yields
\[
\lim_jg_{B_j}(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_\infty A_e)
\ge g_B^+(c_e)
\quad(e=L,U).
\]
Subtracting from the basis-independent uncontrolled terms and taking the endpoint maximum proves \(\limsup_jw(B_j)\le w^+(B)\). \(\square\)
