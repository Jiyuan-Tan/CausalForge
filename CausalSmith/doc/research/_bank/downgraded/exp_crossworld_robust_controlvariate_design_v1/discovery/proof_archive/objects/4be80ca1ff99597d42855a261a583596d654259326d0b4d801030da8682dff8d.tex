\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(K(\xi)=\mathcal B_\kappa(\xi)\).  On the finite assignment space, total-variation convergence \(\nu_j\to\nu\) implies entrywise convergence of \(\Pi_j\) and \(\Omega_j\).  At an exposure-positive instance the diagonal entries of \(\Pi\) are positive, so \(\Pi_j^{-1}\to\Pi^{-1}\).  If \(F_{a,j}\to F_a\) in Wasserstein distance on \([0,1]\), the quantile representation of that distance gives
\[
\int_0^1|F_{a,j}^{-1}(u)-F_a^{-1}(u)|\,du\longrightarrow0.
\tag{1}
\]
All quantiles lie in \([0,1]\), whence
\[
\left|\int_0^1q_{1,j}(u)q_{0,j}(u)\,du-
\int_0^1q_1(u)q_0(u)\,du\right|
\le\lVert q_{1,j}-q_1\rVert_1+\lVert q_{0,j}-q_0\rVert_1,
\tag{2}
\]
with the same bound for the reflected second quantile.  Marginal first and second moments also converge.  Thus \(c_L,c_U,Q(c_L),Q(c_U)\) vary continuously with \(\xi\).

On the product of normalized spheres, both
\[
(\xi,B)\longmapsto\lambda_{\min}\{n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega(\xi)B\}
\tag{3}
\]
and the endpoint formulas are continuous.  On the superlevel set in (3), \(\lVert S_B^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\kappa)^{-1}\).  It follows that
\[
(\xi,B)\longmapsto g_{B,\xi}(0),
\qquad
(\xi,B)\longmapsto w_\xi(B)
\tag{4}
\]
are jointly continuous on the feasible graph.

We next prove upper stability of both optimizer correspondences.  Let \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\), let \(B_j\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)\), and suppose \(B_j\to B\) along a subsequence in the compact normalized quotient.  Closedness of (3) gives \(B\in K(\xi_0)\).  Choose \(B^\circ\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\).  The slack hypothesis gives
\[
\lambda_{\min}\{n^{-1}S_{B^\circ}(\xi_0)\}\ge\kappa+\eta,
\]
so continuity makes the fixed basis \(B^\circ\) feasible at every sufficiently large \(j\).  Optimality and (4) give
\[
w_{\xi_j}(B_j)\le w_{\xi_j}(B^\circ)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
w_{\xi_0}(B)\le w_{\xi_0}(B^\circ)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0).
\]
Feasibility supplies the reverse inequality, hence \(B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\).  The same argument, with the inequality reversed and with \(g(0)\) in place of \(w\), proves upper stability of \(\mathcal A_0\).  The optimizer-wide slack is used exactly to keep the fixed comparison optimizer feasible under perturbation.

The optimizer sets are nonempty and compact.  For each \(j\), select
\[
B_{0,j}\in\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)}w_{\xi_j}(B),
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{mm},j}\in\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)}w_{\xi_j}(B).
\]
Every subsequence has a further subsequence on which both bases converge.  Upper stability and (4) put the limits in the corresponding optimizer sets at \(\xi_0\).  Therefore
\[
\liminf_{j\to\infty}\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)}w_{\xi_j}(B)
\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)}w_{\xi_0}(B)
\tag{5}
\]
and
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\sup_{B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)}w_{\xi_j}(B)
\le\sup_{B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)}w_{\xi_0}(B).
\tag{6}
\]
Subtracting (6) from (5) gives \(\liminf_j\Delta(\xi_j)\ge\Delta(\xi_0)>0\).  If no relative-open neighborhood with positive gap existed, metrizability of the stated product topology would give a sequence \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) with \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le0\), a contradiction. \(\square\)
