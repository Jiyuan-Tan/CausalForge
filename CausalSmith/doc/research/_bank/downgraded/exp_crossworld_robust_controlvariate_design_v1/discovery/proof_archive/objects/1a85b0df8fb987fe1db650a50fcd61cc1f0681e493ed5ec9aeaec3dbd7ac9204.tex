\textbf{Proof.}
From \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\), def:ordinary-intercept-slope-image, and cyclicity of trace,
\[
g_B(c)=a_B+b_Bc.
\tag{1}
\]
Lemma lem:ht-affine-drift gives \(h(c)=h(0)+2c/n\), and hence
\[
\ell_B(c)=h(0)-a_B+(2/n-b_B)c.
\tag{2}
\]
Taking the maximum of (2) at \(c_L,c_U\) proves
\[
w(B)=h(0)-a_B+p_\xi(b_B).
\tag{3}
\]

Under the assumed attainments, put
\[
M=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}\{a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\}.
\]
Equation (3) gives
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)=h(0)-M.
\tag{4}
\]
On the zero-covariance optimizer face \(a_B=a^\star\), the same equation gives
\[
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B_0)
=h(0)-a^\star+inf_{B:\,a_B=a^\star}p_\xi(b_B).
\tag{5}
\]
Subtracting (4) from (5) proves the displayed formula for \(\Delta_Z\).  Because \(M\) is attained, \(\Delta_Z>0\) is equivalent to the existence of \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\) such that
\[
a_B-p_\xi(b_B)
>a^\star-\inf_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C),
\]
which rearranges exactly to
\[
\inf_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C)-p_\xi(b_B)
>a^\star-a_B.
\]
Finally, thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer makes all criteria constant on the fibers of \(q_Z\), so it preserves the values and pulls every attained optimizer correspondence back to its exact inverse image in \(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\).  No step invokes singular ranks, a minimax interchange, or a semidefinite reduction.  \(\square\)
