Suppose ass:exposure-positivity holds.  Let \((C_k)_{k\ge1}\) be the fixed enumeration of normalized rational-support classes of maximal achievable rank \(r_\Omega\) specified in def:pilot-plugin.  Under ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot, this enumeration is nonempty and, for every realized pilot sample,
\[
 \inf_{k\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
 =\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B).
\]
For every finite strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\), the index
\[
 \widehat k_m=
 \min\left\{k\ge1:
 \widehat w_m^+(C_k)
 \le \inf_{j\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_j)+\varepsilon_m
 \right\}
\]
is finite and pilot-measurable.  Hence \(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+=C_{\widehat k_m}\) is a measurable class of rank \(r_\Omega\) in the note's quotient of Zhao's published carrier and satisfies
\[
 \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
 \le
 \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\]
The selector is rank two exactly when rank two is achievable.  No full-carrier minimizer or zero-tolerance selector is used.
