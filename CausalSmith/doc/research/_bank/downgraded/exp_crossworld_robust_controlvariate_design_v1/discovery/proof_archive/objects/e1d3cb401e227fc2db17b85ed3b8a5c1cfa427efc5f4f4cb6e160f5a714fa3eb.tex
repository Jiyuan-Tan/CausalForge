\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\mathbf1=\mathbf1_3\).  Simultaneous permutation decomposition into \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1\}\) and \(\mathbf1^\perp\) reduces \(729\Omega\) to
\[
 M_\parallel=\begin{bmatrix}60&96\\96&186\end{bmatrix},
 \qquad M_\perp=\begin{bmatrix}108&-108\\-108&162\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Their leading entries are positive and their determinants are \(1944\) and \(5832\), so \(\Omega\succ0\).  Therefore rank zero means both arm columns vanish, while rank one means exactly one arm column is nonzero.

On the focal-only stratum normalize \(\lVert x\rVert_2^2=3\) and set \(u=(\mathbf1^{\top}x)^2\in[0,9]\).  The scalar Rayleigh quotient from lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives
\[
 g_x^+(c_L)=\frac{1195u+6561}{1200(81-4u)},
 \qquad g_x^+(c_U)=\frac{243(49-5u)}{400(81-4u)}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 g_x^+(0)=\frac{21141-1225u}{1200(81-4u)},
 \qquad \frac{d}{du}g_x^+(0)=-\frac{4887}{400(4u-81)^2}<0.
\]
Using \(h(c_L)=313/600\) and \(h(c_U)=171/200\), the upper endpoint is always the worse loss and
\[
 \ell_{x,U}=\frac{9(1755-17u)}{400(81-4u)},
 \qquad \frac{d}{du}\ell_{x,U}=\frac{50787}{400(4u-81)^2}>0.
\]
Thus the focal-only maximum zero gain is \(87/400\), and its minimum worst loss is \(39/80\), both at \(u=0\).

On the comparison-only stratum, normalize \(\lVert y\rVert_2^2=3\) and use \(u=(\mathbf1^{\top}y)^2\in[0,9]\).  Direct substitution gives
\[
 g_y^+(c_L)=\frac{1240u+729}{150(4u+243)},
 \qquad g_y^+(c_U)=\frac{81(48-5u)}{50(4u+243)},
\]
and hence
\[
 g_y^+(0)=\frac{25u+12393}{300(4u+243)},
 \qquad \frac{d}{du}g_y^+(0)=-\frac{14499}{100(4u+243)^2}<0,
\]
while the upper endpoint is again worse and
\[
 \ell_{y,U}=\frac{9(256u+2889)}{200(4u+243)},
 \qquad \frac{d}{du}\ell_{y,U}=\frac{113967}{50(4u+243)^2}>0.
\]
Thus the comparison-only extrema are \(17/100\) and \(107/200\).  Rank zero has zero gain and worst loss \(171/200\).  Comparing all three singular cases yields
\[
 \sup_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}g_B^+(0)=\frac{87}{400}<\frac{31}{80},
 \qquad
 \inf_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}w^+(B)=\frac{39}{80}>\frac{2613}{8432}.
\]
Every denominator is positive on \([0,9]\), so all boundary cases are included. \(\square\)
