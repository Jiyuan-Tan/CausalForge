Fix \(G,\nu,(f_i)_{i=1}^n\) satisfying ass:exposure-positivity.  Under ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot, let \(\widehat w_m^+(B)\) and, for \(\varepsilon_m>0\), \(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+\) be the distinct full-class empirical objective and selector in def:pilot-plugin, and put
\[
d_m^+=\sup_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}|\widehat w_m^+(B)-w^+(B)|.
\]
As \(m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty\), \(d_m^+\to0\) almost surely and
\[
\left|\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\right|
\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
For every positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\),
\[
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\]
Consequently, if \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely, then
\[
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
The domain is the note's projective quotient of Zhao's published unquotiented all-block-separable pseudoinverse domain.  No exact selector at zero tolerance, cross-rank outer-argmin convergence, or point convergence is asserted without attainment and rank-separation conditions.
