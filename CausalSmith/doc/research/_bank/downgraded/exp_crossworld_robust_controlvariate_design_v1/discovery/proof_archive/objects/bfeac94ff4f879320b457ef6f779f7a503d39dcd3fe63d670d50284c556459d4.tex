For \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), let \(S_B^+\) be the Moore--Penrose inverse of \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\), exactly Zhao's EC.2 inverse convention.  Define
\[
g_B^+(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[S_B^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c)\Pi^{-1}\Omega B\right],
\qquad \ell_B^+(c)=h(c)-g_B^+(c),
\]
\[
w^+(B)=\max\{\ell_B^+(c_L),\ell_B^+(c_U)\},
\qquad v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B),
\]
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\},
\qquad \mathcal A_0^+=\operatorname*{argmax}_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}g_B^+(0).
\]
The pseudoinverse gain formula and its full block-separable domain are Zhao's published objects.  The Fr\'echet-endpoint worst loss, its infimum, and the equality sets are this note's dependence-robust decision objects.  The set \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) may be empty because the Moore--Penrose loss need not be lower semicontinuous at a rank drop; no full-class minimizer is built into this definition.
