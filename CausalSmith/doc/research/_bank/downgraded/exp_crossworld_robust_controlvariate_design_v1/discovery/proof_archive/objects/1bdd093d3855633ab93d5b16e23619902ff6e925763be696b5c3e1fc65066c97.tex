\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
By lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence, ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot imply
\[
M_m\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely as }m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]
For the fixed design, ass:exposure-positivity gives
\[
\pi_{\min,n}=\min_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}\pi_{i,a}>0.
\]
The fixed-design all-rank estimate in lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate therefore gives
\[
d_m^+
\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{2}
\]
This bound covers ranks zero, one, and two and hence all of \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).

For arbitrary real-valued functions \(f\) and \(g\) on a common nonempty domain,
\[
\left|\inf f-\inf g\right|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{3}
\]
Indeed, \(f\le g+\sup|f-g|\) gives one direction after taking infima, and interchanging \(f\) and \(g\) gives the other.  Apply (3) to \(\widehat w_m^+\) and \(w^+\).  Since def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss defines \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) as the latter infimum,
\[
\left|
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\right|
\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{4}
\]

The condition \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\) permits the normalized rank-two enumeration in def:pilot-plugin.  By lem:measurable-positive-tolerance-full-carrier-selector, for every strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\), the displayed first-qualifying-index rule is measurable and obeys
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{5}
\]
Combining (5) with the definition of \(d_m^+\) twice yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
   +\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Because \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is the infimum of \(w^+\) on the same domain,
\[
0\le
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{7}
\]
Equations (2) and (7) prove the final almost-sure convergence when \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely.  Neither the selector construction nor this argument assumes that the full-carrier infimum is attained, and no singular-rank feasible-estimator variance bridge is used. \(\square\)
