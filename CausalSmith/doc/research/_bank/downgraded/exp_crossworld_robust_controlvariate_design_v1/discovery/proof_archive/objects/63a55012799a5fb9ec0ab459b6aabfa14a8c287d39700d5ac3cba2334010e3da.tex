\textbf{Proof.}
If \(0<\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\), then \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_2}\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}\), proving the inequality between minima.  Proposition prop:kappa-exhaustion gives
\[
 \mathcal B_Z=\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa.
\]
Taking the infimum of \(w\) over this union proves the first value equality.  The quotient-transfer theorem makes \(q_Z\) surjective and \(w\circ q_Z\) constant on every fiber, proving equality with the infimum over \(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\).

If \(B_Z^\star\) attains the middle infimum, prop:kappa-exhaustion places it in \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_0}\) for some \(\kappa_0>0\).  For \(0<\kappa\le\kappa_0\), feasibility of \(B_Z^\star\) gives an upper bound by the full-domain infimum, while \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\) gives the reverse bound.  Equality follows. \(\square\)
