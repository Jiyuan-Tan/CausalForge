\textbf{Proof.}
For \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives the orthogonal projector
\[
 P_B=\Omega^{1/2}B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega^{1/2}.
\]
Thus \(0\preceq P_B\preceq I_{2n}\) for every one of the three rank patterns.  Congruence by \(\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\) gives
\[
 0\preceq
 A_B^+=\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}P_B\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}
 \preceq
 \Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}=A_{\mathrm{HT}}.
\tag{1}
\]
Both matrices in (1) are positive semidefinite, so their nuclear norms equal their traces and
\[
 \lVert A_B^+\rVert_*
 \le\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}})
 =\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{a\in\{1,0\}}
 \frac{1-\pi_{i,a}}{\pi_{i,a}}
 \le2n\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
The trace identity in (2) uses
\(
 \Omega_{(i,a),(i,a)}=\operatorname{Var}_\nu(D_{i,a})
 =\pi_{i,a}(1-\pi_{i,a})
\),
which follows directly from the Bernoulli exposure indicator.

For endpoint \(e\), trace duality gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\widehat\ell_B^+(c_e)-\ell_B^+(c_e)|
 &\le n^{-2}\bigl(
 \lVert A_{\mathrm{HT}}\rVert_*+\lVert A_B^+\rVert_*
 \bigr)
 \lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\\
 &\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
The scalar maximum is one-Lipschitz in the sup norm, so taking the supremum over the entire quotient in (3) proves the fixed-design bound.  Under ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate,
\(
 \pi_{\min,n}^{-1}\le\underline\pi^{-1}n^\beta
\),
which proves (A).

The defining positive-tolerance inequality for \(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+\) and the elementary sup-norm comparison of two infima give pointwise
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 0\le w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 &\le
 \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\\
 &\le
 \inf_{[B]_+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\\
 &\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
Taking pilot expectations in (4) and applying (A) proves (B).

By lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate,
\[
 \mathbb E_P[M_m]
 =O\!\left(\frac{n}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}\right).
\tag{5}
\]
Multiplication of (B) by \(n\), followed by (5), yields
\[
 n\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \right]
 =O\!\left(
 \frac{n^{1+\beta}}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}
 \right)+n\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_m],
\]
which converges to zero under the two displayed conditions.  No step maps a singular selected class to an ordinary-inverse observable estimator; the conclusion is solely full-carrier objective regret. \(\square\)
