\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
 M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
 \lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence}\), \(\texttt{ass:bounded-outcomes}\) and \(\texttt{ass:unpaired-pilot}\) imply
\[
 M_m\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely as }m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]
For the fixed design, \(\texttt{ass:exposure-positivity}\) gives
\[
 \pi_{\min,n}=\min_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}\pi_{i,a}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
The all-rank inequality in \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate}\), which applies simultaneously to the rank-zero, rank-one, and rank-two strata, now gives
\[
 d_m^+
 \le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m
 \longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{3}
\]

For arbitrary real-valued functions \(f\) and \(g\) on the same nonempty domain,
\[
 \left|\inf f-\inf g\right|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, \(f\le g+\sup|f-g|\) gives one inequality after taking infima, and interchanging \(f\) and \(g\) gives the other.  Applying (4) to \(\widehat w_m^+\) and \(w^+\) on \(\mathcal B_Z^+\), and using
\(
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
\), proves
\[
 \left|
 \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
 -v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \right|
 \le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{5}
\]

The condition \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\) permits the fixed positive-definite rank-two chart enumeration in \(\texttt{def:pilot-plugin}\).  The result \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector}\) proves both that the countable empirical infimum equals the empirical infimum over the complete all-rank carrier and that, for every finite strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\), the first qualifying index defines a pilot-measurable selector satisfying
\[
 \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
 \le
 \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (6) with the definition of \(d_m^+\) twice yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
   +\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is the infimum of \(w^+\) on the same domain,
\[
 0\le
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (3) and (8) prove the final almost-sure convergence whenever \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely.

The optimization and both suprema in this proof range over the note's full quotient \(\mathcal B_Z^+\), including all three rank strata.  The countable first-qualifying selector itself is rank two.  The argument uses only positive tolerances and infimum comparisons; it neither assumes full-carrier attainment nor supplies a zero-tolerance selector, cross-rank outer-argmin convergence, point convergence, or a feasible-estimator variance bridge on a singular rank. \(\square\)
