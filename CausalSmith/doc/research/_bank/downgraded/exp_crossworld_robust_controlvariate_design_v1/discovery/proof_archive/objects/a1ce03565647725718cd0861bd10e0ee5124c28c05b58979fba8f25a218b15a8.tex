For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), set \(A_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\) and \(Z_B=\Omega^{1/2}B\).  Every \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\) satisfies
\[
 g_B^+(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e),
 \qquad P_B=Z_B(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top},
\]
where \(P_B\) is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(Z_B)\), \(A_e\succeq0\), and \(\operatorname{rank}(P_B)=\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\in\{0,1,2\}\).  On rank two, \([B]_+=\iota([C])\) for a unique \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), and the gains and losses agree.  If \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\), then for every singular \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\) there is a sequence \([B_j]\in\mathcal B_Z\) such that
\[
 \limsup_{j\to\infty}w(B_j)\le w^+(B).
