\textbf{Proof.}
The bounded armwise strong law and the quantile \(L^1\) identity give
\[
\rho_m:=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{1}
\]
For every completion class and endpoint, lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives
\[
g_B^+(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e),
\qquad \operatorname{rank}(P_B)\le2.
\tag{2}
\]
If \(\widehat A_e\) is formed from \(\widehat Q_e\), then
\[
|\operatorname{tr}\{P_B(\widehat A_e-A_e)\}|
\le2\lVert\widehat A_e-A_e\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\le2\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}^2\rho_m.
\tag{3}
\]
The uncontrolled term satisfies
\[
|\widehat h_e-h_e|
\le n^{-2}\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_*\rho_m.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (3)--(4), subtraction, and the one-Lipschitz property of the endpoint maximum imply \(d_m^+\le K\rho_m\to0\), uniformly over ranks zero, one, and two.

For arbitrary functions on the same nonempty domain, the absolute difference of their infima is at most their sup-norm distance.  Hence
\[
\left|\inf_B\widehat w_m^+(B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\right|\le d_m^+.
\tag{5}
\]
For an empirical \(\varepsilon_m\)-minimizer,
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_m)
&\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_m)+d_m^+\\
&\le\inf_B\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (1), (5), and (6) prove value consistency and vanishing regret.  The proof uses neither cross-rank lower semicontinuity nor attainment, so it yields no outer-argmin or point-convergence claim. \(\square\)
