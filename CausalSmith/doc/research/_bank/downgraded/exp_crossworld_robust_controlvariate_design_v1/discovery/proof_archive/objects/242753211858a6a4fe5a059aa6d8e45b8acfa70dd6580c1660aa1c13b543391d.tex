Suppose ass:known-exposure, ass:exposure-positivity, ass:bounded-outcomes, ass:iid-potential-pairs, ass:unpaired-pilot, ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence, and ass:frechet-covariance-domain hold.  Fix \(\lambda_{1,n}>0\), \(\lambda_{2,n}>0\), and \(\overline a\ge1\).  Let \(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{st},+}\in\mathcal K_n^{\mathrm{st}}\) be the classical pilot-measurable positive-tolerance selector from def:pilot-plugin, where \(\mathbb E_P\varepsilon_m<\infty\), and put
\[
d_m^+=\sup_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}
\left|\widehat w_m^+(B)-w^+(B)\right|.
\]
Define its observable worst-copula ex ante conditional design-variance performance by
\[
V_{\mathrm{st}}
=\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu\!\left(
\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{st},+}}^{\mathrm{CV}}
\mid \widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{st},+},Y
\right)
\right].
\]
With the rank-zero bridge remainder defined to be zero and with \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\) from thm:rank-adaptive-oracle-observable-variance-bridge for \(r\in\{1,2\}\),
\[
\left|V_{\mathrm{st}}-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\right|
\le b_n^{\mathrm{st}}
+2\mathbb E_Pd_m^+
+\mathbb E_P\varepsilon_m
+\max_{r\in\{1,2\}}R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}.
\]
The same conclusion holds for the exact-input stable CAD selector certified by thm:encoded-cad-stable-pilot-selector-certificate.  Consequently,
\[
n\left\{
b_n^{\mathrm{st}}+\mathbb E_Pd_m^+
+\mathbb E_P\varepsilon_m
+\max_{r\in\{1,2\}}R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}
\right\}\longrightarrow0
\]
implies \(n|V_{\mathrm{st}}-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+|\to0\).  Under ass:zhao-dependency-neighborhood-rate, ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate, and ass:zhao-first-order-rate, the last bridge term is \(o(n^{-1})\) when \(\lambda_{1,n}\) and \(\lambda_{2,n}\) are bounded below by fixed positive constants and \(\overline a\) is fixed.  No rate or zero value for \(b_n^{\mathrm{st}}\) is inferred: it is the exact approximation-versus-stability price.
