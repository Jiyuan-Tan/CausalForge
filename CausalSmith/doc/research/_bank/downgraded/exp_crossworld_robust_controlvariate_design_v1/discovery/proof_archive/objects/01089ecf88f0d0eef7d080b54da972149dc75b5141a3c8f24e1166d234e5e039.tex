\(\mathcal B_Z^+\) is Zhao's published block-separable Moore--Penrose domain modulo its exact scale invariance.  Concretely,
\[
 \mathcal B_Z^+=
 \left\{[B]_+:B=\begin{bmatrix}b_1&0\\0&b_0\end{bmatrix},\quad
 b_a\in\{0\}\cup\{b\in\mathbb R^n:\lVert b\rVert_2^2=n\}\right\}.
\]
Here \([B]_+\) identifies independent nonzero rescalings of every nonzero column and keeps a zero column in the zero stratum.  Thus every unquotiented published basis has a normalized representative, and the domain is stratified by \(\operatorname{rank}(B^{\top}\Omega B)\in\{0,1,2\}\).
