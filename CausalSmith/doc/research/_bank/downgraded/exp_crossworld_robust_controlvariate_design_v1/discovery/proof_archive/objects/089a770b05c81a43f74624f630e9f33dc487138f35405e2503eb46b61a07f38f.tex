Suppose ass:known-exposure, ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility, ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate, ass:zhao-dependency-neighborhood-rate, ass:zhao-first-order-rate, ass:bounded-outcomes, and ass:iid-potential-pairs hold.  Uniformly over the nonempty class \(B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), let
\[
\gamma_B^*(Y)=(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y
\]
and let \(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or}}\) be the control-variate estimator using this fixed-array oracle coefficient.  There are finite constants \(K_\Delta,K_{\mathrm{cov}},K_R\), independent of \(n\), \(B\), the bounded array \(Y\), and \(H\), such that the deterministic finite-\(n\) envelope
\[
R_n=K_\Delta n^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}
+2K_{\mathrm{cov}}n^{(7/2)\alpha+3\beta-2},
\qquad K_R=K_\Delta+2K_{\mathrm{cov}},
\]
satisfies, for every \(n\ge3\),
\[
0\le R_n\le K_Rn^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}
\]
and the two uniform absolute bounds
\[
\sup_{\substack{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\\0\le Y_i(a)\le1}}
\left|
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid Y)
-\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or}}\mid Y)
\right|
\le R_n,
\]
\[
\sup_{\substack{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\\H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}}
\left|
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid Y)
-\ell_B(c_H)
\right|
\le R_n.
\]
The oracle ex ante identity is exact:
\[
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or}}\mid Y)
=\ell_B(c_H),
\qquad
c_H=\operatorname{Cov}_H\{Y_i(1),Y_i(0)\}.
\]
Since \(4\alpha+4\beta<1\), one has \(nR_n\to0\); hence the feasible and oracle conditional design variances differ by \(o(n^{-1})\), and the feasible ex ante variance equals \(\ell_B(c_H)+o(n^{-1})\), uniformly over the rate-admissible class.  The expectations over \(H^{\otimes n}\) are ex ante basis-performance averages, not additional randomness in the conditional design-based estimand.  The pseudoinverse coefficient equals the ordinary inverse on this class; no uniform coefficient-rate assertion is made on singular ranks.
