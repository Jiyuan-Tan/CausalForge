Rare network exposures make Horvitz--Thompson estimation noisy, while Zhao's ex ante control-variate basis depends on a cross-world covariance that an unpaired pilot does not identify.  The paper imports the sharp Fr\'echet covariance interval, proves that every fixed-basis gain and residual are affine in that covariance, and reduces worst-copula performance to two endpoint evaluations.  It then gives certified finite-\(\varepsilon\) optimization both on the compact fixed-\(\kappa\) positive-definite rank-two class and, by rank-stratified cylindrical algebraic decomposition, on the note's projective quotient of Zhao's published all-block-separable pseudoinverse carrier.  The fixed-threshold minimax residual weakly dominates the zero-covariance surrogate, and an exact triangle witness strengthens this to a strict reduction of \(4912/39525\), persistent on a relative-open lawful neighborhood; strictness is not universal.  Independent unpaired pilots consistently recover the robust values, with outer argmin consistency only on the compact rank-two class and epsilon-regret consistency on the full carrier.  The Jiangxi PICC reanalysis remains an explicit, reproducible, unexecuted heuristic protocol with no asserted numerical results.
