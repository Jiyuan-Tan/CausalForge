\(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z=\{B:B=\begin{bmatrix}b_1&0\\0&b_0\end{bmatrix},\ b_1,b_0\in\mathbb R^n,\ B^{\top}\Omega B\succ0\}\).  This is the unquotiented positive-definite rank-two restriction of Zhao's published pseudoinverse basis class; on it the published Moore--Penrose inverse equals the ordinary inverse.  Define \(q_Z:\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\to\mathcal B_Z\) by \(q_Z(B)=[B]\).  This restriction is not the full domain of Zhao's Equation (8), whose full domain is def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain.
