\textbf{Proof.}
By ass:kappa-feasibility and prop:kappa-exhaustion, \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is nonempty and compact. Exposure positivity and \(\kappa\)-regularity justify every inverse in def:gain-loss. Matrix inversion is continuous on \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\), so \(B\mapsto w(B)\) and \(B\mapsto g_B(0)\) are continuous. Hence both optimizer sets are nonempty. By def:robust-objective and def:argmax-correspondences, every \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) satisfies \(w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\), whereas every \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\subseteq\mathcal B_\kappa\) satisfies \(w(B_0)\ge v_{\mathrm{mm}}\). Therefore
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0}w(B_0).
\]
No strict inequality follows from minimax optimality alone. \(\square\)
