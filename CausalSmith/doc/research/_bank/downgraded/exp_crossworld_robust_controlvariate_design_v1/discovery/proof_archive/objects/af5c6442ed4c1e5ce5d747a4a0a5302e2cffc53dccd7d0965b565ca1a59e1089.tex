\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
 K_\kappa=\left\{(b_1,b_0):
 \lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
 \ \lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}B(b_1,b_0)^{\top}\Omega B(b_1,b_0)\right)\ge\kappa
 \right\}.
\]
The product of the two radius-\(\sqrt n\) spheres is compact.  The displayed matrix is continuous in \((b_1,b_0)\), as is its smallest eigenvalue.  Hence \(K_\kappa\) is closed and compact.  Independent sign changes preserve every defining condition and are the residual identifications after normalization, so the continuous quotient image \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is compact.

If \([B]\in\mathcal B_\kappa\), then
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(B^{\top}\Omega B)\ge n\kappa>0.
\]
Thus \(S_B\) is positive definite, and the updated def:zhao-basis-class, equivalently the rank-two stratum of def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain, gives \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\).

Conversely, take \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\).  Positive definiteness of \(S_B\) forces both basis columns to be nonzero.  Put
\[
 d_a=\frac{\sqrt n}{\lVert b_a\rVert_2},
 \qquad T=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,d_0),
 \qquad \widetilde B=BT.
\]
Then \(\widetilde B\) is a normalized representative of the same projective class and
\[
 S_{\widetilde B}=T^{\top}S_BT\succ0.
\]
Consequently \(\rho_B=\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_{\widetilde B})>0\).  Taking, for example, \(\kappa_B=\rho_B/2\) proves \([B]\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_B}\).  Hence
\[
 \bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa=\mathcal B_Z.
\]
Every step starts from the rank-two condition \(S_B\succ0\).  The union therefore exhausts precisely the nonsingular ordinary-inverse projective stratum; the rank-zero and rank-one Moore--Penrose strata in def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain remain outside every fixed \(\mathcal B_\kappa\). \(\square\)
