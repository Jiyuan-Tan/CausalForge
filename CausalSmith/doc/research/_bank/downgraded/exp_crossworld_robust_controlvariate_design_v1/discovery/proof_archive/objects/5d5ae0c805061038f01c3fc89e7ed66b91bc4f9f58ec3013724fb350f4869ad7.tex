\textbf{Proof.}
The bounded armwise strong law and the quantile \(L^1\) identity used in thm:plugin-correspondence give
\[
\rho_m:=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{1}
\]
For every published class and endpoint, lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives
\[
g_B^+(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e),
\qquad \operatorname{rank}(P_B)\le2.
\]
If \(\widehat A_e\) is formed from \(\widehat Q_e\), then
\[
|\operatorname{tr}\{P_B(\widehat A_e-A_e)\}|
\le2\lVert\widehat A_e-A_e\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\le2\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}^2\rho_m.
\tag{2}
\]
The uncontrolled term obeys
\[
|\widehat h_e-h_e|
\le n^{-2}\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_*\rho_m.
\tag{3}
\]
Equations (2)--(3), subtraction, and the one-Lipschitz maximum imply \(d_m^+\le K\rho_m\to0\) for a finite fixed-design constant \(K\), uniformly over every rank.

For arbitrary functions on the same nonempty set, the absolute difference of their infima is at most their sup-norm distance.  This proves value consistency.  If \(\widehat B_m\) is an empirical epsilon-minimizer, then
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_m)
&\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_m)+d_m^+\\
&\le\inf_B\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\]
This argument uses neither attainment of \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) nor continuity across rank changes, which is why it yields value and regret but no outer-argmin claim. \(\square\)
