\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
 M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
 \lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
Lemma lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence proves from ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot that
\[
 M_m\longrightarrow0\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{1}
\]
Exposure positivity gives the fixed constant
\(
 \pi_{\min,n}=\min_{i,a}\pi_{i,a}>0
\).
The rank-uniform projector order and trace calculation in lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate therefore give
\[
 d_m^+le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m
 \longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{2}
\]
This bound covers ranks zero, one, and two and hence the entire quotient \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).

For arbitrary real functions \(f\) and \(g\) on the same nonempty domain,
\[
 \left|\inf f-\inf g\right|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{3}
\]
Indeed, \(f\le g+\sup|f-g|\) implies the upper inequality for the infima, and interchanging \(f\) and \(g\) gives the reverse inequality.  Applying (3) to \(\widehat w_m^+\) and \(w^+\), and recalling that \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\inf w^+\), yields
\[
 \left|
 \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \right|\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{4}
\]

By the defining positive-tolerance inequality in def:pilot-plugin,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
 &\le \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
 &\le \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
       +\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
 &\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]
Since \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is an infimum over the same domain, the left-hand regret in (5) is nonnegative.  Thus (2) and \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely imply
\[
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]

The quantitative strengthening is exposed separately and load-bearingly in lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate: under ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate it gives
\[
 d_m^+\le4\underline\pi^{-1}n^{\beta-1}M_m,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P\!left[
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \right]
 \le8\underline\pi^{-1}n^{\beta-1}\mathbb E_P[M_m]
 +\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_m],
\]
and the first-order objective-regret conclusion under
\(
 \sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}/n^{1+\beta}\to\infty
\)
and \(n\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_m]\to0\).  These conclusions require neither attainment of \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) nor continuity across rank changes.  They do not assert an exact zero-tolerance selector, outer-argmin convergence, point convergence, or a feasible-estimator variance bridge for singular ranks. \(\square\)
