\textbf{Proof.}
Use normalized representatives
\[
K_\kappa=\{(b_1,b_0):\lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
\ S_B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Ass:kappa-feasibility makes (1) nonempty, and it is compact.  Prop:kappa-exhaustion identifies its projective quotient with the fixed-threshold rank-two class.  Lem:algebraic-box-feasibility-cad decides exactly whether any rational signed pivot-chart box meets (1), including equality-only feasibility, and returns either an algebraic sample or a finite infeasibility certificate.

For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), set
\[
R_e=\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega,
\quad S(B)=B^\top\Omega B,
\quad T_e(B)=B^\top R_eB.
\]
The algebraic input supplies rational upper bounds \(M_\Omega\ge\lVert\Omega\rVert_2\) and \(M_e\ge\lVert R_e\rVert_2\).  If \(d=\lVert B-C\rVert_F\), normalized representatives obey
\[
\lVert S(B)-S(C)\rVert_2\le2\sqrt{2n}M_\Omega d,
\qquad
\lVert T_e(B)-T_e(C)\rVert_2\le2\sqrt{2n}M_ed.
\tag{2}
\]
On (1), \(\lVert S(B)^{-1}\rVert_2\le(n\kappa)^{-1}\).  The resolvent identity and (2) therefore give an effectively computable rational \(\overline L\) such that
\[
|w(B)-w(C)|\le\overline L\lVert B-C\rVert_F
\quad(B,C\in K_\kappa).
\tag{3}
\]

Choose a rational \(\rho>0\) with \(4\rho<\varepsilon\).  Uniformly bisect the finite signed pivot-chart cover until every retained box \(C\) has diameter \(d_C\le\varepsilon\) and \(\overline Ld_C\le\rho\).  This takes finitely many bisections.  CAD deletes exactly infeasible boxes and supplies an algebraic point \(B_C\) in each feasible box.  Algebraic isolation returns rationals \(\underline q_C,\overline q_C\) satisfying
\[
w(B_C)-\rho\le\underline q_C\le w(B_C)
\le\overline q_C\le w(B_C)+\rho.
\tag{4}
\]
Then
\[
\lambda_C=\underline q_C-\overline Ld_C
\le\inf_{B\in C\cap K_\kappa}w(B).
\tag{5}
\]
Put \(L_\varepsilon=\min_C\lambda_C\), \(U_\varepsilon=\min_C\overline q_C\), and take \(B_\varepsilon\) from a box attaining the second minimum.  A box containing a compact-class minimizer and (3)--(5) yield
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}-2\rho\le L_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le w(B_\varepsilon)\le U_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}+2\rho.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus \(U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon<\varepsilon\).  Prune a feasible box only if its certified lower bound exceeds \(U_\varepsilon\).  Equation (5) shows that no minimizer box is pruned.  The union \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\) of unpruned quotient boxes therefore contains \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), and the diameter refinement gives \(h_\varepsilon\le\varepsilon\).

The finite record consists of CAD signs, algebraic samples, outward rational objective bounds, chart-cover and diameter data, and pruning comparisons.  All are locally checkable.  Equality at the \(\kappa\)-boundary is retained by weak polynomial inequalities.  Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) in (6) and the mesh bound proves the final convergence.  The theorem is only the fixed-\(\kappa\), rank-two solver; it gives neither a uniform complexity bound nor an SDP representation. \(\square\)
