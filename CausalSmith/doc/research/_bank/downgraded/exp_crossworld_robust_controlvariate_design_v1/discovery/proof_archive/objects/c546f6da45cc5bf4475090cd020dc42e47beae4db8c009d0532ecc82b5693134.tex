Rare network exposures make Horvitz--Thompson estimation noisy, while an unpaired pilot identifies the two potential-outcome marginals but not their cross-world copula.  The primary robust design in this paper minimizes the worst endpoint residual \(w(B)\); the smaller endpoint gain \(r(B)\) is retained only as a secondary diagnostic.  The paper imports the sharp Fr\'echet covariance interval, derives affine fixed-basis endpoint reduction, gives an exact triangle separation from the zero-covariance surrogate, certifies finite-tolerance global optimization on the normalized \(\kappa\)-regular class, proves plug-in and feasible-estimator variance transfer, and supplies an explicitly unexecuted Jiangxi PICC protocol.  Rate-class deployment additionally requires the separately checkable coordinate-bounded \(\kappa\)-feasibility condition.
