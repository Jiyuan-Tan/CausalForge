There is a positive, fully explicit triangle witness whose strict separation survives every singular stratum of the novel Moore--Penrose completion.  Let \(n=3\), let
\[
G=\begin{bmatrix}0&1&1\\1&0&1\\1&1&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\nu(w)=\left(\frac13\right)^{\sum_iw_i}
       \left(\frac23\right)^{3-\sum_iw_i},
\]
and, writing \(\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}\), define
\[
f_i(w)=
\begin{cases}
1,&w_i=1\text{ and }w_j=w_k=0,\\
0,&w_i=0\text{ and }w_j+w_k\ge1,\\
\star,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Take \(F_1=F_0=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) and \(\kappa=1/8\).  With \(J_3=\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\top}\) and \(A=(I_3+J_3)/4\), the exact inputs are

\begin{bmatrix}
108I_3-16J_3&68J_3-108I_3\\
68J_3-108I_3&162I_3+8J_3

\[
c_L=-\frac14,
\qquad c_U=\frac14,
\qquad
Q(c_L)=\begin{bmatrix}A&(I_3-J_3)/4\\(I_3-J_3)/4&A\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
Q(c_U)=\begin{bmatrix}A&-A\\-A&A\end{bmatrix}.
\]
For normalized rank-two representatives \(B(x,y)\),
\[
\mathcal A_0=\{[B(x,y)]:\lVert x\rVert_2^2=\lVert y\rVert_2^2=3,
\ \mathbf1^{\top}x=\mathbf1^{\top}y=x^{\top}y=0\},

They are nonempty, lie in \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), and satisfy
\[
