\textbf{Proof.}
Assumption ass:kappa-feasibility makes \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) nonempty, and Proposition prop:kappa-exhaustion makes it compact.  Assumption ass:exposure-positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  For every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\), ass:kappa-regularity, as incorporated in def:kappa-basis-class, gives
\[
S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2,
\qquad
\lVert S_B^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le (n\kappa)^{-1}.
\]
Thus inversion is continuous on the full feasible class.  Matrix multiplication and trace are continuous, so the formulas in def:gain-loss imply that \(B\mapsto g_B(0)\), \(B\mapsto\ell_B(c_L)\), and \(B\mapsto\ell_B(c_U)\) are continuous on normalized representatives.  Each formula is invariant under the independent sign changes that remain after projective normalization, and therefore descends continuously to \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  The maximum of the two continuous endpoint losses is continuous, so \(B\mapsto w(B)\) is continuous as well.

By the extreme-value theorem, the minimum defining \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and the maximum defining \(\mathcal A_0\) are both attained.  Hence both correspondences are nonempty and compact.  For arbitrary \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\), the defining minimization in def:robust-objective gives
\[
w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_0).
\]
The left-hand side is constant over \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Taking the supremum over \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\), and then the infimum over \(B_0\), yields
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0}w(B_0).
\]

Strictness cannot follow from these assumptions.  To see this within the lawful class, fix any design satisfying ass:exposure-positivity and ass:kappa-feasibility and take \(F_1=F_0=\delta_0\).  Then \(\mu_1=\mu_0=\sigma_1^2=\sigma_0^2=0\) and \(c_L=c_U=0\), so def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(0)=0\).  Consequently def:gain-loss gives \(g_B(0)=\ell_B(0)=w(B)=0\) for every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\).  Thus equality holds in the displayed certificate for this lawful instance.  Any strict inequality therefore requires a separate lawful witness. \(\square\)
