\textbf{Proof.}
Fix the design.  Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  For each \(e\in\{L,U\}\), let \(U\) be uniform on \([0,1]\) and use the empirical quantile pair \((\widehat F_1^{-1}(U),\widehat F_0^{-1}(U))\) for \(e=U\) and \((\widehat F_1^{-1}(U),\widehat F_0^{-1}(1-U))\) for \(e=L\).  Taking \(n\) independent copies and applying the sign convention in \(Q\), the empirical endpoint matrix is a second-moment matrix.  Thus
\[
\widehat Q_e\succeq0,
\qquad
\widehat A_e
=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}\widehat Q_e\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}
\succeq0.
\tag{1}
\]
For every carrier class, the projector representation from \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\), applied to the empirical endpoint matrix, gives
\[
\widehat\ell_{B,e}^+
=\widehat h_e-n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_B\widehat A_e),
\qquad e\in\{L,U\},
\tag{2}
\]
where \(\widehat h_e=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Q_e)\) is independent of \(B\).

We first prove density in objective value on the maximal-rank stratum.  If \(r_\Omega=2\), positive definiteness of \(B^{\top}\Omega B\) is open.  Rational directions are dense in every signed-pivot chart, and normalization is continuous away from zero.  Hence rational-support rank-two classes exist, and every rank-two class is the limit of a sequence appearing in \((C_k)_{k\ge1}\).  On a fixed-rank stratum, \(P_B\) is continuous because it is the orthogonal projector onto a continuously varying full-rank column space.

If \(r_\Omega=1\), take any rank-one class.  At least one arm column, say \(b_a\), has nonzero image under \(\Omega^{1/2}\).  Replacing the other arm column by zero does not change the one-dimensional range projector.  Approximate \(b_a\) by nonzero rational vectors and normalize them.  Their images remain nonzero eventually; since \(r_\Omega=1\), their classes cannot have rank two.  The resulting one-dimensional projectors converge to the original projector, and these rational-support classes occur in the enumeration.  If \(r_\Omega=0\), every attainable projector is zero, and the repeated zero class prescribed by the definition is nonempty and has every carrier objective value.

Now fix \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\).  By \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\), there are maximal-rank classes \([B_j]_+\) and a projector \(P_\infty\) such that
\[
P_{B_j}\longrightarrow P_\infty,
\qquad P_\infty-P_B\succeq0.
\tag{3}
\]
Equations (1)--(3) and trace monotonicity imply, endpoint by endpoint,
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\widehat\ell_{B_j,e}^+
\le \widehat\ell_{B,e}^+,
\]
and therefore
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}\widehat w_m^+(B_j)
\le \widehat w_m^+(B).
\tag{4}
\]
Approximate each fixed \(B_j\) by the rational-support maximal-rank classes constructed above.  Continuity of the fixed-rank projector and (2) show that, for every \(\delta'>0\), some \(C_k\) satisfies
\[
\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
\le \widehat w_m^+(B)+\delta'.
\tag{5}
\]
The reverse inequality follows because every \(C_k\) belongs to \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).  Taking infima in (5) and letting \(\delta'\downarrow0\) proves
\[
\inf_{k\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_k)
=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B).
\tag{6}
\]

For measurability, put \(X_k=\widehat w_m^+(C_k)\).  Empirical means, variances, and the two empirical quantile-coupling cross moments are measurable functions of finitely many pilot order statistics.  Each \(C_k\), its rank, and its spectral pseudoinverse are fixed by the design.  Hence every \(X_k\) is pilot-measurable, as is
\[
\rho_m=\inf_{j\ge1}X_j.
\tag{7}
\]
Strict positivity of \(\varepsilon_m\) makes the qualifying set nonempty by the defining approximation property of an infimum.  Moreover,
\[
\{\widehat k_m=k\}
=\{X_k\le\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\}
\cap\bigcap_{j<k}\{X_j>\rho_m+\varepsilon_m\},
\tag{8}
\]
so \(\widehat k_m\) is finite and pilot-measurable.  Equations (6)--(8) prove the asserted positive-tolerance inequality, and the selected class has rank \(r_\Omega\) by construction.  Thus it has rank two exactly when rank two is achievable.

This argument uses the completed real number \(\rho_m\) and the set-theoretic approximation property of an infimum.  It supplies neither a finite stage at which \(\rho_m\) is evaluated nor a certificate that no later enumerated class improves it.  It therefore proves only the stated classical measurable existence rule, with no agreement claim for the separately defined exact-input cylindrical-algebraic selector. \(\square\)
