The paper claims neither a universal minimax interchange nor universal strict improvement.  Strict dominance is proved for the explicit triangle and a relative-open lawful neighborhood, with the full zero-surrogate and minimax correspondences quantified.  Point convergence of a selected plug-in basis requires a singleton population argmin; without it only outer-set consistency is claimed.  The full ordinary-inverse domain result is exact modulo nonzero arm-column rescaling.  Singular bases appear only in the note's separately named novel Moore--Penrose completion.  Sampling inference for the pilot-selected main-experiment estimator and a corrected numerical Jiangxi table remain outside the proved scope.
