\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
K_\kappa=\{(b_1,b_0):\lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
\ B^{\top}\Omega B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0\}.
\tag{1}
\]
It is nonempty by ass:kappa-feasibility and compact, and its finite sign quotient is \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  On (1),
\[
\lVert(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
The trace formulas, the resolvent identity, and (2) make both endpoint losses uniformly continuous.  The effective refinement moduli in \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\) therefore produce, for each rational \(\rho>0\), a rational diameter \(d(\rho)>0\) such that feasible points in one box of diameter at most \(d(\rho)\) have losses differing by at most \(\rho\).

Use the finite signed pivot-chart cover.  Choose \(\rho>0\) with \(4\rho<\varepsilon\), and bisect to the finite depth at which every retained box has diameter at most \(\min\{\varepsilon,d(\rho)\}\).  The exact feasibility interface decides every box, including equality-only boundary boxes.  In each feasible box \(C\), nested exact feasible-child selection provides an exactly feasible inclusion-oracle point \(B_C\), and outward arithmetic gives rationals \(\underline q_C,\overline q_C\) with
\[
w(B_C)-\rho\le\underline q_C\le w(B_C)
\le\overline q_C\le w(B_C)+\rho.
\tag{3}
\]
Uniform continuity makes
\[
\lambda_C=\underline q_C-\rho
\le\inf_{B\in C\cap K_\kappa}w(B).
\tag{4}
\]
Set \(L_\varepsilon=\min_C\lambda_C\), \(U_\varepsilon=\min_C\overline q_C\), and choose \(B_\varepsilon\) from a box attaining the latter minimum.  A box containing a minimizer gives
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}-2\rho\le L_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le w(B_\varepsilon)\le U_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}+2\rho.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon<\varepsilon\).  Prune only exact-infeasibility boxes or boxes whose valid lower bound exceeds the incumbent upper bound.  No minimizer box can be pruned, so the retained union contains \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), and the prescribed refinement gives \(h_\varepsilon\le\varepsilon\).  Every operation terminates by the input interface, producing precisely \(\mathscr K_\varepsilon\).  Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) in (5) and in the mesh bound proves the final convergence, without a uniform complexity or SDP claim. \(\square\)
