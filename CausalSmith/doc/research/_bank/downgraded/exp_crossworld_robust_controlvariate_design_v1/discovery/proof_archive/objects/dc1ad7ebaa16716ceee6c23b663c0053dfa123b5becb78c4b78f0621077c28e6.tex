\textbf{Proof.}
Put
\[
v_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B).
\tag{1}
\]
Attainment and the containment
\(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}\cup\mathcal A_{0,Z}
\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\)
imply
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=v_n,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\mathcal A_{0,Z},
\qquad
\Delta_Z=q_n-v_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Indeed, each global optimizer is feasible in the smaller class, while any smaller-class point attaining the resulting global value is itself a global optimizer.

The robust regret and nominal lower bound in \(\texttt{thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer}\), together with (2), give
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{3}
\]
The two directions of its feasible-estimator bridge give
\[
V_0\ge\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)-R_n,
\qquad
V_{\mathrm{mm}}\le\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+R_n.
\tag{4}
\]
Subtracting (4) and applying (3) proves
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n-2R_n.
\tag{5}
\]

The three explicit deterministic envelopes imply
\[
C_n\mathbb E_PM_n
\le K_CK_M\frac{n^\beta}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}.
\tag{6}
\]
Substitution of (6) and \(R_n\le K_Rn^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}\) into (5) gives exactly the displayed finite check.  If its strict inequality holds, the right side of (5) is positive, so \(V_0>V_{\mathrm{mm}}\).

For the final claim, multiply (5) by \(n\).  The retained exact premise \(nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\to0\), boundedness of \(L_n\), and the repaired directed-local bridge conclusion \(nR_n\to0\) make both error terms vanish.  Hence
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n(V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\Delta_Z>0.
\tag{7}
\]
All variances used here are ex ante expected conditional design variances.  No sampling limit law or Wald assertion is invoked. \(\square\)
