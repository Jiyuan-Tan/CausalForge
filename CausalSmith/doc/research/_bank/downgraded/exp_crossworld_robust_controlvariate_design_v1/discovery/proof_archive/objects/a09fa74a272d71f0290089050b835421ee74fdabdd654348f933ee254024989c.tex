For a proposed basis, both worst-copula residual variance and the adversarial endpoint are computable from the known design matrices and the two pilot marginals.  The minimax-residual basis is the design decision that directly controls the worst ex ante residual variance.  Its weak dominance over the zero-covariance optimizer is definitional optimality on the same \(\mathcal B_\kappa\); a positive, practically meaningful gap requires the unresolved full-correspondence witness.
