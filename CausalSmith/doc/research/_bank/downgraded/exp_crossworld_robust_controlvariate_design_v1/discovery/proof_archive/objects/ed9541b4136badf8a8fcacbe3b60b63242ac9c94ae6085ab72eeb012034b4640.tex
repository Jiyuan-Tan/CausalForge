\textbf{Proof.}
The normalized set \(\mathcal K\) is compact and nonempty, while \(a\) and \(w\) are continuous on it because the \(\kappa\)-eigenvalue bound keeps every ordinary inverse uniformly well defined.  Hence \(a^\star,v,A_0,A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), and \(q\) are well defined, and both optimizer sets are nonempty and compact.

We first prove containment.  A point of \(A_0\) attains \(a^\star\) inside \(\mathcal K\).  The first inequality in (G1) says that every normalized ordinary-domain point outside \(\mathcal K\) has nominal value at most \(a^\star-\zeta_0\), so none is a full ordinary-domain nominal optimizer.  Conversely, every full optimizer must attain \(a^\star\) and therefore lies in \(A_0\).  Thus
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=A_0.
\tag{1}
\]
The same argument with the second inequality in (G1) shows that every point outside \(\mathcal K\) has loss at least \(v+\zeta_{\mathrm{mm}}\), whereas a point of \(A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) attains \(v\).  Hence
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=A_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{2}
\]
The exact feasibility of \(B_R\) and (W) give
\[
v\le w(B_R)\le q_- -\zeta_\Delta,
\qquad
q=\min_{C\in A_0}w(C)\ge q_-.
\tag{3}
\]
Subtracting in (3) proves \(q-v\ge\zeta_\Delta>0\).  This is a witness-and-lower-bound certificate, not an assumed comparison of the two optimized values.

We next derive the local geometry.  Fix a tubular chart and write \(C=\Phi_\ell(s,0)\) and \(B=\Phi_\ell(s,z)\), with \(z=(t,u)\).  The nominal KKT identity cancels the free first-order active-tangent term.  Strict complementarity contributes at least \(\lambda_0\lVert u\rVert_1\) in nominal loss along the feasible active-normal coordinate, and the transverse reduced-Hessian bound contributes at least \((m_0/2)\lVert t\rVert_2^2\).  The certified Taylor remainder is bounded by \(K_a\lVert z\rVert_2^3\).  Therefore
\[
a^\star-a(B)
\ge \lambda_0\lVert u\rVert_1
+\frac{m_0}{2}\lVert t\rVert_2^2
-K_a\lVert z\rVert_2^3.
\tag{4}
\]
No curvature term in a direction tangent to the optimizer stratum occurs in (4): the \(s\)-coordinate merely selects the base point \(C\), and \(a(C)=a^\star\).

For \(\lVert z\rVert_2\le\rho\), condition (R) gives
\[
\lambda_0\lVert u\rVert_1
\ge\frac{m_0}{2}\lVert u\rVert_2^2,
\qquad
K_a\lVert z\rVert_2^3
\le\frac{m_0}{4}\lVert z\rVert_2^2.
\]
Substituting into (4), and then using the upper tube inequality in (T), gives
\[
a^\star-a(B)
\ge\frac{m_0}{4}\lVert z\rVert_2^2
\ge\frac{m_0}{4k_+^2}d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
=\mu d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus the nominal quadratic growth is a consequence of transverse curvature, strict complementarity, the tube constants, and the remainder sign; it was not entered as a certificate field.

For robust loss, let
\[
\overline\ell_C(B)
=\theta_L(C)\ell_B(c_L)+\theta_U(C)\ell_B(c_U).
\]
The endpoint-active support of the weights gives \(\overline\ell_C(C)=w(C)\ge q\), and the maximum dominates every convex combination:
\[
w(B)\ge\overline\ell_C(B).
\tag{6}
\]
The exact normal-cone KKT certificate makes the first feasible transverse derivative of \(\overline\ell_C\) nonnegative.  The interval-Hessian and remainder bounds therefore imply
\[
\overline\ell_C(B)
\ge\overline\ell_C(C)
-\left(\frac{H_w}{2}+K_w\rho\right)\lVert z\rVert_2^2.
\tag{7}
\]
Combining (6), (7), \(w(C)\ge q\), and the lower tube inequality in (T) yields
\[
[q-w(B)]_+
\le \frac{H_w/2+K_w\rho}{k_-^2}
d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
=M d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2.
\tag{8}
\]
This proves the robust second-order excess bound from first-order KKT signs and exact second- and third-order bounds.

On the local tube, division of (8) by (5) is legitimate because \(\mu>0\), and gives
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le(M/\mu)\{a^\star-a(B)\}.
\tag{9}
\]
On the compact away set, (A) and \(\gamma>0\) give
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le(R/\gamma)\{a^\star-a(B)\}.
\tag{10}
\]
The tube and away set cover \(\mathcal K\).  Taking \(L=\max\{M/\mu,R/\gamma\}\) in (9)--(10) proves (G2).  The only divisions are by exact positive algebraic constants.

For an empirical nominal maximizer, uniform error and optimality give
\[
a^\star-a(\widehat B_0)
\le |a^\star-\widehat g_{B^\star}(0)|
+|\widehat g_{\widehat B_0}(0)-a(\widehat B_0)|
\le2e,
\tag{11}
\]
where \(B^\star\in A_0\).  Combining (11) with (G2) gives
\[
w(\widehat B_0)\ge q-2Le.
\tag{12}
\]
Similarly, comparison with any point of \(A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) gives
\[
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\le v+2d.
\tag{13}
\]
The two selected-basis performance quantities \(V_0,V_{\mathrm{mm}}\) are explicitly the worst-coupling ex ante averages of conditional design variances displayed in the statement.  The certified bridge inequalities and (12)--(13) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
&\ge w(\widehat B_0)-w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})-R_0-R_{\mathrm{mm}}\\
&\ge(q-v)-2Le-2d-R_0-R_{\mathrm{mm}}\\
&\ge\zeta_\Delta-2Le-2d-R_0-R_{\mathrm{mm}},
\end{aligned}
\]
which is (G3).  On \(\mathcal K\), the rank-two equality \(\mathcal S_{2,n}(\kappa,\overline a)=\iota(\mathcal K)\) verifies the stability condition needed to obtain such bridge bounds from \(\texttt{thm:rank-adaptive-oracle-observable-variance-bridge}\).  Every asserted primitive check is a first-order semialgebraic sign, KKT multiplier sign, algebraic eigenvalue isolation, interval derivative bound, or CAD extremum check, whose finite decidability follows from \(\texttt{lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination}\) and \(\texttt{lem:algebraic-box-feasibility-cad}\). \(\square\)
