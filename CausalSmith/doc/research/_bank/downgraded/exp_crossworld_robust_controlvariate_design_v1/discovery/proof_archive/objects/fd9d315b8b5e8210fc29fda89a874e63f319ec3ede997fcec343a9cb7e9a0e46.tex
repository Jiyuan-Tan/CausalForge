Under the assumptions of lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate and lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, suppose ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence and the exact sensitivity condition hold and use the independent-pilot selectors of def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator.  Write
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}w(B),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0).
\]
Then
\[
\mathbb E_P w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+o(n^{-1}),
\]
and, for \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\),
\[
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
\right]
=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1}).
\]
Thus pilot selection and feasible Horvitz--Thompson coefficient estimation each have an explicit uniform first-order bridge to the oracle endpoint loss.  In addition, define
\[
g_n^\star=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B).
\]
If there are deterministic constants \(L_n\in[0,\infty)\) with \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\) such that, for every \(B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\),
\[
[q_n-w(B)]_+\le L_n\{g_n^\star-g_B(0)\},
\]
then
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B)-o(n^{-1}).
\]
The displayed linear error bound is a deterministic geometric property of the two ordinary-domain objective surfaces.  Without such quantitative geometry, ordinary outer argmax consistency alone is only \(o(1)\) and does not justify a first-order \(o(n^{-1})\) ordering.
