Under the assumptions of lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate and lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, suppose ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence and the exact sensitivity condition hold and use the independent-pilot selectors of def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator.  Write
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}w(B),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0).
\]
Then
\[
\mathbb E_P w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+o(n^{-1}),
\]
and, for \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\),
\[
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
\right]
=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1}).
\]
Thus pilot selection and feasible Horvitz--Thompson coefficient estimation each have an explicit uniform first-order bridge to the oracle endpoint loss.  For the zero selector, define
\[
g_n^\star=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B),
\qquad
e_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}
|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|,
\]
and, with \([x]_+=\max\{x,0\}\), define the deterministic loss modulus
\[
\omega_n(t)=
\sup\left\{
[q_n-w(B)]_+:
B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a},\ 
g_n^\star-g_B(0)\le t
\right\},
\qquad t\ge0.
\]
If the explicit first-order stability and pilot-integrability condition
\[
n\,\mathbb E_P\{\omega_n(2e_n)\}\longrightarrow0
\]
holds, then
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B)-o(n^{-1}).
\]
The modulus condition is a quantitative property of the two ordinary-domain objective surfaces evaluated at the random pilot error.  Without such an \(L^1(P)\) first-order condition, deterministic outer argmax consistency or convergence in probability alone does not justify an \(o(n^{-1})\) expectation ordering.
