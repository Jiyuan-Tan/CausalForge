\textbf{Proof.}
The entries of \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\) are quadratic polynomials in the \(2n\) coordinates of \((b_1,b_0)\), with real-algebraic coefficients.  The two sphere equalities are polynomial equalities, and rational box and signed pivot-chart restrictions are polynomial weak inequalities.  For a symmetric matrix
\[
 M=\begin{bmatrix}u&v\\v&z\end{bmatrix},
\]
the condition \(M\succeq0\) is equivalent to \(u\ge0\), \(z\ge0\), and \(uz-v^2\ge0\).  Applying this identity to \(M=S_B-n\kappa I_2\) gives exactly the three displayed polynomial inequalities, including equality.

Thus nonempty intersection of a chart box with \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is an existential first-order formula over the real closed field of real-algebraic numbers.  Lemma lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination supplies a terminating CAD decision.  A nonempty cell carries its real-algebraic sample point; an empty formula carries the finite sign-invariant cell decomposition showing that no cell realizes all required signs.  Algebraic-number isolation and exact sign evaluation check the sample or each cell record.  No oracle remains. \(\square\)
