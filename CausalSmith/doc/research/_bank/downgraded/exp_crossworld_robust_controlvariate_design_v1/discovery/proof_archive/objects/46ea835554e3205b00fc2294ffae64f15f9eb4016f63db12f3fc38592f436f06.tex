\textbf{Proof.}
Put
\[
E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\I_n&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad M=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}.
\]
Definition def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\).  Mutual exclusivity implies
\[
\Omega_{i,n+i}=\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(D_{i,1},D_{i,0})
=-\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}.
\]
Exposure positivity permits division, so each corresponding off-diagonal entry of \(M\) is \(-1\), and symmetry gives \(\operatorname{tr}(ME)=-2n\).  Consequently
\[
h(c)-h(0)=-cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(ME)=\frac{2c}{n}.
\]
Subtracting the endpoint gains proves
\[
w(B)=h(0)+\max\left\{\frac{2c_L}{n}-g_B(c_L),
\frac{2c_U}{n}-g_B(c_U)\right\}.
\]

For non-equivalence, let \(a_e=2c_e/n\) and define for a formal pair \(x=(x_L,x_U)\)
\[
\Phi(x)=\max\{a_L-x_L,a_U-x_U\},
\qquad \Psi(x)=-\min\{x_L,x_U\}.
\]
If \(\Phi=\Psi+K\) for every \(x\), evaluating at \((t,0)\) for large positive \(t\) gives \(K=a_U\), while evaluating at \((0,t)\) gives \(K=a_L\).  This contradicts \(c_L<c_U\). \(\square\)
