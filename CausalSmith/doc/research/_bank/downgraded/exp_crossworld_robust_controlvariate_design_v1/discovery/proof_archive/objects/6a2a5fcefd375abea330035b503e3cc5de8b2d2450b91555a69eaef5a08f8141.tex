Rare network exposures make Horvitz--Thompson estimation noisy, while an unpaired pilot identifies only the two potential-outcome marginals.  The project imports the sharp Frechet covariance endpoints, proves the fixed-basis affine endpoint reduction and the drift identity \(h(c)=h(0)+2c/n\), and therefore formulates the faithful robust decision as minimization of worst-copula residual variance rather than maximization of worst-copula gain.  On the explicitly regularized compact class \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), the minimax rule weakly dominates every zero-covariance optimizer in worst-copula loss; strict dominance remains witness-gated.  The plug-in loss, minimax value, and one-sided outer limit of the minimizer set are consistent.  A moment-complete certificate interface is specified, but finite \(\varepsilon\)-global certification and the triangle separation certificate remain open.
