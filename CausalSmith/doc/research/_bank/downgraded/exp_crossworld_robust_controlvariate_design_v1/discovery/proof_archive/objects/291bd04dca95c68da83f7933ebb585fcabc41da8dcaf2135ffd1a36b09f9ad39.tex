\textbf{Proof.}
The strict nominal gap in (G1) says that every point outside \(\mathcal K\) has nominal value strictly below a feasible point attaining \(a^\star\); hence no ordinary-domain nominal optimizer lies outside \(\mathcal K\).  The robust gap gives the analogous conclusion for minimizers.  Therefore
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=A_0,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=A_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{1}
\]

We explain why the local inequalities in (G2) are consequences of the listed KKT and Hessian data.  In a certified stratum, use feasible chart coordinates \((s,t)\), with \(s\) tangent to the optimizer stratum and \(t\) in the active normal cone.  Nominal stationarity cancels the free first-order tangent term.  Strict complementarity gives a positive linear loss in every nonzero feasible active-normal displacement, while the negative reduced Hessian gives a positive quadratic loss in tangent displacement.  The interval remainder bound is quadratic.  After shrinking the positive algebraic chart radius uniformly over the finite cover, these facts give
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\mu d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\tag{2}
\]
for a certified \(\mu>0\).

For \(C\in A_q\), the active endpoint weights obey
\[
w(B)\ge\theta_L(C)\ell_B(c_L)+\theta_U(C)\ell_B(c_U).
\tag{3}
\]
The weighted KKT relation makes the first-order feasible displacement in (3) nonnegative.  The endpoint Hessian and remainder bounds therefore give
\[
w(B)\ge q-Md_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2.
\tag{4}
\]
Points of \(A_0\setminus A_q\) have a positive certified \(w\)-gap, so after the same finite shrinking they cannot make \([q-w(B)]_+\) positive.  Equations (2)--(4) prove (G2) from the primitive record.

If \(B\) lies in the local tube, division of the two inequalities in (G2) gives
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le(M/\mu)\{a^\star-a(B)\}.
\tag{5}
\]
If it lies in the away set, (G3) gives
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le(R/\gamma)\{a^\star-a(B)\}.
\tag{6}
\]
Taking the larger constant proves (G4).

For an empirical nominal maximizer, the usual two-uniform-error comparison gives
\[
a^\star-a(\widehat B_0)\le2e.
\tag{7}
\]
Combining (7) with (G4) yields \(w(\widehat B_0)\ge q-2Le\).  Similarly,
\[
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\le v+2d.
\tag{8}
\]
Finally, subtract the two oracle inequalities and lose at most \(R_0+R_{\mathrm{mm}}\) when passing to the observable estimators.  This is (G5).  Every division uses the strictly positive certified constants \(\mu,\gamma\); all compact extrema occur on the closed normalized chart pieces named in the record. \(\square\)
