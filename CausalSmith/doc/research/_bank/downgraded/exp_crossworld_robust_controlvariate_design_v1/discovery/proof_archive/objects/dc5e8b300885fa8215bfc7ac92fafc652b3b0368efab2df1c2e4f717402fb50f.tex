\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(e\in\{L,U\}\).  Def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(c_e)\succeq0\), and exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  Congruence gives
\[
 A_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\succeq0.
 \tag{1}
\]
Since \(S_B=Z_B^{\top}Z_B\), cyclicity of trace yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 n^2g_B^+(c_e)
 &=\operatorname{tr}\!\left[(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top}A_eZ_B\right]\\
 &=\operatorname{tr}\!\left[Z_B(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top}A_e\right]
 =\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{2}
\]
The singular-value decomposition of \(Z_B\) shows that \(P_B\) is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(Z_B)\) and has rank \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\).  The rank-two equality follows from lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification.

Now choose representatives \(B^\circ\) of a singular published class and \(C^\circ\) of an arbitrary \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\) before performing addition.  Put \(B_t^\circ=B^\circ+tC^\circ\).  It is block separable, and
\[
 p(t)=\det\!\left\{(Z_{B^\circ}+tZ_{C^\circ})^{\top}(Z_{B^\circ}+tZ_{C^\circ})\right\}
\]
is a polynomial with positive leading coefficient \(\det(Z_{C^\circ}^{\top}Z_{C^\circ})\).  It has finitely many roots.  Choose \(t_j\downarrow0\) away from them, independently normalize the two nonzero columns of \(B_{t_j}^\circ\), and then return to the ordinary quotient to obtain \([B_j]\in\mathcal B_Z\).  Normalization does not change its projector.

Rank-two orthogonal projectors form a compact set, so along a subsequence \(P_{B_j}\to P_\infty\).  If \(x=Z_{B^\circ}u\), then \((Z_{B^\circ}+t_jZ_{C^\circ})u\to x\) and belongs to the range defining \(P_{B_j}\).  Hence
\[
 \lVert P_{B_j}x-x\rVert
 \le2\lVert x-(Z_{B^\circ}+t_jZ_{C^\circ})u\rVert\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{range}(P_B)\subseteq\operatorname{range}(P_\infty)\) and \(P_\infty-P_B\succeq0\).  By (1)--(2), simultaneously for both endpoints,
\[
 \lim_{j\to\infty}g_{B_j}(c_e)
 =n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_\infty A_e)
 \ge g_B^+(c_e).
\]
The uncontrolled term is basis independent, so \(\limsup_j\ell_{B_j}(c_e)\le\ell_B^+(c_e)\).  Taking the maximum over the two endpoints proves the last display. \(\square\)
