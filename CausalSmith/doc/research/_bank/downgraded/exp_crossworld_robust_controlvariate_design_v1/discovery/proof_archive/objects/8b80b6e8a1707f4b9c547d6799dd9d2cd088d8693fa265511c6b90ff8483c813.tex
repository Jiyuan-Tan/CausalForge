\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
K_\kappa=\left\{(b_1,b_0):
\lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
\ \lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}B(b_1,b_0)^{\top}\Omega B(b_1,b_0)\right)\ge\kappa
\right\}.
\]
The product of the two radius-\(\sqrt n\) spheres is compact.  The matrix map in this display and the smallest-eigenvalue map are continuous, so \(K_\kappa\) is closed and compact, including when it is empty.  The quotient map identifying independent nonzero column rescalings is continuous; def:kappa-basis-class therefore makes \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) its compact image.

Every member of \(K_\kappa\) satisfies
\[
\lambda_{\min}(B^{\top}\Omega B)\ge n\kappa>0,
\]
so def:zhao-basis-class gives \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\).

Conversely, take \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\), represented by \(B=B(b_1,b_0)\).  Positive definiteness of \(S_B\) forces \(b_1,b_0\ne0\).  Put
\[
d_a=\frac{\sqrt n}{\lVert b_a\rVert_2},
\qquad T=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,d_0),
\qquad \widetilde B=BT.
\]
Then \(\widetilde B\) is a normalized representative of the same projective class and
\[
S_{\widetilde B}=T S_BT\succ0.
\]
Thus \(\rho_B=\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_{\widetilde B})>0\).  With \(\kappa_B=\rho_B/2\), the representative belongs to \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_B}\).  This proves \(\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa=\mathcal B_Z\).

The conclusion is exactly an exhaustion of the nondegenerate ordinary-inverse quotient defined in def:zhao-basis-class.  Since the threshold obtained above depends on \(B\), it does not identify a fixed \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) with Zhao's unrestricted block-diagonal domain; and because the argument starts from \(S_B\succ0\), it says nothing about singular or pseudoinverse bases. \(\square\)
