\textbf{Proof.}
By ass:kappa-feasibility there is at least one normalized block-separable basis satisfying \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_B)\ge\kappa\).  Hence \(\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing\).  Proposition prop:kappa-exhaustion shows that \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is compact and is contained in the positive-definite rank-two ordinary-inverse class \(\mathcal B_Z\).

Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi\) invertible.  On \(\mathcal B_\kappa\),
\[
S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2,
\]
so matrix inversion is continuous there.  For each fixed endpoint \(c_e\), \(e\in\{L,U\}\), def:gain-loss therefore makes \(B\mapsto g_B(c_e)\) and \(B\mapsto\ell_B(c_e)\) continuous on normalized representatives.  These maps are invariant under the residual independent sign changes and hence descend continuously to the projective quotient.  Their finite maximum
\[
w(B)=\max\{\ell_B(c_L),\ell_B(c_U)\}
\]
is continuous.  The same argument at \(c=0\) makes \(B\mapsto g_B(0)\) continuous.  Equivalently, the endpoint form of \(w\) is also given by lem:ht-affine-drift.  Compactness and the Weierstrass theorem now imply that \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(\mathcal A_0\) are both nonempty and that \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}\) is attained.

Every \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) satisfies \(w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Moreover, for each \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\), the definition of the global minimum gives
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)\le w(B_0).
\]
Taking the supremum over the first nonempty correspondence and the infimum over the second yields
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0}w(B_0).
\]

The domain distinction is definitional: \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\subseteq\mathcal B_\kappa\) is an attained fixed-threshold rank-two argmin, whereas \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\subseteq\mathcal B_Z^+\) is the equality set of a full-carrier infimum and is permitted to be empty.

Finally take \(F_1=F_0=\delta_0\).  Both means and variances vanish, both quantile-coupling covariance endpoints equal zero, and def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(0)=0\).  The trace formulas in def:gain-loss then give, for every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\),
\[
h(0)=g_B(0)=\ell_B(0)=w(B)=0.
\]
Thus \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\mathcal A_0=\mathcal B_\kappa\), \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}=0\), and equality holds.  This supplies the claimed lawful non-strictness witness. \(\square\)
