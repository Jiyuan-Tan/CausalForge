Normalize the two projective factors as in prop:kappa-exhaustion.  Their product is compact, the feasible subset is nonempty and closed, and \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\).  Hence the two endpoint losses and their maximum are continuous and uniformly continuous on the feasible set.  The moment-complete interface provides outward-rounded inclusion oracles for every coefficient appearing in those rational matrix expressions, effective refinement moduli, and exact decisions for sphere and \(\kappa\)-boundary feasibility.

Cover both normalized factors by the finitely many signed pivot charts.  On each chart, interval evaluation of matrix multiplication, the two-by-two inverse on \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\), trace, and maximum supplies outward lower and upper loss bounds.  Effective coefficient refinement and uniform continuity imply: for every rational \(\alpha>0\), there is an effectively reachable subdivision depth and arithmetic precision at which each nonempty retained box \(C\) has diameter at most \(\alpha\) and loss oscillation at most \(\alpha\).

Exact feasibility decisions discard precisely empty boxes, including correct decisions when feasibility is confined to \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_B)=\kappa\).  From any nonempty feasible box, repeated bisection and exact feasible-child selection produces nested closed feasible boxes with diameters tending to zero.  Compactness makes their intersection a singleton \(B_C\in\mathcal B_\kappa\); the nested boxes are its exact inclusion-oracle name.  Thus an exactly feasible incumbent exists even when the feasible set has empty relative interior.

Choose rational \(\alpha>0\) with \(4\alpha<\varepsilon\), refine to the finite depth above, compute for every feasible box a certified lower loss bound within \(2\alpha\) of its infimum, and compute an incumbent upper bound within \(2\alpha\) of the best sampled feasible value.
