A corrected minimax certificate \(\mathscr K_{\varepsilon}\) consists of a finite quotient-chart cover, an exactly feasible incumbent \(B_{\varepsilon}\), rational bounds \(L_{\varepsilon},U_{\varepsilon}\), outward-rounded endpoint-loss and constraint bounds for every retained box, a checkable exact-infeasibility or valid lower-bound pruning record for every discarded box, retained boxes whose union is \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\), and their maximum chart diameter \(h_{\varepsilon}\).  The checker requires \(L_{\varepsilon}\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_{\varepsilon})\le U_{\varepsilon}\), \(U_{\varepsilon}-L_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon\), \(h_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon\), and \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\subseteq\mathcal C_\varepsilon\); it never accepts deletion of a box meeting the exact \(\kappa\)-boundary without an exact infeasibility certificate or a valid objective lower bound exceeding the incumbent upper bound.
