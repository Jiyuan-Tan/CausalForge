\textbf{Proof.}
By ass:kappa-feasibility and prop:kappa-exhaustion, \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is a nonempty compact metrizable quotient.  Its representatives satisfy
\[
\lVert B\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=\sqrt n,
\qquad \lVert S_B^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\tag{1}
\]
The armwise bounded strong law gives weak convergence of \(\widehat F_a\) to \(F_a\), convergence of the first two moments, and
\[
\int_0^1|\widehat F_a^{-1}(u)-F_a^{-1}(u)|\,du\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{2}
\]
The identity behind (2) is equality between the \(L^1\) distance of distribution functions and that of quantiles on the line.  Because all quantiles lie in \([0,1]\),
\[
|xy-x'y'|\le|x-x'|+|y-y'|
\]
shows that both empirical quantile-coupling cross moments converge.  Hence
\[
\widehat c_L\to c_L,\quad \widehat c_U\to c_U,\quad
\rho_m:=\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\to0
\tag{3}
\]
almost surely.

Exposure positivity fixes a finite \(\Pi^{-1}\).  Trace and operator-norm inequalities, together with (1), give a constant \(K<\infty\) depending only on the fixed design and \(\kappa\) such that
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|\le K\rho_m\to0
\tag{4}
\]
almost surely.  Explicitly, the uncontrolled trace contributes at most \(2n^{-1}\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\rho_m\), and the two-dimensional gain trace contributes at most \(2\lVert\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}^2(n^2\kappa)^{-1}\rho_m\).

Both criteria are continuous on the compact quotient, so the population and empirical minima are attained.  The elementary inequality between two infima gives
\[
|\widehat v_{\mathrm{mm}}-v_{\mathrm{mm}}|
\le\sup_B|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|\to0.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(B_m\in\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and a subsequence converges to \(B_\infty\), then for any \(B^\star\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), empirical optimality and (4) give
\[
w(B_m)\le w(B^\star)+2\sup_B|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|.
\]
Passing to the limit proves \(w(B_\infty)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\), hence \(B_\infty\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  This is the asserted outer limit on the fixed-\(\kappa\) rank-two restriction; no cross-rank conclusion is used. \(\square\)
