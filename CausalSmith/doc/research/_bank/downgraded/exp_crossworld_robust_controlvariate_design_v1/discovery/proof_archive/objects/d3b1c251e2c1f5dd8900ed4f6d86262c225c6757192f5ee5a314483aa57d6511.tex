Put \(N=2n\), write \(\pi=\mathbb E_\nu D\), and define the centered design vector
\[
Z=D-\pi.
\]
The exact finite-design moment moduli are
\[
\mathfrak M_{4,n}
=\max_{q\in\{-1,1\}^{N}}\mathbb E_\nu(q^{\top}Z)^4
\]
and
\[
\mathfrak M_{3,n}
=\max_{q,r,s\in\{-1,1\}^{N}}
\left|\mathbb E_\nu\{(q^{\top}Z)(r^{\top}Z)(s^{\top}Z)\}\right|.
\]
They are computable from the known finite assignment law by respectively \(2^N\) and \(2^{3N}\) finite evaluations.  Convexity for the fourth moment and separate linearity for the third mixed moment give, for arbitrary real vectors,
\[
\mathbb E_\nu(q^{\top}Z)^4
\le\mathfrak M_{4,n}\lVert q\rVert_\infty^4,
\]
\[
\left|\mathbb E_\nu\{(q^{\top}Z)(r^{\top}Z)(s^{\top}Z)\}\right|
\le\mathfrak M_{3,n}\lVert q\rVert_\infty
\lVert r\rVert_\infty\lVert s\rVert_\infty.
\]
For the directed local neighborhoods in \(\texttt{ass:zhao-dependency-neighborhood-rate}\), retain \(\mathcal G_n=\{(\ell,j):j\in N_\ell\}\) only as a directed relation and define
\[
s_n=\max_{1\le \ell\le N}\#\{k:\Omega_{k\ell}\ne0\},
\qquad
d_n=\max_{1\le \ell\le N}\lvert N_\ell\rvert.
\]
No separated-set independence or undirected dependency-graph property is included in these definitions.

For a normalized representative \(B\) of a class in \(\mathcal B_Z^+\), put
\[
\lVert B\rVert_{\max}
=\max_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}|b_{a,i}|.
\]
For \(r\in\{1,2\}\), \(\lambda>0\), and \(\overline a\ge1\), define
\[
\mathcal S_{r,n}(\lambda,\overline a)
=\left\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:
\operatorname{rank}(S_B)=r,\ 
\lambda_+(S_B)\ge n\lambda,\ 
\lVert B\rVert_{\max}\le\overline a\right\},
\]
where \(\lambda_+(S_B)\) is the smallest strictly positive eigenvalue.  Define
\[
\mathcal S_{0,n}(\overline a)
=\left\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:S_B=0,\ 
\lVert B\rVert_{\max}\le\overline a\right\}.
\]
No positive-eigenvalue condition is imposed at rank zero.  These strata neither identify nearby classes across ranks nor impose stability as \(\lambda_+(S_B)\downarrow0\).
