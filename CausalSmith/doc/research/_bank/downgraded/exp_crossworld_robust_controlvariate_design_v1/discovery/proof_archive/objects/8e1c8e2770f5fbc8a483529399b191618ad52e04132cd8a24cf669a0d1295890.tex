\textbf{Proof.}
Known exposure makes the deployed estimator in def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator agree with the exposure-potential-outcome stack.  Let
\[
v_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\qquad q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B).
\]
The assumed optimizer containment gives \(v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=v_n\) and \(\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\mathcal A_{0,Z}\).  Thus \(\Delta_Z=q_n-v_n\).  The robust and conditional zero-surrogate inequalities in thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer imply
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{1}
\]
Applying its feasible-estimator bridge once in each direction gives
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n-2R_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Substitution of the three finite envelopes proves the checkable strictness condition.  Multiplying (2) by \(n\), the exact sensitivity condition, boundedness of \(L_n\), and \(4\alpha+4\beta<1\) make both remainder terms vanish and prove the limit inferior claim.  No primitive optimizer-containment theorem or uniformly bounded linear-error constant is proved: both are explicit hypotheses of this conditional rank-two transfer. \(\square\)
