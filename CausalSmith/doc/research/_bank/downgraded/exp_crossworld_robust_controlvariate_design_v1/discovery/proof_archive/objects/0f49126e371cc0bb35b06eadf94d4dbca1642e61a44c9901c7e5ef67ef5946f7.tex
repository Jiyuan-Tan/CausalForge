\(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z=\{B:B=\begin{bmatrix}b_1&0\\0&b_0\end{bmatrix},\ b_1,b_0\in\mathbb R^n,\ B^{\top}\Omega B\succ0\}\).  This is the maximal block-separable matrix domain on which the ordinary inverse in Zhao's Equation (8) is well defined; it is implicit in that formula and is not claimed to be a separately named domain in Zhao.  Define the quotient map \(q_Z:\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\to\mathcal B_Z\) by \(q_Z(B)=[B]\).
