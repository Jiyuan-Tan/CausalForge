\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\I_n&0\end{bmatrix}\) and \(A_{\mathrm{HT}}=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\).  Def:moment-matrix gives \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\).  Mutual exclusivity implies
\[
\Omega_{i,n+i}=\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(D_{i,1},D_{i,0})
=-\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}.
\]
Exposure positivity permits division, so symmetry gives \(\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)=-2n\).  Therefore
\[
h(c)=h(0)-cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)
=h(0)+2c/n.
\]
Subtracting the endpoint gains yields the displayed formula for \(w(B)\).

To prove non-equivalence, put \(a_e=2c_e/n\) and define on a formal endpoint-gain pair \(x\)
\[
\Phi(x)=\max\{a_L-x_L,a_U-x_U\},
\qquad \Psi(x)=-\min\{x_L,x_U\}.
\]
If \(\Phi=\Psi+K\) for all \(x\), evaluating at \((t,0)\) gives \(K=a_U\), while evaluating at \((0,t)\) for \(t\ge a_U-a_L\) gives \(K=a_L\).  This contradicts \(c_L<c_U\). \(\square\)
