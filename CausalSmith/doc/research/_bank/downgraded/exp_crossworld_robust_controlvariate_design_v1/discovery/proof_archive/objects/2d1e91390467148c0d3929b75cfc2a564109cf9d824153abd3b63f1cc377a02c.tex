\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
d_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|,
\qquad
e_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|.
\]
Lemma lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate and the exact sensitivity condition give \(n\mathbb E_Pd_n\to0\) and \(n\mathbb E_Pe_n\to0\).  If \(B^\star\) minimizes \(w\) on the compact rate-admissible class, empirical optimality gives
\[
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\le\widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d_n
\le\widehat w(B^\star)+d_n
\le w(B^\star)+2d_n.
\]
The reverse inequality \(w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\ge w(B^\star)\) is definitional.  Taking pilot expectations proves the robust oracle-regret display; it has been derived from the uniform endpoint-matrix rate rather than assumed.

Conditional on the pilot, the selected basis is fixed and independent of the main outcome array and assignment.  Lemma lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence therefore gives, uniformly over the selected basis and every \(H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)\),
\[
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
=\ell_{\widehat B_s}(c_H)+o(n^{-1}). \tag{1}
\]
The remainder is uniform, so it survives pilot expectation and the supremum over \(H\).  Assumption ass:frechet-covariance-domain and the affine endpoint theorem imply
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_B(c_H)=w(B)
\]
for every fixed \(B\).  Conditional on the pilot, apply this equality at \(B=\widehat B_s\), and then integrate over the pilot.  This order is the sequential robust criterion: the endpoint adversary evaluates the realized pilot-selected decision.  Applying it to (1) proves the two bridge identities.

For the zero selector, empirical optimality similarly gives
\[
g_{\widehat B_0}(0)
\ge\max_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0)-2e_n. \tag{2}
\]
The deterministic first-order stability implication, applied on pilot events with \(e_n=o(n^{-1})\), turns (2) into
\[
w(\widehat B_0)
\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B)-o_P(n^{-1}).
\]
All losses are uniformly bounded by the design-rate envelope used in the preceding lemmas, and \(n\mathbb E e_n\to0\); truncating on \(ne_n\le M\) and then letting \(M\to\infty\) upgrades the last relation to the asserted pilot-expectation bound.  The qualification concerning outer consistency follows because compactness alone supplies no modulus converting an \(o(n^{-1})\) objective error into an \(o(n^{-1})\) loss error along a possibly flat argmax correspondence. \(\square\)
