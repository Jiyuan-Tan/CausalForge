\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\widehat B=\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{st},+}\), \(K=\mathcal K_n^{\mathrm{st}}\), \(d=d_m^+\), and \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_m\).  By \(\texttt{def:pilot-plugin}\),
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B)
\le\inf_{B\in K}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon.
\tag{1}
\]
The definition of \(d\) gives, simultaneously on the full carrier,
\[
w^+(B)-d\le\widehat w_m^+(B)\le w^+(B)+d.
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(B^{\mathrm{st}}\in K\) attain \(v_n^{\mathrm{st}}\); attainment follows from the compact stable carrier and continuity on its spectrally separated rank pieces.  Applying (2), (1), and then (2) at \(B^{\mathrm{st}}\) gives
\[
w^+(\widehat B)
\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B)+d
\le\widehat w_m^+(B^{\mathrm{st}})+\varepsilon+d
\le v_n^{\mathrm{st}}+2d+\varepsilon.
\tag{3}
\]
Since \(K\subseteq\mathcal B_Z^+\),
\[
w^+(\widehat B)\ge v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\tag{4}
\]
Taking pilot expectations in (3)--(4) and using \(v_n^{\mathrm{st}}=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++b_n^{\mathrm{st}}\) yields
\[
0\le\mathbb E_Pw^+(\widehat B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le b_n^{\mathrm{st}}+2\mathbb E_Pd_m^++\mathbb E_P\varepsilon_m.
\tag{5}
\]

Pilot--main independence allows conditioning on the realized \(\widehat B\).  At selected rank zero, \(\texttt{thm:rank-adaptive-oracle-observable-variance-bridge}\) says that observable and oracle estimators are exactly the uncontrolled Horvitz--Thompson estimator, so the bridge remainder is zero.  On the event of selected rank \(r\in\{1,2\}\), membership in \(K\) gives
\[
[\widehat B]_+\in\mathcal S_{r,n}(\lambda_{r,n},\overline a),
\]
so the same theorem gives, for every \(H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)\),
\[
\left|
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B,Y)
-\ell_{\widehat B}^+(c_H)\right|
\le R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}.
\tag{6}
\]
The feasible covariance image is \([c_L,c_U]\), and the loss is affine in \(c_H\).  Hence
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_{\widehat B}^+(c_H)
=w^+(\widehat B).
\tag{7}
\]
Taking suprema in (6), using \(|\sup f-\sup g|\le\sup|f-g|\), and averaging over the pilot proves
\[
\left|V_{\mathrm{st}}-\mathbb E_Pw^+(\widehat B)\right|
\le\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\mathbf1\{\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widehat B})>0\}
R_{n,\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widehat B})}^{\mathrm{ad}}\right]
\le\max_{r\in\{1,2\}}R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}.
\tag{8}
\]
Combining the upper side of (8) with (5) gives the required upper deviation from \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  Combining its lower side with (4) gives a negative deviation of magnitude at most the last term in (8), which is no larger than the displayed nonnegative sum.  This proves
\[
\left|V_{\mathrm{st}}-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\right|
\le b_n^{\mathrm{st}}+2\mathbb E_Pd_m^+
+\mathbb E_P\varepsilon_m
+\max_{r\in\{1,2\}}R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}.
\tag{9}
\]

For the exact-input stable CAD selector, \(\texttt{thm:encoded-cad-stable-pilot-selector-certificate}\) supplies (1), stable-carrier membership, exact rank, coordinate envelope, and the applicable spectral margin, so the same proof applies.  Multiplying (9) by \(n\) proves the first-order conclusion under the displayed convergence premise, which explicitly includes \(b_n^{\mathrm{st}}\).  Finally, the rate clause of the rank-adaptive bridge gives \(\max_r nR_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\to0\) under the three Zhao rate assumptions when both positive-rank thresholds are bounded below and \(\overline a\) is fixed.  Nothing in this argument gives a rate or zero value for \(b_n^{\mathrm{st}}\). \(\square\)
