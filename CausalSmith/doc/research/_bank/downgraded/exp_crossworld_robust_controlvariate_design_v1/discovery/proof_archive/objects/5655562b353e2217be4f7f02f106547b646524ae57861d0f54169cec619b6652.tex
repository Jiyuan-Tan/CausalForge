\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence}\), bounded outcomes and the unpaired-pilot sampling law imply
\[
M_m\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely as }m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]
For the fixed design, exposure positivity gives
\[
\pi_{\min,n}=\min_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}\pi_{i,a}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
The all-rank modulus in \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate}\) therefore yields
\[
d_m^+
\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{3}
\]

For arbitrary real-valued functions \(f\) and \(g\) on the same nonempty domain,
\[
|\inf f-\inf g|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, \(f\le g+\sup|f-g|\) gives one inequality after taking infima, and interchanging \(f\) and \(g\) gives the other.  The full carrier is nonempty because it contains the zero class.  Applying (4) to \(\widehat w_m^+\) and \(w^+\) on \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) proves
\[
\left|
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\right|
\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{5}
\]

The corrected \(\texttt{def:pilot-plugin}\) enumerates rational-support classes of maximal achievable rank \(r_\Omega\), including the zero class when \(r_\Omega=0\).  By \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector}\), for every finite strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\), the first qualifying index is finite and pilot-measurable and satisfies
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (6) with the definition of \(d_m^+\) twice gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is the infimum of \(w^+\) over the same domain,
\[
0\le
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (3) and (8) prove the final almost-sure convergence whenever \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely.

The selector lemma also gives \(\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+})=r_\Omega\).  Thus the selector is rank two exactly when rank two is achievable; otherwise it is rank one or zero.  The proof uses only positive tolerances and infimum comparisons.  It assumes neither full-carrier attainment nor a zero-tolerance selector, and it establishes neither cross-rank outer-argmin convergence, point convergence, nor a feasible-estimator variance bridge at singular rank. \(\square\)
