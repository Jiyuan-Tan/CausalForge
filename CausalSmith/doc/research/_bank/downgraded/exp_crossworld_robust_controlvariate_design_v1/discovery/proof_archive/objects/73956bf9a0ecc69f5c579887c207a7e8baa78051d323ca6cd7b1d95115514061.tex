Along a triangular sequence satisfying ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility and ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate, let \(\widehat Q_L,\widehat Q_U\) be the two endpoint matrices obtained from the unpaired empirical marginals, put \(M_n=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\), and define
\[
C_n=n^{-2}\left[
\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_*
+\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}
\left\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\rVert_*
\right].
\]
Under ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot,
\[
M_n=O_{L^1}\!\left(\frac{n}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}\right),
\qquad
C_n\le4\underline\pi^{-1}n^{\beta-1}.
\]
More precisely, with
\[
A_{\mathrm{HT}}=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1},
\qquad
A_B=\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1},
\]
one has
\[
0\preceq A_B\preceq A_{\mathrm{HT}},
\qquad
\lVert A_B\rVert_*
\le\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}})
=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{a\in\{1,0\}}\frac{1-\pi_{i,a}}{\pi_{i,a}}
\le2\underline\pi^{-1}n^{1+\beta}.
\]
The uniform plug-in inequalities are
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|
\le C_nM_n,
\]
and, writing \(\widehat Q(0)\) for the zero-covariance moment matrix formed from the empirical marginal moments,
\[
\lVert\widehat Q(0)-Q(0)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le2M_n,
\]
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|
\le2n^{-2}\sup_B\lVert A_B\rVert_*M_n
\le2C_nM_n.
\]
Hence the exact downstream premise
\[
nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0
\]
implies first-order plug-in accuracy in the explicit sense that the pilot expectations of both displayed uniform criterion errors are \(o(n^{-1})\).  The condition
\[
\frac{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}{n^{1+\beta}}\longrightarrow\infty
\]
is sufficient.  Neither known exposure nor a dependency-neighborhood condition is used in this pilot-only matrix calculation.
