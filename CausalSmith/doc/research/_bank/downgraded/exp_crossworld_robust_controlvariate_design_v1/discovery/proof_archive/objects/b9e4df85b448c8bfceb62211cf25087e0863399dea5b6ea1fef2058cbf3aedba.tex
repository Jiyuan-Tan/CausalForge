Prop:kappa-exhaustion and ass:kappa-feasibility imply that \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is nonempty and compact, while the regularity bound \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\) makes \(w\) continuous there.  Thus \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}\) is attained, \(w\) is uniformly continuous for each fixed encoded instance, and finite projective-chart covers of arbitrarily small mesh exist.  These topological facts do not yet provide an effective refinement modulus or prove finite exact feasibility or objective pruning for every box meeting the equality boundary \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_B)=\kappa\); consequently they do not establish termination of the specified interval branch-and-bound algorithm with both the value-gap and outer-set mesh guarantees.
