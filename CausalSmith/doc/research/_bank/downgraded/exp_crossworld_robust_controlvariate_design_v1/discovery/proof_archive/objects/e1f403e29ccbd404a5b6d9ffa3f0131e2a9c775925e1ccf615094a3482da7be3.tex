\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(N=2n\) and \(Z_\ell=D_\ell-\mathbb E_\nu D_\ell\).  The directed local condition has the following singleton consequence.  If an index \(\ell\) occurs exactly once in a product of at most four centered coordinates and every other distinct index lies outside \(N_\ell\), then
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\!\left[Z_\ell\prod_{j\ne\ell}Z_j^{m_j}\right]
=\mathbb E_\nu Z_\ell\,
\mathbb E_\nu\!\left[\prod_{j\ne\ell}Z_j^{m_j}\right]=0.
\tag{1}
\]
Indeed, the product is measurable with respect to the complementary subvector from which \(D_\ell\) is independent.  Thus a possibly nonzero mixed moment requires every once-occurring index to have another distinct index in its own directed neighborhood.  This criterion gives no inverse-neighborhood bound, and none will be used.

For triples, assign to each position a different position as follows.  A repeated value points to its first equal occurrence, which is allowed because \(\ell\in N_\ell\); a singleton points to its first directed neighbor, whose existence follows from (1).  There are \(2^3=8\) maps of three positions without fixed points.  Each functional digraph is either a directed three-cycle or a directed two-cycle with one inward vertex.  In the first case choose one label freely and follow two directed edges; in the second start at the inward vertex and again follow two directed edges.  Each map therefore has at most \(Nd_n^2\) labelings.  Expansion of the three linear forms and \(|Z_\ell|\le1\) gives
\[
\mathfrak M_{3,n}\le8Nd_n^2.
\tag{2}
\]

For fourth moments, partition ordered quadruples by multiplicity type
\[
4,\qquad 3+1,\qquad 2+2,\qquad 2+1+1,\qquad 1+1+1+1.
\tag{3}
\]
Type \(4\) contributes \(N\) tuples.  In type \(3+1\), the singleton must point to the repeated value, so the count is at most \(4Nd_n\).  Type \(2+2\) contributes exactly \(6\binom N2=3N(N-1)\) tuples.

For type \(2+1+1\), fix the two repeated positions in one of six ways, call their label \(r\), and call the singleton labels \(s,t\) according to the order of their positions.  The singleton criterion is covered by four directed patterns:
\[
s\to r,\ t\to r;\qquad
s\to r,\ t\to s;\qquad
s\to t,\ t\to r;\qquad
s\to t,\ t\to s.
\tag{4}
\]
The first inward-star pattern and the last two-cycle pattern have at most \(N^2d_n\) labelings: choose one singleton, follow its charged edge, and leave the other unconstrained.  Each chain pattern has at most \(Nd_n^2\) labelings by following its two edges.  Since \(d_n\ge1\), the contribution of (4), including all six choices of repeated positions, is at most \(24N^2d_n^2\).  Hence all repeated-label types together contribute at most
\[
(1+4+3+24)N^2d_n^2=32N^2d_n^2.
\tag{5}
\]

It remains to count all-distinct tuples.  The directed graph induced by their four labels has minimum out-degree at least one.  Classify hierarchically.  If it contains a directed two-cycle, choose its unordered pair of positions in six ways, choose once and for all one of the two remaining positions, and choose the head of that position among the other three positions.  This gives \(18\) patterns.  If that head is on the two-cycle, start at the remaining position, follow its edge and one cycle edge, and leave the fourth label free; if the head is the other remaining position, choose that tail and one cycle label freely and follow the two relevant edges.  Either way the count per pattern is at most \(N^2d_n^2\), without controlling an in-degree.  If there is no two-cycle but there is a three-cycle, there are \(4\cdot2=8\) oriented positional cycles, each with at most \(N^2d_n^2\) labelings after choosing the fourth label freely and following two cycle edges.  If there is neither a two-cycle nor a three-cycle, minimum out-degree forces a directed four-cycle.  There are \(6\) oriented positional four-cycles, each with at most \(Nd_n^3\le N^2d_n^2\) labelings because \(d_n\le N\).  Thus the all-distinct type contributes at most
\[
(18+8+6)N^2d_n^2=32N^2d_n^2.
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (5)--(6) show that at most \(64N^2d_n^2\) ordered quadruples can have nonzero centered product.  Expanding \((q^\top Z)^4\) at \(q\in\{-1,1\}^N\) therefore gives
\[
\mathfrak M_{4,n}\le64N^2d_n^2.
\tag{7}
\]

Finally, if \(k\notin N_\ell\), the local condition makes \(D_\ell\) independent of \(D_k\), whence \(\Omega_{k\ell}=0\).  Every nonzero entry in column \(\ell\) of \(\Omega\) consequently has row index in \(N_\ell\), and
\[
s_n\le d_n\le\overline d n^\alpha.
\tag{8}
\]
Substitution of \(N=2n\) into (2) and (7) proves the asserted constants.  The one-cycle charges created by repeated labels, the two-cycle, inward-star, chain, three-cycle, and four-cycle cases exhaust the count, and only singleton-versus-complement independence was used. \(\square\)
