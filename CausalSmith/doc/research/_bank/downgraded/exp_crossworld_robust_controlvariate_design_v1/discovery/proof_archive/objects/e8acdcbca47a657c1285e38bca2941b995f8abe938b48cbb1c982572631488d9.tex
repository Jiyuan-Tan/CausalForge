\textbf{Proof.}
If \(0<\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\), then \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_2}\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}\), so minimizing the same continuous loss over the larger compact set proves the first inequality.  Prop:kappa-exhaustion gives \(\mathcal B_Z=\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa\).  The infimum of a function over a union is the infimum of the infima over its nonempty members, whence
\[
 \inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
 =\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
 \min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\]
If \(B_Z^\star\) attains the left side, prop:kappa-exhaustion supplies \(\kappa_0>0\) with \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_0}\).  For \(0<\kappa\le\kappa_0\),
\[
 \inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
 \le\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
 \le w(B_Z^\star)
 =\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
\]
This proves equality and concerns only the ordinary-inverse class. \(\square\)
