\textbf{Proof.}
Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  Since each endpoint moment matrix is positive semidefinite, congruence gives
\[
A_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\succeq0.
\tag{1}
\]
Put \(Z_B=\Omega^{1/2}B\).  Then \(S_B=Z_B^{\top}Z_B\), and cyclicity of trace yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
n^2g_B^+(c_e)
&=\operatorname{tr}\!\left[(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top}A_eZ_B\right]\\
&=\operatorname{tr}\!\left[Z_B(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top}A_e\right]
=\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
The singular-value decomposition of \(Z_B\) shows that
\[
P_B=Z_B(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top}
\tag{3}
\]
is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(Z_B)\).  Thus
\[
\operatorname{rank}(P_B)=\operatorname{rank}(Z_B)
=\operatorname{rank}(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)
=\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\in\{0,1,2\}.
\tag{4}
\]
The rank-two identification and equality of the ordinary and pseudoinverse gains and losses are exactly \(\texttt{lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification}\).

The zero class belongs to \(\mathcal B_Z^+\), and the ranks in (4) take values in the finite set \(\{0,1,2\}\); hence \(r_\Omega\) exists.  We construct the asserted sequence by cases.

Suppose first that \(r_\Omega=2\).  Choose \([C]_+\) with \(Z_C\) of rank two and, for a representative of \([B]_+\), put
\[
Z(t)=Z_B+tZ_C.
\tag{5}
\]
The function
\[
p(t)=\det\{Z(t)^{\top}Z(t)\}
\tag{6}
\]
is a polynomial.  Its coefficient of \(t^4\) is \(\det(Z_C^{\top}Z_C)>0\), so it is not the zero polynomial.  Choose \(t_j\downarrow0\) outside its finitely many roots.  Then \(Z(t_j)\) has rank two.  Independent normalization of its two nonzero arm columns does not alter its quotient class or projector, so it defines \([B_j]_+\in\iota(\mathcal B_Z)\).

The set of rank-two orthogonal projectors in the finite-dimensional matrix space is compact.  After taking a subsequence and relabeling, let
\[
P_{B_j}\longrightarrow P_\infty.
\tag{7}
\]
For any \(v\in\operatorname{range}(Z_B)\), write \(v=Z_Bx\).  Since \(Z(t_j)x\in\operatorname{range}\{Z(t_j)\}\),
\[
\operatorname{dist}\!\left(v,\operatorname{range}\{Z(t_j)\}\right)
\le |t_j|\,\lVert Z_Cx\rVert_2\longrightarrow0.
\tag{8}
\]
Taking the limit in (8) gives \(P_\infty v=v\).  Therefore
\(
\operatorname{range}(P_B)\subseteq\operatorname{range}(P_\infty)
\), and for orthogonal projectors this is equivalent to
\[
P_\infty-P_B\succeq0.
\tag{9}
\]

Suppose next that \(r_\Omega=1\).  If \(B\) has rank one, take the constant sequence \(B_j=B\).  If \(B\) has rank zero, choose any rank-one class \([C]_+\), take \(B_j=C\), and put \(P_\infty=P_C\); then \(P_B=0\), so (9) again holds.  Finally, if \(r_\Omega=0\), every class has projector zero and the constant sequence suffices.  These cases prove the projector approximation for every design, including designs with no rank-two class.

By (1), (2), (7), (9), and trace monotonicity,
\[
\lim_{j\to\infty}g_{B_j}^+(c_e)
=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_\infty A_e)
\ge n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e)
=g_B^+(c_e)
\tag{10}
\]
for \(e=L,U\).  The uncontrolled endpoint terms do not depend on the basis.  Subtracting (10) from those terms and taking the maximum of the two endpoint residuals gives
\[
\limsup_{j\to\infty}w^+(B_j)\le w^+(B).
\tag{11}
\]
This proves every assertion. \(\square\)
