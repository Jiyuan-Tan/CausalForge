For every rational or real-algebraic input \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\) satisfying ass:exposure-positivity and ass:kappa-feasibility, and every exactly encoded \(\varepsilon>0\), the fixed-\(\kappa\) rank-two algebraic projective interval branch-and-bound procedure terminates after finitely many subdivisions and returns a locally checkable certificate \(\mathscr K_{\varepsilon}\) such that
\[
 L_{\varepsilon}\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_{\varepsilon})\le U_{\varepsilon},
 \qquad U_{\varepsilon}-L_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon,
 \qquad h_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon,
 \qquad \mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\subseteq\mathcal C_{\varepsilon}.
\]
The incumbent \(B_{\varepsilon}\) has finite real-algebraic coordinates, including when every feasible point lies on \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega B)=\kappa\).  The checker verifies CAD sign certificates, algebraic feasibility, outward objective bounds, rational norm and diameter records, chart cover, pruning inequalities, and mesh.  Along every \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), the certified value gaps and meshes converge to zero.  This theorem is the compact regular rank-two solver; thm:zhao-pseudoinverse-rank-stratified-epsilon-solver handles the full published class.  No uniform complexity bound or semidefinite-program reduction is asserted.
