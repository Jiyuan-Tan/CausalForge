Suppose ass:exposure-positivity and ass:kappa-feasibility hold.  On the compact fixed-threshold positive-definite rank-two class \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), the correspondences
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathcal A_0=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}g_B(0)
\]
are nonempty, the value \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)\) is attained, and the unconditional weak worst-copula dominance certificate is
\[
\sup_{B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}}w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0}w(B_0).
\]
These are fixed-threshold rank-two objects, distinct from the full-carrier equality correspondence \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\), which may be empty.  Strict inequality is not universal: if \(F_1=F_0=\delta_0\), then \(c_L=c_U=0\), \(Q(0)=0\), and \(w(B)=g_B(0)=0\) for every \(B\in\mathcal B_\kappa\), so equality holds in the displayed certificate.
