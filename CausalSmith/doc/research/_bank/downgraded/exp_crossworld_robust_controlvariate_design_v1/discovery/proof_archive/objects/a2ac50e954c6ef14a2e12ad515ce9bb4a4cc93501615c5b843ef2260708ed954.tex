Suppose ass:exposure-positivity holds and \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\).  If \(0<\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\) and both regular classes are nonempty, then
\[
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}}w(B)
\le
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_2}}w(B).
\]
Moreover,
\[
\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
=
\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
=
\inf_{B\in\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z}w(q_Z(B)).
\]
If the middle infimum is attained by some \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_Z\), then there is \(\kappa_0>0\) such that, for every \(0<\kappa\le\kappa_0\),
\[
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
\]
Thus the fixed-threshold values exhaust only the positive-definite rank-two restriction in value through the quotient; the rank-zero and rank-one strata in the note's projective quotient of Zhao's published pseudoinverse carrier remain outside this proposition.
