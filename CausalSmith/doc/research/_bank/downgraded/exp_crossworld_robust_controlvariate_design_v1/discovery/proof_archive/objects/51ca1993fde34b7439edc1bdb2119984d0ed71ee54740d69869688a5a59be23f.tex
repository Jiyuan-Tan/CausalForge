If \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\), then \(S_B\succ0\), so both columns are nonzero and \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)=2\).  Independent nonzero rescaling is the equivalence relation used by the ordinary projective class and by the rank-two stratum of the novel completion.  Hence \([B]\mapsto[B]_+\) is well defined and injective.

Conversely, let \([C]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\) have \(\operatorname{rank}(C^{\top}\Omega C)=2\).  Its two columns are nonzero, and \(C^{\top}\Omega C\) is a two-by-two positive-semidefinite matrix of rank two, hence positive definite.  Def:zhao-basis-class therefore defines \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), and \(\iota([C])=[C]_+\).  This proves surjectivity onto the stated stratum.
