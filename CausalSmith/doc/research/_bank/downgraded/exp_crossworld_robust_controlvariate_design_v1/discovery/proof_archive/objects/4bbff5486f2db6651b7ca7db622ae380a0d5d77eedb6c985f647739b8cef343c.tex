For observed outcomes \(Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}=Y_i(f_i(W))\), define Zhao's signed armwise Horvitz--Thompson outcome stack
\[
\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}=
\left(\frac{D_{1,1}Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{1,1}},\ldots,
\frac{D_{n,1}Y_n^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{n,1}},
-\frac{D_{1,0}Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{1,0}},\ldots,
-\frac{D_{n,0}Y_n^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{n,0}}\right)^{\top}.
\]
For a supplied basis \(B\), Zhao's observable coefficient map is
\[
\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)
=(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}.
\]
Thus the signed observed stack is already inverse-probability weighted and the formula applies the additional \(\Pi^{-1}\); in particular
\(\mathbb E_\nu[B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}]
=B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y\).
For \(a\in\{1,0\}\), set \(\widehat1_a=n^{-1}\sum_iD_{i,a}/\pi_{i,a}\), and define \(\widehat\gamma_a^{\mathrm{SN}}(B)=\widehat\gamma_a^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)/\widehat1_a\) when \(\widehat1_a>0\), and zero otherwise.  Deploy
\[
\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}
=n^{-1}\mathbf1^{\top}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}
-\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{SN}}(B)^{\top}
\{n^{-1}B^{\top}D-\mathbb E_\nu[n^{-1}B^{\top}D]\}.
\]
