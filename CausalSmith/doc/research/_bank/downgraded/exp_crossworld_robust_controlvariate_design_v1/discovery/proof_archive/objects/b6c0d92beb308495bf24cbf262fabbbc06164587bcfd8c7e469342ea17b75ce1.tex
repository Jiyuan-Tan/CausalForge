Under ass:bounded-outcomes, ass:unpaired-pilot, and ass:exposure-positivity, suppose \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\), and define
\[
 M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}},
 \qquad
 d_m^+=\sup_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}
 |\widehat w_m^+(B)-w^+(B)|,
\]
and let \(\pi_{\min,n}=\min_{i,a}\pi_{i,a}>0\).  For every rank zero, one, or two class \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\),
\[
 0\preceq A_B^+\preceq A_{\mathrm{HT}},
 \qquad
 A_B^+=\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}P_B\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1},
 \qquad
 A_{\mathrm{HT}}=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1},
\]
and
\[
 d_m^+\le 4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m.
\]
Along a triangular sequence satisfying ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate and \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\) at every \(n\), this yields
\[
 d_m^+\le4\underline\pi^{-1}n^{\beta-1}M_m
\tag{A}
\]
and, for every positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \right]
 \le
 8\underline\pi^{-1}n^{\beta-1}\mathbb E_P[M_m]
 +\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_m].
\tag{B}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}{n^{1+\beta}}\longrightarrow\infty,
 \qquad n\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_m]\longrightarrow0
\]
imply
\[
 n\mathbb E_P\!\left[
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \right]\longrightarrow0.
\]
This is a full-carrier basis-objective regret result on the note's quotient of Zhao's all-rank pseudoinverse domain.  It is not a feasible-estimator variance bridge for a selected singular-rank basis.
