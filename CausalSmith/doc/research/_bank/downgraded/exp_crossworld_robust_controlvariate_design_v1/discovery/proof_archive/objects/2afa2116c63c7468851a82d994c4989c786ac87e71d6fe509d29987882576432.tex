\textbf{Proof.}
Work on the probability-one event supplied by thm:plugin-correspondence.  Suppose first that \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\{B^\star\}\), and let \(\widehat B_m\in\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm}}\) be any measurable selection.  If \(\widehat B_m\) did not converge to \(B^\star\), some open neighborhood \(U\) of \(B^\star\) would contain no tail of the sequence.  There would then be a subsequence in the closed set \(\mathcal B_\kappa\setminus U\).  Compactness of \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), proved in prop:kappa-exhaustion and used by thm:plugin-correspondence, supplies a further convergent subsequence with limit in \(\mathcal B_\kappa\setminus U\).  The outer-limit conclusion of thm:plugin-correspondence says that this limit must belong to \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\{B^\star\}\), a contradiction.  Therefore every such selection converges almost surely to \(B^\star\).

We now give a bounded lawful instance showing that a singleton-type condition cannot simply be omitted.  Take \(n=3\), let \(f_i(w)=w_i\), and assign the three units independently with Bernoulli probability \(1/2\).  Then
\[
 \Pi=\frac12I_6,
 \qquad
 \Omega=\frac14
 \begin{bmatrix}
 I_3&-I_3\\
 -I_3&I_3
 \end{bmatrix}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Indeed, \(D_{i,1}=W_i\), \(D_{i,0}=1-W_i\), distinct units are independent, and the two exposure indicators for the same unit have covariance \(-1/4\).  Thus exposure positivity holds.

Let \(F_1=F_0=\delta_0\), let \(H=\delta_{(0,0)}\), and take \(\kappa=1/8\).  The outcomes are bounded, the two deterministic pilot sequences are unpaired, i.i.d. within arm, and independent of the assignment.  Both covariance endpoints equal zero, all empirical endpoints equal zero at every pilot size, and def:moment-matrix gives
\[
 Q(0)=\widehat Q(0)=0.
\]
Consequently def:gain-loss and def:pilot-plugin give
\[
 w(B)=\widehat w(B)=0
 \quad\text{for every }B\in\mathcal B_\kappa.
 \tag{2}
\]

The feasible class contains at least the two distinct projective points
\[
 B^{(2)}=B(\sqrt3e_1,\sqrt3e_2),
 \qquad
 B^{(3)}=B(\sqrt3e_1,\sqrt3e_3).
\]
Their columns have squared norm three, and (1) gives
\[
 S_{B^{(j)}}=\frac34I_2,
 \qquad
 \lambda_{\min}\!\left(3^{-1}S_{B^{(j)}}\right)=\frac14>\kappa,
 \quad j\in\{2,3\}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Hence ass:kappa-feasibility holds.  Equation (2) shows that
\(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm}}=\mathcal B_\kappa\) at every pair of pilot sizes, so the outer-set conclusion holds exactly.  Define a measurable selection to equal \(B^{(2)}\) when \(m_1\) is even and \(B^{(3)}\) when \(m_1\) is odd.  Along the cofinal sequence \(m_1=m_0=m\), this selection alternates between two distinct projective points and therefore has no point limit.  This proves the asserted counterexample. \(\square\)
