\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\delta_0=\Delta(\xi_0)>0\).  The displayed optimizer-wide eigenvalue slack is exactly the hypothesis of lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity.  If no relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) had positive gap, then, because the relative total-variation--Wasserstein topology in def:lawful-instance-class is metrizable, for each positive integer \(j\) one could choose \(\xi_j\in\mathfrak X_\kappa\) within distance \(1/j\) of \(\xi_0\) and satisfying \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le0\).  Thus \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\), and the lemma would imply
\[
0<\delta_0=\Delta(\xi_0)
\le\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)
\le0,
\]
a contradiction.  Hence some relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\subset\mathfrak X_\kappa\) of \(\xi_0\) satisfies \(\Delta(\xi)>0\) throughout. \(\square\)
