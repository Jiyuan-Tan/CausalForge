\textbf{Proof.}
Thm:triangle-completion-separation-certificate proves on the ordinary projective domain that
\[
\operatorname*{argmax}_{[C]\in\mathcal B_Z}g_C(0)=\mathcal A_0,
\qquad
\operatorname*{argmin}_{[C]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(C)=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}, \tag{1}
\]
with optimal values \(31/80\) and \(2613/8432\), respectively, and with
\[
w(C_0)=\frac{521}{1200}
\qquad(C_0\in\mathcal A_0).                                      \tag{2}
\]
By thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer, \(q_Z:\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\to\mathcal B_Z\) is surjective, each criterion is constant on every fiber, and every attained optimizer correspondence on the matrix domain is exactly the inverse image of its quotient correspondence.  Applying that theorem to (1) gives
\[
\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z}g_{q_Z(B)}(0)
=q_Z^{-1}(\mathcal A_0),
\]
\[
\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z}w(q_Z(B))
=q_Z^{-1}(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}).                              \tag{3}
\]
The same fiber invariance preserves all values in (2).  Therefore the worst-loss reduction is
\[
\frac{521}{1200}-\frac{2613}{8432}
=\frac{4912}{39525}>0.                                            \tag{4}
\]
Equations (3)--(4) cover the entire unquotiented positive-definite rank-two matrix domain and enlarge the equality sets only by independent nonzero rescaling of the two arm columns.  They use no singular matrix; the rank-zero and rank-one statements belong to Zhao's full published pseudoinverse class. \(\square\)
