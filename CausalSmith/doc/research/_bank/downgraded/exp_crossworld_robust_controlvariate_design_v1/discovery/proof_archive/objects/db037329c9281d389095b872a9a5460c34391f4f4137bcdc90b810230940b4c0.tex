For every \(\kappa>0\), the quotient \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is compact and satisfies \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\). Conversely, every \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\) has normalized representatives belonging to \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) for some basis-dependent \(\kappa>0\), so \(\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa=\mathcal B_Z\).
