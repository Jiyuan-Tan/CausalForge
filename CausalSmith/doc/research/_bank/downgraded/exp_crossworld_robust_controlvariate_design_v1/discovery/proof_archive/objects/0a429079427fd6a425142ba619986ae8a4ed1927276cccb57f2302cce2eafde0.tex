Suppose ass:exposure-positivity holds and \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\).  Let \(\mathcal D_Z=([C_j]_+)_{j\ge1}\) be the fixed countable dense enumeration of normalized positive-definite rank-two quotient charts in def:pilot-plugin.  For every realization of the unpaired pilot endpoint matrices,
\[
\inf_{j\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_j)
=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B).
\]
For every strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\), the index
\[
J_{m,\varepsilon_m}
=\min\left\{j\ge1:
\widehat w_m^+(C_j)
\le \inf_{k\ge1}\widehat w_m^+(C_k)+\varepsilon_m\right\}
\]
is finite and pilot measurable.  Consequently \(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+=[C_{J_{m,\varepsilon_m}}]_+\) is a measurable \(\mathcal B_Z^+\)-valued selector and satisfies
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\]
No full-carrier minimizer or zero-tolerance selector is asserted.
