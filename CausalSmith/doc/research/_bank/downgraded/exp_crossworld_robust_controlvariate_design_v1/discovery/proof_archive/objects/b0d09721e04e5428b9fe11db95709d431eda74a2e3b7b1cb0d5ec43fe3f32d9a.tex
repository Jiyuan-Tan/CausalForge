\textbf{Proof.}
From \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\) and cyclicity of trace,
\[
g_B(c)=a_B+b_Bc.
\tag{1}
\]
Lemma lem:ht-affine-drift gives \(h(c)=h(0)+2c/n\), so
\[
\ell_B(c)=h(0)-a_B+(2/n-b_B)c.
\]
Taking the endpoint maximum proves \(w(B)=h(0)-a_B+p_\xi(b_B)\).  Under the assumed attainments, set
\[
M=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}\{a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\}.
\]
Then \(\inf_{\mathcal B_Z}w=h(0)-M\), whereas restriction to the nominal-optimum face gives
\[
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B_0)
=h(0)-a^\star+\inf_{B:a_B=a^\star}p_\xi(b_B).
\]
Subtracting proves the displayed formula for \(\Delta_Z\).  Positivity of that expression is equivalent, by rearrangement and attainment of \(M\), to the displayed existence inequality.  Thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer carries all values and optimizer fibers to \(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\).  The result is deliberately restricted to the positive-definite rank-two stratum; Zhao's published singular strata are addressed separately. \(\square\)
