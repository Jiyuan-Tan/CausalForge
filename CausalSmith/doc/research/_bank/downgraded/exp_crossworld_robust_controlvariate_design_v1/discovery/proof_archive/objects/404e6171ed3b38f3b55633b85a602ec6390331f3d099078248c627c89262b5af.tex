For \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\) and \(c\in\mathcal C(F_1,F_0)\), define the Horvitz--Thompson baseline \(h(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}[\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c)]\), the Zhao gain \(g_B(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}[S_B^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c)\Pi^{-1}\Omega B]\), and the residual \(\ell_B(c)=h(c)-g_B(c)\), all in \([0,\infty)\).
