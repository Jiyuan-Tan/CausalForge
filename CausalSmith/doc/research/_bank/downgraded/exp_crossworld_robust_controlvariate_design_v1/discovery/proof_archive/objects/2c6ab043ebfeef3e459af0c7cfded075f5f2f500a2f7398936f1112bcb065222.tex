Suppose ass:exposure-positivity and ass:frechet-covariance-domain hold and \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\).  For the novel Moore--Penrose completion of def:novel-pseudoinverse-completion,
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
:=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
=\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\]
Define the possibly empty completion equality set
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
=\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\}.
\]
Writing \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\) for the fixed-threshold argmin, its rank-two stratum is exactly
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
=\iota\!\left(
\bigcup_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,
\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing,\ 
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+}}
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\right).
\]
In particular, if the ordinary-domain infimum is attained at \(C\in\mathcal B_Z\), then \(\iota(C)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  Conversely, if \(\iota(C)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\), then \(C\) attains the ordinary-domain infimum and belongs to \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa_C)\) for some \(\kappa_C>0\) satisfying \(\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  The singular equality stratum is
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\setminus\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
=\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1,\ w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\}.
\]
A singular equality point need not imply attainment on \(\mathcal B_Z\).  No unconditional completion attainment, no singular equality point, and no attribution of the completion to Zhao is asserted.
