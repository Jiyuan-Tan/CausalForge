\(\mathcal B_Z\) is the rank-two stratum of def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain:
\[
 \mathcal B_Z=
 \left\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:
 \operatorname{rank}(B^{\top}\Omega B)=2\right\}
 =\left\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\lambda_{\min}(B^{\top}\Omega B)>0\right\}.
\]
On this stratum the Moore--Penrose inverse is the ordinary inverse.  The predicates are ass:block-separable-basis and ass:basis-invertibility; the definition is a projective rank-two repair, not the full published domain.
