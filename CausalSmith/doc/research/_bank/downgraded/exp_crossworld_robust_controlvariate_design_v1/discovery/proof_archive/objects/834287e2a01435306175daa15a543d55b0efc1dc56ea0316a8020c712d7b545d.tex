Fix \(n,G,\nu,(f_i)_{i=1}^n\) and suppose ass:exposure-positivity, ass:frechet-covariance-domain, ass:bounded-outcomes, and ass:unpaired-pilot hold.  Put
\[
\rho_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}},
\qquad
d_m=n^{-2}\!\left\{\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_*+2\lVert\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}^2\right\}\rho_m.
\]
Then \(\rho_m\to0\) and \(d_m\to0\) almost surely as \(m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty\), and
\[
\sup_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}|\widehat w(B)-w^+(B)|\le d_m,
\qquad
|\widehat v_{\mathrm{mm}}-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+|\le d_m
\]
almost surely.  For every \(\varepsilon_m>0\), the empirical \(\varepsilon_m\)-minimizer set is nonempty; every \(\widehat B_m\in\mathcal B_Z^+\) satisfying \(\widehat w(\widehat B_m)\le\widehat v_{\mathrm{mm}}+\varepsilon_m\) obeys
\[
w^+(\widehat B_m)\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m+\varepsilon_m.
\]
If \(\varepsilon_m\to0\), then \(w^+(\widehat B_m)\to v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) almost surely.  This is full-class value and epsilon-regret consistency only; absent attainment and rank-separation premises, it implies no outer-argmin or cross-rank point convergence.
