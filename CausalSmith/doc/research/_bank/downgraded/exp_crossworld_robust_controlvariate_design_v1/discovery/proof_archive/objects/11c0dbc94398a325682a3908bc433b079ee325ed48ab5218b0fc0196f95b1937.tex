\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(\kappa>0\), and let
\[
K_\kappa=\left\{(b_1,b_0):\lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,\ 
\lambda_{\min}\!\left(n^{-1}B(b_1,b_0)^{\top}\Omega B(b_1,b_0)\right)\ge\kappa\right\}.
\]
The product of the two radius-\(\sqrt n\) spheres is compact. The displayed smallest-eigenvalue map is continuous, so \(K_\kappa\) is closed and compact. The quotient map identifying independent nonzero column rescalings is continuous; def:kappa-basis-class identifies \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) with its image. Hence \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is compact.

Every member satisfies \(\lambda_{\min}(B^{\top}\Omega B)\ge n\kappa>0\), so def:zhao-basis-class gives \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\). Conversely, take \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\). Positive definiteness of \(S_B\) implies that neither block vector is zero. Put
\[
d_a=\frac{\sqrt n}{\lVert b_a\rVert_2},
\qquad T=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,d_0),
\qquad \widetilde B=BT.
\]
Then \(\widetilde B\) represents the same projective point, has both block-vector norms equal to \(\sqrt n\), and
\[
S_{\widetilde B}=T S_BT\succ0.
\]
Thus \(\rho_B=\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_{\widetilde B})>0\). With \(\kappa_B=\rho_B/2\), the representative \(\widetilde B\) belongs to \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_B}\). This proves \(\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa=\mathcal B_Z\). The conclusion concerns the note's nondegenerate ordinary-inverse repair of Zhao's block-diagonal domain; it does not cover singular bases or a pseudoinverse objective. \(\square\)
