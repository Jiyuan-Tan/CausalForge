\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(m=m_1\wedge m_0\).  For every \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\) supplies the orthogonal projector
\[
P_B=\Omega^{1/2}B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega^{1/2}
\]
onto \(\operatorname{range}(\Omega^{1/2}B)\).  Hence \(0\preceq P_B\preceq I_{2n}\).  Congruence by \(\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\), legitimate by exposure positivity, gives
\[
0\preceq
A_B^+=\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}P_B\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}
\preceq
\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}=A_{\mathrm{HT}}.
\tag{1}
\]
Both matrices in (1) are positive semidefinite, so their nuclear norms equal their traces.  Since \(D_{i,a}\) is Bernoulli with mean \(\pi_{i,a}\),
\[
\Omega_{(i,a),(i,a)}=\pi_{i,a}(1-\pi_{i,a}).
\tag{2}
\]
Therefore, with \(\pi_{\min,n}=\min_{i,a}\pi_{i,a}>0\),
\[
\lVert A_B^+\rVert_*
\le\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}})
=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{a\in\{1,0\}}
\frac{1-\pi_{i,a}}{\pi_{i,a}}
\le2n\pi_{\min,n}^{-1},
\qquad
\lVert A_{\mathrm{HT}}\rVert_*
\le2n\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}.
\tag{3}
\]

For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), the two loss definitions give
\[
\widehat\ell_{B,e}^+-\ell_B^+(c_e)
=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
(A_{\mathrm{HT}}-A_B^+)\{\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\}
\right].
\tag{4}
\]
Trace duality and (3) imply
\[
\left|\widehat\ell_{B,e}^+-\ell_B^+(c_e)\right|
\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m.
\tag{5}
\]
The maximum of two real coordinates is one-Lipschitz in the supremum norm.  Taking the endpoint maximum and then the supremum over all three rank strata in (5) proves
\[
d_m^+\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m.
\tag{6}
\]
Under ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate,
\(\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}\le\underline\pi^{-1}n^\beta\), so (6) proves (A).

We next establish the endpoint-matrix expectation rate used in the final implication.  For arm \(a\), let
\[
D_a=W_1(\widehat F_a,F_a)
=\int_0^1|\widehat F_a(y)-F_a(y)|\,dy
=\int_0^1|\widehat F_a^{-1}(u)-F_a^{-1}(u)|\,du.
\tag{7}
\]
The one-dimensional identities follow from the layer-cake formula and Tonelli's theorem.  At each \(y\), Jensen's inequality and the Bernoulli variance formula give
\[
\mathbb E_P D_a
\le\int_0^1\sqrt{\frac{F_a(y)\{1-F_a(y)\}}{m_a}}\,dy
\le\frac{1}{2\sqrt{m_a}}.
\tag{8}
\]
Bounded support is used in (7)--(8).  The functions \(y\) and \(y^2\) are respectively one- and two-Lipschitz on \([0,1]\), whence
\[
|\widehat\mu_a-\mu_a|\le D_a,
\qquad
|\widehat\sigma_a^2-\sigma_a^2|\le4D_a,
\qquad
|\widehat\mu_a^2-\mu_a^2|\le2D_a.
\tag{9}
\]
For \(x,x',y,y'\in[0,1]\),
\[
|xy-x'y'|\le|x-x'|+|y-y'|.
\tag{10}
\]
Applying (10) to the comonotone and antimonotone quantile couplings, and then to the product of the marginal means, gives
\[
|\widehat c_e-c_e|\le2(D_1+D_0),
\qquad e\in\{L,U\}.
\tag{11}
\]
Using the block formula for \(Q(c)\), \(\lVert I_n\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=1\), and \(\lVert\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\top}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=n\), equations (9)--(11) imply, for \(n\ge3\),
\[
\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\le7n(D_1+D_0).
\tag{12}
\]
Consequently, for a numerical constant \(K_M<\infty\) independent of the marginals, design rank, and basis,
\[
\mathbb E_PM_m\le K_M\frac{n}{\sqrt m}.
\tag{13}
\]

By \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector}\), the classical maximal-rank selector is pilot-measurable and satisfies pointwise
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{14}
\]
For two real-valued functions on the same nonempty domain,
\[
|\inf f-\inf g|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{15}
\]
The full carrier is nonempty because it contains the zero class.  Applying (15), (14), and the definition of \(d_m^+\) yields
\[
0\le w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{16}
\]
Taking pilot expectations in (16) and applying (A) proves (B).  Finally, (13) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
n\mathbb E_P\!\left[
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\right]
&\le8K_M\underline\pi^{-1}
\frac{n^{1+\beta}}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}
+n\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_m]\\
&\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
The selector has rank \(r_\Omega\) by its construction and the selector lemma.  Equations (1)--(17) concern the basis objective on every rank stratum; they do not provide a feasible-estimator variance bridge at a singular selected rank. \(\square\)
