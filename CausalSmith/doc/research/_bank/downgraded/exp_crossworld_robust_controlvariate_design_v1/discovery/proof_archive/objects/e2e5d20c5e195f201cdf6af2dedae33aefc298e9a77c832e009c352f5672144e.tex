Suppose ass:known-exposure and ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility hold.  Under the assumptions of lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate and lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, suppose ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence holds, and use the independent-pilot selectors of def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator.  Put
\[
M_n=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}},
\]
where \(C_n\) is exactly the deterministic sensitivity coefficient defined in lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate, and assume along the triangular sequence that
\[
nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\longrightarrow0.
\]
Put also
\[
d_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|,
\qquad
e_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|.
\]
For every \(n\), almost surely under the pilot law,
\[
d_n\le C_nM_n,
\qquad e_n\le2C_nM_n.
\]
Write
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}w(B),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0).
\]
Then the robust selector obeys the finite pilot-regret inequality
\[
0\le
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})-v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}
\le2C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\]
Let \(R_n\) be the deterministic finite-\(n\) envelope in lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, and, for \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\), define
\[
V_s=
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
\right].
\]
The feasible-estimator bridge is the finite bound
\[
|V_s-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)|\le R_n,
\qquad s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}.
\]
Under \(nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\to0\),
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+o(n^{-1}),
\qquad
V_s=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1}).
\]
In addition, define
\[
g_n^\star=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B).
\]
If there are deterministic \(L_n\in[0,\infty)\) with \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\) such that, for every \(B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\),
\[
[q_n-w(B)]_+\le L_n\{g_n^\star-g_B(0)\},
\]
then the finite zero-surrogate inequality
\[
q_n-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\le4L_nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n
\]
holds, and consequently
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B)-o(n^{-1}).
\]
The linear error bound is a deterministic geometric property of the two ordinary-domain objective surfaces.  Without it, ordinary outer argmax consistency alone is only \(o(1)\) and does not justify a first-order \(o(n^{-1})\) ordering.  These selectors and limits are secondary regular rank-two deployment results.  No primitive condition in this theorem proves existence of uniformly bounded \(L_n\); the zero-surrogate first-order conclusion is conditional on the displayed global linear error bound.
