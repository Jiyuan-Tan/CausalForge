\textbf{Proof.}
Ass:kappa-feasibility makes \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) nonempty, and prop:kappa-exhaustion makes it compact.  Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  On this class, \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\), so inversion is continuous and bounded.  Hence \(B\mapsto g_B(0)\), both endpoint losses, and \(w(B)\) are continuous and attain their extrema.  Thus \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(\mathcal A_0\) are nonempty.  For arbitrary \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\), the defining minimization gives
\[
 w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_0).
\]
Taking the supremum and infimum gives the displayed certificate.
