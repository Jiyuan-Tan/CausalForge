\textbf{Proof.}
All arguments take place in the finite signed-pivot charts fixed by the certificate.  The normalized class \(\mathcal K=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\) is nonempty and compact, and \(a\), the two endpoint losses, and \(w\) are continuous and semialgebraic on it because \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\).  Therefore \(a^\star,v,q\) are attained and \(A_0,A_{\mathrm{mm}},A_q\) are nonempty compact semialgebraic sets.

\emph{Containment and the positive gap.}
The normalized positive-definite carrier is \(\mathcal Z\), with \(\mathcal K\subseteq\mathcal Z\).  The first inequality in (G1) says that no point of \(\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K\) can attain \(a^\star\); hence every global ordinary-domain nominal optimizer lies in \(\mathcal K\), and within \(\mathcal K\) its equality set is \(A_0\).  Thus
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=A_0.
\tag{1}
\]
The second inequality in (G1) says that no point of \(\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K\) can attain \(v\).  Consequently
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=A_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{2}
\]
The feasible witness in (G3) gives
\[
v\le w(B_R)\le q-\zeta_\Delta,
\qquad\text{so}\qquad
q-v\ge\zeta_\Delta>0.
\tag{3}
\]
This proves (G5) directly from the outside gaps and the witness.

\emph{Nominal transverse quadratic growth.}
Fix a stratum \(V_j\) and one of its certified tubular charts.  Write a feasible point near \(C\in V_j\) in the certified coordinates as
\[
B=\Psi_C(s,z,r),
\tag{4}
\]
where \(s\) is tangent to \(V_j\), \(z\) is feasible tangent and transverse to \(V_j\), and \(r\) is the active-normal coordinate.  The record's lower singular-value bounds provide constants \(c_1,c_2>0\), uniform over its finite chart list, for which
\[
c_1(\lVert z\rVert+\lVert r\rVert)
\le d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)
\le c_2(\lVert z\rVert+\lVert r\rVert)
\tag{5}
\]
after shrinking the tubes.  Motion in \(s\) changes the nearest point within \(V_j\) and therefore does not enter the distance.

The nominal KKT equations make the first derivative vanish in \(z\).  The certified transverse Lagrangian Hessian bound gives a number \(\lambda_j>0\) such that the second-order contribution to \(a^\star-a(B)\) is at least \(\lambda_j\lVert z\rVert^2\).  Linear independence and strict complementarity give a number \(\chi_j>0\) such that the feasible active-normal contribution is at least \(\chi_j\lVert r\rVert\).  The certified derivative and remainder bounds give \(C_j<\infty\) and a tube radius \(\rho_j>0\) for which Taylor's formula yields
\[
a^\star-a(B)
\ge\lambda_j\lVert z\rVert^2+\chi_j\lVert r\rVert
-C_j(\lVert z\rVert+\lVert r\rVert)^3.
\tag{6}
\]
Choose \(\rho_j\) smaller, if necessary, so the remainder is at most one half of the first two terms.  Because \(\lVert r\rVert\le\rho_j\), the linear normal term also satisfies
\(\chi_j\lVert r\rVert\ge(\chi_j/\rho_j)\lVert r\rVert^2\).  Hence there is \(\mu_j>0\) such that
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\mu_j d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\tag{7}
\]
throughout that tube, by (5).  Taking the minimum of the positive constants and radii over the finite refined chart and stratum list gives \(\rho>0\) and \(\mu>0\) for the first inequality in (G4).  Thus quadratic growth has been derived from KKT, transverse Hessian, strict-complementarity, and remainder data; it was not placed in the record.

\emph{Quadratic control of the robust deficit.}
For \(C\in N_q\), let
\[
\phi_C(B)=\theta_L(C)\ell_B(c_L)+\theta_U(C)\ell_B(c_U).
\tag{8}
\]
The weights are nonnegative and sum to one, so
\[
\phi_C(B)\le w(B)
\quad\text{for every }B.
\tag{9}
\]
Because weights are supported only on endpoint losses active at \(C\),
\[
\phi_C(C)=w(C)\ge q.
\tag{10}
\]
The certified KKT normal data state that the first feasible directional derivative of \(\phi_C\) at \(C\) is nonnegative.  Its uniform endpoint-Hessian and Taylor-remainder bounds therefore give \(M_0<\infty\), uniformly over the finitely many strata in \(N_q\), such that
\[
\phi_C(B)\ge\phi_C(C)-M_0d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,C)^2
\tag{11}
\]
in a sufficiently small certified tube.  Equations (9)--(11) imply
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le M_0d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,C)^2.
\tag{12}
\]
Choose \(C\) as a chartwise nearest point in \(A_0\); the finite-chart definition of \(d_{\mathrm{pr}}\) makes the right side in (12) \(M_0d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2\).

On the compact remainder \(A_0\setminus N_q\), the certificate gives a positive gap \(w(C)-q\ge\chi_q>0\).  Uniform continuity and the interval Taylor bounds permit a common smaller tube in which \(w(B)\ge q\); there the positive part in (12) is zero.  Combining the two cases and again taking the maximum over finitely many bounds gives \(M<\infty\) such that
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le Md_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\quad\text{whenever }d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\le\rho.
\tag{13}
\]
Together, (7) and (13) prove (G4).

\emph{Global comparison.}
If \(d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\le\rho\), dividing (13) by (7) gives
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le\frac M\mu\{a^\star-a(B)\}.
\tag{14}
\]
On the compact complement of this tube, (G2) gives
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le R
\le\frac R\gamma\{a^\star-a(B)\}.
\tag{15}
\]
With \(L=\max\{M/\mu,R/\gamma\}\), equations (14)--(15) prove (G6) on all of \(\mathcal K\).

\emph{Plug-in and observable consequences.}
Let \(\widehat B_0\) maximize the empirical nominal gain.  If \(B_0\in A_0\), empirical optimality and the definition of \(e\) give
\[
a^\star-a(\widehat B_0)
\le |a(B_0)-\widehat a(B_0)|
+|\widehat a(\widehat B_0)-a(\widehat B_0)|
\le2e.
\tag{16}
\]
Applying (G6) to (16) yields
\[
w(\widehat B_0)\ge q-2Le.
\tag{17}
\]
Similarly, if \(B^\star\in A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), empirical optimality and the definition of \(d\) give
\[
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\le\widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d
\le\widehat w(B^\star)+d
\le v+2d.
\tag{18}
\]
Finally, the two supplied observable bridge inequalities imply
\[
V_0\ge w(\widehat B_0)-R_0,
\qquad
V_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+R_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{19}
\]
Subtracting (19) and using (17)--(18) proves
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge(q-v)-2Le-2d-R_0-R_{\mathrm{mm}},
\]
which is (G7).  Every constant used above is obtained by a minimum or maximum of finitely many exact algebraic signs, singular-value bounds, interval derivative bounds, and compact-complement CAD bounds in \(\mathscr G_{\mathrm{CAD}}\).  Thus all stated consequences are derived from the primitive record. \(\square\)
