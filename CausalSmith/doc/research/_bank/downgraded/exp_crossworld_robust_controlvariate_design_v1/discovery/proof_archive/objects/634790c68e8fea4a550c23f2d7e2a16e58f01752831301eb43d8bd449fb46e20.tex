Let \(\mathcal K=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), let \(\mathcal Z\) be the normalized positive-definite rank-two carrier, and put \(a(B)=g_B(0)\),
\[
a^\star=\max_{B\in\mathcal K}a(B),
\quad
A_0=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal K}a(B),
\quad
v=\min_{B\in\mathcal K}w(B),
\quad
A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal K}w(B),
\quad
q=\min_{B\in A_0}w(B).
\]
Fix the finite signed-pivot charts and their semialgebraic quotient distance \(d_{\mathrm{pr}}\).  A primitive geometry record \(\mathscr G_{\mathrm{CAD}}\) is a finite exact rational or real-algebraic record with the following components.

First, exact CAD sign and value-isolation records give \(\zeta_0,\zeta_{\mathrm{mm}}>0\) and certify
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K}a(B)
\le a^\star-\zeta_0,
\qquad
\inf_{B\in\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K}w(B)
\ge v+\zeta_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{G1}
\]

Second, an exact finite smooth semialgebraic stratification of \(A_0\) has certified tubular chart maps \(B=\Phi_\ell(s,z)\), where \(\Phi_\ell(s,0)\in A_0\), \(s\) runs along the optimizer stratum, and \(z=(t,u)\) consists only of feasible directions transverse to that stratum: \(t\) is tangent to the active-constraint manifold and orthogonal to the optimizer stratum, while the nonnegative vector \(u\) records normalized inward active-constraint slacks, so the nominal KKT first-order loss in this coordinate is the active-multiplier inner product with \(u\).  Exact Jacobian singular-value and chart-overlap signs certify constants \(0<k_-\le k_+<\infty\) and \(\rho_0>0\) such that
\[
k_-\lVert z\rVert_2
\le d_{\mathrm{pr}}\{\Phi_\ell(s,z),A_0\}
\le k_+\lVert z\rVert_2
\quad\text{for }\lVert z\rVert_2\le\rho_0.
\tag{T}
\]
On every chart, exact nominal KKT and strict-complementarity records certify linear independence of active gradients, active multipliers at least \(\lambda_0>0\), and a reduced-Hessian eigenvalue bound
\[
-t^{\top}\nabla^2_{tt}\mathcal L_a\{\Phi_\ell(s,0)\}t
\ge m_0\lVert t\rVert_2^2
\tag{H}
\]
for some \(m_0>0\).  Condition \((H)\) is imposed only on feasible tangent directions transverse to the optimizer stratum; no curvature is required along the \(s\)-directions on which \(a\) is constant.  Exact interval-Hessian and third-order remainder records certify finite constants \(K_a,H_w,K_w\).  At every \(C=\Phi_\ell(s,0)\in A_0\), exact endpoint-active weights \(\theta_L(C),\theta_U(C)\ge0\), summing to one, and exact KKT normal-cone multipliers certify that the first feasible transverse derivative of
\[
\overline\ell_C(B)=\theta_L(C)\ell_B(c_L)+\theta_U(C)\ell_B(c_U)
\]
is nonnegative, while \(\overline\ell_C(C)=w(C)\).  Every multiplier sign, eigenvalue bound, tube bound, and remainder bound is an exact CAD, KKT, algebraic sign, or interval calculation; neither quadratic growth nor the desired comparison inequality is an input field.

Third, choose an exact \(0<\rho\le\rho_0\) satisfying
\[
\rho\le\frac{2\lambda_0}{m_0},
\qquad
K_a\rho\le\frac{m_0}{4}.
\tag{R}
\]
On the compact away set \(\{B\in\mathcal K:d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\ge k_-\rho\}\), exact CAD isolation supplies \(\gamma>0\) and \(R<\infty\) with
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\gamma,
\qquad
[q-w(B)]_+\le R.
\tag{A}
\]
Finally, the record contains an exactly feasible algebraic witness \(B_R\in\mathcal K\), an algebraic lower bound \(q_-\) on \(w\) over \(A_0\), and \(\zeta_\Delta>0\) such that
\[
w(B_R)\le q_- -\zeta_\Delta,
\qquad
w(C)\ge q_-\quad(C\in A_0).
\tag{W}
\]

Every valid \(\mathscr G_{\mathrm{CAD}}\) proves
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=A_0,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=A_{\mathrm{mm}},
\qquad
q-v\ge\zeta_\Delta>0.
\]
It also derives the local constants
\[
\mu=\frac{m_0}{4k_+^2},
\qquad
M=\frac{H_w/2+K_w\rho}{k_-^2},
\]
and the global error bound
\[
[q-w(B)]_+
\le L\{a^\star-a(B)\}
\quad(B\in\mathcal K),
\qquad
L=\max\{M/\mu,R/\gamma\}.
\tag{G2}
\]
Consequently, if
\[
e=\sup_{B\in\mathcal K}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|,
\qquad
d=\sup_{B\in\mathcal K}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|,
\]
then every \(\widehat B_0\in\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal K}\widehat g_B(0)\) and \(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal K}\widehat w(B)\) satisfy
\[
w(\widehat B_0)\ge q-2Le,
\qquad
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\le v+2d.
\]
For \(s\in\{0,\mathrm{mm}\}\), define the fixed-pilot selected-basis performance
\[
V_s=
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y).
\]
If certified rank-two observable-versus-oracle bounds give
\[
|V_0-w(\widehat B_0)|\le R_0,
\qquad
|V_{\mathrm{mm}}-w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})|\le R_{\mathrm{mm}},
\]
then
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge\zeta_\Delta-2Le-2d-R_0-R_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{G3}
\]
Thus containment, the global comparison constant, the positive population gap, and the finite observable comparison are derived from the primitive record; they are not maintained hypotheses.
