\textbf{Proof.}
\emph{Step 1: compact algebraic feasible set.}
Use normalized representatives and write
\[
 K_\kappa=\left\{(b_1,b_0):
 \lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
 \ S_B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0\right\}.
\]
Assumption ass:kappa-feasibility makes this set nonempty, and prop:kappa-exhaustion makes its projective quotient compact.  On a radius-\(\sqrt n\) sphere some coordinate has absolute value at least one, so the finitely many signed regions \(\{\pm b_j\ge n^{-1/2}\}\) cover each projective factor.  Retaining the sphere equations and fixing the pivot sign gives a finite semialgebraic chart cover.  Lem:algebraic-box-feasibility-cad decides the intersection of every rational chart box with \(K_\kappa\) and returns an algebraic point whenever that intersection is nonempty, including on the equality boundary.

\emph{Step 2: an effective uniform loss modulus.}
For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), put
\[
 R_e=\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega,
 \qquad S(B)=B^{\top}\Omega B,
 \qquad T_e(B)=B^{\top}R_eB.
\]
All input entries are algebraic.  Exposure positivity and exact sign evaluation provide an algebraic positive lower bound for every diagonal entry of \(\Pi\), while \(\kappa>0\).  Algebraic-number isolation therefore computes rational upper bounds \(M_\Omega\ge\lVert\Omega\rVert_2\) and \(M_e\ge\lVert R_e\rVert_2\), and a rational positive lower bound for \(\kappa\).

Every normalized block matrix has \(\lVert B\rVert_F=\sqrt{2n}\).  If \(d=\lVert B-C\rVert_F\), then
\[
 \lVert S(B)-S(C)\rVert_2\le K_Sd,
 \qquad
 \lVert T_e(B)-T_e(C)\rVert_2\le K_{T,e}d,
\]
where \(K_S=2\sqrt{2n}M_\Omega\) and \(K_{T,e}=2\sqrt{2n}M_e\).  On \(K_\kappa\),
\[
 S(B)\succeq n\kappa I_2,
 \qquad \lVert S(B)^{-1}\rVert_2\le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\]
The resolvent identity and \(\lvert\operatorname{tr}(XY)\rvert\le2\lVert X\rVert_2\lVert Y\rVert_2\) give
\[
 \lvert g_B(c_e)-g_C(c_e)\rvert\le L_e d,
\]
with the computable bound
\[
 L_e=n^{-2}\left\{
 \frac{2K_{T,e}}{n\kappa}
 +\frac{4nM_eK_S}{(n\kappa)^2}\right\}.
\]
The uncontrolled term is basis independent and the maximum map is one-Lipschitz, so
\[
 \lvert w(B)-w(C)\rvert\le L\lVert B-C\rVert_F,
 \qquad L=\max\{L_L,L_U\}.
 \tag{1}
\]
All constants admit outward rational upper bounds.

\emph{Step 3: finite exhaustive depth.}
Choose a rational \(\alpha>0\) with \(4\alpha<\varepsilon\).  Uniform bisection reaches a finite common depth at which every chart box has diameter at most \(\varepsilon\) and at most \(\alpha/L\), with the evident convention if \(L=0\).  There are finitely many such boxes.  CAD discards every infeasible box with a finite certificate and returns an exact algebraic sample \(B_C\) for every feasible box \(C\).  Since \(S(B_C)\succeq n\kappa I_2\), its determinant is positive.  The two endpoint losses at \(B_C\) are therefore real-algebraic numbers obtained from field operations, a two-by-two inverse, trace, and a maximum.  Algebraic isolation terminates with rational numbers \(\underline q_C,\overline q_C\) satisfying
\[
 w(B_C)-\alpha\le\underline q_C\le w(B_C)
 \le\overline q_C\le w(B_C)+\alpha.
 \tag{2}
\]
By (1),
\[
 \lambda_C:=\underline q_C-L\operatorname{diam}(C)
 \le\inf_{B\in C\cap K_\kappa}w(B).
 \tag{3}
\]

Let \(U_\varepsilon\) be the least \(\overline q_C\), let \(B_\varepsilon\) be its algebraic sample, and let \(L_\varepsilon=\min_C\lambda_C\) over feasible boxes.  Compactness and continuity give a minimizer \(B^\star\).  If \(C^\star\) contains it, (1)--(2) imply
\[
 U_\varepsilon\le\overline q_{C^\star}
 \le v_{\mathrm{mm}}+2\alpha.
\]
Every feasible sample has loss at least \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}\), so (2)--(3) give \(\lambda_C\ge v_{\mathrm{mm}}-2\alpha\).  The box containing \(B^\star\) also makes \(L_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Consequently
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}-2\alpha
 \le L_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}
 \le w(B_\varepsilon)\le U_\varepsilon
 \le v_{\mathrm{mm}}+2\alpha,
\]
and \(U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon\le4\alpha<\varepsilon\).

\emph{Step 4: outer minimizer boxes and checking.}
Prune a feasible box only if \(\lambda_C>U_\varepsilon\).  Such a box cannot contain a minimizer because (3) and feasibility of \(B_\varepsilon\) give
\[
 \inf_{B\in C\cap K_\kappa}w(B)
 \ge\lambda_C>U_\varepsilon\ge w(B_\varepsilon).
\]
A box containing a global minimizer has \(\lambda_C\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le U_\varepsilon\) and is retained.  Hence the union \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\) of retained boxes contains \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), and the chosen depth gives \(h_\varepsilon\le\varepsilon\).

Every item checked is finite and local: polynomial signs and CAD cells, algebraic coordinates, rational interval inclusions, chart cover, the minimum of recorded lower bounds, pruning comparisons, and mesh.  The checker does not receive \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}\) or \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\); the preceding inequalities derive their advertised relations.  Finiteness of the chart family, subdivision depth, CAD calls, and algebraic isolations proves termination.  Repeating the construction for any \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) makes both the reported value gap and mesh tend to zero. \(\square\)
