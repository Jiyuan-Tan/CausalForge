At the triangle instance, lem:triangle-completion-singular-strata-certificate and thm:triangle-completion-separation-certificate give the strict singular-stratum margins
\[
\frac{31}{80}-\frac{87}{400}=\frac{17}{100}
\quad\text{and}\quad

\tag{1}
\]
The block reduction
\[
729\Omega_0\sim
\begin{bmatrix}60&96\\96&186\end{bmatrix}
\oplus
\begin{bmatrix}108&-108\\-108&162\end{bmatrix}
\]
has positive leading entries and determinants \(1944\) and \(5832\), so \(\Omega_0\succ0\).  Total-variation and Wasserstein convergence, together with lem:restricted-marginal-extrema-continuity, make \(\Pi\), \(\Omega\), \(Q(c_L)\), and \(Q(c_U)\) continuous at \(\xi_0\).  Hence \(\Omega(\xi)\) remains uniformly positive definite on a sufficiently small relative-open neighborhood.

Represent the note's full quotient by the four compact normalized support components: both columns zero, focal only, comparison only, and both columns nonzero.  On the one-column components the pseudoinverse is a scalar reciprocal bounded away from zero.  On the two-column component, block separation and normalization give
\[
\lVert Bz\rVert_2^2=n\lVert z\rVert_2^2,
\]
so \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq n\lambda_{\min}(\Omega)I_2\).  Thus all endpoint gains and losses are jointly continuous, uniformly over each compact component.  The two strict margins in (1) persist after shrinking the neighborhood.  Consequently no full-carrier nominal or minimax optimizer is singular there.

On the compact two-column component, the optimizer correspondences of the continuous nominal and worst-endpoint criteria are upper hemicontinuous by the elementary subsequence argument: any convergent sequence of optimizers has a limit that beats every fixed competitor after passing the defining inequality to the limit.  At \(\xi_0\), every such optimizer has normalized smallest eigenvalue \(4/27=\kappa+\eta\).  Continuity of \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_B)\) and compactness of the triangle optimizer sets therefore yield, after another shrinkage,
\[
\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}S_B)>\kappa
\tag{2}
\]
for every nearby full-carrier optimizer.  Hence each full-carrier optimizer lies in \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  Conversely, any \(\mathcal B_\kappa\)-optimizer attains the same global value because a global optimizer is already feasible in \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  Lemma lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification now gives
\[
\mathcal A_0^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_0(\xi)),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)).
\tag{3}
\]

Finally, lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity and \(\Delta(\xi_0)=\delta\) imply, by the same sequential contradiction used in thm:triangle-relative-open-dominance, that the neighborhood may be chosen so that
\[
\Delta(\xi)>\frac\delta2.
\tag{4}
\]
