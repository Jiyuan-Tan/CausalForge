\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(\mathcal K_n=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\).  Assumption ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility makes \(\mathcal K_n\) nonempty.  Its normalized representatives form a closed subset of a product of spheres, so the residual sign quotient is compact.  The empirical criteria are continuous in \(B\) and measurable in the finite pilot sample.  Successive coordinate minimization in the fixed finite ordered pivot-chart list therefore produces the stated measurable lexicographic selectors.

Let \(M_n,d_n,e_n\) be as in the theorem.  Lemma lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate gives pathwise
\[
d_n\le C_nM_n,\qquad e_n\le2C_nM_n.
\tag{1}
\]
Choose \(B_n^\star\in\mathcal K_n\) with \(w(B_n^\star)=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}\).  Empirical optimality gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
&\le\widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d_n\\
&\le\widehat w(B_n^\star)+d_n\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+2d_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
The population minimum gives the reverse inequality.  Taking pilot expectations in (2) and using (1) proves the finite regret bound.

Conditional on the pilot, \(\widehat B_s\) is fixed and lies in \(\mathcal K_n\).  Assumption ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence therefore permits application of lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence at this pilot-measurable basis.  Assumption ass:frechet-covariance-domain and thm:fixed-basis-endpoints give
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_B(c_H)=w(B).
\tag{3}
\]
Using \(|\sup f-\sup g|\le\sup|f-g|\), and then integrating over the pilot, yields
\[
|V_s-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)|\le R_n,
\qquad s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}.
\tag{4}
\]
The exact sensitivity condition implies \(nC_n\mathbb E_PM_n\to0\), while the oracle lemma gives \(nR_n\to0\).  Equations (2)--(4) prove the first-order assertions.

For the zero-surrogate claim, empirical optimality and (1) give
\[
g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\le2e_n\le4C_nM_n.
\tag{5}
\]
The displayed deterministic linear error bound then yields
\[
[q_n-w(\widehat B_0)]_+\le4L_nC_nM_n.
\tag{6}
\]
Since \(x\ge q-[q-x]_+\), pilot expectation in (6) proves the finite zero-surrogate inequality.  Boundedness of \(L_n\) and exact sensitivity make its right-hand side \(o(n^{-1})\).  No stronger conclusion is used without this separately stated geometric condition. \(\square\)
