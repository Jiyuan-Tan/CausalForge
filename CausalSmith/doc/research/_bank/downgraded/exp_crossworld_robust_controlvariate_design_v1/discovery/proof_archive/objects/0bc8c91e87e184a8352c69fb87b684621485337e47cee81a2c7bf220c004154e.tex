In the secondary fixed-\(\kappa\), rank-two problem, if \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\{B^\star\}\), then every measurable selection
\[
\widehat B\in\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}\widehat w(B)
\]
converges almost surely to \(B^\star\).  Without the singleton condition there are bounded two-marginal instances satisfying the maintained assumptions for which the outer-set conclusion of thm:plugin-correspondence holds but some measurable restricted plug-in selections fail to converge to a point.  No full-class cross-rank point-convergence claim is made.
