For the triangle design and Bernoulli \((1/2)\) marginals in thm:triangle-minimax-separation-certificate, consider the block-separable Moore--Penrose domain of def:zhao-published-pseudoinverse-domain.  Write \(g_B^{+}(c)\) for the gain obtained by replacing \(S_B^{-1}\) by \(S_B^{+}\) in def:gain-loss, and write \(w^{+}(B)=\max\{h(c_L)-g_B^{+}(c_L),h(c_U)-g_B^{+}(c_U)\}\).  The matrix \(\Omega\) is positive definite, so the rank-zero stratum consists only of \(B=0\), and every rank-one block basis has exactly one nonzero arm column.  After normalizing that column to squared norm three, put \(u=(\mathbf1^{\top}x)^2\in[0,9]\) on the focal-only stratum and \(v=(\mathbf1^{\top}y)^2\in[0,9]\) on the comparison-only stratum.  Then
\[
g_{(x,0)}^{+}(0)=\frac{21141-1225u}{1200(81-4u)},
\qquad
w^{+}(x,0)=\frac{9(1755-17u)}{400(81-4u)},
\]
and
\[
g_{(0,y)}^{+}(0)=\frac{25v+12393}{300(4v+243)},
\qquad
w^{+}(0,y)=\frac{9(256v+2889)}{200(4v+243)}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\sup_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}g_B^{+}(0)=\frac{87}{400}<\frac{31}{80},
\qquad
\inf_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}w^{+}(B)=\frac{39}{80}>\frac{2613}{8432}.
\]
More precisely, the comparison-only and rank-zero lower bounds are \(107/200\) and \(171/200\), respectively.  Thus no singular published-domain basis ties either displayed rank-two optimum.
