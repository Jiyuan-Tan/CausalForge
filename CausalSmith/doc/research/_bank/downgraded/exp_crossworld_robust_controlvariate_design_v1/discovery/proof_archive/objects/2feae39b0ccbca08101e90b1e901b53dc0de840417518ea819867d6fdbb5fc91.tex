Under ass:known-exposure, define \(Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}=Y_i(W)=Y_i(f_i(W))\) on every realized exposure event, and define Zhao's signed armwise Horvitz--Thompson stack
\[
\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}=
\left(\frac{D_{1,1}Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{1,1}},\ldots,
\frac{D_{n,1}Y_n^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{n,1}},
-\frac{D_{1,0}Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{1,0}},\ldots,
-\frac{D_{n,0}Y_n^{\mathrm{obs}}}{\pi_{n,0}}\right)^{\top}.
\]
For \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), define Zhao's published pseudoinverse coefficient
\[
\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)
=(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}.
\]
Known exposure and Horvitz--Thompson unbiasedness give
\[
\mathbb E_\nu[B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}]
=B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y.
\]
Define
\[
\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}
=n^{-1}\mathbf1^{\top}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}
-\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)^{\top}
\{n^{-1}B^{\top}D-\mathbb E_\nu[n^{-1}B^{\top}D]\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), \((B^{\top}\Omega B)^+=(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}\), so the coefficient and estimator reduce to the ordinary-inverse rank-two formulas used by the finite-rate results.  Separately define \(\widehat1_a=n^{-1}\sum_iD_{i,a}/\pi_{i,a}\), \(\widehat\gamma_a^{\mathrm{SN}}(B)=\widehat\gamma_a^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)/\widehat1_a\) when \(\widehat1_a>0\) and zero otherwise, and obtain \(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV,SN}}\) by substitution.  No first-order equivalence for the self-normalized variant is asserted.
