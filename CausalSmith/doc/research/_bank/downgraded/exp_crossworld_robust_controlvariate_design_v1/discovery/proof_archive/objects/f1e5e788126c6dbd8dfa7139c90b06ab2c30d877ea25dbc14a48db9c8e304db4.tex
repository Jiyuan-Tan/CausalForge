\textbf{Proof.}
For the \(n\)-th problem, write
\[
v_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\qquad
q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B).
\tag{1}
\]
By hypothesis, the full ordinary-domain minimax and zero-covariance optimizer sets are nonempty and are contained in \(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\).  Since \(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\), a full minimax optimizer is feasible for the restricted problem, and every restricted minimizer is consequently also a full minimizer.  Hence
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=v_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Similarly, a full zero-covariance optimizer is feasible for the restricted maximization, so the restricted maximum equals \(a^\star\).  Every restricted maximizer therefore attains the full-domain maximum, while every full zero-covariance optimizer belongs to the restricted class by hypothesis.  Thus
\[
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\mathcal A_{0,Z}.
\tag{3}
\]
The infimum semantics in thm:ordinary-inverse-strict-improvement-characterization and def:ordinary-intercept-slope-image now give
\[
\Delta_Z=q_n-v_n.
\tag{4}
\]
No attainment of the slope-penalty infimum on \(\mathcal A_{0,Z}\) is used in (4).

The robust-selector conclusion and the bounded deterministic linear error-bound conclusion of thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer, together with (2)--(3), yield
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_n+o(n^{-1}),
\qquad
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)\ge q_n-o(n^{-1}).
\tag{5}
\]
Subtracting the two relations in (5) and using (4) gives
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\Delta_Z-o(n^{-1}).
\tag{6}
\]

For \(s\in\{0,\mathrm{mm}\}\), the feasible-estimator bridge in the same theorem states
\[
\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
\!\left(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}
\mid\widehat B_s,Y\right)
\right]
=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1}).
\tag{7}
\]
Substitute (7) for both selectors in (6).  After multiplication by \(n\), the resulting lower bound is
\[
n\Delta_Z+o(1).
\]
Taking lower limits and using \(\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\Delta_Z>0\) proves the displayed strict ordering.  Every variance in this argument is conditional design variance under \(\nu\); the expectations over the independent pilot and the potential-outcome coupling are ex ante evaluations and do not introduce a superpopulation sampling estimand or a Wald limit law. \(\square\)
