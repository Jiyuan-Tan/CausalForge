We verify that these are global rank-two statements, not artifacts of the threshold restriction.  Lem:triangle-completion-singular-strata-certificate proves \(\Omega\succ0\).  Every normalized block-separable matrix with two nonzero arm columns therefore satisfies \(S_B\succ0\).  In the polynomial certificates of thm:triangle-minimax-separation-certificate, the \(\kappa\)-condition is used only to ensure precisely this positivity of the denominator \(\det(S_B)\).
