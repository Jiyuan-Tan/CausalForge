For a fixed \(\overline a\in[1,\infty)\), define
\[
\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}
=\left\{[B]\in\mathcal B_\kappa:
\max_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}|b_{a,i}|\le\overline a\right\}.
\]
This is the normalized Zhao-rate-admissible class.  The coordinate bound is imposed explicitly and is not inferred from \(\lVert b_a\rVert_2^2=n\).  Its \(\kappa\)-regularity gives \(\lambda_{\min}(B^{\top}\Omega B)\ge n\kappa\), so each supplied member instantiates the boundedness and nondegeneracy clauses of Zhao's Assumption 2\(^{\dagger}\).  This definition alone does not assert nonemptiness; nonemptiness is the separate design-side condition ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility.
