Assume the conditions of thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer, including its explicit first-order stability clause for the zero-surrogate argmax.  Suppose the attained ordinary minimax and zero-covariance optimizer sets lie in \(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\) and
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\Delta_Z>0.
\]
Then the independent-pilot, ordinary-inverse Horvitz--Thompson deployments satisfy
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\left[
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_0}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_0,Y)
\right]
-
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}}}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}},Y)
\right]
\right]>0.
\]
Thus a positive first-order ordinary-domain intercept--slope gap transfers to the feasible independent-pilot estimator.  The conclusion is about ex ante expected conditional design variance; it is not a superpopulation sampling variance or a Wald limit law.
