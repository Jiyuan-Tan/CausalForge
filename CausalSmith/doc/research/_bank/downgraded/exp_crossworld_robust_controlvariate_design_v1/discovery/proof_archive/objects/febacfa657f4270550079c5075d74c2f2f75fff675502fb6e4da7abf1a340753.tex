\textbf{Proof.}
The optimizer-wide slack in the hypothesis is precisely the premise of lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity.  Suppose that no relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) had positive gap.  The relative topology in def:lawful-instance-class is metrizable, so for every integer \(j\ge1\) there would exist \(\xi_j\in\mathfrak X_\kappa\) at distance less than \(1/j\) from \(\xi_0\) with \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le0\).  Then \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\), whereas lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity gives
\[
0\ge\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)
\ge\Delta(\xi_0)>0,
\]
a contradiction.  Hence some relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\) of \(\xi_0\) satisfies \(\Delta(\xi)>0\) for every \(\xi\in\mathcal U\).  \(\square\)
