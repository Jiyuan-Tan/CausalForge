\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\ I_n&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad A_{\mathrm{HT}}=\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}.
\]
By def:moment-matrix, \(Q(c)=Q(0)-cE\).  For each unit \(i\), ass:mutually-exclusive-exposures gives \(D_{i,1}D_{i,0}=0\) almost surely.  Hence
\[
\Omega_{i,n+i}=\operatorname{Cov}_{\nu}(D_{i,1},D_{i,0})
=-\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}.
\]
Ass:exposure-positivity permits division by both probabilities.  Since \(A_{\mathrm{HT}}\) is symmetric,
\[
\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)
=2\sum_{i=1}^n
\frac{\Omega_{i,n+i}}{\pi_{i,1}\pi_{i,0}}
=-2n.
\]
Therefore cyclicity and linearity of trace imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
h(c)
&=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_{\mathrm{HT}}(Q(0)-cE)\}\\
&=h(0)-cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}}E)
=h(0)+\frac{2c}{n}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Substitution into \(w(B)=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\{h(c_e)-g_B(c_e)\}\) proves the displayed expression for \(w\).

It remains to make the non-equivalence precise.  Put \(a_e=2c_e/n\) and, for a formal pair \(x=(x_L,x_U)\), define
\[
F(x)=\max\{a_L-x_L,a_U-x_U\},
\qquad G(x)=\max\{-x_L,-x_U\}.
\]
Minimizing worst residual uses \(F\), up to the constant \(h(0)\), whereas maximizing minimum gain is equivalent to minimizing \(G=-\min\{x_L,x_U\}\).  If \(F(x)=G(x)+K\) for every \(x\in\mathbb R^2\), then taking \((x_L,x_U)=(t,0)\) and sending \(t\to\infty\) gives \(K=a_U\); taking \((x_L,x_U)=(0,t)\) gives \(K=a_L\).  Thus such an algebraic identity requires \(a_L=a_U\), equivalently \(c_L=c_U\).  When \(c_L<c_U\), no such identity exists. \(\square\)
