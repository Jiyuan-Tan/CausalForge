\textbf{Proof.}
If \(0<\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\), then \(\mathcal B_{\kappa_2}\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}\), so minimizing the same continuous loss over the larger compact set proves the first inequality.  Prop:kappa-exhaustion gives \(\mathcal B_Z=\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa\).  The infimum of a function over a union is the infimum of the infima over its nonempty members, whence
\[
\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
=\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B). \tag{1}
\]
Thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer makes \(q_Z\) surjective and shows that \(w(q_Z(B))\) is constant on every fiber.  Hence
\[
\inf_{B\in\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z}w(q_Z(B))
=\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B), \tag{2}
\]
which proves the full value equality.

If \(B_Z^\star\) attains the quotient infimum, prop:kappa-exhaustion supplies \(\kappa_0>0\) with \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_0}\).  For \(0<\kappa\le\kappa_0\),
\[
\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
\le\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
\le w(B_Z^\star)
=\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
\]
This proves equality.  Equations (1)--(2) concern only ordinary-invertible matrices and their rescaling quotient. \(\square\)
