The paper derives the endpoint-affine residual criterion, certifies epsilon-global minimization on the fixed-\(\kappa\) rank-two class and the full published pseudoinverse carrier, characterizes value transfer across rank and threshold strata, proves plug-in value and correspondence guarantees, and gives an exact triangle certificate with relative-open strict dominance over the zero-covariance surrogate.  It also connects independent-pilot basis selection to the displayed observable Horvitz--Thompson-coefficient estimator on a coordinate-bounded regular class, while keeping the unbridged self-normalized application variant separate.
