The causal target is the fixed finite-population exposure contrast \(\tau\).  The law \(H\), its marginals, and the endpoint losses are used only to choose and evaluate a basis before the main randomization; conditional on the chosen basis and fixed potential outcomes, assignment under \(\nu\) is the sole randomness in the estimator.  A smaller value of \(w(B)\) is therefore an ex ante certificate for worst-copula expected conditional design variance, not a new identification formula or a superpopulation variance claim.
