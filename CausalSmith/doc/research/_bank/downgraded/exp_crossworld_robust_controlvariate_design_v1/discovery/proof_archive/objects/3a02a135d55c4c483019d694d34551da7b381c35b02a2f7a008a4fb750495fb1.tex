\textbf{Proof.}
The normalized quotient carrier is a finite union of signed-pivot semialgebraic charts.  On those charts the coordinate envelope is the finite family \(-\overline a\le b_{a,i}\le\overline a\).  Since \(S_B\succeq0\), the three stable rank conditions are exactly
\[
\operatorname{tr}(S_B)=0,
\tag{1}
\]
\[
\det(S_B)=0,qquad \operatorname{tr}(S_B)\ge n\lambda_{1,n},
\tag{2}
\]
and, writing \(S_B-n\lambda_{2,n}I_2=\begin{bmatrix}u&v\\v&z\end{bmatrix}\),
\[
u\ge0,qquad z\ge0,qquad uz-v^2\ge0.
\tag{3}
\]
Condition (1) is rank zero.  In (2), the positive trace and zero determinant give rank one and identify the unique positive eigenvalue with the trace.  Condition (3), together with \(\lambda_{2,n}>0\), makes both eigenvalues of \(S_B\) at least \(n\lambda_{2,n}\), hence gives rank two.  Thus (1)--(3), the normalization, and the coordinate inequalities describe precisely \(\mathcal K_n^{\mathrm{st}}\).  The all-zero class satisfies (1), so this set is nonempty.  Each piece is closed in the compact normalized carrier; hence their finite union is compact.

On (1) the pseudoinverse is zero; on (2) it is \(S_B/\{\operatorname{tr}(S_B)\}^2\), whose denominator is bounded below by \(n^2\lambda_{1,n}^2>0\); and on (3) it is \(\operatorname{adj}(S_B)/\det(S_B)\), whose determinant is bounded below by \(n^2\lambda_{2,n}^2>0\).  Therefore the empirical loss is continuous on each stable piece and has a rational semialgebraic graph after clearing certified positive denominators.  Cylindrical algebraic decomposition and real-algebraic root isolation consequently terminate and return an algebraic stable class within \(\varepsilon_m\) of the attained empirical stable minimum.  The same exact signs certify its coordinate envelope and selected rank.  The characteristic-polynomial argument in the proof of thm:encoded-cad-pilot-selector-certificate gives the positive-root isolations and verifies the applicable margin.

For the population price, run the same value-isolation procedure on the exact population endpoint input once over \(\mathcal K_n^{\mathrm{st}}\) and once over \(\mathcal B_Z^+\).  Refine the two isolations until
\[
L_{\mathrm{st}}\le v_n^{\mathrm{st}}\le U_{\mathrm{st}},
\qquad
L_+\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\le U_+,
\qquad
(U_{\mathrm{st}}-L_{\mathrm{st}})+(U_+-L_+)\le\varepsilon_m.
\tag{4}
\]
Because \(\mathcal K_n^{\mathrm{st}}\subseteq\mathcal B_Z^+\), one has \(b_n^{\mathrm{st}}=v_n^{\mathrm{st}}-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\ge0\).  Hence
\[
\underline b_{n,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{st}}
=\max\{0,L_{\mathrm{st}}-U_+\},
\qquad
\overline b_{n,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{st}}
=U_{\mathrm{st}}-L_+
\tag{5}
\]
enclose \(b_n^{\mathrm{st}}\).  Their width is at most the sum in (4), hence at most \(\varepsilon_m\).  All signs, samples, and isolating intervals used in (1)--(5) are finite and locally checkable. \(\square\)
