\textbf{Proof.}
At \(\xi_0\), thm:triangle-completion-separation-certificate gives the ordinary gap \(\delta>0\), optimizer-wide slack \(\eta>0\), and strict singular-stratum gaps
\[
\frac{31}{80}-\frac{87}{400}=\frac{17}{100}>0,
\qquad
\frac{39}{80}-\frac{2613}{8432}=\frac{468}{2635}>0. \tag{1}
\]
Total-variation convergence of the finite assignment law makes \(\Pi\) and \(\Omega\) continuous, and exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) continuous nearby.  Wasserstein convergence of bounded marginals gives moment convergence, while lem:restricted-marginal-extrema-continuity gives endpoint convergence.  Therefore both endpoint moment matrices converge.  The triangle matrix \(\Omega(\xi_0)\) is positive definite, so \(\Omega(\xi)\) remains uniformly positive definite after restricting to a relative-open neighborhood.

Under the normalization in def:novel-pseudoinverse-completion, the rank-zero component is a singleton, each rank-one component is a sphere modulo sign, and the rank-two component is a product of two such quotients.  These are compact components separated by whether an arm column is zero.  With \(\Omega(\xi)\succ0\), the rank is constant on each component, and the projector representation makes \(g_B^+(c)\) and \(w^+(B)\) jointly continuous there.  Thus every componentwise optimum value is continuous.  The strict inequalities (1) persist on a smaller relative-open neighborhood, forcing every completion optimizer for either criterion to have rank two.

The optimizer-wide \(\kappa\)-slack also persists.  Otherwise, there would be \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) and rank-two completion optimizers whose normalized ordinary preimages have smallest eigenvalue below \(\kappa+\eta/2\).  Compactness supplies a convergent subsequence.  Joint continuity makes its limit a rank-two optimizer at \(\xi_0\), contradicting the exact slack.  Hence every nearby completion optimizer has an ordinary preimage in \(\mathcal B_\kappa(\xi)\).  Rank-two identification now proves
\[
\mathcal A_0^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_0(\xi)),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+(\xi)=\iota_\xi(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)). \tag{2}
\]

Finally, lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity and the exact gap at \(\xi_0\) yield a still smaller relative-open neighborhood on which \(\Delta(\xi)>\delta/2\).  For every pair of ordinary optimizers, the loss difference is at least \(\Delta(\xi)\), hence exceeds \(\delta/2\).  Equation (2) and preservation of loss under \(\iota_\xi\) give the same conclusion for every pair of completion optimizers. \(\square\)
