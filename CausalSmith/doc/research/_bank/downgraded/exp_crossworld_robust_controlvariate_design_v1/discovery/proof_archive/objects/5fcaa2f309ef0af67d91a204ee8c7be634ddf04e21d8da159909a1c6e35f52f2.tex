Along a triangular sequence satisfying ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate and ass:zhao-dependency-neighborhood-rate, let \(\widehat Q_L,\widehat Q_U\) be the two endpoint matrices obtained from the unpaired empirical marginals, and put
\[
C_n=n^{-2}\left[
\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_*
+\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}
\left\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\rVert_*
\right].
\]
Under ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot,
\[
\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_{L^1}\!\left(\frac{n}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}\right),
\qquad
C_n=O\!\left(n^{\alpha+2\beta-1}\right).
\]
Moreover,
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}
|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|
\le C_n\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}},
\]
and the same bound without the uncontrolled term holds for \(\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)\).  Hence the exact sensitivity condition
\[
nC_n\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{in }L^1
\]
implies uniform first-order plug-in accuracy.  A sufficient bounded-pilot condition is
\[
\frac{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}{n^{1+\alpha+2\beta}}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
