For every \(\kappa>0\), the quotient \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is compact and satisfies \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\).  Conversely, every \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\) has normalized representatives belonging to \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) for some basis-dependent \(\kappa>0\), so \(\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa=\mathcal B_Z\).  Here \(\mathcal B_Z\) is the nondegenerate ordinary-inverse projective repair of the block-diagonal Zhao domain: the union statement covers every nonsingular implementable Zhao-type basis through a basis-dependent threshold, but it neither identifies any fixed \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) with the published class nor covers singular or pseudoinverse bases.
