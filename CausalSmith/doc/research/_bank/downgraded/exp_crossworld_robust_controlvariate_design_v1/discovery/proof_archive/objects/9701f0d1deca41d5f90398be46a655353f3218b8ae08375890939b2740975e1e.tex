\textbf{Proof.}
All coefficients lie in the real-algebraic field generated by the input, and exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) algebraic.  For each arm-support pattern \(J\subseteq\{1,0\}\), normalize by
\[
b_a=0\ (a\notin J),\qquad \lVert b_a\rVert_2^2=n\ (a\in J).
\tag{1}
\]
These four compact algebraic sets cover \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) modulo independent signs.  Since \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succeq0\), its ranks are exactly the semialgebraic cases
\[
S_B=0;\qquad \det S_B=0,\ \operatorname{tr}S_B>0;\qquad \det S_B>0.
\tag{2}
\]
Introduce a symmetric \(2\)-by-\(2\) variable \(T\) and impose the polynomial Penrose equations
\[
S_BTS_B=S_B,\quad TS_BT=T,\quad S_BT=TS_B,\quad T=T^{\top}.
\tag{3}
\]
In a simultaneous spectral basis, (3) uniquely forces \(T=S_B^+\): a positive eigenvalue is reciprocated and a zero eigenvalue receives zero.  Thus (3) is the exact graph of Zhao's inverse convention on every rank.

For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), put \(R_e=\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega\) and
\[
L_e(B,T)=h(c_e)-n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(TB^{\top}R_eB).
\tag{4}
\]
On (3), \(L_e=\ell_B^+(c_e)\) and \(w^+(B)=\max_eL_e(B,T)\).  Lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives \(g_B^+(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e)\), where \(A_e\succeq0\) and \(0\preceq P_B\preceq I\).  Since \(h(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_e)\),
\[
0\le L_e(B,S_B^+)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{(I-P_B)A_e\}\le h(c_e).
\tag{5}
\]
The zero basis is feasible, so the full infimum is finite.

For each support pattern and rank, form
\[
\Phi_{J,r}(z):\quad \exists B\,\exists T\,[\text{(1), the rank condition (2), (3), and }L_L(B,T),L_U(B,T)\le z].
\tag{6}
\]
This is a first-order real-closed-field formula.  Lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination returns a finite equivalent quantifier-free formula.  The disjunction over the finitely many \((J,r)\) therefore describes
\[
\mathcal V=\{z:\exists[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+,\ w^+(B)\le z\}.
\tag{7}
\]
A semialgebraic subset of the line is a finite union of points and intervals with real-algebraic finite endpoints.  By (5), \(\mathcal V\) is nonempty and bounded below.  Its lower endpoint is real algebraic and, by (7), equals \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  Exact root isolation and the CAD sign table certify that no cell below it satisfies (6) and that feasible cells occur arbitrarily close above it.  This certifies the infimum without assuming that the endpoint belongs to \(\mathcal V\).

For exactly encoded \(\varepsilon>0\), the definition of infimum supplies a basis with \(w^+(B)<v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon\).  Substituting the algebraic threshold \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++\varepsilon\) in (6), CAD lifting returns an algebraic sample and finite sign certificates for (1)--(4) and both endpoint inequalities.  The rank disjunction certifies the global comparison across ranks zero, one, and two.  The argument is finite instance by instance but provides no exact argmin, outer-set convergence, uniform complexity, or SDP representation. \(\square\)
