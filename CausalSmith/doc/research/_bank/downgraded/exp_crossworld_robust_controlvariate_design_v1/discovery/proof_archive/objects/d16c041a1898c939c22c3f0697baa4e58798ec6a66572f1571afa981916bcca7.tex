\textbf{Proof.}
Enumerating all eight assignments gives \(\pi_{i,1}=4/27\), \(\pi_{i,0}=10/27\), and the covariance table
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&i=j&i\ne j\\ \hline
\operatorname{Cov}(D_{i,1},D_{j,1})&92/729&-16/729\\
\operatorname{Cov}(D_{i,1},D_{j,0})&-40/729&68/729\\
\operatorname{Cov}(D_{i,0},D_{j,0})&170/729&8/729.
\end{array}
\]
This proves the displayed \(\Pi\) and \(\Omega\), with positivity and mutual exclusivity.  Bernoulli \((1/2)\) quantiles give \(c_L=-1/4\), \(c_U=1/4\), and substitution gives the two endpoint matrices.

For normalized \(B(x,y)\), put \(s=\mathbf1^\top x\), \(t=\mathbf1^\top y\), and \(q=x^\top y\).  Direct block multiplication gives
\[
S_B=\begin{bmatrix}4/9-16s^2/729&-4q/27+68st/729\\
-4q/27+68st/729&2/3+8t^2/729\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{1}
\]
Writing \(d=729+12t^2-36s^2-54q^2+68qst-22s^2t^2\), one has \(\det S_B=8d/19683>0\) on \(\mathcal B_{1/8}\).  Exact trace reduction yields
\[
\frac{31}{80}-g_B(0)=\frac{N_0}{1200d},
\quad
N_0=5022q^2-4352qst+670s^2t^2+1629s^2+2148t^2.
\tag{2}
\]
Completing the square in \(q\), the remainder is bilinear in \((s^2,t^2)\in[0,9]^2\) with nonnegative vertex values and equality only at \(s=t=0\).  Thus (2) has equality exactly at \(s=t=q=0\).  Substitution gives \(w=521/1200\) there.

For the minimax loss, with \(\lambda=1407/2635\) and \(v=2613/8432\),
\[
w(B)-v\ge\lambda\ell_B(c_L)+(1-\lambda)\ell_B(c_U)-v
=\frac{3N_{\mathrm{mm}}}{210800d},
\]
where
\[
N_{\mathrm{mm}}=274734\left(q-\frac{70411}{137367}st\right)^2
+s^2\left(34527-\frac{439017980}{137367}t^2\right)\ge0.
\tag{3}
\]
The last coefficient is positive for \(t^2\le9\).  Equality in (3) requires \(s=q=0\), and equality in the convex-combination lower bound further requires
\[
\ell_B(c_L)-\ell_B(c_U)
=-\frac{3(55t^2-216)}{10(4t^2+243)}=0.
\]
Thus the minimizers are exactly \(s=q=0\), \(t^2=216/55\).  Both equality sets are nonempty, and (1) gives normalized smallest eigenvalue \(4/27\) on them.  Therefore the gap and slack are
\[
\frac{521}{1200}-\frac{2613}{8432}=\frac{4912}{39525},
\qquad \frac4{27}-\frac18=\frac5{216}.
\]
Known exposure supplies the causal interpretation of the two labels. \(\square\)
