\textbf{Proof.}
Congruence and exposure positivity give
\[
A_e=\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}Q(c_e)\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\succeq0.
\]
With \(Z_B=\Omega^{1/2}B\), cyclicity of trace yields
\[
n^2g_B^+(c_e)=\operatorname{tr}\!\left{
Z_B(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top}A_e\right}.
\tag{1}
\]
The singular-value decomposition shows that
\(P_B=Z_B(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)^+Z_B^{\top}\) is the orthogonal projector onto \(\operatorname{range}(Z_B)\), and
\[
\operatorname{rank}(P_B)=\operatorname{rank}(Z_B^{\top}Z_B)
=\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\in\{0,1,2\}.
\]
The rank-two conclusion follows from lem:zhao-rank-two-stratum-identification.

For a singular representative \(B^\circ\), choose a rank-two representative \(C^\circ\) and put \(B_t^\circ=B^\circ+tC^\circ\).  The determinant of \((Z_{B^\circ}+tZ_{C^\circ})^{\top}(Z_{B^\circ}+tZ_{C^\circ})\) is a nonzero polynomial, so a sequence \(t_j\downarrow0\) can avoid its finitely many roots.  Normalize both nonzero columns and call the resulting ordinary classes \(B_j\).  A convergent subsequence of their rank-two projectors has a limit \(P_\infty\) whose range contains \(\operatorname{range}(Z_{B^\circ})\), hence \(P_\infty-P_B\succeq0\).  Since \(A_e\succeq0\), (1) gives
\[
\lim_jg_{B_j}(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_\infty A_e)
\ge g_B^+(c_e),\qquad e\in\{L,U\}.
\]
Subtracting from the common uncontrolled endpoint terms and taking the maximum proves \(\limsup_jw(B_j)\le w^+(B)\). \(\square\)
