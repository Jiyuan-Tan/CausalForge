\textbf{Proof.}
The class \(\mathcal K\) is nonempty and compact by normalization, the coordinate envelope, and the weak inequality \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\).  The functions \(a\) and \(w\) are continuous on it because the ordinary inverse is continuous under that uniform eigenvalue bound.  Thus \(A_0\) and \(A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) are nonempty compact sets and \(q\) is attained.

Every ordinary-domain projective class has a normalized representative in \(\mathcal Z\).  By (G1), every point in \(\mathcal Z\setminus\mathcal K\) has nominal value strictly below \(a^\star\) and robust loss strictly above \(v\).  Hence no ordinary-domain optimizer lies outside \(\mathcal K\), and
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=A_0,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=A_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{1}
\]

We next derive, rather than assume, the local inequalities.  Use the common semialgebraic refinement from the proposed record, so every point of \(N_q\) lies on a stratum carrying the displayed weighted-endpoint data.  Fix a chart and a stratum \(V_j\), and let \(C\in V_j\).  In its certified tubular coordinates write a nearby feasible point as
\[
B=\Psi_j(s,t,z),
\tag{2}
\]
where \(s\) is tangent to \(V_j\), \(t\) is feasible tangent and transverse to \(V_j\), and \(z\) consists of inward active-normal coordinates.  The certified singular-value and derivative bounds, after shrinking finitely many tubes to one common radius, give constants \(c_-,c_+>0\) such that
\[
c_-\lVert(t,z)\rVert_2
\le d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)
\le c_+\lVert(t,z)\rVert_2.
\tag{3}
\]
KKT stationarity removes the free tangent term.  Strict complementarity supplies a positive first-order loss in every nonzero feasible active-normal coordinate, while the reduced Lagrangian Hessian supplies a positive quadratic nominal loss in the transverse tangent directions.  The recorded interval remainders and Young's inequality therefore yield, uniformly over the finite chart-stratum cover,
\[
a^\star-a(B)
\ge c_t\lVert t\rVert_2^2+c_z\lVert z\rVert_1
\tag{4}
\]
for constants \(c_t,c_z>0\).  On a fixed tube, the linear normal term dominates a constant multiple of \(\lVert z\rVert_2^2\).  Combining this fact with (3)--(4) produces \(\rho>0\) and \(\mu>0\) such that
\[
a^\star-a(B)\ge\mu d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\quad\text{when }d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\le\rho.
\tag{5}
\]

For \(C\in N_q\), define the supported endpoint combination
\[
\phi_C(B)=\theta_L(C)\ell_B(c_L)+\theta_U(C)\ell_B(c_U).
\tag{6}
\]
Support on active endpoint losses gives \(\phi_C(C)=w(C)\ge q\), and convexity of a maximum gives \(w(B)\ge\phi_C(B)\).  The certified nonnegative first feasible directional derivative at \(C\), together with the endpoint Hessian and remainder bounds, yields
\[
\phi_C(B)\ge\phi_C(C)-K,d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\tag{7}
\]
through a uniform tube.  Hence \([q-w(B)]_+\le Kd_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2\) there.  On the compact set \(A_0\setminus N_q\), the recorded strictly positive \(w-q\) gap and the interval continuity modulus make the same positive part identically zero in a uniform tube.  A finite subcover and a possible further reduction of \(\rho\) therefore give some \(M<\infty\) such that
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le M d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)^2
\quad\text{when }d_{\mathrm{pr}}(B,A_0)\le\rho.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (5) and (8) are (G4).

On the local tube, division of (8) by (5) gives (G6) with constant \(M/\mu\).  On its compact complement, (G2) gives
\[
[q-w(B)]_+\le R\le(R/\gamma)\{a^\star-a(B)\}.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus (G6) holds globally with \(L=\max\{M/\mu,R/\gamma\}\).  From the feasible witness (G3),
\[
v\le w(B_R)\le q-\zeta_\Delta,
\tag{10}
\]
so \(q-v\ge\zeta_\Delta>0\).  Together with (1), this proves (G5).

Let \(e,d\) be the two empirical uniform errors.  Empirical nominal optimality gives
\[
a^\star-a(\widehat B_0)\le2e.
\tag{11}
\]
Applying (G6) to (11) gives \(w(\widehat B_0)\ge q-2Le\).  If \(B^\star\in A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), empirical robust optimality similarly gives
\[
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\le\widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d
\le\widehat w(B^\star)+d
\le v+2d.
\tag{12}
\]
Finally, the two bridge inequalities imply
\[
V_0\ge w(\widehat B_0)-R_0,
\qquad
V_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+R_{\mathrm{mm}}.
\tag{13}
\]
Subtracting (12)--(13) from (11) and using \(q-v>0\) proves (G7).  The bridge errors in (13) may be supplied by \(\texttt{thm:rank-adaptive-oracle-observable-variance-bridge}\) only after its spectral and coordinate conditions have been certified, exactly as stated. \(\square\)
