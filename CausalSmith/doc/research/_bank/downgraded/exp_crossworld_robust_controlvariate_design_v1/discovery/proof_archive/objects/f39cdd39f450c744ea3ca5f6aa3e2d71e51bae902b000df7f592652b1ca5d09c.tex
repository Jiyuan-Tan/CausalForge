\textbf{Proof.}
Because \(\mathcal B_Z\subseteq\mathcal B_Z^+\) and the two losses agree on rank two,
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 =\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
 \le\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
 \tag{1}
\]
Conversely, lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives, for every singular \(B\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), a rank-two sequence with \(\limsup_jw(B_j)\le w^+(B)\).  Hence
\[
 \inf_{C\in\mathcal B_Z}w(C)\le w^+(B)
\]
for every singular \(B\); the same inequality is immediate for rank-two \(B\).  Taking the infimum over the full domain and combining with (1) proves
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
\]
Prop:kappa-value-exhaustion gives the second equality.

For the optimizer identity, take a rank-two \(B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  Prop:kappa-exhaustion supplies \(\kappa_B>0\) with \(B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_B}\).  Since the global infimum is \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=w(B)\),
\[
 v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \le\min_{C\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_B}}w(C)
 \le w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\]
Thus \(B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa_B)\) at a value-attaining threshold.  Conversely, any member of a fixed-threshold argmin whose minimum equals \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is rank two and belongs to the published equality set.  This proves the union formula.  The last displayed identity is the rank-at-most-one part of the definition of \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\); it explicitly records all singular ties and makes no existence assertion across the rank discontinuity. \(\square\)
