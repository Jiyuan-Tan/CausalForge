\textbf{Proof.}
For \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\) supplies the orthogonal projector
\[
P_B=\Omega^{1/2}B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega^{1/2},
\]
so \(0\preceq P_B\preceq I_{2n}\).  Congruence by \(\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\), licensed by exposure positivity, gives
\[
0\preceq A_B^+
=\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}P_B\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}
\preceq \Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}=A_{\mathrm{HT}}.
\tag{1}
\]
Both matrices are positive semidefinite, so their nuclear norms equal their traces.  Since \(D_{i,a}\) is Bernoulli of mean \(\pi_{i,a}\),
\[
\Omega_{(i,a),(i,a)}=\pi_{i,a}(1-\pi_{i,a}).
\tag{2}
\]
Thus, with \(\pi_{\min,n}=\min_{i,a}\pi_{i,a}>0\),
\[
\lVert A_B^+\rVert_*
\le\operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}})
=\sum_{i,a}\frac{1-\pi_{i,a}}{\pi_{i,a}}
\le2n\pi_{\min,n}^{-1},
\qquad
\lVert A_{\mathrm{HT}}\rVert_*le2n\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}.
\tag{3}
\]
For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), the empirical and population loss definitions give
\[
\widehat\ell_{B,e}^+-\ell_B^+(c_e)
=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[(A_{\mathrm{HT}}-A_B^+)
\{\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\}\right].
\tag{4}
\]
Trace duality, (3), and the one-Lipschitz property of the maximum in the supremum norm imply
\[
d_m^+\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m.
\tag{5}
\]
Under \(\texttt{ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate}\), \(\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}\le\underline\pi^{-1}n^\beta\), which proves (A).

For the expectation rate, put \(D_a=W_1(\widehat F_a,F_a)\).  On \([0,1]\), the distribution-function and quantile formulas for \(W_1\), Jensen's inequality, and the binomial variance identity give
\[
\mathbb E_PD_a
\le\int_0^1\sqrt{\frac{F_a(y)\{1-F_a(y)\}}{m_a}}\,dy
\le\frac1{2\sqrt{m_a}}.
\tag{6}
\]
The one- and two-Lipschitz moment maps and
\(|xy-x'y'|\le|x-x'|+|y-y'|\) give
\[
|\widehat\mu_a-\mu_a|\le D_a,
\quad |\widehat\sigma_a^2-\sigma_a^2|\le4D_a,
\quad |\widehat c_e-c_e|\le2(D_1+D_0).
\tag{7}
\]
Using the block formula for \(Q\), \(\lVert I_n\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=1\), and \(\lVert\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\top}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=n\), there is a numerical \(K_M<\infty\) such that, for \(m=m_1\wedge m_0\),
\[
\mathbb E_PM_m\le K_M\frac n{\sqrt m}.
\tag{8}
\]

By \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector}\), pointwise in the pilot,
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{9}
\]
For two functions on the same nonempty domain,
\[
|\inf f-\inf g|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{10}
\]
The carrier is nonempty because it contains the zero class.  Applying (10), (9), and the definition of \(d_m^+\) yields
\[
0\le w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{11}
\]
Taking expectations and using (A) proves (B).  Finally, (8) gives
\[
n\mathbb E_P\!\left[w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\right]
\le8K_M\underline\pi^{-1}\frac{n^{1+\beta}}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}
+n\mathbb E_P\varepsilon_m\longrightarrow0.
\tag{12}
\]
The rank claim follows from the selector construction.  The proof controls the all-rank basis objective and does not assert an observable-estimator bridge at an uncontrolled singular rank. \(\square\)
