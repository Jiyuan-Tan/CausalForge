\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(N=2n\), index exposure coordinates by \(\ell\), and write \(Z_\ell=D_\ell-\pi_\ell\).  Known exposure and the signed convention in \(Y\) give
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\widehat Y_\ell^{\mathrm{obs}}=Y_\ell,
\qquad
\{\Pi^{-1}(\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}-Y)\}_\ell
=\frac{Y_\ell Z_\ell}{\pi_\ell^2}.
\tag{1}
\]
Exposure positivity licenses every division.

If \(S_B=0\), then \(S_B^+=0\).  Since \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B=0\) and \(\Omega\succeq0\), \(\Omega^{1/2}B=0\), hence \(B^{\top}\Omega=0\).  Both observable and oracle coefficients vanish.  Substitution in \(\texttt{def:observable-coefficient-map}\) gives exactly \(n^{-1}\mathbf1^{\top}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}\), proving every rank-zero assertion.

Fix \(r\in\{1,2\}\) and \([B]_+\in\mathcal S_{r,n}(\lambda_r,\overline a)\).  Set
\[
G_B=\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)-\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y).
\]
Equation (1) yields
\[
G_B=\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}u_{B,\ell}Z_\ell,
\qquad
u_{B,\ell}=S_B^+B^{\top}\Omega e_\ell\frac{Y_\ell}{\pi_\ell^2}.
\tag{2}
\]
The positive-eigenvalue margin gives
\[
\lVert S_B^+\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\lambda_r)^{-1}.
\tag{3}
\]
Each covariance column has at most \(s_n\) nonzero entries, every covariance entry has absolute value at most one, and basis coordinates are bounded by \(\overline a\).  Hence
\[
\sup_\ell\lVert B^{\top}\Omega e_\ell\rVert_2
\le\sqrt2\,\overline a s_n,
\qquad
\sup_\ell\lVert u_{B,\ell}\rVert_2\le U_{n,r}.
\tag{4}
\]
The centered basis statistic is
\[
X_B=n^{-1}B^{\top}(D-\mathbb E_\nu D)
=\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}x_{B,\ell}Z_\ell,
\qquad x_{B,\ell}=n^{-1}B^{\top}e_\ell,
\tag{5}
\]
and \(\sup_\ell\lVert x_{B,\ell}\rVert_2\le X_n\).  The fourth-moment envelope in \(\texttt{def:rank-adaptive-stability-strata}\), applied coordinatewise, gives
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\lVert G_B\rVert_2^4
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}U_{n,r}^4,
\qquad
\mathbb E_\nu\lVert X_B\rVert_2^4
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}X_n^4.
\tag{6}
\]
The estimators satisfy
\[
\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}
=\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}+\Delta_B,
\qquad \Delta_B=-G_B^{\top}X_B.
\tag{7}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz and (6) imply
\[
\mathbb E_\nu\Delta_B^2
\le4\mathfrak M_{4,n}U_{n,r}^2X_n^2.
\tag{8}
\]

Put \(T_B=\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}-\tau\).  Equation (1) makes the Horvitz--Thompson term unbiased, and the basis statistic is centered, so \(\mathbb E_\nu T_B=0\).  Direct expansion gives
\[
T_B=\sum_{\ell=1}^{N}t_{B,\ell}Z_\ell,
\qquad
t_{B,\ell}=n^{-1}\left\{\frac{Y_\ell}{\pi_\ell}
-B_{\ell\cdot}\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)\right\}.
\tag{9}
\]
The projector identity in \(\texttt{lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction}\) gives
\[
\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)^{\top}S_B\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)
\le Y^{\top}\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y.
\tag{10}
\]
There are at most \(2ns_n\) nonzero entries in the covariance sum, and every term is at most \(\pi_{\min,n}^{-2}\) in absolute value.  Thus
\[
Y^{\top}\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y
\le2ns_n\pi_{\min,n}^{-2}.
\tag{11}
\]
The coefficient lies in the positive spectral subspace of \(S_B\).  Combining (10)--(11) with \(\lambda_+(S_B)\ge n\lambda_r\) yields
\[
\lVert\gamma_B^{*,+}(Y)\rVert_2\le\Gamma_{n,r},
\qquad
\sup_\ell|t_{B,\ell}|\le T_{n,r}.
\tag{12}
\]
Expanding \(\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(T_B,\Delta_B\mid Y)\) in the two coefficient coordinates and applying the third-moment envelope gives
\[
|\operatorname{Cov}_\nu(T_B,\Delta_B\mid Y)|
\le2\mathfrak M_{3,n}T_{n,r}U_{n,r}X_n.
\tag{13}
\]
The variance identity combines (8) and twice (13) to give exactly \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\).

For the oracle identity, put \(q_B=B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Y\).  Since
\[
q_B\in\operatorname{range}\{(\Omega^{1/2}B)^{\top}\}
=\operatorname{range}(S_B),
\]
one has \(S_BS_B^+q_B=q_B\).  Completing the quadratic variance expression gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{or},+}\mid Y)
=n^{-2}Y^{\top}\left\{\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}
-\Pi^{-1}\Omega BS_B^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\}Y.
\tag{14}
\]
Under the i.i.d. potential-pair assumption, \(\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}(YY^{\top})=Q(c_H)\).  Taking the trace expectation in (14) proves \(\ell_B^+(c_H)\).

Now let \(\widetilde B\) satisfy (S).  Pilot independence permits conditioning on its realized class.  On rank zero the difference is exactly zero.  On \(\{\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widetilde B})=r\}\), (S) licenses the fixed-basis calculation with \(\lambda_r\) and \(\overline a\).  Integrating the eventwise inequalities over the pilot gives
\[
\left|\mathbb E_P\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_{\widetilde B}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widetilde B,Y)
-\mathbb E_P\ell_{\widetilde B}^+(c_H)\right|
\le\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\mathbf1\{\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widetilde B})>0\}
R_{n,\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widetilde B})}^{\mathrm{ad}}\right].
\tag{15}
\]
For an encoded CAD output, the rank and spectral-isolation record and the coordinate check must establish (S); pilot independence alone does not.

Under the directed local assumption, the same singleton-versus-complement count used in the preceding uniform proof gives
\[
s_n\le d_n\le\overline d n^\alpha,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{4,n}\le64(2n)^2d_n^2,
\qquad
\mathfrak M_{3,n}\le8(2n)d_n^2.
\tag{16}
\]
Substitution of (16) and \(\pi_{\min,n}\ge\underline\pi n^{-\beta}\) into the displayed formula for \(R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\) gives a finite \(K_{\mathrm{ad}}\) such that
\[
R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}
\le K_{\mathrm{ad}}\{n^{4\alpha+4\beta-2}
+n^{(7/2)\alpha+3\beta-2}\},
\qquad r\in\{1,2\}.
\tag{17}
\]
The constant is uniform in the basis, its positive rank, the bounded array, and \(H\), for fixed positive lower bounds on \(\lambda_1,\lambda_2\) and fixed \(\overline a\).  Since \((7/2)\alpha+3\beta\le4\alpha+4\beta\), the first-order rate assumption gives \(\sup_r nR_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\to0\).  At rank two the definitions give \(\mathcal S_{2,n}(\kappa,\overline a)=\iota(\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a})\), and the pseudoinverse is the ordinary inverse.

Finally, every \(S(b_1,b_0)\) is a \(2\times2\) positive-semidefinite matrix.  It has rank two exactly when its determinant is positive, rank one exactly when its determinant is zero and trace positive, and rank zero exactly when its trace is zero.  Taking existential maxima proves the first three design-side equivalences.  If \(\Omega_{11}=\Omega_{00}=0\), positive semidefiniteness forces \(\Omega_{10}=\Omega_{01}=0\), so \(r_\Omega=0\); conversely, a nonzero positive-semidefinite diagonal block has a vector with positive quadratic form.  This proves the last design-side equivalence and the individual rank tests. \(\square\)
