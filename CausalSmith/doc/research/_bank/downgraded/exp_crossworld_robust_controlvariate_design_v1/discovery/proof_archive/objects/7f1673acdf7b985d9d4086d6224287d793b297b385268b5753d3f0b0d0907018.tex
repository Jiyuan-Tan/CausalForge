The exploratory Jiangxi PICC record \(\mathcal J_{\mathrm{PICC}}\) is reproduced by the checked script \(\texttt{discovery/jiangxi\_robust\_reanalysis.py}\) using the public Cai--de Janvry--Sadoulet replication tables.  It removes missing identifiers, sentinel \(99\), self-loops, and duplicate directed nominations, leaving \(16997\) valid edges; it retains cross-village nominations.  Under Zhao's disclosed independent Bernoulli \(1/2\) reconstruction, it obtains \(\mathcal I_1,\mathcal I_2,\mathcal I_3\) of sizes \(881,481,168\) and exactly reproduces Zhao's HT estimates \(0.075672,-0.027720,-0.279365\) and H\'ajek estimates \(0.091700,0.140253,-0.037241\).  Connected components of the exposure-overlap graph are kept whole in folds of sizes \((401,480)\), \((229,252)\), and \((90,78)\).  On each training fold the script estimates Bernoulli outcome marginals and sharp Fr\'echet endpoints; on the held-out fold it uses the same four deterministic initializations and \(500\) Adam steps for the robust and \(c=0\) rules, then deploys def:observable-coefficient-map and swaps folds.
