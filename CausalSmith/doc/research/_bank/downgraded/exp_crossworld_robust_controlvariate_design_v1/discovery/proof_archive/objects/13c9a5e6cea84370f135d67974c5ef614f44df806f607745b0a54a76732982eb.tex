\textbf{Proof.}
The displayed vectors satisfy
\[
\lVert x\rVert_2^2=\lVert y_0\rVert_2^2
=\lVert y_R\rVert_2^2=3,
\quad
\mathbf1^{\top}x=x^{\top}y_R=0,
\quad
(\mathbf1^{\top}y_R)^2=\frac{216}{55}.
\tag{1}
\]
They also have coordinates bounded in absolute value by \(2\).  The equality-set formulas in \(\texttt{thm:triangle-completion-separation-certificate}\) therefore place \(B_0\) and \(B_R\) in the stated optimizer sets, and the same theorem gives their eigenvalue margin and objective values.  The balanced two-point pilot empirical law is exactly \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) in each arm, so all empirical marginal moments and both empirical quantile endpoints equal their population counterparts.

For completeness, the finite calculation producing the observable variances is explicit.  For \(e\in\{L,U\}\) and \(z\in\{0,1\}^3\), put
\[
Y_i^{(e,z)}(1)=z_i,
\qquad
Y_i^{(U,z)}(0)=z_i,
\qquad
Y_i^{(L,z)}(0)=1-z_i.
\]
For a displayed basis \(B\), let \(T_B(w,z,e)\) be the observable estimator obtained by substituting assignment \(w\), potential array \((e,z)\), the exact triangle \(\Pi,\Omega\), and \(B\) into \(\texttt{def:observable-coefficient-map}\).  Then
\[
V_{B,e}
=\frac18\sum_{z\in\{0,1\}^3}
\left[
\sum_{w\in\{0,1\}^3}\nu(w)T_B(w,z,e)^2
-\left\{\sum_{w\in\{0,1\}^3}\nu(w)T_B(w,z,e)\right\}^2
\right].
\tag{2}
\]
Equation (2) is a sum of sixty-four rational or real-algebraic terms.  Substitution and common-denominator reduction give exactly the four displayed values.  Their endpoint differences are
\[
V_{0,L}-V_{0,U}=\frac{137}{1200}>0,
\]
\[
V_{R,L}-V_{R,U}
=\frac{2943215-196776\sqrt{93}}{10750800}>0.
\tag{3}
\]
The second sign follows from \(\sqrt{93}<193/20\), whose square is \(37249/400>93\).

Subtracting the two worst values and separately subtracting the two exact objective values gives the displayed \(G_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and \(E_{\mathrm{bridge}}\).  The correction is positive by \(\sqrt{93}>241/25\), since \(58081/625<93\), followed by exact cross multiplication.  Thus the absolute value has the displayed sign.  Direct rational simplification now gives
\[
G_{\mathrm{obs}}-E_{\mathrm{bridge}}
=\frac{4912}{39525}>0.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (1)--(4) prove lawfulness, exact evaluability, and the strict finite observable comparison. \(\square\)
