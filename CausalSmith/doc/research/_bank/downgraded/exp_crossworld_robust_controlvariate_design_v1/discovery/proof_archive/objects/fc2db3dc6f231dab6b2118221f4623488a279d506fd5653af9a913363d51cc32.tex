If \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}=\{B^\star\}\) in the projective quotient, then every measurable selection \(\widehat B\in\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{mm}}\) converges almost surely to \(B^\star\).  Without the singleton condition, there exist bounded two-marginal instances satisfying the maintained assumptions for which the outer-set conclusion of thm:plugin-correspondence holds but some measurable plug-in selections fail to converge to a point.
