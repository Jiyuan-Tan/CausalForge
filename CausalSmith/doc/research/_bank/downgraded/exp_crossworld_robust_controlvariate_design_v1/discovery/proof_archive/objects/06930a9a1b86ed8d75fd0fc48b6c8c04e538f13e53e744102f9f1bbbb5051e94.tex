\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\widehat B=\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{st},+}\), \(K=\mathcal K_n^{\mathrm{st}}\), \(d=d_m^+\), and \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_m\).  By def:pilot-plugin,
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B)
\le\inf_{B\in K}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon.
\tag{1}
\]
The definition of \(d\) gives, simultaneously for every class in the full carrier,
\[
w^+(B)-d\le\widehat w_m^+(B)\le w^+(B)+d.
\tag{2}
\]
Let \(B^{\mathrm{st}}\in K\) attain \(v_n^{\mathrm{st}}\); attainment follows from the compactness built into def:rank-adaptive-stability-strata and continuity on its spectrally separated rank pieces.  Applying (2), then (1), then (2) at \(B^{\mathrm{st}}\), yields the pointwise pilot inequality
\[
w^+(\widehat B)
\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B)+d
\le\widehat w_m^+(B^{\mathrm{st}})+\varepsilon+d
\le v_n^{\mathrm{st}}+2d+\varepsilon.
\tag{3}
\]
Since \(K\subseteq\mathcal B_Z^+\), every selected class also satisfies
\[
w^+(\widehat B)\ge v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\tag{4}
\]
Taking pilot expectations in (3)--(4), using \(v_n^{\mathrm{st}}=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++b_n^{\mathrm{st}}\), gives
\[
0\le\mathbb E_Pw^+(\widehat B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le b_n^{\mathrm{st}}+2\mathbb E_Pd_m^++\mathbb E_P\varepsilon_m.
\tag{5}
\]

It remains to pass from objective loss to the observable estimator.  By ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence, conditioning on the pilot freezes \(\widehat B\) before the main assignment and potential-outcome array are considered.  If \(\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widehat B})=0\), thm:rank-adaptive-oracle-observable-variance-bridge says that the observable and oracle estimators both equal the uncontrolled Horvitz--Thompson estimator; the bridge remainder is exactly zero.  On the event of selected rank \(r\in\{1,2\}\), membership in \(K\) gives
\[
[\widehat B]_+\in\mathcal S_{r,n}(\lambda_{r,n},\overline a),
\]
so that theorem applies conditionally on the pilot and, for every \(H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)\), gives
\[
\left|
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B,Y)
-\ell_{\widehat B}^+(c_H)
\right|
\le R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}.
\tag{6}
\]
By ass:frechet-covariance-domain, the covariance image is \([c_L,c_U]\); the endpoint-affine reduction gives
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_{\widehat B}^+(c_H)=w^+(\widehat B).
\tag{7}
\]
Taking suprema in (6) and using \(|\sup f-\sup g|\le\sup|f-g|\), then averaging over the pilot, proves
\[
\left|V_{\mathrm{st}}-\mathbb E_Pw^+(\widehat B)\right|
\le\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\mathbf1\{\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widehat B})>0\}
R_{n,\operatorname{rank}(S_{\widehat B})}^{\mathrm{ad}}
\right]
\le\max_{r\in\{1,2\}}R_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}.
\tag{8}
\]
Combining the upper side of (8) with (5) gives the required upper deviation from \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\).  Combining its lower side with (4) gives a negative deviation of magnitude at most the last term in (8), which is no larger than the displayed right-hand side because all of its terms are nonnegative.  This proves the absolute inequality.

The exact-input stable CAD selector satisfies (1) by thm:encoded-cad-stable-pilot-selector-certificate and belongs to the same stable carrier with its rank, spectral margin, and coordinate envelope certified, so the argument is unchanged.  Multiplying the finite inequality by \(n\) proves the first-order conclusion under the displayed convergence premise.  Finally, the rate clause of thm:rank-adaptive-oracle-observable-variance-bridge, derived there from Zhao's directed coordinate-versus-complement neighborhoods, gives \(\max_r nR_{n,r}^{\mathrm{ad}}\to0\) under the three Zhao rate assumptions and fixed positive spectral lower bounds.  Nothing in this step bounds \(b_n^{\mathrm{st}}\); it remains the exact stability price. \(\square\)
