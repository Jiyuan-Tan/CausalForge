\textbf{Proof.}
Enumerating the eight assignments gives
\[
\pi_{i,1}=\frac4{27},\qquad \pi_{i,0}=\frac{10}{27},
\]
and the covariance table
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&i=j&i\ne j\\ \hline
\operatorname{Cov}(D_{i,1},D_{j,1})&92/729&-16/729\\
\operatorname{Cov}(D_{i,1},D_{j,0})&-40/729&68/729\\
\operatorname{Cov}(D_{i,0},D_{j,0})&170/729&8/729.
\end{array}
\tag{1}
\]
This is exactly the displayed \(\Pi\) and \(\Omega\), and it verifies exposure positivity and mutual exclusivity.  Bernoulli \((1/2)\) quantiles give \(c_L=-1/4\), \(c_U=1/4\); substitution in def:moment-matrix gives the displayed endpoint matrices.

For normalized \(B(x,y)\), set
\[
s=\mathbf1^{\top}x,qquad t=\mathbf1^{\top}y,qquad q=x^{\top}y.
\]
Direct block multiplication gives
\[
S_B=
\begin{bmatrix}
4/9-16s^2/729&-4q/27+68st/729\\
-4q/27+68st/729&2/3+8t^2/729
\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{2}
\]
Put
\[
d=729+12t^2-36s^2-54q^2+68qst-22s^2t^2.
\]
On \(\mathcal B_{1/8}\), \(\det S_B=8d/19683>0\).  Exact trace reduction yields
\[
\frac{31}{80}-g_B(0)=\frac{N_0}{1200d},
\tag{3}
\]
where
\[
N_0=5022q^2-4352qst+670s^2t^2+1629s^2+2148t^2.
\]
Completing the square in \(q\), the remainder is bilinear in \((s^2,t^2)\in[0,9]^2\); its four vertex values are nonnegative, and equality in the complete expression occurs exactly at \(s=t=q=0\).  Thus (3) proves the stated full nominal optimizer set and value.  Substitution there gives \(w=521/1200\).

For the minimax criterion, put \(\lambda=1407/2635\) and \(v=2613/8432\).  Since a maximum dominates every convex combination,
\[
w(B)-v\ge\lambda\ell_B(c_L)+(1-\lambda)\ell_B(c_U)-v
=\frac{3N_{\mathrm{mm}}}{210800d},
\tag{4}
\]
where
\[
N_{\mathrm{mm}}
=274734\left(q-\frac{70411}{137367}st\right)^2
+s^2\left(34527-\frac{439017980}{137367}t^2\right).
\tag{5}
\]
The last coefficient is positive for \(t^2\le9\), so equality in (5) requires \(s=q=0\).  Equality in the convex-combination bound further requires the two endpoint losses to agree, and direct substitution gives
\[
\ell_B(c_L)-\ell_B(c_U)
=-\frac{3(55t^2-216)}{10(4t^2+243)}.
\]
Thus the minimizers are exactly \(s=q=0\), \(t^2=216/55\), and their value is \(2613/8432\).  Both equality sets are nonempty.  Equation (2) gives \(\lambda_{\min}(3^{-1}S_B)=4/27\) on each set.  Therefore
\[
\delta=\frac{521}{1200}-\frac{2613}{8432}=\frac{4912}{39525},
\qquad
\eta=\frac4{27}-\frac18=\frac5{216}.
\]
Known exposure supplies the causal interpretation of the two labels, completing the witness. \(\square\)
