\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(v_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)\) and \(q_n=\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B)\).  The optimizer containment gives \(v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=v_n\), \(\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\mathcal A_{0,Z}\), and \(\Delta_Z=q_n-v_n\).  The two selection-regret inequalities in thm:pilot-selected-ht-first-order-variance-transfer imply
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)-\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n.
\tag{1}
\]
Applying its feasible-estimator bridge once in each direction gives
\[
V_0-V_{\mathrm{mm}}
\ge\Delta_Z-(4L_n+2)C_n\mathbb E_PM_n-2R_n.
\tag{2}
\]
Substitution of the three finite envelopes proves the displayed checkable strictness condition.  Multiplying (2) by \(n\), exact sensitivity and boundedness of \(L_n\) make the pilot term vanish, while \(4\alpha+4\beta<1\) makes \(nR_n\to0\).  The assumed positive lower limit of \(n\Delta_Z\) proves the final limit inferior. \(\square\)
