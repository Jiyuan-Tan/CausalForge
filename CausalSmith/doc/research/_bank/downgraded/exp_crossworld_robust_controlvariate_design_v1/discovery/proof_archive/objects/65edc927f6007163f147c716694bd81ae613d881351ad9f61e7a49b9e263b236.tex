\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\delta_0=\Delta(\xi_0)>0\).  The stated eigenvalue slack is exactly the hypothesis of lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity.  If no relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) had positive gap, metrizability of the relative total-variation--Wasserstein topology in def:lawful-instance-class would give a sequence \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) with \(\Delta(\xi_j)\le0\) for every \(j\).  The lemma would then imply
\[
 0<\delta_0=\Delta(\xi_0)
 \le \liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)\le0,
\]
a contradiction.  Thus some relative-open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\subset\mathfrak X_\kappa\) satisfies \(\Delta(\xi)>0\) throughout. \(\square\)
