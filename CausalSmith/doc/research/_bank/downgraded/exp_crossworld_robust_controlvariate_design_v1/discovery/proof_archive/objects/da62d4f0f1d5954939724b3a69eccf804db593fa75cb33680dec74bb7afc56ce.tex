The paper combines the sharp endpoint interval with a proved affine endpoint reduction, a compact normalized \(\kappa\)-regular rank-two optimization, a rank-stratified pseudoinverse value analysis, certified finite-\(\varepsilon\) solvers, exact triangle separation with relative-open persistence, and plug-in stability.  The full-carrier pilot selector is repaired by a fixed rational-chart enumeration and a measurable first-qualifying index: a proved one-sided rank-two approximation shows that its empirical infimum equals the all-rank empirical infimum whenever the positive-definite rank-two stratum is nonempty.  The resulting value and regret guarantees require neither full-carrier attainment nor a zero-tolerance selector.
