\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence}\), the bounded-support and independent-unpaired-pilot conditions in \(\texttt{ass:bounded-outcomes}\) and \(\texttt{ass:unpaired-pilot}\) imply
\[
M_m\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely as }m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]
Because the design is fixed and \(\texttt{ass:exposure-positivity}\) holds,
\[
\pi_{\min,n}:=\min_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}\pi_{i,a}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
The uniform all-rank modulus proved in \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate}\) therefore gives
\[
d_m^+
\le 4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{3}
\]
This step is uniform over ranks zero, one, and two; it does not require a positive eigenvalue bound.

For arbitrary real-valued functions \(f\) and \(g\) on the same nonempty set \(\mathcal X\),
\[
\left|\inf_{x\in\mathcal X}f(x)-\inf_{x\in\mathcal X}g(x)\right|
\le \sup_{x\in\mathcal X}|f(x)-g(x)|.
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, if the right-hand side is \(d\), then \(f\le g+d\) and \(g\le f+d\); taking infima proves both one-sided inequalities.  The carrier \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) is nonempty because it contains the zero class.  Applying (4) to \(\widehat w_m^+\) and \(w^+\), and using the definition of \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\), yields
\[
\left|
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\right|
\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely},
\tag{5}
\]
where the convergence follows from (3).

Fix any finite strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable tolerance \(\varepsilon_m\).  By \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector}\), the classical first-qualifying index in \(\texttt{def:pilot-plugin}\) is finite and pilot-measurable, its selected class has rank \(r_\Omega\), and
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{6}
\]
By the definition of \(d_m^+\), first at the selected class and then uniformly over the carrier,
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
+2d_m^++\varepsilon_m\\
&=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is the infimum of \(w^+\) on the same carrier, (7) also gives
\[
0\le
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le 2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{8}
\]
Combining (3) and (8) proves the asserted almost-sure regret convergence whenever \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely.

The rank assertion follows directly from the maximal-achievable-rank construction in \(\texttt{def:pilot-plugin}\) and the selector lemma: the selected class has rank \(r_\Omega\), hence rank two exactly when rank two is achievable.  Nothing in (1)--(8) supplies a zero-tolerance selector, convergence of equality or argmin sets across rank changes, point convergence of bases, or an observable-estimator variance bridge without a positive-eigenvalue stability certificate.  Finally, (6) is a countable set-theoretic selection statement, not a finite stopping rule for arbitrary real pilot inputs; the proof never identifies this selector with the separately defined exact-input cylindrical-algebraic selector. \(\square\)
