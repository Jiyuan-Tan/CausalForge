\textbf{Proof.}
By ass:kappa-feasibility and prop:kappa-exhaustion, \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is nonempty and compact.  Exposure positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined, and membership in \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) gives \(S_B\succeq n\kappa I_2\), so inversion is continuous throughout this compact class.  The finite-dimensional formulas in def:gain-loss therefore make \(w\) and \(B\mapsto g_B(0)\) continuous.  Consequently both \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and \(\mathcal A_0\) are nonempty compact sets.

For every \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and every \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0\), the defining minimum gives
\[
w(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_0).
\]
The left side is constant over \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  Taking the supremum over \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\) and then the infimum over \(B_0\) proves the certificate.  The argument yields only a weak inequality; equality can occur, for example when both marginals are point masses and all endpoint moment matrices vanish. \(\square\)
