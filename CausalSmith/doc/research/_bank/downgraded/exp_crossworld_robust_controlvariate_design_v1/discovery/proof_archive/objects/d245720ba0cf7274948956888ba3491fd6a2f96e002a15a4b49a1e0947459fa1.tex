\textbf{Proof.}
Put
\[
M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence}\),
\[
M_m\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely as }m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]
Because the design and \(n\) are fixed and exposure positivity holds,
\(\pi_{\min,n}:=\min_{i,a}\pi_{i,a}>0\).  The uniform all-rank modulus in \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate}\) therefore gives
\[
d_m^+\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{2}
\]
The carrier is nonempty because it contains the zero class, and the elementary inequality
\[
|\inf f-\inf g|\le\sup|f-g|
\tag{3}
\]
applied on \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) yields
\[
\left|\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\right|
\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{4}
\]

For every finite positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\), \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector}\) gives a finite measurable index, a selected class of rank \(r_\Omega\), and
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{5}
\]
Twice applying the uniform error gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Since \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is the infimum on the same carrier,
\[
0\le w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{7}
\]
Equations (2) and (7) prove the asserted regret convergence when \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely.  The selected rank is \(r_\Omega\), hence is two exactly when rank two is achievable.  The argument uses neither a zero-tolerance selector nor cross-rank argmin convergence, and the countable rule remains distinct from the exact-input CAD selector. \(\square\)
