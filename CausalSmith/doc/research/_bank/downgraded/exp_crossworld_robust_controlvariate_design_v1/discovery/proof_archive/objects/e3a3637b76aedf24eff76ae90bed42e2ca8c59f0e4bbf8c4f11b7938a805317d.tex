\textbf{Proof.}
Positive definiteness makes both arm columns nonzero, so the quotient map from \(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\) onto \(\mathcal B_Z\) is well defined and surjective.  The projective equivalence relation gives
\[
q_Z(B)=q_Z(C)\Longleftrightarrow C=BT,
\qquad T=\operatorname{diag}(t_1,t_0),\quad t_1t_0\ne0.
\tag{1}
\]
For such \(T\),
\[
S_{BT}=T^\top S_BT,
\qquad S_{BT}^{-1}=T^{-1}S_B^{-1}(T^\top)^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
Substitution in the trace formula and cyclicity cancel every factor of \(T\), proving \(g_{q_Z(BT)}(c)=g_{q_Z(B)}(c)\).  The uncontrolled term is basis independent, so the residual and worst-endpoint loss are invariant as well.  Any invariant function is constant on every fiber of a surjection; its extrema over an inverse image equal those over the quotient set, and an attained optimizer pulls back exactly to the inverse image of the quotient optimizer set.  This is an exact transfer for the positive-definite rank-two restriction of Zhao's larger published pseudoinverse class. \(\square\)
