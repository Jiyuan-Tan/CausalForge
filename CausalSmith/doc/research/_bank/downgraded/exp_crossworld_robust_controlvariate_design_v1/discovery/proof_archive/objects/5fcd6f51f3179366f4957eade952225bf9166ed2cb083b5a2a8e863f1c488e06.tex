\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\mathbf 1=\mathbf 1_3\).  The simultaneous permutation decomposition of \(\mathbb R^3\) into \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1\}\) and \(\mathbf1^\perp\) reduces \(729\Omega\) to the two symmetric matrices
\[
M_{\parallel}=\begin{bmatrix}60&96\\96&186\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
M_{\perp}=\begin{bmatrix}108&-108\\-108&162\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Their leading entries are positive and
\[
\det(M_{\parallel})=1944>0,
\qquad
\det(M_{\perp})=5832>0.
\]
Hence \(\Omega\) is positive definite.  Consequently a block-separable matrix \(B(x,y)\) has \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)=2\) when both \(x\) and \(y\) are nonzero, rank one when exactly one is nonzero, and rank zero when both vanish.  This states all rank cases and supplies the boundary condition used below.

The rank-stratified projector identity shows that on a rank-one stratum the Moore--Penrose gain is the corresponding scalar Rayleigh quotient.  Consider first \(B(x,0)\), normalize \(\lVert x\rVert_2^2=3\), and put
\[
u=(\mathbf1^{\top}x)^2.
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(0\le u\le9\), while direct multiplication gives
\[
x^{\top}\Omega_{11}x=\frac{324-16u}{729}>0.
\tag{1}
\]
If \(C_x=\Pi^{-1}\Omega(x,0)^{\top}\), the two endpoint quadratic forms are
\[
C_x^{\top}Q(c_L)C_x=\frac{1195u+6561}{24300},
\qquad
C_x^{\top}Q(c_U)C_x=\frac{3(49-5u)}{100}.
\tag{2}
\]
Substitution of (1)--(2) into the scalar pseudoinverse trace formula, including the factor \(n^{-2}=1/9\), yields
\[
g_x^+(c_L)=\frac{1195u+6561}{1200(81-4u)},
\qquad
g_x^+(c_U)=\frac{243(49-5u)}{400(81-4u)}.
\tag{3}
\]
At these endpoint inputs the uncontrolled variances are
\[
h(c_L)=\frac{313}{600},
\qquad
h(c_U)=\frac{171}{200}.
\tag{4}
\]
Since zero is the midpoint of \([c_L,c_U]\), affinity gives
\[
g_x^+(0)=\frac{21141-1225u}{1200(81-4u)},
\qquad
\frac{d}{du}g_x^+(0)
=-\frac{4887}{400(4u-81)^2}<0.
\tag{5}
\]
Moreover, if \(\ell_{x,L}=h(c_L)-g_x^+(c_L)\) and \(\ell_{x,U}=h(c_U)-g_x^+(c_U)\), then
\[
\ell_{x,U}-\ell_{x,L}
=\frac{27(u+1)}{10(81-4u)}>0,
\]
and
\[
\ell_{x,U}=\frac{9(1755-17u)}{400(81-4u)},
\qquad
\frac{d}{du}\ell_{x,U}
=\frac{50787}{400(4u-81)^2}>0.
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (5)--(6) therefore certify
\[
\max_{0\le u\le9}g_x^+(0)=g_x^+(0)\big|_{u=0}=\frac{87}{400},
\qquad
\min_{0\le u\le9}w^+(B(x,0))
=\ell_{x,U}\big|_{u=0}=\frac{39}{80}.
\tag{7}
\]

For \(B(0,y)\), normalize \(\lVert y\rVert_2^2=3\) and put \(u=(\mathbf1^{\top}y)^2\).  The same multiplication gives
\[
y^{\top}\Omega_{00}y=\frac{486+8u}{729}>0
\]
and
\[
g_y^+(c_L)=\frac{1240u+729}{150(4u+243)},
\qquad
g_y^+(c_U)=\frac{81(48-5u)}{50(4u+243)}.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus
\[
g_y^+(0)=\frac{25u+12393}{300(4u+243)},
\qquad
\frac{d}{du}g_y^+(0)
=-\frac{14499}{100(4u+243)^2}<0.
\tag{9}
\]
The endpoint losses satisfy
\[
\ell_{y,U}-\ell_{y,L}
=\frac{3(59u+27)}{10(4u+243)}>0
\]
and
\[
\ell_{y,U}=\frac{9(256u+2889)}{200(4u+243)},
\qquad
\frac{d}{du}\ell_{y,U}
=\frac{113967}{50(4u+243)^2}>0.
\tag{10}
\]
It follows that
\[
\max_{0\le u\le9}g_y^+(0)=\frac{17}{100},
\qquad
\min_{0\le u\le9}w^+(B(0,y))=\frac{107}{200}.
\tag{11}
\]
Finally, rank zero has zero projector and hence zero gain; (4) then gives worst loss \(171/200\).  Comparing (7), (11), and the rank-zero values yields
\[
\sup_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}g_B^+(0)=\frac{87}{400},
\qquad
\inf_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}w^+(B)=\frac{39}{80}.
\]
The strict comparisons are exact because
\[
\frac{31}{80}-\frac{87}{400}=\frac{17}{100}>0,
\qquad
\frac{39}{80}-\frac{2613}{8432}=\frac{468}{2635}>0.
\]
All denominators used above are positive on \(0\le u\le9\), so the endpoint and boundary cases are included. \(\square\)
