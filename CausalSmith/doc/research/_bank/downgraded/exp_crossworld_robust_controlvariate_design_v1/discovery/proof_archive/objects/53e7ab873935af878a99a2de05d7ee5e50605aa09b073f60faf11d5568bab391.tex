Along a triangular sequence satisfying ass:known-exposure, ass:coordinate-bounded-kappa-feasibility, ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate, and ass:zhao-dependency-neighborhood-rate, let \(\widehat Q_L,\widehat Q_U\) be the two endpoint matrices obtained from the unpaired empirical marginals, and put
\[
C_n=n^{-2}\left[
\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_*
+\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}
\left\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\rVert_*
\right].
\]
Under ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot,
\[
\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_{L^1}\!\left(\frac{n}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}\right),
\qquad C_n=O\!\left(n^{\alpha+2\beta-1}\right),
\]
and
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|
\le C_n\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
Writing \(\widehat Q(0)\) for the zero-covariance moment matrix formed from the empirical marginal moments,
\[
\lVert\widehat Q(0)-Q(0)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\le2\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}},
\]
and
\[
\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|
\le2n^{-2}\sup_B
\left\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\rVert_*
\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\le2C_n\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
Hence \(nC_n\max_e\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\to0\) in \(L^1\) implies first-order plug-in accuracy for both regular rank-two criteria.  The condition \(\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}/n^{1+\alpha+2\beta}\to\infty\) is sufficient.  Known exposure is carried for closure with the observable estimator; the matrix-rate calculation itself is pilot only.
