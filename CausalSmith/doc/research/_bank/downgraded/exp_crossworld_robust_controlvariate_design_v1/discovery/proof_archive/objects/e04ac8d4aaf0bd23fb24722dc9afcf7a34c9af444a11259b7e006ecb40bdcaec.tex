\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\).  Total-variation convergence on the finite assignment space gives \(\Pi_j\to\Pi_0\) and \(\Omega_j\to\Omega_0\); exposure positivity makes \(\Pi_j^{-1}\to\Pi_0^{-1}\) locally.  Wasserstein convergence on \([0,1]\) gives convergence of first and second moments, while lem:restricted-marginal-extrema-continuity gives
\[
c_{Lj}\to c_{L0},\qquad c_{Uj}\to c_{U0}.
\]
Hence both endpoint moment matrices converge.

On normalized representatives,
\[
\rho(\xi,B)=\lambda_{\min}\{n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega(\xi)B\}
\]
is jointly continuous.  On \(\rho\ge\kappa\), inversion is uniformly bounded and \(w_\xi(B)\) and \(g_{B,\xi}(0)\) are jointly continuous.  Every optimizer at \(\xi_0\) has slack at least \(\eta\), hence remains feasible nearby.  A fixed minimax optimizer therefore gives
\[
\limsup_jv_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_j)\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0).
\tag{1}
\]
Choose \(B_{0j}\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)\) minimizing \(w_{\xi_j}\) within that optimizer set.  Compactness gives a convergent subsequence.  Comparing its nominal gain with a fixed slack-interior member of \(\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)\), and then passing to the limit, proves that every subsequential limit lies in \(\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)\).  Joint continuity yields
\[
\liminf_j\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_j)}w_{\xi_j}(B)
\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)}w_{\xi_0}(B).
\tag{2}
\]
Subtracting (1) from (2) proves \(\liminf_j\Delta(\xi_j)\ge\Delta(\xi_0)\). \(\square\)
