Under Zhao's convention that the inverse of every symmetric positive-semidefinite matrix is obtained by taking spectral reciprocals on positive eigenvalues and assigning zero to zero eigenvalues, define
\[
\mathcal B_Z^+=\left\{[B]_+:B=\begin{bmatrix}b_1&0\\0&b_0\end{bmatrix},\ b_1,b_0\in\mathbb R^n\right\}.
\]
Two representatives are equivalent when, separately in each arm, both corresponding columns are zero or one is a nonzero scalar multiple of the other.  Each class is represented with every nonzero arm column having squared norm \(n\), while a zero arm remains zero.  The class includes the rank-zero, rank-one, and rank-two strata of Zhao's published block-separable optimization.  Its rank-two positive-definite stratum is the image of \(\mathcal B_Z\) under \(\iota\).
