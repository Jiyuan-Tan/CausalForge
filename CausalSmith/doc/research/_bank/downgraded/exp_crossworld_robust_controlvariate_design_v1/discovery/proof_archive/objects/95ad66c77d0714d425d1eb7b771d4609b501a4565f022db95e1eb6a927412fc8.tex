\textbf{Proof.}
Let
\[
K_\kappa=\{(b_1,b_0):\lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=n,
\ B^{\top}\Omega B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Ass:kappa-feasibility makes \(K_\kappa\) nonempty.  It is closed in a product of two spheres and hence compact; prop:kappa-exhaustion identifies its finite sign quotient with \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  On (1),
\[
\lVert(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le(n\kappa)^{-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
The trace formulas in def:gain-loss, the resolvent identity, and (2) make both endpoint losses, and hence their maximum \(w\), continuous on \(K_\kappa\).  The refinement moduli in \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\) turn this compactness argument into an effective uniform-continuity modulus: for every positive rational \(\rho\), the inclusion oracles terminate with a rational chart diameter \(d(\rho)>0\) such that two feasible representatives in the same box of diameter at most \(d(\rho)\) have losses differing by at most \(\rho\).

Cover each normalized projective factor by the finitely many signed pivot charts in def:branch-bound-handle.  Fix a rational \(\rho>0\) with \(4\rho<\varepsilon\), and bisect all retained boxes until their diameters are at most \(\min\{\varepsilon,d(\rho)\}\).  This is a finite depth and produces finitely many boxes.  The exact feasibility interface decides every box, including boxes whose feasible intersection lies entirely on
\(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega B)=\kappa\).  In each feasible box, exact feasible-child selection and repeated nested refinement give the certified inclusion-oracle name of an exactly feasible point \(B_C\).  The endpoint inclusion oracles and their effective arithmetic moduli then terminate with outward rational bounds \(\underline q_C,\overline q_C\) satisfying
\[
w(B_C)-\rho\le\underline q_C\le w(B_C)
\le\overline q_C\le w(B_C)+\rho.
\tag{3}
\]
Uniform continuity supplies the valid box lower bound
\[
\lambda_C:=\underline q_C-\rho
\le\inf_{B\in C\cap K_\kappa}w(B).
\tag{4}
\]
Set \(L_\varepsilon=\min_C\lambda_C\), \(U_\varepsilon=\min_C\overline q_C\), and choose \(B_\varepsilon\) from a box attaining the latter minimum.  A box containing a compact-class minimizer, (3), and (4) give
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}-2\rho\le L_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}
\le w(B_\varepsilon)\le U_\varepsilon\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}+2\rho.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon<\varepsilon\).  Prune only boxes certified infeasible or having a valid lower bound strictly above the incumbent upper bound.  No box meeting \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) can satisfy either condition, so the union \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\) of unpruned quotient boxes contains \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\).  The prescribed refinement gives \(h_\varepsilon\le\varepsilon\).

Every stage terminates: the chart cover and subdivision depth are finite, and every requested feasibility, arithmetic, and inclusion-oracle refinement terminates by def:certified-input-interface.  The finite record is exactly \(\mathscr K_\varepsilon\) of def:certified-output.  Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) in (5) and the mesh bound proves convergence of both certified gaps.  The construction is instance dependent and supplies neither a uniform complexity bound nor an SDP reduction. \(\square\)
