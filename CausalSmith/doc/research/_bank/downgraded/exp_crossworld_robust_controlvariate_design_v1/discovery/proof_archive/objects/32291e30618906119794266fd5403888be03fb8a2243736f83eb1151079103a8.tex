Suppose ass:exposure-positivity and ass:frechet-covariance-domain hold and \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\).  For the novel Moore--Penrose completion of def:novel-pseudoinverse-completion, let \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) be the infimum of \(w^+\) and let \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) be its equality set, which is allowed to be empty.  Then
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
=\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
=\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\]
Writing \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\) for the fixed-threshold argmin, the rank-two completion equality set is exactly
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
=\iota\!\left(
\bigcup_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+}}
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\right).
\]
In particular, if the ordinary-domain infimum is attained at \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), then \(\iota(C)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\), and there is \(\kappa_C>0\) such that
\[
C\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa_C),
\qquad
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\]
Conversely, every rank-two point in \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) has the form \(\iota(C)\) for a unique \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), and that \([C]\) attains the ordinary-domain infimum and belongs to a value-attaining fixed-threshold argmin.  The singular equality set is
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\setminus\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
=\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1,\ w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\}.
\]
No unconditional completion attainment is asserted, no singular equality point is required, and no implication from a singular equality point to ordinary-domain attainment is claimed.  No part of this completion is attributed to Zhao.
