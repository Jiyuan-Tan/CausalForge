\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
 M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
 \lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
By lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence, ass:bounded-outcomes and ass:unpaired-pilot imply
\[
 M_m\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely as }m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]
For the fixed design, ass:exposure-positivity gives
\[
 \pi_{\min,n}=\min_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}\pi_{i,a}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
The all-rank inequality in lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate, which applies to the rank-zero, rank-one, and rank-two strata simultaneously, now gives
\[
 d_m^+
 \le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m
 \longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{3}
\]

For arbitrary real-valued functions \(f\) and \(g\) on the same nonempty domain,
\[
 \left|\inf f-\inf g\right|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, \(f\le g+\sup|f-g|\) gives one inequality after taking infima, and interchanging \(f\) and \(g\) gives the other.  Applying (4) to \(\widehat w_m^+\) and \(w^+\), and using
\(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)\), proves
\[
 \left|
 \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
 -v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \right|
 \le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{5}
\]

The assumption \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\) permits the fixed positive-definite rank-two chart enumeration.  Lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector proves both that its empirical infimum equals the empirical infimum over the complete all-rank carrier and that, for every finite strictly positive deterministic or pilot-measurable \(\varepsilon_m\), the first qualifying index defines a pilot-measurable selector satisfying
\[
 \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
 \le
 \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (6) with the definition of \(d_m^+\) twice yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
   +\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is the infimum of \(w^+\) on the same domain,
\[
 0\le
 w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
 \le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (3) and (8) prove the final almost-sure convergence whenever \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely.  The construction and argument use only positive tolerances and infimum comparisons.  They neither assume full-carrier attainment nor produce a zero-tolerance, outer-argmin, point-convergent, or singular-rank feasible-estimator conclusion. \(\square\)
