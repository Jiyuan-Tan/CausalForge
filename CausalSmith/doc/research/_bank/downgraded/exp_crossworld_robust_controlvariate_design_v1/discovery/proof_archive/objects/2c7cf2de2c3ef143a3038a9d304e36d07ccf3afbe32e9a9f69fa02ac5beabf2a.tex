A certificate \(\mathscr K_{\varepsilon}\) is a finite, locally checkable record containing: a signed pivot-chart cover of the two normalized spheres; for each chart box, either a CAD infeasibility certificate for the sphere, chart, and \(S_B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0\) polynomial constraints or an exact real-algebraic feasible sample; an exactly feasible real-algebraic incumbent \(B_{\varepsilon}\); rational outward bounds \(L_{\varepsilon},U_{\varepsilon}\); rational norm, Lipschitz, diameter, and objective lower-bound records for every feasible box; lower-bound pruning inequalities for every discarded feasible box; the quotient image \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\) of the feasible intersections with the unpruned boxes; and its mesh \(h_{\varepsilon}\).  The checker verifies algebraic signs, CAD cell coverage, rational interval arithmetic, chart coverage, \(L_{\varepsilon}\) as the minimum recorded box lower bound, \(w(B_{\varepsilon})\le U_{\varepsilon}\), \(U_{\varepsilon}-L_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon\), and \(h_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon\).  It never checks the unknown \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}\) or \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\) directly; those consequences are derived by thm:algebraic-finite-epsilon-solver.  A box meeting the equality boundary may be removed only by a CAD infeasibility certificate or a recorded objective lower bound exceeding \(U_{\varepsilon}\).
