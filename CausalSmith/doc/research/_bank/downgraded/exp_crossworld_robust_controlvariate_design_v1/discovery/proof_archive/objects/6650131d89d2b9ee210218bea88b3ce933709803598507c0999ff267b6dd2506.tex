If a finite certificate is returned, let \(\mathscr K_{\varepsilon}\) consist of a finite quotient-chart cover together with an exactly feasible incumbent \(B_{\varepsilon}\); rational bounds \(L_{\varepsilon},U_{\varepsilon}\); outward-rounded objective and constraint bounds for every retained box; a checkable exact-infeasibility or valid lower-bound pruning record for every discarded box; retained boxes whose union is \(\mathcal C_\varepsilon\); and their maximum chart diameter \(h_{\varepsilon}\). The checker requires \(L_{\varepsilon}\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}\le w(B_{\varepsilon})\le U_{\varepsilon}\), \(U_{\varepsilon}-L_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon\), \(h_{\varepsilon}\le\varepsilon\), and that no box meeting the exact \(\kappa\)-boundary or containing a global minimizer is discarded without an exact infeasibility or valid lower-bound certificate. This definition specifies the certificate format and does not assert that the branch-and-bound procedure terminates for every certified input.
