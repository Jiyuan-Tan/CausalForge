\textbf{Proof.}
The bounded armwise strong law and the quantile \(L^1\) identity used in thm:plugin-correspondence give
\[
\rho_m:=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{1}
\]
For every quotient class and endpoint, lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction gives
\[
g_B^+(c_e)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(P_BA_e),
\qquad \operatorname{rank}(P_B)\le2.
\tag{2}
\]
If \(\widehat A_e\) is formed from \(\widehat Q_e\), then
\[
|\operatorname{tr}\{P_B(\widehat A_e-A_e)\}|
\le2\lVert\widehat A_e-A_e\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
\le2\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}^2\rho_m.
\tag{3}
\]
The uncontrolled endpoint term obeys
\[
|\widehat h_e-h_e|
\le n^{-2}\lVert\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\rVert_*\rho_m.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (3)--(4), subtraction, and the one-Lipschitz property of the maximum imply \(d_m^+\le K\rho_m\to0\) for a finite fixed-design constant \(K\), uniformly over ranks zero, one, and two.

For arbitrary functions on the same nonempty domain, the absolute difference of their infima is at most their sup-norm distance; hence
\[
\left|\inf_B\widehat w_m^+(B)-\inf_Bw^+(B)\right|\le d_m^+.
\tag{5}
\]
By the defining epsilon inequality in def:pilot-plugin,
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le\inf_B\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (1), (5), and (6) prove the asserted value consistency and regret.  The argument uses neither attainment of \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) nor continuity across rank changes, so it yields no exact or outer-argmin claim. \(\square\)
