\textbf{Proof.}
Put \(m=m_1\wedge m_0\).  The nonemptiness condition \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\) is used only to construct the measurable selector in lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector; all matrix inequalities below cover ranks zero, one, and two.

For every \([B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\), lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction supplies the orthogonal projector
\[
 P_B=\Omega^{1/2}B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega^{1/2}
\]
onto \(\operatorname{range}(\Omega^{1/2}B)\).  Thus \(0\preceq P_B\preceq I_{2n}\).  Congruence by \(\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}\), legitimate because ass:exposure-positivity makes \(\Pi\) invertible, gives
\[
0\preceq
A_B^+=\Pi^{-1}\Omega^{1/2}P_B\Omega^{1/2}\Pi^{-1}
\preceq
\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}=A_{\mathrm{HT}}.
\tag{1}
\]
Both matrices in (1) are positive semidefinite, so their nuclear norms equal their traces.  Since \(D_{i,a}\) is Bernoulli with mean \(\pi_{i,a}\),
\[
 \Omega_{(i,a),(i,a)}=\pi_{i,a}(1-\pi_{i,a}).
\]
Consequently, with \(\pi_{\min,n}=\min_{i,a}\pi_{i,a}>0\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\lVert A_B^+\rVert_*
&\le \operatorname{tr}(A_{\mathrm{HT}})
=\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{a\in\{1,0\}}
\frac{1-\pi_{i,a}}{\pi_{i,a}}
\le2n\pi_{\min,n}^{-1},\\
\lVert A_{\mathrm{HT}}\rVert_*
&\le2n\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]

For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), def:pilot-plugin and def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss give
\[
 \widehat\ell_{B,e}^+-\ell_B^+(c_e)
 =n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
 (A_{\mathrm{HT}}-A_B^+)\{\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\}
 \right].
\]
Trace duality and (2) imply
\[
 \left|\widehat\ell_{B,e}^+-\ell_B^+(c_e)\right|
 \le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m.
\tag{3}
\]
The maximum of two coordinates is one-Lipschitz for the sup norm.  Taking the endpoint maximum and then the supremum over every rank stratum in (3) proves
\[
 d_m^+\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m.
\tag{4}
\]
Under ass:zhao-exposure-probability-rate,
\(\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}\le\underline\pi^{-1}n^{\beta}\), so (4) is (A).

We next derive the endpoint-only pilot rate without imposing a \(\kappa\)-class condition.  For arm \(a\), let
\[
 D_a=W_1(\widehat F_a,F_a)
 =\int_0^1|\widehat F_a(y)-F_a(y)|\,dy
 =\int_0^1|\widehat F_a^{-1}(u)-F_a^{-1}(u)|\,du.
\tag{5}
\]
The equalities follow by the layer-cake identity and Tonelli's theorem.  At fixed \(y\), the empirical distribution function is an average of \(m_a\) Bernoulli variables.  Jensen's inequality and the Bernoulli variance formula give
\[
 \mathbb E_PD_a
 \le\int_0^1
 \sqrt{\frac{F_a(y)\{1-F_a(y)\}}{m_a}}\,dy
 \le\frac1{2\sqrt{m_a}}.
\tag{6}
\]
Bounded support is used here and below.  Since \(y\mapsto y\) and \(y\mapsto y^2\) are respectively one- and two-Lipschitz on \([0,1]\),
\[
 |\widehat\mu_a-\mu_a|\le D_a,
 \qquad
 |\widehat\sigma_a^2-\sigma_a^2|\le4D_a,
 \qquad
 |\widehat\mu_a^2-\mu_a^2|\le2D_a.
\tag{7}
\]
For either endpoint coupling, \(|xy-x'y'|\le|x-x'|+|y-y'|\) on \([0,1]^4\) gives
\[
 |\widehat c_e-c_e|\le2(D_1+D_0),
 \qquad e\in\{L,U\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Using def:moment-matrix, \(\lVert I_n\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=1\), and
\(\lVert\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\top}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}=n\), equations (7)--(8) imply
\[
 \lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le(4n+8)(D_1+D_0)
 \le7n(D_1+D_0),
\tag{9}
\]
where \(n\ge3\) is used in the last inequality.  Equations (6)--(9) give a numerical constant \(K_M<\infty\), independent of the marginals and basis, such that
\[
 \mathbb E_PM_m\le K_M\frac{n}{\sqrt m}.
\tag{10}
\]

By lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector, the displayed selector is pilot-measurable and satisfies, pointwise in the pilot sample,
\[
 \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
 \le \inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{11}
\]
The definition of \(d_m^+\) and the elementary inequality
\[
 \left|\inf f-\inf g\right|\le\sup|f-g|
\tag{12}
\]
for two functions on the same nonempty domain yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
0\le w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
&\le \widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
 +d_m^+-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\\
&\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
The first inequality uses the definition of \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) as the full-carrier infimum.  Taking pilot expectations in (13) and applying (A) proves (B).  Finally, (10) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
n\mathbb E_P\!\left[
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\right]
&\le8K_M\underline\pi^{-1}
\frac{n^{1+\beta}}{\sqrt{m_1\wedge m_0}}
+n\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_m]
\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}
\]
The selector is rank two, but the benchmark, uniform error, and regret are taken over the complete rank-zero, rank-one, and rank-two quotient.  No feasible-estimator variance statement on a singular selected class is invoked or proved. \(\square\)
