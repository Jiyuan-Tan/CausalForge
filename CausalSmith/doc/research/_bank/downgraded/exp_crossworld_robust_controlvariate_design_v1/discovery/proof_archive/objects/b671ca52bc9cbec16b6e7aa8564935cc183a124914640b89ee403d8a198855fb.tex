For every rational signed pivot-chart box, intersection with the normalized feasible set \(\mathcal B_\kappa\) is an existential semialgebraic question.  More explicitly, sphere feasibility, chart and box restrictions, and \(S_B-n\kappa I_2\succeq0\) are finitely many polynomial equalities and weak inequalities with rational or real-algebraic coefficients; for
\[
 S_B-n\kappa I_2=\begin{bmatrix}u&v\\v&z\end{bmatrix},
\]
the matrix inequality is exactly \(u\ge0\), \(z\ge0\), and \(uz-v^2\ge0\).  Cylindrical algebraic decomposition therefore terminates with either an exact real-algebraic feasible sample or a finite infeasibility certificate, including when feasibility occurs only on the equality boundary.
