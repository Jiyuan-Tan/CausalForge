Under the assumptions of lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate and lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence, suppose the exact sensitivity condition holds and use the independent-pilot selectors of def:feasible-ht-pilot-selected-estimator.  Write
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}=\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}w(B),
\qquad
\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0).
\]
Then
\[
\mathbb E_P w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+o(n^{-1}),
\]
and, for \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\),
\[
\mathbb E_P\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}\mid\widehat B_s,Y)
\right]
=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1}).
\]
Thus pilot selection and feasible Horvitz--Thompson coefficient estimation each have an explicit uniform first-order bridge to the oracle endpoint loss.  In addition, if the zero-surrogate argmax is first-order stable in the following explicit sense,
\[
g_{B_n}(0)\ge\max_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}}g_B(0)-o(n^{-1})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
w(B_n)\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B)-o(n^{-1})
\]
for every deterministic sequence \(B_n\in\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), then
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\ge\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}}w(B)-o(n^{-1}).
\]
The final stability clause is a property of the two ordinary-domain objective surfaces; without such a quantitative clause, ordinary outer argmax consistency alone is only \(o(1)\) and does not justify a first-order \(o(n^{-1})\) ordering.
