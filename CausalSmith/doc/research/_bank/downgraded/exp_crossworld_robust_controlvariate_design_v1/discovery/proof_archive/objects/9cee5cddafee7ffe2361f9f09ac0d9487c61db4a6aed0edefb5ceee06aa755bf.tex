\textbf{Proof.}
If \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\), then \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succ0\).  Hence both arm columns are nonzero and \(\operatorname{rank}(S_B)=2\).  On this stratum, the equivalence relation in \(\mathcal B_Z^+\) is exactly the independent nonzero rescaling relation defining \(\mathcal B_Z\).  Thus \(\iota([B])=[B]_+\) is well defined and injective.

Conversely, let \([C]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+\) satisfy \(\operatorname{rank}(C^{\top}\Omega C)=2\).  The matrix \(C^{\top}\Omega C\) is positive semidefinite by construction and is a full-rank two-by-two matrix, so it is positive definite.  Therefore def:zhao-basis-class gives \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), and \(\iota([C])=[C]_+\).  This proves surjectivity onto the stated rank-two stratum.

On rank two, the Moore--Penrose inverse is the ordinary inverse:
\[
(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+=(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}.
\]
Substitution into def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss and def:gain-loss gives, for every \(c\in\mathcal C(F_1,F_0)\),
\[
g_{\iota(B)}^+(c)=g_B(c).
\]
Both definitions subtract this gain from the same uncontrolled term \(h(c)\), so \(\ell_{\iota(B)}^+(c)=\ell_B(c)\).  Taking the maximum of the two endpoint losses yields \(w^+(\iota(B))=w(B)\).  No statement about attainment of the completion infimum is used. \(\square\)
