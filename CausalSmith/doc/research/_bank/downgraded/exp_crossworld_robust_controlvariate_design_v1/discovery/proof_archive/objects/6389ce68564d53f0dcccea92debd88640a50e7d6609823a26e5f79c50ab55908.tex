\textbf{Proof.}
The exact triangle certificate gives
\[
\Delta(\xi_0)=\delta=\frac{4912}{39525}>0
\]
and the optimizer-wide slack
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi_0)\cup\mathcal A_0(\xi_0)}
\left\{\lambda_{\min}(3^{-1}S_B)-\kappa\right\}
=\eta=\frac5{216}>0.
\]
Thus thm:relative-open-persistence supplies a relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) on which the gap is positive.

The stronger numerical margin follows from lem:dominance-gap-lower-semicontinuity.  If every relative-open neighborhood of \(\xi_0\) contained some \(\xi\) with \(\Delta(\xi)\le\delta/2\), metrizability would give a sequence \(\xi_j\to\xi_0\) satisfying that inequality for every \(j\).  The lemma and the exact equality \(\Delta(\xi_0)=\delta\) would imply
\[
\delta
\le\liminf_{j\to\infty}\Delta(\xi_j)
\le\frac{\delta}{2},
\]
a contradiction.  Shrinking the neighborhood supplied by thm:relative-open-persistence if necessary therefore gives a relative-open \(\mathcal U\subset\mathfrak X_\kappa\), containing \(\xi_0\), such that
\[
\Delta(\xi)>\frac\delta2=\frac{2456}{39525}
\qquad(\xi\in\mathcal U).
\]

For \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\), one has \(w_\xi(B_{\mathrm{mm}})=v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\).  By def:dominance-gap,
\[
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)}w_\xi(B_0)
=v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)+\Delta(\xi)
>v_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi).
\]
Hence every \(B_0\in\mathcal A_0(\xi)\) has strictly larger loss than every \(B_{\mathrm{mm}}\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\xi)\), as required. \(\square\)
