\textbf{Proof.}
If \(0<\kappa_1\le\kappa_2\), the common normalization and the eigenvalue inequalities give
\[
\mathcal B_{\kappa_2}\subseteq\mathcal B_{\kappa_1}.
\]
Minimizing the same function over the larger set proves the first displayed inequality.  On each nonempty \(\mathcal B_\kappa\), compactness from prop:kappa-exhaustion and continuity of the ordinary-inverse trace formula imply that the displayed minimum is attained.

Prop:kappa-exhaustion also gives
\[
\mathcal B_Z=\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa.
\]
For an arbitrary function, the infimum over a union equals the infimum of the infima over its nonempty members.  Applying this identity to \(w\) yields
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
=
\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\]

Finally, suppose \(B_Z^\star\) attains the right-hand infimum.  By prop:kappa-exhaustion there is \(\kappa_0>0\) such that \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_0}\).  For every \(0<\kappa\le\kappa_0\), nesting gives \(B_Z^\star\in\mathcal B_\kappa\), while \(\mathcal B_\kappa\subseteq\mathcal B_Z\).  Therefore
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
\le
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)
\le
w(B_Z^\star)
=
\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\]
which proves equality throughout.  No step enlarges \(\mathcal B_Z\) beyond its nondegenerate ordinary-inverse definition. \(\square\)
