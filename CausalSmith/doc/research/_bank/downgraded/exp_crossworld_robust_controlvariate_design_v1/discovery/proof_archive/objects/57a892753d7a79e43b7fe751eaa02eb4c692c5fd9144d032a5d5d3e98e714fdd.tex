Let \(h(c)=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}[\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(c)]\).  Under ass:mutually-exclusive-exposures, ass:exposure-positivity, ass:frechet-covariance-domain, and ass:product-coupling-zero,
\[
h(c)=h(0)+\frac{2c}{n}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
w(B)=h(0)+\max\left\{\frac{2c_L}{n}-g_B(c_L),\frac{2c_U}{n}-g_B(c_U)\right\}.
\]
If \(c_L<c_U\), minimization of \(w(B)\) is not algebraically equivalent to maximization of \(r(B)=\min\{g_B(c_L),g_B(c_U)\}\): as functions of a formal endpoint-gain pair \((x_L,x_U)\), the two loss criteria are not related by a constant independent of \((x_L,x_U)\).
