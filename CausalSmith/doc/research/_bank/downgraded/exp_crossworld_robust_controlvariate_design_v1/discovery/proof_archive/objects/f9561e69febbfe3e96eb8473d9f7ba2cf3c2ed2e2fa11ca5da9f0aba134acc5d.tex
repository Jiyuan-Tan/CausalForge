\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(\mathbf 1=(1,1,1)^{\top}\).  Under the stated assignment and exposure maps,
\[
D_{i,1}=W_i,\qquad D_{i,0}=1-W_i.
\]
Independence gives
\[
\Pi=\frac12 I_6,\qquad
\Omega=\frac14\begin{bmatrix}I_3&-I_3\\-I_3&I_3\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Exposure positivity holds.  Set
\[
b_1=\mathbf 1,\qquad
b_0=\sqrt{\frac32}(1,-1,0)^{\top},\qquad
B=\begin{bmatrix}b_1&0\\0&b_0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Then
\[
\lVert b_1\rVert_2^2=\lVert b_0\rVert_2^2=3,
\qquad b_1^{\top}b_0=0,
\]
and hence
\[
S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B=\frac34I_2,\qquad
\lambda_{\min}(3^{-1}S_B)=\frac14>\kappa.
\]
Thus the design satisfies ass:kappa-feasibility.

For either marginal pair, the comparison-arm quantile is identically zero.  The definitions of the Fr\'{e}chet endpoints therefore yield \(c_L=c_U=0\).  Both instances consequently give exactly the same encoding of every quantity listed in \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{cert}}\), including the same \(\kappa\) and \(\varepsilon\).

For \((F_1,F_0)=(\delta_0,\delta_0)\), all marginal means and variances vanish.  Thus \(Q(0)=0\), so \(g_{\widetilde B}(0)=0\) for every feasible \(\widetilde B\), and \(v^\star=0\).

For \((F_1,F_0)=(\delta_1,\delta_0)\),
\[
\mu_1=1,\qquad \mu_0=0,\qquad
\sigma_1^2=\sigma_0^2=0.
\]
Writing \(J_3=\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^{\top}\), this gives
\[
Q(0)=\begin{bmatrix}J_3&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Since \(\Pi^{-1}=2I_6\), direct block multiplication gives
\[
B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}Q(0)\Pi^{-1}\Omega B
=\begin{bmatrix}9/4&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Using \(S_B^{-1}=\tfrac43I_2\),
\[
g_B(0)
=\frac1{3^2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
\frac43I_2\begin{bmatrix}9/4&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}
\right]
=\frac13.
\]
Because \(c_L=c_U=0\), \(r(B)=1/3\), and therefore \(v^\star\ge1/3\).

An algorithm receiving the identical certified encoding must return the same interval.  Correctness for the first instance requires \(L_{\varepsilon}\le0\), while correctness for the second requires \(U_{\varepsilon}\ge1/3\).  Hence
\[
U_{\varepsilon}-L_{\varepsilon}\ge\frac13>\frac1{10}=\varepsilon,
\]
contradicting the requested certificate.  The failure occurs before questions of finite subdivision, exact incumbents, or outer-set convergence arise.  The omitted information is the pair of endpoint moment matrices \(Q(c_L),Q(c_U)\), equivalently the four marginal moments in addition to the covariance endpoints. \(\square\)
