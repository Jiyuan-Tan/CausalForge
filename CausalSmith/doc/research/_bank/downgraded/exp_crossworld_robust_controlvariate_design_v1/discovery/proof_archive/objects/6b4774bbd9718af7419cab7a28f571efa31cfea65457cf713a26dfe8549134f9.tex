\textbf{Proof.}
Set
\[
M_m=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}
\lVert\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:bounded-unpaired-endpoint-strong-convergence}\), bounded outcomes and the unpaired-pilot law imply
\[
M_m\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely as }m_1\wedge m_0\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]
For the fixed design, exposure positivity gives
\[
\pi_{\min,n}=\min_{1\le i\le n,\,a\in\{1,0\}}\pi_{i,a}>0.
\tag{2}
\]
The all-rank modulus in \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-plugin-objective-regret-rate}\) yields
\[
d_m^+
\le4\pi_{\min,n}^{-1}n^{-1}M_m
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{3}
\]
For arbitrary real functions \(f,g\) on the same nonempty domain,
\[
|\inf f-\inf g|\le\sup|f-g|.
\tag{4}
\]
The carrier is nonempty because it contains the zero class.  Applying (4) to \(\widehat w_m^+\) and \(w^+\) proves
\[
\left|
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\right|
\le d_m^+\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{5}
\]

By \(\texttt{lem:full-carrier-countable-measurable-selector}\), the classical selector satisfies pointwise
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
\le
\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)+\varepsilon_m.
\tag{6}
\]
Using the definition of \(d_m^+\) on both sides of (6) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)
&\le\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)+d_m^+\\
&\le\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}\widehat w_m^+(B)
+\varepsilon_m+d_m^+\\
&\le v_{\mathrm{mm}}^++2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) is the infimum of \(w^+\) on the same domain,
\[
0\le w^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^+)-v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
\le2d_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (3) and (8) prove the asserted almost-sure regret convergence whenever \(\varepsilon_m\to0\) almost surely.  The selector lemma supplies maximal achievable rank \(r_\Omega\), so the selector has rank two exactly when rank two is achievable.

Only the set-theoretic positive-tolerance inequality (6) is used.  It neither turns the countable selector into a finite algorithm on arbitrary real pilot inputs nor identifies it with the separately defined exact-input cylindrical-algebraic selector.  The proof also establishes no zero-tolerance selector, cross-rank argmin convergence, point convergence, or uncontrolled singular-rank feasible-estimator bridge. \(\square\)
