Suppose ass:exposure-positivity and ass:frechet-covariance-domain hold and \(\mathcal B_Z\ne\varnothing\).  For the novel Moore--Penrose completion of def:novel-pseudoinverse-completion, with the infimum convention in def:completion-pseudoinverse-loss,
\[
v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
:=\inf_{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+}w^+(B)
=\inf_{[B]\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)
=\inf_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,\mathcal B_\kappa\ne\varnothing}}
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B).
\]
Let
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+
=\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\},
\]
which is allowed to be empty, and write \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\) for the fixed-threshold argmin.  The rank-two completion equality points are exactly
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\cap\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
=\iota\!\left(
\bigcup_{\substack{\kappa>0:\,
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_\kappa}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+}}
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa)\right).
\]
In particular, if the ordinary-domain infimum is attained at \([C]\in\mathcal B_Z\), then \(\iota([C])\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\), and \([C]\) belongs to \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}(\kappa_C)\) for some \(\kappa_C>0\) satisfying
\[
\min_{B\in\mathcal B_{\kappa_C}}w(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+.
\]
Conversely, every rank-two member of \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\) has the form \(\iota([C])\), where \([C]\) attains the ordinary-domain infimum and belongs to a value-attaining fixed-threshold argmin.  The singular equality stratum is exactly
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\setminus\iota(\mathcal B_Z)
=\{[B]_+\in\mathcal B_Z^+:\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1,\ w^+(B)=v_{\mathrm{mm}}^+\}.
\]
A singular equality point does not by itself imply ordinary-domain attainment.  Neither completion attainment nor a singular tie is asserted unconditionally, and no part of this completion is attributed to Zhao.
