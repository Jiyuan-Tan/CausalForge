For a realized assignment, define the signed Horvitz--Thompson outcome stack
\[
 \widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}
 =\left(\frac{D_{1,1}Y_1(W)}{\pi_{1,1}},\ldots,
 \frac{D_{n,1}Y_n(W)}{\pi_{n,1}},
 -\frac{D_{1,0}Y_1(W)}{\pi_{1,0}},\ldots,
 -\frac{D_{n,0}Y_n(W)}{\pi_{n,0}}\right)^{\top}.
\]
For any published-domain basis, Zhao's observable coefficient map is
\[
 \widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}(B)
 =(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}.
\]
Put \(\widehat 1_a=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nD_{i,a}/\pi_{i,a}\) and define the armwise self-normalized coefficient by
\[
 \widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{SN}}_a(B)=
 \begin{cases}
 \widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{HT}}_a(B)/\widehat 1_a,&\widehat 1_a>0,\\
 0,&\widehat 1_a=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
The deployed estimator is the observable map
\[
 \widehat\tau_B^{\mathrm{CV}}
 =\widehat\tau^{\mathrm{HT}}
 -\widehat\gamma^{\mathrm{SN}}(B)^{\top}
 \left\{n^{-1}B^{\top}D-\mathbb E_\nu[n^{-1}B^{\top}D]\right\},
 \qquad
 \widehat\tau^{\mathrm{HT}}=n^{-1}\mathbf1^{\top}\widehat Y^{\mathrm{obs}}.
\]
The zero-denominator convention makes the map total; coefficient estimation can introduce finite-sample bias and is imported deployment machinery rather than a new limit theorem.
