\textbf{Proof.}
Let \(K_\kappa\) be the set of pairs \((b_1,b_0)\) satisfying the two sphere normalizations and \(\lambda_{\min}(n^{-1}B^\top\Omega B)\ge\kappa\).  It is closed in a product of compact spheres.  Its quotient by the residual independent sign changes is therefore compact and equals \(\mathcal B_\kappa\).  Every member satisfies \(B^\top\Omega B\succeq n\kappa I_2\succ0\), hence lies in the positive-definite rank-two restriction \(\mathcal B_Z\) of Zhao's published unquotiented all-block-separable pseudoinverse domain.

Conversely, if \([B]\in\mathcal B_Z\), both arm columns are nonzero.  With
\[
T=\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac{\sqrt n}{\lVert b_1\rVert_2},
\frac{\sqrt n}{\lVert b_0\rVert_2}\right),
\]
the representative \(BT\) is normalized and \(T^\top B^\top\Omega BT\succ0\).  Thus \(\rho_B=\lambda_{\min}\{n^{-1}(BT)^\top\Omega(BT)\}>0\), and \([B]\in\mathcal B_{\rho_B/2}\).  Hence \(\bigcup_{\kappa>0}\mathcal B_\kappa=\mathcal B_Z\).  Surjectivity and the exact fibers of \(q_Z\), proved in thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer, give the inverse-image equality.  This exhausts only the positive-definite rank-two restriction; ranks zero and one remain in \(\mathcal B_Z^+\). \(\square\)
