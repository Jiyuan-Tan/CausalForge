\textbf{Proof.}
The moment-matrix definition gives
\[
Q(c)=Q(0)-cE.
\tag{1}
\]
Using cyclicity of trace in def:gain-loss and the definitions of \(A_B,a_B,b_B\), equation (1) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_B(c)
&=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_BQ(0)\}
-c n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_BE)\\
&=a_B+b_Bc.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
Lemma lem:ht-affine-drift gives \(h(c)=h(0)+2c/n\).  Subtracting (2) from this identity yields
\[
\ell_B(c)=h(0)-a_B+(2/n-b_B)c.
\tag{3}
\]
Taking the maximum of (3) at \(c_L\) and \(c_U\) and using the definition of \(p_\xi\) proves
\[
w(B)=h(0)-a_B+p_\xi(b_B).
\tag{4}
\]

Set
\[
M=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}\{a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\}.
\]
The maximum exists under the assumed attainment of the minimax loss, because (4) makes minimization of \(w\) equivalent to maximization of \(a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\).  Equation (4) then gives
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)=h(0)-M.
\tag{5}
\]
Attainment of the zero-covariance maximum makes \(a^\star\) and \(\mathcal A_{0,Z}=\{B:a_B=a^\star\}\) well defined and the latter set nonempty.  It need not be compact inside the positive-definite ordinary domain, so only its penalty infimum is automatic.  Restricting (4) to this face gives
\[
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B_0)
=h(0)-a^\star+inf_{B:\,a_B=a^\star}p_\xi(b_B).
\tag{6}
\]
Subtracting (5) from (6) and invoking the definition of \(\Delta_Z\) proves
\[
\Delta_Z
=M-\left[a^\star-inf_{B:\,a_B=a^\star}p_\xi(b_B)\right].
\tag{7}
\]

Because \(M\) is attained, (7) is strictly positive if and only if some \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\) satisfies
\[
a_B-p_\xi(b_B)
>a^\star-inf_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C).
\]
Rearranging gives exactly
\[
\inf_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C)-p_\xi(b_B)
>a^\star-a_B.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus (8), \(\Delta_Z>0\), and strict improvement of the minimax loss over every zero-covariance optimizer are equivalent.

Finally, thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer proves that \(g_B(c)\), \(\ell_B(c)\), and \(w(B)\) are constant on every fiber of the surjection \(q_Z\), and that every attained optimizer correspondence pulls back to its exact inverse image.  Applying that theorem to (4)--(8) proves all matrix-domain assertions. \(\square\)
