\textbf{Proof.}
By lem:rank-stratified-projector-reduction, the Moore--Penrose gain is invariant under independent nonzero rescaling of the arm columns and agrees with the ordinary-inverse gain on the rank-two stratum.  The triangle covariance matrix is positive definite by lem:triangle-singular-strata-certificate, so a block-separable basis is rank two exactly when both arm columns are nonzero.  Normalize those columns to squared norm three and use \(s=\mathbf1^{\top}x\), \(t=\mathbf1^{\top}y\), and \(q=x^{\top}y\).

The algebraic identities (2)--(11) in the proof of thm:triangle-minimax-separation-certificate were obtained from the normalization and the exact input matrices.  Their only denominator condition is \(d>0\).  On the present rank-two stratum, positive definiteness of \(\Omega\) and linear independence of the two block columns imply \(S_B=B^{\top}\Omega B\succ0\), hence \(d>0\) by equation (4) of that proof.  Therefore its square certificate (10)--(11) applies to every published-domain rank-two basis, not only to the fixed \(\kappa\)-regular subclass.  It shows
\[
 g_B^{+}(0)\le\frac{31}{80},
\]
with equality exactly when \(s=t=q=0\).  Likewise, the convex-combination certificate (14)--(17) uses only \(0\le t^2\le9\) and \(d>0\), so it applies to the entire rank-two stratum and shows
\[
 w^{+}(B)\ge\frac{2613}{8432},
\]
with equality exactly when \(s=q=0\) and \(t^2=216/55\).  The existence constructions in the triangle theorem show that both equality sets are nonempty.  Equations (12)--(13) there show that the first equality set has worst loss \(521/1200\), and that every member of either equality set satisfies
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(3^{-1}S_B)=\frac4{27}>\frac18.
\]

It remains to compare with the strata not present in the ordinary-inverse repair.  Lem:triangle-singular-strata-certificate gives
\[
 \sup_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}g_B^{+}(0)
 =\frac{87}{400}<\frac{31}{80}
\]
and
\[
 \inf_{\operatorname{rank}(S_B)\le1}w^{+}(B)
 =\frac{39}{80}>\frac{2613}{8432}.
\]
Thus no singular basis can enter either optimizer correspondence.  Combining the rank-two equality certificates with these strict singular inequalities proves the two complete published-domain correspondences and their exact values.  Finally,
\[
 \frac{521}{1200}-\frac{2613}{8432}
 =\frac{4912}{39525}>0,
\]
which is the asserted full-domain separation.  This also instantiates the value and rank-stratum transfer described in thm:published-domain-value-and-optimizer-transfer with strict exclusion, rather than retention, of singular ties. \(\square\)
