The primary headline is robust minimization of the worst endpoint residual \(w(B)\), with \(r(B)\) retained only as a secondary guaranteed-gain diagnostic.  The sharp Fr\'echet covariance interval is imported; the affine loss reduction, ordinary-domain intercept--slope characterization, algebraic finite-tolerance solver, triangle separation and relative-open persistence, plug-in correspondence, and finite-\(n\) independent-pilot variance bridge are the paper's derived results.  Generic completion claims are limited to infimum and equality-set transfer, while the triangle theorem independently proves its stronger attained minimum.  The scope is the normalized \(\kappa\)-regular class, and the first-order estimator bridge is further restricted to the nonempty coordinate-bounded rate class; no full supplied-basis coverage or universal minimax equality is claimed.
