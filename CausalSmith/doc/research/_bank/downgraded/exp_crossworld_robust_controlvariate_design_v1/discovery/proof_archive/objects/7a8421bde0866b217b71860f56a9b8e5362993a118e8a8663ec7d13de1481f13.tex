\textbf{Proof.}
The moment-matrix definition gives
\[
Q(c)=Q(0)-cE.
\tag{1}
\]
Using cyclicity of trace in def:gain-loss and the definitions of \(A_B\), \(a_B\), and \(b_B\), equation (1) yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_B(c)
&=n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\{A_BQ(0)\}
-cn^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_BE)\\
&=a_B+b_Bc.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
Lemma lem:ht-affine-drift gives \(h(c)=h(0)+2c/n\).  Subtracting (2) from this identity gives
\[
\ell_B(c)=h(0)-a_B+(2/n-b_B)c.
\tag{3}
\]
Taking the maximum of (3) at \(c_L\) and \(c_U\), then invoking the definition of \(p_\xi\), proves
\[
w(B)=h(0)-a_B+p_\xi(b_B).
\tag{4}
\]

Set
\[
M=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}\{a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\}.
\tag{5}
\]
The maximum in (5) exists under the assumed attainment of the ordinary-domain minimax loss, because (4) makes minimizing \(w\) equivalent to maximizing \(a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\).  Equation (4) therefore gives
\[
\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B)=h(0)-M.
\tag{6}
\]
Attainment of the zero-covariance maximum makes \(a^\star\) and the nonempty equality face
\[
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=\{B\in\mathcal B_Z:a_B=a^\star\}
\]
well defined.  This face need not be compact within the positive-definite ordinary domain, so its penalty is represented by an infimum.  Restricting (4) to the face gives
\[
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B_0)
=h(0)-a^\star+
\inf_{B:\,a_B=a^\star}p_\xi(b_B).
\tag{7}
\]
Subtracting (6) from (7), and using the definition of \(\Delta_Z\), yields
\[
\Delta_Z
=M-\left[
a^\star-
\inf_{B:\,a_B=a^\star}p_\xi(b_B)
\right].
\tag{8}
\]
This is the asserted exact gap identity.

By (5) and (8), \(\Delta_Z>0\) holds if and only if
\[
\max_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}\{a_B-p_\xi(b_B)\}
>
a^\star-
\inf_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C).
\tag{9}
\]
Because the maximum on the left of (9) is attained, (9) is equivalent to the existence of \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\) satisfying
\[
a_B-p_\xi(b_B)
>
a^\star-
\inf_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C).
\]
Rearrangement gives exactly
\[
\inf_{C:\,a_C=a^\star}p_\xi(b_C)-p_\xi(b_B)
>a^\star-a_B.
\tag{10}
\]
Thus strict improvement occurs precisely when some basis's reduction in the endpoint slope penalty strictly exceeds its loss of zero-covariance gain.

Finally, thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer proves that \(g_B(c)\), \(\ell_B(c)\), and \(w(B)\) are constant on every fiber of the surjection \(q_Z\), and that every attained optimizer correspondence pulls back to its exact inverse image.  Applying that theorem to (4)--(10) proves all assertions on \(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\).  No singular class or Moore--Penrose inverse enters the argument. \(\square\)
