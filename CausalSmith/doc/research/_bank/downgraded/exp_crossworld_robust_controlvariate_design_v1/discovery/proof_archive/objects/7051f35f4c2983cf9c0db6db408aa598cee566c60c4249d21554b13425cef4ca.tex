\textbf{Proof.}
For \(e\in\{L,U\}\), the empirical endpoint matrix \(\widehat Q_e\) is the second-moment matrix of the signed outcome vector under, respectively, the empirical countermonotone or comonotone coupling.  Hence
\[
\widehat Q_e\succeq0.
\tag{1}
\]
Every coefficient in \(\widehat{\mathfrak I}_{m,\mathrm{alg}}^+\) is rational or real algebraic, and ass:exposure-positivity makes \(\Pi^{-1}\) well defined.  Therefore
\[
\widehat w_m^+(B)=
\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}\!\left[
\left\{\Pi^{-1}\Omega\Pi^{-1}
-\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^+B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}\right\}
\widehat Q_e\right]
\tag{2}
\]
has exactly the rank-stratified semialgebraic form treated in thm:zhao-pseudoinverse-rank-stratified-epsilon-solver.  Applying that theorem with \(Q(c_e)\) replaced by \(\widehat Q_e\) proves finite termination and returns a real-algebraic basis and value-isolation record satisfying
\[
\widehat L_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}
\le\widehat v_m^+
\le\widehat U_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+},
\qquad
\widehat U_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}
-\widehat L_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}
\le\varepsilon_m,
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
\widehat w_m^+(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+})
\le\widehat v_m^++\varepsilon_m.
\tag{4}
\]
Every division used by the rank-stratified formulas occurs only on a cell on which its denominator has a certified positive sign, so rank-boundary points are handled by the appropriate lower-rank cell.

It remains to verify the augmented rank and spectral fields.  Put
\[
\widehat S_m^{\mathrm{CAD}}
=(\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+})^{\top}
\Omega\widehat B_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD},+}.
\]
Its entries are real algebraic and it is positive semidefinite.  For every positive-semidefinite \(2\times2\) matrix \(S\),
\[
\operatorname{rank}(S)=
\begin{cases}
0,&\operatorname{tr}(S)=0,\\
1,&\operatorname{tr}(S)>0\text{ and }\det(S)=0,\\
2,&\det(S)>0.
\end{cases}
\tag{5}
\]
Indeed, the two eigenvalues are nonnegative, their sum is the trace, and their product is the determinant.  Exact ordered real-algebraic arithmetic decides every sign and equality in (5), proving that \(\widehat r_{m,\varepsilon_m}^{\mathrm{CAD}}\in\{0,1,2\}\) is exact.

The eigenvalues are the roots, with multiplicity, of
\[
\chi_m(t)=t^2-\operatorname{tr}(\widehat S_m^{\mathrm{CAD}})t
+\det(\widehat S_m^{\mathrm{CAD}}).
\tag{6}
\]
Exact real-algebraic arithmetic determines multiplicities and equality with zero.  For each distinct positive root, rational bisection with exact sign comparisons terminates at disjoint rational intervals contained in \((0,\infty)\), each containing exactly that root.  Equations (3)--(6), together with the retained CAD projection, sign, and sample data, form the claimed locally checkable finite certificate.  The construction never evaluates the countable infimum defining the classical selector and gives no procedure for arbitrary real-valued pilot input. \(\square\)
