\textbf{Proof.}
Thm:triangle-completion-separation-certificate proves that the complete rank-two quotient equality sets are \(\mathcal A_0\) and \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}\), with zero-covariance gain \(31/80\), zero-covariance-optimizer worst loss \(521/1200\), and minimax worst loss \(2613/8432\).  Every member of \(\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z\) maps to a rank-two quotient class.  Thm:zhao-ordinary-domain-quotient-transfer says that both gain and loss are constant on the independent nonzero column-rescaling fibers and that the matrix-domain optimizer correspondences are exactly the inverse images of their quotient correspondences.  Therefore
\[
\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z}g_{q_Z(B)}(0)
=q_Z^{-1}(\mathcal A_0),
\qquad
\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\widetilde{\mathcal B}_Z}w(q_Z(B))
=q_Z^{-1}(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm}}),
\]
and all three values are preserved.  Subtraction gives
\[
\frac{521}{1200}-\frac{2613}{8432}
=\frac{4912}{39525}>0.
\]
Thus the exact separation extends to the entire maximal ordinary-inverse matrix domain, with no change other than duplication along rescaling fibers.  No singular class is used in this transfer. \(\square\)
