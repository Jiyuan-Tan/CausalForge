\textbf{Proof.}
Write \(\mathcal K_n=\mathcal B_{\kappa,\overline a}\), and define the two uniform pilot errors
\[
d_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal K_n}|\widehat w(B)-w(B)|,
\qquad
e_n=\sup_{B\in\mathcal K_n}|\widehat g_B(0)-g_B(0)|.
\tag{1}
\]
If
\[
M_n=\max_{e\in\{L,U\}}\|\widehat Q_e-Q(c_e)\|_{\mathrm{op}},
\]
then lem:uniform-pilot-endpoint-matrix-rate, including its factor-two zero-covariance bound, gives
\[
d_n\le C_nM_n,
\qquad
e_n\le 2C_nM_n.
\tag{2}
\]
The exact sensitivity condition is convergence of \(nC_nM_n\) to zero in \(L^1(P)\).  Hence (2) implies
\[
n\mathbb E_Pd_n\longrightarrow0,
\qquad
n\mathbb E_Pe_n\longrightarrow0.
\tag{3}
\]

Compactness of \(\mathcal K_n\) and continuity of \(w\) provide a minimizer \(B_n^\star\) with \(w(B_n^\star)=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}\).  By the defining empirical optimality of \(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}}\), pointwise in the pilot sample,
\[
\begin{aligned}
w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
&\le \widehat w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})+d_n\\
&\le \widehat w(B_n^\star)+d_n\\
&\le w(B_n^\star)+2d_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
The definition of \(B_n^\star\) also gives \(w(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})\ge w(B_n^\star)\).  Taking pilot expectations in (4) and applying (3) proves
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_{\mathrm{mm}})
=v_{\mathrm{mm},\overline a}+o(n^{-1}).
\tag{5}
\]

We next transfer the endpoint loss to the deployed estimator.  Conditional on the pilot, each selector \(\widehat B_s\), \(s\in\{\mathrm{mm},0\}\), is fixed.  Assumption ass:pilot-main-outcome-independence makes it independent of the main assignment and the main potential-outcome array.  The uniform conclusion of lem:uniform-zhao-ht-oracle-variance-equivalence therefore yields, uniformly over the realized selected basis and over \(H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)\),
\[
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
\!\left(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}
\mid\widehat B_s,Y\right)
=\ell_{\widehat B_s}(c_H)+o(n^{-1}).
\tag{6}
\]
The remainder in (6) is uniform because the cited lemma is uniform over every bounded fixed array and every \(B\in\mathcal K_n\).  Thus taking a supremum over \(H\) preserves the remainder.  By ass:frechet-covariance-domain and thm:fixed-basis-endpoints,
\[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}\ell_B(c_H)=w(B)
\qquad(B\in\mathcal K_n).
\tag{7}
\]
Applying (7) conditionally at \(B=\widehat B_s\), then integrating over the pilot, proves
\[
\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\sup_{H\in\Gamma(F_1,F_0)}
\mathbb E_{H^{\otimes n}}
\operatorname{Var}_\nu
\!\left(\widehat\tau_{\widehat B_s}^{\mathrm{CV}}
\mid\widehat B_s,Y\right)
\right]
=\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_s)+o(n^{-1})
\tag{8}
\]
for both \(s=\mathrm{mm}\) and \(s=0\).

It remains to use the deterministic geometry condition for the zero-covariance selector.  Choose any \(B_n^0\in\mathcal A_{0,\overline a}\), which is possible by compactness.  Then \(g_{B_n^0}(0)=g_n^\star\).  Empirical optimality of \(\widehat B_0\) and (1) give the pointwise bound
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)
&=g_{B_n^0}(0)-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\\
&\le
|g_{B_n^0}(0)-\widehat g_{B_n^0}(0)|
+|\widehat g_{\widehat B_0}(0)-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)|\\
&\le2e_n.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
The assumed deterministic error bound, evaluated at \(\widehat B_0\), now implies
\[
[q_n-w(\widehat B_0)]_+
\le L_n\{g_n^\star-g_{\widehat B_0}(0)\}
\le2L_ne_n.
\tag{10}
\]
Since \(x\ge q-[q-x]_+\) for all real \(x,q\), (10) yields
\[
\mathbb E_Pw(\widehat B_0)
\ge q_n-2L_n\mathbb E_Pe_n
=q_n-o(n^{-1}),
\tag{11}
\]
where the last equality follows from (3) and \(\sup_nL_n<\infty\).  Substituting the definition of \(q_n\) in (11) is the claimed zero-surrogate conclusion.  The proof uses only a deterministic relation between the two population objective surfaces; it assumes no pilot-dependent modulus at the target rate. \(\square\)
