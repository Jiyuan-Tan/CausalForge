Let
\[
E=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_n\\I_n&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A_B=\Pi^{-1}\Omega B(B^{\top}\Omega B)^{-1}B^{\top}\Omega\Pi^{-1}.
\]
For \(B\in\mathcal B_Z\), define the ordinary-domain intercept and slope
\[
a_B=g_B(0),
\qquad
b_B=-n^{-2}\operatorname{tr}(A_BE),
\qquad g_B(c)=a_B+b_Bc,
\]
and define the endpoint drift penalty
\[
p_\xi(b)=\max\{(2/n-b)c_L,(2/n-b)c_U\}.
\]
Whenever the relevant ordinary-domain extrema are attained, put
\[
a^\star=\max_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}a_B,
\quad
\mathcal A_{0,Z}=\operatorname*{argmax}_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}a_B,
\quad
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{mm},Z}=\operatorname*{argmin}_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B),
\]
\[
\Delta_Z=
\inf_{B_0\in\mathcal A_{0,Z}}w(B_0)
-\inf_{B\in\mathcal B_Z}w(B).
\]
These objects use only the positive-definite ordinary-inverse domain; no singular or Moore--Penrose class is included.
