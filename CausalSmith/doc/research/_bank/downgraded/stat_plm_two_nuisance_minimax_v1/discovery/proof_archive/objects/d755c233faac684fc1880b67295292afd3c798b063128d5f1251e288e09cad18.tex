Fix \(P=P^0_{\beta,v,\mu,\pi}\) in the stated subclass. Since \(X=x_0\) almost surely, \(\pi(X)=\pi(x_0)\) and \(\mu(X)=\mu(x_0)\) are constants. The treatment equation and its conditional moments give
\[
\mathbb E_P[T]=\pi(x_0),\qquad \operatorname{Var}_P(T)=\mathbb E_P[u^2]=v\ge\frac13.
\]
Moreover, \(\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon\mid T]=0\), because \(X=x_0\) almost surely and \(\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon\mid X,T]=0\). Therefore
\[
C:=\operatorname{Cov}_P(T,Y)
=\operatorname{Cov}_P\bigl(T,\beta T+\mu(x_0)+\varepsilon\bigr)
=\beta v,
\qquad V:=\operatorname{Var}_P(T)=v.
\]
Thus \(\beta=C/V\), with \(V\ge1/3\).

For any i.i.d. bounded variable \(W\) satisfying \(|W|\le B\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
\mathbb E\left|m^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^mW_i-\mathbb E W\right|
\le \sqrt{\frac{\operatorname{Var}(W)}m}
\le \frac{B}{\sqrt m}.
\]
Apply this inequality to \(T\), \(Y\), \(TY\), and \(T^2\), whose absolute bounds are respectively \(3\), \(3\), \(9\), and \(9\). Since \(|\overline T|\vee|\overline Y|\vee|\mathbb E T|\vee|\mathbb E Y|\le3\),
\[
\mathbb E\left|\overline T\,\overline Y-(\mathbb E T)(\mathbb E Y)\right|
\le3\mathbb E|\overline Y-\mathbb E Y|+3\mathbb E|\overline T-\mathbb E T|
\le\frac{18}{\sqrt m}.
\]
It follows that
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat C-C|\le\frac{9+18}{\sqrt m}=\frac{27}{\sqrt m}.
\]
Likewise, \(|\overline T^2-(\mathbb E T)^2|\le6|\overline T-\mathbb E T|\), and hence
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat V-V|\le\frac{9+18}{\sqrt m}=\frac{27}{\sqrt m}.
\]
Let \(D=\widehat V\vee(1/6)\). The map \(z\mapsto z\vee(1/6)\) is one-Lipschitz and fixes \(V\), because \(V\ge1/3\). Hence \(|D-V|\le|\widehat V-V|\), while \(D\ge1/6\). Using \(C=\beta V\) and \(|\beta|\le3\),
\[
\left|\frac{\widehat C}{D}-\beta\right|
\le \frac{|\widehat C-C|}{D}+\frac{|\beta|\,|V-D|}{D}
\le6|\widehat C-C|+18|\widehat V-V|.
\]
Projection onto \([-3,3]\) cannot increase distance from \(\beta\in[-3,3]\). Taking expectations and using the two moment bounds gives
\[
\mathbb E\left|\widehat\beta^{\rm deg}-\beta\right|
\le6\frac{27}{\sqrt m}+18\frac{27}{\sqrt m}
=\frac{648}{\sqrt m}.
\]
The argument uses neither atomicity nor any property of the fixed classes beyond membership in the frozen model, so it holds uniformly on the stated non-atomic subclass.
