Fix \(F_\mu,F_\pi\subseteq\mathcal T_3\) and \(P=P^0_{\beta,v,\mu,\pi}\in\mathcal P_{\rm PL}^{0,\rm na}(F_\mu,F_\pi)\). By the definition of the non-atomic subclass, \(P\in\mathcal P_{\rm PL}^{0}(F_\mu,F_\pi)\). Because \(X=x_0\) almost surely, both \(\pi(X)=\pi(x_0)\) and \(\mu(X)=\mu(x_0)\) are constants almost surely. The treatment equation and its conditional moment restrictions therefore give
\[
\mathbb E_P[T]=\pi(x_0),\qquad V:=\operatorname{Var}_P(T)=\mathbb E_P[u^2]=v\ge \frac13.
\]
Furthermore, iterated expectation and \(\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon\mid X,T]=0\) imply \(\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon]=0\) and \(\mathbb E_P[T\varepsilon]=0\). Hence
\[
C:=\operatorname{Cov}_P(T,Y)
=\operatorname{Cov}_P\!\left(T,\beta T+\mu(x_0)+\varepsilon\right)
=\beta V.
\]
In particular, the structural coefficient, which is \(\beta(P)\) under the defining model, satisfies \(\beta(P)=C/V\).

We next bound the empirical covariance and variance. If \(W_1,\ldots,W_m\) are i.i.d. copies of a random variable \(W\) with \(|W|\le B\), then Cauchy--Schwarz and independence yield
\[
\mathbb E\left|m^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^mW_i-\mathbb EW\right|
\le \left\{\mathbb E\left(m^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^m(W_i-\mathbb EW)\right)^2\right\}^{1/2}
=\sqrt{\frac{\operatorname{Var}(W)}m}
\le \frac{B}{\sqrt m}.
\]
The i.i.d. premise is supplied by \(\textnormal{ass:two-n-iid}\). Applying the displayed inequality to \(T\), \(Y\), \(TY\), and \(T^2\), whose absolute bounds are respectively \(3\), \(3\), \(9\), and \(9\), gives
\[
\mathbb E|\overline T-\mathbb ET|\le\frac3{\sqrt m},\quad
\mathbb E|\overline Y-\mathbb EY|\le\frac3{\sqrt m},\quad
\mathbb E\left|m^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^mT_iY_i-\mathbb E[TY]\right|\le\frac9{\sqrt m},
\]
and
\[
\mathbb E\left|m^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^mT_i^2-\mathbb E[T^2]\right|\le\frac9{\sqrt m}.
\]
Because \(|\overline T|,|\overline Y|,|\mathbb ET|,|\mathbb EY|\le3\),
\[
|\overline T\,\overline Y-(\mathbb ET)(\mathbb EY)|
\le 3|\overline Y-\mathbb EY|+3|\overline T-\mathbb ET|.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat C-C|
\le \frac9{\sqrt m}+3\frac3{\sqrt m}+3\frac3{\sqrt m}
=\frac{27}{\sqrt m}.
\]
Similarly, \(|\overline T^2-(\mathbb ET)^2|\le6|\overline T-\mathbb ET|\), and therefore
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat V-V|
\le \frac9{\sqrt m}+6\frac3{\sqrt m}
=\frac{27}{\sqrt m}.
\]

Set \(D=\widehat V\vee(1/6)\). The map \(z\mapsto z\vee(1/6)\) is one-Lipschitz and fixes \(V\), since \(V\ge1/3\). Thus
\[
D\ge\frac16,\qquad |D-V|\le|\widehat V-V|.
\]
Using \(C=\beta(P)V\), \(|\beta(P)|\le3\), and \(D^{-1}\le6\), we obtain
\[
\left|\frac{\widehat C}{D}-\beta(P)\right|
\le \frac{|\widehat C-C|}{D}+\frac{|\beta(P)|\,|V-D|}{D}
\le6|\widehat C-C|+18|\widehat V-V|.
\]
Metric projection onto \([-3,3]\) cannot increase the distance to \(\beta(P)\in[-3,3]\). Taking expectations in the preceding display therefore gives
\[
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes m}}\left|\widehat\beta^{\rm deg}-\beta(P)\right|
\le6\frac{27}{\sqrt m}+18\frac{27}{\sqrt m}
=\frac{648}{\sqrt m}.
\]
The bound is uniform in the fixed classes and in \(P\) because only the model's uniform boundedness and variance restrictions were used. This proves the claim.
