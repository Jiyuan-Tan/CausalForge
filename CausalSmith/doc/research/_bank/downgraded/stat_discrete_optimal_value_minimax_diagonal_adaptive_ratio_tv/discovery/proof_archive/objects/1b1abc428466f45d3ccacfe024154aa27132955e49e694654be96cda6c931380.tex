\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \((\mathbf P,\mathbf Q)\in\Delta_d^2\), and write \(s_x=P_x+Q_x\).  The construction in \(\mathrm{def:l1-embedding}\) gives
\[
 p_x=\frac{s_x}{2},\qquad \pi_x=\frac12.
\]
If \(s_x>0\), multiplication of the cell mass, treatment probability, and conditional outcome probability gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 q_{11,x}&=p_x\pi_x\mu_{1x}
 =\frac{s_x}{2}\frac12\frac{P_x}{s_x}=\frac{P_x}{4},
 &q_{10,x}&=p_x\pi_x(1-\mu_{1x})=\frac{Q_x}{4},\\
 q_{01,x}&=p_x(1-\pi_x)\mu_{0x}=\frac{Q_x}{4},
 &q_{00,x}&=p_x(1-\pi_x)(1-\mu_{0x})=\frac{P_x}{4}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
If \(s_x=0\), nonnegativity implies \(P_x=Q_x=0\), and the zero-ratio convention in \(\mathrm{def:l1-embedding}\) makes the same four identities hold.  Since \(\mathbf P\) and \(\mathbf Q\) are probability vectors,
\[
 \sum_{x=1}^d p_x
 =\frac12\sum_{x=1}^d(P_x+Q_x)=1.
\]
Moreover, \(0<\epsilon<1/2\) implies \(\epsilon<1/2<1-\epsilon\), so the embedded law satisfies fixed overlap on every supported cell.

By \(\mathrm{def:oracle-value}\), zero-mass cells contribute zero and
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 V^\star(P)
 &=\sum_{x=1}^d \frac{P_x+Q_x}{2}
   \max\!\left\{\frac{Q_x}{P_x+Q_x},
                    \frac{P_x}{P_x+Q_x}\right\}\\
 &=\frac12\sum_{x=1}^d\max\{P_x,Q_x\}\\
 &=\frac14\sum_{x=1}^d
   \{P_x+Q_x+\lvert P_x-Q_x\rvert\}\\
 &=\frac12+\frac14\lVert\mathbf P-\mathbf Q\rVert_1.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The slice is proper.  For example, take \(p_1=1\), \(\pi_1=\epsilon\), and \(\mu_{01}=\mu_{11}=0\), with every other cell null.  Conditionally independent Bernoulli potential outcomes and \(Y=Y(A)\) complete this observed law to a member of \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\).  Its only supported-cell propensity equals \(\epsilon\ne1/2\), so it is not on the embedded slice.  Finally, \(\mathrm{def:diagonal-adaptive-estimator}\) computes empirical arm means from the unrestricted arm and success counts and imposes neither equal propensities nor the normalized-slice identities.  It is therefore defined on the full four-cell observed-margin experiment.
