For every sequence in the stated regime that also lies in the range of \(\mathrm{thm:elementary-minimax-bracket}\), multiplication of its bounds by \(n\log n/d\) gives
\[
 c_\epsilon\left\{\frac{\log n}{d}+\frac{\log n}{\log(en)}\right\}
 \le \frac{n\log n}{d}\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le \min\left\{\frac{n\log n}{d},C_\epsilon\log n\right\}.
\]
Because \(d/\log n\to\infty\) and \(\log n/\log(en)\to1\),
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{n\log n}{d}\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\ge c_\epsilon>0.
\]
Both displayed upper envelopes diverge under \(d/\{n\log n\}\to0\), so the current core yields no finite normalized limsup. In particular, any finite limit, if it exists, is strictly positive, but its existence, value, and attainability remain unproved.
