Put \(L_n=\log(en)\), \(m_j=\lvert I_j\rvert\), and \(f(u)=\{\Lambda(u)+\Gamma(u)\}/2\).  For nonzero \(u\in\mathcal C_\epsilon\), put
\[
 \alpha(u)=\frac{s(u)u_{11}}{s_1(u)},\qquad
 \beta(u)=\frac{s(u)u_{01}}{s_0(u)};
\]
set \(\alpha(0)=\beta(0)=0\).  Thus \(f=(\alpha+\beta+\lvert\alpha-\beta\rvert)/2\).  Retain the pilot sets in \(\mathrm{def:pilot-partition}\), and split \(\widehat{\mathcal D}_n\) further into the pilot-sparse set \(\widehat{\mathcal S}_n\), on which either \(\widehat p_x^{(0)}<256L_n/\max\{m_0,1\}\) or \(\min_a\sum_yN^{(0)}_{ay,x}<64L_n\), and the heavy-stripe set \(\widehat{\mathcal T}_n=\widehat{\mathcal D}_n\setminus\widehat{\mathcal S}_n\).

Fix the usual first-kind Chebyshev basis and the height-then-lexicographic enumeration of rational coefficient vectors.  On \(x\in\widehat{\mathcal S}_n\), apply the zero-anchored sparse-square construction of Jiao, Han, and Weissman on the rescaled box \(B_{x,n}\): use the fixed Chebyshev reciprocal polynomial for \(\alpha+\beta\); write
\[
 \lvert\alpha-\beta\rvert
 =\{\sqrt\alpha+\sqrt\beta\}
  \lvert\sqrt\alpha-\sqrt\beta\rvert;
\]
tensorize the degree-\(\lfloor K_n/4\rfloor\) Chebyshev approximants of the two displayed factors, multiply them, and subtract the product's value at the origin.  Among rational roundings of the resulting coefficients, take the first whose certified weighted error is within \(n^{-3}\) of that explicit construction and whose constant coefficient is zero.  Call the result \(P^\star_{x,n}\).  On \(x\in\widehat{\mathcal T}_n\), let
\[
 W_{x,n}=64\sqrt{\widehat p_x^{(0)}L_n/\max\{m_0,1\}}.
\]
Center each coordinate at \(\widehat q^{(0)}_{ay,x}\), standardize it by its pilot radius \(w_{ay,x}\), apply the degree-\(K_n\) centered tensor-Chebyshev approximation to the cusp on the stripe of contrast width \(W_{x,n}\), and take the first rational rounding with certified uniform error within \(n^{-3}\) of that construction.  The certification in both cases also computes the absolute coefficient majorant
\[
 A_{x,n}(v)=\sum_{\lvert r\rvert\le K_n}\lvert c_{r,x}\rvert v^r
\]
on the pilot box enlarged coordinatewise by \(K_n/m_2\); a candidate is accepted only after the interval-arithmetic certificate for the relevant sparse-square or local-stripe majorant succeeds.  If \(\widetilde B_{x,n}=\varnothing\), retain the total fallback \(B_{x,n}=\{0\}\) and \(P^\star_{x,n}\equiv0\).

For \(x\in\widehat{\mathcal R}_n\), use the split-one ratio contribution
\[
 \widehat v_x^{\mathrm{rat}}
 =\widehat p_x^{(1)}\max_{a\in\{0,1\}}\widehat\mu_{ax}^{(1)},
\]
with \(0/0=0\).  If \(m_2=0\), define \(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}=0\).  Otherwise, to implement the factorial part, independently draw \(M\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda_2)\), where \(\lambda_2=m_2/2\).  On \(M\le m_2\), let \(Z_{ay,x}(M)\) be the category count among the first \(M\) observations of \(I_2\).  For \(x\in\widehat{\mathcal D}_n\), expand \(P^\star_{x,n}(u)=\sum_{\lvert r\rvert\le K_n}c_{r,x}u^r\) and lift each monomial by
\[
 U^{\mathrm{poi}}_{x,r}(M)=
 \frac{\prod_{a,y}\{Z_{ay,x}(M)\}_{r_{ay}}}
 {\lambda_2^{\lvert r\rvert}}
\]
when \(\lvert r\rvert\le M\), using zero for positive degrees otherwise and using \(U^{\mathrm{poi}}_{x,0}=1\).  On \(M>m_2\), set the randomized preliminary estimate to zero.  On \(M\le m_2\), sum the ratio and lifted-polynomial contributions and project the sum onto \([0,1]\).  Define \(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}\) as the conditional expectation of this projected randomized estimate given \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\).  This is a nonrandomized, finite, explicit map of the sample because the conditional expectation is the sum over \(M=0,ldots,m_2\) with known Poisson weights.  Every selection is total and arithmetically computable: the Chebyshev coefficients are explicit, rational rounding is enumerated, and weighted-error and coefficient-majorant inequalities over compact semialgebraic boxes are decided by certified interval subdivision.
