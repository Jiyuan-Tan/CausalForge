\textbf{Proof.}
The exact membership, target identity, Markov simulation, decision-theoretic factor \(1/16\), and parametric floor are established in \(\mathrm{lem:exact-l1-transfer-parametric-floor}\).  In particular,
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \ge\frac1{16}\mathfrak R^{L_1}_{n,d},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\ge\frac1{1024n}.
\]
The first inequality is valid for fixed-sample experiments because the kernel produces exactly \(n\) independent causal observations, and conditional Jensen derandomizes the composed estimator without increasing squared risk.  Apply \(\mathrm{lem:two-band-l1-lower}\) to the first display.  For \(2\le d\le\rho n\log(en)\), it gives
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \ge c\frac d{n\log(en)}.
\]
Combining this with the parametric floor and using \(\max\{u,v\}\ge(u+v)/2\) yields
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \ge c_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac d{n\log(en)}\right\}.
\]
The constants may be renamed as functions of \(\epsilon\); the embedded propensity is \(1/2\), so the only overlap requirement is the stated boundary condition \(0<\epsilon<1/2\).  This proves the corrected two-band statement.
