Rescaling \(\mathrm{thm:elementary-minimax-bracket}\) along \(d/\log n\to\infty\) and \(d/(n\log n)\to0\) gives
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{n\log(en)}d\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\ge c_\epsilon,
 \qquad
 \frac{n\log(en)}d\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le C_\epsilon\log(en).
\]
Thus every possible finite limit is strictly positive, but the available upper bound gives neither a finite normalized limsup nor convergence.  The exact identity in \(\mathrm{prop:l1-reduction}\) supplies a normalized equal-propensity lower-bound slice, not an attaining estimator for the unrestricted four-cell model.
