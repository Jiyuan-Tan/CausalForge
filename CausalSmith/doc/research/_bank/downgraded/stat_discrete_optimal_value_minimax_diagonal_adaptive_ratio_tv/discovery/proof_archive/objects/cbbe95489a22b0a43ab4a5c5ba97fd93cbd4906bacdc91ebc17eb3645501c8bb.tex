Fix, for each degree, the enumeration of rational coefficient vectors obtained by increasing maximum numerator-denominator height and then lexicographic order within each finite height shell. For every \(x\in[d]\) and every sample outcome, define \(P^\star_{x,n}\) as follows. If the raw box \(\widetilde B_{x,n}\) in \(\mathrm{def:pilot-partition}\) is empty, use the deterministic fallback \(B_{x,n}=\{0\}\) and \(P^\star_{x,n}\equiv0\). Otherwise, let \(P^\star_{x,n}\) be the first polynomial in that height-then-lexicographic enumeration, among polynomials of total degree at most \(K_n\), whose uniform error on \(B_{x,n}\) is within \(n^{-3}\) of the infimum for \(u\mapsto\{\Lambda(u)+\Gamma(u)\}/2\). For \(x\in\widehat{\mathcal R}_n\), use the split-one ratio plug-in \(\widehat v_x^{\mathrm{rat}}=\widehat p_x^{(1)}\max_a\widehat\mu_{ax}^{(1)}\), with every empirical ratio \(0/0\) set to zero. For \(x\in\widehat{\mathcal D}_n\), expand \(P^\star_{x,n}(u)=\sum_{\lvert r\rvert\le K_n}c_{r,x}u^r\) and replace \(u^r\) by \(\prod_{a,y}(N^{(2)}_{ay,x})_{r_{ay}}/(m_2)_{\lvert r\rvert}\) when \(\lvert r\rvert\le m_2\), and by zero when \(\lvert r\rvert>m_2\); the degree-zero lift equals one because \((m_2)_0=1\). The fallback polynomial therefore contributes zero whenever its raw feasible box is empty. Sum the ratio contributions over \(\widehat{\mathcal R}_n\) and factorial contributions over \(\widehat{\mathcal D}_n\), and truncate the result to \([0,1]\); this is \(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}\). The enumerated near-minimizer is total and arithmetically computable because rational polynomials are uniformly dense and their errors on each nonempty compact semialgebraic \(B_{x,n}\) admit certified interval bounds.
