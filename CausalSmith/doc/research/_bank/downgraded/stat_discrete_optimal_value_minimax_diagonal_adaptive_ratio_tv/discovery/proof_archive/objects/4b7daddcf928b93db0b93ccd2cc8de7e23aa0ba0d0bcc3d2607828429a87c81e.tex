Along every sequence satisfying \(d/\log n\to\infty\), \(d/(n\log n)\to0\), and the range condition in \(\mathrm{thm:sharp-minimax-frontier}\), the normalized minimax risk obeys
\[
 0<c_\epsilon
 \le\liminf_{n\to\infty}
  \frac{n\log(en)}d\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le\limsup_{n\to\infty}
  \frac{n\log(en)}d\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le C_\epsilon<\infty.
\]
Thus the approximation-duality handle fixes the correct normalization up to constants, but the frozen assumptions and constant-factor certificates do not establish equality of the liminf and limsup or attainment of an exact leading constant by a computable fixed-sample estimator.
