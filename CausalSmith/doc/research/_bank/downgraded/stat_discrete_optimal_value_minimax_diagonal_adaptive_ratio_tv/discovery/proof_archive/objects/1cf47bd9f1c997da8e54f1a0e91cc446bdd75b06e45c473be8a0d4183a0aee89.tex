\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), and suppose for contradiction that finite \(C_\epsilon\), positive \(\rho_\epsilon\), and a measurable estimator collection satisfying the displayed uniform bound exist.  Put
\[
 L_n=\log(en),
 \qquad
 d_n=\max\{2,\lfloor L_n\rfloor\}.
\]
For all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 2\leq d_n\leq n^2,
 \qquad
 d_n\leq\rho_\epsilon nL_n,
 \qquad
 \frac{L_n}{2}\leq d_n\leq L_n.
\]
The first condition permits application of \(\mathrm{lem:paired-sign-small-alphabet-floor}\); the second places the same pair \((n,d_n)\) in the range of the supposed certificate.  By \(\mathrm{def:minimax-risk}\), that certificate would imply
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d_n,\epsilon}
 \leq C_\epsilon\left\{
   \frac1n+\frac{d_n}{nL_n}
 \right\}
 \leq\frac{2C_\epsilon}{n}.
 \tag{6}
\]
On the other hand, the paired-sign lemma and \(d_n\geq L_n/2\) give
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d_n,\epsilon}
 \geq\frac{\sqrt{d_n}}{4096\sqrt2\,n}
 \geq\frac{\sqrt{L_n}}{8192n}.
 \tag{7}
\]
Because \(L_n\to\infty\), (7) eventually exceeds (6), which is a contradiction.  Hence the asserted measurable estimator collection does not exist.  A finite computable four-cell overlap-cone construction carrying the proposed uniform certificate would, with its finite tie-breaking convention, be a measurable estimator collection and is excluded as well.

For the final phase statement, \(\mathrm{thm:global-elementary-minimax-bracket}\) proves more than is required here: along every sequence \(d_n\geq2\), without the restriction \(d_n\leq n^2\),
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d_n,\epsilon}=O(n^{-1})
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 d_n=O(1).
\]
When \(d_n=O(1)\), its upper bound is \(O(n^{-1})\) and its parametric floor is \(\Omega(n^{-1})\), so the order is exactly \(n^{-1}\).  The claimed result for sequences with \(2\leq d_n\leq n^2\) follows immediately, while the stronger unrestricted phase conclusion is available from the global theorem.
