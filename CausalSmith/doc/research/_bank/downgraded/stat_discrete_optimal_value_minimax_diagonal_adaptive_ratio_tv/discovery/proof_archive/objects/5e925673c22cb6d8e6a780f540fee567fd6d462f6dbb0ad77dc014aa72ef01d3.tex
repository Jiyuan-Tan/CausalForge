\textbf{Proof.}
Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\), put \(L=\log(en)\), and use the corrected construction in \(\mathrm{def:diagonal-adaptive-estimator}\).  By \(\mathrm{prop:identification-decomposition}\),
\[
 V^\star(P)=\sum_{x=1}^df(q_x),
 \qquad f=(\Lambda+\Gamma)/2.
\]
On the simultaneous pilot event \(\mathcal G_0\) from \(\mathrm{lem:overlap-cone-polynomial-certificate}\), let \(\widetilde V_n^{\mathrm{poi}}\) be the projected randomized estimate in the uncapped Poisson comparison.  Before projection its error decomposes as
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \widetilde V_n^{\mathrm{poi}}-V^\star(P)
 ={}&\sum_{x\in\widehat{\mathcal R}_n}
      \{\widehat v_x^{\mathrm{rat}}-f(q_x)\}\\
 &+\sum_{x\in\widehat{\mathcal D}_n}
   \sum_r c_{r,x}\{\overline U_{x,r}-q_x^r\}\\
 &+\sum_{x\in\widehat{\mathcal D}_n}
   \{P^\star_{x,n}(q_x)-f(q_x)\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Every term is well defined: fixed overlap makes supported arm denominators positive, Poisson factorial denominators are positive on the branch \(m_2>0\), and the zero polynomial is used on an empty raw box.  The branch \(m_2=0\) has squared loss at most one and is absorbed by enlarging \(C_\epsilon\).  Conditional on the pilot, the first two lines use disjoint sample blocks.  The certificate, the inequality \((u+v+w)^2\le3(u^2+v^2+w^2)\), and its mass-weighted bias calculation yield
\[
 E_P\!\left[
  (\widetilde V_n^{\mathrm{poi}}-V^\star(P))^2
  \mathbf1_{\mathcal G_0}
 \right]
 \le C_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac d{nL}\right\}.
\]
On \(\mathcal G_0^c\), projection of the randomized estimate and \(0\le V^\star(P)\le1\) bound squared loss by one, while the certificate gives \(P(\mathcal G_0^c)\le C_\epsilon n^{-4}\).  Its cap error is at most \(C\exp(-m_2/6)\).  Conditional Jensen then transfers the bound from the capped randomized estimate to \(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}\), its conditional expectation given the fixed sample.  Projection onto \([0,1]\) cannot increase distance to the target, so
\[
 E_P[(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}-V^\star(P))^2]
 \le C_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac d{nL}\right\}.
\]
The constants are uniform in \(P\), and the construction is computable by its finite Chebyshev, rational-rounding, factorial-moment, and interval-certification operations.  Taking the supremum over \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\) proves the claim.
