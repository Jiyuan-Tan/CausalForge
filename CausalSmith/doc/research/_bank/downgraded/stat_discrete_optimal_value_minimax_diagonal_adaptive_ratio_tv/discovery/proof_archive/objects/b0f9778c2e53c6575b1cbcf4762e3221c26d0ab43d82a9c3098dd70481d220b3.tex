The formal target is the scalar unrestricted oracle-policy value, not learned-policy regret.  Overlap is fixed, outcomes and treatment are binary, and the confounder alphabet is finite with \(d\le\rho_\epsilon n\log(en)\).  The equal-propensity \(L_1\) experiment is used only as a proper lower-bound slice; the upper bound remains uniform over unknown observational arm masses.  Vanishing overlap, multiple treatments, efficient implementation, confidence intervals, and equality of the normalized-risk liminf and limsup remain outside the proved result.
