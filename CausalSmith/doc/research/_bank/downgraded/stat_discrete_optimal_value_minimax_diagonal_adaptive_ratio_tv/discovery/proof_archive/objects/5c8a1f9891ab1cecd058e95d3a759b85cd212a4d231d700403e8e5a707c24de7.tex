The exact part that does close is the multinomial factorial identity in \(\mathrm{lem:factorial-lift-unbiased}\): conditionally on the pilot, every lifted monomial is unbiased for the corresponding monomial in \(q_x\).  Truncation and \(0\le V^\star(P)\le1\) also give the crude uniform bound
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}E_P[(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}-V^\star(P))^2]\le1.
\]
What is not proved is the asserted sharper order.  In particular, the frozen selector minimizes only uniform approximation error on \(B_{x,n}\); it neither forces \(P^\star_{x,n}(0)=0\) nor supplies the mass-weighted pointwise error and coefficient-growth bounds needed after summing over cells.  The full within-cell and cross-cell factorial covariance bound is also absent.
