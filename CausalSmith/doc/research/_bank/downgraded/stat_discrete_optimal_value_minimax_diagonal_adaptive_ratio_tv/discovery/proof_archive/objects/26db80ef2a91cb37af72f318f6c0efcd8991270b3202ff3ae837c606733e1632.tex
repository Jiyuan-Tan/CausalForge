Zeng, Balakrishnan, Han, and Kennedy, \cite{ZengBalakrishnanHanKennedy2024Discrete}, analyze treatment-specific means with many discrete covariate cells, not the maximum functional.  Jiao, Han, and Weissman, \cite{JiaoHanWeissman2018L1}, provide the normalized \(L_1\) lower theorem and the sparse-square, local-stripe, and factorial-moment toolkit.  Their lower theorem is used only in the band \(d\gtrsim\log^2(en)\), where its uniform-\(\mathbf Q\) dominance condition is verified; the lower band is supplied by the direct paired-sign fuzzy mixture proved here.  Jiao, Venkat, Han, and Weissman, \cite{JiaoVenkatHanWeissman2015Functionals}, provide the zero-constant and coefficient-control ingredients adapted to the four-cell cone.  Luedtke and van der Laan, \cite{LuedtkeVanderLaan2016OptimalValue}, characterize local regularity at treatment-effect ties, while Wu and Yang, \cite{WuYang2016Entropy}, motivate approximation--moment duality.  None of these sources supplies the present uniform observational-ratio-cone upper theorem.
