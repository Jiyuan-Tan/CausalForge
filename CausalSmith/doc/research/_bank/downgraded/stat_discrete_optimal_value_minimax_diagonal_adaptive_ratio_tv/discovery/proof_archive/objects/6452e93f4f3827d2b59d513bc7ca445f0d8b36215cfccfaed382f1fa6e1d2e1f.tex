For each fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \(C_\epsilon<\infty\) and \(\rho_\epsilon>0\) such that the computable estimator \(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}\) satisfies \[\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}E_P[(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}-V^\star(P))^2]\le C_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac{d}{n\log(en)}\right\}\] for every \(2\le d\le\rho_\epsilon n\log(en)\). The bound includes pilot misclassification, approximation bias on every heavy near-tie cell and every sparse overlap-cone cell, and the full within-cell and cross-cell covariance of the factorial lifts.
