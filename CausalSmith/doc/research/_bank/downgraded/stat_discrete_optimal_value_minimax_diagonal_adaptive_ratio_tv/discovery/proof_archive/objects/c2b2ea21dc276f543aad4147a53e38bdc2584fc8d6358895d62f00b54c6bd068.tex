If the constant-factor frontier in \(\mathrm{thm:sharp-minimax-frontier}\) is available, then along the sequences in the question the parametric term is negligible and
\[
 0<c_\epsilon
 \le \liminf\frac{n\log(en)}d\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le \limsup\frac{n\log(en)}d\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le C_\epsilon<\infty.
\]
This proves tightness of the normalization only.  It does not imply equality of the liminf and limsup, identify a limit, or show that the fixed-sample estimator attains an exact constant.
