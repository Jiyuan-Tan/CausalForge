Under \(\mathrm{ass:iid-sampling}\) and fixed overlap on the unchanged unrestricted class \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\), does there exist a finite, computable fixed-sample estimator \(\widehat V_n\) and constants \(C_\epsilon<\infty\) and \(\rho_\epsilon>0\) such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}
 E_P\!\left[\{\widehat V_n-V^\star(P)\}^2\right]
 \le C_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac d{n\log(en)}\right\}
\]
for every \(2\le d\le\rho_\epsilon n\log(en)\)?  In particular, can such an estimator be certified by an explicit four-cell overlap-cone approximation that handles sparse arm denominators, heavy treatment-effect ties, every zero-denominator boundary, and the full covariance of its fixed-sample factorial lifts?
