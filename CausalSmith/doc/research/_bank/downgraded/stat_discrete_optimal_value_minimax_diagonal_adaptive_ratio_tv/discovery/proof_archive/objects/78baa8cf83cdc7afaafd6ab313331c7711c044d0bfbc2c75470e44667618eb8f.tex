Let \(f(u)=\{\Lambda(u)+\Gamma(u)\}/2\), and use the corrected selector in \(\mathrm{def:diagonal-adaptive-estimator}\).  For each fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are \(C_\epsilon<\infty\) and \(\rho_\epsilon>0\) such that, whenever \(2\le d\le\rho_\epsilon n\log(en)\), the following hold.  With probability at least \(1-C_\epsilon n^{-4}\), every true \(q_x\) is in its nonempty pilot box.  Conditionally on that event and on the pilot, the sparse selector is zero-anchored and satisfies
\[
 \lvert P^\star_{x,n}(u)-f(u)\rvert
 \le C_\epsilon\left\{
  \sqrt{\frac{s(u)}{n\log(en)}}+
  \frac1{n\log(en)}
 \right\},
 \qquad u\in B_{x,n},\quad x\in\widehat{\mathcal S}_n,
\]
whereas the heavy-stripe selector has error at most \(C_\epsilon W_{x,n}/K_n+C_\epsilon n^{-3}\) on \(B_{x,n}\).  If \(\overline U_{x,r}\) denotes the uncapped Poisson factorial lift in the comparison used to analyze the corrected estimator, then its certified coefficient majorants imply
\[
 E_P\!\left[
  \left.
  \left\{
   \sum_{x\in\widehat{\mathcal D}_n}
   \sum_{\lvert r\rvert\le K_n}c_{r,x}
    \bigl(\overline U_{x,r}-q_x^r\bigr)
  \right\}^{\!2}
  \right|I_0
 \right]
 \le C_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac{d}{n\log(en)}\right\}.
\]
The uncapped cross-cell covariances are zero.  The ratio contributions over \(\widehat{\mathcal R}_n\), including pilot classification errors, obey the same bound; the cap error is exponentially small; and conditional-expectation derandomization cannot increase squared risk.
