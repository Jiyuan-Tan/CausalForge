For every fixed (0<\epsilon<1/2), there are constants (0<c_\epsilon\leq C_\epsilon<\infty) and (
ho_\epsilon>0) such that, uniformly for (2\leq d\leq\rho_\epsilon n\log(en)),
\[
 c_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac{d}{n\log(en)}\right\}
 \leq \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \leq \min\left\{1,C_\epsilon\frac d n\right\}.
\]
Consequently, (d=o(n\log n)) is necessary for consistency, (d=o(n)) is sufficient for consistency by the explicit empirical cellwise ratio estimator, (d=O(\log n)) is necessary for parametric-order risk, and fixed (d) is sufficient for parametric-order risk.  The gap between these necessary and sufficient regimes is not closed by this theorem.
