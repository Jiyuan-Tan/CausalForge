The proved lemma \(\mathrm{lem:exact-l1-transfer-parametric-floor}\) establishes, without asymptotics,
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\ge\frac1{16}\mathfrak R^{L_1}_{n,d}
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\ge\frac{1}{1024n}.
\]
Thus the exact causal membership, normalization, target separation, Markov reduction, derandomization step, and parametric floor are closed.  The cited known-\(\mathbf Q\) lower theorem has an explicit dominance condition; for uniform \(\mathbf Q\) that condition directly covers \(d\) at least a constant multiple of \(\log^2 n\), while the parametric floor covers the desired combined lower rate when \(d=O(\log n)\).  A uniform argument for the intervening dimensions, or a separate fuzzy-prior construction for the two-unknown fixed-sample experiment, remains to be supplied.
