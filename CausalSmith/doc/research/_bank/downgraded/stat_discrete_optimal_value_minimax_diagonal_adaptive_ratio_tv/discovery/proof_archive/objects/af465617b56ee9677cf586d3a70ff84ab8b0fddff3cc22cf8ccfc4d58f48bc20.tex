There are universal constants \(c,D,\rho>0\) such that the two-sample multinomial risk in \(\mathrm{def:l1-embedding}\) satisfies
\[
 \mathfrak R^{L_1}_{n,d}\geq c\frac{d}{n\log(en)}
\]
for every \(2\leq d\leq\rho n\log(en)\).  For \(d\leq D\{\log(en)\}^2\) this follows from a paired-sign fuzzy mixture, and for \(d\geq D\{\log(en)\}^2\) it follows from \(\mathrm{lem:jiao-han-weissman-known-q-lower}\) with uniform \(\mathbf Q\).
