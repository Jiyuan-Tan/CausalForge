\textbf{Proof.}
The lower inequality is exactly the conclusion of (mathrm{lem:l1-lower-transfer}), after naming its positive range constant (
ho_\epsilon).  By (mathrm{def:minimax-risk}), the minimax infimum is no larger than the worst-case risk of any one measurable data-driven estimator.  Evaluate that infimum at the explicit statistic in (mathrm{lem:empirical-cellwise-ratio-upper}).  That lemma yields
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \leq\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}
 E_P\!\left[
  \{\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{plug}}-V^\star(P)\}^2
 \right]
 \leq\min\left\{1,C_\epsilon\frac d n\right\},
\]
which proves the bracket.

If the minimax risk tends to zero, the lower bound forces
\[
 \frac d{n\log(en)}\longrightarrow0,
\]
so (d=o(n\log n)) is necessary.  If (d=o(n)), the displayed plug-in upper bound tends to zero, proving sufficiency.  If the risk is (O(n^{-1})), multiplication of the lower bound by (n) forces (d/log(en)=O(1)), so (d=O(\log n)) is necessary.  Finally, when (d) is fixed, the plug-in upper bound is (O(n^{-1})), while the parametric component of the lower bound is (Omega(n^{-1})); hence fixed (d) is sufficient for parametric-order risk.  No step identifies the unresolved intermediate regimes.
