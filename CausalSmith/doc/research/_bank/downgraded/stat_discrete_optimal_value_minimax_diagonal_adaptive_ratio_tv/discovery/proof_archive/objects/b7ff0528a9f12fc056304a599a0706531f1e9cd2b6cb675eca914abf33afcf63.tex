\textbf{Proof.}
The membership, normalization, target identity, exact Markov simulation, decision-theoretic factor, and parametric floor are established in \(\mathrm{lem:exact-l1-transfer-parametric-floor}\).  Explicitly, the embedded four-cell probabilities sum to one, their supported-cell propensity is \(1/2\in[\epsilon,1-\epsilon]\), and the potential-outcome completion satisfies consistency and conditional exchangeability.  Moreover,
\[
 V^\star(P)=\frac12+\frac14\lVert\mathbf P-\mathbf Q\rVert_1,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \geq\frac1{16}\mathfrak R^{L_1}_{n,d},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\geq\frac1{1024n}.
\]
The first risk inequality follows by composing any causal estimator with \(\mathsf K_{n,d}\), multiplying by four and subtracting two; conditional Jensen removes the kernel randomization.  Since a Markov kernel contracts total variation, it also verifies the contraction assertion in the statement.

Apply \(\mathrm{lem:two-band-l1-lower}\).  Its proof gives the paired-sign construction below a numerical multiple of \(\{\log(en)\}^2\), checks the dominance condition of \(\mathrm{lem:jiao-han-weissman-known-q-lower}\) for uniform \(\mathbf Q\) above that threshold, and yields universal \(c,\rho>0\) such that
\[
 \mathfrak R^{L_1}_{n,d}\geq c\frac{d}{n\log(en)},
 \qquad
 2\leq d\leq\rho n\log(en).
\]
Combining the last display with the factor \(1/16\) proves the asserted normalized-distance lower bound.  Combining it with the parametric floor and using \(\max\{u,v\}\geq(u+v)/2\) gives, after renaming constants,
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \geq c_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac{d}{n\log(en)}\right\}
\]
on the same band.  The embedded propensity is exactly \(1/2\), so \(0<\epsilon<1/2\) is the only overlap boundary condition, and constants that are universal may harmlessly be indexed by \(\epsilon\).  This proves every assertion.
