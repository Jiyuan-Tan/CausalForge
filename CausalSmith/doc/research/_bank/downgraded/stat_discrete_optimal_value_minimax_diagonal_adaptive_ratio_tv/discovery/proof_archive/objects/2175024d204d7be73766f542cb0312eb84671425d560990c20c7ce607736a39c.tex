There exist numerical constants \(c>0\), \(\rho>0\), and \(D>0\) such that, for every \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\) satisfying \(d\leq\rho n\log(en)\),
\[
 \mathfrak R^{L_1}_{n,d}\geq c\frac{d}{n\log(en)}.
\]
For \(2\leq d\leq D\{\log(en)\}^2\), this conclusion follows from an explicit paired-sign fuzzy mixture.  For \(D\{\log(en)\}^2\leq d\leq\rho n\log(en)\), it follows by applying \(\mathrm{lem:jiao-han-weissman-known-q-lower}\) with \(\mathbf Q\) uniform and then simulating the second, \(\mathbf Q\)-distributed, sample in the known-\(\mathbf Q\) experiment.
