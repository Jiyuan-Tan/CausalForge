\textbf{Proof.}
By the definition of minimax risk, evaluating its infimum at the single data-driven estimator \(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}\) gives
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}
 E_P[(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}-V^\star(P))^2].
\]
The rightmost inequality is \(\mathrm{lem:diagonal-polynomial-upper}\).  The leftmost inequality is \(\mathrm{lem:l1-lower-transfer}\), after decreasing \(\rho_\epsilon\) to the smaller range constant and replacing the two positive constants by their minimum and maximum.  This proves the three-part display.

For the phase statements, multiply the two-sided bound by \(n\).  The result is bounded above and below by positive constant multiples of
\[
 1+\frac d{\log(en)}.
\]
Hence the risk is of order \(n^{-1}\) exactly when \(d/\log(en)\) is bounded, equivalently \(d=O(\log n)\) along diverging sequences.  Likewise, within the stated range,
\[
 \frac1n+\frac d{n\log(en)}\longrightarrow0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 d=o(n\log n).
\]
Finally, \(\mathrm{def:l1-embedding}\) fixes every supported propensity at \(1/2\), whereas \(\mathrm{def:model-class}\) permits, for example, a supported cell with propensity \(\epsilon\).  The embedded experiment is therefore a proper slice of \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\), while the upper lemma takes its supremum over the entire class.  Thus the asserted model-class strengthening concerns the scope of the upper bound and does not identify the full observational experiment with its lower-bound slice.
