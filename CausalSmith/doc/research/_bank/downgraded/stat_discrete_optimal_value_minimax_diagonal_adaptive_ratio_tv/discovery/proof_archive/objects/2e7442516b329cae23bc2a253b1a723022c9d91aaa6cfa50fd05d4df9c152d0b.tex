Let \(m_2=\lvert I_2\rvert\).  Under \(\mathrm{ass:iid-sampling}\), for every \(x\in[d]\) and every \(r\in\mathbb N_0^4\) with \(\lvert r\rvert\le m_2\),
\[
 E_P\!\left[
   \frac{\prod_{a,y}(N^{(2)}_{ay,x})_{r_{ay}}}{(m_2)_{\lvert r\rvert}}
 \right]
 =\prod_{a,y}q_{ay,x}^{r_{ay}}.
\]
The identity also holds conditionally on the pilot block \(I_0\), and the degree-zero lift equals one.
