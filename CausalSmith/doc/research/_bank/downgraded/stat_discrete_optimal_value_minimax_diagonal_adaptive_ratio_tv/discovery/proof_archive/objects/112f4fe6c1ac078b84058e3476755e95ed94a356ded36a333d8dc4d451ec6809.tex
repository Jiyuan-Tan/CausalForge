For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \(0<c_\epsilon\le C_\epsilon<\infty\) and \(\rho_\epsilon>0\) such that, uniformly for \(2\le d\le\rho_\epsilon n\log(en)\),
\[
 c_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac d{n\log(en)}\right\}
 \le\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}
 E_P\!\left[
  \left\{\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}-V^\star(P)\right\}^2
 \right]
 \le\min\!\left\{1,C_\epsilon\frac dn\right\}.
\]
Consequently, along sequences in this range, \(d=o(n\log n)\) is necessary for consistency and \(d=o(n)\) is sufficient.  Likewise, \(d=O(\log n)\) is necessary for minimax risk of order \(n^{-1}\), while fixed \(d\ge2\) is sufficient for that order.  These implications do not close the intermediate consistency or parametric-order regimes.
