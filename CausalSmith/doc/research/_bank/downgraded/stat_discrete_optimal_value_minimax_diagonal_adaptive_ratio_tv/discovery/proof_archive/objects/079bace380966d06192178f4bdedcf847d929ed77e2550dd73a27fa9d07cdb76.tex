The explicit one-pass statistic \(\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}\) satisfies, for every \(n,d\ge1\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}
 E_P\!\left[\{\widehat V_n^{\mathrm{DAT}}-V^\star(P)\}^2\right]
 \le \min\!\left\{1,\left(\frac12+\frac6\epsilon\right)\frac dn\right\}.
\]
Together with the lower bound, this yields the honest bracket
\[
 c_\epsilon\left\{\frac1n+\frac d{n\log(en)}\right\}
 \le\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \le\min\!\left\{1,C_\epsilon\frac dn\right\}.
\]
This is the strongest certified achievability result on the unchanged unrestricted observational class; it is larger than the requested nonparametric term by a factor of order \(\log n\).
