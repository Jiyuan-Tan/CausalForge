\textbf{Proof.} For every \(b'\ge b_*\), monotonicity gives \(d_{b'}\le d_{b_*}\le P_{b_*}\). Therefore
\[
|\widehat\theta_{b_*}-\widehat\theta_{b'}|-P_{b_*}-P_{b'}
\le d_{b_*}+d_{b'}
\le2P_{b_*},
\]
and hence \(\Delta_{b_*}^+\le2P_{b_*}\). By the minimizing property,
\[
\Delta_{\widehat b}^++P_{\widehat b}\le3P_{b_*}.
\]
If \(\widehat b\le b_*\), the definition of \(\Delta_{\widehat b}^+\), applied with \(b'=b_*\), gives
\[
|\widehat\theta_{\widehat b}-\widehat\theta_{b_*}|
\le \Delta_{\widehat b}^++P_{\widehat b}+P_{b_*}
\le4P_{b_*}.
\]
Also \(|\widehat\theta_{b_*}-\theta|\le P_{b_*}+d_{b_*}\le2P_{b_*}\), proving the result in this case. If \(\widehat b>b_*\), then \(d_{\widehat b}\le d_{b_*}\le P_{b_*}\), while \(P_{\widehat b}\le3P_{b_*}\) by the preceding display. Thus
\[
|\widehat\theta_{\widehat b}-\theta|
\le P_{\widehat b}+d_{\widehat b}
\le4P_{b_*}.
\]
The claimed uniform constant six follows. \(\square\)
