\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}
\ge \frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\]
The supported prior behind this inequality is drawn before assignment, belongs to the no-carryover subclass of every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), and leaves one independent counterfactual sign hidden at every time. The conditional Bayes-risk argument applies separately to every \(\pi\in\mathcal D_T\) and every observed-data estimator. Hence the lower chain holds for deterministic, history-dependent, and outcome-adaptive kernels without imposing positivity.

For the upper bound, define
\[
b_\beta=\left\lceil T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\right\rceil.
\]
If \(T=1\), then \(b_\beta=1\). If \(T\ge2\), put \(x=T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\). Since \(\beta>0\), one has \(1\le x<T\), so \(1\le b_\beta\le T\). Moreover,
\[
b_\beta\le x+1\le2x.
\]
Thus \(b_\beta\) is admissible for every \(T\ge1\).

Apply Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) with \(b=b_\beta\). Its stationary Markov design belongs to \(\mathcal D_T\), all homogeneous-exposure probabilities in its Horvitz--Thompson estimator are strictly positive, and
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_\beta)}}
\left|\widehat\vartheta_{b_\beta}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|
\le
\sqrt{12e}\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}
+\frac{2C}{\beta}\frac{(T-b_\beta-1)_+}{T}b_\beta^{-\beta}.
\]
For \(T\ge2\), the ceiling inequalities \(b_\beta\le2x\) and \(b_\beta\ge x\), together with \((T-b_\beta-1)_+/T\le1\), imply
\[
\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}
\le \sqrt2\,T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad
\frac{(T-b_\beta-1)_+}{T}b_\beta^{-\beta}
\le T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
When \(T=1\), the second term is zero and the first term is \(\sqrt{12e}B\le\sqrt{24e}B\), so the resulting bound below also holds. Since \(\beta\ge\beta_->0\), for every \(T\ge1\),
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_\beta)}}
\left|\widehat\vartheta_{b_\beta}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|
\le
\left(\sqrt{24e}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}\right)
T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
The constant contains no additional factor \(e\) in its carryover term: Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) obtains that term from the exact exposure-weight identity \(\mathbb E[H_{t,b,a}/\upsilon_{t,b,a}]=1\). Taking the infimum over estimators and designs proves the oracle upper bound with \(C_0=\sqrt{24e}B+2C/\beta_-\). The proved lower exponent is \(1/2\), whereas the upper exponent is \(\beta/(2\beta+1)\); the argument supplies no matching lower bound at the latter exponent. \(\square\)
