The complete proved bracket is
\[
1\le A_T(C)\le\frac{\sqrt2\,C_0}{B}T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)},
\qquad C_0=32\sqrt2 B+\frac{8C}{\beta_-}.
\]
The lower inequality is uniform over all sequential policies. The upper inequality is attained by one stationary Markov switchback and Horvitz--Thompson estimator tuned to \(\beta_-\). The oracle denominator obeys the stronger exact supported-prior bound
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}.
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:rough-prior-cadence-kl}\) supplies the valid rough fixed-before-assignment prior and policy-uniform likelihood reduction, and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:smooth-block-prior-risk}\) supplies the exact smooth-class penalty \(BL(\sqrt3 T)^{-1}\mathbb E\sqrt{U_L(W)}\). No divergence of \(A_T(C)\), no \(T^{o(1)}\) upper bound, and no sharp order have been proved.
