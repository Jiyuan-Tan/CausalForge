\textbf{Proof.} Every run length in the displayed schedule equals \(2b\) for a dyadic \(b\), and hence is even. Therefore all run starts have one common parity. Let \(H\in\{0,1\}\) denote the parity of the retained position of a tag-one run. The equilibrium offset makes \(H\) uniform. Whenever \(I_{t,1}=1\), necessarily \(t\equiv H\pmod2\).

Consider
\[
Y_t(z_{1:t})=B(-1)^t(2z_t-1).
\]
It is bounded and independent of the past conditional on the current treatment, so its historical oscillation is zero and it belongs to every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\). Its time-\(t\) all-treated versus all-control contrast is \(2B(-1)^t\). For this array, the arm-specific Horvitz--Thompson summand is the same under either realized arm. Thus, up to the declared \(O(T^{-1})\) boundary indices,
\[
\widehat\theta_1
=\frac{2B}{T}\sum_{t:\rho_{t,1}>0}
(-1)^t\frac{I_{t,1}}{\rho_{t,1}}.
\]
If \(t\equiv H\pmod2\), then
\[
\mathbb E\!\left(\frac{I_{t,1}}{\rho_{t,1}}\,\middle|\,H\right)=2,
\]
whereas this conditional expectation is zero for the other parity. Hence
\[
\mathbb E(\widehat\theta_1\mid H)
=2B(-1)^H+O(B/T).
\]
The law of total variance yields
\[
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\theta_1)
\ge \operatorname{Var}\{\mathbb E(\widehat\theta_1\mid H)\}
=4B^2+O(B^2/T).
\]
On the other hand, expanding the variance of the preceding Horvitz--Thompson sum and applying the triangle inequality shows
\[
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\theta_1)
\le \frac{4B^2}{T^2}
\sum_{t,s}
\left|\frac{\rho_{ts,11}}{\rho_{t,1}\rho_{s,1}}-1\right|
=V_1^{\mathrm{sched}}.
\]
Consequently \(\bar V_1\ge V_1^{\mathrm{sched}}\ge 2B^2\) for all sufficiently large even \(T\). Since \(K=O(\log T)\), the proposed upper envelope \((B^2+C^2)K^4/T\) tends to zero. This is a contradiction. The witness also gives the requested smallest-structure stress test: the defect is a design-periodicity obstruction and uses an array with no carryover at all. \(\square\)
