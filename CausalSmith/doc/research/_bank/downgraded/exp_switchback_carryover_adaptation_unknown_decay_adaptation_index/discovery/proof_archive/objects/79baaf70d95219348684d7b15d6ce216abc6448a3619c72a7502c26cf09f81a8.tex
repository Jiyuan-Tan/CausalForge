\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) proves, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\]
The underlying prior is sampled before assignment, is supported on the no-carryover subclass of every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), and leaves one independent counterfactual sign hidden at every time under every sequential policy. Thus the lower bound includes deterministic, history-dependent, and outcome-adaptive kernels and uses no positivity condition.

For the upper bound, put
\[
b_\beta=\left\lceil T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\right\rceil.
\]
For \(T=1\), one has \(b_\beta=1\). For integer \(T\ge2\), \(1<T^{1/(2\beta+1)}<T\), so \(1\le b_\beta\le T\). Moreover,
\[
b_\beta\le T^{1/(2\beta+1)}+1\le2T^{1/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
Apply Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) with \(b=b_\beta\). Its explicit stationary Markov switchback belongs to \(\mathcal D_T\), every homogeneous-exposure denominator is positive, and its observed-data estimator obeys
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_\beta)}}\left\lvert\widehat\vartheta_{b_\beta}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right\rvert
\le8\sqrt7\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}+\frac{8C}{\beta_-}b_\beta^{-\beta}.
\]
The ceiling bounds imply
\[
\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}\le\sqrt2\,T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad
b_\beta^{-\beta}\le T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_\beta)}}\left\lvert\widehat\vartheta_{b_\beta}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right\rvert
\le\left(8\sqrt{14}B+\frac{8C}{\beta_-}\right)T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
Taking the infimum over all designs and estimators proves the upper bound with
\[
C_0=8\sqrt{14}B+\frac{8C}{\beta_-},
\]
uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\). The lower exponent \(1/2\) and the upper exponent \(\beta/(2\beta+1)\) differ; the argument supplies no matching lower bound at the upper exponent. \(\square\)
