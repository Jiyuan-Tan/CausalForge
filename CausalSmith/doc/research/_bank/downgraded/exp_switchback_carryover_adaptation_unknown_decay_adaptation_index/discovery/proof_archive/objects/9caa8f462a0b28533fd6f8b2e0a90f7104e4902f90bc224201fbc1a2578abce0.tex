\textbf{Proof.} The lower bound \(A_T(C)\ge1\) follows because the risk of any fixed procedure over each class is at least the defining oracle infimum, whose strict positivity follows from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\).

For the upper bound, put
\[
b_-=\left\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\right\rceil
\]
and use the stationary Markov design and estimator from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) with this one value of \(b\). For every \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}\le C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta_-)}
\]
for every \(\ell\ge1\). The definitions therefore give the nesting
\[
\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\subseteq\mathcal P_{T,\beta_-}(C).
\]
Applying the Markov-washout bound at \(\beta_-\), together with \(b_-\le2T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\) and \(b_-^{-\beta_-}\le T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)}\), yields simultaneously for all \(\beta\) in the range
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_-)}}
\left|\widehat\vartheta_{b_-}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|
\le C_0T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)}.
\]
On the other hand, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\) gives
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}
\]
for every such \(\beta\). The risk ratio of this single procedure is consequently at most
\[
\frac{C_0T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)}}{B/\sqrt{2T}}
=\frac{\sqrt2\,C_0}{B}T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)}.
\]
Taking the supremum over \(\beta\) and then noting that \(A_T(C)\) infimizes over all procedures proves the upper bound. \(\square\)
