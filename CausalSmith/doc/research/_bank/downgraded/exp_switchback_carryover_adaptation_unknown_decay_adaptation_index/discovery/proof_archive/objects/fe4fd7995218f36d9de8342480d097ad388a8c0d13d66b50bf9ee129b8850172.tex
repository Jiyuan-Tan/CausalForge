The strongest closed finite-horizon conclusion is

1\le A_T(C)\le\frac{N_T^\star}{D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}}\le\frac{N_T^{\mathrm{fin}}}{D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}}\le\frac{\sqrt2}{B}\left[\sqrt{(8e-4)\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_-+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac C{\beta_-}\right]T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)},

where, for \(T\ge2\),
\[
N_T^\star=\min\left\{B,\min_{1\le b\le T}U_{T,\beta_-}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b)\right\}.
\]
The \(B\) branch is the fixed-before-assignment persistent homogeneous-path fair-coin procedure, and its worst-case risk is exactly \(B\). Moreover, \(A_1(C)=1\), while the strongest unconditional two-class factor remains \(g_T=1\). The proved low-energy and endpoint-persistence lower bounds remain valid but do not imply K1 or K2. Thus logarithmic divergence, bounded adaptation, and other unresolved regimes remain compatible with the proved results.
