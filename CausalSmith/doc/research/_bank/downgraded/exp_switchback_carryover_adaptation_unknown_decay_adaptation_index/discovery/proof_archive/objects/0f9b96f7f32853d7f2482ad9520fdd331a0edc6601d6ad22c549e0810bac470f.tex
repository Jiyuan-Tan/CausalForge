\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}
\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\]
The supported prior behind this bound is drawn before assignment, belongs to the no-carryover subclass of every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), and leaves one independent counterfactual sign hidden at each time. The argument therefore applies to every kernel in \(\mathcal D_T\), including deterministic, history-dependent, and outcome-adaptive kernels, without a positivity restriction.

For the upper bound, set
\[
b_\beta=\left\lceil T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\right\rceil.
\]
For \(T=1\), this equals one. For \(T\ge2\), it satisfies \(1\le b_\beta\le T\) and
\[
b_\beta\le T^{1/(2\beta+1)}+1
\le2T^{1/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
Apply Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) with \(b=b_\beta\), using the stronger replacement-bias inequality established in its proof. The stated design belongs to \(\mathcal D_T\), its homogeneous-exposure probabilities are positive, and its observed-data estimator satisfies
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_\beta)}}|\widehat\vartheta_{b_\beta}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\le\sqrt{12e}\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}
+\frac{2C}{\beta}\frac{(T-b_\beta-1)_+}{T}b_\beta^{-\beta}.
\]
The ceiling bounds and \((T-b_\beta-1)_+/T\le1\) imply
\[
\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}
\le\sqrt2\,T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad
\frac{(T-b_\beta-1)_+}{T}b_\beta^{-\beta}
\le T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
Since \(\beta\ge\beta_-\), it follows that
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_\beta)}}|\widehat\vartheta_{b_\beta}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\le\left(\sqrt{24e}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}\right)T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
Taking the infima over estimators and designs proves the oracle upper bound with
\[
C_0=\sqrt{24e}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}.
\]
The lower exponent \(1/2\) and upper exponent \(\beta/(2\beta+1)\) differ; no step supplies a matching lower bound at the upper exponent. \(\square\)
