\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}
\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\]
The supported prior behind this inequality lies in the no-carryover subclass of every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), is fixed before assignment, and leaves an independent counterfactual sign hidden at each time. The lemma applies separately to every \(\pi\in\mathcal D_T\) and every observed-data estimator, so the lower bound includes deterministic, history-dependent, and outcome-adaptive kernels and requires no positivity condition.

We first settle \(T=1\). Let \(W_1\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) and use
\[
\widehat\theta_1=(2W_1-1)Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}.
\]
Because \(\operatorname{GATE}_1(Y)=Y_1(1)-Y_1(0)\), the absolute error equals \(|Y_1(0)|\) when \(W_1=1\) and \(|Y_1(1)|\) when \(W_1=0\). Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass:bounded-array}\) gives
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat\theta_1-\operatorname{GATE}_1(Y)|
=\frac12\{|Y_1(0)|+|Y_1(1)|\}\le B.
\]
Thus \(R_1(\beta;C)\le B\). The exact-binomial lower bound equals \(B\) at \(T=1\), proving \(R_1(\beta;C)=B\).

For \(T\ge2\), Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) constructs, for every \(b\in\{1,\ldots,T\}\), a stationary Markov design and an observed-data estimator with worst-case risk at most \(U_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b)\). It also proves
\[
\upsilon_{t,b,a}\ge\frac{1}{2q_b}>\frac{1}{2e},
\]
with unattained uniform infimum \(1/(2e)\). Independently, boundedness implies
\[
|\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\le\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T\{|Y_t(\mathbf1_{1:t})|+|Y_t(\mathbf0_{1:t})|\}
\le2B.
\]
Hence the zero estimator has risk at most \(2B\). It follows that
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\le\min\left\{2B,\min_{1\le b\le T}U_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b)\right\}
\qquad(T\ge2).
\]
The finite minimum is attained, and a smallest minimizing scale is a deterministic function of \(T,B,C,\beta\). Together with the one-period procedure, this proves \(R_T(\beta;C)\le\mathfrak U_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}\). Notice that the \(T=1\) branch is supplied by the explicit Bernoulli procedure; it does not assert \(U_{1,\beta}^{\sharp}(1)=B\).

Put \(K=8e+4\), \(x=T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\), and \(b_\beta=\lceil x\rceil\). For \(T\ge2\),
\[
\frac{b_\beta}{x}\le1+\frac1x\le1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad b_\beta\ge x.
\]
The rate corollary in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
U_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b_\beta)
&\le \sqrt K\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}+\frac{2C}{\beta}b_\beta^{-\beta}\\
&\le\left[\sqrt{K\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta}\right]T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
At \(T=1\), the middle piecewise certificate equals \(B\), which is no larger than the same right-hand side. This establishes the complete pointwise upper chain for every \(T\ge1\).

Finally, \(\beta\le\beta_+\) implies
\[
2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\le2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)},
\]
and \(\beta\ge\beta_->0\) implies \(1/\beta\le1/\beta_-\). Consequently the pointwise coefficient is at most
\[
\sqrt{(8e+4)\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}=C_0^\star.
\]
The replacement part of \(U_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b)\) is the bounded, endpoint-aware expected replacement-error upper bound from the exact exposure-weight identity; the finite replacement sum is only its rate corollary. Nothing in the proof identifies the optimized certificate with oracle risk, and the lower exponent remains unmatched with the upper exponent. \(\square\)
