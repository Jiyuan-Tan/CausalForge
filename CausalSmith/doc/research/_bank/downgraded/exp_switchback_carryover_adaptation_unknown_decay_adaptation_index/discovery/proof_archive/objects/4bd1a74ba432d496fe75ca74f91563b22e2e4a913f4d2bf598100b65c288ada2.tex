Let \(b_-=\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\rceil\) and \(C_0=\sqrt{24e}B+2C/\beta_-\). The strongest unconditional result is
\[
1\le A_T(C)\le
\frac{\sqrt{12e}\,B\sqrt{b_-/T}+(2C/\beta_-)((T-b_--1)_+/T)b_-^{-\beta_-}}
{B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}}
\le \frac{\sqrt2\,C_0}{B}T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)}.
\]
The upper bound is achieved by one decay-agnostic stationary Markov switchback and observed-data Horvitz--Thompson estimator tuned to \(\beta_-\); the lower bound applies to all sequential outcome-adaptive policies. In addition, the rough prior gives risk at least \(s_b/25\) whenever its expected likelihood energy is at most \(3a^2/64\). When \(\eta=1/2\) satisfies the amplitude condition,
\[
\frac{C}{2^{2+\beta}}b^{-\beta}\le s_b\le\frac C2b^{-\beta}.
\]
For every arm \(\alpha\) and \(1\le K<T\), the persistence construction gives
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}\mathbb E_\pi
\left|\widehat\theta-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|
\ge
\frac{(T-K)\delta_K}{2T}\{1-Q_\pi(E_{\alpha,K})\}.
\]
These results certify only \(g_T=1\) unconditionally and do not settle either K1 or K2.
