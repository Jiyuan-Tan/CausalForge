\textbf{Proof.} Put \(r=r_b=b/(b+1)\), \(s=1/(b+1)\), \(q_b=r^{-b}=(1+1/b)^b\), and \(\lambda=r-s=(b-1)/(b+1)\). The assignment process is the stationary symmetric two-state Markov chain with transition matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}r&s\\s&r\end{pmatrix}.
\]
It is outcome-independent and hence belongs to \(\mathcal D_T\). Stationarity and the Markov property give
\[
\upsilon_{t,b,a}=\frac12r^{k_t}.
\]
Because \(k_t\le b\) and \(0<r<1\),
\[
\upsilon_{t,b,a}\ge\frac12r^b=\frac{1}{2q_b}.
\]
The strict inequality \(q_b<e\) follows from \(b\log(1+1/b)<1\), and therefore
\[
\upsilon_{t,b,a}\ge\frac{1}{2q_b}>\frac{1}{2e}>0.
\]
For every \(b\), taking \(T\ge b+1\) and \(t=b+1\) gives \(k_t=b\) and \(\upsilon_{t,b,a}=1/(2q_b)\). Since \(q_b\to e\), the uniform infimum is \(1/(2e)\); it is approached but not attained at a finite \(b\). Thus every denominator in \(\widehat\vartheta_b\) is positive.

Fix \(Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), put \(y_{t,a}=Y_t(\mathbf a_{1:t})\), and define
\[
Z_t=\frac{H_{t,b,1}y_{t,1}}{\upsilon_{t,b,1}}-\frac{H_{t,b,0}y_{t,0}}{\upsilon_{t,b,0}},
\qquad
\widetilde\theta_b=\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T Z_t.
\]
The two exposure events at a fixed time are disjoint. The inclusion-probability identity gives
\[
\mathbb E Z_t=y_{t,1}-y_{t,0},
\qquad
\mathbb E\widetilde\theta_b=\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y).
\]
Moreover, Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass:bounded-array}\) and \(\upsilon_{t,b,a}=r^{k_t}/2\) give the time-specific second-moment bound
\[
\mathbb E Z_t^2
=\frac{y_{t,1}^2}{\upsilon_{t,b,1}}+\frac{y_{t,0}^2}{\upsilon_{t,b,0}}
=2r^{-k_t}(y_{t,1}^2+y_{t,0}^2)
\le4B^2r^{-k_t}.
\]
Also \(\mathbb E|Z_t|=|y_{t,1}|+|y_{t,0}|\le2B\). If \(1\le u-t\le b\), Cauchy--Schwarz and \(\operatorname{Var}(Z_t)\le\mathbb E Z_t^2\) yield
\[
|\operatorname{Cov}(Z_t,Z_u)|
\le\sqrt{\mathbb E Z_t^2\,\mathbb E Z_u^2}
\le4B^2r^{-(k_t+k_u)/2}.
\]

Now suppose \(u-t>b\) and put \(d=u-b-t\ge1\). Conditional on \(W_{u-b}\), the remaining \(b\) coordinates in the homogeneous window stay equal to that coordinate with probability \(r^b\). Since \(\upsilon_{u,b,a}=r^b/2\),
\[
\mathbb E(Z_u\mid W_{u-b}=1)=2y_{u,1},
\qquad
\mathbb E(Z_u\mid W_{u-b}=0)=-2y_{u,0}.
\]
The symmetric chain has transition identity
\[
\Pr(W_{t+d}=j\mid W_t=i)
=\frac12\{1+(2i-1)(2j-1)\lambda^d\}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\left|\mathbb E(Z_u\mid W_{1:t})-\mathbb EZ_u\right|
=\lambda^d|y_{u,1}+y_{u,0}|
\le2B\lambda^d.
\]
Because \(Z_t\) is measurable with respect to \(W_{1:t}\),
\[
|\operatorname{Cov}(Z_t,Z_u)|
\le \mathbb E|Z_t|\,2B\lambda^d
\le4B^2\lambda^d.
\]
There are \((T-b-d)_+\) pairs at a fixed \(d\). Adding the diagonal and twice the covariance sums proves
\[
\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum_{t=1}^T Z_t\right)
\le B^2V_{T,b}^{\sharp}.
\]
Unbiasedness and Jensen's inequality therefore give
\[
\mathbb E\left|\widetilde\theta_b-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|
\le\frac BT\sqrt{V_{T,b}^{\sharp}}.
\]

We next compare the sharp finite certificate with the former coarser one. Since \(r^{-k_t}\le r^{-b}=q_b\) and \(r^{-(k_t+k_{t+h})/2}\le q_b\), comparison term by term gives \(V_{T,b}^{\sharp}\le V_{T,b}\). The inequality is strict because the diagonal term at \(t=1\) is \(4\), whereas its coarser counterpart is \(4q_b>4\). The far-pair terms are identical. Furthermore, only the diagonal indices \(t\le b\) and the near pairs whose first index satisfies \(t\le b\) can contribute to the difference. There are at most \(b\) such diagonal indices and at most \(b^2\) such near pairs. Since \(q_b<e\),
\[
0<V_{T,b}-V_{T,b}^{\sharp}
\le4eb+8eb^2
\le12eb^2.
\]
This proves the asserted \(O(b^2)\) boundary difference.

For completeness, we verify the uniform coefficient for the coarser certificate. If \(b=1\), direct substitution gives \(V_{T,1}=24T-16\le(8e+4)T\). If \(b=2\), then \(q_b=9/4\), \(\lambda=1/3\), and
\[
V_{T,2}
\le45T-54+4(T-2)
\le49T<(16e+8)T.
\]
For \(b\ge3\),
\[
\sum_{d=1}^{(T-b-1)_+}(T-b-d)\lambda^d
\le(T-b)\frac{\lambda}{1-\lambda}
=(T-b)\frac{b-1}{2}.
\]
Thus
\[
V_{T,b}\le4q_bT+8q_b\left(bT-\frac{b(b+1)}2\right)+4(T-b)(b-1).
\]
Using
\[
q_b=\sum_{k=0}^b\frac1{k!}\prod_{j=0}^{k-1}\left(1-\frac jb\right),
\]
the deficits in the \(k=2\) and \(k=3\) terms imply
\[
e-q_b\ge\frac1b-\frac1{3b^2}.
\]
Also \(q_b\le e<11/4\). Hence
\[
8b(e-q_b)-4(q_b-1)
\ge8-\frac8{3b}-4(e-1)>0.
\]
The difference between \((8e+4)Tb\) and the preceding upper bound on \(V_{T,b}\) is
\[
T\{8b(e-q_b)-4(q_b-1)\}+4b\{q_b(b+1)+b-1\}>0.
\]
Therefore
\[
V_{T,b}^{\sharp}<V_{T,b}\le(8e+4)Tb.
\]
For fixed \(b\), division of the coarser formula by \(Tb\) and passage to \(T\to\infty\) give
\[
\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{V_{T,b}}{Tb}
=\frac{4q_b}{b}+8q_b+\frac8b\frac{\lambda}{1-\lambda}
=\frac{4q_b}{b}+8q_b+4\frac{b-1}{b}.
\]
The boundary-difference bound shows that \(V_{T,b}^{\sharp}\) has the same fixed-\(b\) limit. Since \(q_b\to e\),
\[
\lim_{b\to\infty}\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{V_{T,b}^{\sharp}}{Tb}=8e+4.
\]
Thus \(8e+4\) is the smallest coefficient valid uniformly in the certificate inequality \(V_{T,b}^{\sharp}\le KTb\).

It remains to compare observed outcomes with homogeneous-path outcomes. If \(t\le b+1\), then \(k_t=t-1\), and on \(H_{t,b,a}=1\) the entire history through \(t\) is \(\mathbf a_{1:t}\); the replacement error is zero. If \(t\ge b+2\), Assumptions~\(\mathrm{ass:bounded-array}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:historical-polynomial-envelope}\) give, on \(H_{t,b,a}=1\),
\[
|Y_t(W_{1:t})-y_{t,a}|
\le\min\left\{2B,C\sum_{\ell=b+1}^{t-1}(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}\right\}.
\]
For each arm and time,
\[
\mathbb E\left[\frac{H_{t,b,a}}{\upsilon_{t,b,a}}\right]=1.
\]
The triangle inequality therefore yields
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat\vartheta_b-\widetilde\theta_b|
\le\frac2T\sum_{t=b+2}^T
\min\left\{2B,C\sum_{\ell=b+1}^{t-1}(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}\right\}
=E_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b).
\]
Dropping the boundedness cap and interchanging the two finite sums gives
\[
E_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b)
\le\frac{2C}{T}\sum_{\ell=b+1}^{T-1}(T-\ell)(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}.
\]
Because \(x\mapsto x^{-(1+\beta)}\) is decreasing and \(\beta>0\), the last expression is at most
\[
\frac{2C}{\beta}\frac{(T-b-1)_+}{T}(b+1)^{-\beta}
\le\frac{2C}{\beta}\frac{(T-b-1)_+}{T}b^{-\beta}.
\]
All sums are interpreted as zero when empty. Thus replacement is exactly zero through \(t=b+1\), and the formulas cover \(T=1\), \(b=1\), and \(b=T\) without an endpoint exception.

Combining the sampling and replacement bounds proves
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E|\widehat\vartheta_b-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\le U_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b).
\]
Finally, \(V_{T,b}^{\sharp}<V_{T,b}\le(8e+4)Tb\) and the preceding tail estimate imply
\[
U_{T,\beta}^{\sharp}(b)
\le\sqrt{8e+4}\,B\sqrt{\frac bT}
+\frac{2C}{\beta}\frac{(T-b-1)_+}{T}(b+1)^{-\beta}.
\]
The carryover quantities used here are expected replacement-error upper bounds obtained from the exact exposure-weight identity. No equality with bias or maximal bias has been asserted. \(\square\)
