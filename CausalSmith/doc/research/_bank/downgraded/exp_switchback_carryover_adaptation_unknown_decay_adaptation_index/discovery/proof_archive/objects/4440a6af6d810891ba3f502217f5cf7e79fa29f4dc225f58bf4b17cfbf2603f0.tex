\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\tag{1}
\]
The supported prior behind this bound lies in the no-carryover subclass of every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), and the proof is policy-uniform over all \(\pi\in\mathcal D_T\), including outcome-adaptive kernels without positivity.

For \(T=1\), take \(W_1\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) and \(\widehat\theta_1=(2W_1-1)Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}\). Since \(\operatorname{GATE}_1(Y)=Y_1(1)-Y_1(0)\),
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat\theta_1-\operatorname{GATE}_1(Y)|
=\frac12\{|Y_1(0)|+|Y_1(1)|\}\le B.
\]
The lower bound \((1)\) equals \(B\) at \(T=1\), so \(R_1(\beta;C)=B\).

Now let \(T\ge2\). For each \(b\in\{1,\ldots,T\}\), Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\), in its proposed sharpened form, constructs an explicit stationary Markov switchback and observed-data Horvitz--Thompson estimator with worst-case risk at most \(U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b)\). It also proves the exposure floor \(\upsilon_{t,b,a}\ge1/(2q_b)>1/(2e)\), the exact auxiliary variance \(B^2V_{T,b}^{\dagger}\), and the sharp uniform coefficient \(8e-4\). Independently, boundedness gives \(|\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|\le2B\), so the zero estimator has risk at most \(2B\). Hence
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\le\min\left\{2B,\min_{1\le b\le T}U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b)\right\}.
\tag{2}
\]
The scale set is finite; the minimum is attained, and its smallest minimizer is selected before assignment from \(T,B,C,\beta\). Together with the explicit \(T=1\) branch, \((2)\) proves \(R_T(\beta;C)\le\mathfrak U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}\).

Put \(K=8e-4\), \(x=T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\), and \(b_\beta=\lceil x\rceil\). For \(T\ge2\), one has \(1<x<T\), so \(b_\beta\) is admissible and
\[
\frac{b_\beta}{x}\le1+\frac1x\le1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)},\qquad b_\beta\ge x.
\tag{3}
\]
The sharpened bound from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\), \((T-b_\beta-1)_+/T\le1\), \((b_\beta+1)^{-\beta}\le b_\beta^{-\beta}\), and \((3)\) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b_\beta)
&\le\sqrt K\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}+\frac C\beta b_\beta^{-\beta}\\
&\le\left[\sqrt{K\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac C\beta\right]T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
At \(T=1\), the piecewise certificate is \(B\), and the coefficient in \((4)\) exceeds \(B\), so the displayed chain remains valid. Finally,
\[
2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\le2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)},\qquad \frac1\beta\le\frac1{\beta_-},
\]
and therefore the coefficient in \((4)\) is at most
\[
C_0^\star=\sqrt{(8e-4)\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)}\right)}\,B+\frac C{\beta_-}.
\]
This proves the proposed lower and upper chains. The conditional replacement calculation sharpens only the valid expected replacement-error upper bound. The auxiliary variance remains exact only for the homogeneous-history process; neither the replacement term, the full estimator-risk certificate, nor its finite minimum is asserted to be exact. The lower and upper growing-horizon exponents remain unmatched. \(\square\)
