\textbf{Proof.} The realized history and the constant history \(\mathbf a_{1:t}\) have the same current treatment. If \(t\le b\), all of their preceding coordinates agree, so the difference is zero. If \(t>b\), they agree at lags \(1,\ldots,b\). The historical-envelope assumption therefore gives
\[
\left|Y_t(W_{1:t})-Y_t(\mathbf a_{1:t})\right|
\le C\sum_{\ell=b+1}^{t-1}(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}.
\]
Since the summand is decreasing,
\[
\sum_{\ell=b+1}^{\infty}(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}
\le \int_b^\infty(1+x)^{-(1+\beta)}\,dx
=\frac{(1+b)^{-\beta}}{\beta}
\le \frac{b^{-\beta}}{\beta_-}.
\]
This proves the first inequality. Apply it once with \(a=1\) and once with \(a=0\), and use the triangle inequality, to obtain the contrast bound. \(\square\)
