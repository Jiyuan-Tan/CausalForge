Let \(b_-=\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\rceil\) and \(C_0=\sqrt{24e}\,B+2C/\beta_-\). The complete policy-uniform adaptation statement currently proved is
\[
1\le A_T(C)\le
\frac{\sqrt{12e}\,B\sqrt{b_-/T}+(2C/\beta_-)((T-b_--1)_+/T)b_-^{-\beta_-}}
{B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}}
\le\frac{\sqrt2\,C_0}{B}T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)}.
\]
The upper endpoint is attained by one stationary Markov switchback and observed-data Horvitz--Thompson estimator tuned to \(\beta_-\), and the lower endpoint holds for all sequential and outcome-adaptive kernels. More locally, whenever
\[
\mathbb E_{P_{1,\pi}}\sum_{t=2b}^Tq_t(W_{1:t})^2\le\frac{3a^2}{64},
\]
every estimator has worst-case risk at least \(s_b/25\). If \(\eta=1/2\) is allowed by the amplitude condition, then
\[
\frac{C}{2^{2+\beta}}b^{-\beta}\le s_b\le\frac C2b^{-\beta},
\]
so this is a tail-order lower bound on the low-energy subregime. Independently, for every arm \(\alpha\) and persistence length \(K\),
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}\mathbb E_\pi|\widehat\theta-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\ge\frac{(T-K)\delta_K}{2T}\{1-Q_\pi(E_{\alpha,K})\}.
\]
These pieces imply neither a divergent \(g_T\), a subpolynomial common-design ratio, nor a sharp order. The strongest certified adaptation lower factor remains \(g_T=1\).
