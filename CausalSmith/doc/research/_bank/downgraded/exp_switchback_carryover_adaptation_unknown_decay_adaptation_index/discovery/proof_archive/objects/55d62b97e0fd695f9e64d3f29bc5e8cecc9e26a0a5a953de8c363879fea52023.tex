\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}
\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\tag{1}
\]
The supported prior behind (1) belongs to the no-carryover subclass of every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), is fixed before assignment, and leaves one independent counterfactual sign hidden at each time. The bound applies separately to every \(\pi\in\mathcal D_T\) and every observed-data estimator, including deterministic, history-dependent, and outcome-adaptive kernels without a positivity condition.

For \(T=1\), take \(W_1\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) and
\[
\widehat\theta_1=(2W_1-1)Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}.
\]
Since \(\operatorname{GATE}_1(Y)=Y_1(1)-Y_1(0)\), the absolute error is \(|Y_1(0)|\) when \(W_1=1\) and \(|Y_1(1)|\) when \(W_1=0\). Boundedness gives
\[
\mathbb E|\widehat\theta_1-\operatorname{GATE}_1(Y)|
=\frac12\{|Y_1(0)|+|Y_1(1)|\}\le B.
\]
Thus \(R_1(\beta;C)\le B\), while (1) equals \(B\) at \(T=1\). Hence
\[
R_1(\beta;C)=B.
\tag{2}
\]

Suppose \(T\ge2\). Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) constructs, for every \(b\in\{1,\ldots,T\}\), a stationary Markov design and an observed-data Horvitz--Thompson estimator with worst-case risk at most \(U_{T,\beta}^{\dagger}(b)\). It also proves the exposure floor
\[
\upsilon_{t,b,a}\ge\frac{1}{2q_b}>\frac{1}{2e},
\]
with unattained uniform infimum \(1/(2e)\). Independently, boundedness implies
\[
|\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\le\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T\{|Y_t(\mathbf1_{1:t})|+|Y_t(\mathbf0_{1:t})|\}
\le2B,
\]
so the zero estimator has risk at most \(2B\). Therefore
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\le\min\left\{2B,\min_{1\le b\le T}U_{T,\beta}^{\dagger}(b)\right\}
\qquad(T\ge2).
\tag{3}
\]
The set of scales is finite, so the minimum is attained; the smallest minimizing scale is a deterministic function of \(T,B,C,\beta\) and may be selected before assignment. Equations (2)--(3) prove \(R_T(\beta;C)\le\mathfrak U_{T,\beta}^{\dagger}\). The one-period branch comes from the explicit Bernoulli procedure, not from a claim that \(U_{1,\beta}^{\dagger}(1)=B\).

Put \(K=8e-4\), \(x=T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\), and \(b_\beta=\lceil x\rceil\). When \(T\ge2\),
\[
\frac{b_\beta}{x}\le1+\frac1x\le1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad
b_\beta\ge x.
\tag{4}
\]
The final inequality in Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\), followed by (4), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
U_{T,\beta}^{\dagger}(b_\beta)
&\le\sqrt K\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}+\frac{2C}{\beta}b_\beta^{-\beta}\\
&\le\left[\sqrt{K\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta}\right]T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]
At \(T=1\), the piecewise certificate is \(B\), which is no larger than the right side of (5). This proves the pointwise upper chain for every horizon.

Finally, \(\beta\le\beta_+\) implies
\[
2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\le2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)},
\]
and \(\beta\ge\beta_->0\) implies \(1/\beta\le1/\beta_-\). Hence the coefficient in (5) is at most
\[
\sqrt{(8e-4)\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}=C_0^\star.
\]
The exact identity for \(B^2V_{T,b}^{\dagger}\) is an auxiliary homogeneous-history variance identity. The carryover summand remains a capped, endpoint-aware expected replacement-error upper bound. Thus neither the optimized certificate nor either summand is identified with oracle risk, exact estimator risk, exact bias, or maximal carryover error, and the lower and upper exponents remain unmatched. \(\square\)
