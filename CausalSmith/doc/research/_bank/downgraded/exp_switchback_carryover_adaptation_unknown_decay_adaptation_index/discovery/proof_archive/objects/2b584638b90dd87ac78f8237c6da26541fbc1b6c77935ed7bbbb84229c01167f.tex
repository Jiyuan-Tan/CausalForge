\textbf{Proof.} For \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\) and every lag \(\ell\ge1\),
\[
C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}\le C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta_-)}.
\]
Thus the member conditions in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:poly-class}\) imply
\[
\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\subseteq\mathcal P_{T,\beta_-}(C).
\tag{1}
\]
For a design-estimator pair write
\[
\mathcal R_\beta(\pi,\widehat\theta)=\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}\mathbb E_\pi|\widehat\theta-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|.
\]
By definition, \(\mathcal R_\beta(\pi,\widehat\theta)\ge R_T(\beta;C)\), and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\) makes the denominator positive. Every oracle ratio is therefore at least one, so \(A_T(C)\ge1\).

Suppose \(T\ge2\). Choose the smallest minimizer of \(U_{T,\beta_-}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b)\) over the finite scale set and compare its Markov procedure with the zero estimator, selecting the branch with the smaller displayed certificate. This selection uses only \(T,B,C,\beta_-\) and occurs before assignment. The proposed sharpened Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\), boundedness for the zero branch, and nestedness \((1)\) give one common pair \((\pi^\star,\widehat\theta^\star)\), independent of the realized \(\beta\), such that
\[
\mathcal R_\beta(\pi^\star,\widehat\theta^\star)\le N_T^\star
\qquad\text{for every }\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+].
\tag{2}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\) gives
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}>0.
\tag{3}
\]
Using the common pair in the definition of \(A_T(C)\), \((2)\)--\((3)\) yield
\[
A_T(C)\le\frac{N_T^\star}{D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}}.
\tag{4}
\]
Since \(b_-=\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\rceil\) is admissible,
\[
N_T^\star\le U_{T,\beta_-}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b_-)=N_T^{\mathrm{fin}}.
\tag{5}
\]
The proposed sharpened Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:oracle-rate}\), evaluated at \(\beta_-\) and its explicit scale, gives
\[
N_T^{\mathrm{fin}}
\le\left[\sqrt{(8e-4)\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_-+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac C{\beta_-}\right]T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)}.
\tag{6}
\]
Also \(D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}\ge B/\sqrt{2T}\). Dividing \((6)\) by this positive quantity and using
\[
\frac12-\frac{\beta_-}{2\beta_-+1}=\frac1{4\beta_-+2}
\]
proves the asserted polynomial upper certificate.

At \(T=1\), Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:oracle-rate}\) gives \(R_1(\beta;C)=B\) for every \(\beta\), and its fair-Bernoulli procedure has simultaneous risk at most \(B\). Hence \(A_1(C)\le1\), while the general lower bound gives equality. The piecewise definitions give \(N_1^\star=N_1^{\mathrm{fin}}=D_1^{\mathrm{bin}}=B\).

The Markov exposure floor and its unattained infimum are inherited from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\). The numerator coefficient is endpoint-specific because the common design may use the known lower endpoint \(\beta_-\). The denominator is the exact binomial Bayes lower certificate rather than the unknown oracle risk, except at \(T=1\). The exponent is positive, so this argument proves neither K1, K2, nor a sharp adaptation order. \(\square\)
