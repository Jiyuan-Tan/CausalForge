\textbf{Proof.} Put \(x=T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\). Since \(T\ge2\), \(1\le x\le T\), and hence \(x\le b_\beta\le x+1\le2x\). It follows that
\[
\sqrt{b_\beta/T}
\le \sqrt2\,T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad
Cb_\beta^{-\beta}
\le C T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\frac{b_\beta}{T}
\le 2T^{-2\beta/(2\beta+1)}
\le 2T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)},
\]
and \(T^{-1/2}\le T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}\) because \(\beta/(2\beta+1)<1/2\). The displayed conclusion follows after collecting constants, uniformly over the compact interval \([\beta_-,\beta_+]\). \(\square\)
