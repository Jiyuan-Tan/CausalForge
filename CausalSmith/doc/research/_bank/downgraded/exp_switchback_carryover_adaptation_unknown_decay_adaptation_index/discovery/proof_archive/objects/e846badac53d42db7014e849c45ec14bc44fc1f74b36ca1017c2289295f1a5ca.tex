\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\tag{1}
\]
Its supported no-carryover prior belongs to every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), and its likelihood argument covers every \(\pi\in\mathcal D_T\), including deterministic, history-dependent, and outcome-adaptive kernels without positivity.

For \(T=1\), take \(W_1\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) and \(\widehat\theta_1=(2W_1-1)Y_1^{\mathrm{obs}}\). Direct enumeration gives
\[
\mathbb E\left|\widehat\theta_1-\operatorname{GATE}_1(Y)\right|
=\frac12\bigl\{|Y_1(0)|+|Y_1(1)|\bigr\}\le B.
\tag{2}
\]
The lower bound in \((1)\) equals \(B\) at \(T=1\), proving \(R_1(\beta;C)=B\).

Suppose \(T\ge2\). For every \(b\in\{1,\ldots,T\}\), Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) constructs the stated stationary Markov switchback and observed-data Horvitz--Thompson estimator and proves
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b)}}\left|\widehat\vartheta_b-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|
\le U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b).
\tag{3}
\]
The same lemma proves the exposure floor \(\upsilon_{t,b,a}\ge1/(2q_b)>1/(2e)\), with unattained uniform infimum \(1/(2e)\), and proves that the exact worst-case auxiliary homogeneous-history variance is \(B^2V_{T,b}^{\dagger}\), whose smallest uniform coefficient is \(8e-4\). Boundedness separately gives \(|\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|\le2B\), so the zero estimator has risk at most \(2B\). Therefore
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\le\min\left\{2B,\min_{1\le b\le T}U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b)\right\}
=\mathfrak U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}.
\tag{4}
\]
The index set is finite, so the inner minimum is attained and its smallest minimizing scale is selectable before assignment from \(T,B,C,\beta\). Together with \((2)\), this proves the first upper inequality for all \(T\).

Put \(K=8e-4\), \(x=T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\), and \(b_\beta=\lceil x\rceil\). For \(T\ge2\), one has \(1<x<T\), hence \(b_\beta\in\{1,\ldots,T\}\), and
\[
\frac{b_\beta}{x}\le1+\frac1x\le1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad b_\beta\ge x.
\tag{5}
\]
The final inequality of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\), together with \((T-b_\beta-1)_+/T\le1\), \((b_\beta+1)^{-\beta}\le b_\beta^{-\beta}\), and \((5)\), yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
U_{T,\beta}^{\mathrm{cond}}(b_\beta)
&\le\sqrt K\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}+\frac C\beta b_\beta^{-\beta}\\
&\le\left[\sqrt{K\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac C\beta\right]T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
At \(T=1\), the piecewise certificate is \(B\), and the coefficient in \((6)\) exceeds \(B\), so the same chain holds. Finally,
\[
2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\le2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)},
\qquad \beta^{-1}\le\beta_-^{-1}.
\tag{7}
\]
Substituting \(K=8e-4\) into \((6)\) and applying \((7)\) gives the coefficient \(C_0^\star\).

The conditional mismatch calculation used in \((3)\) bounds an expected replacement error; it is not an exact bias or maximal error. Exactness of \(B^2V_{T,b}^{\dagger}\) concerns only the auxiliary homogeneous-history variance and implies neither exact observed-data risk nor equality between the optimized certificate and oracle risk. Thus the lower and upper growing-horizon exponents remain unmatched. \(\square\)
