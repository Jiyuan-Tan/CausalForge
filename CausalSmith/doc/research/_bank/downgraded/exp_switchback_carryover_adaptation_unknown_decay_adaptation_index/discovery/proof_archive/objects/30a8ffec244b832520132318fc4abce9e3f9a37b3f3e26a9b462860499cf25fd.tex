\textbf{Proof.} For the first array, \(0<a_m\le B/2\), so boundedness holds. If two histories with the same current treatment are compared at time \(t\), the corresponding outcomes can differ only through the lag-\((m+1)\) coordinate. Hence
\[
|Y_t(z_{1:t})-Y_t(z'_{1:t})|
\le a_m\mathbf1\{z_{t-m-1}\ne z'_{t-m-1}\}
\le C(2+m)^{-(1+\beta)}\mathbf1\{z_{t-m-1}\ne z'_{t-m-1}\},
\]
which is one term of the required historical envelope. At \(t=m+2\), choose two histories that agree in coordinates \(2,\ldots,m+2\) and differ in coordinate one. They agree on \(z_{(t-m)\vee1:t}=z_{2:m+2}\), but their outcomes differ by \(a_m>0\); thus the array is not in \(\mathcal F_{T,m}\).

For the second array, put
\[
S=\sum_{u=1}^{T-1}(1+u)^{-(1+\beta)},
\qquad
D=2\max\{1,(C/B)S\}.
\]
Then \(\gamma_\ell=C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}/D>0\), and
\[
0\le Y_t(z_{1:t})
\le \sum_{\ell=1}^{T-1}\gamma_\ell
=\frac{CS}{2\max\{1,(C/B)S\}}
\le \frac B2.
\]
For equal-current histories, the triangle inequality gives
\[
|Y_t(z_{1:t})-Y_t(z'_{1:t})|
\le\sum_{\ell=1}^{t-1}\gamma_\ell
\mathbf1\{z_{t-\ell}\ne z'_{t-\ell}\}
\le\sum_{\ell=1}^{t-1}C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}
\mathbf1\{z_{t-\ell}\ne z'_{t-\ell}\},
\]
because \(D\ge2\). Thus this array is admissible. For any \(m<T-1\), repeat the preceding pair-of-histories argument at \(t=m+2\); the outcomes now differ by \(\gamma_{m+1}>0\). Hence the array is outside \(\mathcal F_{T,m}\). Finally, the zero array belongs to the intersection, while the first witness belongs to \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\) but not to the intersection, proving strict inclusion. \(\square\)
