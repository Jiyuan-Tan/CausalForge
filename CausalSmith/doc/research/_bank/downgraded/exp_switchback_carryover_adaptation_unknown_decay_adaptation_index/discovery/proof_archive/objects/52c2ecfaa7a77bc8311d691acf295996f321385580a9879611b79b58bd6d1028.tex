\textbf{Proof.} For any fixed design-estimator pair and any \(\beta\), its worst-case risk over \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\) is at least \(R_T(\beta;C)\). Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\) makes this denominator strictly positive. Thus every ratio in the definition of \(A_T(C)\) is at least one, and hence \(A_T(C)\ge1\).

For the upper bound, put
\[
b_-=\left\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\right\rceil
\]
and use the single stationary Markov design and observed-data estimator of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) with this value of \(b\). For every \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\) and every \(\ell\ge1\),
\[
C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}
\le C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta_-)}.
\]
Therefore the definitions imply the class nesting
\[
\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\subseteq\mathcal P_{T,\beta_-}(C).
\]
Applying the sharpened Markov-washout bound at \(\beta_-\), together with
\[
b_-\le2T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)},
\qquad
b_-^{-\beta_-}\le T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)},
\]
gives, simultaneously for all \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_-)}}|\widehat\vartheta_{b_-}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\le C_0T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)},
\qquad
C_0=\sqrt{24e}\,B+\frac{2eC}{\beta_-}.
\]
On the other hand, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\) gives
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}
\]
for every such \(\beta\). The risk ratio of this one common procedure is consequently at most
\[
\frac{C_0T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)}}{B/\sqrt{2T}}
=\frac{\sqrt2\,C_0}{B}T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)}.
\]
Taking the supremum over \(\beta\) and then using the infimum defining \(A_T(C)\) proves the upper bound. This calculation gives only the displayed polynomial bracket and makes no sharpness claim. \(\square\)
