Put \(K=8e+4\) and \[C_0^\star=\sqrt{K\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_++1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}.\] For every \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\), the beta-aware oracle satisfies \[\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}\le R_T(\beta;C)\le\min\left\{2B,\min_{1\le b\le T}U_{T,\beta}(b)\right\}\le\left[\sqrt{K\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta}\right]T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}\le C_0^\star T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.\] With \(b_-=\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\rceil\), \(N_T^{\mathrm{fin}}=U_{T,\beta_-}(b_-)\), \(N_T^\star=\min\{2B,\min_{1\le b\le T}U_{T,\beta_-}(b)\}\), and \(D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}=B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}\), one common procedure fixed before assignment satisfies \[1\le A_T(C)\le\frac{N_T^\star}{D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}}\le\frac{N_T^{\mathrm{fin}}}{D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}}\le\frac{\sqrt2}{B}\left[\sqrt{K\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_-+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}\right]T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)}.\] The last coefficient is endpoint-specific; it is not the larger \(\beta_+\)-uniform constant. Under the rough-prior low-energy condition the rough-class risk is at least \(s_b/25\), and the endpoint construction separately gives \[\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}\mathbb E_\pi\left|\widehat\theta-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|\ge\frac{(T-K')\delta_{K'}}{2T}\{1-Q_\pi(E_{\alpha,K'})\}.\] Here \(K'\) denotes the endpoint-bump run length, distinct from the variance constant \(K=8e+4\). These results certify only \(g_T=1\) and a polynomial common-design upper bound; they prove neither (K1) nor (K2), which remain logically compatible.
