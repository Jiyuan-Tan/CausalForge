\textbf{Proof.} Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\ge B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}
\ge\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}>0.
\]
The supported prior behind this inequality is drawn before assignment, belongs to the no-carryover subclass of every \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\), and leaves an independent counterfactual sign hidden at every time. Its conditional Bayes-risk argument applies separately to every \(\pi\in\mathcal D_T\) and every observed-data estimator. Thus the lower chain holds for deterministic, history-dependent, and outcome-adaptive kernels without a positivity restriction.

For the upper bound, put
\[
b_\beta=\left\lceil T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\right\rceil.
\]
If \(T=1\), then \(b_\beta=1\). If \(T\ge2\), let \(x=T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\). Because \(\beta>0\), one has \(1\le x\le T\), and since \(T\) is an integer this gives \(\lceil x\rceil\le T\). Separately,
\[
\lceil x\rceil\le x+1\le2x.
\]
Thus \(b_\beta\) is admissible for every \(T\ge1\); no inequality of the form \(2x\le T\) is used.

Apply Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) with \(b=b_\beta\). Write
\[
q_{b_\beta}=\left(1+\frac1{b_\beta}\right)^{b_\beta},
\qquad
\lambda_{b_\beta}=\frac{b_\beta-1}{b_\beta+1},
\]
and let
\[
V_{T,b_\beta}=4q_{b_\beta}T+8q_{b_\beta}\left(b_\beta T-\frac{b_\beta(b_\beta+1)}2\right)
+8\sum_{d=1}^{(T-b_\beta-1)_+}(T-b_\beta-d)\lambda_{b_\beta}^d.
\]
The lemma supplies the explicit finite guarantee
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\le\frac BT\sqrt{V_{T,b_\beta}}
+\frac{2C}{T}\sum_{\ell=b_\beta+1}^{T-1}(T-\ell)(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}.
\]
Its variance and endpoint-aware replacement-error corollaries imply
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\le \sqrt{10e+2}\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}
+\frac{2C}{\beta}\frac{(T-b_\beta-1)_+}{T}b_\beta^{-\beta}.
\]
For \(T\ge2\), the two separate ceiling bounds \(b_\beta\le2x\) and \(b_\beta\ge x\), together with \((T-b_\beta-1)_+/T\le1\), give
\[
\sqrt{\frac{b_\beta}{T}}
\le\sqrt2\,T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)},
\qquad
\frac{(T-b_\beta-1)_+}{T}b_\beta^{-\beta}
\le T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
When \(T=1\), the finite replacement sum is zero and \(V_{1,1}=8\), so the same final upper bound holds. Since \(\beta\ge\beta_->0\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)
\le\left(\sqrt{20e+4}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}\right)
T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}
=C_0T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}.
\]
The Markov design is beta-aware through \(b_\beta\), every homogeneous-exposure probability is positive, and the estimator uses only \((W,Y^{\mathrm{obs}})\). The carryover term above is an endpoint-aware expected replacement-error upper bound obtained using the exact exposure-weight identity, not an exact bias. The proved lower exponent is \(1/2\), whereas the upper exponent is \(\beta/(2\beta+1)\); no matching lower bound at the latter exponent follows. \(\square\)
