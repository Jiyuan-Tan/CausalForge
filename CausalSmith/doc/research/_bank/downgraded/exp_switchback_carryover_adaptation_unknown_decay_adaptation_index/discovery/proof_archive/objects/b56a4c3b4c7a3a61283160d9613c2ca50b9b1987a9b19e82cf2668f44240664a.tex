\textbf{Proof.} Put \(r_-=\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)\). If \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\), then, for every \(\ell\ge1\), \[C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}\le C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta_-)}.\] The definitions of the envelope classes therefore give the nesting relation \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\subseteq\mathcal P_{T,\beta_-}(C)\). Use, for every value of \(\beta\), the one design--estimator pair supplied by the upper-bound part of Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm:oracle-rate}\) at \(\beta_-\), namely the Markov-washout procedure with \(b=\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\rceil\). Class nesting and that theorem imply \[\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}\mathbb E_\pi\left|\widehat\theta-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)\right|\le C_0T^{-r_-}\] simultaneously for every \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\). Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\) gives \(R_T(\beta;C)\ge B/\sqrt{3T}\) pointwise in \(\beta\). Hence this single pair satisfies \[\sup_{\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]}\frac{\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}\mathbb E_\pi|\widehat\theta-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|}{R_T(\beta;C)}\le \frac{\sqrt3\,C_0}{B}T^{1/2-r_-}=\frac{\sqrt3\,C_0}{B}T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)}.\] Taking the infimum over all common design--estimator pairs proves the upper bound. The lower bound \(A_T(C)\ge1\) is the second conclusion of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:positive-oracle-risk}\). \(\square\)
