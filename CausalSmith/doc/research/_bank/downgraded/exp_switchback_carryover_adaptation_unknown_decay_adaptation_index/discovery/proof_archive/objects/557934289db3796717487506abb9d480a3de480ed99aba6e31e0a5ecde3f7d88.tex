\textbf{Proof.} Fix any design-estimator pair and any \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\). Its worst-case risk over \(\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\) is at least the infimum \(R_T(\beta;C)\). Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\) makes this denominator strictly positive. Hence every ratio in the definition of \(A_T(C)\) is at least one, and taking the supremum over \(\beta\) and then the infimum over procedures gives \(A_T(C)\ge1\).

For the upper bound, put
\[
b_-=\left\lceil T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\right\rceil
\]
and use the single stationary Markov design and observed-data estimator from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) with \(b=b_-\). For every \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\) and \(\ell\ge1\),
\[
C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta)}
\le C(1+\ell)^{-(1+\beta_-)}.
\]
Thus the class definitions imply
\[
\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)\subseteq\mathcal P_{T,\beta_-}(C).
\]
Applying the expected replacement-error upper bound of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:markov-washout-oracle-upper}\) at \(\beta_-\), which was obtained with the exact identity \(\mathbb E[H_{t,b,a}/\upsilon_{t,b,a}]=1\), gives simultaneously for every \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\)
\[
\sup_{Y\in\mathcal P_{T,\beta}(C)}
\mathbb E_{\pi^{(b_-)}}
|\widehat\vartheta_{b_-}-\operatorname{GATE}_T(Y)|
\le N_T,
\]
where
\[
N_T=\sqrt{12e}\,B\sqrt{\frac{b_-}{T}}
+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}\frac{(T-b_--1)_+}{T}b_-^{-\beta_-}.
\]
On the other hand, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:exact-positive-oracle-risk}\) gives, for every such \(\beta\),
\[
R_T(\beta;C)\ge D_T,
\qquad
D_T=B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}>0.
\]
The risk ratio of this one common procedure is at most \(N_T/D_T\). Taking the supremum over \(\beta\) and then the infimum defining \(A_T(C)\) proves
\[
A_T(C)\le
\frac{\sqrt{12e}\,B\sqrt{b_-/T}+(2C/\beta_-)((T-b_--1)_+/T)b_-^{-\beta_-}}
{B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}}.
\]

For the polynomial corollary, if \(T\ge2\), the ceiling inequalities with \(x=T^{1/(2\beta_-+1)}\) give \(b_-\le2x\) and \(b_-\ge x\). Therefore
\[
N_T\le
\left(\sqrt{24e}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}\right)
T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)}
=C_0T^{-\beta_-/(2\beta_-+1)}.
\]
For \(T=1\), \(b_-=1\), the carryover summand is zero, and \(N_T=\sqrt{12e}B\le C_0\), so the same inequality holds. Since \(D_T\ge B/\sqrt{2T}\),
\[
A_T(C)
\le\frac{\sqrt2\,C_0}{B}T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)}.
\]
Both upper bounds use the same procedure, independent of the realized \(\beta\). The argument proves neither divergence, subpolynomial adaptation, nor sharpness. \(\square\)
