For every \(b'\ge b_*\), monotonicity of the deterministic biases gives \(d_{b'}\le d_{b_*}\le P_{b_*}\). By two applications of the triangle inequality and the assumed candidate-wise bounds,
\[
\begin{aligned}
|\widehat\theta_{b_*}-\widehat\theta_{b'}|-P_{b_*}-P_{b'}
&\le |\mu_{b_*}-\mu_{b'}|\\
&\le |\mu_{b_*}-\theta|+|\mu_{b'}-\theta|\\
&\le d_{b_*}+d_{b'}\le 2P_{b_*}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Taking positive parts and the maximum over \(b'\ge b_*\) yields \(\Delta_{b_*}^+\le 2P_{b_*}\). The minimizing property of \(\widehat b\) therefore implies
\[
\Delta_{\widehat b}^++P_{\widehat b}
\le \Delta_{b_*}^++P_{b_*}
\le 3P_{b_*}.
\]
Suppose first that \(\widehat b\le b_*\). Since \(b_*\) is then among the indices in the maximum defining \(\Delta_{\widehat b}^+\),
\[
|\widehat\theta_{\widehat b}-\widehat\theta_{b_*}|
\le \Delta_{\widehat b}^++P_{\widehat b}+P_{b_*}
\le 4P_{b_*}.
\]
Moreover,
\[
|\widehat\theta_{b_*}-\theta|
\le |\widehat\theta_{b_*}-\mu_{b_*}|+|\mu_{b_*}-\theta|
\le P_{b_*}+d_{b_*}
\le 2P_{b_*}.
\]
A final triangle inequality gives \(|\widehat\theta_{\widehat b}-\theta|\le 6P_{b_*}\).

Suppose instead that \(\widehat b>b_*\). Since \(\Delta_{\widehat b}^+\ge0\), the minimizing bound gives \(P_{\widehat b}\le3P_{b_*}\); since the biases are nonincreasing, \(d_{\widehat b}\le d_{b_*}\le P_{b_*}\). Hence
\[
|\widehat\theta_{\widehat b}-\theta|
\le |\widehat\theta_{\widehat b}-\mu_{\widehat b}|+|\mu_{\widehat b}-\theta|
\le P_{\widehat b}+d_{\widehat b}
\le 4P_{b_*}
\le 6P_{b_*}.
\]
This proves the claim. The argument uses comparisons from \(b\) to indices \(b'\ge b\), exactly because the bias bound decreases as the scale increases.
