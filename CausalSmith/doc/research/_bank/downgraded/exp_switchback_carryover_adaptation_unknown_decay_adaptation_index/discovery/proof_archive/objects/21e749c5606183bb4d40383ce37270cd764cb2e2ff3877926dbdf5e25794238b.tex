The closed results give, uniformly over \(\beta\in[\beta_-,\beta_+]\),
\[
\frac{B}{\sqrt{2T}}\le B2^{-(T-1)}\binom{T-1}{\lfloor(T-1)/2\rfloor}\le R_T(\beta;C)\le U_{T,\beta}(b_\beta)\le\left[\sqrt{(8e+4)\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta}\right]T^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)},
\]
where \(b_\beta=\lceil T^{1/(2\beta+1)}\rceil\), and the exact boundary identity is \(R_1(\beta;C)=B\). Every constructive Markov branch satisfies \(\upsilon_{t,b,a}\ge1/(2q_b)>1/(2e)\), with sharp uniform infimum \(1/(2e)\). The rule fixed before assignment and tuned only to \(\beta_-\) proves
\[
1\le A_T(C)\le\frac{N_T^\star}{D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}}\le\frac{N_T^{\mathrm{fin}}}{D_T^{\mathrm{bin}}}\le\frac{\sqrt2}{B}\left[\sqrt{(8e+4)\left(1+2^{-1/(2\beta_-+1)}\right)}\,B+\frac{2C}{\beta_-}\right]T^{1/(4\beta_-+2)},
\]
and \(A_1(C)=1\). For the lower-bound side, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:rough-prior-low-energy-risk}\) gives rough-class risk at least \(s_b/25\) under its stated low-energy condition, and Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem:endpoint-bump-persistence-risk}\) gives the explicit homogeneous-run failure bound. These results certify only \(g_T=1\) and the displayed polynomial common-design upper certificate; they prove neither K1 nor K2.
